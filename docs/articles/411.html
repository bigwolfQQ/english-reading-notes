文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...a folding US$1,999 iPhone aimed at shaking up a category long dominated by Samsung and Chinese companies such as Huawei Technologies Co...
    句中的 "aimed at shaking up..." 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "a folding US$1,999 iPhone"，相當於形容詞子句 "which is aimed at shaking up..." 的簡化版，省略了關係代名詞與be動詞，使商業新聞的句子更為精簡有力。
  • 附加說明性質的名詞片語 (Appositive Phrase)
    Apple’s newly appointed chief executive officer John Ternus criticized existing foldables...
    句中的 "newly appointed chief executive officer" 是同位語（Appositive），用來補充說明其後方人名 "John Ternus" 的職稱與身分，這種結構在財經與人物報導中非常常見。
  • 現在分詞表附帶狀況 (Present Participle Indicating Accompanying Circumstance)
    Samsung Electronics Co launched a publicity campaign for its foldable phones, touting its hardware advantage and its early entry into the segment as it seeks to fend off a major challenge from Apple Inc.
    逗號之後的 "touting its hardware advantage..." 是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明主句動作發生時的伴隨狀況，意指三星在發起宣傳活動的同時，也「大肆宣揚」自身的優勢。
