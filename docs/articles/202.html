來自台灣中部的筆記：台灣的人口未爆彈

Notes from Central Taiwan: Taiwan’s demographic time bomb

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • shrivel v. 枯萎，萎縮，減少
    Another doleful set of demographic announcements this month from the National Development Council (NDC), with the number of young people shriveling as the proportion of old people grows.
    國家發展委員會（國發會）這個月發布了另一組令人沮喪的人口統計公告，隨著老年人口比例增長，年輕人口數正急劇萎縮。
  • workforce n. 勞動力，全體員工
    Although this could include foreign recruits and retraining those already in the workforce, the math is stark...
    儘管這可能包含外籍新血以及對現有勞動人口的再培訓，但計算結果卻是殘酷的...
  • plummet v. 暴跌，急速下降
    Lai’s 500,000 AI talents may be rhetoric, given plummeting birth rates.
    鑑於出生率暴跌，賴清德的 50萬 AI 人才可能只是修辭。
  • vacancy n. 職缺，空缺
    The NDC projects that Taiwan will be short 480,000 workers by 2030, a number widely quoted and likely an underestimate given that the demographic figures released this month were downward revisions of earlier estimates (the labor shortage number was 276,000 job vacancies in September last year).
    國發會預估，到 2030 年台灣將短缺 48萬名勞工，這個數字被廣泛引用，且鑑於本月發布的人口數據是對早期估計的向下修正（去年 9 月的勞動力短缺數為 276,000 個職缺），這很可能是一個低估。
  • retain v. 保留，留住（員工、人才等）
    At home wages and benefits will have to rise as well if firms are to retain personnel, cutting into Taiwan’s major competitive advantage of underpaid, overworked labor.
    如果企業要留住人才，國內的薪資和福利也必須提高，這將侵蝕台灣低薪、過勞勞動力的主要競爭優勢。
  • consequence n. 結果，後果
    A key consequence of aging is slowing economic growth.
    高齡化的一個關鍵後果就是經濟成長放緩。
  • subsidy n. 補助金，津貼（文章中使用形容詞 subsidized）
    The Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) candidate for Taitung, Chen Ying (陳瑩), promised that if elected, she would provide fully subsidized dentures, hearing aids and smart bracelets for the elderly.
    民進黨的台東縣長候選人陳瑩承諾，若當選將為老人提供完全補助的假牙、助聽器和智慧手環。
  • launch v. 發起，推出（新計畫、產品等）
    The Lai administration is going to launch its “health tokens” program next month, a month before the November local elections.
    賴清德政府將於下個月（即 11 月地方選舉前一個月）推出其「健康憑證」計畫。
  • project v. 預估，預測
    The NDC projects that Taiwan will be short 480,000 workers by 2030...
    國發會預估，到 2030 年台灣將短缺 48萬名勞工...

文法重點

  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況（With + 名詞 + 分詞）
    Another doleful set of demographic announcements this month from the National Development Council (NDC), with the number of young people shriveling as the proportion of old people grows.
    此句使用「With + O. + 分詞」的結構，用來補充說明主要子句發生時的附帶狀況或背景。這裡的 shriveling 是現在分詞，用來修飾前面的受詞 the number of young people，表示年輕人口「正在萎縮」的主動概念。
  • 分詞片語作後置修飾語
    For the period from 2010 to 2018, representing people who will be in their 20s and 30s and trainable as AI talents around 2040, the total number of births was just under 1.8 million.
    句中的「representing people who will be...」是由現在分詞 representing 引導的分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞片語 the period from 2010 to 2018，相當於關代子句「which represented people...」，可使句子更為精簡流暢。
  • 假設語氣（過去式表示與現在事實相反）
    One has to wonder: If you were Beijing, would you want to annex all the problems Taiwan is going to experience in the next two decades?
    此句使用與現在事實相反的假設語氣。條件句（if 子句）中的動詞使用過去式 were（代替 was），而主要子句則使用「would + 原形動詞（would want）」，用來表達假設「如果你是北京（事實上你不是）」所產生的情境。
  • 形容詞片語作後置修飾語
    Professionals and technical workers — who may have greater opportunities to work abroad — make up a substantial share of these vacancies, as current labor market shortages show.
    句中夾帶了一個由關係代名詞 who 引導的非限定性關係子句（who may have greater opportunities to work abroad），用來補充說明主詞 Professionals and technical workers 的特徵，逗號前後可視為補充插入語。
