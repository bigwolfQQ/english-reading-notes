美國將禁止加拿大乳製品與酒類進口

US to ban Canadian dairy and alcohol

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • import n. / v. 進口
    The US on Tuesday banned a broad swath of Canadian alcoholic beverages, motorcycles and dairy products from import, sharply escalating an already acrimonious trade spat.
    美國週二禁止進口大量加拿大的酒精飲料、機車與乳製品，使一場本已嚴峻的貿易爭端急劇升級。
  • tariff n. 關稅
    The import bans, which go into effect on Sept. 29 and were published on the White House’s Web site, came after Canada’s own retaliatory tariffs on US goods took effect after midnight on Tuesday.
    這些於 9 月 29 日生效並公布在白宮網站上的進口禁令，是在加拿大針對美國商品的報復性關稅於週二午夜生效後出台的。
  • negotiation n. 談判、協商
    Those Canadian levies themselves followed 50 percent tariffs that the US imposed on US$20 billion of Canadian goods last month, after several rounds of negotiations collapsed.
    在多輪談判破裂後，美國上個月對價值 200 億美元的加拿大商品徵收了 50% 的關稅，加拿大這些徵稅措施本身就是對此的回應。
  • ally n. 盟友、同盟國
    The breakdown has widened a rift between the longtime allies, who have blamed each other for the failed talks...
    這場決裂擴大了這兩個長期盟友之間的裂痕，雙方互相指責談判破裂...
  • cost n. 代價、成本
    That pivot will come at a cost. There’s always a cost to action, but it doesn’t come close to the cost of standing still.
    這種轉型將付出代價。採取行動總是有代價的，但這與原地不動的代價相去甚遠。
  • priority n. 優先事項、首要任務
    “As has been the case for the last 18 months, our first priority remains on protecting and supporting Canadian workers, farmers, families, and businesses from these unjustified actions,” he wrote.
    他寫道：「正如過去 18 個月的情況一樣，我們首要任務依然是保護和支持加拿大工人、農民、家庭和企業免受這些不合理行動的侵害。」
  • export n. / v. 出口
    While the tariffs affect a small amount of exports compared with total trade between the US and Canada, some analysts worry...
    雖然與美加之間的總貿易額相比，這些關稅僅影響少量的出口，但一些分析師擔心...
  • committee n. 委員會
    ...and a member of Carney’s advisory committee on bilateral US economic relations.
    ...以及卡尼雙邊美加經濟關係諮詢委員會成員。
  • leverage n. 施力點、影響力、籌碼
    “But at the same time, we totally understand that the prime minister needs to find areas of leverage.”
    「但同時，我們完全理解總理需要尋找施力點。」

文法重點

  • 非限定關係代名詞子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    The import bans, which go into effect on Sept. 29 and were published on the White House’s Web site, came after Canada’s own retaliatory tariffs on US goods took effect after midnight on Tuesday.
    句中的「which go into effect...」前面有逗號，屬於非限定用法，用來補充說明主詞「The import bans（進口禁令）」的額外資訊，拿掉之後主句的意思依然完整。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Those Canadian levies themselves followed 50 percent tariffs that the US imposed on US$20 billion of Canadian goods last month, after several rounds of negotiations collapsed.
    句中的「that the US imposed on...」修飾 50 percent tariffs，而主句或子句中常運用被動語態（如文章其他段落的「were published」、「were added」等），強調承受動作的對象，是新聞英文中非常常見的句構。
  • 分詞作形容詞/後位修飾 (Participle as Postmodifier)
    Those counter-tariffs cover US$20 billion of US goods, with duties ranging from 15 percent to 50 percent across products from steel and furniture to clothing and electronics...
    句中的「ranging from 15 percent to 50 percent」是由現在分詞帶領的片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「duties（關稅）」，相當於縮寫的形容詞子句「which range from...」，能讓句子更精簡流暢。
