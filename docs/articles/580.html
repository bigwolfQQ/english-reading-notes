文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾語 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The Sept. 3 letter from attorney Alex Spiro, addressed to a producer at Alex Gibney’s Jigsaw Productions, accuses the Oscar-winning documentarian...
    句中的「addressed to a producer...」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「The Sept. 3 letter from attorney Alex Spiro」。其原貌為關代子句「which was addressed to...」，省略了「which was」後直接以過去分詞開頭，用以精簡語句並緊密修飾前方名詞。
  • 不僅……而且……對等連接詞片語 (not only... but [also]...)
    The letter puts not only Gibney and Jigsaw on notice, but everyone involved in the film’s release, including HBO, Bleecker Street and Universal.
    「not only A but B」結構（句中省略了 also）用來連接兩個平行對等的受詞成分（Gibney and Jigsaw 與 everyone involved...），強調範圍的擴大，多益閱讀與寫作測驗中常考其對等平行結構（parallel structure）。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Jigsaw hit back in a statement to The Hollywood Reporter, saying scrutiny of powerful public figures is protected by the first amendment of the US Constitution.
    句中的「is protected」為現在式被動語態（be + 過去分詞）。當動作的承受者（scrutiny of powerful public figures）作為主詞時，常使用被動語態來強調受事者，也是多益文法題（尤其是動詞態與單複數一致）的常客。
