外交部對8國支持台海現狀表達感謝

MOFA hails support from 8 nations on Taiwan Strait status quo

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • ministry n. （政府的）部
    Taiwan's Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) on Thursday thanked eight European and Indo-Pacific countries...
    台灣的外交部於周四感謝八個歐洲及印太國家...
  • oppose v. 反對
    ...for opposing unilateral changes to the status quo across the Taiwan Strait in a joint statement issued earlier this week.
    ...因為在本週稍早發布的一份聯合聲明中，反對片面改變台灣海峽的現狀。
  • statement n. 聲明、陳述
    Foreign Minister Lin Chia-lung (林佳龍) welcomed the statement and expressed his appreciation for the countries' stance...
    外交部長林佳龍對該聲明表示歡迎，並對這些國家的立場表達感謝...
  • appreciation n. 感謝、欣賞
    Foreign Minister Lin Chia-lung (林佳龍) welcomed the statement and expressed his appreciation for the countries' stance, MOFA said in a news release.
    外交部透過新聞稿表示，外交部長林佳龍對該聲明表示歡迎，並對這些國家的立場表達感謝。
  • focus on phr. v. 聚焦於、專注於
    In one paragraph of the joint statement, the eight countries focused on peace and stability in the Indo-Pacific and the status quo in the Taiwan Strait, MOFA said.
    外交部指出，在該聯合聲明的一個段落中，這八個國家聚焦於印太地區的和平穩定以及台海現狀。
  • representative n. 代表
    The statement was issued Tuesday by foreign ministers and senior representatives of Australia, France, Germany, Japan, New Zealand, Poland, South Korea, and the United Kingdom...
    該聲明於周二由澳洲、法國、德國、日本、紐西蘭、波蘭、南韓及英國的外交部長與高階代表...共同發布。
  • emphasize v. 強調
    "We emphasize the importance of maintaining peace and stability in the Indo-Pacific."
    「我們強調維護印太地區和平與穩定的重要性。」
  • encourage v. 鼓勵
    "We encourage dialogue and oppose any unilateral attempts to change the status quo across the Taiwan Strait..."
    「我們鼓勵對話，並反對任何片面企圖改變台海現狀的行為...」
  • strengthen v. 加強、鞏固
    In the face of growing geopolitical and regional challenges, Taiwan will strengthen its self-defense and societal resilience...
    面對日益嚴峻的地緣政治與區域挑戰，台灣將強化自我防衛與社會韌性...
  • cooperation n. 合作
    ...while deepening cooperation with democratic partners, the ministry said.
    ...同時深化與民主夥伴的合作，該部表示。
  • prosperity n. 繁榮
    The eight countries also said in their joint statement that security and prosperity in Europe and the Indo-Pacific are increasingly connected.
    這八個國家也在聯合聲明中表示，歐洲與印太地區的安全與繁榮日益相連。
  • pledge v. 保證、承諾
    They pledged to work more closely together on shared challenges, including threats across multiple domains and the resilience of their societies.
    他們承諾在共同挑戰上更緊密合作，包括跨領域的威脅以及各自社會的韌性。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...in a joint statement issued earlier this week.
    句中的 "issued earlier this week" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "a joint statement"（聯合聲明）。省略了關代與be動詞（即 which was issued），能使句子更精簡。
  • 對等連接詞連接多個動詞片語 (Parallel Verbs)
    Taiwan will strengthen its self-defense and societal resilience, while deepening cooperation with democratic partners...
    句中的 "will strengthen..." 與後方的分詞構句 "while deepening..." 共同表達台灣在面對挑戰時將同時採取的兩項主要行動，結構清晰且具邏輯性。
  • 現在分詞作表現在狀態或附帶狀況 (Present Participle)
    Foreign Minister Lin Chia-lung (林佳龍) welcomed the statement and expressed his appreciation for the countries' stance, MOFA said in a news release.
