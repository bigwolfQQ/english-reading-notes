消息人士：對台軍售對「台版鐵穹」至關重要

Arms sales vital to ‘T-Dome’: source

單字解析

  • closely tied to phr. 與……緊密相連 / 息息相關
    The long-delayed US$14 billion arms sale that Washington put on hold prior to US President Donald Trump’s meeting with Chinese President Xi Jinping (習近平) last week is closely tied to Taiwan’s plans for developing a layered air-defense network known as “T-Dome,” sources said.
    知情人士表示，華府在美國總統川普上週與中國國家主席習近平會晤之前暫緩、延宕多時的 140 億美元對台軍售案，與台灣發展被稱為「台版鐵穹（T-Dome）」的分層防空網路計劃息息相關。
  • put on hold phr. 暫緩、擱置
    The long-delayed US$14 billion arms sale that Washington put on hold prior to US President Donald Trump’s meeting with Chinese President Xi Jinping (習近平) last week is closely tied to Taiwan’s plans for developing a layered air-defense network known as “T-Dome,” sources said.
    知情人士表示，華府在美國總統川普上週與中國國家主席習近平會晤之前暫緩、延宕多時的 140 億美元對台軍售案，與台灣發展被稱為「台版鐵穹（T-Dome）」的分層防空網路計劃息息相關。
  • package n. 包裹、一攬子計畫（商業上常指整套方案或軍售包裹）
    The package is expected to include hundreds of Patriot Advanced Capability-3 Missile Segment Enhancement (PAC-3 MSE) units, nine National Advanced Surface-to-Air Missile Systems (NASAMS) and an Integrated Battle Command System (IBCS).
    該軍售包裹預計包含數百枚愛國者三型增程型飛彈（PAC-3 MSE）單元、九套國家先進防空飛彈系統（NASAMS）以及一套整合戰場指揮管制系統（IBCS）。
  • logistical adj. 後勤的
    The units can use AIM-9X and AIM-120 air-to-air missiles carried by Taiwan’s fighter aircraft, providing logistical advantages.
    這些單位可以使用台灣戰機所攜帶的 AIM-9X 與 AIM-120 空對空飛彈，從而提供後勤優勢。
  • component n. 零件、組件、成分
    Other reported components include the Lower Tier Air and Missile Defense Sensor, which provides 360° detection of aircraft and missiles, and the IBCS, which integrates different radars and interceptors so that targets can be engaged by the most suitable air-defense system.
    其他據報的組件包含「低層防空暨飛彈防禦感測器」，可提供對飛機與飛彈的 360 度偵測，以及整合不同雷達與攔截器、以便由最適合的防空系統接戰目標的 IBCS。
  • purchase v. / n. 購買、採購
    Taiwan has already purchased three NASAMS units and 123 extended-range Advanced Medium-Range Air-to-Air Missiles from the US, as well as 102 PAC-3 MSEs.
    台灣已向美國採購了三套 NASAMS、123 枚增程型先進中程空對空飛彈，以及 102 枚 PAC-3 MSE。
  • milestone n. 里程碑
    The National Chungshan Institute of Science and Technology is also producing Tien Kung III (天弓三, Sky Bow III) surface-to-air missiles and a mobile air-defense system based on the Tien Chien II (天劍二, Sky Sword II) missile, with both programs reaching mass-production milestones this year.
    國家中山科學研究院也正在生產天弓三型地對空飛彈，以及基於天劍二型飛彈所研發的機動防空系統，這兩項計劃都在今年達到量產的里程碑。
  • notify v. 通知、知會
    US lawmakers and retired military officers have been urging the Trump administration to formally notify the US Congress of the package as soon as possible.
    美國國會議員與退役軍官一直敦促川普政府，應盡快將此軍售案正式知會美國國會。
  • preliminary adj. 初步的、預備的
    It received preliminary congressional approval in January.
    該軍售案已於 1 月獲得國會的初步批准。
  • consequence n. 結果、後果
    A senior Taiwanese official familiar with US-Taiwan arms sales on Friday said that delays could have significant consequences, as defense manufacturers are facing heavy demand and limited production capacity.
    一名熟悉美台軍售的台灣資深官員週五表示，由於國防製造商面臨沉重的需求與有限的產能，延誤可能會造成重大後果。
  • capacity n. 產能、容量、能力
    A senior Taiwanese official familiar with US-Taiwan arms sales on Friday said that delays could have significant consequences, as defense manufacturers are facing heavy demand and limited production capacity.
    一名熟悉美台軍售的台灣資深官員週五表示，由於國防製造商面臨沉重的需求與有限的產能，延誤可能會造成重大後果。
  • contractor n. 承包商
    Under the US Foreign Military Sales process, the US Department of State must formally notify Congress before detailed negotiations can begin. Taiwan and the US would then sign a letter of offer and acceptance, after which Taiwan would make an initial payment and the US military could place orders with defense contractors.
    根據美國的「對外軍售」流程，美國國務院必須在展開詳細談判之前正式知會國會。隨後，台灣與美國將簽署發價書與承諾書，接著台灣支付首期款項，美國軍方才能向國防承包商下單。
  • reserve v. 預留、保留、預訂
    Manufacturers generally do not reserve production capacity for Taiwan until a formal contract is in place, the official said, speaking on condition of anonymity.
    該名匿名官員表示，製造商通常在正式合約簽訂之前，不會為台灣預留產能。
  • budget n. 預算
    US senators Thom Tillis and Elissa Slotkin, cochairs of the Senate Taiwan Caucus, have said that continued delays could create disrupt Taiwan’s budget planning and efforts to raise its defense spending to 5 percent of GDP.
    參議院台灣連線共同主席、美國參議員湯姆·提里斯與艾莉莎·斯洛特金表示，持續的延誤可能會打亂台灣的預算規劃，以及將國防支出提升至國內生產毛額 5% 的努力。
  • leverage n. 籌碼、影響力
    They have urged the White House to approve the sale rather than use it as leverage in US-China negotiations, warning that delays could leave gaps in Taiwan’s air-defense capabilities.
    他們敦促白宮批准這筆軍售，而不是將其作為美中談判的籌碼，並警告稱，延誤可能會在台灣的防空能力中留下缺口。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾語 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The long-delayed US$14 billion arms sale that Washington put on hold prior to US President Donald Trump’s meeting with Chinese President Xi Jinping (習近平) last week is closely tied to Taiwan’s plans for developing a layered air-defense network known as “T-Dome,” sources said.
    句中的「long-delayed」是過去分詞當作形容詞用，放在名詞前修飾 arms sale；而句尾的「known as “T-Dome”」則是過去分詞片語作後置修飾語，修飾前面的 a layered air-defense network（意指「被稱為……的」），省略了「which is」。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    Other reported components include the Lower Tier Air and Missile Defense Sensor, which provides 360° detection of aircraft and missiles, and the IBCS, which integrates different radars and interceptors so that targets can be engaged by the most suitable air-defense system.
    句中使用了兩個「which」引導的非限定關係子句（前面皆有逗號），分別用來補充說明前面的「Lower Tier Air and Missile Defense Sensor」與「IBCS」這兩個先行詞的功能與特性。
  • 條件句假設語氣 (Conditional Sentence / Subjunctive Mood)
    If the US$14 billion package includes the expected additional PAC-3 MSEs, NASAMS units and the IBCS, it would expand the density and integration of Taiwan’s layered air defenses...
    此句為假設語氣（第二類條件句的變體或對未來可能情況的假設），「If」子句使用現在式（includes），主要子句則使用「would + 原形動詞」（would expand），用來表達如果達成某個條件，將會產生的推論結果。
  • 現在分詞片語作補充說明 / 結果狀語 (Present Participle Phrase indicating Result)
    Advanced missiles and radar systems are also produced in batches, meaning that missing a scheduled production window could push an order into a later production run, they said.
    句尾的「meaning that...」是現在分詞（-ing）引導的分詞構句，接在逗號後面，用來表達前面整個子句（先進飛彈和雷達系統是分批生產的）所帶來的自然結果或延伸意義（意思是說……）。
  • 附條件的分詞片語 (Participial Phrase with Condition)
    Manufacturers generally do not reserve production capacity for Taiwan until a formal contract is in place, the official said, speaking on condition of anonymity.
    句尾的「speaking on condition of anonymity」是一個獨立的分詞片語，用來修飾主句的主語（the official），補充說明該官員發言時的狀態（在匿名條件下發言），相當於「as the official spoke on condition of anonymity」。
