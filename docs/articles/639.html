  • transport v. 運輸、運送
    The remains would be transported to a port in Coron, Palawan, Philippine Coast Guard spokeswoman Commodore Noemie Cayabyab said in a statement.
    菲律賓海岸防衛隊發言人、準將諾埃米・卡亞比亞布（Noemie Cayabyab）在聲明中表示，這些遺體將被運送到巴拉望省科隆（Coron）的一個港口。
  • temporary adj. 暫時的、臨時的
    Disaster agency workers carried coffins to a cemetery in the resort town, intended for the temporary burial of victims of the fire that tore through the M/V June Aster on Wednesday.
    災難管理局的工作人員將棺材抬到這座度假城鎮的墓園，這些墓園是用來暫時安葬週三席捲「六月阿斯特號」（M/V June Aster）大火的罹難者。

文法重點

  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense)
    Cayabyab said in a radio interview that the fire had begun in the cargo hold, then tore through the ship within seconds.
    用來表示在過去某個動作（say 過去式 said）之前已經發生的動作（起火），強調「過去中的過去」的時間先後順序。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Thirty passengers, including two Chilean nationals, and 13 crew have been rescued, the Philippine Coast Guard said.
    在此句中，「have been rescued」採用現在完成式的被動語態（have/has been + 過去分詞），強調乘客與船員是被海岸防衛隊救起，而非主動去救人。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    One-meter deep graves have been dug to hold the victims’ remains until DNA testing is completed, Kristine Ablana, a tourism official serving as an official spokesperson, told reporters on Saturday.
    句中的「serving as an official spokesperson」是現在分詞片語，用來補充修飾前面的名詞短語「a tourism official」，相當於形容詞子句「who served as an official spokesperson」。
