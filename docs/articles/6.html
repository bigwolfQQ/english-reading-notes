國防預算揭開飛彈計畫的神秘面紗

Defense budget lifts the veil on missile projects

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • lift the veil on phr. 揭開…的神秘面紗、公開
    Defense budget lifts the veil on missile projects
  • speculation n. 揣測、推測
    Taiwan’s Ching Tien I (擎天) supersonic cruise missiles have completed their production run and entered service, the non-classified portion of the proposed military budget for next year shows, indirectly confirming the existence of a system that has long been the subject of speculation.
  • implicitly adv. 暗中地、含蓄地、隱晦地
    Chinese-language media have labeled the budget’s reference to the Fei Chi II system as an “official leak” connected to the Ching Tien II hypersonic missile, which implicitly acknowledged the existence and operational status of Ching Tien I missiles.
  • declassification n. （機密資料等的）解密
    The declassification of the Ching Tien family of missiles signals that the military has moved to publicly acknowledging possessing a strike capability, a measure likely motivated by a desire to enhance deterrence.
  • deterrence n. 威嚇、嚇阻力
    The declassification of the Ching Tien family of missiles signals that the military has moved to publicly acknowledging possessing a strike capability, a measure likely motivated by a desire to enhance deterrence.
  • cut short phr. v. 縮短、中途打斷、腰斬
    The Chinese-language China Times reported that the Yun Feng program was cut short in 2014, resulting in a limited production run of 10 launch systems equipped with 15 to 20 missiles each, with a total production target of 500 missiles.
  • echelon n. （軍隊的）梯隊、階層
    The Ching Tien I reportedly uses a ramjet motor, with a top speed of Mach 3 (about 3,700kph) and an effective range of 1,200km to 1,500km, enough to hit the Chinese People’s Liberation Army’s (PLA) “second echelon” bases in eastern and central China.
  • ponderous adj. 笨重的、沉重的
    Owing to design limitations and the rocket’s ponderous size, the Ching Tien I mostly relies on silos or mountain missile installations as launch facilities, whose visibility to satellite surveillance limits the system’s survivability.
  • survivability n. 生存能力、存活率
    Owing to design limitations and the rocket’s ponderous size, the Ching Tien I mostly relies on silos or mountain missile installations as launch facilities, whose visibility to satellite surveillance limits the system’s survivability.
  • predecessor n. 前身、前代物品
    The Ching Tien II, developed to improve upon its predecessor’s capabilities, is said to use a scramjet engine, with a top speed of Mach 5 and an effective range of more than 2,000km.
  • hinterland n. 腹地、內陸地區
    These parameters suggest the missile can strike targets in China’s hinterland, including Beijing and Wuhan.
  • asymmetric adj. 非對稱的
    The Ching Tien II’s deep-strike capability and mobility make them a highly valuable asymmetric warfare asset for deterring China from attacking Taiwan, they said.

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾（Past Participle Phrase modifying a noun）
    The Ministry of National Defense’s budget proposal submitted to the legislature this week included a curious item called “Fei Chi II (飛戟) weapons system and equipment maintenance program,” which is to be implemented from next year through 2031.
    句中的 `submitted to the legislature this week` 以及 `called...` 皆為過去分詞短語，分別是由關係子句 `which was submitted...` 與 `which is called...` 簡化而來，用來後位修飾主詞 `proposal` 與名詞 `item`，使句構更為簡練。
  • 過去分詞短語插入修飾（Non-restrictive Participles modifying the Subject）
    The Ching Tien II, developed to improve upon its predecessor’s capabilities, is said to use a scramjet engine, with a top speed of Mach 5 and an effective range of more than 2,000km.
    位於雙逗號間的 `developed to improve upon its predecessor’s capabilities` 為過去分詞短語，原形為非限定關係子句 `which was developed to...`。這裡省略了關係代名詞與 be 動詞，作為對主要主詞 `The Ching Tien II` 的補充說明。
  • 關係代名詞所有格 whose 的非限定子句（Non-restrictive Relative Clause with 'whose'）
    Owing to design limitations and the rocket’s ponderous size, the Ching Tien I mostly relies on silos or mountain missile installations as launch facilities, whose visibility to satellite surveillance limits the system’s survivability.
    句尾由 `whose` 引導非限定關係子句，`whose` 代替前面的名詞 `launch facilities`（發射設施）。`whose visibility...` 意為「這些設施被衛星偵測到的暴露度」，用來補充說明發射設施的劣勢如何影響整體系統的生存能力。
  • 現在分詞短語作狀語表示結果（Present Participle Phrase showing Result）
    The Chinese-language China Times reported that the Yun Feng program was cut short in 2014, resulting in a limited production run of 10 launch systems equipped with 15 to 20 missiles each, with a total production target of 500 missiles.
    `resulting in...` 為現在分詞短語作結果狀語，相當於非限定關係子句 `which resulted in...`，用來延續主要子句的動作（計畫腰斬），說明該動作所帶來的最終結果。
