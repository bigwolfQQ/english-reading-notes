文法重點

  • be to + V (表預定計畫或未來安排)
    The Ministry of Agriculture yesterday launched the “Taiwan Rich Flavors” food truck, which is to travel the nation providing free samples to promote Taiwan-produced ingredients.
    「be to V」句型常用於新聞報導中，用來表達官方或正式的預定計畫、未來即將發生的安排或命令。在此句中，is to travel 表示餐車「預計／將要」走訪全國。
  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (分詞構句/形容詞用法)
    The food truck project is expected to reach more local communities, exposing them to different foods and allowing them to purchase products, Minister of Agriculture Chen Junne-jih (陳駿季) told an event in Taipei.
    句尾的「exposing them to... and allowing them to...」是由現在分詞（exposing, allowing）引導的片語，用來補充說明前面的動態結果，主詞皆為前面整個子句所引發的動作，相當於 and expose them... and allow them...
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The food truck project is expected to reach more local communities...
    多益常考的被動語態結構「be + 過去分詞（expected）」。此處因為「計畫」本身是被「期望／預期」的，故使用被動語態 is expected，強調主體所受到的動作或狀態。
  • with + 名詞 + 分詞/形容詞 (附帶狀況片語)
    The workshop offered market export information, including overseas market trends, international trade shows, buyer development, brand marketing and export regulations, with the goal of helping Taiwan-grown organic produce expand into overseas markets, it said.
    「with + 名詞 (the goal) + V-ing (of helping...)」結構在商業英文與多益中非常常見，用來表達伴隨的情況、目的或條件，中譯常為「帶著……的目的／擁有……」。
