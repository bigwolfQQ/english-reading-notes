國防企業高管在論壇上強調自主化作戰能力

Defense company executives underscore autonomous capabilities at forum

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • acquire v. 取得、獲得
    It is important that Taiwan acquire autonomous capabilities to reduce decisionmaking time during wartime...
    台灣取得自主化作戰能力至關重要，這能縮短戰時的決策時間……
  • ensure v. 確保、保證
    ...and ensure the efficient use of resources in peacetime, defense company executives told a forum yesterday.
    ……並確保平時資源的有效利用，國防企業高管昨天在一個論壇上表示。
  • vulnerable adj. 易受傷的、脆弱的
    ...said that Taiwan could be vulnerable to China’s attrition-based tactics.
    ……表示台灣可能容易受到中國消耗戰策略的影響。
  • approach n. 方法、途徑
    That vulnerability is based on the armed forces’ current approach...
    這種脆弱性根源於軍隊目前的方法……
  • resources n. 資源
    Taiwan is spending its rarest resource on the least demanding mission.
    台灣正在最不具挑戰性的任務上，消耗其最珍貴的資源。
  • invest v. 投資
    China has invested “enormously” in electronic warfare and counterspace capabilities...
    中國在電子戰和反太空能力方面進行了「大量」投資……
  • deploy v. 部署、調動
    She called for the deployment of multi-mission autonomous surface vessels...
    她呼籲部署具備模組化酬載的多任務自主式水面載具……
  • procure v. 採購、取得
    ...alongside the approximately 1,300 one-way uncrewed surface vessels (USVs) that Taiwan plans to procure.
    ……並搭配台灣計劃採購的約 1,300 艘單向無人水面載具一同使用。
  • assign v. 指派、分配
    ...commanders could have only seconds to understand an incoming attack and assign an appropriate weapon in response.
    ……指揮官可能只有幾秒鐘的時間來理解來襲的攻擊，並指派適當的武器來做出回應。
  • operate v. 運作、營運、操作
    ...enabling smaller units to operate independently and present an adversary with “more targets and more dilemmas than it can manage,” he said.
    他說，這能使較小的單位能夠獨立運作，並向對手展示「比其所能應對的更多目標和更多困境」。

文法重點

  • 表建議、要求的假設語氣（Subjunctive Mood）
    It is important that Taiwan acquire autonomous capabilities to reduce decisionmaking time...
    在「It is important/crucial/essential that + 主詞 + (should) + 原形動詞」的句構中，that 子句裡的動詞必須使用原形動詞（此處 acquire 省略了 should），用以表達強烈的建議或重要性。
  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾（Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier）
    ...responding to daily harassment by Chinese navy and coast guard vessels, as well as maritime militia vessels operating in the waters near Taiwan...
    句中的「operating in the waters near Taiwan」是由現在分詞片語構成的後位修飾語，用來修飾其前方的名詞「maritime militia vessels」，相當於形容詞子句「which operate in the waters near Taiwan」。
  • 使役動詞用法（Causative Verb）
    Autonomy would also distribute decisionmaking, enabling smaller units to operate independently...
    句型「enable + 受詞 + to V」屬於使役／授權類用法，表示「使……能夠……」。在此句中，enabling 的主詞是前面整件事，帶出後方 smaller units 能夠 independently（獨立）operate（運作）的結果。
  • 目的與結果副詞子句（Adverbial Clause of Purpose）
    Taiwan should work to connect data across disparate systems so that it has an “integrated data layer”...
    片語「so that」引導一個副詞子句，用來表達前面的動作（致力於連接不同系統的數據）所要達到的目的或產生的結果，意為「以便……」、「因此……」。
