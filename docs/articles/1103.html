太陽能業者瞄準美國開放市場

Solar energy producers targeting open US market

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • supplier n. 供應商
    Solar cell and module supplier United Renewable Energy Co (URE) yesterday said its US revenue would double next year...
    太陽能電池與模組供應商聯合再生能源（URE）昨天表示，其美國營收明年將會倍增...
  • revenue n. 營收，收益
    ...said its US revenue would double next year, mainly benefiting from a market realignment...
    ...表示其美國營收明年將會倍增，主要受惠於市場重組...
  • capitalize on v. phr. 利用……以獲利，善用
    To capitalize on the new market opportunities, URE is racing to launch a new solar module line...
    為了善用新的市場商機，聯合再生正競相推出全新的太陽能模組生產線...
  • joint venture n. phr. 合資企業
    ...through a new joint venture with Sino-American Silicon Products Inc (SAS) in the third quarter of next year...
    ...透過明年第3季與中美晶（SAS）成立的新合資企業...
  • capacity n. 產能，容量
    The new production line is designed with an annual production capacity of 1 gigawatt (GW), it said.
    該公司表示，這條新產線的設計年產能為1吉瓦（GW）。
  • competitive edge n. phr. 競爭優勢
    ...it is the only arena where we hold a competitive edge.
    ...這是我們擁有競爭優勢的唯一戰場。
  • tariff n. 關稅
    ...after imposing a 15 percent import tariff and restricting Chinese ownership in subsidized US plants to 25 percent.
    ...此前美國已徵收15%的進口關稅，並將受補貼的美國工廠中中國所有權限制在25%。
  • target v. 瞄準，以……為目標
    URE is targeting the non-China market segment, which is estimated to have 30GW to 35GW of demand per year, he said.
    他說，聯合再生正瞄準非中國的市場區隔，該區隔估計每年有30吉瓦至35吉瓦的需求。
  • anticipate v. 預期，預料
    “We anticipate the US market to contribute a large portion of the company’s revenue in the future,” Hung said.
    洪傳獻表示：「我們預期美國市場未來將為公司貢獻很大一部分的營收。」
  • account for v. phr. 佔（比例），說明
    This year, the US is expected to account for less than 20 percent of URE’s revenue...
    今年，美國預計將佔聯合再生營收不到20%...
  • portfolio n. （公司的）產品組合，投資組合
    URE is accelerating efforts to expand its product portfolio to include solar modules for low Earth orbit satellites.
    聯合再生正加速努力擴展其產品組合，將適用於低軌道衛星的太陽能模組納入其中。
  • net profit n. phr. 淨利
    URE swung back to a net profit of NT$1.52 billion (US$47.9 million) in the first half...
    聯合再生上半年轉虧為盈，淨利達新台幣15.2億元（約合4790萬美元）...
  • disposal gain n. phr. 處分利益，處分收益
    The company attributed the improvement partly to a disposal gain of NT$2.1 billion from selling a solar plant in January.
    公司將改善部分歸因於1月出售一座太陽能廠所產生的21億元處分利益。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表附帶結果或原因）
    ..., mainly benefiting from a market realignment fueled by US solar reshoring policies and crackdowns on Chinese green energy companies.
    句尾的「benefiting from...」為現在分詞片語引導的分詞構句，主動修飾前面的子句，表示「主要受惠於……」的附帶結果。其前的 fueled 及 crackdowns 後方的修飾則使用了過去分詞當形容詞的用法。
  • 關係代名詞子句（限定修飾先行詞）
    ...it is the only arena where we hold a competitive edge.
    句中的「where we hold a competitive edge」是由關係副詞 where（相當於 in which）所引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的先行詞「the only arena（唯一的戰場）」。
  • 被動語態與分詞後位修飾
    Hung’s remarks came as the US administration is tightening restrictions on Chinese clean energy technology by adding minimum import prices from Dec. 4 this year after imposing a 15 percent import tariff and restricting Chinese ownership in subsidized US plants to 25 percent.
    句中的「subsidized US plants」使用了過去分詞 subsidized（受補貼的）置於名詞 plants 前方作為形容詞，用來修飾美國的工廠。而主句則使用了現在進行式（is tightening）表達目前正在進行的政策動作。
  • 從……轉變為……的動詞片語（swing back to）
    URE swung back to a net profit of NT$1.52 billion (US$47.9 million) in the first half, from a loss of NT$599 million in the same period last year, company data showed.
    「swing back to A from B」常用於財經新聞中，生動地描述財務表現從先前的「B（虧損）」擺盪、轉變回「A（獲利）」的狀況。
