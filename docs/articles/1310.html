季節性東北風將使台灣保持涼爽但水氣減少：氣象署

Seasonal winds to keep Taiwan cool but moisture declines: CWA

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • ongoing adj. 進行中的，持續的
    Ongoing seasonal northeasterly winds are expected to continue to keep northern and eastern Taiwan cool on Tuesday...
    持續的季節性東北風預計將在週二繼續使台灣北部和東部保持涼爽...
  • ease v. 減輕，減緩，趨緩
    ...while the rainfall seen in recent days is expected to ease, according to the Central Weather Administration (CWA).
    ...同時近幾日所見的降雨預計將會趨緩，中央氣象署（CWA）表示。
  • reach v. 達到（數量、水準等）
    The CWA said temperatures in northern Taiwan and Yilan and Hualien counties in the east could reach highs of 26-29 degrees Celsius Tuesday...
    氣象署表示，北部以及東部的宜蘭縣和花蓮縣，週二白天高溫可達攝氏26至29度...
  • fall v. 下降，跌落
    ...before falling to 22-24 degrees at night.
    ...夜間則降至22至24度。
  • due to prep. phr. 由於，因為
    Due to the seasonal winds, temperatures Tuesday in low-lying areas of Taiwan fell to lows of 19.2 degrees Celsius...
    受季節性東北風影響，週二台灣低窪地區的氣溫降至低溫...
  • pattern n. 型態，模式
    The CWA said a similar weather pattern is expected to continue until early Thursday morning...
    氣象署表示，類似的天氣型態預計將持續到週四清晨...
  • weaken v. 減弱，變弱
    ...when the current seasonal winds are forecast to weaken, while drier weather is expected as moisture levels decline.
    ...屆時目前的季節性東北風預計會減弱，同時隨著水氣減少，預期將出現更乾燥的天氣。
  • decline v. 下降，減少
    ...while drier weather is expected as moisture levels decline.
    ...同時隨著水氣減少，預期將出現更乾燥的天氣。
  • cite v. 引用，引述
    Citing the latest European weather forecasting model, independent meteorologist Wu Der-rong (吳德榮) said...
    獨立氣象專家吳德榮引用最新的歐洲氣象預報模型表示...
  • period n. 期間，時期
    ...radiative cooling could even push temperatures as low as 16 degrees during that period.
    ...在此期間，輻射冷卻甚至可能將氣溫推低至16度。
  • stable adj. 穩定的
    ...while warm and stable weather is expected to continue in central and southern Taiwan, he said.
    他說，而中南部預計將持續維持溫暖穩定的天氣。

文法重點

  • 受動態表達未來預期（is/are expected to）
    Ongoing seasonal northeasterly winds are expected to continue to keep northern and eastern Taiwan cool on Tuesday...
    「be expected to + 原形動詞」常用於新聞英語中，用來客觀地報導預期會發生或將要發生的事情，避免使用絕對語氣。
  • 非限定關代子句補充說明（which 引導）
    In central and southern Taiwan, which has not been as affected by the seasonal winds, daytime temperatures could reach 30-34 degrees Tuesday...
    句中的「which has not been as affected...」前方有逗號，屬於非限定用法，用來補充說明前面「central and southern Taiwan」的狀況，且 which 在子句中作主詞。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾（V-ing 修飾名詞）
    Citing the latest European weather forecasting model, independent meteorologist Wu Der-rong (吳德榮) said...
    句首的「Citing the latest European weather forecasting model」是由現在分詞 Citing 開頭的分詞片語，用來修飾後方的主詞 independent meteorologist Wu Der-rong，表示「引用了......的氣象專家說」。
