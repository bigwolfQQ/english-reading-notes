暴雨導致台北各地傳出 41 起災情

Heavy rain triggers 41 incidents across Taipei

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • incident n. 事件，事故
    Persistent heavy rain triggered 41 incidents across Taipei on Friday...
    週五持續的豪雨在台北各地引發了 41 起災情...
  • authorities n. 當局，官方
    ...as authorities stepped up disaster response measures across the Greater Taipei area.
    ...隨著大台北地區的主管機關加強了災害應變措施。
  • measure n. 措施，方法
    ...as authorities stepped up disaster response measures across the Greater Taipei area.
    ...隨著大台北地區的主管機關加強了災害應變措施。
  • facility n. 設施，設備
    The incidents reported as of 11:30 a.m. also included landslides and damage to buildings and other facilities...
    截至上午 11 時 30 分通報的災情還包括土石流以及建築物和其他設施的損壞...
  • monitor v. 監控，監測
    Chiang said authorities were responding to incidents as they were reported and closely monitoring the situation.
    蔣萬安表示，當局正針對接獲通報的災情做出反應，並密切監控情勢。
  • damage n. 損害，損失
    The incidents reported as of 11:30 a.m. also included landslides and damage to buildings and other facilities...
    截至上午 11 時 30 分通報的災情還包括土石流以及建築物和其他設施的損壞...
  • urge v. 敦促，力勸
    ...while urging people to avoid mountainous areas and waterways
    ...同時呼籲民眾避免前往山區和水域
  • owner n. 所有人，物主
    ...and urged vehicle owners parked outside the river embankment to monitor conditions and prepare to move their vehicles.
    ...並呼籲停放在河川堤防外的車主注意情況並準備移車。
  • issue v. 發布，發行
    The Central Weather Administration (CWA) issued extremely heavy rain advisories for parts of northern Taipei...
    中央氣象署（CWA）對台北市北部...發布了超大豪雨特報

文法重點

  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況或結果
    Persistent heavy rain triggered 41 incidents across Taipei on Friday, mostly flooding and fallen trees, as authorities stepped up disaster response measures across the Greater Taipei area.
    句中的 "mostly flooding and fallen trees" 是名詞片語作為獨立的分詞結構（省略了 being），用來補充說明前面 41 起災情的具體內容。
  • 過去進行式
    Chiang said authorities were responding to incidents as they were reported and closely monitoring the situation.
    句中的 "were responding" 與 "were... monitoring" 使用過去進行式，表示在過去某個時間點（市長發言時）正在持續進行的動作。
  • 被動語態
    No injuries were reported, according to the city government.
    句中的 "were reported" 採用被動語態（be + 過去分詞），強調「災情被通報」的這個客觀事實，而不一定要強調是誰通報的。
  • 現在分詞作後位修飾
    ...and urged vehicle owners parked outside the river embankment to monitor conditions...
    句中的 "parked outside the river embankment" 是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞 "vehicle owners"，相當於形容詞子句 "who were parked outside the river embankment"。
