台灣 2026 年通報 30 起猴痘病例，境外移入感染數倍增

Taiwan reports 30 mpox cases in 2026, imported infections double

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • confirmed adj. 已確診的、已證實的
    Taiwan has reported 30 confirmed mpox cases so far this year, including two new infections announced Wednesday...
    台灣今年截至目前已累計 30 起確診猴痘病例，其中包括週三公布的 2 起新感染病例……
  • imported adj. （疾病等）境外移入的、進口的
    ...with imported cases doubling from a year earlier and most cases linked to unprotected sexual contact, the Centers for Disease Control (CDC) said.
    ……疾病管制署（CDC）表示，境外移入病例較去年同期倍增，且多數病例與不安全性行為有關。
  • data n. 數據、資料
    The 10 imported cases are double the number recorded during the same period last year, with six linked to travel to Thailand, according to CDC data.
    根據疾管署資料，這 10 起境外移入病例是去年同期記錄數字的兩倍，其中 6 起與泰國旅遊史有關。
  • spokesperson n. 發言人
    CDC spokesperson Lin Ming-cheng (林明誠) told CNA that while overall mpox cases have fallen sharply...
    疾管署發言人林明誠告訴中央社，自整體猴痘病例急劇下降以來……
  • account for phr. 佔……比例、說明、解釋
    ...imported infections now account for a growing share of the total.
    ……境外移入感染目前佔總數的比例卻日益增加。
  • campaign n. （政治、社會或商業）運動、活動
    The figures indicate that Taiwan's vaccination campaign has effectively curbed domestic transmission...
    這些數據顯示台灣的疫苗接種運動已有效抑制國內傳播……
  • curb v. 控制、抑制、約束
    The figures indicate that Taiwan's vaccination campaign has effectively curbed domestic transmission...
    這些數據顯示台灣的疫苗接種運動已有效抑制國內傳播……
  • diagnosed adj. 被診斷出的
    He later developed fever, chills and skin rashes before being diagnosed.
    他在確診前出現發燒、發冷與皮疹等症狀。
  • regimen n. （用藥、飲食等的）療程、養生法
    ...while 80,033 had completed the recommended two-dose regimen.
    ……而 80,033 人已完成建議的兩劑接種療程。
  • severity n. 嚴重性、嚴厲
    Vaccination also reduces disease severity and remains the most effective protection against both clade I and clade II strains, he said.
    他說，接種疫苗也能降低疾病嚴重度，並依然是對抗分支 I 與分支 II 病毒株最有效的防護。
  • eligible adj. 有資格的、具備條件的
    People who are not eligible for the government's free vaccination program but are assessed by physicians as being at risk of exposure...
    不符合政府公費疫苗接種計畫資格、但經醫師評估有暴露風險的民眾……
  • expense n. 費用、支出
    ...can receive the vaccine at their own expense at eight designated hospitals with travel clinics across Taiwan, the agency added.
    ……可在全台設有旅遊醫學門診的 8 家指定醫院自費接種疫苗。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況（with + 名詞 + 分詞）
    Taiwan has reported 30 confirmed mpox cases so far this year, including two new infections announced Wednesday, with imported cases doubling from a year earlier and most cases linked to unprotected sexual contact...
    「with + 受詞 + 分詞」結構用來表示附帶狀況或背景原因。在此句中，「with imported cases doubling」代表「境外移入病例倍增（的情況下）」，「doubling」為現在分詞，主動修飾 imported cases；「most cases linked to...」則省略了 being，表示被動關聯。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    The two latest cases -- one locally acquired and one imported -- involved unvaccinated men who had engaged in unprotected sexual activity.
    句中的「locally acquired」與「imported」皆為過去分詞片語，用來修飾前方代名詞 one（代表 case），意思是「本土感染的」與「境外移入的」。這種用法能簡化形容詞子句（如 which was acquired locally），讓新聞語氣更為精煉。
  • 現在分詞（動名詞）作主詞補語或伴隨動作
    CDC spokesperson Lin Ming-cheng (林明誠) told CNA that while overall mpox cases have fallen sharply since the disease was designated a Category II notifiable infectious disease in Taiwan in June 2022, imported infections now account for a growing share of the total.
    句中的「a growing share」使用現在分詞「growing」當作形容詞，修飾名詞 share，用來表達「正在成長的／日益增加的比例」，這是多益與新聞英文中極常見的動態描述方式。
  • 被動語態的運用
    Epidemiological investigations found that most patients had engaged in unprotected sexual activity, while nearly 80 percent had not been vaccinated against mpox.
    句中的「had not been vaccinated」採用了過去完成式的被動語態（had been + 過去分詞），強調在調查發生之前，這些患者「被接種疫苗」的這個動作尚未發生，突顯過去完成時序與被動受事者的關係。
