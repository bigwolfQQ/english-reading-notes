Anthropic 執行長阿莫德伊呼籲放慢人工智慧發展速度

Anthropic CEO Amodei calls for AI slowdown

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • develop verb 發展、開發
    Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei on Saturday called on artificial intelligence (AI) firms to slow down the development of the powerful technology...
    Anthropic 執行長達里奧·阿莫德伊於週六呼籲人工智慧（AI）企業放慢強大技術的開發腳步...
  • industry noun 產業、工業
    ...decided to leave the industry, accusing both US companies of “gambling with our lives”...
    ...決定離開該產業，並指責這兩家美國公司「拿我們的生命賭博」...
  • assess verb 評估、評價
    ...they agreed with Amodei’s assessment as pressure builds for improved oversight.
    ...他們認同阿莫德伊的評估，因為加強監督的壓力正在增加。
  • pace noun 步調、速度
    “We must slow the pace at which we improve the capabilities of AI models.”
    「我們必須放慢提升 AI 模型能力的步伐。」
  • benefit noun 好處、利益、津貼
    “Not building the technology deprives humanity of benefits or simply places AI in the hands of authoritarian powers...”
    「不開發這項技術會剝奪人類的福祉，或者單純讓 AI 落入威權勢頭手中……」
  • prudence noun 謹慎、審慎
    “But over the last few months, I have become convinced that fully addressing the risks requires even more prudence,” he said.
    「但在過去幾個月裡，我開始確信，要全面應對這些風險需要更多的謹慎，」他說。
  • suspend verb 暫停、中止
    Amodei’s statement echoed another call by Anthropic in early June to slow or suspend development.
    阿莫德伊的聲明呼應了 Anthropic 在 6 月初發出的另一項放慢或暫停開發的呼籲。
  • voluntarily adverb 自願地
    Altman in late July said developers might need to voluntarily tap the brakes.
    奧特曼在 7 月下旬表示，開發人員可能需要自願踩煞車。
  • employee noun 員工、受雇者
    More than 1,000 employees at cutting-edge AI companies including Amodei signed a petition...
    包括阿莫德伊在內，尖端 AI 公司的 1000 多名員工簽署了一份請願書...
  • reveal verb 透露、展現
    OpenAI had revealed that during testing, AI models broke out of their confined environment...
    OpenAI 曾透露，在測試期間，AI 模型打破了受限環境的束縛...
  • incident noun 事件、事故
    In that incident and others, the blame was placed on AI agents...
    在那次及其他事件中，責任都被歸咎於 AI 代理程式...
  • supervise verb 監督、管理
    ...software programs that can carry out tasks without constant supervision by humans.
    ...軟體程式可以在沒有人類持續監督的情況下執行任務。
  • responsible adjective 負責任的
    “Neither company is acting responsibly.”
    「兩家公司都沒有負責任地行事。」
  • target noun 目標
    ...conducting cybersecurity attacks on targets they were not asked to attack...
    ...對他們未被要求攻擊的目標發起網路安全攻擊...
  • generate verb 產生、引起
    ...potentially generating hundreds of billions of dollars in damage, he added.
    並可能造成數千億美元的損失，他補充說。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    ...accusing both US companies of “gambling with our lives” in the race to develop models capable of self-improvement.
    句中的 capable of self-improvement 是形容詞片語，用來修飾前方名詞 models。這種將形容詞片語置於名詞之後的結構，在英文新聞與商業文件中非常常見，能有效精簡句子並提供更多細節。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    ...Hugging Face, a site developers use to store and share code.
    句中的 a site developers use to store and share code 包含了省略了關代（which/that）的限制性關係子句 developers use...，用來修飾先行詞 a site。這在多益閱讀測驗的文法題和長篇閱讀中是極高頻出現的句構。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    In that incident and others, the blame was placed on AI agents...
    句型使用「be +過去分詞」(was placed)，強調動作的承受者（the blame 責任），而不特別強調是誰去歸咎責任。商業與新聞英文經常使用被動語態來客觀陳述事實或事件結果。
  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況 (Participial Phrase indicating accompanying circumstance)
    “Dario is right,” Musk wrote on social media, as Altman said: “I agree with Dario that we need to pace the frontier.”
    此處雖然使用連接詞 as引導子句，但在新聞報導中常透過子句來呈現「同時發生」的動作或對話反應。在多益文法中，常考連接詞或介系詞（如 as, while）引導的時間與同時性子句。
  • 名詞子句作受詞 (Noun Clause as Object)
    Amodei cautioned that the Hugging Face incident should not be dismissed as a one-off.
    that 引導的名詞子句（that the Hugging Face incident should not be dismissed...）在句中作為及物動詞 cautioned 的受詞，用來表達阿莫德伊警告的具體內容。多益常考動詞後方接 that 子句的用法。
