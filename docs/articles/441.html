  • intersection n. 十字路口，交會處

    在肯亞首都一個繁忙的十字路口，當紅燈亮起時，一名穿著豹紋服裝的年輕人走到街上表演後空翻。
  • approach v. 接近，靠近
    One of them, juggling woven hats, breaks away and approaches the drivers of vehicles that have come to a halt.
    其中一人拿著編織帽拋接，脫離隊伍並走向已經停下來的車輛駕駛。
  • generous adj. 慷慨的，大方的
    He’s hoping for generous tips as he presents his hat to driver after driver while his mates continue their routine.
    他希望能獲得慷慨的小費，一邊把帽子遞給一個又一個的駕駛，同時他的同伴們繼續他們的表演。
  • occupation n. 職業，工作
    Mohammed Nyale, 22, said he started doing acrobatics hoping for a life-changing occupation.
    22歲的穆罕默德·尼亞勒（Mohammed Nyale）表示，他開始表演特技是希望能有一個改變人生的職業。
  • basic adj. 基本的，基礎的
    On a good day they might each earn roughly US$6, enough to look after their basic needs, though on some days they make more.
    在生意好的一天，他們每人大約可以賺取6美元，足以應付他們的基本需求，雖然某些日子的收入會更多。
  • wage n. 薪資，工資
    That is equal to the daily wage of most casual laborers in construction or agriculture in a country where unemployment is widespread.
    這相當於建築業或農業中大多數臨時工的日薪，在一個失業率普遍存在的國家。
  • unemployment n. 失業，失業率
    The youth unemployment rate stood between 4.9 percent and 9.9 percent in 2024, according to figures from the Kenya National Bureau of Statistics.

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Postmodifier)
    As the light turns red at a busy intersection in the Kenyan capital, a young man costumed in leopard print emerges into the street to perform somersaults.
    句中的「costumed in leopard print」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前方的主詞「a young man」，相當於形容詞子句「who is costumed in leopard print」，用來補充說明年輕人的穿著。
  • 現在分詞表示附带狀況 (Present Participle indicating Circumstance)
    He’s hoping for generous tips as he presents his hat to driver after driver while his mates continue their routine.
  • 關係副詞 where 引導的形容詞子句 (Adjective Clause introduced by 'where')
    That is equal to the daily wage of most casual laborers in construction or agriculture in a country where unemployment is widespread.
    句中的「where unemployment is widespread」是關係副詞 where 引導的形容詞子句，先行詞為「a country」，用來修飾並形容這個國家失業率普遍的狀況。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞 (Gerund Phrase as Subject)
    Earning money from street performances “is what enables us to live in Nairobi because we moved here from quite far and no one is helping us to pay rent,” Nyale said.
    句首的「Earning money from street performances」是動名詞片語，作為整個句子的主詞，視為單數，因此搭配單數動詞「is」。
