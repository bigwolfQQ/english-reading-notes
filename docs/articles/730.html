文法重點

  • 非限定關係代名詞子句（補充說明用法）
    Panapa, whose portfolio also includes Labour and Trade, made the remarks during a meeting with President Lai Ching-te (賴清德) at the Presidential Office on his first official visit to Taiwan in his current capacity.
    句中的「whose portfolio also includes Labour and Trade」為所有格關代（whose）引導的非限定關係子句，置於逗號後用來補充說明主詞 Panapa 的額外資訊（他的職掌也包含勞動與貿易），在翻譯時通常可順著語意譯出。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    Those ties have been strengthened by the Kaitasi Treaty, signed between the two countries in November 2025, which underpins cooperation in the economy, food security, medicine and public health, among other areas, he added.
  • 現在分詞作結果狀語（分詞構句）
    Going forward, Tuvalu hopes to strengthen cooperation in renewable energy and resilient infrastructure, and to expand opportunities in education and labor mobility, thereby advancing the sustainable development of the Pacific island nation, the minister said.
    句尾的「thereby advancing...」是由現在分詞（advancing）引導的分詞構句，放在主句動作之後，用來表示前面一連串行動所帶來的自然結果（進而推進了……的可持續發展）。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Those ties have been strengthened by the Kaitasi Treaty...
    句中的「have been strengthened」採用了現在完成式的被動語態（have/has been + 過去分詞），強調「這些紐帶」是被凱達席條約所強化、鞏固，焦點放在承受動作的主體上。
