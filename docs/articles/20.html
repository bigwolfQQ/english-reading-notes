文法重點

  • 倒裝句（Inversion）
    Signing the MOU for Lithuania were representatives of the Lithuanian Laser Association and the Center for Physical Sciences and Technology.
    此句採用了分詞倒裝結構。原句語序為「Representatives... were signing the MOU for Lithuania.」。新聞報導為了調整資訊焦點並使前文銜接更加順暢，將現在分詞片語「Signing the MOU for Lithuania」置於句首，連繫動詞「were」與主詞「representatives...」進行位置倒置。
  • 分詞構句（Participle Clause）
    The Central Weather Administration (CWA) yesterday forecast that Taiwan would be affected by the outer rainbands of Tropical Storm Krovanh today and tomorrow, bringing rain to northern Taiwan, although conditions are expected to ease on Monday and Tuesday.
    句中的「bringing rain to northern Taiwan」為現在分詞引導的分詞構句，補充說明主要子句動作（颱風外圍環流影響台灣）所帶來的附帶結果或現象，省略了連接詞（如 and）與重複的主詞。
  • 同位語（Appositive）
    Arieh Warshel, a Nobel laureate in chemistry, has become the first Nobel Prize winner to receive an Employment Gold Card, the National Development Council (NDC) said yesterday.
    句中的「a Nobel laureate in chemistry」為名詞片語，作為專有名詞「Arieh Warshel」的同位語，直接放在主詞後面並以雙逗號隔開，用來簡潔地補充說明該人物的身分與頭銜。
  • 非限定用法關係子句（Non-restrictive Relative Clause）
    NDC Minister Yeh Chun-hsien (葉俊顯) presented the card to Warshel, who won the 2013 Nobel Prize in Chemistry alongside Martin Karplus and Michael Levitt for their development of multiscale models for complex chemical systems.
    句中的「who won the 2013 Nobel Prize...」是由關係代名詞「who」引導的形容詞子句。因為先行詞「Warshel」為特定專有名詞，故使用逗號隔開的非限定用法，補充說明其過去獲得諾貝爾獎的詳細成就。
