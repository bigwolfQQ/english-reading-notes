單字解析

  • forecast v. / n. 預報，預測
    The Central Weather Administration (CWA) yesterday forecast that Taiwan would be affected by the outer rainbands of Tropical Storm Krovanh today and tomorrow
    中央氣象署（CWA）昨天預報，台灣今天與明天將受到熱帶性低氣壓克羅凡的外圍雨帶影響
  • significant adj. 顯著的，重大的
    it is not expected to bring a significant drop in temperatures, it said.
    氣象署也表示，預期並不會帶來氣溫的明顯下降。
  • receive v. 收到，獲得
    Arieh Warshel, a Nobel laureate in chemistry, has become the first Nobel Prize winner to receive an Employment Gold Card
    化學諾貝爾獎得主亞利耶·瓦謝爾（Arieh Warshel）已成為第一位獲得「就業金卡」的諾貝爾獎得主。
  • serve as v. phr. 擔任，充當
    He was invited this year by Chen Kuan-neng (陳冠能), dean of the International College of Semiconductor Technology at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University, to serve as an honorary chair professor
    他今年受國立陽明交通大學國際半導體產業學院院長陳冠能邀請，擔任榮譽講座教授
  • collaborate / collaboration v. / n. 合作，協力
    Taipei would contribute 5.6 million euros (US$6.5 million) over two years to promote private-sector collaboration through international seminars
    台北將在兩年內投入 560 萬歐元，透過國際研討會來推動民間部門的合作
  • aim at v. phr. 旨在，以……為目標
    signing a memorandum of understanding (MOU) aimed at building advanced packaging machinery for the next generation of computer chips.
    簽署了一份旨在為下一代電腦晶片打造先進封裝機械的備忘錄（MOU）。
  • private-sector adj. 民間企業的，私部門的
    The MOU is aimed at getting private businesses to jointly develop advanced chip packaging equipment
    該備忘錄旨在促使民間企業共同開發先進晶片封裝設備
  • representative n. 代表
    Chang signed the MOU on the final day of the three-day Semicon Taiwan expo alongside representatives from two Taiwanese trade groups
    張培仁在為期三天的台灣國際半導體展最後一天，與兩個台灣貿易團體的代表一同簽署了該備忘錄
  • exile n. 交流，交換
    through international seminars, academic and industry exchanges, and technology matchmaking
    透過國際研討會、學術與產業交流以及技術媒合

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    signing a memorandum of understanding (MOU) aimed at building advanced packaging machinery for the next generation of computer chips.
    句中的「aimed at...」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「memorandum of understanding (MOU)」，相當於關代子句「which is aimed at...」，在商業與新聞英語中非常常見，能讓句子更簡潔。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Taiwan would be affected by the outer rainbands of Tropical Storm Krovanh today and tomorrow
    句型使用「would be + 過去分詞 (affected)」，強調台灣是承受颱風外圍雨帶影響的受事者，多益考試常考被動語態的動詞變化及介系詞搭配（此處搭配 by）。
  • 不定詞片語作目的狀語 (Infinitive Phrase of Purpose)
    Taipei would contribute 5.6 million euros (US$6.5 million) over two years to promote private-sector collaboration...
    句尾的「to promote...」為不定詞片語，用來表達撥款的「目的」（為了促進民間部門的合作），這是多益閱讀測驗中常見的句構，常用於回答 Why 相關的細節題。
