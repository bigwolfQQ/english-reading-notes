副總統蕭美琴：儘管受到中國施壓，台灣有權利交朋友

Taiwan has right to make friends despite Chinese pressure: VP Hsiao

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • presence n. 存在、出席、能見度
    Taiwan has the same right as others to make friends and work with like-minded partners despite Beijing's efforts to restrict its international presence
    儘管北京方面努力限制台灣的國際能見度，台灣與其他人擁有相同的權利去結交朋友，並與理念相近的夥伴合作
  • opportunity n. 機會
    she said, adding that it cherishes every opportunity to make its voice heard internationally.
    並補充說台灣珍惜每一個讓國際社會聽到其聲音的機會。
  • participation n. 參與
    Her participation was not announced until after she arrived in Italy, to prevent interference from Beijing.
    她的行程直到抵達義大利後才公布，以防止北京的干預。
  • prevent v. 預防、防止
    Her participation was not announced until after she arrived in Italy, to prevent interference from Beijing.
    她的行程直到抵達義大利後才公布，以防止北京的干預。
  • agenda n. 議程
    Picierno also said the European Parliament's agenda, particularly her own, would not be determined by Beijing.
    皮契諾還表示，歐洲議會的議程，特別是她自己的議程，不會由北京來決定。
  • accept v. 接受
    Picierno also accepted Hsiao's invitation to visit Taiwan, saying the trip would be arranged in the coming weeks and months.
    皮契諾也接受了蕭美琴訪問台灣的邀請，並表示該行程將在未來幾週和幾個月內安排。
  • protest n. / v. 抗議
    Several Italian politicians also criticized Beijing's formal protest over Hsiao's visit.
    幾位義大利政治人物也批評了北京針對蕭美琴訪問提出的正式抗議。
  • response n. 回應、回覆
    In a written response to CNA, European Parliament lawmaker Engin Eroglu said Hsiao's visit reflected Europe's growing recognition of Taiwan as a partner
    歐洲議會議員埃羅格盧在給中央社的書面回覆中表示，蕭美琴的訪問反映出歐洲日益認可台灣作為一個夥伴
  • preserve v. 維護、保存
    During the conference, Hsiao described Ukraine's situation as a warning that Taiwan must do more to preserve peace by strengthening its defenses
    在會議期間，蕭美琴將烏克蘭的局勢形容為一項警告，即台灣必須採取更多措施來維護和平：加強防衛
  • secure v. 確保、獲得
    by strengthening its defenses, securing its role in global technology supply chains, and deepening international partnerships.
    加強防衛、鞏固其在全球科技供應鏈中的角色，以及深化國際夥伴關係。
  • yield v. 屈服、產出
    Taiwan would neither provoke China nor yield to coercion and would not change its democratic way of life, she said.
    她表示，台灣既不會挑釁中國，也不會屈服於脅迫，且不會改變其民主的生活方式。
  • circumstance n. 情況、環境
    Hsiao recalled her nickname "cat warrior," saying cats may appear small but can jump several times their height and survive difficult circumstances.
    蕭美琴回憶起她的綽號「戰貓」，並表示貓雖然看起來很小，但可以跳出自身身高好幾倍的高度，並在艱難的環境中生存。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作補充說明（補充子句）
    she said, adding that it cherishes every opportunity to make its voice heard internationally.
    句尾的「adding that...」是由連接詞結合現在分詞構成的分詞片語，用來補充說明主句動作發生時的伴隨狀況或結果，相當於「and she added that...」的簡化寫法。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Her participation was not announced until after she arrived in Italy, to prevent interference from Beijing.
    句子的主詞 Her participation（她的參與）是動作的承受者，因此使用「was not announced」的過去式被動語態，強調動作的對象而非發動者。
  • 對等連接詞並列多個動詞 (Neither... nor... 結構)
    Taiwan would neither provoke China nor yield to coercion and would not change its democratic way of life, she said.
    使用「neither... nor...」連接兩個否定動作（neither provoke... nor yield...），用來表達「既不……也不……」，在商業與新聞英文中常用於強調雙重的否定立場。
