文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Heavy rain, defined as rainfall exceeding 80 millimeters in 24 hours or 40 mm in one hour, is expected in Keelung City and other north coast areas...
    句中的「defined as...」是由過去分詞引導的片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「Heavy rain」。其完整結構為「which is defined as...」，省略了「which is」以精簡語句，在新聞英語中非常常見。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    ...severe convection, which is often accompanied by thunder, strong winds, and sudden heavy downpours, he said.
    句中的「which is often accompanied by...」是一個非限定關係子句（前面有逗號隔開），用來補充說明前面的先行詞「severe convection」（劇烈對流）。「which」代替先行詞在子句中作主詞，並使用被動語態表達「伴隨著」。
  • 被動語態的預期用法 (Passive Voice with Expect)
    ...the two tropical storms currently over the northeastern Pacific, Saudel and Krovanh, are not expected to directly affect Taiwan on Thursday or Friday.
    此句使用了「be not expected to + V」的被動語態結構，用來表達客觀的預測或預期（「被預期不會...」）。這在商業新聞報導中經常用於談論計畫、時程或預測結果。
