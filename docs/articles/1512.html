巴拉圭與貝里斯譴責中國制裁

Paraguay and Belize condemn China sanctions

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • condemn v. 譴責
    Belize and Paraguay on Monday condemned Beijing sanctions over their countries’ ties with Taiwan
    貝里斯與巴拉圭於週一譴責北京因兩國與台灣的關係而對其實施制裁
  • sanction n. 制裁
    Earlier on Monday, Beijing announced sanctions against Paraguayan Minister of Information and Communication Technologies Gustavo Villate
    週一稍早，北京宣布對巴拉圭資訊與通訊技術部長古斯塔夫·維拉特實施制裁
  • reject v. 拒絕、不接受
    The Paraguayan Ministry of Foreign Affairs said it rejected the sanctions
    巴拉圭外交部表示拒絕接受這些制裁
  • reaffirm v. 重申、再次確認
    It added that it reaffirmed its support for Taiwan and its commitment to democratic values and principles.
    該部補充說，它重申對台灣的支持以及對民主價值與原則的承諾。
  • establish v. 建立、設立
    Hon Hai Precision Industry Co (鴻海精密) had been invited to establish operations in Paraguay
    鴻海精密工業股份有限公司已被邀請在巴拉圭設立營運據點
  • participate v. 參與、參加
    participate in the “Yguazu Digital” project, an artificial intelligence-focused technology hub.
    參與聚焦人工智慧的科技樞紐「伊瓜蘇數位」專案。
  • statement n. 聲明、陳述
    Meanwhile, the Belizean Ministry of Foreign Affairs said in a statement that the sanctions would not alter Belize’s foreign policy
    同時，貝里斯外交部在聲明中表示，制裁不會改變貝里斯的外交政策
  • alter v. 改變、修改
    the sanctions would not alter Belize’s foreign policy and the government could continue to stand behind Fonseca.
    制裁不會改變貝里斯的外交政策，政府將繼續全力支持福 Fonseca。
  • policy n. 政策
    Under the UN Charter, “every state has the right to conduct its foreign policy free from external pressure,” it added.
    根據《聯合國憲章》，「每個國家都有權在不受外部壓力影響的情況下奉行其外交政策」，它補充道。
  • partnership n. 夥伴關係、合作關係
    and the partnership has delivered in development, healthcare, education, infrastructure, the environment and agriculture
    且此夥伴關係在發展、醫療保健、教育、基礎設施、環境和農業方面帶來了實質成效

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    The projects include a planned data center in Nueva Asuncion that incorporates security standards to prevent foreign interference in the companies involved in the project, he said.
    句中的「to prevent...」與後續的分詞結構用來修飾前面的名詞，其中「involved in the project」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾「the companies」，省去了「which are」，讓句子更簡潔。
  • 引述句與連接詞省略 (Reported Speech without 'that')
    Villate told the Spanish-language Paraguayan daily ABC Color the sanctions were “predictable” and linked them to several cooperation projects between Taiwan and Paraguay.
    在動詞 told 之後省略了連接詞 that，直接接上受詞子句「the sanctions were 'predictable'...」。這是新聞英文中非常常見的寫法，能使報導節奏更明快。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    “Measures directed at an individual for carrying out the lawful duties of his office are unwarranted, and we reject them,” it said.
    句中的 "are directed at"（針對）使用了被動語態，強調動作的承受者（Measures 措施），而「directed at an individual...」則是由分詞片語簡化而來的後位修飾語。
  • 名詞子句作受詞 (Noun Clause as Object)
    The ministry reiterated that the Republic of China is a sovereign and independent country, and that it and the People’s Republic of China are not subordinate to each other.
    reiterated（重申）後面接了兩個由「that」引導的名詞子句，作為該動詞的受詞，用來表達官方正式聲明的具體內容。
