文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Postmodifier)
    Yu quoted Trump as warning that communism was making a comeback and threatening the US, and that it had not only deepened divisions within US society but posed a threat greater than those of World War I and World War II combined.
    句中的 "warning that..." 和 "threatening the US" 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的動詞或子句狀態，補充說明川普發出的警告內容與威脅。
  • 過去分詞作後位修飾 (Past Participle as Postmodifier)
    Yu quoted Trump as warning that communism was making a comeback and threatening the US, and that it had not only deepened divisions within US society but posed a threat greater than those of World War I and World War II combined.
    句尾的 "combined" 是過去分詞，用來修飾前面的名詞詞組 "World War I and World War II"（意指兩次世界大戰「合併起來」的威脅），展現了分詞精簡修飾的用法。
  • 表目的與結果的副詞片語 (Adverbial Infinitive of Purpose)
    “Rather than hoping for change to happen with the CCP, we should be prepared for war by building the capacity and resolve to deter China.”
    句中的 "by building the capacity and resolve..." 使用了介係詞 "by + 動名詞" 來表達「透過……方式」達成備戰的手段；而句尾的 "to deter China" 則是不定詞作形容詞用，修飾前面的名詞 "capacity and resolve"。
  • 間接問句 / 名詞子句當受詞 (Noun Clause as Object)
    If China becomes a democratic country, people there could organize a referendum on whether China should overtake Taiwan by force, Lee said.
    句中的 "whether China should overtake Taiwan by force" 是一個引導名詞子句，作為介係詞 "on" 的受詞，用來表達「是否……」的不確定情況。
