Taichung mayoral rivals Ho, Chiang visit transport project sites

單字解析

  • inspect v. 視察、檢查
    Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) Taichung mayoral candidate Ho Hsin-chun (何欣純) on Monday inspected a newly opened bus transit center in the city...
    民主進步黨台中市長參選人何欣純於周一視察了市內一座新啟用的公車轉運中心...
  • opponent n. 對手、敵手
    ...while her Kuomintang (KMT) opponent Johnny Chiang (江啟臣) visited the site of a proposed road extension to the Central Taiwan Science Park.
    ...而她的國民黨對手江啟臣則走訪了中科擬議中的道路延伸工程預定地。
  • complex n. 綜合大樓、建築群
    She criticized the current Taichung City government over the poor design of the complex, which serves several intercity express bus routes.
    她批評現任台中市政府該綜合大樓設計不良，該大樓目前服務數條城際快線公車路線。
  • operate v. 營運、運作
    The entire complex cost NT$4.7 billion (US$147 million), yet only a few buses are currently operating there...
    整座綜合大樓耗費新台幣47億元（合147萬美元），然而目前卻只有少數公車在該處營運...
  • improve v. 改善、改進
    ...she said, calling on the KMT-led city government to make improvements as soon as possible to strengthen the city's bus network.
    ...她並呼籲國民黨執政的市府盡快進行改善，以強化城市的公車網路。
  • coordinate v. 協調、配合
    In response, the Taichung City Transportation Bureau said it would continue coordinating with bus operators to add more routes...
    對此，台中市交通局表示，將持續與公車業者協調，為新啟用的轉運中心增加更多路線...
  • burden n. 負擔、重擔
    The heavy financial burden should not fall solely on the local government...
    這項沉重的財務負擔不應全由地方政府承擔...
  • solely adv. 僅、單獨地、唯一地
    The heavy financial burden should not fall solely on the local government...
    這項沉重的財務負擔不應全由地方政府承擔...
  • urge v. 敦促、力勸
    ...he said, urging the central government to jointly shoulder the cost to help resolve the traffic challenges facing Taichung residents.
    ...他表示，並敦促中央政府應共同分攤經費，以協助解決台中居民面臨的交通挑戰。
  • jointly adv. 共同、聯合地
    ...urging the central government to jointly shoulder the cost to help resolve the traffic challenges facing Taichung residents.
    ...敦促中央政府應共同分攤經費，以協助解決台中居民面臨的交通挑戰。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...urging the central government to jointly shoulder the cost to help resolve the traffic challenges facing Taichung residents.
    句中的 "facing Taichung residents" 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "the traffic challenges"（面對台中居民的交通挑戰）。這相當於一個形容詞子句 "that face Taichung residents" 的簡化版，能讓句子更精簡流暢。
  • 非限定關係代間子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    She criticized the current Taichung City government over the poor design of the complex, which serves several intercity express bus routes.
    逗號後面的 ", which serves several intercity express bus routes" 是一個非限定關係子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞 "the complex"。非限定子句前通常有逗號隔開，且不能使用 "that" 代替 "which"。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The heavy financial burden should not fall solely on the local government, he said, urging the central government to jointly shoulder the cost to help resolve the traffic challenges facing Taichung residents.
    文章多處使用被動語態來客觀陳述新聞事實。例如 "Local elections across Taiwan will be held on Nov. 28."（選舉將被舉行＝將舉行），強調動作的承受者而非施事者。
