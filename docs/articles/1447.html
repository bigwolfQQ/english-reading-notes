老人受虐通報數超過 2万6千件

Reports of elder-abuse exceed 26,000

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • highlight v. 凸顯，強調
    More than 26,000 cases of domestic violence against elderly people were reported last year, Ministry of Health and Welfare data showed, highlighting the need to protect Taiwan’s 4.67 million people aged 65 or older.
    衛生福利部資料顯示，去年針對老年人的家庭暴力案件超過 26,000 件，凸顯出保護台灣 467 萬名 65 歲或以上人口的必要性。
  • suspected adj. 疑似的，嫌疑的
    Reports of suspected domestic violence against elderly people rose to 26,684 cases last year from 20,182 in 2022, Department of Protective Services data showed.
    保護服務司資料顯示，去年疑似虐待老人家的家暴通報案件，從 2022 年的 20,182 件增加至 26,684 件。
  • account for phr. v. 占（比例），說明
    There were 14,292 cases in the first half of this year, with psychological abuse accounting for 44.1 percent, physical abuse 37.9 percent, and stalking or harassment 25.5 percent, with some cases involving multiple forms of abuse, the department said.
    該部門表示，今年上半年有 14,292 件，其中心理虐待占 44.1%，身體虐待占 37.9%，跟蹤或騷擾占 25.5%，部分案件涉及多種形式的虐待。
  • involve v. 涉及，包含
    Nearly 58 percent of the cases involved the elderly person’s descendants, while 27.1 percent involved a spouse or partner, it added.
    該部門補充說，近 58% 的案件涉及長者的直系晚輩，而 27.1% 涉及配偶或伴侶。
  • distribution n. 分配，分發
    Elder abuse is often associated with family dynamics and the distribution of family resources, department Director Kuo Tsai-jung (郭彩榕) said yesterday.
    部門主管郭彩榕昨天表示，老人受虐通常與家庭動力及家庭資源分配有關。
  • promote v. 推廣，促進，宣導
    The ministry has worked with local governments to promote public awareness of elder abuse as well as the 113 hotline for domestic violence, while bolstering reporting mechanisms, Kuo said.
    郭彩榕表示，衛福部已與地方政府合作，提升大眾對老人虐待及家暴 113 專線的意識，同時強化通報機制。
  • bolster v. 強化，支持，改善
    The ministry has worked with local governments to promote public awareness of elder abuse as well as the 113 hotline for domestic violence, while bolstering reporting mechanisms, Kuo said.
    郭彩榕表示，衛福部已與地方政府合作，提升大眾對老人虐待及家暴 113 專線的意識，同時強化通報機制。
  • depend v. 依賴，依靠
    In some cases where adult children depend on money from their parents, they might have feelings of low self-worth due to employment setbacks or health issues, and could turn to emotional manipulation and verbal and physical abuse to control their parents, she said.
    她指出，在某些成年子女依賴父母金錢的情況下，他們可能會因就業挫折或健康問題而產生低自尊感，並可能轉向情感操控以及言語和身體虐待來控制父母。
  • scarce adj. 缺乏的，稀有的
    If family conflicts are not addressed, more serious violence could occur if parents lose their ability to care for themselves, household resources become scarce or their children have substance abuse problems, she added.
    她補充說，如果家庭衝突未獲得處理，當父母失去自理能力、家庭資源變得稀缺或子女有物質濫用問題時，可能會發生更嚴重的暴力。
  • conduct v. 執行，舉辦，進行
    In cases such as these, social workers usually conduct interviews and provide psychological support to help parents establish financial and emotional boundaries, while guiding parents toward legal resources to protect themselves, Kuo said.
    郭彩榕表示，在這些情況下，社工通常會進行訪談並提供心理支持，以協助父母建立財務和情感界線，同時引導父母利用法律資源來保護自己。
  • utilize v. 利用，使用
    One way to prevent elder abuse is to ease the caregiving burden on family members by utilizing long-term care services, Providence University adjunct associate professor of social work Wang Hsiu-yen (王秀燕) said.
    靜宜大學社會工作學系兼任副教授王秀燕表示，預防老人受虐的方法之一，是利用長照服務來減輕家庭成員的照顧負擔。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Phrase acting as an adverbial modifier / Result）作補充說明
    More than 26,000 cases of domestic violence against elderly people were reported last year, Ministry of Health and Welfare data showed, highlighting the need to protect Taiwan’s 4.67 million people aged 65 or older.
    句尾的「highlighting...」是以現在分詞開頭的分詞片語，用來補充說明前面整句所產生的結果或帶有的意義（即「凸顯出……的必要性」），使語氣更為緊湊流暢。
  • 過去分詞作後置修飾語（Past Participle as Postmodifier）
    Reports of suspected domestic violence against elderly people rose to 26,684 cases last year from 20,182 in 2022, Department of Protective Services data showed.
    句中的「suspected」是過去分詞，用來修飾後方的名詞片語「domestic violence」，表示「被懷疑的（家庭暴力）」，具有被動與完成的意涵。
  • 對等連接詞 while 引導對比或同時發生的狀況
    The ministry has worked with local governments to promote public awareness of elder abuse as well as the 113 hotline for domestic violence, while bolstering reporting mechanisms, Kuo said.
    連接詞 "while" 在此處連接兩個動作，表示衛福部在推廣大眾意識的同時，也「同步」強化了通報機制，展現兩個行動同時進行的狀況。
  • 關係副詞 where 引導形容詞子句
    In some cases where adult children depend on money from their parents, they might have feelings of low self-worth due to employment setbacks or health issues...
    句中的 "where" 引導一個形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的先行詞 "cases"（在此處表示「在某些……的情況下」），用以說明該狀況發生的情境。
  • 動狀詞片語（To-infinitive phrase）作目的補語
    One way to prevent elder abuse is to ease the caregiving burden on family members by utilizing long-term care services...
    句首的「To prevent elder abuse」是不定詞片語，用來作形容詞修飾前面的主詞「One way」，表達「預防老人受虐的方法（目的）」。
