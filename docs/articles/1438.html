文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語表附帶狀況（Absolute/Participial phrase）
    The Chiayi and the Penghu departed on Friday for a two-day joint patrol of Palau’s exclusive economic zone about 200 nautical miles southwest of Koror, accompanied by Palauan officials.
    句尾的「accompanied by Palauan officials」是過去分詞片語，用來補充說明主句動作發生時的伴隨狀況，意即「在帛琉官員的陪同下」。這種結構在多益閱讀測驗中常出現在分詞填空題。
  • 現在分詞作補充說明（Present participle as supplementary info）
    Only five people live on Pulo Anna Island, Kloulechad said, adding that the visit was a valuable opportunity to ensure their safety and well-being.
    逗號之後的「adding that...」是由現在分詞「add」引導的片語，用來補充說明說話者（Kloulechad）當時還多說了什麼話，相當於 and added that... 的簡化，能讓新聞報導的句子更為流暢緊湊。
  • 關係代間詞子句（Relative clause）
    It works closely with the US Coast Guard, which can enforce maritime laws in Palau’s exclusive economic zone under a bilateral agreement...
    句中的「which can enforce...」是非限定關係代間詞子句，前面帶有逗號，用來補充修飾先行詞「the US Coast Guard」，提供額外的資訊（說明美方能做什麼）。多益測驗常考relatives（which, who, that）的選擇題。
  • 對等連接詞替代與轉折副詞用法（Instead of）
    Palau does not have an independent national coast guard, instead relying on its marine law enforcement division and international partners to patrol its exclusive economic zone...
    句中的「instead relying on...」是由副詞「instead」加上現在分詞片語構成，用來表達「而不是……，而是轉而依賴……」。這種結構在商業與新聞英文中常用於對比兩種情況。
