勞動部警告 foodpanda：不得因外送延遲而扣減外送員薪資

MOL warns foodpanda over docking couriers’ pay for late deliveries

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • dock v. 扣除（薪資等）
    Delivery platform foodpanda could face fines up to NT$100,000 (US$3,178) per affected worker for illegally docking couriers’ pay for late deliveries based on unilaterally imposed time limits, the Ministry of Labor (MOL) said yesterday.
    勞動部昨天表示，外送平台 foodpanda 因根據單方強加的時間限制，非法扣減外送員因遲到而應得的報酬，每位受影響的勞工最高恐面臨新台幣 10 萬元（約合 3,178 美元）的罰款。
  • unilateral adj. 單方面的，單邊的
    When delivery delays occur, platforms should look into the causes and handle them via proper mechanisms instead of making unilateral pay deductions, the ministry said.
    勞動部表示，當外送延遲發生時，平台應該調查原因並透過適當機制處理，而不是進行單方面的薪資扣減。
  • impose v. 實施，強制徵收（罰款、稅金等）
    Local labor bureaus can impose fines ranging from NT $20,000 to NT$100,000 per affected courier once they determine the validity of the complaints filed by workers...
    地方勞動局在認定勞工所提申訴的有效性後，可對每位受影響的外送員處以新台幣 2 萬元至 10 萬元的罰款...
  • disclose v. 透露，公開
    Wang did not disclose how many complaints had been filed with local authorities, citing a lack of complete data, but said all reported cases involved foodpanda.
    王厚偉未透露向地方主管機關提出申訴的具體人數，理由是缺乏完整數據，但他表示所有通報的案件都指向 foodpanda。
  • deduction n. 扣除額，扣減
    According to Wang, delivery couriers reported facing pay deductions for exceeding estimated delivery times by as little as one to five minutes...
    王厚偉指出，外送員回報稱，只要超過預估外送時間一到五分鐘，就會面臨薪資扣減...
  • come into force phr. （法律等）生效，實施
    Under the law, which came into force on July 21, platforms must pay riders based on the exact time a delivery takes...
    根據於 7 月 21 日生效的法律，平台必須根據外送所花費的確切時間來支付外送員報酬...
  • joint adj. 聯合的，共同的
    Earlier yesterday, the National Delivery Industrial Union and two other unions issued a joint statement accusing foodpanda of “maliciously scapegoating delivery workers for order delays” as a pretext to unlawfully dock their pay.
    昨天稍早，全國外送產業工會與其他兩個工會發表聯合聲明，指控 foodpanda「惡意將訂單延遲歸咎於外送員」，以此作為非法扣減薪資的藉口。
  • pretext n. 藉口，托詞
    Earlier yesterday, the National Delivery Industrial Union and two other unions issued a joint statement accusing foodpanda of “maliciously scapegoating delivery workers for order delays” as a pretext to unlawfully dock their pay.
    昨天稍早，全國外送產業工會與其他兩個工會發表聯合聲明，指控 foodpanda「惡意將訂單延遲歸咎於外送員」，以此作為非法扣減薪資的藉口。
  • call on phr. v. 呼籲，號召
    The union coalition called on couriers to submit formal complaints to local labor departments if they experience illegal pay deductions.
    該工會聯盟呼籲外送員，若遭遇違法的薪資扣減，應向地方勞動部門正式提出申訴。
  • disburse v. 支付，撥款，發放
    In response, Emma Kuo (郭昕宜), foodpanda’s director of public affairs and public relations, told reporters that the platform has never refused to disburse rider pay due to delivery delays.
    對此，foodpanda 公共事務暨公共關係總監郭昕宜向記者表示，該平台從未因外送延遲而拒絕發放外送員的薪資。
  • clarify v. 澄清，說明清楚
    Kuo said the company only asked delivery couriers to clarify unusually long trips, adding that full pay will be disbursed as long as a valid explanation is provided.
    郭昕宜表示，公司僅要求外送員釐清異常長的行程，並補充只要提供合理的解釋，就會發放全額薪資。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    ...based on unilaterally imposed time limits, the Ministry of Labor (MOL) said yesterday.
    句中的 "unilaterally imposed" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾其後方的名詞 "time limits"（時間限制）。這種結構是由形容詞化的被動語態縮減而來，原句概念為「被單方面強加的時間限制」，能讓句子更為精簡流暢。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Under the law, which came into force on July 21, platforms must pay riders based on the exact time a delivery takes...
    句中的 ", which came into force on July 21," 是非限定關係子句，前方用逗號隔開，用來補充說明先行詞 "the law"。即使把這個子句拿掉，主要子句的意思依然完整，主要用於提供額外的背景資訊。
  • 條件副詞子句的簡化 (Conditional Clause with 'as long as')
    ...adding that full pay will be disbursed as long as a valid explanation is provided.
    句尾的 "as long as a valid explanation is provided" 是一個條件副詞子句，意為「只要……就……」。在此使用了被動語態 "is provided"（被提供），用來強調條件達成時，主句的動作（發放全額薪資）就會發生。
