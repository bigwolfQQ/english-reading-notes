亞特蘭大獵鷹隊以 45-24 擊敗紐奧良聖徒隊

Atlanta Falcons defeat New Orleans Saints 45-24

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • spoil v. 破壞，搞砸
    Atlanta Falcons running backs Bijan and Brian Robinson on Monday tore apart the Saints 45-24 to spoil the party on an emotional night in New Orleans, Louisiana.
    亞特蘭大獵鷹隊的跑鋒畢揚與布萊恩·羅賓遜於週一以 45-24 狠狠擊潰聖徒隊，在路易斯安那州紐奧良這個充滿情緒的夜晚攪亂了慶祝派對。
  • festivities n. 慶祝活動，歡慶
    The Robinsons — no relation — scored five rushing touchdowns between them to silence a crowd that had arrived in a boisterous mood for festivities to mark 20 years since the Superdome reopened after Hurricane Katrina.
    這兩位並無親戚關係的羅賓遜聯手攻下五次衝球達陣，讓原本帶著喧鬧心情前來參加慶祝超級巨蛋在卡翠娜颶風後重新開放 20 週年的觀眾們鴉雀無聲。
  • frustrated adj. 感到沮喪的，感到挫折的
    The Saints managed two Alvin Kamara rushing touchdowns, a third caught by Devaughn Vele, and a field goal early in the second half that supporters booed, frustrated by coach Kellen Moore’s apparent lack of faith in his offense on 4th down.
    聖徒隊僅靠著艾爾文·卡瑪拉的兩次衝球達陣、德沃恩·維勒接球得到的第三次達陣，以及下半場初期的記分射門苦撐；球迷們因為不滿教練凱倫·摩爾在第四檔時似乎對進攻缺乏信心而發出噓聲。
  • apparent adj. 顯而易見的，表面上的
    The Saints managed two Alvin Kamara rushing touchdowns, a third caught by Devaughn Vele, and a field goal early in the second half that supporters booed, frustrated by coach Kellen Moore’s apparent lack of faith in his offense on 4th down.
    聖徒隊僅靠著艾爾文·卡瑪拉的兩次衝球達陣、德沃恩·維勒接球得到的第三次達陣，以及下半場初期的記分射門苦撐；球迷們因為不滿教練凱倫·摩爾在第四檔時似乎對進攻缺乏信心而發出噓聲。
  • revitalized adj. 復甦的，重獲生機的
    Victory in the National Football Conference South clash sent the Falcons — revitalized since the return of quarterback Michael Penix Jr in their previous game — top of their division alongside Carolina, on 2-2, while New Orleans sank to 1-3.
    這場國家美式足球聯盟南區對決的勝利，讓自四分衛米高·彭尼斯上一場比賽復出後便重現生機的獵鷹隊，以 2-2 的戰績與卡羅萊納並列分區第一，而紐奧良則跌至 1-3。
  • extensive adj. 廣泛的，大量的，重大的
    The game was a rematch of the original “Domecoming” in late September 2006, when the Superdome reopened after sustaining extensive damage in Hurricane Katrina.
    這場比賽是 2006 年 9 月下旬最初那場「巨蛋回歸」的重賽，當時超級巨蛋在遭受卡翠娜颶風嚴重破壞後重新開放。
  • repair v. 修理，修補
    The Superdome served as a last resort shelter for 30,000 storm evacuees even after the hurricane tore a 15m hole in its giant white roof, and was rapidly repaired.
    儘管颶風在其巨大的白色屋頂上撕開了一個 15 公尺寬的大洞，超級巨蛋仍作為 3 万名風災撤離者的最後避難所，並迅速得到了修復。
  • contribute to phr. 促成，導致，貢獻
    New Orleans contributed to its own downfall, missing a field goal, turning over possession on a fumble, and then conceding a holding penalty when an Atlanta 4th and goal attempt appeared to have failed.
    紐奧良自食惡果，錯失射球、掉球導致球權轉移，接著在亞特蘭大第四檔進攻且達陣區前眼看要失敗時，又因犯規送出持球阻擋罰球。
  • make sb pay phr. 讓某人付出代價
    Bijan Robinson made them pay, rushing for his second.
    畢揚·羅賓遜讓他們付出了代價，再度衝球達陣。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾語 (Past Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    The Superdome served as a last resort shelter for 30,000 storm evacuees even after the hurricane tore a 15m hole in its giant white roof, and was rapidly repaired.
    句中的 "repaired" 是過去分詞，前面省略了主詞與be動詞（which was），用來修飾前面的主詞 The Superdome，表示被動的狀態（迅速被修復）。
  • 分詞構句表附帶結果或狀態 (Participial Phrase indicating result/circumstance)
    New Orleans contributed to its own downfall, missing a field goal, turning over possession on a fumble, and then conceding a holding penalty when an Atlanta 4th and goal attempt appeared to have failed.
    句尾的 "missing...", "turning over...", "conceding..." 均為現在分詞片語構成的分詞構句，用來補充說明主句所發生的連串動作與結果，表示「錯失……、掉球……並吞下……犯規」。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    The Robinsons — no relation — scored five rushing touchdowns between them to silence a crowd that had arrived in a boisterous mood for festivities...
    「that had arrived in a boisterous mood for festivities...」是一個由關係代名詞 that 引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾先行詞 a crowd（人群），說明這群人抵達時的狀態。
  • 插入語用法 (Parenthetical Expression)
    The Robinsons — no relation — scored five rushing touchdowns between them...
    句中的「— no relation —」是破折號夾入的插入語，用來補充解釋這兩個姓 Robinson 的球員彼此「沒有親戚關係」，使讀者不會產生誤解。
