台灣研究顯示 持續進行的自然選擇正在形塑疾病易感性

Taiwan study shows ongoing natural selection shaping disease susceptibility

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • contemporary adj. 當代的，現代的
    A study by researchers at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (NYCU) has documented ongoing natural selection shaping disease susceptibility among Taiwan's contemporary population.
    國立陽明交通大學（NYCU）研究人員的一項研究記錄了台灣當代人口中，持續進行的自然選擇正在形塑疾病易感性。
  • analyze v. 分析
    The study analyzed genetic data from 72,635 Han Taiwanese individuals using the Taiwan Biobank, a national biomedical research database, NYCU said in a news release issued Tuesday.
    陽明交大在周二發布的新聞稿中表示，該研究利用國家級生醫研究資料庫「台灣生物資料庫」，分析了 72,635 名漢人台灣人的基因數據。
  • issue v. 發布，發行
    The study analyzed genetic data from 72,635 Han Taiwanese individuals using the Taiwan Biobank, a national biomedical research database, NYCU said in a news release issued Tuesday.
    陽明交大在周二發布的新聞稿中表示，該研究利用國家級生醫研究資料庫「台灣生物資料庫」，分析了 72,635 名漢人台灣人的基因數據。
  • identify v. 識別，確認，認出
    By comparing frequencies of genetic variants (known as allele frequency) across different age groups, the team identified 168 variants that appeared to be undergoing natural selection, including 159 variants that showed a gradual decline or disappearance across generations, the university said.
    該大學表示，透過比較不同年齡層基因變異（稱為等位基因頻率）的頻率，研究團隊識別出 168 個似乎正在經歷自然選擇的變異，其中包括 159 個在世代間呈現逐漸減少或消失的變異。
  • decline n. / v. 下降，衰退
    By comparing frequencies of genetic variants (known as allele frequency) across different age groups, the team identified 168 variants that appeared to be undergoing natural selection, including 159 variants that showed a gradual decline or disappearance across generations, the university said.
    該大學表示，透過比較不同年齡層基因變異（稱為等位基因頻率）的頻率，研究團隊識別出 168 個似乎正在經歷自然選擇的變異，其中包括 159 個在世代間呈現逐漸減少或消失的變異。
  • associated with phr. 與……相關的
    Researchers noted a "consistent signal" involving red blood cell traits, with 149 variants that were decreasing in frequency among younger age groups associated with larger red blood cell volume and lower hemoglobin concentration, NYCU said.
    陽明交大表示，研究人員注意到一個涉及紅血球特徵的「一致性信號」，在年輕年齡層中頻率正在下降的 149 個變異，與較大的紅血球容積和較低的血紅素濃度有關。
  • prevalence n. 流行，盛行率
    Such a consistent pattern, it said, suggested that natural selection was progressively reducing the prevalence of genetic variants associated with larger red blood cell volume.
    該校表示，這種一致的模式表明，自然選擇正在逐漸降低與較大紅血球容積相關的基因變異的流行率。
  • relate v. 使有關聯，與……有關
    Researchers hypothesized that this trend was related to historical adaptation to infectious diseases like malaria, which was widespread in Taiwan until about 60 years ago.
    研究人員假設，這種趨勢與歷史上對傳染病（如瘧疾）的適應有關，瘧疾直到大約 60 年前在台灣仍很普遍。
  • bring benefits phr. 帶來好處
    Knowledge of such rare and population-specific pathogenic variants could bring benefits in areas such as disease risk assessment and precision medicine, it added.
    該校補充說，了解這類罕見且具群體特異性的致病變異，能為疾病風險評估和精準醫療等領域帶來益處。
  • collaborate v. 合作
    The study was led by Ko Wen-ya (可文亞), associate professor in NYCU's Department of Life Sciences and Institute of Genome Sciences, in collaboration with Yoko Satta of the Graduate University of Advanced Studies in Japan.
    該研究由陽明交大生命科學系暨基因體科學研究所副教授可文亞領導，並與日本綜合研究大學院大學的佐田亞衣子合作完成。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    A study by researchers at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (NYCU) has documented ongoing natural selection shaping disease susceptibility among Taiwan's contemporary population.
    句中的「shaping disease susceptibility...」是一個現在分詞短語，用來修飾前面的名詞片語「ongoing natural selection」，相當於關代子句「which shapes disease susceptibility...」，使語意更為緊湊。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The study analyzed genetic data from 72,635 Han Taiwanese individuals using the Taiwan Biobank, a national biomedical research database, NYCU said in a news release issued Tuesday.
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    By comparing frequencies of genetic variants (known as allele frequency) across different age groups, the team identified 168 variants that appeared to be undergoing natural selection...
    句中使用「that appeared to be undergoing natural selection」這個由 that 引導的限定性關係代名詞子句，用來修飾先行詞 168 variants，補充說明這些變異的特徵。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    This type of natural selection, in which genetic variations detrimental to survival are gradually removed over successive generations, is known as "purifying selection," NYCU said.
    句中的「are gradually removed」與「is known as」皆採用被動語態（be + 過去分詞），強調動作的承受者（基因變異與這種自然選擇類型），是新聞英文與學術寫作中非常常見的句構。
