分析家：賴清德二戰言論警告中國重塑世界秩序的企圖

Lai's WWII remarks warn of China's bid to reshape world order: Analysts

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • emphasize v. 強調、重視
    President Lai Ching-te's emphasis on preserving the historical facts of World War II in his National Day address highlighted concerns over Beijing's efforts to undermine the postwar international order.
    賴清德總統在國慶演說中強調維護第二次世界大戰歷史事實的重要性，凸顯了各界對北京企圖破壞戰後國際秩序的擔憂。
  • undermine v. 逐漸削弱、暗中破壞
    President Lai Ching-te's emphasis on preserving the historical facts of World War II in his National Day address highlighted concerns over Beijing's efforts to undermine the postwar international order.
    賴清德總統在國慶演說中強調維護第二次世界大戰歷史事實的重要性，凸顯了各界對北京企圖破壞戰後國際秩序的擔憂。
  • tolerance n. 容忍、寬容
    "There must be no tolerance for distorting the history of World War II. There must be respect for the right of the people of Taiwan to exist," Lai said.
    「絕不能容忍扭曲第二次世界大戰的歷史。必須尊重台灣人民生存的權利，」賴總統說。
  • ensure v. 確保、保證
    "And above all, democracies in the Indo-Pacific must join forces to ensure the status quo of peace and stability in the region is not changed," he continued.
    「最重要的是，印太地區的民主國家必須齊心協力，確保該地區和平與穩定的現狀不被改變，」他接著說。
  • cooperate v. 合作
    The remarks came after Chinese President Xi Jinping and U.S. President Donald Trump recalled U.S.-China cooperation during World War II at their meeting in Washington late last month.
    這些言論發表於中國國家主席習近平與美國總統川普於上月底在華盛頓會晤時，共同回憶美中在二戰期間的合作之後。
  • relocate v. 搬遷、遷移（辦公室或機關）
    The ROC government relocated to Taiwan after the war.
    中華民國政府戰後遷台。
  • commitment n. 承諾、投入
    Taiwan's commitment to maintaining the status quo is not only about defending the country's right to exist, but also about upholding the postwar principle that peace and freedom should not be coerced through violence or threats, the official said
    該官員表示，台灣對維持現狀的承諾，不僅是為了捍衛國家的生存權，也是為了堅持和平與自由不應透過暴力或威脅來強迫的戰後原則
  • uphold v. 維護、支持
    Taiwan's commitment to maintaining the status quo is not only about defending the country's right to exist, but also about upholding the postwar principle that peace and freedom should not be coerced through violence or threats, the official said
    該官員表示，台灣對維持現狀的承諾，不僅是為了捍衛國家的生存權，也是為了堅持和平與自由不應透過暴力或威脅來強迫的戰後原則
  • consistent adj. 一致的、始終如一的
    On cross-strait relations, Wang said that Lai's remarks were consistent with his administration's previous position and did not represent a change in policy.
    在兩岸關係方面，王宏仁表示，賴清德的言論與其政府先前的立場一致，並未代表政策的改變。
  • reiterate v. 重申
    By reiterating the need to maintain peace and stability across the Taiwan Strait in the address, Lai implicitly endorsed a "no independence, no unification" stance, countering Beijing's repeated claims that Taiwan is responsible for disrupting the cross-strait status quo, Lin said.
    林子立說，賴清德在演說中重申維持台海和平穩定的必要性，等於是隱晦地背書了「不統不獨」的立場，藉此反擊北京一再聲稱台灣應為破壞台海現狀負責的說法。
  • contribute v. 貢獻
    The "light of the world" reflects Taiwan's contributions to the international community through its economic development, technological advances and other areas, the official said.
    該官員表示，「世界的光」反映了台灣透過經濟發展、技術進步及其他領域對國際社會的貢獻。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Present Participle）作附加補充說明
    By pointing to Beijing's "distortion" of World War II history, Lai cautioned against the risks posed by its attempts to overturn existing international rules and disrupt the status quo, a senior official who wished to remain anonymous told CNA.
    句首的「By pointing to...」由介系詞 by 加上動名詞片語構成，用來表示「透過……方式」；而「posed by...」則是過去分詞片語當作形容詞，用來後置修飾前面的名詞 the risks。
  • Not only... but also... 對等連接詞片語
    Taiwan's commitment to maintaining the status quo is not only about defending the country's right to exist, but also about upholding the postwar principle that peace and freedom should not be coerced through violence or threats, the official said
    「not only A, but also B」用來連接兩個對等的結構（此處皆為 about 引導的介系詞片語），強調「不僅 A，而且 B」，在多益考試的克漏字與閱讀測驗中是常見的對等連接詞考點。
  • 關係代名詞子句（Adjective Clause）
    A senior official who wished to remain anonymous told CNA...
    句中的「who wished to remain anonymous」是主格關係代名詞 who 引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾先行詞 A senior official。在商業寫作與新聞英語中，常用此結構來精確補充人物的背景或特徵。
  • 現在分詞片語作後置修飾（Post-modification by Participle Phrase）
    Wang Hung-jen, a political science professor at National Cheng Kung University, said Beijing has long promoted a narrative portraying today's China as having fought side by side with the U.S. in World War II...
    句中的「portraying today's China as...」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 narrative（敘事），相當於完整子句「which portrays today's China as...」，分詞片語能讓英文句子顯得更簡潔流暢。
