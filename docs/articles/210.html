台積電高層表示：晶片製造精進「沒有捷徑」

TSMC executive says 'no shortcuts' to chipmaking excellence

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • executive n. 高階主管、行政人員
    Y.P. Chyn, a top executive at Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. (TSMC), said Monday there are 'no shortcuts' to excellence in chipmaking...
    台積電高階主管秦永沛週一表示，追求晶片製造的卓越「沒有捷徑」...
  • ceremony n. 典禮、儀式
    Chyn, TSMC's executive vice president and co-chief operating officer, made the remarks during a ceremony in Hsinchu at which he was inducted as an Industrial Technology Research Institute (ITRI) laureate.
    身兼台積電執行副總經理暨共同營運長的秦永沛，是在新竹的一場典禮上發表上述談話，他在該典禮上獲選為工業技術研究院院士。
  • contract adj. / v. 合約的、承包的（此處作為形容詞修飾 chipmaker）
    ...reflecting on his career at the world's largest contract chipmaker.
    ...回顧他於全球最大晶圓代工廠的職涯。
  • mass-production adj. 大規模生產的、量產的
    ...turn advanced manufacturing technologies into mass-production capabilities that helped drive global technological innovation.
    ...將先進製造技術轉化為量產能力，進而推動全球的技術創新。
  • foundation n. 基礎、地基
    ...and that the four and a half years he spent there 'laid the foundation for my semiconductor career.'
    ...並表示他在那裡度過的四年半時間『為我的半導體職涯奠定了基礎』。
  • personnel n. 人員、員工、人事部門
    He said ITRI's efforts to cultivate talent and transfer personnel 'laid the talent foundation' for TSMC...
    他表示，工研院在培育人才和移轉人員方面所做出的努力，為台積電『奠定了人才基礎』...
  • founder n. 創辦人、創立者
    Chyn also thanked TSMC founder Morris Chang (張忠謀) for creating the foundry business model...
    秦永沛也感謝台積電創辦人張忠謀創造了晶圓代工商業模式...
  • model n. 模型、模式
    ...for creating the foundry business model, which he described as 'a business model that changed the world.'
    ...創造了晶圓代工商業模式，他形容這是『一個改變世界的商業模式』。
  • leadership n. 領導地位、領導能力
    ...helping cement TSMC's leadership in advanced manufacturing.
    ...從而鞏固了台積電在先進製造領域的領導地位。
  • supply chain n. 供應鏈
    ...remain at the core of the global supply chain in the era of AI and high-performance computing...
    ...在人工智慧與高效能運算時代中，持續居於全球供應鏈的核心地位...
  • contributions n. 貢獻
    Chyn's contributions have also helped Taiwan 'remain at the core of the global supply chain...'
    秦永沛的貢獻也協助台灣『持續居於全球供應鏈的核心地位……』

文法重點

  • 關係代間詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    Chyn also thanked TSMC founder Morris Chang (張忠謀) for creating the foundry business model, which he described as 'a business model that changed the world.'
    句中的 "which he described as..." 是非限定關代子句，用來補充說明前面的名詞片語 "the foundry business model"。而子句裡的 "that changed the world" 則是限定關代子句，用來修飾 "a business model"。
  • 現在分詞片語作結果狀語（分詞構句）
    ...turning advanced manufacturing technologies into mass-production capabilities that helped drive global technological innovation.
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    Chyn, TSMC's executive vice president and co-chief operating officer, made the remarks during a ceremony in Hsinchu at which he was inducted as an Industrial Technology Research Institute (ITRI) laureate.
