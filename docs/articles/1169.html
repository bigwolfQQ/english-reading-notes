北愛爾蘭遊行引爆憤怒與擔憂

N Ireland march fuels anger, concern

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • standoff n. 對峙，僵局
    as a tense standoff with residents seeking to halt a pro-British Protestant march through a primarily Catholic area stretched into the night on Sunday
    因為週日深夜，居民試圖阻止一場親英的新教徒遊行穿過主要為天主教徒的地區，雙方陷入緊張的對峙，並持續到深夜
  • trigger v. 觸發，引起
    The tensions were triggered after a Belfast appeal court overturned a High Court injunction
    在貝爾法斯特上訴法院推翻高等法院的禁令後，緊張局勢隨之引爆
  • overturn v. 推翻，撤銷（判決等）
    a Belfast appeal court overturned a High Court injunction
    貝爾法斯特上訴法院推翻高等法院的禁令
  • injunction n. （法院的）禁令
    a Belfast appeal court overturned a High Court injunction in the early hours of Sunday
    貝爾法斯特上訴法院在週日清晨推翻了高等法院的禁令
  • authorize v. 授權，批准
    The Parades Commission... had authorized 35 Orangemen to march silently
    遊行委員會已授權 35 名奧蘭治兄弟會成員進行無聲遊行
  • disperse v. 散開，驅散
    This led to a standoff, with both sides declining to disperse and police between them
    這導致了對峙局面，雙方都拒絕散去，警方則夾在中間
  • appeal v. 呼籲，上訴
    UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham appealed for “calm on all sides”
    英國首相安迪·伯恩漢呼籲「各方保持冷靜」
  • application n. 申請
    It barred the Orange Order from completing the route that year and has consistently rejected subsequent applications.
    該委員會禁止奧蘭治兄弟會在當年走完全程，並且一直拒絕隨後的申請。
  • condemn v. 譴責
    O’Neill, of the Irish republican Sinn Fein party, condemned the late-night court ruling.
    愛爾蘭共和派新芬黨的奧尼爾譴責了這項深夜法院裁決。
  • escalate v. 逐步升高，惡化
    saying the long-running dispute could escalate across Northern Ireland.
    稱這場長期存在的爭端可能會擴大到整個北愛爾蘭。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附帶狀況/結果 (With + O. + V-ing)
    This led to a standoff, with both sides declining to disperse and police between them, with marchers blocked from proceeding.
    「with + 受詞 + 分詞」結構用來表達伴隨發生的狀況或背景。此句中 “with both sides declining...” 與 “with marchers blocked...” 形容對峙發生時，雙方拒絕散去且遊行者被阻擋的同時狀態。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle as Post-modifier)
    About 2,000 protesters including Northern Ireland First Minister Michelle O’Neill and Claire Hanna, the leader of Irish nationalist party SDLP, blocked the route on Sunday.
    雖然此句示範的是包含 (including) 片語，但文章中常見如 “...marchers blocked from proceeding” 這種過去分詞片語直接置於名詞後方，修飾前方名詞並帶有被動意味，相當於省略了「關係代名詞 + be 動詞」。
  • 名詞子句作受詞 (Noun Clause as Object)
    He said that he wanted to meet “face to face” with political leaders and community leaders “to see if we can get to a settled position”.
    動詞 said 後方接了一個由 that 引導的名詞子句作為受詞，用來表達他所說的內容。而在句尾的 see 後方則接了由 if 引導的名詞子句（作 see 的受詞），表達「是否」的意思。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    The Orange Order is a pro-British organization that takes its name from William of Orange...
    句中的 “that takes its name from William of Orange” 是主格關係代名詞子句，用來修飾先行詞 “a pro-British organization”，補充說明該組織名稱的由來。
