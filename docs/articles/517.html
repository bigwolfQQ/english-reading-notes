    State-owned oil supplier CPC Corp., Taiwan announced Saturday that it will raise domestic gasoline prices...
    國營石油供應商台灣中油公司週六宣布，將調漲國內汽油價格...
  • domestic adj. 國內的
    ...that it will raise domestic gasoline prices by NT$0.7 (US$0.02) per liter and diesel prices by NT$0.6 per liter next week...
    ...將於下週調漲國內汽油價格每公升0.7元（約0.02美元）以及柴油每公升0.6元...

  • average adj. / n. 平均的 / 平均
    ...showed that the average international crude oil price rose from US$99.82 per barrel last week to US$115.61 this week.
    ...顯示國際原油平均價格從上週每桶99.82美元上漲至本週的115.61美元。

  • impact n. 衝擊、影響
    ...helped CPC mitigate the impact from higher crude prices to some extent.

文法重點

  • 受格關代與介系詞（during which）
    The decision ends a six-week period during which CPC kept fuel prices in Taiwan unchanged...
    「during which」是由介系詞加關係代名詞形成的結構，用來修飾前面的先行詞 a six-week period。這裡的 which 代替前面的 period，表達「在這六週期間之內」，是多益閱讀測驗常考的進階文法。
  • 現在分詞當形容詞（修飾名詞）
    Amid escalating military conflicts in the Middle East that have increased risks to crude oil supplies and transportation...
    句中的 escalating 是動詞 escalate（升高、惡化）的現在分詞，在此作為形容詞放在名詞 conflicts 前面，用來形容「正在升高中」的軍事衝突。現在分詞表示主動與進行的意涵。
  • 未來完成式（will have + 過去分詞）
    CPC estimated that by Sunday it will have absorbed NT$19.85 billion in cumulative losses...
    句中的 will have absorbed 採用了「未來完成式」，用來表示某個動作（吸收累積虧損）在未來特定時間點（by Sunday，到了週日）之前將會完成的狀態。
