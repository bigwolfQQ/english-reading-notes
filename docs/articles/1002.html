文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The United States will build two major military bases in Greenland under a deal with Demark to be signed on Tuesday...
    句中的 "to be signed on Tuesday" 是不定詞片語的被動形，用來修飾前面的名詞 "a deal"，相當於關代子句 "which is to be signed on Tuesday" 的簡化用法，在新聞英語中非常常見，能讓句子更簡潔。
  • 分詞片語作形容詞修飾主詞或受詞
    The deal follows months of pressure from US President Donald Trump, who has long said he wants the US to control Greenland and has at times refused to rule out military or economic force to acquire the semi-autonomous Danish territory.
    句尾的 "to acquire the semi-autonomous Danish territory" 是不定詞片語，在此處用來表達目的（為了取得這塊丹麥領土），說明前面動作的意圖。
  • 現在分詞短語作補充說明
    Trump's threats, which peaked in January, triggered a diplomatic crisis within NATO and pushed Denmark and Greenland into negotiations with Washington aimed at defusing the pressure.
    句尾的 "aimed at defusing the pressure" 是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞 "negotiations"（意指「旨在化解壓力的談判」），省略了 "which were"，能有效濃縮資訊並保持緊湊的語氣。
