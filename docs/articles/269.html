  • adjourn v. 休會，延期
    Solomon Islands Prime Minister Matthew Wale yesterday adjourned the Pacific nation’s parliament indefinitely...
    索羅門群島總理馬修·華理昨天無限期休會該太平洋島國的國會...

    ...承諾讓和平隊重返這個美國太平洋軍隊曾在第二次世界大戰中進行關鍵戰役的島國。
  • pivotal adj. 關鍵的，核心的
    ...where US Pacific forces fought a pivotal battle in World War II.
    ...這個美國太平洋軍隊曾在第二次世界大戰中進行關鍵戰役的島國。

  • pact n. 條約，協定

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Solomon Islands Prime Minister Matthew Wale yesterday adjourned the Pacific nation’s parliament indefinitely after staring down a no-confidence vote closely watched by China and US allies.
    句尾的過去分詞片語「closely watched by...」用來修飾前面的名詞片語「a no-confidence vote」，相當於形容詞子句「which was closely watched by...」，省略了關代與be動詞，使語氣更為緊湊專業。
  • 現在分詞表達伴隨狀況 (Present Participle indicating Circumstance)
    The political drama unfolded as US Deputy Secretary of State Christopher Landau arrived in the Solomons on Sunday, pledging the return of the Peace Corps...
    逗號之後的「pledging...」是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明主句動作發生時同時伴隨的狀況或結果，相當於「and pledged...」的連接詞結構。
  • 與過去事實相反的假設語氣 (Subjunctive Mood for Past)
    Diplomats at the Pacific Islands Forum said that the survival of Wale’s government, three months after he embarked on a course to cut reliance on China, would have security consequences for the region.
    在此句中，「would have」用於表達在過去特定條件下（政府存續）將會產生的假設性或推論性結果，展現新聞英語中對未來發展與可能影響的審慎推論。
  • 被動語態的應用 (Passive Voice)
    The motion against him was withdrawn on Sunday by Manasseh Maelanga, its instigator who was sacked as home affairs minister...
    句中的「was withdrawn」與「was sacked」皆為被動語態，強調承受動作的對象（動議與該名官員），在新聞報導中經常用於聚焦事件本身而非主動施事者。
