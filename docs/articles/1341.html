文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    About 35,000 ping of new office space is due to be completed in this quarter, but much of it is expected to be occupied by the owners themselves, limiting the amount of space available for lease and cushioning the impact on vacancy rates...
    句尾的「limiting...」與「cushioning...」是由對等連接詞連接的兩個現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前面子句所產生的連續結果或伴隨狀況（相當於 and thus limit... and cushion... 的簡化）。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Deals that had been under negotiation earlier this year were completed during the quarter...
    句中的「were completed」使用了被動語態，強調交易是被完成的（動作的承受者為主詞）。前半部的「had been under negotiation」則使用了過去完成式的被動語態，表示在過去某個時間點之前就已經持續進行中的狀態。
  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況 (Participial Phrase indicating Concomitant Circumstance)
    Taipei’s high-end office market continued to attract solid demand in the third quarter as companies upgraded their workplaces, helping absorb new supply that pushed vacancies moderately higher...
    句尾的「helping absorb new supply...」是一個現在分詞構句，主詞為前面整個子句所表達的事情，用來修飾並補充說明該狀況所帶來的幫助與後續影響。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    Deals that had been under negotiation earlier this year were completed during the quarter...
    「that had been under negotiation earlier this year」是由關係代名詞 that 引導的限定修飾子句，用來修飾先行詞 Deals，限定說明是哪一些交易。
