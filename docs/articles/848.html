Taipei Times · 2026-09-17

圍繞美國不可靠性進行的佈局

Building around US unreliability

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • agreement n. 協議，合約
    The flurry of new defense agreements signed by Japan suggests Tokyo is attempting to reshape its own security posture.
    日本簽署的一系列新防禦協議表明，東京正試圖重塑自身的安全態勢。
  • response n. 反應，回應
    This shift would come in response to China’s geopolitical advances...
    這一轉變是為了應對中國的地緣政治推進...
  • administer / administration n. 行政管理，政府執政期間
    ...during US President Donald Trump’s administration.
    ...在美國總統唐納·川普政府期間。
  • cooperation n. 合作，協力
    ...to deepen cooperation through joint drills amid growing security concerns in the Indo-Pacific region.
    ...在印太地區安全擔憂日益加劇的情況下，透過聯合演習深化合作。
  • reinforce v. 加強，增援
    In the past few months, Tokyo reinforced bilateral security cooperation with Australia, the Philippines, New Zealand, South Korea and Canada.
    在過去幾個月中，東京加強了與澳洲、菲律賓、紐西蘭、南韓和加拿大的雙邊安全合作。
  • dispute n. 爭端，爭議
    ...where the Philippines is locked in territorial disputes with China...
    ...菲律賓與中國存在領土爭端的海域...
  • boost v. 推動，促進，提高
    Riho Aizawa, a Japanese Ministry of Defense researcher, does not view Tokyo’s efforts to boost regional defense ties with partners as a hedge against dwindling US engagement.
    日本防衛省研究員相澤里穗不認為東京促進與夥伴區域防禦聯繫的努力，是對美國參與度減弱的一種對沖。
  • alliance n. 同盟，聯盟
    ...by maintaining the alliance with America as the cornerstone of its security policy.
    ...透過維持與美國的同盟作為其安全政策的基石...
  • vital adj. 極其重要的，至關重要的
    For instance, Starlink’s use would be vital for Tokyo.
    例如，星鏈（Starlink）的使用對東京至關重要。
  • infrastructure n. 基礎設施
    ...even if ground infrastructure were destroyed by Chinese attacks.
    ...即使地面基礎設施遭到中國攻擊而被摧毀。
  • autonomy n. 自治，自主權
    ...despite its drive to increase defense autonomy from Washington.
    ...儘管歐洲努力提高脫離華盛頓的防禦自主權。
  • rely v. 依靠，依賴
    Japan should build a united front of Indo-Pacific middle powers rather than relying solely on bilateral or trilateral interactions.
    日本應該建立一個印太地區中等強國的統一陣線，而不是單純依賴雙邊或三邊互動。
  • intervention n. 干預，介入
    ...Turkey and Pakistan have so far abstained from direct military intervention against Yemen’s pro-Iran Houthi rebels...
    ...土耳其和巴基斯坦迄今仍未對葉門親伊朗的胡塞反抗軍進行直接軍事干預...
  • package n. 一攬子計畫，包裹
    Meanwhile, Riyadh is set to receive a US$5.75 billion US arms package...
    同時，利雅德將獲得價值 57.5 億美元的美國武器包裹...
  • replace v. 取代，替換
    Any new security framework developed by Japan, or another US ally, can only complement, rather than replace, the alliance with Washington.
    日本或其他美國盟友所開發的任何新安全架構，只能是對與華盛頓同盟的補充，而不能取代它。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The flurry of new defense agreements signed by Japan suggests Tokyo is attempting to reshape its own security posture.
  • 假設語氣過去式 (Subjunctive Mood for Hypothetical Situations)
    ...even if ground infrastructure were destroyed by Chinese attacks.
  • 現在分詞表附帶結果或狀態 (Present Participle indicating Result/Circumstance)
    This shift would come in response to China’s geopolitical advances, compounded by the growing unreliability of the US and the associated risks of strategic disengagement.
  • 關係代名詞非限定用法 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    ...where the Philippines is locked in territorial disputes with China — transits that consistently draw indignant reactions from Beijing.
