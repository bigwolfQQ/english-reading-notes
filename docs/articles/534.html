Taiwan Academy to bring cultural events to Houston in the Fall

  • host v. 主辦、舉辦
    The Taiwan Academy in Houston is set to host a series of arts and cultural events this fall

  • inaugural adj. 首屆的、就職的

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作後位修飾
    featuring award-winning films from Taiwan and more modern performances that showcase Taiwan's gender identity and equality values.
    句中的 "featuring..." 是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前面的 "a series of arts and cultural events"，相當於關代子句 "which feature..." 的簡化用法，在新聞英文中非常常見。
  • 非限定關代子句（附加補充說明）
    Nymphia Wind, who uses the pronoun she as Nymphia Wind, will discuss her formative years in Taiwan...
    逗號後面的 "who uses the pronoun she as Nymphia Wind" 是一個非限定關係代名詞子句，對先行詞 "Nymphia Wind" 進行補充說明，若拿掉此子句並不影響主要句意。
  • 動名詞片語作受詞（介系詞後方）
    Besides being a pioneer on Taiwan's road to gender equality and identity, Nymphia Wind was most recently honored as...
    介系詞 "Besides" 後方接動名詞 "being" 構成動名詞片語作為其受詞，用來表達「除了……之外」。
