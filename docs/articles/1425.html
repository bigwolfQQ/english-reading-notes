俄軍發動重大攻擊 造成至少15人死亡

Major Russian attack kills at least 15

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • wounding v. (動詞ing形作分詞) 使受傷、傷害
    killing at least 12 civilians, including three children, and wounding more than 40 in Moscow’s latest devastating barrage
    在莫斯科最新一波毀滅性的彈幕攻擊中，造成至少12名平民死亡（其中包括3名兒童），並有超過40人受傷
  • anticipate v. (動詞) 預料、預期
    Ukraine had anticipated the attack, with President Volodymyr Zelenskiy warning the previous day
    烏克蘭早已預料到這次攻擊，總統澤倫斯基前一天曾發出警告
  • massive adj. (形容詞) 大規模的、巨大的
    intelligence services had concluded that Russia was building up for a “massive” strike.
    情報部門得出結論，指俄羅斯正在積蓄力量準備發動「大規模」打擊。
  • coincide v. (動詞) 同時發生、巧合
    Although Ukraine knew about the looming attack, which coincided with Russian President Vladimir Putin’s 74th birthday
    儘管烏克蘭知道這場迫在眉睫的攻擊，這剛好與俄羅斯總統普丁的74歲生日巧合
  • counter v. (動詞) 對抗、反擊
    The deaths and damage illustrated the difficulty Ukraine has in countering Russia’s ballistic missiles
    這次的死亡與破壞凸顯了烏克蘭在對抗俄羅斯彈道導彈時所面臨的困難
  • short supply n. phr. (名詞片語) 供應短缺
    Ukraine is looking worldwide for available Patriot missiles, which are in short supply due to the Iran war.
    由於伊朗戰爭導致愛國者導彈供應短缺，烏克蘭正在全球尋找可用的愛國者導彈。
  • vital necessity n. phr. (名詞片語) 生死攸關的必需品、極為重要之物
    “Russian ballistic missiles remain the greatest challenge, and air defense packages for us right now are a vital necessity,” Zelenskiy said.
    澤倫斯基說：「俄羅斯彈道導彈仍然是最大的挑戰，防空包裹對我們來說目前是生死攸關的必需品。」
  • facility n. (名詞) 設施、場所
    The Russian Ministry of Defense said the military carried out a massive strike overnight targeting industrial facilities
    俄羅斯國防部表示，軍方昨夜發動了一場大規模打擊，目標是工業設施
  • cargo n. (名詞) 貨物
    The ministry said that port and ship repair facilities in the southern Odesa region and ships carrying cargo for the Ukrainian military were also hit.
    該部表示，南部敖德薩地區的港口和船舶修理設施，以及載運烏克蘭軍用貨物的船隻也被擊中。
  • verify v. (動詞) 核實、查證
    It was not possible to independently verify the images.
    這些圖片無法得到獨立證實。

文法重點

  • with + 受詞 + 分詞（複合結構/附帶狀況）
    Ukraine had anticipated the attack, with President Volodymyr Zelenskiy warning the previous day that intelligence services had concluded that Russia was building up for a “massive” strike.
    「with + 名詞/代名詞 + 現在分詞(-ing) / 過去分詞(-ed)」結構用來表示附帶狀況或背景，在此句中用來補充說明烏克蘭總統澤倫斯基發出警告的同時，也伴隨著該起攻擊事件的發生。
  • 非限定性關係代名詞子句 (Which 子句)
    Although Ukraine knew about the looming attack, which coincided with Russian President Vladimir Putin’s 74th birthday, it could not fend it off completely.
    句中的「which coincided with...」前面有逗號，屬於非限定性關係子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞（即 looming attack 迫在眉睫的攻擊），為該攻擊事件提供額外的背景資訊。
  • 動名詞片語作受詞 (have difficulty in V-ing)
    The deaths and damage illustrated the difficulty Ukraine has in countering Russia’s ballistic missiles...
    片語「have difficulty (in) V-ing」表示「在做某事時有困難」，句中的 in 可以省略。此處用 countering 作為介系詞 in 的受詞，精確表達烏克蘭在對抗俄羅斯彈道導彈時遭遇的難題。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle as Postmodifier)
    Unconfirmed media reports said it was carrying oil from Novorossiysk to India and was attacked by Ukrainian sea drones.
    句中的 unconfirmed（未經證實的）是由過去分詞轉變而來的形容詞，置於名詞 media reports 前方作修飾語。此外，句子後半部的 "carrying oil..." 則是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的油輪，說明其當時正在執行的動作。
