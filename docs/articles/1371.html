單字解析

  • indict v. 起訴，控告
    The Taiwan New Taipei District Prosecutors Office has indicted 10 people for allegedly reselling restricted military-grade chips...
    台灣新北市地方檢察署已起訴10人，他們涉嫌將限售的軍規晶片轉售...
  • defendant n. 被告
    The defendants include Chen Chien-hsiung (陳建雄), who runs several technology companies in Taiwan, his brother and several others.
    被告包括在台灣經營數家科技公司的陳建雄、其胞弟及其他數人。
  • charge v. 指控，控告
    They have been charged with fraud, forgery and money laundering, the prosecutors office said in its indictment published last week.
    檢方在於上週發布的起訴書中表示，他們已被依詐欺、偽造文書及洗錢罪嫌起訴。
  • sentence n. 徒刑，判決
    Prosecutors are seeking a 10-year prison sentence for Chen and sentences ranging from one to eight years for the other defendants.
    檢方正對陳嫌求處10年有期徒刑，並對其他被告求處1年至8年不等的刑期。
  • distributor n. 經銷商，分銷商
    According to the indictment, Chen, a former salesperson for a chip distributor, learned that the government-funded National Chung-Shan Institute of Science and Technology...
    根據起訴書，曾任晶片經銷商業務員的陳建雄得知，政府資助的國家中山科學研究院...
  • collude v. 勾結，共謀

    陳嫌自2017年起涉嫌與他人勾結，虛偽填報中科院為最終用戶並偽造印章...
  • purchase v. 購買，採購
    They then purchased restricted high-speed chips that could be used for military purposes, prosecutors said.
    檢方表示，隨後他們購入可用於軍事用途的管製高速晶片。
  • profit v. 獲利，營利
    After the chips were imported into Taiwan, the defendants allegedly resold them to buyers in Hong Kong, including China-controlled companies, profiting from the price difference.
    檢方稱，晶片進口至台灣後，被告涉嫌將其轉售給香港買家（含中資公司），從中賺取價差。

    檢方指出，該不法犯罪手法產生的不法所得超過新台幣3億元（約合943萬美元）。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句表附帶結果（V-ing）
    ...including China-controlled companies, profiting from the price difference.
    句尾的「, profiting from...」為現在分詞片語引導的分詞構句，在此表主句動作之後自然產生的結果，主詞皆為前面提到的「the defendants（被告）」。多益閱讀測驗常考此類結構來簡化副詞子句。
  • 儘管子句：Despite + 名詞 / 動名詞
    Prosecutors said the defendants exported the chips illegally for financial gain despite knowing that the U.S. Department of Commerce prohibited their sale...
    「despite」為介系詞，後面接名詞或動名詞片語（本句為 despite knowing...），用來表達「儘管知道……但仍然……」的讓步語氣。在多益文法題中，常考 despite / in spite of 與 although / even though 的詞性與用法區別。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    ...the defendants allegedly relabeled the products they purchased from ADI and TI -- which had originally been manufactured in the Philippines, South Korea and Singapore -- as "Made in Taiwan."
    句中的破折號內夾了一個由「which」引導的非限定關係子句，其中的「manufactured in...」是被動語態的過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的 products，展現新聞英語中常見的長句修飾與資訊補充技巧。
  • 形容詞子句省略關代（受格）
    ...the products they purchased from ADI and TI...
    「the products」後面接了「they purchased from ADI and TI」，這是一個省略了受格關係代名詞（which 或 that）的形容詞子句，用來修飾先行詞 products。多益聽力與閱讀常出現這種省略關代的結構，需具備快速辨識的能力。
