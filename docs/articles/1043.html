Children targeted in Pakistan for exploitation

    Pakistani teenager Zara Shah was targeted by a stranger while playing a computer game, drawn into a conversation that would expose her to terrifying online sexual exploitation.

  • expose v. 使暴露、使接觸（危險等）

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Zara, whose name has been changed for safety reasons, is one of an estimated 1.5 million children in Pakistan who experienced online sexual abuse in one year, a report published by the UN children’s agency this month said.
    句中的 "published by the UN children’s agency this month" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "a report"，相當於形容詞子句 "which was published by..." 的簡化版，多益閱讀測驗常考此類分詞修飾名詞的結構。
  • 關係代間詞所有格 whose (Possessive Relative Pronoun whose)
    Zara, whose name has been changed for safety reasons, is one of an estimated 1.5 million children in Pakistan who experienced online sexual abuse in one year...
    「whose + 名詞」用來連接兩個句子並表示所有格關係（在此指 Zara 的名字）。在多益測驗中，whose 常作為格位選擇題（who / whom / whose / which）的熱門考點。
  • 假設語氣過去式 (Past Subjunctive / Modal + have + p.p.)
    I could have been stopped from going to school or leaving the house altogether...
    「could have + 過去分詞 (p.p.)」可用於表達過去可能發生但實際上未發生，或是過去能力的反事實假設，帶有遺憾或假設語氣意味。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Human rights lawyer Salman Afridi said this issue shows how “economic vulnerability can be weaponized”...
    句中的 "can be weaponized" 使用了情態助動詞的被動語態（can + be + 過去分詞），強調主詞 "economic vulnerability"（經濟脆弱性）是承受動作的對象（被武器化），這是多益聽力與閱讀測驗中最基礎也最重要的文法結構之一。
