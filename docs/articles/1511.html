單字解析

  • forecast n. 預測，預報
    The IMF has sharply raised its forecast for Taiwan’s GDP growth to 10.7 percent this year...
    國際貨幣基金組織已將今年台灣GDP成長預測大幅上調至10.7%...
  • project v. 預估，預測
    ...making Taiwan the only economy in the world projected to post double-digit percentage growth this year.
    ...使台灣成為今年全球唯一預估經濟成長率達雙位數的經濟體。
  • outpace v. 超越，跑得比...快
    Taiwan’s projected growth far outpaces that of neighbors in the Asia-Pacific region...
    台灣預估的成長率遠遠超越亞太地區鄰國...
  • upgrade n. 升級，上修
    Despite the significant upgrade, the IMF’s latest forecast remains below...
    儘管有顯著的上修，IMF的最新預測仍然低於...
  • supply chain n. phr. 供應鏈
    Given the structure of supply chains for technology exports from these two economies...
    鑑於這兩個經濟體科技出口的供應鏈結構...
  • surplus n. 順差，盈餘
    ...the IMF projected Taiwan’s current account surplus would reach 22 percent of GDP this year...
    ...IMF預估台灣今年的經常帳順差將達到GDP的22%...
  • unemployment rate n. phr. 失業率
    ...while the unemployment rate was projected to remain largely unchanged at 3.4 percent.
    ...同時失業率預計將大致維持在3.4%不變。
  • infrastructure n. 基礎設施
    ...technology is most recently playing out as a positive demand shock powered by investment in AI and related hardware and infrastructure...
    ...科技正以正向的需求衝擊形式發揮作用，由對AI及相關硬體與基礎設施的投資所驅動...
  • resilient adj. 具恢復力的，有韌性的
    The global economy has been resilient, but that resilience is under strain...
    全球經濟一直具備韌性，但這種韌性正面臨壓力...
  • headwind n. 逆風，負面因素
    Headwinds from a prolonged war and persistent geopolitical tensions could revive inflationary pressures...
    長期戰爭和持續的地緣政治緊張局勢所帶來的逆風，可能會重新引發通貨膨脹壓力...
  • productivity n. 生產力
    ...if expectations about AI-related profits and productivity gains are reassessed...
    ...如果對AI相關利潤和生產力提升的預期遭到重新評估...

文法重點

  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況或結果 (Present/Past Participle as Adverbial Clause)
    In its latest World Economic Outlook report released yesterday at its annual meetings in Bangkok, the IMF raised its forecast for Taiwan’s growth from the 5.2 percent it projected in April, making Taiwan the only economy in the world projected to post double-digit percentage growth this year.
    句尾的「making Taiwan the only economy...」是由現在分詞片語構成的分詞構句，用來補充說明前面主句所產生的自然結果（即：IMF上調預測的這個動作，『使得』台灣成為唯一的雙位數成長經濟體）。
  • 過去分詞作後置定語 (Past Participle as Post-modifier)
    In its latest World Economic Outlook report released yesterday at its annual meetings in Bangkok...
    過去分詞片語「released yesterday at its annual meetings in Bangkok」放在名詞 report 的後面，用來修飾該份報告，相當於關代子句「which was released yesterday...」的精簡寫法。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Absolute Participial Construction)
    “Given the structure of supply chains for technology exports from these two economies, a handful of commodity exporters and other manufacturing hubs in East Asia are likely to see spillovers from this activity,” the IMF said.
    「Given the structure of...」是多益常見的慣用語型分詞構句（Given 意指「考慮到、鑑於」），在此作為條件或前提的引導片語，連接後面的主要子句。
  • 被動語態與狀態形容詞的結合 (Passive Voice and Predicate Adjectives)
    “The global economy has been resilient, but that resilience is under strain, with risks tilted to the downside,” IMF chief economist Silvana Tenreyro told journalists in Bangkok.
    句中的「risks tilted to the downside」運用了被動概念的過去分詞（tilted）來形容風險傾斜的方向，而前面的「is under strain」則是以介系詞片語作表語，表達經濟韌性正承受著壓力。
