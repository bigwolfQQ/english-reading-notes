Uproar follows soiling at China Hyrox competition

單字解析

  • franchise n. 特許經營權，加盟店
    The Hyrox athletics franchise has apologized for allowing an athlete to complete an event in Beijing...
    Hyrox 運動特許經營公司已為允許一名運動員在北京完成賽事致歉...
  • organizing committee n. phr. 籌備委員會，主辦委員會
    She completed the event without the organizing committee intervening...
    她在主辦委員會沒有介入的情況下完成了賽事...
  • intervene v. 介入，干涉
    ...prevented judges from intervening immediately during the competition.
    ...導致裁判無法在比賽期間立即介入。
  • contamination n. 汙染
    Users pointed out on social media that other participants had to touch the contaminated mats...
    使用者在社群媒體上指出，其他參賽者必須接觸那些受汙染的墊子...
  • mixture n. 混合，混合物
    ...is an indoor fitness race with a mixture of treadmill running and strength exercises...
    ...是一項結合跑步機跑步與肌力訓練的室內體能競賽...
  • statement n. 聲明，陳述
    ...Hyrox co-founder Moritz Furste said in a statement on Monday.
    Hyrox 共同創辦人莫里茨・菲斯特（Moritz Furste）週一在聲明中表示。
  • accept responsibility v. phr. 承擔責任
    He accepted responsibility for failing to foresee the incident...
    他為未能預見這起事件承擔責任...
  • prevent v. 預防，阻止
    ...to 'prevent situations like this from happening again.'
    以『防止此類情況再次發生』。
  • admit v. 承認
    Hyrox China did not apologize, but admitted in an earlier statement to 'shortcomings'...
    Hyrox 中國分公司並未道歉，但在稍早的一份聲明中承認存在『缺失』...
  • undermine v. 逐漸削弱，破壞
    ...that not only harm the participants’ health, but also undermine the very foundation of fairness in any competition.
    ...這不僅損害了參賽者的健康，也破壞了任何競賽公平性的根本基礎。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Posts showed Australian athlete Joanna Wietrzyk with soiled legs and shoes, continuing to use the mats and equipment at the competition held on Saturday in the Chinese capital.
    句中的「continuing to use...」是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前面名詞片語（澳洲運動員 Joanna Wietrzyk）當時的連續動作，相當於副詞子句「and continued to use...」的簡化。
  • 介系詞加動名詞表示否定 (Preposition + Gerund for Negation)
    Hyrox made a mistake by not reacting immediately during the race.
    介系詞「by」後面接動名詞「reacting」，而否定副詞「not」放在動名詞前面（by not reacting），用來表達「透過『沒有』立即反應」的方式來造就某個結果。
  • 「prevent + 受詞 + from + V-ing」句型
    ...regulations which had prevented judges from intervening immediately during the competition.
    這是多益非常常考的動詞搭配片語，意思是「阻止……做……」或「防止……發生」。句中用關係代間詞「which」引導形容詞子句修飾規章（regulations）。
  • 不僅……而且……對稱結構 (Not only... but also...)
    ...regulatory flaws that not only harm the participants’ health, but also undermine the very foundation of fairness in any competition.
    「not only A but also B」用於連接兩個對等的動詞片語（harm... 與 undermine...），強調雙重影響。注意在子句中 A 與 B 的動詞時態與形式需保持一致。
