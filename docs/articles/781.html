文法重點

  • 假設語氣（Conditional sentences）
    ...but if the thieves are hoping to cash in on the artwork, they would be hard-pressed to find a buyer.
    此句使用現在式（are hoping）搭配過去助動詞加原形動詞（would be），屬於混合型或變體的假設語氣，用來假設一個不太可能或想像中的情況，並推論其不太可能發生的結果。
  • 非限定關代子句（Non-defining relative clause）
    Brand, who has investigated art crime for about 20 years, explains that often “nobody will touch” stolen art...
    句中的「who has investigated art crime for about 20 years」為非限定關代子句，前方帶有逗號，用來補充說明主詞 Brand 的背景資訊，若拿掉不影響主要子句的句意。
  • 分詞片語作後置修飾（Participial phrase as postmodifier）
    Theft at the Renoir Museum on Tuesday last week — the last home of the painter in the French Riviera — marks the latest high-profile art crime targeting collections around the world...
    句中的現在分詞片語「targeting collections around the world」用來修飾前方的名詞「art crime」，相當於形容詞子句「that targets collections around the world」，使語氣更為緊湊。
  • 被動語態（Passive voice）
    ...sometimes that means paintings valued at millions of dollars are effectively “worth zero” once they are outside of the museum...
    句中的「are effectively 'worth zero'」雖然是be動詞加形容詞，但前方的「valued at millions of dollars」使用了過去分詞片語作被動用法，用來修飾 paintings（畫作被估價），突顯藝術品本身的受動性質。
