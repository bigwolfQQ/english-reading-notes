北歐研發專業可強化台灣晶片產業：與談人

Nordic R&D expertise can strengthen Taiwan's chip sector: Panelists

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • draw on phr. v. 利用，藉鏡，依靠
    Taiwan can draw on the Nordic countries' advanced research capabilities and specialized talent to complement its strengths in semiconductor integration and mass production, industry and trade representatives said Friday.
    產業與貿易代表於週五表示，台灣可以借鏡北歐國家先進的研究能力與專業人才，來互補其在半導體整合與量產方面的優勢。
  • complement v. 補充，互補
    Taiwan can draw on the Nordic countries' advanced research capabilities and specialized talent to complement its strengths in semiconductor integration and mass production, industry and trade representatives said Friday.
    產業與貿易代表於週五表示，台灣可以借鏡北歐國家先進的研究能力與專業人才，來互補其在半導體整合與量產方面的優勢。
  • absence n. 缺乏，不存在
    Europe's lack of a major chip manufacturer such as TSMC should not be mistaken for an absence of semiconductor capabilities, SEMI Global Chief Marketing Officer and Taiwan President Terry Tsao (曹世綸) said during a panel discussion at the Sweden-Finland Pavilion at SEMICON Taiwan 2026 in Taipei.
    SEMI全球行銷長暨台灣區總裁曹世綸在台北SEMICON Taiwan 2026的瑞典-芬蘭館座談會上表示，歐洲缺乏像台積電這樣的大型晶片製造商，不應被誤認為缺乏半導體能力。
  • expertise n. 專業知識，專長
    Europe has expertise in advanced research, materials, compound semiconductors and other specialized technologies needed to advance the industry, Tsao said.
    曹世綸指出，歐洲在先進研究、材料、化合物半導體以及推動產業所需的其他專業技術方面擁有專業知識。
  • excel at phr. v. 擅長於
    Taiwan, meanwhile, excels at scaling technologies and bringing them into mass production, he said.
    他表示，與此同時，台灣則擅長將技術規模化並投入量產。
  • relevant adj. 相關的，切題的，重要的
    "That does not mean that R&D or advanced research is not relevant," Tsao added.
    曹世綸補充道：「這並不意味著研發或先進研究不重要。」
  • cooperation n. 合作，協力
    Cooperation should therefore extend beyond factory investment to include research, talent development, educational exchanges and access to end markets, he said.
    他表示，合作因此應延伸至工廠投資以外的領域，涵蓋研究、人才培訓、教育交流以及進入終端市場。
  • investment n. 投資
    Finland's representative to Taiwan, Lauri Raunio, said Finland has increased investment in microelectronics, photonics and quantum computing while expanding shared cleanroom facilities that allow smaller companies to conduct research without having to build their own.
    芬蘭駐台代表羅瑞·勞尼奧（Lauri Raunio）表示，芬蘭已增加對微電子、光子學和量子運算的投資，同時擴建共享無塵室設施，讓小型企業無需自行建廠即可進行研究。
  • struggle v. 掙扎，奮力，難以應付
    Finnish companies are strong at developing and applying new technologies but can sometimes struggle to bring them to scale, an area in which Taiwan excels, Raunio said.
    勞尼奧表示，芬蘭企業擅長開發和應用新技術，但有時在將其規模化方面會遇到困難，而這正是台灣的強項。
  • contribute v. 貢獻，促成
    During the discussion, MediaTek Inc.'s investments in Finland and Sweden provided one example of how Nordic expertise has contributed to the development of a Taiwanese technology company.
    在討論過程中，聯發科技在芬蘭和瑞典的投資，為北歐專業知識如何助益台灣科技公司發展提供了一個範例。
  • recruit v. 招募，招聘
    MediaTek Senior Department Manager Marko Kukkohovi said the company recruited a core group of engineers from Nokia's wireless modem team in Finland who helped develop its fourth-generation and, in particular, fifth-generation mobile communications technologies.
    聯發科資深部門經理馬科·庫科霍維（Marko Kukkohovi）表示，該公司在芬蘭從諾基亞的無線數據機團隊招募了一批核心工程師，他們協助開發了第四代、特別是第五代行動通訊技術。
  • recognize v. 認出，承認，認知到
    Taiwanese companies should view Europe as a single market while recognizing the different capabilities offered by individual countries, Larsson said.
    拉松表示，台灣企業應將歐洲視為單一市場，同時認清各個國家所提供的不同能力。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Present Participle）作後置定語
    Taiwanese companies should view Europe as a single market while recognizing the different capabilities offered by individual countries, Larsson said.
    句中的 "while recognizing..." 是由連接詞 "while" 引導的現在分詞片語，用來修飾主句的主語 "Taiwanese companies"，表示「在……的同時」，這在商業新聞與多益閱讀測驗中是表達同時發生動作的常見句構。
  • 關係代間接問句 / 疑問詞引導的名詞子句
    During the discussion, MediaTek Inc.'s investments in Finland and Sweden provided one example of how Nordic expertise has contributed to the development of a Taiwanese technology company.
    句中的 "how Nordic expertise has contributed to..." 是由疑問詞 "how" 引導的名詞子句，在句中作為介系詞 "of" 的受詞，用來具體說明示範的內容。
  • 關代所有格 whose / 關係子句修飾先行詞
    MediaTek Senior Department Manager Marko Kukkohovi said the company recruited a core group of engineers from Nokia's wireless modem team in Finland who helped develop its fourth-generation and, in particular, fifth-generation mobile communications technologies.
    句中的 "who helped develop..." 是主格關係代名詞子句，用來修飾前面的先行詞 "a core group of engineers"，補充說明這群工程師所提供的協助內容。
