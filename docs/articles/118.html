Taiwanese creators develop ‘mecha’ content, characters

單字解析

  • intellectual property n. phr. 智慧財產權（IP）
    Taiwanese creators are bringing original mecha toy intellectual property (IP) featuring diverse designs to local and overseas market.
    台灣創作者正將具有多元設計的原創機甲玩具智慧財產權（IP）推向本地與海外市場。
  • commercial potential n. phr. 商業潛力
    An original IP refers to a product or creative property developed from scratch by a person or a team, featuring unique characters and offering commercial potential across multiple fields.
    原創 IP 是指由個人或團隊從零開始開發的產品或創意產權，具備獨特的角色，並在多個領域展現商業潛力。
  • merchandise n. 商品、周邊商品
    It can be further adapted into comics, games, films or TV productions, or developed into physical merchandise.
    它可以進一步改編成漫畫、遊戲、電影或電視 productions，或開發成實體周邊商品。
  • contract manufacturer n. phr. 合約製造商、代工廠
    Taiwan was once home to numerous plastic injection-molding factories that served as major contract manufacturers for leading international toy companies, but that industry later moved overseas, he said.
    他說，台灣曾是眾多塑膠射出成型工廠的所在地，這些工廠曾是國際領先玩具公司的主要合約製造商，但該產業後來外移至海外。
  • test market n. phr. 測試市場
    However, Taiwan remains an important test market for Japanese companies, with products often piloted here before being launched overseas.
    不過，台灣仍然是日本公司重要的測試市場，產品在海外推出之前，經常先在這裡進行試銷。
  • expansion n. 擴張、擴大
    While it is difficult for Taiwanese original IPs to compete with established franchises, they can look to Southeast Asia for further market expansion, he said.
    他說，雖然台灣原創 IP 要與老牌系列競爭很困難，但他們可以放眼東南亞以尋求進一步的市場擴張。
  • core business n. phr. 核心業務
    Among the Taiwanese creators is Chou Ta-mu (周大木), whose core business is automotive parts and customization, and who has loved mecha models since childhood.
    台灣的創作者中包括周大木，其核心業務是汽車零件與客製化，他自童年起便熱愛機甲模型。
  • customization n. 客製化
    Among the Taiwanese creators is Chou Ta-mu (周大木), whose core business is automotive parts and customization, and who has loved mecha models since childhood.
    台灣的創作者中包括周大木，其核心業務是汽車零件與客製化，他自童年起便熱愛機甲模型。

    With Japanese-style packaging and a mid-range pricing strategy, the Shen Ji series has entered the Japanese market, where its sales outnumber those in Taiwan.
    憑藉日式包裝與中階定價策略，「神姬」系列已進入日本市場，其銷量超越了台灣本地。
  • optimistic adj. 樂觀的
    He said that despite growing competition from low-cost Chinese products in the market, he remains optimistic.
    他說，儘管市場上面臨來自中國低價產品日益激烈的競爭，他依然保持樂觀。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（用以補充說明或表示伴隨狀況）
    Chou said that confident in his team’s creativity and design capabilities, and fueled by his own passion for mecha, he took what he called “a mysterious sense of confidence” and embarked on a journey to create an original Taiwanese mecha IP.
    句首省略了主詞與 be 動詞（原為 Being confident in... 縮寫而來），用形容詞片語「confident in...」開頭，用來修飾主句的主詞 he，表達周大木在充滿信心的狀態下採取了行動。
  • 獨立分詞構句（With + 名詞 + 分詞/形容詞）
    However, Taiwan remains an important test market for Japanese companies, with products often piloted here before being launched overseas.
    「with products often piloted here」結構為「with + 受詞 + 過去分詞」，用來補充說明主句發生物品測試時的伴隨狀況或背景，products 與 pilot（試銷）之間是被動關係，故用過去分詞 piloted。
  • 所有格代間代名詞（Whose 引導的非限定修飾子句）
    Among the Taiwanese creators is Chou Ta-mu (周大木), whose core business is automotive parts and customization, and who has loved mecha models since childhood.
    「whose core business is automotive parts...」是一個由關係代間代名詞 whose 引導的子句，用來修飾先行詞 Chou Ta-mu，表示「他的核心業務是...」，whose 在子句中扮演所有格的角色。
