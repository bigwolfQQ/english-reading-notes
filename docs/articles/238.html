有些「好康」可能是詐騙

Some ‘good deals’ can be fraudulent

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • verify v. 查證、核實
    The Administration for Digital Industries yesterday cautioned the public to verify online advertisements with commercial brands’ official Web site to avoid fraudulent mooncake sales ahead of the Mid-Autumn Festival.
    數位發展部數位產業署昨天呼籲大眾，在中秋節前夕應向商業品牌的官方網站查證網路廣告，以避免遭到詐騙購買月餅。
  • fraudulent adj. 欺詐的、騙取的
    The Administration for Digital Industries yesterday cautioned the public to verify online advertisements with commercial brands’ official Web site to avoid fraudulent mooncake sales ahead of the Mid-Autumn Festival.
    數位發展部數位產業署昨天呼籲大眾，在中秋節前夕應向商業品牌的官方網站查證網路廣告，以避免遭到詐騙購買月餅。
  • discount n. 折扣
    The ad claimed it was a limited-edition gift box that contained artisanal holiday treats, with refrigerated home delivery and an early-bird discount offered to attract consumers, it said.
    該廣告宣稱這是一款限量版禮盒，內含手工節慶點心，並提供冷藏宅配到府以及早鳥優惠來吸引消費者。
  • vendor n. 供應商、攤販、廠商
    The Web site showed product photos, gift box contents, prices, delivery options and an order form after clicking the purchase link to enter its Web site, which looked similar to that of legitimate vendors, it said.
    該署指出，點擊購買連結進入網站後，該網站顯示了商品照片、禮盒內容、價格、配送選項和訂購表單，看起來與合法廠商的網站相似。
  • legitimate adj. 合法的、正當的
    The ad was set up by scammers mimicking the legitimate brand, it said.
    該署表示，該廣告是由冒充合法品牌的詐騙集團架設的。
  • customer service n. phr. 客服、客戶服務
    No customer service or store information was ever found, the administration said, adding the complainants finally checked the brand’s official Web site and realized there was no promotion.
    該署表示，完全找不到任何客服或店家資訊，並補充說申訴人最後檢查了該品牌的官方網站，才驚覺根本沒有這項促銷活動。
  • promotion n. 促銷、宣傳、晉升
    No customer service or store information was ever found, the administration said, adding the complainants finally checked the brand’s official Web site and realized there was no promotion.
    該署表示，完全找不到任何客服或店家資訊，並補充說申訴人最後檢查了該品牌的官方網站，才驚覺根本沒有這項促銷活動。
  • channel n. 管道、通路
    Consumers should first check a brand’s official Web site or social media accounts and enter its shopping Web site via officially approved sales channels, the administration said, cautioning against clicking on links for unknown sources.
    數位產業署表示，消費者應先檢查品牌的官方網站或社群媒體帳號，並透過官方核准的銷售管道進入其購物網站，同時警告切勿點擊不明來源的連結。
  • comprehensive adj. 全面的、綜合的
    Third, people entering a shopping Web site should verify the link with the brand’s official Web site and make sure it provides comprehensive information on company name, contact information, address, returns and exchanges, and after-sales services, it said.
    該署指出，其次，進入購物網站的民眾應與品牌的官方網站核對連結，並確保其提供有關公司名稱、聯絡資訊、地址、退換貨以及售後服務的完整資訊。
  • transfer v. 轉帳、轉移
    Fourth, do not follow instructions on unknown Web sites to transfer money, enter credit card numbers, or share any personal information, it said.
    該署指出，第四，切勿遵循不明網站上的指示轉帳、輸入信用卡號或分享任何個人資訊。

文法重點

  • 分詞作後位修飾 (Participle as Postmodifier)
    The ad was set up by scammers mimicking the legitimate brand, it said.
    句中的「mimicking the legitimate brand」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 scammers（詐騙者），相當於形容詞子句「who mimicked the legitimate brand」，在新聞英語中常用來精簡語句。
  • 否定副詞置於句首的倒裝結構 (Negative Adverb Inversion)
    People who placed an order did not verify the offer with the brand’s official Web site or social media accounts, nor did they make sure the purchase link and the seller’s information was legitimate while entering their information and paying, it said.
    句中使用了「nor did they...」，當否定連接詞 nor 置於句中連接兩個獨立子句且強調前句的否定時，後方的子句會採用「助動詞 + 主詞 + 原形動詞」的倒裝語序。
  • 現在分詞表達附帶結果 (Present Participle for Result/Action)
    No customer service or store information was ever found, the administration said, adding the complainants finally checked the brand’s official Web site and realized there was no promotion.
    句尾的「adding the complainants finally...」是由現在分詞加省略了連接詞 that 的名詞子句構成，用來表達主句動作發生後的同時或接續狀況（補充說明行政機關說了什麼話），讓句子更流暢。
  • 動名詞片語作受詞 (Gerund Phrase as Object)
    That can prevent data breaches, unauthorized credit card charges or getting scammed, the administration said.
    動詞 prevent 後方可接動名詞片語（如 getting scammed）或名詞（如 data breaches）作受詞，用來表達「預防／防止某事發生」。
