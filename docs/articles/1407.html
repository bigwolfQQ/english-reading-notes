主計總處：CPI 激增可能只是暫時的

Sharp increase in CPI likely temporary: DGBAS

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • temporary adj. 暫時的，短期的
    Sharp increase in CPI likely temporary: DGBAS
    主計總處：CPI 激增可能只是暫時的
  • surge n. / v. 激增，急升
    Taiwan’s consumer prices rose at the fastest pace in 31 months last month, driven largely by a surge in vegetable costs after heavy rainfall...
    受強降雨後蔬菜價格飆升推動，台灣上個月消費者物價指數創下 31 個月來最快漲幅...
  • consumer price index (CPI) n. 消費者物價指數
    The consumer price index (CPI) increased 2.73 percent from a year earlier...
    消費者物價指數（CPI）較去年同期上漲 2.73%...
  • target n. 目標
    ...marking the fifth straight month above the central bank’s 2 percent inflation target...
    ...創下連續第五個月高於中央銀行 2% 的通膨目標...
  • fade v. 逐漸消失，褪色
    ...the price shock is likely to fade quickly, she said.
    她表示，價格震盪預計很快就會消退。
  • phenomenon n. 現象
    “We expect the increase in inflation to be a short-term phenomenon, with the CPI growth rate likely to ease in October,” Tsai told a news conference in Taipei.
    蔡秀慧在台北的一場記者會上表示：「我們預期通膨的上升將是一種短期現象，CPI 成長率預計在 10 月份趨緩。」
  • ease v. 減緩，減輕
    This month, consumer inflation is expected to ease partly because of a shift in the timing of the Mid-Autumn Festival...
    本月消費者通膨預計將會趨緩，部分原因在於中秋節的時間點轉移...
  • category n. 種類，類別
    Transportation and communications costs rose 4.07 percent, the most among the major CPI categories.
  • impact n. 影響，衝擊
    However, Tsai said that such products have a relatively small weighting in the CPI and are not purchased frequently, limiting their impact on overall inflation.
    不過，蔡秀慧表示，此類產品在 CPI 中的權重相對較小且不常購買，因此限制了其對整體通膨的影響。
  • exclude v. 排除
    Core CPI, which excludes volatile food and energy prices, rose 2.52 percent from a year earlier...
    剔除波動較大的食物和能源價格後，核心 CPI 較去年同期上漲 2.52%...
  • outlook n. 前景，展望
    Even so, CPI growth is likely to remain above 2 percent this month, while the outlook for vegetable prices would depend largely on weather conditions, she said.
    她表示，即便如此，本月 CPI 成長率預計仍將保持在 2% 以上，而蔬菜價格的前景則很大程度上取決於天候狀況。
  • accelerate v. 加速
    Producer prices also accelerated sharply last month, as the producer price index (PPI) rose 18.12 percent from a year earlier...
    生產者物價也大幅加速，上個月生產者物價指數（PPI）較去年同期上漲 18.12%...

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    Taiwan’s consumer prices rose at the fastest pace in 31 months last month, driven largely by a surge in vegetable costs after heavy rainfall...
    句中的「driven largely by a surge...」是過去分詞引導的片語，用來修飾前面的主句（表被動與原因），相當於副詞子句「because they were driven largely by a surge...」，常出現在新聞英語中以精簡句子結構。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Absolute Nominative Construction)
    “We expect the increase in inflation to be a short-term phenomenon, with the CPI growth rate likely to ease in October,” Tsai told a news conference in Taipei.
    「with + 名詞 + 形容詞/分詞」結構常譯為「附帶...狀況；隨著...」，在此用來補充說明通膨預期短暫的伴隨狀況，是商業與新聞英語中極常見的高階句構。
  • 非限定關係子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Core CPI, which excludes volatile food and energy prices, rose 2.52 percent from a year earlier, indicating that underlying price pressures remain somewhat elevated.
    句中的「which excludes...」帶有逗號，屬於非限定關係子句，對先行詞「Core CPI」提供額外的補充說明。句尾的「indicating that...」則是現在分詞作結果狀語，表達前句所產生的指標意義。
