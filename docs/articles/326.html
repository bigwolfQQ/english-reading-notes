Manila hits out at China after note mishap in Seoul

單字解析

  • dispute n. 爭端，爭議
    a memo asserting Beijing’s position over their longstanding maritime dispute was delivered to him
    一份聲稱北京對其長期海上爭端立場的備忘錄被遞到了他面前
  • respond v. 回應，回答
    as the secretary was responding to a question about “gray zone” coercion in the South China Sea
    當時部長正回答關於南海「灰色地帶」脅迫的問題
  • position n. 立場，位置
    describing it as laying out Beijing’s position on the South China Sea.
    並形容這份便條闡述了北京對南海的立場。
  • attack n. 抨擊，攻擊
    before launching into an attack on what he said was China’s efforts to influence his remarks.
    接著痛批他所指的中國試圖影響他言論的行為。
  • behavior n. 行為，舉止
    “Perhaps basic decency and behavior is what China needs to do,” he said
    「或許中國需要做的是基本的禮貌與行為，」他說
  • impose v. 實施（制裁、稅收等），強制執行
    China also imposed sanctions on Teodoro earlier this year
    中國也在今年稍早對特奧多羅實施制裁
  • accuse v. 指控，指責
    accusing him of making remarks that damaged bilateral ties
    指控他發表損害雙邊關係的言論
  • bilateral adj. 雙邊的
    accusing him of making remarks that damaged bilateral ties and of raising tensions
    指控他發表損害雙邊關係的言論，並升高了緊張局勢
  • tension n. 緊張局勢，緊張關係
    and of raising tensions in a relationship already strained by territorial disputes.
    並升高了因領土爭端而本已緊張的關係。
  • recognize v. 承認，認可
    The memo said China neither accepts nor recognizes a ruling under the UN Convention
    備忘錄內容指出，中國既不接受也不承認根據聯合國公約所做出的裁決
  • ineffective adj. 無效的，不起作用的
    how ineffective its position is in the face of a free audience
    其立場在自由的聽眾面前是多麼無效

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    a memo asserting Beijing’s position over their longstanding maritime dispute was delivered to him while he was speaking on stage.
    在此句中，現在分詞片語「asserting Beijing’s position...」用來修飾前面的名詞 a memo，相當於形容詞子句「which asserted Beijing’s position...」，用以簡化句子並直接補充說明該備忘錄的內容。
  • 過去分詞片語作被動修飾 (Past Participle Phrase)
    The unusual incident was captured on video at the Seoul Defense Dialogue as the secretary was responding to a question...
    句中的「captured on video」是過去分詞片語，用來描述主詞 The unusual incident（不尋常的事件）被捕捉到的被動狀態。分詞片語省略了關代與be動詞（which was captured），讓新聞英語更顯精鍊。
  • 連接詞引导的時間副詞子句 (Adverbial Clause of Time with while)
    ...while he was speaking on stage.
    while 在此引導一個表示同時發生動作的時間副詞子句，意指「在……的同時」，其後接進行式（he was speaking），強調動作正在持續進行中。
  • 動名詞片語作介系詞受詞 (Gerund Phrase as Object of Preposition)
    ...accusing him of making remarks that damaged bilateral ties and of raising tensions in a relationship...
    介系詞 of 後方接了兩個對等的動名詞片語（making remarks... 與 raising tensions...）作為其受詞，用來具體說明中國指控（accusing）的內容細節。
