鴻海 9 月單月營收首度突破新台幣 1 兆元

Hon Hai reports first monthly sales over NT$1 trillion in September

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • manufacturing adj. 製造業的
    Taiwan-based manufacturing giant Hon Hai Precision Industry Co. said on Monday that its monthly sales passed the NT$1 trillion mark...
    總部位於台灣的製造業巨頭鴻海精密工業股份有限公司於週一表示，其單月營收突破新台幣 1 兆元大關...
  • division n. 部門，事業群
    ...largely based on the performance of its cloud and networking division during the current AI era.
    ...主因是當前 AI 時代下其雲端暨網路產品事業群的表現。
  • statement n. 聲明，陳述
    In a statement, Hon Hai, also known as Foxconn globally, said it posted NT$1.15 trillion in consolidated sales in September...
    鴻海在聲明中指出，其全球通稱富士康，9 月合併營收達新台幣 1.15 兆元...
  • consolidated adj. 合併的（指財務報表等）
    ...said it posted NT$1.15 trillion in consolidated sales in September, up 38.42 percent from a year earlier...
    ...指出 9 月合併營收達新台幣 1.15 兆元，較去年同期增加 38.42%...
  • revenue n. 收益，營業收入
    On a year-on-year basis, Hon Hai saw a significant improvement in revenue from cloud and networking products...
    就年對年而言，鴻海的雲端暨網路產品營收顯著改善...
  • shipment n. 出貨，出貨量
    ...while sales posted by the smart consumer electronics division remained little changed as rising product prices affected shipments.
    ...而智慧消費性電子產品事業群的營收則幾乎持平，主因是產品價格上漲影響了出貨量。
  • beneficiary n. 受惠者，受益人
    Analysts said Hon Hai, an iPhone assembler, was one of the largest beneficiaries of the launch of the iPhone 18 series by Apple Inc. last month.
    分析師表示，身為 iPhone 組裝廠的鴻海，是上個月蘋果公司推出 iPhone 18 系列的最大受益者之一。
  • robust adj. 強健的，蓬勃的，強勁的
    Hon Hai said its four major business divisions all reported robust growth in the third quarter.
    鴻海表示，其四大主要業務部門在第三季皆回報了強勁的成長。
  • generate v. 產生（營收、利潤等），創造
    In the first nine months of this year, the company generated NT$7.67 trillion in consolidated sales, up 39.53 percent from a year earlier.
    今年前九個月，該公司創造了新台幣 7.67 兆元的合併營收，較去年同期增加 39.53%。
  • peak season n. phr. 旺季
    Hon Hai said the current AI boom and traditional peak season in the global information and communications technology industry are expected to lend support...
    鴻海表示，當前的人工智慧熱潮以及全球資訊及通訊科技產業的傳統旺季，預期將提供支撐...
  • investor n. 投資人
    ...while it also urged investors to pay close attention to a fast-changing political and economic landscape which could affect demand.
    ...同時也敦促投資人密切關注可能影響需求的瞬息萬變之政治與經濟局勢。
  • landscape n. 局勢，情勢，風景
    ...pay close attention to a fast-changing political and economic landscape which could affect demand.
    ...密切關注可能影響需求的瞬息萬變之政治與經濟局勢。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The latest monthly sales smashed the previous record of NT$837.1 billion set in September 2025, Hon Hai said.
    句中的「set in September 2025」是由過去分詞開頭的片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「the previous record（先前的紀錄）」。這相當於形容詞子句「which was set in September 2025」的簡化形式，在新聞英文中非常常見，能讓句子更為簡鍊。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    ...sales posted by the smart consumer electronics division remained little changed as rising product prices affected shipments.
    句中的「posted by...」使用了被動語態的概念（隱含分詞片語），用來修飾前面的名詞「sales（銷售額/營收）」，表示營收是由該部門所「發布/締造」的。多益考試經常測試主動與被動分詞的選填題。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    ...pay close attention to a fast-changing political and economic landscape which could affect demand.
    句尾使用了「which could affect demand」這個由關係代名詞 which 引導的形容詞子句，用來補充說明先行詞「a fast-changing political and economic landscape（瞬息萬變的政治與經濟局勢）」，說明這個局勢可能會對需求造成影響。
