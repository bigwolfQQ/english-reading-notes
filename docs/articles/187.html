來自美加貿易戰的警告

A warning from the US-Canada trade war

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • suspend v. 暫停，中止
    On Aug. 21, Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney announced that trade negotiations had been suspended and that Canada’s negotiating team recalled.
    8月21日，加拿大總理馬克·卡尼宣布貿易談判已暫停，且加拿大的談判團隊已被召回。
  • impose v. 實施，徵收（稅款等）
    Hours later, the US imposed an additional 50 percent tariff on about US$20 billion of Canadian goods...
    幾小時後，美國對約200億美元的加拿大商品徵收額外50%的關稅...
  • retaliatory adj. 報復性的
    Carney immediately announced “dollar-for-dollar” retaliatory tariffs starting Sept. 8...
    卡尼隨即宣布自9月8日起實施「針鋒相對」的報復性關稅...
  • collapse v. / n. 崩潰，瓦解，談判破裂
    The two sides have offered conflicting accounts of why negotiations collapsed.
    雙方對於談判破裂的原因各執一詞。
  • compromise v. / n. 妥協，妥協方案
    Canada, he said, would not compromise its sovereignty.
    他說，加拿大不會妥協其主權。
  • concession n. 讓步，妥協
    ...the discussion is no longer just about tariff concessions.
    ...討論已不單單是關於關稅讓步。
  • autonomy n. 自治，自主權
    If Canada surrenders policy autonomy today, what leverage remains tomorrow?
    如果加拿大今天放棄政策自主權，明天還剩下什麼籌碼？
  • leverage n. 影響力，籌碼
    If Canada surrenders policy autonomy today, what leverage remains tomorrow?
    如果加拿大今天放棄政策自主權，明天還剩下什麼籌碼？
  • coercion n. 強迫，高壓統治，經濟脅迫
    Carney said earlier this year that middle powers must resist economic coercion.
    卡尼今年稍早曾表示，中等強國必須抵抗經濟脅迫。
  • alternative adj. / n. 替代的，備選的；替代方案
    A trade war depends not just on who can inflict the most pain, but on who has the fiscal leeway, alternative markets, industrial resilience and social consensus to endure it longer.
    貿易戰不僅取決於誰能造成最大的痛苦，更取決於誰擁有財政餘裕、替代市場、產業韌性以及社會共識來支撐更久。
  • resilience n. 恢復力，韌性
    A trade war depends not just on who can inflict the most pain, but on who has the fiscal leeway, alternative markets, industrial resilience and social consensus to endure it longer.
    貿易戰不僅取決於誰能造成最大的痛苦，更取決於誰擁有財政餘裕、替代市場、產業韌性以及社會共識來支撐更久。
  • diversified adj. 多元化的
    It should rest on irreplaceable industrial capabilities, diversified markets and energy sources...
    它應該立基於無可取代的產業能力、多元化的市場與能源來源...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞構句作附帶狀況/結果
    Invoking the “51st state,” he pushed the dispute beyond tariffs and onto Canadian sovereignty.
    句首的「Invoking the “51st state,”」是由現在分詞（Invoking）引導的分詞構句，用來補充說明主句動作發生時的伴隨狀況，相當於「As he invoked...」或「He invoked... and pushed...」的簡化。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    Hours later, the US imposed an additional 50 percent tariff on about US$20 billion of Canadian goods, equivalent to about 5.5 percent of its US-bound exports.
    句中的「equivalent to about 5.5 percent...」是形容詞片語（省略了which is），用來修飾前面的「US$20 billion of Canadian goods」，補充說明該批商品的價值佔比。
  • 假設語氣（與現在事實相反）
    If Canada surrenders policy autonomy today, what leverage remains tomorrow?
    此句使用條件句表達假設情況。雖然動詞用現在式（surrenders, remains），但在此語境中強調一種條件式的推論：假設今天放棄了自主權，明天的情勢將會如何？
  • 名詞子句作受詞
    The two sides have offered conflicting accounts of why negotiations collapsed.
    介系詞 of 後面接了一個由疑問詞 why 引導的名詞子句「why negotiations collapsed」，在句中作為 of 的受詞，用來補充說明 conflicting accounts（衝突的說法）的內容細節。
