台灣通膨率連續四個月突破2%門檻

Taiwan's inflation tops 2% threshold for 4th straight month

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • threshold n. 門檻、界線
    Taiwan's consumer price index (CPI) rose more than 2 percent year-on-year for the fourth consecutive month in August, exceeding a threshold set by the central bank, the Directorate General of Budget, Accounting and Statistics (DGBAS) said Tuesday.
    行政院主計總處（DGBAS）周二表示，台灣8月消費者物價指數（CPI）較去年同期上漲超過2%，連續第四個月超出央行設定的門檻。
  • consecutive adj. 連續的
    Taiwan's consumer price index (CPI) rose more than 2 percent year-on-year for the fourth consecutive month in August, exceeding a threshold set by the central bank, the Directorate General of Budget, Accounting and Statistics (DGBAS) said Tuesday.
    行政院主計總處（DGBAS）周二表示，台灣8月消費者物價指數（CPI）較去年同期上漲超過2%，連續第四個月超出央行設定的門檻。
  • exceed v. 超過、超越
    Taiwan's consumer price index (CPI) rose more than 2 percent year-on-year for the fourth consecutive month in August, exceeding a threshold set by the central bank, the Directorate General of Budget, Accounting and Statistics (DGBAS) said Tuesday.
    行政院主計總處（DGBAS）周二表示，台灣8月消費者物價指數（CPI）較去年同期上漲超過2%，連續第四個月超出央行設定的門檻。
  • ease v. 減緩、減輕、趨緩
    DGBAS data showed the CPI rose 2.04 percent in August from a year earlier, easing from a 2.52 percent increase in July.
    主計總處數據顯示，8月CPI較去年同期上漲2.04%，漲幅較7月的2.52%趨緩。
  • exclude v. 排除、不包括
    Core CPI, which excludes fruit, vegetables and energy, rose 2.30 percent from a year earlier in August, also above the 2-percent threshold.
    8月剔除水果、蔬菜與能源後的核心CPI較去年同期上漲2.30%，同樣高於2%的門檻。
  • freeze v. 凍結（價格、薪資等）
    Tsai Hsiu-hui (蔡秀慧), head of the DGBAS's general statistics section, said that while the government continued to freeze domestic gasoline and diesel prices in August, fuel prices still rose more than 10 percent from a year earlier due to a relatively low comparison base.
    主計總處綜合統計處長蔡秀慧表示，儘管政府在8月持續凍結國內汽柴油價格，但由於去年同期比較基期相對較低，燃料價格較去年同期仍上漲超過10%。
  • pressure n. 壓力
    Tsai said higher group travel costs pushed up education and entertainment expenses, while an increase in dining-out costs also added to inflationary pressure.
    蔡秀慧指出，團體旅遊費用上升推高了教育娛樂支出，而外食費用的增加也加重了通膨壓力。
  • persistent adj. 持續的、堅持不懈的
    Tsai said growth in dining-out expenses, which has remained above 3 percent for about three months, appeared persistent and was unlikely to ease quickly.
    蔡秀慧指出，外食費用的成長已連續約三個月保持在3%以上，顯得相當持續且短期內不太可能趨緩。
  • necessity n. 必需品
    Consumers saw some relief in the cost of a basket of 17 government-monitored household necessities, including rice, pork, bread, eggs, sugar, cooking oil, shampoo, and toilet paper, which rose 1.82 percent in August, down from 1.95 percent in July.
    民眾在17項政府監管的民生必需品（包括米、豬肉、麵包、雞蛋、糖、食用油、洗髮精和衛生紙）的採購成本上感受到一些緩解，這些品項在8月上漲1.82%，低於7月的1.95%。
  • import v. / n. 進口
    The import price index rose 27.89 percent from a year earlier in August in Taiwan dollar terms and 20.45 percent in U.S. dollar terms, while the export price index rose 30.64 percent in Taiwan dollar terms and 23.05 percent in U.S. dollar terms, the data showed.
    數據顯示，8月進口物價指數以新台幣計價較去年同期上漲27.89%，以美元計價上漲20.45%；出口物價指數以新台幣計價上漲30.64%，以美元計價上漲23.05%。

文法重點

  • 關係代間代名詞非限定用法（Which 補充說明）
    Core CPI, which excludes fruit, vegetables and energy, rose 2.30 percent from a year earlier in August, also above the 2-percent threshold.
    句中的「which excludes fruit, vegetables and energy」是用逗號隔開的非限定關係子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「Core CPI（核心CPI）」，去除它不影響主要句意。
  • 分詞構句表附帶結果或狀態
    Tsai said higher group travel costs pushed up education and entertainment expenses, while an increase in dining-out costs also added to inflationary pressure.
  • 被動語態與分詞形容詞（Past Participle as Adjective）
    Consumers saw some relief in the cost of a basket of 17 government-monitored household necessities...
    句中的「government-monitored」是由名詞加上過去分詞構成的複合形容詞，用來修飾「household necessities（家戶必需品）」，意思是「受到政府監管的」，展現了英文中精簡修飾語的用法。
