文法重點

  • 分詞片語作為後置修飾語 (Present/Past Participle Phrases as Post-modifiers)
    The new facilities also reflect Beijing's overarching strategy aimed at safeguarding China's maritime transport routes and access to the sea...
    句中的「aimed at safeguarding...」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「strategy」。這類結構省略了「which is/that is」，能讓商業與新聞英文表達更加精簡流暢。
  • 受詞補語與現在分詞 (Present Participle as Object Complement)
    Since the launch of its "Belt and Road Initiative," China has been laying the groundwork for overseas naval and air bases...
    句中的「laying the groundwork」是現在分詞，在此處作為完成進行式動詞片語的一部分，用來表達持續進行的動作（一直在為...奠定基礎）。
  • 條件假設片語 (In the event of ...)
    In the event of a conflict in the South China Sea or the Taiwan Strait, U.S. Air Force assets... would likely need to fly from west to east...
    「In the event of + 名詞」是多益商業與新聞英文中非常常見的片語，意同「If there is a...」，用來表達假設情況（「萬一發生...時」），其後常搭配主句使用助動詞（如 would）來表達假設結果。
