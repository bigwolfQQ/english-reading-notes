柏林交通展首次設立台灣館

Transportation fair in Berlin features Taiwan pavilion for first time

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • debut v. / n. 首次亮相，初次登場
    Taiwan debuted its first-ever national pavilion at a global transportation technology trade fair in Berlin on Tuesday
    台灣週二在柏林舉行的全球交通技術貿易展上，首次推出了國家館
  • aim at phr. v. 旨在，目的是
    aimed at demonstrating resilient railway solutions and attracting international partners, officials said at the event.
    官員在活動中表示，旨在展示具韌性的鐵路解決方案並吸引國際合作夥伴。
  • partner n. 合作夥伴
    attracting international partners, officials said at the event.
    吸引國際合作夥伴。
  • private sector n. phr. 私部門，民間企業
    gathered 14 partners from academia, research institutes and the private sector, including Taiwan High Speed Rail Corp.
    匯聚了來自學術界、研究機構以及包括台灣高鐵公司在內的私部門等14個合作夥伴。
  • exhibit v. / n. 展出，展覽
    The exhibit is centered on smart transit and rail resilience
    該展覽以智慧交通與鐵路韌性為核心
  • integrate v. 整合
    smart rail system integration
    智慧鐵路系統整合
  • platform n. 平台
    the new pavilion serves as a platform for the domestic rail sector to gain global visibility
    這個新展館作為一個平台，有助於國內軌道產業獲得全球能見度
  • sector n. 產業，部門
    a platform for the domestic rail sector to gain global visibility
    國內軌道產業獲得全球能見度
  • independently adv. 獨立地
    Another 26 Taiwanese companies are exhibiting independently at the event
    另有26家台灣企業在該活動中獨立參展
  • attendee n. 出席者，參加者
    Transportation Minister Chen Shih-kai (陳世凱) told attendees at the opening ceremony, meanwhile
    交通部長陳世凱同時在開幕典禮上向與會者表示
  • apply v. 應用，運用
    Taiwan will apply its expertise in AI, virtual simulations, and information technology
    台灣將運用其在人工智慧、虛擬模擬和資訊技術方面的專業知識
  • expertise n. 專業知識，專長
    Taiwan will apply its expertise in AI, virtual simulations, and information technology
    台灣將運用其在人工智慧、虛擬模擬和資訊技術方面的專業知識
  • strengthen v. 加強，鞏固
    strengthening its base for future rail industry growth.
    從而鞏固其未來鐵路產業成長的基礎。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表補充說明或結果）
    Taiwan debuted its first-ever national pavilion at a global transportation technology trade fair in Berlin on Tuesday, aimed at demonstrating resilient railway solutions and attracting international partners...
    句尾的「aimed at...」是由分詞構句簡化而來，原句形構概念為「which was aimed at...」，省略了主代名詞與be動詞，直接用過去分詞片語來修飾前方的事件，補充說明該國家館設立的目的。
  • 被動語態的運用
    Organized by the Railway Bureau under the Ministry of Transportation and Communications (MOTC), the Taiwan Pavilion at the InnoTrans show gathered 14 partners...
    句首的「Organized by...」是過去分詞片語作為形容詞用（源自被動語態分詞構句），用來修飾主詞「the Taiwan Pavilion」，強調台灣館是由交通部鐵道局所籌辦的。
  • 名詞子句作受詞
    Transportation Minister Chen Shih-kai (陳世凱) told attendees at the opening ceremony, meanwhile, that though Taiwan is small in size, its century-long development... reflects its ambitions in rail transport.
    動詞「told」後面接了一個由「that」引導的名詞子句（thatough Taiwan is small in size... reflects its ambitions...），作為「told [object]」之後的受詞，用以完整表達部長向與會者說了什麼內容。
