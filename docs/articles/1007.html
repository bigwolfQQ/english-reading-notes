陸委會為拒絕中國官員入境進行辯護

MAC defends denying entry to Chinese official

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • defend v. 為……辯護、保護
    The Mainland Affairs Council (MAC) yesterday defended its decision to deny entry to a senior official from the Shanghai Taiwan Affairs Office to attend the Taipei-Shanghai City Forum.
    大陸委員會（MAC）昨天為其拒絕上海市台灣事務辦公室資深官員入境參加台北上海城市論壇（雙城論壇）的決定進行辯護。
  • deny v. 拒絕、否認
    The council denied Shanghai Taiwan Affairs Office Deputy Director Li Xiaodong’s (李驍東) entry.
    該委員會拒絕了上海台辦副主任李驍東的入境。
  • delegate n. 代表
    Li was scheduled to lead a delegation to attend the annual cross-strait event.
    李原本預定率領代表團參加這項年度兩岸活動。
  • approve v. 批准、贊成
    Instead, the council approved the entry of Wang Hao (王皓), a department head at the Chinese agency.
    相對地，委員會批准了中國機構部門負責人王皓的入境。
  • jurisdiction n. 管轄權、司法權
    cross-strait affairs fall under the jurisdiction of the central government.
    兩岸事務屬於中央政府的管轄範圍。
  • liaison n. 聯絡人、聯絡、溝通
    Taipei Department of Labor Commissioner Wang Chiu-tung (王秋冬), who works as a liaison for the event, said the council rejected Li’s entry...
    擔任該活動聯絡人的台北市勞動局局長王秋冬表示，委員會拒絕了李的入境……
  • authorization n. 授權、批准
    Neither the Taipei-Shanghai City Forum nor the Sun Moon Lake-East Lake Forum... can proceed without the approval and authorization of the central government...
    無論是台北上海城市論壇，或是……在沒有中央政府的批准與授權下，都無法進行……
  • proceed v. 進行、繼續進行
    ...can proceed without the approval and authorization of the central government, Chiu said.
    邱表示，……在沒有中央政府的批准與授權下，都無法進行。
  • conduct v. 進行、舉辦、指揮
    “We hope that local government officials can conduct cross-strait exchange with their Chinese counterparts in a healthy and orderly fashion...
    他說：「我們希望地方政府官員能夠在平等與尊嚴的基礎上，以健康且有序的方式與中國對口官員進行兩岸交流。」
  • counterpart n. 相對應的人或物、對口
    “We hope that local government officials can conduct cross-strait exchange with their Chinese counterparts in a healthy and orderly fashion...
    他說：「我們希望地方政府官員能夠在平等與尊嚴的基礎上，以健康且有序的方式與中國對口官員進行兩岸交流。」
  • coordinate v. 協調、配合
    The council coordinated with the Ministry of the Interior to hold a joint review session of the Taipei-Shanghai forum...
    委員會已協調內政部，對台北上海論壇進行聯合審查會議……
  • advisory n. （災害、旅遊等）公告、警報、諮詢報告
    ...adding that the government has raised its travel advisory for China to “orange,” meaning that people should avoid nonessential travel to China.
    並補充說政府已將中國旅遊警示調升至「橙色」，意味著民眾應避免非必要的中國之行。
  • exception n. 例外
    The government would consider an exception when a Chinese official has a letter of recommendation issued by a Taiwanese business association in China, he said.
    他說，如果有中國官員持有中國台灣商會發出的推薦信，政府將會考慮給予例外。
  • participate v. 參加、參與
    ...because the Shanghai Taiwan Affairs Office has prevented Shanghai-based Taiwanese businesspeople from participating in foundation-organized activities in Taiwan, he said.
    他說，因為上海台辦曾阻止上海台商參加基金會在台灣舉辦的活動。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Present Participle）作後位修飾
    Addressing the matter on a talk show on Pop Radio, MAC Minister Chiu Chui-cheng (邱垂正) said that while the forum is intended to address urban governance issues in the two localities, cross-strait affairs fall under the jurisdiction of the central government.
    句首的 "Addressing the matter..." 是由現在分詞 "Addressing" 引導的分詞片語，用來修飾主句的主詞 "MAC Minister Chiu Chui-cheng"，表示「在談論這件事的同時，邱垂正表示……」。這種結構常出現在新聞英語中，用以精簡表達同時發生的動作。
  • 關係代間詞子句（Relative Clause）修飾先行詞
    Taipei Department of Labor Commissioner Wang Chiu-tung (王秋冬), who works as a liaison for the event, said the council rejected Li’s entry...
    句中的 ", who works as a liaison for the event," 是一個非限定性關係子句，用逗號與主句隔開，對先行詞 "Wang Chiu-tung" 進行補充說明，交代他的身分是該活動的聯絡人。
  • 被動語態（Passive Voice）
    That recommendation was accepted by the Straits Exchange Foundation, he added.
    句型 "was accepted" 採用了被動語態，強調動作的承受者（推薦）。在商業與新聞英語中，當動作的發起者較不重要或是為了客觀陳述時，經常會使用被動語態來呈現結果。
