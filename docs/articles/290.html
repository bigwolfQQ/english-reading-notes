紀錄片探討台灣二戰戰俘的經歷

Documentary explores the experiences of Taiwanese WWII prisoners of war

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • detain v. 扣押、拘留
    A documentary exploring the history of Taiwanese soldiers detained by the Soviet Union in prisoner of war camps after fighting for Japan in World War II was screened in Tokyo on Saturday last week.
    一部探討二次世界大戰期間為日本戰鬥後、遭到蘇聯關押在戰俘營中的台灣士兵歷史的紀錄片，於上週六在東京上映。
  • surrender n. / v. 投降
    Memories Frozen in Time (冰封的記憶), directed by Hsu Ming-chun (許明淳), traces the lives of Taiwanese soldiers held by the Soviet Union after Japan’s surrender in 1945.
    由許明淳執導的《冰封的記憶》（Memories Frozen in Time），追溯了台灣士兵在 1945 年日本投降後被蘇聯扣押的人生歷程。
  • interview v. / n. 面試、採訪
    Hsu spent three years researching records and visiting Taiwan, Japan and Russia, and interviewing former prisoners and their families.
    許明淳花了三年時間研究檔案、造訪台灣、日本與俄羅斯，並採訪了前戰俘及其家屬。
  • surviving adj. 倖存的、殘存的
    Among those featured is 99-year-old Wu Cheng-nan (吳正男), believed to be the only surviving Taiwanese ex-prisoner of war who spent time in the Soviet Union.
    受訪者中包括 99 歲的吳正男，他被認為是唯一在世、曾在蘇聯待過的台灣籍前戰俘。
  • fortunate adj. 幸運的
    Wu said he considers himself fortunate to have survived the war and about two years of captivity.
    吳正男表示，他認為自己很幸運，能在戰爭和大約兩年的囚禁中存活下來。
  • transport v. 運輸、運送
    He was transported to a detention camp in Kazakhstan, where he was subjected to two years of forced labor.
    他被運送到哈薩克的集中營，在那裡服了兩年的苦役。
  • repatriate v. 遣返（引渡回國）
    Wu was repatriated in July 1947.
    吳正男於 1947 年 7 月被遣返。
  • settle v. 定居、解決
    He completed high school and university in Japan and eventually settled there.
    他在日本完成高中和大學學業，並最終在那裡定居。
  • opportunity n. 機會
    The screening was followed by a discussion with Hsu and Wu, who said the film was an opportunity to open a dialogue across generations about memories of war and what it means to be Taiwanese.
    電影放映後，觀眾與許明淳及吳正男進行了座談。吳正男表示，這部電影是一個機會，能就戰爭記憶以及身為台灣人的意義，開啟跨世代的對話。
  • endure v. 忍受、忍耐
    “My greatest happiness in life is that I came back alive from the battlefield,” Wu said, adding that despite the hardships he endured, he did not reject the experience...
    吳正男說：「我人生最大的幸福就是從戰場上活著回來。」他並補充說，儘管他忍受了種種艱辛，但他並不排斥這段經歷...
  • responsibility n. 責任
    Reflecting on Taiwan’s complicated historical memories and questions surrounding identity, Hsu said his generation has a responsibility to share different aspects of Taiwanese history with others and younger generations.
    在反思台灣複雜的歷史記憶與身分認同問題時，許明淳表示，他的世代有責任與其他人及年輕世代分享台灣歷史的不同面向。
  • encourage v. 鼓勵
    He said he hoped the film would encourage audiences to ask their parents and grandparents about their experiences.
    他說，他希望這部電影能鼓勵觀眾向父母和祖父母詢問他們的經歷。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作後置修飾語
    A documentary exploring the history of Taiwanese soldiers detained by the Soviet Union... was screened in Tokyo on Saturday last week.
    句中的 "exploring..." 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的主詞 "A documentary"，相當於形容詞子句 "that explores..."。這種結構在新聞英語中非常常見，能簡潔地表達主動意義的修飾。
  • 過去分詞（片語）作後置修飾語與被動概念
    Memories Frozen in Time, directed by Hsu Ming-chun, traces the lives of Taiwanese soldiers held by the Soviet Union after Japan’s surrender in 1945.
    句中的 "directed by Hsu Ming-chun" 與 "held by the Soviet Union..." 都是過去分詞片語，分別用來修飾前面的專有名詞與名詞片語，隱含被動的概念（意指「被許明淳執導的」以及「被蘇聯扣押的」），省略了 "which is/was"。
  • 複合關係代間詞與非限定子句
    Among those featured is 99-year-old Wu Cheng-nan, believed to be the only surviving Taiwanese ex-prisoner of war who spent time in the Soviet Union.
    句中的 "who spent time in the Soviet Union" 是限定修飾先行詞 "ex-prisoner of war" 的關係代間詞子句。此外，"believed to be..." 則是過去分詞片語當作形容詞用，省略了 "who is"，用來補充說明吳正男的身份。
  • 獨立分詞構句與附帶狀況
    Wu said, adding that despite the hardships he endured, he did not reject the experience, because it ultimately became part of the life he was able to live.
    句尾的 ", adding that..." 是現在分詞構句，用來表示主句動作同時伴隨的另一個動作（「補充說...」）。這種結構能讓句子流暢地串接補充資訊，而不需使用額外的主詞和連接詞。
