文法重點

  • 現在完成式 (Present Perfect Tense)
    We have been able to increase our supply as we have gone through 2026, and we’re going to substantially increase our supply in 2027...
    句中使用現在完成式「have been able to」與「have gone through」，用來強調過去某個時間點開始持續到現在的動作或經驗，凸顯超微在 2026 年這段期間持續提升產能的成果。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Asked if Powertech Technology Inc’s (力成科技) fan-out panel-level packaging could serve as an alternative to TSMC’s supply-constrained chip-on-wafer-on-substrate (CoWoS) technology, Su said that...
    句首的「Asked if...」是由過去分詞開頭的分詞片語，省略了主詞與be動詞（原句意為 When she was asked if...），用來修飾主句的主語 Su，是新聞英語中非常常見且簡潔的句構。
  • 名詞子句作受詞 (Noun Clause as Object)
    Asked about AMD’s contingency plans for possible supply disruptions due to the Taiwan Strait tensions, Su said that “Taiwan is absolutely critical to the semiconductor supply chain, and very, very critical for AMD.”
    動詞 said 後面接了一個由「that」引導的名詞子句（“Taiwan is absolutely critical...”），在句中扮演受詞的角色，用來完整表達蘇姿丰所說的內容。
