ESG 作為企業責任

ESG as corporate responsibility

  • listed adj. 上市的

文法重點

  • 關係代名詞子句（Restrictive Relative Clause）
    Over the past year, many friends of mine who are responsible for sustainability at listed and over-the-counter companies have been extremely busy.
    句中的 "who are responsible for..." 是用來修飾先行詞 "many friends of mine" 的關係子句，用以限定並補充說明這群朋友的身份與職責。
  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾（Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier）
    The heavy rains that have caused flooding and other damage in central and southern Taiwan in the past few days and weeks have prompted me to think about what companies can do.
    句中的 "causing flooding and other damage..." 為現在分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞 "The heavy rains"，相當於形容詞子句 "that have caused..."，用來形容大雨所造成的影響。
  • 使役動詞用法（Causative Verb "have"）
    This would give companies greater incentives to contribute to disaster relief and have their efforts to be reflected in ESG assessment scores.
    此處使用 "have + 受詞 + 被動形/原形" 的使役結構（在此處為被動語意 "have their efforts reflected..." 的變體），表示「使某事被……（由他人或自然而然地達成）」。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞（Gerund Phrase as Subject）
    Dynamically adjusting the weightings of the three dimensions in response to changing circumstances, is practical and worthwhile.
    句首的 "Dynamically adjusting..." 是動名詞片語，作為整個句子的主詞。動名詞片語當主詞時，視為單數，因此搭配單數動詞 "is"。
