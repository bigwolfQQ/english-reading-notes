文法重點

  • Demand 後方名詞子句使用原形動詞 (Virtual/Subjunctive Mood)
    The National Teachers’ Association yesterday demanded that the government recall manga-style posters...
    當表示「要求、建議、命令」等動詞（如 demand, suggest, insist）後方的 that 子句時，子句中的動詞需使用原形動詞（省略了 should），不受主詞單複數或時態影響。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle as Postmodifier)
    The government should not treat public information posters displayed at schools as for-profit commercial advertisements...
    句中的 "displayed at schools" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾其前方的名詞 "public information posters"，相當於形容詞子句 "which are displayed at schools" 的簡化版。
  • 助動詞 have + 過去分詞 (should have + p.p.) 表過去未實現的建議
    The government should have checked the content of the posters, and ensured that they were safe...
    「should have + 過去分詞」用來責備或遺憾過去「本應該做卻沒有做」的事情，此處指政府本來應該在張貼前審查內容，但實際上並沒有做。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause with who)
    ...require educators to estimate the number of students who have seen the posters...
    句中的 "who have seen the posters" 是由關係代名詞 who 引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾先行詞 "students"，說明這些學生的特徵（即看過海報的人）。
