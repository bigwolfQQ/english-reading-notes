文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附加說明（補充結果或動作）
    More than 40 civic groups are planning to protest outside the Legislative Yuan today, calling for opposition lawmakers to “stop slacking off,” as the new legislative session opens.
    句尾的「calling for...」是由分詞片語構成的狀語，用來補充說明主句動作（計畫抗議）的同時所伴隨的另一個動作，譯為「呼籲……」。這種結構在新聞英文中極為常見，能有效整合資訊。
  • 非限定關係代間子句（補充說明主詞）
    Lawmakers from the Chinese Nationalist Party (KMT) and the Taiwan People’s Party, who together hold a majority in the legislature, have repeatedly blocked the review of major livelihood bills...
    句中的「who together hold a majority in the legislature」用逗號隔開，是一個非限定關係子句，用來補充說明前面先行詞（國民黨與民眾黨立委）的背景資訊。在多益閱讀測驗中，常考此類句構的文法題或句意理解。
  • 現在分詞作後位修飾
    Second, the PLA is boosting its maritime combat capabilities, with its Type 076 landing helicopter dock the Sichuan (四川) already starting sea trials and three Type 071 amphibious transport dock vessels under construction, he said.
  • 假設語氣（條件句與結果）
    With only 93 days remaining in the year, if the legislature fails to complete the review, the supplementary budget would lose its legal effect, he said.
    此句使用第一類條件句（Conditional Sentence Type 1）的變體，用「if + 現在式動詞 (fails)」表達未來若發生某條件，則主句「would + 原形動詞 (lose)」將會產生的結果，用來強調未能在期限內審查預算的嚴重後果。
