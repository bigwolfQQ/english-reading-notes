文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present/Past Participle Phrases as Post-modifiers)
    ...making final a sentence of 23 months in prison, suspended for five years.
    句中的 "suspended for five years" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞片語 "a sentence of 23 months in prison"。其完整結構為主動轉被動的概念（which was suspended for five years），省略了關代與 be 動詞，使句子更為精簡流暢。
  • 分詞片語表附帶狀況或結果 (Participial Phrase indicating result/accompaniment)
    ...making final a sentence of 23 months in prison, suspended for five years.
    句尾的 ", making final a sentence..." 是現在分詞引導的片語，用來表達前面整個子句動作所產生的自然結果（「因而使……定讞」）。在新聞英文中非常常見，用以串聯前因後果。
  • 對等連接詞連接分詞片語 (Coordination of Participial Phrases)
    On appeal, the Taiwan High Court reduced Chang's prison sentence to 23 months and his deprivation of civil rights to two years, while maintaining the five-year suspended sentence.
    句中的 "while maintaining..." 是一個包含連接詞 "while" 的分詞構句，用來表示在進行前面動作（減刑）的同時，也伴隨著維持緩刑的決定。這種結構能讓複雜的法庭裁決細節在單一句子中清楚交代。
