帕杜：撇開川普的「玩笑」不談，美國對台立場不變

Trump ‘jokes’ aside, US stance unchanged: Perdue

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • stance n. 立場、態勢
    the US stance on Taiwan remains unchanged following the Chinese leader’s state visit to Washington last week
    在中國領導人上週對華盛頓進行國是訪問後，美國對台灣的立場保持不變
  • envoy n. 使節、特使
    the US president’s top envoy to Beijing said
    美國總統駐北京最高特使表示
  • response n. 回應、反應
    during an interview on Sunday in response to a question about Taiwan
    週日在針對台灣問題的提問進行回應時
  • limit v. 限制、侷限
    Xi pressured Trump to limit support for Taiwan during his trip.
    習近平在訪問期間施壓川普以限制對台灣的支持。
  • oppose v. 反對
    The Chinese leader pressed the US to outright “oppose” Taiwanese independence
    這位中國領導人敦促美國公開「反對」台灣獨立
  • appease v. 安撫、姑息
    Asked whether the US is denying what Taiwan needs to defend itself to appease Xi, Perdue said that was “absolutely not the case.”
    當被問及美國是否為了安撫習近平而拒絕台灣防衛所需時，帕杜表示「絕對沒有這回事」。
  • approve v. 批准、贊成
    The Trump administration has already approved US$11 billion in sales of US arms to Taiwan
    川普政府已經批准了11億美元的對台軍售
  • negotiating chip n. phr. 談判籌碼
    Trump described that deal as “a very good negotiating chip.”
    川普將該筆交易形容為「一個非常好的談判籌碼」。
  • supplier n. 供應商
    Taiwan’s role as a key chip supplier to meet artificial intelligence demands
    台灣作為滿足人工智慧需求之關鍵晶片供應商的角色
  • relationship n. 關係
    means the nation has a “symbiotic relationship” with the US
    意味著該國與美國擁有「共生關係」

文法重點

  • 現在分詞當形容詞（修飾名詞）
    With Xi heading toward an expected fourth term in office, Beijing has renewed diplomatic pressure to isolate Taiwan.
    句中的「heading toward an expected fourth term」為現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的主詞 Xi，表示「正朝著......前進」的動態狀況。
  • 過去分詞當形容詞（表被動或狀態）
    Trump delayed a planned US$14 billion arms package for Taiwan for months.
    句中的「planned」是過去分詞，置於名詞之前作前置修飾語，用來修飾「arms package（軍售包裹）」，表示「被計劃的／預定好的」。
  • 名詞子句（間接問句/受詞子句）
    Asked whether the US is denying what Taiwan needs to defend itself to appease Xi, Perdue said that was “absolutely not the case.”
    句中的「whether the US is denying...」作為 asked 的受詞，引導一個是非問句轉變而來的名詞子句，用來表達被詢問的內容。
