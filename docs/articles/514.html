ANALYTICAL ENGLISH 解析英語(常春藤)

ANALYTICAL ENGLISH 解析英語(常春藤)

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • circulation n. （血液）循環
    Some believe it may contribute to high blood pressure or circulation problems.
    有些人認為，這可能會導致高血壓或循環問題。
  • contribute to phr. 導致、促成
    Some believe it may contribute to high blood pressure or circulation problems.
    有些人認為，這可能會導致高血壓或循環問題。
  • temporarily adv. 暫時地
    Crossing the legs tightly may temporarily reduce blood flow in the raised leg, but the effect is minimal.
    將雙腿緊緊交叉可能會暫時減少抬起的那條腿的血流，但影響極小。
  • minimal a. 極小的、最低限度的
    Crossing the legs tightly may temporarily reduce blood flow in the raised leg, but the effect is minimal.
    將雙腿緊緊交叉可能會暫時減少抬起的那條腿的血流，但影響極小。
  • prolonged a. 長期的、拖延的
    Lifestyle factors, such as prolonged sitting or standing, lack of exercise, and conditions like obesity, play a more critical role.
    生活型態因素，如長時間坐著或站著、缺乏運動，以及肥胖等疾病，扮演更關鍵的角色。
  • impact n. 影響、衝擊
    Unlike its effects on blood pressure or varicose veins, the impact on posture, especially in the spinal and pelvic regions, is more complex.
    與其對血壓或靜脈曲張的影響不同，對姿勢的影響（尤其是在脊椎與骨盆區域）更為複雜。
  • related to phr. 與……相關
    Instead, its effects are more closely related to posture and body alignment.
    其影響反而與姿勢和身體排列更緊密相關。
  • pose a risk phr. 造成風險
    Overall, sitting cross-legged is unlikely to pose serious health risks.
    整體而言，蹺二郎腿坐著不太可能會造成嚴重的健康風險。

文法重點

  • Whether 引導的名詞子句（作賓詞）
    Over the years, people have wondered whether the position is bad for their health.
    whether 在此引導名詞子句，作動詞 wondered 的受詞，意為「是否」，用來表達不確定的疑問。
  • 祈使句結合條件句（as long as）
    For most people, it is a generally harmless habit—as long as they switch sides regularly and avoid staying in one position for too long.
    「as long as」意為「只要」，用來引導條件副詞子句，在此表達只要符合特定的條件（經常換邊、避免維持同一個姿勢太久），前述的結論（無害的習慣）便成立。
  • 動名词（V-ing）作介係詞的受詞
    However, this can be avoided by adjusting your seating position regularly.
    介係詞 by 後方接動名詞 adjusting 來表示「透過……方式」，說明達成該動作的方法或手段。
