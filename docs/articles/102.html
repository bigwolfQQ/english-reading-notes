文法重點

  • 關係代間子句（修飾先行詞）
    A total of 431 sites where people can anonymously surrender illegal e-cigarette devices will be set up nationwide from Sept. 8...
    句中的「where people can anonymously surrender illegal e-cigarette devices」是關係副詞子句，用來修飾前面的先行詞 sites（站點/地點），補充說明這些地點的用途。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾（被動語態概念）
    ...on individuals found in possession of e-cigarettes, which are banned in Taiwan.
    句中的「found in possession of e-cigarettes」是由過去分詞開頭的分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞 individuals。原本的結構為「...individuals who are found in possession...」，省略了「who are」以達到精簡新聞句型的效果。
  • be expected to 原形動詞（預期將要...）
    The collection program is expected to continue for a one-month grace period once the amended law takes effect, Lo said.
    「be expected to + V」是多益考試與商業寫作中極常見的句型，用來表達「預期某事將會發生」或「某計畫預計執行...」。
  • 連接詞 once 引導的時間子句
    The collection program is expected to continue for a one-month grace period once the amended law takes effect, Lo said.
    「once」在此處作連接詞用，意為「一旦……」，引導一個條件/時間副詞子句（once the amended law takes effect），用來表示修法生效後「緊接著」發生的狀況。
