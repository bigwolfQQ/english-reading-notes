文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Luo made the comments in an interview with the Chinese-language Liberty Times (sister paper of the Taipei Times) published yesterday.
    句尾的「published yesterday」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 an interview。這相當於形容詞子句「which was published yesterday」，省略了「which was」後留下過去分詞，能讓句子更為精簡緊湊，是多益閱讀常考的句構。
  • 非限定關係子句與關代所有格 (Non-defining Relative Clause with 'whose')
    Some influence campaigns lure the youth with promises of employment, which are likely fake, as the unemployment rate for Chinese urban youths was 17.9 percent in July, he said.
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Additionally, Beijing’s new national security laws could be used to detain visiting Taiwanese for social media comments or being an employee of an entity that has been accused of breaching China’s technology export controls, Luo said.
    句中的「could be used」與「has been accused」皆為被動語態（助動詞/完成式 + be + 過去分詞）。在商業與新聞英文中，當動作的承受者比發動者更重要，或發動者不明/不需明言時，經常會使用被動語態，多益聽力與閱讀測驗對此文法點著墨甚多。
  • 現在分詞表附帶結果/補充說明 (Present Participle for Additional Information)
    Those groups move agents to Taiwan by using Hong Kong or Macao papers, then set up shell corporations to get to potential victims, he said, adding that blood relations had also been utilized.
    逗號之後的「adding that...」是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明主句所描述事件之外的額外資訊（相當於「and he added that...」）。這種用法在新聞報導中極為常見，用以串接言論或後續發展。
