機構警告：針對年輕人的中國詐騙陷阱

Agency warns of Chinese scams targeting the youth

單字解析

  • united front n. 統一戰線（統戰）
    Whether it is a job scam or an employment opportunity linked to Beijing’s “united front” efforts, both carry significant risks, Luo said.
  • incentive n. 誘因；刺激
    Beijing has expanded its influence campaign targeting Taiwanese youths through tourism and cultural exchange programs, and economic incentives, he said.
  • indoctrinate v. 灌輸（思想、教條）
    Chinese organizers of the tour gorup or program typically insert “united front” activities aimed at indoctrinating participants, Luo said.
  • lure v. 誘惑；引誘
    Some influence campaigns lure the youth with promises of employment, which are likely fake, as the unemployment rate for Chinese urban youths was 17.9 percent in July, he said.
  • unhoused adj. 無家可歸的；無處安身的
    Young Taiwanese who travel to China to look for work often find themselves jobless and unhoused, Luo said.
  • pose as phr. 假冒；偽裝成
    Chinese organized crime, especially scam groups posing as legitimate enterprises, is known to lure Taiwanese youths with promises of lucrative short-term work, then disappear once the victims arrive in the country, he said.
  • lucrative adj. 利潤豐厚的；賺錢的
    Chinese organized crime, especially scam groups posing as legitimate enterprises, is known to lure Taiwanese youths with promises of lucrative short-term work, then disappear once the victims arrive in the country, he said.
  • shell corporation n. 空殼公司
    Those groups move agents to Taiwan by using Hong Kong or Macao papers, then set up shell corporations to get to potential victims, he said, adding that blood relations had also been utilized.
  • detain v. 拘留；扣留
    Additionally, Beijing’s new national security laws could be used to detain visiting Taiwanese for social media comments or being an employee of an entity that has been accused of breaching China’s technology export controls, Luo said.
  • incarcerate v. 監禁；關押
    About 160 Taiwanese have disappeared and more than 200 have been detained or incarcerated in China, marking increasing risks for Taiwanese traveling or working in the country, he said.
  • surge v. 激增；急升
    The SEF and the Mainland Affairs Council in 2023 jointly recorded 588 requests for assistance concerning Taiwanese who visited China and vanished or were in danger, he said, adding that the number surged to 1,027 last year.
  • without a trace phr. 毫無蹤跡地；銷聲匿跡地
    Between Jan. 1, 2024, and Thursday last week, the organizations’ data showed that 462 Taiwanese had gone missing in China, including 160 who vanished without a trace, he said.

文法重點

  • Whether A or B 讓步條件子句
    Whether it is a job scam or an employment opportunity linked to Beijing’s “united front” efforts, both carry significant risks, Luo said.
    使用「Whether A or B」引導讓步狀語子句，表示「無論是 A 還是 B」，用來強調無論前提情況為何，主要子句所表達的事實（兩者皆具重大風險）均成立。
  • find + 受詞 + 受詞補語 (Adj.) 結構
    Young Taiwanese who travel to China to look for work often find themselves jobless and unhoused, Luo said.
    此句型使用「find + 受詞 (themselves) + 受詞補語 (jobless and unhoused)」，用來描述主詞在某種經歷後「發現自己處於……的狀態」。
  • 現在分詞引導分詞構句（表示結果或補充說明）
    About 160 Taiwanese have disappeared and more than 200 have been detained or incarcerated in China, marking increasing risks for Taiwanese traveling or working in the country, he said.
    句尾以「marking...」現在分詞形式開頭，作分詞構句，用以修飾前方整句話所提到的狀況，表示該狀況帶來的影響或結果（標誌著風險增加）。
  • he said, adding that... 插入語與分詞構句
    The SEF and the Mainland Affairs Council in 2023 jointly recorded 588 requests for assistance concerning Taiwanese who visited China and vanished or were in danger, he said, adding that the number surged to 1,027 last year.
    新聞報導中極為常見的句構。在主要引述句後加上「he said, adding that...」，其中「adding」為分詞構句，表示說話者在發表上述言論時「同時補充說明了……內容」。
  • just because... (we, too, would be safe) 邏輯迷思駁斥句構
    “We should not think that just because the vast majority of tourists in China were safe, we, too, would be safe.”
    句中「just because S + V」引導原因子句，接續在「think that...」之後，用來警告或駁斥「不能單純因為某事發生，就盲目推論另一件事也會發生」的邏輯誤區。
