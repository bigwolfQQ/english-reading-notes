油價連續第四週維持不變

No change to fuel prices for fourth week in a row

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • volatile adj. 波動劇烈的，易變的
    CPC Corp, Taiwan said on Saturday that it would leave its gasoline and diesel prices unchanged this week, despite volatile international crude oil prices last week.
    台灣中油於週六表示，儘管上週國際原油價格波動劇烈，本週汽油和柴油價格將維持不變。
  • adjustment n. 調整
    Formosa Petrochemical Corp matched CPC’s price adjustments for this week, information posted on the company’s Web site yesterday showed.
    台塑石化股份有限公司配合中油本週的價格調整，該公司網站昨天發布的資訊顯示。
  • consecutive adj. 連續的
    It would be the fourth consecutive week that CPC and Formosa have kept their domestic fuel prices unchanged...
    這將是中油和台塑連續第四週維持國內油價不變...
  • domestic adj. 國內的
    It would be the fourth consecutive week that CPC and Formosa have kept their domestic fuel prices unchanged...
    這將是中油和台塑連續第四週維持國內油價不變...
  • disruption n. 中斷，擾亂
    ...even though crude oil prices rose last week amid concerns over supply disruptions caused by military tensions in the Middle East.
    ...儘管上週因中東軍事緊張局勢引發供應中斷的擔憂，導致原油價格上漲。
  • mechanism n. 機制
    CPC said under its floating fuel price mechanism, which tracks a weighted average of 70 percent Dubai crude and 30 percent Brent crude...
    中油表示，根據其浮動油價機制（追蹤百分之七十杜拜原油與百分之三十布倫特原油的加權平均）...
  • average adj. / n. 平均的 / 平均
    ...the average international crude oil price rose to US$114.65 last week from US$112.54 per barrel the week before.
    ...上週國際原油平均價格從前一週的每桶 112.54 美元上漲至 114.65 美元。
  • purchasing n. 採購
    ...helped CPC lower its oil purchasing costs to some extent, the company’s data showed.
    ...在一定程度上幫助中油降低了購油成本，該公司的數據顯示。
  • stabilization n. 穩定
    State-run CPC said it is leaving retail fuel prices unchanged in line with government price stabilization measures...
    國營的中油表示，維持零售油價不變是為了配合政府的穩定物價措施...
  • competitiveness n. 競爭力
    ...to ease inflationary pressure and keep fuel cheaper than in neighboring markets to improve Taiwan’s competitiveness.
    ...旨在緩解通膨壓力，並使油價低於鄰近市場，以提升台灣的競爭力。
  • retail adj. 零售的
    Retail gasoline prices this week are to remain at NT$31.2, NT$32.7 and NT$34.7 per liter...
    本週汽油零售價將維持在每公升 31.2、32.7 和 34.7 新台幣...

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...supply disruptions caused by military tensions in the Middle East.
    句中的 "caused by military tensions in the Middle East" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "supply disruptions"（供應中斷），相當於形容詞子句 "which are caused by..." 的簡化版，省略了主格關代與be動詞。
  • 非限定關係子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    CPC said under its floating fuel price mechanism, which tracks a weighted average of 70 percent Dubai crude and 30 percent Brent crude, the average international crude oil price rose...
    句中的 ", which tracks..." 是非限定關係子句，前面帶有逗號，用來補充說明前面的先行詞 "floating fuel price mechanism"。這種子句在多益閱讀中常被用來補充商業術語或機制的細節。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Retail gasoline prices this week are to remain at NT$31.2, NT$32.7 and NT$34.7 per liter... while prices of premium diesel would stay at NT$29.9 per liter...
    雖然此處用了 "are to remain"（預計維持/將要），但整篇新聞頻繁使用被動語態（如 be left unchanged、are matched、are posted 等），用來客觀報導官方政策與商業數據，是商業與新聞英語的核心句構。
  • 分詞構句表目的 (Participle Phrase indicating Purpose)
    ...in line with government price stabilization measures meant to ease inflationary pressure and keep fuel cheaper than in neighboring markets to improve Taiwan’s competitiveness.
    句尾的 "to improve Taiwan’s competitiveness" 是不定詞片語作副詞用，用來表達目的（為了提升台灣的競爭力）；而前面的 "meant to..." 則是過去分詞片語作後位修飾，用以形容前面的措施「旨在……」。
