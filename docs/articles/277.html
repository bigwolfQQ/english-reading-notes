Taiwan falls 2-0 to South Korea in U18 Asian Championship opener

單字解析

  • opener n. 開幕戰，第一場比賽
    Taiwan lost 2-0 to South Korea in its opening game of the BFA U18 Asian Baseball Championship on Monday as its offense struggled, wasting a strong start by left-hander Liu Jen-yu.
    台灣週一在亞洲青棒錦標賽的開幕戰中，以0比2不敵南韓，主因是打線陷入低迷，浪費了左投劉任右的好投。
  • struggle v. 掙扎，艱苦奮戰，陷入困境
    Taiwan lost 2-0 to South Korea in its opening game of the BFA U18 Asian Baseball Championship on Monday as its offense struggled, wasting a strong start by left-hander Liu Jen-yu.
    台灣週一在亞洲青棒錦標賽的開幕戰中，以0比2不敵南韓，主因是打線陷入低迷，浪費了左投劉任右的好投。
  • consecutive adj. 連續的，連著的
    Seeking its second consecutive Asian U18 title and fourth overall, Taiwan managed only three hits against the Koreans at Tianmu Baseball Stadium...
    尋求衛冕亞洲U18連續冠軍及隊史第四冠的台灣隊，在天幕棒球場面對南韓投手群僅敲出3安打...
  • manage v. 設法做到，勉強達成
    Seeking its second consecutive Asian U18 title and fourth overall, Taiwan managed only three hits against the Koreans at Tianmu Baseball Stadium...
    尋求衛冕亞洲U18連續冠軍及隊史第四冠的台灣隊，在天幕棒球場面對南韓投手群僅敲出3安打...
  • muster v. 鼓起（勇氣等），聚集，拼湊出
    A two-out triple in the first inning and a single in the seventh by Chang Yi-an (張乙安) and a single by Hu Chen-jui (胡辰睿) with two outs in the sixth were all the offense Taiwan's lineup could muster.
    張乙安在第一局的三壘安打與第七局的一安，加上胡辰睿在第六局兩出局時的一安，就是台灣打線所能發揮的全部火力。
  • relief n. （運動比賽中的）救援投手，替補
    Chen Yu-hsun (陳昱勛) came on in relief of Wu but allowed one run on a fielder's choice and another on a sacrifice fly.
    陳昱勛接替吳昊翔投球，但因野手選擇失掉一分，又因為高飛犧牲打再失一分。
  • charge v. （將責任或過失）歸咎於，記在...帳上
    Wu was charged with the loss.
    敗投則記在吳昊翔身上。
  • earn v. 贏得，獲得（勝利、榮譽等）
    South Korean starter Ha Hyunseung struck out 12 and allowed two hits over 5.2 scoreless innings to earn the win.
    南韓先發投手河賢承主投5.2局無失分、送出12次三振並被敲出2支安打，拿下勝投。
  • record v. 留下（記錄），創下（成績、數據）
    Reliever Eom Junsang recorded the final four outs in the regulation seven-inning game, striking out two to record the save.
    後援投手嚴俊相在正規的七局比賽中抓下最後四個出局數，送出2次三振並拿下救援成功。
  • respectively adv. 分別，各自地
    Taiwan will face Thailand and Singapore on Tuesday and Wednesday, respectively, at Tianmu Baseball Stadium.
    台灣將於週二及週三在天幕棒球場分別迎戰泰國與新加坡。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表附帶狀況或結果）
    Taiwan lost 2-0 to South Korea in its opening game of the BFA U18 Asian Baseball Championship on Monday as its offense struggled, wasting a strong start by left-hander Liu Jen-yu.
    句尾的「wasting a strong start...」是由現在分詞 wasting 引導的分詞構句，用來補充說明前面主句所發生的結果（浪費了左投的好投），主詞一致且省去了連接詞與主詞。
  • 現在分詞作後位修飾
    Seeking its second consecutive Asian U18 title and fourth overall, Taiwan managed only three hits against the Koreans...
    句首的「Seeking its second...」是現在分詞片語置於句首，用來修飾後方的主詞 Taiwan，表達「尋求著...的同時，台灣...」，相當於副詞子句 As it sought... 的簡化。
  • 獨立分詞構句 / 獨立結構
    Liu kept his team in the game with five scoreless innings, striking out six and walking two while throwing 69 pitches.
    句中的「striking out six and walking two」為現在分詞片語，修飾前面的動作，補充說明劉任右在那五局中達成的數據細節。
