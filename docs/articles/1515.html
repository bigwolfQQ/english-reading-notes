無人機攻擊保加利亞外海船隻，造成十一人失蹤

Eleven missing after drones hit ships off Bulgaria

單字解析

  • merchant ship n. phr. 商船
    Eleven people are missing after two merchant ships were yesterday struck by drones off Bulgaria...
    兩艘商船昨天在保加利亞外海遭到無人機襲擊，造成十一人失蹤...
  • cargo ship n. phr. 貨船
    ...drones struck a Ukrainian-owned cargo ship, while another vessel was also hit...
    ...無人機攻擊了一艘烏克蘭擁有的貨船，同時另一艘船也被擊中...
  • summon v. 傳喚、召集、召見
    Bulgarian Prime Minister Rumen Radev summoned the Russian ambassador after drones struck...
    在無人機攻擊後，保加利亞總理魯門·拉德夫召見了俄羅斯大使...
  • evidence n. 證據
    We lack solid, clear evidence to level accusations against any specific party...
    我們缺乏確鑿、明確的證據來指控任何特定政黨...
  • advantage n. 優勢、有利條件
    ...logic suggests the outcome works to the advantage of the Russian Federation...
    ...邏輯顯示這樣的結果對俄羅斯聯邦有利...
  • preliminary adj. 初步的、預備的
    This is preliminary information...
    這是初步資訊...
  • monitor v. 監控、監測
    ...will continue to monitor the security environment...
    ...並將繼續監控安全環境...
  • condemn v. 譴責
    European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen condemned the latest attacks...
    歐洲聯盟委員會主席烏爾蘇拉·馮德萊恩譴稱最新的攻擊...
  • security n. 安全、保全
    The Black Sea is a lifeline for global food security.
    黑海是全球糧食安全的生命線。
  • frequent adj. 頻繁的
    ...drone attacks have become increasingly frequent in neighboring countries.
    ...無人機攻擊在鄰國變得越來越頻繁。
  • increase n. 增加、增長
    ...adding that the increase was driven by attacks further from the coasts...
    ...並補充說，這一增長是由於海岸線更遠處的攻擊所驅動的。
  • commercial adj. 商業的、商用的
    Last week, two commercial vessels were hit in the Black Sea off Bulgaria...
    上週，保加利亞外海黑海域內的兩艘商船遭到攻擊...

文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Eleven people are missing after two merchant ships were yesterday struck by drones off Bulgaria...
    句中的「were struck」為過去式的被動語態（be動詞 + 過去分詞），用來強調受詞（兩艘商船）承受了動作（被無人機襲擊），而不特別強調施事者。
  • 分詞片語作後置修飾 / 補充說明 (Participial Phrase)
    ...the HS Dignity , a Guinea-Bissau flagged cargo vessel that was en route to the Romanian port of Braila
    句中的「flagged」是過去分詞作為形容詞用，修飾前面的名詞短語，用來簡化形容詞子句，精煉地表達該船隻「懸掛...旗幟」的狀態。
  • 現在分詞表達附帶結果或補充動作 (Present Participle as Adverbial Modifier)
    ...adjusting our deterrence and defense posture as needed,
    句尾的「adjusting...」是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明主句動作（will continue to monitor）同時發生的伴隨狀況或目的。
  • 關係代間子句 (Relative Clause)
    ...the HS Dignity , a Guinea-Bissau flagged cargo vessel that was en route to the Romanian port of Braila
    句中的「that was en route to...」是由關係代名詞 that 引導的限定修飾子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「貨船」當時正前往的目的地。
