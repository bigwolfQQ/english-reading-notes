Line 平台伸出援手助農民：研究所

Line platform gives farmers helping hand: institute

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • develop v. 開發，研發
    The Taiwan Agricultural Research Institute said it has developed a Line-based pest-management platform for rice and cucumber farmers...
    台灣農業試驗所表示，該所已針對稻農和小黃瓜農開發了一個基於 Line 的病蟲害管理平台...
  • average adj. 平均的
    ...that reduced average query times by at least 90 percent to less than a minute in field tests.
    ...在實地測試中，平均查詢時間縮短了至少 90%，降至不到一分鐘。
  • output value n. phr. 產值
    Rice is an important staple crop with an annual output value of more than NT$35 billion...
    稻米是重要的主食作物，年產值超過新台幣 350 億元...
  • promptly adv. 迅速地，即時地
    It therefore developed the platform to help farmers promptly get information on pest and disease management, it said.
    該所因此開發了這個平台，以協助農民及時獲得病蟲害管理相關資訊。
  • inquire v. 詢問，查詢
    Farmers previously managed pests and diseases based on experience, or by searching for information online or making telephone inquiries...
    農民以往是根據經驗來管理病蟲害，或是透過網路搜尋資訊或打電話詢問...
  • time-consuming adj. 耗時的
    That can be time-consuming and the information is scattered and inconsistent, it said.
    該所表示，那樣做可能會耗費時間，且資訊零散又不一致。
  • feature n. 功能，特色
    Users can scan pesticide-product barcodes to access safe-use information and use a separate feature to calculate dilution ratios, it said.
    該所表示，使用者可以掃描農藥產品條碼以取得安全使用資訊，並使用獨立的功能來計算稀釋比例。
  • manual adj. 手動的，體力的
    Field testing showed that the platform reduced average query times to less than a minute from about 10 minutes with manual data retrieval...
    實地測試顯示，該平台將平均查詢時間從手動資料檢索的大約 10 分鐘縮短至不到一分鐘...
  • compliance n. 遵守，合規
    It can also assist agribusinesses, grain dealers, agricultural cooperatives or local production and marketing groups in contract farming management and pesticide-use compliance, it said.
    該所指出，它還能協助農業企業、糧商、農業合作社或地方產銷班進行契約農業管理與農藥使用合規。
  • license v. 授權，發給執照
    The institute said it plans to license the technology to an agricultural information service provider...
    該所表示，計畫將這項技術授權給農業資訊服務供應商...
  • maintain v. 維護，保持
    ...to build, operate and maintain the system for eventual use by agribusinesses and their contracted farmers.
    ...以建構、營運並維護該系統，供農業企業及其簽約農民最終使用。

文法重點

  • 關代受格省略（名詞子句與修飾）
    ...for rice and cucumber farmers that reduced average query times by at least 90 percent to less than a minute in field tests.
    句中的關係代名詞 that 引導形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的 platform（平台），並在此子句中擔任主詞，說明該平台具備「減少查詢時間」的功能。
  • 非限定關代子句（which 的用法）
    The new platform uses Line, which most agricultural workers already know how to use, so they do not need to download or learn another app, it said.
    逗號後面的 which 引導非限定關代子句，對先行詞 Line 進行補充說明。「which most agricultural workers already know how to use」意指「大多數農業務工人員已經知道如何使用它」，並在其中包含了一個「疑問詞 + to V」的結構（how to use）。
  • 分詞作形容詞用（過去分詞修飾名詞）
    ...to build, operate and maintain the system for eventual use by agribusinesses and their contracted farmers.
    句尾的 contracted 是過去分詞（contract 的過去分詞）轉化而來的形容詞，用來修飾名詞 farmers，表示「被簽約的農民（與企業簽約的農民）」，展現了多益常考的商業實務用語。
