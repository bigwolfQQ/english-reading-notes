中台灣筆記：誰來決定台灣的未來？

Notes from Central Taiwan: Who gets to decide Taiwan’s future?

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • tussle n. 爭鬥，衝突，角力
    The tussle between the Chinese Nationalist Party (KMT)... and the pro-Taiwan side in politics is in part a struggle over defining the nation’s agency.
    中國國民黨……與政治上的親台陣營之間的角力，部分是一場關於定義國家主體性的鬥爭。
  • agency n. 主體性，代理權，力量
    What kind of agency does Taiwan have?
    台灣擁有什麼樣的主體性？
  • contend v. 主張，爭辯，競爭
    He also asserted that the threat from the PRC was rising and that the future depended on the actions of PRC dictator Xi Jinping.
    他也斷言來自中華人民共和國的威脅正在升高，未來取決於中共獨裁者習近平的行動。
  • solely adv. 僅僅，唯一地
    Taiwan relies “solely” on outside powers, Su claimed...
    蘇聲稱，台灣「完全」依賴外部勢力……
  • accommodate v. 順應，迎合，容納
    ...that Taiwan should accommodate itself to PRC interests and acquiesce in its own subjugation...
    ……台灣應迎合中共利益並默認自身的被征服……
  • acquiesce v. 默認，順從
    ...and acquiesce in its own subjugation, accepting the faux “1992 consensus”...
    ……並默認自身的被征服，接受虛假的「九二共識」……
  • denigrate v. 貶低，詆毀
    Thus, an important thrust of the KMT anti-Taiwan propaganda campaign is denigrating Taiwan’s agency.
    因此，國民黨反台灣宣傳運動的一個重要主軸，就是貶低台灣的主體性。
  • venality n. 貪腐，可買通性
    The long history of US venality and unreliability helps give this campaign a veneer of rationality.
    美國長期以來的可買通性與不可靠性，有助於給這場宣傳運動披上一層理性外衣。
  • commit v. 致力於，承諾
    Mainland Affairs Council (MAC) Deputy Minister Shen Yu-chung (沈有忠) said last week... that the US remains committed to defending the First Island Chain.
    大陸委員會（MAC）副主委沈有忠上週表示……美國仍致力於捍衛第一島鏈。
  • delay v. 延遲，延期
    The PRC demanded earlier this month that the US delay arms sales until after the November Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) Leaders’ summit...
    中共本月初要求美國將軍售案延至11月亞太經濟合作會議（APEC）領袖峰會之後……
  • announce v. 宣布，宣告
    ...and that the US not announce any sales before the meeting between the two nation’s leaders on the 24th of this month.
    ……並要求美國不得在本月24日兩國領導人會晤之前宣布任何軍售。
  • linkage n. 連結，關聯
    But in this case the KMT has worked hard, intentionally or not, to create that linkage...
    但在這種情況下，國民黨無論是有意還是無意，都努力創造這種連結……

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The PRC demanded earlier this month that the US delay arms sales until after the November Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) Leaders’ summit in China’s Shenzhen and that the US not announce any sales before the meeting between the two nation’s leaders on the 24th of this month.
    句中的 "held at a property of US President Donald Trump"（雖然出現在第7段後半句）是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前面的名詞 "a G20 Summit"，相當於關代子句 "which is held at..." 的簡化版，能讓句子更緊湊。
  • 虛主詞 It 開頭的句構 (Dummy 'It' Subject)
    It might be useful to the pro-Taiwan side to ask such candidates whether Taiwan should embrace the “1992 consensus.”
    句首的 "It" 沒有實際指代意義，是用來代替真正的真主詞——不定詞片語 "to ask such candidates whether Taiwan should embrace the '1992 consensus'"。這種句型常用於形容詞（如 useful）之後，避免主詞過長造成頭重腳輕。
  • 否定副詞置於句首的倒裝句 (Inversion after Negative Adverbs)
    All of us who love Taiwan should not waste this opportunity to pin the negatives on the pro-PRC side.
    註：文章第20段使用的是正常語序。多益常考的倒裝結構如 Never have I seen... 或 Not only did they...，在此文章第18段也有類似強調語氣的副詞用法 "Turning up the volume, though, would help."，幫助學習者熟悉多益常見的強調與倒裝考點。
