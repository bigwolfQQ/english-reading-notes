胡塞武裝奪取對全球航運至關重要的海峽

Houthis take over strait vital to global shipping

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • strategic adj. 戰略性的
    after capturing three strategic islands in a lightning offensive the previous day
    此前他們在前一天的閃電攻勢中奪取了三個戰略島嶼
  • commerce n. 商業、貿易
    The developments are likely to deepen the Middle East war’s impact on international commerce
    這些事態發展可能會加劇中東戰爭對國際貿易的衝擊
  • pipeline n. 管線、輸油管
    as Saudi Arabia shut down an oil pipeline following attacks
    隨著沙烏地阿拉伯在遭受攻擊後關閉輸油管
  • blockade n. 封鎖
    and after months of Iran’s blockade of the Strait of Hormuz.
    以及伊朗對荷莫茲海峽進行數月封鎖之後。
  • official n. 官員
    Following a week-long offensive that has killed hundreds, government officials said
    經過一場造成數百人死亡為期一周的攻勢，政府官員表示
  • exporter n. 出口商
    Saudi Arabia, the world’s biggest oil exporter, has relied on the Red Sea
    身為全球最大石油出口國的沙烏地阿拉伯，一直依賴紅海
  • adviser n. 顧問
    Mohammad Mokhber, an adviser to Iranian Supreme Leader
    伊朗最高領袖的顧問穆罕默德·莫赫貝爾
  • announcement v. (announce 的動詞型態，此處對應 announce) 宣布、公告
    The Saudi Ministry of Energy on Friday announced that attacks led to the closure of the pipeline
    沙烏地阿拉伯能源部周五宣布，攻擊導致該輸油管關閉
  • precaution n. 預防措施
    adding that “the pipeline was stopped as a precaution, and the attacks resulted in some injuries.”
    並補充說「出於預防起見，該輸油管已暫停運作，攻擊造成了一些人員受傷。」
  • dismissal n. 解雇、撤職
    The office of Iraqi Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi announced the dismissal of the commander
    伊拉克總理的辦公室宣布解除該指揮官的職務

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Present Participle）作後位修飾
    ...a vital shipping corridor linking Europe and Asia.
    句中的 linking Europe and Asia 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 corridor（走廊），相當於形容詞子句 'which links Europe and Asia'，在新聞英文中常用以精簡句子結構。
  • 被動語態（Passive Voice）
    The East-West pipeline in the Riyadh and Medina regions was subjected to several attacks on Thursday morning
    此句使用被動語態 'was subjected to'（遭受），強調輸油管（主詞）是遭受攻擊的承受者，而非發動者，商業與新聞英文常用被動語態來客觀陳述事實。
  • 現在完成進行式（Present Perfect Continuous）
    ...which they have been targeting since July.
    句中的 'have been targeting' 是現在完成進行式，強調從過去七月開始的動作一直持續到現在，且帶有動作仍在進行的意涵。
