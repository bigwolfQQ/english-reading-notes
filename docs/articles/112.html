MAC details the potential scope of Chinese artillery

單字解析

  • potential adj. 潛在的、可能的
    Potential improvements to munitions used by China’s long-range rocket-artillery units could extend their range...
    中國遠距火箭砲部隊所使用彈藥的潛在改進，可能會將其射程延伸...
  • extend v. 延伸、擴大、延長
    ...could extend their range to cover the entire Miyako Strait...
    ...可能會將其射程延伸至涵蓋整個宮古海峽...
  • deploy v. 部署、調動
    The PLA’s long-range rocket-artillery units deployed along the Taiwan Strait are concentrated along the coast of Fujian Province...
    部署在台灣海峽沿岸的解放軍遠距火箭砲部隊集中在福建省沿海...
  • concentrate v. 集中、聚集
    ...are concentrated along the coast of Fujian Province, including Changle, Pingtan and Shishi, the quarterly report on China said.
    ...集中在福建省沿海，包括長樂、平潭和石獅，這份關於中國的季報指出。
  • upgrade n. / v. 升級、提升
    “Credible imagery” showed that the units have completed equipment upgrades and are fielding PCH191 modular long-range multiple rocket launchers, it said.
    報告稱，「可信的影像」顯示這些部隊已經完成裝備升級，並正在部署 PCH191 模組化遠距多管火箭發射器。
  • position n. 位置、陣地、職位
    ...which are about 600km from the launch positions in China’s福建 and Zhejiang provinces, it said.
    該處距離中國福建與浙江省的發射陣地約 600 公里，報告指出。
  • reduce v. 減少、降低
    ...adding that composite materials could reduce the weight of the missiles, while improved solid propellant could increase propulsion efficiency.
    ...並補充說複合材料可以減輕飛彈重量，而改良的固態推進劑則能提高推進效率。
  • efficiency n. 效率
    ...while improved solid propellant could increase propulsion efficiency.
    ...而改良的固態推進劑則能提高推進效率。
  • integrate v. 整合、結合
    The report said the Feilong-60A could be integrated with the PCH191 to scout the battlefield...
    報告指出，「飛龍-60A」可以與 PCH191 整合，以偵察戰場...
  • combine v. 結合、聯合
    ...giving the PLA a way to combine battlefield intelligence with long-range firepower, it said.
    報告指出，這為解放軍提供一種將戰場情資與遠距火力結合的方法。
  • overcome v. 克服、解決
    ...could overcome the traditional geographic limitations of long-range strikes...
    ...可以克服遠距打擊的傳統地理限制...
  • contributor n. 貢獻者
    ...could make the Chinese army the most important contributor of firepower in a hypothetical joint landing campaign against Taiwan.
    ...可能會使中國陸軍成為針對台灣的假想聯合登陸作戰中最主要的火力貢獻者。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後置定語 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The PLA’s long-range rocket-artillery units deployed along the Taiwan Strait are concentrated along the coast...
    句中的「deployed along the Taiwan Strait」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「units（部隊）」。原句省略了「which are」，還原全貌應為「units which are deployed along the Taiwan Strait」，這種結構在多益閱讀測驗中非常常出現，能有效簡化句子。
  • 分詞構句表附帶結果 (Participial Phrase indicating Result)
    ...giving the PLA the ability to secure the strategic waterway, the Mainland Affairs Council (MAC) said in a report.
    句尾的「giving the PLA the ability...」是由現在分詞「giving」開頭的分詞構句，在此處用來表達前面事件所帶來的自然結果（即：升級射程，『進而賦予』了解放軍某種能力）。
  • 被動語態的應用 (Passive Voice)
    The report said the Feilong-60A could be integrated with the PCH191 to scout the battlefield...
    句中的「could be integrated」使用了被動語態（助動詞 + be + 過去分詞）。因為飛彈或無人機是被「整合」到發射系統上，而非主動去整合，因此使用被動語態來強調受事者。
  • 關係代間詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    ...extend their range to cover the entire Miyako Strait, which might give the Chinese People’s Liberation Army (PLA) the ability...
    句中的「which might give...」是由關係代間詞「which」引導的非限定關係子句，用逗號與前方隔開，對前面的整件事情（延長射程涵蓋宮古海峽）進行補充說明。
