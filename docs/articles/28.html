光寶科技將投資液冷技術

Lite-On Tech to invest in liquid cooling

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • invest v. 投資
    Lite-On Technology Corp (光寶) yesterday said it would invest about US$176 million for a 25-percent stake in liquid-cooling solutions provider DCX Liquid Cooling Systems
    光寶科技昨天表示，將投資約 1.76 億美元取得液冷解決方案供應商 DCX Liquid Cooling Systems 25% 的股權
  • stake n. 股份，股權
    Lite-On Technology Corp (光寶) yesterday said it would invest about US$176 million for a 25-percent stake in liquid-cooling solutions provider DCX Liquid Cooling Systems
    光寶科技昨天表示，將投資約 1.76 億美元取得液冷解決方案供應商 DCX Liquid Cooling Systems 25% 的股權
  • partnership n. 夥伴關係，合作關係
    “Through this partnership, we look forward to advancing innovation and creating new possibilities in the rapidly expanding AI market,” Lite-On chairman Tom Soong (宋明峰) said in a statement.
    光寶董事長宋明峰在聲明中表示：「透過這次的合作，我們期盼能在快速擴張的 AI 市場中推進創新並創造新的可能性。」
  • collaboration n. 合作，協力
    “Lite-On will continue to engage with global partners that possess deep technological expertise and strong strategic synergies, strengthening cross-domain technology integration through strategic investments and long-term collaboration,” Soong said.
    宋明峰表示：「光寶將持續與具備深厚技術專業與強大策略綜效的全球夥伴交流，透過策略投資與長期合作，強化跨領域技術整合。」
  • portfolio n. 產品組合，投資組合
    DCX’s product portfolio includes enterprise-level coolant distribution units (CDU) ranging from 600 kilowatts to 2.6 megawatts, hyperscale-class facility coolant distribution units (FDU) of up to 16MW, as well as cold plates, rack manifolds and modular data center systems, Lite-On said.
    光寶指出，DCX 的產品組合包含功率從 600 千瓦到 2.6 百萬瓦的企業級冷卻液分配單元（CDU）、高達 16 百萬瓦的超大規模級設施冷卻液分配單元（FDU），以及冷板、機櫃分流管和模組化資料中心系統。
  • operator n. 營運商，經營者
    Its products have been deployed by three of the world’s top 10 AI data center operators and shipped to 75 countries, Lite-On said.
    光寶表示，其產品已被全球前十大 AI 資料中心營運商中的三家所採用，並出貨至 75 個國家。
  • phase n. 階段，時期
    In the early phase of the partnership, Lite-On and DCX would focus on in-row CDUs, already widely deployed at large AI data centers, and introduce them to customers, a Lite-On official told the Taipei Times by telephone on Thursday.
    光寶的一位官員週四接受《台北時報》電話採訪時表示，在合作的初期階段，光寶與 DCX 將專注於已在大型 AI 資料中心廣泛部署的機櫃間 CDU，並將其引進給客戶。
  • expand v. 擴展，擴大
    In the long term, Lite-On aims to expand its large FDU business as AI server rack power consumption rises and cooling architectures extend to facility-level systems, the official said.
    該官員表示，長期而言，隨著 AI 伺服器機櫃功耗增加以及冷卻架構延伸至設施級系統，光寶旨在擴展其大型 FDU 業務。
  • supply chain n. 供應鏈
    It plans to combine DCX’s expertise with Lite-On’s customer relationships, manufacturing and supply chain capabilities to serve hyperscalers’ next-generation cooling needs going forward, the official said.
    該官員表示，光寶計劃結合 DCX 的專業技術與光寶的客戶關係、製造及供應鏈能力，以滿足超大規模資料中心未來世代的冷卻需求。
  • complement v. 補充，與…相輔相成
    The investment is also expected to make liquid cooling a key growth pillar of Lite-On’s AI data center business, complementing its existing power solutions, the official said.
    該官員表示，此項投資預期也將使液冷成為光寶 AI 資料中心業務的關鍵成長支柱，與其現有的電源解決方案相輔相成。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作附帶狀況或結果狀語
    Lite-On Technology Corp (光寶) yesterday said it would invest about US$176 million for a 25-percent stake in liquid-cooling solutions provider DCX Liquid Cooling Systems, as it seeks to pair its power-management products with in-row cooling systems for artificial intelligence (AI) data centers.
    句尾的 "as it seeks to..." 為連接詞引导的原因副詞子句，表達光寶進行投資的動機與目的。
  • 現在分詞片語作後置修飾（形容詞功能）
    “Lite-On will continue to engage with global partners that possess deep technological expertise and strong strategic synergies, strengthening cross-domain technology integration through strategic investments and long-term collaboration,” Soong said.
    句中的 ", strengthening cross-domain technology integration..." 是由現在分詞 "strengthening" 引導的分詞片語，置於主句之後，用來補充說明前面整個動作所帶來的結果或伴隨的情況。
  • 被動語態的使用
    Its products have been deployed by three of the world’s top 10 AI data center operators and shipped to 75 countries, Lite-On said.
    此句使用了現在完成式的被動語態（"have been deployed" 與 "shipped"），強調產品「被」全球前十大營運商採用以及「被」運送至各國，焦點在於產品本身的狀態而非動作的執行者。
  • 對等連接詞連接多個受詞
    It plans to combine DCX’s expertise with Lite-On’s customer relationships, manufacturing and supply chain capabilities to serve hyperscalers’ next-generation cooling needs going forward, the official said.
    句中的 "customer relationships, manufacturing and supply chain capabilities" 透過對等連接詞 and 將多個名詞並列，作為 combine... with... 結構中受詞的一部分，展現精煉且結構清楚的商業英文寫作風格。
