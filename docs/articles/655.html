台灣汽車零件企業在法蘭克福國際車展拿下 1.47 億美元商機

Taiwan auto parts firms secure US$147M deals at Frankfurt trade expo

單字解析

  • secure v. 獲得、取得、確保
    Taiwanese auto parts and accessory makers secured US$147 million in business deals while participating in Automechanika Frankfurt held from Sept. 8 to 12...
    台灣汽車零件與配件製造商於 9 月 8 日至 12 日參加在法蘭克福舉行的 Automechanika Frankfurt 車展期間，總計獲得了 1.47 億美元的商業訂單。
  • supplier n. 供應商
    ...a total of 324 Taiwanese auto parts and accessory suppliers, including major manufacturers of car door handles, bumpers, metal components and automotive electronics, took part in the Frankfurt trade exhibition...
    共有 324 家台灣汽車零件與配件供應商參加了法蘭克福國際貿易展覽會，其中包括車門把手、保險槓、金屬組件及車用電子產品等主要製造商...
  • exhibition n. 展覽、展售會
    The MOEA said the five-day exhibition attracted 1,970 potential buyers and visitors to the Taiwan Pavilion and generated deals worth US$147 million.
    經濟部表示，為期五天的展覽吸引了 1,970 名潛在買主與訪客參觀台灣館，並創造了價值 1.47 億美元的交易額。
  • potential adj. 潛在的、可能的
    The MOEA said the five-day exhibition attracted 1,970 potential buyers and visitors to the Taiwan Pavilion and generated deals worth US$147 million.
    經濟部表示，為期五天的展覽吸引了 1,970 名潛在買主與訪客參觀台灣館，並創造了價值 1.47 億美元的交易額。
  • generate v. 產生、引起、創造（營收、利潤等）
    The MOEA said the five-day exhibition attracted 1,970 potential buyers and visitors to the Taiwan Pavilion and generated deals worth US$147 million.
    經濟部表示，為期五天的展覽吸引了 1,970 名潛在買主與訪客參觀台灣館，並創造了價值 1.47 億美元的交易額。
  • cooperation n. 合作
    ...where 350 international buyers and representatives of automotive industry associations met with Taiwanese firms to exchange ideas and discuss cross-border cooperation, the MOEA said.
    經濟部表示，有 350 名國際買主與汽車產業協會代表與台灣企業會面，交流想法並商討跨國合作。
  • allocate v. 分派、分配（經費、資源等）
    The MOEA added that the government will allocate funds from its budget to organize trade promotion delegations to attend international shows...
    經濟部補充說明，政府將自預算中撥款籌組貿易推廣團參加國際展覽...
  • strike a deal phr. 達成協議、成交
    ...while inviting potential foreign buyers to Taiwan to meet Taiwanese suppliers, strike deals and expand their reach overseas.
    ...同時邀請潛在的外國買主來台，與台灣供應商會面、敲定交易並拓展其海外市場版圖。
  • expand v. 擴大、擴展
    ...while inviting potential foreign buyers to Taiwan to meet Taiwanese suppliers, strike deals and expand their reach overseas.
    ...同時邀請潛在的外國買主來台，與台灣供應商會面、敲定交易並拓展其海外市場版圖。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase)
    Taiwanese auto parts and accessory makers secured US$147 million in business deals while participating in Automechanika Frankfurt held from Sept. 8 to 12...
    句中的 "participating in..." 是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明主詞 "Taiwanese auto parts and accessory makers" 在展覽期間「正在參與」的動作，相當於副詞子句 "while they participated in..." 的簡化。
  • 過去分詞作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase)
    ...product launches aimed at connecting Taiwanese businesses with international buyers.
    句中的 "aimed at..." 是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞 "product launches"（產品發表會）。這裡省略了 "which are / that are"，用被動概念的過去分詞來表達「旨在......的發表會」。
  • 附屬連接詞接分詞構句 (Conjunction + Participle)
    The ministry said that with the global auto industry accelerating its digital transformation and the European market emphasizing high-quality... products...
    這裡使用了「with + 受詞 + 現在分詞 (accelerating / emphasizing)」的複合結構，用來表達附帶狀況，意指「隨著全球汽車產業加速數位轉型，且歐洲市場強調...產品」。這是多益閱讀與商業英文中極常出現的高級句型。
