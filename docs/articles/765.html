文法重點

  • be to be held (be + to V 表未來預定計畫)
    Thirteen models showcase outfits by top designers at the Kong Zong Cultural Base in Taipei yesterday to promote Taipei Fashion Week SS27, which is to be held from Oct. 15 to 18.
    「be + to V」句型常用於新聞英語中，用來表示官方或正式排定的未來計畫、預定行程。此處配合被動語態（is to be held），指活動「預定於」某段時間舉行。
  • 非限定關代子句 (which 指代先行詞)
    Thirteen models showcase outfits by top designers at the Kong Zong Cultural Base in Taipei yesterday to promote Taipei Fashion Week SS27, which is to be held from Oct. 15 to 18.
    句尾使用逗號加上「which is to be held...」，這是一個非限定關係代名詞子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「Taipei Fashion Week SS27」，提供該活動舉辦時間的額外資訊。
