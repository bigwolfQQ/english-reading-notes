單字解析

  • account for phr. 佔（比例）
    Taiwan, which accounts for more than half of the global outsourced semiconductor assembly and testing market, is well-positioned to develop copackaged optics...
    台灣佔全球委外半導體封測市場一半以上，極具優勢來發展共同封裝光學元件...
  • well-positioned adj. 處於有利地位的、具備優勢的
    Taiwan... is well-positioned to develop copackaged optics (CPO) and manufacture precision silicon photonics chips, the Ministry of Economic Affairs said yesterday.
    經濟部昨天表示，台灣...極具優勢來發展共同封裝光學元件（CPO）並製造精密矽光子晶片。
  • manufacturing n. 製造業、製造
    Taiwan has key advantages in the transition, including a comprehensive semiconductor ecosystem and more than 90 percent of global advanced semiconductor manufacturing capacity...
    台灣在此一轉型過程中擁有關鍵優勢，包括完整的半導體生態系統，以及佔全球先進半導體製造產能90%以上...
  • reduce v. 減少、降低
    ...the technology can reduce power consumption by 30 to 50 percent, allowing devices to stream high-definition content...
    ...該技術可以減少30%至50%的耗電量，讓裝置能夠更快地串流高畫質內容...
  • consumption n. 消耗、消費
    ...the technology can reduce power consumption by 30 to 50 percent, allowing devices to stream high-definition content...
    ...該技術可以減少30%至50%的耗電量，讓裝置能夠更快地串流高畫質內容...
  • allow v. 允許、讓
    ...allowing devices to stream high-definition content and generate artificial intelligence content more quickly, the ministry said.

  • comprehensive adj. 全面的、綜合的
    Taiwan has key advantages in the transition, including a comprehensive semiconductor ecosystem...
    台灣在此一轉型過程中擁有關鍵優勢，包括完整的半導體生態系統...

    經濟部表示，以及佔全球7奈米或以下先進半導體製造產能90%以上。
  • initiative n. 倡議、新措施
    The government launched a silicon photonics research and development (R&D) initiative under its A+ Enterprise Innovation R&D Program earlier this year...
    政府於今年稍早在「A+企業創新研發計畫」下啟動了一項矽光子研發計畫...

    The alliance aims to overcome key technological hurdles, particularly in CPO, and ensure that Taiwan retains control of the silicon photonics supply chain, it said.
    聯盟旨在克服關鍵技術障礙，特別是在CPO方面，並確保台灣維持對矽光子供應鏈的控制權，它表示。

文法重點

  • 非限定關係代名詞子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Taiwan, which accounts for more than half of the global outsourced semiconductor assembly and testing market, is well-positioned to develop copackaged optics...
    句中的「which accounts for...」前方有逗號，屬於非限定用法，用來補充說明主詞 Taiwan 的背景資訊，若拿掉此子句，主要子句的句意依然完整。
  • 現在分詞作結果狀語 (Present Participle indicating a result)
    ...the technology can reduce power consumption by 30 to 50 percent, allowing devices to stream high-definition content and generate artificial intelligence content more quickly...
    句尾的「allowing...」是由對等子句簡化而來的現在分詞構句，表示前一個動作自然產生的結果（即降低耗電量，進而『允許』裝置……）。
  • 關係代名詞子句修飾先行詞 (Relative Clause modifying a noun)
    Taiwan accounts for more than half of the global semiconductor assembly and testing market, which gives it a key role in developing CPO...
    句中的「which gives it...」用來補充說明前面整句提到的「全球半導體封測市場過半」這件事，對台灣在發展CPO時所扮演的角色有何影響。
