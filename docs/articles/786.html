頂尖標槍選手的標槍在賽會前夕的飛行途中斷裂

Star javelineer’s javelins broken in-flight before Games

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • prospect n. 有希望的候選者、潛在客戶、前途
    Sri Lanka’s Asian Games medal prospect Rumesh Tharanga Pathirage returned home after winning the title at a top international event on Tuesday to find his javelins in pieces.
    斯里蘭卡亞運會奪牌希望魯梅什·塔蘭加·帕蒂拉奇（Rumesh Tharanga Pathirage）周二在贏得一場頂級國際賽事冠軍後返回家鄉，卻發現他的標槍碎成好幾截。
  • discover v. 發現、發覺
    The 23-year-old rising star, who has taken the javelin world by storm this year with a series of impressive victories, was heartbroken to discover that his javelins had snapped.
    這位 23 歲的新星今年憑藉一系列令人印象深刻的勝利席捲了標槍界，他心碎地發現自己的標槍斷裂了。
  • check in phr. （在機場）辦理登機手續、託運（行李）
    “We checked in five javelins — all packed extremely carefully and well — but all of them arrived broken.”
    「我們託運了五支標槍——全部都包裝得極其小心且妥當——但它們運達時全都斷了。」
  • list v. 列出、清單
    Pathirage posted a photograph of his damaged javelins on social media, listing the distance he had thrown them
    帕蒂拉奇在社群媒體上發布了他受損標槍的照片，並列出了他投擲出的距離
  • lodge a complaint phr. 提出申訴、投訴
    Pathirage was returning from the World Athletics Ultimate Championships in Hungary on Turkish Airlines and has lodged a complaint.
    帕蒂拉奇是搭乘土耳其航空從匈牙利的世界田徑終極錦標賽返回，目前已提出申訴。
  • cost n. 成本、費用
    “It is not the cost of a javelin, which is quite high, but the emotional attachment,” Prasanna said.
    「這不是標槍成本的問題，標槍成本相當高，而是情感上的依戀，」普拉桑納說。
  • setback n. 挫折、阻礙
    “This is a psychological setback for Rumesh and I will counsel him on it,” he said.
    「這對魯梅什來說是一個心理上的挫折，我會就此輔導他，」他說。
  • provide v. 提供、供應
    Sri Lanka’s Ministry of Sports said it would provide new javelins for Pathirage to compete at the Asian Games in Japan.
    斯里蘭卡體育部表示，將為帕蒂拉奇提供新標槍，以便他在日本亞運會上參賽。
  • statement n. 聲明、陳述
    “The government will take up this matter with the airline and take appropriate legal action,” the ministry said in a statement.
    該部在聲明中表示：「政府將與航空公司交涉此事，並採取適當的法律行動。」
  • defend v. 防守、保衛（頭銜等）
    India’s javelin star Neeraj Chopra is injured and unable to defend his Asian Games gold.
    印度的標槍明星尼拉吉·喬普拉（Neeraj Chopra）受傷，無法保衛他的亞運金牌。

文法重點

  • 非限定關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    The 23-year-old rising star, who has taken the javelin world by storm this year with a series of impressive victories, was heartbroken to discover that his javelins had snapped.
    句中的「who has taken the javelin world by storm...」是一個由逗號隔開的非限定關係子句，用來補充說明先行詞「The 23-year-old rising star」的額外資訊。若將此子句拿掉，主句的語意依然完整。
  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense)
    The 23-year-old rising star, who has taken the javelin world by storm this year with a series of impressive victories, was heartbroken to discover that his javelins had snapped.
    句尾的「had snapped」使用了過去完成式（had + 過去分詞），用來表示在過去某個時間點（discover 發現的動作）之前已經發生的動作，強調標槍斷裂發生在「他發現」的這個過去時間點之前。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾
    Pathirage was returning from the World Athletics Ultimate Championships in Hungary on Turkish Airlines and has lodged a complaint.
    在「all packed extremely carefully and well」中，過去分詞「packed」引導的分詞片語用來補充說明前面五支標槍是被小心包裝的狀態（被動語態的概念），省略了「which were」，使句子更為簡潔。
