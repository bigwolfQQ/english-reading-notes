隨著航班激增，台灣成為釜山外國旅客最大來源國

Taiwan tops foreign arrivals to Busan as flights surge

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • sharp increase n. phr. 急遽增加
    Taiwan has become the largest source of foreign visitors to Busan, South Korea, following a sharp increase in direct flights, the Seoul-based Yonhap News Agency said Sunday.
    總部設在首爾的韓聯社週日報導，在直飛航班急劇增加之後，台灣已成為南韓釜山最大的外國訪客來源國。
  • a total of phr. 總共
    A total of 2,927,674 foreign tourists visited Busan from January to July, 605,000 of whom were Taiwanese, Yonhap said.
    韓聯社表示，1月至7月共有2,927,674名外國遊客造訪釜山，其中605,000人來自台灣。
  • statistics n. 統計數據
    Citing aviation statistics released Sunday by the Korea Airports Corp, Yonhap reported that 6,609 flights operated between Busan and Taiwan in the first seven months of 2026...
    韓聯社引用南韓機場公社週日發布的航空統計數據報導稱，2026年前七個月，釜山與台灣之間營運了6,609個航班……
  • release v. 發布、公開
    Citing aviation statistics released Sunday by the Korea Airports Corp, Yonhap reported that 6,609 flights operated between Busan and Taiwan in the first seven months of 2026...
    韓聯社引用南韓機場公社週日發布的航空統計數據報導稱，2026年前七個月，釜山與台灣之間營運了6,609個航班……
  • operate v. 營運、運作
    Citing aviation statistics released Sunday by the Korea Airports Corp, Yonhap reported that 6,609 flights operated between Busan and Taiwan in the first seven months of 2026...
    韓聯社引用南韓機場公社週日發布的航空統計數據報導稱，2026年前七個月，釜山與台灣之間營運了6,609個航班……
  • equivalent adj. 相等的、相當於
    ...matching the total for all of 2024 and equivalent to 82.9 percent of the 7,976 flights recorded in all of 2025.
    ……這與2024年全年的總數相當，相當於2025年全年記錄的7,976個航班的82.9%。
  • drive v. 驅動、推進（常用於被動語態表示原因）
    The statistics showed that the increase was driven largely by Taiwanese carriers, including EVA Air and Starlux Airlines, launching new services...
    統計數據顯示，這項成長主要由包括長榮航空和星宇航空在內的台灣航空公司開通新航線所驅動……
  • carrier n. 航空公司、運輸公司
    The statistics showed that the increase was driven largely by Taiwanese carriers, including EVA Air and Starlux Airlines, launching new services...
    統計數據顯示，這項成長主要由包括長榮航空和星宇航空在內的台灣航空公司開通新航線所驅動……
  • launch v. 開辦、推出（新產品、新服務等）
    The statistics showed that the increase was driven largely by Taiwanese carriers, including EVA Air and Starlux Airlines, launching new services...
    統計數據顯示，這項成長主要由包括長榮航空和星宇航空在內的台灣航空公司開通新航線所驅動……
  • maintain v. 維持、保持
    ...with most flights between Busan and Taiwan maintaining load factors above 80 percent.
    ……釜山與台灣之間的絕大多數航班維持在80%以上的載客率。
  • serve v. 服務、供應（航線、班機）
    The Busan-Taoyuan route is currently served by nine airlines...
    目前釜山-桃園航線由9家航空公司營運……

文法重點

  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況（with + 名詞 + 現在分詞）
    ...with most flights between Busan and Taiwan maintaining load factors above 80 percent.
    「with + O. + V-ing」結構用來表示附帶狀況，在此句中用來補充說明航班增加時的伴隨現象，意指「在……的情況下」或「隨著……」。
  • 過去分詞（被動）作後位修飾
    Citing aviation statistics released Sunday by the Korea Airports Corp...
    過去分詞片語「released Sunday by the Korea Airports Corp」置於名詞 statistics 後方，用來修飾該統計數據，相當於形容詞子句「which were released Sunday...」的簡化形式。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句（of whom）
    A total of 2,927,674 foreign tourists visited Busan from January to July, 605,000 of whom were Taiwanese, Yonhap said.
    句尾使用「介係詞 + whom」的非限定關係代名詞子句（605,000 of whom were Taiwanese），用逗號隔開，補充說明前述外遊客中包含多少台灣旅客，這在多益閱讀測驗中是常見的句構。
  • 被動語態（Passive Voice）
    The statistics showed that the increase was driven largely by Taiwanese carriers...
    句型使用「was driven by」，為主詞 the increase 搭配被動語態，強調成長是由台灣航空公司所推動的，常應用於商業報導中說明原因與結果。
