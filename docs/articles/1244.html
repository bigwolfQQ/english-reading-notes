單字解析

  • boast v. 誇耀，自吹自擂
    US President Donald Trump might tout his “truly great friendship” with Chinese President Xi Jinping (習近平), boasting after their summit last month that the two countries “have never gotten along better,”...
    美國總統川普可能會兜售他與中國國家主席習近平之間「真正偉大的友誼」，在上個月的峰會後誇耀兩國「從未像現在這樣融洽」……
  • stark adj. 嚴酷的，明顯的
    ...but beneath the smiles in Washington, the reality remains stark: “strategic stability” is an illusion...
    但在華盛頓的笑容背後，現實依然嚴峻：「戰略穩定」只是一種幻覺……
  • illusion n. 幻覺，錯覺
    ...“strategic stability” is an illusion, while more tit-for-tat military buildup should be expected.
    ……「戰略穩定」只是一種幻覺，同時預期將會出現更多針鋒相對的軍事集結。
  • intention n. 意圖，打算
    Neither Washington nor Beijing has any intention of easing its security posture in East and Southeast Asia.
    華盛頓與北京都沒有放鬆其在東亞和東南亞安全態勢的意圖。
  • prospect n. 前景，前途
    This hard truth dims any realistic prospect for a managed rivalry...
    這個殘酷的現實削弱了任何實現受控競爭的實際前景……
  • dispatch v. 派遣，發送
    The Chinese Communist Party (CCP) continues to dispatch about eight military aircraft and 10 naval vessels a day around Taiwan.
    中國共產黨（中共）持續每天派遣大約8架次軍機和10艘次軍艦在台灣周邊活動。
  • yield v. 屈服，妥協，產出
    Yet, during Xi’s three-day visit, Trump refused to yield to the Chinese leader’s ultimate red line...
    然而，在習近平為期三天的訪問期間，川普拒絕向這位中國領導人的最終紅線妥協……
  • resolute adj. 堅決的，堅定的
    ...he might face a Congress even more resolute to arm Taiwan against Beijing’s daily coercion.
    ……他可能會面臨一個更決心武裝台灣以對抗北京日常脅迫的國會。
  • coercion n. 脅迫，強迫
    ...he might face a Congress even more resolute to arm Taiwan against Beijing’s daily coercion.
    ……他可能會面臨一個更決心武裝台灣以對抗北京日常脅迫的國會。
  • dispute v. 對……提出異議，爭論
    Manila, a treaty ally of the US, disputes the illegal Chinese claims to almost 90 percent of this vast stretch of water.
    作為美國的條約盟友，馬尼拉對中國對這片廣闊水域近90%的非法聲索提出了異議。
  • deploy v. 部署（軍隊、裝備等）
    In 2017, Washington deployed a Terminal High Altitude Area Defense air and missile defense system on the Korean Peninsula...
    2017年，華盛頓在朝鮮半島部署了「終端高空區域防禦系統」（薩德）的空防與飛彈防禦系統……
  • compensation v. 補償，彌補（文章中使用動詞 compensating）
    Allied partners such as Japan, Canada, Australia and New Zealand are increasingly compensating for decreases in publicly announced US passages.
    日本、加拿大、澳洲和紐西蘭等盟友夥伴正日益補償公開宣佈的美國巡航次數的減少。
  • coordination n. 協調，配合
    This could not happen without coordination with Washington and is a direct result of the Trump administration’s persistent demands for burden-sharing.
    若非與華盛頓進行協調，這是不可能發生的大事，這也是川普政府持續要求分擔防衛負擔的直接結果。
  • accelerate v. 加速，促進
    ...devises a new national defense strategy and accelerates the implementation of a new military revolution.
    ……設計出新的國防戰略，並加速實施新的軍事革命。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附加說明（補充結果或狀態）
    US President Donald Trump might tout his “truly great friendship” with Chinese President Xi Jinping (習近平), boasting after their summit last month that the two countries “have never gotten along better,”...
    句尾的「boasting...」是由現在分詞構成的分詞片語，用來補充說明主句主事者（川普）在做主要動作（tout）的同時所伴隨的行為或產生的說詞。
  • 否定詞倒裝句（Neither...）
    Neither Washington nor Beijing has any intention of easing its security posture in East and Southeast Asia.
    「Neither + A nor B」連接兩個否定主語，表達「A與B兩者皆不」，句型本身帶有否定含意，因此動詞使用單數的 has，展現正式且精鍊的寫作語氣。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    This is “gray zone” pressure designed to force reunification without a direct military attack.
    句中的「designed to force...」是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞 pressure（壓力），省略了「which is」，用來簡化句子並精確形容該壓力的目的。
  • 假設語氣（Could not have happened without...）
    This could not happen without coordination with Washington and is a direct result of the Trump administration’s persistent demands for burden-sharing.
    句型「could not happen without...」（若沒有……是不可能發生的）隱含條件句的概念，用來表達對過去或當前情況關鍵因素的強力肯定。
  • 插入語用法（介系詞片語表達條件）
    A Chinese military expert, who spoke on condition of anonymity due to legal jeopardy in China under the CCP’s new national security laws, said that...
    「on condition of anonymity」（在匿名的條件下）作為介系詞片語插入在動詞片語中，用來精準解釋該軍事專家發言的附帶條件。
