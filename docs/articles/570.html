俄軍進攻頓內次克地區，考驗基輔新任指揮官

Russian drive on Donetsk region tests Kyiv’s new commanders

單字解析

  • bracing for phr. 嚴陣以待、作好心理準備以應對（不好的事）
    Ukraine is bracing for months of fierce fighting over key strongholds in the eastern Donetsk region...
    烏克蘭正嚴陣以待，準備迎接未來幾個月在東部頓內次克地區關鍵據點的激烈戰鬥...
  • offensive n. 攻勢、進攻
    ...military analysts expect Russia to intensify its offensive as winter approaches.
    ...軍事分析人士仍預期隨著冬季逼近，俄羅斯將加劇其攻勢。
  • breakthrough n. 突破、重大進展
    ...but there has been no sign of a breakthrough.
    ...但目前尚未出現任何突破的跡象。
  • consequential adj. 影響深遠的、重大的
    The looming battle for Donetsk is shaping up to be one of the most consequential phases of the war this year.
    這場即將到來的頓內次克之戰正逐漸成為今年戰爭中最具重大影響力的階段之一。
  • critical adj. 極為重要的、關鍵的
    For Ukraine, holding it is critical to protecting the backbone of its eastern defenses...
    對烏克蘭而言，守住該地區對於保護其東部防線的中堅力量...至關重要。
  • outcome n. 結果、後果
    However, he warned that the outcome on the battlefield was unpredictable.
    不過，他警告戰場的結果依然難以預料。
  • dismiss v. 對...不予理會、駁回、解散
    Ukraine has routinely dismissed Putin’s demands that it surrender the 15 percent to 20 percent of the Donetsk region...
    烏克蘭例行性地駁回了普丁的要求，即烏方必須交出...頓內次克地區15%至20%的土地...
  • deteriorate v. 惡化、退化
    As the weather conditions deteriorate, drones become less effective...
    隨著氣象條件惡化，無人機的效能降低...
  • prioritizing v. 把...區分優先順序、優先考慮
    ...another new-generation commander who is popular among troops for prioritizing soldiers’ lives.
    ...另一位將士兵生命放在首位而在部隊中深受歡迎的新世代指揮官。
  • delegating v. 授權、委派（權力或職責）
    Kofman said Drapatyi appeared to be delegating more authority to the operational-level commanders...
    科夫曼表示，德拉帕蒂似乎正在向戰術層級的指揮官下放更多權力...
  • manpower n. 人力、勞動力
    Yet Ukraine’s army still lacks the manpower to stage large-scale offensive operations.
    然而，烏克蘭軍隊仍然缺乏發動大規模攻勢的人力。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Ukraine is bracing for months of fierce fighting over key strongholds in the eastern Donetsk region that are at the heart of Russia’s war aims, in the first major test for Kyiv’s incoming top general Mykhailo Drapatyi and his new commanders.
    句中的 "incoming"（即將上任的）是現在分詞轉化為形容詞，用來修飾名詞 "top general"。這種用法在多益閱讀測驗中極常出現，用以簡化形容詞子句（原意為 who is incoming）。
  • 現在分詞表附帶結果或狀態 (Present Participle indicating Result)
    Moscow’s larger forces have been inching toward the so-called “fortress belt” cities of Sloviansk and Kramatorsk, the capture of which would bring Russia closer to its goal of seizing the entire industrialized Donetsk region.
    句尾的 "seizing" 是動名詞，置於介詞 "of" 後方，用來補充說明 "goal" 的具體內容（同位格的概念）。多益考試經常考介詞後面接 V-ing 的文法考點。
  • 被動語態的應用 (Passive Voice)
    Both are under worsening strikes by killer drones and guided bombs, regional officials have said.
    句中使用過去分詞 "guided"（被導引的）來修飾 bombs，構成複合形容詞「導引炸彈」。被動語態與分詞形容詞在多益商業閱讀與聽力中（如報章報導、產品說明）是核心考點。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Independent Participial Phrase)
    Recent appointments under the reform-minded Drapatyi, 43, who replaced Oleksandr Syrskyi in July, have put respected and proven commanders in charge of defending the region.
    句中的 "reform-minded" 與 "respected and proven" 均運用了過去分詞（proven）或複合形容詞來修飾名詞，省略了原本冗長的關係代名詞子句，使商業或新聞英文顯得精鍊。
