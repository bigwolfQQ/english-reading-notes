掌控數據者掌控未來

Who controls the data controls the future

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • facility n. 設施，場所
    In March, Iranian drones targeted three Amazon Web Services (AWS) facilities in Bahrain and the United Arab Emirates (UAE)...
    今年三月，伊朗無人機攻擊了巴林和阿拉伯聯合大公國（UAE）的三處亞馬遜網路服務（AWS）設施...
  • infrastructure n. 基礎設施
    The servers holding a country’s data... are no longer background infrastructure.
    保存一個國家數據的伺服器……不再只是背景基礎設施。
  • strategy n. 策略，戰略
    Roughly 90 countries have some kind of national AI strategy; at least 33 have passed binding legislation.
    大約90個國家擁有某种形式的國家AI策略；至少33個國家已經通過了具約束力的立法。
  • diversification n. 多角化，多樣化
    On paper, this looks like sensible diversification away from a handful of US and Chinese firms.
    從紙面上來看，這看起來像是遠離少數美國和中國企業的明智多元化策略。
  • governance n. 治理，管理
    Governance sovereignty — the ability to set the rules AI systems have to follow... is the one almost nobody talks about.
    治理主權——制定AI系統必須遵守規則的能力……則是幾乎沒有人談論的項目。
  • legislation n. 立法，法律
    ...at least 33 have passed binding legislation.
    ……至少33個國家已經通過了具約束力的立法。
  • penalty n. 懲罰，罰款
    The EU’s AI Act... carries penalties of up to 35 million euros...
    歐盟的《人工智慧法案》……最高可處以3500萬歐元的罰款……
  • turnover n. 營業額，周轉率
    ...or 7 percent of the offender’s global annual turnover.
    ……或違規者全球年營業額的7%。
  • cooperation n. 合作
    China’s Global AI Governance Action Plan offers cooperation on AI governance with developing economies...
    中國的《全球人工智慧治理行動計畫》則提供與發展中國家在AI治理方面的合作……
  • investment n. 投資
    That US$100 billion investment in sovereign compute is happening almost entirely in rich countries.
    那1000億美元的主權算力投資幾乎全部發生在富裕國家。
  • mechanism n. 機制
    The world needs a real mechanism to stop AI sovereignty from turning into just another axis of inequality.
    世界需要一個真正的機制來阻止AI主權變成又一個不平等的軸心。
  • consequential adj. 重大的，隨之而來的
    The infrastructure underpinning a growing share of consequential decisions... sits in buildings most governments do not control...
    支撐著日益增加的重大決策的基礎設施……都位於大多數政府無法控制的建築物內……

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作後置修飾語
    The servers holding a country’s data, and the chips running the artificial intelligence (AI) models on which its economy increasingly leans, are no longer background infrastructure.
    句中的 "holding a country’s data" 與 "running the artificial intelligence (AI) models..." 均為現在分詞片語，用來後置修飾前面的名詞 "servers" 與 "chips"，相當於關代子句 "that hold..." 與 "that run..." 的簡化。
  • 「介系詞 + 關係代名詞」引導的形容詞子句
    ...and the chips running the artificial intelligence (AI) models on which its economy increasingly leans, are no longer background infrastructure.
    片語 "on which" 由介系詞 "on" 加上關係代名詞 "which" 組成，用來修飾前面的先行詞 "artificial intelligence (AI) models"。因為片語 "lean on"（依賴）需要介系詞 "on"，故將其移至關係代名詞前方。
  • 獨立分詞構句（Independent Participial Construction）
    The EU has gone further, treating sovereign AI infrastructure almost as doctrine, which has meant backing Gaia-X...
    句尾的 ", treating..." 是現在分詞構句，其邏輯主詞為主句的主語 "The EU"，表示伴隨發生的動作或結果，用來補充說明歐盟採取進一步行動的具體作法。
  • 否定詞置於句首的倒裝句（或部分倒裝概念）
    None of this requires a grand global treaty.
    雖然此句並非標準的倒裝句，但 "None of this"（此事的任何部分都不……）以否定代名詞開頭，展現了英文中表達全面否定的經典句型，常出現在多益考試的文法與克漏字測驗中。
