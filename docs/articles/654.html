台灣漫畫家在美國博覽會展出作品

Taiwanese comic artists showcase works at U.S. expo

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • showcase v. 展示，表現
    Five Taiwanese comic artists showcased their works at the 2026 Small Press Expo (SPX) over the weekend at the Bethesda North Marriott Hotel & Conference Center in Maryland.
    五位台灣漫畫家於週末在馬里蘭州貝hesda北萬豪飯店暨會議中心舉行的 2026 年小型獨立出版品博覽會（SPX）上展出了他們的作品。
  • interact v. 互動
    The artists -- A ee mi, Rio (里約), Ding Pao-yen (丁柏晏), Monday Recover (星期一回收日) and Crystal Kung (水晶孔) -- held signing events and interacted with fans and fellow artists during the two-day expo.
    這五位藝術家——A ee mi、里約（Rio）、丁柏晏、星期一回收日和水晶孔——在為期兩天的博覽會期間舉辦了簽書會，並與粉絲及同業藝術家互動。
  • separate adj. 單獨的，各自的，不同的
    A ee mi said she originally created her work in both Mandarin and English, so she did not need to prepare a separate translation for the expo.
    A ee mi 表示，她的作品原本就是以中文和英文兩種語言創作，因此她不需要為博覽會準備另外的翻譯。
  • relate to phr. 對...產生共鳴，理解並同情
    She said many U.S. readers told her they could relate to her work.
    她說，許多美國讀者告訴她，他們能夠對她的作品感同身受。
  • publish v. 出版
    Monday Recover spoke about her work, titled "Sea You There and Us" (貓與海的彼端), which was her first book to be published in the United States.
    星期一回收日談到了她的作品《貓與海的彼端》（Sea You There and Us），這是她在美國出版的第一本書。
  • reflect v. 反映，顯示
    Crystal Kung, who works at an animation studio in the U.S., said her works reflect what she wants to express and honestly convey her own experiences and feelings.
    在美國一家動畫工作室工作的水晶孔表示，她的作品反映了她想要表達的內容，並誠實地傳達了她自己的經歷和感受。
  • convey v. 傳達，表達
    Crystal Kung, who works at an animation studio in the U.S., said her works reflect what she wants to express and honestly convey her own experiences and feelings.
    在美國一家動畫工作室工作的水晶孔表示，她的作品反映了她想要表達的內容，並誠實地傳達了她自己的經歷和感受。
  • depict v. 描繪，描寫
    In her new book, "American Overtime" (我很多的美國時間), published in Taiwan in August 2025, Crystal Kung depicts her everyday struggles after moving to the U.S. at the age of 22 to study and spending nearly a decade working there.
    水晶孔於 2025 年 8 月在台灣出版的新書《我很多的美國時間》（American Overtime）中，描繪了她 22 歲赴美求學並在那裡工作近十年後，每天面臨的掙扎。
  • executive director n. 執行董事，執行長
    SPX Executive Director Warren Bernard said he was glad to have the Taiwanese artists at the expo, noting that they had brought works he greatly admired and wanted to add to his own collection.
    SPX 執行董事華倫·伯納德（Warren Bernard) 表示，很高興台灣藝術家能參加這次博覽會，並指出他們帶來的作品令他非常讚賞，且想將其加入自己的收藏中。
  • promote v. 推廣，促進
    Established in 1994, SPX promotes artists and publishers who produce independent comics, according to its official website.
    根據其官方網站指出，SPX 成立於 1994 年，旨在推廣製作獨立漫畫的藝術家與出版商。
  • annual adj. 一年一度的，年度的
    The annual comics and graphic arts festival showcases established independent comic creators while providing exposure and networking opportunities for attending artists and those just starting out, the website said.
    網站表示，這個一年一度的漫畫與繪本藝術節展出了知名的獨立漫畫創作者，同時為參與的藝術家以及剛起步的新人提供曝光和拓展人脈的機會。
  • networking n. 建立人脈，業務交流
    The annual comics and graphic arts festival showcases established independent comic creators while providing exposure and networking opportunities for attending artists and those just starting out, the website said.
    網站表示，這個一年一度的漫畫與繪本藝術節展出了知名的獨立漫畫創作者，同時為參與的藝術家以及剛起步的新人提供曝光和拓展人脈的機會。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Monday Recover spoke about her work, titled "Sea You There and Us" (貓與海的彼端), which was her first book to be published in the United States.
    句中的「titled "Sea You There and Us"」是由過去分詞開頭的片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「her work」。這相當於「which was titled...」的簡化寫法，省略了格代名詞與be動詞，使句子更為精鍊。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Crystal Kung, who works at an animation studio in the U.S., said her works reflect what she wants to express and honestly convey her own experiences and feelings.
  • 現在分詞作附帶狀況/補充說明 (Present Participle indicating a circumstance)
    SPX Executive Director Warren Bernard said he was glad to have the Taiwanese artists at the expo, noting that they had brought works he greatly admired and wanted to add to his own collection.
