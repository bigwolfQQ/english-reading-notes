台股收盤創歷史新高但未能站上 5,000 點

Taiwan shares close at record high but fall short of 50,000 points

單字解析

  • benchmark index n. 基準指數
    Shares in Taiwan closed at a fresh high Tuesday, but gains were capped as the benchmark index remained below the 50,000-point mark...
    台股週二收盤再創歷史新高，但由於基準指數仍低於 5,000 點大關，漲幅因而受限...
  • cautious adj. 謹慎的
    ...with investors turning cautious as it approached the psychologically important level, dealers said.
    交易員表示，隨著指數逼近這個具心理重要性的關卡，投資人轉趨謹慎。
  • turnover n. 營業額、成交量
    Turnover totaled NT$975.94 billion (US$30.70 billion).
    成交金額達新台幣 9,759.4 億元（約合 307 億美元）。
  • lock in v. phr. 鎖定（獲利等）
    "As the 50,000-point mark neared, some investors sold shares to lock in gains," Concord Securities analyst Kerry Huang said.
    協和證券分析師黃先生表示：「隨著 5,000 點大關逼近，部分投資人賣出股票以鎖定獲利。」
  • resilient adj. 具彈性的、有韌性的（常用於形容市場或股價抗跌）
    "Fortunately, TSMC (Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co.) stayed resilient and still posted gains after retreating from an intraday high..."
    「所幸，台積電（TSMC）展現韌性，在從盤中高點回落後仍守住漲幅...」
  • guidance n. （企業對未來營收或獲利的）預測、指引
    "Interest in TSMC showed that investors expect the chipmaker to provide upbeat guidance on its business outlook and overseas expansion..."
    「市場對台積電的興趣顯示，投資人預期這家晶片製造商將...提供樂觀的業務前景與海外擴張指引。」
  • large-cap adj. 大型資本額的、大型股的
    "Profit-taking largely focused on large-cap tech stocks, so investors rotated into select old-economy industries..."
    「獲利了結的操作主要集中在大型科技股，因此投資人轉而投入特定傳統產業...」
  • bargain hunter n. phr. 尋找便宜貨的買家、低接買盤
    Textile stocks attracted bargain hunters due to their relatively low valuations...
    紡織股因其相對較低的估值吸引了低接買盤...
  • valuation n. 估值、評價
    Textile stocks attracted bargain hunters due to their relatively low valuations...
    紡織股因其相對較低的估值吸引了低接買盤...
  • feel the pinch v. phr. 感受到財務壓力、感受到困難
    "I am afraid that if yields go up further, tech firms will feel the pinch from higher fundraising costs..."
    「我擔心如果殖利率進一步走高，科技公司將感受到融資成本上升帶來的壓力...」
  • institutional investor n. phr. 機構投資人（如外資、投信等）
    According to the Taiwan Stock Exchange, foreign institutional investors sold a net NT$6.66 billion worth of shares on the main board Tuesday.
    根據台灣證券交易所的數據，外國機構投資人週二在集中市場淨賣超新台幣 66.6 億元的股票。

文法重點

  • 獨立分詞構句 (Absolute Nominative Construction)
    Shares in Taiwan closed at a fresh high Tuesday, but gains were capped as the benchmark index remained below the 50,000-point mark, with investors turning cautious as it approached the psychologically important level, dealers said.
    「with + 名詞 + 分詞」結構用來表示附帶狀況或背景原因。在此句中，「with investors turning cautious」用來補充說明當時投資人轉趨謹慎的市場氛圍。
  • 非限定關代子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    The stock, which accounts for over 40 percent of the market's total value, contributed about 80 points to the Taiex's rise.
    「which accounts for...」前方有逗號，屬於非限定用法，用來補充說明前面先行詞「The stock（指台積電）」的附加資訊（佔總市值超過 40%），拿掉後不影響主要句意。
  • 過去分詞作後置定語 (Past Participle as Post-modifier)
    ...at an investor conference scheduled for Oct. 15 amid the ongoing AI boom, Huang said.
    「scheduled for Oct. 15」是由過去分詞片語構成的後置定語，用來修飾前面的名詞「an investor conference（法人說明會）」，省略了原本的「which is / that is」。
  • 假設語氣/條件句 (Conditional Clause)
    "I am afraid that if yields go up further, tech firms will feel the pinch from higher fundraising costs, which could affect their operations..."
    此句使用標準的第一類條件句（First Conditional），結構為「If + 現在式動詞, 主詞 +未來式助動詞(will)」，用來表達未來可能發生的真實假設情況及其結果。
