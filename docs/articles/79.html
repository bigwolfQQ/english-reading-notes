台灣正密切監控重疊專屬經濟區內的中國海警船

Taiwan closely monitoring Chinese coast guard vessels in overlapping EEZs

單字解析

  • monitor v. 監控，監測
    Taiwan's Coast Guard Administration (CGA) said Thursday it was closely monitoring two China Coast Guard (CCG) vessels...
    台灣的海巡署（CGA）週四表示，正密切監控兩艘中國海警船...
  • operate v. 運作，航行，操作
    ...two China Coast Guard (CCG) vessels operating off Taiwan's east coast...
    ...兩艘在台灣東部外海活動的中國海警船...
  • dispatch v. 派遣，發送
    The CGA said in a statement that it had dispatched the patrol vessel Chi An to shadow the CCG vessels...
    海巡署在聲明中表示，已派遣巡防艦吉安艦去跟監中國海警船...
  • statement n. 聲明，陳述
    The CGA said in a statement that it had dispatched the patrol vessel Chi An...
    海巡署在聲明中表示，已派遣巡防艦吉安艦...
  • announce v. 宣佈，公佈
    ...since Tokyo and Manila announced in May that they would begin talks...
    ...自東京與馬尼拉於5月宣佈將展開談話以來...
  • talks n. 談判，會談
    ...that they would begin talks over an area where the exclusive economic zones...
    ...將就專屬經濟區重疊的區域展開談話...
  • urge v. 敦促，力勸
    Taiwan's Ministry of Foreign Affairs has urged Tokyo and Manila to include Taipei in any future EEZ talks.
    台灣的外交部已敦促東京與馬尼拉將台北納入任何未來的EEZ談話中。
  • include v. 包含，將...納入
    ...to include Taipei in any future EEZ talks.
    ...將台北納入任何未來的EEZ談話中。
  • reject v. 拒絕，駁斥
    The CGA also rejected the CCG's claim that the two Chinese vessels were conducting "routine law-enforcement patrols"...
    海巡署也駁斥了中國海警聲稱這兩艘中國船隻是在執行「例行執法巡邏」的說法。
  • claim n. / v. 主張，聲稱
    The CGA also rejected the CCG's claim that the two Chinese vessels...
    海巡署也駁斥了中國海警聲稱...
  • conduct v. 執行，舉辦，進行
    ...that the two Chinese vessels were conducting "routine law-enforcement patrols"...
    ...這兩艘中國船隻當時正在執行「例行執法巡邏」...
  • routine adj. 例行的，日常的
    ...conducting "routine law-enforcement patrols" in waters...
    ...在水域執行「例行執法巡邏」...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Phrase as Postmodifier）作後置修飾語
    ...two China Coast Guard (CCG) vessels operating off Taiwan's east coast in an area where the exclusive economic zones of Taiwan, Japan and the Philippines overlap.
    句中的 "operating off Taiwan's east coast" 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "vessels"（船隻），相當於形容詞子句 "which were operating..." 的簡化用法，在新聞英文中非常常見，能讓句子更簡潔。
  • 關係副詞 where 引導的形容詞子句
    ...in an area where the exclusive economic zones of Taiwan, Japan and the Philippines overlap.
    句中的 "where" 是關係副詞，用來引導形容詞子句，修飾先行詞 "an area"（一個區域），並在子句中擔任地方副詞的功能（相當於 in which）。
  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense)
    The CGA said in a statement that it had dispatched the patrol vessel Chi An to shadow the CCG vessels...
    句中主動詞 "said" 是過去式，而海巡署「派遣」巡防艦的動作發生在「說」這個動作之前，因此使用過去完成式 "had dispatched" 來表示「過去中的過去」。
