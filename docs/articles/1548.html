文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附帶狀況/結果狀語
    The Premier League season has been thrust into turmoil while clubs await the appeal verdict, with City sitting top of the table after six successive wins, including a 3-0 rout of Liverpool on Sunday.
    句尾使用「with + 名詞 + 現在分詞 (sitting)」的結構，用來表達伴隨發生的狀況或背景，意指「在……的情況下 / 隨著……」。這在商業與新聞英語中非常常見，用以補充說明主句發生時的附帶情境。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    No sanction has yet been meted out, but a severe points deduction or even expulsion from the Premier League are among the possible punishments.
    句首使用現在完成式的被動語態「has yet been meted out」（尚未被施予/祭出），強調動作的承受者（sanction 制裁）而非執行者。新聞英語常透過被動語態來客觀報導官方行動或裁決。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    Further UEFA action against City is unlikely as the club successfully fended off European soccer’s governing body when they attempted to act on the same leak of documents in 2018 that led to the Premier League’s case.
    句尾的「that led to the Premier League’s case」是由關係代名詞 that 引導的限定修飾子句，用來修飾先行詞 documents（文件）。這類結構能幫助讀者在複雜的句子中釐清修飾關係。
