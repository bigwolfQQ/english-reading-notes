金磚國家呼籲「最大限度的克制」

BRICS nations call for ‘maximum restraint’

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • statement n. 聲明，陳述
    Members of the 11-nation BRICS group including China, Russia, Iran and India yesterday called for calm in the Middle East, in a joint statement issued at a summit in New Delhi.
    包括中國、俄羅斯、伊朗和印度在內的 11 國金磚集團成員昨天在紐德里舉行的峰會上發表聯合聲明，呼籲中東地區保持冷靜。
  • escalation n. 升級，惡化
    We express deep concern over the continued escalation of tensions in Middle East/West Asia and, recalling our respective national positions, call for exercising maximum restraint...
    我們對中東／西亞局勢的持續升級表達深切關切，並重申各自的國家立場，呼籲各方展現最大限度的克制……
  • restraint n. 克制，自制
    ...call for exercising maximum restraint, as well as avoiding actions that could further aggravate the situation...
    ……呼籲各方展現最大限度的克制，同時避免採取可能進一步惡化情勢的行動……
  • aggravate v. 加重，惡化
    ...call for exercising maximum restraint, as well as avoiding actions that could further aggravate the situation...
    ……呼籲各方展現最大限度的克制，同時避免採取可能進一步惡化情勢的行動……
  • agree on phr. v. 對……達成一致意見，取得共識
    Foreign ministers of the BRICS grouping failed to agree on a joint statement when they met in New Delhi in May...
    金磚集團的外長們 5 月在紐德里會晤時，未能就聯合聲明達成共識……
  • diplomatic adj. 外交的
    However, a statement was issued at the leaders’ meeting yesterday after intense diplomatic efforts by host India.
    然而，在主辦國印度積極的外交努力後，領導人會議於昨天發表了一份聲明。
  • condemn v. 譴責
    The statement also condemned attacks on civilian infrastructure and nuclear facilities.
    該聲明還譴責了針對平民基礎設施和核設施的攻擊。
  • infrastructure n. 基礎設施
    The statement also condemned attacks on civilian infrastructure and nuclear facilities.
    該聲明還譴責了針對平民基礎設施和核設施的攻擊。
  • in violation of phr. 違反
    “We express serious concern over deliberate attacks on civilian infrastructure and peaceful nuclear facilities... in violation of international law and relevant resolutions of the IAEA,” it said.
    聲明指出：「我們對針對平民基礎設施以及受國際原子能總署全面保障監督之和平核設施的蓄意攻擊表達嚴重關切……此舉違反了國際法及國際原子能總署的相關決議。」
  • influence n. 影響力
    BRICS was created in 2009 as a forum for major emerging economies seeking greater influence in institutions dominated by Western powers.
    金磚集團成立於 2009 年，作為一個讓尋求在由西方大國主導的機構中獲得更大影響力的主要新興經濟體論壇。
  • common interests n. 共同利益
    ...said that the Middle East war “does not serve the common interests of the international community,” Chinese state news agency Xinhua reported.
    ……中國官媒新華社報導，（習近平）表示中東戰爭「不符合國際社會的共同利益」。
  • supply chains n. 供應鏈
    “We stress the need to work together to maintain smooth flow of global trade, supply chains and energy, in accordance with applicable international law,” it added.
    聲明補充說：「我們強調需要共同努力，根據適用的國際法，維護全球貿易、供應鏈和能源的順暢流動。」

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾語 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Members of the 11-nation BRICS group including China, Russia, Iran and India yesterday called for calm in the Middle East, in a joint statement issued at a summit in New Delhi.
  • 現在分詞片語表示附帶狀況或結果 (Present Participle Phrase indicating accompanying circumstance)
    “We express deep concern over the continued escalation of tensions in Middle East/West Asia and, recalling our respective national positions, call for exercising maximum restraint...”
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    However, a statement was issued at the leaders’ meeting yesterday after intense diplomatic efforts by host India.
  • 現在分詞片語修飾名詞 (Present Participle as Pre/Postmodifier)
    BRICS was created in 2009 as a forum for major emerging economies seeking greater influence in institutions dominated by Western powers.
