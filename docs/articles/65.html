梅花鹿深夜至高雄散步後安全返回

Sika deer safe after escaping to take nighttime stroll in Kaohsiung

單字解析

  • capture v. 捕獲、逮捕
    ...before being safely captured and returned to its owner, police said...
    警方表示，在被安全捕獲並送回飼主身邊之前...
  • contact v. 聯繫、聯絡
    ...and contacted the city's Animal Protection Office for assistance.
    ...並聯繫市府動物保護處尋求協助。
  • assistance n. 協助、幫忙
    ...and contacted the city's Animal Protection Office for assistance.
    ...並聯繫市府動物保護處尋求協助。
  • endanger v. 危及、使遭受危險
    ...endanger itself and drivers, police expanded their search to nearby roads.
    ...危及自身及駕駛的安全，警方將搜尋範圍擴大至附近的道路。
  • expand v. 擴大、擴展
    ...police expanded their search to nearby roads.
    警方將搜尋範圍擴大至附近的道路。
  • subsequently adv. 隨後、接著
    The deer was subsequently spotted near the intersection of Ersheng Road and Kaixuan Road...
    這隻鹿隨後在二聖路與凱旋路交叉口附近被發現...
  • report n. 報導、通報、報告
    At around 8:20 a.m., the precinct received another report that the deer was on a patch of grass near the same intersection.
    大約上午8點20分，該分局接獲另一起通報，指該隻鹿在同一交叉口附近的一片草地上。
  • fine n. 罰款、罰金
    ...the owner could face a fine of NT$300 (US$9.50) to NT$600 under Taiwan's Road Traffic Management and Penalty Act...
    ...飼主可能依台灣的「道路交通管理處罰條例」面臨新台幣300元（約9.50美元）至600元的罰款...
  • supervision n. 監督、管理、看管
    ...for allowing the deer to escape supervision and wander on a road, police said.
    警方表示，是因為讓梅花鹿脫離看管並在道路上流浪。
  • provision n. 條款、規定
    The owner could also face up to three days in detention or a fine of up to NT$12,000 under provisions of the Social Order Maintenance Act, police said.
    警方表示，根據「社會秩序維護法」的規定，飼主也可能面臨最高三天的拘留或最高新台幣12,000元的罰款。
  • notify v. 通知、告知
    The precinct said the owner would be later notified to report to the police station for questioning...
    分局表示，飼主稍後將接到通知至派出所接受訊問...
  • investigate v. 調查
    ...as police investigate why the deer escaped and the circumstances surrounding the incident.
    ...因為警方要調查梅花鹿為何逃脫以及事件發生的相關情況。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The owner, who was not identified, said in a post in response to the Threads message that the deer...
    句中的 "who was not identified" 是形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的名詞 The owner。在英文中，此類關係代名詞加上be動詞經常可以省略，簡化為過去分詞片語來修飾主詞，使語氣更為精煉。
  • 現在分詞表達附帶狀況或結果 (Present Participle Expressing Concomitant Circumstance)
    ...noting that it was "lucky" that no traffic accidents occurred.
    句尾的 "noting that..." 是現在分詞片語，在此處作為分詞構句，表示主句動作發生時的伴隨狀況或順帶補充說明（警察在說話的同時，也指出了這點）。
  • 被動語態的使用 (Passive Voice)
    A sika deer was spotted wandering the streets of Cianjhen District...
    新聞報導中經常使用被動語態（be + 過去分詞，如 was spotted、was captured），用來強調動作的承受者（梅花鹿），或者在不知道或不需要強調動作執行者時保持客觀。
  • 形容詞子句（關係代名詞 who 引導） (Adjective Clause)
    Police rushed to the scene and kept traffic under control to avoid frightening the deer into darting onto a road, while working with the owner and animal protection officials to guide and capture the animal, the precinct said.
