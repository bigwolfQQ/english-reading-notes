美國的伊朗戰爭遺忘了核武問題

The US’ Iran war forgot about nukes

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • arsenal n. 軍火庫，武器庫
    When was the last time you heard a meaningful proposal to prevent Iran from developing a nuclear arsenal?
    你上次聽到防止Iran發展核武庫的有意義提案是什麼時候？
  • Memorandum of Understanding n. 諒解備忘錄（MOU）
    There has been nothing on the subject since the June Memorandum of Understanding between the US and Iran...
    自美伊 6 月簽署諒解備忘錄以來，該議題一直毫無進展……
  • expire v. 期滿，過期
    ...the promised 60 days of talks to settle questions around Tehran’s uranium-enrichment program never began and have long since expired.
    ……承諾進行 60 天以解決德黑蘭鈾濃縮計畫相關問題的會談從未開始，且早已過期。
  • strategy n. 策略，戰略
    That was always a Hail Mary pass, and from the moment it became clear that the Islamic republic would survive, there has been no nuclear strategy whatsoever.
    那一直是一次希望渺茫的豪賭，而從伊斯蘭共和國將存活下來的現實變得清晰的那一刻起，就再也沒有任何核武戰略可言了。
  • negotiation n. 談判，協商
    That was back when US-Iran nuclear negotiations in Geneva got cut short by the surprise airstrike...
    那是在美伊日內瓦核談判被突襲打斷之前……
  • administration n. （政府的）行政部門，管理
    “An agreement may await the next administration in Iran,” US Secretary of Energy Chris Wright told ABC’s This Week on Sunday.
    美國能源部長克里斯·賴特週日在美國廣播公司（ABC）的《本週》節目中表示：「協議可能會等待伊朗下一屆政府。」
  • sanction n. 制裁
    I would argue that this failing was big enough to have justified demanding a renegotiation and extension of the deal’s restrictions once it was due to expire, with a return to sanctions and other forms of pressure as necessary.
    我認為，這一缺陷大到足以證明，一旦該協議到期，就必須要求重新談判併延長其限制，並在必要時恢復制裁和其他形式的壓力。
  • renege v. 背信，違約，反悔
    In fact, the prospect of a nuclear Iran was bad enough that it might also have warranted US President Donald Trump’s premature decision to renege on the deal in 2018...
    事實上，伊朗擁有核武的前景非常糟糕，以至於它也可能為美國總統唐納·川普在 2018 年過早決定背信棄義地撕毀協議提供正當理由……
  • evidence n. 證據，證物
    ...if credible evidence had emerged that the Iranians were weaponizing their fuel stocks.
    ……如果出現了可信的證據表明伊朗人正在將其燃料儲備武器化。
  • disruption n. 中斷，擾亂
    Through their disruption of Hormuz, Iran’s leaders now have international leverage and a deterrent power that they never before possessed.
    透過對荷姆茲海峽的破壞，伊朗領導人現在擁有了他們以前從未擁有過的國際籌碼和威懾力量。
  • leverage n. 影響力，籌碼
    Through their disruption of Hormuz, Iran’s leaders now have international leverage and a deterrent power that they never before possessed.
    透過對荷姆茲海峽的破壞，伊朗領導人現在擁有了他們以前從未擁有過的國際籌碼和威懾力量。
  • executive adj. 行政的，高階主管的
    ...Major General Tamir Hayman, a former Israeli military intelligence chief, said in a podcast on Wednesday last week for Tel Aviv’s Institute for National Security Studies, where he is now executive director.
    ……前以色列軍事情報局長、現任特拉維夫國家安全研究所執行董事塔米爾·海曼少將上週三在該研究所的一場播客中表示。
  • agenda n. 議程，待辦事項
    He needs to put the issue back at the heart of his Iran agenda.
    他需要把這個議題重新放回其伊朗議程的核心位置。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作補充說明 (Participial Phrase)
    That was back when US-Iran nuclear negotiations in Geneva got cut short by the surprise airstrike that killed then-Iranian supreme leader Ali Khamenei and set the conflict in motion, with all the unintended — if widely predicted — consequences that have followed.
    句尾的 "with all the unintended... consequences that have followed" 形成一個「with + 名詞 + 分詞/形容詞」的複合結構，用來補充說明前面動作伴隨產生的狀況或結果。
  • 假設語氣過去完成式 (Past Subjunctive)
    ...if credible evidence had emerged that the Iranians were weaponizing their fuel stocks.
    此句使用 "if + 過去完成式 (had emerged)" 來表達對過去事實相反的假設（意即當時實際上並未出現這樣的證據），常用於條件句與與過去事實相反的假設語氣中。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    The JCPOA was flawed in that it sunset those limitations over a 10 to 15-year horizon.
    句中的 "that it sunset those limitations over a 10 to 15-year horizon" 是形容詞子句（由關係代名詞引導，或在此處作為介系詞 in 的受詞同位補充），用來修飾說明前面的 flawed（有缺陷的）在哪個方面。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Wherever one chooses to stick that pin, it is undeniable that the existential nuclear question that this war was supposedly launched to address has been forgotten...
    句中的 "has been forgotten" 使用現在完成式的被動語態（have/has been + 過去分詞），強調「核問題」這個主詞承受了「被遺忘」的動作，焦點在於狀態的完成與對現在的影響。
