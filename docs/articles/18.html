高雄趴趴走梅花鹿已獲捕

Deer on loose in Kaohsiung caught

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • on the loose phr. 逃跑中的、趴趴走的（指動物或罪犯趴趴走、未受控制）
    Deer on loose in Kaohsiung caught
  • sika deer n. 梅花鹿
    A sika deer was spotted wandering the streets of Kaohsiung’s Cianjhen District (前鎮) early yesterday morning before being safely captured and returned to its owner, police said, adding that it was “lucky” there were no traffic incidents.
  • sneak out phr. v. 偷偷溜出去
    The owner, surnamed Kuo (郭), said in a reply to the Threads message that the deer “sneaked out of the house to play on the slide at a nearby park, but got lost on the way back.”
  • dispatch v. 派遣、調派（人員或車輛等）
    Following the posts, the Cianjhen Precinct of the Kaohsiung City Police Department dispatched officers to the scene and contacted the Kaohsiung Animal Protection Office for assistance.
  • precinct n. 警察局分局、轄區
    However, the deer had fled by the time officers arrived, the precinct said.
  • elude v. 躲避、逃避（抓捕或追查）
    The deer was spotted near the intersection of Ersheng Road and Kaisyuan Road, but it again eluded police.
  • patch n. 一小塊（土地、草地等）
    At about 8:20am, the precinct received another report that the deer was on a patch of grass near the Ersheng-Kaisyuan intersection.
  • dart v. 飛奔、急衝
    Police rushed to the scene and kept traffic under control to avoid frightening the deer into darting onto a road, while working with the owner and animal protection officials to guide and capture the animal, the precinct said.
  • detention n. 拘留、關押
    The owner could also face up to three days in detention or a fine of up to NT$12,000 under provisions of the Social Order Maintenance Act (社會秩序維護法), police said.
  • provision n. （法律或法令的）條款、規定
    The owner could also face up to three days in detention or a fine of up to NT$12,000 under provisions of the Social Order Maintenance Act (社會秩序維護法), police said.

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表補充說明）
    A sika deer was spotted wandering the streets of Kaohsiung’s Cianjhen District (前鎮) early yesterday morning before being safely captured and returned to its owner, police said, adding that it was “lucky” there were no traffic incidents.
    句尾的 `adding that...` 為現在分詞構句，簡化自「and police added that...」，用來補充說明主句（警方發表聲明）時所附帶補充的內容。
  • 過去分詞構句（表原因）
    Concerned that the animal might run into traffic, endangering itself and drivers, police expanded their search to nearby roads.
    句首的 `Concerned that...` 是過去分詞構句，原句為「Because police were concerned that...」，省略連詞及主詞 police，保留過去分詞形容詞狀態（be concerned），用來簡潔說明警方擴大搜尋範圍的原因。
  • By the time 引導的時間副詞子句搭配過去完成貌
    However, the deer had fled by the time officers arrived, the precinct said.
    `by the time` 作連詞表示「到……的時候為止」，引導過去簡單式子句（officers arrived），主要子句則搭配過去完成式（had fled），強調在員警趕到這個過去的時間點之前，梅花鹿「已經」完成了逃跑這個動作。
  • frighten + 受詞 + into + V-ing（使……驚嚇而做出某事）
    Police rushed to the scene and kept traffic under control to avoid frightening the deer into darting onto a road, while working with the owner and animal protection officials to guide and capture the animal, the precinct said.
    動詞介詞片語結構「frighten A into V-ing」意為「驚嚇 A 致使其做某事」。在此句中結合 `avoid`（避免）後接動名詞 `frightening`，表示「避免驚嚇到梅花鹿，導致其急衝向道路」。
