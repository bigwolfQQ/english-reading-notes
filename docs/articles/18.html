高雄脫逃梅花鹿落網

Deer on loose in Kaohsiung caught

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • capture v. 捕獲，逮捕
    A sika deer was spotted wandering the streets of Kaohsiung’s Cianjhen District early yesterday morning before being safely captured and returned to its owner, police said...
    警方表示，昨晨高雄市前鎮區街頭清晨出現一隻梅花鹿漫步遊蕩，隨後已被安全捕獲並交還給飼主...
  • contact v. 聯繫，聯絡
    The owner urged anybody who spotted the deer to contact them immediately.
    飼主呼籲任何發現該鹿蹤跡的人立即與他們聯繫。
  • dispatch v. 派遣，派出
    Following the posts, the Cianjhen Precinct of the Kaohsiung City Police Department dispatched officers to the scene and contacted the Kaohsiung Animal Protection Office for assistance.
    貼文發布後，高雄市警察局前鎮分局派遣員警前往現場，並聯繫高雄市動物保護處尋求協助。
  • assistance n. 協助，幫助
    Following the posts, the Cianjhen Precinct of the Kaohsiung City Police Department dispatched officers to the scene and contacted the Kaohsiung Animal Protection Office for assistance.
    貼文發布後，高雄市警察局前鎮分局派遣員警前往現場，並聯繫高雄市動物保護處尋求協助。
  • endanger v. 危及，使處於險境
    Concerned that the animal might run into traffic, endangering itself and drivers, police expanded their search to nearby roads.
    由於擔心動物可能會衝入車陣，危及自身及駕駛安全，警方將搜索範圍擴大到附近的道路。
  • prevent v. 預防，防止
    Police said they searched the area while monitoring traffic to prevent possible accidents.
    警方表示，他們在搜索該地區的同時監控交通，以防止可能發生的事故。
  • fine n. 罰金，罰款
    The owner could face a fine of NT$300 to NT$600 under the Road Traffic Management and Penalty Act for allowing the deer to escape supervision and wander on a road, police said.
    警方表示，飼主因疏於管束讓梅花鹿逃脫並在道路上遊蕩，可能依《道路交通管理處罰條例》面臨新台幣 300 元至 600 元的罰鍰。
  • investigate v. 調查
    The precinct said the owner would be notified to report to the police station for questioning as police investigate why the deer escaped and the circumstances surrounding the incident.
    分局表示，警方將通知飼主到派出所接受偵訊，以調查梅花鹿為何逃脫以及事件發生的來龍去脈。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附加說明（補充結果）
    Concerned that the animal might run into traffic, endangering itself and drivers, police expanded their search to nearby roads.
    句中的「endangering itself and drivers」是由現在分詞（endangering）帶頭的片語，用來補充說明前面主句動作所產生的直接結果（即搜索擴大的同時，也伴隨著避免危害的情況）。
  • 被動語態
    A sika deer was spotted wandering the streets of Kaohsiung’s Cianjhen District... before being safely captured and returned to its owner...
    句中的「was spotted」與「(was) captured and returned」皆為被動語態（be + 過去分詞），強調動作的承受者（梅花鹿），而省略了動作的執行者（因為在此處不重要或顯而易見）。
  • By the time 引導的時間副詞子句
    However, the deer had fled by the time officers arrived, the precinct said.
    「by the time」意指「到……的時候」，引導一個時間副詞子句。主句使用過去完成式（had fled），表示在從句動作（officers arrived）發生之前，該動作就已經完成。
