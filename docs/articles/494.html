社論：台灣不會輕易放棄

EDITORIAL: Taiwan would not give up easily

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • alternative n. 替代方案
    As the title suggests, Shuster views a deal between Taiwan and China as an increasingly inevitable alternative to a costly war
    正如標題所示，舒斯特將台灣與中國之間的協議視為一場代價高昂的戰爭日益不可避免的替代方案
  • preservation n. 維護、保存
    her party’s ties with the Chinese Communist Party (CCP) and her optimism that Taiwan could strike a deal with China while preserving its freedom.
    以及她對台灣在維護自身自由的同時，能夠與中國達成協議的樂觀態度。
  • conflate v. 混淆、合併
    However, one major flaw with Shuster’s argument is that it risks conflating the KMT’s abandonment of its once staunchly anti-communist position... with the views of the Taiwanese population as a whole.
    然而，舒斯特論點的一個主要缺陷在於，它冒險將國民黨放棄其曾經堅定的反共立場...與整體台灣人口的觀點混為一談。
  • exclusively adv. 專門、排他地（源自動詞 exclude 或形容詞 exclusive）
    These figures do not paint the picture of a nation preparing to quietly surrender, but one that understands the risks of war...
    （註：文章中使用 exclusive 的名詞型態或相關詞，此處對應多益高頻字）
  • negotiation n. 談判、協商
    A June survey by the Taoyuan National Development Education Foundation found that support for cross-strait political negotiations in Taiwan has fallen...
    桃園國家發展教育基金會 6 月的一項調查發現，台灣民眾對兩岸政治談判的支持率已下降...
  • assistance n. 協助、援助
    An Academia Sinica survey found that nearly 60 percent of Taiwanese would be willing to resist a Chinese invasion “at all costs” regardless of US assistance.
    中央研究院的一項調查發現，近 60% 的台灣人表示，無論美國是否提供協助，他們都願意「不惜一切代價」抵抗中國入侵。
  • confidence n. 信心、信任
    Diminishing confidence in the US and distrust of China are not mutually exclusive.
    對美國信心的減弱和對中國的不信任並不是互斥的。
  • interference n. 干涉、干預
    It means maintaining democracy, freedom of speech and the press, the ability to trade freely with the rest of the world and the right to go about life without interference from Beijing.
    這意味著維持民主、言論和新聞自由、與世界其他地區自由貿易的能力，以及過著不受北京干涉的生活的權利。
  • assumption n. 假設、臆測（源自動詞 assume）
    They see what has happened to democratic institutions and political freedoms in Hong Kong, and there is little reason to assume Taiwan would receive a fundamentally different bargain.
    （註：文章動詞形 assume）They see what has happened to democratic institutions and political freedoms in Hong Kong, and there is little reason to assume Taiwan would...（此處符合多益商業常考假設、推測語境）
  • procurement n. 採購、取得
    The nation’s military preparedness, defense procurement and societal resilience require serious attention...
    國家的軍事備戰、國防採購和社會韌性需要受到嚴格關注...
  • alternative n. 替代選擇、兩者擇一
    It would be foolish to ignore these issues; but it would be equally foolish to conclude that the only alternatives are an unwinnable war or submission to Beijing.
    忽視這些問題是愚蠢的；但同樣愚蠢的是，得出唯一的選擇是打不贏的戰爭或向北京屈服的結論。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    In an article published on Tuesday in The Atlantic, titled “Will Taiwan Just Give Up?”...
    句中的「published on Tuesday...」與「titled “Will Taiwan Just Give Up?”」皆為分詞片語，用來修飾前方的名詞「article」，省略了「which was」，使句子更為精煉。
  • 假設語氣與過去分詞表達過去未發生或不可能的情境 (Subjunctive / Conditional Context)
    Gone are the days that Taiwan could assume that the US would automatically come to its defense.
    此句使用了地方副詞置於句首的「完全倒裝句」（Gone are the days...），強調「那樣的日子已經一去不復返了」，加強新聞評論的強烈語氣。
  • regardless of 引導的讓步狀語片語 (Prepositional Phrase of Concession)
    An Academia Sinica survey found that nearly 60 percent of Taiwanese would be willing to resist a Chinese invasion “at all costs” regardless of US assistance.
    「regardless of」意為「不管、不顧」，在此引導名詞片語 US assistance，用來表達條件或情況的讓步，是商業英文與新聞英語中表達「不受...影響」的高頻實用片語。
  • not only / mutually exclusive 等對比句構與否定前置
    Diminishing confidence in the US and distrust of China are not mutually exclusive.
    透過「mutually exclusive」（互斥的）這個形容詞搭配否定動詞，精準表達兩種心理狀態可以同時存在，展現學術與評論文章中嚴謹的邏輯辯證語法。
