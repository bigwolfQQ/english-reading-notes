文法重點

  • be to do (be動詞 + to V) 表示預定的官方計畫或安排
    The New Taipei City Government’s Department of Environmental Protection is to host the New Taipei City International Youth Climate Action Forum...
  • 現在分詞片語 (Present Participle Phrase) 作後位修飾
    The event has 100 places available to the public and is open to anyone interested in sustainability.
  • 現在分詞短語作附帶狀況/結果的補述 (Held under the theme...)
    Held under the theme “From Ideas to Impact,” the forum aims to encourage young people to turn climate ideas into concrete action...
  • 現在完成進行式 (Present Perfect Continuous) 強調動作持續進行
    Eight of the 38 teams advanced to the implementation stage, where they are receiving guidance from mentors as they test and validate their ideas in real-world settings.
  • 介系詞加動名詞複合結構 (by learning from... and adapting...) 表達手段與方式
