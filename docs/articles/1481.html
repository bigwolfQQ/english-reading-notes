單字解析

  • surgery n. 手術
    He felt no pain when he awoke from surgery.
    他手術醒來時並不覺得痛。
  • deadly adj. 致命的、極端的
    Fear and self-doubt are the deadly enemies of creativity.
    恐懼與自我懷疑是創意的死敵。
  • enemy n. 敵人
    Fear and self-doubt are the deadly enemies of creativity.
    恐懼與自我懷疑是創意的死敵。
  • conscientious adj. 認真負責的、盡責的
    Being a conscientious worker, he is respected by his coworkers.
    他做事很認真負責，頗受同事尊敬。
  • worker n. 工作者、員工
    Being a conscientious worker, he is respected by his coworkers.
    他做事很認真負責，頗受同事尊敬。
  • coworker n. 同事
    Being a conscientious worker, he is respected by his coworkers.
    他做事很認真負責，頗受同事尊敬。
  • responsibility n. 責任
    He is conscious of his responsibility.
    他意識到自己的責任。
  • ashamed adj. 感到慚愧的、羞恥的
    The very thought of it made me ashamed.
    我一想起這件事總是感到慚愧。
  • shameful adj. 可恥的
    This is really shameful, and I feel ashamed of it.
    這事真可恥，我感到慚愧。
  • conduct n. 行為、舉止
    He considers this kind of conduct to be completely shameful.
    他認為這是一種完全可恥的行為。
  • anesthetic n. 麻醉劑
    Because she had been given an anesthetic, she felt no pain when her tooth was pulled out.
    因為有打麻醉劑，拔牙時她不覺得痛。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞構句表原因或附帶狀況
    Being a conscientious worker, he is respected by his coworkers.
    句首的 "Being a conscientious worker" 是由 "Because he is a conscientious worker" 簡化而來的分詞構句。去掉連接詞 Because，將主動態動詞 is 改為 being，用來補充說明主句主語 he 的身分或原因。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    he is respected by his coworkers.
    句型為「be 動詞 + 過去分詞 (respected)」，用來強調動作的承受者（he 受尊敬），並透過 "by his coworkers" 帶出動作的執行者。
  • 過去分詞作後位修飾 (Past Participle as Postmodifier)
    Because she had been given an anesthetic, she felt no pain when her tooth was pulled out.
    句尾的 "pulled out" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "her tooth"（她的牙齒被拔掉），省略了 "which was"，使句子更為簡潔。
  • 表原因的附屬連接詞子句 (Adclause of Cause with Because)
    Because she had been given an anesthetic, she felt no pain...
    用 "Because" 引導副詞子句，說明「她不覺得痛」的原因是由於「被注射了麻醉劑」，子句內部使用了過去完成式的被動語態 (had been given) 來表示動作發生在過去某個時間點之前。
