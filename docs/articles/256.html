禁止兒童使用社群媒體只解決了一半的問題

Banning kids from social media is only half a solution

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • effect n. 效力，影響，效果
    More than 81 percent of under-16s still used at least one restricted platform after the ban took effect last December.
    自去年 12 月禁令生效以來，超過 81% 的 16 歲以下青少年仍然使用至少一個受限制的平台。
  • encompass v. 包含，涵蓋
    Qustodio, the firm behind the data, acknowledged the figures only encompass families already using its software to restrict apps, meaning the broader picture could be worse.
    提供這些數據的公司 Qustodio 坦言，這些數字僅涵蓋已經使用其軟體來限制應用程式的家庭，這意味著整體情況可能會更糟。
  • policy n. 政策，方針
    It is far too early to declare the policy a failure.
    現在斷言這項政策失敗還為時過早。
  • effectiveness n. 有效性，效力
    Judging its effectiveness could take years.
    評估其成效可能需要數年時間。
  • expense n. 費用，支出
    An Australian survey published in August found expense was a limiting factor in the ability to participate in community-level sports for 78 percent of children.
    8 月份發布的一項澳洲調查發現，費用是 78% 的兒童無法參與社區級運動的限制因素。
  • participate v. 參加，參與
    The average annual cost to participate in sports was A$4,567.
    參與運動的平均年費為 4,567 澳元。
  • subsidy n. 津貼，補助金
    Many jurisdictions offer subsidies of up to A$200 (US$143) a year per child.
    許多地方政府每年為每個兒童提供高達 200 澳元（合 143 美元）的補助。
  • impact v. 對……產生影響
    In China, where school can be highly competitive from a young age, researchers were trying to see if giving elementary children two hours of extracurricular outdoor physical activity after school would impact their test scores.
    在中國，當地的學校從小就競爭激烈，研究人員試圖了解讓小學生在放學後參加兩小時的課外戶外體育活動是否會影響他們的考試成績。
  • funding n. 資金，經費
    The nation treated adolescent cigarette and alcohol use as a social infrastructure problem, dramatically increased funding for subsidized sports, extracurricular activities and youth centers.
    該國將青少年吸煙和飲酒問題視為社會基礎設施問題，大幅增加了對補助運動、課外活動和青少年中心的資金投入。
  • settlement n. 和解，協議
    In the US, Meta Platforms Inc last week agreed to a settlement of up to US$18 billion to resolve a litany of lawsuits.
    在美國，Meta 平台公司上週同意達成高達 180 億美元的和解協議，以解決一連串的訴訟。
  • penalty n. 罰款，處罰
    Australia could eventually collect penalties from social media companies that fail to keep children off their platforms.
    澳洲最終可能會向未能阻止兒童使用其平台的社群媒體公司收取罰款。
  • operate v. 操作，營運，運作
    The US settlement would force Meta to change how it operates young users’ Facebook and Instagram accounts.
    美國的和解協議將迫使 Meta 改變其營運年輕用戶的 Facebook 和 Instagram 帳戶的方式。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作後置修飾 / 狀語
    Other fast followers, including Malaysia and Indonesia, are finding that children can get around restrictions (as many suspected they would).
    句中的「including Malaysia and Indonesia」是由現在分詞 including 引導的片語，用來補充說明前面的主詞「Other fast followers」，在商業與新聞英語中非常常態地用來舉例。
  • 假設語氣（過去式表示對現在或未來的不確定假設）
    If policymakers want children off social media, they need to give them somewhere else to go.
    此句為第一類條件句（First Conditional），用「If + 現在式動詞, 主詞 + need/will...」的結構來表達真實或極有可能發生的條件與其結果，是表達政策建議與因果關係的經典句型。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞
    Commanding children to get off their phones is the easy part, actually getting them on the sports field (...) takes harder policy work.
    句首的「Commanding children to get off their phones」以及「actually getting them on the sports field...」都是動名詞片語（Gerund phrase），在句中扮演主詞的角色，視為單數，後面接單數動詞 is 與 takes。這類結構常用於比較抽象概念或行動。
  • 關係代名詞子句（限定修飾）
    A separate study in Canada of almost 29,000 seventh-graders found that those who engaged in sports, arts and community programs reported significantly less recreational screen time...
    句中的「who engaged in sports, arts and community programs」是關係代名詞 who 引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的代名詞 those（指代那些學生），提供更詳細的限定條件。
