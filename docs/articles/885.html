台灣選手在日本奪下金、銀、銅牌

Taiwanese win gold, silver and bronze in Japan

單字解析

  • defeat v. 擊敗，戰勝
    defeating a Japanese duo in a nail-biter in the soft tennis mixed doubles final in Nagoya
    在日本名古屋舉行的軟式網球混合雙打決賽中驚險擊敗日本組合
  • secure v. 獲得，取得
    Sun secured Taiwan’s first wushu medal at this year’s game
    孫家閎獲得了今年賽會台灣的第一面武術獎牌
  • combined adj. 綜合的，聯合的
    with a combined score of 19.453 points in men’s taijiquan-taijijian
    在男子太極拳太極劍項目中以總分 19.453 分獲得
  • determine v. 決定，確定
    their scores are added together to determine the final standings
    他們的得分會被加總來決定最後的排名
  • grateful adj. 感激的，感謝的
    adding that he was grateful for his family’s support over the past decade
    並補充說他很感謝家人過去十年來的支持
  • confidence n. 信心，自信
    gave him the confidence he needed ahead of the competition
    給了他在比賽前所需的信心
  • feature v. 以...為特色，由...主演/主演
    Sun’s taijijian routine featured the song Call of Silence
    孫家閎的太極劍套路以《Call of Silence》這首歌為特色
  • rebound v. 振作，從挫折中恢復
    He rebounded from a disappointing campaign at the Hangzhou Asian Games
    他從杭州亞運令人失望的賽事中振作起來
  • advance v. 晉級，前進
    Shih advanced through the early rounds with a series of close victories
    石政中憑藉著一系列的險勝晉級早期賽事
  • consist of phr. v. 組成，包含
    Group A consists of Japan, China, the Philippines and Palestine
    A 組包含日本、中國、菲律賓和巴勒斯坦

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作附帶狀況或結果狀語
    defeating a Japanese duo in a nail-biter in the soft tennis mixed doubles final in Nagoya, Japan, while Johnny Sun won silver...
    句中的 defeating 是現在分詞，用來補充說明主句動作發生時的伴隨狀況，翻譯時通常譯為「同時...」或「...並...」。
  • 關係代間詞子句修飾先行詞
    ...the bottom two teams go to the placement round, while the championship and bronze medal games are to be held on Saturday.
    此處使用地方副詞子句搭配 while 表示對比，說明 A 組賽制的不同走向。
  • be to do 句構（表示預定、計畫發生的未來動作）
    The championship and bronze medal games are to be held on Saturday.
    「are to be held」使用了「be to + 原形動詞」的結構，在新聞英語中經常用來表達按計畫或安排即將發生的官方行程。
