Literature awards under threat from AI, writer says

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • submission n. 提交（的作品）、投遞
    nearly 40 percent of the novel submissions he reviewed this year appeared to be AI-generated.
    他今年審閱的小說投稿中，將近 40% 似乎是由 AI 生成的。
  • community n. 界、社群、體系
    as the literary community attempts to fend off an infinitely replicable industrial production line with limited human labor.
    因為文學界正試圖以有限的人力，來抵禦源源不絕且可無限複製的工業化生產線。
  • noticeable adj. 顯著的、明顯的
    He said he observed a noticeable difference between AI-generated submissions received in the first and second halves of this year.
    他說，他觀察到今年上半年和下半年收到的 AI 生成投稿之間存在著明顯的差異。
  • outstanding adj. 優秀的、傑出的
    Preliminary judging previously involved selecting outstanding works, but judges now have to rank submissions while also trying to determine whether they were written by humans, Chu said.
    朱宥勳表示，初審過去包含選出優秀作品，但評審現在必須對投稿進行排名，同時還要試圖判斷它們是否為人類所寫。
  • effort n. 精力、努力
    Reading 200 submissions containing 20 AI-generated entries required as much effort as reading 400 or 500 works, he said
    他說，閱讀 200 篇包含 20 篇 AI 生成作品的投稿，所需要的精力相當於閱讀 400 到 500 件作品
  • destined adj. 註定的
    adding that the current system is destined to undermine judging quality and cannot effectively defend against AI-generated content.
    並補充說目前的體制註定會破壞評審品質，且無法有效防禦 AI 生成的內容。
  • undermine v. 逐漸削弱、破壞
    adding that the current system is destined to undermine judging quality and cannot effectively defend against AI-generated content.
    並補充說目前的體制註定會破壞評審品質，且無法有效防禦 AI 生成的內容。
  • inevitable adj. 無可避免的
    He added that mistakes were inevitable, adding that he could misidentify human-written works as AI-generated and fail to detect some AI-generated submissions.
    他補充說，錯誤是無可避免的，並補充說他可能會把人類寫的作品誤認為是 AI 生成的，並且無法檢測出某些 AI 生成的投稿。
  • accuracy n. 準確度
    Even if his accuracy rate was as high as 95 percent, reviewing 1,000 entries would still result in 50 incorrect judgements, Chu said.
    朱宥勳表示，即使他的準確率高達 95%，審閱 1,000 件參賽作品仍會導致 50 個錯誤的判斷。
  • compensation n. 報酬、薪資補償
    Working conditions for judges have deteriorated so severely that compensation would need to be raised substantially to maintain quality
    評審的工作條件已經惡化到如此嚴重的地步，以至於報酬需要大幅提高才能維持品質
  • substantially adv. 相當大地、大幅度地
    compensation would need to be raised substantially to maintain quality or every literary award would be riddled with problems, he said.
    報酬需要大幅提高才能維持品質，否則每個文學獎都將漏洞百出。
  • prioritize v. 把……列為優先、優先考慮
    while prioritizing events that adopt stronger preventive measures
    同時優先考慮採取更強有力預防措施的活動
  • prohibit v. 禁止
    refuse to serve as a judge for any literary award that does not explicitly prohibit AI-generated works
    拒絕擔任任何未明確禁止 AI 生成作品的文學獎評審

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（-ing）作形容詞或後置修飾
    Artificial intelligence (AI) is dealing a heavy blow to literature and literary judging, Taiwanese writer Chu Yu-hsun (朱宥勳) said on Saturday, adding that nearly 40 percent of the novel submissions he reviewed this year appeared to be AI-generated.
    句中的 "adding that..." 是現在分詞片語作分詞構句，用來補充說明前面主句所說的內容，意指「並補充說……」。這種用法在新聞英語中非常常見，用以串接同一個人接下來發表的言論。
  • so... that... 如此……以至於……
    Working conditions for judges have deteriorated so severely that compensation would need to be raised substantially to maintain quality...
    "so + 副詞 + that + 子句" 用來表達程度與結果。在此句中，工作條件惡化的程度（so severely）導致了結果（that compensation would need to be raised substantially... 報酬需要大幅提高……）。
  • 關係代間詞子句修飾先行詞
    Chu said that starting next year, he would refuse to serve as a judge for any literary award that does not explicitly prohibit AI-generated works...
    "that does not explicitly prohibit AI-generated works" 是一個限制性關係代間詞子句，用來修飾前面的先行詞 "any literary award"（任何文學獎），精準界定是哪一種文學獎（即未明確禁止 AI 生成作品的文學獎）。
  • 假設語氣（過去式表示與現在事實相反）
    “Once the literary award system collapses, you would not even know where to submit your work or where you can get your writing seen and noticed,” he said.
    句中的 "you would not even know..." 使用了助動詞的過去式 "would" 來表達假設情況下（文學獎體系崩潰時）會產生的結果，帶有強烈的推論語氣。
