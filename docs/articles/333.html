書評：冷戰不為人知的幕後黑手

Book review: The Cold War’s hidden hands

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • covert adj. 秘密的、隱蔽的
    Now, with his latest, The Cold War on Five Continents, McCoy continues to hammer the CIA for its crimes of the Cold War, including anti-democratic political assassinations, coups and other covert foreign interventions.
    如今，麥考伊推出最新著作《五大洲的冷戰》，繼續抨擊中情局在冷戰期間的罪行，包括反民主的政治暗殺、政變及其他祕密海外干預。
  • implication n. 可能產生的影響、含意、暗示
    Taiwan is mentioned only a few times in the book, but McCoy’s grand narrative of the late 20th century has big implications for the country.
    台灣在書中僅被提及數次，但麥考伊對 20 世紀晚期宏觀敘事的觀點對我國具有重大意涵。
  • vye v. 競爭、爭奪
    As scores of new nations stepped out from under the yolk of colonialism to be born in Asia, the Americas, Africa and Eastern Europe, two major powers — the Western liberal bloc led by the USA and the Communist bloc led by the USSR — vyed to bring them into their respective folds.
    當成群結隊的新興國家擺脫殖民主義的桎梏，在亞洲、美洲、非洲和東歐誕生時，兩個主要強權——由美國領導的西方自由陣營與由蘇聯領導的共產陣營——競相將它們納入各自的勢力範圍。
  • operation n. 行動、業務、運作
    But on the whole, the superpowers preferred covert operations — espionage, insurgencies and secret wars.
    但總體而言，超級大國更喜歡秘密行動——間諜活動、叛亂和祕密戰爭。
  • deployment n. 部署、調動
    The chaotic milieu of new nations — which especially from the late 1940s to the 1960s mixed nationalists, democrats, communists and warlords — was perfect for the deployment of covert operatives, or spies.
    新興國家混亂的環境——特別是從 1940 年代末到 1960 年代，將民族主義者、民主主義者、共產主義者和軍閥混雜在一起——非常適合部署祕密特務或間諜。
  • directive n. 指示、指令
    Such covert actors often fueled what were essentially civil wars — though in the new Cold War terminology, they were labeled anti-communist or anti-imperialist insurgencies--with arms, cash and secret directives from Washington or Moscow.
    這些秘密行動者經常透過來自華盛頓或莫斯科的武器、資金和秘密指示，火上加油那些本質上是內戰的衝突——雖然用新冷戰的術語來說，它們被標記為反共或反帝國主義的叛亂。
  • achieve v. 實現、達成
    As the superpowers squared off throughout the third world, McCoy argues that the US found it much easier to achieve its political goals by backing “absolute monarchies, dictatorships and juntas,” as long as they were reliably pro-Western.
    麥考伊認為，當超級大國在整個第三世界展開對峙時，美國發現只要這些政權確實親美，透過支持「絕對君主制、獨裁政權和軍政府」來實現其政治目標要容易得多。
  • fund v. 資助、提供資金
    So the US, in large part through the CIA, funded dictators who ruled with iron fists, jailed and killed their political opponents and in many cases operated death squads.
    因此，美國很大程度上透過中情局，資助了以鐵腕統治、監禁並殺害政治異見人士，在許多情況下還運作行刑隊的獨裁者。
  • tolerant adj. 容忍的、寬容的
    The US Congress also became far less tolerant of anti-democratic rulers and the CIA’s dirty tricks.
    美國國會也變得不再那麼容忍反民主統治者和中情局的卑劣伎倆。
  • regime n. 政權、體制
    In this new climate, repressive regimes from the Philippines to Eastern Europe suddenly fell apart.
    在這個新氣候下，從菲律賓到東歐的威權政體突然崩潰。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況或結果
    Then Washington stuck with his regime through 38 years of martial law, largely turning a blind eye to the human rights abuses of the White Terror.
    句尾的「turning a blind eye...」是由現在分詞構成的分詞構句，用來補充說明主句（華盛頓支持其政權）同時伴隨發生的動作或產生的結果，中文常譯為「同時……」或「因而……」。
  • 關係代名詞子句（非限定用法）
    Alfred McCoy has been a thorn in the side of the CIA at the very least since 1972, when he published The Politics of Heroin in Southeast Asia, a book the agency tried — and failed — to kill.
    句中的「when he published...」是關係副詞引導的非限定子句，用來補充說明 1972 年發生的事；後方的「a book the agency tried...」則是同位語，補充說明前面的著作，省去了關係代名詞（which/that）。
  • 虛受詞（Dummy Object）用法
    As the superpowers squared off throughout the third world, McCoy argues that the US found it much easier to achieve its political goals by backing “absolute monarchies, dictatorships and juntas,” as long as they were reliably pro-Western.
    句型「found it much easier to achieve...」中，it 是虛受詞，用來代替真正的受詞——不定詞片語「to achieve its political goals...」，因為真正的受詞太長而先用 it 代替，形容詞 easier 則放在受詞補語的位置。
