釀酒人隊無私的球風再度奏效

Brewers’ selfless style of ball pays off again

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • on the fringe phr. 處於邊緣、非核心
    Asked how a team that works on the fringe of baseball’s middle class has turned Champagne celebrations into a September ritual, Jacob Misiorowski shrugged.
    當被問到一支處於棒球界中產階級邊緣的球隊，是如何將香檳慶祝變成九月常態時，雅各布·米索羅夫斯基聳了聳肩。
  • ritual n. 慣例、老規矩、儀式
    Asked how a team that works on the fringe of baseball’s middle class has turned Champagne celebrations into a September ritual, Jacob Misiorowski shrugged.
    當被問到一支處於棒球界中產階級邊緣的球隊，是如何將香檳慶祝變成九月常態時，雅各布·米索羅夫斯基聳了聳肩。
  • victory n. 勝利
    The Brewers (94-57) on Tuesday night won their fourth straight National League (NL) Central title, when they rode their flame-throwing star and a couple of timely hits from Luis Lara and Joey Ortiz to a 5-1 victory over Pittsburgh.
    釀酒人隊（94 勝 57 敗）於週二晚間贏得了他們的第四座國家聯盟（NL）中區冠軍，當時他們靠著這位火球王牌以及路易斯·拉臘和喬伊·歐提茲的幾次適時安打，以 5 比 1 擊敗了匹茲堡隊。
  • fixture n. 固定班底、長期處於某位置的人或物
    This part of the journey never gets old, not even for Christian Yelich, who has been a fixture during Milwaukee’s run of eight playoff appearances in nine years.
    這段旅程的這一部分永遠不會讓人感到厭倦，對克里斯蒂安·葉力奇來說也是如此，他在密爾瓦基過去九年八次打進季後賽的過程中一直是陣中核心。
  • overwhelm v. 壓倒、擊潰
    Milwaukee does not overwhelm opponents with power or gaudy stats — outside of Misiorowski’s stream of 161kph-plus fastballs — and instead relies on singles, smart baseball and a selfless approach.
    密爾瓦基並沒有用重砲或華麗的數據來壓倒對手——除了米索羅夫斯基一連串超過 161 公里的速球之外——而是依賴一壘安打、聰明的球風和無私的精神。
  • approach n. 方法、作法
    Milwaukee does not overwhelm opponents with power or gaudy stats — outside of Misiorowski’s stream of 161kph-plus fastballs — and instead relies on singles, smart baseball and a selfless approach.
    密爾瓦基並沒有用重砲或華麗的數據來壓倒對手——除了米索羅夫斯基一連串超過 161 公里的速球之外——而是依賴一壘安打、聰明的球風和無私的精神。
  • on the mound phr. 在投手丘上
    Just like that, Milwaukee was up 1-0 with the NL Cy Young favorite on the mound.
    就這樣，在國聯賽揚獎熱門投手的帶領下，密爾瓦基以 1-0 領先。
  • regular season n. 例行賽
    The Brewers do not just, as Ortiz put it, “single you to death.” They largely avoid the mental mistakes that can make all the difference during the 162-game regular season.
    釀酒人隊不只是如歐提茲所說的「用安打慢慢磨死你」。他們很大程度上避免了會在 162 場例行賽中造成巨大影響的心理失誤。
  • ownership n. 所有權、所有權人（管理高層）
    He believes that culture starts with the ownership group headed by Mark Attanasio and trickles down to general manager Matt Arnold to manager Pat Murphy to a roster whose payroll of about US$140 million is 19th highest in the majors.
    他相信這種文化始於由馬克·阿塔納西奧領導的經營團隊，並向下傳遞給總經理馬特·阿諾德、總教練帕特·墨菲，以及薪資總額約 1.4 億美元、在大聯盟排名第 19 的球員陣容。
  • payroll n. 薪資總額、員工薪津名冊
    He believes that culture starts with the ownership group headed by Mark Attanasio and trickles down to general manager Matt Arnold to manager Pat Murphy to a roster whose payroll of about US$140 million is 19th highest in the majors.
    他相信這種文化始於由馬克·阿塔納西奧領導的經營團隊，並向下傳遞給總經理馬特·阿諾德、總教練帕特·墨菲，以及薪資總額約 1.4 億美元、在大聯盟排名第 19 的球員陣容。
  • underdog n. 處於劣勢者、不被看好者
    Given the Brewers’ success since their renaissance began in 2018, it is hard to paint them as underdogs.
    鑑於釀酒人隊自 2018 年復興以來的成功，很難將他們描繪成弱者。
  • consistently adv. 持續地、一致地
    “You have to go out there and play good baseball, and the Brewers have done that consistently over the last few years.”
    「你必須上場打出好球隊的表現，而釀酒人隊在過去幾年裡一直都做到了。」

文法重點

  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    This part of the journey never gets old, not even for Christian Yelich, who has been a fixture during Milwaukee’s run of eight playoff appearances in nine years.
    句中的「who has been a fixture...」是用來補充說明先行詞 Christian Yelich 的身分與背景。關係代名詞 who 作為主格，帶領形容詞子句修飾前面的專有名詞。
  • 分詞構句（表附帶狀況或結果）
    The Brewers (94-57) on Tuesday night won their fourth straight National League (NL) Central title, when they rode their flame-throwing star and a couple of timely hits from Luis Lara and Joey Ortiz to a 5-1 victory over Pittsburgh.
    句中的「when they rode...」引導一個時間副詞子句，用來描述贏得冠軍時所伴隨的狀況（靠著王牌投手與安打獲勝）。
  • 所有格代名詞與關係代名詞（whose）
    He believes that culture starts with the ownership group headed by Mark Attanasio and trickles down to general manager Matt Arnold to manager Pat Murphy to a roster whose payroll of about US$140 million is 19th highest in the majors.
  • 動名詞片語當主詞或受詞
    Asked how a team that works on the fringe of baseball’s middle class has turned Champagne celebrations into a September ritual, Jacob Misiorowski shrugged.
    句首的「Asked how...」其實是一個省略了主格與be動詞（When he was asked...）的過去分詞片語作分詞構句，而「how a team...」則是 ask 的受詞子句，用來交代被詢問的內容。
