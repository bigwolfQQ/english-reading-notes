文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾與表狀態
    Organized by the Ministry of Sports and hosted by the Taoyuan City Government, this year’s games is to open on Saturday next week at the Taoyuan Arena.
    句首使用兩個過去分詞片語（Organized by... 及 hosted by...）作為分詞構句，省略了主詞與 be 動詞（(Being) Organized and hosted...），用來補充說明主詞 this year’s games 被主辦與承辦的背景。
