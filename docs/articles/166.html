陸軍第二季全國旅級對抗演習將進行為期 10 天

Army’s second nationwide brigade-level exercise to run for 10 days

單字解析

  • exercise n. 演習、訓練
    The second edition of the Army’s nationwide brigade-level force-on-force exercise next month will take place over 10 days instead of seven, a military source said yesterday.
    一名軍方人士昨天表示，陸軍下個月將舉辦第二屆全國旅級實兵對抗演習，時間將從 7 天延長為 10 天。
  • anonymity n. 匿名、化名
    According to the source, who spoke on the condition of anonymity, this year’s Lu Sheng (陸勝) Exercise will involve the Taoyuan-based Army 269th Mechanized Infantry Brigade and the Kaohsiung-based 564th Combined Arms Brigade and take place from Oct. 14 to 23.
    據這名匿名消息人士透露，今年的「陸勝」演習將由駐扎桃園的陸軍機步第 269 旅與駐扎高雄的裝甲第 564 聯隊參與，時間從 10 月 14 日至 23 日舉行。
  • inaugural adj. 首次的、開創的、就職的
    As with the inaugural edition in 2025, this year’s drill will focus on testing 24-hour combat readiness, dispersed command-and-control operations, unscripted and realistic war scenarios, and rules of engagement (ROE) to assess the two key combat units’ defensive capabilities in the event of a Chinese invasion, the source said.
    該消息人士指出，如同 2025 年的首屆演習，今年的演戰將著重於測試 24 小時戰備整備、分散式指管作戰、無劇本且逼真的戰爭情境，以及交戰規則（ROE），以評估這兩個關鍵戰鬥單位在中國入侵時的防衛能力。
  • readiness n. 備妥、準備就緒

    Another key feature of Lu Sheng is the deployment of the US-developed Team Awareness Kit (TAK), which allows Army commanders to track friendly forces, mark enemy units and receive real-time updates.
    陸勝的另一項特色是部署了美國開發的「團隊意識套件」（TAK），這能讓陸軍指揮官追蹤友軍、標記敵軍並接收即時更新。
  • metric n. 衡量標準、指標
    Su Tzu-yun (蘇紫雲), director of the Institute for National Defense and Security Research’s Defense Strategy and Resources Division, said that “firepower value” is a fundamental metric used during military exercises or tabletop war games, similar to a batter’s batting average or a pitcher’s earned run average in baseball.
    國防安全研究院國防戰略與資源研究所所長蘇紫雲表示，「火力值」是軍事演習或兵棋推演中使用的基本指標，類似於棒球中打者的打擊率或投手的防禦率。
  • calculate v. 計算、估算

    該所另一位學者舒孝煌表示，陸勝實兵對抗演習將使陸軍更容易找出各旅內部的潛在弱點，包含火力支援整合、目標獲得以及後勤，並據此做出調整。

文法重點

  • 非限定關係代名詞子句（補充說明）
    According to the source, who spoke on the condition of anonymity, this year’s Lu Sheng (陸勝) Exercise will involve...
    句中的「who spoke on the condition of anonymity」前面有逗號，屬於非限定關係代名詞子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「the source（消息人士）」，即便省略不影響主要句子的完整性。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    According to the source, who spoke on the condition of anonymity, this year’s Lu Sheng (陸勝) Exercise will involve the Taoyuan-based Army 269th Mechanized Infantry Brigade...
    句中的「Taoyuan-based」是由「基於／總部設於」的過去分詞「based」組成的複合形容詞，用來修飾後方的「Army 269th Mechanized Infantry Brigade」，這在英文新聞中非常常見，用來簡潔地表達某組織的所在地點。
  • 比較與對比結構（while）
    Last year’s Lu Sheng exercise lasted seven days and six nights, while this year’s drill has been extended to nine nights and 10 days, the source added.
    連接詞「while」在此處用來表示「然而、而」，對比去年的演習天數與今年的演習天數，突顯出今年演習時間延長的變化。
  • 虛受詞（Dummy Object）與句型 make it easier for... to...
    Another institute scholar, Shu Hsiao-huang (舒孝煌), said the force-on-force Lu Sheng exercise would make it easier for the Army to identify potential vulnerabilities...
    此句使用了「make + it + 形容詞 + for sb. + to do」的句型。因為真正的受詞（不定詞片語「to identify...」）太長，所以用虛受詞「it」代替，置於形容詞「easier」之後，使句子結構平衡、易於閱讀。
