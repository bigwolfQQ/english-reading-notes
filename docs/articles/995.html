文法重點

  • 分詞構句作為附帶狀況 (Present Participle as Circumstance)
    ...Taiwan finished the 2026 World Outdoor Tug of War Championships in South Africa with four gold, two silver and two bronze medals, including titles in two women's weight classes for the fifth consecutive year.
    句尾的 "including..." 是分詞構句（源自分詞片語），用來補充說明前面奪牌的詳細內容，相當於 "which includes..."，在新聞英語中非常常見，用於附加細節。
  • 獨立分詞構句表達伴隨狀態 (Absolute Participial Phrase)
    On the final day of the four-day competition in Mossel Bay on Saturday, Taiwan won two golds, two silvers and one bronze, with the senior women's 540-kilogram team securing its fifth consecutive world title.
    此處使用 "with + 名詞 + 現在分詞 (securing...)" 的結構，用來表達伴隨發生的狀況或結果，說明在贏得獎牌的同時，各個隊伍達成了什麼樣的具體表現。
  • 對等連接詞表對比或同時發生 (While for Contrast/Simultaneity)
    Taiwan also took gold in the women's under-23 500-kg event, while the junior mixed 520-kg team claimed silver.
    連接詞 "while" 在此處用來連接兩個子句，表示「而...」的對比或同時發生的情況，常被用來簡潔地報導不同組別的比賽結果。
