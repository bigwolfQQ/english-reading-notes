性別團體敦促調查「具歧視性」的同志酒吧臨檢

Gender groups urge investigation into 'discriminatory' gay bar raid

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • investigation n. 調查
    Several gender equality groups called on the Taipei City government Sunday to conduct a thorough investigation into a police raid on a gay bar
    多個性別平等團體於週日呼籲台北市政府，對警方臨檢同志酒吧一事展開徹底調查
  • allegation n. 指控、宣稱
    The groups demanded the city government conduct an independent investigation into the allegations
    這些團體要求市政府對這些指控進行獨立調查
  • conduct v. 執行、實施、指揮
    The Taipei City Police Department's Wanhua Precinct conducted the raid on a BDSM-themed gay bar
    台北市政府警察局萬華分局對一家BDSM主題同志酒吧進行了臨檢
  • prohibit v. 禁止、阻止
    Police also allegedly ordered everyone to squat down, prohibited them from talking or using their mobile phones
    據稱警方還命令所有人蹲下，禁止他們交談或使用手機
  • condemn v. 譴責
    Signed by 10 nongovernmental organizations, including the hotline association, the statement condemned what the groups described as excessive enforcement
    由熱線協會等10個非政府組織簽署的聲明譴責了這些團體所形容的執法過當
  • establish v. 建立、設立
    The groups further called on the city to establish guidelines for police operations in gay and gender-diverse spaces
    這些團體進一步呼籲市政府為同志及性別多元空間的警方行動建立指導方針
  • address v. 處理、應對、解決
    they called for more proactive, practical and traceable measures to address systemic problems
    他們呼籲採取更主動、實務且可追溯的措施來解決系統性問題
  • exempt v. 免除、豁免
    The statement underlined that the existence of a search warrant does not exempt police from observing due process
    聲明強調，搜索票的存在並不能免除警方遵守正當法律程序的義務
  • surveillance n. 監視、監控
    Regarding allegations that officers made inappropriate remarks or behaved improperly toward people with disabilities, the precinct said surveillance footage had confirmed the conduct
    關於員警對身心障礙者做出不當言論或行為不當的指控，分局表示監視器畫面已證實該行為
  • in accordance with phr. 依照、與……一致
    the precinct had said that all officers involved in the raid had acted in accordance with the law
    分局曾表示所有參與臨檢的員警皆依法行事

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    Signed by 10 nongovernmental organizations, including the hotline association, the statement condemned what the groups described as excessive enforcement...
    句首的「Signed by...」是由過去分詞引導的片語，用來修飾後方的名詞主詞「the statement」（聲明）。這在新聞英語中非常常見，相當於完整子句「Which was signed by...」的簡化寫法，能讓句子更緊湊。
  • 現在分詞表達附帶結果或伴隨狀況 (Present Participle indicating Result or Concomitant circumstance)
    ...and took the person responsible for the venue and other relevant individuals to the police station for questioning, with several referred to prosecutors on suspicion of public indecency...
    句尾的「with several referred to prosecutors...」使用了「with + 名詞 + 過去分詞」的複合結構（absolute construction），用來補充說明伴隨發生的狀況或結果，表示「有好幾個人被移送給檢察官」。
  • 虛主詞 It 與被動語態的搭配 (Dummy 'It' with Passive Voice)
    The precinct was instructed to review any inappropriate conduct that may have occurred and explain the situation to the public as soon as possible, he said.
    句中使用被動語態「was instructed to...」（被指示去……），主詞是「The precinct」（分局）。這種結構常出現在官方發言或新聞報導中，用來轉述上級或長官的命令，模糊或不強調發號施令者的身分，聚焦於受指示的單位。
