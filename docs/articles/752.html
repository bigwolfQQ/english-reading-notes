Taiwan ranks 7th in civil participation globally

單字解析

  • report n. 報告
    a report published yesterday by the International Institute for Democracy and Electoral Assistance (IDEA) said.
    國際民主及選舉協助研究所（IDEA）昨天發布的一份報告指出。
  • organization n. 組織
    The Stockholm-based intergovernmental organization’s annual Global State of Democracy Report assesses effective representation...
    這個總部位於斯德哥爾摩的政府間組織，其年度《全球民主現狀報告》評估了有效代表性...
  • decline n. 衰退，下降
    suffered a decline in at least one factor of democratic performance compared with their own performance five years earlier.
    與五年前自身的表現相比，在民主表現的至少一項要素上出現了衰退。
  • improvement n. 改善，進步
    Only 13 countries showed improvement in measures of parliamentary effectiveness...
    僅有13個國家的國會效能指標有所改善...
  • category n. 種類，類別
    Taiwan and the US “fell from high to mid-range performance” in the judicial independence category last year...
    台灣與美國去年在司法獨立類別中「從高效能跌至中階表現」...
  • stability n. 穩定，穩定性
    ...affect regional stability.
    ...影響區域穩定。
  • factor n. 因素
    “Regional stability and the risk that securitized pressures will shape democratic governance” would be important factors to watch...
    「區域穩定以及安全化壓力將塑造民主治理的風險」將是需要密切關注的重要因素...
  • economic adj. 經濟的
    The US saw seven statistically significant declines from 2020 through last year across the access to justice, economic equality...
    美國從2020年至去年在取得司法救濟、經濟平等...等類別中出現了七次具統計顯著性的衰退。
  • withdrew v. 撤回，退出（withdraw的過去式）
    It has more than 30 member states, although the US withdrew as an observer this year.
    它擁有30多個成員國，儘管美國已於今年退出了觀察員身分。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後置定語 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    a report published yesterday by the International Institute for Democracy and Electoral Assistance (IDEA) said.
    句中的「published yesterday by...」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "a report"（一份報告）。這相當於形容詞子句 "which was published yesterday..." 的簡化，能讓句子更為精煉。
  • 現在分詞片語作補充說明 (Present Participle Phrase for Additional Information)
    Taiwan and the US “fell from high to mid-range performance” in the judicial independence category last year, with Argentina, Belgium, India and Nigeria’s performance also declining, it said.
    句尾使用了「with + 名詞 + 現在分詞（declining）」的複合結構，用來表達伴隨發生的狀況或背景情況，常譯為「隨著……（而……）」。
  • 名詞子句作受詞 (Noun Clause as Object)
    The Stockholm-based intergovernmental organization’s annual Global State of Democracy Report assesses effective representation, rights, rule of law, civil participation and judicial systems in democracies worldwide.
    動詞 assesses 後面接了一連串並列的名詞（representation, rights, rule of law...）作為其受詞，說明該組織的年度報告所「評估」的對象範圍。
