單字解析

  • defending champion n. phr. 衛冕冠軍
    and the two-time defending champion showed in an overpowering display in the semi-finals how serious she is about a three-peat
    而這位兩屆衛冕冠軍在準決賽中展現出壓倒性的氣勢，證明了她對完成三連霸的決心有多強烈
  • display n. 表現、展現、陳列
    and the two-time defending champion showed in an overpowering display in the semi-finals how serious she is about a three-peat
    而這位兩屆衛冕冠軍在準決賽中展現出壓倒性的氣勢，證明了她對完成三連霸的決心有多強烈
  • matchup n. 對決、比賽組合
    Rybakina set up a matchup between the top two players in women’s tennis by rallying past Coco Gauff 3-6, 6-4, 6-4.
    蕾巴金娜在落後的情況下以 3-6、6-4、6-4 逆轉擊敗可可·高芙，締造了女子網壇前兩名球員在決賽交鋒的局面。
  • rally v. （體育中）急起直追、反擊、集結
    Gauff won her first major title as a teenager when she rallied past Sabalenka in the 2023 US Open final
    高芙在 2023 年美網決賽逆轉擊敗莎芭蓮卡，以青少年之姿贏得她個人的第一座大滿貫冠軍
  • consecutive adj. 連續的
    that changes on Monday after Sabalenka held the top spot for 99 consecutive weeks.
    但在莎芭蓮卡連續 99 週蟬聯榜首後，這個排名將在週一改變。
  • clinch v. 鎖定（勝利、冠軍、合約等）、解決
    Rybakina needed only to reach the semi-finals in New York to clinch the No. 1 ranking.
    蕾巴金娜只需要闖入紐約的準決賽，就能鎖定世界第一的排名。
  • category n. 類別、項目
    she had four aces to one for Rybakina, who leads the WTA Tour in that category
    她擊出 4記愛司球，而領先 WTA 巡迴賽該項數據的蕾巴金娜則只有 1記
  • break v. （網球等運動中）破發
    Rybakina went ahead 5-3 and served for the match, but Gauff broke to give herself one more chance
    蕾巴金娜取得 5-3 領先並擁有發球勝賽局，但高芙成功破發，為自己爭取到又一次機會
  • level n. 水準、程度
    I mean, I thought her level was insanely high.
    我的意思是，我覺得她的水準高得不可思議。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後置修飾語 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    Rybakina can only take that away from Sabalenka by winning it on the court today, and the two-time defending champion showed in an overpowering display in the semi-finals how serious she is about a three-peat — which would be the first at Flushing Meadows since Serena Williams from 2012-14.
    句中的「defending」是由動詞defend轉來的現在分詞，用來修飾名詞champion（冠軍），表示「正在進行衛冕的冠軍」。這種用法在多益商業閱讀中很常見，例如：a leading company（領先的公司）。
  • 地方副詞置於句首的倒裝結構 (Adverbial Inversion)
    The top four seeds made it to the women’s semi-finals at the US Open for the first time since 1975. Sabalenka, Rybakina and Gauff have all won multiple major titles, while Pegula is still looking for her first.
    雖然本句未使用嚴格的完全倒裝，但句型「... have all won ..., while Pegula is still looking for ...」展示了多益常考的對比連接詞 while 的用法。while 在此連接兩個並列子句，表達強烈的對比（前者已經贏得多次大滿貫，而後者仍在尋求第一座）。
  • 關係代名詞非限定用法 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Rybakina went ahead 5-3 and served for the match, but Gauff broke to give herself one more chance while being cheered by a crowd that included former first lady Michelle Obama.
    句尾的「that included former first lady Michelle Obama」是一個由關係代名詞 that 引導的限定修飾子句，用來修飾前面的 a crowd（人群），精確補充說明這群觀眾包含了誰，這在新聞英語中用來提供豐富的背景細節非常典型。
