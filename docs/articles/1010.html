文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 / 形容詞片語
    Meanwhile, the DMS simulators are connected to a screen displaying a simulation of Jioupeng Bay (九棚灣) in Pingtung County, where Jioupeng Military Base is located.
    句中的 "displaying a simulation of..." 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 a screen，相當於關代子句 "which displays a simulation of..."，在新聞英語中常用來精簡句子結構。
  • 對等連接詞連接的多重動詞片語
    The simulators allow trainees to track and lock on to airborne targets, including drones, while communicating through built-in intercoms and sharing location information, the command said.
    句中的 allow 後面接了 "track and lock on to"（追蹤並鎖定），而 while 後面則接了對等的分詞 "communicating... and sharing..."，用來表達在進行主要動作的同時所伴隨的次要動作。
  • 不定詞片語作目的副詞
    Chen said trainees undergo 30 to 45 days of training to acquire the necessary skills, while those seeking to qualify as instructors undergo twice as much training.
    句中的 "to acquire the necessary skills" 是不定詞片語，在此處作副詞用，用來修飾前面的動詞 undergo，表達受訓的目的（為了獲得必要的技能）。
  • 對比結構（while 表「而、然而」）
    Chen said trainees undergo 30 to 45 days of training to acquire the necessary skills, while those seeking to qualify as instructors undergo twice as much training.
    句中的 while 在此不用來表示時間（當……時），而是作為對等連接詞，用來對比兩種不同的情況（一般受訓人員與尋求教官資格者所需訓練時間的差異）。
