衛生所主任因私發減肥藥遭罰款

Health center head fined for giving out weight-loss drugs

單字解析

  • fine v. / n. 處以罰款、罰金
    The head of a public health center in Tainan has been fined NT$100,000 for allegedly purchasing injectable GLP-1 drugs...
    台南市一名公立衛生所的主任因涉嫌購買注射型 GLP-1 藥物...而被處以新台幣 10 萬元罰款。
  • audit n. / v. 稽查、審計、查帳
    An audit by the Tainan Public Health Bureau found that the Sinhua District Health Station had purchased 780 doses...
    台南市衛生局的一項稽查發現，新化區衛生所曾購買 780 劑...
  • inventory n. 庫存、存貨
    ...but only five doses were in inventory.
    ...但庫存中僅剩 5 劑。
  • admit v. 坦承、承認
    Station director Wu Cheng-ta yesterday admitted that he purchased the drugs and provided them to his friends and relatives...
    衛生所主任吳政達昨天坦承他購買了這些藥物並提供給親友...
  • profit n. 利潤、盈利
    ...adding that he sold them at cost without making any profit.
    ...並補充說他是以成本價出售，並未獲利。
  • collaborate v. 合作、協力
    ...adding that the agency has collaborated with local health bureaus since 2024 to conduct special audits.
    ...並補充說該署自 2024 年起已與地方衛生局合作進行專案稽查。
  • distribution n. 配送、分配、分發
    ...requiring pharmaceutical companies to report monthly drug distribution data, she said.
    ...要求製藥公司每月回報藥品配送數據，她說。
  • scope n. 範圍
    The FDA this year expanded the scope of the audit by analyzing drug reporting data to identify abnormal distribution patterns, Liu said.
    劉佳萍說，食藥署今年透過分析藥品回報數據來識別異常配送模式，藉此擴大了稽查範圍。
  • investigation n. 調查
    The agency has provided records to local health bureaus for further investigation, including the Sinhua District Health Station, she added.
    她補充說，該署已將相關紀錄提供給地方衛生局以供進一步調查，其中包括新化區衛生所。
  • acquire v. 取得、獲得
    The FDA urged people not to acquire GLP-1s through relatives, friends, online platforms or other illegal channels...
    食藥署敦促民眾不要透過親友、網路平台或其他非法管道取得 GLP-1 藥物...
  • monitor v. 監控、監測
    The agency would continue to monitor the supply of GLP-1s through drug traceability and reporting systems...
    該署表示將透過藥品追溯與回報系統持續監控 GLP-1 藥物的供應狀況...
  • penalize v. 處罰、懲罰
    ...adding that any illegal supply or usage would be investigated and penalized according to the law.
    ...並補充說任何非法的供應或使用都將依法調查並予以處罰。

文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The head of a public health center in Tainan has been fined NT$100,000 for allegedly purchasing...
    此處使用現在完成式的被動語態（has been + 過去分詞），強調主詞「台南市衛生所主任」是遭受處罰的對象，且動作對現在帶有影響。
  • 現在分詞作附加補充說明 (Present Participle as a Modifier)
    ...adding that he sold them at cost without making any profit.
    句尾的「adding that...」是由現在分詞片語引導的附帶結果或補充說明，表示吳政達在坦承之餘，同時也補充了這項說法，主詞維持一致（即吳政達本人正在補充）。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Post-modifier)
    People seeking to lose weight should see a doctor for professional evaluation...
    句中的「seeking to lose weight」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前方代名詞 People，相當於形容詞子句「People who seek to lose weight」，使句子更為精簡。
