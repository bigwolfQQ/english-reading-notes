流感與腸病毒就醫人次攀升：疾管署

Medical visits for flu and enterovirus climb: CDC

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • increase v. 增加，提高
    Medical visits for flu-like illnesses and enterovirus increased last week...
    上週類流感及腸病毒就醫人次增加...
  • previous adj. 先前的，以前的
    ...a 16.5 percent increase from the previous week.
    ...較前一週增加16.5%。
  • account for phr. 佔（比例），說明
    Influenza A(H1N1) is the dominant flu strain spreading in local communities, accounting for 82.2 percent of positive tests...
    A型流感（H1N1）是目前在社區中傳播的主要流感病毒株，占陽性檢驗的82.2%...
  • estimate v. 估計，估算
    Lin Min-cheng said the centers estimates that flu cases would continue to increase...
    林明誠表示，該署預估流感病例將持續增加...
  • peak n. 高峰，頂峰
    ...reaching a weekly peak number around Mid-Autumn Festival on Friday next week...
    ...在下週五中秋節前後達到單週高峰...
  • predict v. 預測
    The CDC predicts that enterovirus would enter its epidemic period this week...
    疾管署預測腸病毒將於本週進入流行期...
  • gradually adv. 逐漸地
    ...while COVID-19 cases might gradually drop, he said.
    ...而COVID-19病例則可能逐漸下降，他表示。
  • avoid v. 避免
    ...and avoid cross contamination of raw and cooked food, he said.
    ...同時避免生熟食交叉污染，他表示。
  • associated with phr. 與……相關的
    Regarding a hepatitis C cluster associated with injections at Antai Songhe Clinic...
    關於與安泰淞鶴診所注射事件相關的C型肝炎群聚感染...
  • complete v. 完成
    ...the centers estimate the positivity rate might range between 5 and 10 percent after the blood tests are completed.
    ...該署預估在血液檢測完成後，陽性率可能會落在5%到10%之間。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附加說明（補充結果或動作）
    The boy was admitted to a hospital, where he developed convulsions and lethargy, Lin said, adding that the boy tested positive for influenza A.
    句尾的「adding that...」是由現在分詞（adding）引導的分詞片語，用來補充說明主句所描述事件之下的進一步訊息或發言內容，相當於 and added that...
  • 對等連接詞 while 表對比或同時發生
    Medical visits for flu-like illnesses and enterovirus increased last week, while COVID-19 cases stayed at a high level...
    連接詞 while 在此用來連接兩個子句，表示「而／然而」的對比語氣，呈現上週不同疾病就醫趨勢的差異。
  • 非限定性關係子句 (Non-defining relative clause)
    ...the highest weekly death count this flu season, which began in October last year, she said.
    「which began in October last year」前面帶有逗號，屬於非限定性關係子句，用來補充說明先行詞「this flu season」（這個流感季）的起訖時間，拿掉後不影響主要句意。
