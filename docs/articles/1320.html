單字解析

  • prosecutor n. 檢察官
    Prosecutors yesterday said they had indicted a Hong Kong gang member over the assault...
    檢方昨天表示，他們已起訴一名香港幫派成員，因其涉嫌襲擊……
  • indict v. 起訴、控告
    ...they had indicted a Hong Kong gang member over the assault of Taiwan-based political commentator Akio Yaita...
    ……他們已起訴一名香港幫派成員，因其涉嫌襲擊常駐台灣的政治評論員矢板明夫……
  • assault n. / v. 攻擊、襲擊
    Liu Kong-faat (...), described by prosecutors as a ranking member ..., was charged with assault, public insult, public threat and organized crime...
    被檢方描述為……幹部成員的廖港發，被依傷害、公然侮辱、恐嚇公眾及組織犯罪等罪嫌起訴……
  • statement n. 聲明、陳述
    ...the Taichung District Prosecutors’ Office said in a statement.
    台中地方檢察署在聲明中表示。
  • investigate v. 調查
    Prosecutors added that they were also continuing to investigate who was behind the operation...
    檢方補充說，他們也正在持續調查幕後主使者為何人……
  • accomplice n. 共犯、幫兇
    ...and whether there were any accomplices in Taiwan.
    以及台灣是否有任何共犯。
  • contact v. 聯繫、聯絡
    A man using the alias Chen Wei-hao (...) contacted a man surnamed Ng (...)...
    一名使用「陳偉豪」化名的男子聯繫了一名姓吳的男子……
  • organize v. 組織、籌辦
    ...offering him money to organize an operation to publicly assault Yaita...
    向他提供金錢以組織一場公開襲擊矢板明夫的行動……
  • recruit v. 招募、吸收
    ...while Ng recruited Liu and three other suspects to carry out different tasks, they said.
    而吳嫌則招募了廖嫌及另外三名嫌疑人來執行不同的任務，他們說。
  • withdraw v. 退出、撤回
    ...but that person withdrew and left the country after deciding the risk was too high, they said.
    但該名嫌疑人在認為風險太高後決定退出並離境，他們說。
  • attempt v. / n. 試圖、嘗試
    Liu was arrested at Taichung International Airport while attempting to flee to Busan, South Korea...
    廖嫌在試圖逃往南韓釜山時於台中航空站被捕……
  • detain v. 拘留、扣留
    The Taichung District Court approved prosecutors’ request to detain Liu on July 7.
    台中地方法院於 7 月 7 日批准了檢方聲請羈押（拘留）廖嫌的請求。
  • sentence n. / v. 宣判、徒刑、判決
    Prosecutors are seeking a five-year prison sentence for Liu.
    檢方正對廖嫌求處五年有期徒刑。

文法重點

  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense)
    Prosecutors yesterday said they had indicted a Hong Kong gang member over the assault...
    表示在過去某一時間點（said）之前已經完成的動作（had indicted）。檢方發言在先前的過去，而起訴嫌犯的時間又在這之前，因此使用過去完成式 (had + 過去分詞)。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Liu Kong-faat (廖港發), described by prosecutors as a ranking member of the Hong Kong-based Wo Shing Wo (和勝和), was charged with assault...
    句中的 "described by prosecutors as..." 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾主詞 Liu Kong-faat，相當於一個被動語態的形容詞子句 "who was described by prosecutors as..."，省略了關係代名詞與be動詞，使句子更精簡。
  • 現在分詞表附帶狀況或同時發生的動作 (Present Participle)
    Liu was arrested at Taichung International Airport while attempting to flee to Busan, South Korea...
    "while attempting to flee..." 中的 "attempting" 是現在分詞，用來表達在被逮捕的同時所發生的動作。while 後面省略了 "Liu was"，保留分詞讓語意更流暢。
  • 名詞子句作受詞 (Noun Clause as Object)
    Prosecutors added that they were also continuing to investigate who was behind the operation...
    連接詞 "that" 引導名詞子句，作為 added 的受詞；而在子句中，"who was behind the operation" 又是由疑問代名詞 who 引導的名詞子句，作 investigate 的受詞，展現多益常見的巢狀子句結構。
