從鹿皮到半導體：重新思考台灣的經濟奇蹟

From deer hides to semiconductors: Rethinking Taiwan’s economic miracle

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • powerhouse n. 權力中心、強權、實力強大的機構或國家
    Four centuries before Taiwan became a semiconductor powerhouse, its deer hides were finding buyers in Japan.
    在台灣成為半導體重鎮的四個世紀前，其鹿皮就在日本找到買家。
  • emulate v. 仿效、模仿、努力趕上
    For governments hoping to emulate Taiwan’s chip industry, the answer matters.
    對於希望仿效台灣晶片產業的政府而言，這個答案至關重要。
  • transform v. 轉變、徹底改變
    Semiconductors are the latest expression of an economy repeatedly transformed by changing products and markets.
    半導體是一個不斷因產品和市場轉變而重塑的經濟體的最新表現。
  • export v. 出口
    From the late sixteenth century, deer hides from Taiwan were exported to Japan and venison to China.
    從十六世紀末開始，來自台灣的鹿皮被出口到日本，鹿肉則出口到中國。
  • expand v. 擴大、擴展
    Producers also needed conditions that allowed them to expand production and benefit from exchange.
    生產者同時需要能允許他們擴大生產並從交換中獲益的條件。
  • investment n. 投資
    Changes to land rights and administration, alongside investment in transportation and public health, helped establish foundations for a modern economy.
    土地權利與行政體系的變革，伴隨著對交通與公共衛生的投資，有助於為現代經濟奠定基礎。
  • subsidiary / subsidy n. 補助金、津貼
    Financial support can help establish capacity. It cannot, by itself, recreate accumulated knowledge, skilled workers, competitive pressures or relationships with customers and suppliers.
    （對應文章中的補助與財務支持概念）財務支持有助於建立產能。單靠它本身，無法重新創造累積的知識、熟練的勞動力、競爭壓力，或是與客戶和供應商的關係。
  • constraint n. 限制、約束
    Businesses responded rapidly once those constraints eased.
    一旦這些限制放寬，企業便迅速做出回應。
  • accelerate v. 加速、促進
    Exports expanded, private investment accelerated and Taiwan entered the sustained period of growth that became known as its economic miracle.
    出口擴大、私人投資加速，台灣進入了被稱為經濟奇蹟的持續成長期。
  • negotiate / undertake v. 承擔、進行、著手（從事）
    Aid gave US officials leverage to press for reforms that the government had been reluctant to undertake.
    援助賦予了美國官員籌碼，去施壓政府進行那些原本一直不願進行的改革。
  • competitiveness / competitive adj. 競爭性的、具競爭力的
    It cannot, by itself, recreate accumulated knowledge, skilled workers, competitive pressures or relationships with customers and suppliers.
    單靠它本身，無法重新創造累積的知識、熟練的勞動力、競爭壓力，或是與客戶和供應商的關係。
  • supplier n. 供應商
    It cannot, by itself, recreate accumulated knowledge, skilled workers, competitive pressures or relationships with customers and suppliers.
    單靠它本身，無法重新創造累積的知識、熟練的勞動力、競爭壓力，或是與客戶和供應商的關係。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle phrase as post-modifier)
    In a recent interview for the National Taiwan Normal University International Taiwan Studies Center Podcast, produced in collaboration with the International Journal of Taiwan Studies...
    過去分詞片語「produced in collaboration with...」用來修飾前面的名詞 Podcast，相當於關代子句「which was produced in collaboration with...」，省略了主系動詞，使語氣更為緊湊專業。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞 (Gerund phrase as subject)
    Changing the rules was a political struggle as well as an economic undertaking.
    動名詞片語「Changing the rules」作為句子的主詞，視為單數概念，因此後方搭配單數動詞「was」。
  • 過去分詞作形容詞用 (Past participle as adjective)
    An overvalued currency and a complicated exchange-rate system also made it difficult for producers to compete internationally.
    「overvalued」（被高估的）與「complicated」（複雜的）皆是由動詞變來的過去分詞，直接放在名詞前或作形容詞使用，用來修飾貨幣與匯率系統。
  • 虛主詞 it 與受詞補語結構 (Cleft/Dummy object with 'it')
    The issue is whether those rules support productive activity and allow businesses to respond when opportunities change.
    雖然此句為包含連接詞 whether 的名詞子句，但在此結構中展現了多益常見的「allow + 受詞 + to do」句型（allow businesses to respond），表示允許某人或某物做某事。
