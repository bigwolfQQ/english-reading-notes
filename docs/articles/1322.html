中國與寮國基地可見攻擊機、軍用車輛

Attack aircraft, military vehicles visible at China-Laos base

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • facility n. 設施、場所
    Light attack aircraft and military vehicles have arrived at a new China-Laos joint training facility announced on Tuesday last week...
    輕型攻擊機和軍用車輛已抵達上週二宣布的一處新的中寮聯合訓練設施...
  • expansion n. 擴展、擴大
    ...China’s latest expansion of its overseas military presence.
    ...這是中國擴大其海外軍事存在的最新舉動。
  • construction n. 建設、施工
    ...the images show extensive construction work around the runway.
    ...這些圖像顯示跑道周圍有大規模的建設工程。
  • logistics n. 後勤、物流
    ...port access in Ream, Cambodia, and a logistics base in Djibouti.
    ...柬埔寨雲姆的港口權限以及吉布地的後勤基地。
  • access n. / v. 進入權、使用權、接近
    ...a facility that the Chinese People’s Liberation Army could access “is likely more about a target of opportunity than filling an obvious operational need.”
    ...中國人民解放軍可以進入的設施，「與其說是为了填補明顯的營運需求，不如說是一個機會目標。」
  • infrastructure n. 基礎設施
    “Laos does have some geostrategic value as an accessible foothold in mainland Southeast Asia, given its shared border with China and infrastructure links, including [its] railway,” Vandenbrink said.
    范德布林克說：「鑑於寮國與中國接壤以及基礎設施聯結（包括其鐵路），寮國作為中國在中南半島易於接近的據點，確實具有一些地緣戰略價值。」
  • respond v. 回應、回覆
    The Chinese defense ministry did not immediately respond to a request for comment.
    中國國防部沒有立即回應置評請求。
  • export v. / n. 出口
    It was jointly developed by China and Pakistan and is exported to other militaries.
    它是由中國和巴基斯坦共同開發的，並出口到其他軍隊。
  • assessment n. 評估、評價
    ...according to the London-based International Institute for Strategic Studies’ annual assessment of the world’s militaries.
    ...根據倫敦國際戰略研究所對世界軍隊的年度評估。
  • storage n. 儲存、庫存
    As construction continues, diplomats and analysts are keeping watch for any sign of air defenses, ammunition and fuel storage as well as surveillance facilities...
    隨著建設的繼續，外交官和分析師正在密切關注任何防空設施、彈藥和燃料儲存以及監視設施的跡象...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase)
    The images, taken by commercial satellite provider Vantor, show what security analysts believe are eight K-8 light attack jets...
    句中的 "taken by commercial satellite provider Vantor" 是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞 "The images"。這相當於形容詞子句 "which were taken by..." 的簡化版，用來補充說明圖片的來源。
  • 被動語態的進行式 (Present Continuous Passive Voice)
    The swift development of the new facility is being closely watched by regional military attaches and security academics...
    此句使用了現在進行式的被動語態（is/am/are + being + 過去分詞），用來表達這項設施的發展「正在被」區域軍事武官密切關注中，強調動作正在進行。
  • 複合關係代間詞 what 引導的名詞子句 (Noun Clause with what)
    The images... show what security analysts believe are eight K-8 light attack jets...
    句中的 "what security analysts believe are eight K-8 light attack jets" 是由 what 引導的名詞子句，在句中擔任動詞 show 的受詞。what 在子句中兼具先行詞與關係代間詞的功能，相當於 "the things that"。
  • 分詞構句表附帶結果或伴隨狀況 (Participial Phrase)
    In helping Laos build up Ban Keun, China is occupying a site the US used covertly at the height of the Vietnam War.
    句首的 "In helping Laos build up Ban Keun"（在協助寮國建立班耿的同時）是以介詞 In 加上動名詞片語構成的結構，用來表達伴隨發生的動作或背景情境。
