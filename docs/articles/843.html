文法重點

  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況（獨立分詞構句）
    The power of AI is barely diffused through the economy, with only 5 to 10 percent of the value of the technology being used, Amodei said on Tuesday during an appearance with Benioff at Salesforce’s annual Dreamforce conference in San Francisco.
    句中的 "with + 名詞 (only 5 to 10 percent of the value of the technology) + 分詞 (being used)" 構成「with 複合結構」，用來補充說明主句動作發生時的伴隨狀態或條件，在多益閱讀與商業英文中非常常見。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    Those comments, which were endorsed by executives of rival AI companies such as OpenAI CEO Sam Altman and SpaceXAI CEO Elon Musk have led to discussions by policymakers and within the industry about ways to prevent AI from slipping beyond human control.
    原句中包含一個由關係代間詞 "which" 引導的形容詞子句（或可簡化為過去分詞片語），用來修飾前面的名詞 "Those comments"，說明這些評論是「被」哪些人所背書支持的，展現了被動語態在子句中的應用。
  • 條件句（If 子句）與祈使句的結合
    If we reach a point when “the company is out of control and the product’s not going to be safe, take a pause and make sure you get it right,” he said.
    此句使用了 "If + 現在式動詞" 的條件句型來假設未來可能的情境，而在主句部分則直接使用祈使句（"take a pause and make sure..."）來給予建議或指示，這種結構在商務溝通與操作說明中經常用到。
