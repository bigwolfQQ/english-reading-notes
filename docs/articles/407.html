點亮鬼魂？

Lighting ghosts?

  • install v. 安裝，設置
    The Yuanli Township Office installed the display in front of the local railway station to enhance the atmosphere of the annual Ghost Gate Closing night market.
    苑裡鎮公所為了營造年度「鬼門關夜市」的節慶氛圍，在當地火車站前設置了這處燈飾。

文法重點

  • 不定詞片語表達目的
    The Yuanli Township Office installed the display in front of the local railway station to enhance the atmosphere of the annual Ghost Gate Closing night market.
    句尾的「to enhance...」是不定詞（to + V）做副詞用，用來表達主詞進行該動作（installed）的目的，即「為了增強…氣氛」。這是多益閱讀測驗中常見的句構。
