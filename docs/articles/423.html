2001年9月11日恐怖攻擊未曾造成的那個世界

The world that the Sept. 11, 2001, terror attacks did not make

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • anniversary n. 週年紀念日
    Today marks the 25th anniversary of the Sept. 11, 2001 (9/11), terrorist attacks on the US.
    今天是 2001 年 9 月 11 日（9/11）美國恐怖攻擊事件滿 25 週年。
  • operation n. 行動、業務、運作
    All were trained in Afghanistan and four at US flight schools as part of an operation planned, organized and carried out by al-Qaeda.
    所有人都在阿富汗受過訓練，其中 4 人在美國的飛行學校受訓，這是由蓋達組織所策劃、組織並執行之行動的一部分。
  • measure n. 措施、方法
    A wave of attacks on a similar scale did not follow, in no small part because the US and other governments adopted measures that made it more difficult for would-be terrorists to emerge and succeed.
    規模相似的攻擊浪潮並未隨之而來，很大程度上是因為美國和其他政府採取了措施，使得蓄意行凶的恐怖分子更難以出現並得逞。
  • intervention n. 介入、干預
    It led directly to military intervention in Afghanistan, where US-led forces, in coordination with Afghan tribesmen, ousted the ruling Taliban...
    它直接導致了對阿富汗的軍事干預，在那裡，美軍在阿富汗部族民兵的配合下，驅逐了塔利班政權……
  • deploy v. 調動、部署
    Twenty-five years later, after more than 800,000 US soldiers had been deployed, more than US$2 trillion spent and more than 2,400 Americans killed in combat...
    二十五年後，在超過 80,000 名美軍被部署、花費超過 2 兆美元以及超過 2,400 名美軍在戰鬥中喪生……
  • initiate v. 發起、創始
    In March 2003, just a year and a half after 9/11, the US initiated a war of choice against Iraq.
    2003 年 3 月，就在 9/11 發生一年半之後，美國對伊拉克發動了一場選擇性戰爭。
  • possess v. 擁有、具備
    Some in the Bush administration believed that Saddam Hussein, Iraq’s strongman leader, was developing or even possessed a nuclear weapon...
    布希政府中的有些人認為，伊拉克強人領袖海珊正在開發甚至已經擁有核武器……
  • involve v. 涉及、參與
    However, the costly war... turned many Americans against overseas involvement and eventually positioned Iran... as a major power in the Middle East.
    然而，這場所費不貲的戰爭……使許多美國人反對海外介入，並最終將伊朗……推上了中東主要大國的地位。
  • beneficiary n. 受益人、受惠者
    ...and eventually positioned Iran, which had nothing to do with 9/11 even if it was a strategic beneficiary of the war, as a major power in the Middle East.
    ……並最終將伊朗——儘管它與 9/11 無關，哪怕它是該戰爭的戰略受益者——推上了中東主要大國的地位。
  • attribute v. 把……歸因於
    ...little of what has happened since, be it in Gaza, Lebanon or Iran, was preordained or can be attributed to terrorism.
    ……但之後發生的大多數事情（無論是在加薩、黎巴嫩還是伊朗）都不是命中注定的，也不能歸咎於恐怖主義。
  • transformative adj. 有改革能力的、變革性的
    Looking back at the past quarter-century, it becomes apparent that 9/11 was not a transformative event.
    回顧過去的四分之一世紀，顯而易見的是，9/11 並不是一個具變革性的事件。
  • goal n. 目標
    The goal must be to ensure that they do not succeed and that terrorism remains at most a limited problem.
    我們的目標必須是確保他們無法得逞，並將恐怖主義控制在最多是一個有限問題的範圍內。

文法重點

  • 附加形容詞子句（Non-defining relative clause）
    All were trained in Afghanistan and four at US flight schools as part of an operation planned, organized and carried out by al-Qaeda (“the base”), the terrorist group founded by Osama bin Laden.
    句尾的「, the terrorist group founded by Osama bin Laden」是同位語，用來補充說明前面的 al-Qaeda（蓋達組織）；而其中的過去分詞片語「founded by Osama bin Laden」則用來修飾前面的 the terrorist group，省略了「which was」，展現精簡且專業的新聞英文寫作風格。
  • 虛主詞 It 與不定詞片語
    However, with the fading of personal recollections, it seems right and necessary to ask the question: Did 9/11 mark a turning point?
    句型中使用「it seems + 形容詞 + to do...」結構，其中「it」為虛主詞，真正的主詞是後面代指發問動作的不定詞片語「to ask the question...」。這種句型常用於表達客觀看法或評論。
  • 強調句型與分詞構句的混用
    In the wake of the attacks, then-US president George W. Bush and many of his closest advisers became determined to demonstrate that the US was much more than a victim.
    「became determined to demonstrate...」使用形容詞片語作主詞補語，表達堅決的狀態。而子句中的「much more than」則運用了比較級修飾語，強調美國不只是受害者，強化了文章的論述力道。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    Twenty-five years later, after more than 800,000 US soldiers had been deployed, more than US$2 trillion spent and more than 2,400 Americans killed in combat...
    此處運用了獨立分詞結構（或省略 be 動詞的被動分詞片語），「more than 800,000 US soldiers had been deployed, more than US$2 trillion spent and more than 2,400 Americans killed...」接連列出三個附帶狀況，省去了冗長的連接詞，使句意緊湊且具備強烈的數據衝擊感。
