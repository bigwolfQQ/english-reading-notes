駐休士頓台灣書院將舉辦台灣藝術活動

Taiwan Academy in Houston to host Taiwan arts event

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • host v. 主辦，主持
    The Taiwan Academy in Houston is to host arts and cultural events this fall...
    駐休士頓台灣書院今年秋天將舉辦藝術與文化活動...
  • feature v. 以...為特色，特別介紹
    ...featuring award-winning films from Taiwan and performances that showcase the nation’s gender identity and equality values.
    ...包含來自台灣的得獎電影，以及展現國家性別認同與平權價值的表演。
  • collaborate v. 合作
    The academy said it would be collaborating with arts institutions and universities in Texas until November...
    書院表示，將與德州的藝術機構和大學合作至11月...
  • tentatively adv. 暫時地，試探性地
    ...to present a fall-themed showcase, tentatively titled “Taiwanese Sensibility in Texas.”
    ...推出一個暫定名為「德州台灣美學」的秋季主題展演。
  • screening n. 放映（電影等）
    It would include screenings of five films by renowned Taiwanese directors...
    其中將包括放映知名台灣導演...的五部電影。
  • pioneer n. 先驅，先鋒
    A pioneer of Taiwan’s road to gender equality and identity, Nymphia Wind was in December 2024 the inaugural recipient...
    作為台灣邁向性別平等與認同道路的先驅，妮妃雅於2024年12月成為首屆...
  • recipient n. 接受者，領獎人
    ...Nymphia Wind was in December 2024 the inaugural recipient of the Harvard Undergraduate Foreign Policy Initiative’s “Changemaker of the Year” award.
    ...妮妃雅於2024年12月成為哈佛大學本科生外交政策倡議首屆「年度改革推動者」獎項的獲得者。
  • participate v. 參與，參加
    ...she is to participate in speeches and discussions with academics from Houston’s University of St Thomas.
    ...她將參與和休士頓聖湯瑪斯大學學者的演講與座談。
  • recital n. 獨奏會，獨唱會
    Award-winning musicians would also hold recitals as part of the showcase.
    獲獎 musicians 也將舉辦獨奏會，作為此次展演的一部分。
  • tour v. / n. 巡迴演出，旅遊
    Pianist Lu Yi-chih (盧易之) is to tour several universities in Texas...
    鋼琴家盧易之將在德州的幾所大學進行巡迴演出...

文法重點

  • be to + 原形動詞（表示計畫、預定或官方安排）
    The Taiwan Academy in Houston is to host arts and cultural events this fall...
    新聞英語中常用「be to + VR」來報導官方已安排好、即將發生的行程或計畫，比單純用未來式更具正式與確定感。
  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾（V-ing 形容詞化用法）
    ...featuring award-winning films from Taiwan and performances that showcase the nation’s gender identity and equality values.
    句中的 featuring 開頭的現在分詞片語用來補充說明前面的 arts and cultural events，相當於關代子句「which feature...」，用以簡化句子並增加緊湊感。
  • 過去分詞片語作形容詞修飾（Award-winning）
    Award-winning musicians would also hold recitals as part of the showcase.
  • 關代所有格 whose 引導的形容詞子句
    Hong Kong writer Gigi L. Leung (梁莉姿), whose work Everyday Movement (日常運動) has won several awards in Taiwan’s literary world, would also be featured in the showcase.
    「whose work...」是用來修飾先行詞「Hong Kong writer Gigi L. Leung」的非限定關代子句。whose 在子句中代替所有格（her work），用來連接人名與其擁有的作品。
