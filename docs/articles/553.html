警方逮捕涉嫌槍擊情夫妻子的女子

Police arrest woman who allegedly shot lover’s wife

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • suspect v. / n. 懷疑，涉嫌；嫌疑犯
    A woman surnamed Yi (易), suspected of ambushing and shooting her boyfriend’s wife in Taipei on Friday, was arrested yesterday in New Taipei City’s Sanchong District (三重), police said.
    警方表示，一名姓易的女子涉嫌於週五在台北市伏擊並槍擊其男友的妻子，昨日清新北市三重區遭到逮捕。
  • residence n. 住所，住宅
    The victim, a 50-year-old woman surnamed Chao (趙), was shot by a suspect dressed in a black down jacket, baseball cap and face mask at about 2pm while she was leaving her residence, police said.
    警方表示，受害者是一名50歲姓趙的女子，下午2點左右她在離開住所時，遭到一名身穿黑色羽絨外套、頭戴棒球帽與口罩的嫌犯開槍射擊。
  • treatment n. 治療，處理
    Chao remained conscious and was rushed to National Taiwan University Hospital for emergency treatment.
    趙女送醫時保持清意識，並被緊急送往國立台灣大學附設醫院接受急救。
  • investigator n. 調查人員
    After reviewing surveillance footage, investigators concluded that many of the suspect’s movements and physical characteristics were consistent with those of a woman, they said.
    警方表示，調閱監視器畫面後，調查人員斷定嫌犯的許多舉動與身體特徵與一名女性相符。
  • consistent adj. 一致的，相符的
    After reviewing surveillance footage, investigators concluded that many of the suspect’s movements and physical characteristics were consistent with those of a woman, they said.
    警方表示，調閱監視器畫面後，調查人員斷定嫌犯的許多舉動與身體特徵與一名女性相符。
  • fled v. (flee的過去式) 逃跑，逃走
    The shooter fled in a vehicle, which was registered to Chao’s husband, a man surnamed Chen (陳), they said.
    警方說，槍手犯案後搭乘一輛車逃逸，該車登記在趙女的丈夫、一名姓陳的男子名下。
  • registered adj. / v. 登記的，註冊的
    The shooter fled in a vehicle, which was registered to Chao’s husband, a man surnamed Chen (陳), they said.
    警方說，槍手犯案後搭乘一輛車逃逸，該車登記在趙女的丈夫、一名姓陳的男子名下。
  • involvement n. 參與，牽連
    Chen denied any involvement, police said, adding that they are still searching for the firearm used in the shooting.
    警方表示，陳男否認涉案，並補充說他們仍在搜尋作案時使用的槍枝。
  • transfer v. 移交，調動，轉移
    The two suspects were transferred to prosecutors on suspicion of contravening the Controlling Guns, Ammunition and Knives Act (槍砲彈藥刀械管制條例) and attempted murder.
    兩名嫌犯因涉嫌違反《槍砲彈藥刀械管制條例》及殺人未遂，已被移送檢方偵辦。
  • financial support n. phr. 財務支援，經濟資助
    Yi and Chen allegedly met in an online chatroom, developed a romantic relationship and then lived together, with Chen providing her with financial support, police said.
    警方表示，易女與陳男據信是在網路聊天室相識，發展出戀情後便同居，陳男並提供她財務支持。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    A woman surnamed Yi (易), suspected of ambushing and shooting her boyfriend’s wife in Taipei on Friday, was arrested yesterday in New Taipei City’s Sanchong District (三重), police said.
    句中的「suspected of...」是過去分詞片語，省略了「who is / was」，用來補充修飾前面的主詞「A woman surnamed Yi」。這在新聞英語中非常常見，能有效精簡句子結構。
  • 非限定關代子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    The shooter fled in a vehicle, which was registered to Chao’s husband, a man surnamed Chen (陳), they said.
    句尾的「which was registered to...」是由關係代名詞 which 引導的非限定子句，前面加上逗號，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「a vehicle」（車輛），提供額外背景資訊且不影響主句完整性。
  • 現在分詞表附帶狀況 (Present Participle indicating Circumstance)
    Chen denied any involvement, police said, adding that they are still searching for the firearm used in the shooting.
    句中的「adding that...」是由現在分詞（adding）開頭的片語，用來表示主句動作發生時的同時狀況或補充說明，相當於「and they added that...」的簡化用法。
