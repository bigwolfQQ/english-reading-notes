美國紀念九一一事件 25 週年

US marks 25th anniversary of 9/11

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • anniversary n. 週年紀念日
    The US on Friday commemorated the 25th anniversary of the devastating Sept. 11, 2001, attacks...
    美國週五紀念毀滅性的 2001 年 9 月 11 日恐怖攻擊 25 週年...
  • absence n. 缺席、不在
    ...marked by US President Donald Trump’s absence and searing criticism from victims’ relatives.
    ...其特點是美國總統唐納·川普的缺席以及罹難者家屬的嚴厲批評。
  • conflict n. 衝突、爭端
    ...with his current conflict in Iran.
    ...與他目前在伊朗的衝突連結起來。
  • victory n. 勝利
    There can only be victory.
    唯有勝利。
  • annual adj. 年度的、每年的
    ...a huge crowd gathered for the somber annual ceremony...
    ...龐大的人群聚集參加莊嚴的年度儀式...
  • complicity n. 共謀、同謀
    ...accusing it of complicity in the attacks...
    ...指控其串通參與攻擊...
  • campaign n. 競選活動、宣傳運動
    ...despite a campaign urging him to stay away.
    ...無視一場敦促他遠離的競選活動。
  • petition n. 請願書
    The petition gathered more than 100,000 signatures.
    該請願書收集了超過 10 萬個簽名。
  • declassify v. 解除機密、解密
    The CIA also declassified and published dozens of 9/11-related presidential intelligence briefs...
    美國中情局（CIA）也解密並出版了數十份與 9/11 相關的總統情報簡報...
  • collapse v. / n. 倒塌、崩塌
    Two of the jets slammed into the World Trade Center’s Twin Towers, causing the iconic skyscrapers to collapse.
    其中兩架噴射客機撞上世界貿易中心的雙子星大樓，導致標誌性的摩天大樓倒塌。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾語 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The US on Friday commemorated the 25th anniversary of the devastating Sept. 11, 2001, attacks with a poignant ceremony in New York marked by US President Donald Trump’s absence...
    句中的 "marked by US President Donald Trump’s absence..." 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的 ceremony（儀式），相當於形容詞子句 "which was marked by..."，在新聞英語中非常常見，能讓句子更精簡。
  • 關係代名詞非限定用法 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    In an unusual move, Trump paid his respects at the Pentagon, not the event in New York, where hijacked planes destroyed the World Trade Center...
    句尾的 ", where hijacked planes destroyed..." 是非限定用法的關係副詞子句，前面逗點代表補充說明前面提過的地點 New York，不會影響主要子句的完整性。
  • 現在分詞短語作結果狀語 (Present Participle Phrase of Result)
    Two of the jets slammed into the World Trade Center’s Twin Towers, causing the iconic skyscrapers to collapse.
    逗點後的 ", causing the iconic skyscrapers to collapse" 是現在分詞短語，用來表達前面整個主句動作所產生的自然結果（造成了標誌性摩天大樓的倒塌），相當於 "and this caused..."。
