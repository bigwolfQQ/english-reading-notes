公路局警告切勿在淡江大橋惡意破壞或胡鬧

Bureau warns against Danjiang Bridge antics

單字解析

  • authorization n. 授權，許可
    People who vandalize or climb the Danjiang Bridge without authorization could face fines of up to NT$300,000, the Highway Bureau said yesterday.
    公路局昨天表示，未經授權而破壞或攀爬淡江大橋的人，恐面臨最高新台幣30萬元罰款。
  • fines n. 罰款 (fine的複數)
    People who vandalize or climb the Danjiang Bridge without authorization could face fines of up to NT$300,000, the Highway Bureau said yesterday.
    公路局昨天表示，未經授權而破壞或攀爬淡江大橋的人，恐面臨最高新台幣30萬元罰款。
  • connect v. 連接，連結
    The bridge, which connects New Taipei City’s Tamsui (淡水) and Bali (八里) districts, officially opened to traffic on May 12.
    這座連接新北市淡水區與八里區的橋樑於5月12日正式通車。
  • facility n. 設施，設備
    Graffiti or other damage, as well as unauthorized climbing is prohibited throughout the bridge area, including the main bridge, approach roads, pedestrian facilities and bicycle paths, it said.
    聲明指出，橋區全面禁止塗鴉或其他破壞行為，以及未經授權攀爬，範圍包含主橋、引道、人行設施與自行車道。
  • prohibit v. 禁止
    Graffiti or other damage, as well as unauthorized climbing is prohibited throughout the bridge area, including the main bridge, approach roads, pedestrian facilities and bicycle paths, it said.

  • cooperation n. 合作，配合
    Graffiti and other acts of vandalism damage public facilities, and undermine the bridge’s visual appeal and surrounding environment, the branch said, adding that it would step up patrols in cooperation with police and use surveillance equipment to monitor the area.
    分局表示，塗鴉與其他破壞行為會損害公共設施，並破壞橋梁視覺美感與周邊環境，並補充說將與警方合作加強巡邏，並使用監視設備監控該區域。
  • stipulate v. 規定，約定

    分局表示，根據《公路法》第72條規定，被發現未經授權使用公路土地或設施、或造成損害者，將被處以新台幣6萬元至30萬元罰款。

    People should help protect the bridge and its facilities, as the landmark serves transportation and recreational purposes, it said.
    分局表示，民眾應協助保護橋梁及其設施，因為該地標兼具交通與休閒功能。
  • witness v. 目擊，見證

    分局並補充說，目擊破壞或其他非法行為民眾應立即向有關當局舉報。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作為後位修飾 (Reduced Relative Clause)
    People found using highway land or facilities without authorization, or causing damage would be fined NT$60,000 to NT$300,000...
    句中的「found using...」是由「People who are found using...」簡化而來的分詞片語。省略了「who are」後，直接以過去分詞「found」開頭來修飾前面的名詞 People，這是多益閱讀測驗常考的精簡句構。
  • 非限定關係代間子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    The bridge, which connects New Taipei City’s Tamsui (淡水) and Bali (八里) districts, officially opened to traffic on May 12.
    句中的「which connects...」帶有逗號，用來補充說明前面的主詞「The bridge」。這類非限定關係子句在多益考試中常出現在文法題與閱讀測驗中，用來測試關係代名詞（如 which 不能用 that 代替）的用法。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Graffiti or other damage, as well as unauthorized climbing is prohibited throughout the bridge area...
    句型使用了「be + p.d. (is prohibited)」構成被動語態，強調動作的承受者（塗鴉或攀爬行為被禁止）。多益聽力與閱讀常透過主被動語態的轉換來設計考題。
