Taipei Times · 2026-09-22

華擎表示新訂單將大幅推升成長動能

ASRock says new order would drive its momentum far

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • revenue n. 營收、收益
    ASRock Industrial Computer Corp (東擎) yesterday said that strong momentum in its entertainment business would drive low-double-digit-percentage revenue growth this year.
    東擎科技昨天表示，由於接獲一家北美遊戲串流服務供應商針對高階主機板的大筆訂單，其娛樂業務的強勁動能將推動今年營收達到雙位數低標的成長率。
  • account for v. phr. 佔（比例）、說明
    The entertainment business accounted for 49 percent of the company’s revenue of NT$1.76 billion (US$55.5 million) in the first half of this year, largely driven by the North American order, company chairman James Lee (李俊瑩) told a media gathering in Taipei.
    公司董事長李俊瑩在台北的一場媒體聚會上表示，今年上半年娛樂業務占公司 17.6 億新台幣營收的 49%，這主要是受到該筆北美訂單的驅動。
  • shipment n. 出貨、運送的貨物
    ASRock had been in talks with the customer for almost two years and began first-stage shipments this year, Lee said.
    李俊瑩表示，東擎與該客戶已進行了近兩年的洽談，並於今年開始第一階段的出貨。
  • alternative n. / adj. 替代方案、替代的
    Revenue growth this year would also be supported by the company’s latest artificial intelligence (AI) box, the AI BOX-A395, which was launched last month and is suitable for enterprise teams of about 10 to 20 people as a lower-cost alternative to larger AI server platforms, Lee said.
    李俊瑩表示，公司最新推出的 AI 盒子「AI BOX-A395」也將支撐今年的營收成長，該產品於上個月上市，適合約 10 至 20 人的企業團隊，作為大型 AI 伺服器平台低成本的替代方案。
  • inquiry n. 詢問、查訊
    ASRock Industrial is initially marketing the product in Taiwan, saying it has received inquiries from schools and universities for research and teaching purposes, he said.
    他說，東擎目前正在台灣行銷該產品，並表示已收到來自學校和大學出於研究與教學目的的詢問。
  • supplier n. 供應商
    To address this issue, the company is validating alternative components and suppliers while striving to secure components for products set for production next year, he said.
    他表示，為了解決這個問題，公司正在驗證替代零組件與供應商，同時努力確保明年預定生產之產品所需的零組件。
  • net profit n. 淨利
    ASRock Industrial reported revenue rose 24.75 percent year-on-year to NT$1.83 billion and net profit increased 10.15 percent to NT$228 million last year, while earnings per share rose to NT$3.68 from NT$3.32 a year earlier.
    東擎公佈去年營收年增 24.75% 達到 18.3 億新台幣，淨利增加 10.15% 達到 2.28 億新台幣，每股盈餘則從前一年的 3.32 新台幣上升至 3.68 新台幣。
  • subsidiary n. 子公司
    The company, a subsidiary of motherboard maker ASRock Inc (華擎), is slated to debut its shares on the Taiwan Stock Exchange later next month.
    該公司為主機板製造商華擎的子公司，預定於下個月底在台灣證券交易所掛牌上市。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表附帶狀況或補充說明）
    The shipments are expected to continue through the end of this month, with second-stage shipments likely to start in the first quarter of next year, he said, adding that the customer has positioned the project as a long-term deal.
    句尾的「adding that...」是由連接詞引導的分詞構句，主詞為句子的主事者 he。在此用現在分詞表達「一邊說著……，同時補充說……」，省略了連接詞與主詞，能使長句更為精簡緊湊。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句
    Revenue growth this year would also be supported by the company’s latest artificial intelligence (AI) box, the AI BOX-A395, which was launched last month and is suitable for enterprise teams of about 10 to 20 people as a lower-cost alternative to larger AI server platforms, Lee said.
  • 被動語態與分詞形容詞
    To address this issue, the company is validating alternative components and suppliers while striving to secure components for products set for production next year, he said.
