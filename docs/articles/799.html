  • meet with phr. 會面、會晤
    Foreign Minister Lin Chia-lung (林佳龍) met Tuesday with a visiting Honduran congressman in Taipei...
    外交部長林佳龍週二在台北會見了一位來訪的宏都拉斯國會議員...
  • possibility n. 可能性
    ...and said Taiwan is open to all possibilities for future relations...

    ...they had achieved fruitful results through cooperation in various areas, according to MOFA.

  • cherish v. 珍惜
    Taiwan continues to cherish its long-standing friendship with the people of Honduras, Lin was cited as saying in the press release.
    新聞稿引述林佳龍的話指出，台灣持續珍惜與宏都拉斯人民長久以來的友誼。
  • pledge v. 承諾、保證

    He pledged that the Taiwan government would maintain a "pragmatic and open approach" to the prospect of future relations...
    他承諾台灣政府將對未來關係的前景維持「務實且開放的做法」...
  • prospect n. 前景、前途
    ...to the prospect of future relations that could "create all kinds of possibilities," MOFA said.

  • explore v. 探索、探討

    ...he will be exploring opportunities for future cooperation based on the existing foundation, the press release stated.

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾
    Foreign Minister Lin Chia-lung (林佳龍) met Tuesday with a visiting Honduran congressman in Taipei...
    句中的「visiting」是由動詞 visit 轉變而來的現在分詞，在此作為形容詞放在名詞 congressman 前面，用來修飾「來訪的」國會議員。
  • 被動語態的引述句 (be cited as doing)
    Taiwan continues to cherish its long-standing friendship with the people of Honduras, Lin was cited as saying in the press release.
    新聞英語中常使用被動語態「be cited as saying...」（被引述說……）來客觀呈現某人發表的言論，避免直接使用主動語態。
  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense)
    ...they had achieved fruitful results through cooperation in various areas, according to MOFA.
    「had achieved」為過去完成式（had + 過去分詞），用來表達在過去某個時間點（雙方身為邦交國的時期）之前已經完成或持續一段時間的動作與成果。
  • 複合關係代名詞子句
    He is the first Honduran politician to publicly visit Taiwan since the Central American country severed diplomatic ties...
    句中的「to publicly visit Taiwan」是不定式片語，用來修飾前面的序數形容詞「the first」，在句法功能上相當於一個形容詞子句，用來限定「第一位公開訪問台灣的宏都拉斯政治人物」。
