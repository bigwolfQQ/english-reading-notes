單字解析

  • pressure v. 施壓，對……施加壓力
    Chinese President Xi Jinping will try to pressure US President Donald Trump to postpone arms sales to Taiwan...
    中國國家主席習近平將試圖施壓美國總統川普，延後對台灣的軍售……
  • postpone v. 延期，延後
    ...try to pressure US President Donald Trump to postpone arms sales to Taiwan...
    ……將試圖施壓美國總統川普，延後對台灣的軍售……
  • deployment n. 部署，調動
    ...and roll back deployments of US forces in the Indo-Pacific region, a senior Taiwanese official said yesterday.
    ……並削減美軍在印太地區的部署，一名台灣資深官員昨天表示。
  • priority n. 優先事項，首要任務
    Xi’s highest priority is to slow Trump’s approval of arms packages to Taiwan at the meetings...
    習近平在會晤中的最高優先事項是減緩川普對台灣軍售包裹的批准……
  • approval n. 批准，同意
    Xi’s highest priority is to slow Trump’s approval of arms packages to Taiwan...
    習近平在會晤中的最高優先事項是減緩川普對台灣軍售包裹的批准……
  • authorize v. 授權，批准
    ...when he would again press Trump to delay authorizing the weapon packages until Christmas...
    ……屆時他將再次施壓川普將武器包裹的授權延遲到聖誕節……
  • disrupt v. 擾亂，打亂
    Belated delivery of US weapon systems would disrupt Taiwan’s force-building efforts...
    美國武器系統的交貨延誤將打亂台灣的國防建設努力……
  • boost v. 促進，增強，提高
    ...which would boost the Chinese People’s Liberation Army’s ability to threaten the First Island Chain...
    ……這將助長中國人民解放軍威脅第一島鏈的能力……
  • facilitate v. 促進，使便利
    That would facilitate China’s aims to push the US out of the First Island Chain...
    這將助長中國將美國擠出第一島鏈的目標……
  • strategy n. 策略，戰略
    Part of China’s strategy involves promoting the narrative that US military presence in the region is to blame...
    中國戰略的一部分包括宣傳這樣一種論調：美軍在該地區的存在是罪魁禍首……
  • coercion n. 強迫，高壓，脅迫
    ...by using a combination of coercion and incentives on the Trump administration...
    ……對川普政府採取脅迫與利誘並施的組合拳……
  • incentive n. 激勵措施，誘因，好處
    ...by using a combination of coercion and incentives on the Trump administration...
    ……對川普政府採取脅迫與利誘並施的組合拳……
  • in exchange phr. 作為交換
    In exchange, Xi would ask Trump to reduce US forces and security guarantees along the First Island Chain...
    作為交換，習近平將要求美國減少第一島鏈沿線的美軍與安全保障……
  • relocate v. 重新安置，把……遷到新址
    ...recall US military advisers from the nation and relocate US forces to the Second Island Chain...
    ……從我國撤回美軍顧問，並將美軍重新部署至第二島鏈……
  • incompatible adj. 不相容的，矛盾的
    Those signal that Washington is aware of Beijing’s strategy and considers its interests incompatible with China’s...
    這些訊號表明華盛頓已經意識到北京的戰略，並認為自身的利益與中國的利益不相容……

文法重點

  • 假設語氣（省略 if 的倒裝句型）
    Should the US lower its presence in the Indo-Pacific region, it would create a strategic vacuum beneficial to China’s ambitions...
    此句原本是條件句 If the US should lower its presence...，當省略連接詞 if 時，助動詞 Should 移至主詞前面形成倒裝句，常用於正式或書面英文中，表達對未來不大可能發生或假設情況的臆測。
  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾（形容詞功能）
    There is a Beijing-directed campaign designed to make a strategic retreat from Asia palatable in mainstream political circles in the US...
    句中的「designed to make...」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 campaign（運動），相當於關代子句「which is designed to make...」，能讓句子結構更精簡。
  • 動名詞片語作受詞與主詞補語
    Part of China’s strategy involves promoting the narrative that US military presence in the region is to blame...
    動詞 involve 後方常接動名詞（V-ing）作受詞，此處 promoting 意指「推動、宣傳」，而後方的同位語子句（that...）則用來補充說明 narrative（論調）的具體內容。
