台灣羽球選手在越南公開賽奪下男雙冠軍

Taiwan shuttlers win men's doubles final at Vietnam badminton open

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • tournament n. 錦標賽，競賽
    Taiwan's Chen Zhi-ray (陳子睿) and Lin Yu-chieh (林煜傑) won the men's doubles final at the Yonex-Sunrise Vietnam Open badminton tournament on Sunday...
    台灣的陳子睿與林煜傑於週日在優乃克旭日越南羽球公開賽決賽中贏得男子雙打冠軍...
  • defeat v. 擊敗，戰勝
    Chen and Lin defeated Singapore's Terry Hee (許永凱) and Howin Wong (黃家豪) at the Badminton World Federation (BWF) Super 100 event...
    陳子睿和林煜傑在世界羽球聯盟（BWF）超級100賽事中擊敗新加坡的許永凱與黃家豪...
  • victory n. 勝利
    The victory marked the duo's first time winning a BWF Tour event since they paired up in 2024.
    這場勝利標誌著該雙人組自2024年搭檔以來首次贏得BWF巡迴賽事。
  • claim v. 奪得，取得（榮譽、勝利等）
    The 24-year-old Chen and 26-year-old Lin had previously claimed top spots in three lower-level tourneys...
    24歲的陳子睿和26歲的林煜傑先前曾在三個較低級別的巡迴賽中奪得榜首...
  • challenging adj. 具挑戰性的，艱鉅的
    In Sunday's match, Lin said he felt the most challenging part for him was defense...
    在週日的比賽中，林煜傑表示他覺得對他來說最具挑戰性的部分是防守...
  • expectations n. 期待，期望
    "We performed to our expectations in this finals match," Chen said.
    陳子睿說：「我們在這場決賽中發揮符合了我們的期望。」
  • position v. 放置，定位
    "Yu-chieh controlled the front and center court very well to position the shuttlecocks for me in the back court..."
    「煜傑把前場和中場控制得很好，把羽球定位好讓我能在後場...」
  • delegation n. 代表團
    Despite the fact that the duo was not named in Taiwan's delegation to the 2026 Asian Games...
    儘管這對組合未被列入台灣參加2026年亞運會的代表團名單中...
  • participate v. 參加，參與
    ...Chen said he and his teammate will aim to continue participating in international competitions to increase their overall rankings.
    ...陳子睿表示他和隊友將旨在繼續參加國際比賽以提高他們的整體排名。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作補充說明（分詞構句）
    ...before notching a breakthrough second-place finish at the BWF World Tour Super 300 US Open in June.
    句型「before +V-ing」用來表示「在……之前」，此處的 notching 為現在分詞片語，用來連接前一個動作並補充說明隨後取得的成果。
  • 表原因的現在分詞短語 (as 引導的副詞子句)
    ...Lin said he felt the most challenging part for him was defense, as both teams attempted to get ahead early in each game.
    連接詞 as 在此用來引導原因子句（相當於 because），解釋為什麼防守會是最具挑戰性的部分：因為雙方球隊都試圖在每局一開始就取得領先。
  • Despite the fact that 複合連接詞用法
    Despite the fact that the duo was not named in Taiwan's delegation to the 2026 Asian Games in Japan's Aichi Prefecture later this month...
    「Despite the fact that + 子句」意為「儘管事實是……」，用來帶出讓步語氣。Despite 作為介系詞後面通常接名詞，但加上 the fact that 之後就可以直接接完整的子句。
