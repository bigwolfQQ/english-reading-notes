行政院長：電廠工程按計畫進行中

Power site work on schedule: premier

  • dismantle v. 拆卸，拆除
    Work at Taichung Power Plant is on schedule to dismantle two coal-fired units amid its transition from coal to natural gas by 2034, Premier Cho Jung-tai (卓榮泰) said yesterday during an inspection of the site.
  • coal-fired adj. 燃煤的，以煤炭為燃料的
    Work at Taichung Power Plant is on schedule to dismantle two coal-fired units amid its transition from coal to natural gas by 2034, Premier Cho Jung-tai (卓榮泰) said yesterday during an inspection of the site.
  • dispatch n. （電力）調度，派遣
    The plant’s first gas-fired unit is expected to begin commercial operations soon, while the second is expected to begin accepting dispatch next month, with March next year the target for commercial operations, he said.
  • contingency n. 突發事件，意外事故
    The plant’s six coal-fired emergency reserve units would be tightly regulated and used only for limited periods in the event of major contingencies such as national security concerns or natural disasters, he said.
  • tightly regulated phr. 受到嚴格規範／管制的
    The plant’s six coal-fired emergency reserve units would be tightly regulated and used only for limited periods in the event of major contingencies such as national security concerns or natural disasters, he said.
  • reassessment n. 重新評估，再次審核
    Taipower later said in a statement that a formal reassessment of the plans for emergency reserve units is scheduled for 2032.
  • environmental impact assessment n. 環境影響評估（簡稱環評）
    The four gas units planned under the plant’s second-phase expansion have passed an environmental impact assessment, Cho said, adding that they are scheduled to go online in stages from 2031 to 2034, with four of the 10 coal-fired units to be dismantled and six kept as reserve units.
  • go online phr. （設備或系統）投入運作，上線
    The four gas units planned under the plant’s second-phase expansion have passed an environmental impact assessment, Cho said, adding that they are scheduled to go online in stages from 2031 to 2034, with four of the 10 coal-fired units to be dismantled and six kept as reserve units.
  • promptly adv. 迅速地，即時地
    The city government hopes that the central government would promptly present a coal-reduction plan for the plant through 2030, it said.
  • concrete adj. 具體的，明確的
    The Ministry of Economic Affairs and Taipower have proposed reducing coal consumption to 10.5 million tonnes this year, but the next phase of reductions would not take place until after 2031 it said, calling on the central government to quickly make a commitment and set out concrete measures for further reducing coal use.

文法重點

  • 複合結構以 with 引導附帶狀況 (with + 名詞 + 補語/分詞)
    The plant’s first gas-fired unit is expected to begin commercial operations soon, while the second is expected to begin accepting dispatch next month, with March next year the target for commercial operations, he said.
    句中的「with March next year the target for commercial operations」使用了「with + 名詞 + 名詞補語」的獨立結構，用來簡潔地補充說明「以明年 3 月為商業運轉目標」這項附帶資訊，省去了完整的句子結構，使語氣更為精練。
  • 獨立分詞結構表達附帶細節 (with + 名詞 + 不定詞/過去分詞)
    The four gas units planned under the plant’s second-phase expansion have passed an environmental impact assessment, Cho said, adding that they are scheduled to go online in stages from 2031 to 2034, with four of the 10 coal-fired units to be dismantled and six kept as reserve units.
    句尾「with four of the 10 coal-fired units to be dismantled and six kept as reserve units」為 with 引導的附帶狀況結構。其中「to be dismantled」用不定詞被動態表示「未來將被拆除」，而「kept as...」為過去分詞省略 being，表示「被保留為……」，用以清楚對比並交代兩組機組的不同後續處理方案。
  • 分詞構句表伴隨動作或說明 (Participle Clause)
    The Ministry of Economic Affairs and Taipower have proposed reducing coal consumption to 10.5 million tonnes this year, but the next phase of reductions would not take place until after 2031 it said, calling on the central government to quickly make a commitment and set out concrete measures for further reducing coal use.
    句末的「calling on...」為現在分詞引導的分詞構句，修飾前方的動作。主詞同為「the city government」，透過分詞構句可流暢連結「指出事實」與「順勢提出呼籲」兩個伴隨發生的動作，避免連用過多屬性相同的完整句子。
  • not... until... 句型（直到……才……）
    The Ministry of Economic Affairs and Taipower have proposed reducing coal consumption to 10.5 million tonnes this year, but the next phase of reductions would not take place until after 2031 it said, calling on the central government to quickly make a commitment and set out concrete measures for further reducing coal use.
    「would not take place until after 2031」運用了「not... until...」結構，表示「在 2031 年之後以前不會發生」，即「要等到 2031 年之後才會進行下一階段的減量」，用來明確強調時間點的延後。
