洪水過後：尼泊爾受災最嚴重地區難以想像的人類苦難

After the flood: human suffering on an unfathomable scale in Nepal’s worst-hit districts

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • retrieve v. 取回、找回、重新挽回
    Rescue workers have still not reached Dil Bahadur Shrestha’s home, so he returned to try to retrieve her body himself.
    救援人員尚未抵達迪爾·巴哈杜爾·什雷斯塔的家，因此他返回試圖自己找回她的遺體。
  • disaster n. 災難、災害
    He knows in his heart that his wife is gone, but like thousands caught up in the disaster, her body has yet to be found and she is counted among the missing.
    他心裡知道他的妻子已經不在了，但像成千上萬捲入這場災難的人一樣，她的遺體尚未被找到，她也被列入失蹤人員名單。
  • debris n. 殘骸、碎屑
    For now, he has been using his hands to try to find her as he picks through the ruins of his house.
    目前，他只能一邊翻找房屋的殘垣斷壁，一邊用雙手試圖尋找她的下落。
  • evacuate v. 疏散、撤離
    A week after the disaster, survivors who fled up mountainsides were still being collected by army helicopters and evacuated to the army base in Dhunche, Rasuwa district’s administrative headquarters.
    災難發生一週後，逃到山坡上的倖存者仍被陸軍直升機接走，疏散到拉蘇瓦地區行政總部杜恩切的陸軍基地。
  • hinders v. 妨礙、阻礙
    The treacherous conditions have made it difficult for aid to reach the worst-hit areas and hindered the rescue and relief operations.
    險惡的狀況使援助物資難以到達受災最嚴重的地區，並阻礙了救援和救災行動。
  • supplies n. 用品、物資、存貨
    At a small relief camp on the road between Betrawati and Dhunche set up by Nepal’s Dhulikhel hospital, people collected medicine and food supplies.
    在貝特拉瓦蒂和杜恩切之間的道路上，由尼泊爾杜利克爾醫院設立的一個小型救援營地裡，人們領取了藥品和食品供應。
  • displaced adj. 無家可歸的、流離失所的
    However, many of the local families had been displaced by the 2015 earthquake and still lived in informal settlements along the riverbank.
    然而，許多當地家庭在 2015 年地震中流離失所，至今仍住在河岸邊的臨時定居點。
  • operations n. 運作、營運、行動
    On Tuesday, police search and rescue teams confirmed they had finally been able to begin ground operations in the border village of Timure, recovering 90 bodies so far.
    週二，警方搜救隊確認他們終於能夠在邊境村莊蒂穆雷展開地面行動，迄今已尋獲 90 具遺體。
  • damage n. 損害、損失
    The surge of water did reach the vehicle, but thankfully it did not cause much damage.
    水流確實衝到了車上，但謝天謝地，它沒有造成太大的破壞。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾或副詞子句簡化（分詞構句）
    Searching for the body of his wife in the mud, Dil Bahadur Shrestha lamented that he did not even have a shovel left.
    句首的「Searching for the body...」是由「As he was searching for the body...」簡化而來的現在分詞片語，用來修飾主句的主詞 Dil Bahadur Shrestha，表示同時發生的動作或背景。
  • 過去完成被動語態 (Past Perfect Passive)
    Most houses were washed away entirely or flipped on their sides by the sheer force of the floods.
    此句使用被動語態（were washed away / were flipped），強調房屋是「被」洪水的力量沖走或翻轉，凸顯大自然災害的被動承受與破壞力。
  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense)
    The couple had already rebuilt their home once after an earthquake in 2015...
    「had already rebuilt」表示在過去某個時間點（2015年地震之後）之前已經完成的動作，用來表達「過去中的過去」。
  • 假設語氣（與過去事實相反）
    “Maybe she thought the flood wouldn’t reach inside the house,” he said...
    引號內使用「wouldn't reach」表達過去的主觀臆測，即他推測當時妻子心中所想的假設情況。
