5至6月統一發票有6張千萬特獎未領

6 NT$10 million prizes remain unclaimed in May-June receipt lottery

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • unclaimed adj. 無人認領的，未被領取的
    Six NT$10 million special prizes and seven NT$2 million grand prizes in Taiwan's May-June uniform invoice lottery remained unclaimed as of Tuesday...
    截至週二為止，台灣5至6月統一發票共有6張1,000萬元特別獎及7張200萬元特獎無人認領...
  • delivery n. 遞送，外送，交付
    ...including one NT$10 million winner who spent only NT$10 on an Uber Eats delivery order, according to the Ministry of Finance (MOF).
    ...根據財政部（MOF）指出，其中包括一名僅花新台幣10元叫Uber Eats外送訂單的1,000萬元得主。
  • claim v. 認領，領取，索取
    The MOF said the 13 winners need to claim their prizes by Nov. 5.
    財政部表示，這13名得主須於11月5日前領獎。
  • in total phr. 總計，總共
    The eight-digit serial number that won the NT$10 million special prize for May-June was 38548029, with 18 winning receipts in total.
    5至6月新台幣1,000萬元特別獎的8位數中獎號碼為38548029，總計有18張中獎發票。
  • purchase n. / v. 購買，採購；購買的東西
    ...including one for a NT$620 purchase of an app from Google Play and a NT$2,106 receipt issued by Chunghwa Telecom Co. for monthly telecom services.
    ...包括一張在Google Play購買應用程式花費620元的發票，以及一張由中華電信股份有限公司開立、金額2,106元的每月電信服務發票。
  • issue v. 發行，開立（發票、證件等）
    The fourth NT$10 million prize receipt was issued by Yahsin bakery in Sanchong District, New Taipei...
    第四張1,000萬元中獎發票是由新北市三重區的雅馨麵包店所開立...
  • revenue n. （國家）稅收，收益，歲入
    The lottery is funded by 3 percent of annual business tax revenues, which are built into the prices of most goods and services under Taiwan's tax laws.
    該獎金池由每年3%的營業稅收所資助，這些稅收根據台灣稅法已內含在大多數商品和服務的價格中。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾（形容詞用）
    In addition to the receipt issued for an Uber Eats order, the MOF said the other five receipts for the unclaimed NT$10 million special prizes...
    句中的「issued for an Uber Eats order」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「the receipt」，相當於關代子句「which was issued for an Uber Eats order」，在多益閱讀測驗中是常見的精簡句構。
  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況
    ...with 18 winning receipts in total.
    「with + 名詞 + 分詞/形容詞/介系詞片語」的結構常用來表示伴隨狀態或補充說明，在此處意指「總計有18張中獎發票」，在商業英文寫作與多益測驗中非常實用。
  • 關係代名詞子句（限定用法）
    The eight-digit serial number that won the NT$10 million special prize for May-June was 38548029...
    句中的「that won the NT$10 million special prize for May-June」是限制性關係代名詞子句，用來修飾並限定前面的先行詞「The eight-digit serial number」，幫助讀者明確辨識是哪一個號碼。
  • 被動語態
    The lottery is funded by 3 percent of annual business tax revenues...
    主詞「The lottery」接受動作，因此使用現在式被動語態「is funded by」，強調獎金的來源是由營業稅收所提供，多益聽力與閱讀常考被動語態的辨識。
