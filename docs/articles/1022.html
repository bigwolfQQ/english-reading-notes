失業率持穩、偏低且具季節性

Unemployment steady, low and seasonal

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • unemployment rate n. phr. 失業率
    Taiwan’s unemployment rate rose for a third straight month last month as graduates entered the job market...
    隨著畢業生進入求職市場，台灣上個月的失業率連續第三個月上升...
  • job market n. phr. 就業市場、求職市場
    ...as graduates entered the job market, but the increase was largely seasonal...
    ...隨著畢業生進入求職市場，但此一增幅主要具季節性...
  • seasonally adjusted adj. 經季節調整的
    On a seasonally adjusted basis, the rate edged down to 3.32 percent from 3.33 percent...
    經季節調整後，失業率從 3.33% 微降至 3.32%...
  • labor-force participation rate n. phr. 勞動力參與率、勞參率
    “The rise in unemployment is seasonal, as the labor-force participation rate is growing and the seasonally adjusted unemployment rate is declining,”...
    「失業率的上升是季節性的，因為勞動參與率正在成長，而經季節調整後的失業率則在下降，」...
  • workforce n. 勞動人口、工作人口、員工總數
    With more people entering the workforce, Taiwan’s labor participation rate rose to 59.69 percent...
    隨著更多人進入勞動市場，台灣的勞動參與率升至 59.69%...
  • labor shortage n. phr. 勞動力短缺、缺工
    The government has sought to encourage women and older people to remain in or return to the workforce to help ease labor shortages...
    政府一直設法鼓勵女性和年長者留在或重返勞動市場以協助緩解缺工...
  • bear fruit v. phr. 奏效、開花結果
    ...and “the increase indicates that government efforts are bearing fruit,” Tan said.
    ...譚文玲指出，「這項增加顯示政府的努力正開花結果。」
  • educational attainment n. phr. 教育程度
    By educational attainment, people with university degrees had the highest unemployment rate at 4.56 percent...
    按教育程度來看，具備大學學歷者的失業率最高，達 4.56%...
  • employee n. 受僱員工、員工
    ...adding that non-regular workers accounted for about 36.5 percent of employees in Japan and 38.2 percent in South Korea...
    ...她並補充說明，日本的非典型就業勞工約佔受僱員工的 36.5%，南韓佔 38.2%...
  • deterioration n. 惡化、退化
    The increase in unemployment rate last month is unlikely to signal a deterioration in the labor market...
    上個月失業率的上升不太可能預示勞動市場惡化...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附加說明（伴隨狀況）
    Taiwan’s unemployment rate rose for a third straight month last month as graduates entered the job market, but the increase was largely seasonal, with the jobless rate for the month remaining the lowest in more than two decades, government data showed.
    句中的「with + 名詞 + 分詞（remaining）」形成獨立分詞結構，用來補充說明主句發生時同時存在的背景狀況或伴隨結果，意指「在……的情況下」。
  • 分詞構句作原因或結果補充
    The number of unemployed people rose by 2,000 to 411,000, as the number of first-time job seekers increased by 3,000 during the month, Tan said.
    句尾的「as」引導一個原因子句，用來解釋失業人數上升的背景原因。此外，像「driven by increased participation...」（第6段）則是過去分詞片語作後位修飾或原因狀語，常出現在多益閱讀與商務新聞中。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞 / 不定詞作受詞與目的
    The government has sought to encourage women and older people to remain in or return to the workforce to help ease labor shortages...
    句中結合了多益常考的文法結構：「seek to + 原形動詞」（設法要做……）、「encourage + 受詞 + to V」（鼓勵某人做……），以及用不定詞片語「to help ease...」來表達目的（為了幫助緩解……）。
  • 形容詞最高級的用法
    Young workers continued to face the highest unemployment, as the jobless rate among people aged 15 to 24 was 11.74 percent...
    句中的「the highest unemployment」使用了形容詞最高級，用來強調年輕族群面臨最嚴重的失業情況。在多益考試中，常考最高級前必須加定冠詞 「the」的文法規則。
  • 分詞片語與介系詞片語作後位修飾
    By educational attainment, people with university degrees had the highest unemployment rate at 4.56 percent, followed by those with graduate degrees at 3.16 percent...
    句中的「followed by...」是由過去分詞片語簡化而來的結構（原句約略等於 and they were followed by...），在新聞英文和商業報告中非常常見，用來列舉數據的順序或排名。
