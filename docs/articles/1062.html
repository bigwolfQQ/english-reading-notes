文法重點

  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況或結果（Present Participle as Result/Adverbial）
    The five-run inning included two hit-by-pitches, a fielding error, an RBI double by Liu Ji-hong (劉基鴻) and a three-run double by Yang Po-hsiang (陽柏翔), extending Taiwan's lead to 7-0.
    句尾的「extending Taiwan's lead to 7-0」是由分詞片語構成，用來補充說明前面整句（單局灌進5分）所帶來的自然結果。這種用法在新聞英文中非常常見，能讓句子結構更緊湊，省去連接詞。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾（Past Participle Phrase as Post-modifier）
    Taiwan's men's baseball team on Wednesday advanced to the four-team super round at the 2026 Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games after defeating Hong Kong 11-0 in a game shortened to seven innings under the mercy rule.
    句中的「shortened to seven innings under the mercy rule」是一個過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「a game」。原形概念相當於「which was shortened to...」，省略了關代與be動詞，使語氣更為精簡幹練。
  • 獨立分詞構句表同時發生的動作（Absolute Construction / Participle Clause）
    Meanwhile, Taiwan starting pitcher Kuo Tzu-chuan (郭子銓) dominated on the mound, throwing six scoreless innings while allowing just two hits and striking out 10 Hong Kong batters.
    句中「throwing six scoreless innings...」以及後方的「while allowing... and striking out...」運用了現在分詞，用來描寫主詞 Kuo Tzu-chuan 在投手丘上主宰比賽時，同時進行的細部動作與數據表現。
