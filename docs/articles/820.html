部長對於宏都拉斯關係持「開放」態度

Minister ‘open’ to Honduras relations

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • possibility n. 可能性
    Taiwan is open to all possibilities for relations with Honduras, Minister of Foreign Affairs Lin Chia-lung (林佳龍) said on Tuesday during a meeting with a Honduran congressman in Taipei.
    外交部長林佳龍周二在台北與宏都拉斯國會議員會面時表示，台灣對與宏都拉斯關係的所有可能性持開放態度。
  • friendship n. 友誼
    Lin welcomed the visit by Jorge Calix, caucus whip of Honduras’ opposition Liberal Party, saying during the meeting that the visit was “a concrete move to show his friendship and support for Taiwan,” the Ministry of Foreign Affairs said in a statement on Tuesday evening.
    外交部周二晚間發布新聞稿表示，林佳龍對宏都拉斯在野黨自由黨黨鞭喬治・卡利克斯（Jorge Calix）的訪問表示歡迎，並在會中指出這次訪問是「展現對台灣友誼與支持的具體行動」。
  • cooperation n. 合作
    When Taiwan and Honduras were diplomatic allies, they had achieved fruitful results through cooperation in several areas, Lin said.
    林佳龍表示，當台灣與宏都拉斯身為邦交國時，雙方在多個領域的合作都取得了豐碩的成果。
  • maintain v. 維持、保持
    The government will maintain a “pragmatic and open approach” to the prospect of relations that could “create all kinds of possibilities,” he said.
    他說，政府將對可能「創造各種可能性」的關係前景保持「務實且開放的做法」。
  • approach n. 方法、途徑
    The government will maintain a “pragmatic and open approach” to the prospect of relations that could “create all kinds of possibilities,” he said.
    他說，政府將對可能「創造各種可能性」的關係前景保持「務實且開放的做法」。
  • prospect n. 前景、前途、期望
    The government will maintain a “pragmatic and open approach” to the prospect of relations that could “create all kinds of possibilities,” he said.
    他說，政府將對可能「創造各種可能性」的關係前景保持「務實且開放的做法」。
  • model n. 模範、典型、模型
    The statement cited Calix as saying that he sees Taiwan as a model of freedom, democracy and economic development.
    新聞稿引述卡利克斯的話表示，他視台灣為自由、民主與經濟發展的典範。
  • explore v. 探索、探究
    Calix said he would be exploring opportunities for cooperation based on the existing foundation.
    卡利克斯表示，他將在現有基礎上尋求合作機會。
  • foundation n. 基礎、地基
    Calix said he would be exploring opportunities for cooperation based on the existing foundation.
    卡利克斯表示，他將在現有基礎上尋求合作機會。
  • disclose v. 透露、公開
    The ministry did not disclose when the Honduran politician arrived in Taiwan or how long he is staying.
    外交部未透露這名宏都拉斯政治人物何時抵達台灣，也未說明他將停留多久。

文法重點

  • 現在完成進行式 (Present Perfect Continuous Tense)
    Calix has been serving as a member of the Honduran National Congress since 2014.
    結構為「have/has been + V-ing」。用來表示一個動作從過去某個時間點（此處為 2014 年）開始，持續到現在，且這個狀態可能還會持續下去。
  • 形容詞子句（關係代名詞 who 引導）
    He is the first Honduran politician to publicly visit Taiwan since the Central American country severed formal diplomatic ties with Taipei in favor of Beijing in March 2023...
    句中的不定詞片語「to publicly visit Taiwan」在此修飾前面的序數名詞「the first Honduran politician」，在英文中當先行詞有 first、last、only 等最高級或序數修飾時，常使用不定詞片語來代替關係代名詞子句進行後位修飾。
  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense)
    When Taiwan and Honduras were diplomatic allies, they had achieved fruitful results through cooperation in several areas, Lin said.
    結構為「had + 過去分詞 (V-pp)」。用來表示在過去某一時間點（were 當時）之前已經完成的動作或狀態。
