Taiwan Lottery to add 70 NT$1 million prizes for Mid-Autumn Festival

單字解析

  • promotion n. 促銷、宣傳
    Taiwan Lottery will add NT$400 million (US$12.57 million) in prizes to three popular games for a Mid-Autumn Festival promotion, including 70 additional NT$1 million prizes for its Lotto 6/49 game, the company said Tuesday.
    台灣彩券公司週二表示，為了中秋節促銷活動，將針對三款熱門遊戲加碼總計 4 億新台幣（約 1,257 美元）的獎金，其中包括大樂透增開 70 組 100 萬元獎項。
  • additional adj. 額外的、附加的

  • run v. 營運、進行、持續
    The promotion will run from Sept. 15 to Oct. 23, with 12 Lotto 6/49 draws offering 70 special NT$1 million prizes in total, Taiwan Lottery President Hsieh Chih-hong (謝志宏) said.

  • offer v. 提供

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附帶狀況（補充說明）
    The promotion will run from Sept. 15 to Oct. 23, with 12 Lotto 6/49 draws offering 70 special NT$1 million prizes in total, Taiwan Lottery President Hsieh Chih-hong (謝志宏) said.
    句型「with + 名詞 + 現在分詞 (V-ing)」用來表達伴隨發生的狀況或補充說明。在此句中，「with 12 Lotto 6/49 draws offering...」用來補充說明該活動伴隨著 12 期大樂透提供特別獎項的細節。
  • 關係代間詞非限定用法（which 引導的子句）
    Players who match five of the eight special numbers will win NT$5,000, which is a fixed prize and will not be shared among winners.
    句中的逗號後方接「which」引導的非限定子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞（5,000 元獎金）。這種用法不能用 that 代替，主要功能是提供額外的補充資訊。
  • 被動語態 (Be + Vpp)
    If multiple tickets win the same special prize, the prize money will be divided among the winners, the company said.
    句中「will be divided」使用了被動語態，因為獎金（prize money）是被動被贏得者們所平分，強調動作的承受者而非主動發動者。
