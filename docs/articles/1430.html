單字解析

  • campaign v. / n. 從事運動，發起活動
    South Korean activists campaigning for workplace safety in the nation’s booming semiconductor industry yesterday called on the government to recognize the cancer diagnoses of three former SK Hynix employees as work-related illnesses.
    南韓爭取國內蓬勃發展的半導體產業職場安全的活動人士昨天呼籲政府，承認三名前SK海力士員工的癌症診斷為職業病。
  • recognize v. 承認，認可
    South Korean activists campaigning for workplace safety in the nation’s booming semiconductor industry yesterday called on the government to recognize the cancer diagnoses of three former SK Hynix employees as work-related illnesses.
    南韓爭取國內蓬勃發展的半導體產業職場安全的活動人士昨天呼籲政府，承認三名前SK海力士員工的癌症診斷為職業病。
  • key player n. phr. 關鍵參與者，要角
    SK Hynix, along with fellow South Korean tech giant Samsung Electronics, has emerged as a key player in the booming artificial intelligence (AI) industry, with its cutting-edge memory chips crucial to powering the sector’s rapid growth.
    SK海力士與同為南韓科技巨頭的三星電子已成為蓬勃發展的人工智慧（AI）產業中的關鍵參與者，其先進的記憶體晶片對推動該產業的快速成長至關重要。
  • crucial adj. 至關重要的，決定性的
    SK Hynix, along with fellow South Korean tech giant Samsung Electronics, has emerged as a key player in the booming artificial intelligence (AI) industry, with its cutting-edge memory chips crucial to powering the sector’s rapid growth.

  • exposure n. 暴露，接觸
    Establishing direct links between semiconductor work and certain cancers can be contentious, with research inconclusive, but fabrication and assembly plant workers can face exposure to solvents, acids, toxic metals and chemical gases, the US Occupational Safety and Health Administration said.
    美國職業安全與健康管理局表示，在半導體工作與特定癌症之間建立直接聯繫可能會引起爭議，且研究結果尚無定論，但製造廠和組裝廠的工人可能會接觸到溶劑、酸類、有毒金屬和化學氣體。
  • compensation n. 補償，賠償金
    The company, which did not address the specific claims, said it had taken “proactive industrial health measures including the development of a comprehensive support and compensation plan.”
    該公司未對具體指控做出回應，僅表示已採取「積極的工業衛生措施，包括制定全面的支援與補償計畫」。
  • agency n. 局，機構，署
    “I believe the nature of her job meant that she was inevitably exposed to various hazardous substances,” Kim Yong-il, the husband of Yoon Young-mi, told the briefing outside the offices of the service, which is a government agency.
    尹英美的丈夫金永日（音譯）在該政府機構辦公室外的簡報會上表示：「我相信她的工作性質意味著她不可避免地接觸到了各種有害物質。」
  • authorities n. 官方，當局
    “The company and the relevant authorities must protect workers’ right to health when it comes to occupational diseases in the semiconductor industry,” Kim said.
    金永日說：「當涉及半導體產業的職業病時，公司和相關主管機關必須保護工人的健康權。」

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作後位修飾
    South Korean activists campaigning for workplace safety in the nation’s booming semiconductor industry yesterday called on the government...
    句中的 "campaigning for workplace safety in the nation’s booming semiconductor industry" 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "South Korean activists"，相當於形容詞子句 "who campaigned..." 的簡化用法，在新聞英語中非常常見以達到精簡篇幅的效果。
  • With + 名詞 + 形容詞/分詞（獨立分詞構句）
    Establishing direct links between semiconductor work and certain cancers can be contentious, with research inconclusive...
    此處使用 "with + 名詞 (research) + 形容詞 (inconclusive)" 的結構，用來補充說明主句發生時的背景狀況或伴隨的狀況，翻譯為「在……的情況下」。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句
    ...told the briefing outside the offices of the service, which is a government agency.
    句尾的 ", which is a government agency" 是一個非限定關係子句（逗號前置），用來補充說明先行詞 "the service"（該機構）。這種結構在英文新聞中常提供額外背景資訊。
