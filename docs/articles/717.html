  • adopt v. 採用，正式通過

  • resolution n. 決議（案）
    The UN has voted to adopt a resolution for the world to formally phase out the traditional Mercator map...
    聯合國已投票通過一項決議，正式呼籲全球逐步淘汰傳統的麥卡托世界地圖...
  • phase out phr. v. 逐步淘汰，分階段停止使用
    The UN has voted to adopt a resolution for the world to formally phase out the traditional Mercator map...
    聯合國已投票通過一項決議，正式呼籲全球逐步淘汰傳統的麥卡托世界地圖...
  • in favor of prep. phr. 支持，贊成；有利於
    ...in favor of one that more accurately displays Africa’s size.
    ...改採用能更準確顯示非洲實際大小的地圖。
  • abstain v. 棄權，權利不投票
    Only the US voted against it while six other countries... abstained.
    僅美國投下反對票，另有6個國家...棄權。
  • institution n. 機構，制度
    UN resolutions are non-enforceable, so countries and institutions will not be mandated to use only the new projection...
    聯合國決議不具法律強制力，因此各國與機構不會被強制要求只能使用新投影法...
  • mandate v. 強制規定，授權
    ...countries and institutions will not be mandated to use only the new projection or forbidden from using the old one.
    ...各國與機構不會被強制要求只能使用新投影法，亦不會被禁止使用舊投影法。
  • outcome n. 結果，成果
    However, the outcome is expected to result in an update of classroom curriculums...
    然而，此結果預期將帶動教室課程與日常科技的更新。
  • governance n. 治理，管理方式
    ...given the wide usage of the Mercator projection in education, technology and governance.
    ...鑑於麥卡托投影法在教育、科技與治理領域的廣泛應用。
  • spearhead v. 帶頭，擔任先鋒

  • tailored adj. 量身訂做的，特製的

    ...改為採用量身打造以符合特定用途的地圖呈現方式...

    It has often been used as an alternative to the Mercator map.
    它經常被用作麥卡托地圖的替代方案。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Present Participle）作後置定語修飾名詞
    ...in favor of map representations tailored to specific uses, more faithful to actual surface areas and in line with the recommendations of cartographers and scientists...
    句中的 "tailored to specific uses" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "map representations"（地圖呈現方式），相當於形容詞子句 "which are tailored to specific uses" 的簡化，能讓商業與新聞英語的表達更為精鍊。
  • 假設語氣（Subjunctive Mood / Could 應用於假設情境）
    “You could fit the United States, China, India, Japan, Mexico and much of Europe into Africa and still have land to spare,”...
    此處使用助動詞 "could" 表達一種假設或極大的可能性（「你本來就可以把……塞進去」），用誇飾的語氣來具體凸顯非洲實際面積的龐大。
  • 分詞構句（Participle Construction）表附帶狀況或結果
    Some argue that the map has subtly shaped international power dynamics by minimizing the physical presence of the global south and visually inflating northern hemisphere nations while shrinking equatorial regions.
    句尾的 "while shrinking equatorial regions" 是一個連接詞引導的分詞構句（原意為 while it is shrinking...），用來補充說明主句動作同時發生的附帶情況或對比結果。
