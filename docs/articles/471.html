單字解析

  • dismiss v. 解雇，開除，免職
    The Ministry of Sports yesterday dismissed the secretary-general of the Chinese Taipei Gymnastics Association (CTGA)...
    體育署昨天解除了中華民國體協（CTGA）秘書長的職務...
  • unilaterally adv. 單方面地
    ...after the organization unilaterally rejected an invitation for two gymnasts to compete...
    ...在這家組織單方面拒絕了兩名體操選手參賽的邀請之後...
  • deprive v. 剝奪，使喪失
    ...Chiang Chien-tung (江建東) failed to comment on or apologize for the association’s decision to deprive two gymnasts of the opportunity to compete...
    體育署在一份聲明中表示，江建東對於體協剝奪兩名選手參賽機會的決定，既未發表評論也未道歉...
  • deadline n. 截止日期，最後期限
    It said it received an e-mail on Aug. 20 offering reserve spots, with a confirmation deadline of Aug. 21.
    該協會表示，其於8月20日收到一封提供遞補名額的電子郵件，確認截止日期為8月21日。
  • promptly adv. 迅速地，立即地
    As a result, it failed to promptly inform the athletes and their coaches...
    因此，它未能及時通知選手及其教練...
  • implement v. 實施，執行
    ...adding that it would conduct an internal review and implement measures to prevent similar incidents.
    ...並補充說將進行內部檢討並實施措施以防範類似事件發生。
  • investigation n. 調查
    The ministry said that it contacted the CTGA on Thursday, demanding an immediate investigation and public apology...
    體育署表示，其於週四聯繫了體協，要求立即進行調查並公開道歉...
  • comprehensive adj. 全面的，綜合的
    ...adding that it ordered the governing body to submit a comprehensive review, clarify its decisionmaking process and determine responsibility.
    ...並補充說已下令該管理機構提交一份全面檢討報告、釐清其決策過程並追究責任。
  • in accordance with phr. 根據，按照
    It also dismissed Chiang from his post in accordance with Article 43 of the National Sports Act (國民體育法), the ministry said.
    體育署表示，也依據《國民體育法》第43條將江建東免職。
  • vacancy n. 空缺，職缺
    Clear mechanisms are needed to fill vacancies in international competitions and issue emergency notifications, it said.
    該署表示，需要有明確的機制來填補國際賽事的空缺並發布緊急通知。
  • eligibility n. 資格，合格條件
    Notifications about an athlete’s eligibility must be relayed immediately to athletes and their coaches...

  • compensate v. 補償，賠償
    The ministry and sports associations should establish clear rules governing competition eligibility and ensure that the athletes who were affected are compensated.
    體育署和各體育協會應建立明確的競賽資格管理規則，並確保受影響的選手獲得補償。

文法重點

  • 受事主語的被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Gymnasts must not be deprived of opportunities due to internal administrative delays, the ministry said...
    句中使用「must not be deprived of...」（不得被剝奪……）被動語態，強調選手是承受動作的對象，凸顯體育署對於行政疏失導致選手權益受損的嚴正立場。
  • 表建議、命令的子句中使用原形動詞 (Subjunctive Mood)
    Administrative procedures are that the matter be reviewed via the governing body’s athlete selection and training committee...
    在代表規定、要求的名詞子句中，be 動詞後方的補語子句（that the matter be reviewed...）省略了助動詞 should，直接使用原形動詞被動（be reviewed），展現正式公文與新聞報導的語氣。
  • 現在分詞作補充說明 (Present Participle as Supplementary Information)
    ...the CTGA said, adding that it would conduct an internal review and implement measures to prevent similar incidents.
  • 關係代名詞子句修飾先行詞 (Relative Clause)
    Democratic Progressive Party Legislator Hsu Fu-kuei (徐富癸), who has been advocating for the athletes, welcomed the ministry’s decisions...
    句中「who has been advocating for the athletes」是非限定性關係子句（以逗號隔開），用來補充說明立法委員徐富癸的背景與先前持續為選手發聲的行動。
