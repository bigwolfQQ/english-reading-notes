印尼火山爆發，機場取消航班

Indonesia cancels flights at airports as volcano erupts

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • cancelation n. 取消
    Indonesia’s Anak Krakatau volcano erupted early yesterday, prompting the cancelation of all flights at Jakarta’s Soekarno-Hatta International Airport...
    印尼的喀拉喀托之子火山於昨天清晨爆發，促使雅加達蘇卡諾-哈塔國際機場取消所有航班...
  • disrupt v. 中斷，擾亂
    ...and disrupting other domestic airports as volcanic ash drifted across parts of western Indonesia.
    ...並干擾了其他國內機場的運作，因為火山灰在印尼西部部分地區飄散。
  • authorities n. 官方，當局
    The nearest settlement is more than 16km away, but authorities warned residents and visitors to stay at least 3km from the active crater.
    最近的聚落距離超過 16 公里，但當局警告居民和遊客遠離活躍火山口至少 3 公里。
  • closure n. 停業，關閉
    By yesterday evening, the closure had affected 467 flight movements in Soekarno-Hatta airport alone that affected about 150,000 passengers.
    截至昨天傍晚，僅蘇卡諾-哈塔機場的關閉就影響了 467 架次的航班移動，波及約 15 萬名旅客。
  • force majeure n. 不可抗力
    “It’s a natural disaster, it’s a force majeure, so, what can we do?”
    「這是自然災害，這是不可抗力，所以，我們能怎麼辦？」
  • coordinate v. 協調，配合
    Indonesia’s Directorate-General of Civil Aviation said it was closely monitoring the eruption’s impact on air travel and coordinating with airlines, airport operators...
    印尼民航總局表示，正密切監控火山爆發對航空旅行的影響，並與航空公司、機場營運商...進行協調。
  • priority n. 優先事項，首要任務
    “Safety remains our top priority in every operational decision,” Ministry of Transportation director general of civil aviation Lukman F. Laisa said.
    運輸部民航總局局長盧克曼·F·萊薩表示：「在每一項營運決策中，安全始終是我們的首要任務。」
  • operational adj. 營運的，運作的
    He warned flight schedules could face cascading disruptions affecting aircraft rotations, fleet availability and terminal capacity as airlines work to restore operations.
    他警告說，隨著航空公司努力恢復營運，航班時刻表可能會面臨連鎖中斷，進而影響飛機調度、機隊可用性以及航廈容量。
  • impact n. 衝擊，影響
    Indonesia’s Directorate-General of Civil Aviation said it was closely monitoring the eruption’s impact on air travel...
    印尼民航總局表示，正密切監控火山爆發對航空旅行的影響...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附帶狀況（分詞構句）
    Indonesia’s Anak Krakatau volcano erupted early yesterday, prompting the cancelation of all flights at Jakarta’s Soekarno-Hatta International Airport and disrupting other domestic airports as volcanic ash drifted across parts of western Indonesia.
    句中的「prompting...」與「disrupting...」是以現在分詞開頭的片語，用來接續主句發生之後的結果或伴隨狀況，翻譯時通常翻成「進而...」或「導致...」，是多益英文閱讀與寫作中常見表達因果關係的精簡句構。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    No casualties were reported and no evacuation order was issued.
    句型結構為「be 動詞 + 過去分詞 (were reported / was issued)」。新聞報導中經常使用被動語態，當動作的承受者（如 casualties「傷亡人數」、evacuation order「撤離令」）比動作的執行者更重要，或者執行者不明/不須提及時，就會採用被動語態。
  • 對等連接詞連接多個受詞 (Parallel Structure)
    The main hazards include volcanic ash, high concentrations of gas, and the ejection of hot rocks and other material around the crater area, she said.
    在包含三個或以上的項目時，英文會使用對等連接詞 and 來連接。此處 include 後面接了三個平行的名詞片語：(1) volcanic ash, (2) high concentrations of gas, 以及 (3) the ejection of hot rocks and other material，維持了句型的對稱與流暢度。
