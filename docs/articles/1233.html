Counter-drug policies are data-driven: minister

單字解析

  • stance n. 立場，態度
    Minister of Health and Welfare Shih Chung-liang (石崇良) yesterday said the ministry has always held a zero-tolerance stance toward illegal drug use...
    衛生福利部長石崇良昨天表示，衛福部對非法吸毒一向抱持零容忍的立場...
  • evidence n. 證據，證明
    ...anti-drug policies must be based on scientific evidence and implemented strategically.
    ...反毒政策必須基於科學證據並具策略性地執行。
  • implement v. 實施，執行
    ...anti-drug policies must be based on scientific evidence and implemented strategically.
    ...反毒政策必須基於科學證據並具策略性地執行。
  • response n. 回答，回應
    Shih made the remark in response to questions about the heated debate over drug policies...
    石崇良是在回應關於毒品政策激烈辯論的提問時作此表示。
  • campaign n. (政治或商業)活動，競選運動
    The debate was about Taipei Mayor Chiang Wan-an (蔣萬安) of the Chinese Nationalist Party (KMT) and his re-election campaign team...
    這場辯論是關於台北市長、國民黨的蔣萬安及其競選團隊...
  • measure n. 措施，方法
    Shen said that he was referring to drug-related harm reduction measures...
    沈伯洋表示，他當時指的是與毒品相關的減害措施...
  • statistics n. 統計數據，統計資料
    According to ministry statistics, in 2005, injecting drug users accounted for 72 percent of newly reported HIV invfections...
    根據衛福部統計，2005年注射毒品者占新增通報愛滋病感染人數的72%...
  • account for phr. 占（比例），說明
    ...injecting drug users accounted for 72 percent of newly reported HIV invfections, a share that fell to 1.7 percent last year.
    ...注射毒品者占新增通報愛滋病感染人數的72%，這一比例去年降至1.7%。

    透過全面的社區型治療計畫（包括替代療法）...
  • access n. / v. 進入權，使用權；接近

文法重點

  • 現在完成被動語態 (Present Perfect Passive)
    Pilot needle and syringe programs have been implemented in Taiwan since 2005...
    結構為「have/has been + 過去分詞」。用來表示一個動作從過去某個時間點開始（在此例中為2005年），一直持續到現在，且主詞（試辦的針具與針筒計畫）是動作的承受者。
  • 現在完成進行式 (Present Perfect Continuous)
    The number of newly diagnosed HIV cases has been declining year by year since 2018...
    結構為「have/has been + V-ing」。用來強調某個動作從過去開始持續進行到現在，甚至可能持續下去。此處用來描述愛滋病病例數自2018年起「持續逐年下降」的動態趨勢。
  • 關係代名詞子句修飾先行詞 (Relative Clause)
    ...heroin and opioid users have access to medical care that can help them gradually overcome addiction and reintegrate into society...
    句中的 "that can help them..." 為關係子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞 "medical care"（醫療照護），說明這種醫療照護具備什麼樣的功能。
