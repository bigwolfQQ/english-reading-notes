文法重點

  • 假設語氣（Conditional Sentence）
    US President Donald Trump on Wednesday pledged to send every US adult US$5,000 if Republicans retain control of the US House of Representatives and Senate in the midterm elections...
    此句使用連接詞 if 引導條件句（現在式 retain），主句則使用未來意涵的動詞（pledged to send），用來表達對未來特定條件（若共和黨贏得選舉）下會發生的承諾與預測。
  • 現在分詞片語作補充說明（Present Participle Phrase for Explanation）
    With approximately 270 million US adults, the idea would cost about US$1.35 trillion, require congressional approval and would further exacerbate the country’s nearly US$1.8 trillion annual budget deficit and concerns about inflation.
    句首的「With + 名詞短語（approximately 270 million US adults）」是一種常見的介系詞片語結構，用來帶出背景狀況或附帶條件，相當於中文的「鑑於……」或「擁有……」。
  • 讓步副詞子句（Concessive Clause with Although）
    ...although the sum of Trump’s promised payments would far exceed US tariff revenue even before the Supreme Court tossed much of the president’s tariff program last year.
    句中的 although 引導讓步子句，用來呈現兩個相對立或對比的觀念（雖然發放金額遠超關稅收入，但官員仍提出此構想），加強語氣上的轉折。
  • 動名詞片語作受詞（Gerund Phrase as Object）
    Trump has frequently lamented that, during the modern era, the president’s party almost always loses seats in the US Congress during the midterms, and he has looked for unorthodox ways to defy the trend, including this week’s convention.
    句尾的「including this week’s convention」中，including 是介系詞，後面接名詞短語作為受詞，用來補充說明「非傳統方法」包含了哪些具體事例。
