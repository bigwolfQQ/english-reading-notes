「馬德林」賽道將為一級方程式賽車週末帶來混亂

Madring to bring chaos to Formula One weekend

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • venue n. 場地，會場
    Formula One rolls into Madrid this weekend for a first look at the Madring, a new Spanish Grand Prix venue with a banked beast of a corner and enough unknowns to have drivers reaching for the word of the day: challenging.
    一級方程式賽車（F1）本週末駛入馬德里，首次亮相「馬德林」（Madring），這是一個全新的西班牙大獎賽場地，擁有一個傾斜度極大的兇猛彎道，以及足以讓車手們脫口說出今日代表字「具挑戰性」的眾多未知數。
  • exhibition n. 展覽，展覽會
    Spain is hosting two races this season, with Madrid’s street circuit around the IFEMA exhibition center following Barcelona’s Catalunya Grand Prix on the calendar.
    西班牙本賽季將舉辦兩場比賽，圍繞著 IFEMA 展覽中心的馬德里街道賽道，繼賽曆上的巴塞隆納加泰隆尼亞大獎賽之後登場。
  • set-up n. 設定，裝配，組織結構
    Until now, most of the field have only met the circuit in the simulator, leaving teams to untangle set-ups and racing lines on a layout whose pecking order remains guesswork.
    在此之前，大部分的車手都只在模擬器中見過這條賽道，這使得各車隊必須在一條實力排名仍屬猜測的賽道佈局上，理清車輛設定與賽車跑線。
  • conference n. 會議，記者會
    “It looks different. It looks that the track has character, that is charismatic, with a lot of different kinds of corners,” Madrid-born Williams driver Carlos Sainz, an ambassador for the track, told a news conference on Thursday.
    「它看起來很不一樣。看起來這條賽道很有個性、很有魅力，有許多不同類型的彎道，」出生於馬德里的威廉斯車隊車手卡洛斯·塞恩斯（Carlos Sainz）同時也是該賽道的宣傳大使，週四在記者會上表示。
  • representative adj. 具代表性的，典型的
    Sainz has driven a few laps in a road car on an unfinished version of the track, but said that was “nowhere as representative as the laps that I did in the simulator.”
    塞恩斯曾在未完工的賽道版本上開著公路車跑了幾圈，但他表示，那「絕對比不上我在模擬器中跑的那些圈數那麼具有代表性。」
  • advantage n. 優勢，有利條件
    Hamilton, the seven-time world champion, was quick to pour cold water on any suggestion of an advantage.
    身為七屆世界冠軍的漢米爾頓，很快就對任何關於擁有優勢的說法潑了冷水。
  • benefit n. 好處，優勢，津貼
    “We weren’t even flat out in many of the corners. We were low, just low power, low grip. The benefit, the only one thing is I’ve seen which way the track goes. So honestly, I don’t think we have any advantage,” he added.
    「我們在許多彎道甚至都沒有油門踩到底。我們開得很低，只是低動力、低抓地力。唯一的好處、唯一的一點是，我知道賽道是往哪個方向走。所以老實說，我不認為我們有任何優勢，」他補充道。
  • convention center n. phr. 會議中心
    The circuit is built partly inside Madrid’s convention center and partly on the streets of Valdebebas, across from soccer side Real Madrid’s training ground.
    這條賽道部分建在馬德里會議中心內部，部分建在瓦爾德貝巴斯（Valdebebas）的街道上，正對著皇家馬德里足球俱樂部的訓練場。
  • opportunity n. 機會，時機
    “It’s true to say as well that I think coming Sunday the amount of overtaking opportunities there will be will be very little,” he said.
    「話雖相對地說，我認為到了星期日，將會出現的超車機會數量將會非常少，」他說。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    Spain is hosting two races this season, with Madrid’s street circuit around the IFEMA exhibition center following Barcelona’s Catalunya Grand Prix on the calendar.
    句中的「following Barcelona’s Catalunya Grand Prix」為現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞詞組，表示伴隨或隨之發生的狀態，在新聞英語中經常用於描述賽程或事件的先後順序。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Absolute Nominative Construction)
    Until now, most of the field have only met the circuit in the simulator, leaving teams to untangle set-ups and racing lines on a layout whose pecking order remains guesswork.
    句尾的「leaving teams to...」是由「分詞構句」構成，其前面沒有連接詞，主詞與主句不同（前面是指大部份車手/車隊的狀況，此處帶出結果）。這種結構能有效將附加的結果或影響緊湊地連接在主句之後。
  • 所有格關係代名詞 (Possessive Relative Pronoun)
    Until now, most of the field have only met the circuit in the simulator, leaving teams to untangle set-ups and racing lines on a layout whose pecking order remains guesswork.
    句中的「whose pecking order remains guesswork」使用了關係代名詞所有格「whose」，用來代替「layout 的 (the layout's)」，連接先行詞 layout 與其所有格子句，說明該佈局的階級排名仍是猜測。
  • 虛主詞 It 與不定詞片語 (Dummy Subject 'It' with Infinitive Phrase)
    “It’s true to say as well that I think coming Sunday the amount of overtaking opportunities there will be will be very little,” he said.
    句首的「It」為形式主詞（虛主詞），真正的受詞或主詞概念是後面接的內容。這種句型常出現在口語或訪談中，用來先帶出評論或觀點，再鋪陳實際的陳述。
