專家：海軍艦隊需要全面改造

Navy fleet needs overhaul: experts

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • restructure v. 重組，改組
    Taiwan needs to restructure its naval fleet from focusing on large warships...
    台灣需要將其海軍艦隊從專注於大型軍艦進行重組...
  • sufficient adj. 足夠的，充分的
    ...retain sufficient firepower to deter a Chinese invasion...
    ...仍保有足夠的火力來嚇阻中國入侵...
  • deter v. 嚇阻，阻止
    ...retain sufficient firepower to deter a Chinese invasion...
    ...仍保有足夠的火力來嚇阻中國入侵...
  • capable adj. 有能力的
    Taiwan does not need a fleet capable of competing with the Chinese navy...
    台灣不需要一支有能力與中國海軍競爭的艦隊...
  • prevent v. 防止，預防
    ...capabilities that would prevent China from freely using the waterway...
    ...將能防止中國自由使用該水道的能力...
  • favor v. 偏愛，更喜歡
    Taiwan has traditionally favored large ships such as destroyers and frigates...
    台灣傳統上傾向使用驅逐艦和巡防艦等大型船艦...
  • replace v. 取代，替換
    ...adding that Taiwan’s current naval plans include replacing an aging surface fleet...
    ...並補充說台灣目前的海軍計畫包括替換老舊的水面艦隊...
  • liability n. 累贅，負擔，責任
    ...making Taiwan’s large surface ships “a liability,” they said.
    ...這使得台灣的大型水面艦艇成為「一種負擔」，他們說。
  • advantage n. 優勢，有利條件
    Taipei could take advantage of that asymmetry...
    台北可以利用這種不對稱性...
  • operate v. 運作，營運，操作
    The smaller ships could “operate independently while sharing information...
    這些較小的船隻可以「在獨立運作的同時，共享資訊...
  • lower v. 降低，減少
    Sustained production of the ships would lower costs...
    持續生產這些船隻將能降低成本...
  • transform v. 轉型，轉變
    The obstacles to the transformation of Taiwan’s navy are more institutional than technical...
    台灣海軍轉型的障礙更多是制度上的，而非技術上的...
  • redirect v. 重新導向，轉用（資金、資源等）
    ...and redirect scarce resources toward the lighter surface ships it needs...
    ...並將稀缺資源轉向其所需的較輕型水面艦艇...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（V-ing）作後置修飾語
    ...an article published on Friday in the journal Foreign Affairs.
    句中的 published 是過去分詞作後置修飾語，修飾前面的 an article（意即「被發表的文章」）。英文中常利用分詞片語來修飾名詞，以精簡句子結構，省去關係代名詞與be動詞（which was published）。
  • 現在分詞表示附带結果或同時發生的動作
    The smaller ships could “operate independently while sharing information and coordinating their attacks,” they said.
  • 假設語氣（Conditional / Subjunctive meaning）
    Such a navy will not look as impressive during a fleet review, but it will be a more effective deterrent against a potential Chinese invasion.
