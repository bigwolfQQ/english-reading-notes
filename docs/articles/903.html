Civilians clamor for Keelung prison’s ‘criminally good’ mooncakes

單字解析

  • detention center n. 看守所、拘留所
    Mooncakes made at the Keelung Detention Center have proven a surprise hit ahead of this year’s Mid-Autumn Festival...
    基隆看守所製作的月餅在今年中秋節前夕意外大受歡迎...
  • ship out v. phr. 出貨、運送
    ...with a pair of inmate-bakers preparing to ship out about 10,000 of the traditional pastries over the holiday period.
    ...兩名收容人烘焙師傅準備在假期期間出貨大約一萬顆傳統糕點。
  • churn out v. phr. 大量生產、源源不絕地製造
    ...where two inmates, using three ovens, have been churning out trays of egg yolk pastry...
    ...兩名收容人使用三座烤箱，正源源不絕地烤出整盤的蛋黃酥...
  • scale n. 規模
    ...as the scale of production was constrained by the kitchen’s small size.
    ...由於產能受限於廚房的小規模...
  • constrain v. 限制、約束
    ...as the scale of production was constrained by the kitchen’s small size.
    ...由於產能受限於廚房的小規模...
  • demand n. 需求、要求
    ...but if demand continues after the holiday, it would evaluate whether to continue production.
    ...但如果假期過後需求持續，將會評估是否繼續生產。
  • evaluate v. 評估
    ...it would evaluate whether to continue production.
    ...將會評估是否繼續生產。
  • feedback n. 回饋、反應
    ...including the positive feedback from customers, would give the inmates confidence...
    ...包括來自顧客的正面回饋，能讓收容人對自己...建立信心。
  • ability n. 能力
    ...would give the inmates confidence in their ability to reintegrate into society after their release.
    ...能讓收容人對自己刑滿釋放後重新融入社會的能力建立信心。
  • available adj. 可買到的、可得到的、有空的
    ...and are available in boxes of 12 for NT$480 or six for NT$250.
    ...並推出每盒12入480元或6入250元的規格。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附帶狀況（with + 名詞 + 分詞）
    ...with a pair of inmate-bakers preparing to ship out about 10,000 of the traditional pastries over the holiday period.
    「with + O. + 分詞」結構用來表示附帶狀況或背景。在此句中，preparing 為現在分詞，主動修飾前方的 inmate-bakers（烘焙師傅正準備出貨），中文常譯為「隨著……」或「在……的情況下」。
  • 非限定關係代間詞子句（where 引導）
    During a visit to the prison yesterday, the Central News Agency toured the cramped 3 ping (9.92m²) kitchen, where two inmates, using three ovens, have been churning out trays of egg yolk pastry...
    句中的逗號後方接了 "where" 引導的非限定關係子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞 "kitchen"（廚房），且此處 where 等同於 "in which"，在句中作地方副詞用，為多益閱讀測驗常見的關代用法。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    ...as the scale of production was constrained by the kitchen’s small size.
    本句使用了過去式的被動語態 "was constrained by"（受限於……）。當動作的承受者（scale of production 生產規模）是主詞時，動詞需使用「be動詞 + 過去分詞」的被動結構，並用 "by" 引出實際的施動者。
  • 過去分詞作後置修飾語
    ...each received training from a professional baker hired by the detention center, officials said.
    片語 "hired by the detention center" 是過去分詞片語，放在名詞 "a professional baker" 後方作後置修飾語，相當於形容詞子句 "who was hired by the detention center"，省略了「關係代間詞 + be動詞」以達到精簡語意的效果。
