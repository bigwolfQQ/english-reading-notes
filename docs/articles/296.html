志工前往沙洲設置柵欄以防止侵蝕

Volunteers go to shoal to protect it from erosion

單字解析

  • erosion n. 侵蝕，腐蝕
    More than 100 people on Tuesday traveled to Waisanding Sandbar (外傘頂洲), Taiwan’s largest shoal, to help protect it from erosion by building sand-trapping structures from discarded oyster racks.
    週二，100 多人前往台灣最大的沙洲外傘頂洲，利用廢棄的蚵架搭建攔沙結構，以協助防止其遭受侵蝕。
  • discarded adj. 拋棄的，廢棄的
    More than 100 people on Tuesday traveled to Waisanding Sandbar (外傘頂洲), Taiwan’s largest shoal, to help protect it from erosion by building sand-trapping structures from discarded oyster racks.
    週二，100 多人前往台灣最大的沙洲外傘頂洲，利用廢棄的蚵架搭建攔沙結構，以協助防止其遭受侵蝕。
  • carry out phr. v. 執行，實施
    The Chiayi County Environmental Protection Bureau brought together more than 100 staff members and volunteers to travel by recreational fishing boats to Waisanding Sandbar, a roughly 1,000-hectare shoal about 10km off Dongshi Township (東石), to carry out sand-conservation work.
    嘉義縣環境保護局集結了 100 多名工作人員和志工，搭乘觀光漁筏前往距離東石鄉約 10 公里、面積約 1,000 公頃的外傘頂洲，執行護沙工作。
  • abandoned adj. 被拋棄的，廢棄的
    The team used abandoned bamboo oyster racks, oyster shells and other marine debris to build square sand-trapping structures, which were anchored to the shoal, the bureau said yesterday.
    該局昨天表示，團隊利用廢棄的竹製蚵架、蚵殼和其他海洋廢棄物，建造了方形的攔沙結構，並將其固定在沙洲上。
  • complete v. 完成，結束
    The team completed three cubic structures, each measuring 3m in height and width, and built with about 300 discarded oyster racks, it added.
    該局補充說，團隊完成了三個立方體結構，每個結構高寬皆為 3 公尺，並使用了大約 300 個廢棄蚵架建造。
  • collaboration n. 合作，協力
    The project was carried out in collaboration with the Ministry of Economic Affairs’ Water Resources Agency, with technical guidance from National Cheng Kung University’s Tainan Hydraulics Laboratory.
    此專案是與經濟部水利署合作執行，並獲得國立成功大學台南水工實驗室的技術指導。
  • guidance n. 指導，引導
    The project was carried out in collaboration with the Ministry of Economic Affairs’ Water Resources Agency, with technical guidance from National Cheng Kung University’s Tainan Hydraulics Laboratory.
    此專案是與經濟部水利署合作執行，並獲得國立成功大學台南水工實驗室的技術指導。
  • buffer n. 緩衝，緩衝物
    Waisanding Sandbar serves as a natural buffer protecting the coastlines of Chiayi and Yunlin counties from wind and waves while helping stabilize the shoreline and providing habitats for marine life, Chang said.
    張輝川說，外傘頂洲作為天然緩衝區，保護嘉義和雲林縣的海岸線免受風浪侵襲，同時有助於穩定海岸線並為海洋生物提供棲息地。
  • prevent v. 預防，防止
    The county government has continued efforts in the past few years to prevent the shoal from shrinking, he added.
    他補充說，縣政府在過去幾年中持續努力防止沙洲縮小。
  • practical adj. 實際的，實用的
    The campaign serves as a practical effort to protect Taiwan’s coastline from the impacts of climate change, Chang said.
    張輝川表示，這項活動是保護台灣海岸線免受氣候變遷影響的實際努力。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    More than 100 people on Tuesday traveled to Waisanding Sandbar (外傘頂洲), Taiwan’s largest shoal, to help protect it from erosion by building sand-trapping structures from discarded oyster racks.
    句尾的 "building sand-trapping structures..." 是由現在分詞 building 引導的分詞片語，用來補充說明前面主句動作的方法或伴隨情況，相當於「藉由建造……」。多益考試中常在克漏字測驗考介詞 by 後方需接動名詞 (by building)。
  • 非限定關代子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    The team used abandoned bamboo oyster racks, oyster shells and other marine debris to build square sand-trapping structures, which were anchored to the shoal, the bureau said yesterday.
    句中的 ", which were anchored to the shoal" 是一個非限定關係子句（前方有逗號），用來補充說明前面的先行詞 "square sand-trapping structures"。在多益文法題中，若先行詞為事物且前面有逗號，關代只能使用 which，不能使用 that。
  • 過去分詞作形容詞用 (Past Participle as Adjective)
    The team completed three cubic structures, each measuring 3m in height and width, and built with about 300 discarded oyster racks, it added.
    句中的 "discarded"（廢棄的）是由動詞 discard 轉換而來的過去分詞，在此作為形容詞直接放在名詞 oyster racks 前方修飾名詞，這類分詞轉形容詞的用法在多益商業閱讀中極為常見。
