切勿重新啟動核電廠

Do not restart nuclear power plants

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • facility n. 設施，場所
    News reports indicate that an underground nuclear shelter has been installed at a facility associated with the Japan-Taiwan Exchange Association’s Taipei office...
    新聞報導指出，日本台灣交流協會台北辦事處相關設施內已設置地下核避難所...
  • associated adj. 相關的，聯想的
    News reports indicate that an underground nuclear shelter has been installed at a facility associated with the Japan-Taiwan Exchange Association’s Taipei office...
    新聞報導指出，日本台灣交流協會台北辦事處相關設施內已設置地下核避難所...
  • guideline n. 指導方針，準則
    Japan revised its nuclear-emergency planning guidelines with a recommendation that people with 30km of a disaster at a nuclear reactor prepare for indoor sheltering or evacuation.
    日本修訂了核能緊急應變計畫指導方針，建議距離核反應爐災難地點30公里範圍內的人民準備室內避難或疏散。
  • evacuation n. 疏散，撤離
    During the 2011 disaster, the US advised its citizens within 80km of the plant to evacuate the area.
    在2011年的災難期間，美國曾建議其位於該電廠80公里範圍內的公民撤離該地區。
  • employee n. 員工，雇員
    In the event of a major nuclear accident at the Guosheng plant, employees of the Japan-Taiwan Exchange Association have, if the reports are true, an underground nuclear shelter to take refuge in.
    如果在國聖電廠發生重大核災，若報導屬實，日本台灣交流協會的員工將有一個地下核避難所可以避難。
  • accommodate v. 容納，提供住宿
    If a nuclear accident were to occur, would it be possible to evacuate and accommodate that many people?
    如果發生核災，要疏散並容納這麼多人口是否可能？
  • contamination n. 汙染
    Nuclear accidents can also release radioactive contamination over an extended area for many years.
    核災還會在大片區域釋放放射性汙染，長達多年。
  • decommission v. 除役，停用（設施等）
    ...at the very least, it should properly decommission aging nuclear power plants that have been shut down for years...
    ...但至少應該妥善除役那些已經停機多年的老舊核電廠...

文法重點

  • 假設語氣與倒裝句 (Subjunctive Mood & Inversion)
    If the plant were restarted and a major accident were to occur, the association’s office would fall within the evacuation zone.
    此句使用與未來事實相反的假設語氣（If + 主詞 + were / were to + 原形動詞, 主詞 + 조動詞過去式 + 原形動詞）。這類結構在多益閱讀中常出現於商業決策或應變假設情境。
  • 關係代間代名詞子句 (Relative Clause with Reduced Participle)
    ...aging nuclear power plants that have been shut down for years...
    句中的 "that have been shut down for years" 是由關係代名詞帶領的形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的先行詞 "aging nuclear power plants"。其中 "shut down" 是被動語態的分詞片語，用以表達「被關閉、停機」。
  • 所有格與名詞所有格結構 (Possessive / Noun Modifier)
    The Japan-Taiwan Exchange Association’s Taipei office is about 21km from the Guosheng Nuclear Power Plant...
    使用 "Association's Taipei office" 和 "Guosheng Nuclear Power Plant" 呈現了典型的組織名稱與地點的連接方式，在多益聽力與閱讀中，常考驗對於機構名稱、人名與所屬部門、辦公室之間關係的理解。
