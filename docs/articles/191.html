鄭欽文擊敗美國選手麥迪遜·基絲

Zheng Qinwen stuns US’ Madison Keys

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • stun v. 使大吃一驚、震驚、給予深刻印象
    Zheng Qinwen won the last seven games to stun Madison Keys and reach the US Open last 16 on a Saturday of upsets that dented US hopes at Flushing Meadows.
    鄭欽文連贏最後七局，爆冷擊敗麥迪遜·基絲，闖入美國網球公開賽最後16強。
  • upset n. 爆冷門、意料之外的輸贏結果
    Zheng Qinwen won the last seven games to stun Madison Keys and reach the US Open last 16 on a Saturday of upsets that dented US hopes at Flushing Meadows.
    這天爆冷頻傳，也重挫了美國選手在法拉盛公園的奪冠希望。
  • compatriot n. 同胞、同國國民
    Fourth-seeded Coco Gauff battled through in straight sets, and 18-year-old US compatriot Iva Jovic held off Philippines sensation Alexandra Eala in three sets.
    第四種子高芙直落二盤艱難晉級，而18歲的美國同胞伊娃·約维奇則在三盤大戰中擋住了菲律賓新星亞歷山德拉·埃阿拉的反撲。
  • recovery n. 復原、恢復、景氣復甦
    The former world No. 4 had to come through qualifying after a lengthy recovery from elbow surgery saw her ranking plummet.
    這位前世界排名第四的選手在經歷肘部手術後的漫長康復期後，排名暴跌，必須從資格賽打起。
  • plummet v. 暴跌、急遽下降
    The former world No. 4 had to come through qualifying after a lengthy recovery from elbow surgery saw her ranking plummet.
    這位前世界排名第四的選手在經歷肘部手術後的漫長康復期後，排名暴跌，必須從資格賽打起。
  • frustration n. 挫折感、沮喪
    She saved match point at 5-1 and just kept rolling, Keys’ increasing frustration showing as she banged her head with her racquet after one lost point.
    她在5-1落後時挽救了賽末點并一路急起直追，基絲因失分而在輸掉一球後用球拍敲打自己的頭部，顯露出越來越多的挫折感。
  • dominate v. 主導、支配、在...佔優勢
    Swiatek saved three set points in the second set, dominating both tiebreakers in her tight 7-6 (7/3), 7-6 (7/3) win over Czech Maria Bouzkova.
    斯瓦泰克在第二盤挽救了三個盤末點，在與捷克選手瑪麗亞·鮑茲科娃的苦戰中主導了兩次搶七，以7-6 (7/3)、7-6 (7/3)險勝。
  • experience n. 經驗、經歷
    “I think this was one of my craziest experiences so far in my career,” Blockx said after firing 17 aces past Cobolli.
    布洛克斯在轟出17記愛司球戰勝科博利後表示：「我想這是我職業生涯至今最瘋狂的體驗之一。」
  • rally v. （比賽中）重整旗鼓、急起直追、恢復
    Blockx next faces Francisco Cerundolo, who rallied from two sets down to upset ninth-seeded Fritz 3-6, 4-6, 6-3, 6-4, 6-4.
    布洛克斯接下來將對陣弗朗西斯科·塞倫多洛，後者在輸掉前兩盤後上演逆轉戲碼，以3-6、4-6、6-3、6-4、6-4爆冷擊敗第九種子弗里茨。
  • rely on phr. v. 依賴、依靠、信賴
    Former champion Gauff shook off a slow start to beat Spain’s Cristina Bucsa 6-3, 6-4, relying on her defensive skills as she climbed out of a 3-1 hole in the opening set.
    前冠軍高芙擺脫慢熱狀態，以6-3、6-4擊敗西班牙的克里斯蒂娜·布克沙，憑藉防守技巧在首盤從3-1的落後劣勢中爬了出來。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 / 結果狀語
    She saved match point at 5-1 and just kept rolling, Keys’ increasing frustration showing as she banged her head with her racquet after one lost point.
    句尾的 「Keys’ increasing frustration showing as...」 是一個獨立分詞構句（Absolute Phrase），由「名詞 (Keys' increasing frustration) + 現在分詞 (showing)」組成，用來補充說明基絲當時的情緒反應與情況，使兩個子句在語意上緊密相連而不顯冗長。
  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況（表示伴隨發生的動作）
    Blockx next faces Francisco Cerundolo, who rallied from two sets down to upset ninth-seeded Fritz 3-6, 4-6, 6-3, 6-4, 6-4, [省略，對應原句] Down 4-3 on serve in the final set, the 24th seed from Argentina fought back from 0-40 down to hold, coming up with a trio of booming serves...
    句尾的「coming up with...」是現在分詞作分詞構句，主詞與句首的主語（the 24th seed from Argentina）一致，用來修飾說明他「在保住發球局的同時，伴隨著打出三記強勁的發球」這項附帶動作。
  • 形容詞片語置於句首作狀態描述（省略分詞構句）
    Down 4-3 on serve in the final set, the 24th seed from Argentina fought back from 0-40 down to hold...
    句首的「Down 4-3 on serve in the final set」原本是完整副詞子句（如：When he was down 4-3...），此處省略了主詞與be動詞（he was），直接留下形容詞片語置於句首，用來描述主詞在動作發生當下的處境與比分狀態。
  • 關係代名詞（who）引導的限定性關係子句
    Blockx next faces Francisco Cerundolo, who rallied from two sets down to upset ninth-seeded Fritz...
    句中的「who rallied from two sets down to upset...」是關係子句，用來修飾先行詞「Francisco Cerundolo」，提供關於這位球員在比賽中如何克服落後劣勢的額外資訊。
