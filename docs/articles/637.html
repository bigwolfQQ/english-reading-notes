文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附帶狀況與時間表述
    Russian strikes on Saturday killed at least seven people and wounded almost 60 across Ukraine, Kyiv said, as Moscow steps up attacks ahead of another winter of war.
    句尾的 “as Moscow steps up attacks...” 是一個連接詞引導的副詞子句，表示伴隨主句發生的時間或背景狀況。新聞英文常藉由這種結構來補充說明事件發生時的當前背景。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Ukrainian officials said two people were killed in the southern Zaporizhzhia region and another two in the central Zhytomyr region.
    “were killed” 是被動語態（be + 過去分詞），在報導災難、衝突或犯罪新聞時非常常見，因為焦點在於「誰遭受了傷害/死亡」，而非施事者。
  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense)
    Russia had increased strikes on petrol stations in the past few days in Kyiv and on motorways, Ukrainian Prime Minister Sergiy Koretsky said.
    “had increased” 是過去完成式（had + 過去分詞），用來表達在過去某個時間點（總理發言的過去時間）之前已經完成或持續發生的動作。
  • 虛主詞 It 句型配合形容詞與名詞子句
    “When the Russians so blatantly turn ordinary people into targets of their terrorist attacks, it is frustrating that the response from the world’s strongest powers does not match the level of Russia’s escalation,” Zelensky said.
    句型 “it is [adjective] that [clause]” 中，it 是形式主詞，真正的主詞是後面由 that 引導的名詞子句。這種句型常用於表達對某事的評價或情緒（如本句的 frustrating）。
