不只是為了教別人說話而說話

More than speaking to teach others speaking

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • communicate v. 溝通、交流
    If students are expected to study, work and communicate in English, they need opportunities to develop spoken language rather than simply knowledge about it.
    如果期望學生能用英語學習、工作和溝通，他們需要的是發展口語能力的机会，而不僅僅是關於語言的知識。
  • rehearse v. 排練、預演
    They might read a passage aloud, repeat sentences after the teacher or rehearse a textbook dialogue several times with a partner, but are they learning to communicate?
    他們可能會大聲朗讀文章、跟著老師覆述句子，或與同伴多次排練教科書對話，但他們真的在學習溝通嗎？
  • equivalent adj. 相等的、相當的
    Problems arise when producing spoken English is treated as equivalent to learning how to communicate.
    當產出英語口語被視為等同於學習如何溝通時，問題就隨之而來了。
  • contribution n. 貢獻、(對話或討論中的)發言、意見
    Communication adds another person whose contribution cannot be entirely predicted.
    溝通則加入了另一個人，而這個人的迴應是無法完全預測的。
  • agreement n. 同意、協議、協定
    Two students might describe differences between pictures, tell one another stories, solve a problem together or hold different opinions and try to reach agreement.
    兩個學生可能會描述圖片之間的差異、互相講故事、一起解決問題，或持有不同觀點並試圖達成協議。
  • considerable adj. 相當大的、相當多的
    Doing that well requires considerable teacher expertise.
    要做好這一點，需要相當大的教師專業能力。
  • implementation n. 實施、執行
    A 2020 study involving three teachers and 90 students in a junior-high school found that limited understanding of communicative approaches was an obstacle to implementation.
    一項涵蓋三名教師和 90 名國中學生的 2020 年研究發現，對溝通式教學法理解有限是落實該方法的障礙。
  • feedback n. 回饋、反應
    Teachers need opportunities to understand why activities work, see them modeled, try them with learners, examine what happens, receive feedback and try again.
    教師需要機會去理解為什麼活動會有效、觀摩示範、與學習者一起嘗試、檢視發生的情況、接受回饋並再次嘗試。
  • constraints n. 限制、約束
    Teachers in Taiwan work with particular learners, curricula, examinations, class sizes, expectations and time constraints.
    台灣的教師面對的是特定的學習者、課程、考試、班級規模、期望和時間限制。
  • assessment n. 評量、評定、考核
    Fitzpatrick is right that assessment can help make speaking count.
    費茲派翠克認為評量有助於讓口語練習受到重視，這點是正確的。
  • genuinely adv. 真誠地、真正地
    If Taiwan wants students who can genuinely communicate in English, there is a need to ask not only whether learners are being given opportunities to speak, but whether teachers are being given the preparation they need to make those opportunities count.
    如果台灣希望培養出能真正用英語溝通的學生，我們不僅需要詢問是否給予了學習者說話的機會，還需要詢問是否給予了教師所需的培訓，以確保這些機會發揮作用。

文法重點

  • 假設語氣（Conditional Sentences）
    If students are expected to study, work and communicate in English, they need opportunities to develop spoken language rather than simply knowledge about it.
    此句使用第一類假設語氣（If + 現在式, 主詞 + 現在式/助動詞），用來表達真實或極有可能發生的條件與其結果，強調在特定假設前提下所產生的必然需求。
  • 被動語態（Passive Voice）
    Problems arise when producing spoken English is treated as equivalent to learning how to communicate.
    句中的「is treated as」採用了被動語態（be + 過去分詞），用來強調動作的承受者（producing spoken English），而不強調發動動作的人是誰，符合學術與新聞評論客觀陳述的語氣。
  • 動名詞片語當主詞（Gerund Phrase as Subject）
    Doing that well requires considerable teacher expertise.
    句首的「Doing that well」是一個動名詞片語，作為整句的主詞。動名詞片語視為單數，因此後方搭配單數動詞「requires」，這在英文寫作中經常用來表達「從事某項行為」需要什麼條件。
  • 關係代名詞子句（Relative Clause）
    Communication adds another person whose contribution cannot be entirely predicted.
    句中的「whose contribution cannot be entirely predicted」是由所有格關係代名詞 whose 引導的限定修飾子句，用來修飾先行詞「another person」，說明這個人的貢獻有什麼特徵。
