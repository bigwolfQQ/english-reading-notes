美國死刑犯在行刑失敗後清醒

Death row inmate awake after failed execution in US

單字解析

  • attempt n. 嘗試、企圖
    six days after the state’s failed attempt to execute her by lethal injection left her unconscious and on a ventilator.
    在該州試圖透過致命注射對她執行死刑失敗，導致她失去意識並使用呼吸器六天後。
  • survive v. 存活、倖存
    Pike, 50, survived two doses of pentobarbital as executioners attempted to put her to death on Wednesday last week
    現年 50 歲的派克在執行人員於上週三試圖處決她時，挺過了兩劑戊巴比妥
  • administer v. 施藥、管理、執行
    Pike is believed to be the only person to survive after lethal injection drugs were administered.
    據信派克是唯一在被施打致命注射藥物後存活下來的人。
  • seek v. 尋求、尋找
    Pike’s attorneys are seeking more information about what went wrong from the Tennessee Department of Correction.
    派克的律師正向田納西州懲教局尋求更多關於出錯原因的資訊。
  • statement n. 聲明、陳述
    “Against all odds, Christa Pike is conscious, speaking, and continues to receive critical medical care,” attorneys for Pike said in a statement.
    派克的律師在聲明中表示：「克服重重困難，克莉絲塔・派克目前恢復意識、能說話，並持續接受關鍵的醫療照護。」
  • prognosis n. 預後、（疾病的）預期恢復情況
    Pike’s prognosis remains unclear and, at a minimum, she faces a lengthy recovery, the attorneys said.
    律師表示，派克的預後情況仍不明朗，且至少需要一段漫長的復原期。
  • preserve v. 保存、維持
    Pike’s attorneys are seeking a court order to require the prison system to preserve information from the attempted execution
    派克的律師正尋求法院命令，要求監獄體系保留試圖執行死刑過程中的相關資訊
  • evidence n. 證據
    because the prison system is “obliged to maintain this evidence in other cases and must assure it is preserved.”
    因為監獄體系「有義務在其他案件中保留此證據，並必須確保其被妥善保存。」
  • obtain v. 獲得、取得
    They said state officials have taken “the necessary steps to obtain medical care for Pike and ascertain what occurred.”
    他們表示，州政府官員已採取「必要的步驟來為派克尋求醫療照護，並查明發生了什麼事。」
  • comprehensive adj. 全面的、綜合的
    ordered a “comprehensive, third-party review to determine exactly what occurred.”
    並下令進行「全面且具第三方參與的審查，以確切查明發生了什麼事。」
  • determine v. 查明、決定
    ordered a “comprehensive, third-party review to determine exactly what occurred.”
    並下令進行「全面且具第三方參與的審查，以確切查明發生了什麼事。」
  • postpone v. 延期、延後
    The governor postponed the state’s only other execution scheduled for this year.
    州長延期了該州今年預定執行的唯一另一起死刑。

文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Pike is believed to be the only person to survive after lethal injection drugs were administered.
    句中「were administered」使用了被動語態（be + 過去分詞），強調「藥物被施打」的這個動作，而施打藥物的執行者在此處非重點或不需特別指明。
  • 現在分詞作形容詞/補充說明 (Present Participle as Modifier)
    Tennessee death row inmate Christa Pike is awake and speaking at a hospital...
    句中的「speaking」是現在分詞，用來補充說明主詞 Christa Pike 在醫院醒來同時伴隨的狀態（正在說話），賦予句子更生動的動態描述。
  • 名詞子句作受詞 (Noun Clause as Object)
    Pike’s attorneys are seeking more information about what went wrong from the Tennessee Department of Correction.
    介系詞 about 後方接了「what went wrong」作為受詞名詞子句，其中的 what 同時兼任子句中的主詞，用來指代「出了什麼問題的事」。
  • 形容詞片語作後位修飾 (Postpositive Adjective Phrase)
    The governor postponed the state’s only other execution scheduled for this year.
    過去分詞片語「scheduled for this year」放在名詞 execution 的後面，作後位修飾語，用來限定或補充說明是「預定在今年執行的」死刑。
