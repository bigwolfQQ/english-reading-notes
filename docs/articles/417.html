健康獎勵計畫的啟示

Health rewards program’s lessons

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • participant n. 參與者
    The Ministry of Sports has launched its “sweat rewards” program, in which participants who meet any of the weekly requirements...
    體育署推出了「流汗獎勵」計畫，其中符合任一每週要求的參與者……
  • requirement n. 要求，必要條件
    ...in which participants who meet any of the weekly requirements — exercising for at least 30 minutes in a single session...
    ……其中符合任一每週要求的參與者——單次運動至少30分鐘……
  • accumulate v. 累積
    ...accumulating at least 8,000 steps a day, walking or running at least 5km in a single session or cycling at least 15km...
    ……每天累積至少8,000步，單次步行或跑步至少5公里，或單次騎自行車至少15公里……
  • voucher n. 禮券，折價券
    ...could receive a NT$50 voucher each week.
    ……每週可以獲得新台幣50元的禮券。
  • incentive n. 誘因，鼓勵
    I believe that the sports ministry’s exercise incentive program has been quite successful so far.
    我認為體育署的運動獎勵計畫到目前為止相當成功。
  • promotional adj. 宣傳的，推銷的
    After all, getting people to actually exercise and develop regular workout habits is by far more effective than simply repeating slogans or releasing promotional materials.
    畢竟，讓人們真正去運動並養成規律的鍛鍊習慣，遠比單純重複口號或發布宣傳資料要有效得多。
  • emphasis n. 強調，重視
    By comparison, the Ministry of Transportation and Communications’ new “zero traffic infringements” prize draw for motorcycle riders places greater emphasis on publicity...
    相較之下，交通部針對機車騎士推出的「零交通違規」抽獎活動則更強調宣傳……
  • eligible adj. 符合條件的，有資格的
    ...those who own a motorcycle with a valid license plate are eligible to enter as long as they have no record of traffic law infringements or accidents while on the road.
    ……只要擁有有效車牌的機車騎士，在道路上沒有交通違規或事故記錄，即符合參加資格。
  • discourage v. 使沮喪，打消念頭
    That could discourage public participation, making the campaign a mere formality in which publicity outweighs any actual impact.
    這可能會打擊大眾參與的意願，使活動淪為宣傳大於實際影響的形式主義。
  • register v. 登記，註冊
    Motorcycle riders could register once every quarter, with those who meet eligibility requirements receiving bonus vouchers.
    機車騎士可以每季登記一次，符合資格條件的人則可獲得額外禮券。
  • alternative adj. / n. 替代的；替代方案
    Alternatively, the transportation ministry could adopt a system similar to the invoice lottery, with a drawing every two months.
    或者，交通部也可以採用類似發票對獎的系統，每兩個月開獎一次。
  • tangible adj. 有形的，實際的
    A campaign built around the principle that everyone who obeys traffic laws is a winner would give people a tangible incentive to participate and make the program more meaningful.
    一項建立在「遵守交通規則的人都是贏家」原則之上的活動，將能給予人們參與的實質誘因，並使這項計畫更有意義。

文法重點

  • 關係代間代名詞片語（in which）引導的形容詞子句
    The Ministry of Sports has launched its “sweat rewards” program, in which participants who meet any of the weekly requirements... could receive a NT$50 voucher each week.
    句中的 "in which" 等同於 "where"，用來修飾前面的先行詞 "program"。介系詞 "in" 是因為參與者是在這個「計畫之中（in the program）」符合條件。這種結構在商業英文和多益閱讀測驗中非常常見，用以補充說明某個制度、計畫或合約的細節。
  • 現在分詞（-ing）作為後位修飾語
    By Sunday last week, everyone had uploaded records showing that they had walked 8,000 steps during the first week.
    句中的 "showing that..." 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "records"（記錄）。這相當於形容詞子句 "that showed that..." 的簡化版，省略了關代與be動詞，能讓句子更為精煉，也是多益文法題喜歡考的概念。
  • 過去完成式（had + p.v.）
    By Sunday last week, everyone had uploaded records showing that they had walked 8,000 steps during the first week.
    句中的 "had uploaded" 使用了過去完成式，用來表示在過去某個時間點（上週日）之前已經完成的動作。而 "had walked" 則是表示在上週日之前就已經走完步數，凸顯動作先後順序。
  • 條件句片語（as long as）
    ...those who own a motorcycle with a valid license plate are eligible to enter as long as they have no record of traffic law infringements or accidents while on the road.
    "as long as" 意為「只要」，用來引導條件子句，常出現在多益商業合約、規定或公告中，用以界定享有某項權利或優惠的先決條件。
