台灣將在U18亞洲青棒錦標賽爭奪銅牌

Taiwan to vie for U-18 Asian Baseball Championship bronze medal

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • playoff n. 季後賽、決賽、附加賽
    Taiwan is set to face the Philippines in the U-18 Asian Baseball Championship third-place playoff following a 2-1 loss to Japan on Saturday.
    在週六以1比2不敵日本後，台灣將在U18亞洲青棒錦標賽的季軍戰中對陣菲律賓。
  • advance v. 晉級、前進、推進
    The loss ended the Taiwanese team's super-round campaign, with Japan advancing to Sunday's final against South Korea.
    這場敗仗結束了台灣隊的複賽賽程，日本則晉級週日對上南韓的決賽。
  • lead n. 領先地位、領先優勢
    All three runs were scored in the first inning, when Taiwan briefly took the lead before Japan immediately responded.
    這場比賽的所有三分都在第一局攻下，當時台灣短暫取得領先，隨即遭到日本立刻反擊。
  • respond v. 回應、反應
    All three runs were scored in the first inning, when Taiwan briefly took the lead before Japan immediately responded.
    這場比賽的所有三分都在第一局攻下，當時台灣短暫取得領先，隨即遭到日本立刻反擊。
  • remain v. 保持、剩下、維持
    Neither team scored again over the remaining six innings.
    接下來的六個局數中，兩隊皆未再得分。
  • secure v. 獲得、確保、穩固
    Manya pitched a scoreless seventh to secure the win.
    萬谷投出無失分的第七局，鎖定勝局。
  • deficit n. 赤字、落後、虧損
    Wu Hao-siang (吳皓翔) held Japan hitless over the final 1 1/3 innings, but Taiwan could not erase the deficit despite finishing with four hits and drawing five walks.
    吳皓翔在最後的1又2/3局讓日本隊擊不出安打，但台灣儘管敲出四支安打並獲得五次保送，仍無法追平落後的分數。
  • defeat v. 擊敗、戰勝
    Taiwan defeated the Philippines 10-0 in five innings on Friday.
    台灣週五曾在五局以10比0擊敗菲律賓。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（附加狀態）
    The loss ended the Taiwanese team's super-round campaign, with Japan advancing to Sunday's final against South Korea.
    「with + 名詞 + 現在分詞 (V-ing)」構成附帶狀況的獨立結構，用來補充說明主句發生時同時存在的狀況，在此表示日本晉級的附帶結果。
  • 關係副詞引導的形容詞子句
    All three runs were scored in the first inning, when Taiwan briefly took the lead before Japan immediately responded.
    句中的「when Taiwan...」是關係副詞子句，用來修飾先行詞「the first inning（第一局）」，補充說明該局發生的事。
  • 對等連接詞連接轉折意涵
    Wu Hao-siang (吳皓翔) held Japan hitless over the final 1 1/3 innings, but Taiwan could not erase the deficit despite finishing with four hits and drawing five walks.
    使用對等連接詞「but」連接兩個分句，呈現前後兩件事的對比與轉折（投手表現出色，但打線未能克服落後）。
