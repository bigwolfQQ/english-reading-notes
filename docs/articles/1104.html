亞洲開發銀行將今年台灣GDP成長率上修至11%

ADB raises GDP growth for Taiwan to 11% this year

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • forecast n. / v. 預測，預報
    moving the Asian Development Bank (ADB) to raise its forecast for the second time this year.
    促使亞洲開發銀行（ADB）今年第二度上修其預測。
  • pace n. 速度，步調
    putting the economy on track to grow at the fastest pace among the 50 Asia-Pacific economies covered by the bank.
    使台灣經濟有望在該銀行涵蓋的50個亞太經濟體中以最快速度成長。
  • accelerate v. 加速，促進
    The upgrade comes after growth accelerated to 14.1 percent in the first half, the strongest pace in five decades.
    此次上修是在上半年經濟成長加速至14.1%（五十年來最強勁步調）之後發布的。
  • demand n. / v. 需求，要求
    Second-quarter GDP expanded 12.9 percent, fueled by booming demand for chips and technology products
    第二季GDP擴張了12.9%，在晶片與科技產品需求暢旺的推動下
  • investment n. 投資
    Imports increased 22 percent as companies stepped up investment in AI-related capacity ahead of expected demand, the bank said.
    該銀行表示，隨著企業因應預期的需求而加碼投資AI相關產能，進口額增加了22%。
  • consumption n. 消費，消耗
    Private consumption grew almost 6 percent in the first half, compared with 1 percent last year
    上半年民間消費成長近6%，相較於去年的1%顯著提升
  • confidence n. 信心，自信
    Rising equity prices, stable home values and higher wages outside the technology sector have helped improve consumer confidence, the bank said.
    該銀行指出，股市上揚、房價穩定以及科技產業以外薪資的調高，有助於提振消費者信心。
  • momentum n. 動能，氣勢
    That momentum is likely to ease next year.
    這種動能明年可能會趨緩。
  • projection n. 預估，預測
    The bank kept its forecast for this year’s inflation at 2 percent, but cut its projection for next year from 1.8 percent to 1.7 percent.
    該銀行維持今年2%的通膨預測不變，但將明年的預測值從1.8%下修至1.7%。
  • elevated adj. 提高的，升高的（常指價格或指標偏高）
    Energy prices, food costs and firm domestic demand could keep inflation elevated
    能源價格、食品成本以及強勁的國內需求可能會使通膨率保持在高檔
  • outlook n. 前景，展望
    The AI boom remains the biggest upside to the growth outlook
    AI熱潮依然是成長前景最大的上行利多
  • measures n. (多用複數) 措施，手段
    new US trade measures and tighter global financial conditions.
    美國新貿易措施以及全球金融環境緊縮等風險。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附加說明（補充結果）
    Taiwan’s economy is heading for its strongest growth in decades as the global artificial intelligence (AI) boom drives a surge in technology exports and investment, moving the Asian Development Bank (ADB) to raise its forecast for the second time this year.
    句尾的逗號加上「動詞-ing（moving...）」形成現在分詞構句，用來補充說明主句所帶來的結果或連帶發生的動作，常譯為「進而……」或「促使……」。
  • 被動語態
    putting the economy on track to grow at the fastest pace among the 50 Asia-Pacific economies covered by the bank.
    句中的「covered by the bank」是過去分詞片語作後位修飾，省略了「which are」，用來被動修飾前方的「50 Asia-Pacific economies」，指這些經濟體「被」該銀行所涵蓋。
  • 獨立分詞結構（附帶狀況）
    Second-quarter GDP expanded 12.9 percent, fueled by booming demand for chips and technology products, as companies around the world race to build AI computing capacity.
