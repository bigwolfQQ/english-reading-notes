台灣中油：下週汽、柴油價格不調整

Gasoline, diesel prices to remain unchanged next week: CPC

單字解析

  • state-owned adj. 國營的
    State-owned oil supplier CPC Corp., Taiwan, said Saturday it will leave its gasoline and diesel prices unchanged next week...
    國營石油供應商台灣中油公司週六表示，下週汽油和柴油價格將維持不變...
  • volatile adj. 動盪不安的，易變的
    ...despite volatile international crude oil prices this week.
    ...儘管本週國際原油價格波動劇烈。
  • statement n. 聲明，陳述
    In a statement, CPC said its recommended retail prices would stay at NT$31.2...
    中油在聲明中表示，其建議零售價將維持在31.2元...
  • retail price n. phr. 零售價
    In a statement, CPC said its recommended retail prices would stay at NT$31.2...
    中油在聲明中表示，其建議零售價將維持在31.2元...
  • consecutive adj. 連續的
    It will be the fourth consecutive week that CPC has kept its domestic fuel prices unchanged...
    這將是中油連續第四週維持國內油價不變...
  • competitiveness n. 競爭力
    ...to strengthen Taiwan's competitiveness.
    ...以強化台灣的競爭力。
  • disruption n. 中斷，擾亂
    ...concerns over supply disruptions caused by military tensions in the Middle East.
    ...中東軍事緊張局勢引發對供應中斷的擔憂。
  • mechanism n. 機制，體製
    CPC said under its floating fuel price mechanism...
    中油表示，根據其浮動油價機制...
  • average adj. / v. / n. 平均的 / 平均為 / 平均
    ...which tracks a weighted average of 70 percent Dubai crude and 30 percent Brent crude...
    ...該機制追蹤70%杜拜原油與30%布蘭特原油的加權平均...
  • cost n. 成本，費用
    ...helped CPC lower its oil purchasing costs to some extent, the company's data showed.
    ...公司的數據顯示，...在一定程度上幫助中油降低了購油成本。
  • absorb v. 吸收（此處指承擔虧損、吸收成本）
    ...CPC said it expects to absorb a loss of NT$6.9 per liter on gasoline sales...
    ...中油表示預計下週汽油銷售每公升將吸收6.9元虧損...

    中油估計，自週日其累積吸收的虧損總額將達到新台幣267.9億元...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...concerns over supply disruptions caused by military tensions in the Middle East.
    句中的 "caused by..." 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "supply disruptions"（受中東軍事緊張局勢所引起的供應中斷），相當於形容詞子句 "which are caused by..." 的簡化形式。
  • 現在完成式與未來完成式的運用 (Present Perfect & Future Perfect Tense)
    CPC estimated that by Sunday it will have absorbed a total of NT$26.79 billion in losses since the war in the Middle East broke out at the end of February...
    句中結合了兩種時態："since... broke out" 使用簡單過去式表示自過去某個時間點發生的動作；而 "will have absorbed" 則是未來完成式，用來強調「到了未來某一特定時間點（by Sunday）之前」已經完成的動作。
  • 過去分詞作形容詞用法 (Past Participle as Adjective)
    ...said Saturday it will leave its gasoline and diesel prices unchanged next week...
    句中的 "unchanged" 是由動詞 change 變來的過去分詞，在此處作為形容詞用，用來補充說明受詞 "gasoline and diesel prices" 的狀態（保持未改變的）。
