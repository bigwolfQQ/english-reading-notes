文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附帶狀況（補充說明）
    Ben Shelton sent defending champion Carlos Alcaraz packing from the US Open quarter-finals in the early hours yesterday, the pair slugging it out in the latest-finishing match in tournament history...
    句尾使用「逗號 + 名詞 (the pair) + 現在分詞 (slugging it out)」的結構，用來補充說明主句動作發生時的同時狀況或結果，相當於一個對等子句或副詞子句。
  • 關係代名詞子句（where引導地方副詞子句/關係副詞）
    ...and was dragged into a punishing four-hour and 28-minute battle, where Shelton sealed a 6-7(5), 6-1, 6-3, 1-6, 7-6(10-7) victory at 3:33am yesterday in New York.
    句中的「where」在此作為關係副詞，用來修飾先行詞「a punishing four-hour and 28-minute battle」，補充說明這場艱苦戰役中發生的事情。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    The highly anticipated meeting between the Czech Wimbledon winner and the US Open champion lived up to expectations...
    「anticipated」是由動詞 anticipate 轉變而來的過去分詞，在此作為形容詞，置於名詞 meeting 前方作前置修飾（或是由被動語態的分詞片語簡化而來，表達「被預期的」）。
  • 對等連接詞連接複雜對等子句
    “I felt at some point in the third set she was definitely serving better than me and putting so much pressure on me, but I’m glad that threw that thought away and I was able to focus point by point,” Sabalenka said.
    引言內部使用了對等連接詞「but」來連接兩個較長的子句，前半部表達對手帶給她的壓力，後半部表達她自身的調適與慶幸，使語氣轉折流暢。
