文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The Guatemalan embassy yesterday celebrated the Central American country’s 205th independence day at a reception in Taipei attended by Taiwanese officials and members of the diplomatic community.
    句中的 "attended by Taiwanese officials and members of the diplomatic community" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "reception"（招待會）。原句等於形容詞子句 "which was attended by..."，省略了關代與be動詞，使句子更為精簡緊湊，是多益閱讀測驗常見的句構。
  • 現在分詞片語作結果狀語 (Present Participle Phrase indicating Result)
    Taiwan would encourage more companies to establish operations in Guatemala and use the country as a hub for expansion into the Americas, contributing to industrial development and job creation, Ger added.
    句尾的 ", contributing to industrial development and job creation" 是由現在分詞 "contributing" 引導的片語，用來補充說明前面整句動作所帶來的自然結果（促進了產業發展與就職機會）。這種用法常出現在商業新聞中，用以表達政策或行動所產生的正面效益。
  • 現在分詞片語作修飾語 (Present Participle Phrase modifying a noun)
    Ger cited joint projects including Guatemala’s first public specialized cancer hospital, modernization of La Aurora International Airport and a cloud backup data center aimed at improving government cybersecurity.
    句中的 "aimed at improving government cybersecurity" 是現在分詞（此處為被動概念的簡化）片語，用來修飾前方的名詞 "a cloud backup data center"。這類結構在多益職場英語中常用於描述產品、服務或設施的「目的」或「功能」。
