隨著國家聯賽回歸，王牌教練紛紛粉墨登場

Star coaches make their bows as Nations League returns

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • appoint v. 任命，指派
    no fewer than 10 European nations having appointed a new coach since this summer’s tournament in North America.
    自今年夏天北美世界盃結束以來，不少於 10 個歐洲國家任命了新教練。
  • succeed v. 接替，繼任
    who succeeds Didier Deschamps as Les Bleus boss
    他接替迪尚成為藍色軍團的主帥
  • take over phr. 接管，接手
    and former Liverpool manager Klopp, who takes over his native Germany.
    以及前利物浦總教練克洛普，他接掌了自己的祖國德國隊。
  • announce v. 宣布，宣告
    When Deschamps announced this year’s World Cup would signal the end of his 14-year reign as France boss
    當迪尚宣布今年的世界盃將是他 14 年法國隊執教生涯的尾聲時
  • replace v. 取代，替換
    that the three-time Champions League-winning coach was replacing his old teammate and captain at the helm of Les Bleus.
    這位曾三度奪得歐冠的教練將接替他的老隊友兼隊長掌舵法國隊。
  • turn down phr. 拒絕
    I’ve had offers over these four or five years to take charge of a club, and I turned them all down for the French national team
    過去這四、五年裡我曾接到過執教球會的邀約，我都拒絕了，為的是法國國家隊
  • challenge n. 挑戰
    I’m ready for the challenge, that’s what drives me.
    我已經準備好迎接挑戰，這就是驅使我的動力。
  • departure n. 離開，辭職
    Klopp said at his unveiling after Julian Nagelsmann departed following Germany’s disastrous World Cup.
    克洛普在德國隊經歷災難性的世界盃後，隨著納格爾斯曼的離職而舉行的亮相記者會上表示。
  • divide v. 劃分，分割
    by calling up 44 players, divided into two squads, for his first matches in charge.
    透過徵召 44 名球員并分成兩套陣容來迎接他上任後的第一場比賽
  • extension n. 延期，展延（合約等）
    He was on Monday rewarded with a contract extension until 2032.
    他在週一獲得了一份續約至 2032 年的合約獎勵。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表示附帶狀況或結果）
    The end of FIFA World Cup cycles usually herald in a host of new faces; however, this year’s vintage is particularly bountiful, with no fewer than 10 European nations having appointed a new coach since this summer’s tournament in North America.
    「with + 名詞 + 分詞（在此為完成式分詞片語）」形成附帶狀況的獨立分詞構句，用來補充說明當前狀況的細節，意思是指「在有……的情況下」或「隨著……」。
  • 非限定性關係子句
    Most notable are 1998 World Cup winner and France legend Zidane, who succeeds Didier Deschamps as Les Bleus boss, and former Liverpool manager Klopp, who takes over his native Germany.
    句中的 "who succeeds..." 與 "who takes over..." 前面都有逗號，屬於非限定性關係子句，用來對前面的先行詞（Zidane 與 Klopp）做補充說明，不可省略逗號。
  • 現在分詞作形容詞用
    Other arrivals for this opening round of Nations League matches include Jorge Jesus, who will make his Portugal debut when the competition’s reigning champions welcome Wales today.
    句中的 "reigning" 是現在分詞當作形容詞使用，用來修飾名詞 champions，表示「衛冕中的、當權的」冠軍隊伍。
  • 過去分詞作後置定語
    The charismatic 59-year-old Klopp has already shown he would do things his way by calling up 44 players, divided into two squads, for his first matches in charge.
    句中的 "divided into two squads" 是過去分詞片語，後置修飾前面的名詞 players，等於省略了 "who were"，意指「被分成兩套陣容的（球員們）」。
