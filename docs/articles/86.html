文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...as regulators indicated they were close to approving the restart of Taiwan's last operating reactor less than a year and a half after it went offline.
    句中的「operating」是現在分詞，用來修飾前面的名詞「reactor」（反應爐），表主動或進行中的狀態（運作中的反應爐）。
  • 過去分詞片語作形容詞用 (Past Participle Phrase)
    The Kuosheng plant, in New Taipei's Wanli District, shut down and entered decommissioning in March 2023.
    句中的「decommissioned」或「decommissioning」衍生自動詞 decommission（使除役）。在此處作名詞使用，用來指核電廠進入「除役階段」。
  • 連接詞 as 引導的時間子句 (Time Clause with 'as')
    Taipei, Sept. 3 (CNA) The opposition Kuomintang (KMT) said Thursday President Lai Ching-te (賴清德) should apologize for phasing out nuclear power, as regulators indicated they were close to approving the restart...
    連接詞「as」在此處表示「隨著」或「在……同時」，用來連接兩個同時發生或具因果關係的動態情境。
  • 假設語氣中的過去式與過去完成式 (Subjunctive Mood)
    Even if the referendum passes, restarting the plant would still require clearing multiple regulatory and safety hurdles...
    句型「Even if + 現在式動詞 (passes), 主詞 + would + 原形動詞 (would still require)」用於表達對未來不太確定或假設性狀況的推論結果。
