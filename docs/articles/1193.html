外交部：摩洛哥恢復受理台灣旅客觀光簽證申請

Morocco resumes Taiwanese tourism visa applications: MOFA

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • resume v. 恢復、重新開始
    The Taiwan government said Monday it had received confirmation Morocco resumed accepting tourism visa applications from Taiwanese passport holders last week, two months after abruptly suspending the process in July.
    台灣政府週一表示，已接獲確認，摩洛哥在上週恢復受理台灣護照持有者的觀光簽證申請，此前該國在7月時曾突兀地暫停此流程。
  • confirmation n. 確認、證實
    The Taiwan government said Monday it had received confirmation Morocco resumed accepting tourism visa applications from Taiwanese passport holders last week...
    台灣政府週一表示，已接獲確認，摩洛哥在上週恢復受理台灣護照持有者的觀光簽證申請...
  • suspend v. 暫停、中止
    ...two months after abruptly suspending the process in July.
    ...此前該國在7月時曾突兀地暫停此流程。
  • official adj. 官方的、正式的
    ...and was informed that the embassy had officially resumed accepting applications for Taiwanese travelers' Visa Confirmation Letters...
    ...並獲知該大使館已正式自9月21日起恢復受理台灣旅客的簽證確認函申請...
  • purpose n. 目的、意圖
    However, as the Moroccan embassy has still not resumed VCL applications for non-tourism purposes, MOFA said it would not accept visa applications from Moroccan nationals until such restrictions are lifted.
    不過，由於摩洛哥大使館仍未恢復非觀光目的的VCL申請，外交部表示，在這些限制解除之前，將不會受理摩洛哥國民的簽證申請。
  • national n. 國民、公民
    ...MOFA said it would not accept visa applications from Moroccan nationals until such restrictions are lifted.
    ...外交部表示，在這些限制解除之前，將不會受理摩洛哥國民的簽證申請。
  • restriction n. 限制、約束
    ...until such restrictions are lifted.
    ...在這些限制解除之前...
  • announce v. 宣布、宣告
    MOFA's statement to CNA came after a local travel agency announced over the weekend that the Moroccan Embassy in Japan had told the agency it was resuming visa applications from Taiwanese nationals.
    外交部對中央社的這項聲明，是在一家本地旅行社於週末宣佈摩洛哥駐日本大使館已告知該旅行社正恢復受理台灣國民的簽證申請之後發出的。
  • effective adj. 生效的、起作用的
    Citing the principle of reciprocity, MOFA later announced that it would suspend processing visa applications from Moroccan nationals, effective July 31, except for special projects approved by the government.
    援引互惠原則，外交部隨後宣佈自7月31日起暫停處理摩洛哥國民的簽證申請，但經政府批准的特殊專案除外。
  • except for prep. 除了…之外
    ...effective July 31, except for special projects approved by the government.
    ...自7月31日起暫停處理摩洛哥國民的簽證申請，但經政府批准的特殊專案除外。
  • ineligible adj. 無資格的、不符合條件的
    ...noting that Taiwanese passport holders can obtain a VCL only through the Moroccan Embassy in Japan and are ineligible for a Moroccan e-visa.
    ...並指出台灣護照持有人只能透過摩洛哥駐日本大使館取得VCL，而不符合申辦摩洛哥電子簽證的資格。

文法重點

  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense: had + p.p.)
    The Taiwan government said Monday it had received confirmation...
    表示在過去某一時間點（said）之前已經完成的動作（收到確認）。
  • 現在完成進行式 (Present Perfect Continuous Tense: has/have been + V-ing)
    However, as the Moroccan embassy has still not resumed...
    強調從過去持續到現在的狀態（至今仍未恢復）。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Citing the principle of reciprocity, MOFA later announced...
    句首的 "Citing..." 為現在分詞片語，用來補充說明主詞 MOFA 所發動的主動動作（援引……），相當於副詞子句 "Because it cited..."。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice: be + p.p.)
    ...except for special projects approved by the government.
    過去分詞片語 "approved by the government" 用來修飾前面的 "special projects"，隱含被動關係（被政府批准的專案）。
