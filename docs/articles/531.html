馬來西亞男子走私海洛因來台，被判處 9 年有期徒刑

Malaysian men sentenced to 9 years for smuggling heroin into Taiwan

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • in exchange for phr. 作為...的交換
    Two Malaysian men who concealed 26 packets of heroin in their rectums to smuggle the drugs into Taiwan in exchange for US$1,250 each were each sentenced to nine years in prison, the Kaohsiung District Court said Friday.
    高雄地方法院週五表示，兩名馬來西亞男子將 26 包海洛因藏在直腸內，以每人 1,250 美元的報酬走私毒品來台，各被判處 9 年有期徒刑。
  • appeal v. 上訴
    The ruling can be appealed.
    本判決可以上訴。
  • instruct v. 指示、命令
    In its Aug. 27 ruling, the court said the two men had been instructed by a man identified only as "Botak" sometime in August or September last year to smuggle drugs into Taiwan in exchange for US$1,250 each.
    法院在 8 月 27 日的判決中指出，這兩名男子去年 8 或 9 月間受一名僅被辨識為「博塔克（Botak）」的男子指示，以每人 1,250 美元的報酬走私毒品來台。
  • advance payment n. phr. 預付款、訂金
    The two men each received US$250 as an advance payment before leaving for Taiwan.
    這兩名男子在啟程前往台灣之前，各收到了 250 美元的預付款。
  • transfer v. 轉機、轉乘、調動
    They then flew from Phnom Penh to Hong Kong and transferred to a Cathay Pacific flight bound for Kaohsiung, arriving at Kaohsiung International Airport on the evening of Feb. 7.
    他們隨後從金邊飛往香港，並轉乘國泰航空航班前往高雄，於 2 月 7 日傍晚抵達高雄國際機場。
  • suspicion n. 懷疑、嫌疑
    According to the ruling, the two men were stopped for inspection after their behavior raised suspicion.
    根據判決，這兩名男子因行跡可疑而遭到攔查。
  • offense n. 犯罪、違法行為
    Authorities found about 213 grams of heroin concealed inside their bodies, and both men admitted to the offense during the trial.
    當局在他們體內查獲約 213 公克的藏匿海洛因，且兩人於審判期間皆坦承犯行。
  • financial adj. 財務的、金融的
    The court noted, however, that the men were not the masterminds behind the drug-smuggling operation and had taken the risk because of financial difficulties.
    不過法院指出，這兩人並非販毒集團的主謀，且是因為財務困難才鋌而走險。
  • typically adv. 典型地、通常
    The court also said concealing drugs inside their bodies posed serious health risks and that the amount of heroin involved was smaller than the quantities typically smuggled in checked luggage.
    法院亦表示，將毒品藏在體內會構成嚴重的健康風險，且涉及的海洛因數量比通常放在託運行李中走私的數量要少。
  • factor n. 因素、要素
    Taking these factors into consideration, the court reduced their sentences before imposing nine years in prison on each man.
    綜合考量這些因素後，法院對兩人減刑，並判處每人有期徒刑 9 年。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Two Malaysian men who concealed 26 packets of heroin in their rectums to smuggle the drugs into Taiwan in exchange for US$1,250 each were each sentenced to nine years in prison...
    句子中「bound for Kaohsiung」或「concealed inside their bodies」等結構省略了「which/who is/are」，直接使用過去分詞片語來修飾前面的名詞（如 a flight 或 heroin），這是新聞英文中非常常見且能簡化句子的精簡寫法。
  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense, had + p.p.)
    ...the court said the two men had been instructed by a man identified only as "Botak" sometime in August or September last year...
    當文章的主要時敘述過去發生的事情（court said）時，若要表達「在法院說話之前更早發生」的動作，就要使用過去完成式（had been instructed）。此外，這裡也同時運用了被動語態（had been + p.p.）。
  • 現在分詞片語作附帶狀況/結果 (Present Participle Phrase indicating result or circumstance)
    Taking these factors into consideration, the court reduced their sentences before imposing nine years in prison on each man.
    句首的「Taking these factors into consideration」是由主動分詞「taking」引導的片語，其邏輯主詞與主要子句的主詞（the court）一致，意指「在將這些因素納入考量之後」，用來表達時間或條件的前後關係。
