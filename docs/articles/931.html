報告指出：反對聲浪升高，正打亂資料中心計畫

Rising opposition disrupting data center plans: report

單字解析

  • delay v. 延遲，延期
    About 45 data center projects worth US$68 billion were blocked or delayed by local pushback from April to June due to mounting opposition in the US, research group Data Center Watch said yesterday.
    研究機構 Data Center Watch 昨天表示，受美國國內日益高漲的反對聲浪影響，今年 4 月至 6 月期間，大約有 45 個總價值達 680 億美元的資料中心專案遭到當地民眾的阻撓或延宕。
  • apply for phr. 申請
    Communities across the nation are now pushing through moratoriums on new construction, often before developers can apply for permissions.
    全美各地的社區現在紛紛推動針對新建設的暫緩令（禁令），通常是在開發商申請許可之前就這麼做。
  • expansion n. 擴張，擴大
    The research quantifies growing concern about the rapid pace of expansion of artificial intelligence (AI), both in the construction of new computing clusters and its proliferation into daily life.
    該研究量化了人們對人工智慧（AI）快速擴張日益增加的擔憂，這種擔憂涵蓋了新運算叢集的建設及其向日常生活的擴散。
  • infrastructure n. 基礎設施
    Home to the biggest investors in AI infrastructure, the US is experiencing a bipartisan backlash against facilities that critics say harm the environment and siphon away resources to fuel AI systems that many fear would imperil jobs and livelihoods.
    作為 AI 基礎設施最大投資者的所在地，美國正經歷一場跨黨派的反彈，反對者稱這些設施破壞環境並吸走資源，以供應許多人擔憂會危及工作與生計的 AI 系統。
  • investor n. 投資者
    Home to the biggest investors in AI infrastructure, the US is experiencing a bipartisan backlash against facilities that critics say harm the environment and siphon away resources to fuel AI systems that many fear would imperil jobs and livelihoods.
    作為 AI 基礎設施最大投資者的所在地，美國正經歷一場跨黨派的反彈，反對者稱這些設施破壞環境並吸走資源，以供應許多人擔憂會危及工作與生計的 AI 系統。
  • facility n. 設施，設備
    Tech giants such as Amazon.com Inc, Meta Platforms Inc, Microsoft Corp and Alphabet Inc — the so-called hyperscalers — argue they need the facilities to win a race against China to develop potentially world-changing AI.
    諸如亞馬遜公司、Meta 平台公司、微軟公司和Alphabet 公司等科技巨頭（即所謂的超大規模資料中心業者）則主張，他們需要這些設施來贏得與中國開發可能改變世界的 AI 的競賽。
  • argument n. 主張，論點（動詞為 argue）
    Tech giants such as Amazon.com Inc, Meta Platforms Inc, Microsoft Corp and Alphabet Inc — the so-called hyperscalers — argue they need the facilities to win a race against China to develop potentially world-changing AI.
    諸如亞馬遜公司、Meta 平台公司、微軟公司和Alphabet 公司等科技巨頭（即所謂的超大規模資料中心業者）則主張，他們需要這些設施來贏得與中國開發可能改變世界的 AI 的競賽。
  • fluctuate v. 波動，起伏
    The number and value of disrupted projects fluctuates by quarter, with the first three months of this year seeing 75 cases worth roughly US$130 billion facing opposition in the US.
    受阻專案的數量和價值依季度而波動，今年前三個月美國有大約 75 個案件面臨反對，價值約 1,300 億美元。
  • roughly adv. 大約，粗略地
    The number and value of disrupted projects fluctuates by quarter, with the first three months of this year seeing 75 cases worth roughly US$130 billion facing opposition in the US.
    受阻專案的數量和價值依季度而波動，今年前三個月美國有大約 75 個案件面臨反對，價值約 1,300 億美元。
  • represent v. 代表，相當於
    The 45 projects affected during the second quarter represented more than half of all new large-scale developments Data Center Watch began tracking during the period.
    第二季受到影響的 45 個專案，占了 Data Center Watch 在該期間開始追蹤的所有新大型開發案的一半以上。
  • international adj. 國際的
    For the first time since it began its research two years ago, Data Center Watch also reported on protests against data centers outside the US, given increased international opposition.
    自兩年前開始這項研究以來，Data Center Watch 首次報導了美國境外針對資料中心的抗議活動，這反映出國際反對聲浪的增加。
  • anticipate v. 預期，預料
    “We anticipate that this will just become more prominent as data center development expands across the globe.”
    「我們預期隨著資料中心開發在全球擴張，這種情況將會更加顯著。」

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表示附帶狀況或結果）
    Home to the biggest investors in AI infrastructure, the US is experiencing a bipartisan backlash against facilities that critics say harm the environment and siphon away resources to fuel AI systems that many fear would imperil jobs and livelihoods.
    句首的「Home to the biggest investors in AI infrastructure」是由分詞構句省略主詞與be動詞而來（還原完整子句為：[As it is] Home to...），用來修飾主句的主詞 the US，補充說明美國身為最大投資者所在地的背景狀況。
  • 關係子句修飾先行詞
    Communities across the nation are now pushing through moratoriums on new construction, often before developers can apply for permissions.
    句中的「before developers can apply for permissions」是由連接詞 before 引導的時間副詞子句，用來修飾主句動作發生的時間點，表達在開發商申請許可之前，社區就已經採取行動。
  • 插入語用法
    Tech giants such as Amazon.com Inc, Meta Platforms Inc, Microsoft Corp and Alphabet Inc — the so-called hyperscalers — argue they need the facilities to win a race against China to develop potentially world-changing AI.
    句中的「— the so-called hyperscalers —」是被破折號夾在中間的插入語，用來補充說明前面列舉的那幾家科技巨頭在業界被稱為「超大規模資料中心業者」，不影響主要句子的結構。
  • 省略 that 的名詞子句當受詞
    “We anticipate that this will just become more prominent as data center development expands across the globe.”
    動詞 anticipate 後面接了一個由 that 引導的名詞子句（在此句中寫出了 that）作為其受詞，用來表達預期的內容。在英文口語或新聞寫作中，這個 that 有時候也可以被省略。
