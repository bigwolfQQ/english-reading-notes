文法重點

  • 分詞構句作補充說明（現在分詞表主動）
    The trip was not announced in advance to avoid “unnecessary interference,” she said, referring to China’s objections.
    句尾的「referring to China’s objections」是由現在分詞片語構成的分詞構句，用來補充說明前面整句話或主詞她（she）的動作，意指「指的是中國的抗議」，使語意更流暢且避免使用冗長的連接詞子句。
  • 使役動詞 have 的受詞補語（過去分詞表被動）
    The most significant part of the trip was “having Taiwan’s voice heard by so many more people in a place of such historical significance,” Hsiao said
    此處使用「have + 受詞 + 過去分詞（heard）」的結構，表示「使某事被（別人）做」。受詞「Taiwan’s voice」與動詞「hear」之間是被動關係（聲音被聽到），因此用過去分詞 heard。
  • 形容詞片語置於名詞後方修飾
    Vice President Hsiao Bi-khim (蕭美琴) yesterday said that she saw a growing number of international friends willing to learn about Taiwan and hear its story...
    句中的「willing to learn about Taiwan and hear its story」是一個形容詞片語，置於名詞「international friends」後面進行後位修飾，相當於關代子句「who were willing to...」，使句子更為精煉。
