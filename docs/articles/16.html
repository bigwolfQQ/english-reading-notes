文法重點

  • 分詞作為後置修飾語 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    A study conducted in the ICUs of the hospital’s internal medicine and neurosurgical departments garnered data from use of the devices with 424 critically ill patients, it said.
    過去分詞片語「conducted in the ICUs of...」在此作為後置修飾語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "A study"（一項研究），相當於關代子句「A study which was conducted in...」，省略了「which was」以達到精簡句構的效果。
  • 虛主詞 It 與表語形容詞引導的名詞子句 (It is essential that...) 部份
    It is essential that medical lines and tubes connected to patients remain unaffected during the process, Hsiao said.
    句首的 "It" 為虛主詞，真正的主詞是後面由 "that" 引導的名詞子句。由於前面的形容詞 "essential" 具有建議、要求之意（表語形容詞表示必要性），故 that 子句中的動詞需使用原形動詞（此處 "remain" 省略了助動詞 should），是多益閱讀與文法考題中常見的句構。
  • 對比連詞 while 的用法
    Following the introduction of automated repositioning air mattresses, reports of pressure injuries fell from 4.7 percent to 2.9 percent; nurses’ workload associated with repositioning patients was reduced by 69 percent; while time spent on repositioning was shortened by 53 percent, NTUH said.
    在此句中，"while" 用作對立連接詞，意思是「而、然而」，用來連接並列的多項數據與成效，呈現出三種正面結果的並陳對比，使長句更有條理。
  • not... but rather...（不是……而是……）對等連接詞
    NTUH’s Department of Nursing said the mattresses are not intended to replace professional nursing care or all manual repositioning, but rather serve as an important assistant to nurses...
    此句運用了「not intended to ... , but rather serve as ...」的對等結構，用來表達「這項產品不是為了取代……，而是為了充當……」，強烈表達出產品的真實定位與功能，是商業與新聞英文中經常用來澄清誤解、強調目的的精準句型。
