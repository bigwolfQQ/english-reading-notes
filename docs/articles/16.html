研究顯示醫院氣墊床計畫的效益

Study shows benefits of hospital mattress project

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • reposition v. 改變……的位置；（為病人）翻身
    Automated repositioning air mattresses for critically ill patients can improve pressure injury prevention and reduce the physical burden on nurses who manually reposition patients every few hours, National Taiwan University Hospital (NTUH) told a news conference on Wednesday.
  • prone to adj. phr. 有……傾向的；易於……的
    If they remain in the same position for too long, they become prone to pressure injuries that can lead to severe infections, Yu said.
  • garner v. 收集；獲得（資訊、數據或支持等）
    A study conducted in the ICUs of the hospital’s internal medicine and neurosurgical departments garnered data from use of the devices with 424 critically ill patients, it said.
  • occupational injury n. phr. 職業傷害
    NTUH nursing supervisor Hsiao Wei-ling (蕭偉伶) said that manually repositioning patients can sometimes cause occupational injuries, such as lower back, shoulder and neck strains.
  • intervention n. 干預；介入
    Introduction of the mattresses, with collaborative feedback from the hospital based on clinical needs, has allowed low-risk patients to be automatically repositioned every two to three hours without requiring any human intervention, she said.
  • attend to v. phr. 照顧；處理
    NTUH’s Department of Nursing said the mattresses are not intended to replace professional nursing care or all manual repositioning, but rather serve as an important assistant to nurses, helping them reduce the time and physical effort required to reposition patients, which gives them more time to attend to other patient care needs.
  • overlook v. 忽略；忽視
    Minister Without Portfolio Chen Shih-chung (陳時中) told the news conference that compared with pharmaceutical clinical research, medical devices are often overlooked because Taiwan’s medical device market is smaller and has high entry barriers for implementation in clinical settings.
  • entry barrier n. phr. 進入門檻
    Minister Without Portfolio Chen Shih-chung (陳時中) told the news conference that compared with pharmaceutical clinical research, medical devices are often overlooked because Taiwan’s medical device market is smaller and has high entry barriers for implementation in clinical settings.
  • industry-academia cooperation n. phr. 產學合作
    The collaboration between NTUH and Wellell Inc serves as a model for industry-academia cooperation, Chen said.
  • empirical adj. 以經驗或實證為依據的
    Clinical-based empirical studies are crucial for developing Taiwan’s smart medical devices and promoting them globally, so the government would help open hospital clinical settings to promising and innovative smart medical devices, he added.

文法重點

  • 過去分詞短語作後位修飾
    A study conducted in the ICUs of the hospital’s internal medicine and neurosurgical departments garnered data from use of the devices with 424 critically ill patients, it said.
    過去分詞短語「conducted in...」修飾主詞「A study」，相當於關係子句「which was conducted in...」的簡化，用來補充說明該項研究進行的地點與單位。
  • It is essential that + 主詞 + (should) 原形動詞（虛擬語氣）
    It is essential that medical lines and tubes connected to patients remain unaffected during the process, Hsiao said.
    在「It is essential that...」結構中，that 子句表示重要性或必要性，其謂語動詞需使用虛擬語氣（即 should + 原形動詞，其中 should 常被省略）。因此，句中的「remain」為原形動詞。
  • not intended to... but rather...
    NTUH’s Department of Nursing said the mattresses are not intended to replace professional nursing care or all manual repositioning, but rather serve as an important assistant to nurses...
    「not A, but rather B」結構用於表示「不是A，而是B」。句中「are not intended to replace...」與「but rather serve as...」形成對比，清楚說明氣墊床的定位不是全面取代護理人員，而是作為輔助工具。
  • 關係代名詞 which 修飾前面整個句子（非限定關係子句）
    NTUH’s Department of Nursing said the mattresses are not intended to replace professional nursing care or all manual repositioning, but rather serve as an important assistant to nurses, helping them reduce the time and physical effort required to reposition patients, which gives them more time to attend to other patient care needs.
    句末的「, which gives them...」屬於非限定關係子句，其中的「which」先行詞並非單一名詞，而是前面提到的「協助護理人員減少翻身所需時間與體力」這整件事，用以延伸說明這件事帶來的積極結果。
  • 現在分詞構句作補充說明
    It is not an easy task, especially with tubes attached to patients’ bodies, he said, adding that it often requires two or three nurses to work together to complete the task.
    句中的「adding that...」為現在分詞構句，主詞與主要子句主詞（he）一致，用於在主要發言之後補充說明額外資訊或細節。
