外匯存底因市場干預重返6000億美元大關

Forex reserves return to US$600 billion mark on market intervention

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • foreign exchange reserves n. phr. 外匯存底
    Taiwan's foreign exchange reserves rose from a month earlier, and returned to the US$600 billion mark at the end of August...
    台灣的外匯存底較前一個月增加，並在8月底重返6000億美元大關...
  • central bank n. 中央銀行
    ...as the local central bank entered the forex market to prevent the U.S. dollar from falling further against the Taiwan dollar.
    ...隨著台灣央行進入外匯市場以阻止美元兌台幣進一步下跌。
  • depreciate v. (貨幣)貶值
    ...causing the Taiwan dollar to depreciate.
    ...導致台幣貶值。
  • net fund inflow n. phr. 資金淨流入
    ...as foreign institutional investors registered a net fund inflow of about US$1.2 billion to Taiwan...
    ...隨著外國機構投資人登記入超（淨匯入）約12億美元至台灣...
  • intervene v. 干預、介入
    ...Tsai said the central bank intervened by buying the greenback and selling the Taiwan dollar...
    ...蔡表示，央行透過買進美元、賣出台幣進行干預...
  • disclose v. 透露、揭露
    Tsai did not disclose how much the central bank spent in August's market intervention.
    蔡未透露央行在8月市場干預中花費了多少金額。
  • on a quarterly basis adv. phr. 按季、每季
    ...it would release the figure on a quarterly basis.
    ...將按季公布該數據。
  • portfolio management n. phr. 投資組合管理
    ...reflected an increase in returns of the bank's portfolio management...
    ...反映了該行投資組合管理收益的增加...
  • exchange rate n. 匯率
    ...as well as exchange rate movements of other reserve currencies against the U.S. dollar in the month.
    ...以及該月其他儲備貨幣兌美元匯率的變動。
  • benchmark index n. phr. 基準指數
    The Taiex, the Taiwan Stock Exchange's benchmark index, which rose sharply by 6.98%...
    台灣證券交易所的基準指數（台股加權指數）急升6.98%...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附加說明 (Present Participle Phrase as an Adverbial Clause of Result)
    Data released by the central bank on Friday showed the country's forex reserves stood at US$601.90 billion at the end of August, up US$7.63 billion from a month earlier, putting an end to a two-month drop.
    句尾的「putting an end to a two-month drop」是由現在分詞片語引導的結果狀語，用來補充說明前面子句所產生的自然結果（即「終結了連續兩個月的下滑」），在多益閱讀測驗中常考用分詞來表達連續發生的動作或結果。
  • 過去分詞作後位修飾 (Past Participle as Post-modifier)
    ...to hunt for bargains in the local equity market, which had been hammered in the previous month amid global volatility, Tsai said.
    句中的「hammered」是過去分詞，構成被動的形容詞片語，用來修飾前面的「equity market（股市）」，意指該市場在先前幾個月受到全球市場波動的「重擊/重挫」，這種後位修飾結構在財經新聞中非常常見。
  • 與過去事實相反的假設語氣 (Subjunctive Past Perfect)
    Without the central bank's intervention, the greenback could have seen a steeper decline, market analysts said.
    句首的「Without...」隱含了條件句（即 If it had not been for...）。主句使用了「could have + 過去分詞 (could have seen)」，這是標準的與過去事實相反的假設語氣結構，用來表達「如果當時沒有央行干預，美元本來會看到更陡峭的跌幅（但事實上有干預）」。
  • 對等連接詞連接的多重子句 (Parallel Structure with Conjunctions)
    To help the local foreign exchange market remain stable, Tsai said the central bank intervened by buying the greenback and selling the Taiwan dollar to moderate the American unit's losses.
    句中的「by buying the greenback and selling the Taiwan dollar」展現了對等連接詞「and」連接兩個動名詞片語（buying... 和 selling...）的平行結構，介系詞「by」同時支配這兩個動作，表達央行藉由這兩項措施來達成穩定市場的目的。
