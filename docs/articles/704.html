羽球雙打組合在越南公開賽奪冠

Badminton duo win in Vietnam

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • tournament n. 錦標賽、比賽
    Taiwan’s Chen Zhi-ray and Lin Yu-chieh won the men’s doubles final at the Yonex-Sunrise Vietnam Open badminton tournament on Sunday, dispatching their Singaporean opponents in 27 minutes in back-to-back games.
    臺灣選手陳志雷與林煜傑於週日在優乃克-日出越南羽球公開賽的男子雙打決賽中奪冠，他們在 27 分鐘內以直落二擊敗新加坡對手。
  • defeat v. 擊敗、戰勝
    Chen and Lin defeated Singapore’s Terry Hee and Howin Wong at the Badminton World Federation (BWF) Super 100 event 21-14, 21-12 at the Nguyen Du Club in Ho Chi Minh City.
    陳志雷與林煜傑在胡志明市阮杜俱樂部舉行的世界羽球聯盟（BWF）超級 100 賽事中，以 21-14、21-12 擊敗新加坡的許永凱與黃光耀。
  • victory n. 勝利
    The victory is the duo’s first BWF Tour event win since they paired up in 2024.
    這場勝利是這對組合自 2024 年搭檔以來，獲得的第一座 BWF 世界巡回賽冠軍。
  • challenging adj. 具挑戰性的、艱鉅的
    In Sunday’s match, Lin said he felt the most challenging part for him was defense, as both pairs attempted to get ahead early in each game.
    在週日的比賽中，林煜傑表示他覺得對他而言最具挑戰性的是防守，因為雙方組合都在每局比賽一開始就試圖取得領先。
  • offensive adj. / n. 進攻的、攻勢
    “We were just more patient, so we found the right moment to go on the offensive,” Lin said.
    林煜傑說：「我們只是更有耐心，所以找到了發動攻擊的正確時機。」
  • perform v. 表現、履行
    “We performed to our expectations in this finals match,” Chen said.
    陳志雷說：「我們在這場決賽中發揮出了我們的水準。」
  • delegation n. 代表團
    Despite the fact that the duo was not named in Taiwan’s delegation to this year’s Asian Games in Japan’s Aichi Prefecture later this month, Chen said he and his teammate would continue entering international competitions to increase their overall rankings.
    儘管這對雙打組合未被列入本月稍晚參加日本愛知縣亞洲運動會的臺灣代表團名單中，陳志雷表示他和隊友將繼續參加國際比賽，以提高他們的整體排名。

文法重點

  • 現在完成式 (Present Perfect Tense)
    The victory is the duo’s first BWF Tour event win since they paired up in 2024.
    「have/has + 過去分詞」用以表示一個動作從過去某個時間點開始（在此指 2024 年兩人搭檔以來），持續到現在並對現在造成影響的狀態。
  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense)
    Chen, 24, and Lin, 26, had claimed top spots in three lower-level tournaments, before winning a breakthrough second-place finish...
    「had + 過去分詞」用來表達在過去某一事件（贏得亞軍）「之前」已經完成的動作，凸顯過去兩個動作的前後順序。
  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況/原因 (Participial Phrase)
    In Sunday’s match, Lin said he felt the most challenging part for him was defense, as both pairs attempted to get ahead early in each game.
    句尾的 as 引導原因子句，而句中運用分詞與連接詞搭配表達動作的同時發生與背景狀況，是多益閱讀常考的句型結構。
  • 介系詞片語 Despite the fact that 引導讓步子句
    Despite the fact that the duo was not named in Taiwan’s delegation to this year’s Asian Games... Chen said he and his teammate would continue entering international competitions...
    「Despite the fact that + 子句」相當於 Though 或 Although，用來表達「儘管……事實卻是……」的讓步語氣，多益考試中常考 Despite 與 Although 的文法替換。
