‘Taiwan Review’ closes after 75 years. What happens to its archives?

單字解析

  • launch v. 發起、啟動、創辦、發行
    In 1951, the Central Propaganda Department of the Chinese Nationalist Party’s (KMT) Central Reform Committee launched an English-language magazine...
    1951年，中國國民黨中央改造委員會中央宣傳部創辦了一份英文雜誌...
  • termination n. 終止、結束
    Though the Review’s termination is generally accepted as part of a general decline of print media...
    儘管《評論》的終止被普遍接受為紙媒整體衰落的一部分...
  • budget n. 預算
    The Web site removals have come as a result of budget cuts by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA)...
    網站內容的移除是外交部節省預算的結果...
  • extend v. 延伸、擴及、延長
    The cuts extend to Taiwan Today, a daily news platform in nine languages...
    這些刪減擴及了《Taiwan Today》，這是一個涵蓋九種語言的每日新聞平台...
  • discontinue v. 停止、停產、停刊
    MOFA has also discontinued the print edition of Taiwan Panorama...
    外交部也停刊了《台灣光華雜誌》的紙本版...
  • lay off phr. v. 解雇、裁員
    In all, the ministry has cut a total of NT$57 million in annual publication budgets and laid off at least 25 full-time workers.
    總計，外交部總共削減了新台幣5700萬元的年度出版預算，並裁撤了至少25名全職員工。
  • attribute v. 歸因於、歸咎於
    MOFA has attributed the downsizing to declining readership and a changing media environment...
    外交部將業務緊縮歸因於讀者群的減少和不斷變化的媒體環境...
  • redundancy n. 冗員、重複、多餘、重疊
    Ministry personnel have also spoken of redundancy with other government-supported news platforms.
    部內人員也談到與其他政府支持的新聞平台存在重複的情況。
  • mitigate v. 減輕、緩和
    While critics recognize these as good reasons, it has not mitigated their distress over the disappearance of a digital archive...
    雖然批評者認為這些是合理的理由，但這並未減輕他們對一個數位檔案庫消失的痛苦...
  • priority n. 優先事項、優先權
    Physical archives exist in at least one national library, and any future actions will depend on budgets, needs and priorities...
    實體檔案存在於至少一個國家圖書館中，任何未來的行動將取決於預算、需求和優先順序...
  • exclusive adj. 專有的、獨家的、排外的
    In its first decades, the Free China Review was written and edited exclusively by Republic of China nationals...
    在其最初的幾十年裡，《自由中國評論》完全由中華民國國民撰寫和編輯...
  • dismantle v. 解散、拆卸
    That was the year the government dissolved the GIO and reassigned several of its publishing departments to MOFA.
    那是政府解散新聞局並將其多個出版部門重新指派給外交部的一年。（註：原文使用 dissolve，多益亦常考 dismantle/dissolve）

文法重點

  • 非限定性關係子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    In 1951, the Central Propaganda Department of the Chinese Nationalist Party’s (KMT) Central Reform Committee launched an English-language magazine, which, for the next 75 years, would serve as a mouthpiece for Taiwan’s government.
    句中的「which」引導一個非限定性關係子句（前面帶有逗號），用來補充說明前面的先行詞「an English-language magazine」，在句中提供額外的附加資訊。子句中又插入了時間副詞片語「for the next 75 years」，使結構更具文學與新聞敘事感。
  • 分詞構句作後置修飾 (Participial Phrase as Post-modifier)
    MOFA has also discontinued the print edition of Taiwan Panorama, a bilingual English-and-Chinese cultural magazine launched in 1976 as Sinorama...
    過去分詞片語「launched in 1976 as Sinorama」在此省略了「which was」，用來修飾前面的名詞「a bilingual English-and-Chinese cultural magazine」。這種用法在商業與新聞英語中極為常見，能有效簡化句子結構並避免重複。
  • 假設語氣/條件句的應用 (Conditional Context / Subjunctive meaning)
    (Washington would have considered such a move disastrous, as it would have brought the superpowers into direct and open conflict.)
    括號內的句子使用了與過去事實相反的假設語氣結構（would have + 過去分詞），用來推論如果當時美國採行了該項軍事建議，將會導致「把超級大國捲入衝突」的災難性後果。這在表達歷史或商業決策的風險評估時非常實用。
  • 複合關係代間詞引導的名詞子句 (Noun Clause with What)
    As for the fate of the digital archive, the editors and scholars interviewed for this article have various ideas.
    標題中的「What happens to its archives?」是由疑問代間詞「What」引導的名詞子句（在此作為主句的主詞或獨立問句形式），用來探討數位檔案庫未來的走向與命運。在多益聽力與閱讀測驗中，常用「What happens to...」來詢問人或物的下場與處理方式。
