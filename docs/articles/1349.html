AI 能幫助學生更深入思考嗎？

Can AI help students think harder?

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • frustration n. 挫折，沮喪
    Chen Chih-nung (陳啟濃), a former principal at Kuohsin Junior High School in Nantou County, wrote about writer Chu Yu-hsun’s (朱宥勳) frustration with artificial intelligence (AI) in creative writing
    南投縣國興國中前校長陳啟濃針對作家朱宥勳對人工智慧（AI）在創意寫作上感到挫折一事發表了看法
  • prohibit v. 禁止
    Chu said that he would refuse to judge any literary award that did not explicitly prohibit the use of AI.
    朱宥勳表示，他將拒絕擔任任何未明確禁止使用 AI 的文學獎評審。
  • establish v. 確立，建立
    Literary competitions have always had to establish authorship.
    文學競賽一向必須確立著作權（原創性）。
  • evidence n. 證據
    A 30-minute interview with shortlisted writers could provide conclusive evidence to show whether a piece was written by AI
    對入圍作家進行 30 分鐘的訪談，可以提供決定性的證據，以證明一篇作品是由 AI 撰寫的
  • evolution n. 演變，發展
    Writers know their work intimately: the plot, character arcs, structural decisions and evolution of the prose.
    作家對自己的作品暸如指掌：情節、角色發展弧線、結構上的決定以及散文的演變。
  • outline v. 概述，畫出大綱
    Clear rules should outline whether a competition prohibits AI entirely or allows it for editing.
    明確的規則應該概述一項競賽是完全禁止 AI 還是允許將其用於編輯。
  • discourage v. 阻止，勸阻
    Announcing an interview requirement would itself discourage people from flooding competitions with prompt-generated manuscripts.
    宣佈面試要求本身就會阻止人們用提示詞生成的稿件灌爆競賽。
  • extend v. 延伸，擴展
    The question of how to respond to AI extends well beyond literary competitions.
    如何應對 AI 的問題遠遠超出了文學競賽的範疇。
  • misuse v. 濫用
    Chen, like many educators, also worries that students will misuse AI “to the point that their intellectual and creative abilities stagnate.”
    陳啟濃和許多教育工作者一樣，也擔心學生會濫用 AI「到他們的智力和創造力能力停滯不前的程度」。
  • restricting v. 限制（restrict 的動名詞/分詞）
    General solutions often involve restricting AI while returning to handwriting, reading aloud and other traditional methods.
    一般的解決方案通常包括限制 AI，同時回歸手寫、朗讀和其他傳統方法。
  • sophisticated adj. 複雜的，高深成熟的
    A weak argument receives one kind of challenge; a sophisticated argument, a more sophisticated one.
    薄弱的論點會受到某一類型的挑戰；而精細的論點則會受到更精細的挑戰。
  • determine v. 決定
    Each answer determines the next question.
    每一個回答決定了下一個問題。
  • defend v. 辯護，防衛
    The teacher would grade the student’s prompts, writing and reasoning, as well as how well they know the subject, put forward arguments, respond to counterarguments and defend or revise their position
    老師將評量學生的提示詞、寫作和推理能力，以及他們對學科的了解程度、提出論點、回應反論點，並在出現更好的證據時捍衛或修正他們的立場

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    A finished product can no longer reliably demonstrate how much intellectual work occurred inside a student’s head.
    在此句中，「finished」是過去分詞，用來修飾名詞 product（產品），相當於形容詞的功能，表示「被完成的產品」。
  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    For the first time, every student could have something resembling an individual examiner, capable of listening to an answer, challenging it and adapting the next question accordingly.
    句中的「resembling an individual examiner」是由現在分詞 resembling 引導的分詞片語，用來修飾前方的代名詞 something；後方的「capable of...」則是形容詞片語作後位修飾，補充說明 examiner 的能力。
  • 副詞片語置於句首加強語氣 (Adverbial Phrase at the Beginning for Emphasis)
    For the first time, every student could have something resembling an individual examiner...
    「For the first time」（破天荒第一次／有史以來第一次）這個時間副詞片語被放在句首，用來強調接下來發生的轉變或新可能性。
  • 間接問句 (Indirect Question / Noun Clause)
    The question of how to respond to AI extends well beyond literary competitions.
    「how to respond to AI」是一個由「疑問詞 + to V」構成的名詞片語結構，在此作介詞 of 的受詞，用來表達「如何回應 AI」這個具體的問題內容。
