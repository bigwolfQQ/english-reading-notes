OpenAI 今年不會上市：執行長山姆·奧特曼

OpenAI will not go public this year: CEO Sam Altman

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • go public phr. 上市（公司公開發行股票）
    OpenAI will not go public this year, CEO Sam Altman said in remarks published on Saturday...
    OpenAI 執行長山姆·奧特曼（Sam Altman）在週六發布的談話中表示，OpenAI 今年不會上市……
  • concern n. 擔憂、關切
    ...citing safety concerns over artificial intelligence (AI) that render “unacceptable” even a 10 percent risk...
    ……並指出對人工智慧（AI）安全性的擔憂，使得即使只有 10% 的風險……也「令人無法接受」
  • urge v. 敦促、力勸
    The comments come as more US lawmakers urge new rules to govern AI systems...
    這些評論發表之際，美國國會議員紛紛敦促制定新規來管轄 AI 系統……
  • govern v. 管理、統治、規範
    The comments come as more US lawmakers urge new rules to govern AI systems...
    這些評論發表之際，美國國會議員紛紛敦促制定新規來管轄 AI 系統……
  • rival n. 競爭對手
    ...after dire warnings from two researchers from OpenAI rival Anthropic PBC that rapidly progressing AI could lead to the extinction of the human race...
    ……此前，來自 OpenAI 競爭對手 Anthropic PBC 的兩名研究人員發出可怕警告，稱急劇發展的 AI 可能會導致人類滅絕……
  • ill-advised adj. 不明智的、輕率的
    “I actually think that, given everything happening with safety, right now would be an ill-advised moment to go public...”
    「我實際上認為，鑑於安全方面發生的所有事情，現在上市會是一個不明智的時機……」
  • pressure n. 壓力
    “...and we don’t feel pressure on that,” Altman said.
    「……我們對此並沒有感受到壓力，」奧特曼說。
  • estimate n. 估計、估價
    Asked if there was a 10 percent chance of AI-driven extinction, Altman said he did not know how such an estimate could be made...
    當被問及是否有 10% 的機率發生 AI 引發的滅絕時，奧特曼表示他不知道如何做出這樣的估計……
  • tolerate v. 容忍、忍受
    ...but that the risk was serious enough that AI companies and governments should act as though it could not be tolerated.
    ……但這個風險足夠嚴重，以至於 AI 公司和政府的行事方式應該像是這種風險無法被容忍一樣。
  • responsibility n. 責任
    “Whether it’s 10 or eight or six, the point is, we all have a tremendous amount of responsibility...”
    「無論是 10%、8% 還是 6%，重點在於，我們都肩負著巨大的責任……」
  • incentive n. 誘因、鼓勵
    “...and cannot let egos or incentives for profit or anything else get in the way,” Altman added.
    「……不能讓 egos（自我）或利潤誘因或任何其他事物阻礙這一切，」奧特曼補充說。
  • initial public offering n. 首次公開募股（IPO）
    Asked if an initial public offering this year was off the table in favor of next year, Altman said...
    當被問及今年是否不考慮進行首次公開募股而傾向於明年時，奧特曼說……

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作附帶說明 / 原因說明
    OpenAI will not go public this year, CEO Sam Altman said in remarks published on Saturday, citing safety concerns over artificial intelligence (AI)...
    句尾的「citing...」是由分詞構句簡化而來，原意為「because he cited...（因為他舉出……）」，用來補充說明前面「今年不會上市」的原因。
  • as 引導的時間副詞子句（伴隨「隨著」）
    The comments come as more US lawmakers urge new rules to govern AI systems...
    連接詞 as 在這裡表示「隨著」，用來引導一個子句，描述某個動作發生時，另一個背景情境也正在發展或改變。
  • 過去分詞作後置定語
    ...CEO Sam Altman said in remarks published on Saturday...
    過去分詞片語「published on Saturday」用來修飾前面的名詞「remarks（談話/評論）」，相當於形容詞子句「which were published on Saturday」，省略了主格關代與be動詞。
  • as though（彷彿、好像）引導假設語氣子句
    ...that the risk was serious enough that AI companies and governments should act as though it could not be tolerated.
    「as though」後面接子句，用來表達「彷彿 / 就像是……一樣」的假設或比喻語氣，說明應對風險時應採取無法容忍的態度。
