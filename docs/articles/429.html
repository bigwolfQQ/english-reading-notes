單字解析

  • consecutive adj. 連續的
    Japanese rider Ai Ogura is to miss a second consecutive MotoGP race at this weekend’s San Marino Grand Prix...
    日本車手小倉愛將在本週末的聖馬力諾大獎賽中連續第二場缺席 MotoGP 賽事...
  • standings n. （體育競賽的）排名、積分榜
    Ogura slipped from third to fifth in the world championship standings while sitting out the Aragon Grand Prix in late August.
    小倉愛在 8 月下旬缺席亞 Aragón 大獎賽期間，在世界錦標賽積分榜上的排名從第三跌至第五。
  • assessment n. 評估、評價
    “A medical assessment in Barcelona late on Tuesday confirmed his injured left thumb has not healed enough for him to ride,” Aprilia-Trackhouse said in a social media post.
    「巴塞隆納當地週二晚間的醫療評估確認，他受傷的左手拇指尚未完全復原，無法騎車，」Aprilia-Trackhouse 在社群媒體貼文中表示。
  • focus on phr. v. 專注於、聚焦於
    “He will remain in Barcelona, focusing on efforts to regain fitness ahead of the Grand Prix of Austria, one week away.”
    「他將留在巴塞隆納，專注於恢復體能，以趕上一週後的奧地利大獎賽。」
  • statement n. 聲明、說法
    The 11-year-old, who has been karting from an early age, has already secured victories at national and international level, Red Bull said in a statement.
    紅牛在聲明中表示，這位 11 歲的少年從小就開始玩卡丁車，並已在國家及國際級賽事中奪冠。
  • demonstrate v. 展現、證明
    “Robin has already demonstrated exceptional ability in karting.”
    「羅賓已在卡丁車賽場上展現出非凡的能力。」
  • ability n. 能力、才能
    “Robin has already demonstrated exceptional ability in karting.”
    「羅賓已在卡丁車賽場上展現出非凡的能力。」
  • program n. 計畫、方案
    Robin Raikkonen, the son of 2007 Formula One (F1) world champion Kimi Raikkonen, has joined Red Bull’s junior driver program, the team announced on Wednesday.
    2007 年一級方程式（F1）世界冠軍奇米·萊科寧的兒子羅賓·萊科寧已加入紅牛的青年車手培訓計畫，車隊於週三宣布。

文法重點

  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    Japanese baseball legend Isao Harimoto, who survived the atomic bombing of Hiroshima and became the first player from his country to reach 3,000 hits, has died aged 86, reports said yesterday.
    句中的「who survived... 3,000 hits」為非限定用法的關係代名詞子句，用逗號隔開，用來補充說明主詞「Japanese baseball legend Isao Harimoto」的背景生平。
  • 分詞構句（表示附帶狀況或結果）
    He survived the atomic bombing as a five-year-old, his mother jumping in front of him and his sister to shield them from the blast.
    句尾的「his mother jumping...」為獨立分詞構句（分詞有自己的主語 his mother），用來補充說明他當時存活的背景或發生的狀況。
  • 現在分詞片語作後置修飾語
    Ogura slipped from third to fifth in the world championship standings while sitting out the Aragon Grand Prix in late August.
    「while sitting out...」為省略了主格代名詞與be動詞的分詞片語（原形為 while he was sitting out），用來修飾主句的主語 Ogura，表示在……的同時。
