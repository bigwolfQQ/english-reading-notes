農業部尋求規範寵物照護訓練標準

MOA seeks to standardize pet care training

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • standardize v. 使標準化
    The Ministry of Agriculture (MOA) is developing standardized educational materials for pet care professionals to ensure quality of training across institutions...
    農業部（MOA）正在為寵物照護專業人員開發標準化教材，以確保各機構的培訓品質……
  • ensure v. 確保、保證
    ...to ensure quality of training across institutions, and plans to work with the Ministry of Labor to introduce a pet care technician certificate...
    ……以確保各機構的培訓品質，並計劃與勞動部合作推出寵物照護技術士證照……
  • collaborate / work with v. phr. 與……合作
    ...and plans to work with the Ministry of Labor to introduce a pet care technician certificate, an MOA official said.
    農業部官員表示，並計劃與勞動部合作推出寵物照護技術士證照。
  • drive v. 驅動、推動、促使
    The growing pet market is driving demand for pet care professionals...
    日益成長的寵物市場正帶動（驅動）對寵物照護專業人員的需求……
  • demand n. 需求、要求
    The growing pet market is driving demand for pet care professionals, but training materials remain inconsistent in quality and content, the MOA said.
    農業部表示，日益成長的寵物市場正帶動對寵物照護專業人員的需求，但培訓教材在品質和內容上仍然參差不齊。
  • inconsistent adj. 不一致的、矛盾的、參差不齊的
    ...but training materials remain inconsistent in quality and content, the MOA said.
    ……但農業部表示，培訓教材在品質和內容上仍然參差不齊。
  • expertise n. 專業知識、專長
    To standardize pet care professionals’ expertise, the ministry has commissioned the Chinese Society of Animal Science to compile professional teaching materials...
    為了規範寵物照護專業人員的專業知識，該部已委託中國畜牧學會編纂專業教學材料……
  • commission v. 委託、委任
    ...the ministry has commissioned the Chinese Society of Animal Science to compile professional teaching materials covering pet care, physiology and nutrition, it said.
    該部表示，已委託中國畜牧學會編纂涵蓋寵物照護、生理學和營養學的專業教學材料。
  • compile v. 編纂、彙編
    ...to compile professional teaching materials covering pet care, physiology and nutrition, it said.
    ……以編纂涵蓋寵物照護、生理學和營養學的專業教學材料。
  • phase n. 階段、時期
    ...with the first phase focusing on common pets such as dogs and cats, he said.
    他說，第一階段將以狗、貓等常見寵物為主。
  • qualification n. 資格、學歷、條件
    ...must have at least one staff member who meets one of several qualifications, including completion of 200 hours of professional training...
    ……必須具備至少一名符合數項資格之一的員工，包括完成 200 小時專業訓練……
  • relevant adj. 相關的
    The Ministry of Labor already has some relevant certifications, including one for pet grooming...
    勞動部已經具備一些相關證照，包含一項寵物美容證照……

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    ...vocational schools and colleges offering programs in animal science, veterinary medicine and related fields, it said.
    句中的「offering programs in animal science...」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「colleges and vocational schools」，相當於形容詞子句「which offer programs in...」。這種結構在商業及新聞英文中非常常見，能讓句子更簡潔。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    No launch date has been set as details still need to be discussed, he added.
    句中的「has been set」與「need to be discussed」使用了被動語態（be + 過去分詞）。當動作的承受者是焦點，或者發動動作的人不明、不重要時，英文常使用被動語態。在此例中，發佈日期是被決定的，細節是被討論的。
  • 表目的的不定詞片語 (Infinitive Phrase of Purpose)
    To standardize pet care professionals’ expertise, the ministry has commissioned the Chinese Society of Animal Science...
    句首的「To standardize...」是不定詞（to + V）片語置於句首作副詞用，用來表達目的，意思是「為了要標準化……」。這種用法在商業報導與新聞中常出現，用以破題說明某個行動背後的目標。
  • 對等連接詞連接複雜子句 (Compound Sentence with Conjunction)
    The growing pet market is driving demand for pet care professionals, but training materials remain inconsistent in quality and content, the MOA said.
    此句透過對等連接詞「but」連接兩個完整的獨立子句（S + V），表達前後兩者之間的轉折對比關係。前半部說明市場需求增加，後半部則點出教材品質不一的問題。
