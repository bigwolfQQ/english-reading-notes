高等法院依發展與中共關聯組織罪名判處兩人刑責

High court sentences two for developing CCP-linked organization

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • sentence v. 宣判、判刑
    The High Court yesterday sentenced businessman Cheng Ming-chia (鄭明嘉) to 10 years and six months in prison for developing an organization for the Chinese Communist Party’s (CCP) 'united front' authorities.
    高等法院昨天依為中國共產黨「統戰」當局發展組織之罪名，判處商人鄭明嘉有期徒刑10年6個月。
  • attempt v. / n. 企圖、嘗試
    Retired Executive Yuan official Hu Peng-nien (胡鵬年) was sentenced to five years for attempting to do the same.
    退休行政院官員胡鵬年因企圖從事相同行為，被判處有期徒刑5年。
  • appeal v. / n. 上訴
    The ruling can be appealed.
    全案可上訴。
  • approach v. 接近、向...交涉
    From 2021 to last year, Cheng helped Chinese 'united front' officials approach and attempt to recruit targets in Taiwan by providing contact information and arranging meetings in China, the court said.
    法院表示，從2021年至去年，鄭男協助中國「統戰」官員接觸並企圖在台灣物色招募對象，方法是提供聯絡資訊並安排在中國會面。
  • recruit v. 招募、吸收（新成員）
    He recruited Hu as a potential operative, while his efforts to recruit another individual surnamed Huang (黃) failed after Huang did not respond.
    他招募胡男作為潛在情報員，而他試圖招募另一名黃姓人士則因黃男未予回應而失敗。
  • evidence n. 證據
    It said the evidence against Cheng was clear and rejected his defense.
    法院指出，針對鄭男的證據明確，並駁回其辯詞。
  • instruct v. 指示、教授
    Both defendants followed instructions from CCP personnel to facilitate the recruitment of new targets, the court said.
    法院表示，兩名被告皆聽從中共人員的指示，以利招募新目標。
  • facilitate v. 促進、使便利、協助
    Both defendants followed instructions from CCP personnel to facilitate the recruitment of new targets, the court said.
    法院表示，兩名被告皆聽從中共人員的指示，以利招募新目標。
  • transport v. 運輸、運送
    Hu also transported a communications device supplied by the Chinese authorities into Taiwan and handed it to another person, conduct that the court said significantly aided Beijing’s “united front” efforts.
    胡男還將中國當局提供的通訊設備運送至台灣並交給他人，法院指出此行為顯著協助了北京的「統戰」工作。
  • indictment n. 起訴書
    A January indictment by the High Prosecutors’ Office said...
    高等檢察署1月的起訴書指出...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 / 附帶狀況
    From 2021 to last year, Cheng helped Chinese "united front" officials approach and attempt to recruit targets in Taiwan by providing contact information and arranging meetings in China, the court said.
    句中的 "by providing contact information and arranging meetings in China" 是由介系詞 by 接兩個動名詞片語構成，用來具體說明主詞完成動作（協助招募）的「手段或方法」，在多益閱讀與聽力中常用於表達具體做法。
  • 過去分詞片語作形容詞用（修飾名詞）
    Hu also transported a communications device supplied by the Chinese authorities into Taiwan and handed it to another person...
    句中的 "supplied by the Chinese authorities" 是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞 "a communications device"（通訊設備）。這相當於形容詞子句 "which was supplied by the Chinese authorities" 的簡化，能讓商業與新聞英語更加精鍊。
  • 對等連接詞連接兩個對等的子句
    Hu admitted to the offenses, while Cheng denied wrongdoing throughout the investigation and trial, the court said.
    連接詞 "while" 在此用來表示對比（而、然而），連接兩個完整的子句（Hu admitted... 與 Cheng denied...），突顯兩名被告在司法程序中截然不同的態度。
