單字解析

  • tender v. 提出、交出（辭呈等）
    Overseas Community Affairs Council (OCAC) Minister Hsu Chia-ching (徐佳青) yesterday tendered her resignation amid allegations that she and her family misused public resources during her overseas trips...
    僑務委員會委員長徐佳青昨天請辭，起因是她和家人在海外行程中被指控濫用公務資源...
  • resignation n. 辭職、辭呈
    Premier Cho Jung-tai (卓榮泰) approved the resignation yesterday morning, Cabinet spokeswoman Michelle Lee (李慧芝) said.
    行政院發言人李慧芝表示，行政院長卓榮泰已於昨天上午批准了辭呈。
  • allegation n. （未經證實的）指控、指責
    The council, a Cabinet-level agency responsible for relations with overseas Taiwanese and ethnic Chinese communities, and Hsu have denied the TPP’s allegations.
    僑委會這個負責與海外台灣人及華人社群聯繫的內閣級機關以及徐佳青，皆已否認民眾黨的指控。
  • agency n. （政府的）局、署、機構
    The council, a Cabinet-level agency responsible for relations with overseas Taiwanese and ethnic Chinese communities, and Hsu have denied the TPP’s allegations.
    僑委會這個負責與海外台灣人及華人社群聯繫的內閣級機關以及徐佳青，皆已否認民眾黨的指控。
  • statement n. 聲明、陳述
    Hsu said in a statement that during her six-and-a-half years at the OCAC... she had repeatedly faced “unfounded accusations from certain lawmakers.”
    徐佳青在一份聲明中表示，她在僑委會六年半的任期內……曾多次面對「特定立委毫無根據的指控」。
  • investigation n. 調查
    She was also resigning to allow the administrative and judicial authorities to conduct their investigations independently and objectively...
    她同時也為了讓行政與司法機關能獨立且客觀地進行調查而請辭...
  • budget n. 預算
    The TPP lawmakers said Hsu has traveled abroad 47 times for a total of 526 days since 2022, spending NT$23.83 million... or about one-third of the council’s international travel budget.
    民眾黨立委表示，徐佳青自2022年以來已出國47次，總計526天，花費新台幣2,383萬元……約佔該委員會國際差運預算的三分之一。
  • delegate n. 代表（句中 delegation 為代表團）
    During an OCAC trip to Germany and France in 2022, Hsu, then deputy minister, had her son added to the delegation as a “staff photographer”...
    在2022年僑委會前往德國和法國的行程中，當時擔任副委員長的徐佳青將兒子以「隨團攝影師」身分加入訪問團...
  • breach v. 違反、違背（法律、合約等）
    The TPP lawmakers said Hsu’s actions breached conflict-of-interest rules for public servants and violated the Criminal Code.
    民眾黨立委表示，徐佳青的行為違反了公務員利益衝突迴避法，並觸犯了刑法。
  • suspend v. 暫停、使停職
    They called for Hsu to be suspended pending an ethics investigation.
    他們呼籲將徐佳青停職，靜候倫理調查。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作後置修飾或表附帶狀況
    Overseas Community Affairs Council (OCAC) Minister Hsu Chia-ching (徐佳青) yesterday tendered her resignation amid allegations that she and her family misused public resources during her overseas trips, saying she wanted to allow official investigations to proceed.
    句尾的「saying she wanted to allow...」是由現在分詞片語構成的補充說明，用來表達主句動作發生時的附帶狀況或結果，相當於「and she said that...」，在新聞英語中非常常見，用以引述人物在做出某個決定（此處為請辭）時的說詞或動機。
  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾（減化的形容詞子句）
    The council, a Cabinet-level agency responsible for relations with overseas Taiwanese and ethnic Chinese communities, and Hsu have denied the TPP’s allegations.
    句中的「responsible for relations with overseas Taiwanese...」是一個形容詞片語（省略了「which is」的過去分詞/形容詞結構），用來修飾前面的名詞短語「a Cabinet-level agency」，補充說明該機構的職責範圍。
  • 使役動詞 have 的用法 (have + O. + p.p.)
    During an OCAC trip to Germany and France in 2022, Hsu, then deputy minister, had her son added to the delegation as a “staff photographer”...
    此句使用了使役動詞 have 的「have + 受詞 + 過去分詞 (p.p.)」結構（had her son added），表示「讓某人／某物被……」或「使……被處理」，在此指徐佳青讓她的兒子被編入訪問團名單中。
