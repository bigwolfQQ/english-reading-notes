文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾語 (Past Participle as Postmodifier)
    The first group of foreign skilled workers recruited for Taiwan's hospitality sector arrived from Indonesia on Monday
    句中的 "recruitment for Taiwan's hospitality sector" 被簡化為過去分詞片語 "recruited for..."，用來修飾前面的名詞 "foreign skilled workers"（外籍技術勞工），相當於關代子句 "who were recruited for..."。
  • 現在分詞片語作附加說明 (Present Participle Phrase indicating result/circumstance)
    ...marking the rollout of a government program aimed at easing labor shortages in the hotel industry.
    句尾的 "marking..." 是現在分詞引導的片語，用來補充說明前面整件事所帶來的結果或象徵意義（「標誌著...的推出」），讓句子結構更緊湊。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    ...arrived at Taiwan Taoyuan International Airport under the Multinational Skilled Worker Enhancement Program, which Taiwan launched in January to allow hospitality businesses to recruit foreign skilled workers.
    句中的 "which Taiwan launched in January..." 是非限定關係代名詞子句，用來補充說明前面的專有名稱計畫（Multinational Skilled Worker Enhancement Program），並在句中提供額外的背景資訊。
  • As of（截至……）與時間介系詞片語
    As of the end of August, more than 60 hospitality businesses had applied to conduct domestic recruitment...
    "As of + 時間" 是多益常考的片語，意指「截至……為止」。搭配主句使用過去完成式（had applied），表達在過去某個時間點之前已經完成的動作。
