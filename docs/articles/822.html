歐盟執主席范德賴恩提出與加拿大建立夥伴關係

EU President Von der Leyen floats Canada partnership

單字解析

  • call on phr v 呼籲、請求
    European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen yesterday called on EU states to work together and form a new partnership with Canada to tackle climate change...
    歐洲聯盟執行委員會主席烏蘇拉·范德賴恩昨天呼籲歐盟成員國團結合作，並與加拿大建立新的夥伴關係，以應對氣候變遷...
  • tackle v 處理、對付、解決
    ...form a new partnership with Canada to tackle climate change, competition from China and other challenges.
    ...並與加拿大建立新的夥伴關係，以應對氣候變遷、來自中國的競爭以及其他挑戰。
  • priority n 優先事項、首要之務
    ...setting out priorities for the 27-nation EU for the year ahead.
    ...並為這個由 27 個成員國組成的歐盟規劃了未來一年的優先工作。
  • solidarity n 團結
    Solidarity among member states was the only possible solution, she said.
    她表示，成員國之間的團結是唯一可能的解決方案。
  • rivalry n 競爭、對抗
    On trade and other issues, it is also facing stiff rivalry from the US and China.
    在貿易和其他議題上，它也面臨來自美國和中國的激烈競爭。
  • maintain v 維持、保持
    To maintain EU independence in sectors including energy, raw materials and clean technologies...
    為了維持歐盟在能源、原物料和清潔技術等領域的獨立性...
  • sector n 部門、行業、領域
    To maintain EU independence in sectors including energy, raw materials and clean technologies...
    為了維持歐盟在能源、原物料和清潔技術等領域的獨立性...
  • reduce v 減少、縮減
    ...Von der Leyen said that the EU will use every possible means to reduce its “unsustainable” trade deficit with China.
    ...范德賴恩表示，歐盟將動用一切可能的手段來縮減其對中國「不可持續的」貿易逆差。
  • deficit n 赤字、逆差
    ...to reduce its “unsustainable” trade deficit with China.
    ...來縮減其對中國「不可持續的」貿易逆差。
  • disposal n 清除、處理；at one's disposal 意為「可自由使用、任憑差遣」
    “Let me be clear: We will use all the tools at our disposal to rebalance our relationship,” she said.
    她說：「讓我說清楚：我們將動用我們掌握的所有工具來重新平衡我們的關係。」

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附加說明（分詞構句）
    The bloc was dealing with “an openly hostile world,” Von der Leyen said in her annual speech to the European Parliament in Strasbourg, France, setting out priorities for the 27-nation EU for the year ahead.
    句尾的 "setting out..." 為現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前面主句所發生的動作或其帶來的結果，相當於 "and she set out..." 的意思，常出現在新聞英語中以精簡語句。
  • 不定詞片語作目的副詞用
    To maintain EU independence in sectors including energy, raw materials and clean technologies, members need to work together as well as have strong partners like Canada, she said.
  • 對等連接詞 as well as 的用法
    ...members need to work together as well as have strong partners like Canada, she said.
    "as well as" 連接兩個對等的動詞片語（work together 與 have strong partners...），強調「不但……而且……」或「以及」的概念，常出現在多益商業閱讀中。
