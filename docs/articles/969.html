賴總統：高雄新產業園區將鞏固半導體產業

New Kaohsiung industrial park to bolster semiconductor sector: Lai

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • strengthen v. 加強，鞏固
    Construction began Monday on Kaohsiung's Baipu Industrial Park, which President Lai Ching-te (賴清德) said would strengthen Taiwan's semiconductor ecosystem...
    高雄屏埔產業園區於週一動工，總統賴清德表示，這將強化台灣的半導體生態系...
  • boost v. 促進，提升，增加
    ...balance north-south development, and boost the country's global competitiveness.
    ……平衡南北發展，並提升國家的全球競爭力。
  • generation n. 產生，發電，世代（動詞為 generate）
    ...generate about NT$300 billion (US$9.35 billion) in annual production value...
    ……創造約新台幣3,000億元（約93.5億美元）的年產值……
  • cooperation n. 合作，配合
    The park will draw on cooperation among the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Kaohsiung City Government and major semiconductor companies...
    該園區將結合經濟部、高雄市政府以及主要半導體企業的合作……
  • facilities n. 設施，設備
    ...with two buildings housing modular facilities that can be reconfigured as technology evolves.
    ……其中包含兩棟容納模組化設施的建築，這些設施可隨著技術的演進而重新配置。
  • schedule v. 預定，安排（常用被動態 be scheduled for / to）
    ...is scheduled for completion in the fourth quarter of 2029.
    ……預定於2029年第四季完工。
  • infrastructure n. 基礎設施
    The park's entire infrastructure is expected to be completed in 2031, the bureau said.
    局方指出，該園區的整體基礎設施預計於2031年完工。
  • aim v. 旨在，目的是
    Lai said the park aims to become Taiwan's first semiconductor base...
    賴總統表示，該園區旨在成為台灣首座半導體基地……
  • establish v. 建立，設立
    ...and help Taiwanese companies establish a foothold in the global supply chain, he said.
    他說，這能協助台灣企業在全球供應鏈中站穩腳步。
  • complement v. 補足，互補
    ...Baipu Industrial Park is expected to complement an IC assembly and testing cluster at Kaohsiung's Renwu Industrial Park...
    ……屏埔產業園區預期將與高雄仁武產業園區的IC封裝測試聚落相互互補……
  • enhance v. 提升，增加，提高
    The president added that Kaohsiung's strong metal processing and precision machinery industries are expected to enhance the project's value.
    總統補充說，高雄強大的金屬加工與精密機械產業預期將為該計畫提升附加價值。
  • consumption n. 消耗，消費
    On electricity consumption, Lai said state-owned Taiwan Power Co. has agreed to a plan...
    在用電方面，賴總統表示國營的台灣電力公司已同意一項計畫……

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    Construction began Monday on Kaohsiung's Baipu Industrial Park, which President Lai Ching-te (賴清德) said would strengthen Taiwan's semiconductor ecosystem, balance north-south development, and boost the country's global competitiveness.
    句中的關係代名詞子句「which President Lai... said would strengthen...」用來補充說明前面「Baipu Industrial Park」的細節。在商業與新聞英語中，常利用非限定性關係子句來夾帶長篇的官方發言或補充資訊。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The park's entire infrastructure is expected to be completed in 2031, the bureau said.
    多益閱讀與聽力測驗極度常考被動語態。此句中「is expected to be completed」表達「被預期將會被完成」，主詞是 infrastructure（基礎設施），動作的承受者無法自己完成，故使用被動語態。
  • 分詞構句簡化時間副詞子句 (Participial Phrase)
    With the project nearing completion, the previous practice of using water trucks to maintain supplies at science parks will no longer be necessary, Lai said.
    「With + 名詞 + 現在分詞 (nearing)」構成獨立分詞構句，帶有附帶狀況或原因（隨著……接近完工），在商業新聞報導中常用來交代背景或前置條件。
