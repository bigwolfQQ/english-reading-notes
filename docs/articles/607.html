單字解析

  • supply chain n. 供應鏈
    Taiwan cannot be overlooked in building a drone supply chain independent of China, US-based Shield AI’s Taiwan representative Chen Yi-ju (陳奕儒) said in an interview.
    美國極盾人工智慧（Shield AI）公司的台灣代表陳奕儒在接受採訪時表示，在建立一個獨立於中國之外的無人機供應鏈時，台灣是不能被忽視的。
  • supplier n. 供應商
    That figure had risen to 16 percent in May and June this year, and the company expects that Taiwanese suppliers could soon account for as much as 25 percent of the value of an individual drone.
    該數字已上升至百分之十六，且公司預期，台灣供應商很快就能占單個無人機價值高達百分之二十五的比例。
  • account for phr. v. 占（比例、數量等）
    That figure had risen to 16 percent in May and June this year, and the company expects that Taiwanese suppliers could soon account for as much as 25 percent of the value of an individual drone.
    該數字已上升至百分之十六，且公司預期，台灣供應商很快就能占單個無人機價值高達百分之二十五的比例。
  • core adj. 核心的
    Its core products include the Hivemind AI pilot and V-BAT uncrewed aircraft, which enable military platforms to fly autonomously, operate cooperatively and carry out missions in environments subject to electronic interference.
    其核心產品包括 Hivemind AI 飛行員與 V-BAT 無人機，這些產品使軍事平台能夠自主飛行、協同作業，並在面臨電子干擾的環境中執行任務。
  • investment n. 投資
    In an interview with the Liberty Times (sister paper of the Taipei Times ), Chen said that Shield AI’s presence in Taiwan was a long-term investment, citing the nation’s mature drone, electronics, software and hardware industries.
    在接受《自由時報》（《台北時報》的姊妹報）採訪時，陳奕儒表示，Shield AI 在台灣的據點是一項長期投資，並提及台灣成熟的無人機、電子、軟體與硬體產業。
  • potential n. 潛力
    Taiwan has considerable potential to contribute to physical AI, including through academic researchers involved in AI model training, while its hardware industry has strengths in making drones lighter, extending flight times and improving heat dissipation, he said.
    他說，台灣在促成實體人工智慧（physical AI）方面具有相當大的潛力，包括透過參與 AI 模型訓練的學術研究人員，而其硬體產業在讓無人機更輕量化、延長飛行時間以及改善散熱方面也具備優勢。
  • procure v. 採購、取得
    Beyond procuring components, Shield AI wants to develop more capabilities locally.
    除了採購零組件外，Shield AI 還希望在當地發展更多能力。
  • logistics n. 後勤、物流
    Chen said the company hopes to establish local capabilities in logistics, maintenance, training and some assembly, so products introduced to Taiwan would not have to be sent to the US for support during special or wartime circumstances.
    陳奕儒表示，公司希望在後勤、維護、訓練及部分組裝建立在地能力，如此一來，引進台灣的產品在特殊或戰時情況下，就不必送回美國尋求支援。
  • maintenance n. 維護、保養
    Chen said the company hopes to establish local capabilities in logistics, maintenance, training and some assembly, so products introduced to Taiwan would not have to be sent to the US for support during special or wartime circumstances.
    陳奕儒表示，公司希望在後勤、維護、訓練及部分組裝建立在地能力，如此一來，引進台灣的產品在特殊或戰時情況下，就不必送回美國尋求支援。
  • objective n. 目標、目的
    Following flight tests, it demonstrated that two different mission autonomy systems could alternate during a single mission, achieve the same objective and safely return to base.
    在飛行測試後，它證明了兩種不同的任務自主系統可以在單一任務中交替運作、達成相同的目標並安全返回基地。
  • contract n. 合約、契約
    The US Air Force has awarded Shield AI a contract for autonomous mission system software for Collaborative Combat Aircraft.

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（現在分詞表附帶結果或補充說明）
    In an interview with the Liberty Times (sister paper of the Taipei Times ), Chen said that Shield AI’s presence in Taiwan was a long-term investment, citing the nation’s mature drone, electronics, software and hardware industries.
    句尾的「citing...」是由連接詞與主詞簡化而來的分詞構句，用來補充說明前面的動詞片語「was a long-term investment」，表示「舉出／引用……作為佐證」。這在財經與新聞英語中非常常見，用以精簡語句並交代原因或依據。
  • 關係代間詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    Its core products include the Hivemind AI pilot and V-BAT uncrewed aircraft, which enable military platforms to fly autonomously, operate cooperatively and carry out missions in environments subject to electronic interference.
    句中的「which enable...」是非限定關係代間詞子句，前面加了逗號，用來補充說明前面的先行詞（Hivemind AI pilot 與 V-BAT uncrewed aircraft）。「which」代替前面兩項產品，並在子句中擔任主詞。
  • 被動語態
    Taiwan cannot be overlooked in building a drone supply chain independent of China...
    句中的「cannot be overlooked」使用了助動詞加被動語態（be + 過去分詞 overlooked），強調台灣這個受詞承受「被忽視」的動作。多益考試經常測試主動與被動語態的辨識，特別是在主詞無法自行執行該動作的情境下。
  • 動名詞片語當受詞
    Beyond procuring components, Shield AI wants to develop more capabilities locally.
    介系詞「Beyond」後面接動名詞「procuring」形成動名詞片語（procuring components），用來當作介系詞的受詞，表達「除了……之外」。在多益中，介系詞（如 by, for, without, beyond 等）後面若接動詞，一律必須使用動名詞（-ing）型態。
