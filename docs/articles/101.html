文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後置修飾 / 附屬子句
    Su, competing in the group for swimmers turning 16 or 17 in 2026, swam the freshwater lake course in Santa Fe...
    句中的 "competing in the group..." 是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明主詞 Su 的狀態，相當於 "Su, who competed in the group..." 的簡化用法，在新聞英文中非常常見，能讓句子更緊湊。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句
    The bronze was Taiwan's first medal at the World Aquatics Junior Open Water Swimming Championships, which was first held in Brazil in 2006.
    逗號後方的 "which was first held in Brazil in 2006" 為非限定關係子句，對前面的賽事名稱做補充說明，且使用被動語態 "was... held" 表情「被舉辦」。
  • 分詞片語作形容詞用（修飾名詞）
    In the women's 5-kilometer race, open to those turning 14 or 15 this year, seventh-placed 14-year-old Liu Pin-cen was the best Taiwanese finisher...
    句中的 "open to those turning 14 or 15 this year" 是形容詞片語，用來修飾前面的 race；其中的 "turning" 則是現在分詞，用來修飾代名詞 those，指「正要滿...歲的人」。
