文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作補充說明 (Present Participle Phrase for Additional Information)
    ...a political science scholar said Sunday, adding that the speech gave Beijing no pretext for military coercion or provocation.
    句尾的「adding that...」是由連接詞省略與分詞構句而來的現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前面主句所發生的事件之後，學者所作出的額外補充或說法，能讓長句的節奏更緊湊。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    In the speech, titled "Taiwan's Future: A City upon a Hill, the Salt and Light of the World," delivered Saturday...
    句中的「titled...」與「delivered Saturday」都是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「speech」。這種結構省略了「which was」，能精簡句子，在商業與新聞英文中非常常見。
  • WH-名詞子句作受詞 (Wh- Noun Clause as Object)
    Chen Shih-min... said that Lai used the metaphor to highlight Taiwan's decades of efforts...
    雖然此處省略了 that，但動詞 said 後面接的是一個完整的子句（包含主詞 Lai 與動詞 used），作為 said 的受詞，用來引述別人的言論內容。
  • 多重對等名詞片語與動名詞列舉 (Multiple Parallel Gerunds in a List)
    Tsai said Lai emphasized that peace must be maintained through strength, including the development of asymmetric weapons, enhanced defense capabilities and cooperation with Indo-Pacific partners.
    句型利用 including 後面接了一連串的名詞片語（the development of..., enhanced defense capabilities, and cooperation with...）來具體列舉「實力」包含哪些項目，維持了句型的平行對稱。
