外交部敦促民眾切勿前往緬甸高風險地區

MOFA urges people not to go to high-risk areas in Myanmar

單字解析

  • urge v. 敦促、強力推薦
    The Ministry of Foreign Affairs yesterday urged people to make every effort to dissuade their family or friends...
    外交部昨天敦促民眾，若家人或朋友正計畫前往...
  • dissuade v. 勸阻、打消念頭
    ...to make every effort to dissuade their family or friends if they are planning to travel to Myanmar’s cyberfraud compounds...
    ...務必盡一切努力勸阻，如果他們計畫前往緬甸的網路詐騙園區...
  • encounter v. 遭遇、遇到
    ...as there are almost no good options for rescue if they encounter safety issues there.
    ...因為如果他們在那裡遇到安全問題，幾乎沒有好的救援選項。
  • verify v. 證實、查證
    Taiwan’s representative office in Myanmar verified that Kyauktaw in western Myanmar’s Rakhine State is controlled by the Arakan Army...
    我國駐緬甸代表處證實，位於緬甸西部若開邦的皎漂鎮是由若開軍所控制...
  • evacuate v. 撤離、疏散
    The Bureau of Consular Affairs has since April 7 had a “Level 4: Red” Travel Alert — meaning “evacuate, leave as soon as possible” — for the area...
    領事事務局自 4 月 7 日起對該地區發布「紅色燈號」旅遊警示——意即「宜儘速撤離、離開」...
  • emergency n. 緊急事件、緊急狀況
    ...so if an emergency event occurs, rescue operations would be extremely difficult...
    ...因此若發生緊急事件，救援行動將極為困難...
  • assist v. 協助、幫忙
    While the ministry would make every effort to assist travelers in distress...
    雖然外交部會盡一切努力協助陷入困境的旅客...
  • disrupt v. 干擾、打亂、中斷
    ...heavy rains in Thailand caused flooding in Bangkok and severely disrupted operations at Suvarnabhumi International Airport last week...
    ...泰國的大雨造成曼谷淹水，並嚴重打亂了蘇凡納布機場的營運...
  • complaint n. 抱怨、投訴
    The ministry received a report complaining about Thai Airways’ handling of flight delays and services on Tuesday last week...
    外交部於上週二接獲投訴泰國航空處理航班延誤及服務不周的報告...
  • departure n. 出發、離開
    ...confirm the flight status with airlines before departure, he said...
    ...並在出發前與航空公司確認航班狀態...
  • misuse v. 濫用、誤用
    ...accusation on Monday that Overseas Community Affairs Council Minister Hsu Chia-ching (徐佳青) misused her position during overseas trips...
    ...指控僑務委員會委員長徐佳青在海外行程中濫用職權一事...
  • investigate v. 調查
    ...it has asked the overseas missions involved to investigate and clarify the issue.
    ...已要求相關的駐外館處進行調查並釐清問題。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 / 形容詞用
    The ministry received a report complaining about Thai Airways’ handling of flight delays and services on Tuesday last week...
    句中的「complaining about...」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「a report」，相當於關代子句「which complained about...」。這種用法在商業與新聞英語中很常見，能讓句子更簡潔。
  • 主動詞一致性與間接引句的時態
    Department of East Asian and Pacific Affairs Director-General Michael Lin (林昭宏) said the Burmese military launched an airstrike in Kyauktaw town on Monday last week...
    報導過去發生的言論（said）時，引述的內容（the Burmese military launched...）通常也會使用過去式（past tense）來保持時態的一致性。
  • 條件句 (Conditional sentence with subjunctive/modal meaning)
    “The Ministry of Foreign Affairs reminds people that there are still many armed conflicts going on between ethnic armed groups and the military regime in Myanmar, so if an emergency event occurs, rescue operations would be extremely difficult,” he said.
    此句使用「if + 現在式動詞（occurs）」來表達未來可能發生的條件，主句則使用「would + 原形動詞（would be）」來表達假設性的結果，屬於標準的假設語氣或條件句型。
  • 讓步子句 (Concessive clause with While)
    While the ministry would make every effort to assist travelers in distress, it is hobbled by practical realities and there are limits to what it can achieve...
    句首的「While」引導讓步子句，意指「雖然、儘管」，用來表達兩個相對或轉折的概念，常用於新聞英語中平衡說法。
