幾乎所有家庭都擁有網際網路存取權：調查

Almost all households have Internet access: survey

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • survey n. 調查
    A survey released by the Directorate-General of Budget, Accounting and Statistics (DGBAS) showed.
    行政院主計總處發布的一項調查顯示。
  • disparity n. 差異、不一致
    found Taiwan’s highest and lowest-income households show the largest disparity in ownership rates for cars, computers and dehumidifiers
    發現台灣最高和最低所得家庭在汽車、電腦和除濕機的擁有率上差距最大
  • universal adj. 普遍的、共同的
    basic home appliances, mobile phones and Internet access have become nearly universal across income levels.
    基本的家用電器、手機和網際網路存取在各所得水準之間已變得幾乎全面普及。
  • penetration rate n. phr. 普及率
    with a national penetration rate of about 60 to 61 percent
    全國普及率約為 60% 至 61%
  • bracket n. 級距、所得階層
    More than 95 percent of households in the highest income bracket own a car
    最高所得級距中有超過 95% 的家庭擁有汽車
  • barrier n. 障礙、阻礙
    indicating that the high price of vehicles and maintenance costs remain significant barriers for low-income households.
    這表明高昂的車價和維護成本仍然是低收入家庭的重要障礙。
  • prioritize v. 優先考慮、把……列為優先
    suggesting that low-income families prioritize essential needs over quality-of-life appliances.
    這表明低收入家庭將基本需求置於生活品質家電之上。
  • expenditure n. 支出、花費
    The increase in healthcare expenditure is largely driven by Taiwan’s rapidly aging population
    醫療保健支出的增加很大程度上是由台灣迅速老齡化的社會所驅動的
  • attribute to v. phr. 歸因於
    DGBAS officials attributed the rise in dining and travel spending to the growing number of dual-income households
    主計總處官員將餐飲和旅遊支出的增加歸因於雙薪家庭數量的不斷增加
  • burden n. 負擔
    has become a growing financial burden, they added.
    他們補充說，已成為日益沉重的財務負擔。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表示伴隨狀況或結果）
    indicating that the high price of vehicles and maintenance costs remain significant barriers for low-income households.
    句尾的 "indicating..." 是現在分詞引導的分詞構句，用來補充說明前面整句（高達 50 個百分點的差距）所代表的含意或結果，相當於 "which indicates that..."。
  • 非限定關係子句（補充說明）
    The latest Survey of Family Income and Expenditure, which compared the top 20 percent of income earners with the bottom 20 percent, found...
    逗號後方的 "which compared the top 20 percent..." 是一個非限定關係子句，對前面的 "The latest Survey of Family Income and Expenditure" 進行額外補充說明，若刪除該子句，主要子句的句意依然完整。
  • 被動語態
    The increase in healthcare expenditure is largely driven by Taiwan’s rapidly aging population, they said.
    句中的 "is... driven by..." 使用了被動語態，強調「醫療保健支出的增加」是受到「台灣迅速老齡化的人口」所驅動、影響的動作對象。
  • 現在分詞作後位修飾
    A survey released by the Directorate-General of Budget, Accounting and Statistics (DGBAS) showed.
    片語 "released by the Directorate-General of Budget..." 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "A survey"，相當於形容詞子句 "which was released by..."，在新聞英語中非常常見以簡化句子。
