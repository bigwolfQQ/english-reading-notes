學者：習近平對二戰的說法模糊了中華民國的角色，並引發對日緊張關係：學者

Xi's WWII account obscures ROC role, evokes Japan tensions: Scholars

單字解析

  • cooperation n. 合作
    When Chinese President Xi Jinping (習近平) recalled U.S.-China cooperation during World War II at the White House on Thursday, he left out a name central to that history: the Republic of China (ROC).
    中國國家主席習近平週四在白宮回顧二戰期間的美中合作時，漏掉了一個該歷史的核心名稱：中華民國（ROC）。
  • ceremony n. 典禮，儀式
    "During World War II, China and the United States fought side by side against fascist aggression," Xi said at a White House welcoming ceremony before his closed-door talks with U.S. President Donald Trump.
    習近平在與美國總統唐納·川普進行閉門會談前的白宮歡迎儀式上表示：「在第二次世界大戰期間，中國和美國並肩作戰對抗法西斯侵略。」
  • summit n. 高峰會，高層會議
    At a forum in Taipei on Tuesday on the Trump-Xi summit, Taiwanese scholars said leaving the ROC unnamed was likely aimed at helping Beijing portray the wartime alliance as part of the PRC's own history.
    台灣學者週二在台北舉行的川習會論壇上表示，未提及中華民國的目的，很可能是為了協助北京將這場戰時同盟描繪成中華人民共和國自身歷史的一部分。
  • alliance n. 同盟，結盟
    Wang Hsin-hsien (王信賢), director of National Chengchi University's Institute of International Relations, said Xi stood to gain if the alliance came to be remembered simply as one between "China" and the United States.
    國立政治大學國際關係研究中心主任王信賢表示，如果這場同盟被單純記為「中國」與美國之間的同盟，習近平將從中獲益。
  • apparent adj. 顯而易見的，明顯的
    Wang warned that repeated use of such language by Washington could make "the erasure of the Republic of China ... very apparent."
    王信賢警告，華府若一再使用這種措辭，可能會使「中華民國的抹滅……變得非常顯眼」。
  • identity v. 識別，確認（文章中為 identifying）
    It said the leaders recalled that the United States and "China" were World War II allies who fought side by side, without identifying the ROC as the Chinese government at the time.
    清單指出，兩國領導人回顧了美國與「中國」是並肩作戰的二戰盟友，但並未指明中華民國是當時的中國政府。
  • protest v. 抗議，反對
    Taiwan's Mainland Affairs Council protested Xi's remarks Friday, saying they did not reflect the fact that it was the ROC that fought alongside the United States while the PRC did not exist yet.
    台灣的大陸委員會週五對習近平的發言表示抗議，稱這些言論並未反映當時與美國並肩作戰的是中華民國、而中華人民共和國當時尚未存在的事實。
  • replace v. 取代，替換
    One of them was to "suggest that the Republic of China has completely disappeared and been replaced by the People's Republic of China."
    郭育仁說，其中之一是「暗示中華民國已經完全消失，並被中華人民共和國所取代」。
  • highlight v. 強調，突顯
    Another, Kuo said, was for Xi to highlight Japanese wartime militarism through his reference to fighting fascism amid strained China-Japan relations.
    郭育仁表示，另一個目的是習近平在美中關係緊張之際，藉由提及打擊法西斯來凸顯日本的戰時軍國主義。
  • permanent adj. 永久的，常任的
    He linked it to Tokyo's campaign for a permanent United Nations Security Council seat and its call to remove the U.N. Charter's "enemy state" clauses...
    他將此與東京爭取聯合國安全理事會常任理事國席位，以及要求刪除聯合國憲章中「敵國」條款的運動連結起來...
  • controversy n. 爭議
    Despite the controversy over Xi's remarks, Tzeng Wei-feng (曾偉峯), an associate research fellow at NCCU's institute, saw no clear sign that the visit had immediately changed Washington's Taiwan policy.
    儘管對習近平的言論存在爭議，政大國關中心副研究員曾偉峯認為，沒有明顯跡象表明這次訪問立即改變了華府的台灣政策。
  • policy n. 政策
    Despite the controversy over Xi's remarks, Tzeng Wei-feng (曾偉峯), an associate research fellow at NCCU's institute, saw no clear sign that the visit had immediately changed Washington's Taiwan policy.
    儘管對習近平的言論存在爭議，政大國關中心副研究員曾偉峯認為，沒有明顯跡象表明這次訪問立即改變了華府的台灣政策。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The White House's own fact sheet, issued Friday after the visit, also referred simply to "China."
    句中的 "issued Friday after the visit" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾其前的名詞 "fact sheet"（事實清單）。這相當於形容詞子句 "which was issued Friday after the visit" 的簡化版，省略了關係代名詞與be動詞，使商業或新聞英文顯得精簡有力。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause with Preposition)
    He pointed to the 1972 Shanghai Communiqué, in which the United States "acknowledged" that people on both sides of the Taiwan Strait maintained there was one China...
    句中的 "in which..." 是由「介系詞 + 關係代名詞」所引導的形容詞子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞 "the 1972 Shanghai Communiqué"。在正式寫作與新聞英文中，這種結構常用來為前面的事物提供詳細的背景或內容說明。
  • 假設語氣/過去可能性 (Modal Verb 'stood to' for Likelihood)
    Wang Hsin-hsien (王信賢), director of National Chengchi University's Institute of International Relations, said Xi stood to gain if the alliance came to be remembered simply as one between "China" and the United States.
    片語 "stand to (do something)" 在英文中常被用來表示「有望、很有可能獲得（某利益）或面臨（某狀況）」。在此句中，用來表達如果歷史記憶被改變，習近平「將很有機會從中獲益」的預期情境。
  • 使役與結果形容詞子句 (Resultative Construction with 'make')
    Wang warned that repeated use of such language by Washington could make "the erasure of the Republic of China ... very apparent."
    此處使用了 "make + 受詞 + 形容詞"（make sth. apparent）的句型，表示「使某事變得……」。句中受詞較長（the erasure of the Republic of China），後面接形容詞 "very apparent" 作受詞補語，精準描述華府的用詞會造成的客觀結果。
