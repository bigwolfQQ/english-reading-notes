單字解析

  • joint adj. 聯合的、共同的
    Leaders from Canada, Brazil, Kenya and the EU unveiled a joint diplomatic front on the eve of the UN General Assembly...
    來自加拿大、巴西、肯亞和歐聯的領導人在聯合國大會前夕公布了一份聯合外交陣線...
  • framework n. 架構、體制
    ...it aims to “create a flexible, open framework for countries from different regions and political traditions...
    ...旨在「為來自不同地區、政治傳統的國家創造一個靈活、開放的架構」...
  • practical adj. 實際的、務實的
    ...and to translate into practical action shared principles, including those enshrined in the UN Charter.
    ...並將包括聯合國憲章中所銘記在內的共同原則轉化為實際行動。
  • convene v. 召開、集合、開會
    They planned to convene yesterday on the sidelines of the UN Assembly general debate in New York.
    他們計劃昨天在紐約聯合國大會總辯論的場邊會晤。
  • represent v. 代表
    Representing key middle powers and developing nations, P4M leaders say cooperation is...
    代表關鍵中等強權與開發中國家的 P4M 領導人表示，合作是...
  • bolster v. 支持、強化、鞏固
    ...cooperation is “not an act of idealism, it is a practical necessity” to bolster longstanding multilateral institutions...
    ...合作「不是理想主義的行為，而是實際的需求」，旨在強化長期的多邊機構...
  • disruption n. 中斷、擾亂
    ...and address shared threats like climate change, artificial intelligence and supply chain disruptions.
    ...並應對氣候變遷、人工智慧和供應鏈中斷等共同威脅。
  • purpose n. 目的、意圖
    “Its purpose is the opposite,” to create a flexible and open network to support the work of the UN...
    「其目的是恰恰相反」，即創建一個靈活開放的網路，以支持聯合國的工作...
  • initiative n. 新措施、倡議
    The initiative comes as world leaders gather against the backdrop of US President Donald Trump’s administration...
    這項倡議出台之際，正值世界領導人齊聚一堂，其背景是美國總統唐納·川普的政府...
  • administration n. 政府（執政團隊）、行政部門
    ...against the backdrop of US President Donald Trump’s administration, which has been defined by trade tariffs...
    ...其背景是美國總統唐納·川普的政府，該政府的特點是貿易關稅...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作補充說明 (Present Participle Phrase for Background/Action)
    Representing key middle powers and developing nations, P4M leaders say cooperation is “not an act of idealism, it is a practical necessity”...
    句首的「Representing...」是由分詞引導的片語，用來修飾主句的主語「P4M leaders」，表示「代表著...的 P4M 領導人同時表示...」。這在商業與新聞英語中常用來精簡句子，表達伴隨或同時發生的狀態。
  • 關係代名詞子句修飾先行詞 (Relative Clause)
    The leaders said the mechanism, which they shorthanded P4M, would serve to build coalitions for reform...
    句中的「which they shorthanded P4M」是一個非限定性關係代名詞子句，用逗號隔開，用來補充說明前面的名詞「the mechanism」。這類子句常在多益閱讀測驗中測試考生對主詞與動詞距離拉長時的句構分析能力。
  • 被動語態的使用 (Passive Voice)
    Structural reforms at the UN have stalled for years due to veto powers among the Security Council’s five permanent members, leaving the body repeatedly unable to act on emerging global conflicts.
    句中的「have stalled」雖然在此為主動形式（stall 作不及物動詞表示陷入停滯），但在新聞英語中常透過被動或狀態動詞來客觀描述政策或改革的受阻狀況。而後方的「leaving the body repeatedly unable...」則使用了分詞構句作結果狀語。
