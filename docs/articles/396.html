陸軍舉辦首場自願性射擊訓練計畫

Army holds first voluntary marksmanship program

單字解析

  • voluntary adj. 自願的，志願的
    The army’s first voluntary live-fire shooting program for reservists, with newly relaxed eligibility rules, took place in Taichung on Monday...
    陸軍針對後備軍人舉辦的首場自願性實彈射擊計畫於週一在台中登場，此次放寬了資格限制...
  • eligibility n. 合格，資格
    The army’s first voluntary live-fire shooting program for reservists, with newly relaxed eligibility rules, took place in Taichung on Monday...
    陸軍針對後備軍人舉辦的首場自願性實彈射擊計畫於週一在台中登場，此次放寬了資格限制...
  • refresh v. 使恢復，使想起，溫習
    ...allowing those who were discharged more than a decade ago to refresh their combat skills with T91 assault rifles.
    ...讓退伍超過十年的後備軍人能夠使用T91步槍重溫戰鬥技能。
  • incorporate v. 納入，結合，包含
    The Taichung Reserve Command held the exercise amid Taiwan’s efforts to incorporate reserve infantry brigades in its war plans.
    台中市後備指揮部在台灣努力將後備步兵旅納入作戰計畫的背景下舉行了這場演訓。
  • restriction n. 限制，約束
    The drill was among the first to be held after the Ministry of National Defense lifted a restriction limiting participation in marksmanship refresher training to soldiers who left the service less than 12 years ago.
    這次演習是國防部解除「限制參與射擊複訓僅限於退役未滿12年的士兵」規定後，首批舉辦的演習之一。
  • participate v. 參與，參加
    A reporter said 35 former service members took part in the target practice at Chenggong Ling (成功嶺) military training camp.
    一名記者表示，有35名前軍方人員在成功嶺軍事訓練營參加了靶擊練習。
  • consist of phr. v. 由……組成，包含
    The marksmanship exercise consisted of two parts — a virtual reality training component and a live-fire component — to ease them into weapons handling.
    射擊演習包含兩個部分——虛擬實境訓練單元與實彈射擊單元——以協助他們輕鬆上手武器操作。
  • handle v. 處理，操作，掌控
    A command sergeant major of a Taichung-based reserve brigade walked the reservists through the design and handling of the unfamiliar rifles in the virtual reality training facility.
    一名駐台中的後備旅士官長在虛擬實境訓練設施中，引導後備軍人了解不熟悉步槍的設計與操作。
  • simulate v. 模擬，模仿
    They simulated shooting in prone, half-kneel and standing positions for realistic training.
    他們模擬了臥姿、半跪姿和立姿射擊，以進行擬真訓練。
  • supervise v. 監督，指導，管理
    The senior non-commissioned officer supervised the live-fire component of the exercise...
    這位資深士官監督了演習的實彈射擊部分...
  • observe v. 觀察，觀測
    A new spotting scope enabled the instructor and trainee to observe hits and misses without inspecting the target up close...
    新型觀測鏡讓教練和受訓人員能夠觀察命中與未命中情況，而無需近距離檢查目標...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The army’s first voluntary live-fire shooting program for reservists, with newly relaxed eligibility rules, took place in Taichung on Monday, allowing those who were discharged more than a decade ago to refresh their combat skills with T91 assault rifles.
    句尾的「allowing...」是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明主句所發生的動作所帶來的結果或伴隨的情況，相當於連接詞子句「which allowed...」。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    The drill was among the first to be held after the Ministry of National Defense lifted a restriction limiting participation in marksmanship refresher training to soldiers who left the service less than 12 years ago.
    句中的「who left the service less than 12 years ago」是限定用法的關係子句，用主格關係代名詞「who」開頭，用來修飾先行詞「soldiers」。
  • 介系詞片語作為獨立補充說明 (Prepositional Phrase with 'with')
    The participants included former draftees and retired professional soldiers, with many of the latter wearing their old helmets, uniforms, boots and other accoutrements.
    「with + 名詞 + 分詞」結構（即「with many of the latter wearing...」）在句中表示附帶狀況，用來描述參與者當中的細節畫面。
  • 動名詞作受詞 (Gerund as Object)
    Loading only a few rounds in each rifle magazine helped the trainee practice reloading magazines as they shoot, a military spokesperson said.
    句中動詞「practice」後面接動名詞「reloading」作為其受詞，用來表達「練習做某事」的語意。
