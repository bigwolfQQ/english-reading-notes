Kenyan start-up makes limbs to sign for deaf kids

單字解析

  • demonstrate v. 示範，證明
    As a team demonstrated how a robotic arm could revolutionize their studies.
    當一個團隊示範機器手臂如何徹底改變他們的學習時。
  • enrolled v. 註冊入學，招收（enroll 的過去分詞）
    Just 20,000 were enrolled in schools as of 2024.
    截至 2024 年，僅有 2 萬人入學。
  • cofounded v. 共同創辦（cofound 的過去分詞）
    Kenyan start-up ZeroBionic, cofounded by 22-year-old Norah Kimathi, hopes to tackle some of the educational difficulties they face.
    由 22 歲的諾拉・基馬蒂（Norah Kimathi）共同創辦的肯亞新創公司 ZeroBionic，希望藉此解決他們所面臨的一些教育困難。
  • tackle v. 處理，解決（問題、困難）
    Hopes to tackle some of the educational difficulties they face.
    希望藉此解決他們所面臨的一些教育困難。
  • translate v. 翻譯
    It has built a mechanical hand that can translate a teachers’ voice into sign language in real time.
    它製造了一隻機械手，能即時將老師的聲音翻譯成手語。
  • counterpart n. 相應的人或物，職位相當的人
    Allowing deaf children to learn alongside their hearing counterparts.
    讓失聰兒童能與聽力正常的同儕一同學習。
  • element n. 要素，元素
    There is another element of the technology that is even more promising.
    這項技術還有另一個更有前途的元素。
  • database n. 資料庫
    They are building a database of technical terms.
    他們正在建立一個技術術語資料庫。
  • challenge n. 挑戰
    The biggest challenge we faced actually was in Africa we don’t have a standardized African sign language dataset.
    我們實際上面臨的最大挑戰是，在非洲我們沒有標準化的非洲手語資料集。
  • reduce v. 減少，降低
    Using recycled materials to reduce costs and environmental impact.
    使用回收材料來降低成本和環境影響。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞（片語）作後置定語
    Kenyan start-up ZeroBionic, cofounded by 22-year-old Norah Kimathi, hopes to tackle...
    句中的「cofounded by 22-year-old Norah Kimathi」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的專有名詞「ZeroBionic」。這相當於一個省略了「which was」的形容詞子句（...ZeroBionic, which was cofounded by...），用以補充說明該新創公司的創辦背景。
  • 現在分詞（片語）作結果或伴隨狀語
    It has built a mechanical hand that can translate a teachers’ voice into sign language in real time, allowing deaf children to learn alongside their hearing counterparts.
  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    The robotic arms are trialed by deaf teachers to record and store signs using a bodysuit, which ZeroBionic imported for the task.
  • SVO + 過去分詞（被動意涵的受詞補語）
    Everything has been designed and built in-house, using recycled materials to reduce costs and environmental impact, produced using 3D printers...
