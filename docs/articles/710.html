單字解析

  • minister n. 部長
    the Ukrainian minister of foreign affairs said
    烏克蘭外交部長說
  • conference n. 會議、研討會
    on the way back from an annual conference in Kyiv.
    從基輔的年度會議回程的路上。
  • increase v. / n. 增加、提高
    calling for Europe to increase sanctions against Russia.
    呼籲歐洲增加對俄羅斯的制裁。
  • sanction n. 制裁
    calling for Europe to increase sanctions against Russia.
    呼籲歐洲增加對俄羅斯的制裁。
  • operator n. 營運商、操作員
    Ukraine’s state railway operator confirmed a strike on a train in Volyn
    烏克蘭國家鐵路營運商證實沃倫的一列火車遭到襲擊
  • delay n. / v. 延誤、延遲
    would continue on to Poland after a delay at the border of about six hours
    在邊境延誤約六小時後將繼續前往波蘭
  • achieve v. 達到、實現（目標）
    Unable to achieve its goals on the battlefield
    無法在戰場上達到其目標
  • infrastructure n. 基礎設施
    turns to systematic attacks against civilians, trains, and the infrastructure that keeps the country moving
    轉向對平民、火車以及維持國家運作的基礎設施進行系統性攻擊
  • emergency n. / adj. 緊急情況、緊急的
    speaking after an emergency meeting with top civilian and military leaders
    在與高級文職和軍事領導人召開緊急會議後發言
  • disrupt v. 干擾、打斷、使中斷
    including attempts to disrupt or paralyze border crossings.
    包括企圖干擾或癱瘓邊境關卡。
  • mobilize v. 動員
    Poland would consider how to mobilize allies to be prepared for a common response if needed
    波蘭將考慮如何動員盟友，以便在必要時為共同應對做好準備
  • threat n. 威脅
    unable to leave Ukraine because of the Russian drone threat.
    由於俄羅斯的無人機威脅而無法離開烏克蘭。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後置修飾語 (Present/Past Participle as Post-modifier)
    The locomotive of a passenger train traveling from Kyiv to Warsaw was on Sunday morning hit by a Russian drone...
    句中的「traveling from Kyiv to Warsaw」是由現在分詞帶領的片語，用來修飾前方的主詞「passenger train」（行駛中的火車）。這相當於一個形容詞子句「that traveled/was traveling...」，能讓句子更為精簡。
  • 現在分詞引導附帶狀況 / 伴隨動作 (Present Participle indicating Circumstance)
    ...the Ukrainian minister of foreign affairs said, as Moscow’s airstrikes push closer to NATO territory.
    句尾使用連接詞 as引導子句「as Moscow’s airstrikes push closer to NATO territory」，用來表達背景狀況或同時發生的動作（隨著莫斯科的空襲步步逼近……），有助於交代新聞事件發生的時空背景。
  • 關係代名詞子句作修飾語 (Relative Clause)
    The train, which had 208 people aboard, would continue on to Poland after a delay...
    句中使用了由 which 引導的非限定性關係代名詞子句（「which had 208 people aboard」），對前面的先行詞「The train」做補充說明。非限定子句通常用逗號隔開，提供額外的背景資訊。
