文法重點

  • 非限定性關係代間子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    ...announcing plans for the launch next year of the Taiwan International Comics Festival, which he said would be curated by a British comics historian.
    句中的「which...」前面有逗號，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「Taiwan International Comics Festival」，提供額外的附加資訊。句中還夾入了一個插入語「he said」（他說），用以表達轉述。
  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況 (Participle Clause of Concomitant Circumstance)
    With next year marking a decade of those efforts, the ministry would launch the inaugural Taiwan International Comics Festival...
    此處使用「With + 名詞 + 現在分詞 (marking)」的結構，用來表示附帶狀況或背景原因，相當於「Because next year marks...」，在多益閱讀測驗中常見於商業或新聞英語的描述。
  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    Translated by Lin King (金翎), the novel is beautiful, complex and sweet, Wood said...
    句首的「Translated by Lin King」是由過去分詞開頭的片語，用來修飾後面的主詞「the novel」（小說），原意為「Because the novel was translated by Lin King...」，省略了主代名詞與be動詞，使語氣更為緊湊。
