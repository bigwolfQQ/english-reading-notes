垚鋐科技預測營收穩健成長

Yaho System forecasts steady revenue growth

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • forecast v. / n. 預測，預報
    Yaho System forecasts steady revenue growth
    垚鋐科技預測營收穩健成長
  • steady adj. 穩定的，平穩的
    Yaho System Technology Co yesterday said it expected steady revenue growth through 2028
    垚鋐科技股份有限公司昨天表示，預期直到 2028 年營收將呈穩健成長
  • revenue n. 收益，營業收入
    Yaho aims to boost semiconductor installation-related business to contribute half of the company’s total revenue by 2029
    垚鋐的目標是將半導體安裝相關業務提高至占公司 2029 年總營收的一半
  • expansion n. 擴張，擴大
    buoyed by aggressive factory expansions across its customer base
    在客戶群積極進行全球廠房擴建的帶動下
  • overseas adj. / adv. 海外的，在海外
    In addition to the US, Yaho has set up overseas subsidiaries in Japan, Germany and Singapore
    除了美國之外，垚鋐也已在日本、德國和新加坡設立了海外子公司
  • scale up phr. 擴大，按比例增加
    helping chipmakers building chip manufacturing facilities overseas scale up production
    協助晶片製造商建立海外晶片製造設施以擴大產能
  • invest v. 投資
    Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co has announced an ambitious expansion plan by investing US$265 billion
    台積電已宣布一項雄心勃勃的擴張計畫，將投資 2,650 億美元
  • subsidiary n. 子公司
    Yaho has set up overseas subsidiaries in Japan, Germany and Singapore
    垚鋐已在日本、德國和新加坡設立了海外子公司
  • sustainable adj. 永續的，可持續的
    In search of a sustainable revenue stream, the company is accelerating its expansions
    為了尋找永續的營收來源，該公司正加速擴展
  • gross margin n. 毛利率
    the semiconductor equipment installation segment, which delivers a robust gross margin
    半導體設備安裝領域，該領域能提供穩健的毛利率
  • demand n. / v. 需求，要求
    there will be relentless demand for equipment installation
    對設備安裝的需求將持續不斷
  • contribute v. 貢獻，促成（此處指占一定比例）
    Trusval expected overseas business to contribute a larger revenue share of more than 10 percent
    信紘科預期海外業務的營收占比將提高至 10％ 以上

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    Semiconductor hookups service start-up Yaho System Technology Co yesterday said it expected steady revenue growth through 2028, buoyed by aggressive factory expansions across its customer base.
    句尾的 "buoyed by..." 是過去分詞片語作後位修飾，用來補充說明前面主句動作的原因（被客戶群積極的擴廠所支撐）。這種結構在商業財經新聞中非常常見，能簡潔地表達因果或伴隨狀態。
  • 動名詞片語作受詞 (Gerund Phrase as Object)
    Hookups service providers such as Yaho play an increasing critical role in helping chipmakers building chip manufacturing facilities overseas scale up production.
    介系詞 "in" 後面接動名詞片語 "helping chipmakers..." 作受詞。其中 "helping" 的受詞補語使用原形動詞 "scale up"（help + 受詞 + 原形動詞），這在多益閱讀與文法測驗中是極高頻的考點。
  • 分詞構句表伴隨或連續動作 (Participial Phrase indicating accompanying action)
    In addition to the US, Yaho has set up overseas subsidiaries in Japan, Germany and Singapore, following in the footsteps of its customers, it said.
    句尾的 ", following in the footsteps of its customers" 是現在分詞構句，主詞與前面子句主語一致，表示「隨著……」的伴隨狀態或結果，使長句顯得流暢而不顯冗長。
