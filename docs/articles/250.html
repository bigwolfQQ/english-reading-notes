文法重點

  • 現在分詞作附加說明（分詞構句）
    In addition, employment data for June and July were revised up, with July rising from a job loss to a gain of 21,000 jobs and June rising to a gain of 31,000 jobs, while the unemployment rate held at 4.1 percent, the data showed.
    句型「with + 名詞 + 分詞（如 rising）」用來補充說明主句動作發生時的伴隨狀況或結果，常翻譯為「隨著……」。在此用來描述 6 月與 7 月就業數據各自上升的細節。
  • 假設語氣（與現在事實相反）
    In that scenario, the Fed would be even less likely to cut rates, and instead be forced to raise them to curb inflation.
    此處使用「would + 原形動詞」來表達在假設的未來情境（前面提及的滯脹情況發生時）所產生的結果，屬於假設語氣的一種應用，強調其為推論或假設的狀況。
  • 插入語結構（用破折號補充說明）
    However, Trump’s renewed pressure on the Fed to cut rates further complicates matters for Fed Chairman Kevin Warsh, who — at his debut at the annual gathering of global central bankers in Jackson Hole, Wyoming, late last month — reaffirmed the central bank’s 2 percent inflation target as non-negotiable...
    夾在兩個破折號之間的「— at his debut at... late last month —」是一個冗長的插入語，用來補充說明聯準會主席 Kevin Warsh 發表談話的時間與地點，不影響主句「who reaffirmed...」的句構完整性。
  • 關係代名詞子句（非限定用法）
    ...the US releases the consumer price index for last month, which is the final major inflation gauge before the Fed policymakers meet next week.
    句中的「which is...」為非限定用法的關係子句（前面有逗點），用來補充說明前面的先行詞「the consumer price index for last month」，對該指標提供額外的描述與資訊。
