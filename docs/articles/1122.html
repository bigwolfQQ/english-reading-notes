Trump to ask XI to free US scientist, his wife says

單字解析

  • expert n. 專家
    The wife of a US expert on North Korean nuclear tests who has been imprisoned in China since late 2024 on Tuesday said...
    一名北韓核試驗美國專家自2024年底起在中國被捕，其妻子於週二表示...
  • collaboration n. 合作
    ...even though his US-funded research was openly published, used publicly available data and was done in collaboration with Chinese scientists.
    ...儘管他由美國資助的研究是公開發表的、使用了公開可得的數據，且是與中國科學家合作完成的。
  • priority n. 優先事項，首要任務
    ...making their release a top priority.
    ...這使得他們的獲釋成為首要任務。
  • press v. 施壓，敦促
    Rong said she was told that Trump also would press Xi to release Min Zin.
    榮玉芳表示，她被告知川普也將施壓習近平釋放敏金。
  • on the condition of anonymity phr. 在匿名條件下
    “Resolving the wrongful detentions of US citizens is always a top priority for the president,” a senior US official said, speaking on the condition of anonymity to discuss sensitive matters.
    一名美國資深官員在匿名討論敏感事務的情況下表示：「解決美國公民遭錯誤拘留的問題一直是總統的首要任務。」
  • suspect v. 懷疑，涉嫌
    Chinese Ministry of Foreign Affairs spokesman Guo Jiakun (郭嘉昆) said that the “relevant US person” is suspected of engaging in activities that endanger China’s national security...
    中國外交部發言人郭嘉昆表示，該名「相關美方人員」涉嫌從事危害中國國家安全的活動...
  • contractor n. 承包商
    Chen, whose detention was revealed in July, is a seismologist with a US government contractor.
    陳有林的拘留於7月曝光，他是一名美國政府承包商的地震學家。
  • deny v. 否認
    China, which like the US has signed but not ratified a 1996 international ban on nuclear tests, denies conducting the test.
    中國與美國一樣，已簽署但尚未批准1996年的一項國際禁止核試驗條約，並否認進行了該試驗。
  • observe v. 觀察
    “They record his appearance, his mental status and just anything that they observe,” she said...
    她說：「他們記錄他的外表、精神狀態以及他們觀察到的任何事情。」
  • substandard adj. 標準以下的，劣質的
    Rong is concerned about the conditions of her husband’s confinement, where she said he received substandard medical care and food.
    榮玉芳對她丈夫的關押條件感到擔憂，她表示他在那裡接受了水準以下的醫療照護與食物。
  • optimistic adj. 樂觀的
    “Be strong. Be optimistic. There are a lot of people helping you. You will come back for sure.”
    「堅強點。樂觀點。有很多人在幫你。你一定會回來的。」

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾語 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The wife of a US expert on North Korean nuclear tests who has been imprisoned in China since late 2024 on Tuesday said...
    句中的「imprisoned in China since late 2024」為過去分詞片語，省略了關代與be動詞（原為 who has been imprisoned...），用來修飾前面的名詞 expert，表示「被關押在中國的」專家。
  • 現在分詞作附帶狀況的補語 (Present Participle Indicating Concomitant Circumstance)
    “Resolving the wrongful detentions of US citizens is always a top priority for the president,” a senior US official said, speaking on the condition of anonymity to discuss sensitive matters.
    句尾的「speaking on the condition of anonymity...」是由現在分詞片語引導的結構，用來補充說明主句動作發生時的伴隨狀況或條件（即官員在說這話的同時，要求匿名）。
  • 關代所有格子句 (Relative Clause with 'Whose')
    Chen, whose detention was revealed in July, is a seismologist with a US government contractor.
    「whose detention was revealed in July」是一個由 whose 引導的非限定性關係子句。whose 代替前行詞 Chen 的所有格（即 Chen's detention），用來連接並補充說明陳的拘留是在七月份曝光的。
  • 過去分詞片語作受詞補語/形容詞用 (Past Participle as Complement)
    The US has accused China of using decoupling to try to mask a low-yield nuclear test blast on June 22, 2020.
    句中「low-yield」是由形容詞和過去分詞複合而成的修飾語，用來形容核試驗爆炸（nuclear test blast）的當量是「低產量的/低當量的」。
