國防部：憲兵任務將擴大至作戰

Military police’s role to be expanded to combat: MND

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • expand v. 擴大，擴充
    Taiwan is expanding the Military Police Command’s mission from policing to also include combat operations...
    臺灣正在擴大憲兵指揮部的任務，從維持軍紀延伸至包含作戰行動……
  • drastically adv. 大幅度地，徹底地
    ...and drastically increasing the size of its forces, disclosures from the Ministry of National Defense (MND) showed.
    ……並大幅增加其兵力規模，國防部（MND）揭露的資訊顯示。
  • structure n. 結構，建築物，體系
    The military police would be expanded from 5,600 troops to 11,000 troops, the ministry said in the five-year force structure plan.
    國防部在五年兵力結構計畫中表示，憲兵兵力將從 5,600 人擴編至 11,000 人。
  • infrastructure n. 基礎設施
    ...to defend the metropolitan Taipei region and critical infrastructure against decapitation strikes, the document showed.
    ……以防衛大臺北都會區及關鍵基礎設施，抵禦「斬首」打擊，該文件顯示。
  • cooperate v. 合作，配合
    ...and would cooperate with the 66th Marine Brigade out of Songshan Air Base and the Army Guandu Area Command, it said.
    ……並將與駐守松山機場的海軍陸戰隊第六六旅及陸軍關渡地區指揮部協同合作，文中指出。
  • personnel n. 人員，員工
    If Taiwan is attacked, the Military Police Command’s volunteer personnel and conscripts would be tasked with the defense...
    若臺灣遭到攻擊，憲兵指揮部的志願役人員與義務役士兵將受命防衛……
  • budget n. 預算
    ...a senior defense official on Sept. 25 said on condition of anonymity that the ministry’s proposed budget for next year indicates the military police’s intended future role.
    一名高階國防官員於 9 月 25 日在匿名情況下表示，國防部明年度的預算草案顯示了憲兵預期的未來角色。
  • procurement n. 採購，物資籌措
    The military police’s procurement plans are focused on urban fighting, asymmetric warfare and air defense...
    憲兵的採購計畫聚焦於城鎮戰、不對稱作戰以及防空……
  • emphasis n. 強調，重視
    ...with considerable emphasis on the last mission category, they said.
    ……其中對最後一項任務類別給予了相當大的重視，他們指出。
  • capability n. 能力，性能
    The important features of the M-ACE vehicles are the artificial intelligence-driven targeting software’s capability for precision engagement...
    M-ACE 車輛的重要特色在於人工智慧驅動的標定軟體具備精準交戰能力……
  • priority n. 優先權，優先
    The military police has been given priority for new T112 assault rifle and rifle-mounted Smartshooter smart optics...
    憲兵已被優先配發新款 T112 突擊步槍及槍用 Smartshooter 智慧光學瞄準鏡……
  • earmark v. 指定（資金等）作特定用途
    Funds are earmarked for a presidential command vehicle, disguised as a civilian car...
    資金已指定用於一輛總統指揮車，該車偽裝成民用車輛……

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    ...carrying anti-tank guided missiles, which are likely to be mounted on light tactical vehicles or Clouded Leopard infantry fighting vehicles with Javelin missiles.
    句中的 carrying 為現在分詞片語，用來修飾前方帶有底線的名詞片語 weapon stations（武器站），相當於形容詞子句「... which carry anti-tank guided missiles ...」，在英文新聞中常用於精簡句子結構。
  • 被動語態的應用 (Passive Voice)
    The military police has been given priority for new T112 assault rifle and rifle-mounted Smartshooter smart optics...
    此句使用現在完成式被動語態 (has been given)，強調憲兵這個對象「被給予」了優先權。在商業與正式報導中，當動作的承受者比施動者更重要時，常使用被動語態。
  • 過去分詞作形容詞/後位修飾 (Past Participle as Modifier)
    Funds are earmarked for a presidential command vehicle, disguised as a civilian car, featuring heavy armor protection and a satellite communication system hardened against jamming.
    句中的 disguised 與 hardened 皆為過去分詞，分別用來修飾前方名詞 a presidential command vehicle 與 a satellite communication system，表達被動的概念（「被偽裝成……的」與「被強化以抵抗……的」），使句子在保留詳細資訊的同時保持簡潔。
  • 條件句假設語氣 (Conditional Sentence)
    If Taiwan is attacked, the Military Police Command’s volunteer personnel and conscripts would be tasked with the defense of key government structures...
    此句為第一類條件句（真實條件句），用「If + 現在式動詞 (is attacked)」來表達未來可能發生的條件，主句則使用「would + 原形動詞 (would be tasked)」來表達在該條件成立時的結果或推論。
