單字解析

  • compensation n. 補償金、賠償金、薪酬
    A sharp rise in compensation to be paid by air force officers who leave before completing their required service could indicate an increase in pilots leaving the military early...
    空軍軍官在服滿規定役期前退伍所須支付的賠償金急劇增加，這可能暗示提早離隊的飛行員人數有所增加……
  • shortage n. 短缺、不足
    ...warning that the trend could worsen a shortage of experienced personnel.
    ……並警告此一趨勢恐會惡化資深人員短缺的問題。
  • headcount n. 員額、人數清點
    The planned headcount for volunteer military personnel, covering officers, noncommissioned officers (NCO) and enlisted soldiers, is 158,223 for next year...
    明年志願役軍人（涵蓋軍官、士官與士兵）的計畫員額為 158,223 人……
  • attribute v. 把……歸因於
    ...which Chieh attributed to increases in duty and combat allowances that took effect in April last year, as well as a reduction in nonessential duties.
    ……揭仲將此歸因於去年 4 月生效的職務與戰鬥加給調高，以及非必要勤務的減少。
  • revenue n. 收入、收益
    ...with headquarters revenue from compensation paid by officers who left before completing their required service more than doubling from last year, he said.
    他說，軍官因未服滿規定役期而離隊所支付的賠償金，其總部歲入比去年增加了一倍以上。
  • obligation n. 義務、責任
    Because air force pilots face substantially higher compensation obligations than other personnel when they leave before completing their required service...
    由於空軍飛行員在未服滿規定役期而離隊時，須面臨比其他人員實質上更高的賠償義務……
  • consecutive adj. 連續的
    ...incentive payments earmarked for pilots across the armed forces who remain in service beyond their minimum required period have declined for four consecutive years...
    ……針對三軍中服役超過最低規定年限的飛行員所編列的留營獎勵金，已連續四年下降……
  • incentive n. 獎勵、誘因
    The incentives are intended to retain majors, lieutenant colonels and colonels who have completed their minimum required service period...
    這些獎勵金旨在留住已服滿最低規定役期的少校、中校與上校……
  • retain v. 保留、留住（員工等）
    The incentives are intended to retain majors, lieutenant colonels and colonels who have completed their minimum required service period...
    這些獎勵金旨在留住已服滿最低規定役期的少校、中校與上校……
  • imbalance n. 不平衡、失衡
    “That could lead to an imbalance in the ratio between senior and junior pilots,” Chieh said...
    揭仲表示：「這可能導致資深與資淺飛行員之間的比例失衡」……
  • commit v. 承諾、投入
    ...that will operate the 66 F-16C/D Block 70 jets Taiwan has committed to purchasing from the US, he said.
    他表示，該聯隊將操作台灣已承諾向美國採購的 66 架 F-16C/D Block 70 戰機。
  • turnover n. 人員流失率、離職率
    Asked what could have contributed to the high turnover among air force pilots...
    當被問及是什麼原因造成空軍飛行員的高流失率時……

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作為後置修飾語 (Participle phrase as post-modifier)
    A sharp rise in compensation to be paid by air force officers who leave before completing their required service could indicate an increase in pilots leaving the military early...
    句中的 "to be paid by air force officers..." 是不定詞片語的被動形，用來修飾前面的名詞 "compensation"（賠償金）。而字尾的 "leaving the military early" 則是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "pilots"，表達「正在……的飛行員」，能讓句子結構更精簡。
  • 現在分詞表達附帶結果或伴隨動作 (Present participle indicating result)
    ...warning that the trend could worsen a shortage of experienced personnel.
    句尾的 "warning that..." 是現在分詞作分詞構句，用來補充說明前面主句所產生的動作或帶來的警告意味，翻譯時可譯為「並警告……」。
  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾語 (Past participle phrase as post-modifier)
    ...incentive payments earmarked for pilots across the armed forces who remain in service beyond their minimum required period have declined for four consecutive years...
    句中的 "earmarked for pilots..." 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "incentive payments"（獎勵金）。因為獎勵金是被「指定/編列」給特定對象，所以此處使用過去分詞 (earmarked) 表被動與完成的概念。
