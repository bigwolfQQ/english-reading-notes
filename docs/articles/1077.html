民進黨鄉民代表候選人台上昏倒後逝世

DPP township council candidate dies after collapsing on stage

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • candidate n. 候選人
    Chiu Feng-ing (邱鳳英), a Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) candidate for township councilor in Hualien County's Xincheng Township, died Wednesday morning after collapsing onstage at a Moon Festival event Tuesday night.
    民進黨籍花蓮縣新城鄉鄉民代表候選人邱鳳英於週二晚間在中秋節活動台上昏倒後，於週三上午宣告不治。
  • collapse v. 倒塌、昏倒、癱倒
    Chiu was singing onstage when she suddenly collapsed, according to sources.
    據消息來源指出，邱鳳英當時正在台上唱歌，突然昏倒。
  • personnel n. 人員、員工
    She was in cardiac arrest when ambulance personnel arrived and was pronounced dead after resuscitation efforts failed.
    救護人員抵達時她已心博停止，經急救無效後宣告死亡。
  • effort n. 努力、嘗試
    She was in cardiac arrest when ambulance personnel arrived and was pronounced dead after resuscitation efforts failed.
    救護人員抵達時她已心博停止，經急救無效後宣告死亡。
  • aide n. 助理、幕僚
    Chen Ching-feng (陳景豐), head of the DPP's Hualien chapter, said Chiu was a longtime party member who had worked as a councilor's aide.
    民進黨花蓮縣黨部主委陳景豐表示，邱鳳英是黨內資深黨員，曾擔任議員助理。
  • run for office phr. 參選、競選公職
    After moving to Xincheng Township eight years ago, Chiu decided to run for office this year for the first time to speak up for local residents and help ordinary residents get more resources, Chen said.
    陳景豐指出，邱鳳英於8年前搬到新城鄉後，今年決定首次參選，為當地居民發聲並協助一般民眾爭取更多資源。
  • resource n. 資源
    After moving to Xincheng Township eight years ago, Chiu decided to run for office this year for the first time to speak up for local residents and help ordinary residents get more resources, Chen said.
    陳景豐指出，邱鳳英於8年前搬到新城鄉後，今年決定首次參選，為當地居民發聲並協助一般民眾爭取更多資源。
  • carry on phr. v. 繼續、承繼
    The DPP's Hualien chapter said in a Facebook post Wednesday that it would carry on Chiu's love for the land, honor her spirit, and continue working for Hualien and its residents.
    民進黨花蓮縣黨部週三在臉書發文表示，將延續邱鳳英對這片土地的愛、紀念她的精神，並繼續為花蓮及當地居民努力。
  • consult v. 諮詢、請教、查閱
    The commission said it would consult the Central Election Commission on follow-up procedures following Chiu's death.
    選委會表示，將針對邱鳳英逝世後的後續程序諮詢中央選舉委員會。

文法重點

  • 過去進行式結合連接詞 when 表「當……時（突然）」
    Chiu was singing onstage when she suddenly collapsed, according to sources.
    句型「S + was/were + V-ing ... when + S + 過去式動詞」用來表達某個動作正在進行時，另一個突發動作介入發生。
  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense)
    She was in cardiac arrest when ambulance personnel arrived and was pronounced dead after resuscitation efforts failed.
    句子中的 "resuscitation efforts failed" 發生在過去某一時間點（救護人員抵達並宣告死亡）之前，因此使用過去完成式（had failed，此處因句型結構簡化或動詞型態表達過去發生的動作序列）。
  • 關係代間詞 who 引導的形容詞子句
    Chen Ching-feng (陳景豐), head of the DPP's Hualien chapter, said Chiu was a longtime party member who had worked as a councilor's aide.
    代名詞 "who" 用來引導形容詞子句，修飾前面的先行詞 "a longtime party member"，補充說明該黨員過去曾擔任議員助理的背景。
  • 不定詞片語 (To-infinitive phrase) 表目的
    After moving to Xincheng Township eight years ago, Chiu decided to run for office this year for the first time to speak up for local residents and help ordinary residents get more resources...
    句尾的 "to speak up for..." 是不定詞片語，用來修飾前面的動詞片語 "decided to run for office"，說明她決定參選的「目的」是為了替當地居民發聲。
