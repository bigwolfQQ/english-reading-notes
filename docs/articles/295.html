密集體育訓練恐影響學業：調查發現

Intensive sports training may affect schoolwork: survey

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • achievement n. 成就，達成
    While a majority of elementary-school students in sports-focused classes feel a strong sense of achievement from their training, about half fall behind in their schoolwork due to frequently missing class for competitions, a survey by the Child Welfare League Foundation found.
    根據兒童福利聯盟基金會的一項調查發現，雖然體育重點班的大多數國小學生從訓練中感受到強烈的成就感，但約有一半的人因經常請假參加比賽而導致學業落後。
  • amendment n. 修正案，修改
    The non-profit organization disclosed the survey results after the Executive Yuan on Thursday last week approved amendments to the National Sports Act (國民運動法), which are designed to protect student athletes in elementary schools from high-intensity sports training.
    該非營利組織是在行政院於上週四通過《國民運動法》修正草案後公布這項調查結果，該修法旨在保護國小學生運動員免受高強度體育訓練的影響。
  • establish v. 建立，創辦
    The Executive Yuan also ruled that no new sports-focused classes would be established at the elementary-school level.
    行政院還裁定，國小階段將不再設立新的體育重點班。
  • transform v. 轉變，改造
    The existing sports-centered classes would be transformed into sports clubs and community sports to balance learning and sports training, and to prevent students from undertaking high-intensity sports training.
    現有的體育重點班將轉型為運動社團與社區體育，以平衡學習與體育訓練，並防止學生接受高強度體育訓練。
  • consecutive adj. 連續的
    The survey found that 66.6 percent of respondents had experienced training for more than three hours in a single day during the week, while 18.6 percent underwent extreme training sessions lasting three consecutive hours without a single break.
    調查發現，66.6% 的受訪者在平日曾經歷單日超過三小時的訓練，而 18.6% 的人則經歷過連續三小時且中途沒有任何休息的極端訓練課程。
  • regulation n. 規章，規定
    Some schools did not follow the Regulations Governing the Establishment of Physical Education Classes in Senior High Schools and Below (高級中等以下學校體育班設立辦法), which limits the training to no more than three hours per day during the week, the foundation said.
    該基金會表示，部分學校未遵守《高級中等以下學校體育班設立辦法》，該辦法限制平日每天訓練時間不得超過三小時。
  • limit v. 限制，限定
    Some schools did not follow the Regulations Governing the Establishment of Physical Education Classes in Senior High Schools and Below (高級中等以下學校體育班設立辦法), which limits the training to no more than three hours per day during the week, the foundation said.
    該基金會表示，部分學校未遵守《高級中等以下學校體育班設立辦法》，該辦法限制平日每天訓練時間不得超過三小時。
  • endure v. 忍耐，忍受
    Of the respondents, 60.6 percent said that they had experienced training for more than three hours in a single day, while 15.6 percent said they had endured training sessions of more than eight hours in a single day.
    在受訪者中，60.6% 表示曾經歷單日超過三小時的訓練，15.6% 表示曾忍受單日超過八小時的訓練課程。
  • take up phr. v. 佔用（時間或空間）
    In addition, 38.7 percent of the student athletes said training had taken up so much of their time that they had little time left to study their academic classes.
    此外，38.7% 的學生運動員表示訓練佔用了他們大部分的時間，以致於幾乎沒有時間溫習學科課程。
  • performance n. 表現，績效
    Meanwhile, 47 percent said they were concerned that poor academic performance would hurt their job prospects, while 17.8 percent said they were concerned that they lack other skills and have very limited career options.
    同時，47% 的人表示擔心學業表現不佳會損害他們的就業前景，而 17.8% 的人則擔憂自己缺乏其他技能且職涯選擇非常受限。
  • prospect n. 前景，前途（常用複數）
    Meanwhile, 47 percent said they were concerned that poor academic performance would hurt their job prospects, while 17.8 percent said they were concerned that they lack other skills and have very limited career options.
    同時，47% 的人表示擔心學業表現不佳會損害他們的就業前景，而 17.8% 的人則擔憂自己缺乏其他技能且職涯選擇非常受限。
  • limited adj. 有限的
    Meanwhile, 47 percent said they were concerned that poor academic performance would hurt their job prospects, while 17.8 percent said they were concerned that they lack other skills and have very limited career options.
    同時，47% 的人表示擔心學業表現不佳會損害他們的就業前景，而 17.8% 的人則擔憂自己缺乏其他技能且職涯選擇非常受限。

文法重點

  • 分詞作後置修飾語（Present Participle as Postmodifier）
    ...amendments to the National Sports Act (國民運動法), which are designed to protect student athletes in elementary schools from high-intensity sports training.
    句中的「which are designed...」是關係子句，用來修飾前面的「amendments」。在英文中，這類被動語態的關係子句常可簡化為過去分詞片語（在此例中為設計成...的修飾），不過原句保留了完整的關係代名詞與be動詞，能清楚表達法案修訂的目的。
  • so... that... 如此……以至於……結果句型
    ...training had taken up so much of their time that they had little time left to study their academic classes.
    「so + 形容詞/副詞/名詞片語 + that + 子句」是用來表達因果關係的經典句型。在此句中，訓練佔用了「如此多」的時間，以至於（that）他們幾乎沒有剩餘時間唸書。
  • 被動語態（Passive Voice）
    About 27 percent said they were asked to undergo high-intensity training when they were injured.
    被動語態由「be 動詞 + 過去分詞」構成。在此句中，「they were asked」用來強調學生運動員是「被要求」接受高強度訓練，焦點在於承受動作的對象，而非發起動作的人。
