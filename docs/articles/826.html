烏克蘭非政府組織官員敦促支持台灣

Ukrainian NGO official urges support for Taiwan

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • representative n. 代表、代理人
    a representative of a Ukrainian non-governmental organization (NGO) said in Taipei yesterday
    一個烏克蘭非政府組織（NGO）的代表昨天在台北表示
  • call on phr. v. 呼籲、要求
    calling on her country to help strengthen Taiwan.
    呼籲她的國家協助強化台灣。
  • respondser n. 回應者、反應者
    Taiwan was one of the first responders after Russia invaded her country in February 2022.
    在俄羅斯於2022年2月入侵她的國家後，台灣是最早做出回應的國家之一。
  • shipment n. 運送、出貨、運送的貨物
    One of the first shipments of humanitarian aid that reached Ukraine was sent by Taiwan
    運抵烏克蘭的第一批人道救援物資之一就是由台灣寄出的
  • express v. 表達、表示
    expressing thanks for the quick response.
    表達對迅速回應的感謝。
  • hesitate v. 猶豫、遲疑
    while many countries hesitated over whether we would be able to stand against the Russian army or not
    而當時許多國家還在猶豫我們是否能夠抵擋俄羅斯軍隊
  • exchange v. 交流、交換
    how we can exchange this experience with Ukraine of resistance and our defense
    我們如何能與烏克蘭就抵抗和我們的防衛交流這些經驗
  • outcome n. 結果、後果
    the outcome of the Russia-Ukraine war would have “direct consequences on all of our lives
    俄烏戰爭的結果將對我們所有人的生活產生直接後果
  • consequence n. 結果、後果
    would have “direct consequences on all of our lives and also on the future of Taiwan.”
    將對我們所有人的生活以及台灣的未來產生直接影響。
  • organize v. 組織、籌辦
    It was organized by the German Institute Taipei and the Liberal Democratic League of Ukraine
    該論壇由德國在台協會與烏克蘭自由民主聯盟共同主辦

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾
    Liberal Democratic League of Ukraine’s East Asian Office head Mariia Makarovych said at a Taipei forum that Taiwan was one of the first responders after Russia invaded her country in February 2022.
    句中的「calling on her country to help strengthen Taiwan」為現在分詞片語，置於句尾用以補充說明主句主語（代表）同時正在進行或隨之發生的動作，中文常譯為「並……」。
  • 被動語態
    One of the first shipments of humanitarian aid that reached Ukraine was sent by Taiwan...
    句型「was sent」為過去式的被動語態（be 動詞 + 過去分詞），強調動作的承受者（運送的物資）而非動作的執行者（台灣），在商業與新聞英語中非常常見。
  • whether 引導的間接問句（當作介系詞的受詞）
    ...while many countries hesitated over whether we would be able to stand against the Russian army or not...
    片語 hesitate over 後面接了由 whether 引導的名詞子句（whether... or not），用來表達「對於……感到猶豫」，是多益閱讀測驗常考的句構。
  • 關係代名詞子句
    It was organized by the German Institute Taipei and the Liberal Democratic League of Ukraine, an independent, youth-led NGO that lists its mission as to promote liberal democratic values...
    句中的「that lists its mission as...」是由關代 that 引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾先行詞 an independent, youth-led NGO，補充說明該非政府組織的使命。
