單字解析

  • contention n. 爭奪，競爭
    Alonso went hitless for the Baltimore Orioles during their 2-1 win that eliminated the Mets from playoff contention
    在巴爾的摩金鶯隊以 2-1 獲勝、將大都會隊淘汰出季後賽爭奪戰的比賽中，阿隆索全場無安打
  • appreciate v. 感激，欣賞
    “I appreciate their sentiment and their support
    「我很感激他們的情感與支持
  • performance n. 表現，績效
    for just showing appreciation for the years of the effort and performance and doing whatever I could do to help the team win
    感謝他們對我多年來的努力、表現以及為幫助球隊贏球所付出的一切肯定
  • tribute n. 致敬，進貢
    the Mets aired a video paying tribute to Alonso
    大都會隊播出了向前效力該球隊七季的阿隆索致敬的影片
  • record n. 紀錄
    who hit a franchise-record 264 homers and made five All-Star teams over seven seasons with the club.
    擊出隊史紀錄 264 支全壘打並五次入選全明星賽
  • impact n. 影響，衝擊
    Just shows how much impact a player can make on a fanbase, this city.
    這充分展現了一名球員能對球迷群體、這座城市產生多大的影響力。
  • anticipate v. 預料，預期
    because you never can anticipate things that happen.
    因為你永遠無法預料會發生什麼事。
  • payroll n. 薪資總額，員工名冊
    Despite an opening-day payroll of more than US$350 million
    儘管開幕日的團隊薪資超過 3.5 億美元
  • endure v. 忍受，遭受（困境等）
    the Mets endured a 12-game losing streak in April
    大都會隊仍在 4 月吞下 12 連敗
  • grateful adj. 感激的，感謝的
    I’m beyond grateful and appreciative for the Baltimore Orioles
    同時我對巴爾的摩金鶯隊也充滿無限的感恩與謝意
  • milestone n. 里程碑
    “I know Paco is chasing down that same milestone
    「我知道帕科也在追逐同一個里程碑

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present/Past Participle as Post-modifier)
    Shortly before first pitch, the Mets aired a video paying tribute to Alonso, who hit a franchise-record 264 homers and made five All-Star teams over seven seasons with the club.
    句中的「paying tribute to Alonso」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「a video」（影片），相當於「which paid tribute to Alonso」的簡化寫法，省略了關代與be動詞，使句子更為緊湊。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Alonso, who was warming up along the left-field line, tipped his cap and tapped his mitt to acknowledge the cheering crowd...
    逗號後方的「who was warming up...」是非限定關係子句，對主詞 Alonso 提供額外的補充說明。如果把這個子句拿掉，主要句意「Alonso tipped his cap...」依然完整清晰。
  • 虛主詞 It 的用法
    “It’s going to be interesting to see what emotions kind of bubble up...
    句首的 "It" 是虛主詞（形式主詞），真正的主詞是不定詞片語 "to see what emotions kind of bubble up..."。這種句型在英文中常被用來避免頭重腳輕的情況。
  • 現在分詞表附帶狀況或結果 (Present Participle indicating result/simultaneous action)
    ...before grounding out in the fifth and striking out in the eighth immediately before Gunnar Henderson’s tiebreaking solo homer.
    句中的 "striking out" 與前方的 "grounding out" 一起構成連串的動作描述，表達在特定局數中發生的打擊結果，展現動態的敘事感。
