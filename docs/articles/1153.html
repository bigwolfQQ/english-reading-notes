文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    An exhibitor, right, explains a drone made by Russian drone developer and manufacturer UAS Glory Air to a visitor at the Drone Expo in New Delhi yesterday.
    句中的「made by Russian drone developer and manufacturer UAS Glory Air」是一個過去分詞片語，用來修飾前方的名詞 a drone（無人機），相當於形容詞子句「which was made by...」，省略了關代與be動詞，使句子更為精簡。
