男子在機場持刀挾持航空公司實習生後被捕

Man arrested in airport after holding airline intern at knifepoint

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • allegedly adv. 據稱，涉嫌
    A man who allegedly held a Cathay Pacific Airways intern at knifepoint and injured him at Taiwan Taoyuan International Airport on Saturday evening was subdued and arrested by aviation police later that day, police said.
    據警方表示，一名男子週六傍晚在臺灣桃園國際機場涉嫌持刀挾持國泰航空的一名實習生並致其受傷，當天稍晚遭到航空警察制伏並逮捕。
  • suspect n. 嫌疑犯
    The suspect, surnamed Tsai (蔡), was subdued at 5:58 p.m. and sustained only superficial injuries, according to the police.
    警方指出，該名蔡姓嫌犯於下午 5 時 58 分遭到制伏，且僅受到輕傷。
  • questioning n. 審問，質詢，訊問
    He was taken to the Aviation Police Bureau for questioning about his motives, police said.
    警方表示，他被帶往航空警察局接受偵訊以釐清其動機。
  • motives n. 動機，原因（motive的複數）
    He was taken to the Aviation Police Bureau for questioning about his motives, police said.
    警方表示，他被帶往航空警察局接受偵訊以釐清其動機。
  • confiscate v. 沒收，充公
    Police also confiscated Tsai's backpack, which had been left in the arrivals lobby, and found no dangerous items inside, they said.
    警方並表示，他們沒收了蔡嫌遺留在入境大廳的背包，並在裡面未發現任何危險物品。
  • treatment n. 治療，療程
    Meanwhile, the man held at knifepoint, an airline intern surnamed Huang (黃), sustained lacerations to his right hand and abdomen and was taken to a hospital for treatment, police said.
    同時，警方表示，遭持刀挾持的黃姓航空公司實習生右手與腹部受到撕裂傷，已被送往醫院治療。

    Cathay Pacific later issued a statement confirming that an unidentified individual had entered its staff lounge and was taken into custody by authorities.
    國泰航空隨後發布聲明證實，一名身分不明的個人闖入其員工休息室，並已被官方當局拘留。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作為後置修飾語 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    A man who allegedly held a Cathay Pacific Airways intern at knifepoint and injured him at Taiwan Taoyuan International Airport on Saturday evening was subdued and arrested by aviation police later that day...
    句中的「who allegedly held...」是關係代間子句，用來修飾前面的名詞 A man。另外也可以透過分詞片語簡化，在此處示範了形容詞子句的用法，用以精確限定或補充說明特定人物。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    ...was subdued and arrested by aviation police later that day, police said.
    英文新聞報導常使用被動語態（be + 過去分詞），當動作的承受者（在此為主詞 A man）比動作的執行者更重要，或者執行者為眾人皆知時特別常用。此句中強調男子被警方制伏與逮捕。
  • 非限定關代子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Police also confiscated Tsai's backpack, which had been left in the arrivals lobby, and found no dangerous items inside, they said.
  • 對等連接詞引導的現在分詞作時間副詞子句省略 (Reduced Time Clause)
    The airline said it was highly concerned about Huang's condition and would provide him with any necessary assistance while fully cooperating with the police investigation.
    句尾的「while fully cooperating with...」原本是一個完整的時間副詞子句（while it was fully cooperating...），為了簡化句子結構並保持流暢，省略了主詞與 be 動詞，直接保留連接詞搭配現在分詞（cooperating）使用。
