Taiwan's Hsu, Yang take silver at Pair-Go tournament in Japan

單字解析

  • tournament n. 錦標賽，競賽
    Nagoya, Japan, Sept. 27 (CNA) Taiwanese Go players Hsu Hao-hung... finished second at World Pair Go Championship 2026 in Nagoya on Sunday.
    （中央社記者日本名古屋27日專電）台灣圍棋選手...週日在名古屋舉行的2026年世界混雙圍棋錦標賽中獲得第二名。
  • defeat v. 擊敗，打敗
    The Taiwanese duo reached Sunday's final after defeating South Korea's Shin Min Jun and Kim Eun Ji on Saturday...
    台灣雙人組合在週六擊敗南韓的申旻埈和金恩持後晉級週日的決賽...
  • gain the upper hand phr. 佔上風，取得優勢
    The Japanese pair gained the upper hand early, and although Hsu and Yang tried to turn the match around...
    日本組合在早期取得了上風，儘管許皓鋐和楊子萱試圖扭轉戰局...
  • prize n. 獎品，獎金
    ...Ichiriki and Fujisawa went on to win the 5 million yen (US$31,798) top prize.
    ...一力遼和藤澤里菜最終贏得了500萬日圓（約合31,798美元）的頭獎。
  • receive v. 收到，得到，獲得
    Hsu and Yang received 2 million yen for finishing second.
    許皓鋐和楊子萱獲得了200萬日圓的亞軍獎金。
  • crucial adj. 至關重要的，決定性的
    Speaking to CNA through Taiwan's HaiFong Go Association, Hsu said a move by Yang at a crucial moment in the final helped set up his next move.
    許皓鋐透過台灣的海峰棋院接受中央社採訪時表示，楊子萱在決賽關鍵時刻的一步棋，有助於為他的下一步棋鋪路。
  • importance n. 重要性
    His comment highlighted the pair's camaraderie and the importance of coordination in Pair-Go...
    他的評論凸顯了這對搭檔的默契以及混雙圍棋中協調的重要性...
  • prohibit v. 禁止，阻止
    ...where partners are generally prohibited from speaking to each other except to confirm the order of play...
    ...在該賽事中，原則上禁止搭檔互相交談，除非是確認下棋順序...
  • communicate v. 溝通，交流
    Players are also barred from using body language or facial expressions to communicate strategy...
    選手也被禁止使用肢體語言或面部表情來傳達策略...
  • improve v. 改善，提高，進步
    ...but their coordination improved as the competition progressed from Friday through Sunday.
    ...但隨著比賽從週五進行到週日，他們的默契有所改善。
  • confidence n. 信心，自信
    ...but gained confidence after winning their second game against China's Ding Hao and Zhou Hongyu.
    ...但在贏了對陣中國丁浩和周泓余的第二場比賽後，她重拾了信心。
  • grateful adj. 感謝的，感激的
    She said she was grateful for the patience of the higher-ranking Hsu and hoped to partner with him again in the future.
    她表示，感謝排名較高的許皓鋐的耐心，並希望未來能再次與他搭檔。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle Phrases as Postmodifiers)
    Taiwanese Go players Hsu Hao-hung (許皓鋐), a 2023 Hangzhou Asian Games gold medalist, and Yang Tzu-hsuan (楊子萱)... finished second...
    句中的「a 2023 Hangzhou Asian Games gold medalist」是同位語兼具修飾功能，用來補充說明主詞許皓鋐的身份背景。在新聞英語中，常利用這種名詞片語或分詞片語來緊湊地交代人物頭銜，省去冗長的關係代名詞子句。
  • 非限定關係子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Hsu, a ninth-dan player, and Yang, who holds a sixth-dan rank, lost in the final to Japan's ninth-dan Ryo Ichiriki and seventh-dan Rina Fujisawa...
    句中的「who holds a sixth-dan rank」前面帶有逗號，屬於非限定關係子句，用來補充說明 Yang Tzu-hsuan 的段位。若將此子句拿掉，主句的意思依然完整，主要提供額外的附加資訊。
  • 地方副詞片語置於句首的倒裝結構 (Adverbial Placement / Inversion context)
    The tournament was held inside the Hommaru Palace at the historical Nagoya Castle.
    雖然此句並未使用完全倒裝，但示範了英語新聞中常見的空間描寫結構：將主詞與動詞置於前面，後面緊接著詳細的地點副詞片語（inside the Hommaru Palace at the historical Nagoya Castle），讓讀者迅速建立新聞發生的場景畫面。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Players are also barred from using body language or facial expressions to communicate strategy...
    句型「are barred from...」使用了被動語態（be + 過去分詞），用來表達選手「被禁止」進行某些行為。在商業契約、規則說明或新聞報導中，常用被動語態來客觀地強調規範或受事者。
