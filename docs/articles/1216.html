統一超商宣布五位千萬發票特獎得主

7-Eleven announces five NT$10 million invoice prize winners

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • purchase n. 購買、採購、購買的物品
    Five customers who made purchases at 7-Eleven stores across Taiwan won the top NT$10 million (US$318,123) special prize in the July-August uniform invoice lottery
    五位在全台7-Eleven門市消費的顧客贏得了7-8月統一發票1000萬元特別獎
  • operator n. 經營者、營運商
    According to 7-Eleven operator President Chain Store Corp., the five special prize winners made their purchases at stores in Taoyuan, Taipei, Taichung and Kaohsiung.
    根據7-Eleven營運商統一超商表示，這5位特別獎得主分別在桃園、台北、台中和高雄的門市進行消費。
  • daily necessities n. phr. 日常生活用品
    One customer spent NT$149 on daily necessities and ice cream at a store in Taipei's Neihu District
    一名顧客在台北市內湖區一家門市花費149元購買日用品與冰淇淋
  • exclusive adj. 專屬的、獨家的
    The four winners of the NT$1 million prize exclusive to cloud invoices made purchases at stores in Keelung, Taoyuan, New Taipei and Kaohsiung.
    4位雲端發票專屬100萬元獎項得主，分別在基隆、桃園、新北和高雄的門市進行消費。
  • announce v. 宣布、公布
    The Ministry of Finance announced the winning numbers for the July-August uniform invoice lottery on Sept. 25.
    財政部於9月25日公布了7、8月統一發票的中獎號碼。
  • claim v. 領取（獎金等）、聲稱、索取
    The ministry said the winners can claim their prizes from Oct. 6 to Jan. 5, 2027.
    財政部表示，中獎人可在10月6日至2027年1月5日期間領獎。
  • encourage v. 鼓勵
    The uniform invoice lottery is held every two months and designed to encourage consumers to request receipts and curb tax evasion.
    統一發票彩券每兩個月舉辦一次，旨在鼓勵消費者索取收據並遏止逃漏稅。
  • curb v. 控制、抑制、遏止
    The uniform invoice lottery is held every two months and designed to encourage consumers to request receipts and curb tax evasion.
    統一發票彩券每兩個月舉辦一次，旨在鼓勵消費者索取收據並遏止逃漏稅。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作為後置定名修飾語
    Five customers who made purchases at 7-Eleven stores across Taiwan won the top NT$10 million special prize... including one who spent just NT$27 on photocopying...
    句中的「who made purchases...」是形容詞子句，修飾前面的 customers；而句尾的「including one who spent...」則是包含分詞的片語，用來補充說明獲得特別獎其中一位顧客的細節，能讓句子結構更緊湊。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The uniform invoice lottery is held every two months and designed to encourage consumers to request receipts...
    句中使用「is held」與「is designed」兩個被動語態結構（be + 過去分詞），強調「統一發票是每兩個月被舉辦一次的」以及「它是被設計來鼓勵消費者的」，在新聞英語中用來客觀描述制度或常態非常常見。
  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾語
    The four winners of the NT$1 million prize exclusive to cloud invoices made purchases at stores...
    片語「exclusive to cloud invoices」（專屬於雲端發票的）是形容詞片語，放在名詞 prize 的後面做後置修飾，省略了「which is」，讓商業或新聞英文表達更為簡潔有力。
