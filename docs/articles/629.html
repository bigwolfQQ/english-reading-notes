文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    Hunter Goodman blasted a three-run homer for the Rockies (55-93), who have lost six straight.
    句中的 "who have lost six straight" 是非限定關代子句，修飾前面的落磯隊。而在新聞英文中，常會使用分詞片語或形容詞短語來精簡表達，此處用現在分詞子句補充說明球隊目前的連敗狀態。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Absolute Participial Construction)
    Down 7-4 in the eighth, the Tigers rallied against Jaden Hill (1-4).
  • 對等連接詞連接連續動作 (Coordination of Sequential Actions)
    After sliding into third, Lee leapt to his feet, pumped his fists and let out a roar that fired up the home crowd.
  • 引述句與直接引語 (Direct Quotation with Reporting Verbs)
    “I’m hoping this experience for Lee... everything matters,” MLB.com’s Jason Beck quoted Detroit manager A.J. Hinch as saying.
    此句採用了商業與新聞英文常見的倒裝與夾敘夾議結構：先放直接引言（"..."），後面才接主詞與動詞（MLB.com’s Jason Beck quoted...），並透過 "as saying" 來補充引述的內容，使長句重心更靈活。
