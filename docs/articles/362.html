台灣在U18亞洲青棒錦標賽以18比0擊敗泰國

Taiwan routs Thailand 18-0 in U18 Asian Baseball Championship

單字解析

  • defeat v. 擊敗，打敗
    Taiwan defeated Thailand 18-0 in four innings Tuesday to secure its first victory in the preliminary round of the BFA U18 Asian Baseball Championship in Taipei.
    台灣隊週二在台北舉行的BFA U18亞洲青棒錦標賽預賽中，以4局18比0擊敗泰國隊，取得預賽的首場勝利。
  • secure v. 獲得，取得，確保
    Taiwan defeated Thailand 18-0 in four innings Tuesday to secure its first victory in the preliminary round of the BFA U18 Asian Baseball Championship in Taipei.
    台灣隊週二在台北舉行的BFA U18亞洲青棒錦標賽預賽中，以4局18比0擊敗泰國隊，取得預賽的首場勝利。
  • preliminary adj. 預賽的，初步的
    Taiwan defeated Thailand 18-0 in four innings Tuesday to secure its first victory in the preliminary round of the BFA U18 Asian Baseball Championship in Taipei.
    台灣隊週二在台北舉行的BFA U18亞洲青棒錦標賽預賽中，以4局18比0擊敗泰國隊，取得預賽的首場勝利。
  • record n. 戰績，紀錄
    The win brought Taiwan's record to 1-1 after losing its opener to South Korea 2-0 on Monday.
    這場勝利使得台灣隊的戰績來到1勝1敗，此前他們在週一的開幕戰以0比2不敵南韓隊。
  • opener n. 開幕戰
    The win brought Taiwan's record to 1-1 after losing its opener to South Korea 2-0 on Monday.
    這場勝利使得台灣隊的戰績來到1勝1敗，此前他們在週一的開幕戰以0比2不敵南韓隊。
  • capitalize on phr. v. 利用，佔便宜，乘機利用
    Taiwan scored 14 runs in the top of the first inning at Taipei Tianmu Baseball Stadium, capitalizing on seven hits, three walks and six errors by Thailand while stealing 10 bases.
    台灣隊在台北天母棒球場的第一局上半攻下14分，利用泰國隊的7支安打、3次保送與6次失誤，同時盜了10個壘包。
  • extend v. 擴大，延長
    After being held scoreless in the second, Taiwan added two runs in both the third and fourth innings to extend its lead to 18-0.
    在第二局未能得分後，台灣隊在第三局與第四局各添得2分，將領先優勢擴大至18比0。
  • rule n. 規則，條例
    The game was called after the bottom of the fourth under the tournament's mercy rule, which ends a game when one team leads by at least 15 runs after four innings.
    比賽在第四局下半結束後，根據大會的扣倒規則提前結束；該規則規定，若比賽進行4局後有一方領先至少 15 分，比賽即告結束。
  • combine v. 結合，聯合，共同完成
    Taiwan pitchers Yang Yao-cheng and Wu Chieh-jui each worked two innings, combining for a perfect game with nine strikeouts.
    台灣投手楊曜丞與吳杰叡各投2局，聯手投出有9次三振的完全比賽。
  • commit v. 犯（錯誤、罪行等）
    Taiwan finished with 11 hits and no errors, while Thailand committed six errors and had no baserunners.
    台灣隊全場擊出11支安打且沒有失誤，而泰國隊則發生了6次失誤且沒有任何打者上壘。
  • face v. 面臨，對陣，迎戰
    Taiwan will face Singapore in its third preliminary-round game on Wednesday.
    台灣隊將於週三在第三場預賽中對陣新加坡隊。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作附加結果或伴隨狀況 (Participial Phrase for Result/Accompaniment)
    Taiwan scored 14 runs in the top of the first inning at Taipei Tianmu Baseball Stadium, capitalizing on seven hits, three walks and six errors by Thailand while stealing 10 bases.
    句尾的「capitalizing on...」與「while stealing...」為現在分詞片語，用來補充說明主句動作發生時同時進行的動作或造成的結果，強調主動與伴隨狀態。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    After being held scoreless in the second, Taiwan added two runs in both the third and fourth innings...
    「being held scoreless」採用了被動語態的動名词形式（after 介詞後接動名詞），用來表達台灣隊「被」對方壓制而沒有得分的情況。
  • 關係代間詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    The game was called after the bottom of the fourth under the tournament's mercy rule, which ends a game when one team leads by at least 15 runs after four innings.
    「which ends a game...」是由關係代間詞 which 引導的非限定性關係子句，用來補充說明先行詞「the mercy rule（扣倒規則）」的定義與功能。
