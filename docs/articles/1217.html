U.S. shows goodwill over late delivery of F-16Vs: Taiwan defense chief

單字解析

  • confirm v. 證實、確認
    Taiwan's Defense Minister Wellington Koo on Tuesday confirmed that the United States has "shown goodwill" over the late delivery...
    台灣國防部長顧立雄週二證實，美國已針對延遲交機一事「展現善意」...
  • contract n. 合約、契約
    Koo said the contract signed between Taipei and Washington for the fighters did not include financial penalties...
    顧立雄表示，台北與華府為這批戰機簽署的合約中，並未包含財務罰則...
  • financial adj. 財務的、金融的
    ...did not include financial penalties for late delivery of the 66 F-16Vs, meaning Taiwan will not receive financial compensation.
    ...並未包含針對 66 架 F-16V 延遲交機的財務罰則，這意味著台灣將不會獲得金錢賠償。
  • compensation n. 補償、賠償金
    ...meaning Taiwan will not receive financial compensation.
    這意味著台灣將不會獲得金錢賠償。
  • spare parts n. phr. 備用零件
    There could, however, be more technical support, spare parts and an extended warranty for Taiwan's F-16Vs...
    不過，台灣的 F-16V 可能會獲得更多的技術支援、備用零件以及延長保固...
  • warranty n. 保固、保證書
    There could, however, be more technical support, spare parts and an extended warranty for Taiwan's F-16Vs...
    不過，台灣的 F-16V 可能會獲得更多的技術支援、備用零件以及延長保固...
  • procure v. 採購、取得
    Taiwan's Air Force spent NT$247.2 billion between 2020 and 2026 to procure 66 new F-16V Block 70 jets from the United States.
    台灣空軍於 2020 年至 2026 年間斥資新台幣 2,472 億元，向美國採購 66 架全新的 F-16V Block 70 戰機。
  • scheduled adj. 預定的、安排好的
    The jets were originally scheduled to all be delivered to Taiwan by the end of this year...
    這些戰機原本預定在今年底前全部交付給台灣...
  • factor n. 因素、要素
    ...but the program was delayed by such factors as the relocation and restart of the production line and supply chain disruptions.
    ...但該計畫因生產線遷移與重啟、供應鏈中斷等因素而延遲。
  • public adj. 公眾的、公開的
    ...Koo told reporters in Taipei that the ministry will make the information public as soon as the delivery date is confirmed.
    顧立雄在台北告訴記者，國防部將在確認交機日期後立即對外公布該資訊。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Koo said the contract signed between Taipei and Washington for the fighters did not include financial penalties...
    句中的 "signed between Taipei and Washington for the fighters" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "the contract"（合約）。其完整結構為「which was signed...」，省略了「關係代名詞 + be 動詞」，使句子更為精簡緊湊，是多益閱讀測驗中常見的句構。
  • 現在分詞片語作結果或補充說明 (Present Participle Phrase indicating Result)
    ...meaning Taiwan will not receive financial compensation.
    句尾的 "meaning..." 是現在分詞引導的分詞構句，在此處表示前面整件事情所帶來的自然結果或延伸含意（相當於 "which means that..."），常用於商業財經與新聞英語中表達「意味著／導致……」。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The jets were originally scheduled to all be delivered to Taiwan by the end of this year...
    句型使用被動語態 "were... scheduled to be delivered"，強調戰機「被預定」以及「被交付」的動作，焦點在於主體（戰機）承受的動作而非施事者，這是商業與官方新聞報導中最常出現的基礎句型之一。
  • 副詞子句引導時間與條件 (Adverbial Clause of Time)
    ...the ministry will make the information public as soon as the delivery date is confirmed.
    片語 "as soon as"（一……就……）用來引導時間副詞子句。需注意雖然中文常說「一但確認就會……」，但在英文的條件或時間副詞子句中，要用現在式（is confirmed）代替未來式，這也是多益文法測驗（Part 5 & 6）的必考題型。
