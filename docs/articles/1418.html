印度 Z 世代正迎面直面人工智慧的未來

India’s Gen Z is facing down the artificial intelligence future

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • frontier n. 前沿、邊界
    How long will it take for the country to find the next growth frontier in the age of artificial intelligence (AI)?
    在人工智慧（AI）時代，這個國家需要花多久才能找到下一個成長前沿？
  • enterprise n. 企業、公司
    Autonomous AI agents are already shrinking the value that coders in Bengaluru and Hyderabad bring to global enterprises.
    自主式 AI 代理已經在縮減班加羅爾和海得拉巴的程式碼編寫者為全球企業所提供的價值。
  • subsidize v. 補助、補貼
    Now local politicians are wooing Western cloud computing companies with liberal land grants and subsidized solar energy...
    現在當地政客正透過慷慨的土地劃撥和補貼太陽能來爭取西方雲端運算公司...
  • ambition n. 雄心、抱負
    ...that ambition looks more daunting than ever.
    ...這種雄心顯得比以往任何時候都更加艱巨。
  • incentive n. 激勵措施、獎勵
    However, despite committing billions of dollars in taxpayer-funded incentives over the past five years, India’s share of global merchandise exports is stuck at less than 2 percent.
    然而，儘管在過去五年中投入了數十億美元納稅人資助的獎勵措施，印度在全球商品出口中所佔的份額仍停留在不到 2% 的水準。
  • component n. 零件、組件
    However, assembling iPhones with imported components creates jobs that pay 18,000 rupees (US$186) a month...
    然而，用進口零組件組裝 iPhone 所創造的工作每月薪資為 18,000 盧比（186 美元）...
  • restructuring n. 重組、改組
    Even when corporate restructuring became inevitable during the 1997 Asian Financial Crisis, Seoul protected the deep learning embedded in today’s SK Hynix Inc.
    即使在 1997 年亞洲金融危機期間企業重組變得不可避免時，首爾仍保護了深植於今日 SK 海力士之中的深厚學習能力。
  • surplus n. 過剩、盈餘
    The surplus talent would drive a start-up boom where the founders’ ambitions go beyond 10-minute food delivery.
    過剩的人才將推動一場新創企業熱潮，其創辦人的雄心將超越 10 分鐘送餐服務。
  • revenue n. 收益、營收
    Multinationals already employ 2.4 million professionals in India, generating nearly US$100 billion in annual revenue.
    跨國公司已經在印度僱用了 240 萬名專業人員，創造了近 1000 億美元的年營收。
  • emulate v. 效仿、模仿
    If India wants to emulate Taiwan’s success in manufacturing, then it must also follow its path and build up its small businesses first.
    如果印度想要效仿台灣在製造業上的成功，那麼它也必須走同樣的道路，首先建立並發展其小型企業。
  • outsourcing n. 委外代工、外包
    However, those specialist positions will not follow the logic of labor-cost arbitrage that made outsourcing such a lucrative trade.
    然而，那些專家職位將不會遵循勞動成本套利的邏輯，而正是這種邏輯使外包成為一項利潤豐厚的貿易。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作後置修飾 / 伴隨狀語
    Drawing, twisting and winding 16 or more threads at a time, it outproduced the spinners making some of the world’s finest yarn.
    句首的「Drawing, twisting and winding...」是由現在分詞開頭的片語，用來修飾主詞 it（珍妮紡紗機），表達伴隨發生的動作或狀態。句中「making some...」則是另一個現在分詞片語，用來後置修飾前面的名詞 spinners（紡紗工）。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Absolute Participial Construction)
    With global businesses such as Starbucks seeking to become their own software companies by replacing companywide systems from International Business Machines, Microsoft and Oracle — applications Indian programmers specialize in maintaining — that ambition looks more daunting than ever.
    「With + 名詞 + 現在分詞（seeking）」構成獨立分詞構句，用來補充說明主句發生時的背景狀況或原因。其中夾帶了一個由關係代名詞省略而成的形容詞子句「applications Indian programmers specialize in maintaining」，用來修飾前面的 systems。
  • 假設語氣（與現在事實相反）
    If nominal per capita dollar income keeps growing at the past decade’s 5.5 percent rate, India would be old before it is rich.
    此句雖然前半段是條件子句（If + 現在式），但後半段使用了「would + 原形動詞（would be）」來表達假設性的結果，意指在特定成長率推演下未來必然會達到的推論結果，具有強烈的修辭與警示效果。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Even when corporate restructuring became inevitable during the 1997 Asian Financial Crisis, Seoul protected the deep learning embedded in today’s SK Hynix Inc.
    片語「embedded in...」是以過去分詞為核心的被動修飾語，省略了「which was」，用來修飾前面的名詞 the deep learning（深厚學習能力），指該能力被「嵌入」或「內化」在企業之中。
