文法重點

  • 過去分詞作後置修飾語 (Past Participle as Postmodifier)
    Tomorrow is the seventy-fifth anniversary of the San Francisco Peace Treaty, signed by forty-nine nations on September 8, 1951.
    句中的「signed by forty-nine nations on September 8, 1951」是由過去分詞開頭的片語，用來後置修飾前面的名詞「the San Francisco Peace Treaty」。這相當於完整形容詞子句「which was signed by...」的簡化形式，是多益閱讀測驗中常見的句構。
  • 條件句與假設語態 (Conditional Sentence)
    If an international treaty imperfectly addresses a point of sovereignty, then the default sovereignty must reside with the people of the territory in question.
    此句使用「If + 現在式動詞 (addresses)，then + 主動詞 + 助動詞 (must reside)」的句型，表達真實條件下的推論與必然結果，常用於商業及法律合約或論述中。
  • 現在分詞表附帶結果或狀態 (Present Participle)
    But the Nationalists’ rapacious occupation of Taiwan delegitimized Chiang Kai-shek’s (蔣介石) authority, tarnished his reputation and jeopardized democracy in the entire post-War Pacific.
  • 動名詞片語作主詞 (Gerund Phrase as Subject)
    On April 15, the State Department stated that “the determination of the final status of Formosa must await the conclusion of a peace settlement for Japan.”
    句中的「the determination of the final status of Formosa」是由冠詞、名詞與介詞片語組成的名詞片語（核心為動名詞概念的名詞 determination），作為動詞「must await」的主詞，展現正式書面英文的客觀與權威感。
