中國亞太經合會會議未有「降格」：部長

No ‘downgrading’ at China APEC meeting: minister

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • subjected to phr. 遭受、蒙受、使服從
    Taiwan was not subjected to any “downgrading” during at an APEC ministerial meeting in China and was granted the same rights as other members, Minister of Labor Hung Sun-han (洪申翰) said yesterday.
    勞動部長洪申翰昨天表示，台灣在中國舉行的APEC部長級會議期間，並未遭受任何「降格」待遇，並獲賦予與其他成員相同的權利。
  • grant v. 授與、給予、准許
    Taiwan was not subjected to any “downgrading” during at an APEC ministerial meeting in China and was granted the same rights as other members, Minister of Labor Hung Sun-han (洪申翰) said yesterday.
    勞動部長洪申翰昨天表示，台灣在中國舉行的APEC部長級會議期間，並未遭受任何「降格」待遇，並獲賦予與其他成員相同的權利。
  • encounter v. 遭遇、遇到（問題、困難等）
    “During our participation in the meeting, we did not encounter any so-called downgrading,” Hung told reporters after arriving at Taiwan Taoyuan International Airport yesterday afternoon.
    「我們在參與會議期間，並未遭遇任何所謂的降格。」洪申翰昨天下午抵達台灣桃園國際機場後告訴記者。
  • assistance n. 協助、援助
    “through our continued insistence and with the assistance of the APEC secretariat, the Chinese side granted us rights equal to those of other members in accordance with APEC rules.”
    「透過我們持續的堅持以及APEC秘書處的協助，中方依照APEC規則，賦予了我們與其他成員相等的權利。」
  • in accordance with phr. 依照、與……一致
    “through our continued insistence and with the assistance of the APEC secretariat, the Chinese side granted us rights equal to those of other members in accordance with APEC rules.”
    「透過我們持續的堅持以及APEC秘書處的協助，中方依照APEC規則，賦予了我們與其他成員相等的權利。」
  • consistent adj. 一致的、符合的
    That was consistent with Taiwan’s insistence on “reciprocal dignity,” Hung said, referring to a principle he had laid out before departing for China for APEC...
    洪申翰指出，這符合台灣對「對等尊嚴」的堅持。他指的是他在出發前往APEC之前所闡述的一項原則……
  • principle n. 原則、主義
    That was consistent with Taiwan’s insistence on “reciprocal dignity,” Hung said, referring to a principle he had laid out before departing for China for APEC...
    洪申翰指出，這符合台灣對「對等尊嚴」的堅持。他指的是他在出發前往APEC之前所闡述的一項原則……
  • confirm v. 確認、證實
    The minister also confirmed that he neither applied for nor used a “Taiwan compatriot permit” — a travel document issued by China to Taiwanese, whose passports Beijing does not recognize for travel to China — for the trip.
    這位部長也證實，他在這趟行程中既未申請也未使用「台胞證」——這是中國發給台灣人的旅行證件，北京當局並不承認台灣護照可用於前往中國。
  • practice n. 慣例、作法、實務
    “We followed the usual practice and entered China using an invitation letter,” Hung said, referring to the arrangements for Taiwanese officials attending APEC meetings in China.
    「我們遵循慣例，憑邀請函進入中國。」洪申翰談到台灣官員出席在中國舉行的APEC會議之安排時表示。
  • constitute v. 構成、組成
    Asked whether the nameplate reading “Chinese Taipei” in front of him at the meeting constituted downgrading, Hung reiterated that Chinese Taipei is Taiwan’s formal membership name in APEC.
    當被問及會議中擺在他面前、印有「中華台北」字樣的名牌是否構成降格時，洪申翰重申，中華台北是台灣在APEC的正式會籍名稱。
  • reiterate v. 重申、反覆地說
    Asked whether the nameplate reading “Chinese Taipei” in front of him at the meeting constituted downgrading, Hung reiterated that Chinese Taipei is Taiwan’s formal membership name in APEC.
    當被問及會議中擺在他面前、印有「中華台北」字樣的名牌是否構成降格時，洪申翰重申，中華台北是台灣在APEC的正式會籍名稱。
  • handle v. 處理、應付
    Ahead of Hung’s trip, China’s Taiwan Affairs Office on Sept. 2 said that Beijing would handle Taiwan’s participation according to its “one China” principle, APEC memorandums and established practices.
    在洪申翰此行之前，中國國務院台灣事務辦公室於9月2日表示，北京將根據其「一個中國」原則、APEC備忘錄及既定慣例來處理台灣的參與事宜。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Hung was returning from the eighth APEC Human Resources Development Ministerial Meeting in Nanjing, where he became the first ministry head in President William Lai’s (賴清德) administration to travel to China.
    句中的 "in President William Lai’s administration" 作為介系詞片語修飾前面的 "the first ministry head"。而在新聞英語中，常利用過去分詞或形容詞片語來修飾名詞，例如文章後段的 "nameplate reading..." 或是 "travel document issued by China..." 等，都是精簡句子結構的重要多益文法與閱讀技巧。
  • 現在分詞片語作主詞補語／附加說明 (Present Participle Phrase)
    Speaking after the three-day trip, Hung said that “through our continued insistence and with the assistance of the APEC secretariat, the Chinese side granted us rights equal to those of other members in accordance with APEC rules.”
    句首的 "Speaking after the three-day trip" 是由分詞構句簡化而來，原意為 "While he was speaking after the three-day trip..."。在多益閱讀與商業英文中，常將連接詞省略，直接用現在分詞（V-ing）來表示主動的同時發生或補充說明。
  • 對等連接詞並列否定結構 (Neither... nor...)
    The minister also confirmed that he neither applied for nor used a “Taiwan compatriot permit” — a travel document issued by China to Taiwanese, whose passports Beijing does not recognize for travel to China — for the trip.
    此句使用了 "neither A nor B" 的結構，用來連接兩個動詞片語 "applied for" 與 "used"，表示「既沒有申請、也沒有使用」。這在多益的文法選擇題（Part 5）中是常見的對等連接詞考點，要注意詞性前後必須一致。
  • 關係代間詞子句作補充說明 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    ...a travel document issued by China to Taiwanese, whose passports Beijing does not recognize for travel to China — for the trip.
    句中使用逗號隔開的 "whose passports Beijing does not recognize..." 是一個所有格關係代間詞（whose）引導的非限定子句，用來補充說明前面的 "Taiwanese"（台灣人）。這種結構在多益測驗中經常出現在聽力與閱讀部分，用以測試考生對代名詞格位及子句修飾功能的理解。
