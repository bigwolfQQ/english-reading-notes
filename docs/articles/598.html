更多訪問團訪台：外交部

More delegations visit Taiwan: MOFA

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • delegation n. 代表團
    Foreign diplomatic and business visits to Taiwan are reaching new highs, with landmark firsts and large-scale delegations, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) said...
    外交部表示，外國外交及企業訪台人數正創下新高，包含具里程碑意義的首次訪問與大型代表團...
  • substantive adj. 實質性的，重大的
    ...pledging to deepen mutual trust and substantive cooperation.
    ...並誓言深化彼此互信與實質合作。
  • representative n. 代表，代理人
    Arizona Commerce Authority president and CEO Sandra Watson led a delegation of more than 100 representatives from industry, government and academia...
    亞利桑那商務局總裁兼執行長珊卓·華森率領涵蓋產、官、學界超過 100 人的代表團...
  • transformation n. 轉型，轉變
    ...focusing on the transformation of the Taiwan-US defense supply chain and technological research and development (R&D)...
    ...聚焦於台美防務供應鏈轉型及技術研發...
  • coalition n. 聯合，聯盟，同盟
    ...marking the party’s first major visit since joining the ruling coalition with Japan’s Liberal Democratic Party, it added.
    外交部補充說，這是該黨自加入日本自由民主黨執政聯盟以來首次大型訪問。
  • mutually beneficial adj. 互惠互利的
    International support for Taiwan is not only about the number or size of visiting delegations, and greater importance lies in further transforming these exchanges into sustained, mutually beneficial and concrete cooperation...
    國際對台灣的支持不只在於來訪代表團的數量或規模，更重要的在於進一步將這些交流轉化為持續、互利且具體的合作...
  • concrete adj. 具體的
    The transition marks a shift from the “a Taiwan contingency is a Japan contingency” strategic concept to concrete tactical execution aimed at solving practical issues, he said.
    他表示，這項轉變標誌著戰略概念從「台灣有事就是日本有事」轉變為旨在解決實際問題的具體戰術執行。
  • leverage v. 善用，發揮（優勢等）
    The ministry will continue to leverage “integrated diplomacy”... to deepen Taiwan’s partnerships with like-minded nations...
    外交部將繼續發揮「總合外交」的力量......來深化台灣與理念相近國家的夥伴關係...
  • resilience n. 恢復力，韌性
    The ministry will continue deepening bilateral mutual trust and exchanges at all levels, and leverage Taiwan’s strengths in technology, industry, healthcare, disaster prevention and democratic resilience...
    外交部將持續深化各層級的雙邊互信與交流，並發揮台灣在科技、產業、醫療、防災及民主韌性等方面的優勢...
  • ally n. 同盟國，盟友
    ...and it would continue to work with like-minded partners to serve as a reliable ally, upholding a rules-based international order...
    ...並將持續與理念相近的夥伴合作，扮演可靠的盟友，維護以規則為基礎的國際秩序...
  • institutionalized adj. 制度化的
    Amid exchanges with the foreign delegations, the next step for Taiwan should be turning these short visits into lasting relationships and institutionalized cooperation, he said.
    他表示，在與外賓代表團交流的同時，台灣的下一步應為將這些短暫訪問轉化為持久關係與制度化合作。
  • scale up v. phr. 擴大，逐步增加規模
    ...expanding from “2+2” talks to “4+4” talks, and will further scale up to “5+5” talks during the seventh policy dialogue in Tokyo today.
    ...從「2+2」對話擴大至「4+4」對話，並將在今日於東京舉行的第七次政策對話中進一步規模化至「5+5」對話。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（附加狀況與結果）
    Foreign diplomatic and business visits to Taiwan are reaching new highs, with landmark firsts and large-scale delegations, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) said, pledging to deepen mutual trust and substantive cooperation.
    句尾的「pledging to deepen...」是由主動分詞片語構成的分詞構句，用來補充說明前面的主句「訪台人數創新高」所伴隨的動作與意圖（即外交部承諾深化互信與實質合作）。
  • 關係代名詞子句（非限定用法）
    Italian Senate Vice President Gian Marco Centinaio led the largest-ever delegation of Italian lawmakers to Taiwan from Aug. 31 to Sept. 5, where he met with President William Lai... and attended Semicon Taiwan.
    句中的「where he met with...」是關係副詞 where 引導的非限定子句，先行詞為前面的地方「Taiwan」。用逗點隔開，對台灣做補充描述，說明他在當地見了哪些人、參加了什麼活動。
  • 被動語態
    International support for Taiwan is not only about the number or size of visiting delegations, and greater importance lies in further transforming these exchanges into sustained, mutually beneficial and concrete cooperation, he said.
    雖然此句主體為主動，但文章中大量運用被動語態來客觀陳述官方說法或事實（例如第 7 段的「since joining...」或結構中常見的「is said/noted」等）。本句則展現了名詞子句作受詞的用法（that 省略）。
  • 現在分詞作後置修飾語
    The transition marks a shift from the “a Taiwan contingency is a Japan contingency” strategic concept to concrete tactical execution aimed at solving practical issues, he said.
    句中的「aimed at solving practical issues」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「tactical execution（戰術執行）」，相當於形容詞子句「which is aimed at solving practical issues」，省略了「which is」以求簡潔。
