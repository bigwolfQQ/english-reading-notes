二戰後東德如何成為極右派的堅固堡壘

How the east became Germany’s far-right stronghold after World War II

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • reunification n. 重新統一
    More than three decades after Germany’s 1990 reunification, lasting east-west divisions may be thrown into stark relief at Sunday’s election in the eastern state of Saxony-Anhalt.
    在德國 1990 年重新統一三十多年後，持續存在的東西德分裂可能會在週日薩克森-安哈特邦的選舉中被鮮明地凸顯出來。
  • state-owned enterprise n. phr. 國營企業
    When the Berlin Wall fell in 1989, spelling the end of the communist German Democratic Republic (GDR), many state-owned enterprises were shuttered, sparking large-scale unemployment and population flight, especially by young women.
    當柏林圍牆於 1989 年倒塌、宣告共產主義的德意志民主共和國（GDR）走向終結時，許多國有企業被迫關閉，引發了大規模的失業與人口外流，特別是年輕女性。
  • shutter v. 關閉（店鋪、工廠等）

  • unemployment n. 失業（率）

  • persist v. 持續、存留
    “That was a difficult experience for many East Germans, one that has persisted across generations, becoming part of a collective east German memory,” Kaiser said.
    「對許多東德人來說，那是一段艱難的經歷，這種經歷跨代持續著，成為東德集體記憶的一部分，」凱iser 表示。
  • devaluation n. 貶低、貶值
    “It involved this sense of devaluation, this experience of loss and the realization that everything can change overnight.”
    「這包含了一種貶低感、失落的體驗，以及意識到一切都可能在一夜之間改變的覺悟。」
  • on average prep. phr. 平均
    Decades on, wages and savings remain lower on average, most big business is still based in the west, and eastern Germans — one-fifth of the population — are under-represented in politics.
    數十年過去了，平均薪資與儲蓄依然較低，大多數大企業仍總部位於西部，而東德人——佔人口的五分之一——在政治上代表性不足。
  • stimulate v. 刺激、促進（經濟等）
    Billions spent on infrastructure and to stimulate the economy have born some fruit — with the region now home to many of the “industries of the future,” Kaiser said.
    用於基礎設施和刺激經濟的數十億資金已經取得了一些成果——該地區現在是許多「未來產業」的所在地，凱iser 說。
  • disparity n. 不一致、懸殊、差異
    Nevertheless, there remain “big disparities in wealth” and, among many, a feeling of being “disadvantaged and not seen,” she said.
    儘管如此，她表示，這裡仍然存在「巨大的財富差距」，並且在許多人心中，有一種「處於劣勢且未被看見」的感覺。
  • disadvantaged adj. 弱勢的、處於不利地位的
    Nevertheless, there remain “big disparities in wealth” and, among many, a feeling of being “disadvantaged and not seen,” she said.

  • contract worker n. phr. 契約工、合約勞工
    While West Germany’s post-WWII economic recovery was fueled by “guest workers,” the East invited contract workers from socialist “brother states” such as Vietnam and Mozambique.
    西德在二戰後的經濟復甦是由「客工」推動的，而東德則從越南和莫扎比克等社會主義「兄弟國家」邀請了契約工人。
  • subsidise v. 補貼、資助
    Some in the west have suggested the richer part of Germany stop “subsidising” the east, which Kaiser said she finds “disastrous” and annoying as an east German.

  • on the rise prep. phr. 在上升中、在增加中
    In parts of the west, the AfD and their far-left competitors are also on the rise, she said, adding that Germany as a whole should view the east as a political “seismograph.”
    她說，在西部的部分地區，AfD 及其極左翼競爭對手也在崛起，並補充說整個德國應該將東部視為政治「地震儀」。

文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    More than three decades after Germany’s 1990 reunification, lasting east-west divisions may be thrown into stark relief at Sunday’s election in the eastern state of Saxony-Anhalt.
    句中的 "may be thrown into stark relief" 採用了被動語態（助動詞 may + be + 過去分詞 thrown），強調動作的承受者（東西德的分裂），而不是主動發動者，常用於新聞英語中來客觀描述情況。
  • 現在分詞作補充說明 (Present Participle as Result/Background)
    When the Berlin Wall fell in 1989, spelling the end of the communist German Democratic Republic (GDR), many state-owned enterprises were shuttered...
    句中的 ", spelling the end of..." 是由現在分詞 "spelling" 引導的分詞片語，用來補充說明前面主句事件所帶來的結果或隨之發生的情況，相當於 "which spelled the end of..."。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Nominative Absolute Construction)
    Billions spent on infrastructure and to stimulate the economy have born some fruit — with the region now home to many of the “industries of the future,” Kaiser said.
    句尾的 "with the region now home to..." 結構為「with + 名詞 + 形容詞/介系詞片語」，用來補充說明主要子句發生時的背景狀況或伴隨的狀態。
  • 代名詞所有格加動名詞複合結構
    Cutting transfer funds would “only strengthen the victim role that the AfD always likes to adopt,” she added.
    句首的 "Cutting transfer funds" 是動名詞片語作為主句的主詞，"Cutting" 為動名詞，帶受詞 "transfer funds"，展現了英文中將動作名詞化的精煉表達方式。
