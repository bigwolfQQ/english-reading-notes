文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    Taiwanese memory producers Nanya Technology Co (南亞科技) and Macronix International Co (旺宏) have allocated 6 percent of pre-tax profit and 15 percent of net profit to reward employees respectively, the union said.
    句中的 Boise, Idaho-based 或後文修飾的分詞結構，常用來精簡地描述名詞的狀態或來源。在此處的另一句「...complying with...」或分詞片語如「...tying employee bonuses directly to corporate profits」中，現在分詞片語「tying...」用來補充說明類似做法（similar approaches）的具體內容，相當於關代子句「which tie employee bonuses directly to corporate profits」。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Micron employees’ annual payouts are mainly subject to the terms of the incentives pay plan (IPP) with several limitations.
    多益閱讀與聽力常出現被動語態來描述規定、合約或商業流程。在此句中，「are ... subject to」雖然常用作形容詞片語結構（受...約束/視...而定），但也體現了主詞 payouts 受到條款規範的被動意涵。
  • 非限定關係代間子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Micron Taichung Union, which represents about 10,000 Micron employees in Taichung and Tainan, yesterday said it is to launch a second-round negotiation with management on Oct. 22.
  • 表時間背景的現在進行式 (Present Continuous for Current Background)
    The vote comes as Micron’s board of directors is meeting today and tomorrow in the US.
