文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The sample was collected by the ITA during out-of-competition testing on September 17.
    被動語態的結構為「be 動詞 + 過去分詞 (V-pp)」，用來強調動作的承受者（在此例中是「樣本」the sample），而不是動作的執行者（由 ITA 採集）。
  • 分詞作形容詞修飾 (Participles as Adjectives)
    The 19-year-old returned a positive test for banned substance furosemide and has been provisionally suspended, the International Testing Agency said.
    句中的 banned 是過去分詞作形容詞用，用來修飾名詞 substance（物質），表示「被禁止的」物質。
  • prevent... from... 片語與被動結合
    “The athlete is prevented from competing, training, coaching or participating in any activity during the Asian Games Aichi-Nagoya 2026,” the ITA said.
    「prevent sb. from doing sth.」意為「阻止某人做某事」。在此句中改為被動語態「be prevented from doing sth.」，表示「某人被阻止／被禁止做某事」，後面接多個動名詞（competing, training, coaching, participating）形成平行結構。
