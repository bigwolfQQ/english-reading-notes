台灣支持者在紐約遊行爭取加入聯合國

Taiwan supporters march in New York for U.N. inclusion

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • inclusion n. 納入，包含
    More than 100 Taiwanese Americans, Taiwanese lawmakers and members of civic groups marched through New York City on Saturday to call for the inclusion of the Republic of China (Taiwan) in the United Nations...
    超過100名台裔美國人、台灣立法委員及公民團體成員於週六在紐約市進行遊行，呼籲將中華民國（台灣）納入聯合國...
  • participant n. 參與者，參加者
    Participants chanted "UN for Taiwan" and "Keep Taiwan Free" as they marched through central Manhattan...
    參與者在穿過曼哈頓市中心時高喊「聯合國要台灣」與「保持台灣自由」等口號...
  • annual adj. 年度的，一年一度的
    The annual march brought together Taiwanese community groups from across the U.S. East Coast.
    這場年度遊行匯聚了來自美國東岸各地的台灣人社區團體。
  • representative n. 代表
    Silk, permanent representative to the U.N. of the Republic of the Marshall Islands, expressed hope that Taiwan would one day be able to participate in U.N. meetings...
    馬紹爾群島共和國常駐聯合國代表席爾克表達了希望台灣有朝一日能參與聯合國會議的期望...
  • participate v. 參與，參加
    ...expressed hope that Taiwan would one day be able to participate in U.N. meetings as a member...
    ...表達了希望台灣有朝一日能以成員身分參與聯合國會議...
  • opportunity n. 機會，時機
    Wang told CNA that the annual march provides an opportunity for Taiwan to make its voice heard near the U.N. headquarters.
    王義川告訴中央社，這場年度遊行提供了一個機會，讓台灣的聲音得以在聯合國總部附近被聽見。
  • promote v. 推廣，促銷，晉升
    "This is a very good opportunity to promote Taiwan."
    「這是一個推廣台灣的絕佳機會。」
  • campaign n. （政治或社會）活動，競選運動
    He expressed hope that the campaign would continue to ensure Taiwan's voice is heard by U.N. member states...
    他表達希望這項活動能持續進行，確保台灣的聲音被聯合國會員國聽見...
  • awareness n. 意識，認識，察覺
    ...Taiwan's government is also conducting promotional activities in the Big Apple during the ongoing U.N. General Assembly session to raise international awareness of its exclusion from the U.N. system.
    ...台灣政府也在正在進行的聯合國大會期間於紐約展開宣傳活動，以提高國際社會對其被排除在聯合國體系之外的關注。
  • organization n. 組織，機構
    ...eventually help pave the way for Taiwan to join the organization under its own name.
    ...最終有助於為台灣以自身名義加入該組織鋪平道路。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Present Participle）作附帶狀況/結果的補充說明
    Participants chanted "UN for Taiwan" and "Keep Taiwan Free" as they marched through central Manhattan, passing Times Square and Bryant Park before ending near the New York Public Library.
    句尾的 "passing..." 與前面的主句同時發生（或緊接著發生），用來補充說明參與者在遊行時「經過了」哪些地方，這是英文新聞報導中非常常見且道地的句型。
  • 使役動詞 make 的受詞補語用法（make + O. + p.p.）
    Wang told CNA that the annual march provides an opportunity for Taiwan to make its voice heard near the U.N. headquarters.
    在此句中，"make its voice heard" 的結構為 make + 受詞 (its voice) + 過去分詞 (heard作受詞補語)，表示「使某人的聲音被聽見」。因為「聲音」是被聽見，所以用過去分詞表被動。
  • 不定詞片語作後位修飾（Infinitive as Postmodifier）
    He expressed hope that the campaign would continue to ensure Taiwan's voice is heard by U.N. member states and eventually help pave the way for Taiwan to join the organization under its own name.
    句中的 "to join the organization..." 是不定詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞片語 "the way"（pave the way for... to do...），說明是為了什麼事情鋪路，在多益閱讀與商業英文中這類結構很常出現。
  • 現在分詞片語作形容詞修飾（Present Participle as Adjective）
    ...Taiwan's government is also conducting promotional activities in the Big Apple during the ongoing U.N. General Assembly session to raise international awareness of its exclusion from the U.N. system.
    句中的 "ongoing" 是由現在分詞轉化而來的形容詞，意為「進行中的、持續中的」，用來修飾後方的名詞 "U.N. General Assembly session"（聯合國大會會議）。
