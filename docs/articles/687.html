台灣球迷之夜

Taiwanese Fan Night

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後置定名詞修飾語
    Expatriates and supporters display the Republic of China flag and hold up a banner reading: “We are Team Taiwan” at Fenway Park in Boston on Saturday for its 912 Taiwan Fan Night organized by the Brookline Taiwanese Chamber of Commerce.
    句尾的「organized by the Brookline Taiwanese Chamber of Commerce」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「Night」，相當於形容詞子句「which was organized by...」，省去了主系動詞，使句子更為精簡。
  • 現在分詞片語作後置修飾語（表主動）
    Expatriates and supporters display the Republic of China flag and hold up a banner reading: “We are Team Taiwan” ...
    句中的「reading: “We are Team Taiwan”」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「banner」，表示該布條「上面寫著」的意思，與被修飾的名詞之間具有主動關係。
  • 關係代名詞 who 引導的形容詞子句
    The event drew 1,325 participants who formed the name “Taiwan” in the stands.
    句中的「who formed the name “Taiwan” in the stands」是由關係代名詞 who 引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾先行詞「participants（參與者）」，補充說明這些參與者做了什麼事。
