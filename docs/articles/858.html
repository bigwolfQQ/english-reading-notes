沙烏地阿拉伯尋求埃及的支持

Saudi Arabia seeks Egypt’s backing

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • backing n. 支持、贊助
    Saudi Arabia’s crown prince on Tuesday sought backing from the president of Egypt
    沙烏地阿拉伯的王儲於週二尋求埃及總統的支持
  • infrastructure n. 基礎設施
    as the kingdom faces increased attacks by Yemen’s Houthi rebels on shipping and infrastructure
    因為該王國正面臨葉門青年運動叛軍對航運和基礎設施不斷增加的攻擊
  • export n. / v. 出口
    that has increased their ability to choke off Saudi Arabian oil exports through the Red Sea.
    這增強了他們切斷沙烏地阿拉伯透過紅海出口石油的能力。
  • statement n. 聲明、陳述
    The crown prince said in a statement that they confirmed “their shared commitment to ensuring freedom and security of maritime navigation”
    王儲在聲明中表示，雙方確認了「他們對確保海上航行自由與安全的共同承諾」
  • commitment n. 承諾、委身
    that they confirmed “their shared commitment to ensuring freedom and security of maritime navigation”
    雙方確認了「他們對確保海上航行自由與安全的共同承諾」
  • position n. 立場、位置
    Al-Sisi’s office in a separate statement expressed “Egypt’s unwavering position in supporting Saudi Arabia.”
    塞西的辦公室在另一份聲明中表達了「埃及支持沙烏地阿拉伯堅定不移的立場」。
  • details n. 詳細資訊、細節
    Neither side gave firm details on any future steps.
    雙方皆未透露關於未來任何具體步驟的詳細資訊。
  • influential adj. 有影響力的
    While Egypt is unlikely to offer military help to Saudi Arabia, the country is influential in the Arab world
    雖然埃及不太可能向沙烏地阿拉伯提供軍事援助，但該國在阿拉伯世界具有影響力
  • strategic adj. 戰略性的
    seizing the Red Sea port of Mokha and three strategic islands in and near the Bab el-Mandeb Strait.
    佔領了紅海的穆哈港以及曼德海峽內及附近的三個戰略島嶼。
  • facilities n. 設施、設備
    “Our operations target its oil facilities and military bases, which are far removed from the sacred sites,”
    「我們的行動目標是其石油設施和軍事基地，這些都遠離聖地，」

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...as the kingdom faces increased attacks by Yemen’s Houthi rebels on shipping and infrastructure, while the militants yesterday said that they had shot down a Saudi Arabian fighter jet.
    句中的 "facing" 或 "as... faces" 引導原因子句，而後方的 "shipping and infrastructure, while the militants yesterday said..." 透過連接詞 while 連接對比或同時發生的狀況。此外，文章如 "...carrying out hostile operations..." 也是使用現在分詞片語來補充說明動作發生時的背景狀況。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    The Iranian-backed rebels have captured territory in the past week that has increased their ability to choke off Saudi Arabian oil exports through the Red Sea.
    句中使用 "that has increased..." 作為關係代名詞子句，用來修飾先行詞 "territory"，補充說明該領土被佔領後所帶來的影響與結果。
  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense)
    ...the militants yesterday said that they had shot down a Saudi Arabian fighter jet.
    句中的 "said" 是一般過去時，代表過去說話的動作；而 "that" 後面的子句使用過去完成式 "had shot down"，用來表達「擊落戰機」這個動作發生在「說話」這個過去時間點之前。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    He said the jet had been targeted “using a locally made air-to-air missile.”
    句中的 "had been targeted" 採用了過去完成式的被動語態（had been + 過去分詞），強調戰機是「被」目標鎖定攻擊，焦點在於受事者（jet）而非施事者。
