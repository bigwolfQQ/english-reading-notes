透過民主建立韌性

Resilience building through democracy

  • prosperity n. 繁榮，興旺
    Diversity is strength. However, divergent views might polarize a society to the extent that those would deny a nation the opportunity to continue on its path of prosperity.

    然而，選舉可能會受到選民威脅、賄賂、虛假資訊和畫分選區的損害。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞當作原因或理由的補語/附帶說明 (分詞構句)
    In metallurgy, alloys are stronger than pure metals, because adding different-sized atoms to fill into the voids among atoms prevents atomic layers from sliding or breaking.
    句中的 preventing（此處原形為 prevents，但概念上為分詞/動名詞引導的結果）用來表達前面動作所產生的直接結果。另外文中有許多現在分詞片語（如 leading to greater resilience）作為結果的補充說明，省略連接詞並直接用分詞表達自然的連鎖反應。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Adjective Clause)
    KMT Legislator Weng Hsiao-ling (翁曉玲) even declared herself Chinese, betraying voters who are troubled by the Chinese Communist Party’s (CCP) aggressive military posture...
    句中使用「who are troubled by...」這個由關係代名詞 who 引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾先行詞 voters（選民），補充說明這些選民所面臨的狀況與擔憂。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    However, elections may be compromised by voter intimidation, bribery, disinformation and gerrymandering.
    此句使用了助動詞加被動態（may be compromised），強調選舉（主詞 elections）是承受了外在威脅、賄賂、假訊息和畫分選區等行為的影響，凸顯受事者的處境。
