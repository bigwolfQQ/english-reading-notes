國立清華大學校長應該辭職嗎？

Should NTHU’s president resign?

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • resignation n. 辭職，辭職信
    Academic and industrial leaders requested his resignation.
    學術界與產業界領袖要求他辭職。
  • contract n. 合同，契約
    Unless an employment contract explicitly protects them, they can be terminated at any time without cause under at-will employment doctrines.
    除非有僱傭合約明確保護他們，否則根據隨意雇用原則，他們隨時可能被免職而無需正當理由。
  • terminate v. 終止，解雇
    If the board finds out a president is interviewing elsewhere, it can legally terminate their presidency simply by citing a “loss of confidence” in their long-term commitment.
    如果董事會發現校長正在其他地方接受面試，只需以對其長期承諾「失去信心」為由，即可合法終止其校長職務。
  • authority n. 權力，權威
    However, the internal academic affairs councils of universities in Taiwan generally do not have the direct, unilateral legal authority to fire a sitting president.
    然而，台灣各大學的內部校務會議通常沒有直接、單方面合法解任現任校長的權力。
  • stipulate v. 規定，訂明
    The University Act (大學法) stipulates that a president can only be removed for major misconduct if a high threshold — usually a one-third petition and a two-thirds majority vote by the council — is met.
    《大學法》規定，只有在達到高門檻（通常需要校務會議三分之一連署以及三分之二多數決）的情況下，校長才因重大違失而被免職。
  • dismissal n. 解雇，免職
    The case would be forwarded to the Ministry of Education to officially execute a dismissal.
    該案件將被轉呈教育部以正式執行解任。
  • precedent n. 先例，前例
    There is legal precedent in Taiwan for the ministry to remove a president directly.
    台灣有教育部直接撤換校長的法律先例。
  • credentials n. 證書，資歷，資格
    In 2005, Huang Kuan-tsae (黃光彩) of National Taiwan Normal University was removed only eight months after taking office due to inadequate credentials.
    2005年，國立台灣師範大學的黃光彩因資歷不符，在就職僅八個月後即被撤換。
  • ratify v. 批准，認可
    In 2018, the ministry refused to ratify the election of National Taiwan University (NTU) president-elect Kuan Chung-ming (管中閔) for 11 months due to conflict-of-interest allegations regarding his role as an independent board director of Taiwan Mobile.
    2018年，教育部因台哥大獨立董事利益迴避的指控，拒絕核定台大校長當選人管中閔的任命案長達11個月。
  • transparency n. 透明度
    Pro-Kuan alliances argued that delaying his appointment violated university autonomy, which was countered by the North America Taiwanese Professors’ Association in a public statement that true institutional autonomy is warranted only when universities rigorously follow baseline regulations and maintain transparency rather than bypassing them under the guise of independence.
    挺管陣營主張延宕其任命案違反大學自主，北美台灣人教授協會則發表公開聲明反擊，認為唯有當大學嚴格遵守底線法規並維持透明度，而非以獨立為藉口規避時，真正的機構自主才具正當性。
  • appoint v. 任命，委派
    Pro-Kuan alliances argued that delaying his appointment violated university autonomy...
    挺管陣營主張延宕其任命案違反大學自主...
  • promote v. 晉升，促銷，推廣
    Kuan’s alleged plagiarism should have disqualified him as a professor at NTU, let alone being promoted to president.
    管中閔涉嫌抄襲本應取消他擔任台大教授的資格，更不用說升任校長了。
  • retain v. 保留，保持
    It is vitally important to retain capable and moral leaders to help Taiwan, instead of terminating their services over trivial matters.
    保留有能力且有品德的領導者來幫助台灣至關重要，而不是因為瑣事而終止他們的職務。

文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    National Tsing Hua University (NTHU) president John Kao (高為元) was invited for a job interview in Hawaii...
    句子中使用「be + 過去分詞」(was invited) 構成被動語態，強調動作的承受者（高為元校長）而非發動者，常應用於新聞報導中客觀陳述事實。
  • 條件句與假設語氣 (Conditional / Subjunctive)
    Unless an employment contract explicitly protects them, they can be terminated at any time without cause under at-will employment doctrines.
    「Unless...」引導條件子句（相當於 if... not），表達「除非……否則……」，後方主句使用情態動詞 can 配合原形動詞表達在特定條件下的可能性或權力。
  • 分詞作為形容詞 (Participle as Adjective)
    However, the internal academic affairs councils of universities in Taiwan generally do not have the direct, unilateral legal authority to fire a sitting president.
    句中的「sitting」是由動詞 sit 轉變而來的現在分詞，在此作為形容詞放在名詞 president 前面，意為「現任的、在職的」。
  • 過去分詞表被動與完成 (Past Participle indicating Passive/Completion)
    Kuan’s alleged plagiarism should have disqualified him as a professor at NTU, let alone being promoted to president.
    句中的「should have + 過去分詞 (should have disqualified)」用來表達過去「本應該發生卻未發生」的遺憾或反事實推測，是多益職場與日常情境中常見的語法結構。
