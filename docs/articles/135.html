Zhang Ziyu looms over opponents at World Cup

單字解析

  • opponents n. 對手，敵手
    Zhang Ziyu looms over opponents at World Cup
    張子宇在世界盃對手面前高居臨下
  • challenge n. 挑戰
    The 19-year-old Zhang is 2.21 meters tall and gave the US a tough physical challenge to overcome...
    19歲的張子宇身高2.21公尺，在...中給美國隊帶來了艱難的身體對抗考驗
  • efficient adj. 有效率的
    She was really efficient from the free throw line as well.
    她在罰球線上也非常高效。
  • debut n. 初次登場，處女秀
    ...ultimately the teenager finished with 13 points on her worlds debut...
    ...最終這位青少年在她的世界盃處女秀中拿下13分...
  • forthcoming adj. 樂意提供資訊的，坦誠的
    Lawson was more forthcoming with her praise.
    勞森對她的稱讚則更加直言不諱。
  • upside n. 正面潛力，有利方面
    So I think there’s a lot of upside to her...
    所以我認為她有很多上升空間...
  • program n. 計劃，方案
    ...so the coaching team designed her own program for her to warm up...
    ...因此教練團隊為她設計了她自己的專屬計劃，讓她熱身...
  • performance n. 表現，績效
    Han praised her performance at that tournament and said she was ready to step onto the world stage.
    韓旭稱讚了她在該賽事中的表現，並表示她已經準備好踏上世界舞台。
  • responsibility n. 責任
    As one of the oldest players in the Chinese roster at 26, Han takes on the responsibly of looking out for her younger teammates.
    作為中國隊陣中26歲的老將之一，韓旭肩負著照顧較年輕隊友的責任。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Phrase as Modifier /分詞片語）作後置修飾
    China’s Zhang Ziyu had everyone looking up to her on the opening day of the Women’s Basketball World Cup on Friday.
    句型「have + 受詞 + 現在分詞 (ing)」表示「使得某人/某物持續進行某動作」。在此句中，loking up to her 形容 everyone 的狀態，意指「讓每個人都對她仰望」。
  • 假設語氣（Conditional Sentence）表與過去事實相反
    If not for a passport mishap, Zhang and Han would have been joined by another center — 2.01m Li Yueru...
    「If not for + N」相當於「If it had not been for...」，用以表達「要不是因為……（過去的事實）」，主句則使用「would have + 過去分詞」來表達與過去事實相反的假設結果。
  • 動名詞片語（Gerund Phrase）作主詞補語或目的
    ...so the coaching team designed her own program for her to warm up and keep her fit and ready for the game...
    句尾的「to warm up and keep...」為不定詞片語，在此處作副詞用，用來修飾前面的動詞 designed，說明設計專屬計劃的「目的」是為了讓她熱身並保持體能。
