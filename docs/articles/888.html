文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾語 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The US has deported more than 25,000 people under secretive agreements with 35 countries to take migrants with no ties to those nations, an investigation published yesterday said.
    句中的「published yesterday」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞片語「an investigation」，相當於形容詞子句「which was published yesterday」，省略了「which was」以精簡語句。
  • 現在完成被動語態 (Present Perfect Passive Voice)
    At least 25,447 people had already been deported to third countries by Aug. 31 this year, data compiled by Forbidden Stories with a consortium of international journalists showed.
    句中的「had already been deported」採用了過去完成被動語態（had been + 過去分詞），強調在過去特定時間點（今年8月31日）之前，動作已經被完成，且主詞 people 是被遣返的受害者。
  • 現在分詞片語作後置修飾語 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Data compiled by Forbidden Stories with a consortium of international journalists showed...
    句中的「compiled by Forbidden Stories...」是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞「data」，用來指資料是由誰所彙編的，具有被動意味。
