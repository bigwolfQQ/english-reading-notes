文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...a tank containing hydrochloric acid at Nanpu Power Plant...
    句中的「containing hydrochloric acid」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 tank（槽體），相當於形容詞子句「that contained hydrochloric acid」，使句子更簡鍊。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The plant’s power generation equipment was not affected and its generating units were operating normally...
    「was not affected」使用了被動語態（be + 過去分詞），強調發電設備「受到影響」這個動作的承受者，而非發電設備本身去影響別人。
  • 時間副詞子句與被動語態 (Adverbial Clause of Time with Passive Voice)
    When work is being done above storage tanks, employers should ensure that...
    「When work is being done」是一個由 when引導的時間副詞子句，內含現在進行式的被動語態（is being done），用來表達「當工作正在被進行時」的狀況。
  • 名詞子句作受詞 (Noun Clause as Object)
    ...employers should ensure that safety structures such as scaffolding and work platforms are installed...
    「that safety structures... are installed」是由連接詞 that 引導的名詞子句，在句中作為動詞 ensure 的受詞，說明要確保的具體內容。
