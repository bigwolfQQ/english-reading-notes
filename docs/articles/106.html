文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Under the Tobacco Hazards Prevention Act (菸害防制法), the manufacture, importation, sale, supply, display, advertisement, and use of e-cigarettes are prohibited.
    句型「be + 過去分詞 (prohibited)」構成被動語態，強調動作的承受者（各項電子煙相關行為）遭到禁止，而非主動去禁止別人。
  • 假設語氣 (Subjunctive Conditional)
    Although the dates for legislative review and enactment of the amendment have not yet been determined, if passed, there would be a one-month grace period after it comes into effect, the agency said.
    「if passed」是省略了主詞與be動詞的條件句（原句為 if [it is] passed），用以表達未來可能實現的假設狀況；後方主句則使用助動詞過去式 would 搭配原形動詞表達假設情境的結果。
  • 關係代間詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    The agency urged people to avoid purchasing or using e-cigarettes, and for those who possess such devices to dispose of them at the collection spots, which can be found on the HPA’s Web site.
    句尾的「which can be found on the HPA’s Web site」為非限定用法的關係代間詞子句（前面有逗號），用來補充說明前面的先行詞「collection spots（回收點）」，並帶有被動語態。
