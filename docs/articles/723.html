Over 1,000 apply for 15 new Taiwan High Speed Rail driver positions

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾
    The first batch of new drivers reported for work in September and is undergoing training, while a second batch is scheduled to begin training in November, THSRC said.
    句中的 "is undergoing training" 是現在式進行式動詞片語，用來描述第一批駕駛目前正在進行中的狀態。而 "while a second batch is scheduled to begin..." 則利用連接詞 while 連接兩個對比或同時發生 子句，表達第二批的預定計畫。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Applicants with science or engineering backgrounds and good English communication skills are preferred.
  • 介系詞片語作後位修飾 / 分詞簡化概念
    Candidates must pass a written test, aptitude test, interview and medical examination, followed by about eight months of training before they can qualify as train drivers.
