文法重點

  • 介系詞片語作後置修飾語 (Prepositional phrase as a postmodifier)
    Military police from the Rapid Response Company practice a formation drill on motorcycles during a rehearsal for the Double Ten National Day celebrations in the plaza in front of the Presidential Office yesterday.
    句中的 "from the Rapid Response Company"、"on motorcycles"、"for the Double Ten National Day celebrations" 以及 "in front of the Presidential Office" 都是介系詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞（如 military police, drill, rehearsal, plaza），這在英文新聞和多益閱讀測驗中是非常常見的長句結構，能提供豐富的細節。
