文法重點

  • Despite 引導的讓步片語
    Handset camera lens manufacturer Largan Precision Co reported a 11.47 percent year-on-year decline in revenue for the third quarter yesterday, despite revenue last month hitting its highest level since October last year.
    “despite” 是介系詞，後面通常接名詞或動名詞（在此為 “revenue last month hitting...”），用來表達「儘管……」，表示前後兩個子句之間有轉折、讓步的語氣。
  • 分詞構句表附帶結果
    ...resulting in some shipments being delayed until the fourth quarter and others being postponed to the first quarter of next year, meaning that the fourth quarter would remain busy for Largan...
    句中的 “resulting in...” 與 “meaning that...” 均為現在分詞（-ing）引導的分詞構句，用來補充說明前面主句所引發的結果或附加意義，省去了連接詞與主詞，讓長句的結構更為精簡流暢。
  • be to + V 未來安排語氣
    Largan is to release its third-quarter earnings results at an investors’ conference on Thursday and give sales guidance for the fourth quarter.
    “be to + 原形動詞” 常見於新聞英語中，用來表示官方正式的計畫、預定行程或即將發生的重要活動，此處指大立光預定在週四發布財報。
  • 過去分詞（ed）作為後置定語與被動概念
    The company has sent fiber array samples to customers for validation and plans to establish pilot lines using semi-automated production and invite potential clients for factory visits, with full automation to be introduced upon mass production next year, Yuanta said.
    句中的 “with full automation to be introduced...” 使用了 “with + 受詞 + 過去分詞/不定詞” 的複合結構，用來補充說明伴隨發生的狀況，其中 automation 與 introduce 之間具備被動關係。
