睡眠不足的日本人競相爭奪「最充分休息」

Sleep-deprived Japanese compete for best-rested

單字解析

  • compete v. 競爭，比賽

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    In diligent, exhausted Japan, self-described elite “competitive sleepers” vied to lie down, relax and nap the fastest and deepest in a contest organized by a mattress company.
    句中的「organized by a mattress company」是由過去分詞開頭的片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「a contest」，相當於形容詞子句「which was organized by a mattress company」的簡化用法，常出現在多益閱讀測驗中以節省篇幅。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The winner was chosen based on how quickly and soundly they slept.
    句型「was chosen」使用了「be + 過去分詞」的被動語態結構，強調動作的承受者（The winner 優勝者）而非發動者。在商業與新聞英語中，被動語態常被用來客觀陳述事實或流程。
  • 獨立分詞結構/附帶狀況 (Nominative Absolute Structure)
    In the Tokyo competition, dubbed “Matt Race,” dozens of participants on Monday fitted with sleep tracking devices sprawled on comfy mattresses...
    句中的「fitted with sleep tracking devices」省略了主格與 be 動詞（原為 who were fitted with...），作為形容詞片語直接補充說明「dozens of participants」的狀態，表示「被配備了...的狀態」，是多益文法題常考的分詞簡化概念。
