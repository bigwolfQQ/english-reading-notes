自 2016 年起已有 400 名外籍高階人才歸化為中華民國國民

400th foreign high-level expert naturalized as ROC citizen since 2016

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • professional n. 專業人才，專家
    A total of 400 foreign "high-level" professionals have been naturalized as Republic of China (Taiwan) citizens over the past decade
    過去十年來，已有總計 400 名外籍「高階」專業人才歸化為中華民國（台灣）國籍
  • attract v. 吸引
    as the government has eased nationality rules to attract talent from a wider range of fields
    因為政府已放寬國籍法規以吸引來自更廣泛領域的人才
  • industry n. 工業，產業
    The 400th high-level professional, who works in the electronics industry, was approved for ROC citizenship on Aug. 26, the MOI said.
    內政部表示，這名在電子產業工作的第 400 位高階專業人才於 8 月 26 日獲准取得中華民國國籍。
  • approve v. 批准，核准
    The 400th high-level professional, who works in the electronics industry, was approved for ROC citizenship on Aug. 26, the MOI said.
    內政部表示，這名在電子產業工作的第 400 位高階專業人才於 8 月 26 日獲准取得中華民國國籍。
  • disclose v. 透露，公開
    The MOI did not disclose the new ROC citizen's original nationality.
    內政部未透露這位新中華民國公民的原國籍。
  • data n. 數據，資料
    The data showed that the naturalized professionals came from a wide range of backgrounds and areas of expertise, the ministry said.
    內政部指出，數據顯示這些歸化的專業人才來自廣泛的背景與專業領域。
  • amend v. 修正（法規等）
    In December 2016, the act was amended to allow high-level foreign professionals to retain their original nationalities after obtaining ROC citizenship.
    2016 年 12 月，《國籍法》經修正，允許外籍高階專業人才在取得中華民國國籍後保留其原國籍。
  • requirement n. 要求，必要條件
    In May 2024, the act was again amended to reduce the residency requirement for high-level professionals applying for naturalization from five years to two.
    2024 年 5 月，《國籍法》再次修正，將高階專業人才申請歸化的居留年限要求從五年縮短為兩年。
  • apply for phr. 申請
    The MOI said Taiwan has various channels through which foreign talent can apply for naturalization.
    內政部表示，台灣有多種管道供外籍人才申請歸化。
  • review n. 審查，審核
    High-level professionals who already hold a Plum Blossom Card, for example, can apply for ROC citizenship after a review and do not need to renounce their original citizenship after naturalization.
    例如，已經持有梅花卡的高階專業人才，經過審查後即可申請中華民國國籍，且歸化後無需放棄原國籍。
  • compete v. 競爭
    As many countries compete for talent, Taiwan is determined to create a friendly legal and living environment to attract more outstanding foreign professionals to develop their careers and settle in the country, the MOI said.
    內政部表示，由於許多國家都在競逐人才，台灣決心打造友善的法規與生活環境，以吸引更多優秀的外籍專業人才來台發展職涯並安居樂業。
  • outstanding adj. 優秀的，傑出的
    As many countries compete for talent, Taiwan is determined to create a friendly legal and living environment to attract more outstanding foreign professionals to develop their careers and settle in the country, the MOI said.
    內政部表示，由於許多國家都在競逐人才，台灣決心打造友善的法規與生活環境，以吸引更多優秀的外籍專業人才來台發展職涯並安居樂業。

文法重點

  • 非限定關係代名詞子句（附加補充說明）
    The 400th high-level professional, who works in the electronics industry, was approved for ROC citizenship on Aug. 26, the MOI said.
    句中的「who works in the electronics industry」前方有逗號，屬於非限定用法（Non-defining relative clause），用來補充說明前面「第 400 位高階專業人才」的背景，若將此子句拿掉，主要子句的意思依然完整。
  • 現在完成被動語態
    A total of 400 foreign "high-level" professionals have been naturalized as Republic of China (Taiwan) citizens over the past decade...
    句型結構為「have/has been + 過去分詞 (V-pp)」，用來表達過去某個動作持續到現在，且主詞是承受動作的對象（被歸化）。配合「over the past decade（過去十年來）」的時間副詞，非常適合使用現在完成式。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾
    The latest high-level professional to become an ROC citizen is the 400th to benefit from Taiwan's efforts to ease restrictions under the Nationality Act.
    句中的「to become an ROC citizen」與「to benefit from...」是不定詞片語，用來修飾前方名詞（professional 與 400th），在英文中常以此結構來代替冗長的關係子句，使句子更為精簡俐落。
  • 介系詞加關係代名詞（Preposition + Which）
    The MOI said Taiwan has various channels through which foreign talent can apply for naturalization.
    「through which」引導形容詞子句修飾前面的「channels（管道）」。因為外籍人才是在這些「管道之中/透過這些管道」申請，所以介系詞 through 必須放在關係代名詞 which 的前方，這在多益閱讀與商務英文中是相當常見的高階句構。
