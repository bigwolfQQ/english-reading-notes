文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Shen issued the warning ahead of a forum in Taipei on the new rules, organized by the Asia-Pacific Elite Interchange Association (APEIA).
    句尾的「organized by the Asia-Pacific Elite Interchange Association」是由過去分詞開頭的片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 forum（論壇）。其還原的主動或完整結構為「...a forum... which was organized by...」，此處省略了關代與be動詞，使語氣更為精鍊緊湊，是新聞英文常見的高效寫法。
  • 現在分詞表附帶結果或伴隨狀況 (Present Participle Expressing Result/Circumstance)
    As a result, he warned, individuals may only become aware of the restriction when they are preparing to leave the country, Shen said.
    句中的「when they are preparing to leave the country」是一個時間副詞子句，其中的「preparing」為現在分詞，用來表示當事人在準備離開時的當下狀態。此結構讓讀者能具體想像受限制者發現自己被境管的時機點。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The rules also stipulate that people who violate export restrictions or technology management regulations, or who are deemed to pose a threat to the security of Chinese industries or technologies, can be barred from leaving China, Shen said.
    句中的「can be barred from leaving China」使用了被動語態（can be + 過去分詞 barred），強調「遭禁止出境」的這個動作是施加在這些人身上，突顯出中國官方行政處分的強制性與被動受害的語氣。
