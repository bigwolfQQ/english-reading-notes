融合教育在學校教育中正走向失敗

Inclusive education is failing at schools

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • discipline n. 紀律；訓練
    Early last month, an incoming junior-high school student in Taichung with discipline issues allegedly attacked a teacher with a broom...
    上個月初，台中一名有紀律問題的國中新生據稱用掃帚攻擊老師……
  • imperative adj. 必要的，極重要的
    It is imperative that the fundamental systemic problems behind the issue be confronted.
    當務之急是必須正視問題背後的根本系統性問題。
  • absence n. 缺乏，不存在
    In the absence of complementary measures, sufficient professional educators and adaptive streaming mechanisms, has so-called “inclusive education” deviated from its original ideals?
    在缺乏配套措施、充足的專業教育人員以及適性分流機制的狀況下，所謂的「融合教育」是否已經偏離了它最初的理想？
  • complementary adj. 補充的，配套的
    In the absence of complementary measures, sufficient professional educators and adaptive streaming mechanisms...
    在缺乏配套措施、充足的專業教育人員以及適性分流機制的狀況下……
  • originate v. 起源，發源
    Inclusive education originated from international human rights advocacy in the late 20th century...
    融合教育源自於20世紀末的國際人權倡議……
  • enact v. 制定（法律），頒布
    Taiwan enacted the Special Education Act (特殊教育法) in 1984 and amended it in 2023 to implement inclusive education.
    台灣於1984年頒布《特殊教育法》，並在2023年進行修正以落實融合教育。
  • amend v. 修正，修改（法律等）
    Taiwan enacted the Special Education Act (特殊教育法) in 1984 and amended it in 2023 to implement inclusive education.
    台灣於1984年頒布《特殊教育法》，並在2023年進行修正以落實融合教育。
  • implement v. 實施，執行
    Education authorities should promptly and comprehensively review the challenges in implementing the Special Education Act...
    教育主管機關應迅速且全面地檢視《特殊教育法》在實施上的挑戰……
  • shortage n. 短缺，不足
    The ostensibly well-meaning legislation has significantly departed from its original intent due to insufficient resources and a shortage of personnel.
    然而，這項表面上立意良善的立法，由於資源不足和人員短缺，已嚴重背離其最初的意圖。
  • disrupt v. 干擾，打斷
    Their emotional outbursts or disciplinary problems frequently disrupted teaching...
    他們的情緒爆發或紀律問題經常干擾教學……
  • exhaust v. 使筋疲力盡，耗盡
    Those kinds of situations not only exhaust teachers physically and mentally...
    那種情況不僅讓老師身心俱疲……
  • deprive v. 剝奪，使喪失
    ...but also deprive the majority of students of the right to learn in peace...
    ……也剝奪了大多數學生安靜學習的權利……
  • comprehensive adj. 全面的，綜合的
    Education authorities should promptly and comprehensively review the challenges in implementing the Special Education Act...
    教育主管機關應迅速且全面地檢視《特殊教育法》在實施上的挑戰……
  • compulsory adj. 強制性的，必修的
    In that case, the grand exodus of parents transferring their children from public schools to private schools, where inclusive education is not compulsory, would continue.
    在這種情況下，家長將小孩從不必強制實施融合教育的公立學校轉學到私立學校的「大出走潮」將會持續下去。

文法重點

  • It is + adj. + that 子句（使用原形動詞/虛擬語氣）
    It is imperative that the fundamental systemic problems behind the issue be confronted.
    當句型為「It is + 形容詞 (如 imperative, important, necessary) + that 子句」時，that 後面的子句通常會使用「should + 原形動詞」，或者直接省略 should 使用原形動詞（在此句中 be confronted 即為被動語態的原形動詞），表達強烈的建議或必要性。
  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾
    In that case, the grand exodus of parents transferring their children from public schools to private schools, where inclusive education is not compulsory, would continue.
    句中的 "transferring their children from public schools to private schools" 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 parents，相當於形容詞子句 "who transfer their children..."，用以簡化句子並直接補充說明家長的動作。
  • 地方副詞置於句首的倒裝句型
    The case is not isolated, but the tip of the iceberg of long-hidden teacher-student conflict.
    雖然此句並非完全倒裝，但文章中常見將否定詞或特定副詞片語置於句首以加強語氣的結構。另如 "In the absence of..." 也是將介系詞片語置於句首，先建立背景條件，再帶出主句的疑問，有助於提升論述的邏輯與流暢度。
  • 關係代名詞子句（限定與非限定用法）
    ...to private schools, where inclusive education is not compulsory, would continue.
    句中的 ", where inclusive education is not compulsory" 是一個非限定性關係副詞子句（where 代指 private schools），前面加上逗號，用來對前面的私立學校做補充說明。這在多益閱讀測驗中是常見的子句修飾考點。
