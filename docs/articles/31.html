雙語 2030：各抒己見的時代已過

Bilingual 2030: time’s up for opinions

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • substantial adj. 重大的、大量的
    While Bilingual 2030 has generated substantial debate through recent opinion pieces and letters to the editor published in the Taipei Times, the time has come to shift from opinion to rigorous academic analysis.
    雖然「雙語 2030」政策透過最近發表在《台北時報》的評論文章和讀者投書引發了大量辯論，但現在是時候從各抒己見轉向嚴謹的學術分析了。
  • attrition n. 人員耗損、流失率
    Smallwood’s cited Saudi and Japanese models also faced massive challenges, high attrition and steep costs, proving that successful EMI relies on factors far beyond methodology, training and funding.
    小伍德所引用的沙烏地阿拉伯和日本模式也面臨了巨大的挑戰、高流失率和高昂成本，這證明了成功的 EMI 依賴於遠超出方法論、培訓和資金的各種因素。
  • fund v. / n. 提供資金、資助 / 資金、基金
    In reality, failure stems from perverse institutional incentives, such as universities rushing to label courses “EMI” simply to secure a slice of government funding.
    實際上，失敗源於扭曲的體制誘因，例如大學急忙將課程貼上「EMI」的標籤，單純只是為了確保能分得一杯羹的政府補助。
  • incentive n. 誘因、鼓勵
    In reality, failure stems from perverse institutional incentives, such as universities rushing to label courses “EMI” simply to secure a slice of government funding.
    實際上，失敗源於扭曲的體制誘因，例如大學急忙將課程貼上「EMI」的標籤，單純只是為了確保能分得一杯羹的政府補助。
  • surplus n. 過剩、盈餘
    Finally, Garrelfs’ assertion that Taiwan possesses a surplus of qualified English teachers is inaccurate.
    最後，加雷爾夫斷言台灣擁有過剩合格英語教師的說法是不準確的。
  • inadequate adj. 不充足的、不適當的
    A 120-hour online Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages certificate is inadequate for institutional teaching...
    120 小時的線上英語教學證書對於體制內教學來說是不足夠的...
  • exceed v. 超過、超出
    ...and I would be surprised if the percentage of teachers on ARCs with a Teaching English as a Foreign Language qualification exceeds 20 percent.
    ...而且如果有工作簽證（ARC）教師中具備對外英語教學（TEFL）資格的比例超過 20%，我會感到非常驚訝。
  • strategy n. 策略、戰略
    Applying a 10 to 15-minute micro-pedagogical speaking tactic to a multimillion-dollar national strategy overlooks the fact that teachers...
    將一個 10 到 15 分鐘的微觀教學口語策略應用於數百萬美元的國家戰略，忽略了一個事實...
  • execution n. 執行、實行
    ...whereas policy execution lacks such standardized reference points.
    ...而政策執行卻缺乏這種標準化的參考點。
  • supplier n. 供應商
    ...unlike the multi-supplier approaches used in Vietnam and Indonesia.
    ...這與越南和印尼所使用的多供應商方法不同。
  • metrics n. 衡量標準、指標
    The Ministry of Education must also overhaul its funding metrics and teacher-training models.
    教育部也必須徹底改革其資助指標和師資培訓模式。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    While Bilingual 2030 has generated substantial debate through recent opinion pieces and letters to the editor published in the Taipei Times, the time has come to shift from opinion to rigorous academic analysis.
    句中的「published in the Taipei Times」是一個過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「letters to the editor」，相當於形容詞子句「which were published in the Taipei Times」，省略了「which were」以達到精簡新聞語氣的效果。
  • 假設語氣用於與現在事實相反的狀況 (Subjunctive Mood)
    ...and I would be surprised if the percentage of teachers on ARCs with a Teaching English as a Foreign Language qualification exceeds 20 percent.
    此句使用了「would + 原形動詞」搭配「if」條件句，表達作者對於某種情況若發生時的假設性主觀反應（即：作者認為實際比例高於 20% 的可能性極低）。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞 (Gerund Phrase as Subject)
    Applying a 10 to 15-minute micro-pedagogical speaking tactic to a multimillion-dollar national strategy overlooks the fact that teachers operate from a shared linguistic knowledge base...
    句首的「Applying a 10 to 15-minute micro-pedagogical speaking tactic to a multimillion-dollar national strategy」是一個動名詞片語，作為整個句子的主詞，視為單數，因此後方接單數動詞「overlooks」。
  • 對比連接詞引導的讓步子句 (Concessive Clause with While)
    While his critique of applied EMI methodology is valid, his example of the economics professor highlights a pedagogical flaw...
    句首的「While」在此處表示「雖然、儘管」，引導一個讓步子句，用來先承認對方部分觀點合理，隨後再用主句轉折帶出真正的批評與核心論點。
