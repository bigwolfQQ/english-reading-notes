雙語 2030：各抒己見的時代已過

Bilingual 2030: time’s up for opinions

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • irony n. 具有諷刺意味的事，反諷
    The irony of writing an opinion piece with this title is not lost on me.
  • substantial adj. 重大的，大量的
    While Bilingual 2030 has generated substantial debate through recent opinion pieces...
  • rigorous adj. 嚴謹的，嚴格的
    ...the time has come to shift from opinion to rigorous academic analysis.
  • grounding n. 基礎知識，訓練
    ...demonstrates sound academic grounding, and I largely agree with his points.
  • false binary n. phr. 虛假二分法
    ...creates a false binary where any real-world application is deemed incorrect by default.
  • translanguaging n. 跨語言交際（教學法術語）
    This risks ignoring essential teaching strategies, such as translanguaging...
  • scaffolding n. 鷹架（教學支援）
    ...and scaffolding (providing temporary learning support to help students grasp complex concepts).
  • pedagogical adj. 教學法的
    ...his example of the economics professor highlights a pedagogical flaw...
  • attrition n. 耗損率，流失率
    Smallwood’s cited Saudi and Japanese models also faced massive challenges, high attrition and steep costs...
  • perverse adj. 走樣的，反常的，度外（指誘因產生負面效果）
    In reality, failure stems from perverse institutional incentives...
  • scrutiny n. 仔細檢視，審查
    ...Paul Garrelfs relies on intuitive notions that fail under academic scrutiny.
  • moot adj. 無討論實益的，無意義的
    His comparison to Ontario’s French immersion program is moot due to fundamental differences...
  • paradoxically adv. 矛盾的是
    Regulations paradoxically prohibit world-renowned experts... from teaching in Taiwan...
  • scale fallacy n. phr. 尺度謬誤
    ...in his article... involves a severe scale fallacy.
  • inertia n. 慣性，僵化
    ...leads directly to institutional inertia, public waste and stakeholder burnout.

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾
    While Bilingual 2030 has generated substantial debate through recent opinion pieces and letters to the editor published in the Taipei Times...
    句中的「published in the Taipei Times」是由過去分詞引導的片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「letters to the editor」，相當於關代子句「which were published in the Taipei Times」，使句子更為精煉。
  • 假設語氣（與現在事實相反）
    ...the problem would exist even in the professor’s first language...
    此處使用「would + 原形動詞」，隱含了一種對假設情況的推論（即：即便教授是用母語教書，這個問題依然存在），表達出強烈的邏輯辯證語氣。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞
    Applying a 10 to 15-minute micro-pedagogical speaking tactic to a multimillion-dollar national strategy overlooks the fact that teachers operate from a shared linguistic knowledge base...
    句首的「Applying a 10 to 15-minute micro-pedagogical speaking tactic...」是由動名詞（Applying）帶領的片語，整個動名詞片語作為主詞，視為單數，因此搭配單數動詞「overlooks」。
