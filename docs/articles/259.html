Ohtani ‘unlikely’ to pitch again this season: Roberts

單字解析

  • manager n. 經理，（球隊）總教練
    Los Angeles Dodgers two-way superstar Shohei Ohtani is unlikely to pitch again this season, team manager Dave Roberts said on Sunday.
    洛杉磯道奇隊的投打二刀流超級巨星大谷翔平本季不太可能再次登板投球，球隊總教練戴夫·羅伯茲於週日表示。
  • consecutive adj. 連續的
    The Japanese has sustained multiple injuries, missing four consecutive games this week for the first time since he joined the Dodgers in 2023.
    這位日本選手遭受了多處傷勢，本週連續缺席四場比賽，這是他自2023年加入道奇隊以來首次發生。
  • experience v. 經歷，體驗
    He has battled inflammation and soreness, and experienced injury setbacks after bullpen throwing sessions.
    他一直在對抗發炎與酸痛，並在牛棚練投階段經歷了傷勢復發。
  • prospect n. 前景，可能性
    However, with just three weeks remaining of the regular season, Roberts downplayed the prospect of Ohtani returning to the mound as a full-fledged starter this year.
    然而，隨著例行賽僅剩三週，羅伯茲淡化了大谷今年以完整先發投手身分重返投手丘的可能性。
  • regular season n. 例行賽
    However, with just three weeks remaining of the regular season, Roberts downplayed the prospect of Ohtani returning to the mound as a full-fledged starter this year.
    然而，隨著例行賽僅剩三週，羅伯茲淡化了大谷今年以完整先發投手身分重返投手丘的可能性。
  • role n. 角色，職責
    Ohtani has played a crucial role as the Dodgers have won back-to-back World Series the past two seasons, but his pitching role has been limited.
    在大奇過去兩個賽季助道奇隊贏得世界大賽冠軍的過程中，大谷扮演了至關重要的角色，但他的投球任務一直受到限制。
  • limited adj. 有限的
    Ohtani has played a crucial role as the Dodgers have won back-to-back World Series the past two seasons, but his pitching role has been limited.
    在大奇過去兩個賽季助道奇隊贏得世界大賽冠軍的過程中，大谷扮演了至關重要的角色，但他的投球任務一直受到限制。
  • target v. 以...為目標
    After a rehab-free off-season, this was meant to be the year Ohtani returned as a full-time two-way player, with the Japanese star targeting the Cy Young Award for outstanding pitching.
    在經歷了一個無需復健的休賽季後，今年本該是大谷以全職二刀流球員身分回歸的一年，這位日本球星當時以爭取表彰優秀投手的賽揚獎為目標。
  • outstanding adj. 傑出的，優秀的
    After a rehab-free off-season, this was meant to be the year Ohtani returned as a full-time two-way player, with the Japanese star targeting the Cy Young Award for outstanding pitching.
    在經歷了一個無需復健的休賽季後，今年本該是大谷以全職二刀流球員身分回歸的一年，這位日本球星當時以爭取表彰優秀投手的賽揚獎為目標。

文法重點

  • be unlikely to + V（不太可能...）
    Los Angeles Dodgers two-way superstar Shohei Ohtani is unlikely to pitch again this season...
    「be unlikely to」是一個在多益考試和商業英文中極常出現的句型，用來表達某事發生的機率不高。主詞通常可以是人也可以是事物。
  • 現在分詞片語作附帶狀況（分詞構句）
    ...missing four consecutive games this week for the first time since he joined the Dodgers in 2023.
    句尾的「missing...」是由對等子句簡化而來的分詞構句，表示主詞「The Japanese（指大谷）」在受傷的同時，也伴隨著「連續缺席四場比賽」的結果，能讓句子更緊湊。
  • with + 受詞 + 形容詞/分詞（附帶狀況）
    However, with just three weeks remaining of the regular season, Roberts downplayed the prospect...
    「with + 名詞 + 現在分詞（remaining）」用來表示附帶的狀況或背景條件，意指「在...的情況下」，在英文新聞寫作與多益閱讀測驗中是極為高頻的句構。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    However, that has been derailed by injuries to his left knee, right biceps and neck.
    句中使用現在完成式的被動語態「has been derailed（已被破壞/阻礙）」，強調動作的承受者（that，指大谷重返二刀流的計畫）受到後方「by injuries」所引發的影響。
