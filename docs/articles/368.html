文法重點

  • 非限定關係代間詞子句 (where 引導)
    Taiwanese manga creators Eli Lin (依萊) and MurJi attended the Kadokawa World Manga Contest awards ceremony in Tokyo on Monday, where they received the gold and bronze prizes, respectively, in the contest's Wordless Manga category.
    句中的 "where" 是一個關係副詞，用來補充說明前面的先行詞 "the Kadokawa World Manga Contest awards ceremony in Tokyo on Monday"（在東京舉行的頒獎典禮）。在逗號後面接 "where + 主詞 + 動詞" 的結構，可以在不中斷句子的情況下，為前方的地點或場合補充更多細節，這是多益閱讀測驗常見的高階句構。
  • 分詞構句作後置修飾 (organized by...)
    The contest, organized by leading Japanese publisher Kadokawa, attracted 1,959 entries from 118 countries and regions.
    句中的 "organized by leading Japanese publisher Kadokawa" 是過去分詞片語，夾在主詞 "The contest" 與動詞 "attracted" 之間，用來修飾主詞。其完整原貌是被動語態的形容詞子句 "which was organized by..."，省略了關代與 be 動詞後形成分詞構句，能使商業與新聞英語更加簡潔流暢。
  • 使役動詞用法 (enable + O. + to V.)
    During her acceptance speech, Lin said support from Taiwan's Ministry of Culture enabled her to transition from an office worker to a professional manga creator just a few years ago.
    句型 "enable + 受詞 + to do sth" 意為「使……能夠……」。在多益測驗中經常出現在文法題（考 to V. 的搭配）以及閱讀測驗裡，用來表達某事（此處為 support「支持」）提供了能力或條件，讓某人去完成某個動作。
  • 現在分詞作表狀態的補語 (feeling exhausted or discouraged)
    She said she hopes to create manga that can be a companion for readers in the same way manga has been for her, bringing comfort and a smile to those feeling exhausted or discouraged.
    句尾的 "feeling exhausted or discouraged" 是由現在分詞 "feeling" 引導的片語，用來修飾前方代間詞 "those"（those who feel... 的簡化）。其中 "exhausted"（感到疲憊的）與 "discouraged"（感到氣餒的）皆為情緒類形容詞，用以描述人的心理狀態。
