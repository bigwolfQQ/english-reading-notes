美國冷凍牛舌因檢出寄生蟲於邊境遭到攔截

U.S. frozen beef tongues blocked at border over parasite detection

單字解析

  • import v. / n. 進口
    Two shipments of frozen beef tongues imported from the United States were rejected at the border...
    兩批從美國進口的冷凍牛舌在邊境遭到退回...
  • reject v. 拒絕、退回、不合格
    Other rejected items included Kanpyo... and frozen coconut jelly from Thailand...
    其他遭到退回的品項包括來自日本的乾瓢……以及泰國的冷凍椰果……
  • importer n. 進口商
    The affected shipments, imported by Shun Sen Co. weighed 179.2 kg and 276.8 kg...
    這些受影響的貨品由順森有限公司進口，重量分別為179.2公斤和276.8公斤...
  • origin n. 起因、源頭、原產地
    ...were ordered either returned to their country of origin or destroyed...
    ...被命令退運回原產國或予以銷毀...
  • inspection n. 檢查、檢驗、視察
    ...the TFDA has placed the importer under a batch-by-batch inspection regime...
    ...TFDA已對該進口商實施批批檢驗制度...
  • require v. 要求、規定
    ...requiring a 100-percent inspection rate for its future imports, Liu said.
    劉表示，這項制度要求其未來的所有進口商品必須達到100%的檢驗率。
  • fail v. 失敗、未能通過（檢驗等）
    The two beef tongue shipments were among 10 imported food products that failed the TFDA's latest border inspections.
    這兩批牛舌是TFDA最新一批邊境檢驗中不合格的10項進口食品之一。
  • excessive adj. 過多的、過度的
    All were ordered returned or destroyed after failing inspections due to excessive pesticide residues...
    所有這些品項在因農藥殘留過量而檢驗不合格後，皆被命令退運或銷毀...
  • statistic n. 統計數據
    According to TFDA statistics, only one out of 1,034 shipments of Vietnamese seasoning sauces...
    根據TFDA統計，1,034批越南調味醬中僅有一批...
  • contamination n. 汙染
    ...failed border inspections due to Sudan dye contamination.
    ...因蘇丹紅染劑汙染而未通過邊境檢驗。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾（形容詞功能）
    Two shipments of frozen beef tongues imported from the United States were rejected at the border...
    句中的 "imported from the United States" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "Two shipments of frozen beef tongues"（兩批冷凍牛舌），相當於關代子句 "which were imported from the United States" 的簡化版，能使句子更精煉。
  • 被動語態的運用
    ...were ordered either returned to their country of origin or destroyed, according to Liu Fang-ming...
    此句使用了被動語態（were ordered... returned... or destroyed），強調貨品是被動接受命令被退回或銷毀，符合新聞報導中客觀陳述官方處置作為的語氣。
  • 現在分詞片語作原因狀語
    ...failing inspections due to excessive pesticide residues or other violations.
    句尾的 "failing inspections due to..." 是現在分詞片語引導的附屬子句概念，說明前面主詞所發出的動作（被命令退運或銷毀）背後的原因，即「因為未能通過檢驗」。
