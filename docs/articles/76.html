高雄 34 人涉嫌非法傾倒營建廢棄物遭起訴

34 indicted over illegal dumping of construction waste in Kaohsiung

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • indict v. 起訴，控告
    Kaohsiung prosecutors have indicted 34 people for allegedly illegally dumping construction waste at multiple farmland sites in Kaohsiung
    高雄檢方已起訴 34 人，他們涉嫌在高雄多處農地非法傾倒營建廢棄物
  • illicit adj. 非法的，違法的
    generating more than NT$62 million (US$1.97 million) in illicit profits.
    獲取超過 6,200 萬元新台幣（約 197 萬美元）的不法利益。
  • contract n. 合約，契約
    signed a contract with a construction company in 2024 to handle the removal and disposal of construction waste
    於 2024 年與一家營建公司簽訂合約，負責處理營建廢棄物的清除與處理
  • handle v. 處理，應付
    to handle the removal and disposal of construction waste generated during the building of a multistory parking facility
    負責處理興建多層立體停車場期間所產生的營建廢棄物之清除與處理
  • take on phr. v. 承接，承擔（工作、責任等）
    In 2025, Huang represented a company that took on a similar contract to handle construction waste from a social housing project
    2025 年，黃男代表一家公司承接了一份類似合約，處理社會住宅工程的營建廢棄物
  • authorization n. 授權，批准（句中為 authorizing 分詞修飾）
    Prosecutors said Huang and others obtained approval documents authorizing them to transport the waste to two legal soil and gravel facilities.
    檢方表示，黃男等人取得了核准文件，授權他們將廢棄物運送到兩家合法的土石方資源掩埋場。
  • instruct v. 指示，命令
    Huang allegedly instructed his staff to tell truck drivers to enter the authorized facilities
    黃男涉嫌指示其員工告訴卡車司機進入獲授權的場地
  • operator n. 營運商，經營者
    The prosecutors also alleged that operators of the two authorized soil and gravel facilities stamped the disposal manifests
    檢方亦指控，兩家合法土石方資源掩埋場的營運業者在清除流向證明文件上蓋章
  • estimate v. 估計，估算
    prosecutors estimated that 4,045 truck trips involving illegal dumping took place over a three-month period
    檢方估計在為期三個月的期間內，發生了 4,045 車次的非法傾倒
  • defendant n. 被告
    They recommended prison sentences of more than four years and 10 months for Huang and other key defendants.
    他們建議對黃男及其他主要被告判處 4 年 10 個月以上的有期徒刑。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Phrase modifying noun / Result clause）表附帶結果
    Huang allegedly instructed his staff to tell truck drivers to enter the authorized facilities, tilt their truck beds and leave the site without unloading the waste, creating the appearance through photographs that the waste had been properly disposed of...
    句尾的「creating...」是由分詞構句所引導的結果狀語，用來補充說明前面一連串動作所造成的結果（即「製造了廢棄物已被妥善處理的假象」）。在商業與新聞英文中非常常見，用以精鍊地表達某個動作所引發的後續影響。
  • 過去分詞作後位修飾語（Past Participle as Post-modifier）
    Based on manifests seized during the investigation, prosecutors estimated that 4,045 truck trips involving illegal dumping took place...
    片語「seized during the investigation」是過去分詞片語，用來後位修飾前面的名詞「manifests（證明文件）」。被動意味濃厚（被查扣的證明文件），省略了「which were」，能讓句子結構更緊湊，多益閱讀測驗常考此類句構。
  • 名詞子句作受詞（Noun Clause as Object）
    The Kaohsiung District Prosecutors' Office released the indictment Thursday, saying the main suspect... signed a contract...
    句中的「saying」後面接了一個完整的子句作其受詞（省略了連接詞 that），用以交代起訴書「所說的內容」。新聞報導中極常使用「saying + 子句」來引述說法或官方文件內容。
