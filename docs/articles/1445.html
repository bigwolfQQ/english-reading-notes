史瓦帝尼國王感謝台灣捐贈直升機

King of Eswatini thanks Taiwan for donated helicopters

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • donation n. 捐贈，捐款
    King Mswati III of Eswatini yesterday thanked Taiwan for its donation of military helicopters to the African kingdom as he toured a flight training center in Tainan accompanied by President William Lai (賴清德).
    史瓦帝尼國王恩史瓦帝三世昨天在總統賴清德的陪同下，參訪位於台南的飛行訓練指揮部，並感謝台灣對這個非洲王國捐贈軍用直升機。
  • primarily adv. 主要地，根本上
    Mswati arrived in Taiwan on Friday last week for his 20th visit to the country since he was crowned king in 1986, primarily to attend the Republic of China National Day celebrations.
    恩史瓦帝於上週五抵達台灣，展開他自1986年加冕為國王以來的第20次訪台行程，主要目的是出席中華民國國慶慶典。
  • personnel n. 人員，員工，人事部門
    Between 2019 and 2020, the Tainan-based command trained 11 Eswatini pilots and maintenance personnel to fly and maintain the helicopters.
    在2019年至2020年間，這個位於台南的指揮部為11名史瓦帝尼飛行員及維修人員進行了飛行與維修直升機的培訓。
  • express v. 表達，表示
    Lai expressed his gratitude to the personnel involved in the mission for their contributions to diplomatic ties between the two countries.
    賴總統對參與該任務的人員表達感謝，感謝他們對兩國外交關係的貢獻。
  • gratitude n. 感謝，謝意
    Lai expressed his gratitude to the personnel involved in the mission for their contributions to diplomatic ties between the two countries.
    賴總統對參與該任務的人員表達感謝，感謝他們對兩國外交關係的貢獻。
  • contribution n. 貢獻
    Lai expressed his gratitude to the personnel involved in the mission for their contributions to diplomatic ties between the two countries.
    賴總統對參與該任務的人員表達感謝，感謝他們對兩國外交關係的貢獻。
  • assist v. 協助，幫忙
    Mswati thanked Taiwan for donating the helicopters and assisting in the training of Eswatini’s pilots.
    恩史瓦帝感謝台灣捐贈直升機並協助培訓史瓦帝尼的飛行員。
  • crucial adj. 決定性的，關鍵性的
    The helicopters, which were formally handed over to the Umbutfo Eswatini Defence Force air wing, have already played a crucial role, including medevac flights to transport casualties in urgent need of advanced medical care, the king said.
    國王表示，這些已正式移交給史瓦帝尼國防軍航空聯隊的直升機，已經發揮了關鍵作用，其中包括執行醫療後送飛行，以運送急需先進醫療照護的傷患。
  • sector n. 部門，行業
    Mswati said that during this visit to Taiwan, he saw the continued development and progress of Taiwan’s aviation sector.
    恩史瓦帝表示，在這次訪台期間，他見證了台灣航空產業的不斷發展與進步。
  • expertise n. 專家意見，專門技術，專業知識
    He said he looked forward to deepening exchanges and sharing expertise and experience between the two countries.
    他表示期待深化兩國之間的交流，並分享專業知識與經驗。
  • itinerary n. 行程，旅行計畫
    The king’s itinerary includes observing a joint ground-air military exercise and a visit to an agricultural research center.
    國王的行程包括觀摩一場海空聯合軍事演習以及參訪農業研究中心。
  • conclude v. 結束，推斷
    Mswati’s Taiwan visit is expected to conclude on Oct. 17.
    恩史瓦帝的訪台行程預計於10月17日結束。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾語（縮寫自形容詞子句）
    King Mswati III of Eswatini yesterday thanked Taiwan for its donation of military helicopters to the African kingdom as he toured a flight training center in Tainan accompanied by President William Lai (賴清德).
    句尾的「accompanied by President William Lai」為過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的主詞 King Mswati III。其還原的完整子句概念為「...as he was accompanied by President William Lai」，省略了主代名詞與be動詞，使句子更簡鍊、符合新聞英語的緊湊風格。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句（使用 which）
    The helicopters, which were formally handed over to the Umbutfo Eswatini Defence Force air wing, have already played a crucial role...
    逗號後面的「which were formally handed over...」是非限定關係子句，對前面的先行詞「The helicopters」提供額外的補充說明。若將此子句拿掉，主要子句的意思依然完整。
  • 對等連接詞連接的對稱結構（While 引導對比或同時發生的動作）
    Both got a chance to operate a military helicopter flight simulator, while the king sat in the cockpit of a Black Hawk helicopter.
    連接詞 「while」在這裡用來表達「而 / 同時」的對比或並列概念，連接兩個完整的子句（Both got a chance... 與 the king sat...），展現多益閱讀中常見的複合句型結構。
  • 現在分詞片語作補充說明
    The king’s itinerary includes observing a joint ground-air military exercise and a visit to an agricultural research center.
    句中的「observing a joint ground-air military exercise」是由動名詞（或現在分詞）所引導的片語，在此處作為 include 的受詞，展現商業與新聞英語中常使用動名詞片語來精簡表達活動項目的用法。
