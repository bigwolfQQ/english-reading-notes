豬腎讓一名男子存活了九個月

Pig kidney keeps man alive for nine months

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • transplant n. / v. 移植（手術）
    A genetically modified kidney from a pig called Wilma has kept a 66-year-old man with kidney failure alive for a record nine months and successfully acted as a “bridge” to a human transplant in a world first.
    一隻名叫威爾瑪的豬身上基因改造過的腎臟，讓一名患有腎衰竭的 66 歲男子創下紀錄存活了九個月，並在全球首例中成功充當了人類移植的「橋樑」。
  • demand n. 需求
    Kidney transplantation provides the best survival and quality of life for patients with end-stage kidney disease, but global demand for human organs far exceeds supply.
    腎臟移植能為末期腎病患者提供最佳的存活率和生活品質，但全球對人類器官的需求遠遠超過供應。
  • exceed v. 超過、超出
    Kidney transplantation provides the best survival and quality of life for patients with end-stage kidney disease, but global demand for human organs far exceeds supply.
    腎臟移植能為末期腎病患者提供最佳的存活率和生活品質，但全球對人類器官的需求遠遠超過供應。
  • temporary adj. 暫時的
    It was also the first time a pig’s kidney had been used as a temporary “bridge” to a human transplant.
    這也是豬腎首次被用作人類移植的臨時「橋樑」。
  • solution n. 解決辦法、解決方案
    Transplanting genetically engineered pig organs has long been proposed as a solution to the organ shortage.
    長期以來，移植基因改造豬器官一直被提出作為解決器官短缺問題的方案。
  • compatible adj. 相容的
    Some of these were designed to dodge viruses that might pose a risk to humans, while others made the organ more compatible with humans, reducing the odds of immune rejection.
    其中一些編輯旨在躲避可能對人類構成風險的病毒，而另一些則使該器官與人類更加相容，降低免疫排斥的機率。
  • treatment n. 治療、療法
    We know that the best treatment option for patients with end-stage kidney disease who have developed renal failure is transplantation, but we simply don’t have enough human organs to transplant in a timely manner.
    我們知道，對於已發展成腎衰竭的末期腎病患者來說，最佳的治療選擇是移植，但我們根本沒有足夠的人口器官以及時進行移植。
  • participate v. 參與、參加
    By stepping forward and being willing to participate in these early stage studies, they are advancing the science.
    透過站出來並願意參與這些早期階段的研究，他們正在推動科學的發展。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    A genetically modified kidney from a pig called Wilma has kept a 66-year-old man with kidney failure alive...
    句中的「called Wilma」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 a pig，相當於形容詞子句「which was called Wilma」的簡化形式。這在英文新聞中非常常見，能讓句子更緊湊。
  • 過去完成被動語態 (Past Perfect Passive Voice)
    It was also the first time a pig’s kidney had been used as a temporary “bridge” to a human transplant.
    句型「It was the first time + 過去完成式（had been p.p.）」用來描述在過去某個時間點之前「從未發生過、而這是第一次」發生的事情。由於豬腎被使用是一個被動動作，因此採用 had been used 的被動語態。
  • 對比連接詞 while 的用法
    Some of these were designed to dodge viruses that might pose a risk to humans, while others made the organ more compatible with humans...
    在此處，while 作為對比連接詞，意思是「而、然而」，用來連接並對比兩種不同的基因編輯目的（一部分是為了躲避病毒，而另一部分是為了增加相容性）。
  • 條件子句與假設語態的隱含 (Conditional Meaning)
    Without their trust and courage, we would not be able to do what we do.
    「Without + 名詞」在此等於「If it were not for...」（如果沒有……），帶有假設語態的意味，主句則使用「would + 動詞原形」來表達如果沒有這些患者，現在就無法進行這些醫療行為的假設結果。
