全台單間獨立戶攀升至40.5%

Single-person households rise to 40.5% nationwide

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • account for phr. v. 占（比例）；說明、解釋
    The number of single-person households nationwide reached about 3,993,100 last year, accounting for 40.5 percent of all registered households, Ministry of the Interior data showed.
    內政部數據顯示，去年全國單人戶數量達到約399萬3,100戶，占所有登記戶數的40.5%。
  • exceed v. 超過、勝過
    Single-person households have become the dominant structure in Taiwan, exceeding the combined share of two-person and three-person households for the first time, the data showed.
    數據顯示，單人戶已成為台灣的主流家戶結構，首次超越兩人戶和三人戶的合併占比。
  • statistic n. 統計數據
    There were 8.933 million households nationwide in 2020, of which single-person households accounted for about 34 percent, two-person households about 20.5 percent and three-person households about 18.1 percent, ministry statistics showed.
    部會統計顯示，2020年全國有893萬3,000戶，其中單人戶約占34%，兩人戶約占20.5%，三人戶約占18.1%。
  • substantial adj. 重大的、大量的、實質的
    However, the total number of households nationwide increased to about 9.85 million last year, marking a substantial growth of about 917,000 households in five years.
    然而，去年全國總戶數增加至約985萬戶，標誌著五年內大幅成長了約91萬7,000戶。
  • surge v. / n. 急遽上升、激增
    During the same period, the number of single-person households surged by 951,100, significantly exceeding the growth in total households over the same time span.
    在此期間，單人戶數量激增了95萬1,100戶，顯著超過了同期總戶數的成長。
  • proportion n. 比例、部分
    The proportion of single-person households rose sharply to 40.5 percent last year, exceeding the combined share of two-person and three-person households at 38.19 percent, the data showed.
    數據顯示，去年單人戶的比例急劇上升至40.5%，超過了兩人戶和三人戶合計的38.19%。
  • outpace v. 跑得比...快、超越、勝過
    The growth rate of single-person households is significantly outpacing the number of marriages and new housing unit handovers each year, he said.
    他說，單人戶的成長速度明顯超過了每年的結婚對數和新屋交屋數。
  • policy n. 政策、方針
    Aside from home purchases by single people, a new house tax policy that took effect in 2024 might have been another factor, Tseng said.
    曾敬德表示，除了單身者的購屋之外，2024年生效的一項新房屋稅政策可能是另一個因素。
  • take effect phr. 生效、實施
    Aside from home purchases by single people, a new house tax policy that took effect in 2024 might have been another factor, Tseng said.
    曾敬德表示，除了單身者的購屋之外，2024年生效的一項新房屋稅政策可能是另一個因素。
  • qualify for phr. 取得...的資格、符合...
    To qualify for preferential taxes for self-occupied homes, owners must complete household registration for the property, Tseng said.
    曾敬德表示，為了符合自住房屋的優惠稅率，業主必須完成該不動產的戶籍登記。
  • preferential adj. 優先的、優惠的
    To qualify for preferential taxes for self-occupied homes, owners must complete household registration for the property, Tseng said.
    曾敬德表示，為了符合自住房屋的優惠稅率，業主必須完成該不動產的戶籍登記。
  • inflate v. 使膨脹、誇大、高估
    Given that taxes on newer properties are higher, the policy has prompted a wave of household registrations, so the number of single-person households between 2023 and last year might be inflated, he added.
    他補充說，鑑於新屋的稅金較高，該政策引發了一波戶籍登記潮，因此2023年至去年之間的單人戶數量可能會被高估。

文法重點

  • 分詞作為後置定語 (Participle as Post-modifier)
    The growth rate of single-person households is significantly outpacing the number of marriages and new housing unit handovers each year, he said.
    句中的 "completed"（完工的）是由過去分詞轉化而來的形容詞，放在名詞後面修飾 "new housing units"，相當於形容詞子句 "that are completed" 的簡化用法，在新聞英語中非常常見。
  • 關係代名詞所有格 (Whose / Of Which)
    There were 8.933 million households nationwide in 2020, of which single-person households accounted for about 34 percent... ministry statistics showed.
    句中的 "of which" 用來引導非限定關係子句，代替前一句提及的 893 萬戶，用以補充說明其中的細項比例，展現正式且精準的書面語法結構。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Absolute Participial Phrase)
    Given that taxes on newer properties are higher, the policy has prompted a wave of household registrations...
    "Given that..."（鑑於... / 考慮到...）是一個常見的慣用語氣分詞結構（源自 give 的分詞用法），在多益閱讀與商業英文中經常用來引入條件、前提或考量因素。
