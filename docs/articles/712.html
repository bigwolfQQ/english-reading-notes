南韓旨在拯救數位時代中迷失的老年人

S Korea aims to save older people lost in digital age

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • session n. 培訓期、會議、場次
    after she managed to place an online order at a training session in South Korea
    在她於南韓的一場培訓課程中成功線上訂餐後
  • campaign n. 活動、運動
    prompting national campaigns to bridge it.
    促使國家發起各項全國性運動來弭平差距。
  • commercial adj. 商業的
    struggle to use public and commercial services due to lack of digital skills
    因為缺乏數位技能而難以使用公共及商業服務
  • access n. 管道、接近或使用的權利
    or limiting their access to medical services, experts say.
    專家表示，或是限制他們取得醫療服務管道。
  • survey n. 調查
    A South Korean state survey last year found that 60 percent of South Koreans
    南韓國家機構去年的調查發現，60%的南韓人
  • economy n. 經濟體、經濟
    is now the world’s 15th largest economy, according to the IMF.
    根據國際貨幣基金組織（IMF）的數據，如今已成為全球第15大經濟體。
  • rely on phr. v. 依賴、依靠
    she has made it this far by relying on her children for daily tasks
    她是靠著依賴孩子來完成日常任務而撐到現在的
  • in person phr. 親自、本人
    Before, I could easily do all of these just by calling or showing up in person
    以前，我只要打個電話或親自出現，就能輕輕鬆鬆搞定所有這些事
  • provide v. 提供
    Officials now provide nationwide training for the “digitally vulnerable”
    官員們現在為「數位弱勢族群」提供全國性的培訓
  • confidence n. 信心、自信
    said the lessons helped her confidence
    並表示這些課程提升了她的信心

文法重點

  • 非限定性關係代間子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    A little cheer rose for 79-year-old Hwang Sun-hee after she managed to place an online order at a training session in South Korea, which has one of the world’s starkest digital divides for older people.
    句中的「which has...」前面帶有逗號，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「South Korea」，提供額外的背景資訊，翻譯時可順著語意譯為「其中……」或直接接續翻譯。
  • 現在分詞作補充說明/結果 (Present Participle as Result/Adverbial)
    Age gaps in digital literacy are a global concern, yet the gulf has widened massively in the hyper-connected and fast-aging country, prompting national campaigns to bridge it.
    句尾的「prompting national campaigns...」是由現在分詞（prompting）引導的分詞構句，在此處表示前句狀況所導致的自然結果（促使了……）。
  • 過去分詞作後位修飾 (Past Participle as Post-modifier)
    Vans packed with tech devices and mock kiosks drive to community centers and regional festivals popular among older people.
    片語「packed with tech devices and mock kiosks」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「Vans」，相當於形容詞子句「which are packed with...」，精簡了句構。
  • 與過去事實相反的假設語氣 (Subjunctive Past Perfect)
    “I wish I had been born young in today’s world,” she said.
    句型「I wish + 過去完成式 (had been)」用來表達對過去已經發生或無法改變的事實感到遺憾或期望相反（我真希望當初自己能……），在多益考試中常出現在文法題的動詞時態選擇題。
