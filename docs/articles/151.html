賴總統頒褒揚令悼念已故前立法院副院長饒穎奇

President Lai honors late former Deputy Legislative Speaker Yao Eng-chi

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • posthumous adj. 死后的，身后的
    President Lai Ching-te on Sunday presented a posthumous presidential citation honoring former Deputy Legislative Speaker Yao Eng-chi
    總統賴清德週日頒發總統褒揚令，悼念已故前立法院副院長饒穎奇
  • citation n. 褒揚令，引用，傳票
    President Lai Ching-te on Sunday presented a posthumous presidential citation honoring former Deputy Legislative Speaker Yao Eng-chi
    總統賴清德週日頒發總統褒揚令，悼念已故前立法院副院長饒穎奇
  • commendation n. 稱讚，表揚，褒揚令
    Lai presented the commendation to Yao's family at a memorial service in Taitung City
    賴總統在台東市的一場追悼會上將褒揚令頒發給饒穎奇的家屬
  • contribution n. 貢獻
    praising the veteran politician's decades of public service and contributions to Taiwan's democratic development
    讚揚這位資深政治人物數十年來的公職服務以及對台灣民主發展的貢獻
  • dispute n. 爭端，糾紛
    describing him as 'an important stabilizing force' during a period of political change who helped 'resolve disputes and bridge differences.'
    形容他在政局變遷期間是「一股重要的穩定力量」，有助於「化解爭端、彌合分歧」。
  • administration n. 管理，行政，（政府的）執政期間，內閣
    He later served as a senior adviser to the president from 2009 to 2016 under the Ma Ying-jeou administration.
    他隨後在2009年至2016年馬英九總統執政期間擔任總統府資政。
  • incumbent adj. 現任的
    Yao was also the father of incumbent Taitung County Magistrate Yao Ching-ling
    饒穎奇也是現任台東縣長、國民黨政治人物饒慶鈴的父親。
  • dedication n. 奉獻，投入
    saying it recognized her father's lifelong dedication and contributions.
    並表示這肯定了她父親一生的奉獻與貢獻。
  • career n. 職業生涯，事業
    she paid tribute to her father, recalling his influence on her political career and lifelong dedication to Taitung.
    她在另一篇臉書貼文中向父親致敬，回憶父親對她政治生涯的影響以及對台東的一生奉獻。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾
    President Lai Ching-te (賴清德) on Sunday presented a posthumous presidential citation honoring former Deputy Legislative Speaker Yao Eng-chi (饒穎奇), who died last month at the age of 93.
    句中的「honoring...」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 citation（褒揚令），補充說明該褒揚令的目的與對象。這種結構在新聞英語中非常常見，能簡潔地串聯額外資訊。
  • 非限定關代子句（who引導）
    Yao Eng-chi died at his home in Taitung's Beinan Township on Aug. 21, with family members by his side.
    句中的「with family members by his side」是由「with + 名詞 + 介系詞片語」構成的獨立複合結構（Absolute Construction），用來補充說明主句動作發生時的伴隨狀況（在家中去世時，家屬隨侍在側）。
  • 對等連接詞與並列結構
    Lai, who had served alongside Yao in the Legislature for two terms, also praised him for his contributions to Taitung County's development, saying Yao had played a role in many of the county's major transportation, education, and public infrastructure projects.
    句尾使用了「transportation, education, and public infrastructure projects」的並列名詞組合，並在最後一個項目加上 and。此外，句中也夾雜了關係代名詞子句（who had served...）與分詞構句（saying...）來豐富句子層次。
