  • alternative adj. 替代的，另類的

  • initiative n. 新措施，倡議

文法重點

  • 非限定關係子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    The PanStar Acro, which embarked on a trial voyage along the Arctic shipping route on August 22, docked at Jaseongdae Pier in Busan Port,...
    句中的「which embarked on...」用逗號隔開，作為非限定關係子句，用來補充說明主詞「The PanStar Acro」的額外資訊，如果拿掉這個子句，主句的意思依然完整。
  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況/結果 (Participial Phrase)
    The Northern Sea Route is generally more accessible during the warmer months, when reduced sea ice makes navigation easier, although ice conditions remain unpredictable and some vessels still require icebreaker assistance.
  • 過去分詞作後位修飾 (Past Participle as Post-modifier)
    The Middle East conflict, sparked by US-Israeli strikes on Iran in February, has roiled global shipping...
    片語「sparked by US-Israeli strikes on Iran in February」是過去分詞片語，放在名詞「The Middle East conflict」後面修飾它，相當於完整的形容詞子句「which was sparked by...」，用來精簡語意。
