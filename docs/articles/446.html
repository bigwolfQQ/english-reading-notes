高院判決兩人發展與中共相關組織罪名成立

High court sentences two for developing CCP-linked organization

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • sentence v. / n. 宣判、判刑；判決
    The Taiwan High Court on Thursday sentenced Taiwanese businessman Cheng Ming-chia (鄭明嘉) to 10 years and six months in prison for developing an organization for the Chinese Communist Party's (CCP) united front authorities.
    台灣高等法院於週四依為中國共產黨統一戰線工作部發展組織罪名，判處台灣商人鄭明嘉有期徒刑10年6個月。
  • attempt v. / n. 企圖、嘗試
    Retired Executive Yuan official Hu Peng-nien (胡鵬年), meanwhile, was sentenced to five years for attempting to do the same.
    退休行政院官員胡鵬年則因企圖從事相同行為，被判處有期徒刑5年。
  • appeal v. / n. 上訴
    The ruling can be appealed.
    全案仍可上訴。
  • approach v. 接近、向……接洽
    According to the court, from 2021 to 2025 Cheng helped CCP united front officials approach and attempt to recruit targets in Taiwan by providing contact information and arranging meetings in China.
    根據法院指出，自2021年至2025年間，鄭男協助中共統戰官員接觸並企圖在台灣招募目標，提供聯繫方式並安排在中國的會面。
  • recruit v. / n. 招募、吸收（成員）；新兵、新進人員
    He successfully recruited Hu as a potential operative, while his efforts to recruit another individual surnamed Huang (黃) failed after Huang did not respond.
    他成功將胡男招募為潛在特務，而他試圖招募另一名黃姓人士的企圖則因黃男未予回應而告失敗。
  • convict v. 宣告有罪、定罪
    None responded, leading the court to convict him of attempted development of an organization and hand down a sentence of five years in prison.
    由於均未獲回應，法院依未遂發展組織罪判處其有期徒刑5年。
  • evidence n. 證據
    It found the evidence against Cheng clear and rejected his defense.
    法院認定對鄭男不利的證據明確，並駁回其辯詞。
  • instruct v. 指示、指導
    Both defendants, the court said, followed instructions from CCP united front personnel to facilitate the recruitment of new targets.
    法院指出，兩名被告皆聽從中共統戰人員的指示，以利招募新目標。
  • transport v. 運輸、運送
    Hu also transported a communications device supplied by the Chinese authorities into Taiwan and handed it to another person...
    胡男還將中國官方提供的一部通訊設備偷運回台灣並交給其他人……
  • indictment n. 起訴書
    According to a January indictment by the Taiwan High Prosecutors' Office...
    根據台灣高等檢察署今年1月起訴書……
  • target n. / v. 目標；將……作為攻擊或招募目標
    Prosecutors said Hu targeted current and retired military personnel, political workers, ward chiefs, retired civil servants and defense contractors...
    檢方表示，胡男將現退役軍人、政治工作者、里長、退休公務員及國防承包商作為目標……
  • detention n. 拘留、扣留
    Cheng and Hu have remained in detention since their arrest following raids launched in September 2025...
    鄭明嘉與胡鵬年自2025年9月發動搜索遭逮捕後一直被羈押……

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作後置修飾與伴隨狀況
    According to the court, from 2021 to 2025 Cheng helped CCP united front officials approach and attempt to recruit targets in Taiwan by providing contact information and arranging meetings in China.
    句中的「by providing contact information and arranging meetings in China」使用介系詞 by 搭配動名詞片語，用來表達「透過……方式」達成前面的動作。此外，文章中也常運用分詞片語來補充說明主句動作發生的方式或結果。
  • 分詞構句表結果
    None responded, leading the court to convict him of attempted development of an organization and hand down a sentence of five years in prison.
    句尾的「leading the court to...」是由對等子句簡化而來的分詞構句，用來接續前面子句所引發的自然結果（沒有人回應，『因而導致』法院判他有罪）。這種結構在新聞英語中非常常見，能讓句型更緊湊。
  • 過去分詞作後置修飾語
    Hu also transported a communications device supplied by the Chinese authorities into Taiwan and handed it to another person...
    句中的「supplied by the Chinese authorities」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「a communications device」（通訊設備）。原貌為「which was supplied by the Chinese authorities」，關係代名詞與be動詞省略後，直接由過去分詞帶出被動修飾意味。
  • 現在分詞引導的時間與條件附屬子句簡化
    According to a January indictment by the Taiwan High Prosecutors' Office, Cheng recruited Hu -- then teaching in China -- after becoming involved with CCP-affiliated united front organizations in Guangzhou...
    句中夾敘的「-- then teaching in China --」省略了主詞與be動詞（原為 who was then teaching in China），用來補充說明當時胡男的身分與狀態；而「after becoming involved with...」則是連接詞接動名詞，表示在『涉及……之後』。
