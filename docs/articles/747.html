文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    A woman who appeared to be about 30 years old asked him to take a photograph of her and the two began talking, during which he noticed she had a northern Chinese accent, Kremb said.
    句中的「who appeared to be about 30 years old」是關係代名詞子句，用來修飾前面的名詞「A woman」。在英文新聞與商業寫作中，常用這種結構來補充說明某個特定人物或事物的特徵。
  • 被動語態的進行式 (Present Continuous Passive Voice)
    The infiltration of Taiwan is not a local problem, but a “global warning sign,” it said.
    句中動詞結構雖然在此處為簡單式，但在文章其他部分（如第19段的 are already being used）常出現「be + being + p.p.」的現在進行式被動語態，用來強調某個動作目前正在被執行。
  • 不定詞片語作受詞補語 (Infinitive Phrase as Object Complement)
    Becoming fearful of them would play directly into China’s hands, helping Beijing to instill fear in people, he added.
    句型「help + 受詞 + (to) + 原形動詞」表示「幫助某人做某事」。在此句中，「Beijing」是 help 的受詞，「to instill fear in people」是不定詞片語作為受詞補語，說明北京被協助做了什麼事。
  • 過去分詞作形容詞修飾名詞 (Past Participle as Adjective)
    The report was published in June in the Ministry of Justice Investigation Bureau’s Prospect & Exploration Monthly...
