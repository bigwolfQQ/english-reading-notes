單字解析

  • departure n. 出發，離開
    The departure from Moldova of a plane carrying Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskiy to Norway was delayed...
    載有烏克蘭總統澤倫斯基前往挪威的飛機自摩爾達瓦出發的時間遭到延誤……
  • carry v. 運送，載運
    The departure from Moldova of a plane carrying Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskiy to Norway was delayed...
    載有烏克蘭總統澤倫斯基前往挪威的飛機自摩爾達瓦出發的時間遭到延誤……
  • delay v. / n. 延誤，耽擱
    ...was delayed when authorities closed the country’s airspace over a drone alert...
    ……當當局因無人機警報而關閉該國領空時遭到延誤……
  • authority n. 官方，當局
    ...when authorities closed the country’s airspace over a drone alert, a Moldovan government spokesperson said on Wednesday.
    摩爾達瓦政府發言人於週三表示，當局因無人機警報而關閉了該國領空。
  • spokesperson n. 發言人
    ...a Moldovan government spokesperson said on Wednesday.
    摩爾達瓦政府發言人於週三表示。
  • statement n. 聲明，陳述
    ...the spokesperson said in a written statement to Reuters.
    發言人在給路透社的書面聲明中表示。
  • establish v. 確認，確立，查明
    ...once it had been established that a Russian drone had come down on Moldovan territory...
    ……一旦確認有一架俄羅斯無人機墜毀在摩爾達瓦領土上之後……
  • territory n. 領土，地盤
    ...that a Russian drone had come down on Moldovan territory...
    ……有一架俄羅斯無人機墜毀在摩爾達瓦領土上……
  • official n. 官員
    Two Russian drones entered Moldovan airspace, officials said on Tuesday.
    官員於週二表示，兩架俄羅斯無人機進入了摩爾達瓦領空。
  • threat n. 威脅
    Moldovan President Maia Sandu denounced the drone intrusions as a “serious threat to people’s lives”...

  • condemn v. 譴責
    ...and condemned a Russian drone attack on a border crossing with Ukraine that killed two people.
    ……並譴責俄羅斯對烏克蘭邊境口岸發動的無人機攻擊，該次攻擊造成兩人死亡。
  • source n. 來源，消息來源
    ...who did not say what his source was for the information or how close the drone came.
    ……他並未說明該消息的來源為何，也未透露無人機接近到什麼程度。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾語 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The departure from Moldova of a plane carrying Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskiy to Norway was delayed...
    句中的 "carrying Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskiy to Norway" 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "a plane"，相當於形容詞子句 "that carried..." 或 "which carried..."，能讓句子更簡潔。
  • 被動語態的過去完成式 (Past Perfect Passive)
    ...once it had been established that a Russian drone had come down on Moldovan territory...
    句中的 "had been established" 採用了被動語態的過去完成式 (had been + 過去分詞)，用來表示在過去某一時間點（專機獲准起飛）之前，確認無人機墜毀這件事已經被完成。
  • 過去進行式表示過去某時正在發生的動作 (Past Continuous Tense)
    Zelenskiy was traveling to Oslo for the funeral of the king of Norway.
    句型使用 "was traveling"（be動詞過去式 + V-ing），用來描寫在過去某個特定時間段中，正在進行或持續的行程或動作。
  • 表狀態的「即將/正要」片語 (Be about to)
    “His plane was almost hit by a drone as it was about to take off from Moldova.
    片語 "be about to" 用來表示某個動作「正要」、「即將」發生。在此句中結合過去式為 "was about to take off"（正要起飛）。
