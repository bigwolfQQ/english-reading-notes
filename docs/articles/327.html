英國重大系統大當機後，航空交通主管遭召見

UK summons air traffic boss after major outage

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • resume v. 恢復、重新開始
    Flights from British airports resumed yesterday after a major air traffic failure
    在經歷一次重大的空中交通故障後，英國各機場的航班已於昨天恢復
  • reliability n. 可靠性、可信賴性
    questions mounted about the reliability of the UK’s aviation systems
    各界對英國航空系統可靠性的質疑聲浪不斷升高
  • outage n. 斷電、系統中斷、停擺
    The hours-long outage on Tuesday
    週二長達數小時的系統故障
  • cancelation n. 取消
    which resulted in the cancelation of more than 1,000 flights
    這導致1,000多個航班被取消
  • stranded adj. 滯留的、受困的
    left hundreds of thousands of passengers stranded
    讓數十萬名旅客滯留
  • provider n. 供應商、提供者
    air traffic control provider National Air Traffic Services
    空中交通管制服務提供商國家航空交通服務公司
  • infrastructure n. 基礎設施
    amid concerns that the infrastructure and technology are not up to scratch
    在此之前，各界擔憂其基礎設施和技術並不達標
  • disrupt v. 打亂、中斷
    A radar-related technical issue also disrupted flights in July last year
    去年7月發生的雷達相關技術問題也打亂了航班
  • resign v. 辭職
    has been calling for Rolfe to resign since 2023
    自2023年以來一直呼籲羅爾夫辭職
  • apologize v. 道歉
    Rolfe, who apologized to those affected by the issue
    羅爾夫向受此問題影響的人道歉
  • operations n. 營運、運作
    As operations restarted, Heathrow said passengers should check with their airline
    隨著營運重啟，希斯洛機場表示旅客應向航空公司確認
  • impact n. 影響、衝擊
    We are expecting knock-on impacts as aircraft and crew reposition
    我們預期會產生連鎖效應，因為飛機和機組人員需要重新定位

文法重點

  • 非限定關係代間詞子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    The hours-long outage on Tuesday, which resulted in the cancelation of more than 1,000 flights and left hundreds of thousands of passengers stranded, has ramped up pressure...
    句中的「which resulted in...」前面有逗號，屬於非限定用法，用來補充說明前面的主詞「The hours-long outage on Tuesday」，不能省略，且不能用 that 代替。
  • 分詞構句作形容詞修飾 (Participle as Modifier)
    British Secretary for Transport Heidi Alexander summoned Rolfe to explain what happened, amid concerns that the infrastructure and technology are not up to scratch.
    句尾的「concerned that...」被簡化為介系詞片語「amid concerns that...」，用來表達「在……擔憂之中」，其中「that」引導同位語子句，說明 concerns 的具體內容。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    Rolfe, who apologized to those affected by the issue, denied that the UK was worse than other countries...
    片語「affected by the issue」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的代名詞「those」（指Those people），等於「those who were affected by the issue」，省略了主格關代與be動詞，使句子更簡鍊。
