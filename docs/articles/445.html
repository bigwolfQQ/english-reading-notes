Two-story building damaged by adjacent construction work in Taipei

文法重點

  • 時間副詞子句 (when引導)
    A two-story building in Taipei sustained severe damage on Thursday when adjacent construction work caused its ground floor to crack and leak yellowish muddy water...
    “when”在此處作為連接詞，引導一個時間副詞子句，用來補充說明主句動作發生時的背景狀況（即建築物受損的時間點，正值鄰近施工導致地基龜裂漏水時）。
  • 現在分詞作形容詞/補充說明 (resulted in)
    ...causing water and sand leakage, resulting in soil loss in the adjacent area, according to the Construction Management Office.
    句中的 “resulting in” 為現在分詞片語作結果狀語，用來表達前面「水砂滲漏」這件事所直接導致的結果（造成鄰近地區土壤流失），在多益商業閱讀與新聞英語中非常常見。
  • 被動語態 (has been / will not resume until...)
    The Construction Management Office said construction work has been suspended due to failure to maintain the safety of adjacent public facilities and buildings, and work will not resume until an improvement plan has been submitted and approved.
    此句包含兩個重要的被動語態結構。「has been suspended」強調施工「被」主管機關勒令暫停的狀態；後半段的「has been submitted and approved」則表示改善計畫「被」提交與批准。被動語態在多益公文、新聞及商業書信中是用來客觀陳述事實或政策的高頻句型。
