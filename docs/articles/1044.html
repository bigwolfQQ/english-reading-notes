委內瑞拉男子遭移民局執法人員槍擊，體內留有子彈就被送進監獄

Man shot by ICE, sent to jail with bullet inside him

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • agent n. 代理人、探員、經紀人
    A Venezuelan national shot and wounded by a US immigration agent in Texas was jailed with the bullet still inside his body, his lawyer said on Monday.
    他的律師於週一表示，一名在德州被美國移民局探員開槍打傷的委內瑞拉國民，在身體裡還卡著子彈的情況下就被關進了監獄。
  • detention center n. phr. 拘留中心、收容所
    She told a news conference that he was transferred from a hospital to a detention center in Pearsall, about 215km southwest of Austin.
    她在新聞發布會上表示，他被從醫院轉送到位於皮爾沙爾的一處拘留中心，該中心位於奧斯汀西南方約 215 公里處。
  • transfer v. 轉移、調動、轉學
    “He was shot here in his back, and they didn’t take the bullet out, and they didn’t give him pain medication, and they transferred him to ICE, to a processing center where he spent the night without a bed,” Lincoln-Goldfinch said.
    林肯-戈德芬奇說：「他背部中槍，而他們沒有取出子彈，也沒有給他止痛藥，就把他轉移給 ICE，送到一個連床都沒有、讓他在那裡過夜的處理中心。」
  • medication n. 藥物、藥劑
    “He was shot here in his back, and they didn’t take the bullet out, and they didn’t give him pain medication, and they transferred him to ICE...
    「他背部中槍，而他們沒有取出子彈，也沒有給他止痛藥，就把他轉移給 ICE...」
  • demand v. 強烈要求、索取
    Then a black pickup cut him off, and an agent got out and demanded his documents, Garces Perez said.
    加雷斯·佩雷斯表示，隨後一輛黑色皮卡車切入擋住他的去路，一名探員下車並要求他出示證件。
  • refuse v. 拒絕
    Garces Perez asked to be allowed to pull over from the middle of the road, but agents refused.
    加雷斯·佩雷斯要求允許他將車從路中間開到路邊停好，但探員拒絕了。
  • chase v. 追逐、追趕
    “I made a U-turn and that’s when he started chasing me, thinking I was going to run away, but I was looking for a place to leave my car safely,” Garces Perez said.
    加雷斯·佩雷斯說：「我做了一個迴轉，那就是他開始追我、以為我要逃跑的時候，但我當時是在找一個地方安全地停放我的車。」
  • justification n. 正當理由、辯解
    “There is absolutely zero justification for having shot him,” Lincoln-Goldfinch said.
    林肯-戈德芬奇說：「向他開槍絕對是毫無正當理由的。」
  • discharge v. 允許離開、釋放、使出院
    “A doctor comes in and tells me: ‘no,’ because they were in a hurry to discharge me and have me taken” to the detention center, Garces Perez said.
    加雷斯·佩雷斯說：「一個醫生走進來對我說：『不行（不能做手術）』，因為他們急著把我辦理出院，並把我帶到拘留中心。」
  • adequate adj. 足夠的、適當的
    He maintained he did not receive adequate medical care at the hospital.
    他堅稱自己在醫院沒有得到適當的醫療照顧。
  • ongoing adj. 進行中的、持續的
    On Monday, a work crew painted over expletive-laced graffiti nearby, which expresses local outrage about ICE and the ongoing immigration crackdown.
    週一，一個工作班底將附近的粗口塗鴉漆掉，這些塗鴉表達了當地居民對 ICE 以及持續進行的移民打擊行動的憤怒。
  • figure n. 數字、數據、人物
    About 51,000 undocumented migrants were arrested in the US last month, according to the department’s figures.
    根據該部門的數據，上個月美國約有 51,000 名無證移民被捕。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾語 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    A Venezuelan national shot and wounded by a US immigration agent in Texas was jailed with the bullet still inside his body...
    句中的「shot and wounded by a US immigration agent in Texas」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「A Venezuelan national」（一個委內瑞拉國民），相當於關代子句「who was shot and wounded...」的簡化，能讓句子更為精鍊。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Absolute Nominative Construction)
    ...was jailed with the bullet still inside his body...
    「with + 名詞 (the bullet) + 形容詞片語 (still inside his body)」形成獨立分詞構句，用來補充說明主句動作發生時伴隨的狀況或背景，常譯為「帶著……」或「在……的情況下」。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    ...he was transferred from a hospital to a detention center in Pearsall, about 215km southwest of Austin.
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    Homeland Security Investigations, which is a part of ICE, is leading the investigation into the shooting...
    句中的「which is a part of ICE」是非限定性關係代名詞子句，前面加上逗號，用來補充說明前面的主詞「Homeland Security Investigations」的額外資訊。
