伊朗尋求解除對葉門的封鎖

Iran seeks end to blockade of Yemen

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • call for phr. 呼籲、要求
    The Iranian government yesterday called for an end to Saudi Arabia’s blockade of Yemen and the immediate resumption of talks...
    伊朗政府昨天呼籲結束沙烏地阿拉伯對葉門的封鎖並立即恢復談判...
  • resumption n. 恢復、重新開始
    ...and the immediate resumption of talks, in its first public statement since Iran-backed Houthi rebels entered a key Yemeni port...
    ...並立即恢復談判，這是自伊朗支持的胡塞反抗軍進入葉門主要港口...
  • blockade n. 封鎖
    In a statement carried by the state-run Islamic Republic News Agency, the Iranian Ministry of Foreign Affairs said that Yemen’s sovereignty must be respected and its problems cannot be resolved through a blockade or military alliances.
    在由國營伊斯蘭共和國通訊社發布的聲明中，伊朗外交部表示，必須尊重葉門的主權，其問題無法透過封鎖或軍事同盟來解決。
  • resolve v. 解決、消除
    ...and its problems cannot be resolved through a blockade or military alliances.
    ...其問題無法透過封鎖或軍事同盟來解決。
  • negotiation n. 談判、協商
    Iran said it was ready to help support negotiations.
    伊朗表示已準備好協助支持談判。
  • leverage n. 籌碼、影響力
    Meanwhile, experts say the developments in Yemen could give Tehran more leverage in any negotiations with the US after more than six months of war.
    同時，專家表示，葉門的局勢發展可能會在長達半年多的戰爭後，為德黑蘭在與美國的任何談判中提供更多籌碼。
  • alternative n. 替代方案、替代品
    ...an important alternative for global shipping as Iran targets vessels on the Strait of Hormuz.
    ...隨著伊朗將荷姆茲海峽的船隻作為攻擊目標，曼德海峽成為全球航運的重要替代路線。
  • vessel n. 船隻、艦艇
    ...as Iran targets vessels on the Strait of Hormuz.
    ...隨著伊朗將荷姆茲海峽的船隻作為攻擊目標。
  • ally n. 盟友、同盟國
    The Bab el-Mandeb at the southern end of the Red Sea is especially important to neighboring Saudi Arabia, a key US ally...
    位於紅海南端的曼德海峽對鄰國、美國的關鍵盟友沙烏地阿拉伯尤為重要...
  • defend v. 防衛、保護
    ...also has said it has the right to defend itself in response to the Houthis’ actions under the blockade they declared on the kingdom in July.
    ...亦表示有權根據胡塞反抗軍在 7 月對該王國宣布封鎖的行動進行自我防衛。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾
    In a statement carried by the state-run Islamic Republic News Agency, the Iranian Ministry of Foreign Affairs said that Yemen’s sovereignty must be respected...
    句中的「carried by the state-run Islamic Republic News Agency」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 statement（聲明），相當於關代子句「which was carried by...」，省略了關代與be動詞，使句子更為精簡。
  • 關係代名詞子句（限定用法）
    The sharply escalating fighting threatens to end a fragile truce that had largely halted Yemen’s civil war since 2022.
    句中的「that had largely halted Yemen’s civil war since 2022」是一個關係代名詞子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞 truce（休戰協議），說明這個協議過去所發揮的作用。
  • 過去分詞作形容詞修飾名詞
    ...bringing many others to the brink of famine.
    句中的 recognized（獲...承認的）、escalating（不斷升級的）、famine 等字詞常以分詞形態作為形容詞使用，用來修飾後方或前方的名詞，是多益閱讀測驗常見的詞性變化結構。
