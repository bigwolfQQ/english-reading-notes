DeepSeek 引發對需求疑慮，測試南韓記憶體大廠股價

DeepSeek tests South Korean memory makers’ stocks with demand doubts

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • demand n. 需求
    DeepSeek’s latest artificial intelligence (AI) model raised doubts on the strength of demand.
    DeepSeek 最新的人工智慧（AI）模型引發對需求強度的質疑。
  • respectively adv. 分別地
    SK Hynix Inc and Samsung Electronics Co shares dipped 2.2 and 3.5 percent respectively yesterday, paring their nascent rebound from July’s steep selloff.
    SK海力士公司與三星電子公司的股價昨日分別下跌 2.2% 與 3.5%，削減了他們自 7 月份急劇拋售潮以來的初現反彈。
  • valuation n. 估值、估價
    Sentiment toward the chip duo had been improving on signs of continued Big Tech spending, along with cheap stock valuations, bumper earnings and generous shareholder returns.
    由於持續的科技巨頭支出跡象、便宜的股票估值、亮眼的獲利以及豐厚的股東回報，市場對這對晶片雙雄的情緒原本正在改善。
  • setback n. 挫折、阻礙
    While most investors see the latest DeepSeek development as a short-term setback, the latest declines show the rebound would not be linear.
    雖然大多數投資人將最新的 DeepSeek 發展視為短期的挫折，但最新的下跌顯示反彈不會是直線上升的。
  • slowdown n. 減緩、經濟放緩
    “DeepSeek’s new model could stoke concerns about a slowdown in semiconductor demand in the near term,” Eugene Asset Management chief investment officer Ha SeokKeun said.
    「DeepSeek 的新模型可能會加劇人們對近期半導體需求放緩的擔憂，」尤金資產管理公司投資長河錫根表示。
  • fundamentals n. 基本面
    Still, new models from the likes of Meta Platforms Inc and OpenAI “will have a greater impact on the industry’s fundamentals by boosting actual AI usage and semiconductor demand.”
    不過，來自 Meta Platforms Inc 和 OpenAI 等公司的「新模型將透過推動實際的 AI 使用和半導體需求，對產業的基本面產生更大的影響。」
  • reverse course phr. 改變方向、逆轉
    Local retail traders drove a feverish rally in Samsung and SK Hynix earlier this year before abruptly reversing course on a severe bout of AI skepticism.
    今年稍早，當地散戶推動了三星和 SK 海力士的狂熱漲勢，隨後在歷經一陣嚴重的 AI 懷疑論後急轉直下。
  • earnings n. 盈餘、收益
    The South Korean memory stocks are trading at about four times forward estimated earnings, versus 19 times for the gauge of global chipmakers.
    這些南韓記憶體股票的預估遠期本益比交易價格約為 4 倍，而全球晶片製造商的指標則為 19 倍。
  • volatile adj. 波動大的、善變的
    “These names are going to be volatile. For most investors, there are ways to get the same returns with a lot less volatility,” Tuttle Capital Management chief executive officer Matthew Tuttle said.
    「這些股票將會波動很大。對大多數投資人來說，有方法可以在波動小得多的情況下獲得相同的報酬率，」圖特爾資本管理公司執行長 Matthew Tuttle 表示。
  • implement v. 實施、執行
    Despite the decrease in leverage and measures implemented by regulators to restore calm, daily moves of 5 percent or more are still common for Samsung and SK Hynix.
    儘管槓桿率下降且監管機構採取了恢復平靜的措施，但三星和 SK 海力士單日漲跌幅達 5% 或以上的情況依然常見。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Fund managers wading back into South Korean memory makers’ stocks got a fresh gut check after DeepSeek’s latest artificial intelligence (AI) model raised doubts on the strength of demand.
    句中的 "wading back into South Korean memory makers’ stocks" 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前方名詞 "Fund managers"，相當於形容詞子句 "who were wading back into..."，常出現在新聞英語中以簡化句子結構。
  • 對等連接詞與副詞並用 (While 引導的讓步子句)
    While most investors see the latest DeepSeek development as a short-term setback, the latest declines show the rebound would not be linear.
    由 "While" 引導的副詞子句表示「雖然／儘管」，用來表達兩件事之間的對比或讓步。句中主詞為 most investors，動詞為 see，形成「see A as B（視 A 為 B）」的實用句型。
  • 被動語態的應用 (Passive Voice)
    Despite the decrease in leverage and measures implemented by regulators to restore calm, daily moves of 5 percent or more are still common for Samsung and SK Hynix.
    片語 "implemented by regulators" 是過去分詞片語作後位修飾，其完整概念含有被動意味（措施是由監管機構所實施的），常省略 "which were" 來使新聞標題或內文更為精鍊。
