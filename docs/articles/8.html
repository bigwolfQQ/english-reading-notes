Rescuers save two trapped tunnel workers in Nepal

文法重點

  • 現在分詞表結果（分詞構句）
    Rescuers in Nepal yesterday pulled two workers alive from a tunnel at a hydropower plant, raising hopes of reaching dozens of others believed to be trapped nine days after deadly floods swept away villages and submerged the Himalayan region in mud and debris.
    主要子句說明搜救人員救出兩名工人（pulled two workers alive...），句尾接上逗號加上現在分詞短語「raising hopes...」，用以說明主要動作發生的結果或所引申出的後續效應，相當於「and it raised hopes...」。
  • 過去分詞後位修飾（簡化關係子句）
    Images showed a rescuer carrying one of the workers, covered in mud, on his back, while soldiers on either side helped.
    「covered in mud」是過去分詞短語，修飾前面的名詞「one of the workers」。這相當於關係子句「who was covered in mud」省略了關係代名詞與 be 動詞，用精簡的被動分詞形式補充該工人的狀態。
  • with + 名詞 + 過去分詞（獨立主格結構 / 附加說明）
    About 900 workers are missing from 12 hydropower projects in Nepal, with roughly 500 believed to be trapped in various tunnels, authorities said.
    這裡使用了「with + 受詞 (roughly 500) + 分詞/補語 (believed to be trapped...)」的結構，在主要子句（說明有 900 名工人失蹤）之後，補充說明其中約 500 人的具體受困狀況。
  • 被動語態與不定詞省略結構
    About 115 are believed trapped in the tunnel at Trishuli 3A alone, the Independent Power Producers Association of Nepal said.
    此句型原為「...are believed to be trapped...」，在新聞報導中常省略不定詞「to be」，直接保留過去分詞「trapped」。這表示主觀推測或客觀傳聞的語氣（「據信有……受困」）。
