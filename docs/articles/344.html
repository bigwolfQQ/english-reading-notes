專家：無人機不太可能為台灣提供對抗中國的不對稱優勢

Drones unlikely to offer Taiwan asymmetric edge over China: Expert

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • alter v. 改變，修改
    Drones are unlikely to fundamentally alter the military balance across the Taiwan Strait despite their growing prominence in recent conflicts, an Australian security expert said Wednesday.
    一名澳洲安全專家週三表示，儘管無人機在近期衝突中的重要性日益增加，但它們不太可能從根本上改變台灣海峽兩岸的軍事平衡。
  • dispute v. 反駁，對...表示異議，爭論
    Speaking during a panel at the 2026 Taipei Security Dialogue, Roggeveen disputed the idea that drones are inherently asymmetric weapons through which a smaller power such as Taiwan can impose disproportionate costs on a larger adversary.
    羅格文在2026年台北安全對話的一場座談會上發言時，反駁了無人機本質上是不對稱武器的觀點；台灣這樣的小國可以透過無人機對較大的對手施加不成比例的代價。
  • adversary n. 對手，敵手
    Speaking during a panel at the 2026 Taipei Security Dialogue, Roggeveen disputed the idea that drones are inherently asymmetric weapons through which a smaller power such as Taiwan can impose disproportionate costs on a larger adversary.
    羅格文在2026年台北安全對話的一場座談會上發言時，反駁了無人機本質上是不對稱武器的觀點；台灣這樣的小國可以透過無人機對較大的對手施加不成比例的代價。
  • disadvantage v. / n. 使處於劣勢 / 缺點，劣勢
    Both Taiwan and China can make use of drone technology, he said, adding that there is little reason to believe the People's Liberation Army will be significantly disadvantaged in a cross-strait drone arms race.
    他說，台灣和中國都可以利用無人機技術，並補充說，沒有什麼理由相信中國人民解放軍會在兩岸無人機軍備競賽中處於明顯的劣勢。
  • emphasis n. 強調，重視
    Timothy A. Walton, a senior fellow at the Hudson Institute, agreed that Taiwan should not try to match China's production scale but instead place greater emphasis on the operational value of unmanned systems.
    哈德遜研究所資深研究員提摩太・沃爾頓認同台灣不應試圖與中國的生產規模相匹敵，而應將更多重點放在無人機系統的作戰價值上。
  • focus on phr. v. 專注於，聚焦於
    Rather than pursuing large numbers for their own sake, Taiwan should focus on systems that solve specific operational problems and disrupt key Chinese "kill chains," he said.
    他說，台灣不應為了追求數量而盲目追求數量，而應專注於能解決特定作戰問題並打斷關鍵中國「殺戮鏈」的系統。
  • adapt v. 適應，改造，改編
    Walton also highlighted the need for new classes of weapons that can be produced at greater scale and adapted rapidly through software upgrades and feedback between intelligence personnel and developers.
    沃爾頓還強調，需要有新類型的武器，這些武器能以更大規模生產，並透過軟體升級以及情報人員與開發者之間的意見回饋進行快速調整。
  • capacity n. 容量，生產能力，能量，辦事能力
    Capability refers to possessing advanced autonomous weapons, technology and operational skills, while capacity means having the industrial, logistical, technological and human infrastructure necessary to keep them operating once a conflict begins, Yau said.
    姚宏旻說，能力指的是擁有先進的自主武器、技術和操作技能，而能量則意味著具備在衝突爆發後維持其運作所需的工業、後勤、技術和人力基礎設施。
  • repair v. / n. 修理，修補
    Taiwan must be able to produce, repair, replace and improve its systems after a conflict begins, while integrating them into command-and-control networks under common technical standards, doctrine and procedures, Yau added.
    姚宏旻補充說，衝突爆發後，台灣必須能夠生產、修理、替換和改進其系統，同時在共同的技術標準、準則與程序下，將它們整合到指揮管制網路中。
  • sustain v. 維持，支撐，承受
    Instead, it should build the industrial capacity to sustain its defenses and outlast a stronger adversary.
    相反地，它應該建立工業能量來維持其防禦並撐過更強大的對手。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Speaking during a panel at the 2026 Taipei Security Dialogue, Roggeveen disputed the idea that drones are inherently asymmetric weapons...
    句首的 "Speaking during a panel..." 是現在分詞引導的片語，用來修飾主詞 Roggeveen，表示「在...發言的同時/時」。這在新聞英語中常用於簡化主動語態的時間或附帶狀況子句。
  • 表目的與結果的副詞片語 (Adverbial Infinitive Phrase of Purpose)
    Taiwan should focus on systems that solve specific operational problems and disrupt key Chinese "kill chains," he said.
    句中的 "that solve... and disrupt..." 是關係代名詞子句，用來修飾先行詞 systems。子句中的動詞不定片語或對等動詞則具體描繪這些系統所要達到的功能與目的。
  • 被動語態的應用 (Passive Voice)
    Drones are unlikely to fundamentally alter the military balance across the Taiwan Strait despite their growing prominence in recent conflicts...
    本句雖然主詞 drones 是主動執行 alter，但新聞常使用被動或否定句型來表達可能性與預測。另外如 "will be significantly disadvantaged" 也是標準的被動語態，用來強調承受動作的對象（解放軍）。
  • 對等連接詞連接多個動詞 (Parallel Verbs)
    Taiwan must be able to produce, repair, replace and improve its systems after a conflict begins...
    句中的 produce, repair, replace 和 improve 透過對等原則串接在一起，共享助動詞與情態動詞結構（must be able to），使句子在列舉多項行動時保持簡潔流暢。
