文法重點

  • 主動語態與簡單現在式
    A Vellink Intelligent Metro/Regional Train by Chinese CRRC is on display at the InnoTrans International Trade Fair for Transport Technology in Berlin yesterday.
    句子的核心述語使用 be 動詞 is（此處搭配狀態片語 on display 表達「正在展出」），用來描述目前展示的狀態。
  • 簡單未來式 (Future Simple Tense)
    About 600 companies from the railway infrastructure and tunnel construction sectors will present their latest products and services at the fair.
    使用「助動詞 will + 原形動詞 (present)」來表達未來預計要發生的動作，這在商業新聞報導預告展覽、會議或活動時非常常見。
  • 介系詞片語表達來源與地點
    About 600 companies from the railway infrastructure and tunnel construction sectors will present their latest products and services at the fair.
