文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The anticipated second meeting, expected to take place on Sept. 24 in Washington, should be viewed not only through the lens of bilateral relations, but also in terms of the two leaders’ domestic needs, he said.
    句中的「expected to take place...」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞短語「The anticipated second meeting」。其完整結構為「which is expected to take place...」，省略了「which is」以精簡語句，多益閱讀測驗常考此類分詞片語的簡化用法。
  • 不只……而且……對等連接詞句構 (not only ... but also ...)
    The anticipated second meeting, expected to take place on Sept. 24 in Washington, should be viewed not only through the lens of bilateral relations, but also in terms of the two leaders’ domestic needs, he said.
    「not only A but also B」為多益常考的對等連接詞片語，連接兩個介詞片語（through the lens of ... 與 in terms of ...），用來強調這場會晤必須從雙邊關係與國內需求「兩個面向」來共同看待。
  • 獨立分詞構句與條件附帶 (Absolute Participial Construction)
    That would be partly because the US would have more influence over the agenda this time, given that the meeting would be held in Washington, he said.
    句中的「given that the meeting would be held in Washington」是一個常見的慣用分詞構句（源自 give 的分詞用法，意指「考慮到、鑑於」），用來引導條件或前提子句，在商業英文與新聞英語中非常實用。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    The book, which combines major theories with contemporary case studies, examines China’s foreign policy decisionmaking, and its relations with major powers and neighboring countries.
    句中的「which combines major theories with contemporary case studies」帶有逗號，屬於非限定關係子句，用來補充說明先行詞「The book」的特點。這種句型在多益聽力與閱讀中經常出現，用以在長句中附加重要細節。
