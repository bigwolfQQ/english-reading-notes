US-China meeting likely to focus on each country’s domestic needs: expert

單字解析

  • drive the agenda phr. 主導議程、決定議題方向
    Domestic political considerations in the US and China are likely to drive the agenda of the next meeting between US President Donald Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping (習近平), a Taiwanese academic said yesterday.
  • tactical adj. 戰術上的、權宜之計的
    Both leaders sought “strategic stability” during their meeting in Beijing in May, but the outcome was closer to “tactical stability,” he said.
  • through the lens of phr. 從……的角度或觀點來看
    The anticipated second meeting, expected to take place on Sept. 24 in Washington, should be viewed not only through the lens of bilateral relations, but also in terms of the two leaders’ domestic needs, he said.
  • bilateral adj. 雙邊的、兩國間的
    The anticipated second meeting, expected to take place on Sept. 24 in Washington, should be viewed not only through the lens of bilateral relations, but also in terms of the two leaders’ domestic needs, he said.

    Ahead of the US midterm elections on Nov. 3, Trump would likely seek commitments from China to purchase more US products, including Boeing aircraft, soybeans and beef, Wang said.

  • maximize v. 使最大化、充分利用

文法重點

  • 關聯連接詞 not only... but also...
    The anticipated second meeting, expected to take place on Sept. 24 in Washington, should be viewed not only through the lens of bilateral relations, but also in terms of the two leaders’ domestic needs, he said.
    「not only A, but also B」用來連接兩個結構對等的成分（此處為介系詞片語「through the lens of...」與「in terms of...」），意為「不僅 A，而且 B」，能同時強調這兩個觀點。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾（省略關係子句）
    The anticipated second meeting, expected to take place on Sept. 24 in Washington, should be viewed not only through the lens of bilateral relations, but also in terms of the two leaders’ domestic needs, he said.
    句中的「expected to take place...」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的主詞「The anticipated second meeting」。此結構是由非限定關係子句「which is expected to take place...」簡化而來，讓新聞句型更加精煉。
  • 連接詞片語 given that...
    That would be partly because the US would have more influence over the agenda this time, given that the meeting would be held in Washington, he said.
    「given that + S + V」為正式英文中常見的片語連接詞，相當於「considering that...」，意為「考慮到……」或「鑑於……」，用來引出前述情況的合理背景或前提條件。
  • 非限定關係子句 (the title of which)
    The event marked the publication of the Chinese-language book, the title of which could be roughly translated to “An Introduction to Contemporary China’s Foreign Relations (當代中國對外關係概論).”
    「, the title of which...」屬於非限定關係子句，相當於「, and its title...」。這裡使用「介系詞 of + 關係代名詞 which」補充說明前面這本書（book）的書名翻譯，是極具正式書面語風格的文法結構。
