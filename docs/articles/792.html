聯合國：白俄羅斯刑事司法體系遭「武器化」

Belarus criminal justice system ‘weaponized’: UN

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • authorities n. 當局，官方
    Belarusian authorities are using the country’s criminal justice system as an instrument of repression...
    白俄羅斯當局正將該國的刑事司法體系作為壓迫工具……
  • systematically adv. 系統性地，有計畫地
    ...to systematically target government opponents, UN investigators said yesterday.
    聯合國調查人員昨天表示，此舉是為了系統性地針對政府反對者。
  • investigator n. 調查人員
    A group of independent UN experts at the Office of the High Commissioner for Human Rights tasked with probing the rights situation in Belarus determined that...
    聯合國人權事務高級專員公署轄下的一個獨立專家小組負責調查白俄羅斯的人權狀況，該小組認定……
  • sanction n. 制裁，處罰
    ...“creating a system of punishment and legal sanctions against any form of dissent, real or perceived.”
    「針對任何形式的異議（無論是真實存在的還是被認為有的），建立了一套懲罰和法律制裁體系。」
  • fundamental adj. 基本的，根本的
    “What emerges is not a picture of isolated violations, but of a system in which state institutions have systematically been used to suppress dissent and fundamental freedoms,”...
    「浮現的景象並非零星的違法行為，而是一個國家機構被系統性地用來壓制異議和基本自由的體系」……
  • prosecute v. 起訴，檢訴
    “their impacts are felt not only by those arrested, prosecuted or imprisoned, but by an entire society being warned about the price of dissent.”
    「其影響不僅由被捕、起訴或監禁的人感受到，而且由整個被警告異議代價的社會所承受。」
  • violation n. 違反，違法，侵犯
    The expert group was created in April 2024 by the office to investigate violations committed in Belarus since 2020...
    該專家小組於2024年4月由該公署成立，旨在調查白俄羅斯自2020年以來犯下的侵權行為……
  • arbitrary adj. 任意的，專斷的
    Arrests were often “arbitrary” and “violent,” and carried out by plain-clothes officers without warrants...
    逮捕行動通常是「任意的」且「暴力的」，由便衣警察在沒有手令的情況下執行……
  • convicted adj. 被定罪的
    Once convicted, detainees held on politically motivated grounds were marked with yellow tags...
    一旦被定罪，因政治動機被關押的拘留者會被標上黃色標籤……
  • discrimination n. 歧視
    ...subjected to “discrimination, inhuman conditions of detention, cruel, inhuman or degrading treatment and incommunicado detention,” the report said.
    報告指出，……遭受「歧視、不人道的拘留條件、殘酷、不人道或有辱人格的待遇以及祕密拘留」。
  • release v. / n. 釋放，發布
    The experts called on Belarusian authorities to immediately release all those held on politically motivated charges...
    專家呼吁白俄羅斯當局立即釋放所有因政治動機被關押的人……
  • impunity n. 免罰，免責，不受懲罰
    “Those responsible for serious human rights violations must know that impunity is not a permanent shield...”
    「嚴重侵犯人權的責任人必須知道，有罪不罰並不是永久的盾牌……」

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    A group of independent UN experts at the Office of the High Commissioner for Human Rights tasked with probing the rights situation in Belarus determined that...
    句中的「tasked with probing the rights situation in Belarus」是由過去分詞片語開頭的修飾語，用來補充說明前面的名詞「A group of independent UN experts」（聯合國獨立專家小組）。這種結構省略了「who were」，能讓句子更緊湊、避免冗長。
  • 被動語態的進行式與完成式 (Passive Voice in Continuous and Perfect Tenses)
    ...a system in which state institutions have systematically been used to suppress dissent and fundamental freedoms...
    此句使用現在完成式的被動語態「have systematically been used」。當強調某個動作從過去持續到現在、且主體是承受動作（國家機構被用來……）時，會使用「have/has been + 過去分詞」的結構。
  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況 (Participial Phrase indicating Concomitant Circumstances)
    ...forcing them to record humiliating “repentance” videos, the experts said.
    句尾的「forcing them to record...」是一個現在分詞構句，前面通常帶有連續動作或結果的意味，用來補充說明前面主句發生的逮捕行動中，警察還伴隨著「強迫他們錄製影片」的行為。
  • 形容詞片語作後位修飾與獨立結構 (Adjective Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Detainees were held in “overcrowded, excessively hot and unsanitary cells, often without enough beds and infested with bedbugs, while being deprived of adequate food and sleep,” the report found.
    句中的「while being deprived of...」是連接詞加上分詞構句的用法，用來表達在被拘留的同時「被剝奪了充足的食物和睡眠」。這種寫法能簡潔地表達兩個同時發生或因果相連的狀態。
