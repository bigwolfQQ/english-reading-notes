ENGLISH DIGEST 實用美語(空中美語)

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • intersection n. 十字路口
    An ambulance, its siren blaring, attempted to pass through traffic, yet the vehicles ahead remained still.
    一輛鳴笛的救護車試圖穿過車陣，然而前方的車輛卻紋絲不動。
  • wary adj. 謹慎的、防範的
    Drivers, wary of automatic fines for crossing the stop line or running a red light, chose compliance over compassion and didn’t move.
    顧慮跨越停止線或闖紅燈會被自動開罰的駕駛們，選擇了遵守交通規則而非惻隱之心，並沒有移動。
  • deter v. 威懾、阻止
    By deterring reckless behavior through consistent monitoring, technology theoretically creates a more disciplined driving environment.
    透過持續監測來阻嚇魯莽的行為，科技在理論上創造了一個更守紀律的駕駛環境。
  • reckless adj. 魯莽的、不計後果的
    By deterring reckless behavior through consistent monitoring, technology theoretically creates a more disciplined driving environment.
    透過持續監測來阻嚇魯莽的行為，科技在理論上創造了一個更守紀律的駕駛環境。
  • theoretically adv. 理論上
    By deterring reckless behavior through consistent monitoring, technology theoretically creates a more disciplined driving environment.
    透過持續監測來阻嚇魯莽的行為，科技在理論上創造了一個更守紀律的駕駛環境。
  • subsequent adj. 隨後的、後續的
    While these cameras capture violations, the subsequent human review process often fails to account for emergency situations.
    雖然這些攝影機捕捉到了違規行為，但後續的人工審核程序常常未能考量到緊急情況。
  • administrative adj. 行政的
    When the administrative cost of basic civic responsibility becomes too high, social cooperation inevitably weakens.
    當實踐基本公民責任的行政成本過高時，社會合作必然會削弱。
  • abolish v. 廢除、廢止
    The solution is not to abolish technology, but to refine its implementation.
    解決之道並非廢除科技，而是優化其執行方式。
  • implementation n. 實施、執行
    The solution is not to abolish technology, but to refine its implementation.
    解決之道並非廢除科技，而是優化其執行方式。
  • robust adj. 健全的、強健的、完善的
    More robust review mechanisms are needed, potentially including systems that compare ambulance GPS data with camera footage.
    我們需要更完善的審查機制，可能包括將救護車的GPS數據與攝影機畫面進行比對的系統。

文法重點

  • 獨立分詞構句 (Absolute Phrase)
    An ambulance, its siren blaring, attempted to pass through traffic, yet the vehicles ahead remained still.
    「its siren blaring」是由「代間代詞 (its) + 現在分詞 (blaring)」所構成的獨立分詞構句，用來補充說明主詞 an ambulance 的伴隨狀態（警報器呼嘯著），多益閱讀測驗常考此類結構的格位與標點符號用法。
  • 形容詞片語後置修飾 (Post-positive Adjective Phrase)
    Drivers, wary of automatic fines for crossing the stop line or running a red light, chose compliance over compassion and didn’t move.
    形容詞片語「wary of automatic fines...」放在主詞 Drivers 的後面作後置修飾，相當於一個省略了「who are」的形容詞子句（Drivers, who were wary of...），能使句子更為精鍊，是進階英語閱讀常見的句型。
  • 關係代間詞引導的非限定子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    As a result, the ambulance was delayed for four crucial minutes, which could have meant the difference between life and death.
    句中的「which could have meant...」是關係代間詞 which 引導的非限定子句，先行詞是前面整子句發生的事件（救護車被延誤四分鐘），「which」代替整句話並補充說明其可能帶來的嚴重後果。
  • 假設語氣過去完成式 (Past Unreal Conditional)
    If the basic social virtue of yielding to an ambulance is replaced by the fear of an automatic fine, the system is fundamentally broken.
    雖然本句混合了現在事實的條件句，但文章中也常見表達過去假設或推論的句構（如文章第一段的 could have meant）。此處 If 子句使用簡單現在式表達條件，主句表達結果，呈現清晰的邏輯因果關係，是多益文法題（尤其是動詞時態題）的必考重點。
