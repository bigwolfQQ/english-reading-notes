隨著畢業生進入勞動市場，台灣失業率微幅上升

Taiwan jobless rate edges up as graduates enter labor market

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • unemployment rate n. 失業率
    Taiwan's unemployment rate rose for the third consecutive month in August as more first-time jobseekers entered the labor market after graduating, the Directorate General of Budget, Accounting and Statistics (DGBAS) said Tuesday.
    行政院主計總處（DGBAS）於週二表示，由於越來越多首次求職者在畢業後進入勞動市場，台灣 8 月份的失業率連續第三個月上升。
  • consecutive adj. 連續的
    Taiwan's unemployment rate rose for the third consecutive month in August as more first-time jobseekers entered the labor market after graduating, the Directorate General of Budget, Accounting and Statistics (DGBAS) said Tuesday.
    行政院主計總處（DGBAS）於週二表示，由於越來越多首次求職者在畢業後進入勞動市場，台灣 8 月份的失業率連續第三個月上升。
  • labor market n. 勞動市場
    Taiwan's unemployment rate rose for the third consecutive month in August as more first-time jobseekers entered the labor market after graduating, the Directorate General of Budget, Accounting and Statistics (DGBAS) said Tuesday.
    行政院主計總處（DGBAS）於週二表示，由於越來越多首次求職者在畢業後進入勞動市場，台灣 8 月份的失業率連續第三個月上升。
  • edge up phr. v. 微幅上升、稍微增加
    The jobless rate edged up 0.02 percentage points from July to 3.41 percent, its highest level since August 2025, when it stood at 3.45 percent.
    失業率較 7 月微升 0.02 個百分點至 3.41%，創下自 2025 年 8 月（當時為 3.45%）以來的最高水準。
  • average v. 平均為、平均達到
    In the first eight months of 2026, the unemployment rate averaged 3.33 percent, down 0.02 percentage points from a year earlier, the DGBAS said.
    主計總處表示，2026 年前八個月，平均失業率為 3.33%，較去年同期下降 0.02 個百分點。
  • agency n. （政府）機構、局、處
    The number of unemployed people rose by 2,000, or 0.63 percent, from July to 411,000 in August, as the number of first-time jobseekers increased by 3,000, according to the agency.
    據該機構指出，8 月份失業人數較 7 月增加 2,000 人（或 0.63%）達到 411,000 人，主因是首次求職者人數增加了 3,000 人。
  • employment n. 就業、雇用
    Meanwhile, employment grew by 4,000, or 0.03 percent, to 11.66 million, while the labor force participation rate rose 0.03 percentage points from July to 59.66 percent.
    同時，就業人數成長 4,000 人（或 0.03%）達到 1,166 萬人，勞動力參與率則較 7 月上升 0.03 個百分點，達到 59.66%。
  • reflect v. 反映、顯示
    Tan Wen-ling (譚文玲), deputy director of the DGBAS Census Department, told reporters that the increase in the August jobless rate largely reflected seasonal factors.
    主計總處國勢普查處副處長譚文玲告訴記者，8 月失業率的上升主要反映了季節性因素。
  • decline v. 下降、減少
    She said the seasonally adjusted unemployment rate had declined, while employment and labor force participation had increased.
    她表示，經季節調整後的失業率有所下降，而就業人數和勞動力參與率則有所增加。
  • indicate v. 指出、表明
    Taken together, the latest employment figures indicated that Taiwan's labor market remained stable, Tan said.
    譚文玲表示，綜合來看，最新的就業數據表明台灣勞動力市場保持穩定。
  • classify v. 將……分類、歸類
    DGBAS data also showed that 119,000 people were classified as time-related underemployed workers in August, down 3,000, or 2.62 percent, from July and 2,000, or 1.92 percent, from a year earlier.
    主計總處數據還顯示，8 月份有 119,000 人被歸類為時間相關不足就業工人，較 7 月減少 3,000 人（或 2.62%），較去年同期減少 2,000 人（或 1.92%）。
  • define v. 定義、給……下定義
    The International Labour Organization defines time-related underemployment as a situation in which people work fewer hours than they would like and are willing and available to work more.
    國際勞工組織將時間相關不足就業定義為：人們的工作時數少於他們所希望的時數，並且願意且能夠接受更多工作的情況。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Postmodifier)
    Taiwan's unemployment rate rose for the third consecutive month in August as more first-time jobseekers entered the labor market after graduating...
    句尾的「after graduating」省略了主體（當代名詞子句還原應為 after they graduated），直接使用現在分詞 graduating，用來簡化時間副詞子句，表達「在畢業之後」這個動作。
  • 過去分詞作形容詞修飾 (Past Participle as Adjective)
    The jobless rate edged up 0.02 percentage points from July to 3.41 percent, its highest level since August 2025, when it stood at 3.45 percent.
    句中的「seasonally adjusted」或「unemployed」等常見結構，是將過去分詞當作形容詞使用，用來修飾名詞（如 adjusted unemployment rate 經季節調整的失業率），在多益商業財經新聞中極為常見。
  • 附屬連接詞引导的原因副詞子句 (Adclause of Cause with 'as')
    The number of unemployed people rose by 2,000, or 0.63 percent, from July to 411,000 in August, as the number of first-time jobseekers increased by 3,000, according to the agency.
    連接詞 「as」在此處等於 because，用來引導原因子句，說明失業人數上升的背景原因是由於首次求職者人數增加。
  • 對比連接詞 while 的用法 (Contrast with 'while')
    Meanwhile, employment grew by 4,000, or 0.03 percent, to 11.66 million, while the labor force participation rate rose 0.03 percentage points from July to 59.66 percent.
    「while」在此處表示「同時」或「而（表示對比或並列情況）」，連接兩個同時發生且數據各自變化的子句，使句子結構緊湊流暢。
  • 關係副詞 when 引導的非限定子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause with 'when')
    ...its highest level since August 2025, when it stood at 3.45 percent.
    句中的「when it stood at 3.45 percent」為非限定用法（前面有逗號），用來補充說明前面的時間點「August 2025」，交代當時的具體數據。
