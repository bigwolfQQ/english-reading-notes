知情人士表示，向美國採購的「天空衛士」無人機已進行測試

SkyGuardian drones bought from US tested, source says

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • purchase v. / n. 購買，採購
    The first two of the four MQ-9B “SkyGuardian” drones Taiwan purchased from the US underwent their first flight tests at an air base in eastern Taiwan early yesterday, a source familiar with the matter said.
    知情人士表示，台灣向美國採購的四架 MQ-9B「天空衛士」（SkyGuardian）無人機中，首批兩架於昨早台灣東部的一座空軍基地進行了首次飛行測試。
  • disclose v. 透露，公開
    The unnamed source did not disclose further details about the test flights, saying that they were conducted at Hualien Air Base.
    這名匿名消息人士未透露關於試飛的進一步細節，僅表示測試是在花蓮空軍基地進行的。
  • conduct v. 實施，執行，舉辦
    The tests were conducted after the military and the drone manufacturer completed the assembly of the two drones delivered to Taiwan earlier this year, the source added.
    消息人士補充說，這些測試是在軍方與無人機製造商完成今年稍早運抵台灣的兩架無人機組裝後進行的。
  • deliver v. 遞送，交貨
    The US has agreed to deliver the four MQ-9Bs to Taiwan in two batches this year and next year.
    美國已同意在今年和明年分兩批將四架 MQ-9B 交貨給台灣。
  • batch n. 批，批次
    The US has agreed to deliver the four MQ-9Bs to Taiwan in two batches this year and next year.
    美國已同意在今年和明年分兩批將四架 MQ-9B 交貨給台灣。
  • enhance v. 提高，增加，增強
    Su Tzu-yun (蘇紫雲), a research fellow at the government-funded Institute for National Defense and Security Research (INDSR), previously said that the role of the MQ-9Bs in Taiwan is to enhance battlefield reconnaissance and surveillance capabilities rather than serve as a primary offensive weapon.
    政府資助的「國防安全研究院」（INDSR）研究員蘇紫雲先前表示，MQ-9B 在台灣的角色是增強戰場偵察與監視能力，而不是作為主要的進攻武器。
  • approve v. 批准，核准
    INDSR researcher Hsieh Pei-shiue (謝沛學) wrote in 2020, when the US first approved the sale to Taiwan, that the drone is capable of linking to satellite communications networks shared among US allies.
    INDSR 研究員謝沛學在 2020 年美國首次批准對台軍售時撰文指出，該無人機具備連接美國盟友之間共享的衛星通訊網路之能力。
  • cooperate v. 合作，配合
    That means Taiwan and the US could share battlefield intelligence instantly, providing opportunities for the two sides to cooperate during a conflict.
    這意味著台灣和美國可以即時共享戰場情報，為雙方在衝突期間進行合作提供機會。
  • take advantage of phr. 利用，善用
    For example, if a cross-strait conflict breaks out, Washington could take advantage of anti-ship missile systems deployed along the First Island Chain to prevent Chinese People’s Liberation Army vessels from sailing into waters east of Taiwan to blockade the nation, Hsieh said.
    謝沛學表示，舉例來說，如果兩岸爆發衝突，華盛頓可以利用部署在第一島鏈沿線的反艦飛彈系統，阻止中國人民解放軍艦艇航行至台灣以東水域以封鎖該國。
  • deploy v. 部署（軍力、設備等）
    For example, if a cross-strait conflict breaks out, Washington could take advantage of anti-ship missile systems deployed along the First Island Chain to prevent Chinese People’s Liberation Army vessels from sailing into waters east of Taiwan to blockade the nation, Hsieh said.
    謝沛學表示，舉例來說，如果兩岸爆發衝突，華盛頓可以利用部署在第一島鏈沿線的反艦飛彈系統，阻止中國人民解放軍艦艇航行至台灣以東水域以封鎖該國。
  • prevent v. 預防，防止，阻止
    For example, if a cross-strait conflict breaks out, Washington could take advantage of anti-ship missile systems deployed along the First Island Chain to prevent Chinese People’s Liberation Army vessels from sailing into waters east of Taiwan to blockade the nation, Hsieh said.
    謝沛學表示，舉例來說，如果兩岸爆發衝突，華盛頓可以利用部署在第一島鏈沿線的反艦飛彈系統，阻止中國人民解放軍艦艇航行至台灣以東水域以封鎖該國。
  • target v. 把...作為目標，瞄準
    MQ-9Bs are capable of long-term, long-range surveillance, meaning the drones could fly near Chinese vessels and provide critical intelligence for US forces to target them, Hsieh wrote at the time.
    謝沛學當時寫道，MQ-9B 具備長期、長程監視能力，這意味著無人機可以飛近中國艦艇，並為美軍鎖定目標提供關鍵情報。

    因此，他表示，軍方不應該過度依賴少數大型無人機，而應該利用 MQ-9B 來進行廣域掃描並標定高價值目標。
  • utilize v. 利用，使用
    Consequently, the military should not rely solely on a handful of large drones, but should instead utilize the MQ-9B to conduct wide-area scans and designate high-value targets, he said.
    因此，他表示，軍方不應該過度依賴少數大型無人機，而應該利用 MQ-9B 來進行廣域掃描並標定高價值目標。
  • distribute v. 分發，分配
    That intelligence could then be distributed to small and medium-sized drones, shore-based radars and other combat units to build a more resilient “integrated reconnaissance and strike” network, he added.
    他補充說，這些情報隨後可以分發給中小型無人機、岸基雷達和其他作戰單位，以建立更具韌性的「聯合偵打」網路。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞（片語）作後置定語
    The first two of the four MQ-9B “SkyGuardian” drones Taiwan purchased from the US underwent their first flight tests at an air base in eastern Taiwan early yesterday, a source familiar with the matter said.
    句中的「Taiwan purchased from the US」是省略了關係代名詞的形容詞子句（或者視為過去分詞片語修飾前面的 drones），用來限定說明這四架無人機是台灣向美國購買的。
  • 現在分詞片語作結果狀語
    That means Taiwan and the US could share battlefield intelligence instantly, providing opportunities for the two sides to cooperate during a conflict.
  • 未來可能性假設語氣（First Conditional）
    For example, if a cross-strait conflict breaks out, Washington could take advantage of anti-ship missile systems deployed along the First Island Chain...
    此句使用條件句型，由「if + 現在式動詞（breaks out）」表示可能的未來條件，主句則使用「情態動詞 could + 原形動詞（could take advantage of）」，表達在該條件成立時具備的可能性或能力。
  • 對比結構（not... but instead...）
    Consequently, the military should not rely solely on a handful of large drones, but should instead utilize the MQ-9B to conduct wide-area scans and designate high-value targets, he said.
    句型「should not rely... but should instead utilize...」展示了強烈的對比語氣，用來否定某種作法（僅依賴少數大型無人機），並轉而肯定、建議另一種替代作法（利用 MQ-9B 進行廣域掃描）。
