英國村莊因難民安置計畫投票決定「脫離」英國

Village votes to ‘secede’ from UK over migrant plans

單字解析

  • facility n. 設施，場所
    An English village of about 350 people yesterday announced that a vast majority of residents had voted yes in an “independence referendum” to protest plans to house more than 1,200 migrants in a nearby facility.
    英國一個約有 350 人居住的村莊昨天宣布，絕大多數居民在一次「獨立公投」中投下贊成票，以抗議在鄰近設施收容 1,200 多名難民的計畫。
  • protest v. / n. 抗議，反對
    An English village of about 350 people yesterday announced that a vast majority of residents had voted yes in an “independence referendum” to protest plans to house more than 1,200 migrants in a nearby facility.
    英國一個約有 350 人居住的村莊昨天宣布，絕大多數居民在一次「獨立公投」中投下贊成票，以抗議在鄰近設施收容 1,200 多名難民的計畫。
  • legal adj. 法律的，合法的
    The village’s parish council said that with 312 ballots counted, 285 had voted to secede from Britain over plans to house asylum seekers in a nearby disused military site, in a symbolic vote with no real legal basis.
    該村的教區委員會表示，在清點完 312 張選票後，有 285 票投票贊成從英國獨立，以抗議在附近荒廢的軍事基地安置尋求庇護者的計畫，這是一場沒有實際法律效力的象徵性投票。
  • support v. / n. 支持，贊助
    Residents were handed a ballot paper with the statement: “I support Piddington seeking to secede from the United Kingdom and pursuing self-determination.”
    居民們拿到一張選票，上面寫著：「我支持皮丁頓尋求脫離大英國協（英國）並追求自決。」
  • concern n. / v. 關切，擔憂，企業/公司
    “I think what this result shows is that Piddington campaigners have done an incredible job to highlight their concerns about the proposed asylum center,” the leader of Oxfordshire county council, Tim Bearder, told the BBC.
    「我認為這個結果顯示，皮丁頓的活動人士在凸顯他們對擬議庇護中心的擔憂方面，做了非常出色的工作。」牛津郡議會領袖蒂姆·比德爾（Tim Bearder）告訴英國廣播公司（BBC）。
  • propose v. 提議，計畫
    Bearder accused the British government of proposing the plan “without engaging with local people.”
    比德爾指責英國政府在提出該計畫時「沒有與當地民眾進行溝通」。
  • burden n. 負擔，重擔
    British Secretary of State for Digital, Culture, Media and Sport Lisa Nandy told Sky News she would “gently say to the people of Piddington that we inherited a massive asylum backlog” and it is unacceptable for the whole burden to “fall on poorer communities.”
    英國數位、文化、媒體暨體育大臣麗莎·南迪（Lisa Nandy）告訴天空新聞網，她想「溫和地對皮丁頓人民說，我們接手了龐大的難民庇護積壓案件」，且讓整個負擔「落在較貧困的社區身上」是令人無法接受的。
  • exempt adj. 被免除的，豁免的
    In the 1949 classic, a small group of Londoners discover treasure and declare independence making them exempt from post-World War II rationing and other bureaucratic restrictions.
    在這部 1949 年的經典電影中，一小群倫敦人發現了寶藏並宣布獨立，使他們免受二戰後的配給制和其他官僚限制。
  • ahead of prep. phr. 在……之前
    “What we’re doing is a very British thing — we’re voting,” the local parish council chair, Tim McNally, said ahead of the result.
    當地教區委員會主席蒂姆·麥克納利（Tim McNally）在結果揭曉前表示：「我們正在做的事情非常具有英國風格——我們在投票。」

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    An English village of about 350 people yesterday announced that a vast majority of residents had voted yes in an “independence referendum” to protest plans to house more than 1,200 migrants in a nearby facility.
    句中的 "to house more than 1,200 migrants in a nearby facility" 是不定詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "plans"，說明計畫的內容細節。這在多益閱讀測驗的長句解析中非常常見。
  • 過去分詞作形容詞修飾名詞 (Past Participle as Adjective)
    The village’s parish council said that with 312 ballots counted, 285 had voted to secede from Britain over plans to house asylum seekers in a nearby disused military site...
    句中的 "disused" 是過去分詞當作形容詞使用，用來修飾名詞 "military site"，表示「荒廢的、廢棄的」。此外，"with 312 ballots counted" 則是「with + 名詞 + 過去分詞」的附帶狀況獨立構句。
  • 關係代名詞引導的名詞子句 (Noun Clause introduced by Wh-word)
    “I think what this result shows is that Piddington campaigners have done an incredible job to highlight their concerns about the proposed asylum center,” the leader of Oxfordshire county council, Tim Bearder, told the BBC.
    句首的 "what this result shows" 是一個由關係代名詞 what 引導的名詞子句，在句中擔任動詞 think 的受詞子句裡的主詞。這種結構常用於表達抽象觀念或強調某個事實。
  • 虛主詞 It 與不定詞片語 (Dummy Subject 'It' and Infinitive Phrase)
    ...and it is unacceptable for the whole burden to “fall on poorer communities.”
    句中的 "it" 為虛主詞（代名詞），真正的主詞是後面的不定詞片語 "to fall on poorer communities"。"for the whole burden" 則是該不定詞片語的語意主詞（logical subject），用介系詞 for 引導。
