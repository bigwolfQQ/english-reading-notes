沉迷於 AI 的人不會成為更好的員工

AI addicts will not make better employees

單字解析

  • productivity n. 生產力
    US Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh is convinced, and prominent economists agree, that artificial intelligence (AI) would meaningfully increase labor productivity.
    美國聯準會主席凱文・華許深信，且知名經濟學家也同意，人工智慧將顯著提高勞動生產力。
  • adoption n. 採用，採納
    In fact, wider adoption of these models could reduce output per worker.
    事實上，這些模型的更廣泛採用可能會降低每位勞工的產出。
  • affordability n. （價格、成本的）可負擔性
    In the Internet era, accessible user interfaces and affordability have been indispensable enablers.
    在網際網路時代，親民的使用者介面與可負擔性一直是不可或缺的推動力量。
  • indispensable adj. 不可或缺的，必需的
    In the Internet era, accessible user interfaces and affordability have been indispensable enablers.
    在網際網路時代，親民的使用者介面與可負擔性一直是不可或缺的推動力量。
  • efficiency n. 效率
    Google’s success crushed rival search engines and traditional print publishers, and its engineers miraculously maintained the efficiency of computing infrastructure...
    Google 的成功粉碎了競爭對手的搜尋引擎和傳統平面出版商，其工程師奇蹟般地維持了計算基礎設施的效率……
  • monopolist n. 獨占者，壟斷者
    As Google became a monopolist, search-engine optimizers flooded results with useless links.
    隨著 Google 成為獨占者，搜尋引擎最佳化人員用無用的連結淹入了搜尋結果。
  • disappointing adj. 令人失望的
    Bard — the chatbot that Google rushed to release — was equally disappointing.
    Google 倉促發布的聊天機器人 Bard 同樣令人失望。
  • discretion n. 裁量權，斟酌決定權
    This gives users more discretion to exercise their judgement, which reduces misfires and costs.
    這賦予使用者更多的裁量權來行使判斷力，從而減少失誤與成本。
  • operating costs n. phr. 營運成本
    Once users are hooked, hyperscalers would surely charge high prices to recoup their steep development and operating costs.
    一旦使用者上癮，超大規模雲端運調商肯定會收取高價，以收回其高昂的開發與營運成本。
  • investment n. 投資
    The pretense that they do has enabled AI peddlers to push chatbots indiscriminately, especially as the narrower applications for which LLMs are actually useful cannot justify multitrillion-dollar investments.
    它們假裝自己有這種能力，這使得 AI 販子得以毫無區別地推銷聊天機器人，特別是當 LLMs 實際上真正有用、較窄幅的應用無法證明數兆美元的投資是合理的時候。
  • resources n. 資源
    Despite seemingly unlimited resources, Google has had at least as many flops as hits.
    儘管擁有看似無限的資源，Google 的失敗作品至少和成功作品一樣多。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Bard — the chatbot that Google rushed to release — was equally disappointing.
    句中的「that Google rushed to release」是形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的名詞「the chatbot」。在英文中，當關係代名詞受格可省略時，有時也會看到直接用過去分詞或不定詞片語來修飾名詞，此處則是完整的形容詞子句，用來補充說明 Google 倉促發布這項產品的背景。
  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況 (Participial Phrase indicating Concomitant Circumstance)
    LLMs do not merely inform; they use metaphors and humor in what the philosopher Ludwig Wittgenstein called “language-games” that reassure, explain, flatter, assert and cajole.
    雖然句中包含多重子句與名詞子句（what the philosopher...），但「that reassure, explain, flatter, assert and cajole」是由關係代名詞 that 引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的複數名詞 language-games，精準列舉了這些語言遊戲所具有的多種溝通功能。
  • 假設語氣（與現在事實相反）(Subjunctive Mood)
    One might hope that even the most powerful firms cannot dupe consumers into submission.
    句中的 「might hope」 表達了講者一種較為保留、不確定或較弱的臆測語氣，比 「may」 或 「will」 更能表達出「縱使有這種希望，現實可能依然令人悲觀」的虛擬或保留情境。
  • 主詞補語與現在分詞作形容詞 (Present Participle as Subject Complement)
    In my experience, the results remain maddeningly unreliable, even for simple queries.
    句中的「remain」是连系动词（Linking Verb），後面接形容詞作為主詞補語。此處使用「unreliable」，而其前的「maddeningly」則是副詞，用來修飾形容詞 unreliable，表示結果「令人抓狂地」不可靠。
