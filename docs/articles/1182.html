跨欄選手林仲威奪銅，台灣週一再添四面亞運獎牌

Hurdler Lin wins bronze as Taiwan adds four Asian Games medals Monday

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • collect v. 收集、獲得、採集
    Lin Chung-wei won bronze in the men's 400-meter hurdles at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games on Monday, one of four bronze medals Taiwan collected during the day.
    林仲威週一在愛知-名古屋亞運男子400公尺跨欄項目中贏得銅牌，這是台灣當天收穫的四面銅牌之一。
  • following prep. 在……之後
    His finish gave Taiwan a track and field medal for the second straight day, following Fu Chao-hsuan's gold in the men's high jump Sunday.
    繼傅兆玄週日在男子跳高項目奪金後，他的完賽為台灣連續第二天贏得田徑獎牌。
  • enter v. 進入、參加、報名
    Lin entered the final with a time of 49.10 seconds, the second-fastest time in the heats.
    林仲威以49.10秒晉級決賽，這是預賽第二快的成績。
  • place v. 獲得名次、名列
    Taiwan's Chen Jian-rong placed fourth, also setting a personal best of 49.25 seconds.
    台灣的陳建榮名列第四，也創下49.25秒的個人最佳成績。
  • previous adj. 先前的、以前的
    Lin's previous best was 48.77 seconds, set at a meet in Japan in May.
    林仲威之前的最佳成績是48.77秒，是5月在日本的一場賽事中創下的。
  • event n. 比賽項目、活動、事件
    Taiwan's other bronzes came in badminton mixed doubles, table tennis men's singles and the women's 25-meter pistol team event.
    台灣的其他銅牌來自羽球混合雙打、桌球男子單打以及女子25公尺手槍團體項目。
  • semifinal n. 準決賽、半決賽
    Badminton pair Ye Hong-wei and Nicole Gonzales Chan lost their semifinal to China's Jiang Zhenbang and Wei Yaxin 21-13, 21-9...
    羽球雙打組合葉宏蔚與詹又蓁在準決賽以21-13、21-9不敵中國的蔣振邦與魏雅欣……
  • force v. 迫使、強迫
    ...forcing them to settle for a bronze medal, Taiwan's first Asian Games mixed doubles badminton medal since 2010.
    ……被迫滿足於銅牌，這也是台灣自2010年以來首面亞運羽球混雙獎牌。
  • combine v. 結合、聯合、合計
    Shooters Cheng Yen-ching, Tien Chia-chen and Chen Yu-ju combined for 1,734 points to place third in the women's 25-meter pistol team event...
    射擊選手鄭晏晴、田家榛與陳俞如聯手拿下1,734分，在女子25公尺手槍團體項目中名列第三……
  • as of prep. 截至……
    As of Monday night, Taiwan had won three golds, seven silvers and 23 bronzes.
    截至週一晚間，台灣已贏得3面金牌、7面銀牌與23面銅牌。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作附帶結果或補充說明
    He stayed among the leaders and was level for second after clearing the last hurdle, before finishing third in a personal-best 48.74 seconds.
    句尾的「before finishing third...」使用介詞連接動名詞片語，表達在跨越最後一個欄架之後，所產生的接續動作或結果，在新聞英語中常用來流暢地串接比賽的過程與最終成績。
  • 獨立分詞構句作補充說明
    Badminton pair Ye Hong-wei and Nicole Gonzales Chan lost their semifinal to China's Jiang Zhenbang and Wei Yaxin 21-13, 21-9, forcing them to settle for a bronze medal...
    逗號之後的「forcing them to...」是由前句整個子句所引發的結果。此處省略了主詞，直接用現在分詞片語表達「因而迫使他們……」，能使句子結構更為精簡、緊湊。
  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense)
    As of Monday night, Taiwan had won three golds, seven silvers and 23 bronzes.
    句中使用「had won（過去完成式：had + 過去分詞）」來表示在過去某一特定時間點（週一晚間）之前已經完成的動作，強調累積到該時間點為止的獎牌總數。
