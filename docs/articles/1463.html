都市樹木不只是數字

Urban trees more than numbers

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • determine v. 決定、影響
    The number of trees does not necessarily determine how much shade a city has.
    樹木的數量不一定能決定一個城市擁有多少遮陰。
  • factor n. 因素、要素
    The most important factor for street shade, urban cooling and the quality of public spaces is the canopy cover built up over years of growth.
    街頭遮陰、都市降溫以及公共空間品質最重要的因素，是經過多年生長所建立起來的樹冠覆蓋。
  • merely adv. 僅僅、只是
    The changes to Taipei’s urban trees in the past few years are not merely a landscaping issue, but a urban environment and public governance matter.
    台北市過去幾年都市樹木的變化，不僅僅是一個景觀問題，更是一個都市環境與公共治理的事項。
  • extent n. 程度、範圍、廣度
    To understand what is happening to Taipei’s tree cover, we can look at two sets of numbers: the number of trees removed and the extent of canopy cover shown in satellite imagery.
    為了了解台北市樹木覆蓋正發生什麼事，我們可以查看兩組數據：被移除的樹木數量，以及衛星影像顯示的樹冠覆蓋範圍。
  • reflect v. 反映、顯示
    This reflects the area of ground covered by leaves and branches when viewed from above.
    這反映了從上方俯瞰時，樹葉和樹枝所覆蓋的地面面積。
  • decline n. / v. 減少、下降
    This net decline of approximately 13.7 hectares over two years is equivalent to almost 20 soccer fields.
    這兩年間約13.7公頃的淨減少量，相當於將近20座足球場。
  • equivalent adj. 相當的、等同於
    This net decline of approximately 13.7 hectares over two years is equivalent to almost 20 soccer fields.
    這兩年間約13.7公頃的淨減少量，相當於將近20座足球場。
  • attribute to phr. v. 將……歸因於
    The 13.7 hectare decline in tree canopy cannot be directly attributed to trees being cut down by the city government.
    樹冠減少13.7公頃，不能直接歸咎於市政府砍伐樹木。
  • contractor n. 承攬商、包商
    This year, a 40-year-old rosewood tree near Dongmen Market was scheduled to be transplanted, but was instead cut down by a contractor.
    今年，東門市場附近一棵40歲的花櫚木原本預定要被移植，但卻被包商擅自砍伐。
  • penalty n. 懲罰、罰款
    The authorities penalized the contractor and sought NT$325,000 in compensation.
    當局對該包商處以罰款，並要求32.5萬元新台幣的賠償。
  • compensation n. 補償、賠償金
    The authorities penalized the contractor and sought NT$325,000 in compensation.
    當局對該包商處以罰款，並要求32.5萬元新台幣的賠償。
  • measure v. 衡量、測量
    Urban greening should not be measured simply by how many trees have been planted.
    都市綠化不應該單純以種植了多少樹木來衡量。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The most important factor for street shade, urban cooling and the quality of public spaces is the canopy cover built up over years of growth.
    句中的 "built up over years of growth" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "the canopy cover"（樹冠覆蓋），等於形容詞子句 "which is built up..." 的簡化，能讓句子更精簡緊湊。
  • 現在分詞片語表示附帶狀況或結果 (Present Participle Phrase)
    This reflects the area of ground covered by leaves and branches when viewed from above.
    句尾的 "when viewed from above" 是省略了主詞與be動詞的分詞構句（原句為 when it is viewed from above），用來修飾整個動作或提供條件背景。
  • 被動語態的應用 (Passive Voice)
    Tree removal, land development, heavy pruning, transplantation and natural disasters all cause changes in tree canopy.
    文章中常使用被動語態來強調客觀事實或動作承受者（例如："Data on each of the 3,164 trees need to be made available." 或 "was scheduled to be transplanted"），在商業與新聞英語中非常常見。
  • 間接問句/名詞子句作受詞 (Noun Clause as Object)
    To understand what is happening to Taipei’s tree cover, we can look at two sets of numbers...
    "what is happening to Taipei’s tree cover" 是一個由疑問代名詞引導的名詞子句，在句中作為不定詞 "To understand" 的受詞，語法上視為單數第三人稱。
  • 虛主詞 It 的用法 (Dummy Subject 'It')
    It should be established what their species, ages and trunk diameters were...
    句首的 "It" 為虛主詞，真正的主詞是後面的名詞子句 "what their species, ages and trunk diameters were"，此結構常出現在正式或公文報導中，用以平衡句子結構。
