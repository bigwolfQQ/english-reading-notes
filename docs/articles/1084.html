軍方展示「獵豹」戰車

Military shows off Cheetah tank

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • mass production n. 量產
    The Armaments Bureau yesterday showcased the Cheetah (獵豹), a 105mm wheeled armored fighting vehicle, which is to enter mass production next year.
    軍備局昨天展示了105公釐輪型裝甲戰鬥車「獵豹」，該車預計於明年進入量產。
  • jointly adv. 共同、聯合地
    The vehicle, jointly developed by the Armaments Bureau and the National Chungshan Institute of Science and Technology, first passed army tests in 2023.
    該車由軍備局與國家中山科學研究院共同研發，於2023年首次通過軍方測驗。
  • budget n. 預算
    The military has ordered 178 of the vehicles, with production to run from next year through 2033 under a budget of NT$57.07 billion (US$1.8 billion) budget.
    軍方已訂購了178輛該型車輛，生產期程從明年起至2033年止，預算編列新台幣570億7000萬元（約18億美元）。
  • deliver v. 交付、遞送
    The first eight vehicles are expected to be delivered next year, 202nd Arsenal Director Major General Chen Chien-chung (陳健中) said.
    202廠廠長陳健中少將表示，首批8輛車預計於明年交付。
  • demonstrate v. 展示、示範
    At the Army Southern Area Joint Training and Evaluation Center in Tainan, the vehicle demonstrated “rapid synchronized firing upon target acquisition” from a stationary vehicle at a moving target...
    在台南的陸軍南區聯合測考中心展示了「靜止對移動目標進行目標獲得後快速同步射擊」...
  • requirement n. 要求、必要條件
    The Cheetah meets the army’s requirements for operation across all types of terrain, including underpasses and rough roads, and would provide greater convenience and maneuverability in urban combat operations, he said.
    他說，獵豹符合陸軍在各種地形（包括地下道和崎嶇道路）上作戰的要求，並將在城市作戰行動中提供更大的便利性和機動性。
  • component n. 零組件、成分
    Lin Yu-min (林育民), head of the artillery development division at the 202nd Arsenal, said the 105mm gun was developed in-house, with all components, from the barrel to the recoil mechanism, manufactured domestically.
    202廠火砲開發組組長林育民表示，105公釐火砲為自行研發，從砲管到後座力機構的所有零組件均在國內製造。
  • activate v. 啟動、啟用
    While the gunner aims at a first target, the commander can search for a second and, when appropriate, activate “hunter-killer mode” to take control of the main gun and engage it, Lin said.
    林育民表示，當砲手瞄準第一個目標時，車長可以搜尋第二個目標，並在適當時機啟動「獵殺模式」來接管主砲並進行交戰。
  • priority n. 優先事項、首要之務
    Wang Chien-ching (王劍青), a translator with the army’s Training Command, said the net would be given top priority during the first phase of mass production next year.
    陸軍訓練指揮部翻譯官王劍青表示，該防護網將在明年第一階段量產期間被列為最高優先事項。
  • modify v. 修改、更改
    The mount on the remote weapon station would also be modified to accommodate chain guns or automatic cannons of 25mm caliber or larger...
    遙控武器站上的槍架也將進行修改，以容納25公釐或更大口徑的鏈砲或自動加農砲...

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作為後置修飾語 (Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    The vehicle, jointly developed by the Armaments Bureau and the National Chungshan Institute of Science and Technology, first passed army tests in 2023.
    句中的「jointly developed by...」是由過去分詞開頭的片語，用來修飾前面的主詞 The vehicle（意指「被共同研發的車輛」），省略了「which was」，使句子更精簡流暢。
  • with + 名詞 + 分詞/介系詞片語 (附帶狀況複合結構)
    The military has ordered 178 of the vehicles, with production to run from next year through 2033 under a budget of NT$57.07 billion (US$1.8 billion) budget.
    「with + 名詞 + 不定詞 (to run)」構成附帶狀況的獨立結構，用來補充說明前面採購案的生產期程與條件，是商業與新聞英語中常用來附加細節的高級句構。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The first eight vehicles are expected to be delivered next year, 202nd Arsenal Director Major General Chen Chien-chung (陳健中) said.
    句中使用「are expected to be delivered」被動語態，強調「車輛被預期將在明年被交付」，當動作的承受者是主詞、或發動者不明/不須強調時，新聞英語經常使用被動語態。
  • 現在分詞引導的附帶狀況 (Present Participle indicating Circumstance)
    While the gunner aims at a first target, the commander can search for a second and, when appropriate, activate “hunter-killer mode” to take control of the main gun and engage it, Lin said.
    句中結合了連接詞 while 引導的時間子句，並在後方使用對等連接詞 and 連接多個動詞動作（search, activate, take control, engage），展現多重動作同時或依序發生的複雜句型結構。
