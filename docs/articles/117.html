文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Present Participle）作後置修飾語
    A documentary tracing the development of Taiwan’s semiconductor industry over the past five decades was on Thursday screened at a special showing in The Hague.
    句中的「tracing the development...」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「A documentary（紀錄片）」，相當於形容詞子句「that traced...」，用以精鍊地表達該紀錄片所涵蓋的內容。
  • 虛主詞 It 與時間花費句型 (It takes + 某人/某事物 + 期間 + to do sth)
    Tien said that, like Odysseus, it took several decades for Taiwan’s semiconductor industry to overcome obstacles and bring success home...
    此句使用多益常考的「it takes + 時間 + to V」句型。句首的「it」為虛主詞，真正的主持是後方的不定詞片語「to overcome obstacles...」，而「for Taiwan's semiconductor industry」則是不定詞的語意上的主詞。
  • 分詞構句表補充說明 (Present Participle as an Adverbial Clause)
    Taiwan’s technological advancements were the result of a positive talent cultivation environment that was founded upon the respect for democracy and human rights, Tien said, adding that the nations would continue to work with like-minded governments...
    句尾的「adding that...」是由連接詞引導的現在分詞構句，主動修飾前句主詞 Tien 的動作，表示「一邊說著……、同時補充表示……」，常用於新聞英文中延續引述內容。
  • 關係代名詞子句（Relative Clause）修飾先行詞
    The film is Taiwan’s first documentary spotlighting its world-leading semiconductor industry and chronicling the nation’s rise from underdog to becoming the central hub of the global chip supply chain over the past five decades.
    雖然本句在 documentary 後直接接分詞，但句中另一處使用了關係代名詞子句「that was founded upon...」（第10段）來修飾 environment，用來補充說明該人才培養環境所建立的基礎。
