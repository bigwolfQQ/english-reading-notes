單字解析

  • cooperation n. 合作
    A senior member of the Japan Innovation Party (JIP) on Tuesday called for closer Tokyo-Taipei cooperation, including on drone and space-based technologies...
    日本維新會（JIP）的一位資深成員於週二呼籲深化東京與台北之間的合作，包括在無人機與太空技術方面的合作...
  • delegation n. 代表團
    Former JIP leader Nobuyuki Baba said at a press event in Taipei that he and a delegation of JIP lawmakers met with President Lai Ching-te... earlier Tuesday...
    日本維新會前黨魁馬場伸幸在台北的一場記者會上表示，他和由維新會議員組成的事務代表團於週二稍早會見了總統賴清德...
  • bilateral adj. 雙邊的
    ...discussed bilateral relations, U.S.-China relations and other issues.
    ...討論了雙邊關係、美中關係及其他議題。
  • infrastructure n. 基礎設施
    ...causing widespread infrastructure damage and transportation disruptions and killing at least 38 people.
    ...造成廣泛的基礎設施損壞與交通中斷，並導致至少38人死亡。
  • campaign n. 活動、競選運動
    In addition to the fundraising campaign, the Taiwan government donated NT$5 million to support reconstruction efforts...
    除了募款活動外，台灣政府也捐贈新台幣500萬元，以支援重建工作...
  • reconstruction n. 重建
    The July 28 earthquake in Kumamoto Prefecture also prompted Taiwanese people to donate to disaster relief and reconstruction efforts in Japan, Lai said.
    賴總統說，7月28日熊本縣的地震也促使台灣人民捐款協助日本的災害救援與重建工作。
  • partnership n. 夥伴關係
    Lai said Taiwan looks forward to an even stronger partnership between the two countries under the LDP-JIP coalition...
    賴總統表示，台灣期盼在自民黨與維新會的執政聯盟下，兩國能有更緊密的夥伴關係...
  • enhance v. 提高、增強
    ...thereby enhancing the resilience of global democratic supply chains and strengthening economic and trade cooperation.
    ...進而提升全球民主供應鏈的韌性，並強化經貿合作。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Phrase as Modifier / Adverbial）表附帶結果或補充說明
    ...causing widespread infrastructure damage and transportation disruptions and killing at least 38 people.
    句中的 "causing... and killing..." 是現在分詞片語，置於句尾用來補充說明前面主句所引發的結果（地震造成了破壞並奪走人命），使語意緊湊，不需使用額外的連接詞。
  • In addition to... 介系詞片語表示「除了……之外」
    In addition to the fundraising campaign, the Taiwan government donated NT$5 million to support reconstruction efforts...
    "In addition to" 是複合介系詞，後面接名詞或動名詞（在此接名詞片語 the fundraising campaign），用來表達「除了前述的事物之外，還包含……」，是多益閱讀與寫作測驗中極常見的轉折片語。
  • 過去分詞作後位修飾語（Past Participle as Postmodifier）
    ...a bond truly tested and proven in times of adversity...
    片語 "tested and proven in times of adversity" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "a bond"（連結）。省略了「which is/was」，使句子更為精鍊，具備標準的新聞英語寫作風格。
