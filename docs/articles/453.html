台積電受惠於強勁AI需求，8月單月營收創歷史新高

TSMC posts record monthly revenue for August on strong AI demand

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • contract chipmaker n. phr. 合約晶片製造商，晶圓代工廠
    Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. (TSMC), the world's largest contract chipmaker, on Thursday reported record monthly revenue for August...
    全球最大晶圓代工廠台灣積體電路製造股份有限公司（台積電）於週四公佈，8月單月營收創下歷史新高...
  • revenue n. 營收，收益
    ...on Thursday reported record monthly revenue for August, up more than 53 percent from a year earlier...
    ...於週四公佈，8月單月營收創下歷史新高，較去年同期成長超過53%...
  • driven by v. phr. 受...驅動，由...帶動
    ...driven by robust demand linked to artificial intelligence (AI) and new smartphone launches, according to company data and analysts.
    ...受人工智慧（AI）相關強勁需求及新智慧型手機上市的帶動，根據公司數據與分析師表示。
  • robust adj. 強勁的，穩健的
    ...driven by robust demand linked to artificial intelligence (AI) and new smartphone launches...
    ...受人工智慧（AI）相關強勁需求及新智慧型手機上市的帶動...
  • statement n. 聲明，發言
    In a statement, TSMC said consolidated revenue for August reached a record high...
    台積電在聲明中表示，8月合併營收達到歷史新高...
  • consolidated revenue n. phr. 合併營收
    In a statement, TSMC said consolidated revenue for August reached a record high of NT$514.806 billion...
    台積電在聲明中表示，8月合併營收達到新台幣5,148億6,000萬元的歷史新高...
  • cumulative adj. 累積的，漸增的
    Cumulative revenue for the first eight months of this year totaled NT$3.387 trillion...
    今年前八個月的累計營收總計達新台幣3兆3,870億元...
  • total v. 總計，總達
    Cumulative revenue for the first eight months of this year totaled NT$3.387 trillion, an increase of 39.3 percent from the same period last year...
    今年前八個月的累計營收總計達新台幣3兆3,870億元，較去年同期增加39.3%...
  • guidance n. （企業對未來業績的）預測，指導方針
    ...putting TSMC on track to meet and possibly exceed its third-quarter guidance, analysts said.
    ...這讓台積電有望達成並可能超越其第三季的財測目標，分析師說。
  • forecast v. 預測，預報
    The company has forecast third-quarter revenue of US$44.6 billion to US$45.8 billion...
    該公司先前預測第三季營收將介於446億美元至458億美元之間...
  • exchange rate n. phr. 匯率
    ...based on an assumed exchange rate of NT$32 to the U.S. dollar.
    ...基於新台幣兌美元32比1的假設匯率。
  • capital expenditure n. phr. 資本支出
    To support growing customer demand, the chipmaker has also raised its planned 2026 capital expenditure to between US$60 billion and US$64 billion.
    為了因應不斷成長的客戶需求，這家晶圓製造商也已將其2026年計畫資本支出調高至600億美元至640億美元之間。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...driven by robust demand linked to artificial intelligence (AI) and new smartphone launches...
    句中的 "driven by..." 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的 "record monthly revenue"（當作被動意義的補充說明，省略了 "which was driven by"），使句子更簡潔精煉，是多益閱讀測驗常見的高階句構。
  • 分詞構句作附帶說明 (Participial Phrase indicating Concurrently/Result)
    Combined revenue for July and August reached NT$982.386 billion, putting TSMC on track to meet and possibly exceed its third-quarter guidance, analysts said.
    句尾的 ", putting TSMC on track..." 是現在分詞（-ing）引導的分詞構句，用來表達前一個子句所產生的結果（相當於 "and it puts TSMC on track..."），在商業財經新聞中常用於描述數據帶來的實質影響或趨勢。
  • 介系詞片語作條件/依據 (Prepositional Phrase of Basis)
    ...based on an assumed exchange rate of NT$32 to the U.S. dollar.
    句中的 "based on..."（基於……）是常用的分詞介系詞片語，用來修飾前方預測金額的計算基礎或前提條件，在商業合約、財報與預測報導中頻繁出現。
