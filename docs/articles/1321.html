Tsai receives warm welcome in San Francisco

單字解析

  • arrive v. 到達，抵達
    Former president Tsai Ing-wen (蔡英文) on Monday arrived in San Francisco...
    前總統蔡英文週一抵達舊金山...
  • launch v. 發起，展開（活動、計畫等）
    ...as she launched a week-long visit.
    ...並展開為期一週的訪問。
  • deliver v. 發表（演說），講話
    Tsai is today to deliver the opening keynote speech at the Stanford Conference on Technology and National Security.
    蔡英文今日將在史丹佛大學科技與國家安全會議上發表開幕主題演說。
  • announce v. 宣布，宣告
    The Stanford Gordian Knot Center for National Security Innovation announced the event on LinkedIn last week...
    史丹佛大學戈迪安紐結國家安全創新中心上週在 LinkedIn 上宣布了這項活動...
  • itinerary n. 行程，旅行日程
    The address is one of the few events on Tsai’s itinerary that her office has said would be open to the public.
    這場演說是大衛辦公室表示將對公眾開放的蔡英文行程中少數活動之一。
  • registration n. 登記，註冊，報名
    ...all registration slots have been filled.
    ...所有報名名額均已額滿。
  • attend v. 出席，參加
    Tsai tomorrow is to attend a forum on democracy, resilience and global security...
    蔡英文明天將出席一場關於民主、韌性與全球安全的論壇...
  • organize v. 組織，籌辦
    ...a forum on democracy, resilience and global security jointly organized by the Hoover Institution’s Taiwan in the Indo-Pacific Region program...
    ...一場由胡佛研究所的「印太地區中的臺灣」計畫共同舉辦的關於民主、韌性與全球安全的論壇...
  • express v. 表達，表示
    Tsai expressed her gratitude for their warm welcome and support for Taiwan.
    蔡英文對他們的熱烈歡迎以及對臺灣的支持表達了感謝。
  • gratitude n. 謝意，感謝
    Tsai expressed her gratitude for their warm welcome and support for Taiwan.
    蔡英文對他們的熱烈歡迎以及對臺灣的支持表達了感謝。
  • opportunity n. 機會，時機
    “In the coming days, I will continue to seize every opportunity to interact with more friends...
    「在接下来的幾天裡，我將繼續把握每一個機會與更多朋友互動...」
  • schedule v. 預定，安排
    Tsai is scheduled to return to Taiwan on Sunday.
    蔡英文預定於週日返回臺灣。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附帶狀況/結果狀語
    ...receiving a warm welcome from hundreds of overseas Taiwanese as she launched a week-long visit.
    句首主詞 Tsai 抵達舊金山，逗號後面的 「receiving a warm welcome...」是由現在分詞片語引導的結構，用來補充說明主詞在抵達時同時發生的動作或狀況，相當於 and received a warm welcome 的簡化。
  • 過去分詞片語作分詞構句（被動語態）
    Accompanied by Representative to the US Alexander Yui... and head of Taiwan’s San Francisco office Wu Chih-hsiang..., Tsai arrived at her hotel...
    「Accompanied by...」是由過去分詞片語開頭的分詞構句，原句意指「因為被...所陪同」，省略了主詞和連接詞，用來修飾主詞 Tsai，在商業新聞與正式寫作中非常常見。
  • be to + V 表未來預定計畫
    Tsai is today to deliver the opening keynote speech at the Stanford Conference on Technology and National Security.
    「is to deliver」結構常出現在新聞報導中，用來表示官方或正式排定的未來計畫、行程或安排，語氣比單純使用未來式更具確定性。
  • 關代受格省略的形容詞子句
    The address is one of the few events on Tsai’s itinerary that her office has said would be open to the public.
    句中的「that her office has said would be open to the public」是用來修飾先行詞 events 的形容詞子句。子句中的動詞 said 後面其實省略了名詞子句的連接詞 that，這在多益閱讀測驗中是極為常考的文法點。
