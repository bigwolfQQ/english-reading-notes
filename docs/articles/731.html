台灣死刑犯因病身亡；尚餘 34 人

Taiwan death row inmate dies of illness; 34 remain

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • convict v. 宣判有罪、定罪
    Death row inmate Lin Wang-jen (林旺仁), who was convicted of setting fire to a karaoke bar that killed five people in 2004, died of an illness on Monday, authorities confirmed.
    當局證實，因2004年縱火燒卡拉OK店造成5人死亡而被定罪的死刑犯林旺仁，於週一因病過世。
  • confirm v. 證實、確認
    With Lin's passing, the number of inmates on death row has dropped to 34, correctional authorities confirmed late Monday, after local media reported that the prisoner had died in hospital.
    矯正署週一深夜在當地媒體報導該囚犯在醫院死亡後證實，隨著林員離世，死刑犯人數已降至34人。
  • sentence v. / n. 宣判、判決 / 刑期
    Lin, an Yilan County resident, was sentenced to death in a 2004 case that reached the Supreme Court, which in 2011 upheld his death sentence for murder.
    宜蘭縣民林旺仁在2004年的案件中被判處死刑，該案上訴至最高法院，最高法院於2011年維持其謀殺死刑判決。
  • petition n. / v. 請願（書）、訴願
    In 2022, Lin was among 14 death row inmates who filed a petition seeking a presidential pardon or commutation.
    2022年，林旺仁是提出請願尋求總統特赦或減刑的14名死刑犯之一。
  • file v. 提出（申請、訴訟等）、歸檔
    The petition was filed with the Taipei High Administrative Court, which rejected it, saying it had no jurisdiction over the matter.
    該請願書向台北高等行政法院提出，但遭到駁回，法院表示對此案沒有管轄權。
  • reject v. 拒絕、駁回
    Both requests were ultimately rejected by the courts.
    這兩項請求最終都被法院駁回。
  • jurisdiction n. 司法權、管轄權
    The petition was filed with the Taipei High Administrative Court, which rejected it, saying it had no jurisdiction over the matter.
    該請願書向台北高等行政法院提出，但遭到駁回，法院表示對此案沒有管轄權。
  • ultimately adv. 最終、最後
    Both requests were ultimately rejected by the courts.
    這兩項請求最終都被法院駁回。
  • suspend v. 暫緩、中止、停職
    Lin in 2015 filed for a retrial and sought a suspension of his execution based on the ruling.
    林旺仁於2015年基於該裁定聲請再審並尋求暫緩執行死刑。
  • demise n. 死亡、崩潰、倒閉
    Lin's demise follows that of another death row inmate, Ouyang Jung (歐陽榕) , who died of an illness in January.
    林旺仁的過世繼另一名死刑犯歐陽榕之後，後者於1月因病身亡。

文法重點

  • 非限定關係代名詞子句（補充說明）
    Death row inmate Lin Wang-jen (林旺仁), who was convicted of setting fire to a karaoke bar that killed five people in 2004, died of an illness on Monday, authorities confirmed.
    句中的「who was convicted of...」前方有逗號，屬於非限定用法，用來補充說明主詞 Lin Wang-jen 的背景身分。在多益閱讀測驗中，常考這種逗號後方接關係代名詞的結構，考生需學會辨識主句的核心動詞（本句為 died）。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾
    With Lin's passing, the number of inmates on death row has dropped to 34, correctional authorities confirmed late Monday, after local media reported that the prisoner had died in hospital.
    片語「on death row」作為形容詞片語，放在名詞 inmates 後方修飾，意指「死刑犯」。多益考試中經常出現介系詞片語後位修飾名詞的考點。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Both requests were ultimately rejected by the courts.
    句型採用「be +過去分詞 (were rejected)」，強調動作的承受者（requests）而非動作的執行者（courts）。多益文法題經常考主動與被動語態的選擇，特別是在描述法律程序、公司決策時多用被動語態。
