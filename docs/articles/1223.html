特使就停滯不前的對台軍售敦促美國

Envoy prods US on stalled arms sale

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • representative n. 代表
    Taiwan’s representative to the US projected confidence that US President Donald Trump would ultimately approve additional weapons sales to Taiwan
    台灣駐美代表對美國總統川普最終會批准對台額外武器銷售表示信心
  • approve v. 批准、贊成
    US President Donald Trump would ultimately approve additional weapons sales to Taiwan
    美國總統川普最終會批准對台額外武器銷售
  • package n. 一攬子計畫、包裹
    Taiwan, which has been waiting for a US$14 billion arms package.
    台灣目前正等待價值 140 億美元的軍售包裹。
  • substantial adj. 重大的、大量的
    Air defenses make up a substantial share of the proposed package
    防空系統佔了擬議軍售包裹中的相當大比例
  • determination n. 決定、決心
    Trump would make a determination on the arms package in a fairly short time
    川普將在相當短的時間內對軍售包裹做出決定
  • negotiating chip n. phr. 談判籌碼
    Trump has previously described arms sales to Taiwan as a “very good negotiating chip” in his dealings with Beijing
    川普先前將對台軍售描述為他與北京打交道時的一個「非常好的談判籌碼」
  • relinquish v. 放棄（權利、主權等）
    China did not sign the treaty, which forced Japan to relinquish its claims to Taiwan
    中國沒有簽署該條約，該條約迫使日本放棄對台灣的主權聲索
  • defuse v. 化解、緩和（緊張局勢）
    As US officials look to defuse concerns around support for Taipei
    正當美國官員試圖化解外界對台北支持力度的擔憂時
  • indispensable adj. 不可或缺的
    making it indispensable to the artificial intelligence boom
    這使得台灣對於人工智慧的蓬勃發展不可或缺
  • intervene v. 介入、干涉
    while intentionally leaving ambiguous whether US forces would intervene militarily in a conflict.
    同時刻意保持模糊，不表態若發生衝突美軍是否會進行軍事介入。

文法重點

  • 非限定性關係代間子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Taiwan’s representative to the US projected confidence that US President Donald Trump would ultimately approve additional weapons sales to Taiwan, which has been waiting for a US$14 billion arms package.
    句中的「which has been waiting for a US$14 billion arms package」前面有逗號，用來補充說明前面的先行詞 Taiwan（台灣）。非限定性關係子句不能用 that，必須使用 which 或 who，且通常帶有附加的補充資訊。
  • 分詞片語作後置修飾 (Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Air defenses make up a substantial share of the proposed package, people familiar with the matter have said.
    句中的「familiar with the matter」是一個形容詞片語，修飾前面的名詞 people。這類結構省略了關代與be動詞（即 people [who are] familiar with the matter），在新聞英語中非常常見，能讓句子更精鍊。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    China did not sign the treaty, which forced Japan to relinquish its claims to Taiwan without specifying which government would assume sovereignty over the island.
    句中的「which forced Japan to relinquish...」使用了主動語態來表達條約迫使日本放棄權利。而多數新聞報導會頻繁使用被動語態（如 be expected to、is reported by）來強調動作的承受者或客觀性，例如第 12 段的「first reported by National Public Radio」。
