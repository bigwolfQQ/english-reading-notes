南韓「下垂」的珊瑚讓潛水員憂心

‘Slumping’ S Korean corals worry divers

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • collapse verb / noun 倒塌、崩潰、瓦解
    Watching the coral garden collapse and turn white, it was so disorienting, Ko said...
    高明孝說：「看著珊瑚花園崩塌並變成白色，真的讓人感到非常迷失。」
  • cluster noun 叢、群、聚集
    Roughly half of one cluster off the islet of Beomseom has been struggling in past months, the group said.
    該團體表示，法島外海的一處珊瑚叢中，大約有一半在過去幾個月裡一直在苦苦掙扎。
  • disrupt verb 破壞、擾亂
    Environmental changes can disrupt that pressure, leading them to sag or collapse.
    環境變化會破壞這種壓力，導致牠們下垂或倒塌。
  • deteriorate verb 惡化、變壞
    ...corals partially recover and then a lot break off and deteriorate again...
    ...珊瑚部分恢復後，又有許多斷裂並再次惡化...
  • manage verb 管理、經營
    In an e-mail, Korea Heritage Service, which manages the soft coral colonies designated a national monument...
    管理被指定為國家紀念物的軟珊瑚群落的韓國遺產廳在一封電子郵件中表示...
  • investigation noun 調查
    ...added that it plans detailed investigation to gauge the scale of damage.
    ...補充說該部門計劃進行詳細調查以評估受損規模。
  • persist verb 持續、堅持
    ...but could worsen should high water temperatures persist through this month...
    ...但如果高水溫持續到本月，情況可能會惡化...
  • consistent adjective 一致的、連續的
    However, he called for consistent and quantitative monitoring of the loss of soft corals through slumping...
    不過，他呼籲對因下垂導致的軟珊瑚損失進行一致且定量的監測...
  • abundant adjective 豐富的、充裕的
    ...when species such as turban shells, abalone and sea cucumber were abundant and thick seaweed festooned the rocks.
    ...當時夜光螺、鮑魚和海參等物種十分豐富，濃密的巨藻裝飾著岩石。
  • formerly adverb 以前、從前
    ...and her workday has shrunk to three hours from five formerly, as there is less to gather...
    ...而且由於可採集的東西變少，她的工作時間已經從過去的五小時縮短到三小時...

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表附帶狀況或結果）
    “Watching the coral garden collapse and turn white, it was so disorienting,” Ko said after two years of rising temperatures and harsher weather brought by climate change have led to many corals slumping over and dying.
    句首的 "Watching..." 為現在分詞片語引導的分詞構句，表示主詞 Ko 在說話時的同時動作或狀態。而句尾的 "...and dying" 則是對應前面的分詞或連接動態，說明珊瑚不僅下垂，還同時走向死亡。
  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    The “slumping” phenomenon is showing up again this year, said the Paran Ocean Citizen Science Center, a group of divers who monitor the island’s UNESCO-listed soft corals...
    句中 "who monitor the island’s UNESCO-listed soft corals..." 為主格關係代名詞 who 引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的先行詞 divers（潛水員），補充說明這群人的職責與任務。
  • 省略 if 的假設語氣倒裝句
    ...but could worsen should high water temperatures persist through this month...
    原句 "should high water temperatures persist..." 是條件句 "If high water temperatures should persist..." 的省略 if 倒裝句法。當條件句中有助動詞 should 時，可將 should 提到主語前面以增添正式語氣。
  • 被動語態與過去分詞作後置修飾
    ...Korea Heritage Service, which manages the soft coral colonies designated a national monument...
    句中的 "designated a national monument" 是過去分詞片語作後置修飾語，修飾前面的 colonies（群落），其完整結構隱含了「which are designated as a national monument（被指定為國家紀念物）」的被動與關代省略概念。
