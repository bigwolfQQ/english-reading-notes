單字解析

  • holiday weekend n.片語 連假週末
    What’s your plan for the three-day “Double Ten” National Day holiday weekend starting tomorrow?
    明天開始的為期三天「雙十節」國慶連假週末，你有什麼計畫？
  • is set to v.片語 預定、即將
    The South Korean supergroup BigBang is set to stage three shows at the Taipei Dome.
    南韓超級天團BigBang即將在台北大巨蛋舉辦三場演出。
  • stage v. 舉辦、上演、策劃
    The South Korean supergroup BigBang is set to stage three shows at the Taipei Dome.
    南韓超級天團BigBang即將在台北大巨蛋舉辦三場演出。
  • in advance adv.片語 事先、預先
    Since I became a BigBang VIP member in advance, I could participate in the “presale.”
    因為我事先成為了BigBang的VIP會員，所以我可以參與「預售」。
  • participate in v.片語 參加、參與
    Since I became a BigBang VIP member in advance, I could participate in the “presale.”
    因為我事先成為了BigBang的VIP會員，所以我可以參與「預售」。
  • presale n. 預售、預售票
    Since I became a BigBang VIP member in advance, I could participate in the “presale.”
    因為我事先成為了BigBang的VIP會員，所以我可以參與「預售」。
  • record n. 紀錄
    But the highest ticket price of NT$9,430 was so expensive that it even broke the Taipei Dome record.
    但高達9,430元台幣的最高票價實在太貴了，甚至打破了台北大巨蛋的紀錄。
  • anniversary n. 週年紀念
    Having debuted in 2006, BigBang is celebrating their 20th anniversary.
    於2006年出道，BigBang正在慶祝他們的20週年。
  • reunite v. 重聚、再結合
    Seeing G-Dragon, Tae-yang and Dae-sung reunite again will make this experience truly worthwhile.
    看到G-Dragon、太陽和大聲再度合體，會讓這趟體驗非常值得。
  • worthwhile adj. 值得的
    Seeing G-Dragon, Tae-yang and Dae-sung reunite again will make this experience truly worthwhile.

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語表原因（分詞構句）
    Having debuted in 2006, BigBang is celebrating their 20th anniversary.
    句首的 "Having debuted in 2006" 是現在分詞的完成式片語，用來修飾主詞 BigBang，表示「在 2006 年出道」這個動作發生在主要子句「慶祝 20 週年」之前，相當於副詞子句 "Since they debuted in 2006..." 的簡化。
  • so... that... 如此……以至於……
    But the highest ticket price of NT$9,430 was so expensive that it even broke the Taipei Dome record.
    此句型用 "so + 形容詞/副詞 + that子句" 來表達極端的程度及其造成的結果。"so expensive"（如此昂貴）後面接 "that it even broke..."（以至於它甚至打破了……）。
  • be set to do sth 預定/準備要做某事
    The South Korean supergroup BigBang is set to stage three shows at the Taipei Dome.
    "be set to" 常用於新聞英語中，表示某個活動或計畫已經安排妥當、即將發生，等同於 "is scheduled to" 或 "is about to"。
