歐洲官員訪問期間讚賞蕭美琴為「榜樣」

European official hails ‘role model’ Hsiao during visit

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • invitation n. 邀請
    European Parliament Vice President Pina Picierno on Saturday praised Vice President Hsiao Bi-khim (蕭美琴) as a role model for freedom-loving women around the world and said she would soon begin planning a trip to Taipei after accepting Hsiao’s invitation to visit.
    歐洲議會副議長皮內爾齊爾諾於週六讚揚副總統蕭美琴為全世界愛好自由女性的榜樣，並表示在接受蕭美琴的訪問邀請後，她將很快開始規劃訪問台北的行程。
  • organizer n. 主辦單位、組織者
    Hsiao visited Italy to attend the Second Ventotene European Conference for Freedom and Democracy on the Italian island of Ventotene at the invitation of event organizers, despite strong protests from Beijing.
    蕭美琴應活動主辦單位邀請，前往義大利溫托滕島出席第二屆溫托滕自由與民主歐洲會議，儘管北京方面發出強烈抗議。
  • protest n. 抗議
    The Italy trip once again infuriated the Chinese government, prompting Beijing to lodge protests with Italy and the EU.
    這次義大利之行再次激怒了中國政府，促使北京向義大利和歐盟提出抗議。
  • concern v. / n. 擔心、關心
    Asked if she was concerned about her personal safety from China, Picierno said she had already been under the protection of Italian security details for more than a year due to threats against her from Russia and other authoritarian regimes.
    當被問及是否擔心來自中國的人身安全時，皮內爾齊爾諾表示，由於來自俄羅斯和其他威權政權的威脅，她已經在義大利維安人員的保護下生活了一年多。
  • protection n. 保護
    Asked if she was concerned about her personal safety from China, Picierno said she had already been under the protection of Italian security details for more than a year due to threats against her from Russia and other authoritarian regimes.
    當被問及是否擔心來自中國的人身安全時，皮內爾齊爾諾表示，由於來自俄羅斯和其他威權政權的威脅，她已經在義大利維安人員的保護下生活了一年多。
  • agenda n. 議程、待辦事項
    The clear message we are sending from Ventotene is that we do not fear threats or intimidation; the European Parliament’s agenda is determined neither in Moscow nor in Beijing.
    我們從溫托滕發出的明確訊息是，我們不畏懼威脅或恐嚇；歐洲議會的議程既不由莫斯科決定，也不由北京決定。
  • compromise n. / v. 妥協、妥協方案
    Picierno said she believes there is absolutely “no room for compromise with authoritarian regimes that violate human rights.”
    皮內爾齊爾諾表示，她認為對於「侵犯人權的威權政權，絕對沒有妥協的空間」。
  • collaborate v. 合作（衍生字 collaboration n.）
    Our collaboration — between myself and the vice president of Taiwan — is just beginning; there is much to be done and much to be discussed in the future.
    我們之間的合作——我和台灣副總統之間的合作——才剛開始；未來有許多事情要做，也有許多議題要討論。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況或結果（Present Participle as Adverbial Clause）
    The Italy trip once again infuriated the Chinese government, prompting Beijing to lodge protests with Italy and the EU.
    句尾的「prompting Beijing to...」是由分詞構句（prompting 代替原先的 and prompted）所構成，用來表達前面主句動作（義大利之行激怒中國政府）所自然引發的後續結果（促使北京向義大利和歐盟抗議）。
  • 被動語態（Passive Voice）
    The clear message we are sending from Ventotene is that we do not fear threats or intimidation; the European Parliament’s agenda is determined neither in Moscow nor in Beijing.
    句子中的「is determined」使用了被動語態（be + 過去分詞），強調「歐洲議會的議程」是承受「被決定」的動作，主體是議程本身而非決定議程的人。搭配「neither... nor...」結構表示兩者皆非。
  • 關係代間接引句 / 名詞子句（Noun Clause with Whether/If）
    Asked if she was concerned about her personal safety from China, Picierno said she had already been under the protection of Italian security details for more than a year...
    句首的「Asked if...」省略了主詞與be動詞（When she was asked if...），其中「if」引導一個名詞子句，當作「問」的受詞，用來表達「是否」關心個人安全的疑問內容。
