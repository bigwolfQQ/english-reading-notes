Taiwanese artist receives Primetime Emmy for animation

單字解析

  • achieve v. 獲得，達成，實現
    Chen won for Outstanding Individual Achievement in Animation for his background design...
    陳人豪以其背景設計贏得動畫類傑出個人成就獎……
  • production company n. phr. 製作公司
    After graduating in 2011, he worked with the production company Motion Theory...
    2011 年畢業後，他曾與製作公司 Motion Theory 合作……
  • studio n. 工作室，攝影棚
    ...before beginning his career in animation with Titmouse animation studio.
    ……之後在 Titmouse 動畫工作室展開他的動畫職涯。
  • participate v. 參加，參與
    ...participating in different styles of animated productions.
    ……參與了各種風格的動畫製作。
  • passion n. 熱情，熱愛
    Chen said his passion for animation began when he was a child...
    陳人豪表示，他對動畫的熱情始於小時候……
  • relly on phr. v. 依賴，依靠
    ...without relying heavily on text and dialogue.
    ……而不需過度依賴文字與對話。
  • appeal v. 有吸引力，對……產生興趣
    Animation is appealing, as “a team works together to create an imagined world,” Chen said.
    陳人豪指出，動畫之所以吸引人，是因為「一個團隊齊心協力創造出一個想像的世界」。
  • consider v. 考慮，考量
    ...while also considering the world’s history, culture, geography and building materials.
    ……同時也考量該世界的歷史、文化、地理與建築材料。
  • enhance v. 提高，增加，增強
    Backgrounds should enhance storytelling without grabbing attention...
    背景應該在不搶走注意力的情況下提升故事性……
  • recognition n. 讚賞，認可，承認
    ...saying he felt surprised and grateful for the recognition.
    ……並表示他對這項肯定感到驚喜與感激。

文法重點

  • 非限定關係代名詞子句（補充說明）
    Artist Howard Chen (陳人豪), who earlier this month won a Primetime Emmy Award, said the cartoons he grew up with in Taiwan planted the seeds for his career in the US.
    句中的「who earlier this month won a Primetime Emmy Award」為非限定關係子句（前方有逗號），用來補充說明主詞 Artist Howard Chen 的身分，拿掉這個子句不影響主要句意。
  • 現在完成式 (Present Perfect Tense)
    He has worked in character and background design over the past 15 years, participating in different styles of animated productions.
    「has worked」使用現在完成式，搭配時間副詞片語「over the past 15 years」（過去 15 年來），表示這個動作從過去某個時間點開始，持續進行到現在。
  • 對等連接詞 as 引導原因子句
    Chen struggled when he first arrived in the US, as he did not speak any English, Chen told the Central News Agency yesterday in an interview at his alma mater.
    在此句中，「as」相當於「because」，用來引導原因子句（as he did not speak any English），解釋他剛到美國時遇到挫折的原因。
  • 現在分詞片語作附加說明
    Backgrounds should enhance storytelling without grabbing attention, Chen said, adding that they should blend seamlessly into the story...
    句尾的「adding that...」是由現在分詞（-ing）所引導的片語，用來補充說明主句主詞 Chen 所說的話，相當於對話中「並補充說……」的概念。
