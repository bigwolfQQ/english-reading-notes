海巡署查獲16.2億元毒品，創下十年來最大規模大麻查緝案

CGA seizes NT$1.62 billion worth of drugs in largest marijuana bust in a decade

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • seize v. 查獲、沒收、扣押
    The Coast Guard Administration (CGA) yesterday at a news conference displayed 815kg of marijuana and other narcotics seized on June 3, marking Taiwan’s largest marijuana bust in a decade.
    海洋委員會海巡署昨天在記者會上展示了6月3日查獲的815公斤大麻及其他毒品，創下台灣近十年來最大規模的大麻查緝紀錄。
  • estimate v. / n. 估計、估價
    The drugs had an estimated market value of nearly NT$1.62 billion, the CGA said.
    海巡署表示，這批毒品的估計市價將近新台幣16.2億元。
  • investigation n. 調查
    The CGA said the investigation was led by its Keelung Enforcement Team under the direction of the Keelung District Prosecutors' Office...
    海巡署表示，此案是由其基隆查緝隊在基隆地方檢察署指揮下主導偵辦...
  • deploy v. 部署、調動
    ...and deployed teams along the coast of Keelung and in New Taipei City’s Rueifang District (瑞芳), it said.
    ...並在基隆海岸及新北市瑞芳區沿線部署人力。
  • transfer v. 轉移、調動、轉運
    ...as suspects were transferring items from the truck to a passenger car.
    ...當時嫌犯正將物品從貨車搬運到一台乘用车。
  • arrest v. 逮捕、拘留
    CGA personnel arrested three suspects at the scene and later tracked down four others...
    海巡人員當場逮捕三名嫌犯，隨後循線追查另外四人...
  • refer v. 提交、送交、參考
    The cases were referred to prosecutors for investigation on charges of contravening the Narcotics Hazard Prevention Act...
    全案已依違反《毒品危害防制條例》移送檢方偵辦...
  • indict v. 控告、起訴
    Prosecutors indicted the suspects on Aug. 24, it added.
    海巡署並補充，檢察官已於8月24日將嫌犯起訴。
  • attend v. 出席、參加
    Premier Cho Jung-tai (卓榮泰), who attended yesterday’s news conference, said...
    出席昨日記者會的行政院長卓榮泰表示...
  • quantity n. 數量
    ...Cho said the large quantity of drugs could have harmed students’ physical and mental health...
    ...卓榮泰指出，該批大量毒品可能已危害學生的身心健康...
  • expertise n. 專業知識、專長
    Cho thanked frontline law enforcement officers for their patience and expertise in carrying out the interagency drug crackdown.
    卓榮泰感謝第一線執法官員在執行跨機關掃毒行動展現的耐心與專業。
  • pledge v. / n. 保證、承諾
    He pledged the government would not only reward their efforts, but would also provide stronger support...
    他承諾政府不僅會獎勵他們的努力，還會提供更強而有力的支持...
  • upgrade v. / n. 升級、提升
    ...by increasing staff and upgrading equipment and technology...
    ...透過增加人力以及升級設備與技術...
  • resource n. 資源
    ...ensuring that frontline personnel have the resources they need to combat drug trafficking.
    ...確保第一線同仁擁有打擊販毒所需的資源。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The Coast Guard Administration (CGA) yesterday at a news conference displayed 815kg of marijuana and other narcotics seized on June 3...
    句中的「seized on June 3」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「815kg of marijuana and other narcotics」（被查獲的大麻與其他毒品），相當於形容詞子句「which were seized on June 3」的簡化形式，多益閱讀測驗常考此類分詞簡化結構。
  • 現在分詞表達附帶結果或動作 (Present Participle as Circumstance)
    ...marking Taiwan’s largest marijuana bust in a decade.
    句尾的「marking...」是由現在分詞構成的分詞構句，用來補充說明前面整起事件所帶來的結果或意義（意指「這標誌著……」），能讓句子更簡鍊流暢。
  • 與過去事實相反的假設語氣 (Subjunctive Conditional for Past)
    ...Cho said the large quantity of drugs could have harmed students’ physical and mental health had the drugs made their way into schools and society.
    此句表達對過去假設情況的推論。「had the drugs made their way...」是條件句省略 if 的倒裝句型（原句為 if the drugs had made their way...），主句則使用「could have + 過去分詞（could have harmed）」，用來假設「假使當初毒品流入校園（事實上沒有），本來可能會造成危害」。
  • 現在分詞領導附帶狀況的獨立分詞構句 (Present Participle Phrase of Concomitant Circumstance)
    Noting that students across Taiwan were finishing the school year and preparing for summer vacation in June, Cho said...
    句首的「Noting that...」為現在分詞片語，其邏輯主詞為主句的主語 Cho。這個結構表達「在注意到……的同時 / 鑑於……」，用來交代說話者發表該言論時的背景脈絡。
