單字解析

  • pronounce v. 宣告，宣判
    A tourist from Myanmar was pronounced dead at a hospital Sunday...
    一名來自緬甸的遊客週日在醫院被宣告死亡……
  • unconscious adj. 失去知覺的，昏迷的
    ...after being found unconscious in a shallow-water wave pool...
    ……在淺水造浪池被發現昏迷後……
  • branch n. 分公司，分部，分院
    ...before being taken to the Dajia branch of Lee's General Hospital...
    ……在被送到李綜合醫院大甲分院之前……
  • refer v. 移交，送交，參考
    Police said on Monday that they will refer the case to prosecutors for an autopsy...
    警方於週一表示，他們將把此案移送檢察官進行相驗……
  • operator n. 營運商，經營者
    The park operator expressed its condolences on Monday over the man's death...
    該樂園營運商週一對該男子的死訊表達哀悼……
  • condolences n. 哀悼，弔唁
    The park operator expressed its condolences on Monday over the man's death...
    該樂園營運商週一對該男子的死訊表達哀悼……
  • investigation n. 調查
    ...but said the cause of the incident remained under investigation.
    ……但表示事件原因仍在調查中。
  • decline v. 婉拒，謝絕，下降
    It declined to provide further details, citing the privacy of the visitor and his family.
    基於遊客及其家屬的隱私，該樂園拒絕提供進一步細節。
  • detail n. 細節，詳情
    It declined to provide further details, citing the privacy of the visitor and his family.
    基於遊客及其家屬的隱私，該樂園拒絕提供進一步細節。
  • suspect v. 懷疑
    According to local media, police said the man was initially suspected of having drowned...
    根據當地媒體報導，警方表示該男子最初被懷疑溺水……

文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    A tourist from Myanmar was pronounced dead at a hospital Sunday after being found unconscious...
    句中的「was pronounced」與「was found」皆為被動語態（be + 過去分詞），強調動作的承受者（緬甸遊客），在新聞英文中常用於客觀描述事件發生經過。
  • 現在分詞作時間/原因狀語 (Present Participle as Adverbial Clause)
    It declined to provide further details, citing the privacy of the visitor and his family.
    句尾的「citing...」是由「because it cited」簡化而來的分詞構句，用現在分詞表達樂園拒絕提供細節的原因。
  • 關係副詞引導的非限定子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause with Relative Adverb)
    The incident occurred during the Mid-Autumn Festival holiday weekend, when the water park was crowded with visitors.
    「when the water park was crowded with visitors」為非限定關係子句，先行詞為「the Mid-Autumn Festival holiday weekend」，用來補充說明該週末的時間背景。
