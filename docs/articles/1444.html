國民黨主席：民進黨剝奪了台灣的未來

DPP depriving Taiwan of future: KMT head

單字解析

  • champion v. 擁護、支持、為……而戰
    Chinese Nationalist Party (KMT) Chairwoman Cheng Li-wun (鄭麗文) accused the Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) government in an interview of championing Taiwan’s identity while depriving Taiwan of its ability to shape its own future...
    中國國民黨主席鄭麗文在接受訪問時指責民進黨政府，一方面擁護台灣的身分認同，另一方面卻剝奪了台灣塑造自身未來的能力……
  • deprive v. 剝奪、使喪失
    ...accused the Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) government in an interview of championing Taiwan’s identity while depriving Taiwan of its ability to shape its own future...
    ……指責民進黨政府，一方面擁護台灣的身分認同，另一方面卻剝奪了台灣塑造自身未來的能力……
  • pursue v. 追求、尋求
    ...saying that Taiwan should pursue peace and propose practical solutions to reduce cross-strait risks.
    ……並表示台灣應該追求和平，並提出具體可行的解決方案以降低兩岸風險。
  • practical adj. 實際的、務實的
    ...saying that Taiwan should pursue peace and propose practical solutions to reduce cross-strait risks.
    ……並表示台灣應該追求和平，並提出具體可行的解決方案以降低兩岸風險。
  • initiative n. 主動權、倡議
    ...how Taiwan can take the initiative amid shifts in the international situation.
    ……以及台灣如何在國際局勢變遷中掌握主動權。
  • prosperous adj. 繁榮的
    The KMT pursues stable cross-strait relations to safeguard Taiwan’s democracy, freedom and prosperity, Cheng said...
    鄭麗文表示，國民黨追求穩定的兩岸關係以捍衛台灣的民主、自由與繁榮……
  • concession n. 讓步
    Taiwan must safeguard its democratic values and should not make unilateral concessions, she said...
    她表示，台灣必須捍衛民主價值，不應做出片面讓步……
  • advocate v. 提倡、主張
    The KMT advocates easing tensions in the Taiwan Strait and resuming cross-strait dialogue based on the so-called “1992 consensus,” she said.
    她表示，國民黨主張基於所謂的「九二共識」緩和台海緊張局勢並恢復兩岸對話。
  • imply v. 意味著、暗示
    The DPP rejects the “1992 consensus,” saying that Beijing does not recognize the Republic of China and acceptance of the understanding would imply agreement with China’s claim over Taiwan.
    民進黨拒絕「九二共識」，並表示北京不承認中華民國，接受這項默契將暗示同意中國對台灣的主權主張。
  • impact v. / n. 影響、衝擊
    Cheng voiced concerns about how Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co’s (台積電) expansion in the US might impact Taiwan’s strategic value...
    鄭麗文對台積電在美國擴廠可能如何衝擊台灣的戰略價值表達擔憂……
  • integration n. 整合、結合
    Easing cross-strait tensions would help integrate regional talent, technology, markets and capital...
    緩和兩岸緊張局勢將有助於整合區域人才、技術、市場和資本……
  • strategy n. 策略、戰略
    The DPP has stirred anti-China sentiment, but this strategy is losing its effectiveness as people become increasingly aware of the risks of war, Cheng said.
    鄭麗文表示，民進黨一直在煽動反中情緒，但隨著人民日益意識到戰爭的風險，這項策略正在失去效力。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附加說明（補充結果與伴隨狀況）
    Chinese Nationalist Party (KMT) Chairwoman Cheng Li-wun (鄭麗文) accused the Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) government in an interview of championing Taiwan’s identity while depriving Taiwan of its ability to shape its own future, saying that Taiwan should pursue peace and propose practical solutions to reduce cross-strait risks.
    句尾的「saying that...」是現在分詞（V-ing）引導的的分詞片語，用來補充說明前面主句的動作所產生的內容或說法，省略了原本的對等連接詞與主詞（原意為 and she said that...）。
  • 動名詞片語作介系詞的受詞
    Since taking office as party chair, she has expanded communication with the international community...
    在此句中，「taking office」是由介系詞「Since」接續的動名詞片語（動名詞當作介系詞的受詞），用來表達「自從……以來」的時間點。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    The interview with Japanese digital news outlet Shincho QUE, published on Friday last week, covered cross-strait relations...
    句中的「published on Friday last week」是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞短語「The interview...」。其完整結構為關代與be動詞的省略（原為 which was published on Friday last week），用來補充說明該專訪發布的時間。
  • 名詞子句（that 子句）作受詞
    The KMT pursues stable cross-strait relations to safeguard Taiwan’s democracy, freedom and prosperity, Cheng said, adding that the KMT should play an active role in promoting peace.
    句中的「that the KMT should play an active role in promoting peace」是由連接詞 that 引導的名詞子句，在句中作為動詞「adding」的受詞，用以說明補充陳述的內容。
