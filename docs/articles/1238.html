鴻海單月營收突破新台幣 1 兆元

Hon Hai monthly revenue surpasses NT$1 trillion

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • revenue n. 營收、收入
    Hon Hai Precision Industry Co yesterday reported a 38.42 percent year-on-year increase in revenue last month to a record NT$1.16 trillion.
    鴻海精密工業股份有限公司昨天公布上個月營收較去年同期大增 38.42％，達到創紀錄的新台幣 1.16 兆元。
  • attribute to phr. v. 把……歸因於
    On a monthly basis, revenue rose 25.7 percent from NT$921.77 billion in August, which the company attributed to strong sales of smart consumer electronics products, mainly smartphones.
    就月增率而言，營收較 8 月份的新台幣 9,217.7 億元成長 25.7％，公司將此歸因於智慧消費性電子產品（主要是智慧型手機）的強勁銷售。
  • exceed v. 超過、超越
    Last month’s figure exceeded NT$1 trillion for the first time in Hon Hai’s history, the company said in a statement.
    鴻海在聲明中表示，上個月的數字在鴻海歷史上首次突破新台幣 1 兆元。
  • analyst n. 分析師
    Analysts on average expected revenue to rise to about NT$2.83 trillion in the third quarter, Bloomberg News reported.
    《彭博社》報導指出，分析師原本平均預期第三季營收將增至約新台幣 2.83 兆元。
  • performance n. 表現、業績
    Hon Hai said the better-than-expected third-quarter performance was driven mainly by strong growth in its cloud and networking products, electronic components and computing products businesses.
    鴻海表示，優於預期的第三季表現主要歸功於其雲端網路產品、電子元件以及運算產品業務的強勁成長。
  • assembler n. 組裝廠、組合業者
    Hon Hai, also known as Foxconn Technology Group (富士康科技集團), is Apple Inc’s primary iPhone assembler and makes servers powered by Nvidia Corp’s AI accelerators.
    亦被稱為富士康科技集團的鴻海，是蘋果公司（Apple Inc.）的主要 iPhone 組裝廠，並製造搭載輝達（Nvidia Corp.）AI 加速器的伺服器。
  • shipment n. 出貨、出貨量
    The company in August said it expected AI server rack shipments to grow by a high double-digit percentage sequentially in the third quarter...
    該公司在 8 月時表示，預期第三季 AI 伺服器機櫃出貨量將較前一季以高雙位數百分比成長……
  • surpass v. 超越、勝過
    In the first nine months of this year, revenue rose 39.53 percent year-on-year to NT$7.67 trillion, surpassing the previous record of NT$5.5 trillion set in the same period last year, it said.
    該公司表示，今年前九個月營收較去年同期成長 39.53％，達到新台幣 7.67 兆元，超越去年同期創下的新台幣 5.5 兆元歷史紀錄。
  • segment n. 部分、部門、區段
    The company said the cloud and networking products and electronic components segments posted the strongest annual growth among its businesses during the period.
    公司表示，在該期間內，雲端網路產品與電子元件部門在各項業務中展現出最強勁的年度成長。
  • uncertainty n. 不確定性
    ...adding that uncertainties in the global political and economic environment warrant close monitoring.
    ……並補充說，全球政治與經濟環境的不確定性值得密切關注。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    Hon Hai Precision Industry Co yesterday reported a 38.42 percent year-on-year increase in revenue last month to a record NT$1.16 trillion (US$36.49 billion), driven by strong growth in its cloud and networking, electronic components and computing products businesses.
    句尾的「driven by ...」是由過去分詞引導的片語，用來修飾前面的主要子句或營收成長的結果，相當於省略了「which was driven by ...」。這種結構在多益商業新聞閱讀中非常常見，用以簡潔地表達原因或驅動因素。
  • 非限定關代子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    On a monthly basis, revenue rose 25.7 percent from NT$921.77 billion in August, which the company attributed to strong sales of smart consumer electronics products, mainly smartphones.
    句中的「which the company attributed to ...」是個非限定關代子句，前面帶有逗號，用來補充說明前面的整個事件（8月份營收成長 25.7% 這件事）。關係代名詞「which」代替前面整句的概念，在子句中作受詞。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The company in August said it expected AI server rack shipments to grow by a high double-digit percentage sequentially in the third quarter, with full-year shipments expected to more than double this year.
    句中使用了「are expected to...」與「expected to...」的被動語態結構，強調「預期」這個動作是基於市場分析或公司內部的預估而施加於主詞（shipments）上，常用於商業財經報導中客觀陳述未來預測。
