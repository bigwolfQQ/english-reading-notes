單字解析

  • deploy v. 部署、調動（軍隊、裝備等）
    Japan deployed 300 crew members, landing ship JS Kunisaki and three CH-47 helicopters, which arrived in Kalimantan.
    日本部署了 300 名船員、「國東號」登陸艦（JS Kunisaki）以及三架 CH-47 直升機，這些裝備與人員已抵達加里曼丹。
  • operation n. 行動、運作、手術
    The mission marked the first known overseas firefighting operation carried out by the Japan Self-Defense Forces.
    這項任務標誌著日本自衛隊首次執行已知的海外消防作業。
  • strive v. 努力、奮鬥
    “To this end, we will learn from the Indonesian military’s operational experience and work in coordination and cooperation with them, striving to ensure that the Indonesian people can lead stable lives as soon as possible,” ...
    「為此，我們將借鏡印尼軍方的作戰經驗，並與他們保持協調與合作，努力確保印尼人民能夠盡快過上穩定的生活，」...
  • successor n. 繼任者、接班人
    Leader Kim Jong-un’s daughter and apparent successor is believed to have a younger sibling, the South Korean National Intelligence Service said yesterday...
    南韓國家情報院昨天表示，北韓領導人金正恩的女兒兼顯然的接班人被認為還有一個年幼的手足...
  • confirm v. 確認、證實
    Seoul had initially indicated that Kim Jong-un and his wife, Ri Sol-ju, had their first child, a boy, in 2010, and that Kim Ju-ae was their second child, but in 2023, Seoul said it was “unable to confirm” the existence of the son.
    首爾最初曾指出，金正恩與妻子李雪主於 2010 年迎來了他們的第一個孩子（男孩），而金主愛是他們的第二個孩子，但在 2023 年，首爾表示「無法確認」該兒子的存在。
  • surrender v. 投降、自首
    A police officer who allegedly gunned down his wife at a kindergarten where she was a teacher surrendered yesterday after an overnight standoff with authorities, officials said.
    官員表示，一名據稱在妻子任教的幼稚園槍殺妻子的警察，在與當局對峙一整晚後，於昨天投降。
  • evacuate v. 疏散、撤離
    All 44 students at the school were unharmed and evacuated with other teachers after the shooting.
    槍擊案發生後，學校的所有 44 名學生均毫髮無傷，並與其他老師一同撤離。
  • submit v. 提交、呈遞
    The government is to submit a bill in February to ban whaling, a move that would leave Norway and Japan as the only two countries in the world to continue commercial whaling.
    政府將於 2 月提交一項禁止捕鯨的法案，此舉將使挪威和日本成為全球僅存的兩個繼續進行商業捕鯨的國家。
  • commercial adj. 商業的、營利的
    The government is to submit a bill in February to ban whaling, a move that would leave Norway and Japan as the only two countries in the world to continue commercial whaling.
    政府將於 2 月提交一項禁止捕鯨的法案，此舉將使挪威和日本成為全球僅存的兩個繼續進行商業捕鯨的國家。
  • quota n. 配額、限額
    The quota allows 150 fin whales to be killed this year.
    今年的配額允許殺死 150 頭長鬚鯨。
  • support n. / v. 支持
    “There is considerable political and public support for this bill to ban commercial whaling, and compelling ethical, veterinary, legal and conservation reasons to hope it succeeds,” ...
    「對於這項禁止商業捕鯨的法案，有相當大的政治和公眾支持，並且有令人信服的倫理、獸醫、法律和保育理由，讓人希望它能成功，」...

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（後置修飾 / 形容詞化）
    ...to help fight wildfires that are causing a toxic haze impacting people’s health in other countries.
    句中的「impactful people's health」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「a toxic haze（有毒霾害）」，相當於關代子句「that are impacting people's health」，分詞直接接在名詞後方可讓語氣更為精煉。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    All 44 students at the school were unharmed and evacuated with other teachers after the shooting.
    此句使用了被動語態「were evacuated（被疏散）」，強調學生們是接受安排而被動撤離的狀態，主詞是動作的承受者。
  • be to + 原形動詞（表示預定或計劃的未來）
    The government is to submit a bill in February to ban whaling...
    「be to V」結構常出現在新聞英語中，用來表示官方或正式的「預定計畫、安排」或是即將發生的重要官方事件，此處指政府預計在二月提交法案。
