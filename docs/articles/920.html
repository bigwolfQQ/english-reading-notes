尼泊爾與西藏的災害成了採取氣候行動的催化劑

Nepal-Tibet disaster is a spur to action on climate

單字解析

  • subside v. 平息、減弱
    Following a familiar pattern, coverage of the glacier collapse on the Nepal-Tibet border last month has subsided with the initial shock.
    遵循熟悉的模式，上個月尼泊爾與西藏邊界冰川崩塌的報導，隨著最初的震驚已經平息。
  • collapse n. 崩塌、瓦解
    Following a familiar pattern, coverage of the glacier collapse on the Nepal-Tibet border last month has subsided with the initial shock.
    遵循熟悉的模式，上個月尼泊爾與西藏邊界冰川崩塌的報導，隨著最初的震驚已經平息。
  • recovery n. 復甦、恢復
    However, inexperienced though they are, Nepal’s leaders are right to insist that other countries should pay toward its recovery.
    然而，儘管尼泊爾的領導人們缺乏經驗，他們堅持其他國家應為其復甦支付費用是正確的。
  • estimate v. 估計、估算
    Nepal is estimated to be responsible for less than 0.1 percent of global greenhouse gas emissions.
    據估計，尼泊爾對全球溫室氣體排放的責任小於 0.1%。
  • responsible adj. 負有責任的
    Nepal is estimated to be responsible for less than 0.1 percent of global greenhouse gas emissions.
    據估計，尼泊爾對全球溫室氣體排放的責任小於 0.1%。
  • submission n. 提交、呈遞（動詞為 submit）
    About 120 countries have already submitted claims adding up to a total of nearly US$3 billion...
    大約 120 個國家已經提交了總計近 30 億美元的索賠...
  • target n. 目標
    Other donors have also failed to meet targets.
    其他捐助國也未能達到目標。
  • accelerate v. 加速
    However, as well as a spur to accelerate the green transition and intensify decarbonization, the plight of the Nepali people should be a wake-up call...
    然而，尼泊爾人民的困境不僅應作為加速綠色轉型和加強去碳化的催化劑...
  • prioritize v. 把...列為優先事項
    Boosting the loss and damage fund must be prioritized at those talks if climate justice is to remain a credible goal.
    如果要讓氣候正義繼續成為一個可信的目標，促進損失與損害基金必須在這些會談中被列為優先事項。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表附帶狀況或結果）
    On Wednesday last week, four bodies were recovered from one of these tunnels, with searches ongoing.
    「with + 名詞 + 分詞」結構用來表示附帶狀況，在此句中表示「搜尋工作仍在進行的情況下」。
  • 讓步倒裝句（Adverbial clause inversion with though/as）
    However, inexperienced though they are, Nepal’s leaders are right to insist that other countries should pay toward its recovery.
    形容詞 inexperienced 提前，搭配 though（或 as），構成讓步狀語子句，意為「儘管他們沒有經驗」。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Nepal is estimated to be responsible for less than 0.1 percent of global greenhouse gas emissions.
    主詞 Nepal 接受估計，使用「be + 過去分詞 (is estimated)」的被動語態結構，常出現在新聞報導中表達客觀說法。
