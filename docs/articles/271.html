星空下的電影院

Cinema under the stars

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • collaborator n. 合作者，合夥人
    When Joe Chao and his collaborators staged their first screening in 2016, however, it was still an experiment inspired by the open-air cinemas he had encountered in London.
    然而，當趙冠喬和他的合作夥伴在 2016 年舉辦他們的第一次放映時，這仍然只是一場受到他在倫敦所接觸過的露天電影院所啟發的實驗。
  • concept n. 概念，觀念
    Back in 2016, this rooftop cinema concept started out as a side project for Chao.
    回顧 2016 年，這個屋頂電影院的概念對趙冠喬來說最初只是一個副業。
  • operational adj. 營運的，操作的
    Then in 2016, the first ever rooftop screening became a reality after many months of preparation to secure the location, equipment, screening licenses and other operational hurdles.
    接著在 2016 年，經過了許多個月的籌備，以確保場地、設備、播映授權和其他營運障礙後，史上第一場屋頂電影院放映終於成為現實。
  • hurdle n. 障礙，難關
    Then in 2016, the first ever rooftop screening became a reality after many months of preparation to secure the location, equipment, screening licenses and other operational hurdles.
    接著在 2016 年，經過了許多個月的籌備，以確保場地、設備、播映授權和其他營運障礙後，史上第一場屋頂電影院放映終於成為現實。
  • regular adj. 定期的，經常的
    The eventual success led Chao to hold regular viewings in 2017.
    隨之而來的成功促使趙冠喬在 2017 年舉辦了定期的放映活動。
  • preference n. 偏好，喜好
    Initially, the movie curation process was based primarily on our own personal preference, as it was difficult to know what the audience liked.
    最初，電影的策展過程主要依據我們個人的喜好，因為很難知道觀眾喜歡什麼。
  • feedback n. 回饋，反應
    Chao said that he received a lot of negative feedback after a screening of the black comedy Frank.
    趙冠喬表示，在放映黑色喜劇《法蘭克》之後，他收到了許多負面回饋。
  • beverage n. 飲料
    A concession stand offers snacks and beverages (no outside refreshments allowed).
    小賣部提供零食和飲料（禁止攜帶外食）。
  • adjustable adj. 可調整的
    Each guest receives a pair of noise-cancelling wireless headphones with adjustable volume.
    每位嘉賓都會收到一副帶有可調節音量的抗噪無線耳機。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    Wearing wireless headphones and surrounded by city lights, they watch everything from romantic favorites to independent films and Hollywood hits.
    句首的 "Wearing wireless headphones..." 是由現在分詞帶領的分詞片語，用來修飾主句的主語 they（他們），表示「戴著無線耳機」的同時也在觀賞電影，相當於副詞子句 As they wear wireless headphones... 的簡化。
  • 過去分詞作表語/被動狀態 (Past Participle as Predicate/Passive State)
    When Joe Chao and his collaborators staged their first screening in 2016, however, it was still an experiment inspired by the open-air cinemas he had encountered in London.
  • 地方副詞片語置於句首的倒裝現象 (Inversion with Prepositional Phrase)
    As the credits roll, a few people hang around for one last look at the night sky and a take few quick snaps before heading for the exits.
    雖然此處為標準語序，但像 "After checking in, audiences can explore..." 這種將時間或條件副詞片語置於句首的結構，在英文新聞與商業寫作中非常常見，用來先交代背景或步驟，再帶出主句的主體行動。
