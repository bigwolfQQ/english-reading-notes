文法重點

  • 分詞片語作為後置修飾語 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    About one in six people receiving care for non-communicable diseases (NCDs) experience preventable harm...
    句中的「receiving care for non-communicable diseases (NCDs)」是一個現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 people。這相當於形容詞子句「who receive care for non-communicable diseases」，精簡了句型並讓商業與新聞英語更為緊湊。
  • 過去分詞作為形容詞修飾 (Past Participle as Adjective)
    As part of World Patient Safety Day, observed annually on Sept. 17...
    句中的「observed annually on Sept. 17」是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的 World Patient Safety Day。被動意味濃厚，原意相當於「which is observed annually on Sept. 17」，在多益閱讀測驗中常用於簡化被動語態的子句。
  • 被動語態的運用 (Passive Voice)
    Institutionalized mechanisms should also be established to involve patients and advocacy groups...
    句型「should also be established」使用了助動詞加被動語態（should be + 過去分詞），強調「機制（mechanisms）」是被建立的，焦點在於動作的承受者而非執行者，是商業契約、政策說明與正式新聞報導中非常常見的句型。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞 (Gerund Phrase as Subject)
    ...as relying only on the efforts of the government and healthcare providers yield very limited results...
    句中的「relying only on the efforts of the government and healthcare providers」是由動名詞 relying 開頭的片語，整個片語作為副詞子句中的主詞。動名詞片語當主詞時視為單數概念，是多益文法題（尤其是句型結構與主謂一致）常考的重點。
