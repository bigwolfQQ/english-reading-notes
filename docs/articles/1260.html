緬甸從馬來西亞遣返一萬名移民

Myanmar repatriates 10,000 migrants from Malaysia

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • repatriate v. 遣返（引A國公民回國）
    Myanmar has repatriated nearly 10,000 migrants from Malaysia this year...
    緬甸今年已從馬來西亞遣返近一萬名移民...
  • scheme n. 方案、計畫
    ...under an opaque scheme it says cannot guarantee safe homecomings to the war-torn nation.
    ...在一個被其稱無法保證安全返回這個飽受戰亂之苦國家的不透明計畫下。
  • agree to v. phr. 同意
    ...that Kuala Lumpur says Myanmar agreed to take back amid a wave of anti-migrant sentiment in Malaysia.
    ...吉隆坡表示緬甸同意在馬來西亞反移民情緒高漲的情況下接收（移民）。
  • organize v. 組織、安排
    ...Kuala Lumpur has not commented on the returns Myanmar says it organized by weekly flights.
    ...吉隆坡並未對緬甸表示透過每週航班組織的遣返發表評論。
  • voluntary adj. 自願的
    Malaysia said that returnees under its scheme agreed to voluntary repatriation from detention centers...
    馬來西亞表示，在其計畫下回鄉的人同意從拘留中心自願遣返...
  • facility n. 設施、場所
    However, rights groups report dire conditions in the facilities...
    然而，人權團體報告稱這些設施的條件惡劣...
  • estimate v. 估計、估算
    More than 100,000 people are estimated to have been killed in Myanmar since a military coup...
    估計自軍事政變以來，緬甸已有超過 10 萬人喪生...
  • flee v. 逃跑、逃離
    The Rohingya started fleeing the country en masse to Malaysia before that...
    在此之前，羅興亞人就已開始大規模逃往馬來西亞...
  • charge n. 指控、控告
    ...deemed a genocide by rights groups — a charge the armed forces have always denied.
    ...被人權團體視為種族滅絕——這是一項武裝部隊始終否認的指控。
  • make up v. phr. 組成、佔（比例）
    The Rohingya make up two-thirds of the 190,000 Myanmar refugees and asylum seekers in Malaysia...
    羅興亞人佔馬來西亞 19 万名緬甸難民和尋求庇護者的三分之二...
  • arrangement n. 安排、準備工作
    “Necessary arrangements are continuing to facilitate their swift reunion with relatives...”
    「相關必要安排正在持續進行中，以利他們與親屬迅速團聚……」
  • facilitate v. 促進、使便利、使順利
    “Necessary arrangements are continuing to facilitate their swift reunion with relatives and family members...”
    「相關必要安排正在持續進行中，以利他們與親屬和家人迅速團聚……」

文法重點

  • 現在完成式的被動語態 (Present Perfect Passive)
    Myanmar has repatriated nearly 10,000 migrants from Malaysia this year...
    句型為「have/has + been + 過去分詞」，用來強調從過去某個時間點持續到現在的動作，且主詞承受該動作（注意第1段是主動，第6、8段則常使用被動語態如 are repatriated 來表達移民「被」遣返）。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The Rohingya started fleeing the country en masse to Malaysia before that, escaping a military crackdown starting in 2017 deemed a genocide by rights groups...
    句中的「escaping...」為現在分詞片語，用來補充說明主詞在逃離時伴隨的動作或結果；而後方的「deemed a genocide...」則是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的 crackdown（鎮壓），省略了「which was」。
  • 不定詞片語作目的副詞用 (Infinitive Phrase as Adverb of Purpose)
    “Necessary arrangements are continuing to facilitate their swift reunion with relatives and family members...”
    句尾的「to facilitate...」是不定詞片語，用來修飾前面的動詞片語 are continuing，表達進行這些必要安排的「目的」是為了促成他們與親屬迅速團聚。
