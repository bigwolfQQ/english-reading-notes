Woman in Lai's son case suspected of spying on Taiwan official: FBI

單字解析

  • suspect v. 懷疑，涉嫌
    A woman arrested for allegedly surveilling President Lai Ching-te's son on behalf of China is also suspected of monitoring a Taiwanese elected official...
    一名因涉嫌代表中國監視總統賴清德兒子而被捕的女子，同時也涉嫌監視一名台灣民選官員……
  • surveil v. 監視，跟監
    A woman arrested for allegedly surveilling President Lai Ching-te's son on behalf of China is also suspected of monitoring a Taiwanese elected official...
    一名因涉嫌代表中國監視總統賴清德兒子而被捕的女子，同時也涉嫌監視一名台灣民選官員……
  • affidavit n. 宣誓陳述書，具結書
    In an affidavit dated Oct. 3 and filed in a federal court in California, FBI Special Agent Kevin Holt said Wanying Zhang was suspected of surveilling a 'Taiwanese elected official...'
    在一份日期為10月3日並提交至加州聯邦法院的宣誓陳述書中，聯邦調查局特別探員凱文・霍爾特表示，張婉瑩涉嫌監視一名「台灣民選官員」……
  • associated with phr. 與……有關聯，和……相關
    ...that the address, which was redacted in the filing, had previously been associated with a Taiwanese elected official who had vocal anti-CCP views.
    ……該地址在文件中已被塗黑刪去，先前曾與一名具有公開反共觀點的台灣民選官員有關聯。
  • obtain v. 獲得，取得
    ...I believe it is possible that PRC Official 1 tasked Zhang with obtaining information about the residents of this address...
    ……我相信中國官員1號有可能指派張婉瑩取得該地址居民的資訊……
  • verify v. 核實，查證
    CNA contacted the FBI to verify the identity of the Taiwanese official mentioned by Holt but has yet to receive a response as of press time.
    中央社已聯繫聯邦調查局以核實霍爾特所提及的台灣官員身分，但截至截稿時尚未收到回覆。
  • agent n. 代理人，經紀人，探員
    Zhang was arrested Sunday at Los Angeles International Airport before boarding a flight to Shanghai, on suspicion of acting as an unregistered agent of a foreign government.
    張婉瑩週日在洛杉磯國際機場準備登機前往上海之前遭到逮捕，罪嫌是擔任未登記的境外政府代理人。
  • complaint n. 控訴，告訴，抱怨
    ...a criminal complaint filed by a member of the FBI's counterintelligence squad in Los Angeles accused Zhang and an accomplice of traveling to Seattle...
    ……聯邦調查局洛杉磯反情報小組成員提交的一份刑事訴訟指控，張婉瑩與一名同夥前往西雅圖……
  • accomplice n. 同謀，幫兇
    ...accused Zhang and an accomplice of traveling to Seattle in September 2025 and photographing the suburban home of the family of a high-ranking Taiwanese government official.
    ……指控張婉瑩與一名同夥於2025年9月前往西雅圖，並拍攝了一名台灣高層政府官員家屬的郊區住宅。
  • release v. 發布，發表，釋放
    In a statement released Tuesday by the U.S. House Select Committee on the Strategic Competition Between the United States and the Chinese Communist Party...
    在美國眾議院「美國與中國共產黨戰略競爭特別委員會」週二發布的聲明中……
  • condemn v. 譴責
    'I condemn the CCP's violation of American sovereignty and its dangerous belligerence against Taiwan,' Moolenaar said.
    「我譴責中共對美國主權的侵犯以及對台灣的危險好戰行徑，」穆勒納爾說。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾語 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    A woman arrested for allegedly surveilling President Lai Ching-te's son on behalf of China is also suspected of monitoring a Taiwanese elected official...
    句中的「arrested for allegedly surveilling President Lai Ching-te's son on behalf of China」是由過去分詞 arrested 開頭的片語，用來後置修飾前面的名詞 A woman（一個女子），原意為「A woman who was arrested...」，省略了「who was」以達到簡潔的新聞報導語氣。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    ...Holt said Wanying Zhang (張婉瑩), a California real estate agent and China-born naturalized U.S. citizen, met with a Chinese official... [註：此處以同位語呈現，亦可參考第6句的非限定子句] ...the address, which was redacted in the filing, had previously been associated with a Taiwanese elected official...
    在「which was redacted in the filing」這個子句中，使用了關係代名詞 which 來補充說明前面的先行詞 the address（該地址）。逗號隔開代表這是補充性質的非限定用法，若將該子句拿掉，主要子句的句意依然完整。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Zhang was arrested Sunday at Los Angeles International Airport before boarding a flight to Shanghai, on suspicion of acting as an unregistered agent of a foreign government.
    此句使用了被動語態「was arrested」（被逮捕），強調動作的承受者（張婉瑩）而非執行者。新聞英語為了客觀陳述事實並聚焦於事件對象，經常大量使用被動語態。
