Germany’s far-right AfD triumphs, aims fire at chancellor

單字解析

  • defeat n. 失敗，挫敗
    German Chancellor Friedrich Merz’s conservatives yesterday licked their wounds after a crushing state election defeat in the ex-communist east
    德國總理弗里德里希·默茨的保守派昨天在經歷了前共產主義東部地區慘重的州選舉失敗後正在舔舐傷口
  • burden n. 負擔，重任
    no chancellor before has been as unpopular as Friedrich Merz, who has become a major burden on Germany’s prosperous development.
    此前沒有任何一位總理像弗里德里希·默茨這樣不受歡迎，他已經成為德國繁榮發展的沉重負擔。
  • stagnation n. 停滯，蕭條
    an outcome widely blamed on voter anger against the ruling coalition at a time of economic stagnation.
    這一結果被普遍歸咎於在經濟停滯時期選民對執政聯盟的憤怒。
  • majority n. 多數
    He said that although his party had narrowly missed out on an absolute majority, it was now reaching out to other parties
    他表示，儘管該黨以微輕差距錯失了絕對多數，但目前正主動接觸其他政黨
  • contribute v. 貢獻，捐獻
    provided they are integrated, speak German ... and contribute to the economy here.
    前提是他們融入社會、會說德語……並為這裡的經濟做出貢獻。
  • demand v. 要求，強烈要求
    she said, demanding an end to “illegal migration, open borders...
    她說，並要求終止「非法移民、開放邊境...
  • policy n. 政策，方針
    Weidel also slammed Germany’s “disastrous energy policy”
    魏idel還猛烈抨擊了德國的「災難性能源政策」
  • struggle v. 掙扎，奮力，艱難努力
    Pressure has grown on Merz not just from the far-right, but also from within his coalition, which has struggled to reboot a stagnant economy
    默茨面臨的壓力不僅來自極右翼，也來自他自己的執政聯盟內部，該聯盟一直努力重振停滯的經濟
  • promise n. 承諾，保證
    Merz took office with the promise of pushing back the AfD, yet the party has grown stronger
    默茨上台時承諾擊退另類選擇黨，然而該黨卻變得更加強大
  • strategy n. 策略，戰略
    What strategy can the CDU use to reverse this trend?
    基民盟可以採取什麼策略來扭轉這一趨勢？

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作補充說明 / 形容詞用 (Present Participle Phrase)
    German Chancellor Friedrich Merz’s conservatives yesterday licked their wounds after a crushing state election defeat in the ex-communist east, as the triumphant far-right Alternative for Germany (AfD) launched a withering attack against him.
    句中的「as the triumphant... launched...」引導一個時間子句，而在其他段落中常見如「...basking in the almost 44 percent landslide...」這種現在分詞片語，用來補充說明主詞在該動作發生時的狀態或背景。
  • 關代所有格子句 (Relative Clause with Whose)
    For the first time since the end of World War II, the AfD — a party whose state branch is classified as right-wing extremist — is close to securing an absolute majority, he said.
    「whose state branch is classified as right-wing extremist」是一個由關係代名詞所有格 whose 引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾先行詞「a party」，表示「該政黨的州分部」。
  • 過去分詞作後置修飾語 (Past Participle as Post-modifier)
    The AfD in Saxony-Anhalt is labeled a “right-wing extremist” group by Germany’s domestic intelligence agency, and some of Siegmund’s staff are former members of a since-banned neo-Nazi organization.
    句中的「since-banned」是由副詞與過去分詞構成的複合形容詞，用來修飾後方的「neo-Nazi organization」，意指「自那時起已被禁止的」。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞 / 受詞 (Gerund Phrase as Object)
    Merz has vowed to return the CDU to its conservative roots and launched a crackdown on irregular immigration while trying to turn the economy around.
    連接詞 while 後方接了現在分詞「trying to turn the economy around」，構成分詞構句，省略了主詞與 be 動詞（while he was trying...），表達「在試圖……的同時」。
