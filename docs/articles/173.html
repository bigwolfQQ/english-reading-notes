文法重點

  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    A woman used scam-prevention tactics she learned from a report on identifying fraud to save herself from losing NT$1.5 million...
    句中的 "she learned from a report on identifying fraud" 是省略了關係代名詞 that/which 的形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的先行詞 tactics（技巧）。在英文中，當關係代名詞在子句中作受詞時通常可以省略。
  • 非限定關係子句（補充說明）
    The alleged money mule, a woman surnamed Lai (賴), arrived by taxi and when she entered Chiu’s home to collect the money, police arrested her, police said.
    句中的 ", a woman surnamed Lai (賴)," 是一個同位語，用逗號隔開來補充說明前面主詞 "The alleged money mule"（涉案車手）的身分，這是英文新聞寫作中非常常見的句構。
  • 現在分詞作後置修飾語
    Police seized a mobile phone carried by Lai, and after questioning her, referred the case to the Keelung District Prosecutors’ Office for further investigation...
    句中的 "carried by Lai" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾其前的名詞 "a mobile phone"，相當於完整關係子句 "which was carried by Lai" 的簡化版，能讓句子更精簡流暢。
