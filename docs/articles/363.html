Taiwan wins 3 silver, 4 bronze medals at International AI Olympiad

單字解析

  • organize v. 組織、籌辦
    according to National Central University (NCU), which organized Team Taiwan.
    根據組訓（籌辦）台灣代表隊的國立中央大學指出。
  • competition n. 競賽、比賽
    The third edition of the competition was held in Astana, Kazakhstan, from Aug. 2-8, attracting 466 students from 108 countries and regions, NCU said in a press release on Tuesday.
    中央大學於週二發布新聞稿表示，第三屆的競賽於8月2日至8日在哈薩克阿斯塔納舉行，吸引來自108個國家與地區的466名學生參加。
  • press release n. 新聞稿
    NCU said in a press release on Tuesday.
    中央大學於週二發布的新聞稿中表示。
  • select v. 挑選、選拔
    The university's Department of Computer Science and Information Engineering selected and trained eight students to represent Taiwan at the competition...
    該校的資訊工程學系選拔並培訓了八名學生代表台灣參加此次競賽...
  • subsidy n. 補助金、津貼
    ...with subsidies from the K-12 Education Administration under the Ministry of Education.
    ...在教育部國民及學前教育署的補助下。
  • apply v. 應用、適用、申請
    ...that tested their ability to apply AI tools and machine learning to solve real-world problems...
    ...以測試他們應用人工智慧工具與機器學習來解決真實世界問題的能力...
  • participant n. 參與者、參加者
    Silver medals went to the next 72 participants, while bronze medals were awarded to the next 110 contestants.
    銀牌頒給接下來的72名參與者，而銅牌則頒給接下來的110名參賽者。
  • award v. 授予、頒發
    Gold medals were awarded to the top 37 contestants in the individual contest.
    金牌頒發給個人賽的前37名參賽者。
  • overall adv. / adj. 總共、全面地；整體的
    ...who placed 44th overall...
    ...總排名第44...
  • in terms of phr. 就……而言、在……方面
    NCU said this year's IOAI was the second-largest international science Olympiad competition in terms of participants...
    中央大學表示，就參賽人數而言，今年的IOAI是第二大國際科學奧林匹亞競賽...
  • take part phr. 參與、參加
    ...with students from 108 countries and regions taking part.
    ...共有來自108個國家與地區的學生參與。
  • promising adj. 有前途的、大有為的、有希望的
    The university said it plans to continue selecting and training promising AI talent to participate in international competitions.
    該大學表示，計畫持續選拔並培訓具備潛力的AI人才，以參加國際競賽。

文法重點

  • 非限定關係代名詞子句（補充說明用法）
    ...according to National Central University (NCU), which organized Team Taiwan.
    句尾使用逗號加上「which + 動詞」的非限定關係代名詞子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞 National Central University (NCU)，在譯成中文時可順著語意接在後面翻譯，不需中斷。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The third edition of the competition was held in Astana, Kazakhstan, from Aug. 2-8...
    句中的「was held」使用了被動語態（be + 過去分詞），因為主詞「The third edition of the competition（第三屆競賽）」是舉辦這個動作的承受者，而非發動者。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾（現在分詞表主動）
    ...attracting 466 students from 108 countries and regions, NCU said in a press release on Tuesday.
    逗號之後的「attracting 466 students...」是由現在分詞帶領的分詞片語，用來修飾前面整子句發生的動作所帶來的結果或伴隨狀況，主詞 NCU 主動吸引了這些學生。
  • 對等連接詞表達對比 (while)
    Silver medals went to the next 72 participants, while bronze medals were awarded to the next 110 contestants.
    連接詞「while」在此處用來連接兩個子句，表示「而……、然而」，藉此呈現不同名次對應不同獎項的對比關係。
  • 不定詞片語作目的副詞用
    The university said it plans to continue selecting and training promising AI talent to participate in international competitions.
    句尾的「to participate in international competitions」是不定詞片語，在此作副詞用，用來修飾前面的動詞片語「selecting and training」，表達挑選與培訓人才的「目的」是為了參加國際競賽。
