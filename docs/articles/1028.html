EDITORIAL: Getting the GDP to workers

單字解析

  • statutory adj. 法定的，法令的
    Taiwan is set to raise its statutory minimum wage to a new high for the 11th consecutive year during an annual review scheduled for tomorrow.
    在強勁經濟成長與通膨上升的推動下，台灣預定在明天排定的年度審議中，將法定基本工資調高至新高，為連續第11年調升。
  • consecutive adj. 連續的，連貫的
    Taiwan is set to raise its statutory minimum wage to a new high for the 11th consecutive year during an annual review scheduled for tomorrow.
    在強勁經濟成長與通膨上升的推動下，台灣預定在明天排定的年度審議中，將法定基本工資調高至新高，為連續第11年調升。
  • stagnation n. 停滯，不景氣
    However, the constant hikes of the minimum wage mask the core issues of real-wage stagnation and a growing imbalance of wealth distribution.
    然而，基本工資的不斷調高，掩蓋了實質薪資停滯以及財富分配日益失衡的核心問題。
  • distribution n. 分配，分發
    However, the constant hikes of the minimum wage mask the core issues of real-wage stagnation and a growing imbalance of wealth distribution.
    然而，基本工資的不斷調高，掩蓋了實質薪資停滯以及財富分配日益失衡的核心問題。
  • project v. 預估，預測
    Taiwan’s economic growth is projected to accelerate at an annual rate of 11.48 percent this year, the fastest in 39 years...
    根據央行估計，台灣今年經濟成長率預計將以年增率 11.48% 加速成長，創下 39 年來最快紀錄...
  • median adj. 中位數的，中央的
    The median monthly wage — which is considered a more accurate gauge of standard income... rose 3.37 percent annually...
    主計總處（DGBAS）的資料顯示，今年前七個月，每月薪資中位數（被認為是標準收入更準確的衡量標準...）年增 3.37%...
  • attribute v. 把...歸因於，歸咎於
    The agency attributed the surge in regular average wage to sharp pay increases within the technology sector...
    該機構將經常性平均薪資的激增歸因於科技產業內部的薪資大幅調高...
  • surpass v. 超越，勝過
    ...significantly surpassing the NT$37,803 paid to workers in the overall manufacturing sector in the first seven months of this year, the data showed.
    資料顯示，...顯著超過整體製造業勞工的 37,803 元。
  • implement v. 實施，執行
    The government has to implement a better approach to address anemic income growth — something other than hiking the minimum wage.
    政府必須實施更好的方法來解決貧血式的收入成長問題——除了調高基本工資之外的其他做法。
  • curb v. 控制，抑制，約束
    Some economists have suggested that the share of GDP going to workers should be increased to curb a decline over the past few years.
    一些經濟學家建議，應提高分配給勞工的 GDP 比重，以遏制過去幾年的下滑趨勢。
  • surplus n. 盈餘，過剩
    That compared with an upward trajectory of the share of GDP that went to operating surplus, which rose to 35.06 percent of GDP from 31.57 percent over the same period.
    相較之下，分配給營業盈餘的 GDP 比重呈現向上軌跡，在同一期間從 31.57% 上升至 35.06%。
  • negotiate v. 談判，協商
    ...an annual collective bargaining process in Japan in which unions and management negotiate wage increases and working conditions.
    ...這是一項由工會和資方談判調薪與工作條件的年度集體協商程序。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（用作補充說明或結果）
    Driven by explosive demand for AI chips and servers, employees at electronics and components companies... were paid on average...
    句首的「Driven by...」是過去分詞片語引導的分詞構句，原句意涵為「Because they were driven by...（由於受到...的驅動）」，省略了連接詞與主詞，用來修飾後方的主詞 employees。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句（補充說明）
    The median monthly wage — which is considered a more accurate gauge of standard income, as extremely high or low wages do not skew it — rose 3.37 percent...
    句中的「which is considered a more accurate gauge...」是以逗號隔開的非限定關係子句，對前面的先行詞 the median monthly wage 作額外的補充解釋。非限定子句拿掉後，主句意思依然完整。
  • 被動語態的運用
    The agency attributed the surge in regular average wage to sharp pay increases within the technology sector, particularly among artificial intelligence (AI)-related companies.
