THSR sees record ridership, overcrowding over long weekend

單字解析

  • operator n. 營運商，經營者
    Taiwan High Speed Rail (THSR) carried a record 348,000 passengers on Thursday, the day before the Mid-Autumn Festival long weekend, followed by 315,000 passengers on Friday, according to the railway operator.
    根據鐵路營運商表示，台灣高鐵（THSR）在中秋節連續假期前一天的週四，載運了破紀錄的 34.8 萬人次旅客，隨後在週五也有 31.5 萬人次的旅客搭乘。
  • prohibit v. 禁止，阻止
    One person who identified themselves as a business-class passenger complained on Threads, saying they had chosen business class to avoid being disturbed and noting that current regulations prohibit passengers with non-reserved seat tickets from entering business-class cars.
    有一名自稱為商務艙旅客的網友在 Threads 上抱怨，表示他們選擇商務艙是為了避免受到干擾，並指出目前的規定禁止持有自由座車票的旅客進入商務車廂。
  • fare n. 票價，車費
    The poster said passengers with non-reserved seat tickets who stand in a business-class car should pay the difference between the non-reserved and business-class fares.
    該貼文者表示，站在商務車廂內的自由座旅客應該支付自由座與商務艙票價之間的差額。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The 663,000 passengers carried over Thursday and Friday led to overcrowding on some trains...
    句中的「carried over Thursday and Friday」為過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 passengers（被載運的旅客）。這是一種常見的簡化形容詞子句（who were carried...）的寫法，多益考試常考分詞與被修飾名詞之間的主被動關係。
  • 現在分詞表附帶結果或補充說明 (Present Participle indicating Result or Concomitant Action)
    ...leading to overcrowding on some trains, with passengers with non-reserved seat tickets spilling into reserved-seat and even business-class cars, prompting complaints on social media.
    句尾的「prompting complaints...」是由現在分詞帶出的分詞構句，表示前一個動作所自然產生的結果或隨之而來的狀況，相當於「and this prompted complaints...」。
  • With 複合結構 (With + Noun + Participle)
    ...with passengers with non-reserved seat tickets spilling into reserved-seat and even business-class cars...
    「with + 受詞 (passengers...) + 現在分詞 (spilling...)」結構用來描述伴隨發生的狀況或背景，意指「在……的情況下」或「隨著……」。這在多益閱讀測驗的文法題和長篇閱讀中非常常出現。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    One person who identified themselves as a business-class passenger complained on Threads...
    由關係代名詞 who 引導的形容詞子句「who identified themselves as a business-class passenger」，用來修飾先行詞 One person。在此用以指稱特定的一位發文者。
