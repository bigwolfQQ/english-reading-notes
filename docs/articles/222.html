文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾
    Taiwan stocks remain strong as Goldman Sachs forecasts 54,000 points
    句中的「as Goldman Sachs forecasts 54,000 points」為連接詞 as 引導的時間/原因副詞子句，用來補充說明台股維持強勢的原因。在此表現「隨著…」的動態情況。
  • 分詞構句表伴隨狀況或原因
    Campaigning for Hsu Hsin-ying could lead to expulsion, TPP says, as TPP-KMT cooperation in Hsinchu County collapses
