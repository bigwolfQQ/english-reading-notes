副總統蕭美琴：台灣不會向中國日益增加的脅迫屈服

Taiwan will not yield to China's growing coercion: VP Hsiao

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • compromise v. 妥協、退讓、危及
    Taiwan will not compromise its freedoms or democracy in the face of intensifying Chinese military threats and gray-zone pressure...
    面對日益加劇的中國軍事威脅與灰色地帶壓力，台灣不會在自由或民主上妥協……
  • intensify v. 加劇、強化、增強
    ...adding that military pressure had intensified in recent years.
    ……並補充說，軍事壓力近年來已經加劇。
  • submission n. 屈服、服從、提交
    "The reality is also that the Communist Party of China claims Taiwan and is coercing us into submission," Hsiao said...
    蕭美琴表示：「現實情況是，中國共產黨聲稱擁有台灣主權，並正脅迫我們屈服……」
  • yield v. 屈服、產出、讓步
    "We will not provoke, but at the same time, we will not yield by compromising our basic freedoms and our democracy," she said.
    她說：「我們不會挑釁，但同時，我們也不會透過妥協我們基本的自由與民主來屈服。」
  • approach n. 方法、途徑、接近
    Taiwan must respond to growing coercion and threats by taking a more dynamic approach to strengthening and fortifying itself, Hsiao added.
    蕭美琴補充說，台灣必須透過採取更具動態的方式來強化與鞏固自身，以回應日益增加的脅迫與威脅。
  • advantage n. 優勢、有利條件
    Sky News described gray-zone coercion as the gradual normalization of actions that give China an advantage.
    天空新聞網將灰色地帶脅迫描述為將有利於中國的行動逐步常態化。
  • average adj. 平均的
    The estimated daily average number of vessels rose 37.5 percent from summer 2025.
    估計每日平均船隻數量較2025年夏季增加了37.5%。
  • conflict n. 衝突、爭端
    Sky News described the waters east of Taiwan as strategically vital in a conflict.
    天空新聞網將台灣以東的水域描述為衝突中具有戰略至關重要地位的地方。
  • adjust v. 調整、適應
    He said the vessels increasingly resembled warships and maneuvered and adjusted their speed in attempts to breach Taiwan's defensive lines.
    他說，這些船隻越來越像軍艦，並透過機動與調整速度，企圖突破台灣的防線。
  • attempt n. / v. 企圖、嘗試
    He said the vessels increasingly resembled warships and maneuvered and adjusted their speed in attempts to breach Taiwan's defensive lines.
    他說，這些船隻越來越像軍艦，並透過機動與調整速度，企圖突破台灣的防線。
  • response n. 回應、反應
    In response, China's Foreign Ministry said China had an exclusive economic zone and continental shelf in waters east of Taiwan...
    作為回應，中國外交部表示，中國在台灣以東水域擁有專屬經濟區和大陸礁層……
  • maintain v. 維持、保持、維護
    The ministry described the patrols as legitimate exercises of jurisdiction intended to maintain maritime order and regional stability.
    中國外交部將這些巡邏描述為旨在維護海上秩序與區域穩定的合法管轄權行使。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...Vice President Hsiao Bi-khim (蕭美琴) said in an interview aired Monday, ahead of Chinese President Xi Jinping (習近平) and U.S. President Donald Trump's meeting this week.
    句中的 "aired Monday" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "interview"（受訪談是被播出的，故用被動分詞）。這種結構在新聞英語中非常常見，能有效精簡子句，省去 "which was aired" 的冗長贅字。
  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況 (Participle Clause of Concomitant Circumstance)
    Taiwan must respond to growing coercion and threats by taking a more dynamic approach to strengthening and fortifying itself, Hsiao added.
    句尾的 "by taking a more dynamic approach..." 使用了介詞片語結合動名詞（by + V-ing），用來表達「透過……的方式」來達成某個動作。這在商業與政治新聞中經常用於說明政策、手段或解決方案。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    An analysis conducted with the Futures Lab at the Center for Strategic and International Studies showed a record Chinese Coast Guard presence...
    句中的 "conducted with the Futures Lab..." 是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞 "analysis"（分析是被進行的，所以用過去分詞 conducted）。這也是將關係代間子句（that was conducted）精簡的高頻文法寫法。
  • 關係代名詞子句作修飾 (Relative Clause)
    Hsu Wei-hung (徐瑋鴻), a coast guard captain who has served for over 20 years, said 2026 was the worst year he had seen...
    句中使用 "who has served for over 20 years" 這個由關係代名詞 who 引導的形容詞子句，用來補充說明海巡隊長 Hsu Wei-hung 的背景資歷；同時子句內使用現在完成式 (has served)，表示從過去持續到現在的狀態。
