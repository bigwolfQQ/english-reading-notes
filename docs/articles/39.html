林郁婷在奪得奧運金牌後曾考慮「優雅退役」

Lin considered ‘graceful exit’ after Olympic gold

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • retiring adj. 退休的
    Taiwanese boxer Lin Yu-ting said that she considered retiring after winning gold at the Paris Olympics
    台灣拳擊手林郁婷表示，她在巴黎奧運奪金後曾考慮退休
  • eligibility n. 資格，合格性
    wanted to carry on fighting in part to defy those who questioned her eligibility.
    部分原因是因為想繼續奮戰，以回擊那些質疑她參賽資格的人。
  • disqualified adj. 被取消資格的
    The two boxers had been disqualified from the 2023 world championships
    這兩位拳擊手先前曾被取消2023年世界錦標賽的資格
  • achieve v. 達成，實現
    I achieved everything I had worked for.
    我實現了為之奮鬥的一切。
  • career n. 職業生涯
    Initially the choice was taken out of her hands and her career forced on hold.
    起初，這個選擇並不由她自己決定，她的職業生涯被迫暫停。
  • abilities n. 能力，才能
    And I also wanted to see how far my own abilities could go
    而且我也想看看自己的能力極限能到哪裡
  • goal n. 目標
    It’s a new goal, a new challenge and a new milestone
    這是一個新目標、新挑戰，也是一個新里程碑
  • commit v. 投入，奉獻
    Once she set her eyes on something, she would commit to it wholeheartedly
    一旦她鎖定了某個目標，就會全心全意地投入
  • setbacks n. 挫折，障礙
    She meets setbacks head-on, resetting her goals
    她迎難而上處理挫折，重新設定目標
  • standards n. 標準
    Lin admits to “very high standards” and a “rigorous attitude” towards training
    林郁婷坦承對訓練抱持「非常高的標準」和「嚴格的態度」
  • manage v. 管理，經營
    I manage risk very strictly
    我非常嚴格地管理風險
  • control v. 控制
    That’s something I can control
    這是我可以控制的事情

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表示伴隨狀況或結果）
    Lin and Imane Khelif of Algeria found themselves in the middle of a major gender row at the 2024 Games, with US President Donald Trump, Elon Musk and author J.K. Rowling among those wading in.
    「with + 名詞 + 分詞/介詞片語」為常見的分詞構句結構，用來補充說明主句發生的同時所伴隨的狀況，在此處表示「在美國總統川普、伊隆·馬斯克等人加入議論的情況下」。
  • 現在分詞當形容詞修飾名詞
    Taiwanese boxer Lin Yu-ting said that she considered retiring after winning gold at the Paris Olympics, but wanted to carry on fighting in part to defy those who questioned her eligibility.
    句中的 "questioned her eligibility" 是由關係代名詞 who 引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾先行詞 "those"（那些人）。子句中的動詞 questioned 為過去式，用以指稱過去質疑她資格的人。
  • 被動語態的運用
    Initially the choice was taken out of her hands and her career forced on hold.
  • 動名詞片語當主詞或受詞
    Dwelling on negatives would get her trapped in a “pessimistic mindset,” she said.
    句首的 "Dwelling on negatives" 是動名詞片語（意指「沉溺於負面事物」），作為整個子句的主詞。動名詞片語當主詞時，動詞要使用單數形（在此為 would）。
