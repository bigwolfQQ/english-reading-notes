文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Nvidia detailed in a technical blog titled “The Machines that Make the Machines” how its operations team and Hon Hai trained robots...
    句中的 "titled “The Machines that Make the Machines”" 是一個過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "a technical blog"（一本名為……的技術部落格）。這種結構省略了「which was」，能夠讓句子更簡潔緊湊，是商業與新聞英文中常見的修飾手法。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    Nvidia also said the two companies focused on assembling the GB300’s tester tray, which is used to verify the functionality and performance of GB300 computing modules before shipment.
  • 使役動詞的用法 (Causative Verb)
    For bus bar assembly, Nvidia said a major challenge was getting robots to grasp and transport a metal bar...
    句中的 "getting robots to grasp..." 使用了使役動詞 get 的結構（get + 受詞 + to V）。其意思為「使／讓……去執行某動作」，強調促使某人或某物完成特定任務，在多益閱讀與職場英文中非常實用。
  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況／結果 (Participial Phrase)
    ...the automation system hit a success rate of more than 95 percent for bus bar assembly, which requires tightening 16 screws, with a target completion time of 124 seconds.
    句尾的 ", which requires tightening..." 也是一個關係代名詞子句，而其中的 "tightening 16 screws" 則是動名詞片語作 require 的受詞。這種層層遞進的修飾方式在表達複雜的規格或數據時非常常見。
  • 形容詞片語作後位修飾 (Adjective Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Robots need to be adaptive and robust enough to handle variations in the assembly process, it said.
    句中的 "robust enough to handle..." 顯示了形容詞搭配 enough 的句型（形容詞 + enough + to V），用來表達「足夠……以至於能夠做某事」。這在描述機器、系統或人員的能力時是標準的高頻句型。
