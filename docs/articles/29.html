單字解析

  • rights holder n. 權利人，版權所有者
    The Japanese rights holders for the Dragon Ball character have denied consenting to the construction of a theme park...
    「七龍珠」角色的日本版權所有者已否認同意興建一座主題樂園……
  • deny v. 否認，拒絕
    The Japanese rights holders for the Dragon Ball character have denied consenting to the construction of a theme park...
    「七龍珠」角色的日本版權所有者已否認同意興建一座主題樂園……
  • consent v. 同意，准許
    The Japanese rights holders for the Dragon Ball character have denied consenting to the construction of a theme park...
    「七龍珠」角色的日本版權所有者已否認同意興建一座主題樂園……
  • construction n. 建設，建造
    The Japanese rights holders for the Dragon Ball character have denied consenting to the construction of a theme park...
    「七龍珠」角色的日本版權所有者已否認同意興建一座主題樂園……
  • dedicated adj. 專門的，奉獻的
    ...the construction of a theme park dedicated to the famous manga character in the Paris region.
    ……在巴黎地區興建一座專門紀念這位著名漫畫角色的主題樂園。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作後置定語
    ...the construction of a theme park dedicated to the famous manga character in the Paris region.
    句中的 "dedicated to the famous manga character..." 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "theme park"（主題樂園），相當於關代子句 "which is dedicated to..." 的簡化用法，在多益閱讀測驗中非常常見。
  • 動詞 deny 後接動名詞 (Gerund)
    ...have denied consenting to the construction of a theme park...
    動詞 "deny"（否認）後面若要接另一個動作，必須使用動名詞形式（V-ing），在此句中為 "consenting"。這是多益文法考題中常出現的動詞搭配考點。
