  • declaration n. 宣言，宣告，聲明
    Five former senior military officers were yesterday jailed for up to 18 years for their roles in the country’s short-lived martial law declaration of 2024.

  • attempt v. / n. 嘗試，企圖
    Moon Sang-ho, former head of the Defence Intelligence Command who attempted to seize control of the National Election Commission, was sentenced to 12 years in prison.
    試圖奪取國家選舉委員會控制權的前國軍情報司令部司令文相鎬被判處 12 年有期徒刑。
  • operation n. 行動，運作，業務
    The blast occurred during an unspecified operation near the demarcation line within the border buffer zone between the two nations...
    這起爆炸發生在兩國之間邊境緩衝區內 demarcation line 附近的一場未明行動中...
  • response n. 迴應，回答
    Punjab police did not respond to reporters’ requests for comment.
    旁遮普省警方未回應記者置評的請求。
  • charge n. 指控，控告，費用
    Khan has been in jail since 2023 and was sentenced late last year, along with his wife, to 17 years in jail on corruption charges they both deny.
    伊姆蘭·汗自 2023 年起入獄，並於去年底與妻子因貪腐指控被判處 17 年徒刑，兩人皆否認這些指控。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    An explosion yesterday injured three South Korean military officers inside the heavily fortified demilitarized zone (DMZ) separating the Koreas, officials said.
    句中的「separating the Koreas」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「the heavily fortified demilitarized zone (DMZ)」，相當於關代子句「which separated the Koreas」，用以簡化句子並直接表達該非軍事區所具有的地理分隔功能。
  • 過去分詞作形容詞修飾 (Past Participle as Adjective)
    The government is working to allay fears among investors and local communities, he said, adding that the AI hub would not host data centers and would not grant diplomatic immunity to US companies.
    句尾的「adding that...」是現在分詞片語作對等補充說明，而前文中的「US-backed project」則使用了過去分詞「backed」來修飾專案，意指「由美國所支持的」，這是商業與新聞英語中非常常見且簡潔的複合形容詞構法。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    Moon Sang-ho, former head of the Defence Intelligence Command who attempted to seize control of the National Election Commission, was sentenced to 12 years in prison.
    句中使用「who attempted to seize control of the National Election Commission」這個由關代 who 引導的限定修飾子句，用來補充說明先行詞「Moon Sang-ho（文相鎬）」在過去所做過的具體行為。
