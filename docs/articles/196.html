歐盟執委會主席將訪問格陵蘭以深化雙邊關係

EU chief due in Greenland to boost ties

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • reaffirm v. 重申、再次確認
    European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen headed to Greenland yesterday for a two-day visit aimed at reaffirming the bloc’s support for the Danish autonomous territory...
    歐洲聯盟執行委員會主席范德賴恩昨天前往格陵蘭展開為期兩天的訪問，旨在重申歐盟對這個丹麥自治領地的支持...
  • autonomous adj. 自治的、自主的
    ...aimed at reaffirming the bloc’s support for the Danish autonomous territory coveted by US President Donald Trump.
    ...旨在重申歐盟對這個受到美國總統川普覬覦的丹麥自治領地的支持。
  • geopolitical adj. 地緣政治的
    “Greenland is becoming increasingly important to the EU as a gateway to the Arctic and because of its geopolitical location, critical raw materials and role in Arctic security,”...
    「格陵蘭作為通往北極的門戶，且因其地緣政治位置、關鍵原物料以及在北極安全中的角色，對歐盟的重要性日益增加，」...
  • cooperation n. 合作
    A Danish-Greenlandic-US working group has since met regularly to find an agreement on cooperation going forward.
    此後，一個丹麥-格陵蘭-美國工作小組定期開會，尋求就未來的合作達成協議。
  • investment n. 投資
    ...in January, Von der Leyen vowed “massive” EU investment in Greenland to step up security in the Arctic.
    ...在 1 月份，范德賴恩誓言歐盟將對格陵蘭進行「大規模」投資，以加強北極的安全。
  • aid n. 援助、資助
    Brussels has also proposed doubling direct EU aid to Greenland to 530 million euros...
    布魯塞爾也提議將歐盟對格陵蘭的直接援助加倍至 5.3 億歐元...
  • additional adj. 額外的、附加的
    The Financial Times said Von der Leyen is expected to propose an additional 200 million euros in support during her visit.
    《金融時報》報導稱，預計范德賴恩將在訪問期間提議額外提供 2 億歐元的支援。
  • guarantee n. 保證、擔保
    “The EU cannot provide Greenland with a military security guarantee in the way NATO can...”
    「歐盟無法像北約那樣提供格陵蘭軍事安全保證……」
  • complementary adj. 互補的
    In this area, Greenlandic and European interests are “highly complementary,” Jacobsen said.
    雅各布森表示，在這一領域，格陵蘭與歐洲的利益「高度互補」。
  • infrastructure n. 基礎設施
    “Greenland needs investment, infrastructure and markets if it is to realize its mining ambitions...”
    「格陵蘭若要實現其採礦雄心，就需要投資、基礎設施和市場……」
  • ambition n. 雄心、抱負、野心
    “Greenland needs investment, infrastructure and markets if it is to realize its mining ambitions...”
    「格陵蘭若要實現其採礦雄心，就需要投資、基礎設施和市場……」
  • operation n. 運作、營運、運作中
    About 135 mining permits are held by more than 60 companies, but only two mines are in operation.
    大約 135 個採礦許可證由 60 多家公司持有，但只有兩個礦場在運營。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...for a two-day visit aimed at reaffirming the bloc’s support for the Danish autonomous territory coveted by US President Donald Trump.
    句中的「aimed at...」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的「a two-day visit」（訪問被針對/目的是...），省略了「which is」。同樣地，「coveted by...」也是過去分詞片語，用來修飾「the Danish autonomous territory」（領地被覬覦），這在多益閱讀與商業新聞中是非常常見的精簡表達方式。
  • be due to + 原形動詞 (預定要...)
    Today, she is due to sign a joint declaration between the EU and Greenland.
    「be due to」常用於商業或新聞英語中，表示某事「預定、預期在某個時間發生」。在此句中用來表達范德賴恩預定在今天簽署聯合宣言。
  • 表條件或未來的連接詞片語 if it is to...
    “Greenland needs investment, infrastructure and markets if it is to realize its mining ambitions, while the EU is looking for more secure and diversified access to critical raw materials,” he said.
    句型「if 主語 is/are to + 動詞原形」用來表達「如果（某人/某事）意圖或為了達成（某個未來的目標）」。此外，句中的「while」則用來表達「而 / 與……同時（形成對比）」。
  • 現在分詞短語作結果或伴隨狀語 (Present Participle as Result/Adverbial)
    Her visit coincides with the start of NATO’s Arctic Shield military exercise, bringing together soldiers from 10 countries.
    逗號之後的「bringing together...」是現在分詞短語，在此處表示伴隨或自然產生的結果（她的訪問剛好與演習撞期，進而匯集了來自10國的士兵），這能讓句子在不使用額外連接詞的情況下顯得更緊湊流暢。
