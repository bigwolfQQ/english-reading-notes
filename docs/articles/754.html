知情人士表示，中國可能會為部長核發臺胞證

China might issue permit for minister, source says

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • arrange v. 安排、籌備
    Beijing might arrange a Taiwan compatriot permit for Minister of Labor Hung Sun-han (洪申翰) on his behalf when he attends an APEC ministerial meeting in Nanjing later this month...
    北京當局可能會在本月稍晚勞動部長洪申翰出席在南京舉行的 APEC 部長級會議時，代他安排申辦臺胞證...
  • present v. 出示、提交
    ...although the host has already confirmed he is to enter China by presenting his invitation letter, a source said.
    ...不過知情人士表示，主辦方已經確認他將透過出示邀請函來進入中國。
  • commence v. 開始、展開
    The APEC Human Resources Development Ministerial Meeting is to commence on Monday next week in Nanjing.
    APEC 人力資源開發部長級會議將於下週一在南京揭幕。
  • administration n. （政府的）行政部門、內閣、管理
    Hung is to attend, making him the first minister in President William Lai’s (賴清德) administration to visit China.
    洪申翰將出席，這使他成為總統賴清德政府中首位訪問中國的部長。
  • participation n. 參與、參加
    ...saying only that Taiwan’s participation in APEC meetings and activities has always been handled in accordance with the “one China” principle...
    ...僅表示臺灣參與 APEC 會議與活動一向是按照「一個中國」原則來處理...
  • in accordance with phr. 按照、依據
    ...saying only that Taiwan’s participation in APEC meetings and activities has always been handled in accordance with the “one China” principle, and the provisions and practices of APEC memorandums of understanding.
    ...僅表示臺灣參與 APEC 會議與活動一向是按照「一個中國」原則，以及 APEC 諒解備忘錄的規定與慣例來處理。
  • provision n. 條款、規定
    ...and the provisions and practices of APEC memorandums of understanding.
    ...以及 APEC 諒解備忘錄的規定與慣例。
  • liaison n. 聯絡、聯繫、聯絡人
    The source said Taiwan has a liaison mechanism involving senior APEC officials and recently received the invitation letter through the mechanism.
    知情人士表示，臺灣擁有一個涉及 APEC 資深官員的聯繫機制，並透過該機制最近收到了邀請函。
  • authorities n. 官方、當局
    The authorities would provide it to the Ministry of Labor, and Hung would enter China using the invitation letter, the source said.
    知情人士表示，相關當局將把邀請函提供給勞動部，而洪申翰將憑邀請函入境中國。
  • designate v. 指定、指派、定性
    ...adding that Hung is not among those Beijing has designated as a “Taiwan independence diehard.”
    ...並補充說洪申翰不在北京所指定的「臺獨頑固份子」名單之列。
  • caution v. 警告、提醒
    However, the source cautioned that China’s legal system continues to regard Taiwanese nationals as citizens of the People’s Republic of China.
    然而，知情人士警告說，中國的法律體系仍然視臺灣國民為中華人民共和國公民。
  • regard v. 將……視為、看待
    ...China’s legal system continues to regard Taiwanese nationals as citizens of the People’s Republic of China.
    ...中國的法律體系仍然視臺灣國民為中華人民共和國公民。
  • apply for phr. 申請
    Even if Hung neither applies for nor receives a Taiwan compatriot permit...
    即便洪申翰既沒有申請也沒有收到臺胞證...

文法重點

  • be to + 動詞原形 (表示預定、即將發生的官方計畫)
    The APEC Human Resources Development Ministerial Meeting is to commence on Monday next week in Nanjing.
    句型「is to + 原形動詞」常用於新聞英語中，用以表達官方排定的行程、預定要發生或即將執行的官方計畫。
  • 現在分詞當作附加補充說明 (..., making him... / ..., adding that...)
    Hung is to attend, making him the first minister in President William Lai’s (賴清德) administration to visit China.
    逗號後面的 「making him...」是由現在分詞構成的分詞構句，用來補充說明前面子句所產生的結果或帶來的影響，相當於 and makes him...
  • 被動語態的應用 (is/are to be done 或 have been held)
    Several APEC ministerial meetings have been held in China, with more than 200 Taiwanese officials attending over the years without incident, the official said.
    句中的 「have been held」使用了現在完成式的被動語態 (have/has been + 過去分詞)，強調會議「被」在中國舉辦的這個狀態。此外，後半部的 「with more than 200 Taiwanese officials attending...」則是標準的 「with + 受詞 + 分詞」複合結構，用來表達伴隨狀況。
  • 讓步子句 (Even if...)
    Even if Hung neither applies for nor receives a Taiwan compatriot permit, Chinese authorities may, without his knowledge, arrange for someone to obtain one on his behalf, the source said.
    「Even if」意即「即使、就算」，用來引導一個讓步副詞子句，表示即便前面假設的情況發生了，主要子句所敘述的事情（中國當局仍可能安排他人代辦）依然會成立。
