紐約客參加舊地鐵標誌與座椅拍賣會

New Yorkers attend auction for old subway signs, seats

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • annual adj. 年度的，每年的
    ...at a unique annual auction.
    ……在一場獨特的年度拍賣會上。
  • organize v. 組織，籌辦
    ...an open-air market organized by city authorities.
    ……由城市官方組織的露天市場。
  • maximum n. / adj. 最大量，最高限度
    Each person gets a maximum of 45 minutes to search...
    每個人最多有 45 分鐘的搜尋時間……
  • bear v. 帶有，負擔，承受
    ...a large sign bearing the name of Grand Central station...
    ……一個印有（帶有）中央車站名稱的大型標誌……
  • cost v. 花費（金錢）
    ...a medium-sized sign cost about US$500...
    ……一個中型標誌大約要價 500 美元……
  • director n. 主管，董事，主任
    ...said Paul Dvoskin, the director in charge of the program.
    ……負責該計畫的主管 Paul Dvoskin 表示。
  • purchase v. / n. 購買
    ...and to let people just come in and purchase these items...
    ……讓人們親自前來購買這些物品……
  • significantly adv. 顯著地，重大地
    ...it just ended up being significantly more popular than we ever thought it would be.
    ……結果它的受歡迎程度遠遠超出了我們的預期。
  • disclose v. 透露，公開
    Officials do not disclose how much the two-day sale brings in...
    官員們沒有透露這場為期兩天的特賣會帶來多少收入……

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作後置定語
    New York’s Metropolitan Transportation Authority has always resold its signs to collectors, but more recently opened up sales to the public, said Paul Dvoskin, the director in charge of the program.
    句尾的「in charge of the program」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前方的名詞 the director（主管），相當於關代子句 who is in charge of the program 的簡化版，是多益閱讀測驗中常見的修飾結構。
  • 過去分詞（片語）作後置定語
    ...an open-air market organized by city authorities.
  • 名詞子句作受詞
    Officials do not disclose how much the two-day sale brings in...
    句中的「how much the two-day sale brings in」是由疑問詞 how much 引導的名詞子句，在句中擔任及物動詞 disclose 的受詞，語序為「主詞 + 動詞」（the two-day sale brings in）。
  • 地方副詞片語置於句首的倒裝（部分倒裝的變體/強調）
    So are the MetroCards, the small yellow magnetic cards emblematic of the city...
    句型「So + be動詞 + 主詞」用來表示「……也是如此」，用以承接前一句肯定句的概念，達到言簡意賅的效果，這在多益的日常對話或短文閱讀中很常看到。
