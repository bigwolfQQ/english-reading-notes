Medical professionals, social workers speak up amid drug policy dispute

單字解析

  • issue v. 發布、發行
    On Sunday, the Taiwan Association of Clinical Psychology and the Association of Taiwan Clinical Psychologists issued a joint statement supporting harm-reduction measures...
    週日，台灣臨床心理學會與台灣臨床心理師協會發表聯合聲明，支持減害措施...
  • joint adj. 聯合的、共同的
    On Sunday, the Taiwan Association of Clinical Psychology and the Association of Taiwan Clinical Psychologists issued a joint statement supporting harm-reduction measures...
    週日，台灣臨床心理學會與台灣臨床心理師協會發表聯合聲明，支持減害措施...
  • apply to phr. 適用於
    However, the statement noted that methadone treatment applies to opioid dependence, such as heroin addiction.
    然而，聲明指出，美沙酮治療適用於鴉片類依賴，例如海洛因成癮。
  • continuity n. 連續性、持續性
    Treating addictions involving stimulants such as amphetamines, meanwhile, depends on the availability, continuity and quality of psychological and social interventions...
    同時，針對安非他命等興奮劑成癮的治療，則取決於心理與社會介入措施的可得性、持續性與品質...
  • emphasize v. 強調
    The Taiwan Union of Nurses Association also issued a statement Saturday, emphasizing that harm-reduction policies do not encourage drug use.
    台灣護理產業工會也於週六發布聲明，強調減害政策並不鼓勵吸毒。
  • urge v. 敦促、力勸
    It urged politicians to respect professional expertise and avoid sending unclear messages that could deepen social divisions.
    該工會敦促政治人物尊重專業專業，並避免發出可能加深社會分化的模糊訊息。
  • strengthen v. 加強、鞏固
    The nursing association also called on the government to strengthen drug prevention and addiction treatment systems across the medical...
    護理公會同時呼籲政府強化醫療...等各界的毒品防制與成癮治療體系
  • call on phr. 號召、呼籲
    The nursing association also called on the government to strengthen drug prevention and addiction treatment systems...
    護理公會同時呼籲政府強化醫療、護理、心理、社工、教育及司法等各界的毒品防制與成癮治療體系...
  • apologize v. 道歉
    ...called on opposition Kuomintang (KMT) lawmaker Lee Yen-hsiu (李彥秀) to apologize for suggesting earlier that day...
    ...呼籲在野黨國民黨立委李彥秀，為其稍早暗示...一事道歉
  • reintegrate v. 使重新融入（社會等）
    The union said social workers help people who use drugs recover and reintegrate into society.
    該工會表示社工協助藥癮者恢復並重新融入社會。
  • division n. 分工、劃分、部門
    The NUSW also urged the Ministry of Health and Welfare to explain the division of responsibilities among professionals involved in Taiwan's drug prevention and harm-reduction programs.
    全國社會工作人員產業工會也敦促衛生福利部說明參與台灣毒品防制與減害計畫各專業人員之間的權責劃分。
  • effective adj. 有效的
    In a statement issued Saturday, Lee said she 'completely agreed' with the NUSW that effective anti-drug policies should be based on scientific evidence...
    李彥秀在週六發布的聲明中表示，她『完全同意』社工工會的看法，即有效之反毒政策應基於科學證據...
  • oppose v. 反對
    However, she said she 'firmly opposed establishing a community center to provide drug users with clean needles and drugs, and incorporating social worker support.'
    然而，她表示『堅決反對設立社區中心以提供吸毒者乾淨針具與毒品，與納入社工支援』。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（用現在分詞表示附帶情況或主動動作）
    ...amid a political debate fueled largely by Taipei mayoral candidates ahead of the Nov. 28 local elections.
    句中的 "fueled largely by..." 是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞 "a political debate"，代表該政治辯論「被」台北市長參選人大致上所 fueled（點燃/推動）。
  • 關係代間質與限定修飾（用現在分詞片語修飾名詞）
    The dispute has centered on Taipei Mayor Chiang Wan-an (蔣萬安), whose campaign questioned whether Shen's position on drugs and his proposed policies would encourage drug abuse.
    句中使用所有格關係代間詞 "whose" 來連接主句與市長蔣萬安的競選團隊（campaign），說明該競選團隊做了什麼事，使句子結構緊湊。
  • 被動語態的運用
    Administered orally under medical supervision, it is used to treat heroin dependence.
    句首省略了 "It is" 的分詞片語 "Administered orally under medical supervision"（在醫療監督下以口服方式給藥），以及主句的 "it is used to treat..."（它被用來治療...），皆為新聞英語中常見精簡且運用被動語態的寫法。
