專題報導：中國海警轉向太平洋

FEATURE:Chinese coast guard turns to the Pacific

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • deploy v. 部署、調動
    China has deployed on average two Coast Guard cutters per month east of Taiwan since June...
    自 6 月以來，中國平均每個月在台灣以東部署兩艘海警船……
  • enforce v. 執行、實施（法律、封鎖等）
    Controlling the east would become essential to enforcing an effective blockade of Taiwan during a Chinese invasion of the island.
    在中國對該島發動侵略期間，控制東部對於實施對台灣的有效封鎖將變得至關重要。
  • disrupt v. 打亂、擾亂、中斷
    Disrupting shipping off the Pacific coast would have global implications for commodities.
    破壞太平洋海岸的航運將對大宗商品產生全球影響。
  • implication n. 可能的影響、含意、暗示
    Disrupting shipping off the Pacific coast would have global implications for commodities.
    破壞太平洋海岸的航運將對大宗商品產生全球影響。
  • strategic adj. 戰略性的
    While the Taiwan Strait handles almost half the world’s container fleet, the nation’s eastern coast holds strategic importance by forming a maritime link with the Philippines and wider Pacific.
    雖然台灣海峽承載了全球近一半的貨櫃船隊，但台灣東海岸透過與菲律賓及更廣闊的太平洋形成海上連結，具有戰略重要性。
  • strengthen v. 加強、鞏固
    The Chinese Coast Guard (CCG) has described such deployments as “routine law-enforcement” patrols, and has pledged to strengthen patrols to safeguard Beijing’s maritime rights.
    中國海警局（CCG）將此類部署描述為「routine law-enforcement」巡邏，並誓言要加強巡邏以維護北京的海上權利。
  • response n. 回應、反應
    Vessels have been dispatched in a timely manner in response, it added.
    該署並補充說，已適時調派艦船進行因應。
  • negotiation n. 談判、協商
    Days earlier, Japan and the Philippines had announced plans to start negotiations to delimit their sea boundaries...
    幾天前，日本和菲律賓宣佈計畫開始談判以劃定其海上邊界……
  • pretext n. 藉口、托詞
    “Beijing is using the joint statement issued by Japan and the Philippines as a pretext for greatly expanding its presence in these waters,” said Ryan Martinson...
    雷恩·馬丁森表示：「北京正以日本和菲律賓發表的聯合聲明作為藉口，大幅擴大其在這些水域的存在。」
  • commercial adj. 商業的、商務的
    During their five-day voyage, the vessels for the first time questioned foreign commercial ships passing along Taiwan’s Pacific coast.
    在為期五天的航行中，這些船隻首次盤問了沿著台灣太平洋海岸航行的外國商船。
  • authority n. 當局、官方、權力
    “If those interactions become routine, the next question is whether Chinese authorities begin to request more information,” said Zack Liao...
    廖彥棋表示：「如果這些互動變成例行公事，接下來的問題就是中國當局是否會開始索取更多資訊。」
  • persistent adj. 持續的、堅持不懈的
    “Using the CCG rather than the People’s Liberation Army Navy avoids military confrontations and allows Beijing to portray these activities as law enforcement while establishing the persistent presence necessary for a future blockade,” the US-China Economic and Security Review Commission said.
    美中經濟與安全審議委員會表示：「使用中國海警局而非中國人民解放軍海軍，可以避免軍事對抗，並允許北京將這些活動描繪為執法行動，同時為未來的封鎖建立必要的持續存在。」

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Phrase as Modifier / Adverbial）表附帶狀況或結果
    Ships stayed 30 nautical miles (55km) from the nation’s eastern coast, remaining outside Taiwan-claimed waters.
    句尾的「remaining outside...」是由現在分詞帶領的分詞片語，用來補充說明主句動作發生時的同時狀態（即船隻停留在 30 海里處，同時保持在台灣宣稱的水域之外）。
  • 關係代名詞子句（Relative Clause）修飾先行詞
    Sea lanes in the area lead to key ports and military facilities, where the US and its regional allies could deliver supplies...
    句中的「where the US and its regional allies could deliver supplies」是非限定用法的地方關係子句（where 代替 in which），用來補充說明前面的先行詞「key ports and military facilities」。
  • 動名詞片語當作主詞（Gerund Phrase as Subject）
    Controlling the east would become essential to enforcing an effective blockade of Taiwan during a Chinese invasion of the island.
    句首的「Controlling the east」是動名詞片語，作為整個句子的主詞，視為單數，因此搭配助動詞 would become。
  • 名詞子句（Noun Clause）作受詞
    “What Beijing is testing east of Taiwan is whether it can gradually rewrite expectations about who governs navigation and maritime order in the Western Pacific,” said Liao.
    引言中的「What Beijing is testing...」是由疑問代名詞 what 引導的名詞子句，在句中作主詞；而句尾的「whether it can gradually rewrite...」則是名詞子句作表語（is 的補語），用來指明測試的內容。
