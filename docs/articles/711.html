單字解析

  • authorities n. 當局，官方
    Turkish police on Sunday swooped on gay bars and the homes of LGBTQ activists, arresting at least 60 people in raids authorities said were aimed at boosting traditional family values, officials and activists said.
    土耳其警方於週日突襲了同性戀酒吧以及 LGBTQ 活動人士的住所，逮捕了至少 60 人。官方與活動人士表示，這些突襲行動旨在提倡傳統家庭價值。
  • target v. 把...作為攻擊目標，針對
    Turkey’s LGBTQ community has frequently been targeted by the government, with Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdogan lashing it as a “deviant movement” of “perverts” responsible for the falling birthrate.
    土耳其的 LGBTQ 社群經常成為政府的目標，土耳其總統雷傑普·塔伊普·埃爾多安痛批這是導致生育率下降的「變態份子」之「偏差運動」。
  • collaboration n. 合作，協力
    “As part of the ‘My Family is Safe’ operations ... carried out in collaboration with our police and gendarmerie forces, legal proceedings have been initiated against 162 suspects, 9 organizations and 13 companies in ... a total of 15 provinces,” Turkish Minister of Justice Akin Gurlek posted on social media.
    土耳其司法部長阿金·居爾萊克（Akin Gurlek）在社群媒體上發文稱：「作為在我們警方與憲兵部隊合作下展開的『我的家庭很安全』行動的一部分……已對 15 個省份的 162 名嫌疑人、9 個組織和 13 家公司啟動了法律訴訟。」

    Judicial authorities in Ankara had alleged that Kaos GL made “posts containing obscenity” on its Web site and social media channels, and that the content was accessible to children, the group said.

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    “As part of the ‘My Family is Safe’ operations ... carried out in collaboration with our police and gendarmerie forces, legal proceedings have been initiated against 162 suspects...
    句中的 "carried out in collaboration with..." 是一個過去分詞片語，用來修飾其前面的名詞 "operations"。這相當於一個被動語態的形容詞子句 "which were carried out in collaboration with..." 的簡化版，省略了關係代名詞與be動詞，使句子更為精煉。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Turkey’s LGBTQ community has frequently been targeted by the government...
    此句使用了現在完成式的被動語態 ("has frequently been targeted")。當句子的重點在於承受動作的對象（此處為 LGBTQ 社群）而非執行者時，常使用被動語態。結構為：主詞 + has/have been + 過去分詞 (V-pp)。
  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    “This imposition of a moral agenda is a blatant violation of fundamental rights & Turkey’s international commitments taking the country down a dark path...”
    句尾的 "taking the country down a dark path..." 是現在分詞片語，在此處作分詞構句或補充說明，用來描述前面整件事情所導致的結果或伴隨的狀態，相當於 "which takes the country down a dark path"。
  • 讓步副詞子句 (Concessive Adverbial Clause)
    While same-sex relations are not a criminal offense in Turkey, homophobia is widespread.
    句首的 "While" 引導一個讓步副詞子句，意為「雖然」或「儘管」，用來表達兩個子句之間語意上的轉折與對比（雖然同性關係合法，但恐同症依然普遍）。
