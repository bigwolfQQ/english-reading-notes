中國微短劇扭曲歷史：學者

Chinese microdrama distorts history: academic

單字解析

  • fund v. 資助，提供資金
    The microdrama is funded and presented by Jingcai Taiwan, a Chinese state-run media platform of the Beijing Municipal Taiwan Affairs Office, with its premiere held in Beijing on Friday last week.
    該微短劇由北京市人民政府台灣事務辦公室旗下的中國官媒平臺「精彩台灣」出資並製作，其首映會於上週五在北京舉行。
  • anonymous adj. 匿名的
    The Taiwanese academic, specializing in Qing and Taiwan history, said on condition of anonymity that historical records clearly show that during the more than 200 years of Qing rule over Taiwan, no Qing emperor, including Emperor Jiaqing (嘉慶), ever set foot in the nation.
    該名專門研究清代及臺灣史的臺灣學者在匿名情況下表示，歷史紀錄清楚顯示，在清朝統治臺灣的兩百多年期間，沒有任何一位清朝皇帝（包括嘉慶皇帝）曾經踏上過這個國家（臺灣）的土地。
  • priority n. 優先事項（句中用 prioritize 動詞形式）
    Taiwanese in the 19th century created the story of Emperor Jiaqing traveling secretly to Taiwan mainly because the Beijing-based Qing Dynasty adopted a repressive policy that prioritized guarding against Taiwan over governing Taiwan, the academic said.
    學者表示，19世紀的臺灣人之所以創作嘉慶皇帝微服出巡到臺灣的故事，主要是因為以北京為基地的清朝採取了「防臺重於治臺」的壓迫性政策。
  • restrict v. 限制，限定
    At the time, the Qing court regarded Taiwan as a hotspot of rebellion and strictly restricted cross-strait travel with a ban on bringing family to Taiwan, as the nation was primarily populated by remnants of the previous dynasty, they said.
    他們指出，當時清廷視臺灣為叛亂溫床，並嚴格限制兩岸往來，實施禁止攜眷赴臺的渡臺禁令，因為當時該地主要居住著前朝的遺民。
  • deploy v. 調動，部署（軍隊或設備）
    The situation was compounded by exorbitant taxation for Taiwan, exclusion of Taiwanese from official positions and the deployment of garrison forces using only troops from China’s Fujian or Guangdong provinces, they said.
    他們指出，雪上加霜的是，臺灣承受了苛捐雜稅、臺灣人被排擠在官方職位之外，且駐防部隊僅部署來自中國福建或廣東省的軍隊。
  • exclusion n. 排斥，排除
    The situation was compounded by exorbitant taxation for Taiwan, exclusion of Taiwanese from official positions and the deployment of garrison forces using only troops from China’s Fujian or Guangdong provinces, they said.
    他們指出，雪上加霜的是，臺灣承受了苛捐雜稅、臺灣人被排擠在官方職位之外，且駐防部隊僅部署來自中國福建或廣東省的軍隊。
  • populace n. 平民百姓，大眾
    The populace had no choice but to pin their hopes on the imaginary arrival of a savior monarch, they said, adding that the folktale was not only a psychological compensation during years of hardships, but a silent indictment and satire of the local officials’ tyranny.
    他們表示別無選擇的人民只能將希望寄託於救世君王的虛構降臨，並補充說，這則民間故事不僅是多年苦難中的心理補償，更是對地方官員暴政無聲的控訴與諷刺。
  • attempt n. / v. 企圖，嘗試
    China’s attempt to use the micro drama to bring the two sides of the Strait closer would only backfire, the scholar said.
    學者表示，中國試圖利用微短劇拉近兩岸距離的做法只會適得其反。
  • backfire v. 事與願違，產生反效果
    China’s attempt to use the micro drama to bring the two sides of the Strait closer would only backfire, the scholar said.
    學者表示，中國試圖利用微短劇拉近兩岸距離的做法只會適得其反。
  • stunt n. 噱頭，政治花招
    他們表示，這場政治特技不僅重新揭開了中國中央政府歷史上對臺灣實施歧視性待遇與不平等政策的傷疤，也向國際社會和臺灣大眾證明，北京既不懂臺灣的歷史，也不懂現代臺灣社會的心態與民主價值。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作為後置修飾語 (Present/Past Participle as Postmodifier)
    The Taiwanese academic, specializing in Qing and Taiwan history, said on condition of anonymity...
    句中的 "specializing in Qing and Taiwan history" 是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明主詞 "The Taiwanese academic" 的背景（專門研究清代及臺灣史）。這類結構在多益閱讀測驗中極常出現，可省略 "who specializes in" 來簡化句子。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Nominative Absolute Construction)
    ...with its premiere held in Beijing on Friday last week.
    此處使用 「with + 名詞 + 過去分詞 (held)」 的複合結構，用來表達附帶狀況。名詞 "its premiere" 與動詞 "hold" 之間是被動關係，因此使用過去分詞 "held"。這個句型在多益商業書信或新聞報導中經常用於描述伴隨發生的事件。
  • 過去分詞作形容詞修飾名詞 (Past Participle as Adjective)
    The situation was compounded by exorbitant taxation for Taiwan, exclusion of Taiwanese from official positions...
    句中的 "compounded" 是過去分詞作被動狀態形容詞，用來形容情況「加劇的、雪上加霜的」。原句使用被動語態 "was compounded by..."，在商業情境或正式寫作中常用來表達問題或負面狀況遭到其他因素惡化。
  • 雙重否定表達強烈肯定 / 頻率否定 (Double Negative / Correlation)
    ...no Qing emperor, including Emperor Jiaqing (嘉慶), ever set foot in the nation.
    句中運用了否定詞 "no" 搭配副詞 "ever"（強調從來不曾），精準表達強烈的否定語氣。在多益聽力與閱讀中，注意像 "no... ever" 或 "hardly... ever" 這類表示絕對否定的句型，常是細節題的關鍵。
