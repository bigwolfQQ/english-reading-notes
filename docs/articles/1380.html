文法重點

  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense)
    The Coast Guard Administration (CGA) said Wednesday that two of its patrol vessels had docked at Palau's Malakal Harbor...
    句子中的主句動詞「said」是用過去式，用來描述過去發生的動作；而在說話之前，兩艘巡防艦「已經停泊」完成的動作，則使用過去完成式（had docked）來表達「過去某一時間之前已經完成的動作」。
  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...after it sent its first-ever goodwill fleet, comprising the Hualien and Taipei patrol vessels, to Palau in 2025.
    句中的「comprising the Hualien and Taipei patrol vessels」是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前面的名詞「its first-ever goodwill fleet（敦睦艦隊）」是由哪些艦艇所組成的，相當於形容詞子句「which comprised...」的簡化形式。
  • 被動語態搭配預期用法 (Passive Voice with be expected to)
    During the visit, a delegation led by CGA Deputy Director-General Hsu Ching-chih (許靜芝) is expected to meet with Palauan President Surangel Whipps Jr...
    「is expected to」是被動語態的常見結構，字面意思是「被預期去...」，在新聞英語中常用來報導尚未發生但按計畫極可能進行的官方行程或預測。
  • 對等連接詞引導的非限定性子句 (Non-defining Clause with during which)
    The two sides are scheduled to hold a banquet aboard the Chiayi, during which the national flags of Taiwan and Palau will be raised, according to the branch.
    片語「during which」是由介系詞「during」加上關代「which」組成，用來引導非限定性子句，修飾前面的先行詞「a banquet」，並指在該餐宴「進行的期間」會同時發生升旗儀式。
