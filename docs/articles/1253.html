亞特蘭大勇士隊擊敗道奇隊，將系列賽扳平

Atlanta Braves overcome Dodgers to level series

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • series n. 系列賽、連續
    The Atlanta Braves on Sunday shut down the Los Angeles Dodgers’ star-studded batting lineup to score a series-leveling 3-2 victory in MLB playoffs
    亞特蘭大勇士隊於週日封鎖了洛杉磯道奇隊眾星雲集的打線，在美國職棒大聯盟季後賽中以 3 比 2 奪勝，將系列賽扳平
  • performance n. 表現、績效
    A superb collective Braves pitching performance set up a vital win at Dodger Stadium
    勇士隊極佳的投手群整體表現，在道奇體育場拿下這場至關重要的勝利
  • vital adj. 至關重要的、不可或缺的
    A superb collective Braves pitching performance set up a vital win at Dodger Stadium
    勇士隊極佳的投手群整體表現，在道奇體育場拿下這場至關重要的勝利
  • contribution n. 貢獻
    Braves catcher Drake Baldwin saluted the contribution of the 21-year-old Fuentes
    勇士隊捕手德雷克·鮑德溫對 21 歲選手富恩特斯的貢獻表示讚賞
  • confidence n. 信心、自信
    We’ve got a ton of confidence in him, and he went out and showed it
    我們對他非常有信心，他上場並證明了這點
  • advantage n. 優勢
    San Diego seized a 1-0 lead in the first inning and capitalized on Brewers errors to push the advantage to 2-0 in the fifth.
    聖地牙哥教士隊在第一局率先拿下 1 分領先，並利用釀酒人隊的失誤在第五局將優勢擴大到 2 比 0。
  • capitalize on phr. v. 利用……獲利、把握機會
    San Diego seized a 1-0 lead in the first inning and capitalized on Brewers errors to push the advantage to 2-0 in the fifth.
    聖地牙哥教士隊在第一局率先拿下 1 分領先，並利用釀酒人隊的失誤在第五局將優勢擴大到 2 比 0。
  • opportunity n. 機會
    We’re going to keep going, and we’re going to battle for every opportunity that we get.
    我們會繼續前進，為我們得到的每一個機會奮戰到底。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Present Participle）作後位修飾
    The Dodgers — the reigning back-to-back World Series champions who are chasing a rare treble of championships — were left ruing an out-of-sorts performance from ace starter Blake Snell and a toothless display from their bats, who were for 0-for-8 with runners in scoring position.
    句中的 "chasing a rare treble of championships" 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的主詞 "the reigning back-to-back World Series champions"，等於形容詞子句 "who are chasing..."，在新聞英語中常用來補充說明人物或事物的狀態。
  • 分詞構句表結果
    A superb collective Braves pitching performance set up a vital win at Dodger Stadium that leaves the best-of-five National League division series finely balanced at 1-1 ahead of Game 3 in Atlanta, Georgia, today.
    句尾的 "that leaves the best-of-five..." 關係子句帶有結果的意味，用來表達前述的投手表現促成了這場勝利，並「使得」系列賽呈現 1 比 1 的平手局面。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞 / 慣用語型
    For the second straight day the Brewers rallied from 2-0 down to beat the Padres, who are now in survival mode as the best-of-five National League division series shifts to San Diego for game three on Tuesday.
    句中的 "be in survival mode" 是一個常見的慣用表現（生存模式／背水一戰），用來形容面臨淘汰邊緣時的緊繃狀態。
