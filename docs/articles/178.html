累得像狗

Dog-tired

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • retired adj. 退休的
    Seven retired rescue dogs and their adoptive families pose for a group photograph...
    七隻退役的搜救犬和牠們的領養家庭合影留念...
  • host v. 主辦，主持
    ...during the “Retired Search-and-Rescue Dogs Homecoming” event hosted by the Taichung City Fire Department...
    ...在由臺中市政府消防局主辦的「退役搜救犬回娘家」活動中...
  • department n. 部門，局，處
    ...hosted by the Taichung City Fire Department in the city’s Wufeng District yesterday.
    ...昨天於該市霧峰區（舉辦）。
  • event n. 活動，事件
    ...during the “Retired Search-and-Rescue Dogs Homecoming” event...
    ...在「退役搜救犬回娘家」活動期間...

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾
    ...during the “Retired Search-and-Rescue Dogs Homecoming” event hosted by the Taichung City Fire Department in the city’s Wufeng District yesterday.
    句中的「hosted by...」是由過去分詞片語構成的後置修飾語，用來修飾前面的名詞 event（活動），相當於形容詞子句「which was hosted by...」。在多益閱讀測驗中，常考分詞與被修飾名詞之間的受動（被動）關係。
