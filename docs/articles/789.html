泰國表示，柬埔寨在「裝受害者」

Cambodia ‘playing the victim,’ Thailand says

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • dispute n. 爭端，糾紛
    Thailand yesterday said that Cambodia was playing the victim in a dispute over maritime resources...
    泰國昨天表示，在針對海洋資源爭端的爭議中，柬埔寨是在裝作受害者……
  • resolution n. 解決，決議
    ...said it hoped the hearings would yield a peaceful resolution for both countries.
    ……並表示希望聽證會能為兩國帶來和平的解決方案。
  • unilaterally adv. 單方面地
    In May, Thailand unilaterally pulled out of a bilateral framework agreement...
    今年五月，泰國單方面退出了一項雙邊框架協議……
  • bilateral adj. 雙邊的
    ...that aimed to resolve overlapping maritime border claims, but denied any link to their land dispute.
    ……旨在解決重疊海洋邊界聲索，但否認這與他們的陸地爭端有任何關聯。
  • implement v. 實施，落實
    Thailand has said it withdrew from the framework agreement, called “MOU 44,” because “no progress had been made” in implementing it.
    泰國表示，其退出了名為「44號備忘錄」的框架協議，是因為在落實該協議方面「沒有取得任何進展」。
  • terminate v. 終止，結束
    Cambodia said last week that it had resorted to conciliation “after Thailand unilaterally terminated the agreed bilateral framework”...
    柬埔寨上週表示，在「泰國單方面終止了雙邊同意的框架」之後，它才訴諸調解……
  • objective n. 目標，目的
    “Our overriding objective is to achieve a resolution that serves the people of both nations...”
    「我們的首要目標是達成一項服務於兩國人民的解決方案……」
  • contribute to v. phr. 貢獻於，促成
    “...and contribute to peace, cooperation and share prosperity in the region,” Prak Sokhonn said.
    巴速坤表示：「……並為該地區的和平、合作與共享繁榮做出貢獻。」
  • constructively adv. 建設性地
    “Cambodia sincerely hopes that Thailand will engage constructively in this process,” he added.
    他補充說：「柬埔寨 sincerely 希望泰國能建設性地參與這一進程。」
  • domestic adj. 國內的
    Analysts have said that Thailand’s withdrawal from the 2001 agreement and Cambodia’s conciliation case were driven by domestic political calculations.
    分析人士表示，泰國退出 2001 年協議以及柬埔寨的調解案，都是受到國內政治算計的驅動。

文法重點

  • 非限定關係代間詞子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Cambodia, which initiated the case before the Permanent Court of Arbitration (PCA) in Singapore, said it hoped the hearings would yield a peaceful resolution for both countries.
    句中的「which initiated the case...」是用逗號隔開的非限定關係子句，用來補充說明主詞 Cambodia 的額外資訊，去掉這個子句不影響主要句意。
  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況/結果 (Participial Phrase)
    Last year’s clashes in July and December left dozens of people dead and displaced more than a million before a truce was agreed.
    句尾的「displaced more than a million...」是現在分詞片語引導的分詞構句，用來表達伴隨前面動詞 left 產生的連續結果。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The 2001 memorandum of understanding covers a resource-rich maritime territory of about 27,000km2 to which both Cambodia and Thailand lay claim.
    文章中常使用被動語態來客觀陳述新聞事實，例如「a truce was agreed」（達成停火協議）或「no progress had been made」（未取得進展）。
  • 介系詞 + 關係代間詞 (Preposition + Relative Pronoun)
    ...a resource-rich maritime territory of about 27,000km2 to which both Cambodia and Thailand lay claim.
    「to which」中的 to 是動詞片語 lay claim to（對……聲索主權）的介系詞，往前移至關係代間詞 which 前面，用來連接修飾前面的 maritime territory。
