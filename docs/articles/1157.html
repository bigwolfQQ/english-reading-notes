無人機策略超越生產製造

Drone strategy beyond production

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • resilience n. 韌性，應變能力
    Over the past two weeks, the Taipei Security Dialogue and the Whole-of-Society Defense Resilience International Forum brought together leading figures working on Taiwan’s defense and resilience...
    過去兩週裡，「台北安全對話」與「全民國防韌性國際論壇」匯聚了致力於台灣國防與韌性的重要人物...
  • initiative n. 新措施，倡議
    At the Taipei Security Dialogue, Stanford University Freeman Spogli Institute for International Studies fellow Oriana Skylar Mastro made an interesting observation that many of Taiwan’s defense initiatives are intended to influence the audience in Washington.
    在台北安全對話中，史丹佛大學弗里曼·斯波利國際問題研究所研究員奧莉安娜·斯凯拉·馬斯特羅提出了一個有趣的觀察：台灣許多的國防倡議旨在影響華盛頓的受眾。
  • deterrence n. 嚇阻，威懾
    Taiwan faces an adversary considerably larger in military and economic terms, hence continued US support is an important element of deterrence.
    台灣面臨的對手在軍事和經濟規模上都大得多，因此持續的美國支持是嚇阻的重要元素。
  • objective n. 目標，目的
    Ukraine, Iran and other countries have all employed forms of asymmetric warfare, but under different circumstances, force structures and strategic objectives.
    烏克蘭、伊朗和其他國家都曾採用過各種形式的不對稱作戰，但處境、兵力結構與戰略目標各有不同。
  • accelerate v. 加速，促進
    Taiwan subsequently accelerated drone and counterdrone development, while the “Drone National Team” and strong government support helped bring together manufacturers and technology companies.
    隨後台灣加速了無人機及反無人機的發展，而「無人機國家隊」與政府的強大支持則促成了製造商與科技公司的結合。
  • procurement n. 採購，尋源
    Much of the attention surrounding Taiwan’s drones now concerns manufacturing capacity, export growth, international partnerships and procurement targets.
    目前圍繞台灣無人機的關注，很大一部分涉及製造產能、出口成長、國際夥伴關係與採購目標。
  • inventory n. 存貨，庫存
    Procurement increases inventory quickly, but institutional adaptation is considerably harder.
    採購能迅速增加庫存，但體制上的調適卻困難得多。
  • adapt v. 適應，調適
    Their models cannot simply be transferred to Taiwan, but they demonstrate a common principle: Hardware becomes strategically meaningful only when institutions adapt around it.
    他們的模式無法直接套用到台灣，但他們展示了一個共同原則：唯有當體制圍繞著硬體進行調適時，硬體才具備戰略意義。
  • bolster v. 支撐，加強，鞏固
    A self-sustaining drone industry, secure supply chains and access to international markets can bolster economic and national security.
    具備自我永續能力的無人機產業、安全的供應鏈以及進入國際市場的管道，可以鞏固經濟與國家安全。
  • component n. 零件，組件，核心要素
    If the objective is also to make uncrewed systems a central component of asymmetric defense, manufacturing and procurement must be accompanied by less visible work on doctrine, command and control, training, maintenance and force integration.
    如果目標同時也是要讓無人系統成為不對稱防衛的核心組件，那麼製造與採購就必須伴隨著在準則、指揮與管制、訓練、維修以及兵力整合方面較不顯眼的工作。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The appearance of Chinese civilian drones over military positions in Kinmen County in 2022 provided an early indication of how inexpensive drones could create disproportionate operational problems.
    句中的 "over military positions in Kinmen County in 2022" 是介系詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞短語 "Chinese civilian drones" 的所在位置。而在文章其他地方（例如第1段的 "working on Taiwan’s defense and resilience"）則常見現在分詞片語置於名詞後方，修飾前面的名詞，表示主動的動作。
  • 條件句與假設語氣句構 (Conditional Structure with If)
    If Taiwan’s objective is primarily to become a globally competitive drone manufacturing hub, current industrial progress is encouraging.
    此句使用 "If + 現在式動詞, 主句現在式動詞" 的條件句型，用來表達基於某個假設前提（如果目標是……）所對應的現實狀況與評估（當前的進展是令人鼓舞的），在商業與政策論述中非常常態地用來推演不同情境。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    If the objective is also to make uncrewed systems a central component of asymmetric defense, manufacturing and procurement must be accompanied by less visible work on doctrine, command and control, training, maintenance and force integration.
    句中的 "must be accompanied by..." 採用了被動語態（助動詞 must + be + 過去分詞 accompanied），強調主體（製造與採購）必須「被」哪些看不到的幕後工作所伴隨，藉此突顯主被動關係與重要配合事項。
  • 形容詞比較級修飾語 (Modifiers of Comparatives)
    Taiwan faces an adversary considerably larger in military and economic terms, hence continued US support is an important element of deterrence.
    句中使用副詞 "considerably"（相當大地、顯著地）來修飾形容詞比較級 "larger"，用以加強語氣，精準描述對手在軍事與經濟規模上「大得多」的程度。
