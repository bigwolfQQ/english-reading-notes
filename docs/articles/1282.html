台灣彩券推出 2 款新刮刮樂，總獎金池達 21.1 億元

Taiwan Lottery launches 2 new scratch cards with NT$2.11B prize pool

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • launch v. 推出，發布
    Taiwan Lottery Co. on Monday launched two new scratch cards offering more than 3.48 million prizes...
    台灣彩券公司於週一推出兩款新刮刮樂，提供超過348萬個獎項...
  • offer v. 提供
    ...two new scratch cards offering more than 3.48 million prizes with a combined prize pool exceeding NT$2.11 billion...
    ...兩款新刮刮樂，提供超過348萬個獎項，總獎金池超過新台幣21.1億元...
  • exceed v. 超過，勝過
    ...with a combined prize pool exceeding NT$2.11 billion (US$66.39 million).
    ...總獎金池超過新台幣21.1億元（約合6,639萬美元）。
  • statement n. 聲明，陳述
    In a statement, Taiwan Lottery said the first scratch card -- "Adding Gold to 10 (10來添金)" -- is priced at NT$500.
    台灣彩券在聲明中表示，第一款刮刮樂「10來添金」每張售價為新台幣500元。
  • price v. 為……標價，定價
    ...the first scratch card -- "Adding Gold to 10 (10來添金)" -- is priced at NT$500.
    ...第一款刮刮樂「10來添金」每張售價為新台幣500元。
  • corresponding adj. 相應的，對應的
    ...with prizes awarded for the corresponding combinations.
    ...即可依相應的組合贏得獎項。
  • total adj. 總計的，全部的
    ...and a total prize pool of NT$1.35 billion.
    ...總獎金池達新台幣13.5億元。
  • expect v. 預期，期待
    About 41 percent of tickets are expected to win prizes.
    預計約有41%的彩券會中獎。
  • match v. 相配，相符
    ...when any of their "Your Numbers" match the "Lucky Numbers."
    ...玩家的「您的號碼」若與「幸運號碼」相符...
  • identical adj. 相同的，完全相同的
    In Game 2, players who reveal three identical prize amounts win that prize, Taiwan Lottery added.
    台灣彩券補充說明，在玩法2中，玩家若刮出三個相同的獎金金額，即可贏得該筆獎金。
  • add v. 補充說，增加
    ...adding that about 30.4 percent of tickets are expected to win a prize.
    ...並補充預計約有30.4%的彩券會中獎。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾
    Taiwan Lottery Co. on Monday launched two new scratch cards offering more than 3.48 million prizes...
    句中的 "offering more than 3.48 million prizes" 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "two new scratch cards"，相當於形容詞子句 "which offer more than 3.48 million prizes"，在新聞英語中非常常見，能讓句子更簡潔。
  • 被動語態表示預期狀況
    About 41 percent of tickets are expected to win prizes.
    此句使用被動語態 "are expected to..."（被預期……），用來客觀地表達機率或預估結果，避免直接說「我們預期」，使商業與新聞報導更具公信力與客觀性。
  • 關係代名詞子句（主格）
    In Game 2, players who reveal three identical prize amounts win that prize...
    句中的 "who reveal three identical prize amounts" 是由關係代名詞 who 引導的形容詞子句，用來限定修飾先行詞 players（玩家）。
