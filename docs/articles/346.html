逾百人登外傘頂洲對抗沙土侵蝕

More than 100 land on Caisancho Island to combat sand erosion

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • combat v. 對抗，打擊
    More than 100 people traveled to Caisancho Island (外傘頂洲), Taiwan's largest offshore shoal, on Tuesday to help protect it from erosion by building sand-trapping structures from discarded oyster racks.
    週二，超過100人前往台灣最大的離岸沙洲外傘頂洲，利用廢棄蚵架建造攔沙構造物，以協助保護該沙洲免受侵蝕。
  • erosion n. 侵蝕，腐蝕
    Lin Chun-ju (林春如), a division chief at the Chiayi County Environmental Protection Bureau, said the campaign turns discarded marine debris into structures that help combat sand erosion, putting the principles of a circular economy into practice.
    嘉義縣環保局科長林春如表示，這項活動將廢棄的海洋廢棄物轉化為有助於對抗沙土侵蝕的構造物，將循環經濟的原則付諸實踐。
  • organize v. 組織，籌辦
    Organized by the county's Environmental Protection Bureau, more than 100 staff members and volunteers traveled by recreational fishing boats to Caisancho Island...
    在該縣環境保護局的組織下，100多名員工和志願者搭乘休閒漁船前往外傘頂洲……
  • staff n. 員工，全體職員
    Organized by the county's Environmental Protection Bureau, more than 100 staff members and volunteers traveled by recreational fishing boats to Caisancho Island...
    在該縣環境保護局的組織下，100多名員工和志願者搭乘休閒漁船前往外傘頂洲……
  • carry out phr. v. 執行，實施
    ...to carry out sand-conservation work.
    ……以執行護沙工作。
  • reuse v. 重複使用
    The team reused abandoned bamboo oyster racks, oyster shells and other marine debris to build square-shaped sand-trapping structures...
    該團隊重複利用廢棄的竹製蚵架、蚵殼和其他海洋廢棄物，建造了正方形的攔沙構造物……
  • project n. 專案，計畫
    The project was carried out in collaboration with the Water Resources Agency under the Ministry of Economic Affairs...
    該計畫是由經濟部水利署協同執行……
  • collaboration n. 合作，協力
    The project was carried out in collaboration with the Water Resources Agency under the Ministry of Economic Affairs...
    該計畫是由經濟部水利署協同執行……
  • guidance n. 指導，引導
    ...with technical guidance from the Tainan Hydraulics Laboratory at National Cheng Kung University.
    ……並由國立成功大學台南水工試驗所提供技術指導。
  • decline v. / n. 下降，減少
    Chang Hui-chuan (張輝川), director of the Chiayi County Environmental Protection Bureau, said the shoal has long been threatened by erosion due to extreme weather and a declining sediment supply.
    嘉義縣環境保護局局長張輝川表示，由於極端氣候和泥沙供應量減少，該沙洲長期以來一直受到侵蝕的威脅。
  • stabilize v. 使穩定，穩固
    Caisancho Island serves as a natural buffer that protects the coastlines of Chiayi and Yunlin counties from wind and waves while helping stabilize the shoreline and providing habitat for marine life, Chang said.
    張輝川指出，外傘頂洲作為天然緩衝區，保護嘉義縣和雲林縣的海岸線免受風浪侵襲，同時有助於穩定海岸線並為海洋生物提供棲息地。
  • prevent v. 預防，防止
    The government has continued efforts in recent years to prevent the shoal from shrinking, he added.
    他補充說，政府近年來持續努力防止沙洲縮小。
  • impact n. 衝擊，影響
    Chang said the campaign serves as a practical effort to protect Taiwan's coastline from the impacts of climate change.
    張輝川表示，這項活動是保護台灣海岸線免受氣候變遷影響的實際行動。
  • principle n. 原則，信條
    Lin Chun-ju (林春如), a division chief at the Chiayi County Environmental Protection Bureau, said the campaign turns discarded marine debris into structures that help combat sand erosion, putting the principles of a circular economy into practice.
    嘉義縣環保局科長林春如表示，這項活動將廢棄的海洋廢棄物轉化為有助於對抗沙土侵蝕的構造物，將循環經濟的原則付諸實踐。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Organized by the county's Environmental Protection Bureau, more than 100 staff members and volunteers traveled by recreational fishing boats to Caisancho Island...
    句首的「Organized by...」是由過去分詞引導的分詞片語，修飾後方的主詞「more than 100 staff members and volunteers」。原句省略了「(Who were) Organized by...」，在多益閱讀測驗中常考這種分詞與被動語態的簡化結構。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The project was carried out in collaboration with the Water Resources Agency under the Ministry of Economic Affairs...
    句型「was carried out」使用了被動語態（be + 過去分詞），強調「計畫」本身被執行，而隱去了執行者的動作發起方式（或於後方用介系詞交代）。多益考試中經常需要辨識主動與被動語態的選填題。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    Caisancho Island serves as a natural buffer that protects the coastlines of Chiayi and Yunlin counties from wind and waves...
    句中的「that protects the coastlines...」是由關係代名詞 that 引導的限定性關係子句，用來修飾先行詞「a natural buffer」，補充說明該緩衝區的功能。這是多益文法題（尤其是代名詞與關係詞選填）的絕對核心考點。
