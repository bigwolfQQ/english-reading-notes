澳洲將為中國彙編報告的男子判處監禁

Australia jails man who compiled reports for China

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • convicted adj. 被定罪的
    An Australian man convicted of reckless foreign interference for compiling reports for Chinese intelligence was yesterday sentenced to more than three years in jail by a Sydney court.
    一名因替中國情報部門彙編報告而被判定犯有魯莽干預外國事務罪的澳洲男子，昨日被雪梨法院判處三年多監禁。
  • executive n. 主管，高階經理
    Marketing executive Alexander Csergo, 58, was arrested in Sydney by Australian police in 2023, shortly after he returned from living in Shanghai.
    58歲的行銷主管亞歷山大・瑟戈於2023年在雪梨被澳洲警方逮捕，當時距離他從上海生活返回不久。
  • partnership n. 夥伴關係，合作關係
    He was accused of accepting cash from Chinese intelligence agents to write reports on topics including Australia’s AUKUS nuclear submarine partnership with the US and the UK, and critical minerals.
    他被指控接受中國情報特工的現金，撰寫關於澳洲與美國及英國的「奧庫斯」核潛艇夥伴關係以及關鍵礦產等主題的報告。
  • eligible adj. 有資格的，具備條件的
    Sydney’s district court sentenced Csergo to three years and six months in jail, eligible for parole from next month, a court official said.
    雪梨地方法院判處瑟戈三年六個月監禁，法院官員表示，他從下個月起即可獲得假釋資格。
  • sector n. 部門，行業
    “In this case, the spies wanted the Australian to target individuals in Australia’s intelligence community, Prime Minister’s Office, defense sector, judiciary and think tanks,” he added.
    他補充說：「在此案中，間諜希望這名澳洲人將目標對準澳洲情報界、總理辦公室、國防部門、司法界和智庫的個人。」
  • criminalize v. 將……定為刑事犯罪
    Csergo is the second person to be convicted under foreign interference laws introduced in 2018 to criminalize activity that helps a foreign power interfere with Australia’s sovereignty or national interest.
    瑟戈是第二位根據2018年引入的外國干預法被定罪的人，該法旨在將協助外國勢力干預澳洲主權或國家利益的活動定為刑事犯罪。
  • network v. / n. 建立社交（或業務）網絡，聯網
    The court heard that Csergo had met two suspected Chinese agents, known to him as Ken and Evelyn, in Shanghai after they made contact on professional networking platform LinkedIn.
    法庭聽取證詞稱，瑟戈在專業社交平臺LinkedIn上與兩名自稱是肯和伊芙琳的嫌疑中國特工在上海會面。
  • exchange v. 交換，交流
    The court heard that Csergo exchanged thousands of WeChat messages with the pair between 2021 and 2023, and accepted cash in envelopes.
    法庭聽取證詞稱，瑟戈在2021年至2023年間與這兩人交換了數千條微信消息，並接受了信封裝的現金。
  • defense n. 防禦，辯護
    The Australian Federal Police Assistant Commissioner Cyber and Special Investigations Sandra Booth said the best defense against foreign interference was community awareness.
    澳洲聯邦警察網球與特別調查助理專員桑德拉・布斯表示，抵禦外國干預的最佳防線是社區意識。
  • influence v. 影響
    “Often foreign powers will use covert, deceptive or coercive activities to influence political processes, harm national security or threaten communities,” she said.
    她說：「外國勢力經常利用祕密、欺騙性或強迫性的活動來影響政治進程、危害國家安全或威脅社區。」

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾語
    An Australian man convicted of reckless foreign interference for compiling reports for Chinese intelligence was yesterday sentenced to more than three years in jail by a Sydney court.
    句中的 "convicted of reckless foreign interference..." 是一個過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的主詞 "An Australian man"。這相當於一個省略了主格與be動詞的關係代名詞子句（who was convicted of...），在多益閱讀測驗中常用來測試分詞的修飾用法。
  • 現在分詞短語作附帶狀況或原因說明
    Marketing executive Alexander Csergo, 58, was arrested in Sydney by Australian police in 2023, shortly after he returned from living in Shanghai.
    句尾的 "shortly after he returned from living in Shanghai" 包含了一個動名詞 "living"，置於介詞 "from" 後面。動名詞片語常跟在介詞後面作受詞，表示某個動作或狀態的持續。
  • 被動語態的運用
    He was accused of accepting cash from Chinese intelligence agents to write reports on topics...
    此句使用了被動語態 "was accused of"（被指控），在新聞報導中，當不強調施事者或施事者不明時，經常用被動語態來客觀陳述事實或指控。
  • 不定詞片語作目的副詞用
    Csergo is the second person to be convicted under foreign interference laws introduced in 2018 to criminalize activity that helps a foreign power...
    句尾的 "to criminalize activity..." 是不定詞片語，在此處用作副詞，用來修飾前面的動詞或形容詞，說明2018年引入這些法律的「目的」是為了將特定活動定為刑事犯罪。
