文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The National Taiwan University Hospital (NTUH) yesterday released the Healthy Taiwan Guidelines for Appropriate Use of Healthcare Services, providing healthcare recommendations for 1,067 common symptoms.
    句尾的「providing...」是由現在分詞帶領的片語，用來補充說明前面子句所發生的結果或伴隨動作，相當於「and it provides...」的簡化寫法，在新聞英語中非常常見。
  • 不定詞片語作目的副詞用 (Infinitive Phrase of Purpose)
    The NTUH Referral Management Center released the guidelines with the aim of addressing the phenomenon of seeking treatment at large hospitals for mild illnesses...
    片語「with the aim of...」後面接動名词，表達發布指引的「目的」。此類結構在商業與新聞英文中常用來精煉地表達某項行動的意圖。
  • 受詞補語／使役與知覺相關結構 (Object Complement / Relative Clause with Preposition)
    ...the center invited 256 medical experts from different levels of care for conducting a comprehensive review and held an in-person consensus meeting attended by 80 experts to finalize its conclusions.
    句中的「attended by 80 experts」是過去分詞片語作後位修飾，用來修飾前面的名詞「consensus meeting」（共識會議），表示該會議是被 80 位專家所出席的，省去了「which was attended by...」的關代與be動詞。
  • 非限定關係子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    The hotline would operate from 8am to 5pm daily and the service would be staffed by nurses and healthcare professionals, who would assess callers’ symptoms and urgency and provide guidance on the appropriate course of action...
    句中的「who would assess...」是一個非限定關係子句（前面有逗號），用來補充說明前面的先行詞「nurses and healthcare professionals」，提供關於這些醫護人員職責的額外資訊。
