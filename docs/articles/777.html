Taipei Times · 2026-09-15

重新思考臺灣的網路戰略

Rethinking Taiwan’s cyberstrategy

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • allocate v. 分派、分配（預算、資源等）
    The Ministry of National Defense has proposed establishing a military cyberhunt team and allocated NT$95.93 million (US$3.01 million) for the plan.
    國防部已提議成立一支軍事網路獵捕團隊，並為此計畫編列了 9,593 萬元新臺幣（合 301 萬美元）的預算。
  • budget n. 預算
    Taiwan’s attempt at a strategic leap and execution of a world-class threat-hunting strategy could potentially be strangled by a shoestring budget and a legal firewall...
    臺灣試圖實現戰略飛躍並執行世界級的威脅獵捕戰略，很可能會被微薄的預算以及將軍事獵捕團隊與重要民用基礎設施隔離開來的法律防火牆所扼殺。
  • strategy n. 策略、戰略
    Unfortunately, the era of modern digital warfare is incompatible with static defense strategies.
    不幸的是，現代數位戰爭的時代與靜態防禦戰略格格不入。
  • enhance v. 提高、增加、增強
    In addition to national military reform, the primary concern of the command’s contribution is its capacity to enhance the construction of alliance subdiplomacy.
    除了國家軍事改革之外，該指揮部貢獻的主要關注點在於其增強聯盟次外交建設的能力。
  • implement v. 實施、執行
    Taiwan’s implementation of a “persistent engagement” strategy is an essential and overdue move in long-term military strategy.
    臺灣實施「持續接觸」戰略是長期軍事戰略中一項必不可少且早就該做出的舉動。
  • sector n. 部門、行業
    That shows an institutional crisis, as the Ministry of Digital Affairs is responsible for civilian cyberissues while the defense ministry operates in the military sector.
    這顯示了一場制度危機，因為數位發展部負責民用網路問題，而國防部則在軍事部門運作。
  • investment n. 投資
    First, it is important to analyze the financial investment into the project.
    首先，分析對該專案的財務投資是很重要的。
  • priority n. 優先事項、優先權
    If Taiwan wants to make “perpetual engagement” a key aspect in its digital asymmetric defense, the Legislative Yuan must make cybercapabilities an investment priority.
    如果臺灣想讓「持續接觸」成為其數位非對稱防禦的關鍵面向，立法院必須將網路能力列為投資優先事項。
  • infrastructure n. 基礎設施
    A legal mechanism should be established to let military hunt teams conduct joint operations with critical civilian infrastructure.
    應建立法律機制，讓軍事獵捕團隊能夠與關鍵民用基礎設施進行聯合行動。
  • cooperation n. 合作
    Moreover, Cyber Flag-type exercises should be held annually in the framework of Taiwan-US cybercooperation.

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作後置修飾 / 補充說明
    Modern campaigns such as the Volt Typhoon state-sponsored cyberthreat actor represent less noisy campaigns aimed at gathering intelligence and maintaining an inactive presence in systems for months or years until hour zero of a conflict.
    句中的「aimed at...」是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的「less noisy campaigns」，省去了「which are」，用以精簡語句並補充說明這些行動的目的。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞與受詞
    Embracing the “Assume Breach” strategy means acknowledging that Chinese threats are already hiding inside domestic servers.
    句首的「Embracing the “Assume Breach” strategy」是動名詞片語作為全句的主詞（中譯為「擁抱……的戰略」）；而動詞 mean 後面接的「acknowledging that...」則是動名詞片語作為受詞，用來表達具體的動作概念。
  • 現在分詞作主詞補語 / 狀態形容
    The goal of cybersabotage is to create chaos among civilians and disrupt internal logistics, leaving the leadership of the country disconnected.
    句尾的「leaving the leadership of the country disconnected」使用了「分詞構句（V-ing）」，用來表示前一個動作所產生的連續結果或伴隨狀況，意指「使得國家領導階層處於失聯狀態」。
