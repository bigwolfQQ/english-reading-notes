台灣製造，歐洲自主：法國不同的晶片策略

Made in Taiwan, sovereign in Europe: France's different chip strategy

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • capacity n. 產能、能力、容量
    SiPearl would be happy to manufacture in Europe if suitable capacity became available in five or 10 years...
    如果適合的產能在 5 或 10 年內出現，SiPearl 將很樂意在歐洲製造...
  • strategy n. 策略、戰略
    Made in Taiwan, sovereign in Europe: France's different chip strategy
    台灣製造，歐洲自主：法國不同的晶片策略
  • collaboration n. 合作、協力
    ...while expanding cooperation with international partners through Strategic Partnerships on Semiconductors.
    ...同時透過半導體戰略夥伴關係擴大與國際夥伴的合作。
  • revenue n. 收益、營業收入
    Taiwanese customers currently generate around 10 percent of Aniah's revenue.
    台灣客戶目前創造了大約 10% 的安亞營收。
  • estimate v. 估計、估算
    Chen estimated that markets outside China were roughly three times the size of the Chinese market...
    陳估計，中國以外的市場大約是中國市場的三倍大...
  • subsidiary n. 子公司
    The company plans to recruit around five employees within a year and is considering upgrading its representative office into a subsidiary, he said.
    他說，公司計畫在一年內招募大約五名員工，並正考慮將代表處升級為子公司。
  • profitability n. 獲利能力、盈利能力
    Aniah is also considering an initial public offering in Taiwan if its business achieves stable growth and profitability.
    如果業務能達到穩定成長與獲利能力，安亞也正考慮在台灣進行首次公開募股。
  • commercialize v. 商業化
    ...combining French strengths in fundamental science, engineering and algorithms with Taiwan's ability to commercialize and scale technologies.
    ...結合法國在基礎科學、工程與演算法上的優勢，以及台灣將技術商業化與規模化的能力。
  • budget n. 預算
    Aniah's annual budget for Taiwan operations is expected to increase from its current NT$6 million-NT$7 million...
    安亞台灣營運的年度預算預計將從目前的 600 萬至 700 萬新台幣增加...
  • preliminary adj. 初步的（註：文章中使用 primary 或 priority 等相關字，此處對應多益高頻商業字彙 priority）
    "The customer's voice is the first priority," Chen said...
    「客戶的聲音是第一優先，」陳說...
  • requirement n. 要求、需求條件
    Stricter requirements for domestic control should apply to limited areas directly related to national defense...
    針對國內控制更嚴格的要求，應適用於直接與國防相關的有限領域...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Present Participle）作後位修飾
    Developed by French semiconductor company SiPearl, the processor was manufactured in Taiwan by Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. (TSMC).
    句首的「Developed by...」是過去分詞片語當作形容詞用，修飾主詞「the processor」，表被動關係（被法國公司所開發）。
  • 分詞構句（Participial Phrase indicating circumstance）
    The shift comes as the United States seeks to reduce its reliance on Taiwan-based chip production by drawing more Taiwanese investment and manufacturing capacity onto U.S. soil.
    句尾的「by drawing more...」是以介系詞 by 搭配動名詞（drawing），用來表達「透過……方式」達成前面的動作，是多益閱讀常考表達手段與方法的句型。
  • 假設語氣（Subjunctive / Conditional）
    SiPearl would be happy to manufacture in Europe if suitable capacity became available in five or 10 years...
    此句為與未來事實相反或可能性低的假設語氣（Second Conditional）。「if」子句使用過去式動詞「became」，主句使用「would + 原形動詞（would be）」，表達對未來假設情境的推測。
  • 關係代間質引導的形容詞子句
    Knowledge, skills and production can be distributed among trusted partners, Tchagaspanian said, with Taiwan retaining the manufacturing capabilities in which it excels.
    句尾的「in which it excels」是「介系詞 + 關係代名詞（which）」引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾先行詞「manufacturing capabilities」，在正式英文中相當常見。
