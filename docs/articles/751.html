Taipei Times · 2026-09-15

立法院延宕總預算：卓榮泰

Legislature stalling general budget: Cho

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • submit v. 提交，呈遞
    The Legislative Yuan has not submitted this year’s general budget to the president for signature...
    立法院尚未將今年的總預算提交給總統簽署……
  • signature n. 簽名，簽署
    The government needs President William Lai’s (賴清德) signature on the budget...
    政府需要賴清德總統在預算上簽署……
  • fund v. 提供資金，資助
    The government needs President William Lai’s (賴清德) signature on the budget to fund the already-approved supplementary budgets...
    政府需要賴清德總統在預算上簽署，以便為已經批准的追加預算提供資金……
  • benefit n. 福利，津貼，利益
    ...to increase government worker wages and improve welfare benefits.
    ……以提高政府員工薪資並改善福利津貼。
  • opposition n. 對立，反對黨，在野黨
    “The government is using the lack of funding as a tool to incite the people’s anger against the opposition parties,” she said.
    她說：「政府正利用資金的缺乏作為工具，來煽動人民對在野黨的憤怒。」
  • proposal n. 提案，建議
    ...lawmakers would not be able to discuss those proposals until after Sept. 29.
    ……立法委員將無法在 9 月 29 日之後討論這些提案。
  • implement v. 實施，執行
    The legislature’s failure to file has ramifications for the implementation schedule of proposed supplementary budgets, they said.
    他們表示，立法院未送交預算，對所提追加預算的執行時程造成了影響。
  • subsidy n. 補助金，津貼
    The Cabinet on Sept. 3 proposed a NT$607.63 billion supplementary budget, which is to increase subsidies for older farmers...
    行政院在 9 月 3 日提出了 6,076.3 億元新台幣的追加預算，旨在增加老農津貼……
  • allocate v. 分配，撥出（預算等）
    Since supplementary budgets can only be activated on top of a general budget already allocated for the year...
    由於追加預算只能在當年已編列的總預算基礎上才能動支……
  • utilize v. 利用，使用
    The portion of the general budget that could not be utilized totals NT$299.2 billion.
    無法動支的總預算部分總計達 2,992 億元新台幣。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（expressing condition/time/concession）
    The Legislative Yuan has not submitted this year’s general budget to the president for signature, despite having passed it more than a month ago...
    句中的 "despite having passed it more than a month ago" 是由介系詞 despite 引導的分詞構句（動名詞片語），用來表達「儘管在一個多月前就已經通過」，呈現出前後子句之間的轉折語氣。
  • 現在分詞作形容詞／補充說明
    The government needs President William Lai’s (賴清德) signature on the budget to fund the already-approved supplementary budgets to increase government worker wages and improve welfare benefits.
    句中的 "already-approved" 是過去分詞作為複合形容詞，用來修飾其後的 supplementary budgets（追加預算），表示「已經被批准的」狀態。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    These subsidies are to be paid retroactively from July 1, with an estimated 890,000 beneficiaries, they said.
    句中的 "are to be paid" 採用了被動語態（be + 過去分詞），強調補助金是被發放的，而動作的承受者（subsidies）成為主詞，符合新聞報導中客觀陳述政策的語氣。
  • 條件句與時間副詞子句 (Time Clause)
    Since supplementary budgets can only be activated on top of a general budget already allocated for the year, lawmakers would not be able to discuss those proposals until after Sept. 29.
    句首由 "Since"（既然／由於）引導原因子句，說明追加預算無法動支的背景條件；後半句則透過 "until after Sept. 29" 明確指出立委能夠討論提案的時間點。
