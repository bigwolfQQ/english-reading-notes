台積電德國廠建設進度順利：ESMC 總裁表示

Construction of TSMC fab in Germany on track: ESMC president

單字解析

  • project n. 專案，計畫
    ...according to the head of a joint venture company in charge of the project.
    ……根據負責該專案的合資企業負責人表示。
  • joint venture n. phr. 合資企業，合資公司
    ESMC is a joint venture between TSMC, Bosch, Infineon, and NXP...
    ESMC 是台積電、博世、英飛凌和恩智浦之間的合資企業……
  • schedule n. 時程表，計畫表
    ...and he is confident that construction would be completed on schedule...
    ……且他有信心建設工作將如期完成……
  • milestone n. 里程碑，重要階段
    A topping out ceremony typically marks the construction milestone of laying the final beam...
    上樑典禮通常標誌著鋪設最後一根樑的建設里程碑……
  • equipment n. 設備，裝備
    The next milestone for the fab will be the installation of the first manufacturing process equipment in the second half of 2027...
    該晶圓廠的下一個里程碑將是在 2027 年下半年安裝第一批製造製程設備……
  • production n. 生產，產量
    Before production can begin, the interior of the fab's cleanroom will have to be completed...
    在生產開始之前，必須完成晶圓廠無塵室的內部裝修……
  • infrastructure n. 基礎設施，公共建設
    ...and infrastructure work completed, including electricity and water supply...
    ……並完成包括電力和供水在內的基礎設施工程……
  • commitment n. 承諾，奉獻
    The smooth progress of the project, as marked by the Richtfest, showed that TSMC... had kept their commitments...
    以 Richtfest 為標誌的專案順利進展，表明台積電……都履行了他們的承諾……
  • cooperation n. 合作，協力
    The mutual trust established during the process would provide a basis for further cooperation, he said...
    他說，在此過程中建立的相互信任將為進一步合作奠定基礎……
  • supplier n. 供應商，供應者
    ...and that more suppliers and companies would settle in Dresden...
    ……並且會有更多供應商和公司在德勒斯登落戶……
  • estimate v. 估計，估算
    Meanwhile, Dresden Mayor Dirk Hilbert estimated that 15,000 to 20,000 workers would move to the area by 2030...
    同時，德勒斯登市長德 Dirk Hilbert 估計，到 2030 年將有 15,000 至 20,000 名工人搬遷到該地區……
  • demand n. 需求，要求
    The city is preparing to establish a more systematic economic area to meet projected demand for residential, industrial and commercial land...
    該市正準備建立一個更具系統性的經濟區，以滿足對住宅、工業和商業用地的預期需求……

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（用作附加說明或結果）
    A topping out ceremony was held Monday at the site of a plant being built in Germany by Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. (TSMC), indicating that the fab is progressing as planned and within budget...
    句尾的「indicating that...」是由連接詞加子句（and it indicates that...）簡化而來的分詞構句，用來補充說明前面主句所發生的上樑典禮所代表的意義與結果。
  • 被動語態的進行式
    ...at the site of a plant being built in Germany by Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. (TSMC)...
    片語「being built」是由現在分詞的被動語態（am/is/are/being + 過去分詞）所構成，用來修飾前方正在被興建中的工廠（a plant）。
  • 間接引語（報導引述）
    Christian Koitzsch, president of European Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. (ESMC), said about 2,000 workers... are working around the clock... and he is confident that construction would be completed on schedule, according to a report by German news outlet Sächsische Zeitung (SZ).
    新聞報導經常使用間接引語來轉述他人的說法。句中的「said」帶領後面省略了 that 的名詞子句，且子句中的時態通常會配合主句做時態移轉（例如將 will 轉為 would）。
  • 被動語態的完成式
    Before production can begin, the interior of the fab's cleanroom will have to be completed, the automated material handling system installed, and infrastructure work completed...
    句中的「will have to be completed」與「installed（省略了 will have to be）」使用了未來被動語態的完成構造，強調在某個動作（生產開始）之前，這些工程「必須被完成」與「被安裝」。
