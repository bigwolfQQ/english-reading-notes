史瓦帝尼新任特使承諾支持臺灣參與國際事務

New Eswatini envoy pledges support for Taiwan’s int’l participation

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • credential n. 證書、資格；（外交官的）國書
    Eswatini will continue advocating for Taiwan’s meaningful participation in international organizations, the African country’s new ambassador to Taiwan said yesterday at a ceremony where he presented his credentials to President William Lai (賴清德).
    史瓦帝尼駐臺新任大使昨天在向總統賴清德呈遞到任國書的儀式上表示，史瓦帝尼將繼續為臺灣有意義地參與國際組織發聲。
  • ally n. 盟友、邦交國
    Eswatini is one of the Republic of China’s 12 diplomatic allies and its only ally in Africa.
    史瓦帝尼是中華民國的 12 個友邦之一，也是其在非洲唯一的邦交國。
  • maintain v. 維持、保持
    The two countries have maintained diplomatic ties for 58 years.
    兩國已維持 58 年的外交關係。
  • disruption n. 中斷、擾亂
    In his remarks, Vulindlela Simphiwe Kunene said global challenges including pandemics, cybercrime and disruptions to international supply chains could not be effectively addressed while 'the knowledge, resources and experience of more than 23 million people were excluded.'
    伏倫迪萊·辛菲韋·庫內內在致詞時表示，在全球面臨大流行病、網路犯罪以及國際供應鏈中斷等挑戰之際，若「將 2,300 多萬人的知識、資源與經驗排除在外」，這些挑戰就無法獲得有效解決。
  • contribution n. 貢獻
    'Eswatini will therefore continue to advocate for Taiwan’s meaningful participation in international organizations and mechanisms where its contribution can benefit humanity,' he said.
    他說：「因此，史瓦帝尼將繼續支持臺灣有意義地參與國際組織與機制，讓臺灣的貢獻能造福全人類。」
  • cooperation n. 合作
    Kunene, who was previously posted to the UN, said such support was not only 'an expression of friendship,' but also 'a principled position grounded in fairness, inclusion and the practical requirements of effective international cooperation.'
    曾派駐聯合國的庫內內表示，這種支持不僅是「友誼的表現」，也是一項「根基於公平、包容以及有效國際合作之實際需求的原則性立場」。
  • bilateral adj. 雙邊的
    In terms of bilateral ties, Kunene highlighted Taiwan-backed rural electrification as an example of how the partnership had improved people’s lives...
    在雙邊關係方面，庫內內特別指出臺灣支持的農村電氣化計畫，作為夥伴關係改善人民生活的實例...
  • investment n. 投資
    He also pledged to promote closer cooperation in areas including trade and investment, renewable energy, agriculture, healthcare, digital transformation and artificial intelligence.
    他也承諾推動更緊密的合作，領域涵蓋貿易與投資、再生能源、農業、醫療保健、數位轉型以及人工智慧。
  • post n. 職位、職缺、駐地
    Meanwhile, Lai welcomed Kunene to his new post and said the friendship between Taiwan and Eswatini had 'stood the test of time' and remained strong despite changes in the international landscape.
    與此同時，賴總統對庫內內履新表達歡迎，並表示臺灣與史瓦帝尼之間的友誼已「經得起時間的考驗」，儘管國際情勢變遷，依然穩固。
  • expand v. 擴大、擴展
    Taiwan will also continue working with Eswatini on projects including the Taiwan Industrial Innovation Park while expanding cooperation in agriculture, healthcare, education, trade and technology, Lai said.
    賴總統表示，臺灣也將繼續與史瓦帝尼在臺灣工業創新園區等專案上合作，同時擴大在農業、醫療保健、教育、貿易和科技領域的合作。
  • postpone v. 延期、延後
    Taiwan-Eswatini ties drew international attention earlier this year when Lai’s planned visit to the African country in late April was postponed after Seychelles, Mauritius and Madagascar canceled overflight permits for his chartered aircraft...
    今年稍早，賴總統原定於 4 月下旬訪問該非洲國家的行程，因塞席爾、模里西斯和馬達加斯加取消其包機的飛越領空許可而延期...

文法重點

  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況或結果
    Taiwan-Eswatini ties drew international attention earlier this year when Lai’s planned visit to the African country in late April was postponed after Seychelles, Mauritius and Madagascar canceled overflight permits for his chartered aircraft, a move Taiwan attributed to pressure from China.
    句尾的「a move Taiwan attributed to pressure from China」是由代間代名词「a move」引發的名詞片語，後面省略了關代與be動詞（which was a move...），用來補充說明前面整件事（取消飛越許可），並將「Taiwan attributed to...」作為修飾語，展現精煉的新聞文法結構。
  • 關係代間詞子句修飾先行詞
    Kunene, who was previously posted to the UN, said such support was not only 'an expression of friendship,' but also...
    句中的「who was previously posted to the UN」是非限定關係子句（用逗號隔開），用來補充說明主詞 Kunene 的背景（曾被派駐至聯合國），在多益閱讀測驗中常考這種插入式的關係子句以測試主動詞的一致性與句意理解。
  • 被動語態的使用
    ...while 'the knowledge, resources and experience of more than 23 million people were excluded.'
    句中的「were excluded」為過去式的被動語態（be + p.p.），用來強調這 2,300 萬人的知識與經驗是「被動地被排除在外」，凸顯臺灣被國際社會排擠的不公與無奈，符合新聞報導中客觀陳述事實與立場的語氣。
