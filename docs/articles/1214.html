Focus Taiwan (CNA) · 2026-09-29

9月消費者信心升至18個月來新高

Consumer confidence rises to 18-month high in September

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • consumer confidence index n. phr. 消費者信心指數
    Taiwan's consumer confidence index (CCI) hit an 18-month high in September...
    台灣的消費者信心指數（CCI）在9月創下18個月以來的最高水準...
  • survey n. 調查
    ...according to the latest CCI survey conducted from Sept. 18 to 21 by NCU's Research Center for Taiwan Economic Development.
    ...根據中央大學台灣經濟發展研究中心於9月18日至21日進行的最新CCI調查。
  • sentiment n. 情緒、市場情緒、觀點
    The CCI measures sentiment over the next six months across six factors...
    CCI衡量未來六個月對六大項目的情緒...
  • durable goods n. phr. 耐久財（多益常考商業與經濟詞彙）
    ...durable goods purchases, employment prospects and family finances.
    ...耐久性財貨採購、就業前景以及家庭經濟狀況。
  • employment n. 就業、雇用
    The sub-indexes for employment, durable goods purchases and consumer prices also moved higher...
    就業、耐久性財貨採購和物價子指數也分別較前一個月上升...
  • momentum n. 氣勢、動能
    ...investors had previously worried that momentum in Taiwan's stock market would moderate in the second half of 2026.
    ...投資人先前曾擔心台灣股市的動能會在2026年下半年放緩。
  • exports n. 出口（商品）
    ...with Taiwan's exports and export orders consistently hitting highs on strong global demand for AI applications...
    ...在對AI應用程式的強勁全球需求帶動下，台灣的出口與出口訂單持續創下新高...
  • threshold n. 門檻、開始
    ...NCU said they still had not reached the threshold for 'optimism.'
    ...中央大學表示，它們仍未達到『樂觀』的門檻。
  • optimism n. 樂觀
    CCI sub-index scores of 100-200 show optimism, while 0-100 scores indicate pessimism...
    CCI子指數分數在100至200之間表示樂觀，而0-100的分數則表示悲觀...
  • pessimism n. 悲觀
    ...while 0-100 scores indicate pessimism, according to the university.
    ...而0-100的分數則表示悲觀，該大學指出。
  • compiled v. (pp. 當形容詞用) 編製的、收集匯編的
    Also in September, the home-buying index compiled by NCU and Taiwan Realty soared 8.81 points...
    同樣在9月份，由中央大學和台灣房屋編製的購屋指數月增8.81點...
  • surge n. 急升、暴增
    The surge in September was the second-highest in any individual month since the home-buying index began...
    9月份的飆升幅度是自購屋指數開始以來各單月中的第二高...
  • relaxed v. (pp. 當動詞或形容詞用) 放寬、放鬆（規定或限制）
    ...came after the central bank further relaxed restrictions on the use of loans in the property market.
    ...是在中央銀行進一步放寬房地產市場貸款使用限制之後出現的。
  • subsidiary / financed v. (pp.) 融資的、提供資金的
    ...and removed the deadline for developers to start construction on land financed through loans in its quarterly policymaking meeting...
    ...並取消了開發商對透過貸款融資取得的土地必須動工興建的期限...
  • margin of error n. phr. 誤差範圍
    It had a 95 percent confidence level with a margin of error of plus or minus 2.0 percentage points.
    其信賴水準為95%，抽樣誤差為正負2.0個百分點。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後置修飾（Past Participle as Post-modifier）
    Also in September, the home-buying index compiled by NCU and Taiwan Realty soared 8.81 points month-on-month...
    句中的 "compiled by NCU and Taiwan Realty" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "the home-buying index"（購屋指數），相當於關代子句 "which was compiled by..." 的簡化版，是多益閱讀測驗中常見的句構縮寫方式。
  • 獨立分詞構句（Absolute Nominative Absolute Structure）
    ...with Taiwan's exports and export orders consistently hitting highs on strong global demand for AI applications, which sent the shares of tech companies higher.
    句型 "with + 名詞 + 分詞/形容詞" 用來表示附帶狀況或背景原因，在此句中 "with Taiwan's exports and export orders consistently hitting highs..." 解釋了為什麼股價會持續攀升，是多益商業寫作與閱讀中常用來表達因果或伴隨狀態的高級句構。
  • 時間副詞子句與過去完成式的搭配（Past Perfect in Time Clauses）
    Yau Ruey (姚睿), a professor in NCU's Department of Economics, told reporters that investors had previously worried that momentum in Taiwan's stock market would moderate in the second half of 2026.
    主句動詞 "told" 使用過去式，而其受詞子句內的動作「投資人先前已經擔心」發生在 told 之前，因此使用過去完成式 "had previously worried"，清楚呈現兩個過去動作的時間先後順序。
  • 對比連接詞 while 的用法
    CCI sub-index scores of 100-200 show optimism, while 0-100 scores indicate pessimism, according to the university.
