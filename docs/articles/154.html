文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Speaking to CNA, Chen Kuang-yi (陳貺怡), director of the National Taiwan Museum of Fine Arts (NTMoFA), which curated the pavilion in Gwangju, South Korea, said she was elated after "striving through official channels for the chance to open the exhibition properly."
    句首的「Speaking to CNA」是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明主句主語 Chen Kuang-yi 的動作（一邊受訪一邊說），相當於時間或原因副詞子句的簡化，常出現在新聞英語中以精簡版面並保持流暢。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The foundation had initially listed the pavilion as "The NTMoFA Pavilion" in promotional materials, but agreed to allow it to use the name "Taiwan Pavilion" on Aug. 26, amid accusations of political censorship from artists and Taiwan's government.
    「accusations of political censorship from artists and Taiwan's government」中的介系詞片語與前面的名詞結合。此處雖非純分詞，但句中的「listed」與「agreed」為並列動詞（過去式），展示了標準的對等連接詞 but 連接兩個謂語動詞的結構。
  • 與現在事實相反的假設語氣 (Subjunctive Mood for Present Unreal Condition)
    "If our Pavilion's name is not given back, we would rather not participate."
    雖然此句嚴格來說使用「If + 現在式 (is not), would rather + 原形動詞」表達真實條件或決心，但「would rather」結構表達了強烈的個人意願與假設情境的抉擇，是多益職場溝通中常出現表達偏好與決定的實用句型。
