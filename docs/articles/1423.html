  • opponent n. 對手，敵手

  • retire v. (運動比賽中) 退賽，退休
    ...after his opponent Alex de Minaur retired from the final, extending an astonishing unbeaten streak in Beijing...
    ...因其對手亞歷克斯·德米瑙爾在決賽中退賽，這延續了他在北京驚人的不敗戰績...
  • extend v. 延長，延伸，擴大
    ...extending an astonishing unbeaten streak in Beijing and winning the 102nd title of his career.
    ...這延續了他在北京驚人的不敗戰績，並贏得了他職業生涯的第 102 座冠軍。
  • challenge adj. 具挑戰性的

  • issue n. 問題，議題
    ...adding that it had been a challenging year with “physical and health issues.”
    ...並補充說，這是有著「身體與健康問題」充滿挑戰的一年。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句表結果或伴隨狀況
    Novak Djokovic on Tuesday won the China Open after his opponent Alex de Minaur retired from the final, extending an astonishing unbeaten streak in Beijing and winning the 102nd title of his career.
    句尾的「extending...」與「winning...」是由對等連接詞或分詞構句簡化而來，用來補充說明主句動作所產生的自然結果，是多益閱讀測驗中常見的句型結構。
  • 過去分詞作後置定語
    De Minaur advanced to the final after his opponent Hubert Hurkacz on Monday retired injured from their semi-final...
    句中的「retired injured」裡，「injured」是過去分詞作形容詞用，用來修飾主詞 Hurkacz，表示他「受傷了」的狀態。
  • 被動語態的使用
    ...while Djokovic moved through when Daniil Medvedev was disqualified for hitting a fan with a ball.
    子句中的「was disqualified」使用了被動語態（be + 過去分詞），用來表達梅德韋傑夫「被取消資格」這項由外部裁判或賽事單位所作出的決定。
