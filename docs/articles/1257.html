高市誓言建立穩定的中日關係

Takaichi vows to build stable China relations

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • pledge v. / n. 保證，誓言，承諾
    Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi yesterday called China an “important neighbor” and pledged to build a stable relationship between the rival nations in a policy address.
    日本首相高市早苗昨天在一場施政演說中，稱中國為「重要的鄰國」，並誓言在這兩個對手國家之間建立穩定的關係。
  • intervene v. 介入，干涉
    Diplomatic relations between the Asian economic giants have been at rock bottom since Takaichi suggested in November that Tokyo might intervene in any attack on Taiwan.
    自從高市在 11 月暗示東京可能會介入任何針對台灣的攻擊以來，這兩個亞洲經濟巨頭之間的外交關係就一直處於谷底。
  • promote v. 促進，推動，晉升
    “We will work to ensure communication on shared challenges, promote dialogue to safeguard our national interests, and build a constructive and stable relationship,” she said.
    她表示：「我們將致力於確保就共同挑戰進行溝通，促進對話以維護我們的國家利益，並建立建設性且穩定的關係。」
  • safeguard v. / n. 維護，保護，保障

    她表示：「我們將致力於確保就共同挑戰進行溝通，促進對話以維護我們的國家利益，並建立建設性且穩定的關係。」

  • draft v. / n. 草擬，起草；草稿

    Despite enjoying unusually high approval ratings a year into office, Takaichi is navigating a number of challenges, including rising inflation, market concerns about Japan’s mounting debts and a diplomatic spat with China that has been rumbling since making the comments about Taiwan last year.
    儘管上任一年來享有異常高的支持率，高市仍面臨著許多挑戰，包括不斷上升的通膨、市場對日本日益增加的債務的擔憂，以及自去年發表關於台灣的言論以來一直持續發酵的中日外交口角。

    在此政策演說發表前夕，中國最高外交官上週在北京會見率領經貿代表團的前日本外相時表示，與東京的關係「正等待重新正常化」。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Present Participle）作後置修飾語
    Takaichi also signaled her government would draft a comprehensive plan to deal with population decline, which she called a “quietly advancing national crisis.”
    句中的「quietly advancing」是由副詞 quietly 與現在分詞 advancing 組成的形容詞片語，用來修飾名詞危機（national crisis）。現在分詞帶有主動或進行的意味，在此表示該危機正在「默默地推進、發展中」。
  • 過去分詞（Past Participle）作後置修飾語與被動概念
    The parliamentary session, scheduled to run through December, is to discuss several bills, including plans to cut a consumption tax on food for two years to curb the impact of inflation on voters’ daily lives.
    句中的「scheduled to run through December」是一個省略了「which is」的過去分詞片語，用來補充說明前面的名詞片語「The parliamentary session」（國會會議）。過去分詞帶有被動意味，表示會議是被「安排/預定」在 12 月結束。
  • 非限定性關係代名詞子句（Non-defining Relative Clause）
    Takaichi also signaled her government would draft a comprehensive plan to deal with population decline, which she called a “quietly advancing national crisis.”
    逗號後的「which she called a “quietly advancing national crisis.”」是一個非限定性關係子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「population decline（人口減少）」。關係代名詞 which 在子句中作為受詞，前方加上逗號表示補充說明，不可省略。
  • be to + V 句型（表預定、安排或計畫）
    The parliamentary session, scheduled to run through December, is to discuss several bills, including plans to cut a consumption tax on food for two years to curb the impact of inflation on voters’ daily lives.
    句中的「is to discuss」使用了「be + to V」的結構，在新聞英語中經常用來表示官方的計畫、預定行程或即將發生的重要官方安排（相當於 is scheduled to discuss）。
