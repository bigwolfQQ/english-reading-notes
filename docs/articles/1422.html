文法重點

  • 名詞子句（由 that 引導）
    UEFA on Tuesday confirmed that it has opened an investigation into the tunnel bust-up...
    句中的 "that it has opened..." 為名詞子句，作為及物動詞 "confirmed" 的受詞，用來補充說明確認的具體內容。
  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense)
    ...a member of Ireland coaching staff claimed he had been spat at by an Israel player.
    子句中的 "had been spat at" 使用過去完成式，用來表示「過去某個時間點（claimed 宣稱）」之前已經發生的動作（被吐口水）。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    ...a UEFA Ethics and Disciplinary Inspector has been appointed to conduct the appropriate disciplinary investigation...
    句型 "has been appointed" 採用現在完成式的被動語態，強調督察人員「被」任命、指派的這個動作，而未強調是誰指派的。
  • 分詞構句（或形容詞片語修飾）
    It was the second time in a week that the two teams had played amid tensions over the war in Gaza.
