檢方依共諜嫌疑起訴前海軍士官

Prosecutors indict ex-navy sergeant on spy allegations

單字解析

  • indict v. 起訴，控告
    The Tainan branch of the High Prosecutors’ Office has indicted a retired navy sergeant surnamed Ma (馬) on charges of contravening the National Security Act...
    高等檢察署台南分署已依違反《國家安全法》起訴一名姓馬的退役海軍士官...
  • contravene v. 違反，牴觸
    ...on charges of contravening the National Security Act (國家安全法) for allegedly attempting to develop an intelligence gathering organization for China.
    ...因其涉嫌企圖為中國發展情報蒐集組織而違反《國家安全法》的罪名。
  • solicit v. 索取，請求，招攬
    Ma was also indicted for allegedly soliciting bribes from officials and contravening the Anti-Corruption Act (貪污治罪條例).
    馬員也因涉嫌向官員索賄及違反《貪污治罪條例》而被起訴。
  • official n. 官員，公務員
    Ma was also indicted for allegedly soliciting bribes from officials and contravening the Anti-Corruption Act (貪污治罪條例).
    馬員也因涉嫌向官員索賄及違反《貪污治罪條例》而被起訴。
  • collect v. 收集，採集
    ...and was directed to develop an organization in Taiwan to collect intelligence on military, maritime and coast Guard Administration affairs, prosecutors said.
    檢方表示，並奉命在台灣發展組織，以蒐集有關軍事、海事及海巡署事務的情報。
  • approach v. 接近，洽談，接觸
    In early 2021, Ma allegedly approached a retired major general surnamed Wang (王) and introduced him to Chinese officials, they said.
    檢方指出，在 2021 年初，馬員涉嫌接觸一名姓王的退役少將，並將他介紹給中國官員。
  • recruit v. 招募，吸收（成員）
    Ma allegedly gave Wang NT$42,100 and attempted to recruit him to collect intelligence related to the Ministry of National Defense...
    起訴書指出，馬員涉嫌給予王員 4萬2,100 元新台幣，並企圖吸收他蒐集與國防部相關的情報...
  • cooperate v. 合作，配合
    Wang refused to cooperate, it added.
    起訴書補充，王員拒絕配合。
  • promise v. 承諾，保證
    Ma allegedly offered Yu up to US$100,000 per information and promised to pay him a monthly amount equivalent to his salary...
    起訴書指出，馬員涉嫌向于員每筆情報提供高達 10 萬美元的報酬，並承諾支付相當於其薪資的月薪...
  • equivalent adj. 相等的，相當的
    ...and promised to pay him a monthly amount equivalent to his salary if he agreed to continue working on a long-term basis, the indictment said.
    ...並承諾如果他同意長期配合，將支付相當於其薪資的月薪，起訴書指出。
  • agree v. 同意
    ...if he agreed to continue working on a long-term basis, the indictment said.
    ...如果他同意長期配合，起訴書指出。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...a retired navy sergeant surnamed Ma (馬) on charges of contravening the National Security Act...
    句中的 "surnamed Ma" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "a retired navy sergeant"（姓馬的退役海軍士官）。這種結構省略了「關係代代詞 + be 動詞」（即 ...sergeant who was surnamed Ma），能讓句子更簡潔。
  • 被動語態的運用 (Passive Voice)
    Ma was also indicted for allegedly soliciting bribes from officials...
    本句使用被動語態（was indicted），強調動作的承受者（馬員被起訴），而發動起訴的檢調單位在此處並非句首焦點，符合新聞報導常使用被動語態來客觀陳述司法程序的習慣。
  • 假設語氣/條件句 (Conditional Clause with Past Tense)
    ...if he agreed to continue working on a long-term basis, the indictment said.
    此處使用 "if he agreed..."（如果他同意……）的條件句結構。雖然敘述的是過去情境下的假設或條件，但用過去式動詞 (agreed) 來表達當時若達成該條件會產生的後續承諾。
