文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾語 (Past Participle Phrase as Post-Modifier)
    The Tainan branch of the High Prosecutors’ Office has indicted a retired navy sergeant surnamed Ma (馬) on charges of contravening the National Security Act...
    句中的 "surnamed Ma (馬)" 為過去分詞片語，置於名詞 "a retired navy sergeant" 之後進行後位修飾，等同於關係子句 "who is surnamed Ma" 的省略，簡化了句構。
  • 被動語態結合不定詞結構 (Passive Voice with Infinitive Pattern)
    Ma allegedly began taking instructions from China as early as 2008 and was directed to develop an organization in Taiwan...
    句中 "was directed to develop..." 使用了被動語態 (was directed) 加上不定詞 (to develop)。這類結構（be + 過去分詞 + to V）常用於表達「被指示、被要求或被命令去做某事」。
  • 條件句與過去時間點的時態搭配 (Conditional Clause with Past Context)
    Ma allegedly offered Yu up to US$100,000 per information and promised to pay him a monthly amount equivalent to his salary if he agreed to continue working on a long-term basis, the indictment said.
    此句為間接引述敘事，主要動詞用過去式 (offered, promised)，其附屬的條件子句 "if he agreed to continue..." 亦使用過去簡單式 (agreed) 以保持時態一致，表達當時提出的假設條件。
  • 不定詞被動式修飾名詞 (Passive Infinitive Modifying a Noun)
    Ma allegedly gave Yu NT$50,000 and a cellphone to be used for intelligence gathering...
    句中的 "to be used for intelligence gathering" 為不定詞的被動形式（to + be + p.p.），用來修飾前面的名詞 "cellphone"，說明該手機「將被用來進行」情報搜集的用途。
