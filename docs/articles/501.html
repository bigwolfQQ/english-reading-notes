文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表附帶狀況或結果）
    And their players were cheered warmly as they ran out onto the sprawling, oval-shaped playing field having endeared themselves to locals with their high praise for Melbourne’s coffee and food scene.
    句尾的「having endeared...」為現在分詞的完成式（having + p.p.），用來補充說明主詞球員們跑上球場時的狀態，表示「先前已經贏得當地人喜愛」的這個動作發生在跑上球場之前。
  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    “This was the worst version of myself that I’ve put out there,” Garrett said.
  • 獨立分詞構句（Absolute Phrase）
    Both teams were moving the ball well on the ground, but interceptions prevented any further scores until Demarcus Robinson caught a 39-yard pass from Purdy to put the 49ers 10-7 up with four minutes 28 seconds left in the half.
    句尾的「with four minutes 28 seconds left in the half」屬於「with + 名詞 + 分詞（形容詞/副詞）」的結構，常用於表達附帶狀況，意指「在剩下...的情況下」。在多益閱讀與商務書信中非常實用。
