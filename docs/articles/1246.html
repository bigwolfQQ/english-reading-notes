健保的新預算能幫助醫護人員嗎？

Will the NHI’s new budget help the staff?

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • negotiation n. 談判，協商
    On Sept. 23, negotiations over next year’s National Health Insurance (NHI) global budget reached a conclusion.
    9月23日，關於明年度全民健康保險總額預算的談判達成了結論。
  • expenditure n. 支出，花費
    The NHI Committee set the growth rate for overall expenditure — encompassing hospitals, primary care clinics, dental services and traditional Chinese medicine — at 5.5 percent for next year.
    健保會將明年度整體醫療給支付（包含醫院、基層診所、牙醫與中醫）的成長率設定為5.5%。
  • consecutive adj. 連續的
    It is the second consecutive year that an agreement has been reached to increase the budget, reflecting a consensus among medical professionals and payers on the need for greater investment in healthcare.
    這是連續第二年達成增加預算的協議，反映出醫療專業人員與付費者對於增加醫療保健投資必要性的共識。
  • consensus n. 共識
    It is the second consecutive year that an agreement has been reached to increase the budget, reflecting a consensus among medical professionals and payers on the need for greater investment in healthcare.
    這是連續第二年達成增加預算的協議，反映出醫療專業人員與付費者對於增加醫療保健投資必要性的共識。
  • allocate v. 分配，分派
    How effectively this is allocated remains to be seen.
    這筆經費的分配效率如何仍有待觀察。
  • surplus n. 盈餘，過剩
    It cannot be treated as a surplus that hospitals and clinics are free to spend as they see fit.
    它不能被視為醫院和診所可以隨心所欲花費的盈餘。
  • reimbursement n. 核銷，補償，給付
    Increased government reimbursements under NHI do not directly translate to higher wages for medical staff; healthcare institutions must first decide how to internally distribute the funds.
    健保政府給付的增加並不直接等於醫療人員薪資的提高；醫療機構必須先決定如何內部分配這些資金。
  • compensation n. 薪酬，補償
    These averages alone cannot tell us how compensation has changed; if higher earnings are coming from working more night shifts and overtime, it cannot be claimed that working conditions have materially improved.
    單靠這些平均值無法告訴我們薪酬是如何改變的；如果較高的收入是來自於加班更多夜班與超時工作，就不能宣稱工作條件已經實質改善。
  • vacancy n. 職缺，空缺
    Comparing salaries, actual working hours, vacancies and turnover rates is the only way to understand how much conditions for frontline workers have actually changed.
    比較 salaries、實際工時、職缺與離職率，是了解第一線工作人員條件實際改變多少的唯一方法。
  • turnover rate n. phr. 離職率，人員流動率
    Comparing salaries, actual working hours, vacancies and turnover rates is the only way to understand how much conditions for frontline workers have actually changed.
    比較薪資、實際工時、職缺與離職率（turnover rates），是了解第一線工作人員條件實際改變多少的唯一方法。
  • constraint n. 限制，約束
    Clarifying how funds are distributed within institutions requires accounting for the different services provided and financial constraints faced by different facilities.
    釐清資金如何在機構內部分配，需要將不同設施所提供的不同服務以及所面臨的財務限制納入會計考量。
  • allocation n. 分配，配置
    However, responsible resource allocation can still exist under financial hardship; concrete measures for staff retention must be offered alongside explanations of cost pressures.
    然而，在財務困境下依然可以存在負責的資源配置；在解釋成本壓力的同時，必須提出留任員工的具體措施。

文法重點

  • 強調句構 (It is... that...)
    It is the employers who control salaries and working arrangements that must provide answers to these questions.
    英文常用 「It is + 被強調的對象 + that (或 who)」 的句型來凸顯句中的某個成分。此處用來強調「正是那些掌控薪資與工作安排的雇主」，必須回答前面提到的問題。
  • 分詞片語作修飾語
    The NHI Committee set the growth rate for overall expenditure — encompassing hospitals, primary care clinics, dental services and traditional Chinese medicine — at 5.5 percent for next year.
    句中的現在分詞片語「encompassing hospitals...」用來補充說明前面的「overall expenditure（整體支出）」包含了哪些項目，等於是一個未簡化的關係代名詞子句（which encompasses...）。
  • 現在分詞 (Present Participle) 作形容詞用法
    It is the second consecutive year that an agreement has been reached to increase the budget, reflecting a consensus among medical professionals and payers on the need for greater investment in healthcare.
    句尾的「reflecting a consensus...」是由分詞構句轉來的現在分詞片語，用來表達前面整件事情所帶來的自然結果或伴隨狀況，翻譯時通常譯為「反映出……」。
  • 間接問句 (Indirect Question)
    The question is whether this additional funding can improve working conditions and help retain staff.
    「whether this additional funding can improve working conditions...」是由「whether」引導的名詞子句，在句中擔任主詞補語，用來具體說明「The question」的內容。間接問句的語序為「疑問詞 + 主詞 + 動詞」。
