在大都會歌劇院，蛇類也得為歌劇試鏡

At the Met, snakes have to audition for the opera

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • adaptation n. 改編作品，適應
    ...even the snake who would briefly take center stage in an upcoming adaptation of a Mozart classic.
    ……甚至連一隻將在即將上演的莫札特經典改編劇中短暫成為舞臺焦點的蛇也不例外。
  • in charge of phr. 負責，掌管
    In charge of deciding who gets the s-s-s-s-starring reptilian role is Zoe Ziegfeld...
    負責決定誰能獲得這個「嘶——嘶——」閃耀爬蟲類角色的是佐伊·齊格菲……
  • evaluate v. 評估，評價
    Ziegfeld looks each one in the eye, almost face-to-face as they evaluate the snake’s demeanor.
    齊格菲注視著每一條蛇的眼睛，幾乎是面對面地評估蛇的脾性。
  • on a trial basis phr. 試用階段，試行地
    We will be moving forward with Princess... in rehearsals on a trial basis!
    我們將在試用基礎上讓 Princess 繼續參與排練！
  • fidelity n. 忠誠，忠實
    In Mozart’s original, two Italian soldiers decide to test their lovers’ fidelity by pretending to head off to war.
    在莫札特的原作中，兩名義大利士兵決定透過假裝前往參戰來測試其愛人的忠誠度。
  • disguise v. 偽裝，掩飾
    They then disguise themselves and try to seduce each other’s girlfriends.
    接著他們偽裝自己，並試圖誘惑彼此的女朋友。
  • setting n. 背景，環境，設定
    In this version, the plot remains mostly the same, but the setting is a 1950s Coney Island carnival...
    在這個版本中，劇情大致保持不變，但背景被設定為一個 1950 年代康尼島嘉年華……
  • vibrant adj. 充滿活力的，鮮艷的
    ...complete with vibrant scenery and sideshow performers.
    ……配上充滿活力的佈景與雜耍表演者。
  • key n. 關鍵，關鍵因素
    The key is personality, Novograd said.
    「關鍵在於個性，」諾沃格拉德說。
  • demonstrate v. 示範，展現，證明
    Sometimes they have a set type of behaviors that they have to demonstrate.
    有時牠們必須展現一套特定的行為。

文法重點

  • 地方副詞片語置於句首的完全倒裝句 (Full Inversion)
    In charge of deciding who gets the s-s-s-s-starring reptilian role is Zoe Ziegfeld...
    當表地方或狀態的介係詞片語（在此為 In charge of deciding...）移到句首以加強語氣或維持上下文銜接時，若主詞較長或為了平衡語氣，動詞（is）會直接移到主詞（Zoe Ziegfeld）之前，形成主動詞倒裝結構。
  • 分詞構句表伴隨狀況 (Participle Phrase for Accompaniment)
    Ziegfeld takes each snake, lets it slither and wrap around, then hoists the animal over their head, rehearsing what they would do under the bright lights at center stage for each performance.
    句尾的「rehearsing what they would do...」是由現在分詞 rehearsing 引導的分詞構句，用來補充說明主句動作同時發生或接續發生的狀況，主詞 Ziegfeld 既是執行前面動作的人，也是「排練」這個動作的發起者。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    ...live snakes were not allowed in the audition space.
    本句使用過去式的被動語態（were not allowed），強調「活蛇」是不被允許進入試鏡空間的受事者，焦點放在規定本身而非禁止牠們的人。
