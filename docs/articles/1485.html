松山機場：台北「另一座機場」的起落

Songshan: The ups and downs of Taipei's 'other airport'

單字解析

  • proposal n. 提案，建議
    Taipei Mayor Chiang Wan-an (蔣萬安) waded into a long-running debate last week with a proposal to close Songshan Airport and build an "AI park" in its place.
    台北市長蔣萬安上周拋出一項關閉松山機場並在其原址建立「AI園區」的提議，捲入了一場長期的辯論中。
  • operations n. 營運，運作
    Operations at the facility gradually expanded over the following years, eventually handling flights by 14 international airlines.
    該設施的營運在隨後的幾年裡逐漸擴大，最終處理了14家國際航空公司的航班。
  • relocate v. 遷移，重新安置
    As international flights were gradually relocated, Songshan Airport shifted to focus on domestic flights, while facing a steep decline in passenger volume.
    隨著國際航班逐漸被轉移，松山機場轉向專注於國內航班，同時面臨旅客運量的急劇下降。
  • decline n. 下降，衰退
    The following year, passenger volume at Songshan plummeted to 3.1 million.
    隔年，松山的旅客運量驟降至310萬人次。
  • revive v. 使復甦，使興盛
    However, after Taiwan's government launched its "open skies" policy in 1987, deregulating the airline industry, Songshan's fortunes were revived, as new carriers opened and domestic flights rapidly expanded.
    然而，在台灣政府於1987年推出「開放天空」政策、放鬆對航空業的管制後，松山機場的命運得以復甦，因為新航空公司的開闢使得國內航班迅速擴大。
  • volume n. 總量，容量，流量
    By 1997, passenger volume at Songshan Airport reached an annual high of 15.39 million people.
    到了1997年，松山機場的旅客運量達到了全年1539萬人次的歷史新高。
  • consolidation n. 合併，鞏固
    As the 2000s began, several factors -- including airline consolidation, the expansion of the highway system and the opening of Taiwan High Speed Rail -- caused a contraction in domestic air travel.
    隨著2000年代的開始，包括航空公司整合、高速公路系統擴建以及台灣高鐵開通在內的幾個因素，導致國內航空旅行出現收縮。
  • contraction n. 收縮，縮減
    As the 2000s began, several factors -- including airline consolidation, the expansion of the highway system and the opening of Taiwan High Speed Rail -- caused a contraction in domestic air travel.
    隨著2000年代的開始，包括航空公司整合、高速公路系統擴建以及台灣高鐵開通在內的幾個因素，導致國內航空旅行出現收縮。
  • cancellation n. 取消
    In 2002, annual passenger volume at Songshan fell below the 10 million mark, while the opening of Taiwan High Speed Rail in 2007 led to the cancellation of many flight routes across western Taiwan.
    2002年，松山的全年旅客運量跌破1000萬大關，而2007年台灣高鐵的開通則導致台灣西部許多航線被取消。
  • launch v. 發起，推出，開辦
    Two years later, in 2010, Songshan Airport announced the launch of direct flights to and from Shanghai's Hongqiao Airport in June, followed by the opening of a direct service to Tokyo Haneda Airport in October.
    兩年後的2010年，松山機場宣布於6月開通往返上海虹橋機場的直飛航班，隨後於10月開通直飛東京羽田機場的航線。
  • utilize v. 利用，使用
    In recent years, Taiwanese carriers have increasingly utilized Songshan for short-haul flights to destinations such as Japan and South Korea.
    近年來，台灣航空公司日益將松山用於飛往日本和南韓等目的地的短程航班。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作後位修飾
    With the airport's future again under discussion, CNA has compiled a short history of the 90-year-old airport, which, especially in the last half-century, has seen its fortunes rise and fall in response to various events in Taiwan's development.
    句中的「which...」非限定關係子句用來補充說明前面「90-year-old airport」的背景，其中夾帶了介係詞片語「in the last half-century」作時間修飾，說明該機場在過去半個世紀命運起伏的情況。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (With + 名詞 + 分詞/形容詞)
    With the airport's future again under discussion, CNA has compiled a short history of the 90-year-old airport...
    「With + 名詞 + 介係詞片語/形容詞/分詞」結構用來表示附帶狀況或背景原因，意指「隨著機場的未來再次處於討論之中」，常出現在多益閱讀測驗中表達背景情境。
  • 分詞構句表示結果 (resulting in / leading to)
    As international flights were gradually relocated, Songshan Airport shifted to focus on domestic flights, while facing a steep decline in passenger volume.
    句尾的「while facing...」是由連接詞引導的分詞構句（省略了主詞與be動詞，還原為 while it was facing），用來表達「在...的同時也面臨了...」，在多益考試中常考連接詞或分詞的正確形式。
  • 附帶狀況的獨立分詞構句 (with + 名詞 + 現在分詞)
    As of 2025, total passenger volume at Songshan stood at 5.62 million, with the number of passengers on international and cross-strait flights (3.02 million) exceeding those on domestic flights (2.60 million) for the second consecutive year...
    句中的「with the number of passengers... exceeding...」是多益高頻的「with + O + V-ing」結構，用來補充說明總運量達到該數字時，伴隨發生的具體比例與狀態。
