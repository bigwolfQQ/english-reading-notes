  • categorize v. 將……分類、歸類

    衛生福利部部長石崇良昨天表示，他將要求食品藥物管理署（FDA）討論，包含保力達B與維士比等含有酒精且被歸類為指示藥品（OTC）的飲品，是否應該重新分類。

文法重點

  • 非限定關係代名詞子句（補充說明）
    Minister of Health and Welfare Shih Chung-liang (石崇良) yesterday said he would ask the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) to discuss whether beverages such as Paolyta B (保力達B) and Sanyo Whisbih (維士比), which contain alcohol and are categorized as over-the-counter (OTC) medicine, should be recategorized.
    句中的「which contain alcohol and are categorized as...」前方帶有逗號，屬於非限定關係代名詞子句。用來補充說明前面的名詞（beverages），即使拿掉也不影響主要子句的句意。
  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾（分詞構句概念）
    However, they have an alcohol content up to 10 percent, which is more than double that found in most beer products in Taiwan, he said, adding that no doctor would prescribe a beverage with 10 percent alcohol to help someone feel “refreshed and invigorated.”
    句尾的「adding that...」是由現在分詞片語所引導的補充說明，主詞（he）主動做了這項動作（補充說...），相當於原句「and he added that...」的簡化形式。
  • 被動語態
    Nevertheless, he said he would ask the FDA to further discuss whether the products’ drug license should be revoked.
    句中的「should be revoked」使用了被動語態（情態動詞 should + be + 過去分詞）。因為藥證（drug license）是被撤銷，而不是主動去撤銷別人，所以此處使用被動語態。
