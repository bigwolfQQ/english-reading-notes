印尼野火影響東南亞

Indonesian fires impact Southeast Asia

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • impact v. / n. 影響
    Indonesian fires impact Southeast Asia
    印尼野火影響東南亞
  • contain v. 控制、遏制
    as the vast archipelago for weeks battles to contain fires that have burned large swathes of Borneo and Sumatra.
    因為這個幅員遼闊的群島國家數週以來一直在努力控制已燒毀婆羅洲和蘇門答臘大片土地的野火。
  • hazardous adj. 有害的、危險的
    The hazardous smoke has led to an increase in respiratory problems
    有害的煙霧導致呼吸道疾病增加
  • estimated v. (pp. / adj.) 估計的
    nearly 300,000 hectares of land have burned in Indonesia, the Nusantara Atlas estimated.
    努山塔拉地圖集估計，印尼已有將近 30 萬公頃的土地被燒毀。
  • uptick n. 小幅上升、增加
    Indonesia saw an uptick in carbon emissions since late July
    自 7 月下旬以來，印尼的碳排放量出現上升趨勢
  • comparable adj. 可比較的、比得上的
    by the end of last month, the levels were comparable to previous El Nino years of 2015 and 2019
    到了上個月底，其水平已可與先前的聖嬰年（2015 年和 2019 年）相提並論
  • contribute v. 促成、貢獻、是...的原因之一
    Analysts said the El Nino weather phenomenon has contributed to creating drier conditions in Indonesia
    分析師表示，聖嬰天氣現象促成了印尼更乾燥條件的形成
  • founder n. 創辦人
    Nusantara Atlas founder David Gaveau said.
    努山塔拉地圖集創辦人大衛・加沃表示。
  • extinguish v. 熄滅、撲滅
    The fires are difficult to extinguish, because many occurred on peatlands
    這些野火很難撲滅，因為許多火災發生在泥炭地
  • spokesman n. 發言人
    Pantau Gambut spokesman Wahyu Perdana said.
    「Pantau Gambut」發言人瓦尤・佩爾達納表示。
  • declare v. 宣布、宣告
    prompted the government on Friday to declare an emergency in a region of the Sarawak state in Borneo
    促使政府於週五宣佈婆羅洲砂勞越州的一個地區進入緊急狀態

文法重點

  • 現在分詞當形容詞或修飾語（分詞片語）
    Thick smog has blanketed parts of Indonesia as the vast archipelago for weeks battles to contain fires that have burned large swathes of Borneo and Sumatra.
    句尾的「that have burned...」是關係代間詞子句，用來修飾前面的「fires（火災）」，說明這些火災已經燒毀了婆羅洲和蘇門答臘的大片土地。
  • 過去分詞作形容詞修飾名詞
    The hazardous smoke has led to an increase in respiratory problems, forced schools to close and has been drifting to neighboring countries including Malaysia, Singapore and the Philippines.
    句中的「neighboring」是由動詞 neighbor 轉變而來的現在分詞，在此作為形容詞使用，用來修飾名詞「countries」，意指「鄰近的國家」。
  • 對等連接詞連接多個動詞片語
    The hazardous smoke has led to an increase in respiratory problems, forced schools to close and has been drifting to neighboring countries including Malaysia, Singapore and the Philippines.
    主詞「The hazardous smoke」後面接了三個並列的動詞結構：1. has led to...、2. forced...、3. has been drifting...，用來描述有害煙霧所造成的連續結果與動作。
  • because引導原因副詞子句
    The fires are difficult to extinguish, because many occurred on peatlands, a wetland ecosystem with layers of partially decomposed organic materials that become flammable when dry.
    「because...」引導一個原因副詞子句，用來解釋為什麼「這些野火很難被撲滅（The fires are difficult to extinguish）」。
  • 被動語態的應用
    Between January and last month, more than 210,000 hotspots were detected in peatland areas across the country
    句型使用「were detected」（被偵測到），強調熱點是被偵測到的對象，符合新聞報導中客觀陳述數據與事實的常用語氣。
