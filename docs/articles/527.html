文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    As seasonal northeasterly winds are forecast to weaken, daytime temperatures in western Taiwan are expected to reach as high as 34 degrees Celsius on Saturday...
    英文新聞報導氣象或官方說法時常使用被動語態（be + 過去分詞），例如「are forecast」(被預報) 與「are expected」(被預期)，藉此強調客觀事實並避免直接指稱特定主觀消息來源。
  • 分詞片語作形容詞用 (Participial Phrase as Adjective)
    Chang said cloudy to sunny skies are forecast across most of Taiwan Saturday, although brief showers are possible in the greater Taipei area, Keelung and along the north coast, as well as in eastern Taiwan and the Hengchun Peninsula in the south.
    句中的「cloudy to sunny」用來修飾「skies（天空/氣象狀況）」，「cloudy」與「sunny」皆為形容詞，直接置於名詞前方修飾，讓句子結構更精簡。
  • 對等連接詞連接多個地方副詞片語
    ...although brief showers are possible in the greater Taipei area, Keelung and along the north coast, as well as in eastern Taiwan and the Hengchun Peninsula in the south.
    句中運用「as well as」連接多處可能降雨的地點，使得長篇幅的地名羅列層次分明，有助於讀者在一句話中掌握多個不同地理區域的氣象資訊。
