老鷹隊迪克森被放入傷兵名單

Eagles’ Dickerson on injured reserve

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • injured reserve n. phr. 受傷保留名單，傷兵名單
    The Philadelphia Eagles are placing three-time Pro Bowl guard Landon Dickerson on injured reserve after he struggled with a knee injury in Sunday’s season opener, according to multiple reports.
    根據多方報導，費城老鷹隊在明星前鋒 Landon Dickerson 於週日的球季開幕戰中飽受膝傷困擾後，正將他放入受傷保留名單。
  • assessment n. 評估，評價
    The 27-year-old offered a blunt assessment of his performance on Monday during his weekly appearance on Philadelphia radio station WIP.
    這位 27 歲的球員週一在其每週固定出現在費城廣播電台 WIP 的節目中，對自己的表現做出了坦率的評估。
  • liability n. 累贅，負擔，責任
    “I was a complete liability,” he said.
    「我完全是個累贅，」他說。
  • undergo v. 經歷，接受（手術、檢查等）
    He underwent imaging on Monday before the decision was made to take an extended recovery period.
    他在週一接受了造影檢查，之後才做出進行較長時間復原期的決定。
  • repair v. 修理，修復
    He underwent surgery in August last year to repair a meniscus injury, then returned to start 15 games, later acknowledging he came back before he was fully recovered.
    他在去年 8 月接受了手術以修復半月板損傷，隨後歸隊先發了 15 場比賽，後來坦承他在完全康復之前就回來了。
  • acknowledge v. 承認
    He underwent surgery in August last year to repair a meniscus injury, then returned to start 15 games, later acknowledging he came back before he was fully recovered.
    他在去年 8 月接受了手術以修復半月板損傷，隨後歸隊先發了 15 場比賽，後來坦承他在完全康復之前就回來了。
  • defend v. 辯護，防守
    Coach Nick Sirianni defended his longtime starter, pointing to Dickerson’s power in the running game and his role in the Eagles’ success over the past several seasons.
    總教練 Nick Sirianni 為他的長期先發球員辯護，指出 Dickerson 在跑陣進攻中的力量，以及他在老鷹隊過去幾個賽季成功中所扮演的角色。
  • candidate n. 候選人，人選
    Drew Kendall is among the leading candidates to take over at left guard.
    Drew Kendall 是接替左護鋒領先的候選人之一。
  • take over phr. v. 接管，接替
    Drew Kendall is among the leading candidates to take over at left guard.
    Drew Kendall 是接替左護鋒領先的候選人之一。
  • option n. 選擇，選項
    A fifth-round pick out of Boston College last year impressed during the offseason and gives Philadelphia a young in-house option while Dickerson recovers.
    這位去年來自波士頓學院的第五輪選秀球員在休賽季表現出色，在 Dickerson 復原期間為費城提供了一個年輕的內部人選。
  • goal n. 目標
    Dickerson said the goal is to avoid spending another season trying to play through an injury and return healthy for the latter portion of the schedule.
    Dickerson 表示，目標是避免再次度過一個試圖帶傷打球的球季，並在賽程的後半段健康回歸。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾
    Coach Nick Sirianni defended his longtime starter, pointing to Dickerson’s power in the running game and his role in the Eagles’ success over the past several seasons.
    句尾的 「pointing to...」 是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前面教練 Nick Sirianni 採取這個辯護舉動時同時伴隨的動作，相當於「and pointed to...」的簡化。
  • 動名詞片語作受詞
    Dickerson said the goal is to avoid spending another season trying to play through an injury...
    動詞 avoid 後方接動名詞（avoid spending...）作為受詞，表達「避免花費...」，這是多益考試非常常考的動詞搭配型態。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    A fifth-round pick out of Boston College last year impressed during the offseason and gives Philadelphia a young in-house option while Dickerson recovers.
    句中 「out of Boston College last year」 用於修飾前面的選秀球員，指「來自波士頓學院的」，幫助精準限定該球員的背景。
  • 省略連接詞 that 的名詞子句
    Dickerson said the goal is to avoid spending another season...
    動詞 said 後方接了一個當作受詞的名詞子句，用來交代 Dickerson 所說的內容，子句前面的連接詞 that 在口語與新聞英語中經常被省略。
