台南宿舍發生火災，一名菲籍移工送醫，逾300人疏散

Filipino worker hospitalized, over 300 evacuated in Tainan dorm fire

單字解析

  • evacuate v. 疏散，撤離
    while more than 300 workers were evacuated, according to the city's Fire Department.
    根據市消防局指出，同時有超過300名工人被疏散。
  • department n. 部門，局，處
    The department said it received a report on the fire at 7:23 a.m.
    該部門表示，他們於上午7時23分接獲關於這起火災的通報。
  • dispatch v. 派遣，發送
    and dispatched 32 fire trucks, ambulances and other related vehicles and 68 firefighters and rescue personnel to the scene.
    並派遣32輛消防車、救護車及其他相關車輛，以及68名消防與救援人員趕赴現場。
  • personnel n. 人員，員工
    and 68 firefighters and rescue personnel to the scene.
    以及68名消防與救援人員趕赴現場。
  • injure v. 傷害，受傷
    leaving four people injured, including a Filipino man who was hospitalized
    造成四人受傷，其中包括一名住院治療的菲律賓籍男子
  • hospitalize v. 送醫住院
    including a Filipino man who was hospitalized
    其中包括一名住院治療的菲律賓籍男子
  • decline v. 婉拒，拒絕
    Three declined hospital treatment
    三人拒絕就醫
  • resident n. 居民，住戶
    The dormitory housed more than 400 residents
    該宿舍收容了400多名居民
  • property n. 財產，房地產
    The cause of the fire and the extent of the property damage remain under investigation
    起火原因及財產損失程度目前仍在調查中
  • investigation n. 調查
    remain under investigation
    仍在調查中

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表示附帶結果或同時發生的狀況）
    A fire broke out at a migrant worker dormitory in Tainan on Saturday morning, leaving four people injured, including a Filipino man who was hospitalized...
    句尾的「leaving four people injured...」為現在分詞片語作分詞構句，用來補充說明主句發生火災後所造成的結果（留下四人受傷的狀況）。
  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    Local media reported that the worker, who was in his 30s, was taken to Chi Mei Hospital after inhaling smoke...
    句中的「who was in his 30s」是非限定關係代名詞子句，用逗號隔開，用來補充說明前方工人「the worker」的年紀。
  • 被動語態
    More than 300 migrant workers were evacuated, and four people were injured.
