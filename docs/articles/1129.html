  • holiday weekend n. 連假、週末假期
    The four-day Mid-Autumn Festival-Teachers’ Day holiday weekend will begin tomorrow.
    為期四天的中秋節與教師節連假明天就要開始了。
  • admire v. 欣賞、觀賞
    I’m going to several concerts, and then Maokong hill to admire the moon.
    我要去參加幾場音樂會，然後去貓空賞月。
  • release v. 發布、推出、發行

  • predict v. 預測、預言

    我聽說占星師Celine發布了一個以月相為主題的運勢測驗，可以預測你的運勢。

文法重點

  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    I heard astrologer Celine has released a lunar phase-themed luck quiz that can predict your fortune.
    句中的「that can predict your fortune」為形容詞子句，用來修飾先行詞「a lunar phase-themed luck quiz」，補充說明這個心理測驗的功能。
  • 對等連接詞 not just... but also...
    “Think about not just yourself but also those around you...
    「not just A but also B」意為「不僅 A，還有 B」，用來連接兩個並列的名詞或受詞（yourself 與 those around you），強調兩者兼具。
  • 祈使句的運用 (Imperative Sentences)
    “Think about not just yourself but also those around you, make corresponding efforts, and break old bad habits. . .
    這三句連續使用原形動詞開頭（Think, make, break），構成祈使句，帶有建議或指令的語氣，常用於測驗結果或指引說明中。
