台北市長選戰：沈伯洋、蔣萬安均對藥品政策辯論持開放態度

Shen, Chiang open to debate over drug policy in Taipei mayoral race

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • propose v. 提議、建議
    Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) Taipei mayoral candidate Puma Shen (沈伯洋) proposed a debate on drug policy Tuesday
    民主進步黨台北市長候選人沈伯洋於週二提議就毒品政策進行辯論
  • controversy n. 爭議、爭論
    while Mayor Chiang Wan-an (蔣萬安) said the issue could be discussed at a formal debate as controversy over Shen's past writings continued.
    而市長蔣萬安則表示，隨著外界對沈伯洋過去文章的爭議持續延燒，此議題可以在正式辯論中進行討論。
  • negotiate v. 談判、協商
    The two candidates have reached a preliminary agreement to take part in a TVBS-organized debate, with details to be negotiated by their aides
    兩位候選人已達成初步協議，將參與由TVBS舉辦的辯論會，細節將由雙方助理進行協商
  • accuse v. 指控、指責
    Shen accused Chiang's team of misrepresenting his drug policy positions
    沈伯洋指控蔣萬安的團隊曲解了他的毒品政策立場
  • implement v. 實施、執行
    citing methadone treatment and needle-exchange programs already implemented in Taipei.
    並引用了台北市已經實施的美沙酮治療和換針計畫。
  • classification n. 分類、分級（動詞形式為 classify）
    Methadone is classified as a Category 2 narcotic in Taiwan but also has lawful medical uses.
    美沙酮在台灣被列為第二級管制藥品，但也具有合法的醫療用途。
  • supervision n. 監督、管理
    It is administered orally under medical supervision to treat heroin dependence
    它在醫療監督下以口服方式投藥以治療海洛因成癮
  • purpose n. 目的、意圖
    The mayor argued that Shen had either misunderstood those policies or was now reinterpreting his earlier position for electoral purposes.
    市長主張，沈伯洋要麼是誤解了這些政策，要麼是為了選舉目的而重新詮釋他先前的立場。
  • distort v. 扭曲、曲解
    Tsai accused the KMT of distorting academic research for electoral purposes
    蔡一愷指控國民黨為了選舉目的扭曲學術研究

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作為補充說明 (Participial Phrase)
    Speaking while distributing campaign newspapers outside Zhongxiao Fuxing MRT station Tuesday morning, Shen accused Chiang's team of misrepresenting his drug policy positions...
    句首的「Speaking while distributing...」為現在分詞片語，用來修飾主詞 Shen，表示「在做某事同時（開口）說話」，省略了連接詞與主詞（原句結構約等於 While he was speaking...）。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Absolute Participial Construction)
    The two candidates have reached a preliminary agreement to take part in a TVBS-organized debate, with details to be negotiated by their aides, Chiang said.
    「with + 名詞 + 過去分詞 (details to be negotiated / details being negotiated)」形成獨立分詞構句，用來補充說明主句動作伴隨的狀況或細節，在多益閱讀測驗中是常見的高階句構。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Methadone is classified as a Category 2 narcotic in Taiwan but also has lawful medical uses.
    句中使用「is classified as」的被動語態，強調美沙酮「被歸類、被列為」某種等級的管制藥品，焦點在於藥品本身的狀態，而非執行分類的人。
  • 假設語氣 (Subjunctive Mood)
    If decriminalization was considered too radical, he suggested Taiwan could follow other countries by establishing community centers...
    此處使用過去式動詞 (was considered) 來表達對未來或假設情況的臆測（如果除罪化被認為過於激進的話），主句則搭配助動詞的過去式 (could) 表達建議的可能性。
