來自德國「中國震撼」的政治地震

The political earthquake from Germany’s China shock

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • turning point n. phr. 轉捩點
    Sept. 6 could mark a turning point in Germany’s post-World War II political history.
    9月6日可能是德國二戰後政治史上的轉捩點。
  • majority n. 多數，過半數
    In the Saxony-Anhalt state election, the far-right Alternative for Germany (AfD) party came within three seats of an absolute majority...
    在薩克森-安哈特邦的州議會選舉中，極右翼的德國選擇黨（AfD）距離取得絕對多數僅差三個議席...
  • at the expense of prep. phr. 以……為代價
    The AfD’s gains came at the expense of the center-right Christian Democratic Union...
    德國選擇黨的斬獲是以犧牲中右翼的基督教民主聯盟為代價的...
  • incumbent adj. 現任的
    ...the state’s incumbent governing party and the party of German Chancellor Friedrich Merz...
    ...該邦現任執政黨，也是德國總理 Friedrich Merz 的政黨...
  • surge n. 激增，暴增
    There is no shortage of explanations for the surge in support for the AfD in parts of Germany.
    德國部分地區對德國選擇黨的支持率激增，這並不缺乏解釋。
  • deterioration n. 惡化，退化
    After more than three decades of economic deterioration, Saxony-Anhalt appeared to be recovering.
    經過三十多年的經濟惡化，薩克森-安哈特邦似乎正在復甦。
  • contract v. 收縮，縮小（經濟學上指萎縮）
    Saxony-Anhalt’s economy has contracted since 2021, bringing GDP close to its 2013 level.
    薩克森-安哈特邦的經濟自2021年以來一直在萎縮，導致GDP接近其2013年的水平。
  • exporter n. 出口商
    Intensifying competitive pressure from Chinese exporters is threatening to make matters much worse.
    來自中國出口商日益激烈的競爭壓力正威脅著使情況變得更加糟糕。
  • stagnate v. 停滯，不景氣
    Germany’s economy has stagnated since 2019, with output increasing by only about 0.12 percent annually...
    德國經濟自2019年以來一直停滯不前，產出每年僅增長約0.12%...
  • supplier n. 供應商
    ...home to automotive giants such as Mercedes-Benz and Porsche, major suppliers such as Bosch...
    ...這裡是賓士和保時捷等汽車巨頭、博世等主要供應商的所在地...
  • domestic demand n. phr. 國內需求
    At a time of weak domestic demand, high energy costs, structural changes in the automotive industry...
    在國內需求疲軟、能源成本高昂、汽車產業結構性变化的時期...
  • prosperity n. 繁榮，興旺
    ...but the prosperity of all three depends heavily on industries exposed to Chinese competition.
    ...但這三個邦的繁榮都極大地依賴於暴露在中國競爭之下的產業。
  • electoral adj. 選舉的
    Economic decline is only one of many forces that shape electoral outcomes...
    經濟衰退只是塑造選舉結果的眾多力量之一...
  • market share n. phr. 市場佔有率，市佔率
    The risk is not simply that Germany loses market share in automobiles, chemicals or machinery.
    風險不僅僅是德國失去在汽車、化学品或機械領域的市場份額。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況/結果
    In the Saxony-Anhalt state election, the far-right Alternative for Germany (AfD) party came within three seats of an absolute majority, winning 43.8 percent of the vote — more than twice its share in the 2021 election.
    句尾的「winning 43.8 percent of the vote」是由現在分詞 winning 引導的分詞構句，用來補充說明前面主句發生時所伴隨的結果或狀況，相當於「and it won 43.8 percent of the vote」。在多益閱讀測驗的句子填空或克漏字中常考分詞與連接詞的搭配。
  • 過去分詞（片語）作後置定語
    Economic decline is only one of many forces that shape electoral outcomes, but the pattern is too striking to ignore, especially as it has precedents elsewhere.
    句中的「exposed to Chinese competition」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 industries（或隱含在前面的受詞）。這種結構常出現在多益商業文章中，用以簡化形容詞子句（原句形如：...industries which are exposed to Chinese competition），能有效提升文章的緊湊度。
  • 倒裝句 / 否定副詞置於句首
    None of this is to say that Chinese imports automatically lead to votes for the AfD.
    此處使用「None of + 名詞」作為主語，其中 none 表否定。雖然這不是傳統的助動詞倒裝，但「None of this is to say that...」是英文新聞與商業評論中非常實用且高頻的慣用句構，用來釐清觀點、避免讀者過度推論。
  • 關係代名詞子句（限定用法）
    For decades, Germany’s stability rested on an implicit bargain: A high-value manufacturing would provide good jobs, strong export revenues and broad-based prosperity, which political institutions would translate into social cohesion.
    句尾的「which political institutions would translate into social cohesion」是由關係代名詞 which 引導的非限定子句，先行詞為前面整段有關「高品質製造業所帶來的繁榮」的概念。which 在子句中作 translate 的受詞，有助於串連複雜的商業與政治因果關係。
