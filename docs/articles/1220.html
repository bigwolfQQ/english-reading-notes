明起氣溫可能降至攝氏16度低溫

Temperatures could drop to low of 16°C from tomorrow

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • strengthen v. 增強，加強
    The northeast monsoon is expected to strengthen, bringing rain and cooler temperatures to northern and eastern Taiwan...
    東北季風預計將增強，為台灣北部和東部帶來降雨與較涼爽的天氣...
  • adjunct adj. 兼任的，附屬的
    Wu, an adjunct associate professor in the Department of Atmospheric Sciences at National Central University, said in his daily weather column.
    國立中央大學大氣科學系兼任副教授吳在其每日氣象專欄中指出。
  • stable adj. 穩定的
    From tomorrow to Friday, the west side of Taiwan is expected to remain mostly sunny and stable...
    從明天到週五，台灣西部預計維持多雲時晴且穩定...
  • difference n. 差異，差距
    The large difference between daytime and nighttime temperatures could make mornings feel particularly cool, he added.
    他補充說，日夜溫差大可能會使早晨感覺特別涼爽。
  • increase v. 增加，提高
    On Friday and Saturday, moisture is expected to increase slightly on the windward side...
    週五與週六，迎風面的水氣預計會略微增加...
  • threat n. 威脅
    That could leave three typhoons simultaneously active in the western Pacific, but none is expected to pose a threat to Taiwan, the agency said.
    該機構表示，這可能會使得西太平洋同時有三個颱風活躍，但預計沒有任何一個會對台灣構成威脅。
  • active adj. 活躍的
    That could leave three typhoons simultaneously active in the western Pacific...
    這可能會使得西太平洋同時有三個颱風活躍...
  • remain v. 保持，維持
    The rest of the nation should remain mostly sunny and stable...
    全台其餘地區預計維持多雲時晴且穩定...

文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The northeast monsoon is expected to strengthen, bringing rain and cooler temperatures to northern and eastern Taiwan...
    新聞英語中常使用「is expected to...」（被預期會……）這樣的被動語態結構來報導尚未發生、但基於氣象預報或官方說法將會出現的情況，語氣客觀且具權威性。
  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況 (Participle Phrase indicating accompanying circumstance)
    ...bringing rain and cooler temperatures to northern and eastern Taiwan, with temperatures in low-lying areas likely to drop to as low as 16°C from tomorrow through Friday...
    句尾的「bringing...」是由對等子句簡化而來的分詞構句，用來補充說明東北季風增強所帶來的隨之而來的結果或動作。
  • with + 受詞 + 形容詞/分詞 (With-construction)
    ...with temperatures in low-lying areas likely to drop to as low as 16°C from tomorrow through Friday...
    「with + 名詞 + 形容詞片語」結構在多益閱讀測驗與商務英文中極為常見，用來表示「在……的情況下」或伴隨發生的狀態。
