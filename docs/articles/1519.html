政府嚴厲譴責利雅德主要機場遭攻擊事件

Government slams strikes on main airport in Riyadh

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • target v. 針對，以...為目標
    The government yesterday condemned a series of attacks last week targeting King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh that resulted in deaths and civilian casualties...
    政府昨天譴責上週針對利雅德哈立德國王國際機場的一系列攻擊，這些攻擊造成人員死亡和平民傷亡...
  • result in phr. v. 導致，結果造成
    ...that resulted in deaths and civilian casualties, adding that one Taiwanese was injured in one of the attacks and hospitalized in Saudi Arabia.
    ...這些攻擊造成人員死亡和平民傷亡，並補充說有一名台灣人在其中一次攻擊中受傷，目前正在沙烏地阿拉伯住院治療。
  • disclose v. 透露，公開
    The Ministry of Foreign Affairs did not disclose when or where the Taiwanese was injured or their identity, citing privacy concerns.
    基於隱私考量，外交部未透露該名台灣人受傷的時間、地點或其身分。
  • official n. 官員，公務員
    Officials from Taiwan’s representative office in Saudi Arabia visited the person at the hospital, the ministry said.
    外交部表示，台灣駐沙烏地阿拉伯代表處的官員已前往醫院探視該名傷者。
  • cease v. 停止，終止
    The ministry slammed the Houthis in Yemen for the series of attacks targeting civilian infrastructure in Saudi Arabia and urged the group to immediately cease its attacks.
    外交部嚴厲譴責葉門什葉派青年運動對沙烏地阿拉伯民用基礎設施發動的一系列攻擊，並敦促該團體立即停止攻擊。
  • urgent adj. 緊急的，急迫的（相關字彙: emergency n. 緊急情況）
    In an emergency, Taiwanese in Saudi Arabia can call the representative office for assistance at +966-505-223-725...
    如果在沙烏地阿拉伯遇到緊急情況，台灣人可以撥打代表處的緊急求助電話 +966-505-223-725...
  • assistance n. 協助，幫助
    In an emergency, Taiwanese in Saudi Arabia can call the representative office for assistance at +966-505-223-725...
    如果在沙烏地阿拉伯遇到緊急情況，台灣人可以撥打代表處的緊急求助電話 +966-505-223-725...
  • announce v. 宣布，宣告
    A day later, another attack killed one person and injured nearly a dozen others, said Saudi Arabian authorities, who have since announced that the airport would remain closed until Saturday.
    一天後，另一次攻擊造成 1 人死亡、近十人受傷。沙烏地阿拉伯當局表示，並自此宣布機場將關閉至週六。
  • remain v. 保持，維持（某種狀態）
    A day later, another attack killed one person and injured nearly a dozen others, said Saudi Arabian authorities, who have since announced that the airport would remain closed until Saturday.
    一天後，另一次攻擊造成 1 人死亡、近十人受傷。沙烏地阿拉伯當局表示，並自此宣布機場將關閉至週六。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    The government yesterday condemned a series of attacks last week targeting King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh...
    句中的「targeting King Khalid International Airport...」是由現在分詞片語構成的後位修飾語，用來補充說明前面的名詞 attacks（攻擊事件）。這等於是形容詞子句「that targeted King Khalid International Airport...」的簡化寫法，在新聞英語中非常常見，能讓句子更簡潔流暢。
  • 現在分詞表達附帶結果 (Present Participle indicating Result)
    ...targeting King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh that resulted in deaths and civilian casualties, adding that one Taiwanese was injured in one of the attacks...
    句尾的「adding that...」是現在分詞作對等或接續的動作，表達伴隨發生的情況或進一步補充說明（主詞 the government 做了發動攻擊的譴責，同時也『補充說明』了...）。這種用法常出現在報導中，用來串接官員的額外發言。
  • 分詞構句表示條件/原因 (Participle Clause used as Adverbial)
    The Ministry of Foreign Affairs did not disclose when or where the Taiwanese was injured or their identity, citing privacy concerns.
    句尾的「citing privacy concerns」是分詞構句，由「Because they cited privacy concerns」簡化而來。省略了主體並將動詞改為現在分詞 citing，用以說明外交部「未透露身分與時間」的原因（基於隱私考量）。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    ...said Saudi Arabian authorities, who have since announced that the airport would remain closed until Saturday.
    句中的「who have since announced...」是一個非限定關係代名詞子句（前面有逗號隔開），用來補充說明先行詞「Saudi Arabian authorities（沙烏地阿拉伯當局）」。這種子句提供額外的附加資訊，若拿掉也不會影響主要子句的完整意思。
