單字解析

  • candidate n. 候選人
    Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) Legislator and Taipei mayoral candidate Puma Shen (沈伯洋) yesterday laid out proposed policies...
    民主進步黨（民進黨）立法委員兼台北市長參選人沈伯洋昨天提出了...
  • propose v. 提議，提出
    Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) Legislator and Taipei mayoral candidate Puma Shen (沈伯洋) yesterday laid out proposed policies to encourage four forms of environmentally friendly commuting...
    民主進步黨（民進黨）立法委員兼台北市長參選人沈伯洋昨天提出了旨在鼓勵台北市四種環保通勤方式的政策...
  • prioritize v. 優先考慮，把...列為優先
    The policies would prioritize commuter safety and convenience...
    這些政策將優先考慮通勤者的安全與便利...
  • convenience n. 便利，方便
    The policies would prioritize commuter safety and convenience, and include proposals to add shared electric scooters to the TPass program...
    這些政策將優先考慮通勤者的安全與便利，其中包括提案將共享電動機車納入TPass方案...

    ...include proposals to add shared electric scooters to the TPass program and provide 30 minutes for free on the electric YouBike 2.0E during commuter hours...
    ...其中包括提案將共享電動機車納入TPass方案，並在通勤尖峰時段提供YouBike 2.0E前30分鐘免費...
  • establish v. 建立，設立
    Shen said he would improve pedestrian safety by establishing school commuter zones around all of Taipei’s more than 150 elementary schools within eight years.
    沈伯洋表示，他將在八年內於台北市所有150多所國小周邊設立學童通勤專區，藉此改善行人安全。
  • official adj. 官方的，正式的
    Official data showed that the peak period for traffic deaths and injuries in Taipei in 2024 was between 7am and 9am...
    官方數據顯示，2024年台北市交通事故死傷的高峰期是在上午7點至9點...
  • recommend v. 建議，推薦
    The central government recommends defining an area within 150m of a school as a school commuting zone...
    中央政府建議將學校周邊150公尺內的區域定義為學校通勤專區...
  • enact v. 實施，通過（法律、政策等）
    Secondly, Shen said he would enact bus safety reforms, focused on subsidizing bus drivers’ salaries...
    其次，沈伯洋表示他將推動公車安全改革，重點在於補助公車司機薪資...
  • subsidize v. 給予...補助，津貼補助
    ...focused on subsidizing bus drivers’ salaries, redesigning driver seats, establishing driver rest stations at major turnaround points...
    ...重點在於補助公車司機薪資、重新設計駕駛座椅、在主要調度折返點設立司機休息站...
  • promote v. 促進，推廣，宣傳
    Thirdly, Shen said he would work to connect, maintain and supplement Taipei’s bicycle network to promote bicycle riding.
    第三，沈伯洋表示他將致力於連接、維護並增補台北的自行車路網，以推廣騎自行車。
  • surpass v. 超越，勝過
    After YouBike resumed making the first 30 minutes of rides free, the number of rides increased by 55 percent, surpassing 400 million rides, he said.
    他說，在YouBike恢復前30分鐘免費騎乘後，騎乘次數增加了55%，突破了4億人次。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞當後置修飾語（分詞片語）
    Official data showed that the peak period for traffic deaths and injuries in Taipei in 2024 was between 7am and 9am, when students are traveling to school, he said.
    句中的 "traveling to school" 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的學生（students），等於形容詞子句 "who are traveling to school" 的簡化，讓語氣更為緊湊流暢。
  • 對等連接詞連接多個動詞原形
    Thirdly, Shen said he would work to connect, maintain and supplement Taipei’s bicycle network to promote bicycle riding.
    句中的 "connect, maintain and supplement" 透過對等連接詞 "and" 平行並列，共用前面的不定詞 "to"（省略了後面的 to），用來描述他打算對自行車路網採取的聯動動作。
  • 過去分詞片語作被動修飾語
    The policies would prioritize commuter safety and convenience, and include proposals to add shared electric scooters to the TPass program...
    句中的 "shared electric scooters" 中的 shared 是過去分詞作形容詞用，修飾 electric scooters（電動機車），表示「被共享的／共乘的」，在多益商業閱讀中非常常見。
