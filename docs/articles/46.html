美國女性主義標誌性人物葛蘿莉亞·斯坦能逝世，享壽 92 歲

US feminist icon Gloria Steinem dies at age of 92

單字解析

  • announce v. 宣布，宣告
    Gloria Steinem, the US journalist, author and activist who was one of the most recognizable and enduring faces of the feminist movement, has died at age 92, her foundation announced on Thursday.
    美國記者、作家兼活動家葛蘿莉亞·斯坦能是女性主義運動中最具辨識度且持久的人物之一，她的基金會於週四宣布，她已於 92 歲高齡逝世。
  • statement n. 聲明，陳述
    “Yesterday, Gloria Steinem passed away peacefully at her home in New York City, surrounded by some of the many who loved her,” Gloria’s Foundation said in a statement.
    葛蘿莉亞基金會在聲明中表示：「昨天，葛蘿莉亞·斯坦能在紐約市的家中安詳離世，身邊環繞著幾位深愛她的人。」
  • specify v. 具體說明，指定
    It did not specify the cause of death.
    聲明中並未說明死因。
  • responsibility n. 責任
    “It is now our responsibility to keep it lifted into the future,” said tennis legend Billie Jean King...
    網球傳奇比利·珍·金說：「現在我們的責任是讓它繼續在未來高高舉起。」
  • approval n. 批准，同意
    ...denouncing the inequalities American women endured at a time when many needed their husband’s approval to open a bank account.
    ...當時許多女性需要丈夫的同意才能開設銀行帳戶，她公開譴責她們所忍受的不平等現象。
  • organize v. 組織，籌辦
    In the 1970s, she founded Ms. magazine and the National Women’s Political Caucus and organized the first National Women’s Conference in Houston.
    在 1970 年代，她創辦了《Ms.》雜誌和美國國家女性政治黨團，並在休士頓組織了第一屆全國婦女大會。
  • position n. 立場，職位
    Asked by a reporter why she had changed her position on single life, she said: “I didn’t change. Marriage changed.”
    當記者問她為什麼改變對單身生活的立場時，她回答說：「我沒有變。婚姻變了。」
  • defend v. 辯護，防守
    She was criticized for defending Bill Clinton in 1989 when the then-governor of Arkansas was accused of sexual harassment.
    1989 年，時任阿肯色州州長的比爾·柯林頓被指控性騷擾時，斯坦能因替他辯護而受到批評。
  • garner v. 獲得，收集
    In 2016, when Hillary Clinton was running for president but struggling to garner support among women, Steinem suggested young women preferred her rival Bernie Sanders because young men liked him.
    2016 年，當希拉蕊·柯林頓競選總統但在女性中難以爭取支持時，斯坦能暗示年輕女性更喜歡她的競爭對手伯尼·桑德斯，是因為年輕男性喜歡他。
  • achieve v. 達到，實現
    “Today, generations of women are walking a little taller and aiming a little higher because of what she fought for and achieved,” the former president posted on X.
    這位前總統在 X 平台上發文寫道：「今天，世世代代的女性因為她所為之奮鬥並取得的成就，走路時腰桿挺得更直了一些，目標也定得更高了一些。」

文法重點

  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    Gloria Steinem, the US journalist, author and activist who was one of the most recognizable and enduring faces of the feminist movement, has died at age 92...
    句中的 "who was one of the most recognizable..." 為主格關係代名詞引導的形容詞子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞 "the US journalist, author and activist"（美國記者、作家兼活動家葛蘿莉亞·斯坦能）。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    “Yesterday, Gloria Steinem passed away peacefully at her home in New York City, surrounded by some of the many who loved her,”...
    句中的 ", surrounded by some of the many..." 是一個過去分詞片語，省略了 "who were"，用來補充描述主詞 Gloria Steinem 在離世時的狀態（被許多愛她的人所環繞）。
  • 現在分詞片語表附帶狀況或結果
    The announcement sparked an outpouring of tributes honoring her decades of boundary-pushing activism.
  • 地方副詞倒裝（強調主詞或動作）
    Supporters stopped outside Steinem’s Manhattan apartment to pay respects.
    雖然本句為主動語態，但介系詞片語 "outside Steinem’s Manhattan apartment" 作為地方副詞片語放在動詞 "stopped" 後方，清楚交代了支持者聚集悼念的實際地點。
