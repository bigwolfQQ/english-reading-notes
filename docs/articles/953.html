總統賴清德：台灣透過全新災害聯盟準備好回饋世界

Taiwan ready to give back through new disaster alliance: Lai

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • contribute v. 貢獻，捐獻
    27 years later, it is Taiwan's turn to contribute to the world
    27年後，換台灣為世界做出貢獻
  • delegation n. 代表團
    Lai said while meeting delegation heads from the World Alliance for Disaster Management (WADM) at the Presidential Office.
    賴總統在總統府會見世界災害管理聯盟（WADM）各國代表團團長時表示。
  • organization n. 組織，機構
    The WADM, formally launched in Taipei on Saturday, is the first Taiwan-initiated intergovernmental organization in 55 years, according to Lai.
    賴總統指出，於週六在台北正式成立的世界災害管理聯盟，是台灣55年來首度發起成立的跨政府組織。
  • participate v. 參與，參加
    Central and local governments and disaster management organizations from 21 countries... are participating as observers or partners.
    來自21個國家的中央與地方政府及災害管理組織...正以觀察員或夥伴身分參與。
  • comprehensive adj. 全面的，綜合的
    Over the ensuing 27 years, Taiwan has established a comprehensive disaster management framework...
    在隨後的27年中，台灣建立起一套完善的災害管理體系...
  • hub n. 中心，樞紐
    Under the WADM framework, the center will serve as a major Indo-Pacific hub for disaster management training and exchanges...
    在WADM的架構下，該中心將作為印太地區災害管理訓練與交流的重要樞紐...
  • cooperate v. 合作，配合
    ...with members cooperating through joint training and exercises, mutual assistance and information sharing, Lai said.
    賴總統表示，成員們將透過聯合訓練與演習、互助以及資訊分享來進行合作。
  • goal n. 目標
    Our goal is not only to build back better, but also to prepare better together
    我們的目標不僅是重建得更好，更是要共同做好更好的準備
  • preparedness n. 準備就緒，防備
    Oilouch said WADM delegates were "quite impressed" by Taiwan's disaster preparedness and management...
    雷蒙傑洛表示，WADM代表們...對台灣的防災備災與管理能力「印象深刻」。
  • capacity n. 能力，容量
    I remain confident in our collective capacity to overcome them through shared purpose and collaboration
    我對於我們透過共同目標與合作來克服這些挑戰的集體能力保持信心

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況或結果說明
    The earthquake killed more than 2,400 people and caused over 100,000 buildings to collapse, making it Taiwan's worst disaster since the end of World War II, according to Lai.
    句尾的「making it...」是由對等子句簡化而來的分詞構句，用來補充說明前面整起地震所造成的結果（即「使得它成為台灣自二戰以來最慘重的災害」），主詞一致且主動表達。
  • 現在分詞當形容詞修飾名詞
    Recognizing that each member nation faces its own distinct and pressing challenges, I remain confident...
    句首的「Recognizing that...」是以現在分詞開頭的分詞片語，用來表達「體認到……」，修飾主句的主詞 I，在多益閱讀測驗中常見於商業信件或演說開頭表達原因或狀態。
  • 被動語態
    The WADM, formally launched in Taipei on Saturday, is the first Taiwan-initiated intergovernmental organization in 55 years...
    句中的「formally launched」是過去分詞片語形成的被動用法（省略了 which was），用來修飾主詞 The WADM，表示該聯盟是被正式發起與成立的。
  • Not only... but also... 對等連接詞句構
    Our goal is not only to build back better, but also to prepare better together
    「not only... but also...」是用來連接兩個對等成分（此處為兩個不定詞片語 to build... 與 to prepare...）的常用句構，強調「不但……而且……」，在多益考試的文法選擇題和寫作中頻繁出現。
