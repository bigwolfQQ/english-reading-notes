消息人士稱，日本考慮將國防開支提升至 GDP 的 3.5%

Japan eyes defense spending at 3.5% of GDP, sources say

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • target n. 目標
    Japan is considering a new midterm defense spending target of 3.5 percent of GDP...
    日本正在考慮訂定新的中期國防開支目標，即達到 GDP 的 3.5%...
  • financial market n. phr. 金融市場
    ...a move that could send a shock wave through financial markets concerned about Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi’s spending plans.
    ...此舉可能會對擔心日本首相高市早苗支出計畫的金融市場掀起震撼波。
  • counterpart n. 相對應的人或機關（對口）
    Japanese defense officials have already signaled a willingness to sharply increase defense spending in meetings with their US counterparts...
    日本國防官員在與美國對口官員的會談中，已經表態強烈願意大幅增加國防支出...
  • commitment n. 承諾、保證
    One scenario under consideration is to match a commitment made by South Korea to increase defense spending to 3.5 percent of GDP over 10 years...
    其中一個考慮中的方案是效仿南韓作出的承諾，在 10 年內將國防支出增加至 GDP 的 3.5%...
  • boost v. 提升、促進、增加
    Like other US allies, Tokyo has been under pressure from US President Donald Trump’s administration to boost its defensive strength...
    如同其他美國盟友一樣，東京一直面臨來自美國總統川普政府的壓力，要求其提升防衛力量...
  • reliance n. 依賴、信賴
    ...and reduce its reliance on the US military.
    ...並減少對美軍的依賴。
  • accelerate v. 加速
    Takaichi has already accelerated defense spending to almost 2 percent of GDP in the financial year ended in March...
    高市早苗已將國防開支加速提升至幾乎占截至 3 月底之財年 GDP 的 2%...
  • fiscal policy n. phr. 財政政策
    ...even though Takaichi has pledged to follow a “responsible, proactive fiscal policy.”
    ...儘管高市早苗已誓言要遵循「負責且積極的財政政策」。
  • investment n. 投資
    ...but they have made clear that they expect significantly more investment.
    ...但他們已明確表示期待有顯著更多的投資。
  • request v. / n. 要求、請求
    The ministry did not immediately provide a response to a request for comment.
    國防部未立即回覆置評請求。
  • forecast n. 預測
    Measured against the Cabinet Office’s nominal GDP forecast for this fiscal year, spending would come to 1.5 percent, he said.
    他說，若以內閣府對本會計年度名目 GDP 的預測來衡量，支出將達到 1.5%。
  • alternative n. 替代方案
    “Whether it’s over five years or 10 years, I don’t see any alternative given how important the US alliance is,” Ward said.
    沃德表示：「無論是透過五年還是十年達成，鑑於美日同盟是如此重要，我看不到任何其他替代方案。」

文法重點

  • 分詞片語當後位修飾語 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...financial markets concerned about Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi’s spending plans.
    句中的「concerned about...」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 markets（金融市場）。原句省略了「which are」，還原全貌為「...financial markets which are concerned about...」，在新聞英語中為了簡潔，常直接使用分詞片語作形容詞用。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    One scenario under consideration is to match a commitment made by South Korea...
    「under consideration」是由被動概念延伸而來的片語（考慮中），而「made by South Korea」則是過去分詞片語作後位修飾語，修飾前面的 commitment（承諾），表示該承諾是「被（南韓）做出的」，突顯被動語態的應用。
  • 獨立分詞構句 / 讓步與條件混合句構 (Independent Clause with a Prepositional Phrase)
    Committing to 3.5 percent could unsettle market players wary of large debt issuance, even though Takaichi has pledged to follow a “responsible, proactive fiscal policy.”
    句首的「Committing to 3.5 percent」是動名詞片語作為句子的主詞。後方連接「even though」引導的副詞子句（讓步狀語子句），用來表達轉折語氣，是多益閱讀測驗中常見的長句結構。
