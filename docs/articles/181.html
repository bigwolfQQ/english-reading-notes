鴻海公布歷年最強 8 月營收

Hon Hai reports strongest August sales on record

    這家 iPhone 組裝廠與人工智慧（AI）伺服器製造商所公布的數據顯示，鴻海（又稱為富士康科技集團）上個月營收達新台幣 9,217.7 億元（約合 291.4 億美元），較去年同期大幅成長 51.98%。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Present Participle）作後置定語與附帶說明
    Hon Hai, also known as Foxconn Technology Group (富士康), posted NT$921.77 billion (US$29.14 billion) in sales last month, up 51.98 percent from a year earlier, data released by the iPhone assembler and artificial intelligence (AI) server maker showed.
    句尾的「data released by... showed」中，過去分詞片語「released by the iPhone assembler and artificial intelligence (AI) server maker」用來修飾前方的名詞「data（數據）」，表被動關係，意思是「由……所發布的數據」。
  • 對比連接詞 while 的用法
    Hon Hai said its cloud and networking division enjoyed significant year-on-year revenue growth amid accelerating AI momentum, while its smart consumer electronics division saw sales growing significantly as clients rebuilt inventories for the upcoming debut of new products and as shipment prices moved higher.
    句中的「while」在此處作對比連接詞使用，意為「然而、而」，用來連接並對比兩個不同的子句，說明鴻海不同事業群在同一期間內截然不同的業績表現。
  • 複合形容詞（Hyphenated Compound Adjectives）
    In the first eight months of this year, Hon Hai’s consolidated sales stood at NT$6.51 trillion, up 39.73 percent from a year earlier, it said.
    片語「year-on-year」是由名詞加上前置詞再加名詞所組成的複合形容詞，用來修飾後方的名詞（如 revenue growth 或 sales），在商業與財經新聞中非常常見，意思是「年對年的、與去年同期相比的」。
