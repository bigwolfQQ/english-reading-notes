文法重點

  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense, had + pp)
    Prosecutors on Tuesday said they had indicted a Hong Kong gang member over the assault...
    用以表達在過去某一時間點（said）之前已經完成的動作（had indicted，即起訴發生在說這句話之前）。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle as Post-modifier)
    Liu Kong-faat (廖港發), described by prosecutors as a ranking member of the Hong Kong-based Wo Shing Wo (和勝和), was charged with assault...
    逗號之間的 "described by prosecutors as..." 是過去分詞片語，用來補充說明主詞 Liu Kong-faat 的身分，相當於關代子句 "who was described by prosecutors as..." 的簡化。
  • 現在分詞片語表附帶狀況或結果 (Present Participle Phrase)
    Liu was arrested at Taichung International Airport while attempting to flee to Busan, South Korea, while the other suspect had already left Taiwan, prosecutors said.
    句中的 "while attempting to flee..." 是現在分詞片語，省略了主詞與be動詞（while [he was] attempting），用來修飾主句動作發生的同時所進行的狀態。
  • 間接問句/名詞子句作受詞 (Indirect Question / Noun Clause)
    Prosecutors added that they were also continuing to investigate who masterminded the operation and whether there were any accomplices in Taiwan.
    動詞 investigate 後面接了兩個名詞子句（who masterminded... 與 whether there were...）作為其受詞，用「疑問詞/連接詞 + 主詞 + 動詞」的語序來表達不確定的內容。
