資安官員計劃進行人工智慧測試

Cyber official plans AI testing

單字解析

  • prioritize v. 把...列為優先，優先處理
    The institute’s new director, Dennis Kung (龔化中), told a news conference in Taipei that this would be one of the tasks he would prioritize during his term.
    資安院新任院長龔化中在台北的一場記者會上表示，這將是他任內優先推動的工作任務之一。
  • focus n. 焦點，中心
    The focus should be on closely linking R&D units with frontline defense units, with the experiences gained from defense units guiding the work of the R&D units, Kung said.
    龔化中表示，重點應在於將研發單位與第一線防禦單位緊密連結，並以防禦單位獲得的經驗來指導研發單位的工作。
  • meet the needs phr. 符合需求，滿足需要
    “With the addition of AI-proficient staff, we can produce a cybersecurity defense that truly meets the needs of the government and private sectors in the fast-changing AI age,” he said.
    他說：「藉由加入精通 AI 的人員，我們能夠打造出真正符合政府與民間部門在瞬息萬變的 AI 時代中需求的資安防禦。」
  • aim v. 旨在，目標是
    Kung said that he aims to lead the team at the National Institute of Cybersecurity in upgrading four areas of defense.
    龔化中表示，他旨在帶領國家資通安全研究院團隊升級四大防禦領域。
  • conduct v. 執行，進行
    Second, the institute would work with international AI firms to use on-premises models to conduct automated source-code scans of software used in key government and critical infrastructure projects, addressing vulnerabilities at the development stage, he said.
    他說，其次，該研究院將與國際 AI 企業合作，使用地端模型對政府關鍵及基礎設施專案所使用的軟體進行自動化原始碼掃描，在開發階段解決漏洞。
  • address v. 處理，解決（問題等）
    Second, the institute would work with international AI firms to use on-premises models to conduct automated source-code scans of software used in key government and critical infrastructure projects, addressing vulnerabilities at the development stage, he said.
    他說，其次，該研究院將與國際 AI 企業合作，使用地端模型對政府關鍵及基礎設施專案所使用的軟體進行自動化原始碼掃描，在開發階段解決漏洞。
  • assist v. 協助，幫助
    Fourth, the institute would assist the government and the private sector in building a controlled environment for high-intensity AI-powered cybersecurity attacks, Kung said, adding that this would help build a strong, ingrained defensive response.
    第四，該研究院將協助政府與民間部門建立一個高強度 AI 驅動資安攻擊的受控環境，龔化中指出，這將有助於建立強大且根深蒂固的防禦反應。
  • comprehensive adj. 全面的，綜合的
    Government agencies should first establish a comprehensive software bill of materials inventory and prioritize repair based on risk levels, he said, adding that they can deploy virtual patches as temporary measures to block threats, rather than attempting to patch every vulnerability indiscriminately.
    他表示，政府機關應首先建立完整的軟體物料清單庫存，並根據風險等級優先進行修復...
  • temporary adj. 暫時的，臨時的
    Government agencies should first establish a comprehensive software bill of materials inventory and prioritize repair based on risk levels, he said, adding that they can deploy virtual patches as temporary measures to block threats, rather than attempting to patch every vulnerability indiscriminately.
    ...並補充說明，機關可以部署虛擬修補程式作為阻擋威脅的暫時措施...
  • be committed to phr. 致力於，承諾
    Kung said the institute would be committed to strengthening Taiwan’s overall cybersecurity capabilities by working with its incident response center and the Administration for Cybersecurity to help domestic companies establish product security incident response teams (PSIRTs).
    龔化中表示，該研究院將致力於透過與其事件應變中心及數位發展部資通安全署合作，協助國內企業建立產品資安incident應變團隊（PSIRT），以強化台灣整體資安能力。
  • ensure v. 確保，保證
    This year, the institute plans to assist at least 30 companies in establishing PSIRTs to ensure that vulnerabilities can be reported and patched promptly when discovered, he added.
    他補充說，今年該研究院計劃協助至少 30 家企業建立 PSIRT，以確保在發現漏洞時能夠及時通報並進行修補。
  • target v. 以...為攻擊目標，瞄準
    Regarding frequent DDoS attacks targeting media Web sites, Kung said that the institute and the Taiwan Computer Emergency Response Team/Coordination Center are always ready to provide support.
    針對頻繁針對媒體網站的 DDoS 攻擊，龔化中表示，該研究院與台灣電腦網路危機處理暨協調中心隨時準備提供支援。

文法重點

  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    Kung, who has private-sector experience in researching and developing networking equipment, smartphones and cybersecurity software, said cybersecurity research should not be limited to product development in laboratories.
    句中的「who has private-sector experience...」是非限定關係代名詞子句，用來補充說明主詞 Kung（龔化中）的背景經歷，逗號前後可將此子句視為補充資訊。
  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況（with + 名詞 + 分詞）
    “With the addition of AI-proficient staff, we can produce a cybersecurity defense that truly meets the needs of the government and private sectors in the fast-changing AI age,” he said.
    此處使用「With + 賓語 (the addition of...) + 過去分詞/形容詞」的結構，用來表達「在...的情況下」或「隨著...」，在多益閱讀測驗中是非常常見的高級表述方式。
  • 現在分詞作補充說明（分詞構句）
    Kung said, adding that this would help build a strong, ingrained defensive response.
    句尾的「adding that...」是由分詞構句簡化而來，原意為「and he added that...」，用來連接並補充說明前面所說的話，使語氣流暢且避免重複主詞。
  • 動名詞片語作受詞
    Kung said that he found that AI can identify vulnerabilities at least 10 times faster than traditional methods.
    動詞 find 後面接了名詞子句「that AI can identify...」，而子句中的動詞 identify 後面則接了名詞 vulnerabilities 作為受詞，展現多層次的子句嵌套結構。
