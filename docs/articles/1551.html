幹員搜查美國議員住宅

Agents search US lawmaker’s home

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • investigation n. 調查
    Federal agents on Monday searched the home of Democratic US Representative Ilhan Omar, a startling escalation in a criminal investigation...
    聯邦幹員於週一搜查了民主黨美國聯邦眾議員伊漢·奧瑪的住處，這是一場刑事調查中令人震驚的升級...
  • protocol n. 禮節、規章、慣例
    ...given longstanding US Department of Justice (DOJ) protocol to avoid overt investigative steps involving political candidates in the direct run-up to an election.
    ...鑑於美國司法部長期以來的慣例，即在選舉的直接倒數階段，避免對政治候選人採取公開的調查步驟。
  • target v. 把...作為攻擊目標、對象
    Omar, an outspoken critic of US President Donald Trump, said she was being targeted as one of the Republican’s “political enemies.”
    身為美國總統唐納·川普直言不諱的批評者，奧瑪表示她正成為共和黨人「政治敵人」的打擊目標。
  • scope n. 範圍、領域
    The scope of the investigation was unclear, but Trump and officials in his administration have repeatedly referred to the existence of an inquiry into Omar...
    調查的範圍尚不清楚，但川普及其政府官員多次提到針對奧瑪的調查之存在...
  • fraud n. 詐欺、騙局
    US Secretary of Homeland Security Markwayne Mullin last month cited an “ongoing investigation” that he suggested was examining potential fraud in her family’s immigration paperwork.
    美國國土安全部長馬克韋恩·穆林上個月提到一項「正在進行的調查」，他暗示該調查正在檢視她家族移民文書作業中的潛在欺詐行為。
  • attempt n. 企圖、嘗試
    In a post on X, Omar called the search an “attempt to target political enemies and weaponize federal law enforcement.”
    奧瑪在社群平台 X 上的一篇貼文中，將這次搜查稱為「針對政治敵人並將聯邦執法部門武器化的企圖」。
  • official n. 官方人員、高級官員
    The scope of the investigation was unclear, but Trump and officials in his administration have repeatedly referred to the existence of an inquiry into Omar...
    調查的範圍尚不清楚，但川普及其政府官員多次提到針對奧瑪的調查之存在...
  • statement n. 聲明、陳述
    The DOJ said in a statement that agents had executed a judicially authorized search warrant...
    司法部在一份聲明中表示，幹員執行了一份經法官授權的搜查令...
  • execute v. 執行、實施（法律文件、命令等）
    The DOJ said in a statement that agents had executed a judicially authorized search warrant in the Washington area...
    司法部在一份聲明中表示，幹員在華盛頓地區執行了一份經法官授權的搜查令...
  • allegation n. （未經證實的）指控、指責
    Vance has in the past accused Omar of committing immigration fraud, an allegation she has denied...
    范斯過去曾指控奧瑪犯下移民詐欺罪——這項指控已被她否認...

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The search of the Minnesota congresswoman’s home so close to next month’s midterm contests that would determine control of the US House and the Senate was swiftly condemned by top Democrats in Congress, who raised questions about the timing given longstanding US Department of Justice (DOJ) protocol to avoid overt investigative steps involving political candidates in the direct run-up to an election.
    句中的「involving political candidates」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的「overt investigative steps」。分詞片語可以簡化原本的形容詞子句（如：...steps that involve political candidates），使商業與新聞英語的表達更為精煉。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    The search of the Minnesota congresswoman’s home so close to next month’s midterm contests that would determine control of the US House and the Senate was swiftly condemned by top Democrats in Congress, who raised questions about the timing...
    句尾的「, who raised questions about the timing」是一個非限定關係子句，前方帶有逗號，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「top Democrats in Congress」。這種句型在多益閱讀測驗中常考，用來測試學生對句子主幹與補充說明的辨識能力。
  • 過去完成式/過去分詞修飾語 (Past Participle as Adjective/Modifier)
    The DOJ said in a statement that agents had executed a judicially authorized search warrant in the Washington area related to an ongoing federal criminal investigation, but provided no other details.
