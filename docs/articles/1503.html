歐洲商會新任理事長葛倫特敦促台灣軟體與能源產業展現更大應變能力

New ECCT chair urges greater agility in Taiwan's software, energy sectors

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • potential n. 潛力、可能性
    Taiwan is known for its strength in hardware manufacturing, but Frank Grunert, the new chairperson of the European Chamber of Commerce Taiwan (ECCT), sees considerable potential in software as well.
    台灣以硬體製造實力聞名，但歐洲商會（ECCT）新任理事長葛倫特（Frank Grunert）也看到了軟體領域的相當潛力。
  • procurement n. 採購、尋源
    Once authorities identify the need for a substation, power plant or grid upgrade, budgeting and procurement must precede construction, he said.
    他說，一旦當局確認有變電所、發電廠或電網升級的需求，預算編列和採購必須在施工前進行。
  • precede v. 在...之前、先於
    Once authorities identify the need for a substation, power plant or grid upgrade, budgeting and procurement must precede construction, he said.
    他說，一旦當局確認有變電所、發電廠或電網升級的需求，預算編列和採購必須在施工前進行。
  • implement v. 實施、執行
    He proposed dividing projects into smaller pieces and eliminating unnecessary administrative steps to accelerate implementation.
    他建議將專案拆分成較小的部分，並消除不必要的行政步驟以加速執行。
  • digitization n. 數位化
    Public procurement could become more efficient through digitization, he added.
    他補充說，透過數位化，政府採購可以變得更有效率。
  • bid v. 投標、出價
    Currently, companies bidding for large contracts still have to print extensive documentation and submit multiple copies.
    目前，投標大型合約的公司仍然必須列印大量的書面文件並提交多份副本。
  • shortage n. 短缺、不足
    In his inaugural message to ECCT members, Grunert identified energy supply and costs, labor and talent shortages, and deeper European-Taiwanese economic ties as issues that will shape business conditions into the next decade.
    在給歐洲商會會員的就職致詞中，葛倫特將能源供應與成本、勞動力與人才短缺，以及深化歐台經濟聯繫確定為將塑造未來十年商業環境的議題。
  • complement v. 補充、互補
    During the interview, Grunert said European equipment, chemicals and materials suppliers complement Taiwan's semiconductor industry.
    在訪談期間，葛倫特表示歐洲的設備、化學品和材料供應商與台灣的半導體產業相輔相成。
  • withdrawal n. 撤回、退出
    On offshore wind, Grunert said canceled projects, slower development and companies withdrawing from the market were thinning the pipeline of new projects.
    關於離岸風電，葛倫特表示，專案取消、發展放緩以及企業退出市場，正在縮減新專案的儲備管道。
  • rely on phr. v. 依賴、依靠
    Renewable power generation also strengthens resilience by reducing reliance on imported fuel, he said, while battery storage helps balance fluctuations in wind and solar power.
    他說，再生發電透過減少對進口燃料的依賴也增強了韌性，而電池儲能則有助於平衡風力與太陽能發電的波動。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Present Participle）作後位修飾
    On offshore wind, Grunert said canceled projects, slower development and companies withdrawing from the market were thinning the pipeline of new projects.
    句中的「withdrawing from the market」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「companies」，相當於形容詞子句「who were withdrawing from the market」，說明這些正在撤出市場的公司。
  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況或結果
    Renewable power generation also strengthens resilience by reducing reliance on imported fuel, he said, while battery storage helps balance fluctuations in wind and solar power.
    句中的「by reducing reliance on imported fuel」使用了動名詞片語，用來表達達成前述動作（加強韌性）的方式或手段（藉由減少對進口燃料的依賴）。
  • 被動語態（Passive Voice）
    Public procurement could become more efficient through digitization, he added.
    雖然本句主要使用助動詞 could 加上原形動詞，但在文章其他地方常出現被動語態（如「Projects could be divided」、「permits could be delayed」），用來強調承受動作的對象（專案、許可證），符合商業與新聞報導客觀、聚焦事件本身的語氣。
