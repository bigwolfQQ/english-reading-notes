中國貨輪火災死亡人數攀升至 25 人：官媒

Death toll from cargo ship fire in China rises to 25: state media

單字解析

  • cargo ship n. 貨輪、貨船
    The death toll from a fire that erupted yesterday on a foreign-flagged cargo ship docked in a Chinese port has risen to 25, including five people who had been listed as missing, state media said.
    官媒表示，一艘停泊在中國港口、懸掛外國旗幟的貨輪昨天發生火災，死亡人數已上升至 25 人，其中包括 5 名先前被列為失蹤的人員。
  • dock v. 停泊（於碼頭）、入港
    The death toll from a fire that erupted yesterday on a foreign-flagged cargo ship docked in a Chinese port has risen to 25, including five people who had been listed as missing, state media said.
    官媒表示，一艘停泊在中國港口、懸掛外國旗幟的貨輪昨天發生火災，死亡人數已上升至 25 人，其中包括 5 名先前被列為失蹤的人員。
  • hub n. 中心、樞紐
    The bulk carrier, moored in the eastern shipping hub of Qingdao, had 42 people on board when the fire broke out, state broadcaster China Central Television (CCTV) reported.
    據中國官媒中央電視台（CCTV）報導，這艘停泊在東部航運樞紐青島的散裝貨輪，起火時船上有 42 人。
  • evacuate v. 撤離、疏散
    Seventeen of those were evacuated, including five who were injured.
    其中 17 人已撤離，包括 5 名受傷人員。
  • extinguish v. 熄滅、撲滅（火災）
    “The open flames were extinguished” by about 2:30pm, it said.
    報導稱，「明火已於」下午 2 時 30 分左右「被撲滅」。
  • maintenance n. 維護、保養
    Xinhua news agency said earlier that the fire broke out at about 11:15am during the ship’s port call for maintenance, without specifying the cause of the fire.
    新華社稍早報導稱，火災大約在上午 11 時 15 分該船停靠港口進行維修期間發生，但未說明起火原因。
  • subsidiary n. 子公司
    The Beihai Shipbuilding yard operates as a subsidiary of China State Shipbuilding Corp (中國船舶集團).
    北海造船廠是中國船舶集團的子公司。
  • account n. 責任、帳戶（片語 hold sb to account 意為「追究某人責任」）
    Chinese President Xi Jinping (習近平) “stressed the need to ... strictly hold those responsible to account,” Xinhua reported.
    新華社報導，中國國家主席習近平「強調必須……嚴肅追究相關責任人責任」。
  • incident n. 事件、事故
    “All regions and relevant departments must draw profound lessons from this incident, thoroughly investigate and rectify all types of fire hazards,” Xi said.
    習近平表示：「各地區和有關部門必須深刻汲取此次事件的教訓，徹底排查並整改各類火災隱患。」
  • investigate v. 調查
    “All regions and relevant departments must draw profound lessons from this incident, thoroughly investigate and rectify all types of fire hazards,” Xi said.
    習近平表示：「各地區和有關部門必須深刻汲取此次事件的教訓，徹底排查並整改各類火災隱患。」
  • regulation n. 規章、法規
    There have been several major industrial accidents in China over the past year, where safety regulations are often lax.
    過去一年中，中國發生了多起重大工業事故，當地的安全法規往往較為鬆散。
  • campaign n. 活動、運動、競選活動
    China launched a campaign against fire hazards in high-rise buildings in November last year after a huge blaze engulfed several residential tower blocks in Hong Kong, killing 168 people.
    去年 11 月，香港發生多棟住宅大樓遭大火吞噬、造成 168 人死亡的慘劇後，中國隨即發起了一場針對高層建築火災隱患的整治行動。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞（片語）作後置定語修飾名詞
    The death toll from a fire that erupted yesterday on a foreign-flagged cargo ship docked in a Chinese port has risen to 25...
    句中的「docked in a Chinese port」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「cargo ship（貨輪）」，相當於關代子句「that was docked in a Chinese port」。在多益閱讀測驗中，常考這種分詞簡化結構來測試考生對修飾關係的理解。
  • 含有插入語的報導引述句型
    The bulk carrier, moored in the eastern shipping hub of Qingdao, had 42 people on board when the fire broke out, state broadcaster China Central Television (CCTV) reported.
    句尾的「state broadcaster China Central Television (CCTV) reported」是主句的來源插入語，放在句尾用來交代消息來源。而主詞 the bulk carrier 後面又夾了一個過去分詞片語「moored in...」作補充說明，是標準的英文新聞複雜句型。
  • 非限定性關係代間子句（帶有介系詞與關代）
    There have been several major industrial accidents in China over the past year, where safety regulations are often lax.
    句尾的「where safety regulations are often lax」是非限定性子句（前面有逗號），其中的「where」等於「in which」，用來補充說明先行詞「China（或 accidents 發生的情境）」，用以描述該國安全法規鬆散的背景。
  • 現在分詞作結果狀語（分詞構句）
    An explosion and ensuing fire at a shipyard in China’s Fujian Province last month killed one fire and rescue worker and injured 12 others, state media reported.
