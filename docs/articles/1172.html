Cambodia says it is fighting scammers, but skeptics persist

單字解析

  • evidence n. 證據
    Cambodia is gathering evidence against “masterminds” of massive cyberfraud operations...
    柬埔寨正在收集針對大規模網路詐騙行動「幕後主謀」的證據...
  • crackdown n. 取締，打擊行動
    ...amid criticism that the government’s crackdown has failed to get to those ultimately responsible.
    ...在此同時，外界批評政府的打擊行動未能揪出真正的最終負責人。
  • legal action n. phr. 法律行動
    Legal action, “whether it involves the mastermind, the co-conspirator, owner, enabler or protector, it requires time and also it requires evidence,”...
    法律行動，「無論涉及主謀、共犯、擁有者、協助者或保護者，都需要時間，也需要證據」...
  • investigation n. 調查
    ...and that premature disclosure of investigations could allow suspects to flee.
    ...且過早透露調查內容可能會讓嫌疑人逃跑。
  • tackle v. 處理，應對，打擊
    Cambodia this week hosted representatives of more than a dozen countries for discussions about tackling scam groups.
    柬埔寨本週接待了來自十多個國家的代表，就如何打擊詐騙集團進行討論。
  • disclose v. 透露，揭露
    ...and that premature disclosure of investigations could allow suspects to flee.
    ...且過早透露調查內容可能會讓嫌疑人逃跑。
  • dismantle v. 拆除，瓦解，解散
    Chhay said last month that Cambodia had dismantled large-scale scam compounds since it began a year-long crackdown...
    柴西納瑞上個月表示，自展開為期一年的打擊行動以來，柬埔寨已瓦解大規模的詐騙園區...
  • conglomerate n. 企業集團
    Chhay said authorities are expanding their investigation into the financial network of Prince Group, a Cambodia-based conglomerate...
    柴西納瑞表示，當局正在擴大調查太子集團的金融網路，這是一家總部位於柬埔寨的企業集團...
  • deport v. 驅逐出境
    Its Chinese-born founder, Chen Zhi, was arrested and deported to China from Cambodia earlier this year.
    其出生於中國的創辦人陳志於今年稍早被捕，並從柬埔寨被驅逐出境到中國。
  • facility n. 設施，場所
    Reporters toured two facilities outside of Phnom Penh that Cambodian authorities said were scam centers belonging to Prince Group...
    記者參觀了金邊郊外的兩處設施，柬埔寨當局稱這些設施是屬於太子集團的詐騙中心...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Chhay said Senior Minister Chhay Sinarith, who heads a government commission on combating fraud, said on the sidelines of a global anti-scam conference...
    句中的 "who heads a government commission on combating fraud" 是關係代間詞子句，用來補充說明主詞 Senior Minister Chhay Sinarith 的身分與職責。而在子句中 "combating fraud" 的 combating 則是現在分詞，用來修飾前面的名詞 commission（委員會），表示「負責打擊詐騙的委員會」。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    ...where workers are trafficked, detained in grim conditions and forced to defraud strangers online.
    此句連續使用了三個被動語態（are trafficked, are detained, are forced），結構為「be 動詞 + 過去分詞 (V-pp)」。在新聞英文中，當動作的承受者（此處指 workers 工人）是敘述核心，且強調其遭遇而非施事者時，經常使用被動語態。
  • 讓步副詞子句與對比語氣 (Concessive Adverbial Clause)
    ...although it did not provide a full list of the sites it has raided.
    句首的 "although"（雖然）引導讓步副詞子句，用來表達前後句之間的語氣轉折與對比——即柬埔寨雖然宣稱瓦解了園區，但（對比之下）並沒有提供完整的被突擊搜查地點清單。
  • 分詞構句作補充說明 (Participial Phrase for Additional Detail)
    ...saying that the operation left high-level figures and guardians of the industry unscathed.
    句中的 "saying that..." 是現在分詞 (Present Participle) 引導的分詞構句，接在主句動作之後，用來補充說明分析人士（Analysts）在抱持懷疑時所持的具體說法，相當於 "and they said that..." 的簡化。
