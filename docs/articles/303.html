出口創歷史新高，成長率達 41%

Exports hit record on 41% rise

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • export n. / v. 出口
    Exports hit a record last month as artificial intelligence (AI) demand continued to spread
    由於人工智慧需求持續擴散，上個月出口創下歷史新高。
  • demand n. 需求
    Exports hit a record last month as artificial intelligence (AI) demand continued to spread across semiconductors
    由於人工智慧（AI）需求持續擴散至半導體，上個月出口創下歷史新高。
  • consecutive adj. 連續的
    The gain marked the 34th consecutive month of year-on-year growth
  • expectation n. 預期，期望
    and exceeded the ministry’s expectations of growth of 31 to 35 percent on better-than-expected AI demand.
    且在優於預期的 AI 需求帶動下，超越了財政部預估 31% 至 35% 的成長預期。
  • momentum n. 動能，氣勢
    The momentum is likely to continue into the final months of the year
    這種動能很可能會持續到今年的最後幾個月。
  • supply chain n. phr. 供應鏈
    The concentration highlights Taiwan’s pivotal role in the global AI supply chain
    這種集中現象凸顯了台灣在全球 AI 供應鏈中的關鍵角色。
  • inventory n. 庫存
    The upcoming launch of new mobile devices also provided an additional boost as international brands began building inventories
    隨著國際品牌開始建立庫存，即將推出新款行動裝置也提供了額外的推動力。
  • shipment n. 出貨，運送的貨物
    supporting prices and increasing the value of shipments, she said.
    這支撐了價格並增加了出貨價值，她說。
  • contributor n. 重要因素，貢獻者
    Mineral products were another major contributor to Taiwan’s exports growth
    礦產品是台灣出口成長的另一大主要推手。
  • import n. / v. 進口
    Last month, imports rose even faster, climbing 44.3 percent to US$60.1 billion, it said.
    該部表示，上個月進口成長速度更快，攀升 44.3% 達到 601 億美元。
  • capacity n. 容量，生產能力
    pointing to continued investment by local technology companies as they expand production capacity
    顯示本土科技公司為擴大產能而持續進行投資。
  • surplus n. 順差，盈餘
    The export performance pushed the trade surplus to a record US$22.3 billion that month
    該月的出口表現將貿易順差推高至創紀錄的 223 億美元。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（-ing）表附帶結果或補充說明
    The increase reflected the increasingly international division of labor in AI supply chains, as Taiwanese manufacturers sourced more components and materials from overseas, including memory chips from South Korea, the ministry said.
    句尾的「including memory chips from South Korea」是以現在分詞開頭的分詞片語，用來補充說明前面的 materials（材料）包含了哪些項目，是多益閱讀測驗常見的修飾用法。
  • with + 受詞 + 現在/過去分詞（附帶狀況複合結構）
    The momentum is likely to continue into the final months of the year, with the ministry expecting exports to grow by more than 40 percent this month as the peak season for high-tech products begins.
    「with + 名詞 + 分詞」結構用來表示伴隨的情況或原因。在此句中，「with the ministry expecting...」解釋了動能得以持續的背景原因，是多益進階文法與商用英文的常見高頻句型。
  • 關係代間詞子句作修飾語
    The export performance pushed the trade surplus to a record US$22.3 billion that month, up 32.9 percent from a year earlier, the ministry said.
    句中「up 32.9 percent from a year earlier」為形容詞片語作後位修飾，簡化自「which was up 32.9 percent from a year earlier」，省略了關代與be動詞，使新聞標題或報導語氣更加緊湊精鍊。
