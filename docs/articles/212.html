漢翔與龐巴迪公司簽署為期20年的合作協議

ADIC secures 20-year partnership agreement with Bombardier

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • partnership n. 合作關係、合夥關係
    Taipei, Sept. 7 (CNA) Taiwan aircraft maker Aerospace Industrial Development Corp. (ADIC) announced Monday that it has secured a 20-year partnership agreement with Canada-based Bombardier.
    台北，9月7日（中央社）— 台灣飛機製造商漢翔航空工業股份有限公司（ADIC）於週一宣布，已與總部設在加拿大的龐巴迪公司（Bombardier）取得了一項為期20年的合作協議。
  • agreement n. 協議、合約
    ADIC said the two companies held a ceremony in Taipei Monday to exchange signed copies of the agreement, formally launching the 20-year partnership under which the Taiwanese company will supply cockpit and forward fuselage structural components for Bombardier's super-midsize Challenger 3500 business jets.
    漢翔表示，兩家公司週一在台北舉行了儀式，互換已簽署的協議文本，正式啟動這項為期20年的合作。根據該協議，這家台灣公司將為龐巴迪的超中型「挑戰者3500」（Challenger 3500）商務噴射機供應駕駛艙及前機身結構組件。
  • supply v. 供應、提供
    ADIC said the two companies held a ceremony in Taipei Monday to exchange signed copies of the agreement, formally launching the 20-year partnership under which the Taiwanese company will supply cockpit and forward fuselage structural components for Bombardier's super-midsize Challenger 3500 business jets.
    漢翔表示，兩家公司週一在台北舉行了儀式，互換已簽署的協議文本，正式啟動這項為期20年的合作。根據該協議，這家台灣公司將為龐巴迪的超中型「挑戰者3500」（Challenger 3500）商務噴射機供應駕駛艙及前機身結構組件。
  • supplier n. 供應商
    According to ADIC, the agreement represents a new milestone in cooperation between the two companies, which dates back to 1999, with the Taiwanese supplier having provided rear fuselage structural components for more than 1,000 Challenger 3500 aircraft.
    據漢翔指出，這項協議代表兩家公司合作的一個新里程碑。雙方的合作可追溯至1999年，當時這位台灣供應商已為超過1,000架「挑戰者3500」飛機供應後機身結構組件。
  • capabilities n. 能力、才能（常用複數）
    Bombardier completed a review of the Taiwanese supplier's production capabilities in April, according to ADIC.
    據漢翔稱，龐巴迪已於4月完成對這家台灣供應商生產能力的審查。
  • clients n. 客戶、委託人
    Minister of Economic Affairs Kung Ming-hsin (龔明鑫), who witnessed the exchange, said ADIC's supply of fuselage structural components to international clients, including Bombardier, demonstrated international confidence in "Made in Taiwan" products.
    經濟部長龔明鑫見證了這項互換儀式，他表示漢翔向包括龐巴迪在內的國際客戶供應機身結構組件，展現了國際社會對「台灣製造」產品的信心。
  • expand v. 擴大、擴展
    Kung said ADIC has demonstrated its ability to produce fuselage structural components, and that by expanding its technological capabilities to include forward fuselage structures and securing the long-term agreement with Bombardier, Taiwan has become one of the few countries capable of supplying fuselage structural components for large and medium-sized aircraft.
    龔明鑫表示，漢翔已展現其生產機身結構組件的能力，且透過將技術能力擴展至前機身結構並與龐巴迪取得長期協議，台灣已成為少數幾個能夠為大型及中型飛機供應機身結構組件的國家之一。
  • resilient adj. 具彈性的、有韌性的、恢復力強的
    Kung added that amid restructuring of the global aerospace supply chain, many international aircraft makers are seeking partners, and ADIC has demonstrated its capabilities as a resilient supplier.
    龔明鑫補充說，在全球航太供應鏈重組之際，許多國際飛機製造商都在尋找合作夥伴，而漢翔已展現其作為一個具韌性供應商的能力。
  • fulfill v. 履行、執行（訂單等）、實現
    He said that given the substantial value of the Bombardier orders, ADIC is expected to enlist other Taiwanese suppliers to help fulfill the orders, adding that the agreement is expected to benefit Taiwan's entire aerospace supply chain.
    他表示，鑑於龐巴迪訂單的龐大價值，預計漢翔將會召集其他台灣供應商來協助履行訂單，並補充說該協議預期將造福台灣整個航太供應鏈。
  • deliver v. 交付、遞送
    ADIC added that it expects to deliver structural components for at least 500 Challenger 3500 fuselages to Bombardier over the 20-year term of the agreement.
    漢翔並補充說，預期在該協議為期20年的期間內，將向龐巴迪交付至少500架「挑戰者3500」機身的結構組件。
  • reflect v. 反映、顯示
    Filion said the latest agreement with ADIC reflects the long-standing and stable relationship between the two companies, adding that Bombardier will continue to cooperate with qualified suppliers worldwide to meet customer expectations for quality, performance and reliability.
    費利翁表示，與漢翔的最新協議反映了兩家公司長期且穩固的關係，並補充說龐巴迪將繼續與全球合格的供應商合作，以滿足客戶對品質、性能和可靠性的期望。
  • expectations n. 期望、期待（常用複數）
    Filion said the latest agreement with ADIC reflects the long-standing and stable relationship between the two companies, adding that Bombardier will continue to cooperate with qualified suppliers worldwide to meet customer expectations for quality, performance and reliability.

文法重點

  • 非限定關係代間子句（帶逗號的 which）
    According to ADIC, the agreement represents a new milestone in cooperation between the two companies, which dates back to 1999, with the Taiwanese supplier having provided rear fuselage structural components for more than 1,000 Challenger 3500 aircraft.
    句中的「which dates back to 1999」前方有逗號，屬於非限定用法，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「the two companies」（或整個合作關係），對句意起補充而非限定範圍的作用。
  • 介系詞加關代（under which）
    ADIC said the two companies held a ceremony in Taipei Monday to exchange signed copies of the agreement, formally launching the 20-year partnership under which the Taiwanese company will supply cockpit and forward fuselage structural components for Bombardier's super-midsize Challenger 3500 business jets.
    「under which」引導形容詞子句，修飾前面的「20-year partnership」。因為是在該「協議／合夥關係之下（under the partnership）」供應零件，介系詞 under 被移到關係代間詞 which 的前方，這是正式商業英文中常見的嚴謹句構。
  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況（with + 名詞 + 分詞）
    According to ADIC, the agreement represents a new milestone in cooperation between the two companies, which dates back to 1999, with the Taiwanese supplier having provided rear fuselage structural components for more than 1,000 Challenger 3500 aircraft.
    「with the Taiwanese supplier having provided...」是「with + O. + 分詞」的複合結構，用來表達伴隨發生的狀況或背景。此處用現在分詞完成式（having provided），強調該動作在主要子句動作發生之前就已經持續進行。
  • 被動語態的應用
    ADIC said that the forward fuselage structural components for the Challenger 3500 require a high level of technical expertise. Bombardier completed a review of the Taiwanese supplier's production capabilities in April, according to ADIC.
    在新聞報導中，為了客觀陳述事實或強調承受動作的對象，經常使用被動語態（如新聞標題中的 secures 與內文中的 are seeking, is expected 等，或此處提及審查被完成）。新聞體例常藉由被動語態讓焦點放在事件本身而非施動者。
