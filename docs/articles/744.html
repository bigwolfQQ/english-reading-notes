文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作後位修飾
    A Taiwan compatriot permit is a document issued by China to Taiwanese nationals because Beijing does not recognize Taiwan's passport and other identity documents for travel to China.
    句中的「issued by China to Taiwanese nationals」是由過去分詞片語構成的後位修飾語，用來修飾前面的名詞「a document」。這相當於形容詞子句「which is issued by China to Taiwanese nationals」的簡化版，省略了主格關代與be動詞，使句子更為精煉。
  • 關係代名詞子句（in which）
    ...APEC is an important multilateral economic forum in which Taiwan is a formal member -- under the name "Chinese Taipei."
    句中的「in which」引導一個非限定（或限定）的關係子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「forum（論壇）」。介系詞「in」原本放在 member 的後面（Taiwan is a formal member in the forum），為了符合正式英文寫作習慣，將介系詞移至關係代名詞 which 的前方。
  • be scheduled to + 原形動詞（預定做某事）
    Hung is scheduled to travel to Nanjing to attend the APEC Human Resources Development Ministerial Meeting on Sept. 21...
    「be scheduled to」是一個在新聞與商業書信中非常實用的句型，用來表達「預定於何時、何地進行某項行程或計畫」，後面需接原形動詞。
