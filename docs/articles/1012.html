宏都拉斯國會議員來台參加培訓課程：外交部

Honduran lawmaker visits Taiwan for training course: MOFA

單字解析

  • organize v. 組織、籌辦
    a training course organized by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA)
    由外交部舉辦的培訓課程
  • in charge of phr. 負責、主管
    a ministry official in charge of the program said yesterday
    一名負責該計畫的官員昨天表示
  • head n. 主管、負責人、首長
    Department of Latin American and Caribbean Affairs head Jose Han
    拉丁美洲及加勒比海司司長韓志正
  • annual adj. 年度的、每年的
    The annual training course, which has been held for decades...
    這項已舉辦數十年的年度培訓課程...
  • intend v. 意欲、旨在、打算
    is intended to provide professional training for leaders
    旨在為領導人提供專業培訓
  • sever v. 斷絕（關係等）
    since the Central American country severed diplomatic ties with Taipei
    自該中美洲國家與台北斷絕外交關係以來
  • pledge v. 承諾、保證
    had pledged during his election campaign to restore ties with Taipei
    曾在競選期間承諾恢復與台北的關係
  • press statement n. phr. 新聞稿
    A MOFA press statement released on Wednesday last week said...
    外交部上週三發布的新聞稿指出...
  • maintain v. 維持、保持
    Taiwan’s government would maintain a “pragmatic and open approach”
    台灣政府將保持「務實且開放的做法」
  • prospect n. 前景、前途、可能性
    toward the prospect of future relations
    對未來關係的前景
  • representative n. 代表
    Representatives from 20 countries in Latin America attended the course
    來自拉丁美洲20個國家的代表參加了該課程

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作為後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    A Honduran lawmaker who made a rare visit to Taiwan earlier this month was in the country to attend a training course organized by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA)...
    過去分詞片語「organized by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs」用來修飾其前方的名詞「a training course」，相當於形容詞子句「which was organized by...」的簡化寫法，省略了主格關代與be動詞。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    The annual training course, which has been held for decades, is intended to provide professional training...
    句中的「which has been held for decades」為非限定關係子句，前方有逗號隔開，用來補充說明先行詞「The annual training course」的額外資訊，即便刪除也不影響主要子句的完整性。
  • 現在分詞表附帶結果或狀態 (Present Participle indicating State/Result)
    ...ending 82 years of diplomatic relations.
    句尾的「ending 82 years...」是由對等子句或結果子句簡化而來的分詞構句，表示前一個動作所產生的自然結果（結束了長達82年的外交關係）。
  • 間接問句 (Indirect Question)
    However, Han would not comment on whether Calix’s visit was related to the possibility of re-establishing ties...
    「whether...」引導的子句作為介詞「on」的受詞，形成間接問句。在多益考試中，常考介詞後方接wh-子句或whether引導的名詞子句結構。
