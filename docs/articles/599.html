紐約遊行敦促聯合國納入台灣

March in New York urges Taiwan’s inclusion in UN

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • participation n. 參與，加入
    More than 100 Taiwanese-Americans, Taiwanese lawmakers and members of civic groups on Saturday marched through New York City to call for Taiwan’s participation in the UN, with Marshall Island Permanent Representative to the UN John Silk joining the event to show support.
    本週六，超過100名台裔美國人、台灣立法委員及公民團體成員在紐約市遊行，呼籲聯合國讓台灣參與，馬紹爾群島常駐聯合國常任代表席克（John Silk）也參加了該活動以示支持。
  • representative n. 代表
    More than 100 Taiwanese-Americans, Taiwanese lawmakers and members of civic groups on Saturday marched through New York City to call for Taiwan’s participation in the UN, with Marshall Island Permanent Representative to the UN John Silk joining the event to show support.
    本週六，超過100名台裔美國人、台灣立法委員及公民團體成員在紐約市遊行，呼籲聯合國讓台灣參與，馬紹爾群島常駐聯合國常任代表席克（John Silk）也參加了該活動以示支持。
  • annual adj. 年度的，每年的
    The annual march brought together Taiwanese community groups from across the US East Coast.
    這場年度遊行集結了來自美國東海岸各地的台灣社群團體。
  • delegation n. 代表團
    In addition to the Taiwan United Nations Alliance (TAIUNA), Democratic Progressive Party Legislator Wang Yi-chuan (王義川), Chinese Nationalist Party (KMT) Legislator Yeh Yuan-chih (葉元之) and Taiwan People’s Party Legislator Tsai Chun-chou (蔡春綢) also formed a delegation to visit New York.
    除了台灣聯合國協進會（TAIUNA）外，民主進步黨立法委員王義川、中國國民黨立法委員葉元之以及台灣民眾黨立法委員蔡春綢也組成代表團訪問紐約。
  • absent adj. 缺席的，不在的
    However, the TAIUNA said that only Wang attended the march from start to finish, while Yeh and Tsai were absent.
    不過，台灣聯合國協進會表示，只有王義川全程參與遊行，葉元之與蔡春綢則缺席。
  • opportunity n. 機會，時機
    Wang said the annual march provides an opportunity for Taiwan to make its voice heard near the UN headquarters.
    王義川表示，這場年度遊行提供了一個讓台灣在聯合國總部附近發聲的機會。
  • promote v. 推廣，促進，宣傳
    “This is a very good opportunity to promote Taiwan,” he added.
    他補充說：「這是宣傳台灣的絕佳機會。」
  • ensure v. 確保，保證
    He expressed hope that the campaign would continue to ensure Taiwan’s voice is heard by UN member states and eventually help pave the way for the nation to join the organization under its own name.
    他表達希望這場運動能持續下去，確保聯合國會員國聽到台灣的聲音，並最終協助為該國以自身名義加入該組織鋪平道路。
  • awareness n. 意識，認識
    In addition to Saturday’s march, the government is also conducting promotional activities in the city during the ongoing UN General Assembly session to raise international awareness of its exclusion from the UN system.
    除了周六的遊行外，政府也正在聯合國大會開議期間於該市進行宣傳活動，以提高國際社會對台灣被排除在聯合國體系之外的意識。
  • exclusion n. 排斥，排除
    In addition to Saturday’s march, the government is also conducting promotional activities in the city during the ongoing UN General Assembly session to raise international awareness of its exclusion from the UN system.
    除了周六的遊行外，政府也正在聯合國大會開議期間於該市進行宣傳活動，以提高國際社會對台灣被排除在聯合國體系之外的意識。
  • schedule v. 預定，安排
    The 81st Session of the UN General Assembly opened on Tuesday last week, with the high-level General Debate scheduled for Tuesday next week to Saturday and Sept. 28.
    聯合國大會第81屆會議已於上週二開幕，高層總體辯論預定於下週二至週六以及9月28日舉行。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（附加伴隨狀況 with + 名詞 + 分詞）
    More than 100 Taiwanese-Americans, Taiwanese lawmakers and members of civic groups on Saturday marched through New York City to call for Taiwan’s participation in the UN, with Marshall Island Permanent Representative to the UN John Silk joining the event to show support.
    句型「with + 名詞/代名詞 + 現在分詞(-ing)」用來表示伴隨發生的狀況或背景。在此句中，with 引導補充說明馬紹爾群島常駐聯合國代表 John Silk 同時也參與了這場活動的動作（Silk 與 joining 之間為主動關係，故使用現在分詞 joining）。
  • 現在分詞當形容詞修飾名詞
    In addition to Saturday’s march, the government is also conducting promotional activities in the city during the ongoing UN General Assembly session to raise international awareness of its exclusion from the UN system.
    句中的 ongoing（持續進行中的）是現在分詞轉化而來的形容詞，置於名詞 session 之前，用來修飾並形容「聯合國大會會議」目前正在進行的狀態。
  • 過去分詞當形容詞表達被動狀態
    The 81st Session of the UN General Assembly opened on Tuesday last week, with the high-level General Debate scheduled for Tuesday next week to Saturday and Sept. 28.
    句尾的 scheduled 是一個過去分詞，前面省略了 be 動詞（或是與 with 結合的獨立分詞結構 with... scheduled），用來被動修飾前面的名詞片語 the high-level General Debate（高層總體辯論），表示辯論是被安排在特定時間舉行。
