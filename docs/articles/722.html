台股收低，科技股因人工智慧疑慮下跌

Taiwan shares close lower as tech stocks fall on AI concerns

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • sector n. 部門、產業、類股
    Shares in Taiwan closed lower Tuesday, led by the electronics sector amid worries over AI development...
    台股週二收低，主要是受到電子類股拖累，此前市場對 AI 發展感到擔憂...
  • turnover n. 營業額、成交量
    Turnover totaled NT$603.45 billion (US$18.99 billion).
    成交金額總計新台幣 6,034.5 億元（約合 189.9 億美元）。
  • plunge v. 暴跌、急速下降
    ...and selling continued today after AI-related stocks in the United States plunged overnight...
    ...且在美國 AI 相關股票隔夜暴跌之後，今天賣壓持續...
  • tumble n. / v. 暴跌、下跌
    ...referring to a 5.86 percent tumble in the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index.
    他指的是費城半導體指數大跌 5.86%。
  • resilient adj. 有彈性的、具恢復力的、表現強韌的
    So TSMC, one of the major AI stocks, remained resilient, keeping the Taiex from falling further.
    因此，作為主要 AI 股票之一的台積電表現具韌性，使台股大盤未進一步下跌。
  • contract chipmaker n.phr. 晶圓代工廠
    Despite the electronics index falling 0.65 percent, contract chipmaker Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. (TSMC)...
    儘管電子類股指數下跌 0.65%，但晶圓代工廠台灣積體電路製造股份有限公司（台積電）...
  • supplier n. 供應商
    ...and memory chip supplier Nanya Technology Corp. ended down 1.59 percent at NT$465.50.
    ...記憶體晶片供應商南亞科技收盤下跌 1.59%，報新台幣 465.50 元。
  • gain n. 獲利、漲幅
    Tsai said the financial sector reversed Monday's 1.94 percent gain, falling 1.10 percent Tuesday...
    蔡明翰表示，金融類股吐回了週一 1.94% 的漲幅，週二下跌 1.10%...
  • subsidiary n. 子公司
    Reflecting the decline in shares of its subsidiary Nanya Technology, Nan Ya Plastics Corp. shed 5.34 percent...
    反映其子公司南亞科技股價下跌，南亞塑膠工業股份有限公司下跌 5.34%...
  • affiliate n. 附屬機構、關係企業
    ...while affiliate Formosa Plastics Corp. ended down 1.43 percent at NT$62.00.
    ...而關係企業台灣化學纖維（台塑）收盤下跌 1.43%，報新台幣 62.00 元。
  • price in v.phr. 將...反映在價格中、預先計入（多益常用於市場與定價情境）
    "I think the impact of AI concerns has largely been priced in," Tsai said.
    蔡明翰表示：「我認為 AI 疑慮的衝擊已大致被市場消化。」
  • institutional investor n.phr. 機構投資人（如外資、投信等）
    According to the Taiwan Stock Exchange, foreign institutional investors sold a net NT$62.70 billion worth of shares...
    根據台灣證券交易所的數據，外國機構投資人淨賣超總值新台幣 627.0 億元的股票...

文法重點

  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況或結果 (Present Participle / Past Participle)
    After falling 0.70 percent Monday, the Taiex, the Taiwan Stock Exchange's benchmark index, ended down 351.03 points...
    句首的 "After falling..." 為分詞構句（或介系詞動名詞片語），省略了主詞並簡化子句，用來接續說明台股大盤在週一跌幅之後的週二表現。
  • 分詞作為形容詞修飾名詞 (Participles as Adjectives)
    ...led by the electronics sector amid worries over AI development after the heads of three U.S. AI giants... warned of risks and called for a slowdown in development...
    句中的 "leading" 或過去分詞 "worried" 的同源結構，此處 "AI-related stocks" 或 "contract chipmaker" 等用法，展現了分詞或名詞修飾名詞（Noun adjunct）在財經新聞中高效率傳遞訊息的句構特點。
  • 獨立分詞構句表伴隨狀況 (Absolute Participial Construction)
    The Fed is scheduled to begin a two-day meeting later Tuesday.
    此句使用被動語態 "is scheduled to begin"，說明聯準會會議的預定時程。多益考試極常考 be scheduled to + 動詞原形 的結構。
  • 現在分詞短語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Yageo Corp., the world's third-largest multilayer ceramic capacitor supplier, closed down 1.47 percent at NT$537.00, while power management solutions provider Delta Electronics Inc. rose 3.27 percent to end at NT$1,670.00.
    逗號後的 "the world's third-largest... supplier" 是同位語，用來補充說明主詞 Yageo Corp. 的身分與產業地位，這在商業與新聞英語中是非常經典且高頻的句型。
