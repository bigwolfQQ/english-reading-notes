央行在即將到來的會議上面臨升息壓力：經濟學家

Central bank under pressure to raise rates at upcoming meeting: Economists

單字解析

  • consecutive adj. 連續的，不間斷的
    With domestic inflation topping the 2 percent alert level for a fourth consecutive month in August...
    隨著國內通膨率在8月份連續第四個月突破2%的警戒線...
  • hike n. / v. （價格、利率等）大幅調高，提高
    The Bank of Korea hiked its benchmark interest rates from 2.50 percent to 3.00 percent...
    南韓央行將基準利率從2.50%調高至3.00%...
  • forecast v. / n. 預測，預報
    ...the central bank had forecast in June that Taiwan's consumer price index (CPI) would rise by less than 2 percent in 2026.
    ...央行在6月時曾預測台灣2026年消費者物價指數（CPI）將年增不到2%。
  • take into account phr. 把……納入考量，考慮到
    In addition, Lin said the central bank needs to take into account the monetary policy decisions of other major central banks...
    此外，林啟超表示，央行在決定是否調整利率時，需要將其他主要央行的貨幣政策決策納入考量...
  • benchmark n. 基準，水準點
    The Bank of Korea hiked its benchmark interest rates from 2.50 percent to 3.00 percent...
    南韓央行將基準利率從2.50%調高至3.00%...
  • basis point n. 基點（等於0.01個百分點）
    The European Central Bank raised its key interest rates by 25 basis points on Thursday...
    歐洲央行週四將關鍵利率調高25基點...
  • absorb v. 承受，吸收（衝擊、成本等）
    ...the local economy is expected to be able to absorb the impact of a rate hike, he added.
    ...預期本地經濟能夠承受升息的衝擊，他補充道。
  • follow suit phr. 照著做，跟進
    ...which could serve as an indication for Taiwan's central bank to follow suit.
    ...這可能成為台灣央行跟進的指標。
  • spread n. （金融市場中的）差價，利差
    Sun said a wider interest rate spread between Taiwan and the United States would be an important factor...
    孫明德指出，台美之間擴大的利差將是一個重要因素...
  • curb v. 控制，抑制，約束
    Sun said a central bank rate hike would not be primarily aimed at curbing inflation...
    孫明德表示，央行升息的主要目的將不是壓抑通膨...
  • burden n. 負擔，重負
    ...meaning higher interest rates could place a heavier financial burden on traditional industries, he said.
    ...這意味著較高的利率可能會對傳統產業造成更沉重的財務負擔，他表示。

文法重點

  • With + 名詞 + 分詞（複合結構 / With-structure）
    With domestic inflation topping the 2 percent alert level for a fourth consecutive month in August...
    「With + O. + V-ing/P.P.」是多益閱讀與進階英文常見的附帶狀況句構，用來補充說明背景或原因。在此句中，「domestic inflation」是受詞，而「topping」是現在分詞，表示主動的持續上升。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Taiwan's strong economic growth is largely driven by AI development...
    被動語態結構為「be + 過去分詞 (P.P.)」，強調承受動作的對象。句中「Taiwan's strong economic growth」是主詞，接受由「AI development」所驅動的動作。
  • 對比並列句構 (while 引導對比子句)
    Taiwan's strong economic growth is largely driven by AI development, while the traditional economy has lagged behind...
    連接詞「while」在此處可用於連接兩個相對的情況，表達「然而、而」的對比語氣，凸顯 AI 產業與傳統經濟表現的落差。
  • 現在分詞作補充說明 / 結果狀語
    ...meaning higher interest rates could place a heavier financial burden on traditional industries, he said.
    句尾的「meaning...」是由分詞片語引導的結果狀語，相當於「which means...」，用來補充前面子句所引發的後果或意義。
