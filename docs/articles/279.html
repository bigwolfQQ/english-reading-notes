台北匯市：美元走貶

U.S. dollar down in Taipei trading

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • trade v. 交易
    The U.S. dollar was traded at NT$31.476 at 10 a.m. Tuesday on the Taipei Foreign Exchange...
    台北外匯市場週二上午10點美元兌新台幣匯率為31.476元...
  • foreign exchange n. 外匯
    ...at 10 a.m. Tuesday on the Taipei Foreign Exchange, down NT$0.076 from the previous close.
    ...較前一交易日收盤價下跌0.076元。
  • down adj. / adv. 下降的、下跌
    The U.S. dollar was traded... down NT$0.076 from the previous close.
    美元交易價格...較前一交易日收盤價下跌0.076元。
  • previous adj. 先前的、以前的
    ...down NT$0.076 from the previous close.
    ...較前一交易日收盤價下跌0.076元。
  • close n. 收盤、收盤價
    ...down NT$0.076 from the previous close.
    ...較前一交易日收盤價下跌0.076元。

文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The U.S. dollar was traded at NT$31.476 at 10 a.m. Tuesday on the Taipei Foreign Exchange...
    用來強調動作的承受者（美元）。結構為「主詞 + be 動詞 + 過去分詞 (ed)」，在此句中表示美元「被交易」在特定價位。
  • 分詞片語作補充說明 (Participial Phrase)
    ...at 10 a.m. Tuesday on the Taipei Foreign Exchange, down NT$0.076 from the previous close.
    句尾的「down NT$0.076 from the previous close」省略了主詞與 be 動詞（原本可能是 which was down...），直接用形容詞片語補充說明前面匯率變動的狀態。
  • 特定時間與地點的介詞搭配 (Prepositions of Time and Place)
    Taipei, Sept. 8 (CNA) The U.S. dollar was traded at NT$31.476 at 10 a.m. Tuesday on the Taipei Foreign Exchange...
    英文中表達日期、具體時間與地點時，介詞的使用有嚴格區分：「Sept. 8」用在日期，「at 10 a.m.」用在精確時間點，「on the Taipei Foreign Exchange」用在特定的市場或平台名稱前。
