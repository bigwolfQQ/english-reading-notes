Burmese leader seeks legitimacy in Malaysia visit

單字解析

  • repatriate v. 遣返（國民或難民）
    The two-day visit comes as Malaysia pushes ahead with a controversial move to repatriate thousands of Myanmar nationals...
    這次為期兩天的訪問之際，正值馬來西亞積極推進一項備受爭議的舉措，即遣返數千名緬甸國民...
  • sanction n. 制裁
    The military engineered a widely criticized election that allowed Min Aung Hlaing, who is subject to a raft of Western sanctions, to take up a civilian role...
    軍方策劃了一場廣受批評的選舉，讓受到西方一系列制裁的敏昂萊得以擔任文職角色...
  • implement v. 實施、落實（計畫、協定等）
    ...over their failure to implement a peace road map brokered by the group...
    ...是因為他們未能落實該組織斡旋的和平路線圖...
  • cooperation n. 合作
    “Under pressure to send refugees back, Anwar needs Naypyitaw’s cooperation, and a visit is the price,”...
    「在遣返難民的壓力下，安華需要奈比多的合作，而訪問就是代價，」
  • recognition n. 承認、認可
    “He says this is not recognition, but Min Aung Hlaing will present it as exactly that.”
    「他說這不是承認，但敏昂萊會把它包裝成正是如此。」
  • engage v. 接觸、參與、互動
    ...saying that the Migrant returns are the “only reason” Malaysia wants to engage with Myanmar.
    ...並稱移工遣返是馬來西亞想要與緬甸接觸的「唯一原因」。
  • deport v. 驅逐出境
    Malaysia has deported about 10,000 Myanmar nationals since January...
    馬來西亞自1月以來已驅逐了約10萬名緬甸國民...
  • assurance n. 保證、承諾
    ...according to Anwar, who said Burmese authorities had given assurances those returning would not be harmed.
    ...根據安華說法，並表示緬甸當局已保證返回者不會受到傷害。
  • halt v. 停止、暫停
    Rights groups have also urged Malaysia to halt the returns, citing the ongoing violence and risk of forced conscription.
    人權團體也敦促馬來西亞停止遣返行動，並引用持續的暴力情勢以及強行徵兵的風險。
  • injury n. 受傷、傷害（文章中用動詞 injuring）
    ...killing at least 58 people and injuring dozens of others, including at a busy market.
    ...造成至少58人死亡，數十人受傷，其中包括在一個繁忙的市場。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...a nationwide civil war that has killed an estimated 100,000 people and displaced more than 3.5 million.
    句中的「that has killed... and displaced...」是關係代間詞子句，用來修飾前面的名詞 a nationwide civil war。而子句內部的過去分詞 estimated 則用來修飾 100,000 people（估計有十萬人），展現精煉且具專業新聞報導感的句構。
  • 非限定關係子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    ...according to Anwar, who said Burmese authorities had given assurances those returning would not be harmed.
    在人名或專有名詞後面加上逗號，並接上「who」引導的非限定關係子句，用來補充說明該人物的言論或進一步資訊。這在多益閱讀與商業英文中非常常見，用於附加補充細節。
  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況或結果 (Participial Phrase indicating Result/Condition)
    ...killing at least 58 people and injuring dozens of others, including at a busy market.
    句尾的「killing... and injuring...」是由對等連接詞連接的分詞構句，省略了主詞並將動詞改為現在分詞，用來表達前面 airstrikes（空襲）所造成的直接結果（死傷人數），使句子緊湊而不冗長。
  • 形容詞子句省略關係代間詞 (Omitted Relative Pronoun)
    ...burmese authorities had given assurances those returning would not be harmed.
    在 assurances 後方省略了引導名詞子句的連接詞 that（即 assurances [that] those returning would not be harmed），用來補充說明保證的內容。這種省略結構在商務郵件與新聞報導中極為普遍，能加快閱讀節奏。
