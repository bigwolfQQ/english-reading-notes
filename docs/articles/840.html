台股收高，但成交量仍然偏低

Taiwan shares close higher, but turnover still low

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • turnover n. 營業額、成交量
    Taiwanese shares closed higher yesterday, but turnover remained low as investors stayed cautious...
    台灣股市昨天收盤走高，但由於投資人保持謹慎，成交量仍然維持低檔...
  • cautious adj. 謹慎的、小心的
    ...as investors stayed cautious ahead of a US Federal Reserve policy decision due later in the day...
    ...由於投資人在美國聯準會當天稍晚將公布政策決策前保持謹慎...
  • rate hike n. phr. 升息、利率調升
    ...with high expectations of a rate hike.
    ...且市場對升息有很高的預期。
  • ex-dividend adj. 除息的
    The stock, which went ex-dividend yesterday, returned to its pre-ex-dividend price within 27 minutes of the market opening.
    該檔股票昨天進行除息，在開盤後 27 分鐘內即回到除息前的價格。
  • recovery n. 復甦、恢復
    The quick recovery indicated investor confidence in the outlook for TSMC...
    快速的復甦顯示出投資人對台積電前景的信心...
  • beneficiary n. 受惠者、受益人
    ...a major beneficiary of the artificial intelligence (AI) boom...
    ...身為人工智慧（AI）浪潮的主要受惠者...
  • optimism n. 樂觀、樂觀情緒
    The optimism over AI-related demand also supported shares of memory suppliers...
    對 AI 相關需求的樂觀情緒也支撐了記憶體供應商的股價...
  • analyst n. 分析師
    “Today’s gains were technical in nature as the TAIEX rebounded from losses in the past few sessions,” Hua Nan Securities Co analyst Kevin Su said.
    華南永昌證券分析師蘇俊宏表示：「今天的漲幅本質上屬於技術性反彈，因為加權指數從過去幾個交易日的跌勢中彈升。」
  • projection n. 預估、預測
    ...and the Fed’s dot plot, which shows policymakers’ projections for interest rates.
    ...以及顯示政策制定者對利率預測的點陣圖。
  • elevated adj. 偏高的、升高的
    There is widespread expectation that the Fed would hike rates for the first time in three years as inflation remains elevated in the US...
    由於美國通膨持續居高不下，市場普遍預期聯準會將進行三年來的首次升息...
  • boost v. 促進、提高、增強
    “Today, financial stocks moved higher on hopes that a rate hike by the Fed will boost their overseas interest income,” Su said.
    蘇俊宏表示：「今天金融股走高，是基於預期聯準會升息將能提高其海外利息收入。」

文法重點

  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況 (with + 名詞 + 分詞)
    ...as investors stayed cautious ahead of a US Federal Reserve policy decision due later in the day, with high expectations of a rate hike.
    「with + 名詞 + 分詞/形容詞/介系詞片語」結構常用於表示附帶狀況或伴隨發生的情形，在此句中用來補充說明投資人在等待決策時，伴隨著「對升息抱持高預期」的心理狀態。
  • 非限定關代子句補充說明
    The stock, which went ex-dividend yesterday, returned to its pre-ex-dividend price within 27 minutes of the market opening.
    句中的 「which went ex-dividend yesterday」 帶有逗號，屬於非限定關係代名詞子句，用來補充說明前面主詞 The stock（該檔股票）的額外資訊（昨天進行除息），拿掉後不影響主要句意。
  • 形容詞子句修飾先行詞
    ...and the Fed’s dot plot, which shows policymakers’ projections for interest rates.
    這裡的 「which shows policymakers’ projections for interest rates」 是形容詞子句，用來修飾並解釋前面的先行詞 「the Fed’s dot plot」（聯準會的點陣圖），說明該點陣圖具體呈現了什麼內容。
