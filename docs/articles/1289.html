氣象署周一對台灣部分地區發布大雨特報

Heavy rain alert issued Monday for parts of Taiwan

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • issue v. 發布、發行
    The Central Weather Administration (CWA) issued a heavy rain advisory for northern and eastern areas of Taiwan on Monday...
    中央氣象署（CWA）於周一對台灣北部與東部地區發布大雨特報...
  • advisory n. 特報、警報、公告
    The Central Weather Administration (CWA) issued a heavy rain advisory for northern and eastern areas of Taiwan on Monday...
    中央氣象署（CWA）於周一對台灣北部與東部地區發布大雨特報...
  • approach v. 接近、靠近
    ...saying an approaching seasonal northeasterly wind system will bring downpours and cooler temperatures.
    ...表示逼近的季節性東北風系統將帶來陣雨與較涼爽的氣溫。
  • cover v. 涵蓋、覆蓋
    The heavy rain alert covers Keelung and other north coast areas, Taipei and New Taipei, and the eastern county of Hualien...
    大雨警戒範圍涵蓋基隆及其他北海岸地區、台北與新北，以及東部的花蓮縣...
  • exceed v. 超過、超出
    ...warning of accumulated rainfall exceeding 200 millimeters in 24 hours or 100 mm within three hours.
    ...並警告累積雨量可能在24小時內超過200毫米，或在3小時內超過100毫米。
  • remain in effect phr. 持續有效、生效
    The heavy rain advisory is expected to remain in effect through Monday night...
    大雨特報預計將持續有效至周一晚間...
  • forecast v. / n. 預報、預測
    Nighttime lows in northern and eastern Taiwan are forecast to drop to around 22 degrees, according to the CWA.
    根據氣象署資料，台灣北部與東部的夜間低溫預計將降至約22度。
  • persist v. 持續、堅持
    If the seasonal winds persist, nighttime temperatures in northern and eastern Taiwan may drop to 21-22 degrees Tuesday and Wednesday...
    如果季節性東北風持續，台灣北部與東部的夜間氣溫在周二與周三可能會降至21至22度...
  • affect v. 影響
    Meanwhile, central and southern Taiwan are less likely to be affected by the seasonal winds...
    同時，中南部地區較不受季節性東北風影響...
  • weaken v. 減弱
    The seasonal wind system will weaken on Thursday, but another one is expected to arrive Friday...
    季節性風力系統將於周四減弱，但預計另一個系統將於周五抵達...
  • stable adj. 穩定的
    That means relatively stable weather conditions can be expected over the upcoming National Day weekend...
    這意味著在即將到來的國慶日連假期間，可以預期相對穩定的大氣狀況...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作形容詞（修飾名詞）
    ...saying an approaching seasonal northeasterly wind system will bring downpours and cooler temperatures.
    句中的「approaching」是由動詞 approach 變化而來的現在分詞，在此置於名詞 system 前方，用來修飾「正在接近的」季節性東北風系統，具備主動與進行的意味。
  • 被動語態的應用
    Nighttime lows in northern and eastern Taiwan are forecast to drop to around 22 degrees, according to the CWA.
    句中的「are forecast」是現在式被動語態（be + 過去分詞），用來表達氣象署對未來低溫的預測。在新聞英文中，氣溫、航班或活動安排經常使用被動語態來客觀陳述預期發生的事。
  • Conditional Sentence Type 1（第一類條件句）
    If the seasonal winds persist, nighttime temperatures in northern and eastern Taiwan may drop to 21-22 degrees Tuesday and Wednesday, but the rain will ease on Tuesday, the CWA said.
    此句使用「If + 現在式動詞（persist）, 主詞 + 조동詞（may/will）+ 原形動詞」的結構，用來表達對未來可能發生之條件的合理預測與其結果。
  • 分詞構句（表原因或附帶說明）
    ...temperatures in low-lying areas of northern and eastern Taiwan may fall to lows of around 16 degrees Wednesday through Friday due to possible radiative cooling -- the cooling of the ground under clear skies, light winds and dry conditions.
    破折號後的「the cooling of the ground...」為名詞片語的同位語，用來補充說明前面的專有名詞 radiative cooling（輻射冷卻），幫助讀者理解其科學定義。
