社論：臺灣政壇中的陸配

EDITORIAL: Chinese spouses in Taiwan’s politics

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • warrant v. 使有必要，正當化
    The growing number of Chinese spouses participating in Taiwanese politics should not, in itself, be cause for alarm, but does warrant scrutiny of candidates...
    越來越多中國配偶參與臺灣政治，本身不應成為引起恐慌的理由，但確實有必要對候選人進行審查……
  • scrutiny n. 詳細審查，仔細檢查
    ...but does warrant scrutiny of candidates, their political networks and their sources of support.
    ……但確實有必要對候選人及其政治網路和支持來源進行審查。
  • coercion n. 強制，高壓，脅迫
    ...provided that candidates and voters are participating free from coercion, concealed foreign funding and other forms of interference.
    ——前提是候選人和選民的參與不受脅迫、隱匿的境外資金及其他形式干預的影響。
  • prominent adj. 顯著的，突出的，重要的
    The issue has become more prominent ahead of the Nov. 28 local elections.
    隨著 11 月 28 日地方選舉的臨近，這個議題變得更加顯著。
  • substantial adj. 重大的，大量的，實質的
    Taiwan places substantial restrictions on the political participation of people from China.
    臺灣對來自中國的人士之政治參與施加了實質限制。
  • integration n. 融合，整合
    Residency and integration do not by themselves establish where a person’s loyalties lie...
    居住和融合本身並不能確立一個人的忠誠度在哪裡……
  • entitled adj. 有權利的，有資格的
    A Chinese-born candidate is entitled to appeal to those views, provided they meet all legal requirements.
    出生於中國的候選人有權訴求這些觀點，前提是他們符合所有法律要求。
  • convict v. 宣判有罪，定罪
    Last month, the New Taipei District Court convicted Chinese-born spouse Xu Chunying (徐春鶯) of, among other offenses...
    上個月，新北市地方法院裁定，出生於中國的配偶徐春鶯因……等罪名成立
  • substitute v. 代替，替代
    ...that assumptions about political integration should not substitute for evidence about a person’s political activities and relationships.
    ……而是對政治融合的假設不應取代關於一個人政治活動和關係的證據。
  • rigorous adj. 嚴格的，嚴密的
    Taiwan should apply rigorous transparency and enforcement rules to campaign financing and political relationships.
    臺灣應對競選資金和政治關係適用嚴格的透明度和執行規則。
  • transparent adj. 透明的，易懂的
    ...and their ties to China should be transparent enough for voters and election authorities to assess.
    ……他們與中國的聯繫應該足夠透明，供選民和選舉機關評估。
  • compliance n. 遵守，依從（法規、標準）
    ...that becomes a matter of election integrity and compliance with laws.
    這就成了選舉廉潔度以及是否遵守法律的問題。
  • susceptible adj. 易受影響的，敏感的
    ...nor would it be reasonable to assume that Chinese spouses are uniquely susceptible to political influence because of their origins.
    ……認為中國配偶因其出身而獨特地容易受到政治影響也是不合理的。

文法重點

  • Provided that / Provided 引導的條件句
    ...provided that candidates and voters are participating free from coercion, concealed foreign funding and other forms of interference.
    “provided that” 或 “provided” 在此當作連接詞，意為「假設、如果、以……為條件」，等於 “if” 或 “on condition that”，常用於法律、正式文件或新聞評論中表達先決條件。
  • 否定主詞開頭的倒裝句（部分倒裝）
    That 26 such people have registered as candidates does not demonstrate coordination or outside influence, nor would it be reasonable to assume that Chinese spouses are uniquely susceptible to political influence...
    句中的 “nor would it be reasonable...” 使用了部分倒裝。當句首出現否定對等連接詞 “nor” 或 “neither” 時，後方接否定或遞進的子句必須將助動詞（在此為 would）提到主詞（it）之前。
  • 間接問句（名詞子句作受詞）
    The question is not where a candidate was born, but whether the Chinese government or organizations are using that person to attempt to influence Taiwan’s electoral process.
    句型中的 “where a candidate was born” 與 “whether the Chinese government... are using...” 都是名詞子句，在句中作主詞補語。間接問句的語序為「疑問詞/whether + 主詞 + 動詞」，不需像直接問句那樣做主動詞倒裝。
