總統府將舉辦國際韌性論壇

Presidential Office to hold international resilience forum

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • representative n. 代表
    with about 500 defense experts, officials and business representatives expected to discuss whole-of-society resilience
    預計有約 500 名國防專家、官員與企業代表參與探討全社會防衛韌性
  • resilience n. 韌性、恢復力
    expected to discuss whole-of-society resilience and to examine Taiwan’s preparedness against threats from China
    預計探討全社會防衛韌性，並檢視台灣對中國威脅的備戰狀態
  • preparedness n. 準備狀態、整備
    to examine Taiwan’s preparedness against threats from China, the office said yesterday.
    總統府昨天表示，檢視台灣對中國威脅的備戰狀態。
  • statement n. 聲明、陳述
    the office said in a statement.
    總統府在聲明中表示。
  • cover v. 涵蓋、報導
    It would cover topics including whole-of-society defense resilience preparedness, civil-military integration...
    它將涵蓋全社會防衛韌性整備、軍民整合等主題...
  • response n. 反應、因應
    response to “gray zone” activities and the protection of critical infrastructure, it said.
    它表示，因應「灰色地帶」活動以及保護關鍵基礎設施。
  • deliver v. 發表（演說）、遞送
    Anduril Industries founder Palmer Luckey is to deliver an opening keynote speech
    安杜里爾工業公司創辦人 Palmer Luckey 將發表開幕主題演說
  • observe v. 觀察、遵守
    share their observations of Taiwan’s efforts to build up whole-of-society defense resilience
    分享他們對台灣建立全社會防衛韌性努力的觀察
  • conclude v. 結束、結語
    The forum would conclude with a discussion between National Security Council Secretary-General Joseph Wu...
    論壇將以國家安全會議秘書長吳釗燮與...之間的討論作為結語
  • promote v. 促進、推廣、晉升
    to promote whole-of-society resilience over the past two years in response to natural disasters
    在過去兩年中推動全社會韌性，以因應自然災害
  • participation n. 參與
    The expected participation of international security experts and officials... sends a “clear message”
    國際安全專家與官員的預期參與...發出了「明確訊息」

文法重點

  • be to + V (表預定計畫、安排)
    The Presidential Office is to hold an international forum on Saturday
    新聞英語中常使用「be to + 原形動詞」結構來表示官方預定要執行的計畫或行程，此處 is to hold 相當於 is scheduled to hold。
  • with + 受詞 + 分詞 (附帶狀況片語)
    with about 500 defense experts, officials and business representatives expected to discuss whole-of-society resilience
    「with + 名詞/代代名詞 + 過去分詞(ed)」用來補充說明主句發生物件時的附帶狀況。句中 expected 為過去分詞，被動修飾前方 500 名專家與代表。
  • 名詞片語作為介系詞的受詞
    to examine Taiwan’s preparedness against threats from China
    介系詞 against 後方接名詞片語 threats from China，用來修飾前方 preparedness（對...的防備、對抗...的威脅），展現多益測驗常見的名詞搭配用法。
