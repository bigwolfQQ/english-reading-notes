網際網路尚未準備好迎接數百萬個愛投機取巧的機器人

The Web is not ready for millions of rule-bending bots

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • agent n. 代理程式，經紀人，代理商
    Earlier this year an Australian tech worker asked his OpenClaw artificial intelligence (AI) agent whether it could get him higher up on the waiting list for a popular gym class.
    今年稍早，一名澳洲科技工作者詢問他的 OpenClaw 人工智慧（AI）代理程式，是否能幫他在某個熱門健身課程的候補名單上排得前面一點。
  • flaw n. 缺點，瑕疵，漏洞
    It did exactly what he wanted, but with an unexpected twist: It found a flaw in the system and canceled the booking of another person on the list.
    它確實做到了他想要的，但帶有一個意料之外的轉折：它發現了系統中的一個漏洞，並取消了名單上另一個人的預約。
  • bargain n. 特價商品，便宜貨，協議
    AI’s next big magic trick is to start doing things for you across the Internet, with new tools like Muse from Meta Platforms Inc and dots from OpenAI working 24/7 to find online bargains, book restaurant tables or check your e-mails for unpaid bills.
    AI 的下一個重大把戲是開始跨越網際網路為你處理事情，利用諸如 Meta Platforms Inc 的 Muse 和 OpenAI 的 dots 等新工具，全天候 24 小時尋找網路特價商品、預訂餐廳座位，或是檢查你的電子郵件以處理未付帳單。
  • exploit v. 利用，剝削，開采
    The Australian tech worker’s AI agent had found a loophole and exploited it, without being told to.
    該名澳洲科技工作者的 AI 代理程式發現了一個漏洞並加以利用，且是在未被指示的情況下這麼做的。
  • outcome n. 結果，效果
    In the best-case scenario, we would see a smooth and well-functioning network of bots that play by the rules to get the best outcomes for their humans.
    在最好的情況下，我們將看到一個順暢且運作良好的機器人網路，它們按規矩行事，為人類爭取最佳結果。
  • activity n. 活動
    Security firm DataDome said that 65 percent of the more than 20,000 Web sites it tested had systems for detecting or blocking AI agents, and that bot activity hitting login pages rose more than eightfold in the first half of this year.
    安全公司 DataDome 表示，在其測試的 20,000多個網站中，有 65% 擁有偵測或封鎖 AI 代理程式的系統，且在今年上半年，造訪登入頁面的機器人活動暴增了八倍以上。
  • request n. 請求，要求
    Muse is kept within an isolated virtual machine — essentially a private computer in Meta’s cloud — while a separate security system controls every request it makes to the Internet, handling payments and making sure a human is consulted before any major task like buying something or sending a message.
    Muse 被限制在一個隔離的虛擬機中——本質上是 Meta 雲端中的一個私人電腦——同時由一個獨立的安全系統控制它對網際網路發出的每個請求，處理付款並確保在執行任何重大任務（如購買物品或發送訊息）之前諮詢人類。
  • underlying adj. 潛在的，根本的
    However, even these safeguards do not fix an underlying problem baked into today’s generative AI models through their training, which is a tendency to look for shortcuts, a phenomenon researchers call reward-hacking.
    然而，即使是這些安全防護也無法修復當今生成式 AI 模型在其訓練過程中內建的根本問題，那就是尋找捷徑的傾向，研究人員稱這種現象為「獎勵駭客行為」（reward-hacking）。
  • optimize v. 優化，完善
    Now multiply that type of behavior by million of agents, as consumers explore using AI to optimize their lives and work.
    現在，當消費者探索利用 AI 來最佳化他們的生活和工作時，將這種行為模式乘以數百萬個代理程式。
  • transaction n. 交易，業務
    When a consumer tech reviewer in Toronto named Matt J. Robb tried using Muse to sell a keyboard on Facebook Marketplace last month, the agent angered the buyer by saying Robb was at home to carry out the transaction, which was not true, according to an account posted on Threads.
    據 Threads 上發布的一則貼文描述，多倫多的一位消費性科技評論員馬特·J·羅布（Matt J. Robb）上個月嘗試使用 Muse 在臉書市場上出售鍵盤時，該代理程式向買家聲稱羅布人在家可以進行交易，這並非事實，因而惹怒了買家。
  • rating n. 評等，評價
    Robb was left with a negative rating from his angry buyer.
    羅布最終收到了憤怒買家的負面評價。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況/結果
    It did exactly what he wanted, but with an unexpected twist: It found a flaw in the system and canceled the booking of another person on the list.
    此處運用冒號後方的獨立子句表達結果，而在文章其他地方（如第7段 handling payments...）則常使用現在分詞作分詞構句，用以表示伴隨發生的動作或結果，是商業英文與新聞寫作中常見的精簡表達方式。
  • 受詞補語（Object Complement）
    When a consumer tech reviewer in Toronto named Matt J. Robb tried using Muse to sell a keyboard on Facebook Marketplace last month, the agent angered the buyer by saying Robb was at home to carry out the transaction...
    句型「angered the buyer by saying...」中，動詞 anger 的受客是 the buyer，而整句結構展現了動詞後接受詞及介係詞片語表達原因。另一個常見結構如「make sth. / sb. + 形容詞」用來表達使某人事物處於某狀態。
  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    Security firm DataDome said that 65 percent of the more than 20,000 Web sites it tested had systems for detecting or blocking AI agents...
    句中「it tested」為省略了關代（that/which）的形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的先行詞「the more than 20,000 Web sites」。這在英文新聞中極為常見，能使句子更緊湊、節奏明快。
  • 被動語態（Passive Voice）
    Muse is kept within an isolated virtual machine — essentially a private computer in Meta’s cloud — while a separate security system controls every request it makes to the Internet...
    句型「is kept」使用了被動語態（be + 過去分詞），強調主詞 Muse 是被「維持/限制」在虛擬機中，焦點在於動作的承受者而非執行者，符合科技與規格說明時常用的客觀語氣。
