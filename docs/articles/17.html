文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The goal was set along with 19 strategies and 59 measures centered around four key pillars about a year earlier than the implementation of national net zero regulations, he said.
    句中的「was set」使用了被動語態（be + 過去分詞），強調「目標」是被設定的，而非它自己去設定，這在商業與新聞英文中非常常見，用來客觀陳述政策、決議或事件。
  • 現在分詞當形容詞/修飾語 (Present Participle as Modifier)
    The power demand is projected to rise by 100 billion kilowatt-hours with the continued expansion of the semiconductor industry, he said.
    句中的「continued」是由動詞 continue 轉變而來的過去分詞（在此作形容詞用，修飾名詞 expansion），表示「持續中的擴張」。分詞修飾語能讓句子在描述商業趨勢時更簡鍊。
  • 關係代間詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    Agriculture is on the front lines against threats from climate change, particularly as most plants and animals cannot adapt to temperature changes by wearing clothes and retreating to air-conditioned buildings like human beings, Chen told the forum, which the Ministry of Agriculture organized to present net zero innovations from domestic agriculture.
    句尾的「which the Ministry of Agriculture organized...」是一個非限定性的關係代間詞子句，用「which」來補充說明前面的先行詞「the forum（論壇）」，並在子句內說明該論壇是由農業部所舉辦的。
  • 假設語氣/與過去事實相反的推測 (Conditional Structure / Past Modal for Hypothetical)
    Although some people question why emissions have not been reduced significantly, the figures could have risen to 7 million tonnes without the carbon-reduction efforts of the past few years, he said.
    句中的「could have risen」搭配「without...（若沒有……）」構成了一種含蓄的假設語氣，用來推論「如果過去幾年沒有做出減碳努力，排放量本來『可能』會上升到 700 萬公噸（但實際上沒有）」。
