單字解析

  • relegate v. 降級、降職、貶抑
    who on Wednesday walked out of the camp when coach Jorge Jesus relegated him to the bench.
    他在週三因總教練豪爾赫·熱蘇斯將他降至替補席而離開了訓練營。
  • secure v. 獲得、確保、取得
    Goncalo Ramos secured victory in the 86th minute
    貢薩洛·拉莫斯在第 86 分鐘為球隊鎖定勝局
  • level v. 使平手、扳平
    after Joao Cancelo’s powerful finish had leveled Kristoffer Ajer’s stunning opener
    在若昂·坎塞洛強勢補射扳平克里斯托弗·阿耶爾的精彩首開紀錄後
  • decision n. 決定、決策
    but that it would have to be the player’s decision.
    但這必須由球員自己決定。
  • resolute adj. 堅決的、堅定的
    Klopp’s Germany failed to break down a resolute Greek side
    克洛普的德國隊未能突破防守堅韌的希臘隊
  • effort n. 努力、嘗試、射門嘗試
    but his effort was disallowed.
    但他的進球被判無效。
  • substitute n. 替補球員、替代品
    The Manchester United forward was a second-half substitute in Eindhoven
    這位曼聯前鋒在恩荷芬擔任下半場替補
  • appearance n. 出賽、露面、登場
    The match also marked Virgil van Dijk’s 100th international appearance.
    這場比賽也標誌著維吉爾·范戴克第 100 次代表國家隊出賽。
  • protest v. 抗議、反對
    after Irish players had worn them in the previous match in Hungary to protest the war in Gaza.
    在愛爾蘭球員先前在匈牙利舉行的比賽中佩戴黑色臂章以抗議加薩戰爭後
  • host v. 主辦、主持
    The Football Association of Ireland had refused to host Israel on Irish soil for security reasons
    愛爾蘭足球協會基於安全考量，拒絕在愛爾蘭本土主辦對陣以色列的比賽
  • complicate v. 使複雜化
    The result complicates Ireland’s qualification task
    這個結果讓愛爾蘭的晉級任務變得複雜
  • concede v. 認輸、讓步、失球（體育中指送給對手罰球或失分）
    but he conceded the penalty when he fouled Denmark midfielder Gustav Isaksen.
    但當他對丹麥中場古斯塔夫·伊薩克森犯規時，送給了對方十二碼球。

文法重點

  • 關代子句（Relative Clause）修飾先行詞
    The Portuguese showed they did not need Cristiano Ronaldo, who on Wednesday walked out of the camp when coach Jorge Jesus relegated him to the bench.
    句中的「who on Wednesday walked out of the camp...」是非限定關代子句，用逗號隔開，用來補充說明前面的專有名詞 Cristiano Ronaldo 的額外狀況。
  • 過去完成式（Past Perfect Tense）表達動作先後順序
    Goncalo Ramos secured victory in the 86th minute after Joao Cancelo’s powerful finish had leveled Kristoffer Ajer’s stunning opener...
    句中的「had leveled」使用過去完成式，用來表示「扳平比分」這個動作發生在主句的過去動作（secured victory）之前。
  • 分詞片語（Participial Phrase）作結果狀語
    However, Peretz stabbed in an equalizer two minutes after half-time, sending in from close range after a shot deflected off Ireland defender Liam Scales and into his path.
    句尾的「sending in...」是現在分詞片語，用來接續前面主句發生的動作之後所產生的自然結果或伴隨狀況。
  • 條件句與假設語氣（Conditional Sentence）
    “If he wants to, he’ll return to the national team... We can talk before the next call-up, but Cris is the one who’s going to decide,” Jesus told reporters.
    使用第一類條件句（If + 現在式, 未成年/未來將會...），表達如果滿足特定條件（如果他想回來），未來就會發生對應的結果（他就會重返國家隊）。
