台灣在英語教育方面落後於馬來西亞與菲律賓

Taiwan trailing Malaysia, Philippines in English education

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • improve v. 改善，增進
    Taiwan should improve its English-language education by more closely aligning with English proficiency indicators...
    台灣應該透過更緊密地與英語能力指標對齊，來改善其英語教育...
  • proficiency n. 熟練，精通，能力
    ...by more closely aligning with English proficiency indicators and learning from systems used in Malaysia and the Philippines...
    ...透過更緊密地與英語能力指標對齊，並借鏡馬來西亞和菲律賓所使用的體制...
  • indicator n. 指標
    ...by more closely aligning with English proficiency indicators and learning from systems used in Malaysia and the Philippines...
    ...透過更緊密地與英語能力指標對齊，並借鏡馬來西亞和菲律賓所使用的體制...
  • publish v. 出版，發布
    ...the National Academy for Educational Research said in a report published earlier this month.
    國家教育研究院在本月初發布的一份報告中表示。
  • policy n. 政策
    She cited research on elementary-school curricula, education policy and textbook materials from Malaysia and the Philippines...
    她引用了自2022年7月至2023年底對馬來西亞和小學課程、教育政策及教科書教材的研究。
  • emphasize v. 強調
    ...with instruction beginning at age six, emphasizing communication skills and real-world proficiency.
    ...教學從六歲開始，強調溝通技巧與真實世界的熟練度。
  • proportion n. 比例，部分
    Textbooks also included 2.5 to four times more vocabulary than Taiwanese textbooks, and a large proportion of vocabulary was above the most basic A1 level...
    教科書所含的字彙量也是台灣教科書的2.5到4倍，且有很大一部分的字彙高於最基礎的 A1 級別...
  • adopt v. 採用，採取
    ...with Malaysia adopting a language policy placing Malay first, followed by English and a third language...
    ...馬來西亞採取的語言政策是將馬來語放在第一位，其次是英語和第三語言...
  • prioritize v. 優先考慮，把...列為優先
    ...while the Philippines’ policy prioritizes native language, then Tagalog, then English, Li said.
    李指出，而菲律賓的政策則是優先考慮母語，然後是塔加洛語，接著才是英語。
  • emphasis n. 強調，重視
    ...while the Philippines places greater emphasis on students’ native language and allows English to be used as a medium of instruction from fourth grade...
    ...而菲律賓則更強調學生的母語，並允許從四年級開始使用英語作為教學媒介...
  • define v. 定義，界定
    First, Taiwan should foster a multilingual learning environment and clearly define the role and function of each language, it said.
    報告指出，首先，台灣應營造多語言的學習環境，並明確定義每種語言的角色與功能。
  • strengthen v. 加強，鞏固
    Third, it should strengthen vocabulary instruction through scientific methods, it added.
    它補充說，第三，應透過科學方法加強字彙教學。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Phrase as Modifier）作後置定語
    ...the National Academy for Educational Research said in a report published earlier this month.
    句中的 "published earlier this month" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "a report"，相當於關代子句 "which was published earlier this month" 的簡化版，省略了主詞與be動詞，使語氣更為精煉。
  • 現在分詞構句（Participial Phrase indicating accompanying circumstance）
    Textbooks also included 2.5 to four times more vocabulary than Taiwanese textbooks, and a large proportion of vocabulary was above the most basic A1 level of the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR), the study found.
    雖然此句為對等子句連接，但在文章第4段中常見 "..., with instruction beginning at age six, emphasizing communication skills..." 的 "with + 名詞 + 分詞" 複合結構，用來補充說明附帶狀況，是多益閱讀測驗極常考的句型。
  • 被動語態（Passive Voice）
    Malaysia allows English to be used as a medium of instruction from preschool, while the Philippines places greater emphasis on students’ native language and allows English to be used as a medium of instruction from fourth grade...
    句中的 "to be used" 採用了被動語態的 infinitive（不定詞）形式，用來表達「英語『被』使用作為教學媒介」，強調動作的承受者而非發動者。
