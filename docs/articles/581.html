渡輪大火罹難者遺體運抵，死亡人數攀升至 35 人

First bodies of ferry fire victims land as toll hits 35

單字解析

  • port n. 港口
    The bodies of passengers killed in a ferry fire off the Philippines arrived at a port near the resort town of Coron yesterday morning
    在菲律賓外海發生的一場渡輪大火中喪生的乘客遺體，於昨天上午運抵科隆度假小鎮附近的一個港口
  • investigator n. 調查人員
    The remains would be turned over to police forensic investigators for “proper disposition and identification”
    遺體將交給警方鑑識人員進行「妥善處置與身分辨認」
  • confirm v. 確認、證實
    confirming that the bodies had been found in the ship’s economy accommodation area.
    並證實遺體是在船上的經濟艙區域被發現的。
  • capacity n. 容量、能力
    the first transfer had been capped at 30 bodies due to the capacity of the patrol vessel transporting them
    第一批轉移作業上限為 30 具遺體，是因為運送他們的巡邏艦容量有限
  • transport v. 運輸、運送
    due to the capacity of the patrol vessel transporting them and that additional cadavers remained on the ferry.
    是因為運送他們的巡邏艦容量有限，且還有其他遺體留在渡輪上。
  • business trip n. phr. 出差
    who had taken the ferry from Manila on a business trip with his brother-in-law.
    他與小叔一起從馬尼拉搭乘這艘渡輪進行商務旅行。
  • manifest n. 載貨清單、旅客名單
    The ferry’s manifest listed 134 crew and passengers, but two had not boarded
    該渡輪的載貨清單列出了 134 名船員和乘客，但有兩人並未登船
  • transportation n. 交通運輸
    Many rely on cheap and poorly regulated boats and ships for transport between the country’s more than 7,000 islands
    許多人依賴廉價且管理不善的船隻在該國 7,000 多個島嶼之間進行交通運輸
  • cooperate v. 合作、配合
    the company that owns the M/V June Aster, Atienza Interisland Ferries Inc, said it was fully cooperating with the investigation.
    擁有「六號星號」的公司 Atienza Interisland Ferries Inc 表示，將全力配合調查。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The bodies of passengers killed in a ferry fire off the Philippines arrived at a port near the resort town of Coron yesterday morning, as the country’s coast guard said the death toll of 35 would grow.
    句中的「killed in a ferry fire...」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 passengers（乘客是被殺害/喪生，故用被動概念的過去分詞）。此結構省略了「who were」，能讓句子更精簡流暢。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Tents were set up to accept 30 bodies brought from the M/V June Aster, which ran aground near the tourist hotspot after a fire erupted on Wednesday evening and tore through the vessel “in seconds.”
    逗號之後的「which ran aground...」是非限定關係子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞 M/V June Aster。非限定子句前通常有逗號，不能用 that 代替，主要功能是提供額外的補充資訊。
  • 對等連接詞 as 引導的時間/原因子句
    The bodies of passengers killed in a ferry fire off the Philippines arrived at a port near the resort town of Coron yesterday morning, as the country’s coast guard said the death toll of 35 would grow.
    句中的「as」在此處連接主句與子句，帶有「隨著……」的意思，用來表達兩個動作或狀況同時發生，常出現在新聞英文中以串連背景發展。
  • 過去完成被動語態 (Past Perfect Passive Voice)
    confirming that the bodies had been found in the ship’s economy accommodation area.
    「had been found」由「had + been + 過去分詞」組成，表示在過去某個時間點（發言當時）之前，遺體被發現的動作已經完成，且遺體是被找到，因此使用被動語態。
