高市早苗在川習會登場前，與川普就中國議題進行了「及時的」會談

Takaichi has ‘timely’ talk with Trump about China ahead of his planned summit with Xi

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • summit n. 高峰會、領袖會議
    Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi yesterday said she had a “timely” discussion about China with US President Donald Trump ahead of his planned summit with Chinese President Xi Jinping today.
    日本首相高市早苗昨天表示，在美國總統唐納·川普今天預計與中國國家主席習近平舉行峰會之前，她與川普就中國議題進行了「及時的」討論。
  • stability n. 穩定、安定
    Japan considers it important that US-China relations contribute to the stability of the international community, including Japan.
    日本認為，美中關係有助於包括日本在內的國際社會穩定，這非常重要。
  • concerning prep. 關於、涉及
    She did not give details of what she and Trump discussed with regard to China, only saying they “conducted a timely exchange of views on various issues concerning China.”
    她沒有透露她和川普就中國議題討論的細節，僅表示雙方「就各種涉華議題及時交換了意見」。
  • coordinate v. 協調、配合
    Hours before she took the stage, Trump and Takaichi agreed to closely coordinate on China issues during a roughly 30-minute huddle.
    在她登台演說前幾小時，川普和高市在一場大約30分鐘的密談中，同意就中國議題密切協調。
  • deal n. 交易、協議
    Japan and other US allies in Asia are concerned that Trump could soften Washington’s support for Taiwan in return for an economic deal with Xi.
    日本與美國在亞洲的其他盟友擔心，川普可能會為了與習近平達成經濟協議而軟化華府對台灣的支持。
  • demonstrate v. 展現、證明
    Takaichi also said her meeting with Trump demonstrated the strength of ties between the two countries against a worsening security backdrop in Asia.
    高市也表示，她與川普的會面展現了在亞洲安全背景惡化下，兩國之間緊密的紐帶。
  • amendment n. 修正、修改（法案等）
    A complicated amendment process has stood in the way of achieving that goal.
    繁複的修正程序一直是實現該目標的阻礙。
  • contribution n. 貢獻
    I make this point strongly, with pride in and respect for Japan’s honor after 70 years of making major contributions to the work of the UN.
    我強力提出這一點，帶著對日本榮譽的驕傲與敬意，日本在過去70年中對聯合國的工作做出了重大貢獻。
  • retract v. 撤回、收回（言論等）
    The charter has become a new point of tension between Tokyo and Beijing, as China continues to try and force Takaichi to retract remarks she made about Taiwan.
    由於中國持續試圖逼迫高市撤回她先前關於台灣的言論，聯合國憲章已成為東京與北京之間的新緊張點。

文法重點

  • S + consider + it + adj. + that 子句 (虛受詞用法)
    Japan considers it important that US-China relations contribute to the stability of the international community, including Japan
    句中的 it 為虛受詞（dummy object），用來代替後面真正的受詞子句「that US-China relations contribute...」。當真正的受詞是較長的子句時，英文通常會先用 it 放在受詞位置，再把子句移到句尾，避免頭重腳輕。
  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾
    Takaichi was one of the last speakers on the opening day of the General Assembly, and the Chinese delegation remained to hear her remarks.
    句中的「opening day」由現在分詞「opening」修飾名詞「day」，表示「正值開幕中的那一天」。現在分詞作形容詞用時，表主動或進行中的狀態。
  • 動名詞片語作介詞受詞
    I make this point strongly, with pride in and respect for Japan’s honor after 70 years of making major contributions to the work of the UN.
    介詞「after」後面接動名詞「making」，構成動名詞片語「making major contributions to...」，作介詞 after 的受詞，用來表達「在做了……之後」。
  • 現在分詞表達附帶狀況
    In November last year, she told Japanese lawmakers that a Chinese invasion of Taiwan would be an existential threat to Japan — implying Tokyo might support any collective effort to defend Taiwan.
    句尾的「— implying...」是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前面整件事情所帶來的含意或結果，等於「which implied that...」的簡化用法。
