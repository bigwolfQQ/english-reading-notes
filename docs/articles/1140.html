警方查獲超過 7 萬件仿冒戰鬥陀螺

More than 70,000 fake Beyblades seized: police

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • seize v. 查扣、沒收、截獲
    The National Police Agency’s Third Special Police Corps yesterday said that from May to July, it intercepted five smuggling operations from China, seizing more than 70,000 counterfeit Beyblades...
    內政部警政署保安警察總隊昨天表示，從 5 月到 7 月，該總隊攔截了 5 起來自中國的走私行動，查獲超過 7 萬個仿冒戰鬥陀螺……
  • counterfeit adj. 仿冒的、偽造的
    ...seizing more than 70,000 counterfeit Beyblades with an estimated infringement value of NT$34.45 million...
    ……查獲超過 7 萬個仿冒戰鬥陀螺，估計侵權市值達新台幣 3,445 萬元……
  • estimated adj. 估計的、預估的
    ...with an estimated infringement value of NT$34.45 million (US$1.08 million).
    ……估計侵權市值達新台幣 3,445 萬元（約合 108 萬美元）。
  • distribute v. 分發、經銷、配銷（文章中使用衍生名詞 distributor）
    The officers then contacted the authorized distributor in Taiwan, Lih Yen Co, to examine photographs and videos...
    隨後員警聯繫了台灣的授權經銷商麗嬰國際股份有限公司，以檢視照片和影片……
  • authorize v. 授權、批准（文章中使用衍生形容詞 authorized）
    The officers then contacted the authorized distributor in Taiwan, Lih Yen Co, to examine photographs and videos of the seized items...
    隨後員警聯繫了台灣的授權經銷商麗嬰國際股份有限公司，以檢視被查扣物品的照片和影片……
  • retailer n. 零售商
    The Third Special Police Corps advised parents to accompany their children when purchasing Beyblades and make sure to look for the official certification mark and avoid buying from unverified retailers.
    保三總隊建議家長在購買戰鬥陀螺時應陪同孩子，並務必尋找官方認證標章，避免向未經核實的零售商購買。
  • impersonate v. 冒充、模仿（他人以行騙）
    The department warned that fraudsters usually draw in buyers by offering “ultra-low prices” or impersonate popular shopping Web sites to trick users...
    警方警告，詐騙集團通常以「超低價」誘捕買家，或是冒充熱門購物網站來欺騙使用者……
  • legitimate adj. 合法的、正當的
    ...to trick users into believing they are legitimate platforms, but victims never receive the goods after transferring money.
    ……來欺騙使用者相信這是合法平台，但被害人匯款後卻從未收到商品。
  • transaction n. 交易、業務
    It reminded people that if they receive any messages requesting private transactions, off-platform payments, personal information... they should immediately stop...
    它提醒民眾，若收到任何要求私底下交易、平台外付款、個人資料……的訊息，應立即停止……
  • monitor v. 監控、監測
    The Third Special Police Corps said it would continue to closely monitor maritime borders and would not tolerate any products that compromise the health and safety...
    保三總隊表示將持續嚴密監控海上邊境，絕不容許任何危害健康與安全的產品……

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾或補充說明（分詞構句）
    The National Police Agency’s Third Special Police Corps yesterday said that from May to July, it intercepted five smuggling operations from China, seizing more than 70,000 counterfeit Beyblades...
    句尾的「seizing...」是由連接詞和主詞簡化而來的現在分詞構句，用來補充說明前面主句所發生的動作（攔截走私）所帶來的結果或同時進行的狀況，意為「同時查獲了超過 7 萬個……」。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    The Third Special Police Corps, dedicated to safeguarding maritime borders and trade lines, said it seized 720 counterfeit Beyblades...
    句中的「dedicated to safeguarding...」是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的主詞「The Third Special Police Corps」，相當於一個形容詞子句「which is dedicated to safeguarding...」，省略了「which is」以簡化句子結構。
  • be prone to + V（易於……、傾向於……）
    During high-speed spinning and intense collisions, they are prone to shatter and send sharp debris flying...
    「be prone to」是多益常考的重要片語，意指某物「很容易發生某種負面情況」，後面接原形動詞（如 shatter）。此句用來描述仿冒陀螺在高速碰撞下容易碎裂的特性。
  • 使役動詞的用法（help / make / let / have）
    ...to trick users into believing they are legitimate platforms, but victims never receive the goods after transferring money.
    句中的「trick users into believing...」雖非傳統四大使役動詞，但「trick/force/get sb. into V-ing」是多益常見的「促使/誘使某人做某事」的句型結構，表示透過某種手段讓某人陷入某種狀態或做出某種行為。
