文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Yang and Cheng both remain in TVGH's Department of Neurosurgery after losing their directorships in oncology and minimally invasive neurosurgery, respectively, following a January referral to prosecutors.
    句中的 "after losing their directorships..." 是由介系詞 after 接現在分詞片語所構成，用來簡化時間副詞子句（原本可能是 after they lost their directorships...），在新聞英文中非常常見，能讓句子更緊湊流暢。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The unauthorized procedures included using electrocautery to stop bleeding, harvesting tissue, implanting screws, and assembling connecting rods, according to the indictment.
    句首的 "The unauthorized procedures" 當中，"unauthorized" 是由過去分詞轉化而來的形容詞，用來修飾名詞 "procedures"（被授權的/未經授權的程序），多益測驗常考這種分詞轉形容詞的用法。
  • 讓步副詞子句簡化或連接詞用法 (Concession)
    Although three vendors confessed and surrendered their entire NT$330,000 share of illicit gains, prosecutors still requested appropriate penalties for them.
    使用 "Although"（雖然）引導讓步子句，表達兩個事實之間的轉折對比（雖然業者自白並繳回贓款，但檢方仍求處適當刑罰）。注意英文中雖有雖然 (Although)，但不能跟 but 同時出現在同一個句構中。
