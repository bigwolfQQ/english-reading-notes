台灣與美國合作開發「非紅色」充電器與電池供應鏈

Taiwan, US develop non-red charger and battery supply chain

單字解析

  • supply chain n. 供應鏈
    The military worked with the US to develop a non-China supply chain for batteries and chargers...
    軍方已與美國合作，為電池和充電器開發非中國供應鏈...
  • manufacture v. 製造、生產
    ...with the batteries to be manufactured in Taiwan, Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) Legislator Wang Ting-yu (王定宇) said yesterday.
    ...其中電池將在台灣製造，民主進步黨立法委員王定宇昨天表示。
  • procure v. 採購、取得
    Reports that the drones had been procured without chargers — as the original chargers were made in China — were only half correct...
    有關無人機採購時未附充電器（因為原廠充電器為中國製造）的報導只說對了一半...
  • alternative n. / adj. 替代方案、替代的
    ...and that as of April, no alternative supplier had been found, requiring US technical representatives to come to Taiwan to charge the drones.
    ...且截至4月為止仍未找到替代供應商，導致美國技術代表必須來台為無人機充電。
  • component n. 零組件、成分
    Some components of the original charging system were made in China and could not be procured under a government policy...
    原充電系統的一些零組件為中國製造，根據政府政策無法被採購...
  • policy n. 政策、方針
    ...and could not be procured under a government policy restricting the use of any components made in that country, he said.
    ...且根據一項限制使用該國任何零組件的政府政策，這些零件無法被採購，他說。
  • eventually adv. 最終、最後
    The new supply chain would meet the military’s needs and could eventually allow Taiwan to become a supplier of batteries...
    新的供應鏈將能滿足軍方的需求，並最終可能讓台灣成為電池供應商...
  • procurement n. 採購、向外採購
    The Ministry of National Defense late last month announced a NT$26.9 billion (US$852.67 million) procurement of 1,554 Altius-700M...
    國防部上個月底宣布斥資新台幣269億元（約合8億5267萬美元）採購1,554架Altius-700M...
  • schedule v. / n. 預定、排定、時程表
    The manufacturer has also developed a non-China supply chain for chargers, which is scheduled to be delivered according to the procurement timetable.
    製造商也為充電器開發了非中國供應鏈，預定將按照採購時程表進行交付。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況說明 (with + 名詞 + 分詞)
    The military worked with the US to develop a non-China supply chain for batteries and chargers used by its Altius-600 drones, with the batteries to be manufactured in Taiwan...
    「with + 受詞 + 分詞/形容詞」結構在新聞英語中非常常見，用來補充說明主要的動作或事件發生的同時所伴隨的狀況。此處「with the batteries to be manufactured in Taiwan」用來補充說明該供應鏈具體的生產安排。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    Reports that the drones had been procured without chargers — as the original chargers were made in China — were only half correct...
    句中的「procured without chargers」與「made in China」都是過去分詞片語，分別用來修飾前面的「the drones」與「the original chargers」，省略了原本的關係代名詞與be動詞（which were），能讓句子更緊湊簡潔。
  • 現在分詞片語表達附加結果 (adding that...)
    The military and the US have since completed work on alternatives for the batteries and charging systems, Wang said, adding that the batteries would be manufactured in Taiwan...
    句尾的「adding that...」是現在分詞片語作結果或補充說明的狀語，表示王定宇在說完前面那句話的同時，也「順便補充了」後面的內容，常用於新聞報導中引述發言時延續引言。
