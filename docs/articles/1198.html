週一全台各地發布高溫特報

Heat warnings issued Monday across Taiwan

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • issue v. 發布、發行
    The Central Weather Administration (CWA) issued heat advisories for eight municipalities across Taiwan on Monday
    中央氣象署於週一對全台8個縣市發布高溫特報
  • advisory n. 特報、公告、警報
    The Central Weather Administration (CWA) issued heat advisories for eight municipalities across Taiwan on Monday
    中央氣象署於週一對全台8個縣市發布高溫特報
  • exceed v. 超過、超越
    warning that temperatures may exceed 36 degrees Celsius around noon.
    警告中午前後氣溫可能超過攝氏36度。
  • reduce v. 減少、降低
    The CWA advised the public to reduce outdoor activities and strenuous exercise
    氣象署建議民眾減少戶外活動與劇烈運動
  • strenuous adj. 費力的、劇烈的
    The CWA advised the public to reduce outdoor activities and strenuous exercise
    氣象署建議民眾減少戶外活動與劇烈運動
  • range v. （在一定範圍內）變動、範圍介於
    temperatures are expected to range from 23-35 degrees Celsius in northern Taiwan
    北部氣溫預計介於攝氏23至35度
  • strengthen v. 加強、增強
    Seasonal northeasterly winds will strengthen on Thursday and begin affecting Taiwan on Friday
    季節性的東北風將在週四增強，並於週五開始影響台灣
  • affect v. 影響
    Seasonal northeasterly winds will strengthen on Thursday and begin affecting Taiwan on Friday
    季節性的東北風將在週四增強，並於週五開始影響台灣
  • forecast v. 預測、預報
    They will bring showers to areas north of Taoyuan, eastern Taiwan, the Hengchun Peninsula in the south, and mountainous areas nationwide, the CWA forecast.
    氣象署預測，東北風將為桃園以北地區、東部、南部的恆春半島以及全國山區帶來陣雨。
  • remain v. 保持、維持
    Elsewhere, conditions will remain mostly cloudy to sunny, it said.
    氣象署表示，其他地區的天氣狀況將維持多雲到晴。

文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The advisories were issued for Taipei, New Taipei and Tainan...
    當句子的主語是動作的承受者時，使用「be動詞 + 過去分詞 (V-pp)」構成被動語態。在此句中，特報 (advisories) 是被氣象署發布的，因此使用 were issued。
  • 現在分詞作修飾語 (Present Participle as Modifier)
    ...warning that temperatures may exceed 36 degrees Celsius around noon.
    句尾的現在分詞片語「warning that...」用來補充說明前面主句發布高溫特報的同時所伴隨的警告內容，為主動語態的補充說明。
  • be expected to 原形動詞 (預期將...) 底下的用法
    During this period, temperatures are expected to range from 23-35 degrees Celsius...
    「be expected to + V」是多益測驗極常見的商業與新聞句型，用來表達客觀預期某事將會發生或達到某種狀態。
