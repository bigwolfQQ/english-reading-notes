文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作後置修飾語
    The U.S. International Trade Commission (USITC) has launched a Section 337 investigation naming Taiwanese technology companies, including Foxconn, Delta Electronics Inc. and Quanta Computer Inc., as respondents...
    句中的「naming Taiwanese technology companies...」是由現在分詞帶領的分詞片語，用來補充說明前方「a Section 337 investigation」的細節，相當於關代子句「which names...」的簡化用法，在新聞英文中非常常見。
  • 過去分詞作後置修飾語
    The agency described the case as a patent dispute between private companies being handled under U.S. Section 337 procedures.
    句中的「handled under U.S. Section 337 procedures」是由過去分詞片語充當形容詞，用來修飾前方的「patent dispute」，省略了「which is」，表達被動與完成的意味。
  • 連接詞片語與條件句省略（if needed / if necessary）
    ...with Taiwan's Ministry of Economic Affairs (MOEA) pledging assistance if needed.
    句尾的「if needed」是條件副詞子句的省略用法，完整結構應為「if it is needed」。在商業與新聞英語中，為了簡潔流暢，常會省略主詞及 be 動詞。
  • 否定副詞與條件句的複合運用（unless...）
    Any remedial orders issued would take effect immediately and become final after 60 days unless disapproved by the U.S. Trade Representative on policy grounds, it added.
    「unless」等於「if not」（除非），在此引導條件副詞子句，表示「除非遭到美國貿易代表否決，否則將在 60 天後成為最終決定」。子句中同樣省略了主詞與 be 動詞（完整的結構應為 unless they are disapproved...）。
