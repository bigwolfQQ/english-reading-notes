台東空軍基地發生火災，男子受到重傷

Man critically injured in fire at Taitung air base

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • contractor n. 承包商，立約人
    A contractor fell into a 12-meter-deep oil tank at Chihhang Air Base in Taitung County and suffered severe burns after a fire broke out while he was handling residual oil Friday afternoon, according to the county's Fire Department.
    據縣消防局指出，週五下午，台東縣志航基地一名承包商在處理殘油時發生火災，不慎墜入一座12公尺深的油槽中，並遭受嚴重燒傷。
  • severe adj. 嚴重的，劇烈的

  • handle v. 處理，應付，操作

  • accident n. 意外，事故
    The fire department said the contractor, surnamed Chang (張), fell into the tank and showed no vital signs after the accident.
    消防局表示，該名姓張的承包商在事故發生後墜入油槽，且在事發後沒有生命跡象。
  • critical adj. 危急的，關鍵的
    Although Chang regained a pulse after being rushed to a hospital, he remained in critical condition, the fire department said.
    消防局指出，張男經緊急送醫搶救後雖恢復心跳，但仍處於危急狀態。
  • personnel n. 人員，員工，人事部門
    The department said it received a report at around 4 p.m. Friday that a man had fallen into an underground oil tank at the air base and immediately sent rescue personnel to the scene.
    消防局表示，其於週五下午4點左右接獲通報，指一名男子墜入該空軍基地的地下油槽，並立即派遣救援人員趕赴現場。
  • control v. / n. 控制
    Before rescue crews arrived at the scene, air base personnel had already brought the fire under control, the department said.
    消防局說，在救援人員抵達現場之前，空軍基地人員已經將火勢控制住。
  • operation n. 作業，運作，手術
    As the operation was being carried out under the Army Logistics Command, the Army will provide further information about the incident, military authorities said.
    軍方當局表示，由於該作業是在陸軍後勤指揮部管轄下進行，陸軍將提供有關該事件的進一步資訊。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞當作附帶狀況（Time Clause 省略與附加）
    ...after a fire broke out while he was handling residual oil Friday afternoon...
    連接詞 while 後方接過去進行式子句表示「當……的時候」，用以描述在火災發生當時，傷者正在進行的動作。
  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense)
    Before rescue crews arrived at the scene, air base personnel had already brought the fire under control...
    表達在過去某個動作（救援人員抵達）之前已經完成的動作（火勢被控制），使用「had + 過去分詞 (brought)」。
  • 關係代名詞非限定用法 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Air base personnel found Chang, who is in his 60s, in out-of-hospital cardiac arrest (OHCA)...
    逗號後方的「who is in his 60s」是關係子句的非限定用法，用來補充說明先行詞 Chang 的年齡，若去掉該子句不影響主要句意。
  • 過去進行式被動語態 (Past Continuous Passive Voice)
    As the operation was being carried out under the Army Logistics Command...
    結構為「was/were + being + 過去分詞 (carried out)」，用來強調在過去某個特定時間點，該項作業「正在被」執行中。
