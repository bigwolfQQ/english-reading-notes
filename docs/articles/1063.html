受強勁 AI 需求推動，台灣 8 月工業生產創下歷史新高

Taiwan's industrial production hits new high in August on strong AI demand

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • demand n. 需求
    Taiwan's August industrial production set a new record, with the index soaring more than 23 percent from a year earlier due to robust global demand for artificial intelligence applications, the Ministry of Economic Affairs (MOEA) said on Wednesday.
    經濟部於週三表示，由於全球對人工智慧應用的強勁需求，台灣 8 月工業生產創下新紀錄，指數較去年同期飆升超過 23%。
  • index n. 指數
    Data compiled by the MOEA showed the local industrial production index rose 23.47 percent from a year earlier to 145.97, beating the previous high of 143.32 in July, while the subindex of the manufacturing sector, which accounts for over 90 percent of the country's total production, rose 24.61 percent to reach 148.42.
    經濟部彙整的數據顯示，本地工業生產指數較去年同期上升 23.47%，達到 145.97，打破了 7 月份 143.32 的前高；而佔該國總產值 90% 以上的製造業次指數則上升 24.61%，達到 148.42。
  • sector n. 產業、部門
    The old economy sector, however, appeared mixed due to uneven strength in recovery, the MOEA's data showed.
    然而，經濟部的資料顯示，傳統產業表現互有增減，原因是復甦力道不均。
  • supplier n. 供應商
    In August, production in the machinery industry sector rose 18.65 percent from a year earlier largely because semiconductor suppliers were keen to expand capacity through equipment purchases to meet global AI demand, the MOEA's data showed.
    經濟部的資料顯示，8 月份機械產業的產值較去年同期上升 18.65%，主要是因為半導體供應商積極透過採購設備來擴充產能，以滿足全球 AI 需求。
  • capacity n. 產能、容量
    In August, production in the machinery industry sector rose 18.65 percent from a year earlier largely because semiconductor suppliers were keen to expand capacity through equipment purchases to meet global AI demand, the MOEA's data showed.
    經濟部的資料顯示，8 月份機械產業的產值較去年同期上升 18.65%，主要是因為半導體供應商積極透過採購設備來擴充產能，以滿足全球 AI 需求。
  • decline n. / v. 下降、衰退
    However, the base metal industry suffered a 0.36 percent year-on-year decline in production in August as demand for select products such as hot rolled steel sheets stayed slow, while the chemical material and fertilizer industry also posted a 5.81 percent fall in production on weak demand, with some firms cutting production in response to the market reality, the MOEA said.
    然而，經濟部指出，基本金屬產業 8 月產值年減 0.36%，因為熱軋鋼板等特定產品的需求持續疲軟；化學材料及肥料產業產值也因需求疲弱而下降 5.81%，部分企業因應市場現實而減產。
  • response n. 回應、反應
    However, the base metal industry suffered a 0.36 percent year-on-year decline in production in August as demand for select products such as hot rolled steel sheets stayed slow, while the chemical material and fertilizer industry also posted a 5.81 percent fall in production on weak demand, with some firms cutting production in response to the market reality, the MOEA said.
    然而，經濟部指出，基本金屬產業 8 月產值年減 0.36%，因為熱軋鋼板等特定產品的需求持續疲軟；化學材料及肥料產業產值也因需求疲弱而下降 5.81%，部分企業因應市場現實而減產。
  • export n. / v. 出口
    Looking ahead, the MOEA said while the global economy remains impacted by various trade policies and geopolitical unease, Taiwan's exports are expected to continue to benefit from an AI boom, which will boost production in the manufacturing sector.
    展望未來，經濟部表示，儘管全球經濟持續受到各種貿易政策與地緣政治不安的影響，台灣的出口預期將繼續受惠於 AI 榮景，這將推動製造業的產值。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作附加說明（分詞構句）
    Riding the waves of AI applications and cloud services, which boosted demand for AI servers, switches, semiconductor inspection equipment and automation equipment, the local computer and optoelectronics industry posted a 95.72 percent year-on-year increase in production in August.
    句首的「Riding the waves of...」是由現在分詞帶領的片語，用來補充說明主詞「the local computer and optoelectronics industry」在當時伴隨的動作與狀態，意思是「乘著……的浪潮，……產業實現了……」。這種結構在財經新聞中常用於描述企業或產業背後的驅動因素。
  • 受詞補語結構（with + 名詞 + 分詞）
    Taiwan's August industrial production set a new record, with the index soaring more than 23 percent from a year earlier due to robust global demand for artificial intelligence applications, the Ministry of Economic Affairs (MOEA) said on Wednesday.
    句中使用「with + the index (名詞) + soaring (現在分詞)」的結構，用來補充主句發生時同時存在的附帶狀況。在這裡，「the index」與「soar」之間為主動關係，故使用現在分詞，表示指數同時正向上飆升。
  • 非限定關係子句
    Data compiled by the MOEA showed the local industrial production index rose 23.47 percent from a year earlier to 145.97, beating the previous high of 143.32 in July, while the subindex of the manufacturing sector, which accounts for over 90 percent of the country's total production, rose 24.61 percent to reach 148.42.
    逗點後的「which accounts for over 90 percent of the country's total production」是一個非限定關係子句，前方加上逗點，用來補充說明先行詞「the subindex of the manufacturing sector」的背景資訊（佔全國總產值 90% 以上），省略該子句並不影響主要句意。
  • 被動語態的應用
    Looking ahead, the MOEA said while the global economy remains impacted by various trade policies and geopolitical unease, Taiwan's exports are expected to continue to benefit from an AI boom, which will boost production in the manufacturing sector.
    句中的「remain impacted by...」與「are expected to...」均使用了被動語態（be動詞/連綴動詞 + 過去分詞）。因為全球經濟是「受到」政策影響，而台灣出口是「被」預期，故使用被動語態來強調受事者。
