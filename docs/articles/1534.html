經濟學家認為AI熱潮將維持台灣成長，並敦促實現多元化

Economists see AI boom sustaining Taiwan growth, urge diversification

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • outlook n. 前景，展望
    strong demand for artificial intelligence (AI) chips is boosting Taiwan’s economic outlook
    對人工智慧（AI）晶片的強勁需求正在提振台灣的經濟前景
  • mitigate v. 減輕，緩和（風險等）
    urge the country to use the boom to diversify its economy and mitigate market risks.
    敦促該國利用這波熱潮來實現經濟多元化並降低市場風險。
  • forecast n. / v. 預測，預報
    The IMF yesterday raised its forecast for Taiwan’s economic growth this year to 10.7 percent
    國際貨幣基金組織昨天將今年台灣經濟成長預測調高至10.7%
  • project v. 預估，預測（常用被動態）
    making Taiwan the only economy projected to post double-digit growth this year.
    使台灣成為今年唯一預測呈雙位数成長的經濟體。
  • drive v. 推動，驅使
    Taiwan’s growth is being driven mainly by its semiconductor industry
    台灣的成長主要由其半導體產業推動
  • take advantage of phr. 利用，佔...的便宜
    Masha urged Taiwan to take advantage of the current economic expansion
    馬沙敦促台灣利用當前的經濟擴張
  • revenue n. （政府）稅收，（企業）營收
    by using additional government revenue and other resources to develop industries beyond technology.
    透過動用額外的政府稅收和其他資源，發展科技以外的產業。
  • anticipate v. 預期，預料
    if the technology fails to deliver the anticipated returns.
    如果該技術未能達到預期的回報。
  • hedge v. 避險，防範（風險）
    I think the most important thing for the Taiwan government if they’re really going to hedge their risk
    我認為台灣政府若要真正對沖風險
  • resilience n. 恢復力，韌性
    its long-term resilience will depend on how effectively it uses the current boom
    其長期韌性將取決於其如何有效地利用當前的熱潮
  • capitalize on phr. 利用（機會等）獲利
    adding that cross-border cooperation would be needed to capitalize on the trend.
    並補充說需要進行跨國合作才能利用這股趨勢。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾
    Economists attending the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and World Bank annual meetings in Bangkok said...
    句中的 "attending the International Monetary Fund..." 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 Economists，相當於形容詞子句 "who attended..." 的簡化版，常用於新聞英語中以節省版面並使語氣緊湊。
  • 被動語態的進行式
    Taiwan’s growth is being driven mainly by its semiconductor industry...
    此句使用了現在進行式的被動語態（is/am/are + being + 過去分詞），用來強調台灣的經濟成長「正持續被」半導體產業所推動，突顯當下的動態變化。
  • 譲步副詞子句 (Although / Even as)
    ...AI could help Taiwan and other economies achieve strong growth even as many countries around the world struggle economically.
    "even as" 在此引導一個副詞子句，表示「正當...的時候 / 儘管...」，用來突顯對比：當其他國家在經濟上掙扎時，台灣與其他經濟體仍能藉由 AI 達成強勁成長。
  • 間接問句 / 名詞子句作介系詞受詞
    ...its long-term resilience will depend on how effectively it uses the current boom to strengthen other parts of the economy.
    介系詞 on 後面接了一個由 "how effectively..." 引導的名詞子句作為受詞。在結構上，疑問副詞 how 修飾副詞 effectively，後面接著主詞 it 與動詞 uses。
