醫師、醫療器材商遭判緩刑

Doctors, vendors handed suspended jail sentences

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • participate in v. phr. 參與，參加
    Two doctors were handed suspended sentences yesterday after they allowed three medical device vendors to participate in 13 neurological surgeries at Taichung Veterans General Hospital between March 2023 and October last year.
    兩名醫師昨天被判處緩刑，此前他們允許三名醫療器材商於 2023 年 3 月至去年 10 月間，在台中榮民總醫院參與 13 次神經外科手術。
  • vendor n. 供應商，攤販
    Two doctors were handed suspended sentences yesterday after they allowed three medical device vendors to participate in 13 neurological surgeries at Taichung Veterans General Hospital between March 2023 and October last year.
    兩名醫師昨天被判處緩刑，此前他們允許三名醫療器材商於 2023 年 3 月至去年 10 月間，在台中榮民總醫院參與 13 次神經外科手術。
  • respectively adv. 分別，各自
    The Taichung District Court sentenced Yang Meng-yin (楊孟寅), former director of the hospital’s oncology neurosurgery division, and Cheng Wen-yu (鄭文郁), former director of minimally invasive neurosurgery, to one year and four months, and one year and two months respectively.
    台中地方法院分別判處該院前腫瘤神經外科主任楊孟寅、前微創神經外科主任鄭文郁有期徒刑一年四個月及一年兩個月。
  • plead guilty v. phr. 認罪
    The three medical vendors, who did not hold physician qualifications, pleaded guilty and received sentences of one year, eight months and eight months suspended for three years.
    三名不具醫師資格的醫療器材商當庭認罪，分別被判處一年、八個月及八個月徒刑，緩刑三年。
  • appeal v. 上訴
    All five verdicts may be appealed.
    這五起判決皆可上訴。
  • settlement n. 和解，協議
    As the doctors admitted to the offenses and reached settlements with the victims, they asked the court to grant them suspended sentences.
    由於醫師們坦承犯行並與被害人達成和解，他們請求法院給予緩刑。
  • indict v. 起訴，控告
    In January, the Taichung District Prosecutors’ Office indicted Yang, Cheng and the three medical device suppliers on suspicion of fraud under the Criminal Code and contraventions of the Physicians Act (醫師法).
    今年一月，台中地方檢察署依《刑法》詐欺罪及違反《醫師法》之嫌，起訴楊孟寅、鄭文郁以及三名醫療器材供應商。
  • compromise v. 損害，危及，妥協
    The procedures seriously endangered the patients’ lives and physical safety, and compromised the quality of medical care, prosecutors said.
    檢察官表示，這些程序嚴重危害病患的生命與身體安全，並損及醫療照護品質。
  • workload n. 工作量
    The investigation found that Yang and Cheng were seeking to reduce their workload and consolidate their working relationships with the medical device suppliers.
    調查發現，楊孟寅和鄭文郁此舉是為了減輕工作負擔，並鞏固他們與醫療器材供應商的工作合作關係。
  • eligible adj. 有資格的，合乎條件的
    The five defendants also applied to the National Health Insurance Administration for consultation and surgical fees, despite knowing that procedures performed by unlicensed people are not eligible for coverage, the indictment said.
    起訴書指出，這五名被告明知由無照人員執行的醫療程序不符合給付資格，仍向中央健康保險署申請諮詢費與手術費。

文法重點

  • 非限定關係子句 (Non-defining relative clause)
    The three medical vendors, who did not hold physician qualifications, pleaded guilty and received sentences of one year, eight months and eight months suspended for three years.
    使用逗號隔開的「who did not hold physician qualifications」，用來補充說明前面「the three medical vendors」的背景資訊，拿掉之後不影響主要句意。
  • 現在分詞作後置修飾 (Present participle as post-modifier)
    The indictment alleged that Yang and Cheng allowed the three vendors to illegally perform medical procedures such as bone screw implants, electrocoagulation and tissue extraction during surgeries such as endoscopic spinal fusion operations.
    句中的「endoscopic spinal fusion operations」等雖然是名詞片語，但在其他段落中如「procedures performed by unlicensed people」則使用了過去分詞片語「performed by unlicensed people」後置修飾前面的名詞 procedures，表被動關係。
  • 讓步語氣介系詞片語 (Prepositional phrase of concession)
    The five defendants also applied to the National Health Insurance Administration for consultation and surgical fees, despite knowing that procedures performed by unlicensed people are not eligible for coverage, the indictment said.
    片語「despite」後面接動名詞短語「knowing that...」，用來表達「儘管知道……」的讓步語氣，是多益閱讀測驗常考的轉折與語意邏輯考點。
