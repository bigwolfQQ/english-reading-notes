文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    Premier Cho Jung-tai (卓榮泰) on Wednesday defended the government's use of a controversial "countersignature" power to block the implementation of bills passed by the opposition-controlled Legislature...
    句中的 "passed by the opposition-controlled Legislature" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "bills"（法案），相當於關代子句 "which were passed by..." 的簡化版，省略了主格關代與be動詞，使語氣更為精煉。
  • 條件句假設語氣 (Conditional Clause)
    At a press conference on Thursday, DPP Legislator Chuang Jui-hsiung (莊瑞雄) said that if the opposition wants to resolve the current "constitutional controversy," it can always pass a motion of no confidence against the premier in the Legislature...
    此處使用 "if" 引導的真實條件句（First Conditional），結構為「If + 現在式動詞, 主詞 +助動詞(can/will) + 原形動詞」，用來表達在某個條件成立時，會產生的對應結果。
  • 關代非限定用法 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    This immobilized the 15-seat court, which has had only eight members left after seven completed their terms at the end of October 2024...
    句中的 ", which..." 引導非限定關係子句，對前面的先行詞 "the 15-seat court" 補充說明額外的背景資訊。非限定子句前通常有逗號隔開，且不能用 "that" 代替 "which"。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The KMT, meanwhile, has long viewed the court as politically biased, since all its members were appointed and confirmed when the presidency and Legislature were controlled by the DPP.
    句尾的 "were appointed and confirmed" 以及 "were controlled" 皆採用被動語態（be動詞 + 過去分詞），強調動作的承受者（大法官成員、總統府與立法院），符合新聞報導客觀陳述事實的語氣。
