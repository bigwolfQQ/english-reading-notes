文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    In addition to changes stemming from government restructuring and updates to references to relevant laws and regulations, the main amendment adds provisions to Article 7 explicitly stating that Taiwanese may not compete in international sporting events as members of China’s national teams.
    句中的 "stemming from..." 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 changes（源自於政府組織改造的變動）；而後方的 "explicitly stating that..." 則是另一個現在分詞片語，用來補充說明 provisions（條文）的內容。這種結構在多益閱讀測驗中常用於簡化子句、增加句子的緊湊感。
  • 動名詞片語作受詞 (Gerund Phrase as Object)
    The ministry said that serving as an athlete on China’s national team involves issues of national identity...
    句中的 "serving as an athlete on China’s national team" 是一個動名詞片語，作為動詞 involves 的主詞/受詞位置（在此處作主詞子句的動名詞片語）。動名詞片語在句中具備名詞性質，常出現在多益文法題（句型結構與詞性判斷）中。
  • 對等連接詞連接多個並列動作 (Parallel Structure with Conjunctions)
    They should not participate in activities with “united front” objectives or nationwide events organized by Chinese authorities, and must not join China’s national teams as athletes, the ministry said, urging them to protect their individual rights and Taiwan’s sovereignty.
    句中透過對等動詞片語 "should not participate in..." 與 "must not join..." 形成並列結構，表達強烈的禁止與建議。結尾處的 ", urging them to protect..." 則是分詞構句，補充說明部會提出上述呼籲時的目的或同時發生的動作。多益考試非常喜歡考平行結構與分詞構句的搭配。
