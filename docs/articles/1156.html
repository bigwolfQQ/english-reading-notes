正視生育獎勵的現實問題

Getting real about birth incentives

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • subsidize v. 補助，資助
    the government is planning to extend statutory marriage leave for workers from eight days to 14, subsidizing employers for the six additional days of wages.
    政府正計畫將勞工的法定婚假從8天延長至14天，並補貼雇主這額外6天的薪資。
  • incentive n. 激勵，誘因，獎勵
    An extra six days of marriage leave is welcome, but as a policy designed to encourage marriage and having children, is it truly a meaningful incentive?
    多出6天的婚假固然受到歡迎，但作為一項旨在鼓勵結婚和生育的政策，它真的是一個有意義的誘因嗎？
  • afford v. 負擔得起（金錢或時間）
    The issue is not just about whether young people can afford to marry and have children, but about whether they even want to.
    問題不僅在於年輕人是否負擔得起結婚和生子，而在於他們是否甚至想要這樣做。
  • expense n. 費用，支出
    Once a couple has children, they begin racking up housing, childcare and education expenses.
    一旦夫妻有了孩子，他們便開始累積房屋、托育和教育開銷。
  • responsibility n. 責任
    Marriage leave lasts two weeks, but the marriage might last 50 years, and raising a child is a responsibility lasting two decades.
    婚假持續兩週，但婚姻可能會持續50年，而扶養孩子是持續二十年的責任。
  • priority n. 優先事項
    These are values worth cherishing, but in teaching the next generation to prioritize “what I want” over “who am I willing to take responsibility for?”, settling down goes from a shared project of building a life together into a cost-benefit analysis.
    這些是值得珍惜的價值觀，但在教導下一代將「我想要什麼」置於「我願意對誰負責？」之上時，定下來結婚這件事，就從一個共同打造生活的計畫，變成了一場成本效益分析。
  • burden n. 負擔
    When marriage is seen as sacrificing a person’s freedom, having children as meaning a lower quality of life and family responsibilities as a burden, subsidies alone are unlikely to rekindle the desire to build a family.
    當婚姻被視為犧牲個人的自由、生孩子意味著生活品質下降、家庭責任被看作是一種負擔時，單靠補貼是不太可能重新點燃建立家庭的渴望的。
  • confidence n. 信心，自信
    The third issue is most critical for the government: young people’s confidence in the future.
    第三個問題對政府而言最為關鍵：年輕人對未來的信心。
  • earnings n. 薪資，收益，收入
    if the gap between their earnings and the cost of living remains a constant source of anxiety; and if new subsidies are rolled out without a stable outlook on housing, employment, childcare and long-term prospects, then the government loses that confidence.
    如果他們的收入與生活成本之間的差距仍然是焦慮的根源；而且如果在沒有穩定的房屋、就業、托育和長期前景的展望下推出新的補貼，那麼政府就會失去這種信心。
  • effectiveness n. 效力，有效性
    Quantity does not guarantee effectiveness; providing more subsidies does not mean the core problem has been addressed.
    數量不能保證成效；提供更多補貼並不代表核心問題已經獲得解決。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表達附帶結果或動作）
    Once a couple has children, they begin racking up housing, childcare and education expenses.
    句中的 "racking up" 為現在分詞片語，用來補充說明夫妻有了孩子之後隨之而來的連續動作或結果（開始累積……開銷）。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞
    When marriage is seen as sacrificing a person’s freedom, having children as meaning a lower quality of life and family responsibilities as a burden, subsidies alone are unlikely to rekindle the desire to build a family.
    句中的 "having children"（生孩子）為動名詞片語，作為句中被視為（seen as）對象的一部分，動名詞片語當作名詞使用，在句中扮演重要角色。
  • 假設語氣（與未來事實相反或極度懷疑）
    When young people no longer believe in tomorrow, can six extra days of marriage leave truly bring about the next generation?
    此處運用了 "If...; if...; and if..., then..." 的條件句結構（Conditional sentence），連續使用多個假設語氣的條件子句來堆疊情境，藉此強調在這些前提假設下，政府將會失去人民的信心。
  • 被動語態（Passive Voice）
    providing more subsidies does not mean the core problem has been addressed.
    句尾的 "has been addressed" 使用了現在完成式的被動語態（have/has been + 過去分詞），強調「核心問題」是被動地被處理、解決，焦點在於問題本身有沒有被對待，而非誰去解決它。
