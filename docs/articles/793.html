文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The recovery of hundreds of bodies from the rubble of buildings destroyed by Israeli strikes in Gaza during the war that began nearly three years ago has raised fresh concerns...
    句中的「destroyed by Israeli strikes in Gaza」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前方的名詞 buildings，相當於形容詞子句「which were destroyed by Israeli strikes in Gaza」，省略了關係代名詞與be動詞，使句子更為精簡。
  • 現在分詞片語作補充說明 (Present Participle Phrase)
    The Space Force on Monday said it has weapons deployed in space, confirming the presence of such capabilities in orbit for the first time.
    句尾的「confirming...」是由現在分詞構成的分詞構句，用來補充說明前面主句所發生的動作所帶來的結果或伴隨的情況，相當於「and it confirms...」。
  • 名詞子句作受詞 (Noun Clause as Object)
    The South Australia Court of Appeal yesterday ruled that one of three serial killers convicted over the murders of 11 people in one of the country’s most notorious criminal cases can be released from prison...
    「that one of three serial killers... can be released from prison」是由連接詞 that引導的名詞子句，在句中擔任動詞 ruled 的受詞，用以表達法院裁定或宣判的內容。
