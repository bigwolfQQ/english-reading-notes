文法重點

  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    Thai authorities yesterday said they are investigating a Bangkok clinic where a Nigerian-born British businessman and influencer underwent a penis enlargement procedure that led to his death.
    句中的 "where a Nigerian-born..." 是關係副詞子句，用來修飾前面的地方先行詞 "a Bangkok clinic"；而句尾的 "that led to his death" 則是關係代名詞子句，用來修飾前面的 "a penis enlargement procedure"。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾（簡化形容詞子句）
    A coroner in London last month ruled that the March 6 death of Igho Ubiribo, 43, in the Thai capital was caused by the hyaluronic acid injection he received hours earlier.
    句中的 "(who was) born" 或過去分詞結構常用來修飾名詞。在此句中，"he received hours earlier" 是省略了關係代名詞的子句，用來修飾前面的 "hyaluronic acid injection"。
  • 動名詞片語作受詞（with + 名詞 + 分詞）
    The case has reignited debate about medical tourism, with Thailand a leading destination for visitors from abroad seeking healthcare, including aesthetic procedures.
    此處使用 "with + 名詞 + 形容詞/分詞" 的附帶狀況句構（"with Thailand a leading destination..."），用來補充說明醫療旅遊背景下的主狀況，常出現在多益閱讀測驗中表達伴隨發生的狀態。
  • 過去分詞表被動與狀態
    He was taken by ambulance to hospital, suspected of having a pulmonary embolism, but lost consciousness before he could have a computed tomography scan.
    句首的 "He was taken..." 採用被動語態（be + 過去分詞），強調主詞承受動作；而逗號後的 "suspected of having..." 則是省略了 "[He was] suspected of..." 的被動分詞構句，用來補充說明主詞當時的狀況。
