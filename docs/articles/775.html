社論：真正的愛國者

EDITORIAL: The real patriots

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • sentence v. 宣判、判刑
    A Hong Kong court on Friday sentenced three former leaders... to prison terms of up to seven years and three months
    香港一家法院周五將三名前領導人判處最高達七年三個月的有期徒刑
  • term n. 刑期、期間、任期
    ...to prison terms of up to seven years and three months for “inciting subversion”
    因「煽動顛覆」罪名被判處最高達七年三個月的徒刑
  • release v. / n. 釋放、發布
    They are expected to be released between 2028 and 2030.
    他們預計將於2028年至2030年間出獄。
  • fine v. / n. 處以罰款、罰金
    The alliance, which was disbanded in 2021, was fined HK$1.5 million (US$191,238).
    該組織於2021年解散，被罰款150萬港元（約合191,238美元）。
  • convict v. / n. 宣判有罪、囚犯
    Friends and families of those convicted called it a “sad day”
    被定罪者的親友稱這是「悲傷的一天」
  • demand v. / n. 要求、需求
    demand accountability of the June 4th massacre
    追究六四屠殺責任
  • ban v. / n. 禁止、禁令
    The Hong Kong government in 2020 cited COVID-19 pandemic curbs to ban the vigils
    香港政府於2020年以COVID-19疫情限制為由，禁止了這些集會
  • vendor n. 小販、攤販、供應商
    A carnival that draws vendors selling Chinese products at a former commemoration site is considered “patriotic”
    在一個昔日的紀念地點舉辦吸引小販販售中國商品的嘉年華被認為是「愛國的」
  • optimistic adj. 樂觀的
    Liu said he was optimistic about a future in China in which everyone could fearlessly speak their mind
    劉說他對中國的未來感到樂觀，在那裡每個人都可以無所畏懼地說出心裡話
  • persecute v. 迫害
    ...without being politically persecuted.
    而不必擔心受到政治迫害。
  • pursuit n. 追求
    ...boils down to saying that pursuing democracy is a crime...
    歸根結底就是說追求民主就是犯罪……
  • apologize v. 道歉
    To plead or apologize for everything you believe in is the most hypocritical thing imaginable
    為你所信仰的一切辯護或道歉，是能想像到的最虛偽的事情
  • betray v. 背叛
    ...then I would rather be a criminal than a person who betrays their conscience
    ...那麼我寧願做一個罪犯，也不願做一個背叛良知的人

文法重點

  • 非限定關係子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    The alliance, which was disbanded in 2021, was fined HK$1.5 million (US$191,238).
    句中的「which was disbanded in 2021」前後用逗號隔開，是一個非限定關係子句，用來補充說明主詞「The alliance」（支聯會）已被解散的附加資訊，即使拿掉這句，主句的意思依然完整。
  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Friends and families of those convicted called it a “sad day”...
    句中的「convicted」是過去分詞，用來修飾前面的代名詞「those」（指被定罪的人）。相當於「those who were convicted」的簡化形式，能讓句子更精簡流暢。
  • 虛主詞與名詞子句 (Dummy Subject 'It' & Noun Clause)
    It is so profoundly insecure that it sees hostility at every turn and relies on hatred to maintain its grip...
    句首的「It」為虛主詞，真正的主詞是後面由「that」引導的名詞子句，構成「such... that...」或「so... that...」（如此……以至於……）的因果句構，用來強調政權缺乏安全感的程度。
