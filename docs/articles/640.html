烏克蘭經濟受俄羅斯攻擊而陷入癱瘓

Ukraine’s economy hobbled by Russian strikes

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • sector n. 部門，行業
    Ukraine’s economy is being crippled by intensifying Russian strikes hitting key sectors
    烏克蘭的經濟正因打擊關鍵產業的日益加劇的俄羅斯攻擊而陷入癱瘓
  • slump v. 暴跌，驟降
    and its GDP could slump by 1.5 points in 2026, Kyiv officials said on Saturday
    基輔官方週六表示，其 2026 年的國內生產毛額（GDP）可能會下滑 1.5 個百分點
  • revenue n. 收益，收入
    These have severely restricted grain exports in the Black Sea — a crucial source of Kyiv’s revenue.
    這些嚴重限制了黑海地區的糧食出口——這是基輔收入的重要來源。
  • supply chain n. 供應鏈
    The strikes have also targeted steel companies, another key sector, as well as supply chains — notably in the rail sector — and major warehouses.
    攻擊還鎖定了鋼鐵公司（另一個關鍵產業）、供應鏈（特別是鐵路部門）以及大型倉庫。
  • anticipate v. 預期，預料
    “We anticipate a very difficult winter in terms of both critical infrastructure...
    「我們預期，無論是在關鍵基礎設施方面……都將迎來非常艱難的冬天」
  • asset n. 資產
    Kyiv estimates that “the total destruction of infrastructure and assets are likely to be close to US$10 billion” this year.
    基輔估計，今年「基礎設施和資產的總體損失可能接近 10十億美元」。
  • reconstruct v. 重建
    “It is not something that you could reconstruct quickly.”
    「這不是你能迅速重建的東西。」
  • conditional adj. 附帶條件的
    The release of about US$30 billion in international aid intended to finance this year’s budget deficit is conditional upon parliament passing draft laws demanded by Western countries, Koretsky said.
    科雷茨基表示，大約 300 億美元旨在為今年預算赤字融資的國際援助之釋放，取決於國會是否通過西方國家要求的法律草案。
  • deficit n. 赤字，虧損
    The release of about US$30 billion in international aid intended to finance this year’s budget deficit is conditional upon...
    旨在為今年預算赤字融資的大約 300 億美元國際援助之釋放取決於...
  • impose v. 徵收（稅款），實施（規章）
    However, the Ukrainian parliament is struggling to approve suggested laws, which include unpopular measures such as imposing duties on parcels from abroad, intended to boost revenues.
    然而，烏克蘭國會正努力批准這些建議中的法律，其中包括一些不受歡迎的措施，例如對海外包裹徵收關稅，旨在增加收入。
  • boost v. 提高，促進，增長
    such as imposing duties on parcels from abroad, intended to boost revenues.
    例如對海外包裹徵收關稅，旨在增加收入。

文法重點

  • 被動語態的進行式 (Present Continuous Passive)
    Ukraine’s economy is being crippled by intensifying Russian strikes hitting key sectors
    句型「is/are being + 過去分詞」表示某個動作「正在被……進行中」。這裡用來強調烏克蘭經濟目前正持續遭受俄羅斯攻擊的破壞。
  • 分詞構句作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    Ukraine’s economy is being crippled by intensifying Russian strikes hitting key sectors
    句中的「hitting key sectors」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「intensifying Russian strikes」，相當於形容詞子句「that hit key sectors」，使語意更為緊湊。
  • 形容詞片語作主詞補語 (Adjective Phrase as Subject Complement)
    The release of about US$30 billion in international aid intended to finance this year’s budget deficit is conditional upon parliament passing draft laws...
    句型「is conditional upon...」中，「conditional upon」作為主詞補語，用來形容前方援助釋放的條件限制，是多益商業英文中常見的實用句構。
