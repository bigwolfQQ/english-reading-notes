伊朗與美國在新一波攻擊中互襲油輪

Iran, US hit tankers in new wave of attacks

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • benchmark n. 基準，基準點
    the international Brent crude benchmark breaching US$100 a barrel for the first time since July.
    國際布蘭特原油基準價格自7月以來首次突破每桶100美元。
  • retail price n. phr. 零售價
    The politically sensitive average retail price of diesel fuel in the US hit an all-time high of US$5.94 a gallon.
    在美國，政治上敏感的柴油平均零售價創下每加侖5.94美元的歷史新高。
  • escalate v. 逐步擴大，升高，加劇
    Iran’s Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) said it would sharply escalate their response to any further attacks
    伊朗伊斯蘭革命衛隊（IRGC）表示，他們將大幅升級對任何進一步攻擊的迴應
  • target v. 瞄準，以...為目標
    The US Central Command called its attacks a response to the IRGC targeting a US Navy warship twice with ballistic missiles over the previous two days.
    美國中央司令部稱其攻擊是對伊斯蘭革命衛隊在過去兩天內兩度以彈道飛彈瞄樑美國海軍軍艦的迴應。
  • retaliation n. 報復
    Washington has announced a new policy of attacking Iranian tankers in retaliation for fire that threatens its warships
    華盛頓已宣佈一項新政策，將攻擊威脅其軍艦火力的伊朗油輪以示報復
  • attempt v. 嘗試，企圖
    Iran continues to try to hit US naval ships, and for every time they do that or try to do that, they’re going to lose tankers
    伊朗持續企圖攻擊美國海軍艦艇，他們每一次這樣做或試圖這樣做，都將失去油輪
  • impact n. 衝擊，影響
    It was not immediately able to confirm casualties or environmental impact.
    目前無法立即確認傷亡或環境影響。
  • indicate v. 顯示，表明
    A vessel was reported listing, possibly indicating it had taken on water after being struck by a projectile off Port Rashid in the United Arab Emirates
    據報有一艘船隻傾斜，可能顯示其在阿拉伯聯合大公國拉希德港外被投射物擊中後進水

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表示伴隨狀況或結果）
    The attacks in and around the vital waterway sent the price of oil surging, with the international Brent crude benchmark breaching US$100 a barrel...
    句尾的 "with + 名詞 + 現在分詞 (breaching)" 構成獨立分詞構句，用來補充說明主句動作所伴隨的同時發生或產生的結果狀況，在多益閱讀測驗中非常常考。
  • 過去分詞作後置定語（修飾名詞）
    A vessel was reported listing, possibly indicating it had taken on water after being struck by a projectile off Port Rashid...
    句中的 "struck by a projectile" 是過去分詞片語，放在名詞 vessel 的後面作為後置定語，修飾該艘被投射物擊中的船隻。這類被動語態的簡化結構在多益職場新聞或商業合約中很常見。
