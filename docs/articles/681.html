中華航空在阿姆斯特丹航班中提高永續航空燃料使用量

China Airlines ups SAF use in Amsterdam flight

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • operate v. 營運、操作、執行
    operating a direct flight from Taipei to Amsterdam with sustainable aviation fuel (SAF) equivalent to 40 percent of the flight’s fuel use.
    執行從台北直飛阿姆斯特丹的航班，其使用的永續航空燃料（SAF）佔該航班總燃油量的 40%。
  • proportion n. 比例、部分
    It was the first time the airline had used such a high proportion of SAF on a long-haul flight.
    這是該航空公司首次在長途航班上使用如此高比例的永續航空燃料。
  • estimate v. 估計、估算
    China Airlines used a “book and claim” model to raise the SAF use on the Amsterdam flight, which was estimated to reduce carbon dioxide emissions by 93.2 tonnes...
    中華航空透過「預訂與聲明」模式來提高阿姆斯特丹航班的永續航空燃料使用量，這估計可減少 93.2 公噸的二氧化碳排放量...
  • transition v. / n. 轉型、過渡
    The use of SAF on flights is key to helping the aviation industry transition toward net zero carbon emissions.
    在航班上使用永續航空燃料是協助航空業轉型邁向淨零碳排放的關鍵。
  • commit v. 致力於、奉獻於（常用 be committed to + V-ing）
    We are committed to expanding the use of SAF on flights to Europe, North America and Asia...
    我們致力於擴大在飛往歐洲、北美和亞洲的航班上使用永續航空燃料...
  • incorporate v. 納入、結合
    ...while incorporating low-carbon initiatives into our operations and passenger services...
    ...同時將低碳倡議納入我們的營運和旅客服務中...
  • consumption n. 消耗、消耗量
    The airline used tableware in premium business class that is 30 percent lighter than previous versions to help reduce fuel consumption, he said.
    他說，該航空公司在豪華商務艙中使用了比以往版本輕 30% 的餐具，以協助減少燃油消耗。
  • encourage v. 鼓勵
    Passengers were encouraged to check-in online before arriving at the airport...
    乘客受鼓勵在抵達機場前線上報到...
  • preference n. 偏好、喜好
    This not only helps us better understand passengers’ preferences, but also helps reduce food waste...
    這不僅有助於我們更好地了解乘客的喜好，還有助於減少食物浪費...
  • collaboration n. 合作
    The new Marc Jacobs cobranded travel kits also feature sustainable beauty products and toiletries developed in collaboration with Copenhagen-based design brand FRAMA, it said.
    該公司表示，全新的 Marc Jacobs 聯名旅行組也採用了與哥本哈根設計品牌 FRAMA 合作開發的永續美容產品和盥洗用品。
  • expect v. 預期、期待
    Meanwhile, the airline expects to receive two Boeing 787-9 aircraft by the end of this year...
    同時，該航空公司預計在今年底前接收兩架波音 787-9 客機...
  • sponsor v. 贊助
    The Taipei-Amsterdam flight was exclusively sponsored by CTBC Bank, which also sponsored China Airlines’ aviation challenge in 2024.
    台北至阿姆斯特丹航班由中國信託銀行獨家贊助，該銀行也贊助了華航 2024 年的永續飛行挑戰。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（V-ing）作後位修飾
    China Airlines yesterday completed its showcase flight for its fifth participation in the SkyTeam’s Aviation Challenge, operating a direct flight from Taipei to Amsterdam...
    句尾的「operating a direct flight...」是由現在分詞片語引導的補充說明，用來形容前面主句所完成的飛行動作及其細節，相當於「and it operated...」的簡化用法，在新聞英文中非常常見。
  • 過去分詞（V-ed）作後位修飾
    ...sustainable aviation fuel (SAF) equivalent to 40 percent of the flight’s fuel use.
    形容詞片語「equivalent to...」省略了「which is」，直接由過去分詞/形容詞equivalent開頭，用來修飾前面的名詞「sustainable aviation fuel (SAF)」，使句子更為精簡緊湊。
  • 被動語態的應用
    The Taipei-Amsterdam flight was exclusively sponsored by CTBC Bank...
    此句使用被動語態「was ... sponsored by」，強調受事者（The Taipei-Amsterdam flight，台北-阿姆斯特丹航班）以及其贊助者，當新聞焦點在於活動或航班本身被誰支持時，常使用被動語態。
  • 關係代名詞子句（which引導）
    ...which are expected to reduce fuel consumption by 20 percent and noise levels by 30 percent, Chen said.
    句中的「which」為關係代名詞，用逗點隔開形成非限定用法（補充說明），先行詞為前面的「two Boeing 787-9 aircraft」，用來補充這兩架新飛機預期帶來的成效。
