教育部表揚學生發明家

Ministry honors student-inventors

單字解析

  • commend v. 表揚，稱贊
    The Ministry of Education yesterday commended Taiwanese student-inventors who have won international awards...
    教育部昨天表揚了獲得國際獎項的台灣學生發明家...
  • preside over phr. v. 主持，主辦
    Deputy Minister of Education Liu Kuo-wei (劉國偉) presided over a ceremony honoring students...
    教育部政務次長劉國偉主持了一場表揚學生的典禮...
  • enhance v. 提高，增加，增強
    The game Attention Master (專注力大師) — designed to enhance the attention span and interpersonal skills of children with ADHD — won first prize...
    這款名為「專注力大師」的遊戲旨在提高ADHD兒童的專注力持續時間和人際交往能力，贏得了第一獎...
  • public use n. phr. 公眾使用，大眾使用
    ...adding that they would make free copies of the program for public use.
    ...並補充說他們將製作該程式的免費複本供公眾使用。
  • feedback n. 回饋，反應
    ...whose judges gave them valuable feedback to improve it to the refined version that won gold medals, he said.
    ...他說，當時的評審給予他們寶貴的回饋，以將其改進為贏得金牌的精進版本。
  • consist of phr. v. 由……組成，包含
    Codesigner Cheng Hui-han (鄭暉翰) said Attention Master consists of six modes...
    共同設計者鄭暉翰表示，「專注力大師」由六種模式組成...
  • instant adj. 立即的，即時的
    An important element is that the exercises were gamified and capable of giving instant feedback to keep users entertained, Cheng said.
    鄭暉翰說，一個重要的元素是這些練習經過遊戲化，且能夠提供即時回饋以保持使用者的樂趣。
  • panel n. 專家小組，評審小組
    Liu said he used to serve on the panel of science fairs and the hard work of the students and teachers who guided them had never failed to impress.
    劉表示，他過去曾擔任科學展覽的評審委員，學生們以及指導他們的老師所付出的努力從未讓人感到失望。
  • educator n. 教育工作者，教育家
    Taiwanese students have won 106 gold medals in youth science and invention competitions in the past two years, thanks in no small part to the work of educators, he added.
    他補充說，多虧了教育工作者的努力，台灣學生在過去兩年中已在青年科學與發明競賽中贏得了 106 面金牌。

文法重點

  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    The Ministry of Education yesterday commended Taiwanese student-inventors who have won international awards...
    句中的 "who have won international awards" 是用來修飾前方先行詞 "Taiwanese student-inventors" 的主格關係子句，補充說明這些獲獎學生的身分。
  • 過去分詞（分詞片語）作後置修飾
    The game Attention Master (專注力大師) — designed to enhance the attention span and interpersonal skills of children with ADHD — won first prize...
    破折號夾入的 "designed to enhance..." 是過去分詞片語，省略了 "which is"，用來修飾前面的主詞 "The game Attention Master"。
  • 對等連接詞引導的分詞構句（補充說明）
    “The game has been tested in hospitals and the design team’s school, and the results have been positive,” team members told reporters, adding that they would make free copies of the program for public use.
    句尾的 ", adding that..." 是現在分詞構句，主詞與前面子句的主詞一致（team members），用來補充說明他們在受訪時所說的額外內容。
  • 被動語態
    An important element is that the exercises were gamified and capable of giving instant feedback...
    句中 "the exercises were gamified" 使用了被動語態（be + 過去分詞），強調練習本身「被賦予遊戲化（gamified）」。
