生育率下降可能會削弱澳洲的好運

The baby bust could dent Australia’s lucky streak

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • benefit v. 受益，得益
    It rode a resources boom, enjoyed the glory days of China’s expansion and benefitted from the dismantling of trade barriers.
    它搭上了原資源繁榮的順風車，享受了中國經濟擴張的光輝歲月，並受益於貿易壁壘的拆除。
  • contribution n. 貢獻，促成因素
    Yet population growth, a vital contributor, has been downplayed, despite its crucial role in the record 29-year stretch without a recession until the COVID-19 pandemic.
    然而，作為一個至關重要的促成因素，人口增長卻一直被淡化，儘管在新冠疫情爆發之前，它在長達 29 年未經歷經濟衰退的紀錄中扮演了關鍵角色。
  • implement v. 實施，執行
    The hope is that by highlighting the changes early, businesses, consumers and officials across a range of institutions can make adjustments before drastic measures have to be implemented.
    人們希望透過及早突顯這些變化，各機構的企業、消費者和官員可以在必須實施激烈措施之前做出調整。
  • transition n. 轉變，過渡
    There is already much anxiety around the world about the transition to much smaller families or households with no children.
    對於向規模小得多或沒有孩子的家庭過渡一事，世界各地已經存在許多焦慮。
  • impact n. 衝擊，影響
    That has been the experience of countries that thought the real impact of the baby bust was still a way off — and are now scrambling to steady the slide in fertility.
    那些曾以為嬰兒潮低谷的真正衝擊還很遙遠、而現在正倉促平抑生育率下滑的國家，都有過這樣的經驗。
  • labor force n. phr. 勞動力，勞動人口
    The economy would grow more slowly, as would the labor force.
    經濟成長將放緩，勞動力也將如此。
  • forecast v. / n. 預測，預報
    Whatever the nation looks like mid-century, there would be fewer Australians than previously forecast to experience it — and those who are there would be more elderly.
    無論世紀中葉的國家面貌如何，正如先前預測的那樣，體驗它的澳洲人將會減少，而在那裡的人口也將更加高齡化。
  • prior adj. 先前的，先前的
    The fertility rate would probably drop to 1.34 by 2065, less than the 1.62 that the prior paper in 2023 anticipated.
    到 2065 年，生育率可能會降至 1.34，低於 2023 年上一份報告預期的 1.62。
  • project v. 預測，推測
    The UN caused waves a few years ago when it projected that the number of Earth’s inhabitants would peak in the 2080s and then retreat.
    幾年前，聯合國預測地球居民數量將在 2080 年代達到頂峰隨後回落，這曾在當時引發了轟動。
  • priority n. 優先事項，首要之務
    The assessment chimes with the center-left Labor government’s political priorities.
    這項評估與中間偏左工黨政府的政治優先事項不謀而合。
  • approach n. 方法，途徑
    Banning smartphones is not a practical approach.
    禁止智慧型手機並不是一個實際的方法。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況/結果
    Behind the sober analysis in the Australian report lurks a confronting question: Does a world where deaths outnumber births and a tight rein on new arrivals mean the best of times would be left behind?
    句首使用了地方副詞片語「Behind the sober analysis...」引起倒裝（主動詞對調：lurks a confronting question），而在句子後半段則是利用子句與修飾語堆疊。此處雖非標準分詞構句，但文章常見由介系詞片語開頭倒裝的結構，用來強調地點或來源，並將真正的主語（a confronting question）置於句尾保持平衡。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    The fertility rate would probably drop to 1.34 by 2065, less than the 1.62 that the prior paper in 2023 anticipated.
  • 假設語氣 (Subjunctive / Unreal Conditional structure)
    If anything, the changes would be more pronounced than leaders are prepared to countenance.
    句中的 "would be" 表達了一種推測或假設性的語氣，用來描述如果發生某些情況時可能產生的結果，比直接用現在式或未來式更能表達委婉或不確定性。
  • 同位語 (Appositive)
    (A reading of 2.1 in the total fertility rate, a measure of the average number of children a woman has during childbearing years, is widely regarded as the level at which society can replenish itself.)
    括號中的逗點後方「a measure of the average number of children...」是前面「the total fertility rate」的同位語，用來補充說明總生育率的定義與計算方式，使讀者不需另外查閱即可理解其含意。
