解決 STEM 教育工作者短缺問題

Addressing the shortage in STEM educators

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • shortage n. 短缺，不足
    The shortage of natural science, physics and chemistry teachers in elementary and secondary schools has become a serious issue.
    國民中小學自然科學、物理和化學教師的短缺已成為一個嚴重問題。
  • issue n. 議題，問題
    The social responsibility of caring for and supporting local education has become an issue of growing importance for many universities.
    關懷與支持地方教育的社會責任，對許多大學而言已成為日益重要的議題。
  • assist v. 協助，幫助
    Faced with a short-term lack of teachers, universities could consider fulfilling their social responsibility by assisting elementary and secondary schools in addressing their needs.
    面對短期的師資荒，大學可以透過協助中小學解決其需求，來實踐其社會責任。
  • establish v. 建立，設立
    For example, in situations where there is a shortage of certified teachers, a system could be established allowing doctoral students in science and engineering to teach on a short-term, part-time basis.
    例如，在合格教師短缺的情況下，可以建立一套制度，允許理工科系的博士生以短期、兼任的方式授課。
  • certified adj. 具備證照的，合格的
    Although doctoral students might not hold teaching certification, their professional training would make them an important source of supplementary faculty.
    儘管博士生可能未持有教師證照，但他們的專業訓練將使他們成為補充師資的重要來源。
  • compensation n. 補貼，薪酬，賠償
    The system should be designed to supplement — rather than replace — certified teachers, and establish training, compensation and mentoring mechanisms to ensure teaching quality.
    該系統的設計應當是補充——而不是取代——合格教師，並建立培訓、薪資補貼與指導機制以確保教學品質。
  • abundant adj. 豐富的，大量的
    Furthermore, Taiwan’s thriving tech industry has created abundant career opportunities and relatively generous salaries for science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM) graduates...
    此外，台灣蓬勃發展的科技產業為科學、技術、工程及數學（STEM）畢業生創造了豐富的職涯機會與相對優渥的薪資...
  • incentive n. 誘因，鼓勵
    ...diminishing the incentive for talented STEM professionals to enter the fields of elementary and secondary-school education.
    ...降低了優秀的 STEM 專業人才進入中小學教育領域的意願（誘因）。
  • investment n. 投資
    In addition to increasing public investment in education, the government could encourage companies and foundations that recognize the importance of education...
    除了增加公共教育投資外，政府可以鼓勵體認到教育重要性的企業與基金會...
  • contribute v. 貢獻，捐獻
    ...to contribute resources by establishing a science education support program.
    ...透過建立科學教育支援計畫來貢獻資源。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase)
    Faced with a short-term lack of teachers, universities could consider fulfilling their social responsibility by assisting elementary and secondary schools in addressing their needs.
    句首的「Faced with...」是由過去分詞引導的分詞片語，用來修飾主詞 universities（原句等於 Because they are faced with...），在英文新聞與商業寫作中非常常見，能簡潔地表達原因或條件。
  • 關係代名詞子句作修飾語 (Relative Clause)
    Doctoral students who have received teaching or teaching-assistant training and possess the necessary professional expertise could be selected to teach natural science...
    句中的「who have received... and possess...」是用來修飾先行詞「Doctoral students」的限制性關係代名詞子句，用以精確界定是哪一類博士生能被選派授課。
  • 虛擬語氣 / 假設情況 (Subjunctive / Conditional)
    If the trend continues, the impact would extend beyond class schedules and teaching quality — it could potentially undermine students’ scientific foundations...
    此句使用第一型條件句（If + 現在式, will/would + 原形動詞），用來推測當某個現狀持續時，未來可能會造成的潛在衝擊與連鎖反應。
