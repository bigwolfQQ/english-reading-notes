文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Insect repellent or pesticides containing pyrethrins or pyrethroids will be required to include a cat warning label...
    句型為「be + 過去分詞 (required)」，用來強調動作的承受者（殺蟲劑被要求），而不需要特別指出是誰發出這個要求。
  • 分詞片語作後置修飾 (Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    Some commercially available insect repellents or pesticides containing pyrethrins... are labeled with the statement...
    句中的「containing pyrethrins」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「insect repellents or pesticides」，相當於形容詞子句「which contain pyrethrins」。
  • 對等連接詞連接的並列結構 (Parallel Structure)
    The animal shelter is well-ventilated and well-lit with an advanced drainage system to ensure good living quality for sheltered animals...
    句中的「well-ventilated」與「well-lit」透過「and」連接，形成形容詞的並列結構，共同用來描述主詞 the animal shelter 的狀態。
