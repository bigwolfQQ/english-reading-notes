Thailand, Myanmar flood deaths increasing

單字解析

  • declare v. 宣布，宣告
    Bangkok, which declared a disaster this weekend following heavy rains, the Thai government said yesterday.
    泰國政府昨天表示，在經歷強降雨後，曼谷已於本週末宣布進入災難狀態。
  • mitigation n. 減輕，緩和
    according to the Thai Department of Disaster Prevention and Mitigation (DDPM)
    根據泰國防減災廳（DDPM）指出
  • exclude v. 把...排除在外
    Excluding Bangkok, more than 500,000 households and 1.7 million people across 27 provinces in Thailand have been affected by flooding
    防減災廳表示，除了曼谷之外，泰國 27 個省份已有超過 50 萬戶家庭和 170 萬人口受洪災影響。
  • impact v. 影響，衝擊
    About 52,000 households were impacted in the capital
    大約 5特2千戶家庭在首都受到影響
  • stabilize v. 使穩定，平穩
    No deaths have been reported in Bangkok, and the DDPM said water levels had stabilized.
    曼谷尚未傳出死亡通報，防減災廳表示水位已經穩定。
  • urge v. 敦促，力勸
    Schools have been closed, and people urged to work from home
    學校已經關閉，民眾也被敦促居家辦公
  • official adj. 官方的，正式的
    Authorities have not yet released any official toll in a country where a civil war complicates aid efforts.
    在內戰使救援工作變得複雜的國家裡，當局尚未發布任何官方傷亡人數。
  • complicate v. 使複雜化
    Authorities have not yet released any official toll in a country where a civil war complicates aid efforts.
    在內戰使救援工作變得複雜的國家裡，當局尚未發布任何官方傷亡人數。
  • scale n. 規模，範圍
    The conflict often makes even the scale of natural disasters hard for humanitarian organizations to judge.
    這場衝突往往讓國際人道組織難以評估自然災害的規模。
  • lack n. 缺乏，短缺
    We are inundated not only with the scale of the flood but also with the lack of resources
    我們不僅被洪災的規模所淹沒，也面臨資源匱乏的困境。
  • vulnerable adj. 易受影響的，脆弱的
    Myanmar, a country of 51 million people, ranks among the 10 nations most vulnerable to climate change
    緬甸這個擁有 5,100 萬人口的國家，名列全球最易受氣候變遷衝擊的 10 個國家之一。
  • assistance n. 協助，援助
    It has suffered from severe aid cutbacks following US President Donald Trump’s decision to slash Washington’s humanitarian assistance budget.
    在美國總統唐納德·川普決定大砍華盛頓的人道援助預算後，該國遭受了嚴重的人道援助削減。

文法重點

  • 非限定關係代名詞子句（補充說明）
    Bangkok, which declared a disaster this weekend following heavy rains, the Thai government said yesterday.
    句中的「which declared a disaster this weekend following heavy rains」前面有逗號，屬於非限定用法，用來補充說明主詞 Bangkok（曼谷）的狀況，不能省略關代，且不能用 that。
  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾
    Myanmar’s southernmost Tanintharyi region has been hit by heavy rains since Friday night, part of an intense weather system hammering the region.
    句尾的「hammering the region」是由現在分詞（-ing）引導的片語，用來修飾前方的名詞片語「an intense weather system」（強烈的天氣系統），相當於形容詞子句「that hammers the region」。
  • 被動語態
    Schools have been closed, and people urged to work from home
    此處使用現在完成式的被動語態（have been + p.p.），用來強調學校「被關閉」這個動作的結果。第二個子句中省略了重複的助動詞 have been（people [have been] urged...）。
  • 對等連接詞連接的對稱結構（Not only... but also...）
    We are inundated not only with the scale of the flood but also with the lack of resources
    此句使用「not only A but also B」的對等句構，連接兩個介詞片語（with the scale... 與 with the lack...），強調受災戶不僅面臨洪水規模，也面臨資源不足的問題。
