針對 SGA 嬰兒監測的提案正式公布

Proposal for monitoring babies with SGA unveiled

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • proposal n. 提案，建議
    Proposal for monitoring babies with SGA unveiled
    針對 SGA 嬰兒監測的提案正式公布
  • unveil v. 公布，推出，揭幕
    Health officials and doctors yesterday unveiled proposed measures for the long-term monitoring of infants
    衛生官員與醫師於昨日公布了針對嬰兒進行長期監測的擬議措施
  • measure n. 措施，方法
    Health officials and doctors yesterday unveiled proposed measures for the long-term monitoring of infants
    衛生官員與醫師於昨日公布了針對嬰兒進行長期監測的擬議措施
  • seminar n. 研討會
    The Health Promotion Administration (HPA) and Taiwan Society of Neonatology members made the remarks at a seminar in Taipei
    國民健康署與台灣新生兒科醫學會成員在台北的一場研討會上發表了這些言論
  • ensure v. 確保，保證
    HPA Director-General Shen Ching-fen (沈靜芬) said the government is considering including SGA in the “infant healthbook” to ensure primary care pediatrians are fully informed
    國健署署長沈靜芬表示，政府正在考慮將 SGA 納入「兒童健康手冊」中，以確保基層小兒科醫師充分了解
  • mechanism n. 機制，體制
    The mechanism allow hospitals and clinics caring for a child to be aware of childhood problems such as SGA
    這項機制能讓照顧兒童的醫院與診所意識到 SGA 等兒童期問題
  • crucial adj. 至關重要的，關鍵的
    The mechanism allow hospitals and clinics caring for a child to be aware of childhood problems such as SGA, which is crucial for their future health, she said.
    她表示，這項機制能讓照顧兒童的醫院與診所意識到 SGA 等兒童期問題，這對他們未來的健康至關重要。
  • regular adj. 定期的，規律的
    Many parents are not aware that infants with SGA must undergo regular check-ups past the age of two, Shen said.
    沈靜芬說，許多家長並不知道患有 SGA 的嬰兒在兩歲之後必須接受定期檢查。
  • essential adj. 不可或缺的，必要的
    Monitoring the growth of such children is essential to making sure that they receive the care they need
    監測這些兒童的發育對於確保他們獲得所需的照護是不可或缺的
  • evaluate v. 評估
    many of the metrics used to evaluate SGA infants are the same as those already being recorded
    用於評估 SGA 嬰兒的許多指標，與已經在記錄的指標相同
  • facilitate v. 促進，使容易
    People born as SGA babies must be tracked for disorders linked to these conditions to faciliate preventive care or early treatment, he said.
    他表示，必須追蹤出生時為 SGA 的嬰兒是否有與這些狀況相關的疾病，以促進預防性照護或早期治療。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Health officials and doctors yesterday unveiled proposed measures for the long-term monitoring of infants who are “small for gestatational age” (SGA), ahead of Sunday’s Growth Awareness Day.
    句中的「proposed」是過去分詞作形容詞，用來修飾其後的名詞「measures」；而「monitoring」則是動名詞。過去分詞表被動或完成，用以簡化形容詞子句（原句形狀如 which are proposed）。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    The mechanism allow hospitals and clinics caring for a child to be aware of childhood problems such as SGA, which is crucial for their future health, she said.
    句中的「which is crucial for their future health」是非限定關係子句，以關代「which」開頭，補充說明前面整件事（SGA 等兒童期問題）對未來健康的重要性，逗號後不可改用「that」。
  • 條件與假設語氣的簡化 (Conditional / Participial construction)
    Infants with SGA often make up for their small size in growth spurts, if given good nutrition and care...
    「if given good nutrition and care」是省略了主詞與be動詞（原貌為 if they are given...）的分詞構句，用來表示條件，使句子更為精簡有力，常出現在新聞英語中。
