文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...approved the incommunicado detention of a man who allegedly held an airline intern at knifepoint at Taiwan Taoyuan International Airport a day earlier, following a request from the Taoyuan District Prosecutors' Office.
    句尾的「following a request from...」是由現在分詞 "following" 引導的分詞片語，用來補充說明前面整個裁定動作發生的背景或依據（即「在接到……的請求之後」）。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The suspect, surnamed Tsai (蔡), was ordered detained for two months from Sunday after prosecutors sought his detention...
    句中的 "was ordered detained" 採用了被動語態，用來表達嫌犯「被法院命令羈押」，強調動作的承受者而非主動發起者。
  • 獨立分詞結構表達附帶結果 (Participial Phrase indicating Result)
    The court said Tsai has no permanent residence or contact phone number and that his wife and children live in Hong Kong, making him a flight risk.
    句尾的 ", making him a flight risk" 是現在分詞 "making" 所帶動的結果分詞構句，用來表達前面所提種種狀況所自然導致的結果（即「使他成為有逃亡之虞的人」）。
