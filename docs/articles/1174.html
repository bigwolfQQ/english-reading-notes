Five detained in RAF base counterterror operation

單字解析

  • law enforcement n. 執法，執法部門
    British law enforcement personnel were questioning five men on Monday on suspicion of explosives and terrorism offenses...
    英國執法人員於週一正在訊問五名男子，因他們涉嫌爆炸物和恐怖主義犯罪...
  • suspected adj. 嫌疑的，涉嫌的
    British law enforcement personnel were questioning five men on Monday on suspicion of explosives and terrorism offenses after they were detained near an air base...
    英國執法人員於週一正在訊問五名男子，因他們涉嫌爆炸物和恐怖主義犯罪，此前他們在一個空軍基地附近被拘留...
  • detain v. 拘留，扣留
    ...after they were detained near an air base that has been used by the US to attack Iran.
    ...此前他們在一個曾被美國用於攻擊伊朗的空軍基地附近被拘留。
  • investigation n. 調查
    ...and had been under investigation by British and US authorities.
    ...並且一直受到英國和美國當局的調查。
  • authority n. 官方，當局
    ...and had been under investigation by British and US authorities.
    ...並且一直受到英國和美國當局的調查。
  • source n. 消息來源
    ...but a source familiar with the investigation said an Iranian-linked motive was considered the most likely...
    ...但一名熟悉調查的消息人士表示，與伊朗有關的動機被認為是最有可能的...
  • motive n. 動機
    ...said an Iranian-linked motive was considered the most likely...
    ...表示與伊朗有關的動機被認為是最有可能的...
  • speculate v. 推測，臆測
    UK Secretary of State for Defence Wes Streeting said in an interview that he would not speculate about motives of a live investigation.
    英國國防大臣韋斯·斯特里廷在受訪時表示，他不會對一項進行中的調查動機進行臆測。
  • deny v. 否認
    Iran has repeatedly denied involvement in attacks or plots in the UK and elsewhere.
    伊朗已多次否認涉及在英國及其他地方的攻擊或陰謀。
  • authorization n. 授權，批准
    The British government had given authorization to the US to use bombers based at Fairford to strike at Iranian missile sites...
    英國政府曾授權美國使用駐扎在費爾福德的轟炸機，以打擊伊朗飛彈陣地...
  • target n. 目標
    ...saying any base used to launch attacks would be a legitimate target.
    ...並稱任何被用於發動攻擊的基地都將是合法的目標。
  • increase v. 增加，提高
    Security was increased at other US bases in Britain.
    英國其他美軍基地的安全戒備已提高。

文法重點

  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    ...near an air base that has been used by the US to attack Iran.
    使用關代 "that" 引導形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的先行詞 "an air base"（空軍基地），補充說明該基地的用途。
  • 現在完成式被動語態
    ...after they were detained near an air base that has been used by the US to attack Iran.
    子句中的 "has been used" 結合了現在完成式（has + 過去分詞）與被動語態（be + 過去分詞），強調該基地「過去持續至今一直被美國所使用」的狀態。
  • 過去完成進行式（被動語態）
    ...and had been under investigation by British and US authorities.
    "had been under investigation" 採用過去完成式的結構，強調在過去某個時間點之前，調查行動已經持續進行了一段時間。
  • 被動語態的綜合運用
    Security was increased at other US bases in Britain.
    句型為「主詞 + was + 過去分詞 (increased)」，強調動作的承受者（安全戒備 Security）而非執行者，是新聞報導中極常見的客觀敘述句型。
