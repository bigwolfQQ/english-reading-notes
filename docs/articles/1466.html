赫格塞斯看到了戰爭的未來，卻無法引領方向

Hegseth saw the future of war, but cannot lead the way

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • address v. 向……發表演說、對付、處理
    US Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth got one big thing almost right when he addressed junior leaders of the joint force the other day.
    美國國防部長皮特·赫格塞斯日前在向聯合部隊的初級領導人發表演說時，有一件大事幾乎說對了。
  • strategy n. 戰略、策略（文章中出現衍生形容詞 strategic）
    Hegseth chose the analogy because it shows the strategic importance of so-called cost-exchange asymmetries.
    赫格塞斯選擇這個比喻，是因為它展示了所謂「成本交換不對稱」的戰略重要性。
  • counter v. 反擊、反制、應對
    The other side — the English then, the Iranians or Ukrainians today — can then counter with weapons that are just barely good enough individually, but devastating in vast numbers...
    而另一方（當時的英國，如今的伊朗或烏克蘭）則可以用單兵表現勉強足夠、但數量龐大時具備毀滅性破壞力的武器來反擊……
  • rely v. 依賴、信賴（通常接 on/upon）
    That is, it relies far too much on exquisite weapons that are great for, say, bombing Iranian nuclear facilities out of the blue...
    也就是說，它過分依賴那些精密的武器——這些武器非常適合用來執行諸如去年那樣出其不意地轟炸伊朗核設施的任務……
  • procurement n. 採購、採購部
    So the US military needs to shift its procurement and its tactical and strategic models away from exquisite weaponry and toward what Horowitz calls “precise mass.”
    因此，美軍需要將其採購以及戰術和戰略模式從精美武器轉向霍洛維茨所稱的「精確量產」。
  • objective n. 目標、目的
    That, in a nutshell, is the objective of Project Agincourt.
    簡而言之，這就是「阿金庫爾計畫」的目標。
  • prioritize v. 把……列為優先、優先考慮
    “I am often asked whether the US military should prioritize high-volume, low-cost equipment or exquisite programs,” Driscoll writes...
    「我經常被問到美軍是否應該優先考慮高產量、低成本的設備或精美計畫，」德里斯科爾寫道……
  • dismiss v. 解雇、開除、駁回
    However, George was dismissed by Hegseth in April, and Driscoll resigned just weeks before Hegseth’s address.
    然而，喬治在4月遭到赫格塞斯解職，而德里斯科爾則在赫格塞斯發表演說前幾周辭職。
  • substantive adj. 實質的、重大的
    The clashes extend beyond personalities and include substantive differences.
    這些衝突超越了個人恩怨，還包括實質的分歧。
  • capacity n. 容量、能力、生產力
    “The United States’ greatest deterrent, the foundation of its diplomatic clout and the primary factor in its ability to win on the modern battlefield will be its collective allied industrial capacity and military interoperability,” Driscoll wrote.
    「美國最大的威懾力量、其外交影響力的基石，以及在現代戰場上獲勝能力的關鍵因素，將是其集體的盟友工業產能與軍事互通性，」德里斯科爾寫道。

文法重點

  • 關聯詞 / 附屬連接詞引導的副詞子句（Even though...）
    What he got right, and also captured elegantly in a historical analogy, was the direction in which warfare is developing, and in which the Pentagon must go as well, even though it is not set up to do so.
    「even though」意為「雖然、儘管」，用來引導一個讓步子句，表達兩個事實之間的對比（五角大廈必須往某個方向走，儘管它目前的體制並未準備好這麼做）。
  • 關係代間質與介系詞提前（in which）
    What he got right, and also captured elegantly in a historical analogy, was the direction in which warfare is developing, and in which the Pentagon must go as well...
    句中的「in which」代替先行詞 direction。因為原本的片語是 develop in the direction 以及 go in the direction，當 direction 變成先行詞被拿到前方時，介系詞 in 跟著移到關係代名詞 which 的前面，形成較正式的句構。
  • 現在分詞作後位修飾（...working near the front lines）
    ...the English showed up with archers carrying longbows and supplied with vast numbers of arrows made by fletchers working near the front lines.
    句尾的「working near the front lines」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 fletchers（製箭匠），相當於形容詞子句「who worked near the front lines」，分詞片語能讓句子更精簡流暢。
  • 強調句構與倒裝/插入結構（That is...）
    That is, it relies far too much on exquisite weapons that are great for, say, bombing Iranian nuclear facilities out of the blue, as in last year, but are uneconomic against seemingly limitless barrages of attritable Iranian ordnance, as in this year.
    「That is」意即「也就是說」，常用於申論或補充說明前一句的觀點。句中夾雜了「say」（比方說）與「as in last year」（如同去年那樣）等插入語，使得長句的結構層次更加分明。
