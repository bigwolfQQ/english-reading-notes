活到老，學到老：永遠不會太遲去學習新事物

You are never too old to learn new things

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • advance v. 前進，發展，進步
    The faster technology advances, the easier it is for many retired people to mistakenly think...
    科技進步得越快，許多退休人士就越容易誤以為...
  • obstacle n. 障礙，阻礙
    After using artificial intelligence (AI) and smart-home technology, I have become convinced that age is not the real obstacle.
    在使用人工智慧（AI）與智慧家庭科技之後，我深信年齡並不是真正的阻礙。
  • appliance n. 家用器具，家電
    Smart cameras can allow people to check on their homes and talk to family members, smart plugs allow people to turn off appliances while they are away...
    智慧攝影機可以讓人們查看家裡狀況並與家人對話，智慧插座讓人在外出時能關閉電器...
  • alert n. 警報，警戒
    ...and sensors can send alerts when doors or windows are opened or a water leaks occur.
    ...且感應器則能在門窗被打開或發生漏水時發送警報。
  • encounter v. 遭遇，遇到
    Upon first encountering ChatGPT, Gemini and other generative AI systems, many feel intimidated.
    初次接觸 ChatGPT、Gemini 等生成式 AI 系統時，許多人都會感到畏懼。
  • intimidated adj. 受到威脅的，感到害怕的，畏懼的
    Upon first encountering ChatGPT, Gemini and other generative AI systems, many feel intimidated.
    初次接觸 ChatGPT、Gemini 等生成式 AI 系統時，許多人都會感到畏懼。
  • assistant n. 助理，助手
    ...they can simply treat it as a personal assistant that can chat and answer questions.
    ...他們只需將其視為可以聊天、回答問題的個人助理。
  • organize v. 組織，整理
    When traveling abroad, they can ask it to organize information.
    出國旅遊時，他們可以請它整理資訊。
  • guidance n. 指導，引導
    Try, make a mistake, receive guidance from a teacher, try again, succeed and build confidence.
    嘗試、犯錯、接受老師的指導、再次嘗試、成功並建立信心。
  • independent adj. 獨立的
    After retirement, people can learn how to use it for life — to make themselves more independent, make life safer and more convenient...
    退休後，人們可以為了生活而學習使用科技——讓自己更獨立、生活更安全便利...

文法重點

  • the + 比較級 ..., the + 比較級 ...（越……，越……）
    The faster technology advances, the easier it is for many retired people to mistakenly think that tech is only for young people...
    此句型用來表示兩件事物同時變化。前半句「The faster technology advances」中的 faster 是 fast 的比較級；後半句「the easier it is」中的 easier 是 easy 的比較級，用來表達科技進步得越快，退休人士就越容易產生某種誤解。
  • it is + 形容詞 + for + 某人 + to V（對某人而言做某事是……的）
    ...the easier it is for many retired people to mistakenly think that tech is only for young people...
    句中的 it 是虛主詞，真正的主持人是後方的不定詞片語「to mistakenly think...」。介詞片語「for many retired people」則用來指明這個動作是對哪些人而言。
  • 間接問句（疑問詞引導的名詞子句）
    What matters more is how we learn and whether we are willing to take the first step.
    句中的「how we learn」與「whether we are willing to take the first step」是疑問詞引導的名詞子句，在句中擔任主詞補語。注意子句內部的語序必須是「主詞 + 動詞」（即 we learn，而不是 how do we learn）。
  • 分詞構句簡化時間副詞子句
    Upon first encountering ChatGPT, Gemini and other generative AI systems, many feel intimidated.
    「Upon first encountering...」是由介詞 Upon 加上動名詞片語構成，這裡省去了原本的副詞子句主體（例如 When they encounter...），用來表示「一接觸到……時」的時間點，使句子更加簡潔流暢。
