搭車數日後被發現陳屍公車上；客運業者遭罰款

Dead passenger found on bus days after boarding; operator fined

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • operator n. 經營者、業者
    A man was found dead on a bus in New Taipei on Sunday, several days after he boarded, and the bus operator has been fined, according to authorities.
    據相關當局表示，一名男子週日在新北市的一輛公車上被發現死亡，距離他上車已經過了好幾天，且該公車業者已被處以罰款。
  • fine v. / n. 處以罰款、罰金
    The Taipei City government told CNA Sunday that after an initial investigation of the incident, its Public Transportation Office has fined the bus operator Sindian Bus NT$30,000 (US$943.93).
    台北市政府週日向中央社表示，經過對此事件的初步調查，其公共運輸處已對新店客運處以新台幣 30,000 元（約合 943.93 美元）的罰款。
  • issue v. 發布、發行
    Also on Sunday, Sindian Bus issued a statement, saying that at around 7 a.m. that day, a passenger who boarded in New Taipei's Xindian District reported to the driver that a man on the bus appeared to be unconscious.
    同在週日，新店客運發布聲明表示，當天上午 7 點左右，一名在新北市新店區上車的乘客向司機通報，稱車上一名男子似乎意識不清。
  • terminal n. 總站、終點站
    The bus, which runs in Taipei and New Taipei, returned to its terminal in Xindian at around 10:30 p.m. Thursday, with the man still aboard, police said.
    警方指出，這輛行駛於台北市與新北市的公車於週四晚上 10 點 30 分左右返回新店的總站，當時該名男子仍在車上。
  • resume v. 恢復、重新開始
    After the bus was parked at the terminal Thursday night, it did not resume service again until Sunday morning, because of the long Moon Festival weekend, which began Friday, Kao said.
    高燦煌表示，自週五開始的中秋節連續假期因此公車在週四晚上停妥於總站後，直到週日上午才又恢復營運。
  • cooperate v. 合作、配合
    The company will cooperate with the police investigation and will strengthen training for its drivers to ensure that they check the buses properly before parking them at the end of the day, Kao said.
    高燦煌表示，公司將配合警方調查，並加強對司機的培訓，以確保他們在每天結束行程將公車停妥前確實進行檢查。
  • strengthen v. 加強、鞏固
    The company will cooperate with the police investigation and will strengthen training for its drivers to ensure that they check the buses properly before parking them at the end of the day, Kao said.
    高燦煌表示，公司將配合警方調查，並加強對司機的培訓，以確保他們在每天結束行程將公車停妥前確實進行檢查。
  • ensure v. 確保、保證
    The company will cooperate with the police investigation and will strengthen training for its drivers to ensure that they check the buses properly before parking them at the end of the day, Kao said.
    高燦煌表示，公司將配合警方調查，並加強對司機的培訓，以確保他們在每天結束行程將公車停妥前確實進行檢查。
  • suspend v. 暫停、停職
    Meanwhile, Chen has been suspended from driving duties for failing to follow the proper procedure for clearing the bus, the company said.
    該公司表示，同時，陳姓司機因未遵循適當的清車程序已被暫停駕駛職務。
  • regulation n. 規章、法規
    Kuo Chien-chen (郭建辰), an official at the Taipei City Public Transportation Office, told CNA on Sunday that under his office's regulations, bus operators are required to train their drivers to clear their buses properly at the end of their service routes.
    台北市公共運輸處官員郭建辰週日告訴中央社，根據該處的規章，公車業者必須訓練其司機在服務路線結束時確實清空公車。
  • require v. 要求、規定
    Kuo Chien-chen (郭建辰), an official at the Taipei City Public Transportation Office, told CNA on Sunday that under his office's regulations, bus operators are required to train their drivers to clear their buses properly at the end of their service routes.
    台北市公共運輸處官員郭建辰週日告訴中央社，根據該處的規章，公車業者必須訓練其司機在服務路線結束時確實清空公車。
  • penalty n. 懲罰、罰款
    The office has fined Sindian Bus NT$30,000 for failing to fulfill its managerial responsibilities in connection with the incident, Kuo said, adding that the penalty was imposed in accordance with the Highway Act.
    郭建辰表示，該處已對新店客運處以新台幣 30,000 元罰款，理由是其未能善盡與此事件相關的管理責任，並補充說明該處罰是依據公路法規所開罰。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語表附帶狀況 (with + O. + 分詞)
    The bus, which runs in Taipei and New Taipei, returned to its terminal in Xindian at around 10:30 p.m. Thursday, with the man still aboard, police said.
    此處使用「with + 受詞 + 形容詞/副詞（或分詞）」的結構，用來補充說明主句動作發生時的伴隨狀況或背景情形，意指「在……的情況下」。
  • 現在分詞片語作補充說明 (V-ing)
    A forensic examination found no signs of trauma and ruled out foul play for the time being, police said, adding that the Taipei District Prosecutors Office has ordered an autopsy to determine the cause of death.
    句尾的「adding that...」是由現在分詞構成的附加說明分詞片語，主詞 police 同時發出 found/ruled out 與 adding 的動作，用來補充警方進一步說的話或新資訊。
  • 被動語態 (Be + 過去分詞)
    Meanwhile, Chen has been suspended from driving duties for failing to follow the proper procedure for clearing the bus, the company said.
    此句使用現在完成式的被動語態（has been suspended），強調陳姓司機「被」公司暫停職務的狀態，焦點在於動作的承受者而非發動者。
  • be required to + V (被動語態的衍生用法)
    Kuo Chien-chen (郭建辰), an official at the Taipei City Public Transportation Office, told CNA on Sunday that under his office's regulations, bus operators are required to train their drivers to clear their buses properly at the end of their service routes.
    「be required to」是由 require（要求）轉變而來的被動用法，意為「被要求去做某事」或「必須做某事」，在商業契約、法律條文或規定報導中非常高頻。
