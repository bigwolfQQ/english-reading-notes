Do not cut ties after losing allies

文法重點

  • 關係代名詞子句（使用介系詞 + which）
    Participation requires some channel through which prospective participants learn about the program and coordinate their attendance with Taiwanese authorities.
    句中的 "through which" 是「介系詞 + 關係代名詞」的結構，用來引導形容詞子句修飾先行詞 "channel"。在這裡，participants 是透過（through）這個管道（channel）來獲得資訊，故將介系詞置於關代 which 之前，營造出正式且精確的書面語氣。
  • 分詞構句作後位修飾 / 形容詞片語
    Juan Fernando Herrera Ramos is a Honduran journalist based in Taipei.
    句尾的 "based in Taipei" 是由過去分詞片語構成的後位修飾語，用來補充說明主詞 Juan Fernando Herrera Ramos（常駐於台北的）。這種結構省略了 "who isbased in Taipei"，使句子更為精簡流暢。
  • 讓步副詞子句（regardless of whether...）
    Those relationships could help introduce foreign lawmakers to members of the Legislative Yuan, facilitate participation in programs such as the Yuan Peng Class, identify opportunities for parliamentary delegations and maintain communication with political parties regardless of whether they are the ruling party or the opposition.
    句中的 "regardless of whether they are the ruling party or the opposition" 是一個表示讓步的片語結構（無論是否……）。此結構在商業與政治新聞中非常常見，用來表達條件不影響結果的發生。
