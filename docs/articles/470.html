文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle phrase as postmodifier)
    President William Lai (賴清德) yesterday promulgated a Chinese Nationalist Party (KMT)-led bill aimed at promoting defense autonomy and the uncrewed vehicle industry, following its passage by the legislature last month.
    句中的「aimed at promoting...」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞短語「a ... bill」。這類分詞片語相當於一個形容詞子句（也就是「which was aimed at promoting...」），但在英文新聞英語中為了簡潔，常省略關代與be動詞，直接用分詞來表達被動或狀態。
  • 對比與並列連接詞 (While introducing a contrast)
    The Legislative Yuan on Aug. 27 passed the Act on Strengthening Defense Autonomy and Developing the Unmanned Vehicles Industry..., which in principle provides NT$240 billion... over six years, while allowing additional funding subject to legislative approval.
    連接詞「while」在此處用來引導一個對比或伴隨的狀況，意指「同時也...」或「然而...」。前面說明了法案原則上提供的基本金額，而後面用「while allowing...」補充說明在特定條件（經立法院同意）下也允許額外資金的彈性。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞補語 (Gerund phrase as subject complement)
    As uncrewed vehicle technologies evolve rapidly, relying on special budgets tied to long-term procurement contracts would leave the military unable to keep pace with operational requirements...
    句首的「relying on special budgets tied to long-term procurement contracts」是一個動名詞片語，作為句子的主詞。動名詞片語本身具有動詞的性質（可接受詞 tied to...），但整體視為單數名詞，因此後面接助動詞 would。
  • 假設語氣（過去式/would）表達未來不太可能發生的推測 (Subjunctive/Modal of hypothetical result)
    ...and would ultimately weaken, rather than strengthen, the armed forces’ combat capabilities, he said.
    這裡的「would ultimately weaken」搭配前一句的假設情境，用過去助動詞 would 來表達若採取某種錯誤做法所可能產生的未來結果或推論語氣，展現出論述中的 hypothetical（假設性）觀點。
