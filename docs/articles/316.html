文法重點

  • 獨立結構 (Absolute Structure)
    It did so with two top aides removed after an investigation aired on Channel 4, its finances under police scrutiny and seven in 10 voters...
    「with + 名詞 + 過去分詞/介系詞片語」構成附帶狀況的獨立結構，用來補充說明主句發生時的同時狀態，在新聞英語中常用來緊湊地列舉多項背景事實。
  • 假設語氣過去式 (Subjunctive / Second Conditional)
    This, if true, would be unlawful.
  • 過去分詞作後置定語 (Past Participle as Post-modifier)
    A party accused of plotting how to get around the law is saying it would impose a “national emergency”...
    片語 "accused of..." 是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞 "A party"，相當於形容詞子句 "A party that is accused of..."，能使句子更精簡流暢。
