文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Postmodifier)
    Someone exposed to a language continuously from a young age...
    句中的「exposed to a language...」是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的代名詞 Someone，相當於關代子句「Someone who is exposed to a language...」的簡化，多益閱讀測驗常考這種分詞修飾結構。
  • 與現在事實相反的假設語氣 (Subjunctive Mood)
    If Taiwan develops a genuine bilingual education system from a young age, students would enter university with a stronger foundation in Mandarin and English.
    此句使用「If + 現在式動詞, 主詞 + would + 原形動詞」的結構，用來表達對現在或未來假設情況的推論，多益文法題常用於測試動詞時態的對應。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞與受詞
    That is very different from expecting university professors to bring students up to a required level of English...
    介系詞 from 後方接動名詞 expecting 作為受詞，而 expecting 後面又帶有「expect + O. + to do」的句型（expecting university professors to bring...），是多益相當常見的動詞搭配與句構。
  • Rather than... 複合片語用法
    Rather than dismissing teachers because they do not yet possess every qualification required by the Ministry of Education, Taiwan could invest in those who...
    「Rather than + V-ing」意指「而不是……」，用來連接兩個對比的動作，在多益商業閱讀與寫作中常用於表達決策的替代方案。
