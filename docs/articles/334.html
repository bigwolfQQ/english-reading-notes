炸彈之鄉的愛

Love in the time of bombs

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • delay n. 延誤，耽擱
    After repeated delays caused by the war with Russia, she was only hours away from marrying her partner...
    在經歷與俄羅斯戰爭導致的反覆延誤後，她距離嫁給伴侶僅剩幾個小時...
  • schedule v. 預定，安排
    ...threatening to stop the wedding scheduled for Tuesday morning.
    ...威脅著要阻止原定於周二上午舉行的婚禮。
  • resumable v. 重新開始，恢復（原動詞 resume）
    The assaults then resumed with drones and missiles.
    隨後，無人機和導彈攻擊又恢復了。
  • derail v. 破壞，阻撓（計畫等）
    Like many others in Kyiv, the couple decided the attacks would not derail their lives and forged ahead with the ceremony.
    就像基輔的许多其他人一樣，這對情侶決定這些攻擊不會破壞他們的生活，並繼續推進婚禮。
  • submit v. 提交，呈遞
    For six days straight, the couple went to the registry office but could not submit their documents because of constant air-raid sirens.
    連續六天，這對情侶去了登記處，但由於不斷的空襲警報而無法提交文件。
  • damage v. 損害，破壞
    Nobody could have predicted that their apartment would be badly damaged on the same day.
    沒有人能預料到他們的公寓會在同一天嚴重受損。
  • matter v. 重要，具有重大關係
    What mattered most to him, he said, was for his bride to be smiling.
    他說，對他來說最重要的是看到新娘微笑。
  • survive v. 倖存，活下來
    “We should all be celebrated, that we survived,” Liudmyla replied.
    「我們都應該為自己慶祝，因為我們倖存下來了，」柳德米拉回答。
  • match v. 相配，一致
    Leonid wore an embroidered vyshyvanka, a traditional Ukrainian shirt, with a pattern that matched the one on Liudmyla’s dress.
    列昂尼德穿著一件繡花的傳統烏克蘭襯衫，其圖案與柳德米拉禮服上的圖案相匹配。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...threatening to stop the wedding scheduled for Tuesday morning.
    句中的 "scheduled for Tuesday morning" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "the wedding"。這相當於形容詞子句 "which was scheduled for..." 的簡化版，省略了主格關代與be動詞，使句子更為精煉。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Absolute Participial Phrase)
    Their ears deafened by the explosion, the couple’s only immediate thought was the risk of a second strike.
    句首的 "Their ears deafened by the explosion" 是一個獨立分詞構句。因為分詞片語的邏輯主語（Their ears）與主要子句的主語（the couple's only immediate thought）不同，所以必須保留其專屬的主語。這裡省略了 be 動詞 being，用來表達原因或伴隨狀況。
  • 假設語氣過去式 (Subjunctive Mood)
    In a different scenario, “I wouldn’t be wearing this dress. Someone would be picking out something else for me instead, something darker.”
    此處使用假設語氣（would + 原形動詞 / would be + V-ing），用來表達與現在事實相反的假設情境。說話者並非身處黑暗的情境中，而是假設如果情境不同，現在身上穿的就會是別的衣服。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    Leonid wore an embroidered vyshyvanka, a traditional Ukrainian shirt, with a pattern that matched the one on Liudmyla’s dress.
    句中的 "that matched the one on Liudmyla’s dress" 是由關係代名詞 "that" 引導的限定性子句，用來修飾先行詞 "a pattern"，說明該圖案具體符合什麼特徵。
