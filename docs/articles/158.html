Taipei Times · 2026-09-06

國防預算與更多軍備生產

Military budgets more arms production

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • production capacity n. phr. 生產能量、產能
    The military is significantly expanding domestic production capacity to improve self-sufficiency in equipment and ammunition during wartime, the Ministry of National Defense’s proposed budget for next year showed.
    國防部明年度的預算案顯示，軍方正在顯著擴大國內生產能量，以提高戰時裝備與彈藥的自給自足能力。
  • invest v. 投資、投入（資金、時間等）
    The Armaments Bureau’s Production and Manufacturing Center plans to invest more than NT$14.15 billion (US$447.4 million) from this year to 2032 to establish or expand production capacity at four of its factories, it showed.
    預算案顯示，軍備局生產製造中心計畫從今年到2032年投入超過141.5億元新台幣（約4.474億美元），在其屬下的四個兵工廠建立或擴大生產能量。
  • project n. 計畫、專案
    The project covers production capacity for armored vehicles, artillery, ammunition, explosives, optical equipment, specialized batteries, and chemical, biological, radiological and nuclear defense gear.
    該計畫涵蓋了裝甲車、火砲、彈藥、炸藥、光學設備、特種電池，以及化學、生物、放射性與核子防護裝備的生產能量。
  • demand n. 需求
    Demand for various types of ammunition is expected to increase as new tanks are fielded, troop numbers grow and reserve combat capabilities are reinforced, the bureau said.
    軍備局表示，隨著新戰車服役、部隊人數增加以及後備戰力強化，各型彈藥的需求預計將會增加。
  • allocate v. 分配、撥出（經費、資源等）
    Budget documents show that the bureau allocated more than NT$328.8 million for the project this year, and funding would rise to more than NT$1.52 billion next year.
    預算文件顯示，軍備局今年已為此計畫編列超過3.288億元新台幣，明年資金將增至超過15.2億元新台幣。
  • procurement n. 採購
    With the military’s procurement of 108 M1A2T tanks, the project plans to establish domestic production lines for 120mm tank rounds.
    配合軍方採購108輛M1A2T戰車，該計畫計畫建立120公厘戰車砲彈的國內生產線。
  • bids n. 出價、投標
    The bureau sought bids last year for a 120mm tank ammunition assembly system.
    軍備局去年為120公厘戰車彈藥組裝系統尋求招標。
  • commission v. 委任、委託製作
    The army would allocate more than NT$691.7 million to commission the National Chungshan Institute of Science and Technology to produce Thunderbolt-2000 high explosive rockets, the budget proposal showed.
    預算案顯示，陸軍將撥款超過6.917億元新台幣，委託國家中山科學研究院生產雷霆2000高爆火箭。
  • operational adj. 操作的、營運的、作戰的
    The Thunderbolt-2000 can be equipped with different rocket pods depending on operational requirements.
    雷霆2000可根據作戰需求配備不同的火箭莢艙。
  • component n. 零件、組件、構成要素
    The Thunderbolt-2000 is an important component of the army’s counter-landing planning, as MK-30 rockets are suitable for hitting landing aircraft and troops advancing toward the shore or assembling for an assault.
    雷霆2000是陸軍反登陸規畫的重要組成部分，因為MK-30 rockets適合打擊登陸載具以及向岸邊推進或集結以發動突擊的部隊。

文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The project was intended to be financed through the Executive Yuan’s proposed NT$1.25 trillion special defense budget...
    此句使用了被動語態「was intended」與「to be financed」，強調動作的承受者（即計畫本身），而未特別強調發起動作的主體，這在新聞英語及商業合約中經常用來客觀描述事實或安排。
  • 現在分詞作形容詞/後置修飾 (Present Participle as Modifier)
    The MK-45 extends the range farther offshore to amphibious transfer areas, as it can engage targets about 25km to 45km away.
    句中的「advancing toward the shore」是由現在分詞 advancing 引發的分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 troops，相當於形容詞子句「who are advancing toward the shore」，讓句子更簡潔流暢。
  • 對等比較句型 (Comparative Structure)
    By comparison, the HIMARS offers longer-range precision-strike capabilities.
    句中使用比較級形容詞「longer-range」來對比不同武器系統的射程與效能，多益考試常在閱讀測驗中利用此類比較（如比較不同產品、服務或方案）來出題考細節。
