台灣在亞運會上奪得雙不定向飛靶與體操銅牌

Taiwan wins skeet, gymnastics bronzes at Asian Games

單字解析

  • event n. （比賽）項目，活動
    Taiwanese sharpshooter Lee Meng-yuan (李孟遠) won bronze in the men's skeet event at the 2026 Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games on Wednesday, becoming the first Taiwanese athlete to medal in the event at the Asian Games.
    台灣射擊好手李孟遠於週三在 2026 年愛知-名古屋亞運會男子不定向飛靶項目中奪得銅牌，成為首位在亞運該項目奪牌的台灣運動員。
  • clinched v. 獲得，奪得（勝利、獎項等）
    The 32-year-old clinched the bronze with 29 hits, finishing behind Qatar's Rashid Saleh Al-Athba...
    現年 32 歲的李孟遠以 29 次擊中目標拿下銅牌，落後於卡達選手拉希德·薩利赫·阿爾-阿特巴……
  • record n. 紀錄
    ...finishing behind Qatar's Rashid Saleh Al-Athba, who won gold with an Asian record of 34 hits...
    ……落後於以 34 次擊中創下亞運紀錄奪金的卡達選手拉希德·薩利赫·阿爾-阿特巴……
  • dedicate v. 把……奉獻給，把……貢獻給
    Lee told Taiwanese reporters that he wanted to dedicate his hard-earned medal to his late father...
    李孟遠告訴台灣記者，他想把這枚得來不易的獎牌獻給他已故的父親……
  • pressure n. 壓力
    Team coach Lin Yu-hsin (林育信) said the gymnasts were under considerable pressure to win another medal at this year's Asian Games.
    代表隊教練林育信表示，體操選手們在今年亞運會上承受了爭取另一枚獎牌的巨大壓力。
  • credit v. 把……歸功於
    He credited the team's two veterans, Lee and Tang, with helping their younger teammates secure the bronze medal.
    他將團隊兩位資深選手李智凱與唐嘉鴻歸功於協助年輕隊友確保銅牌的關鍵。
  • veteran n. 資深老手，經驗豐富的人
    He credited the team's two veterans, Lee and Tang, with helping their younger teammates secure the bronze medal.
    他將團隊兩位資深選手李智凱與唐嘉鴻歸功於協助年輕隊友確保銅牌的關鍵。
  • secure v. 獲得，確保
    He credited the team's two veterans, Lee and Tang, with helping their younger teammates secure the bronze medal.
    他將團隊兩位資深選手李智凱與唐嘉鴻歸功於協助年輕隊友確保銅牌的關鍵。
  • competing v. 參賽，競爭

    截至週三下午，以「中華台北」名義參賽的台灣代表隊已在亞運會上贏得 12 面獎牌……

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 / 補充說明 (Present Participle as Modifier)
    ...finishing behind Qatar's Rashid Saleh Al-Athba, who won gold with an Asian record of 34 hits, and China's Lyu Jianlin (吕健林), who took silver with 33.
    句中的 "finishing behind..." 是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明主詞在前一句奪牌後的名次結果，表示「結果……」，在多益閱讀測驗中常考這種分詞構句的用法。
  • 非限定關代子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    ...finishing behind Qatar's Rashid Saleh Al-Athba, who won gold with an Asian record of 34 hits...
    逗號後面接由 "who" 引導的非限定關係代名詞子句，對先行詞 Rashid Saleh Al-Athba 做補充說明，多益常考關代格位（如 who vs. whom 或 whose）。
  • be under + 名詞 (處於……狀態中)
    Team coach Lin Yu-hsin (林育信) said the gymnasts were under considerable pressure to win another medal at this year's Asian Games.
    句型 "be under considerable pressure" 意指「承受相當大的壓力」，在多益職場情境（如工作壓力、期限壓力）中非常實用。
  • credit A with B (把 B 歸功於 A)
    He credited the team's two veterans, Lee and Tang, with helping their younger teammates secure the bronze medal.
    片語 "credit A with B" 意指「將功勞或成就歸於某人」，在多益商業或新聞英文中很常見，被動語態 "A be credited with B" 也經常出現。
