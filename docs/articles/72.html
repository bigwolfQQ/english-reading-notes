文法重點

  • 現在分詞當作後置定語（修飾名詞）
    even under newly implemented rules requiring travel agencies to help clients do so
    句中的 "requiring travel agencies to help clients do so" 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "rules"，相當於形容詞子句 "which require travel agencies to help clients do so"，使句子更為精簡。
  • 受詞補語（Verb + Object + Complement）
    "We only hope travel agencies can assist [with the registration], but if individuals are unwilling, they will not be forced," MAC deputy head and spokesperson Liang Wen-chieh (梁文傑) said
    被動語態 "they will not be forced" 後面省略了不定詞補語（如 to register），用來補充說明主詞在被強迫時的狀態。
  • 現在分詞構句表示附帶結果或補充說明
    saying the system showed that 51 Taiwanese had registered as being in Tibet at the time, giving authorities a general picture of how many Taiwanese were in the region.
  • 被動語態（Passive Voice）
    Agencies that fail to complete the required documentation as prescribed can be fined between NT$10,000 and NT$50,000
    使用情態動詞加上被動語態（can be fined），強調旅行社「被」罰款，焦點在於受罰的對象與結果，而未直接指明是哪一個執法單位開罰。
  • 間接問句（Indirect Question）當作名詞子句
    Asked whether an agency would be exempt from responsibility if travelers declined to register after being informed of the requirement...
    片語 "whether an agency would be..." 是一個由連接詞 whether 引導的間接問句，在此作主要動詞或過去分詞片語所帶出的受詞或討論主題，意為「是否……」。
