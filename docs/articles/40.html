  • proposal n. 提案、計畫
    Last week, UEFA filed requests for discovery of evidence connected to Infantino’s proposal in three US courts.

  • file v. 提出（訴訟、申請等）、歸檔

  • support n. / v. 支持
    “While these applications are styled as legal pleadings with captions and docket numbers, they are little more than press releases in further support of UEFA’s smear campaign against FIFA and its leadership,” FIFA said in a filing in US District Court in Colorado.
    國際足總在美國科羅拉多州地區法院提交的文件中表示：「雖然這些申請被包裝成帶有案頭標題和案號的法律訴訟文書，但它們實際上只不過是新聞稿，進一步支持歐洲足總針對國際足總及其領導層的抹黑運動。」
  • investment firm n. phr. 投資公司
    One of UEFA’s discovery requests was filed in the Southern District of New York and seeks disclosure of documents from Josh Kushner and his investment firm Thrive Capital Management.
    歐洲足總的一項證據開示請求是在紐約南區聯邦地區法院提交的，尋求披露來自喬許·庫許納及其投資公司 Thrive Capital Management 的文件。
  • disclosure n. 揭露、公開
    One of UEFA’s discovery requests was filed in the Southern District of New York and seeks disclosure of documents from Josh Kushner and his investment firm Thrive Capital Management.
    歐洲足總的一項證據開示請求是在紐約南區聯邦地區法院提交的，尋求披露來自喬許·庫許納及其投資公司 Thrive Capital Management 的文件。
  • private equity n. phr. 私募股權
    Thrive was involved in Infantino’s FIFA Forward Enterprise (FFE) proposal to sell stakes in the World Cup to private equity for US$4.2 billion.
    Thrive 曾參與因凡蒂諾的「國際足總前進企業」提案，該提案擬以 42 億美元將世界盃股份出售給私募股權。
  • withdraw v. 撤回、撤銷
    FIFA withdrew the proposal on Aug. 1, just days after it was made public, after intense pushback.
    在面臨強烈反彈後，國際足總在該提案公開僅幾天後的 8 月 1 日撤回了該提案。
  • purchase v. / n. 購買、採購
    Kushner also agreed last month to purchase the NBA’s Los Angeles Lakers with partner Bob Iger.
    庫許納上個月也同意與合夥人鮑勃·艾格一同收購美國職籃 NBA 的洛杉磯湖人隊。
  • complaint n. 控告、投訴
    UEFA has said in court documents that it is preparing a criminal complaint against Infantino in Swiss courts for possible financial mismanagement.
    歐洲足總在法院文件中表示，正準備針對因凡蒂諾涉嫌財務管理不當，向瑞士法院提出刑事指控。
  • assets n. 資產
    “In truth, it was a vehicle for Infantino and that small circle to acquire a permanent stake in and otherwise profit from FIFA’s most valuable assets to the detriment of FIFA, its members, and other parties within the football family.”
    「事實上，這是因凡蒂諾及其小圈子取得國際足總最具價值資產的永久股份並從中獲利，進而損害國際足總、其會員以及足球大家庭內其他各方利益的工具。」
  • authority n. 權限、權力、官方
    FIFA said that UEFA’s request “suffers from a fundamental defect: UEFA seeks discovery in aid of a foreign criminal proceeding that does not exist and that UEFA admittedly lacks the authority to initiate.”
    國際足總表示，歐洲足總的請求「有一個根本缺陷：歐洲足總尋求的證據開示是為了一項並不存在、且歐洲足總坦承自己缺乏權限發起的境外刑事訴訟服務。」

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    Other court filings seeking discovery of evidence related to the World Cup sell-off were filed in Florida...
    句中的 "seeking discovery..." 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "court filings"（法院文件），相當於形容詞子句 "which sought discovery..."。這種結構在商業及新聞英文中非常常見，能讓句子更簡潔。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    FIFA’s filing in Colorado on Thursday was a motion to intervene, asking that the discovery process be deferred or dropped.
    句中的 "be deferred or dropped" 使用了被動語態的原形動詞結構。在此處的 "asking that + S + (should) + 原形動詞"（表建議或要求的名詞子句後方）中，主詞 discovery process 是被延期（deferred）或被撤銷（dropped），因此使用被動語態。
  • 過去分詞作形容詞／後位修飾 (Past Participle as Modifier)
    FIFA withdrew the proposal on Aug. 1, just days after it was made public, after intense pushback.
    片語 "made public" 中的 made 是過去分詞，用來補充說明主詞 it（指代 proposal）被公開的狀態。"after it was made public" 被簡化為時間副詞子句前後的呼應，常出現在多益閱讀測驗的文法考點中。
