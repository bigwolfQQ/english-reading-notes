罕見「紅色精靈」閃電現身台東

Rare 'red sprite' lightning spotted in Taitung

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • capture v. 拍攝、捕捉、捕獲
    A rare "red sprite" phenomenon -- in which red lightning flashes in the sky above a thunderstorm -- was captured on video late Sunday, illuminating the sky off the Taitung coast.
    一場罕見的「紅色精靈」奇觀——即紅色閃電在雷雨上方的天空中閃爍——於週日深夜被影片捕捉到，照亮了台東外海的天空。
  • illuminate v. 照亮、闡明
    A rare "red sprite" phenomenon -- in which red lightning flashes in the sky above a thunderstorm -- was captured on video late Sunday, illuminating the sky off the Taitung coast.
    一場罕見的「紅色精靈」奇觀——即紅色閃電在雷雨上方的天空中閃爍——於週日深夜被影片捕捉到，照亮了台東外海的天空。
  • operate v. 運作、經營、操作
    The upper-atmospheric lightning was recorded at around 10 p.m. by a real-time surveillance camera operated by the East Coast National Scenic Area Administration near Jinzun Fishing Harbor.
    這場高層大氣閃電是在晚上 10 點左右，由金樽漁港附近、交通部觀光署東部海岸國家風景區管理處營運的即時監控攝影機所記錄下來。
  • director n. 主管、主任、董事
    Yang Yi-ching (楊義清), director of the Center for Earth System Science Research at National Taitung University, told reporters on Monday that "red sprites" are a type of electrical discharge that occurs in Earth's middle and upper atmosphere.
    國立台東大學地球系統科學研究中心主任楊義清週一告訴記者，「紅色精靈」是一種發生在地球中層和大氣高層的放電現象。
  • significant adj. 顯著的、相當大的、重要的
    When storm clouds form at high altitudes, they build up a significant electrical charge that can then be discharged toward areas of lower potential, such as the ground, other clouds or the upper atmosphere, he explained.
    他解釋說，當暴風雲在高海拔地區形成時，會累積顯著的電荷，接著這些電荷會向電位較低的區域（例如地面、其他雲層或高層大氣）進行放電。
  • include v. 包含、包括
    Yang said these discharges can include so-called blue jets above the tops of clouds and red sprites at higher altitudes, while Sunday's event may have been a distinct type known as a "gigantic jet."
    楊表示，這些放電可能包括雲頂上方的所謂「藍色噴流」以及更高處的「紅色精靈」，而週日的事件可能是一種被稱為「巨大噴流」的獨特類型。
  • distinct adj. 明顯不同的、獨特的
    Yang said these discharges can include so-called blue jets above the tops of clouds and red sprites at higher altitudes, while Sunday's event may have been a distinct type known as a "gigantic jet."
    楊表示，這些放電可能包括雲頂上方的所謂「藍色噴流」以及更高處的「紅色精靈」，而週日的事件可能是一種被稱為「巨大噴流」的獨特類型。
  • relatively adv. 相對地
    Because of the strong atmospheric convection currents in Taiwan, such phenomena can be seen relatively "easily" during thunderstorms in the summer and fall, Yang said.
    楊說，由於台灣強烈的大氣對流作用，在夏秋兩季的雷雨期間，相對「容易」看到此類現象。

文法重點

  • 非限定性關係代名詞子句 (in which)
    A rare "red sprite" phenomenon -- in which red lightning flashes in the sky above a thunderstorm -- was captured on video late Sunday...
    句中的「in which」引導一個非限定性關係子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「phenomenon」（現象）。介系詞「in」是因為原本的片語是「flash in the phenomenon」，將介系詞移至關代「which」前方，展現正式且精確的英文寫作風格。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (operated by)
    ...a real-time surveillance camera operated by the East Coast National Scenic Area Administration near Jinzun Fishing Harbor.
    過去分詞片語「operated by...」用來修飾前方的名詞「camera」。這相當於一個省略了主格與be動詞的被動語態關係子句（即 which is operated by...），能使句子更為精簡流暢。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (that can then be discharged)
    ...they build up a significant electrical charge that can then be discharged toward areas of lower potential...
    句中的「that」為關係代名詞，引導限定性關係子句，用來修飾前面的先行詞「a significant electrical charge」。子句內部使用了被動語態「can then be discharged」，強調電荷是被排放、釋放的過程。
