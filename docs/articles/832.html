單字解析

  • department n. 部門、局、處
    The Chiayi County Agriculture Department yesterday made a pitch for locally raised crocodile meat...
    嘉義縣農業處昨天為當地飼養的鱷魚肉進行宣傳……
  • make a pitch for phr. 推銷、強力推薦、為……爭取支持
    The Chiayi County Agriculture Department yesterday made a pitch for locally raised crocodile meat...
    嘉義縣農業處昨天為當地飼養的鱷魚肉進行宣傳……
  • division n. 部門、科、處、分割
    Chang Chien-cheng (張建成), a section chief in the department’s aquaculture division said in an interview...
    該處漁業科科長張建成在接受訪問時表示……
  • ideal adj. 理想的、完美的
    ...Chiayi aquaculture products such as oysters, hard clams, whiteleg shrimp, tilapia and eels are low-fat, high in protein and ideal for grilling.
    ……嘉義的水產養殖產品如鮮蚵、文蛤、白蝦、台灣鯛和鰻魚等，具有低脂肪、高蛋白質的特點，是燒烤的理想選擇。
  • texture n. 口感、質地
    Chang described crocodile meat as mild in flavor with a firm texture “similar to chicken.”
    張建成形容鱷魚肉味道溫和，肉質結實，「類似雞肉」。
  • devise v. 設計、發明、想出（方法等）
    Restaurants have devised several ways to prepare crocodile, including grilling, deep-frying, boiling, baking and stewing...
    餐廳已經研發出多種烹調鱷魚肉的方法，包括燒烤、油炸、水煮、烘烤以及燉煮……
  • stand out phr. 顯眼、突出、脫穎而出
    ...as the dish would stand out in a Mid-Autumn Festival barbecue for its fresh taste and as “a topic of conversation.”
    ……因為這道菜在的中秋節烤肉中，會以其鮮美的口感以及作為「話題焦點」而脫穎而出。
  • in contrast phr. 相比之下
    In contrast, skin-on crocodile leg is best used in soups or dishes like “three cup crocodile”...
    相對地，帶皮鱷魚腿最適合用於湯品或「三杯鱷魚」等料理……
  • require v. 需要、要求
    Chiayi’s charcoal-roasted white shrimp offer sweet and tender meat, requiring nothing more than a light sprinkle of salt or pepper to shine...
    嘉義的炭烤白蝦肉質鮮甜軟嫩，只需撒上少許鹽或胡椒粉就能展現美味……
  • browse v. 瀏覽、翻閱
    People can browse and purchase more than 220 Chiayi aquaculture products by visiting the county’s “Chiayum” Web site...
    民眾可以造訪該縣的「Chiayum」網站瀏覽並購買 220 多種嘉義水產養殖產品……
  • purchase v. 購買
    People can browse and purchase more than 220 Chiayi aquaculture products by visiting the county’s “Chiayum” Web site...
    民眾可以造訪該縣的「Chiayum」網站瀏覽並購買 220 多種嘉義水產養殖產品……

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Phrase as Adjective / Modifier）作後位修飾
    The Chiayi County Agriculture Department yesterday made a pitch for locally raised crocodile meat, touting county aquaculture products as suitable Mid-Autumn Festival barbecue fare.
    句尾的逗點後接 “touting...”，是由分詞片語構成的附加說明，用來補充說明前面主句動作所伴隨的結果或同時發生的動作（在此指農業處在推銷之餘，同時也大力讚賞水產）。這種結構在商業新聞和多益閱讀測驗中非常常見。
  • 形容詞片語作後位修飾（Adjective Phrase modifying Noun）
    Chiayi has another “special” aquaculture product — crocodile — which is also well-suited for grilling, he said.
  • 條件/讓步意味的現在分詞短語（Conditional/Adverbial Participle）
    Chiayi’s charcoal-roasted white shrimp offer sweet and tender meat, requiring nothing more than a light sprinkle of salt or pepper to shine, he added.
    句中的 “requiring nothing more than...” 是由現在分詞引導的附屬子句簡化而來，用來補充說明白蝦美味所需的條件（只需灑一點鹽）。這類結構常出現在多益文法題（尤其是分詞填空）中。
