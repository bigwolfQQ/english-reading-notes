  • representative n. 代表，代理人

    總統府將於週末舉辦一場國際論壇，預計約有 500 名國防專家、官員與企業代表出席，共同討論全社會防衛韌性的概念……
  • concept n. 概念，觀念
    ...expected to discuss the concept of whole-of-society resilience and to examine Taiwan's preparedness amid threats from China...
    ……預計……共同討論全社會防衛韌性的概念，並在面對中國威脅時檢視臺灣的準備情況……
  • prepare v. 準備（文章中使用名詞 preparedness：準備狀態）
    ...and to examine Taiwan's preparedness amid threats from China, according to a press release issued Wednesday.
    ……並在面對中國威脅時檢視臺灣的準備情況，根據週三發布的新聞稿。
  • issue v. 發布，發行
    ...according to a press release issued Wednesday.
    ……根據週三發布的新聞稿。
  • take place phr. 舉行，發生
    The second edition of the Resilient Taiwan for Sustainable Democracy International Forum will take place Saturday at the Grand Hyatt Taipei...
    第二屆「韌性臺灣：永續民主」國際論壇將於週六在台北君悅酒店舉行……

  • response n. 回應，反應
    ...cybersecurity, response to gray-zone activities, and the protection of critical infrastructure, the Presidential Office said.

    ...on how democracies can adapt to modern warfare and respond to emerging threats, the press release said.
    ……探討民主國家如何適應現代戰爭並因應新興威脅，新聞稿指出。

  • effort n. 努力，盡力
    ...share their observations of Taiwan's efforts to build up whole-of-society defense resilience over the past year, the office said.

文法重點

  • with + 名詞 + 分詞（複合結構/附帶狀況）
    The Presidential Office will hold an international forum on the weekend, with around 500 defense experts, officials, and business representatives expected to discuss the concept of whole-of-society resilience...
    「with + O. + 過去分詞 (expected)」構成附帶狀況的獨立結構，用來補充說明主句發生時同時存在的狀況或背景。在此表示「在預期有約 500 名……參與的情況下」，其中 expected 與前面 500 名專家官員之間是被動關係（預期被討論/出席）。
  • 過去分詞片語作後置定語（修飾名詞）
    ...according to a press release issued Wednesday.
    句中的 "issued Wednesday" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "a press release"（新聞稿）。原本完整的關係子句結構為 "which was issued Wednesday"，省略了「主代名詞 + be動詞」後，直接留下過去分詞片語作後置修飾，使商業與新聞英語更加簡潔有力。
  • 動名詞片語作介詞受詞（on how...）
    Anduril Industries founder Palmer Luckey will deliver an opening keynote speech on how democracies can adapt to modern warfare and respond to emerging threats...
    介詞 "on" 後方接了一個完整的名詞子句 "how democracies can adapt..." 作為其受詞。"how" 引導間接問句，在句中說明演講的「主題/內容關於如何……」，這是多益閱讀與聽力中經常用來表達「關於……的討論、演講、報告」的實用句構。
  • 現在分詞片語表示結果或伴隨動作
    ...next week sends a "clear message" that the international community cares deeply about peace across the Taiwan Strait and represents concrete action in support of it.
    動詞 "sends" 與後方的 "represents" 共同以主詞 "the expected participation..." 來發動。而 "sends a clear message that..." 後方接了同位語子句（that 引導），用來具體解釋「明確訊息」的內容是什麼，這種長主詞搭配多個並列動詞的結構常出現在多益長句中。
