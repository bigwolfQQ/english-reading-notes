文法重點

  • 含 wh- 疑問詞的名詞子句當受詞
    Counterpoint senior analyst Parv Sharma added that how much the AI and chipmaking gap between China and the US narrows will also depend on other factors...
    句型中的「how much the AI and chipmaking gap between China and the US narrows」是由「how much」引導的名詞子句，在句中作為動詞 depend on 的受詞。名詞子句內部採用「主詞 + 動詞」的正常語序（gap narrows），而非疑問句的倒裝語序。
  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    Open Chinese models, which are often more affordable than cutting-edge closed models from leading US AI labs, have also been making inroads globally...
    句中的「which are often more affordable...」是非限定關係子句，使用逗號隔開，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「Open Chinese models」。關係代名詞 which 在子句中擔任主詞，後面直接接動詞 are。
  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾語
    Alibaba Group Holding Ltd (阿里巴巴) unveiled new artificial intelligence (AI) chip technologies and plans for more powerful models yesterday, including what it said was China’s most powerful AI chip.
    句尾的「including what it said was...」是由現在分詞 including 引導的分詞片語，用來補充說明前面提及的新晶片技術與計畫。其中 what 帶領一個名詞子句作為 including 的受詞。
  • 現在分詞表附帶結果或伴隨狀態
    ...bringing it closer to the most advanced models from the US.
    句中的「bringing it closer...」是以現在分詞開頭的分詞片語，置於主句動作之後，用來表達自然而然產生的結果（前面提及訓練 5 到 10 兆參數模型的計畫，進而帶來了這個結果）。
