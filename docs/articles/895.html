單字解析

  • deputy n. 副主管，副手
    Mainland Affairs Council Deputy Minister Shen Yu-chung (沈有忠) yesterday said that the government was not surprised by a YouTuber’s recent claim...
    大陸委員會副主委沈有忠昨天表示，對於一名YouTuber最近聲稱……政府並不感到意外。
  • decline v. 婉拒，謝絕
    Shen declined to comment on individual cases, saying that the government has a comprehensive understanding of the situation...
    沈有忠拒絕就個案發表評論，並表示政府對整體情況有全面性的了解……
  • comprehensive adj. 全面的，綜合的
    Shen declined to comment on individual cases, saying that the government has a comprehensive understanding of the situation...
    沈有忠拒絕就個案發表評論，並表示政府對整體情況有全面性的了解……
  • judicial adj. 司法的，審判的
    ...and that the vast majority of cases would enter the judicial process.
    ……且絕大多數案件將進入司法程序。
  • in response to phr. 回應……，對……做出反應
    Shen made the remarks in response to media queries for comment on claim made by Taiwanese influencer Wen Tzu-yu...
    沈有忠是在回應媒體詢問有關台灣網紅溫子瑜所提出的聲明時，作出了上述表示。
  • recruit v. 招募，吸納
    The video recently posted a video on his YouTube channel about Chinese efforts to recruit sympathetic Taiwanese...
    該影片最近在他的YouTube頻道上發布了一部關於中國招募親中台灣人的努力的影片……
  • intermediary n. 中間人，調解人
    He also said intermediaries allegedly help other Taiwanese privately apply for Chinese IDs...
    他還表示，據稱有中間人協助其他台灣人私下申請中國身分證……
  • in exchange for phr. 作為……的交換
    ...in exchange for profits of up to 6 million yuan (US$896,060).
    ……藉此換取高達600萬元人民幣（約89萬6,060美元）的利潤。
  • authority n. 官方，當局，權威
    Government agencies would work closely with judicial authorities to monitor the situation, he said.
    他表示，政府機關將與司法機關密切合作，以監控局勢。
  • permit v. 允許，准許
    ...citizens are not permitted to simultaneously hold Taiwan and People’s Republic of China citizenship.
    ……國民不得同時擁有台灣與中華人民共和國公民身分。
  • consequently adv. 因此，結果
    Rights arising from the person’s status as a Taiwanese citizen could consequently also be lost, he said.
    他說，因該人員身為台灣公民身分而產生的權利，因此也可能會喪失。
  • caution n. 謹慎，小心
    Chung urged the public to exercise caution when engaging in exchanges with China and not to be tempted or pressured by others.
    鍾佳濱呼籲大眾在與中國進行交流時應保持謹慎，切勿受到他人的誘惑或施壓。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Present Participle）作後置定語
    Shen made the remarks in response to media queries for comment on claim made by Taiwanese influencer Wen Tzu-yu (溫子瑜), who said that some Taiwanese working in China have allegedly obtained Chinese identity cards.
    句中的「working in China」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「Taiwanese」，相當於形容詞子句「who are working in China」，用以精簡語意並描述當時正在進行的狀態。
  • 關係代間詞子句（Relative Clause）補充說明
    Wen, better known as Pa Jung (八炯), recently posted a video on his YouTube channel about Chinese efforts to recruit sympathetic Taiwanese to make videos for Douyin...
    句中的「to make videos for Douyin」為不定詞片語，在此作形容詞用，用來修飾前面的名詞「Taiwanese」，說明招募這些台灣人的目的。
  • 被動語態（Passive Voice）
    If a Taiwanese citizen establishes household registration in China, the person could lose Taiwanese household registration and status, and be treated as a resident of the mainland area, Chung said.
    句尾的「be treated as a resident...」使用了被動語態（由 조동사 could 帶原形 be），強調主詞（該名台灣公民）在法律上「被視為」大陸地區居民，符合新聞報導中客觀陳述法律後果的語氣。
