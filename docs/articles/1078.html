文法重點

  • With 複合結構 (With + 名詞 + 分詞/形容詞)
    The opening ceremony of the 48th WorldSkills Competition was held in Shanghai on Tuesday night, with Taiwan's delegation participating and flying the Olympic flag, as expected.
    「with + 名詞 + 現在分詞(V-ing)」用來表達伴隨的狀況或同時發生動作，在多益閱讀測驗中常用於描述商業或活動現場的附帶情形，意指「在……的情況下 / 隨著……」。
  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾
    Taking part in the event as "Chinese Taipei," Taiwan has sent 53 competitors to vie for honors in 47 skill categories at the biennial competition, often described as the "Olympics of skills," which runs from Sept. 22 to 27.
    句首的「Taking part...」是現在分詞片語作分詞構句，表示主動的附帶動作。另外，「often described as...」則是省略了「which is」的過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的競賽名稱，這是英文新聞精簡句型常見的手法。
  • 非限定關係代間詞子句 (Which 子句)
    Taking part in the event as "Chinese Taipei," Taiwan has sent 53 competitors to vie for honors in 47 skill categories at the biennial competition, often described as the "Olympics of skills," which runs from Sept. 22 to 27.
    逗號後方的「which runs from Sept. 22 to 27」是非限定關係子句，用來補充說明前面的「biennial competition」。在多益測驗中，常用來測試關係代名詞（which/who）的用法與主詞一致性。
  • 對等連接詞表達對比 (While)
    Event organizers noted that most competitions are open to contestants aged 22 or younger, while the age limit is extended to 25 for several skills that require longer periods of training and experience.
    句中的「while」在此處作對等連接詞使用，用來連接兩個對比的情況（大多數競賽開放給22歲以下，相較之下某些技能年齡限制延長至25歲），在多益聽力與閱讀的邏輯轉折題中非常實用。
