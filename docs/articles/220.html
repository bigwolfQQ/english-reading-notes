單字解析

  • delegation n. 代表團
    A visiting delegation of Japan Innovation Party (JIP) members is scheduled to meet with President Lai Ching-te...
    日本維新會（JIP）的一支訪問團預定將會晤總統賴清德……
  • schedule v. 預定，安排
    A visiting delegation of Japan Innovation Party (JIP) members is scheduled to meet with President Lai Ching-te...
    日本維新會（JIP）的一支訪問團預定將會晤總統賴清德……
  • official n. 官員
    ...is scheduled to meet with President Lai Ching-te (賴清德) and other senior government officials to promote relations...
    ……預定將會晤總統賴清德及其他政府高層官員以促進關係……
  • promote v. 促進，推動
    ...to promote relations between the two countries, Taiwan's foreign ministry said Monday.
    ……以促進兩國之間的關係，台灣的外交部於週一表示。
  • focus on phr. v. 聚焦於，專注於
    During the meetings, the discussions will focus on the latest developments in bilateral relations...
    在會談期間，討論焦點將放在雙邊關係的最新發展……
  • bilateral adj. 雙邊的
    ...the discussions will focus on the latest developments in bilateral relations and the broader situation...
    ……討論焦點將放在雙邊關係的最新發展以及更廣泛的局勢……
  • press release n. 新聞稿
    ...the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) said in a press release.
    ……外交部在新聞稿中指出。
  • lead v. 領導，率領
    The 13-member delegation, led by former JIP leader Nobuyuki Baba, is scheduled to meet with Lai...
    這個由13名成員組成的代表團由日本維新會前黨魁馬場伸幸率領，預定將會晤賴總統……
  • donate v. 捐贈，捐獻
    ...the JIP donated 30 million Japanese yen (US$192,369) to Taiwan for post-disaster reconstruction.
    ……日本維新會曾捐贈30萬日圓（約合192,369美元）給台灣，用於災后重建。
  • reconstruction n. 重建
    ...the JIP donated 30 million Japanese yen (US$192,369) to Taiwan for post-disaster reconstruction.
    ……日本維新會曾捐贈30萬日圓（約合192,369美元）給台灣，用於災后重建。

文法重點

  • be scheduled to (預定要……)
    A visiting delegation of Japan Innovation Party (JIP) members is scheduled to meet with President Lai Ching-te...
    「be scheduled to + 原形動詞」是多益考試與商業英文中非常常用的句型，用來表示某個行程或計畫「預定、排定」在特定時間舉行。
  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾 (ed-participle phrase)
    The 13-member delegation, led by former JIP leader Nobuyuki Baba, is scheduled to meet with Lai...
    句中的「led by former JIP leader Nobuyuki Baba」是由過去分詞 led 開頭的片語，用來修飾前方的名詞「The 13-member delegation」，相當於形容詞子句「which is led by...」的簡化形式，能使句子更精簡流暢。
  • 介系詞片語置於句首作修飾 (Prepositional Phrase for Context)
    During the meetings, the discussions will focus on the latest developments in bilateral relations...
    「During the meetings」是一個表示時間的介系詞片語，置於句首用來提供背景脈絡，後面接逗號隔開主句，有助於提升文章的邏輯連貫性。
