美、日議員力挺台灣

US, Japanese lawmakers back Taiwan

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • contingency n. 可能發生 A 的事（多益常指緊急事故、意外狀況）
    while “a Taiwan contingency is a Japan contingency and therefore a US-Japan alliance contingency,” media reported after a meeting between officials of the two nations in Washington on Tuesday.
    根據媒體報導，在兩國官員週二於華盛頓會面後，美國與日本國會議員達成共識，認為美國必須防守包括台灣在內的第一島鏈，而不應退守至第二島鏈，同時「台灣有事就是日本有事，因此也是美日同盟有事」。
  • delegation n. 代表團
    A delegation of Japanese lawmakers from the Liberal Democratic Party met with US Senator Thom Tillis, co-chair of the Senate Taiwan Caucus, and US Representative Ami Bera, co-chair of the US Congressional Taiwan caucus, to discuss issues concerning East Asia and the Taiwan Strait.
    來自自由民主黨的日本國會議員代表團會見了參議院台灣連線共同主席、美國參議員湯姆·提利斯以及美國眾議院台灣連線共同主席、眾議員阿米·貝拉，以討論涉及東亞和台灣海峽的議題。
  • concur v. 同意、一致贊同
    The Japanese and US lawmakers concurred that deterring China is key to preventing a conflict across the Taiwan Strait, Nippon.com reported.
    據 Nippon.com 報導，日美兩國議員一致認為，遏制中國是防止台海衝突的關鍵。
  • deter v. 嚇阻、阻止
    The Japanese and US lawmakers concurred that deterring China is key to preventing a conflict across the Taiwan Strait, Nippon.com reported.
    據 Nippon.com 報導，日美兩國議員一致認為，遏制中國是防止台海衝突的關鍵。
  • exercise v. 行使（權力等）、實行、發揮
    The US lawmakers expressed support for Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi’s statement in the Diet in November last year that the use of force by China could constitute a “survival-threatening situation” for Japan, potentially satisfying a legal condition for exercising collective self-defense, it said.
    報導指出，美國議員對日本首相高市早苗去年 11 月在國會的發言表示支持，該發言指出中國動用武力可能構成日本的「存亡危機事態」，從而可能滿足行使集體自衛權的法律條件。
  • coordination n. 協調、配合
    Takinami said the participants agreed on the connection between a Taiwan contingency and the security of Japan and the US-Japan alliance, and on the value of strengthening coordination through parliamentary exchanges, in line with Takaichi’s and former Japanese prime minister Shinzo Abe’s stance on Taiwan.
    瀧波宏文表示，與會者同意台海危機與日本及美日同盟安全之間的聯繫，並認同透過國會交流加強協調的價值，這符合高市早苗和前日本首相安倍晉三對台灣的立場。
  • intrinsic adj. 內在的、固有的、本質的
    They acknowledged that the semiconductor industry in Taiwan plays a critical role in US and Japanese economic security, which is intrinsic to their national security interests, he said.
    他說，雙方承認台灣的半導體產業在美日經濟安全中扮演關鍵角色，而這與他們的國家安全利益息息相關。
  • bipartisan adj. 兩黨的、獲得兩黨支持的
    “There remains strong bipartisan support in Congress for maintaining peace and stability across the Indo-Pacific and supporting our democratic partner Taiwan.
    「國會中對於維持印太地區的和平與穩定以及支持我們的民主夥伴台灣，依然存在強烈的跨黨派支持。
  • uphold v. 維護、支持、擁護
    Congress and the Japanese Diet have an important role to play in strengthening cooperation at the legislative level to help deter conflict, uphold the rules-based international order, and preserve a free and open Indo-Pacific.”
    國會與日本國會對於在立法層面上加強合作，以協助遏制衝突、捍衛以規則為基礎的國際秩序，並維護自由開放的印太地區，扮演著重要的角色。」
  • tolerate v. 容忍、忍受
    Separately, US Senator Pete Ricketts said that the US would not tolerate any attempt to change the status of Taiwan by force.
    另一方面，美國參議員皮特·里基茨表示，美國絕不容忍任何以武力改變台灣地位的企圖。
  • priority n. 優先事項、當務之急
    Asked about a proposed US$14 billion arms package for Taiwan that has not yet been approved by the Trump administration, he said that the immediate priority should be delivering weapons already promised to Taiwan.
    當被問及一項尚未獲得川普政府批准的擬議 140 億美元對台軍售案時，他表示，當務之急應該是交付先前已承諾給台灣的武器。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Postmodifier)
    A delegation of Japanese lawmakers from the Liberal Democratic Party met with US Senator Thom Tillis, co-chair of the Senate Taiwan Caucus, and US Representative Ami Bera, co-chair of the US Congressional Taiwan caucus, to discuss issues concerning East Asia and the Taiwan Strait.
    句中的 "concerning East Asia and the Taiwan Strait" 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "issues"。這相當於形容詞子句 "which concern East Asia..."，在新聞英語中為了簡潔，常直接省略關代與be動詞，改用分詞片語來表達「與...相關的」。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾與被動意涵 (Past Participle Phrase)
    Asked about a proposed US$14 billion arms package for Taiwan that has not yet been approved by the Trump administration, he said that the immediate priority should be delivering weapons already promised to Taiwan.
    句首的 "Asked about..." 是過去分詞引導的修飾語，原句意指 "When he was asked about..."（當他被問及...時）。因主詞（he）與動詞（ask）之間是被動關係，故直接使用過去分詞開頭，省略了主詞與 be 動詞，多益閱讀測驗常考這種分詞構句的還原與文法對應。
  • 名詞子句作受詞 (Noun Clause as Object)
    The Japanese and US lawmakers concurred that deterring China is key to preventing a conflict across the Taiwan Strait, Nippon.com reported.
    動詞 "concurred" 後方接了由 "that" 引導的名詞子句 ("that deterring China is key...")，在句中作為動詞的受詞。這種結構在商業與新聞英語中非常常見，用來表達某人「認為、同意或指出某事」。
