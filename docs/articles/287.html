台灣正在發展多層次防衛

Taiwan developing multilayered defense

單字解析

  • bolster v. 強化，改善，支持
    Taiwan is working to bolster its defenses against rapidly evolving threats by developing multilayered air defenses...
    台灣正致力於透過發展多層次防空...來強化防衛能力以應對快速演變的威脅。
  • integrate v. 整合，結合
    ...integrating emerging technologies and building a fully non-red drone supply chain.
    ...整合新興技術以及建立完全「去紅色供應鏈」的無人機供應鏈。
  • cost-effective adj. 具成本效益的，划算的
    ...to make cost-effective adjustments as threats and tactics evolve, she said at the Taipei Security Dialogue.
    ...以便隨著威脅與戰術的演變做出具成本效益的調整，她在台北安全對話上表示。
  • capability n. 能力，才能
    The Middle East conflict has highlighted the importance of cost-effective air defense systems and counter-drone capabilities, she added.
    她補充說，中東衝突凸顯了具成本效益的防空系統與反無人機能力的重要性。
  • logistics n. 後勤，物流
    Artificial intelligence (AI) applications in intelligence analysis, target selection, interception and logistics warrant further study, she said.
    她表示，人工智慧（AI）在情報分析、目標選擇、攔截與後勤方面的應用，值得進一步研究。
  • depend on phr. v. 依賴，取決於
    Its effectiveness would depend on integrating high-end and low-cost sensors and interceptors...
    其效能將取決於整合高階與低成本的感測器與攔截器...
  • procurement n. 採購，取得
    The government has submitted a special budget proposal for drone procurement...
    政府已提交一份用於無人機採購的特別預算案...
  • cooperation n. 合作
    Chiayi hopes to expand cooperation and enter new markets, Weng said.
    翁章梁表示，嘉義希望擴大合作並進入新市場。
  • sector n. 部門，行業
    Separately, Chiayi County Commissioner Weng Chang-liang, and representatives from Taiwan’s drone industry and research sectors visited Poland on Monday...
    另一方面，嘉義縣長翁章梁與台灣無人機產業及研究部門的代表週一訪問了波蘭...
  • memorandum of understanding n. 備忘錄（MOU）
    ...and signed a memorandum of understanding with the Pomerania Development Agency.
    ...並與波美拉尼亞發展局簽署了一份合作備忘錄。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Present Participle）作修飾語與附帶狀況
    Taiwan is working to bolster its defenses against rapidly evolving threats by developing multilayered air defenses, integrating emerging technologies and building a fully non-red drone supply chain.
    句中的「rapidly evolving」是用現在分詞當作形容詞來修飾名詞 threats，「developing...」、「integrating...」與「building...」則是由對等連接詞連接的現在分詞片語，用來表達具體的作法或手段（透過發展……、整合……以及建立……）。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞與受詞
    Weapons delivery is only one step toward developing effective combat capabilities, Hsiao said, adding that personnel, training, system integration and the ability to sustain combat readiness are all essential.
    介系詞 toward 後面接動名詞「developing」，構成動名詞片語作為介系詞的受詞。此外，「adding that...」是由現在分詞構成的分詞構句，用來補充說明蕭美琴說話時的同時動作或附加說明。
  • 過去分詞（Past Participle）作後位修飾語
    The National Security Council and Ocean Affairs Council are working with international partners to identify ways to counter maritime coercion, Hsiao said, citing undersea cables — which carry communications supporting financial transactions, business operations and public services — as an example of infrastructure requiring protection.
    句尾的「requiring protection」是現在分詞片語修飾前面的 infrastructure；而夾在破折號中間的「which carry communications supporting financial transactions...」則是關係代名詞子句，用來補充說明 undersea cables 的功能，其中「supporting...」又是另一個現在分詞片語修飾 communications。
  • 被動語態與分詞構句的應用
    The government has submitted a special budget proposal for drone procurement, with coastal surveillance and reconnaissance drones, coastal attack drones and uncrewed attack boats to be domestically manufactured, she said.
    句中包含現在完成式的主動語態「has submitted」，以及結尾使用「with + 名詞 + to be + 過去分詞」（with ... to be domestically manufactured）的附帶狀況句型，用來描述伴隨該預算案而產生的未來規劃狀態。
