國際足總證實英凡提諾將競選明年的主席職位

FIFA confirms Infantino to run in next year’s vote

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • backlash n. 強烈反彈，強烈反應
    Gianni Infantino is to stand in the FIFA presidential election in March next year, despite the backlash over his now-abandoned plan to bring private investment into the World Cup, soccer’s global governing body announced on Sunday.
    國際足球總會於週日宣布，儘管他先前引入私人投資世界盃的計畫引發強烈反彈並隨後作罷，國際足總主席詹尼·英凡提諾仍將參加明年三月的國際足總主席選舉。
  • investment n. 投資
    The 56-year-old Swiss has taken a pummeling since the announcement in late July of his short-lived FIFA Forward Enterprise plan to bring private investment into FIFA events.
    自從七月下旬宣布其短命的「國際足總前進企業計畫」（旨在為國際足總賽事引入私人投資）以來，這位 56 歲的瑞士人一直遭到猛烈抨擊。
  • reiterate v. 重申，反覆說
    However, now for the first time since the plan became public, FIFA has reiterated Infantino’s intention to run for a fourth and final term in next year’s elections.
    然而，這是該計畫公開以來，國際足總首次重申英凡提諾有意在明年的選舉中競選第四個也是最後一個任期。
  • term n. 任期，期限
    Should he be re-elected, he would continue to serve as head of world soccer until 2031.
    如果他順利連任，他將繼續擔任世界足球掌門人至 2031 年。
  • query n. 疑問，詢問
    Infantino is in Poland for the FIFA Women’s Under-20 World Cup, but refused to answer queries about his future when questioned by journalists on Saturday.
    英凡提諾目前人在波蘭觀看國際足總 U-20 女子世界盃，但在週六面對記者提問時，拒絕回答有關他未來的問題。
  • boycott v. / n. 杯葛，聯合抵制
    UEFA has accused Infantino of “criminal mismanagement” over the scheme and was the ringleader of the initial fightback to the announcement, including threatening to boycott all FIFA competitions, including the men’s and women’s World Cups, if it was not shelved.
    歐洲足總指控英凡提諾該計畫涉及「刑事管理不善」，並且是最初反擊該公告的帶頭者，包括威脅如果不將該計畫擱置，將杯葛所有國際足總賽事，包含男子和女子世界盃。
  • ally n. 盟友，同盟國
    Montagliani, a FIFA vice-president, had long been regarded as a close ally of Infantino, but there were signs, even before he distanced himself over the now-scrapped plan to allow private investment into World Cups, that he viewed FIFA’s purpose somewhat differently to the Swiss.
    身為國際足總副主席的蒙塔格利亞尼長期以來被視為英凡提諾的親密盟友，但有跡象顯示，甚至在他因允許私人投資世界盃的現已廢棄計畫而與之保持距離之前，他對國際足總宗旨的看法就與這位瑞士人有些許不同。
  • candidate n. 候選人
    As the only declared candidate so far for the election, Infantino would need a simple majority of votes from the 211 federations affiliated to FIFA to extend his term of office, which began in 2016 and was renewed in 2023.
    作為目前選舉中唯一宣布的候選人，英凡提諾若想延長其自 2016 年開始、並於 2023 年獲連任的任期，需要獲得隸屬於國際足總的 211 個成員協會的簡單多數選票。
  • submit v. 提交，呈遞
    Candidate nominations for next year’s election must be submitted by Nov. 18.
    明年選舉的候選人提名必須在 11 月 18 日前提交。

文法重點

  • be to + VR (表未來計畫或預定安排)
    Gianni Infantino is to stand in the FIFA presidential election in March next year...
    「be + to + 原形動詞」經常用於新聞英語中，用來表示官方預定的計畫、安排或即將發生的正式事件，此處指英凡提諾預定將參加明年的總統選舉。
  • 省略 if 的假設語氣倒裝句
    Should he be re-elected, he would continue to serve as head of world soccer until 2031.
  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾語
    ...there were signs, even before he distanced himself over the now-scrapped plan to allow private investment into World Cups...
    句中的「now-scrapped」是由副詞與過去分詞構成的複合形容詞，用來修飾名詞 plan，意思是「現已廢棄的計畫」，精簡了原本需要用形容詞子句（which is now scrapped）的冗長結構。
