  • discipline v. 懲戒，處分

    台北市警察局已對六名員警因臨檢期間的違紀行為進行懲處
  • misconduct n. 違紀，不當行為
    The Taipei City Police Department has disciplined six officers over misconduct during a raid
    台北市警察局已對六名員警因臨檢期間的違紀行為進行懲處
  • promptly adv. 迅速地，立即地
    the lead investigator at the scene was given a demerit for failing to promptly stop the remarks
    現場帶隊官則因未能及時制止這些言論而被記過一次
  • inadequate adj. 不充分的，不夠的
    noting that Chen had requested two reprimands over inadequate supervision and that the request was approved.
    並提到陳已自行請處申誡二次以示監督不周，該請求已獲批准。

  • investigation n. 調查

  • lawful adj. 合法的

  • acknowledge v. 承認

文法重點

  • 非限定關係代間子句（Non-defining Relative Clause）
    Taipei Mayor Chiang Wan-an (蔣萬安), who is seeking reelection in November as the Kuomintang candidate, said Monday...
    句中的「who is seeking reelection in November as the Kuomintang candidate」是用逗號隔開的非限定關係代間子句，用來補充說明先行詞 Taipei Mayor Chiang Wan-an 的額外資訊。若將此子句拿掉，主句的意思依然完整。
  • 現在分詞作後置修飾語（Present Participle as Postmodifier）
    The incident has also sparked concerns among some members of Southeast Asia's LGBTQ+ communities, who questioned whether Taiwan remained "a safe and friendly" travel destination.
    句中的「Southeast Asia's LGBTQ+ communities」後面接了「who questioned...」，或者像文章其他地方常見的「people demanding an apology」，都是用分詞或關係代間子句來修飾前方名詞，使句子表達更精鍊。
  • 被動語態（Passive Voice）
    Four officers were found to have used inappropriate language during the raid...
    「were found」是典型的被動語態結構（be + 過去分詞），強調動作的承受者（在此為 4 名員警），經常用於新聞報導中客觀陳述調查結果或官方說法。
  • 副詞子句簡化為分詞構句（Participial Phrase indicating Purpose/Time）
    Midnight told CNA that with Taipei preparing to host Taiwan Pride on Oct. 31...
    句中的「with Taipei preparing to host...」是「with + 名詞 + 分詞」的複合結構（常被視為廣義的分詞構句），用來表達附帶狀況或背景原因，表示「在台北準備主辦...的情況下」。
