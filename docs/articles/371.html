台灣央行總裁獲《全球金融》雜誌評定為「A-」級

Taiwan central bank governor gets 'A-' grade from Global Finance

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • governor n. 總裁、行長、州長
    Yang Chin-long, governor of the Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan), has received an "A-" grade...
    中華民國（台灣）中央銀行總裁楊金龍獲得了「A-」級的評價...
  • consecutive adj. 連續的、接連的
    It marked the second consecutive year that Yang received an "A-" grade from Global Finance.
    這標誌著楊總裁連續第二年獲得《全球金融》授予「A-」級評價。
  • ranking n. 排名、名次
    Commenting on the latest ranking in the annual report on central bankers, Yang was quoted by an aide as saying...
    針對這份年度央行總裁報告的最新排名，楊總裁透過幕僚轉述表示...
  • economic growth n. phr. 經濟成長
    The magazine said its grading scale from "A" to "F" is based on "success in areas such as inflation control, economic growth goals..."
    該雜誌表示，其從「A」到「F」的評分標準是基於「在控制通膨、經濟成長目標...等領域的成功表現。」
  • currency stability n. phr. 貨幣穩定
    The magazine said its grading scale from "A" to "F" is based on "success in areas such as inflation control, economic growth goals, currency stability..."
    該雜誌表示，其從「A」到「F」的評分標準是基於「在控制通膨、經濟成長目標、貨幣穩定...等領域的成功表現。」
  • performance n. 表現、績效
    An "A" represents excellent performance, while an "F" signifies outright failure.
    「A」代表優秀表現，而「F」則象徵徹底失敗。
  • publish v. 出版、發布、刊載
    The magazine added that the Central Banker Report Cards, published annually since 1994, grade the performance of central bank governors...
    該雜誌補充說，自 1994 年起每年發布的「中央銀行銀行家報告卡」，評估了央行總裁的表現...
  • confidence n. 信心、信任
    ...while maintaining the confidence of markets and the public, Global Finance founder and editorial director Joseph Giarraputo said in a statement.
    《全球金融》創辦人兼總編輯喬瑟夫・基亞拉普托在聲明中表示：「...同時維持市場和公眾的信心。」
  • environment n. 環境
    ...while guiding their economies through an always unpredictable global environment, Giarraputo said.
    基亞拉普托表示：「...在引導經濟穿越始終難以預測的全球環境時。」
  • serve as v. phr. 擔任、充當
    Yang, who has worked at Taiwan's central bank since 1989, has served as governor since February 2018.
    自 1989 年以來一直在台灣央行服務的楊總裁，自 2018 年 2 月起擔任總裁職務。

文法重點

  • 現在完成式 (Present Perfect Tense)
    Yang Chin-long, governor of the Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan), has received an "A-" grade from New York-based Global Finance magazine...
    表示過去發生的動作（獲得評級）持續到現在，或對現在造成的影響。
  • 分詞構句修飾名詞 (Participle Phrases as Adjectives)
    Commenting on the latest ranking in the annual report on central bankers, Yang was quoted by an aide as saying...
    句首的「Commenting...」為現在分詞片語，用來補充說明主句主語 Yang 在評論時的同時所發生的動作，等於 As he commented...
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Yang was quoted by an aide as saying...
    強調動作的承受者 Yang（被轉述），結構為「be + 過去分詞 (quoted)」，由片語「by an aide」帶出動作的發動者。
  • 非限定關係代間子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Yang, who has worked at Taiwan's central bank since 1989, has served as governor since February 2018.
    「who has worked...」前方有逗號，用來補充說明 Yang 的額外背景資訊，若拿掉此子句並不影響主要句子的完整性。
