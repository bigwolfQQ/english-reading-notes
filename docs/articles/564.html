文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The first, led by Haruo Kitamura of the Conservative Party of Japan, represented the Diet Members’ Association for Peace and Stability in the Taiwan Strait cross-party parliamentary group...
    句中的 "led by Haruo Kitamura of the Conservative Party of Japan" 是一個過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "The first"（指代第一個代表團）。這種結構省略了「關係代代名詞 + be動詞」（原形為 "which was led by..."），能讓句子更為簡潔緊湊，是英文新聞與商業寫作中非常常見的句型。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    However, the utility of parliamentary groups is that they can reveal things that official government representatives are prohibited from saying.
    句中的 "are prohibited from saying" 採用了被動語態（be動詞 + 過去分詞）。因為官方政府代表是被「禁止」發言的對象，動作的承受者為主詞，故使用被動語態來強調受事者，在多益測驗中常考動詞搭配及語態轉換。
  • 現在分詞片語表附帶結果或動作 (Present Participle Phrase indicating Result or Action)
    On Sept. 8 last year, the CCP placed sanctions on him, citing the spreading of “fallacies” about Taiwan, Xinjiang, Tibet and Hong Kong, freezing his assets in China and prohibiting him and his family from entering China, Hong Kong or Macau.
    句尾的 ", citing... , freezing... and prohibiting..." 連續使用了三個現在分詞片語，用來補充說明主句動作（中共對他實施制裁）所伴隨的行為或產生的細節。這種結構能在一句話中串聯多個相關動作，避免使用過多的連接詞，使行文流暢。
  • 假設語氣 (Subjunctive Mood / Unreal Conditional)
    When Seki talks of alliances, they should probably think about inviting Manila to the party, too.
    句中的 "they should probably think about..." 帶有建議與推論的語氣。雖然此句非典型的如果假設句，但用了情態動詞 should 表達「應該要……」，在社論或評論性文章中經常用來提出作者對未來行動的建言與假設情境。
