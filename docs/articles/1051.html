台灣在 2026 年亞運會贏得首面電競獎牌

Taiwan wins its first esports medal at 2026 Asian Games

單字解析

  • secure v. 獲得、確保、取得
    Taiwan secured its first esports medal at the 2026 Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games on Thursday, claiming bronze...
    台灣週四在 2026 年愛知-名古屋亞運會上獲得了首面電競獎牌，奪得銅牌……
  • claim v. 奪得、取得、聲稱
    ...claiming bronze in the "Naraka: Bladepoint" tournament after falling 0-3 to China in the semifinals.
    ……在準決賽以 0 比 3 不敵中國後，於《永劫無間》競賽中拿下銅牌。
  • comprise v. 由……組成、包含
    The Taiwanese squad, comprising Liu Tang-shiun (柳棠曛), Chen Shang-yi (陳尚億)... had already secured a place...
    由劉棠曛、陳尚億……組成的台灣代表隊，已經確保了一個席位……
  • advance v. 晉級、推進、前進
    ...when it advanced to the final four as the runner-up in its preliminary group.
    ……當它以預賽分組第二名晉級最後四強時。
  • runner-up n. 第二名、亞軍
    ...when it advanced to the final four as the runner-up in its preliminary group.
    ……當它以預賽分組第二名晉級最後四強時。
  • preliminary adj. 預賽的、初步的
    ...when it advanced to the final four as the runner-up in its preliminary group.
    ……當它以預賽分組第二名晉級最後四強時。
  • defeat v. 擊敗、戰勝
    ...the Taiwan players were defeated 0-3 and had to settle for third place...
    ……台灣選手以 0 比 3 吞敗，不得不滿足於第三名……
  • settle for phr. v. 勉強接受、屈就於
    ...and had to settle for third place in the "Naraka: Bladepoint" competition.
    ……並且在《永劫無間》競賽中不得不滿足於第三名。
  • incorporate v. 結合、納入、包含
    The game incorporates martial arts-inspired melee combat.
    該遊戲結合了受武術啟發的近戰搏鬥。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後置修飾（補充說明主詞）
    Taiwan secured its first esports medal at the 2026 Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games on Thursday, claiming bronze in the "Naraka: Bladepoint" tournament...
    句尾的「claiming bronze...」是由現在分詞片語構成的附加說明，用來補充前面主句發生的結果或伴隨動作，相當於「and claimed bronze...」的簡化寫法。
  • 分詞片語作形容詞修飾主詞（主動關係）
    The Taiwanese squad, comprising Liu Tang-shiun (柳棠曛), Chen Shang-yi (陳尚億), Cheng Hsu-yu (鄭勗佑), Yen Kai-en (顏慨恩) and Yeh Chih-yu (葉芝妤), had already secured a place on the podium...
    「comprising...」是現在分詞片語，置於主詞 The Taiwanese squad 後方，用來修飾並列舉代表隊的成員。因為代表隊「包含」這些成員，具備主動關係，故使用現在分詞。
  • 被動語態（Passive Voice）
    ...the Taiwan players were defeated 0-3 and had to settle for third place in the "Naraka: Bladepoint" competition.
    句中的「were defeated」採用了「be + 過去分詞」的被動語態結構，強調台灣選手是「被」中國隊擊敗的，突顯比賽的勝負結果。
  • 關係代名詞子句（限定用法）
    "Naraka: Bladepoint" is an electronic game in which the players fight each other to be the last one standing.
    「in which the players fight each other...」是由「介系詞 + 關係代名詞 (which)」引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾先行詞 an electronic game。in which 等同於 where，指在該遊戲中。
