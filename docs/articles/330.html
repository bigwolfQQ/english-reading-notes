單字解析

  • evacuate v. 疏散，撤離
    where 44 children and three other teachers were evacuated safely, authorities said.
    當局表示，當時有 44 名兒童和另外 3 名老師安全撤離。
  • suspect n. 嫌疑人，嫌犯
    Media reports identified the suspect as a police officer at the nearby Bang Lamung station.
    媒體報導指出，嫌疑人是附近邦拉蒙警察局的一名警察。
  • founder n. 創辦人，創立者
    The Seoul Family Court yesterday ordered the founder of Smilegate... to pay 2.55 trillion won...
    首爾家庭法院昨天命令 Smilegate 的創辦人支付 2.55 兆韓元...
  • settlement n. 協議，和解，結算
    ...to pay 2.55 trillion won (US$1.91 billion) in a divorce settlement, in what could be a record payout in the country.
    ...支付 2.55 兆韓元（約合 19.1 億美元）作為離婚協議金，這可能是該國記錄中金額最高的支付。
  • transfer v. 轉移，轉讓，調動
    The court ruled that Smilegate chief vision officer... must transfer 35 percent of the unlisted company’s shares and cash to his wife...
    法院裁定 Smilegate 首席願景官...必須將這家未上市公司 35% 的股份與現金轉移給他的妻子...
  • valuation n. 估價，估值
    The court official could not immediately confirm the valuation.
    法院官員無法立即確認該估值。
  • attract v. 吸引
    ...adding that the government cannot attract top talent into politics if salaries remain unchanged.
    ...並補充說，如果薪資保持不變，政府將無法吸引頂尖人才從政。
  • framework n. 架構，體制
    Wong said that under a revised salary framework, the benchmark annual pay for a minister... would rise...
    黃循財表示，在修正後的薪資架構下，部長的基準年薪將會調高...
  • gift n. 禮物，贈品
    President Donald Trump gave his executive assistant Natalie Harp a US$45,000 cash gift, media reported on Tuesday.
    媒體週二報導，總統唐納德·川普贈予其行政助理娜塔莉·哈珀 45,000 美元的現金禮物。
  • display n. 展出，陳列
    The unusual work... was on display for two months at the Boijmans van Beuningen museum in Rotterdam.
    這件不尋常的作品...在鹿特丹博伊曼斯·范伯寧恩美術館展出兩個月。

文法重點

  • 非限定關係代名詞子句 (Non-restrictive Relative Clause)
    ...in Chonburi Province, where 44 children and three other teachers were evacuated safely, authorities said.
    句中的「where 44 children and three other teachers were evacuated safely」是非限定關係子句（前面有逗號），用來補充說明前面的地點「Chonburi Province（春武里府）」，關係副詞 where 在子句中作地方副詞用。
  • 分詞構句作附帶說明或結果 (Participle Construction)
    The shooter, who is believed to be the victim’s husband, fled the scene, with his house surrounded by police, officials said.
    句中的「with his house surrounded by police」是「with + 名詞 + 過去分詞」的複合結構（屬分詞構句的一種），用來表示伴隨的情況或狀態，譯為「隨著他的住宅被警方包圍」。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The Seoul Family Court yesterday ordered the founder of Smilegate... to pay 2.55 trillion won... in a divorce settlement, in what could be a record payout in the country.
    本句使用被動語態「could be a record payout」以及不定詞的修飾，強調該筆離婚協議金在國家紀錄中可能達到前所未有的金額。
