單字解析

  • airspace n. 領空
    NATO fighter jets shot down a drone that entered Lithuanian airspace overnight, officials said yesterday...
    官員們昨天表示，北約戰機昨晚擊落了一架進入立陶宛領空的無人機...
  • investigation n. 調查
    Authorities did not comment on the drone’s origin or purpose, saying the incident needed further investigation.
    當局未對無人機的來源或目的發表評論，並表示該事件需要進一步調查。
  • bolster v. 加強、支持、改善
    He also pledged to bolster the 32-nation alliance’s own defenses along its long eastern border with Russia.
    他也承諾加強這個擁有32個成員國的聯盟沿著其與俄羅斯漫長東部邊界自身的防禦。
  • ally n. 盟友、同盟國
    The menace of drones has repeatedly loomed over the skies of Lithuania, which shares borders with Belarus, a key ally of Moscow...
    無人機的威脅一再籠罩立陶宛的天空，該國與莫斯科的關鍵盟友白俄羅斯...接壤。
  • intercept v. 攔截、阻止
    It remained in Lithuanian airspace for about 30 minutes and flew along the outskirts of populated areas before being intercepted, he said.
    他說，它在立陶宛領空中停留了大約30分鐘，並在被攔截之前沿著人口稠密地區的外圍飛行。
  • mission n. 任務、使命
    The Fennec military helicopter had been on a routine mission to photograph the Russian frigate, the Danish Defence Command said in a statement.
    丹麥國防司令部在一份聲明中表示，這架芬尼克軍用直升機當時正在執行例行任務，以拍攝俄羅斯巡防艦。
  • response n. 回應、反應
    The Russian embassy did not immediately respond to a request for comment.
    俄羅斯大使館未立即回應置評請求。
  • strengthen v. 加強、鞏固
    “Russia wants to sow fear and discord. Our answer is to stand closer together and strengthen the defense of Denmark and Europe,” she said in a statement.
    她在聲明中表示：「俄羅斯想要散布恐懼與不和。我們的回應是緊密團結，並加強丹麥和歐洲的防禦。」

文法重點

  • 關代子句修飾先行詞 (Relative Clause)
    NATO fighter jets shot down a drone that entered Lithuanian airspace overnight...
    句中的 "that entered Lithuanian airspace overnight" 是由關係代間詞 "that" 引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的先行詞 "a drone"（一架無人機），補充說明這架無人機在夜間進入了立陶宛領空。
  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    ...less than a day after NATO Secretary-General Mark Rutte said that strikes close to the alliance’s territory would drive the organization to increase its support for Kyiv.
    片語 "close to the alliance’s territory" 是形容詞片語，由副詞加介系詞構成，用來修飾前面的名詞 "strikes"（攻擊），表示「靠近該聯盟領土的」攻擊。
  • 過去分詞作後位修飾與被動語態 (Past Participle for Passive/Post-modifier)
    The drone was downed near the village of Pratknai in the Kaiiadorys District...
    句首主詞 "The drone" 後接be動詞 "was" 與過去分詞 "downed"，構成被動語態（「被擊落」），清楚表達無人機是遭受外力擊落而非自行墜毀。
  • 現在分詞短語表附帶狀況 (Present Participle Phrase indicating Circumstance)
    Authorities did not comment on the drone’s origin or purpose, saying the incident needed further investigation.
    逗號後方的 ", saying the incident needed further investigation" 是現在分詞短語，用來補充說明主句動作發生時的伴隨狀況或理由，相當於 "and said that..." 的簡化用法。
  • 名詞子句作受詞 (Noun Clause as Object)
    Vilmantas Vitkauskas, head of the crisis center, told Lithuanian public broadcaster LRT that the drone most likely entered from Belarus.
    動詞 "told" 後方接了由 "that" 引導的名詞子句 "that the drone most likely entered from Belarus"，在句中擔任 "told" 的受詞，用以說明告知的內容細節。
