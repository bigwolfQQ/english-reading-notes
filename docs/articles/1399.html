交通部為火車旅客的繁忙假期做好準備

MOTC readying for busy holiday for train travelers

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • measure n. 措施，方法
    The transport ministry is taking several measures to cushion ride conditions and seating capacity ahead of the holiday, officials said at a news conference at the ministry in Taipei.
    官員們在台北交通部舉行的記者會上表示，交通部正採取多項措施，以在假期前緩解乘車狀況和運載量。
  • capacity n. 容量，產能，容納人數
    The ministry is coordinating with Taiwan Railway Corp (TRC) and THSRC to boost transport capacity during the holiday period, he said.
    他說，交通部正與台灣鐵路公司（TRC）及台灣高鐵公司協調，以提升假期期間的運輸能力。
  • designate v. 指定，指派
    Today, eight trains travelling from Taipei or Nangang to Taichung between 5pm and 9pm would each have eight carriages designated for non-reserved seating, Deputy Minister Wu Sheng-yuan (伍勝園) said.
    政務次長伍勝園表示，今天下午5點到9點之間，從台北或南港開往台中的8列火車，每列都將有8列車廂指定為自由座。
  • coordinate v. 協調，配合
    The ministry is coordinating with Taiwan Railway Corp (TRC) and THSRC to boost transport capacity during the holiday period, he said.
    他說，交通部正與台灣鐵路公司（TRC）及台灣高鐵公司協調，以提升假期期間的運輸能力。
  • accommodate v. 容納，提供住宿或空間
    Designating more cars for non-reserved seating allows each train to accommodate 1,600 to 2,200 people, which should help bleed off seating pressure, Wu said.
    伍勝園表示，指定更多車廂為自由座可使每列火車容納1,600至2,200人，這應有助於紓解座位壓力。
  • feature v. 以……為特色，由……主演
    THSRC president Bob Chen (陳惠裕) said that extra seating would be featured in two trains per hour during rush hour, and that trains are to park on the platform for rapid cleaning so they can return to ready status.
    台灣高鐵總經理陳惠裕表示，尖峰時段每小時將有兩班車提供額外座位，且列車將停留在月台進行快速清潔，以便能回到準備就緒的狀態。
  • volume n. 量，總額，營業額
    Despite the increase in reserved-seat ticket sales, THSRC expects an overall decrease in passenger volume for the National Day holidays, he said.
    他說，儘管對號座車票銷量增加，但台灣高鐵預期國慶假期的整體旅客量將會減少。
  • forecast n. / v. 預測，預報
    The forecast is that 290,000 to 330,000 passengers would take the high-speed trains today, while no more than 340,000 people would use the high-speed rail to go back to work, Chen said.
    陳惠裕表示，預測今天將有29萬至33萬名旅客搭乘高鐵，而使用高鐵返回工作崗位的人數將不會超過34萬人。
  • surge n. 急遽上升，激增
    However, people travelling south for vacation are expected to join commuters going home from work during rush hour, resulting in surges in crowds, he said.
    不過，他說，南下度假的民眾預計將與下班返家的通勤族在尖峰時段匯流，導致人潮暴增。
  • absorb v. 吸收，承受（成本、需求等）
    TRC president Feng Hui-sheng (馮輝昇) said the railway company would run 123 additional trains to absorb demand during the holiday period.
    台鐵公司董事長馮輝昇表示，鐵路公司將加開123列次列車，以吸收假期期間的需求。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作後位修飾
    Today, eight trains travelling from Taipei or Nangang to Taichung between 5pm and 9pm would each have eight carriages designated for non-reserved seating...
    句中的「travelling from Taipei or Nangang to Taichung between 5pm and 9pm」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「eight trains」，相當於形容詞子句「which travel from...」。
  • 過去分詞（片語）作後位修飾
    ...each have eight carriages designated for non-reserved seating, Deputy Minister Wu Sheng-yuan (伍勝園) said.
    句中的「designated for non-reserved seating」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「carriages」。因為車廂是被指定的，所以使用過去分詞表達被動概念，相當於「which are designated for...」。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句（補充說明）
    Designating more cars for non-reserved seating allows each train to accommodate 1,600 to 2,200 people, which should help bleed off seating pressure, Wu said.
    句尾的「which should help bleed off seating pressure」是一個非限定關係子句（前面有逗號），其中的「which」代指前面整件事情（指定更多自由座車廂這件事），用來補充說明其帶來的效果。
  • 對等連接詞連接對稱結構
    THSRC has crowd control measures in place to separate passengers heading to reserved and non-reserved cars, and is fielding additional staff members to maintain orderliness, Chen said.
    句中的主詞「THSRC」同時進行了兩個動作，透過對等連接詞「and」連接兩個動詞片語：「has crowd control measures in place...」與「is fielding additional staff members...」，使句子結構精簡而不重複主詞。
