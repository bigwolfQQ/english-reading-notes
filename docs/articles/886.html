三人因涉嫌「跨國代理孕母集團」遭起訴

Three indicted over ‘cross-border surrogacy ring’

單字解析

  • indict v. 控告，起訴
    The Taichung District Prosecutors’ Office has indicted a Chinese national surnamed Cheng (程), 34, and two Taiwanese accomplices on suspicion of operating a cross-border surrogacy ring spanning Taiwan, Malaysia and Thailand.
    台中地方檢察署已起訴一名34歲、姓程的中國籍嫌疑人及兩名台灣共犯，罪嫌是涉嫌經營橫跨台灣、馬來西亞與泰國的跨該國代孕集團。
  • accomplice n. 同謀，共犯

    The group, which operated under names including UT Li She (UT麗舍) and Tonghe Xinsheng (同合新生), allegedly solicited customers online and charged between NT$2 million and NT$4.5 million (US$62,976 and US$141,697) per newborn, prosecutors said.
    檢察官表示，該集團以「UT麗舍」和「同合新生」等名稱營運，涉嫌在網路招攬客戶，每名新生兒收取新台幣200萬元至450萬元（約合62,976美元至141,697美元）不等的費用。
  • charge v. 索價，收費

    Prospective Taiwanese fathers traveled to Malaysia to provide sperm, then selected Thai egg donors based on their educational and professional backgrounds, as well as Thai surrogate mothers, they said.
    他們指出，有意願成為父親的台灣男子前往馬來西亞提供精子，然後根據泰國卵子捐贈者的學歷與職業背景進行挑選，並挑選泰國代理孕母。
  • commission n. 佣金，委託，代辦事項
    The group allegedly accepted commissions from 22 Taiwanese men, resulting in the births of 25 babies, prosecutors said.
    檢察官指出，該集團涉嫌接受22名台灣男子的委託，促成了25名嬰兒的誕生。
  • investigation n. 調查
    The brigade reported its findings to the Taichung District Prosecutors’ Office, which launched an investigation.
    該專勤隊將調查結果報請台中地檢署指揮偵辦。
  • warrant n. 搜索票，逮捕令
    On June 4, investigators obtained a warrant and searched an obstetrics and gynecology clinic in Taichung.
    6月4日，調查人員取得搜索票，搜查了台中一家婦產科診所。
  • seize v. 查扣，沒收，扣押
    They seized delivery records involving surrogate mothers and found several surrogate mothers waiting to give birth.
    他們查扣了涉及代理孕母的分娩記錄，並發現幾名等待生產的代理孕母。

    專勤隊調查發現，程嫌及其涉嫌共犯已在泰國成立了一家人工生殖與代孕公司。

文法重點

  • 非限定關係代間子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    The group, which operated under names including UT Li She (UT麗舍) and Tonghe Xinsheng (同合新生), allegedly solicited customers online and charged between NT$2 million and NT$4.5 million...
    句中的「which operated under names...」是用逗號隔開的非限定關係代間子句，對先行詞「The group」做補充說明。如果把這個子句拿掉，主要子句「The group allegedly solicited customers...」語意依然完整。
  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況/結果 (Participial Phrase)
    The group allegedly accepted commissions from 22 Taiwanese men, resulting in the births of 25 babies, prosecutors said.
  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense)
    The brigade’s investigation found that Cheng and his alleged accomplices had established an assisted-reproduction and surrogacy company in Thailand.
    句中的動詞「found」（發現）是用過去式，而「had established」（已經成立）則用過去完成式（had + 過去分詞）。這是因為「成立公司」這個動作發生在「調查發現」這個過去時間點之前，用來突顯動作的前後順序。
