美元在台北外匯市場收盤走低

U.S. dollar closes lower on Taipei forex market

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • close v. 收盤
    The U.S. dollar fell against the Taiwan dollar Tuesday, shedding NT$0.007 to close at NT$31.781.
    美金周二對台幣貶值，下跌0.017元，以31.781元作收。
  • turnover n. 營業額、成交量
    Turnover totaled US$1.604 billion during the trading session.
    交易期間總成交金額達16.04億美元。
  • total v. 總計達
    Turnover totaled US$1.604 billion during the trading session.
    交易期間總成交金額達16.04億美元。
  • trading session n. phr. 交易時段、交易期間
    Turnover totaled US$1.604 billion during the trading session.
    交易期間總成交金額達16.04億美元。
  • greenback n. 美元（美鈔）
    The greenback opened at NT$31.760, and moved between NT$31.699 and NT$31.823 before the close.
    美元開盤為31.760元，收盤前在31.699元與31.699元與31.823元之間波動。
  • open v. 開盤
    The greenback opened at NT$31.760, and moved between NT$31.699 and NT$31.823 before the close.
    美元開盤為31.760元，收盤前在31.699元與31.823元之間波動。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作附帶狀況（結果）
    The U.S. dollar fell against the Taiwan dollar Tuesday, shedding NT$0.007 to close at NT$31.781.
    句尾的「shedding...」為現在分詞片語，用來補充說明主句動作所產生的自然結果或伴隨發生的狀況，相當於「and shed...」。
  • 介系詞片語表達數值範圍
    The greenback opened at NT$31.760, and moved between NT$31.699 and NT$31.823 before the close.
    使用「between... and...」結構來精確描述匯價在盤中的波動區間，是商業與財經新聞中常見的句構。
