文法重點

  • 受詞補語（用分詞表示被動）
    On Thursday last week, Changhua County Council Speaker Hsieh Dien-lin (謝典霖) and his father were taken into custody and denied access to visitors or communications in connection with a June banquet that authorities allege constituted vote-buying and embezzlement of public funds.
    句中的 "were taken into custody and denied access..." 使用了被動語態，主詞被剝奪了（deny）探視權與通訊權，這裡省略了主動語態中的受詞補語結構，呈現當事人遭到限制的狀態。
  • 關係代名詞子句（附加修飾）
    The incident has dealt a severe blow to the protective umbrella that has long shielded powerful local political families from scrutiny.
    以 "that has long shielded..." 的關係代名詞子句來修飾先行詞 "the protective umbrella"，用以補充說明這個保護傘長期以來所發揮的庇護功能。
  • 介系詞 + 關代形成的形容詞子句
    Corrupt systems, in which public institutions are treated as private property, are commonplace across areas governed by KMT-aligned local factions.
    句中使用了 "in which"（代替 systems）引導非限定性關係子句，用來補充說明 "Corrupt systems" 的內部運作狀況，即公有機構被當成私人財產對待。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾
    On Sept. 1, six current and former KMT Taitung County councilors, including Deputy Speaker Lin Tsung-han (林琮翰), were investigated over alleged abuse of their positions to benefit the family of Taitung County Council Speaker Wu Hsiu-hua (吳秀華) to develop and widen a farm road leading to a private resort owned by the Wu family.
    句尾的 "owned by the Wu family" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "a private resort"，相當於形容詞子句 "which is owned by the Wu family" 的簡化版。
