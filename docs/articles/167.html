單字解析

  • cooperation n. 合作
    The Taiwan People’s Party (TPP) yesterday said it would halt all electoral cooperation with the Chinese Nationalist Party (KMT) in Hsinchu County...
    台灣民眾黨昨天表示，該黨將暫停在新竹縣與中國國民黨的所有選舉合作...
  • halt v. 暫停，停止
    The Taiwan People’s Party (TPP) yesterday said it would halt all electoral cooperation with the Chinese Nationalist Party (KMT) in Hsinchu County...
    台灣民眾黨昨天表示，該黨將暫停在新竹縣與中國國民黨的所有選舉合作...
  • prefer v. 更喜歡，偏好
    ...after the allied opposition parties refused to defer to each other’s preferred candidate in the Jhubei City mayoral race.
    ...在此盟友反對黨拒絕在竹北市市長選戰中禮讓彼此心儀的候選人之後。
  • agreement n. 協議，同意
    ...said on Friday during an interview that the parties had not made any agreement on who should defer to whom in the Jhubei race...
    ...週五在接受採訪時表示，雙方並未就竹北選戰中誰該禮讓誰達成任何協議...
  • pledge v. 保證，承諾
    ...and that the TPP had never formally pledged to support a primary.
    ...且民眾黨從未正式承諾支持初選。
  • suspend v. 暫停，中止
    At a meeting of the TPP’s Central Committee yesterday morning, the party passed a resolution suspending electoral cooperation with the KMT on all races in Hsinchu County.
    在昨天上午舉行的民眾黨中央委員會會議上，該黨通過了一項決議，暫停在新竹縣所有選戰中與國民黨的選舉合作。
  • foundation n. 基礎
    ...had “seriously damaged the foundation of mutual trust that the TPP and KMT have gradually built over the last two years.”
    ...已「嚴重破壞了民眾黨與國民黨過去兩年逐步建立的互信基礎」。
  • coordinate v. 協調
    The larger opposition KMT and smaller TPP reached an agreement in March to coordinate candidates and cooperate on policy positions in the elections.

  • proximity n. 鄰近，接近
    Jhubei, and Hsinchu County in general, have grown rapidly and gained prominence in the past few years because of their proximity to Hsinchu Science Park.
    竹北市及整個新竹縣，由於鄰近新竹科學園區，在過去幾年中迅速發展並備受矚目。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作後位修飾
    At a meeting of the TPP’s Central Committee yesterday morning, the party passed a resolution suspending electoral cooperation with the KMT on all races in Hsinchu County.
    句中的「suspending electoral cooperation...」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「resolution（決議）」，相當於形容詞子句「...that suspended electoral cooperation...」。這種用法在商業與新聞英文中非常常見，能讓句子更精簡流暢。
  • 過去分詞（片語）作後位修飾
    ...after the allied opposition parties refused to defer to each other’s preferred candidate in the Jhubei City mayoral race.
    句中的「preferred」是過去分詞作形容詞用，放在名詞「candidate」前面作前置修飾。過去分詞帶有被動或完成的意味（被偏好的、心儀的候選人）。
  • 名詞子句作受詞（間接問句/名詞子句）
    ...said on Friday during an interview that the parties had not made any agreement on who should defer to whom in the Jhubei race...
    介系詞「on」後面接了一個由「who」引導的名詞子句「who should defer to whom in the Jhubei race」，作為介系詞的受詞，用來具體說明「agreement（協議）」的內容。
  • 複合介系詞片語表達原因
    Jhubei, and Hsinchu County in general, have grown rapidly and gained prominence in the past few years because of their proximity to Hsinchu Science Park.
    片語「because of」後面接名詞片語「their proximity to Hsinchu Science Park」，用來表達竹北快速成長的「原因」。注意 because 後面接子句（主詞+動詞），而 because of 後面接名詞或名詞片語。
