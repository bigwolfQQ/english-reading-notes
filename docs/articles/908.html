單字解析

  • economic adj. 經濟的
    US and Chinese economic officials discussed creating a communication channel...
    美國和中國的經濟官員討論了建立溝通管道...
  • communication channel n. phr. 溝通管道
    ...discussed creating a communication channel for artificial intelligence (AI) concerns...
    ...討論了針對人工智慧（AI）疑慮建立溝通管道...
  • cooperation n. 合作
    ...offering a rare incentive for cooperation despite an intensifying race for technological supremacy.
    ...這為雙方提供了難得的合作誘因，儘管科技霸權的競賽正在加劇。
  • negotiator n. 談判代表
    China’s top trade negotiator, Li Chenggang, said the talks were held in a “good atmosphere”...
    中國首席貿易談判代表李成鋼表示，會談在「良好的氣氛」中舉行...
  • atmosphere n. 氣氛、氛圍
    ...said the talks were held in a “good atmosphere” as he left JPMorgan headquarters on Sunday...
    ...週日離開摩根大通總部時表示，會談在「良好的氣氛」中舉行...
  • mutual adj. 相互的、彼此的
    Asked about the possibility of mutual tariff reduction on certain goods...
    在被問及對某些商品實施相互減免關稅的可能性時...
  • reduction n. 減少、降低
    Asked about the possibility of mutual tariff reduction on certain goods, Greer said teams “are continuing to work through this.”
    在被問及對某些商品實施相互減免關稅的可能性時，格里爾表示，雙方團隊「正繼續努力解決這個問題」。
  • consumer goods n. phr. 消費品
    Such products could cover consumer goods from the Chinese side...
    此類產品可能涵蓋中方的消費品...
  • vital adj. 極其重要的、至關重要的
    ...ensure the continued flow of rare earth magnets and critical minerals that are vital for US manufacturers.
    ...確保稀土磁鐵和關鍵礦物的持續供應，這些對美國製造商至關重要。
  • pledge v. / n. 承諾、保證
    Earlier negotiations saw China pledge purchases of US agriculture goods too...
    先前的談判見證了中國承諾採購美國農產品...
  • scrutiny n. 審查、詳細檢查
    ...and their fulfilment of the terms could be an issue under scrutiny.
    ...而他們對條款的履行可能會成為受審查的問題。
  • intention n. 意圖、打算
    “I think you will hear [Trump] announce the intention to meet with Xi Jinping two more times,”...
    “我想你會聽到 [Trump] 宣布與習近平再會面兩次的意圖，”...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The discussions, which included US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer, went for about eight hours at JPMorgan Chase & Co’s headquarters in New York.
    在此句中，關係代名詞子句「which included US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer」用來補充說明前面的主詞 The discussions（各項討論）。在英文中，若要對前面的名詞提供額外的補充細節，常用「which + 動詞」引導的非限定性關係子句。
  • 形動詞片語作分詞構句 (Participle Clause indicating simultaneous action/circumstance)
    The talks come as the countries confront broadening fears that increasingly capable AI systems could wipe out humanity, offering a rare incentive for cooperation...
    句尾的「offering a rare incentive...」為現在分詞構句，作結果或伴隨狀況的補充說明，主詞為前面的整件事情（兩國面臨 AI 威脅的局勢），主動提供了一個罕見的合作誘因。
  • 名詞子句作受詞 (Noun Clause as Object)
    Bessant said the two sides discussed setting up a way to communicate about AI issues called the “US-China AI dialogue.”
    動詞 said 後面接了一個省略了連接詞 that 的名詞子句（the two sides discussed...），用來當作 said 的受詞，表達貝森特所說的內容。
