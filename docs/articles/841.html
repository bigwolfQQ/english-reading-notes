單字解析

  • ranking n. 排名，名次
    Taipei has climbed to 42nd place in a global ranking of financial centers released yesterday...
    台北在昨天發布的一項全球金融中心排名中爬升至第 42 名...
  • attribute ... to ... phr. v. 將……歸因於……
    ...attributing the rise to the government’s push to develop Taiwan into an Asian asset management hub.
    ……並將此次排名上升歸因於政府推動台灣成為亞洲資產管理中心的努力。
  • hub n. 中心，樞紐
    ...attributing the rise to the government’s push to develop Taiwan into an Asian asset management hub.
    ……並將此次排名上升歸因於政府推動台灣成為亞洲資產管理中心的努力。
  • assess v. 評估，評價
    The index assessed financial centers on key policy priorities for the next five years.
    該指數針對未來五年的關鍵政策優先事項來評估金融中心。
  • priority n. 優先事項，首要之務
    The index assessed financial centers on key policy priorities for the next five years.
    該指數針對未來五年的關鍵政策優先事項來評估金融中心。
  • initiative n. 倡議，新措施
    The latest ranking reflects the rapid transformation of Taiwan’s financial market since the Asian Asset Management Center initiative was launched two years ago...
    最新排名反映出自兩年前啟動亞洲資產管理中心倡議以來，台灣金融市場的快速轉型……
  • launch v. 發起，啟動，推出
    The latest ranking reflects the rapid transformation of Taiwan’s financial market since the Asian Asset Management Center initiative was launched two years ago...
    最新排名反映出自兩年前啟動亞洲資產管理中心倡議以來，台灣金融市場的快速轉型……
  • recognition n. 認可，承認，賞識
    “The improvement reflects growing international recognition of Taiwan’s efforts to open its financial market...”
    「這項進步反映出國際社會對台灣努力開放金融市場……的認可度不斷提高。」
  • regulatory adj. 管理機構的，監管的
    ...promote regulatory innovation, and strengthen global connectivity...
    ……推動法規創新以及加強全球互聯性……
  • deregulation n. 放寬管制，解除管制
    ...the Financial Supervisory Commission has sought to expand wealth management through deregulation, new business lines, pilot programs and cross-border cooperation...
    ……金融監督管理委員會一直尋求透過鬆綁法規、開展新業務線、試辦計畫以及跨境合作來擴大財富管理……
  • institution n. 機構，金融機構
    Financial institutions have also increased their investment in products, services and talent...
    金融機構也增加了對產品、服務和人才的投資……
  • top v. 居……之首，名列第一
    Internationally, New York topped the index, followed by London, Hong Kong and Singapore.
    在國際方面，紐約名列指數榜首，其次是倫敦、香港和新加坡。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（用作附加說明/結果）
    Taipei has climbed to 42nd place in a global ranking of financial centers released yesterday, up from 73rd two years ago, the Taiwan Stock Exchange Corp (TWSE) said, attributing the rise to the government’s push to develop Taiwan into an Asian asset management hub.
    句尾的「attributing the rise to...」是由現在分詞（attributing）引導的分詞構句，用來補充說明前面子句所發生的動作（排名上升）所帶來的結果或原因，主詞與主要子句的主持者（Taipei 的上升這件事）一致。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    Taipei has climbed to 42nd place in a global ranking of financial centers released yesterday...
    句中的「released yesterday」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「a global ranking of financial centers」，相當於形容詞子句「which was released yesterday」，省略了「which was」以簡化句子。
  • 被動語態的運用
    The index assessed financial centers on key policy priorities for the next five years.
  • 分詞片語修飾名詞
    ...in the twice-yearly index compiled by the China Development Institute in Shenzhen, China, and Z/Yen Partners in London.
    句中的「compiled by...」是過去分詞片語，放在名詞「index」後面作為後位修飾語，用以指明該指數是由哪些機構所「編製」的，具有被動的概念。
