秋葉

Fall foliage

單字解析

  • resort n. 渡假村
    People take photographs at the Sun-Link-Sea Forest and Nature Resort in Nantou County yesterday...
    人們昨日在南投縣的杉林溪森林生態渡假園區拍照……
  • temperature n. 溫度
    With the increasing temperature gap between day and night signaling the arrival of autumn...
    隨著日夜溫差擴大預示著秋天的到來……
  • gap n. 差距
    With the increasing temperature gap between day and night signaling the arrival of autumn...
    隨著日夜溫差擴大預示著秋天的到來……
  • signal v. 預示、標誌、發出信號
    ...signaling the arrival of autumn, thousands of ornamental plants have begun transitioning from green to red...
    ……預示著秋天的到來，數千株觀賞植物已開始由綠轉紅……
  • arrival n. 到達、來臨
    ...signaling the arrival of autumn, thousands of ornamental plants have begun transitioning from green to red...
    ……預示著秋天的到來，數千株觀賞植物已開始由綠轉紅……
  • ornamental adj. 裝飾性的、觀賞用的
    ...thousands of ornamental plants have begun transitioning from green to red, the resort said.
    ……數千株觀賞植物已開始由綠轉紅，園區表示。
  • transition v. 轉變、過渡
    ...thousands of ornamental plants have begun transitioning from green to red, the resort said.
    ……數千株觀賞植物已開始由綠轉紅，園區表示。

文法重點

  • With 複合結構 (With + 名詞 + 現在分詞)
    With the increasing temperature gap between day and night signaling the arrival of autumn, thousands of ornamental plants have begun transitioning from green to red...
    「With + 名詞 + 現在分詞 (V-ing)」構成附屬子句，用來表示伴隨的情況或原因。「the increasing temperature gap」是受詞，「signaling」為主動意味的現在分詞，用來補充說明溫差擴大所帶來的狀況。
  • 現在完成式 (Present Perfect Tense)
    ...thousands of ornamental plants have begun transitioning from green to red, the resort said.
    「have begun」由「have + 過去分詞」組成，表示過去開始的動作（植物開始轉變顏色）一直持續到現在，對現況造成影響。
  • 分詞當形容詞使用
    With the increasing temperature gap...
    「increasing」是由動詞 increase 轉變而來的現在分詞，在此置於名詞 temperature gap 前方，作為形容詞使用，用來修飾正在增加中的「溫差」。
