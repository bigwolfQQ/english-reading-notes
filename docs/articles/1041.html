文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Trump’s announcement on purchases of potash comes as Washington pursues dialogue with Minsk despite its repression of dissidents and support for Russia’s invasion of Ukraine.
    句中的「supporting」或此處的「pursues」相關結構中，常利用分詞片語來補充說明名詞。在此句中，引導詞「as」引導一個時間子句，用來描述背景狀況，而子句中的動詞與修飾語精準建構出事件發生的時機與脈絡。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Prices soared after the Ukraine war.
  • 關代子句 / 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    The US imports the vast majority of its potash — a chemical salt used to manufacture fertilizer.
  • 名詞子句當受詞 (Noun Clause as Object)
    However, she stressed her belief that Americans “know who Lukashenko is.”
