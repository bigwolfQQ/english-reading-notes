賴清德在亞運表現不如預期後讚揚台灣運動員

Lai lauds Taiwan athletes after lackluster Asian Games performance

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • praise v. 稱讚，讚賞
    President William Lai yesterday praised Taiwan’s athletes for their performances at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games...
    總統賴清德昨天稱讚台灣運動員在愛知-名古屋亞洲運動會上的表現...
  • delegation n. 代表團
    Meeting members of Taiwan’s delegation at the Presidential Office in Taipei, Lai congratulated the medalists...
    賴總統在台北總統府接見台灣代表團成員時，向奪牌選手表達祝賀...
  • congratulate v. 恭喜，祝賀
    ...Lai congratulated the medalists in his remarks, saying that “regardless of the color, every medal is an honor for the country.”
    ...賴總統在致詞中向奪牌選手表達祝賀，並表示「無論成色為何，每一枚獎牌都是國家的榮譽」。
  • overall adj. 總體的，全面的
    It was Taiwan’s lowest gold medal haul and lowest overall ranking since the 1990 Asian Games in Beijing...
    這是台灣自 1990 年北京亞運以來最低的金牌斬獲與最低總排名...
  • significant adj. 重大的，有意義的
    “Your years of effort, enduring injuries and setbacks, and every step you have taken are equally significant to the country,” Lai said.
    賴總統表示：「你們多年的努力、忍受傷痛與挫折，以及你們所踏出的每一步，對國家而言都同樣意義重大。」
  • highlight v. 突顯，強調
    Lai highlighted the performances of several Taiwanese medalists, including high jumper Fu Chao-hsuan...
    賴總統特別點名表揚（突顯）多位台灣奪牌選手的表現，包括跳高選手傅兆玄...
  • overcome v. 克服
    Lai also applauded boxer Lin Yu-ting (林郁婷) for winning a second consecutive Asian Games gold after overcoming setbacks over the past two years...
    賴總統也讚賞拳擊手林郁婷在克服過去兩年的挫折後，贏得第二面連續的亞運金牌...
  • achievement n. 成就，達成
    ...but stressed that individual achievements were built on the work of an entire support team.
    ...但她強調，個人的成就建立在整個後勤支援團隊的努力之上。
  • support n. 支援，支持
    ...what I want everyone to see is the group of people who made that result possible,” she said, thanking her coach, sparring partners and training team for their support.
    她說：「我想讓大家看到的是讓這個成績成為可能的這群人」，並感謝她的教練、陪練員和訓練團隊的支持。
  • logistical adj. 後勤的，物流的
    He said his ministry would continue improving logistical support, including transportation and meals, to ensure athletes were better cared for at future competitions.
    他說，他的部會將持續改善後勤支援，包括交通和餐飲，以確保運動員在未來的比賽中受到更好的照顧。
  • ensure v. 確保，保證
    ...to ensure athletes were better cared for at future competitions.
    ...以確保運動員在未來的比賽中受到更好的照顧。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作表語／形容詞用法
    ...saying their effort was a source of national pride regardless of the amount of medals won.
    句中的 saying 為現在分詞，引導分詞片語作補充說明，用來修飾前面的主句動作，表達「一邊說著……」或「表示……」。
  • 被動語態的分詞後位修飾
    ...ranking 15th among 46 delegations based on the number of gold medals won.
    句尾的 won 是過去分詞（為 win 的過去分詞），用來修飾前面的 medals（獎牌是被贏得的，故用過去分詞表被動），形成簡化的形容詞子句（即 medals (which were) won）。
  • 關係代名詞子句（限定用法）
    ...and swimmer Wang Kuan-hung (王冠閎), whose victory ended Taiwan’s 28-year wait for an Asian Games swimming gold.
    句中的 whose 是所有格關係代名詞，用來連接先行詞 swimmer Wang Kuan-hung 與後方的子句（whose victory 即王冠閎的勝利），在句中補充說明游泳選手王冠閎的成就。
  • 虛主詞 It 的用法
    It was Taiwan’s lowest gold medal haul and lowest overall ranking since the 1990 Asian Games in Beijing...
    句首的 It 為虛主詞，用來指代前面提到的「這次參賽結果／表現」，真正的語意重心在後方的屬格名詞片語（Taiwan’s lowest gold medal haul...）。
  • 動名詞作受詞
    He said his ministry would continue improving logistical support...
    動詞 continue 後方接動名詞 improving 作為受詞，表達「持續進行改善後勤支援的動作」。
