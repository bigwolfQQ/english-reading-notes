文法重點

  • 地方副詞片語置於句首的倒裝句 (Inversion)
    Behind the confrontation lies a longstanding workplace dilemma in the medical field — the unrealistic expectation that healthcare workers should exhibit unlimited tolerance and understanding.
    當表示地方或方向的介係詞片語（如 Behind the confrontation）置於句首時，為了強調並保持句型平衡，主詞（a longstanding workplace dilemma...）與不及物動詞（lies）位置會對調，形成「地方片語 + 動詞 + 主詞」的完全倒裝結構。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    A recent incident in which a public representative took their young daughter to an emergency room and loudly berated medical staff has sparked outrage in the medical community.
    句中的 "in which a public representative..." 是形容詞子句，用來修飾先行詞 incident。此處用「介係詞 in + 關係代名詞 which」代替原本在子句中作地方副詞的片語，用以連接兩個句子，使語氣更為正式流暢。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    During peak election season or periods of overcrowding in emergency departments, politicians always pay lip service to the vital importance and hardworking nature of healthcare workers.
    句中的 "overcrowding in emergency departments" 或者是後面提到的 "critically ill patients" 都是利用現在分詞或過去分詞作為形容詞，放在名詞前後修飾名詞，能精簡語意並增加文章的緊湊感。
  • 同位語子句 (Appositive Clause)
    Behind the confrontation lies a longstanding workplace dilemma in the medical field — the unrealistic expectation that healthcare workers should exhibit unlimited tolerance and understanding.
    句中的 "that healthcare workers should exhibit unlimited tolerance and understanding" 是一個同位語子句，由連接詞 that 引導，用來詳細補充說明前方抽象名詞 "the unrealistic expectation" 的具體內容。
