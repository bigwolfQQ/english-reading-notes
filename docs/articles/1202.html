文法重點

  • 分詞作後位修飾 (Participle as Postmodifier)
    ...after a spectator at the 2026 Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games was asked to put away a towel bearing the word "Taiwan."
    句中的 "bearing the word 'Taiwan'" 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "a towel"（毛巾），相當於形容詞子句 "that bore the word 'Taiwan'"，在多益閱讀測驗中常用於簡化句子結構。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    ...the signs outside the stadium had been removed, though there was no indication that the rules inside the venue had changed.
    句中的 "had been removed" 採用了過去完成式的被動語態（had been + 過去分詞），強調「告示牌被移除」這個動作是在過去某個時間點之前已經完成，且動作的承受者是 signs（告示牌）。
  • 關係代間詞子句 (Relative Clause with Which)
    The Asian Games are overseen by the Olympic Council of Asia, which is recognized by the International Olympic Committee (IOC)...
    句中的 "which is recognized by..." 是非限定性關係代間詞子句，用逗號隔開，補充說明前面的先行詞 "the Olympic Council of Asia"（亞洲奧林匹克理事會），在多益中常考關係代間詞的格位與選用。
  • 分詞構句表條件或原因 (Participial Phrase as Prepositional Equivalent)
    The rules are therefore "quite strict," given the challenge of bringing "206 national Olympic committees together in one place"...
    句首的 "given the challenge..." 中的 given 作為介系詞使用（源自過去分詞），意思是「考慮到、鑑於」，引出規則嚴格的原因，這是多益商業英文中常見表達因果關係的高級用法。
