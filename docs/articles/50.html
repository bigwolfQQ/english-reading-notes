印尼最高人權機構呼籲調查大規模中毒事件

Indonesia top rights body calls for probe of mass poisoning

單字解析

  • investigate v. 調查，偵查
    Indonesia’s top human rights body yesterday urged the police to investigate several new outbreaks of food poisoning...
    印尼最高人權機構昨天敦促警方，調查幾起與食物中毒相關的最新爆發事件……
  • program n. 計畫，方案
    ...linked to President Prabowo Subianto’s flagship program to provide free meals to students...
    ……與普拉伯沃・蘇比安托總統為學生提供免費餐點的旗艦計畫相關……
  • launch v. 發起，推出（新計畫、產品等）
    ...as part of a scheme to eradicate malnutrition launched by Prabowo last year.
    ……作為普拉伯沃去年發起、旨在根除營養不良的方案的一部分。
  • statement n. 聲明，陳述
    “Rights to food, especially safe-to-consume food, are human rights,” the Indonesian independent rights body known as Komnas HAM said in a statement.
    被稱為 Komnas HAM 的印尼獨立人權機構在聲明中表示：「食物權，尤其是安全可食用的食物，是基本人權。」
  • standard n. 標準
    “Without a rigorous sanitation standard ... the nutrition intervention that the state is doing will lose its moral legitimacy,” it said.
    該委員會表示：「若沒有嚴格的衛生標準……國家正在推動的營養干預措施將失去其道德正當性。」
  • suspend v. 暫停，暫時中止
    The rights group also called on the National Nutrition Agency, which oversees the program, to suspend the kitchens allegedly responsible for the poisoning outbreaks.
    該人權團體也呼籲負責監督該計畫的國家營養局，暫停營運涉嫌導致中毒事件的廚房。
  • oversee v. 監督，監管
    The rights group also called on the National Nutrition Agency, which oversees the program, to suspend the kitchens allegedly responsible for the poisoning outbreaks.
    該人權團體也呼籲負責監督該計畫的國家營養局，暫停營運涉嫌導致中毒事件的廚房。
  • handle v. 處理，應付
    It did not immediately respond to a request for comment, but agency head Sudaryono on Thursday said that the poisoning cases would be handled seriously.
    該機構未立即回應置評請求，但局長蘇達里約諾於週四表示，中毒案件將被嚴肅處理。
  • delay v. 延期，延誤
    The overseeing agency was slated to hold a press conference yesterday, but delayed it without providing a reason or a rescheduled time.
    負責監督的機構原定於昨天舉行記者會，但未說明理由或重新安排時間便延期了。
  • survey v. 民意調查，調查
    ...with only one-third of Indonesians surveyed in July expressing satisfaction with it...
    ……7 月接受調查的印尼人中僅有三分之一對該計畫表示滿意……
  • satisfaction n. 滿意，滿足
    ...with only one-third of Indonesians surveyed in July expressing satisfaction with it...
    ……7 月接受調查的印尼人中僅有三分之一對該計畫表示滿意……
  • cancel v. 取消
    “My hope as a parent is for the central or regional government to cancel the free meal program.”
    「我身為家長的希望，是中央或地方政府能夠取消免費餐點計畫。」

文法重點

  • 過去分詞（片語）作後置定語修飾名詞
    Indonesia’s top human rights body yesterday urged the police to investigate several new outbreaks of food poisoning linked to President Prabowo Subianto’s flagship program...
    句中的「linked to President Prabowo Subianto’s flagship program...」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「outbreaks of food poisoning」。這種結構省略了「which are」，能讓句子更精簡，是新聞英語中非常常見的高頻句構。
  • 關係代名詞子句（主格）修飾先行詞
    The rights group also called on the National Nutrition Agency, which oversees the program, to suspend the kitchens allegedly responsible for the poisoning outbreaks.
    句中的「which oversees the program」是一個非限定性關係子句，用逗號隔開，對先行詞「the National Nutrition Agency」進行補充說明。「which」在子句中擔任主格，代替機構並接續動詞「oversees」。
  • be slated to (do sth.) 表定預定 / 預計
    The overseeing agency was slated to hold a press conference yesterday, but delayed it without providing a reason or a rescheduled time.
    片語「be slated to」意指「預定、預計要……」，在商業與新聞英語中常用来表達原訂的行程或計畫。句中用過去式「was slated to」表示原本昨天要舉行記者會，但後來延期了。
  • 獨立分詞結構（Absolute Phrase / 附帶狀況）
    As of Tuesday, 50,000 individuals had been affected by food poisoning, lawmaker Charles Honoris said on Wednesday, citing health department data...
    句尾的「citing health department data」是由現在分詞「citing」引導的分詞構句，主詞與主要子句的主語（lawmaker Charles Honoris）一致，表示議員在發言的同時，也一併引用了健康部門的數據，補充說明消息來源。
