台北市衛生局調查某高中疑似食物中毒事件

Taipei health department probes high-school over suspected food poisoning

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • inspectors n. 檢查員、視察員
    The Taipei Department of Health yesterday said it sent inspectors to a high school in the city to check its cafeteria, after two students reported gastrointestinal symptoms following their school lunch.
    台北市衛生局昨天表示，在兩名學生食用學校午餐後回報腸胃道症狀，已派員前往市內一所高中檢查其學生餐廳。
  • cafeteria n. 員工餐廳、學校餐廳
    A Taipei Municipal Zhongshan Girls’ High School student on Sunday evening wrote on social media that she began feeling ill about two hours after she ate fishcake tempura from the school’s cafeteria on Monday last week.
    台北市立中山女子高級中學的一名學生週日晚間在社群媒體上發文表示，她上週一吃了學校餐廳的甜不辣後，大約兩小時開始感到身體不適。
  • confirm v. 確認、證實
    The school yesterday confirmed that the student had felt discomfort after lunch on Monday last week and was diagnosed with gastroenteritis.
    校方昨天證實，該學生在上週一午餐後感到不適，並被診斷出腸胃炎。
  • cooperate v. 合作、配合
    The cafeteria has been ordered to temporarily stop selling deep-fried food, while the school would continue to follow up on the student’s blood test results and cooperate with inspections, it added.
    校方補充說，學生餐廳已被下令暫停販售油炸食品，同時學校將繼續追蹤學生的血液檢查結果並配合稽查。
  • hygiene n. 衛生
    The department said it did not receive a report of suspected food poisoning from the hospital or the school, but has sent inspectors to check the personnel hygiene of workers in the cafeteria, food handling, pest control and workplace hygiene.
    衛生局表示，未接獲醫院或學校通報疑似食物中毒，但已派員前往檢查餐廳員工的個人衛生、食品處理、病蟲害防治及工作場所衛生。
  • specified adj. 指定的、規定的
    If food safety issues are found, the cafeteria would be required to improve the situation within a specified time, and might face a fine if it fails to meet required standards in a second inspection, it said.
    衛生局表示，若發現食品安全問題，餐廳將被要求在指定時間內改善，若複查未達規定標準，可能會面臨罰款。
  • fine n. 罰款、罰金
    If food safety issues are found, the cafeteria would be required to improve the situation within a specified time, and might face a fine if it fails to meet required standards in a second inspection, it said.
    衛生局表示，若發現食品安全問題，餐廳將被要求在指定時間內改善，若複查未達規定標準，可能會面臨罰款。
  • vendor n. 供應商、小販
    Two high schools in Taipei — Chengyuan High School and Da Li Senior High School — also reported suspected food poisoning cases after students ate school lunch provided by the same contracted vendor last week.
    台北市兩所高中——成淵高中和大理高中——上週也傳出學生食用同一家外包廠商提供的學校午餐後，通報疑似食物中毒案件。
  • temporarily adv. 暫時地
    The cafeteria has been ordered to temporarily stop selling deep-fried food, while the school would continue to follow up on the student’s blood test results and cooperate with inspections, it added.
    校方補充說，學生餐廳已被下令暫停販售油炸食品，同時學校將繼續追蹤學生的血液檢查結果並配合稽查。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Two high schools in Taipei — Chengyuan High School and Da Li Senior High School — also reported suspected food poisoning cases after students ate school lunch provided by the same contracted vendor last week.
    句中的「provided by the same contracted vendor」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的「school lunch (學校午餐)」，相當於形容詞子句「which was provided by the same contracted vendor...」的簡化形式，能讓句子更簡潔。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The cafeteria has been ordered to temporarily stop selling deep-fried food, while the school would continue to follow up on the student’s blood test results and cooperate with inspections, it added.
    句中的「has been ordered」使用了現在完成式的被動語態 (have/has been + 過去分詞)，強調學生餐廳是被衛生局或校方「下令」停業的，著重於動作的承受者與結果。
  • 名詞子句作受詞 (Noun Clause as Object)
    The school yesterday confirmed that the student had felt discomfort after lunch on Monday last week and was diagnosed with gastroenteritis.
    「that the student had felt discomfort... and was diagnosed with...」是由連接詞 that 引導的名詞子句，在句中擔任動詞 confirmed 的受詞，用來表達校方證實的具體內容。
