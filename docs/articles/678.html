文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Present Participle）作後置定語
    Gullner made the remarks during the opening ceremony of a workshop organized by the Taipei-based Forward Alliance, a Taiwanese national security and civil defense think tank.
    句中的「organized by the Taipei-based Forward Alliance」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 a workshop。分詞片語放在名詞後面，相當於一個省略了關代與be動詞的形容詞子句（即 which was organized by...），在新聞英文中是非常常見的精簡表達法。
  • 現在完成進行式（Present Perfect Continuous）
    Over the past several years, Europe has been strengthening its civil protection mechanisms, particularly in light of Russia’s invasion of Ukraine, he said.
    句型「has been + V-ing」表示某個動作從過去某個時間點開始，持續進行到現在，且可能還會持續下去。用來強調歐洲在過去幾年「持續不斷地」加強民防機制。
  • 現在分詞表達附帶結果或補充說明
    ...saying such efforts were crucial for both the EU and Taiwan to enhance resilience.
  • 動名詞（Gerund）作介系詞的受詞
    The EU, meanwhile, has more experience dealing with disasters such as wildfires and civil protection mechanisms, he said.
    介系詞（如 with 省略或隱含在片語中，在此為 experience [in] dealing with... 的概念，或直接修飾）後方接動詞時須使用動名詞（V-ing）。此處 dealing with 當作處理災害的動名詞用法，在多益測驗中經常出現在「have experience/trouble/difficulty (in) doing sth」的句型裡。
