社論：中國無法封殺言論自由

EDITORIAL: China cannot silence free speech

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • recruitment n. 招募，吸納（文章中使用動詞 recruit）
    recruited a 35-year-old man surnamed Kuo (郭)
    招募了一名 35 歲、姓郭的男子
  • property n. 財產，房地產
    Property was damaged, staff were frightened and some customers sought refunds
    財產受損、員工受到驚嚇，且部分顧客要求退費
  • refund n. / v. 退款，退費
    some customers sought refunds
    部分顧客要求退費
  • restrict v. 限制，限定
    intended to restrict freedom of expression
    旨在限制言論自由
  • deny v. 否認，拒絕
    China’s Taiwan Affairs Office denied knowledge of the incident
    中國國務院台灣事務辦公室否認知悉此事件
  • dismiss v. 對……不予理會，駁回，解散
    and dismissed the accusation of transnational repression.
    並駁斥關於跨國鎮壓的指控。
  • target v. / n. 把……作為攻擊目標，對象
    Joshua Wong (黃之鋒) was targeted by pro-China protesters
    黃之鋒成為親中抗議人士的目標
  • guideline n. 指導方針，準則

  • jurisdiction n. 司法權，管轄權

  • boycott n. / v. 聯合抵制

  • promotional adj. 宣傳的，促銷的

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Present Participle）作後位修飾
    A 33-year-old man surnamed Liu (劉), allegedly acting on instructions from an unidentified person in China, recruited a 35-year-old man surnamed Kuo (郭)...
    句中的「allegedly acting on instructions...」是由現在分詞片語引導的修飾語，用來補充說明主詞「A 33-year-old man」的動作背景，相當於一個原因或附帶狀況的副詞子句。
  • 關係代名詞子句（Relative Clause）修飾先行詞
    The incident happened on a night when comedian Huang Yi-hao (黃逸豪), who had mocked Chinese President Xi Jinping (習近平), was scheduled to perform.
    句中包含兩個子句：「when comedian Huang Yi-hao... was scheduled to perform」作為關係副詞子句修飾時間先行詞 "night"；而「who had mocked Chinese President Xi Jinping」則是非限定關係代名詞子句，用來補充說明喜劇演員黃逸豪的背景。
  • 被動語態（Passive Voice）
    Property was damaged, staff were frightened and some customers sought refunds, prosecutors said.
    在新聞報導中，常使用被動語態（如 was damaged, were frightened, is protected）來強調動作的承受者（例如財產、員工、言論），或是當施動者不明或不須強調時聚焦於事件本身。
  • 分詞構句表條件與結果（Conditional Participle Construction）
    If fear of Beijing causes people to silence themselves, Taiwan risks surrendering the freedoms that distinguish its democratic system from the Chinese regime while encouraging further attempts at transnational repression.
    雖然此句是以連接詞 "If" 引導的條件句，但後半部的 "while encouraging further attempts..." 則是省略了主詞與be動詞的分詞構句（while (it is) encouraging...），用來表達在主句發生同時伴隨產生的結果。
