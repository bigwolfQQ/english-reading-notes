單字解析

  • compensate v. 補償、賠償
    Workers at Taiwanese snack maker Kuai Kuai Co. protested outside the Presidential Office on Monday, demanding compensation after the company sold the site of its factory...
    台灣零食製造商乖乖公司的員工週一前往總統府外抗議，在公司出售工廠土地後要求補償...
  • relocate v. 遷移、搬遷（企業或營運）
    ...and announced plans to relocate operations to Hsinchu.
    ......並宣布將營運遷至新竹的計畫。
  • safeguard v. 保護、維護（權益等）
    ...and accused San-Ti Group, which controls the snack maker, of failing to adequately safeguard the interests of long-serving workers.
    ......並指控掌控這家零食製造商的三地集團未能妥善保障資深員工的權益。

    "Now that the group has made substantial profits from selling the land, it should give priority to compensating long-serving, low-paid workers," Liu said...
    劉表示：「既然集團從售地中獲取了鉅額利潤，就應該優先補償長期服務、薪資低廉的員工。」
  • priority n. 優先權、首要之務
    ...it should give priority to compensating long-serving, low-paid workers...
    ......就應該優先補償長期服務、薪資低廉的員工......
  • formally adv. 正式地
    ...and were not formally informed until Sept. 1 of plans to relocate the factory to Hsinchu...
    ......直到 9 月 1 日才被正式告知工會搬遷至新竹的計畫......

    ...making the proposed relocation impractical for many employees.
    ......這使得擬議中的搬遷對許多員工來說不切實際。

    ......並指控公司希望員工自願離職，以便規避支付資遣費......

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    Workers at Taiwanese snack maker Kuai Kuai Co. protested outside the Presidential Office on Monday, demanding compensation after the company sold the site...
    句尾的「demanding compensation...」是由現在分詞（demanding）帶領的分詞片語，用來補充說明主詞 workers 在抗議當下的伴隨動作或狀態，相當於「and demanded compensation...」的簡化寫法。
  • 現在完成式與被動語態 (Present Perfect Passive)
    CNA has reached out to Kuai Kuai for comment but had received no response as of press time.
    句中的「has reached out」使用現在完成式，表示從過去持續到現在的動作；而後段的「had received」則使用了過去完成式（搭配時間點 as of press time），用以表達在過去某一時間點之前尚未發生的動作。
  • 形動詞／名詞化形容詞 (Adjective used as a noun / Long-serving)
    ...failing to adequately safeguard the interests of long-serving workers.
    複合形容詞「long-serving」（服務多年的、資深的）由副詞/形容詞與現在分詞結合而成，用來修飾名詞 workers，是多益職場英文中常出現的高效表達法。
