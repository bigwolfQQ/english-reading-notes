民進黨播映紀念女權運動人士彭婉如的紀錄片

DPP screens film honoring feminist Peng Wan-ru

單字解析

  • attend v. 出席，參加
    President William Lai yesterday attended the screening of a film marking the 30th anniversary of the murder of prominent feminist and women’s rights activist Peng Wan-ru.
    總統賴清德昨天出席了一部紀錄片的放映會，該片旨在紀念傑出女權主義者與婦女運動權益倡議者彭婉如遇害 30 週年。
  • department n. 部門，系
    Peng became the first director of the Democratic Progressive Party’s (DPP) Department of Women’s Affairs in 1996.
    彭婉如於 1996 年成為民主進步黨（DPP）婦女發展部的第一任主任。
  • disappear v. 消失，失蹤
    On Nov. 30 that year, while in Kaohsiung for a party meeting, she disappeared after apparently taking a taxi.
    同年 11 月 30 日，她在高雄參加黨內會議期間，疑似搭乘計程車後失蹤。
  • participate v. 參與，參加
    The DPP’s Department of Gender Equality spent 18 months producing Dear Sisters (親愛的姊妹), a commemorative film about Peng and the history of women’s participation in politics.
    民進黨性別平等部耗時 18 個月製作了《親愛的姊妹》，這是一部關於彭婉如以及女性參與政治歷史的紀念影片。
  • establish v. 建立，設立
    This year is also the 40th anniversary of the party’s founding and the 30th anniversary of the establishment of its women’s affairs department.
    今年也是該黨創黨 40 週年，以及其婦女事務部門成立 30 週年。
  • pursue v. 追求，追尋
    Lai said it was meaningful to gather to remember Peng, adding that the party has continued to pursue her vision of greater participation by women in politics.
    賴清德表示，聚在一起追思彭婉如深具意義，並補充說該黨一直持續追求她對於女性更多參與政治的願景。
  • keep in mind phr. 記住，銘記在心
    “For me, Peng has never left us,” he said, adding that he keeps in mind Peng’s proposed “one-quarter quota” to guarantee women’s participation in politics.
    「對我而言，彭婉如從未離開我們，」他表示，並補充說他一直銘記彭婉如所提出的「四分之一名額保障」以確保女性參與政治。
  • guarantee v. 保證，擔保
    “For me, Peng has never left us,” he said, adding that he keeps in mind Peng’s proposed “one-quarter quota” to guarantee women’s participation in politics.
    「對我而言，彭婉如從未離開我們，」他表示，並補充說他一直銘記彭婉如所提出的「四分之一名額保障」以確保女性參與政治。
  • adopt v. 採納，採取
    The DPP and Chinese Nationalist Party (KMT) later adopted the provision, and the government passed the Sexual Assault Crime Prevention Act and Domestic Violence Prevention Act, he said.
    他表示，民進黨與中國國民黨（KMT）隨後採納了這項規定，政府也通過了《性侵害犯罪防治法》與《家庭暴力防治法》。
  • pass v. 通過（法案等）

    他表示，民進黨與中國國民黨（KMT）隨後採納了這項規定，政府也通過了《性侵害犯罪防治法》與《家庭暴力防治法》。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    President William Lai yesterday attended the screening of a film marking the 30th anniversary of the murder of prominent feminist and women’s rights activist Peng Wan-ru.
    句中的「marking the 30th anniversary...」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 a film，相當於形容詞子句「that/which marked the 30th anniversary...」，用來精簡地補充說明該部電影的內容。
  • 對等連接詞連接的多重分詞構句與並列子句
    On Nov. 30 that year, while in Kaohsiung for a party meeting, she disappeared after apparently taking a taxi.
    「while in Kaohsiung...」省略了主詞與be動詞（while she was in Kaohsiung），是一種常見的時間副詞子句簡化結構。而「after apparently taking a taxi」則是介系詞片語，其中的動名詞 taking 前面加上副詞 apparently 來修飾動作發生的狀態。
  • 名詞子句作受詞 (Noun Clause as Object)
    Lai said it was meaningful to gather to remember Peng, adding that the party has continued to pursue her vision of greater participation by women in politics.
    動詞 adding 後面接了一個由 that引導的名詞子句（that the party has continued to pursue her vision...），在句中作為 adding 的受詞，表達賴總統額外補充說明及陳述的內容。
  • 虛主詞 It 與不定詞片語 (Dummy Subject 'It' and Infinitive Phrase)
    Lai said it was meaningful to gather to remember Peng
    句型中的 it 是虛主詞，用來代替真正的長主詞「to gather to remember Peng」（聚集追思彭婉如）。為了避免頭重腳輕，英文習慣將不定詞片語移至句尾，而用 it 作為句首的先導主詞。
