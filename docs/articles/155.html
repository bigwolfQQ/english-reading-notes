因竹北口角，民眾黨暫停新竹縣與國民黨之合作

TPP halts cooperation with KMT in Hsinchu County after Zhubei spat

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • cooperation n. 合作
    The Taiwan People's Party (TPP) on Sunday said it would halt all electoral cooperation with the Kuomintang (KMT) in Hsinchu County...
    台灣民眾黨（TPP）於週日表示，將暫停與中國國民黨（KMT）在新竹縣的所有選舉合作...
  • halt v. 停止，暫停
    ...said it would halt all electoral cooperation with the Kuomintang...
    ...表示將暫停與國民黨的所有選舉合作...
  • agreement n. 協議，同意
    ...claimed during an interview on Friday that the parties had not made any agreement on who should defer to whom...
    ...週五在受訪時聲稱，雙方政黨並未就誰該禮讓誰達成任何協議...
  • pledge v. 保證，承諾
    ...and that the TPP had never formally pledged to support a primary.
    ...且民眾黨從未正式承諾支持初選。
  • suspend v. 暫停，中止
    ...the party passed a resolution suspending electoral cooperation with the KMT on all races in Hsinchu County.
    ...黨通過決議，暫停與國民黨在新竹縣所有選戰的選舉合作。
  • foundation n. 基礎
    ...had "seriously damaged the foundation of mutual trust that the TPP and KMT have gradually built over the last two years."
    ...已「嚴重破壞了民眾黨與國民黨過去兩年來逐步建立的互信基礎」。
  • unite v. 團結
    Chou also called on TPP members to unite behind its nominee in the Zhubei race...
    周榆修也呼籲民眾黨黨員團結支持黨提名的竹北候選人...
  • coordinate v. 協調
    The larger opposition KMT and smaller TPP reached an agreement in March to coordinate candidates and cooperate on policy positions...
    較大的在野黨國民黨與較小的民眾黨於3月達成協議，將協調候選人並在政策立場上進行合作...
  • propose v. 提出（文章中使用 put forward）
    ...while the TPP did not put forward a candidate.
    ...而民眾黨未推出候選人。
  • proximity n. 鄰近，接近
    ...because of their proximity to Hsinchu Science Park.
    ...因其鄰近新竹科學園區。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Hsu Hsin-ying, the KMT's nominee for Hsinchu County magistrate, was the founder in 2015 of the Minkuotang, a KMT splinter party.
    句中的「a KMT splinter party」是名詞同位語，用來補充說明前面的名詞短語「the Minkuotang」。而在其他段落中常見如「...warning that any party members who...」或分詞構句，此處展示了名詞同位語如何緊接在專有名詞後方提供額外背景資訊。
  • 關係代間子句 (Relative Clause)
    ...the foundation of mutual trust that the TPP and KMT have gradually built over the last two years.
    使用關代「that」引導限定修飾子句（在此作為受格，故可省略），用來修飾先行詞「the foundation of mutual trust」，精確交代這個互信基礎是過去兩年內建立起來的。
  • 現在分詞表達附帶結果或狀態 (Present Participle indicating Result/Action)
    ...accusing the KMT and Hsu of "going back on their word," and then making matters worse by "continuing to lie about it in public."
    句中的「accusing...」與「making...」為現在分詞片語，用來接續主句之後，表示伴隨發生的動作或帶有因果關係的結果，使語氣更具連貫性與動態感。
