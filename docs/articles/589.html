台灣西部週日氣溫炎熱

Western Taiwan sees hot weather Sunday

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • issue v. 發布、發行、核發
    The Central Weather Administration (CWA) on Sunday issued high-temperature warnings for 10 cities and counties across Taiwan...
    中央氣象署於週日針對全台10個縣市發布高溫特報……
  • warning n. 警告、警報
    The Central Weather Administration (CWA) on Sunday issued high-temperature warnings for 10 cities and counties across Taiwan...
    中央氣象署於週日針對全台10個縣市發布高溫特報……
  • across prep. 遍及、橫跨
    ...issued high-temperature warnings for 10 cities and counties across Taiwan, with the mercury reaching 37.4 degrees Celsius...
    ……針對全台10個縣市發布高溫特報，氣溫達到攝氏37.4度……
  • indicate v. 顯示、指出、表明
    A yellow alert indicates temperatures of 36-38 degrees.
    黃色燈號表示氣溫達36至38度。
  • maximum adj. 最大值的、最高點的
    According to CWA data, Sunday's maximum temperature at around 2 p.m. was recorded in Dapu...
    根據氣象署資料，週日下午2點左右的最高溫記錄在大埔……
  • record v. 記錄、記載
    ...Sunday's maximum temperature at around 2 p.m. was recorded in Dapu, followed by a reading of 36.7 degrees...
    ……週日下午2點左右的最高溫記錄在大埔，其次是測得36.7度……
  • follow v. 接著、隨後
    ...followed by a reading of 36.7 degrees in Xihu Township, Changhua County and 36.5 degrees in Nansi District, Tainan.
    ……其次是彰化縣西湖鄉測得36.7度，以及台南市楠西區測得36.5度。
  • data n. 數據、資料
    According to CWA data, Sunday's maximum temperature at around 2 p.m. was recorded in Dapu...
    根據氣象署資料，週日下午2點左右的最高溫記錄在大埔……

文法重點

  • with + 名詞 + 分詞（獨立分詞構句）
    ...with the mercury reaching 37.4 degrees Celsius in Chiayi County's Dapu Township in western Taiwan in the early afternoon.
    「with + O. + V-ing/P.P.」結構常在新聞英語中用來補充說明背景狀況、附帶發生的動作或結果，在此句中用來補充說明氣溫達到特定數值的狀況。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Warnings were issued for Taoyuan in the north; Taichung, Changhua County... in central Taiwan; Tainan...
    當動作的承受者是句子的主詞時，使用「be 動詞 + 過去分詞 (P.P.)」。新聞報導中常使用被動語態來強調動作的承受者（此處為警告、特報），而不特別強調發布的執行者。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾
    ...followed by a reading of 36.7 degrees in Xihu Township...
    句中的「followed by...」是由被動分詞片語所構成，用來修飾前面的數據或情況，表達「接下來是……」或「隨後為……」，能讓冗長的句子更為精簡流暢。
