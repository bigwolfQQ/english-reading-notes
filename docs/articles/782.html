日本為數千名運動員做好準備

Japan readies for thousands of athletes

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • organizer n. 主辦單位，組織者
    The number of participants — more than the Olympics — has heavily taxed the organizers, who initially expected 15,000 people and have had to scramble to find accommodation for the rest.
    參賽人數——甚至超過奧運規模——帶給主辦單位沉重的負擔，他們最初預期有 15,000 人，因而不得不倉促應變以替剩下的人尋找住宿。
  • accommodation n. 住宿，膳宿
    They choose from the outset to house athletes in temporary accommodation in a bid to cut costs, ranging from a cruise liner and wooden containers to hotel rooms.
    為了削減成本，他們從一開始就選擇將運動員安置在臨時住所中，其範圍從遊輪、組合屋貨櫃到飯店房間不等。
  • cost n. 成本，費用
    They choose from the outset to house athletes in temporary accommodation in a bid to cut costs, ranging from a cruise liner and wooden containers to hotel rooms.
    為了削減成本，他們從一開始就選擇將運動員安置在臨時住所中，其範圍從遊輪、組合屋貨櫃到飯店房間不等。
  • shortage n. 短缺，不足
    With just days to go, a South Korean Olympic committee official said that there was “a serious shortage of accommodation.”
    距離開幕僅剩幾天，一名南韓奧會官員表示，「住宿嚴重短缺。」
  • disrupt v. 擾亂，中斷
    Weather has already disrupted preparations, with record rainfall forcing athletes to evacuate their accommodation briefly last week.
    天氣已經打亂了籌備工作，破紀錄的降雨迫使運動員上週短暫撤離住所。
  • damage n. 損害，破壞
    Rainwater leaked into some competition venues, although organizers said there was no serious damage.
    雨水滲入了一些比賽場館，不過主辦單位表示沒有造成嚴重損害。
  • compete v. 競爭，參賽
    Athletes from 45 countries and territories are competing in more than 40 sports across the two weeks.
    來自 45 個國家和地區的運動員將在為期兩週的時間裡參加 40多項運動的競賽。
  • attract v. 吸引
    In a bid to attract a new audience there would be eSports, breakdancing and BMX cycling.
    為了吸引新的觀眾群，將會引入電子競技、霹靂舞和 BMX 自由式單車。
  • eligibility n. 資格，合格性
    Taiwanese boxer Lin Yu-ting is competing, two years after she won Paris Olympics gold in the middle of a gender row that saw her eligibility questioned.
    台灣拳擊手林郁婷也將參賽，兩年前她在巴黎奧運奪得金牌，當時她正處於一場性別爭議的風波中，資格也遭到質疑。
  • showcase v. 展示，展現
    “We would showcase China’s standard and strength on the regional and global stage,” diver Wang Zongyuan said.
    跳水選手王宗源表示：「我們將在地區和全球舞台上展現中國的水準與實力。」
  • absence n. 缺席，缺乏
    Sooryavanshi would be part of an India cricket team looking to make a statement before the sport returns to the Olympics in LA after an absence of 128 years.
    蘇里瓦什將成為印度板球隊的一員，該隊期望在板球闊別奧運 128 年後重返洛杉磯奧運之前宣示實力。

文法重點

  • 關係代名詞非限定用法 (which)
    More than 17,000 competitors and officials are expected at the continent’s biggest sporting spectacular, which takes place in Nagoya and the wider Aichi Prefecture until October 4.
    句中的 「which takes place...」 前面有逗號，屬於關係代名詞的非限定用法，用來補充說明前面的先行詞 「the continent’s biggest sporting spectacular」（該洲最大的體育盛會），補充其舉辦地點與時間，且在此不能代換成 that。
  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況 (V-ing)
    The number of participants — more than the Olympics — has heavily taxed the organizers, who initially expected 15,000 people and have had to scramble to find accommodation for the rest.
  • 被動語態 (Be + 過去分詞)
    The unusual accommodation plans have been met with concern by some countries, with India building its own replica container huts to brace athletes for their stay.
    句中的 「have been met with」 採用了現在完成式的被動語態，強調「不尋常的住宿計畫」是被一些國家「以……態度來面對（或引發關注）」，凸顯主語承受動作的語氣。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (With + 名詞 + 分詞)
    With just days to go, a South Korean Olympic committee official said that there was “a serious shortage of accommodation.”
    「With + 名詞 + to go / V-ing / V-ed」是一種常見的獨立分詞構句結構，用來表達伴隨狀況或背景條件。在此 「With just days to go」 意指「距離（比賽）僅剩幾天」，用來點出發言時的時間緊迫性。
