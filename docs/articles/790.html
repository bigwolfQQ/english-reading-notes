喀什米爾：湖泊雜草結合傳統藝術

Lake weeds meet traditional art in Kashmir

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • ingredient n. 原料、成分
    A Kashmiri artisan is turning weeds pulled from Srinagar’s famous Dal Lake into a new ingredient for the centuries-old craft of papier-mache...
    一名喀什米爾工匠正將從斯里納加著名的達爾湖中拔除的雜草，轉化為具有數百年歷史的紙漿藝術的新原料...
  • famed adj. 聞名的、著名的
    Dal Lake is famed for its floating gardens and houseboats against a Himalayan backdrop.
    達爾湖以其以喜馬拉雅山為背景的花園浮島和船屋而聞名。
  • struggle with v. phr. 苦於、努力掙扎應對
    However, the lake has long struggled with heavy growth of aquatic weeds, which Indian authorities regularly remove to keep its waterways clear.
    然而，該湖長期以來一直苦於水生雜草的大量生長，印度官方會定期清除這些雜草以保持水道暢通。
  • remove v. 移除、清除
    ...which Indian authorities regularly remove to keep its waterways clear.
    ...印度官方會定期清除這些雜草以保持水道暢通。
  • rising adj. 上漲的、升高的
    ...after the rising cost of paper made traditional papier-mache increasingly expensive.
    ...在紙張成本上漲導致傳統紙漿藝術日益昂貴之後...
  • export v. 出口
    ...as well as Christmas ornaments exported worldwide.
    ...以及出口到世界各地的聖誕裝飾品。
  • material n. 材料、素材
    Irshad searched for a cheaper, locally available material and eventually turned to aquatic vegetation regularly cleared from the lake as waste.
    伊爾沙德尋找更便宜、當地可取得的材料，最終轉而利用作為廢棄物從湖中定期清除的水生植物。
  • production n. 生產、產量
    “Why not use something that can increase production, make it durable and also make it cheaper?” she said.
    她說：「為什麼不使用能夠提高產量、使其經久耐用且成本更低的東西呢？」
  • durable adj. 耐用的、持久的
    “Why not use something that can increase production, make it durable and also make it cheaper?” she said.
    她說：「為什麼不使用能夠提高產量、使其經久耐用且成本更低的東西呢？」
  • process v. 加工、處理
    Irshad collects the weeds by boat before drying and processing them at her workshop.
    伊爾沙德乘船收集雜草，然後在她的工作室進行乾燥和加工。
  • suitable adj. 合適的、適當的
    She tested different vegetation before finding the lake weeds most suitable because of their higher moisture content.
    她測試了不同的植物，才發現湖泊雜草因其較高的水分含量而最為合適。
  • expand v. 擴大、擴展
    Irshad hopes to expand the initiative, but said she needs more support to provide work for additional women.
    伊爾沙德希望擴大這項計畫，但也表示她需要更多的支持來為更多的婦女提供工作機會。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    A Kashmiri artisan is turning weeds pulled from Srinagar’s famous Dal Lake into a new ingredient for the centuries-old craft...
    句中的 "pulled from Srinagar’s famous Dal Lake" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 weeds（雜草），相當於形容詞子句 "which are pulled from..." 的簡化版，省略了主格關代與be動詞，使語氣更為緊湊精鍊。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Among its past visitors was Beatles guitarist George Harrison, who stayed on a houseboat in 1966 and met sitar maestro Ravi Shankar.
    逗號後方的 "who stayed on a houseboat..." 是非限定關係子句，對先行詞 George Harrison 補充說明額外資訊。在多益閱讀測驗中，常考此類結構的格位選擇（如 who 當主詞 vs. whom 當受詞）。
  • 省略句型：Why not + 動詞原形
    “Why not use something that can increase production, make it durable and also make it cheaper?” she said.
    句型 "Why not + V." 用於提出建議、反問或表達「為什麼不……呢？」，是由 "Why don't you / we use..." 簡化而來的實用口語及商務溝通句型，常出現在多益聽力測驗的建議與回應題組中。
  • 對等連接詞連接多個並列動詞 (Parallel Verbs)
    Irshad collects the weeds by boat before drying and processing them at her workshop.
    句中的 "drying and processing" 是由對等連接詞 and 連接的兩個動名词（動詞加 -ing，在介詞 before 後方作受詞），共享同一個受詞 them，展現了英文精簡表達多個連續動作的句構技巧。
