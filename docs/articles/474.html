台灣將以演習紀念國家防災日

Taiwan to mark Disaster Prevention Day with drills

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • mobilization n. 動員
    A large-scale earthquake drill in eastern Taiwan, nationwide emergency alarm tests and the mobilization of more than 176,000 civilian disaster responders are among the activities scheduled for National Disaster Prevention Day...
    台灣東部的大規模地震演習、全國性緊急警報測試以及動員超過 17萬 6千名民間防災應變人員，是國家防災日的預定活動之一...
  • scheduled adj. 預定的，安排的
    ...are among the activities scheduled for National Disaster Prevention Day, with the government yesterday launching the annual campaign.
    ...是國家防災日的預定活動之一，政府昨日正式啟動了這項年度活動。
  • campaign n. 活動，運動（指為達到某個商業或政治目的而進行的一系列有計畫的行動）
    ...with the government yesterday launching the annual campaign.
    ...政府昨日正式啟動了這項年度活動。
  • preparedness n. 準備狀態，預防準備
    The Ministry of the Interior and the Ministry of Education announced the disaster response exercises at an event in New Taipei City to promote public preparedness for national emergencies.
    內政部與教育部在新北市的一場活動中宣布了災害應變演練，以提升公眾對國家緊急狀況的準備意識。
  • interval n. 間隔，距離
    The tsunami siren would be sounded twice in five-second alerts, with a five-second interval before a recorded message ordering an evacuation is played, they said.
    他們表示，海嘯警報將以五秒的警示聲響起兩次，並在播放要求疏散的錄音訊息前有五秒的間隔。
  • evacuation n. 疏散，撤離
    ...with a five-second interval before a recorded message ordering an evacuation is played, they said.
    ...並在播放要求疏散的錄音訊息前有五秒的間隔，他們說。
  • conduct v. 執行，舉辦，實施
    First responders are to be mobilized from Wednesday to Friday next week to conduct simulated search and rescue operations...
    第一線應變人員將於下週三至週五進行動員，以執行模擬搜救行動...
  • efficiently adv. 有效率地
    The exercises would test key capabilities, as well as the government’s ability to control its emergency response assets efficiently, the spokesperson said.
    發言人表示，這些演習將測試關鍵能力，以及政府有效掌控其緊急應變資產的能力。
  • comprise v. 包含，由……組成
    The Ministry of the Interior has helped establish 275 Taiwan Community Emergency Response Teams, comprising 5,000 volunteer first responders and 176,000 certified disaster relief volunteers...
    內政部已協助成立了 275 個台灣韌性社區防災士團隊，由 5,000 名志願第一線應變人員以及 176,000 名認證防災志工組成...
  • goal n. 目標
    The interior ministry’s goal is to have a force of 200,000 certified disaster relief volunteers by the end of the year, Liu said.
    劉表示，內政部的目標是在年底前擁有由 200,000 名認證防災志工組成的力量。
  • revolve around phr. v. 圍繞著……轉，以……為中心
    The certified disaster relief volunteer concept revolves around training people who already work in communities, enterprises, schools and large buildings using their first aid and other skills to protect others, she added.
    她補充說，認證防災志工的理念核心在於培訓已在社區、企業、學校及大型建築物工作的人員，利用他們的急救及其他技能來保護他人。

文法重點

  • be to + 預定動詞原形 (be to do)
    On Sept. 21, emergency broadcast systems, as well as earthquake, tsunami and air raid sirens, are to be tested, a spokesperson told the event.
    「be to V」常出現在新聞報導或官方公告中，用來表示官方安排、預定將要發生的計畫或正式命令。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (with + 名詞 + 分詞/形容詞)
    ...with the government yesterday launching the annual campaign.
    「with + O. + 分詞」結構在句中作附帶狀況或原因狀語。在此句中，launching 為現在分詞，表主動，用來補充說明政府啟動年度活動的背景狀況。
  • 現在分詞片語作後置修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The Ministry of the Interior has helped establish 275 Taiwan Community Emergency Response Teams, comprising 5,000 volunteer first responders and 176,000 certified disaster relief volunteers...
    逗號後的「comprising...」是由現在分詞引導的片語，用來補充修飾前面的名詞片語「275 Taiwan Community Emergency Response Teams」，相當於關代子句「which comprise...」，使句子更為精簡流暢。
