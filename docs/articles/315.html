Taipei Times · 2026-09-09

你不需要信任中國的人工智慧

You do not have to trust Chinese AI

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • reliable adj. 可靠的
    Most companies are not turning to these systems because they need a reliable history of Chinese Communist Party (CCP) flashpoints.
    大多數公司轉向這些系統並不是因為它們需要中共政治敏感事件的可靠歷史記錄。
  • expand v. 擴展，擴充
    As Chinese companies expand overseas, their origins — sometimes fairly, sometimes not — tend to taint their trustworthiness scores.
    隨著中國企業向海外擴張，它們的出身背景——有時合理，有時不然——往往會玷污它們的信任分數。
  • alternative n. 替代方案，選擇
    Open models can offer an alternative to blind faith: scrutiny.
    開源模型可以提供盲目信任以外的另一種選擇：審查。
  • infrastructure n. 基礎設施
    Companies can inspect, tinker and even run them inside their own infrastructure.
    企業可以檢查、調整甚至在自己的基礎設施內部運行它們。
  • administer v. 管理，執行（文章中為 public administration 公共行政）
    The Swedish Defence Research Agency earlier this year examined whether DeepSeek could be trusted in public administration.
    瑞典國防研究局今年稍早檢視了 DeepSeek 是否可以在公共行政中被信任。
  • valuation n. 估值，評價
    The coding start-up, set to reach a US$47 billion valuation in its latest funding round, developed one of its marquee models...
    這家新創編碼公司在其最新一輪融資中預計將達到 470 億美元的估值，它開發了其旗艦模型之一...
  • funding round n.phr. 融資輪
    The coding start-up, set to reach a US$47 billion valuation in its latest funding round, developed one of its marquee models...
    這家新創編碼公司在其最新一輪融資中預計將達到 470 億美元的估值，它開發了其旗艦模型之一...
  • imperative n. / adj. 必須的事物，極重要的
    The San Francisco-based company sells autonomous coding tools to major financial institutions and government agencies, making trust a business imperative.
    這家總部位於舊金山的公司向主要金融機構和政府機構銷售自主編碼工具，這使得信任成為一項商業要務。
  • evaluation n. 評估，評價
    So it devised a two-part evaluation covering propaganda and censorship, as well as security and vulnerabilities.
    因此它設計了一個涵蓋宣傳與審查、以及安全與 vulnerabilities（漏洞）的兩部分評估。
  • vulnerability n. 漏洞，弱點
    Researchers at CrowdStrike last year found that DeepSeek’s R1 model produced code with security vulnerabilities when given prompts...
    CrowdStrike 的研究人員去年發現，當被給予提示時，DeepSeek 的 R1 模型會產生帶有安全漏洞的程式碼...
  • transparent adj. 透明的（文章中使用其名詞 transparency 資訊透明度）
    Regulators should focus less on enforcing some supposedly neutral worldview and more on disclosure and transparency.
    監管機構應該減少強制推行某些所謂的中立世界觀，而應更多地關注資訊披露和透明度。
  • respondent n. 受訪者
    About 87 percent of respondents in China said they trusted AI, versus only 32 percent in the US...
    中國約有 87% 的受訪者表示信任 AI，而美國僅有 32%...

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況/結果
    The San Francisco-based company sells autonomous coding tools to major financial institutions and government agencies, making trust a business imperative.
  • Whether 引導的名詞子句（當作主詞或受訪動詞的對象）
    What matters more is whether a model can be independently scrutinized.
  • 假設語氣（條件句與結果）
    You do not have to trust any company if you can independently verify it for yourself.
  • 被動語態的應用
    The conclusion was that DeepSeek models could be used in government settings if they underwent local security analysis and were modified for the intended domain.
