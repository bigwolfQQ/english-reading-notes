文法重點

  • 獨立分詞構句 (Absolute Nominative Construction)
    With candidate registration for this year’s local elections closed on Sept. 4 and the elections less than 80 days away, Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) Legislator and Taipei mayoral candidate Puma Shen’s rapidly growing popularity has become the focus...
    句首使用「With + 名詞 + 過去分詞/形容詞片語」的結構，用來表達附帶狀況或背景原因，相當於副詞子句，常出現在多益閱讀測驗的文法題中。
  • 關係代名詞非限定用法 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    ...a play on the last character of Shen’s name, which literally means “ocean”, and the character, which can mean “current,” “flow” or “trend”...
    句中使用「which」引導非限定關係子句（前方有逗號），對先行詞（character）進行補充說明，能提供更多細節而不中斷主要句意。
  • 現在分詞作後置修飾語 (Present Participle as Postmodifier)
    Shen on Wednesday last week launched a Taipei residents’ participation platform on Line called “Mayor, listen to me,” which allows users to leave messages regarding their municipal concerns and see other users’ messages.
    句中的「regarding」是分詞轉來的介系詞（相當於 about 或 concerning），用來修飾前面的名詞短語 messages，在商業英文和新聞英語中非常常見。
  • 虛主詞 It 的用法
    The interview gained even more attention after pan-blue supporters questioned why Wang did not interview Chiang, and the radio station responded that it had reached out to the mayor several times, but received no reply — reinforcing the impression that he often dodges questions.
    句尾的「reinforcing」是現在分詞作分詞構句，補充說明前面整件事情所帶來的結果或影響，翻譯時可譯為「進而強化了……」。
