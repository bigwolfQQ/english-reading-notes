社論：央行如履薄冰

EDITORIAL: Central bank walking tightrope

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • liquidity n. 流動性、現金流
    Taiwan’s financial market has undergone a significant liquidity squeeze...
    台灣的金融市場經歷了顯著的流動性緊縮...
  • repatriate v. 把（資金、利潤等）匯回國
    ...as overseas investors repatriated stock gains and dividends...
    ...隨著海外投資人將股票收益和股息匯回...
  • differential n. 差異、差距
    ...while others cited the widening interest rate differential between the US and Taiwan...
    ...另一些專家則指出美國和台灣之間日益擴大的利率差...
  • capital expenditures n. phr. 資本支出
    As large technology companies have an urgent need for capital expenditures and financing for AI projects...
    由於大型科技公司對AI專案的資本支出和融資有迫切的需求...
  • financing n. 融資、財務支援
    ...it has become more difficult for individuals and businesses to apply for mortgages, loans and corporate financing.
    ...個人與企業在申請房貸、信貸和企業融資時變得更加困難。
  • prioritize v. 優先處理、把...列為優先
    ...which has in turn caused banks to prioritize funding for tech firms over traditional industries...
    ...這反過來又導致銀行優先將資金提供給科技公司，而非傳統產業...
  • margin financing n. phr. 融資（股市交易用語）
    ...as official data showed margin financing and securities account balances continued to hit new highs...
    ...官方數據顯示，融資餘額與證券劃撥存款餘額持續創下新高...
  • stringent adj. 嚴格的、嚴厲的
    ...and banks have become more stringent in extending credit as they prioritize larger or high-asset clients.
    ...且銀行在放貸時變得更加嚴格，優先考慮大型或高資產客戶。
  • cash flow n. phr. 現金流
    For businesses, cash flow is key to building factories, purchasing equipment and raw materials...
    對企業而言，現金流是建造工廠、採購設備與原物料的關鍵...
  • allocation n. 分配、配置
    Given uneven economic development and uneven capital allocation among local industries...
    鑑於本土產業經濟發展不均以及資本分配不均...

文法重點

  • with + 名詞 + 分詞（獨立分詞結構）
    Taiwan’s financial market has undergone a significant liquidity squeeze, with many banks raising loan rates to new highs...
    「with + O. + V-ing/P.P.」結構用來表示附帶狀況，在此句中用來補充說明台灣金融市場經歷流動性緊縮的同時，伴隨許多銀行調升貸款利率的現象。
  • 關係代名詞子句補充說明
    ...while the broader M2 measure, which includes M1B, time deposits, foreign currency deposits and mutual funds, increased 7.42 percent year-on-year...
    句中的「which includes...」是非限定關係子句，夾在主詞與動詞之間，用來補充解釋 M2 廣義貨幣總計數所包含的項目內容。
  • 現在分詞當作形容詞修飾名詞
    ...making some exporters more willing to hold US dollars than convert to New Taiwan dollars.
    句中的 widening（擴大的）是由動詞 widen 衍生而來的現在分詞，置於名詞 interest rate differential 前面作為形容詞使用，用來形容利率差距正在擴大。
  • 假設語氣（過去式表達與現在事實相反的假設）
    If the central bank keeps rates unchanged longer than expected, it could create market expectations that it is in no hurry to raise rates.
    此句使用「If + 現在式動詞, 主詞 + 조動詞(could/would) + 原形動詞」的條件句型，表達對未來可能發生情況的推測與假設結果。
