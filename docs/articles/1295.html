主要政黨領袖全台走透透，為地方選舉候選人造勢

Major party leaders rally across Taiwan in support of local election candidates

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • campaign verb, noun 競選活動、拉票；從事競選
    Leaders of Taiwan's major political parties fanned out across the country on Sunday to campaign for their mayoral and city council candidates
    台灣主要政黨的領袖於週日分赴全台各地，為他們的市長及市議員候選人拉票。
  • opponent noun 對手、敵手
    rather than attacking his opponent, incumbent Taipei Mayor Chiang Wan-an
    而不是攻擊他的對手、現任台北市長蔣萬安。
  • incumbent adjective, noun 現任的；現任者
    He urged Taipei voters to back Shen, saying the incumbent at-large lawmaker has the ability and vision
    他敦促台北市選民支持沈伯洋，並表示這位現任不分區立委具備能力與願景。
  • strategy / policy (policy 政策) noun 政策、方針
    focused on policy and the future of the capital
    聚焦於政策與首都的未來。
  • attend verb 出席、參加
    Former President Tsai Ing-wen also attended the rally after announcing earlier in the afternoon
    前總統蔡英文在下午稍早宣布消息後，也出席了這場造勢大會。
  • organizer noun 主辦單位、組織者
    according to organizers.
    根據主辦單位表示。
  • promote verb 促進、推動、晉升
    so that the central and local governments could work in concert to promote economic development and peace.
    以便中央與地方政府能夠齊心協力，促進經濟發展與和平。
  • efficient adjective 有效率的
    Huang said Kaohsiung needs a new, forward-looking and efficient model of governance
    黃國昌表示高雄需要一個嶄新、前瞻且高效的治理模式
  • approach noun, verb 方法、途徑、作風
    and argued that Ko could bring such an approach to the port city
    並主張柯志恩能為港都帶來這種作風
  • common adjective 共同的、常見的
    in a way that would serve the common interests of all Kaohsiung residents.
    以符合全體高雄市民的共同利益。
  • resolve verb 解決（問題等）
    leaving many problems unresolved
    留下許多未解的問題

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Postmodifier)
    Leaders of Taiwan's major political parties fanned out across the country on Sunday to campaign for their mayoral and city council candidates ahead of the Nov. 28 local elections, with events held in special municipalities including Taipei, Taichung and Kaohsiung.
    句中的 "including Taipei, Taichung and Kaohsiung" 是現在分詞片語作為形容詞性質，用來補充修飾前面的名詞 "special municipalities（直轄市）"，表達「包含……在內」的意思。
  • 連接詞 rather than 的用法
    Lai said Shen is running what he described as a 'positive campaign' focused on policy and the future of the capital, rather than attacking his opponent...
    "rather than" 意指「而不是……」，用來連接兩個對比的動作或概念（此處為「聚焦政策」與「攻擊對手」），在多益閱讀與商務寫作中常用於表達排除某個選項、選擇另一個選項。
  • so that 表目的的副詞子句
    Cheng said the KMT needed to win both the 2026 local elections and the 2028 presidential election so that the central and local governments could work in concert to promote economic development and peace.
    "so that" 後方接助動詞（could/can 等）與原形動詞，用來引導目的副詞子句，表示前句動作發生的目的或意圖，即「為了讓……能夠……」。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Absolute Nominative Construction)
    He said Kaohsiung has long been dominated by members of the DPP's New Tide faction, leaving many problems unresolved...
    句尾的 ", leaving many problems unresolved" 是一個分詞構句，邏輯主語為前面整句所表達的事情，表「結果」，意思是「因而導致許多問題未被解決」。
