單字解析

  • customer n. 顧客，客戶
    A customer buys pomeloes at a market in Nantou County yesterday.
    昨天南投縣一處市場有顧客正在購買柚子。
  • consumption n. 消費，消耗量
    Local pomelo consumption picks up steam around the Mid-Autumn Festival, but Nantou County’s harvest this year is 30 percent less than the average of the previous years due to heavy rains last month, farmers said.
    農民表示，在中秋節前後，當地柚子的消費量會逐漸加溫，但由於上個月降雨量大，南投縣今年的收成量比往年平均少了百分之三十。
  • pick up steam phr. 加快進度，逐漸加溫，盛行
    Local pomelo consumption picks up steam around the Mid-Autumn Festival, but Nantou County’s harvest this year is 30 percent less than the average of the previous years due to heavy rains last month, farmers said.
    農民表示，在中秋節前後，當地柚子的消費量會逐漸加溫，但由於上個月降雨量大，南投縣今年的收成量比往年平均少了百分之三十。
  • harvest n. 收成，收穫
    Local pomelo consumption picks up steam around the Mid-Autumn Festival, but Nantou County’s harvest this year is 30 percent less than the average of the previous years due to heavy rains last month, farmers said.

  • average n. / adj. 平均，平均數

  • due to prep. 由於，因為

文法重點

  • 對等連接詞 but 連接子句
    Local pomelo consumption picks up steam around the Mid-Autumn Festival, but Nantou County’s harvest this year is 30 percent less than the average of the previous years due to heavy rains last month, farmers said.
    使用對等連接詞 but 連接兩個獨立子句（主詞加動詞的完整句子），表達前後句意之間的轉折對比關係。
  • 介係詞片語表達原因 (due to)
    Nantou County’s harvest this year is 30 percent less than the average of the previous years due to heavy rains last month
    due to 後面接名詞或名詞片語（在此為 heavy rains），用來指明某事發生的原因，相當於 because of，在多益測驗中常考其與 because（接子句）的用法區別。
