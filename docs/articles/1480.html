中台灣筆記：北京在台灣及其他地區的威嚇戰術

Notes from Central Taiwan: Beijing’s intimidation tactics in Taiwan and beyond

單字解析

  • investigation n. 調查
    PRC media in October last year reported that the Chongqing Municipal Public Security Bureau had opened an investigation into Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) Legislator Puma Shen...
    中國媒體去年10月報導，重慶市公安局已對民主進步黨立法委員沈伯洋展開調查……
  • jurisdiction n. 司法管轄權，管轄範圍
    This was a cognitive warfare ploy, designed to unsettle the targeted person and to establish the idea that it has legal jurisdiction over Taiwan and its people.
    這是一場認知戰計謀，旨在讓目標人物感到不安，並確立中國對台灣及其人民擁有法律管轄權的概念。
  • surveillance n. 監視，跟監
    Cheng was said to have accepted NT$360,000 (US$11,299) from two Hong Kong residents to surveil Shen.
    據稱鄭永津接受了兩名香港居民36萬新台幣的資金來跟監沈伯洋。
  • purpose n. 目的，意圖
    The purpose of this steady drip-drip of PRC threats... against Shen is intended to instill fear into the candidate.
    中國對沈伯洋這種源源不絕的威脅的目的，是為了在參選人心中植入恐懼。
  • pressure n. / v. 壓力，施壓
    It is also intended to put pressure on others who might be tempted to adopt his political stances.
    這也是為了對其他可能試圖採取其政治立場的人施加壓力。
  • sentence n. / v. 宣判，徒刑
    ...the Taichung District Prosecutors Office was seeking a five-year sentence for the perpetrator, Liao Gangfa...
    ……台中地方檢察署正向法院對加害人廖港發求處五年有期徒刑……
  • perpetrator n. 加害人，作惡者
    ...the Taichung District Prosecutors Office was seeking a five-year sentence for the perpetrator, Liao Gangfa...
    ……台中地方檢察署正向法院對加害人廖港發求處五年有期徒刑……
  • indict v. 起訴，控告
    The Taichung District Prosecutors’ Office indicted Liao on Tuesday on charges of assault, public insult and participation in a criminal organization.
    台中地檢署於週二依傷害、公然侮辱及參與犯罪組織等罪嫌起訴廖嫌。
  • leverage n. / v. 籌碼，施力點，利用
    Lai is well aware of the leverage his son represents.
    賴清德很清楚他的兒子所代表的籌碼。
  • revenue / profit n. 利潤，收益（文章中使用 reaping profits）
    The post... appeared to accused him of “reaping profits in mainland China while flirting with Taiwan independence”...
    這篇貼文似乎指責他「在中國大陸撈好處，同時勾搭台獨」……
  • proposal n. 提案，建議
    The piece mapped out various policy proposals by PRC “scholars.”
    這篇文章規劃了中國「學者」提出的各種政策提案。
  • status n. 地位，狀態
    The SCMP article followed that latter approach, saying that Taiwan’s future status under PRC control will depend on how much resistance there is to annexation.
    《南華早報》的文章採取了後一種方法，稱台灣在中國控制下的未來地位將取決於對兼併有多少抵抗。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句 / 過去分詞作後置修飾 (Past Participle as Post-modifier)
    This was a cognitive warfare ploy, designed to unsettle the targeted person and to establish the idea that it has legal jurisdiction over Taiwan and its people.
    句中的 "designed to unsettle..." 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 ploy（計謀），省略了 "which is/was"。這種結構在多益閱讀測驗中非常常見，用以簡化形容詞子句並描述事物的用途或設計目的。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The Taichung District Prosecutors’ Office indicted Liao on Tuesday on charges of assault, public insult and participation in a criminal organization.
    雖然此句為主動語態（indicted），但文章中大量使用被動語態（例如：was charged with, is intended to, will be governed），用以強調承受動作的對象（如受害者、政策或遭起訴者），符合新聞報導客觀敘述事件的語氣。
  • 同位語 (Appositive)
    Japanese activist in Taiwan Yaita Akio, who was attacked in Taichung in July, said on Twitter that the Taichung District Prosecutors Office was seeking a five-year sentence for the perpetrator, Liao Gangfa...
    句中的 "Liao Gangfa" 是名詞 "the perpetrator" 的同位語，用來補充說明加害人的真實姓名。同位語前後常以逗號隔開，能有效在單一子句中塞入更多識別資訊。
  • 名詞子句作受詞 (Noun Clause as Object)
    Yaita said that violence on the PRC’s behalf is occurring regularly in democratic countries.
    句中的 "that violence on the PRC’s behalf is occurring regularly in democratic countries" 是由連接詞 that 引導的名詞子句，在句中擔任動詞 said 的受詞，用來表達某人所說的內容或觀點。
  • 條件句 / 假設語氣應用 (Conditional Structure)
    The SCMP article followed that latter approach, saying that Taiwan’s future status under PRC control will depend on how much resistance there is to annexation.
