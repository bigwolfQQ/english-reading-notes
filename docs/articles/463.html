文法重點

  • 現在分詞作附加結果（分詞構句）
    Hu Chen-jui (胡辰睿) then sparked a five-run fourth with an inside-the-park home run, giving Taiwan a 15-run advantage and ending the game early under the tournament's mercy rule.
    句尾的「giving... and ending...」是由對等連接詞連接的現在分詞片語，用來補充說明主句動作所產生的連續結果，相當於「and it gave... and ended...」。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句
    Taiwan, which won the previous edition in 2024, is seeking to claim consecutive titles for the first time and its fourth championship overall.
  • 對比連接詞 while 的用法
    Hu went 2-for-2 with three runs and four RBIs, while his team finished with 14 hits and committed no errors.
