新北市議員涉嫌以洗衣精賄選案獲交保

New Taipei councilor bailed in suspected laundry detergent vote-buying case

單字解析

  • suspected adj. 涉嫌的、疑為...的
    New Taipei City Councilor Yang Chun-mei was released on NT$500,000 (US $15,700) bail and barred from leaving Taiwan after prosecutors said she is suspected of distributing laundry detergent to voters as part of a vote-buying scheme, the New Taipei District Prosecutors’ Office said yesterday.
    新北市地檢署昨天表示，新北市議員楊春妹以 50 萬新台幣（約 15,700 美元）交保並被限制出境，因為檢察官表示她涉嫌發放洗衣精給選民作為賄選計畫的一部分。
  • distribute v. 分發、散布、分配
    Prosecutors said they are investigating allegations that Yang recently distributed laundry detergent and other household goods to voters at several community activity centers, in what is the first suspected vote-buying case involving a New Taipei City councilor in this year’s local election.
    檢察官表示，他們正在調查相關指控，指楊女日前在多個社區活動中心向選民發放洗衣精及其他日用品，這是今年地方選舉中首起涉及新北市議員的疑似賄選案。
  • investigate v. 調查、偵查
    Prosecutors said they are investigating allegations that Yang recently distributed laundry detergent and other household goods to voters at several community activity centers...
    檢察官表示，他們正在調查相關指控，指楊女日前在多個社區活動中心向選民發放洗衣精及其他日用品...
  • allegation n. 指控、指稱
    Prosecutors said they are investigating allegations that Yang recently distributed laundry detergent and other household goods to voters...
    檢察官表示，他們正在調查相關指控，指楊女日前向選民發放洗衣精及其他日用品...
  • quantity n. 數量、分量
    According to prosecutors, they searched Yang’s home, office and other locations on Tuesday, finding large quantities of laundry detergent and other household goods allegedly intended for distribution to voters.
    檢察官指出，他們於週二搜索了楊女的住家、辦公室及其他地點，查獲大量疑似預謀發放給選民的洗衣精和其他日用品。
  • intend v. 打算、預定、意欲
    ...finding large quantities of laundry detergent and other household goods allegedly intended for distribution to voters.
    ...查獲大量疑似預謀（意圖）發放給選民的洗衣精和其他日用品。
  • assistant n. 助理、助手
    After questioning 21 people, including Yang, two assistants and witnesses, prosecutors said Yang and the two assistants are suspected of offering or giving bribes to people with voting rights in exchange for votes.
    在訊問了包括楊女、兩名助理及證人在內的 21 人後，檢察官表示楊女與兩名助理涉嫌向具投票權之人期約或交付賄賂，以換取選票。
  • exchange n. / v. 交換、交流
    ...offering or giving bribes to people with voting rights in exchange for votes.
    ...向具投票權之人期約或交付賄賂，以換取選票。
  • register v. 登記、註冊
    Yang, a four-term Kuomintang councilor representing New Taipei’s electoral constituency No. 12 for Plains Indigenous Tribes, recently registered to seek a fifth term in this year’s local elections.
    代表新北市第 12 選區（平地原住民）的四屆國民黨議員楊女，最近才剛登記爭取今年地方選舉的第五任連任。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The key issue stipulated under the Public Officials Election And Recall Act is whether the gift is provided as an improper benefit...
    句中的「stipulated under the Public Officials Election And Recall Act」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「The key issue（關鍵問題）」，相當於完整關係代間詞子句「which is stipulated...」的簡化形式，在多益閱讀測驗中非常常出現以節省篇幅。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    New Taipei City Councilor Yang Chun-mei was released on NT$500,000 (US $15,700) bail and barred from leaving Taiwan...
    新聞英語（特別是司法與犯罪報導）極常使用被動語態來客觀陳述事實，強調動作的承受者而非施事者。在此句中，「was released（被釋放）」與「was barred（被禁止）」皆為被動語態結構（be + 過去分詞）。
  • 現在分詞表附帶狀況或結果 (Present Participle Phrase)
    ...finding large quantities of laundry detergent and other household goods allegedly intended for distribution to voters.
    句尾的「finding...」是由現在分詞引導的分詞構句，用來補充說明前面主句搜尋行動所產生的結果或當下發生的狀況，相當於「and they found...」的簡化寫法。
