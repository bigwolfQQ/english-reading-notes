賴清德總統國慶演說全文

Full text of President Lai Ching-te's National Day address

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • pledge v. 保證、承諾
    President Lai Ching-te (賴清德), in his National Day address on Saturday, pledged to improve people's well-being, safeguard regional peace and stability, and sustain Taiwan's economic competitiveness.
    賴清德總統於週六的國慶演說中，承諾將提升人民福祉、維護區域和平穩定，並維持台灣的經濟競爭力。
  • sustain v. 維持、持續、支撐
    President Lai Ching-te (賴清德), in his National Day address on Saturday, pledged to improve people's well-being, safeguard regional peace and stability, and sustain Taiwan's economic competitiveness.
    賴清德總統於週六的國慶演說中，承諾將提升人民福祉、維護區域和平穩定，並維持台灣的經濟競爭力。
  • transition n. 轉變、過渡、政權交接
    Over those 30 years, we transformed into a democracy, we saw the peaceful transition of power between political parties, and we endured many challenges.
    過去 30 年來，我們轉型成為民主國家，見證了政黨的和平政黨輪替，並歷經了許多挑戰。
  • accomplishment n. 成就、成績、功績
    Just look at the recent accomplishments of our athletes at the Asian Games in Aichi-Nagoya.
    看看我們運動員近期在愛知-名古屋亞洲運動會上的成就。
  • projected adj. 預計的、預估的
    Our per capita GDP is projected to reach US$45,332 this year, almost double that of 2016.
    我們今年的每人平均 GDP 預計將達到 45,332 美元，幾乎是 2016 年的兩倍。
  • bilateral adj. 雙邊的
    Together, we witnessed our countries' bilateral cooperation in the fields of energy, agriculture, healthcare, women's empowerment, and education and culture.
    我們共同見證了兩國在能源、農業、醫療衛生、女性賦權以及教育文化等領域的雙邊合作。
  • infrastructure n. 基礎設施
    Taiwan placed highly in its four major factors of economic performance, government efficiency, business efficiency, and infrastructure, and it climbed up to fourth globally, our best performance to date.
    台灣在經濟表現、政府效能、企業效能和基礎設施四大主要因素中名列前茅，並攀升至全球第四，創下我們迄今的最佳表現。
  • subsidize n. (文中為 subsidies) 補助金、津貼
    This includes increased subsidies, lighter loads, greater flexibility, and expanded care from the stages of childbirth and childrearing to education, making support for families even more comprehensive.
    這包括提高補助、減輕負擔、增加彈性，並將支持從生育、養育階段擴大到教育，讓對家庭的支持更加全面。
  • implement v. (文中為 implementing) 實施、執行
    To ease housing pressure, we will continue implementing our program to support 1 million renter households...
    為緩解居住壓力，我們將持續落實支援 100 萬戶租屋家庭的計畫...
  • amend v. (文中為 amended) 修正、修改（法規等）
    We have also amended the Act for the Recruitment and Employment of Foreign Professionals, expanding opportunities for international talent and foreign students to come to and stay in Taiwan.
    我們也修正了《外國專業人才延攬及僱用法》，擴大國際人才與外國學生來台及留台的機會。
  • revoked adj. / v. 被撤銷、被吊銷的
    There will be no cap on fines for repeat offenders; their driver's licenses will be revoked and their vehicles forfeited.
    累犯之罰款將無上限，其駕車執照將被吊銷，車輛將被沒收。
  • competitiveness n. 競爭力
    This will ensure that Taiwan maintains its competitive edge through the next generation.
    這將確保台灣在下一世代繼續保持競爭優勢。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況/結果
    Taiwan dominated the top two spots in women's tennis doubles, with Hsieh Su-wei (謝淑薇) and Liang En-shuo (梁恩碩) taking gold and Chan Hao-ching (詹皓晴) and Wu Fang-hsien (吳芳嫺) taking silver.
    「with + 名詞 + 分詞」結構在句中用來補充說明主句發生的同時所伴隨的情況或結果，在多益閱讀與商業英文寫法中非常常見，能讓句子資訊更豐富。
  • 現在分詞（片語）作後置定語
    In this year's World Competitiveness Ranking compiled by the Swiss-based Institute for Management Development...
    句中的「compiled by...」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「Ranking」，相當於一個省略了「which was」的形容詞子句（...Ranking which was compiled by...），使語氣更精鍊。
  • It is... that... 強調句型
    These achievements, without a doubt, are owed to the 23 million people of Taiwan. They are the result of every ounce of strength given by the government and countless citizens, across all fields, working hand in hand.
    （註：文章第 5 段也有典型的強調句 It is the people who...）雖然此處為代名詞指代，但文章中頻繁運用「It is + 被強調部分 + that/who」的結構來加強語氣（例如強調責任或成果歸屬），是英文演說中增強說服力的典型技巧。
  • 否定副詞置於句首的倒裝句（部分倒裝）
    Neither did Lin Yu-ting (林郁婷) disappoint, taking home gold for the second consecutive Asian Games.
    當否定副詞「Neither」（也不）置於句首時，其後的子句必須使用主謂倒裝（助動詞 did 移至主語 Lin Yu-ting 之前），藉此加強語氣，突顯前述否定狀況的延伸。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞
    To support a family is to safeguard Taiwan's future.
    句中的「To support a family」或動名词作主詞的句型，在此處用來定義一個抽象概念。不定詞片語置於句首作為主詞，使語氣顯得正式且具有宣示性。
