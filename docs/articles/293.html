台北將加強食品安全稽查

Taipei to launch more food-safety inspections

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • conduct v. 執行，舉辦，進行
    The Taipei City Government is to conduct more unannounced inspections to ensure food safety at schools
    台北市政府將進行更多無預警稽查，以確保學校的食品安全。
  • ensure v. 確保，保證
    The Taipei City Government is to conduct more unannounced inspections to ensure food safety at schools
    台北市政府將進行更多無預警稽查，以確保學校的食品安全。
  • suspend v. 暫停，中止
    The school was ordered to suspend lunch services on Tuesday.
    該校已被勒令於週二暫停午餐供應。
  • receive v. 收到，接到
    Lin said she has received complaints from parents
    林表示她已接到家長的投訴
  • clarify v. 釐清，澄清
    Lin said she has asked the Taipei Department of Health to clarify the situation.
    林表示，她已要求台北市衛生局釐清狀況。
  • investigate v. 調查
    The department sent inspectors to the school to investigate and collect samples for examination
    該局已派稽查員前往該校調查並採集檢體以供檢驗
  • implement v. 實施，落實，執行
    the department ordered the school to implement improvements before tomorrow
    衛生局已命該校於明天前落實改善
  • fine n. 罰款，罰金
    or face fines of up to NT$200 million
    否則將面臨高達新台幣 2 億元的罰款
  • follow up phr. v. 後續追蹤，進一步跟進
    the city government would set up a special project to follow up on the case and the children’s health
    市政府將成立專案，後續追蹤此案及孩童的健康狀況
  • source n. 來源，出處
    The ingredients are being traced to the source
    食材正在追溯源頭
  • increase v. 增加，提高
    adding that the city government would increase unannounced campus food safety inspections.
    並補充表示市政府將增加校園食品安全的無預警稽查。
  • preliminary adj. 初步的，預備的
    and the preliminary inspection showed that the meat in Monday’s lunch met Certified Agricultural Standards requirements
    且初步稽查顯示，週一午餐中的肉品符合驗證要求
  • avoid v. 避免
    avoid raw or undercooked food
    避免食用生食或未煮熟的食物
  • promptly adv. 迅速地，立即，準時地
    and eat meals promptly to prevent food poisoning
    並儘快食用完畢以預防食物中毒

文法重點

  • be to do 句型（表達預定、計畫或即將發生的官方行程）
    The Taipei City Government is to conduct more unannounced inspections to ensure food safety at schools...
    「be + to V」常出現在新聞報導中，用來表示官方或正式的計畫、預定即將發生的動作（在此指台北市政府預計要執行更多稽查）。
  • 現在分詞當形容詞（修飾名詞）
    ...following an alleged food poisoning incident at a high school on Monday, in which 87 students became ill.
    句中的 unannounced（未預先宣布的、突擊的）是由動詞 announce 加上否定字首 un- 與分詞字尾 -ed 構成的形容詞，用來修飾 inspections，在多益商業英文中常用於形容突擊檢查或無預警政策。
  • 分詞片語當作補充說明（後置修飾）
    The health department yesterday evening said that education department officials also went to the school to investigate the case, and the preliminary inspection showed that the meat in Monday’s lunch met Certified Agricultural Standards requirements for traceability.
    句中的 traceability 藉由前方的 requirements for 形成名詞片語，用來修飾 Certified Agricultural Standards 的要求，呈現商業與行政法規中常見的「產品追溯性/履歷」概念。
  • 省略連接詞 that 的名詞子句
    Taipei Mayor Chiang Wan-an (蔣萬安) said yesterday, following an alleged food poisoning incident...
    動詞 said 後面接了一個名詞子句作為受詞，用以引述蔣萬安所說的話。在英文新聞報導中，受詞子句的連接詞 that 常被省略，使句子更為緊湊。
