高雄發生致命毒品債務糾紛，3嫌落網、1人在逃

3 suspects arrested, 1 at large after fatal drug debt row in Kaohsiung

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • dispute n. 爭端、糾紛
    Three suspects have been arrested and one remains at large after a dispute over drug debt in Kaohsiung left one man dead and two others seriously injured, local authorities said.
    當地 autorità 表示，高雄一起因毒品債務糾紛導致1死2傷的事件後，3名嫌疑人已被捕，1人在逃。
  • authorities n. 當局、官方
    Three suspects have been arrested and one remains at large after a dispute over drug debt in Kaohsiung left one man dead and two others seriously injured, local authorities said.
    當地當局表示，高雄一起因毒品債務糾紛導致1死2傷的事件後，3名嫌疑人已被捕，1人在逃。
  • occur v. 發生、出現
    The Kaohsiung City Police Department said the incident occurred in the early hours of Monday at an out-of-business health spa on Baotai Road in Fengshan District.
    高雄市警察局表示，這起事件發生在週一凌晨，地點位於鳳山區保泰路上一家停業的健康三溫暖。
  • investigation n. 調查
    As part of their investigation, police used surveillance video footage to track down four suspects in the attack, who were observed leaving the scene together in a car.
    作為調查的一部分，警方利用監視器畫面追查到四名攻擊嫌疑人，他們被目擊一起搭車離開現場。
  • negotiate v. 談判、協商
    The two groups met to negotiate around 2 a.m. at the spa, which had recently gone out of business and had rental studio apartments above it, the report said, though it did not specify why that location was chosen.
    報導指出，兩群人於凌晨2點左右在該三溫暖會面進行談判，該三溫暖最近剛歇業且上方有出租套房，不過報導並未指明為何選擇該地點。
  • accomplice n. 同夥、共犯
    According to the report, the Tung brothers and their accomplices came armed with knives and clubs and blocked the door to the spa while Lee and his friends were inside.
    根據報導，童姓兄弟及其同夥帶著刀械和棍棒前來，趁李男及其友人身處三溫暖內時堵住大門。
  • specify v. 具體說明、詳述
    The two groups met to negotiate around 2 a.m. at the spa, which had recently gone out of business and had rental studio apartments above it, the report said, though it did not specify why that location was chosen.
    報導指出，兩群人於凌晨2點左右在該三溫暖會面進行談判，該三溫暖最近剛歇業且上方有出租套房，不過報導並未指明為何選擇該地點。
  • on credit phr. 賒帳、記帳
    The Liberty Times reported, however, that the attack was not due to a "debt" but came after the attackers were told by Lee, Lin and Chuang that they could not buy about NT$10,000 worth of a zombie drug on credit.
    然而，《自由時報》報導稱，這起攻擊並非起因於「債務」，而是攻擊者被李男、林與莊告知無法賒帳購買價值約1萬台幣的殭屍毒品之後發生的。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Three of the suspects were later arrested, while one remained at large as of Monday afternoon.
    句中的 "remaining at large"（在此簡化為 "while one remained at large" 搭配對等連接詞）用來補充說明主詞在特定時間點的狀態。多益常考分詞主動與被動的用法區分。
  • 非限定關代子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    The two groups met to negotiate around 2 a.m. at the spa, which had recently gone out of business and had rental studio apartments above it, the report said...
    關係代名詞 "which" 前方有逗號，用來補充說明先行詞 "the spa（三溫暖）" 的額外資訊（已經歇業且上方有出租套房），拿掉該子句不影響主要句意。多益閱讀測驗常在空格處測試關代（如 which vs. where vs. what）。
  • 附帶狀況的分詞構句 (Participial Phrase indicating Circumstances)
    According to the report, the Tung brothers and their accomplices came armed with knives and clubs and blocked the door to the spa while Lee and his friends were inside.
    句中的 "armed with..." 是過去分詞片語作修飾，省略了 "(who were) armed with..."，用來形容主體（童姓兄弟及其同夥）來的時候「攜帶了武器」的狀態。
