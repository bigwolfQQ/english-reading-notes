校園監視器並非解決之道

Surveillance cameras in schools not the answer

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • mandate v. 授權，強制執行
    The Keelung City Government last month floated the idea of mandating the installation of surveillance cameras in every classroom.
    基隆市政府上個月提出了強制在每間教室安裝監視器的構想。
  • proposal n. 提案，建議
    The proposal has drawn collective protest from the National Federation of Teachers’ Union and five major early-childhood education organizations nationwide.
    這項提議引發了全國教師工會總聯合會以及全國五大幼兒教育團體的共同抗議。
  • deterrent n. 嚇阻物，威懾力量
    While the idea might appear to offer an easy deterrent, at its core it is a classic case of cutting corners and lazy governance.
    雖然這個構想表面上看起來提供了一個簡單的嚇阻方法，但它的核心卻是典型偷工減料且怠政的案例。
  • eliminate v. 排除，消除
    Believing that a few cameras can eliminate incidents of abuse not only misses the root of the problem, but risks undermining trust between teachers and parents on the front lines...
    認為幾個監視器就能消滅虐童事件，不僅錯失了問題根源，更冒著破壞第一線教師與家長信任的風險……
  • personnel n. 人員，員工
    Of course, there should be zero tolerance for any inappropriate or abusive treatment in schools, and unsuitable personnel should be removed from their positions in accordance with the law.
    當然，對於學校中任何不當或虐待的对待應採取零容忍態度，且不適任的人員應依法予以革職。
  • workload n. 工作量
    They cannot prevent a crisis in the first place, nor can they ease the chronic pressures and heavy workloads that teachers face.
    它們無法在一開始就預防危機，也無法緩解教師所面臨的長期壓力與沉重工作負擔。
  • administrative adj. 行政的
    Non-public preschool educators earn an average monthly salary of only NT$32,000 to NT$45,000, yet each day they are expected to teach, care for children with special needs and handle a host of administrative duties.
    非營利幼兒園教育人員的平均月薪僅新台幣 3萬2,000元 至 4萬5,000元，然而他們每天卻被期望要教學、照顧有特殊需求的兒童，並處理繁多的行政職責。
  • penalty n. 處罰，罰款
    At the same time, they face the risk of penalties that can easily reach several times their monthly salary.
    同時，他們還面臨著輕易就能達到其月薪數倍的罰款風險。
  • shortage n. 短缺，不足
    This combination of low pay, high pressure and heavy responsibility has left the early-childhood education sector with a severe staff shortage.
    低薪、高壓與重責大任的結合，讓幼兒教育界出現了嚴重的缺工潮。
  • coordinate v. 協調，統籌
    The Ministry of Education should take a more active role in coordinating policy and providing resources, using systemic reform to establish sound, consistent standards nationwide.
    教育部應在協調政策和提供資源方面扮演更積極的角色，透過系統性改革在全國建立健全、一致的標準。
  • transparent adj. 透明的
    ...and there must be transparent, credible communication guidelines and channels established through which parents may raise concerns and teachers may seek outside assistance when under extreme pressure.
    ……且必須建立透明、可信的溝通準則與管道，讓家長得以提出疑慮，而老師在承受極大壓力時能尋求外部協助。
  • direct v. 將（資源、精力等）導向、指向
    If we truly want to protect children, resources should be directed toward systemic reforms that improve working conditions.
    如果我們真心想保護兒童，資源應導向改善工作條件的系統性改革。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    To quell public anxiety sparked by allegations of child abuse in preschools...
    過去分詞片語「sparked by...」在此作後位修飾語，用來修飾前面的名詞「public anxiety」，原意為「which was sparked by...（由……所引發的）」，省略關代與be動詞後形成分詞片語，能讓句子更為精簡。
  • 否定副詞置於句首的倒裝句 (Inversion with Negative Adverb)
    They cannot prevent a crisis in the first place, nor can they ease the chronic pressures and heavy workloads that teachers face.
    當否定連接詞「nor」置於句首連接兩個句子時，後方的子句必須使用主謂倒裝的結構（助動詞 can 移至主詞 they 之前），用以加強否定語氣。
  • 與現在事實相反的假設語氣 (Subjunctive Mood for Present Unreal Condition)
    If the Keelung City Government were to add round-the-clock surveillance, it would send the wrong message to frontline early-childhood educators...
    此句使用「If + 主詞 + were to + 原形動詞..., 主詞 + would + 原形動詞」的句型，用來表達對未來或現在極度不可能發生、或純屬假設的情況之推測與結果。
  • 介系詞加關係代名詞的複合結構 (Preposition + Relative Pronoun)
    ...and there must be transparent, credible communication guidelines and channels established through which parents may raise concerns and teachers may seek outside assistance when under extreme pressure.
    句中的「through which」引導一個形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的「guidelines and channels」。介系詞「through」原本放在句尾（...channels established which parents may raise concerns through），移至關係代名詞「which」前方能使正式書寫更為流暢嚴謹。
