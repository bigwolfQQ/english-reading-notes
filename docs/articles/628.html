生育率下降：現金買不到歸屬感

Falling birthrates: cash cannot buy belonging

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • fertility rate n. phr. 生育率
    In 2000, its fertility rate matched the US’ at 1.6 children per woman.
    在 2000 年時，其生育率與美國相當，每位女性生育 1.6 個孩子。
  • support n. 支援，補助，資助
    Each child, including those born to unmarried parents, would get US$55,000 in government support by the time they turn 17.
    每個孩子（包括未婚父母所生的孩子）在滿 17 歲之前，將獲得 55,000 美元的政府補助。
  • intend v. 打算，計畫
    Yet a quarter of Singaporeans say they do not intend to have children.
    然而，四分之一的新加坡人表示他們不打算生孩子。
  • guarantee v. 保證，擔保
    Wealth and security, clearly, do not guarantee more babies.
    顯然，財富和安全並不能保證會有更多嬰兒。
  • exceed v. 超過，勝過
    In Nigeria, for example, the fertility rate of 4.4 exceeds its replacement rate of 2.6 children per woman.
    例如在奈及利亞，其生育率為 4.4，超過了每位女性 2.6 個孩子的替代率。
  • barrier n. 障礙，阻礙
    More than half said the biggest barriers to a durable relationship were economic constraints.
    半數以上的人表示，維持穩定關係的最大障礙是經濟壓力。
  • economic adj. 經濟的
    More than half said the biggest barriers to a durable relationship were economic constraints.
    半數以上的人表示，維持穩定關係的最大障礙是經濟壓力。
  • stability n. 穩定，穩定性
    Young people want partnership and parenthood, but questioned achieving the “emotional and financial” stability needed for a shared life.
    年輕人渴望伴侶關係和為人父母，但對於能否實現共同生活所需的「情感與財務」穩定性抱持懷疑態度。
  • subsidized adj. 有補助的，津貼的
    The Nordic region — consisting of five states and three territories — is viewed as a good place to have children thanks to generous parental leave and subsidized childcare.
    北歐地區——由五個國家和三個屬地組成——由於擁有豐厚的育兒假和補助托兒服務，被視為生育的好地方。
  • shrinking adj. 萎縮中的，縮小的
    While the state transfers cash worth 10 percent of the median wage for every child born each month, Poland’s population is shrinking.
    雖然政府每個月為每一個出生的孩子發放相當於中位薪資 10% 的現金補貼，但波蘭的人口仍在萎縮。

文法重點

  • 包含分詞片語當形容詞後置修飾 (Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    Each child, including those born to unmarried parents, would get US$55,000 in government support by the time they turn 17.
    句中的 "born to unmarried parents" 是過去分詞片語，用來後置修飾代名詞 those（指代 children）。原本的完整結構是「those who are born to unmarried parents」，省略了「who are」以達到簡潔的效果。
  • 過去分詞片語表示附帶狀況與被動 (Past Participle Phrase)
    The results, released in July, were striking: Two-thirds said they want to marry or live with a partner.
    句中的 ", released in July, " 是由過去分詞引導的片語，夾在主詞 The results 與動詞 were 之間，用來補充說明調查結果是被發布的（被動語態），相當於一個被動的關係子句「which were released in July」。
  • 現在分詞片語作後置修飾語 (Present Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    What is emerging is a demographic divide between ageing, shrinking rich societies and population growth in poorer ones.
    句中的 "ageing" 和 "shrinking" 是現在分詞作形容詞用，直接放在名詞 societies 前面修飾，表達「正在老化中」與「正在萎縮中」的動態過程。
