賴清德：台灣需要各產業形成「護國群山」

Taiwan needs a 'chain of guardian mountains' across industries: Lai

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • broaden v. 拓寬，擴大
    President Lai Ching-te said on Saturday that Taiwan needed many industries across its economy to become a "chain of guardian mountains" to broaden the foundation of its economic strength.
    總統賴清德週六表示，台灣需要經濟中的許多產業成為「護國群山」，以拓寬其經濟實力的基礎。
  • overseas adv. / adj. 海外的，在海外
    In his National Day address outside the Presidential Office, Lai said leading companies expanding overseas should help their supply chains establish deeper roots in Taiwan.
    賴清德在總統府前發表的國慶演說中表示，在海外擴張的領頭企業應協助其供應鏈在台灣扎根更深。
  • rely on phr. v. 依賴，依靠
    "We cannot rely on just one guardian mountain; we must ensure that all sectors join together to form a chain of guardian mountains," Lai said.
    「我們不能只依賴一座護國群山；我們必須確保所有產業結合在一起，形成護國群山，」賴清德說。
  • exceed v. 超過，勝過
    Full-year growth could exceed 11 percent, which would be the highest rate in 39 years, while per capita gross domestic product was projected to reach US$45,332, almost double its 2016 level.
    全年增幅可能超過11%，這將是39年來最高速率，而人均國內生產毛額預計將達到45,332美元，幾乎是2016年水準的兩倍。
  • project v. 預計，預估（常用被動態）
    Full-year growth could exceed 11 percent, which would be the highest rate in 39 years, while per capita gross domestic product was projected to reach US$45,332, almost double its 2016 level.
    全年增幅可能超過11%，這將是39年來最高速率，而人均國內生產毛額預計將達到45,332美元，幾乎是2016年水準的兩倍。
  • benchmark n. 基準，基準點
    The benchmark Taiex had reached 49,000 points, Lai said, with Taiwan's stock market becoming the world's fourth largest by market capitalization.
    賴清德表示，基準台股加權指數已達到49,000點，台灣股市以市值計算成為全球第四大。
  • competitiveness n. 競爭力
    To sustain Taiwan's competitiveness, Lai said the Executive Yuan was accelerating the development of strategic industries and emerging technologies.
    為了維持台灣的競爭力，賴清德表示，行政院正在加速發展戰略產業和新興技術。
  • priority n. 優先事項，首要任務
    Other priorities include critical minerals, biotechnology and healthcare, an Asian asset management hub, public infrastructure and urban renewal, cultural and creative industries, and developing tourism into a trillion-NT-dollar industry.
    其他優先事項包括關鍵礦物、生物科技與醫療保健、亞洲資產管理中心、公共基礎設施與都市更新、文化與創意產業，以及將觀光業發展成為兆元新台幣的產業。
  • financing n. 融資，財務支援
    A separate program for micro-, small- and medium-sized enterprises, with a scale of NT$100 billion (US$3.13 billion), would support financing, workforce training, digital transformation, net-zero upgrades and access to international distribution channels, Lai said.
    賴清德表示，一項規模達1000億新台幣（31.3億美元）的針對微型、小型及中型企業的專門計畫，將支持融資、勞動力培訓、數位轉型、淨零升級以及進入國際分銷管道。
  • shortage n. 短缺，不足
    To address labor and talent shortages, Lai said the government would maintain Taiwanese workers as the primary workforce, supplemented by workers recruited from overseas.
    為了解決勞動力和人才短缺問題，賴清德表示，政府將維持台灣勞工為主要勞動力，並輔以自海外招募的勞工。
  • supplement v. 補充，增補
    To address labor and talent shortages, Lai said the government would maintain Taiwanese workers as the primary workforce, supplemented by workers recruited from overseas.
    為了解決勞動力和人才短缺問題，賴清德表示，政府將維持台灣勞工為主要勞動力，並輔以自海外招募的勞工。
  • goal n. 目標
    The goal, he said, was to ensure that prosperity was reflected not only in production and exports, but also in better pay and lighter burdens for families.
    他說，目標是確保繁榮不仅反映在生產和出口上，也反映在家庭更好的薪資和較輕的負擔上。

文法重點

  • 非限定關係子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Full-year growth could exceed 11 percent, which would be the highest rate in 39 years, while per capita gross domestic product was projected to reach US$45,332...
    句中的「which would be the highest rate in 39 years」前面帶有逗號，用來補充說明前面整句提到的全年經濟增長率預測，提供額外的資訊而不影響主要子句的完整性。
  • 分詞片語作後置修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    To address labor and talent shortages, Lai said the government would maintain Taiwanese workers as the primary workforce, supplemented by workers recruited from overseas.
    句尾的「supplemented by workers recruited from overseas」是由過去分詞片語引導的結構，修飾前面提到的勞動力，省略了「which are」，意思是「（這些勞動力）受到從海外招募的工人的補充」。
  • 否定副詞置於句首的倒裝意涵 (Negative / Restrictive Condition)
    "We will not rule out options solely for ideological reasons, nor will we compromise the sustainability of future generations for short-term gains," he said.
    句中的「nor will we...」使用了部分倒裝結構（nor + 助動詞 will + 主詞 we + 動詞原形 compromise），用來連接前一句的否定語氣，表示「我們也不會……」。
  • 複合形容詞的構成 (Compound Adjectives)
    A separate program for micro-, small- and medium-sized enterprises, with a scale of NT$100 billion...
    句中的「medium-sized」（中型的）是由名詞加過去分詞組成的複合形容詞，用來修飾企業（enterprises），在多益測驗中常考此類由連字號連接的複合詞形。
