台灣企業足球甲級聯賽定於週日開賽

Taiwan Premier League set to kick off on Sunday

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • defending adj. 防禦的、衛冕的
    as clubs seek to dethrone Tainan City TSG as defending champions.
    各球會力圖扳倒尋求衛冕的台南台鋼。
  • roster n. 名單、工作人員名冊
    with the Kaohsiung Attackers added to the roster after they won the second-division title last season
    其中高雄攻擊手在上一季贏得乙級聯賽冠軍...後，被納入參賽名單中
  • promotion n. 晉升、升級、促銷
    earning promotion to the top flight.
    獲得晉升至頂級聯賽的資格。
  • host v. 主辦、主持、作東
    New Taipei Hang Yuan host TSG in the first game
    新北市航源在首場比賽中迎戰台南台鋼
  • unveil v. 公布、揭幕、展出
    unveiled the new season of 21 regular-season rounds
    公布了共 21 個常規賽輪次的新賽季
  • milestone n. 里程碑
    This is an important milestone for the TFPL reaching its 10th year
    對於 TFPL 邁入第 10 年而言，這是一個重要的里程碑
  • expansion n. 擴大、擴展
    initiate new programs and plan for expansion of the TFPL.
    啟動新計畫，並規劃擴大 TFPL 的規模。
  • operate v. 營運、運作、操作
    The plan is for each club to operate at its own home ground
    計畫是讓每個球會都在自己的主場營運
  • cultivate v. 培養、栽培、耕作
    the top-league TFPL is the stage for them to grow, cultivate and hone their skills
    頂級聯賽 TFPL 是他們成長、培養並磨練球技的舞台
  • budget n. 預算（衍生詞彙概念） / 財務負擔
    to reduce the financial burden on clubs
    以減輕球會的財務負擔
  • sponsorship n. 贊助、贊助金
    as well as more corporate sponsorship, citing the cost of booking certified soccer stadiums.
    以及更多企業贊助，並引用了預訂認證足球場的成本作為例證。
  • fund n. / v. 資金、基金 / 提供資金
    The sport needs more resources and funding from local governments
    這項運動需要來自地方政府更多的資源和資金
  • address v. 處理、解決、向...發表演說
    We must address this important task now
    我們現在必須解決這項重要任務

文法重點

  • be to + V (預定要、即將)
    The Taiwan Football Premier League (TFPL) is to start on Sunday
    「is to start」用來表示按計畫安排、即將發生的官方行程或未來事件，在新聞英文中非常常見。
  • with + 名詞 + 分詞 (附帶狀況)
    with four matches on the day, as clubs seek to dethrone Tainan City TSG as defending champions.
    「with + O. + 分詞」結構用來補充說明伴隨發生的狀況。在此例中，「with four matches on the day」表達當天伴隨著四場比賽的進行。
  • 現在分詞當作形容詞修飾名詞
    Eight teams are contesting the 10th season of Taiwan’s top men’s competition, with the Kaohsiung Attackers added to the roster after they won the second-division title last season, earning promotion to the top flight.
    句尾的「earning promotion...」是由分詞片語構成的結果狀語，補充說明前面贏得冠軍並被納入名單後所帶來的自然結果（獲得晉升）。
  • so that + 子句 (目的或結果)
    We are working toward adding two new teams to the current format so that 10 clubs are competing in the TFPL’s 2027-2028 season
    「so that」在此引導一個副詞子句，用來表達前面動作（增加兩支新球隊）的目的或預期達到的結果（讓 10 家球會得以參賽）。
