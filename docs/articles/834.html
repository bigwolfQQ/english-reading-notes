自國外攜帶減肥針劑須備有處方箋：食藥署

Prescription needed for foreign weight loss injections: FDA

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • prescription n. 處方箋，處方藥
    The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) on Tuesday reminded the public that GLP-1 receptor agonists require a prescription when brought from abroad.
    食品藥物管理署（FDA）於週二提醒民眾，從國外攜帶 GLP-1 受體促效劑入境時需要處方箋。
  • medication n. 藥物，藥劑
    GLP-1 receptor agonists are prescription medications primarily used to treat type 2 diabetes and support weight loss for people with obesity by suppressing appetite.
    GLP-1 受體促效劑是處方藥物，主要用於治療第 2 型糖尿病，並透過抑制食慾來協助肥胖症患者減重。
  • exceed v. 超過，超出
    The amount brought into Taiwan must not exceed the prescription, or be greater than six months’ supply, it said.
    食藥署表示，攜帶入境台灣的數量不得超過處方箋規定，或不得大於六個月的用藥量。
  • comply with phr. v. 遵守，符合（規定/法律）
    People carrying the drugs might use the green “Nothing to Declare” channel when entering Taiwan if the amount complies with regulations, but they might still be subject to random inspections, the FDA said.
    食藥署指出，若攜帶的藥品數量符合法規，入境台灣時可走綠色「免申報」通道，但仍可能接受隨機檢查。
  • be subject to phr. 易受...的，遭受...，須接受...
    People carrying the drugs might use the green “Nothing to Declare” channel when entering Taiwan if the amount complies with regulations, but they might still be subject to random inspections, the FDA said.
    食藥署指出，若攜帶的藥品數量符合法規，入境台灣時可走綠色「免申報」通道，但仍可能接受隨機檢查。
  • illegal adj. 非法的，違法的
    So far this year, authorities have had 448 cases involving imported GLP-1 drugs, 80 of which were found to be illegal, it said.
    食藥署表示，今年截至目前為止，有關部門已處理 448 起進口 GLP-1 藥品的案件，其中 80 起被發現屬違法。
  • fine n. 罰金，罰款
    A total of NT$2.36 million (US$74,009) in fines were meted out for contraventions...
    針對違規情事已開罰總計新台幣 236 萬元（合 74,009 美元）的罰款...
  • implement v. 實施，執行
    The agency said it has asked pharmacists to implement risk-control measures...
    該署表示已要求藥師落實風險管控措施...
  • purchase v. / n. 購買
    The FDA said it has not found any travel agencies arranging overseas trips for people to purchase the drugs...
    食藥署表示，並未發現任何旅行社安排海外行程供民眾購買該藥品...
  • channel n. 管道，通道
    It urged the public not to purchase or self-administer the drugs through unofficial channels...
    該署敦促大眾切勿透過非官方管道購買或自行施用這些藥品...

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作為後置修飾語 (Present/Past Participle as Post-modifier)
    GLP-1 receptor agonists are prescription medications primarily used to treat type 2 diabetes and support weight loss for people with obesity by suppressing appetite.
    句中的「primarily used to...」與「by suppressing...」皆為分詞片語。過去分詞片語「used to...」用來修飾前面的名詞「medications」，省略了「which are」；現在分詞「suppressing」則用來表達「藉由...方式」的手段與動作。
  • 關代所有格子句 (Relative Clause with 'of which')
    So far this year, authorities have had 448 cases involving imported GLP-1 drugs, 80 of which were found to be illegal, it said.
    此句使用「數量詞 + of which」引導非限定關係代間子句（例如 80 of which）。用來補充說明前面 448 起案件中，其中 80 起的情況，使兩個句子能緊密結合而不流於冗長。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    A total of NT$2.36 million (US$74,009) in fines were meted out for contraventions...
    新聞英語中常使用被動語態來強調動作的承受者（罰款被開罰），隱去執行動作的主體，使語氣更顯客觀、正式。
  • 現在完成式的應用 (Present Perfect Tense)
    The agency said it has asked pharmacists to implement risk-control measures...
    「has asked」為現在完成式，用來表示過去發生的動作（要求實施風險管控）持續影響到現在，或是強調該動作剛完成且對現況具有重要性。
