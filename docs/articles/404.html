今年前八個月梅毒病例增加 3%

Syphilis cases rise 3% through August

單字解析

  • statement n. 聲明，發言
    the Centers for Disease Control (CDC) said in a statement yesterday.
    疾病管制署（CDC）昨日在聲明中表示。
  • period n. 期間，時期
    The CDC said that 78 percent of cases in the period were male
    疾管署表示，該期間內的病例有 78% 為男性
  • respectively adv. 分別地
    Reported cases among women and people aged 15 to 24 were 1,538 and 1,340 respectively
    女性及 15 至 24 歲族群通報病例分別為 1,538 例及 1,340 例
  • measures n. 措施，方法
    highlighting the need for measures to control the disease.
    凸顯採取措施控制該疾病的必要性。
  • diagnosed v. (过去分词/形容词) 被診斷出
    if their mother was diagnosed with the disease while pregnant
    如果他們的母親在懷孕期間被診斷出罹患該疾病
  • subsequent adj. 隨後的，後來的
    with subsequent testing confirming that they did not have the disease.
    隨後的檢測則證實她們並未罹患該疾病。
  • timely adj. 及時的，適時的
    If pregnant women diagnosed with syphilis do not seek timely treatment
    若被診斷出罹患梅毒的孕婦未尋求即時治療
  • effective adj. 有效的
    Benzathine penicillin G is the only effective treatment for preventing mother-to-child transmission
    長效盤尼西林是預防母嬰傳染的唯一有效治療方法
  • delay v. 延誤，拖延
    leading people to delay seeking medical care and assume that the infection has gone away
    導致民眾延誤就醫，並誤以為感染已經消失
  • appropriate adj. 適當的，合適的
    Early diagnosis and appropriate treatment are vital to stopping the spread
    早期診斷與適當治療對於阻止疫情傳播至關重要
  • initial adj. 最初的，開始的
    If pregnant women test positive in an initial rapid plasma reagin or venereal disease screening
    如果孕婦在產前檢查的初步快速血漿反應素或性病篩檢中呈陽性
  • promptly adv. 迅速地，立即地
    and, if diagnosed with syphilis, undergo appropriate treatment promptly
    若確診感染梅毒，應立即接受適當治療

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Syphilis cases in Taiwan rose by 3 percent year-on-year in the first eight months of this year, driven by a 4 percent increase among women and young adults...
    句中的「driven by a 4 percent increase...」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的整句主干，表被動與原因（意指「是由……所驅動的」）。這種結構在商業與新聞英文中非常常見，用來精簡地補充說明原因或背景。
  • 對等連接詞 while 表對比 (While Indicating Contrast)
    The CDC said that 78 percent of cases in the period were male, while 70 percent were aged 15 to 44.
    在此句中，「while」用作對等連接詞，連接兩個子句，表示前後兩者數據的對比（「而……」）。這在商務圖表分析或新聞報導中，是用來同時呈現兩組數據時極為好用的句型。
  • 條件句與假設語氣 (Conditional Sentences)
    If pregnant women diagnosed with syphilis do not seek timely treatment, it could lead to stillbirth, miscarriage or premature birth, she said.
    此句使用「If + 現在式動詞 (do not seek) ..., 主詞 + could + 原形動詞 (lead to)」的句型，表達未來可能發生的條件與其潛在結果。用 could 代替 will 可以讓語氣顯得較為客觀且具潛在可能性。
  • 現在分詞片語作結果狀語 (Present Participle Phrase Expressing Result)
    Symptoms can also temporarily subside without treatment, leading people to delay seeking medical care and assume that the infection has gone away, she said.
    句尾的「leading people to delay...」是由現在分詞（leading）引導的片語，用來表達前一個動作所導致的自然結果（「進而導致人們……」）。這種用法能有效串聯兩個動作，避免使用過多的冗長子句。
