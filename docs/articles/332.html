奪回街道：將歐洲以汽車為中心的空間重新爪回的建築師們

Reclaiming the streets: the architects clawing back Europe’s car-centric spaces

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • infrastructure n. 基礎設施
    The European Prize for Urban Public Space tries to show that cities don’t have to be locked in the automotive age for ever.
    歐洲城市公共空間獎試圖展現，城市不必永遠被鎖在汽車時代中。
  • convert v. 轉變、轉換
    The prize’s latest shortlist, announced yesterday, highlights architectural projects that convert infrastructure for cars into spaces that help urban dwellers cope with the soaring temperatures of the climate crisis...
    該獎項於昨天公布的最新決選入圍名單，突顯了多項建築專案，這些專案將汽車基礎設施轉換為有助於都市居民應對氣候危機飆升氣溫的空間...
  • cope with phr. 應付、處理
    ...convert infrastructure for cars into spaces that help urban dwellers cope with the soaring temperatures of the climate crisis...
    ...將汽車基礎設施轉換為有助於都市居民應對氣候危機飆升氣溫的空間...
  • soaring adj. 急升的、暴漲的
    ...spaces that help urban dwellers cope with the soaring temperatures of the climate crisis...
    ...有助於都市居民應對氣候危機飆升氣溫的空間...
  • revive v. 復興、使復活
    The Swiss architect’s project sought to revive what he called a “lost space” on one of the main thoroughfares in the city...
    這位瑞士建築師的專案旨在復興他所謂的「失落空間」——位於該市主要交通要道之一...
  • repurpose v. 改變用途、重新利用
    The existing water basin became a shallow pool, while cast-iron flower pots were repurposed as fire bowls that heat a steam room.
    現有的水池變成了一個淺水池，鑄鐵花盆則被重新利用為加熱蒸汽室的火盆。
  • restrict v. 限制、約束
    It restricts our movement so much that we almost can’t see it anymore.
    這嚴重限制了我們的活動範圍，以至於我們幾乎視而不見了。
  • permanently adv. 永久地
    ...inadvertently showing how difficult it is to permanently alter car-centric infrastructure.
    ...這無意中顯示了永久改變以汽車為中心的基礎設施是多麼困難。
  • alter v. 改變、修改
    ...inadvertently showing how difficult it is to permanently alter car-centric infrastructure.
    ...這無意中顯示了永久改變以汽車為中心的基礎設施是多麼困難。
  • modify v. 修改、更改
    The rules around car traffic are hard to modify, and any requests usually go all the way up to municipal authorities...
    關於汽車交通的規則很難修改，任何請求通常都要一路向上呈報到市政當局...
  • launch v. 發起、推出、開辦
    In 2015, municipal authorities launched a competition for a public space to fill the site.
    2015年，市政當局為填補該基地的公共空間舉辦了一場競圖。
  • commission n. 委託、佣金
    “I have done projects like this for 25 years, but this park had to be completely different to anything we’ve done before, and that’s because of the climate issue,” said Bart Van Gassen of Tractebel, the urban architecture company that won the commission.
    「我做過這類專案25年了，但這個公園必須與我們以前做過的任何事情完全不同，那是因為氣候問題，」贏得該委託案的都市建築公司特拉克特貝爾（Tractebel）的巴特·範加森（Bart Van Gassen）說。

文法重點

  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    The European Prize for Urban Public Space tries to show that cities don’t have to be locked in the automotive age for ever.
    句中的 "that cities don’t have to be..." 為名詞子句，作動詞 show 的受詞；而文章中也常使用 "that"引導形容詞子句（例如修飾先行詞的子句）來補充說明名詞，這是多益閱讀測驗中長句解析的必備文法結構。
  • 分詞作形容詞 (Participles as Adjectives)
    ...highlights architectural projects that convert infrastructure for cars into spaces that help urban dwellers cope with the soaring temperatures of the climate crisis...
    句中的 "soaring temperatures" 使用現在分詞 "soaring" 當作形容詞，用來修飾名詞 temperatures，表示「正在飆升的溫度」。多益考試經常測驗現在分詞（表主動/進行）與過去分詞（表被動/完成）的詞性變化。
  • 假設語氣 (Subjunctive Mood)
    “If we had built something more permanent, we would have had to engage with safety concerns”, said Banchini...
    此句為與過去事實相反的假設語氣（Past Subjunctive）。句型為 "If + 過去完成式 (had built), 主詞 + would/could/might + have + 過去分詞 (would have had)"。用來表達「如果當初建造了更永久的東西，我們（當時）就不得不處理安全問題了（事實上並沒有建造）」。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The winner of the award, organized by Spain’s Center de Cultura Contemporania de Barcelona with six other European architecture institutions, will be announced on 15 October.
    句中的 "will be announced" 採用未來式的被動語態（will be + 過去分詞），強調得獎者「將被」宣布。前面的 "organized by..." 則是分詞片語簡化自被動關係代名詞子句（which is organized by...），多益商務英文與新聞報導中非常常使用被動語態來聚焦於事件或物件本身。
