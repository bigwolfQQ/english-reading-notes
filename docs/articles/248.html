單字解析

  • partnership n. 夥伴關係、合夥關係
    Taiwanes aircraft maker Aerospace Industrial Development Corp (AIDC) yesterday announced that it has secured a 20-year partnership agreement with Canada-based Bombardier Inc.
    台灣飛機製造商漢翔航空工業（AIDC）昨天宣布，已與總部位於加拿大的龐巴迪公司（Bombardier Inc.）簽署了一項為期20年的夥伴關係協議。
  • agreement n. 協議、合約
    The two companies held a ceremony in Taipei yesterday to exchange signed copies of the agreement, launching the 20-year partnership...
    這兩家公司昨天在台北舉行儀式，交換已簽署的協議副本，正式啟動為期20年的夥伴關係...
  • supply v. 供應、提供
    ...under which AIDC would supply cockpit and forward fuselage structural components for Bombardier’s super-midsize Challenger 3500 business jets, the Taiwanese company said.
    ...根據該協議，漢翔將為龐巴迪的超中型挑戰者3500（Challenger 3500）公務機供應駕駛艙及前機身結構組件，該台灣公司表示。

    ...with the Taiwanese supplier having provided rear fuselage structural components for more than 1,000 Challenger 3500 aircraft, it said.
    ...該台灣供應商已為超過1,000架挑戰者3500飛機提供後機身結構組件，它表示。

    ...並補充說，龐巴迪已於4月份完成了對這家台灣供應商生產能力的審查。
  • client n. 客戶、委託人

    經濟部長龔明鑫見證了這項交換，他表示，漢翔向包括龐巴迪在內的國際客戶供應機身結構組件，展現了國際社會對「台灣製造」產品的信心。

    他表示，在全球航太供應鏈重組的背景下，許多國際飛機製造商正在尋找合作夥伴，而漢翔已證明其作為具有韌性供應商的能力。
  • seek v. 尋找、尋求
    Amid restructuring of the global aerospace supply chain, many international aircraft makers are seeking partners, and AIDC has demonstrated its capabilities as a resilient supplier, he said.
    他表示，在全球航太供應鏈重組的背景下，許多國際飛機製造商正在尋找合作夥伴，而漢翔已證明其作為具有韌性供應商的能力。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The two companies held a ceremony in Taipei yesterday to exchange signed copies of the agreement, launching the 20-year partnership under which AIDC would supply cockpit and forward fuselage structural components...
    句中的「launching the 20-year partnership...」是由現在分詞片語引導的結構，用來補充說明前面主句所發生的動作及其帶來的結果，翻成中文時常譯為「......，從而啟動了……」。
  • 關係代間質與介系詞的結合 (Preposition + Relative Pronoun)
    ...launching the 20-year partnership under which AIDC would supply cockpit and forward fuselage structural components for Bombardier’s super-midsize Challenger 3500 business jets...
    「under which」是由介系詞「under」加上關係代間質「which」組成，用來代替前面的先行詞「the 20-year partnership」。在合約或協定（partnership / agreement）的語境下，常使用「under the agreement / under the partnership」的搭配，因此變為關係子句時，介系詞會移至關係代間質前方。
  • 非限定關係子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Minister of Economic Affairs Kung Ming-hsin (龔明鑫), who witnessed the exchange, said AIDC’s supply of fuselage structural components to international clients...
    逗號後方的「who witnessed the exchange」是一個非限定關係子句，用來對前面的專有名詞「Minister of Economic Affairs Kung Ming-hsin」做額外的補充說明。如果把這個子句拿掉，主句的意思依然完整清楚。
  • 過去分詞作形容詞用 (Past Participle as Adjective)
    Amid restructuring of the global aerospace supply chain, many international aircraft makers are seeking partners, and AIDC has demonstrated its capabilities as a resilient supplier, he said.
  • 分詞構句表原因或條件 (Participial Construction indicating Condition/Cause)
    Given the value of the Bombardier orders, AIDC is expected to enlist other Taiwanese suppliers to help fulfill the orders, he said...
    「Given the value of...」是一個常見的分詞構句（源自介系詞用法，相當於 considering 或 in view of），意指「鑑於……」、「考慮到……」。用來在句首引發評估某情況時所依據的理由或前提。
