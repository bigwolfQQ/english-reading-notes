台北遊行要求推動友善生態政策

Taipei march to demand ecology-friendly policies

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • incorporate v. 納入、結合
    Wildlife conservation groups yesterday urged the government to incorporate ecological considerations into policymaking rather than responding only after environmental harm has occurred...
    野生動物保育團體昨天敦促政府將生態考量納入政策制定中，而不是在環境破壞發生後才做出回應...
  • schedule v. 預定、安排
    The march, organized by Taiwan Walk for Wildlife (TWW), is to be held outside the Legislative Yuan and is scheduled to begin at 2pm on Saturday...
    這場遊行由「為野生動物而走」台灣行動聯盟（TWW）組織，預定於週六下午2點在立法院外舉行...
  • campaign n. 活動、運動
    At a news conference in Taipei, TWW chairman Lee Tsung-chen (李宗宸) said this year’s campaign builds on calls from the previous two editions in 2023 and 2024...
    在台北的一場記者會上，TWW主席李宗宸表示，今年的活動延續了2023年和2024年前兩屆遊行的訴求...
  • measure n. 措施、方法
    “We eventually realized that the problem was not simply that one measure was missing, but that different policies were operating independently...”
    李宗宸說：「我們最終意識到，問題不單單是缺少了一項措施，而是不同的政策各自獨立運作……」
  • extend v. 延伸、擴及
    The problem extends beyond animal protection, with ecological concerns often left out of decisionmaking by central and local governments...
    這個問題不僅限於動物保護，在中央和地方政府的決策中，生態考量經常被排除在外……
  • invest v. 投資、投入（時間、金錢等）
    Taiwan has invested substantial resources in researching the species, but coastal industrial development... have continued...
    台灣已投入大量資源研究該物種，但沿海工業開發仍持續進行……
  • substantial adj. 重大的、大量的、實質的
    Taiwan has invested substantial resources in researching the species...
    台灣已投入大量資源研究該物種……
  • adequate adj. 足夠的、適當的
    ...without adequately taking their ecological impact into account, Kuo said.
    郭佳雯表示，且未充分將其生態影響納入考量。
  • establishment n. 建立、設立
    Among TWW’s demands for the march is the establishment of a biodiversity committee jointly by central and local governments...
    TWW針對此次遊行的訴求之一，是由中央與地方政府共同成立生物多樣性委員會……
  • authority n. 權力、權限、官方
    ...with authority to coordinate across agencies and track implementation.
    ……賦予其跨部會協調並追蹤執行情況的權限。
  • subject to phr. 受...支配、須接受...（審查等）
    “Any policy that could affect the environment should be subject to review, with scientific data and expert opinions considered before decisions are made,” Lee said...
    李宗宸說：「任何可能影響環境的政策都應該接受審查，在做出決策之前必須考量科學數據和專家意見。」

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The march, organized by Taiwan Walk for Wildlife (TWW), is to be held outside the Legislative Yuan...
    句中用過去分詞片語「organized by...」來修飾前面的名詞 the march（被動關係）；而第1段的「...ahead of the Walk for Wildlife march, which is to be held in Taipei on Saturday.」則使用了關係子句。分詞片語常被用來簡化形容詞子句，在多益閱讀測驗中是常見的句構考點。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Independent Absolute Phrase)
    ...with ecological concerns often left out of decisionmaking by central and local governments on issues such as land use, energy and public works, he added.
    此處使用「with + 名詞 + 過去分詞 (left out)」的結構，用來補充說明附帶的狀況或結果。這個「with + O + 補語」的句型在多益文法題（尤其是受詞補語的選擇）中非常常考。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    “Any policy that could affect the environment should be subject to review, with scientific data and expert opinions considered before decisions are made,” Lee said...
    句中出現了多處被動語態，如「should be subject to review」以及「before decisions are made」。新聞英文或商業公文為了強調客觀事實或動作承受者，經常大量使用被動語態。
