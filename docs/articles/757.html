文法重點

  • 現在完成被動語態 (Present Perfect Passive)
    The 29th Taiwan-France Cultural Award has been presented to Tamkang University’s Wu Hsi-deh...
    句型為「have/has been + 過去分詞」，用來強調某個動作在過去發生、持續對現在造成影響，且主語是動作的承受者（獎項被頒發）。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    According to the Ministry of Culture, which jointly organizes the award with the Academie des Sciences Morales et Politiques of the Institut de France, Wu founded...
    句中的「which...」前面帶有逗號，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「the Ministry of Culture」，提供額外資訊，若拿掉該子句不影響主要句意。
  • 分詞構句作後置修飾 / 分詞片語修飾名詞 (Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    Established in 1996 and held annually since then, the Taiwan-France Cultural Award recognizes institutions...
    句首的「Established in 1996 and held annually...」是由過去分詞開頭的片語，省略了主格與be動詞（原句約等於 Which was established in 1996...），用來修飾後方的名詞主詞「the Taiwan-France Cultural Award」。
