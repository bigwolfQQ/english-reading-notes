Imported mpox cases rise from last year: CDC

單字解析

  • imported adj. 境外移入的、進口的
    The imported case is a man in his 30s, who traveled to Cambodia last month...
    境外移入病例則為一名30多歲男性，上個月曾前往柬埔寨...
  • confirmed adj. 確診的、已證實的
    The two new confirmed cases include a local case and an imported case, the centers said.
    疾管署表示，這兩起最新確診病例包含一起本土病例及一起境外移入病例。
  • onset n. （疾病等）發作、開始
    Both cases reported having unsafe sex before the onset of symptoms and were unvaccinated against mpox, it said.
    該署指出，兩起病例皆回報在症狀出現前曾有不安全性行為，且皆未接種M痘疫苗。
  • decline v. 下降、減少
    While the number of local cases has been declining, the percentage of imported cases has increased, he said...
    他指出，雖然本土病例數一直在下降，但境外移入病例的佔比卻在上升...
  • percentage n. 百分比、比例
    While the number of local cases has been declining, the percentage of imported cases has increased, he said...
    他指出，雖然本土病例數一直在下降，但境外移入病例的佔比卻在上升...
  • risk n. 風險
    The risk of contracting mpox overseas cannot be overlooked, he said.
    他說，在海外感染M痘的風險不容忽視。
  • overlook v. 忽視、忽略
    The risk of contracting mpox overseas cannot be overlooked, he said.
    他說，在海外感染M痘的風險不容忽視。
  • severity n. 嚴重性
    The overall symptoms and fatality rates of Clade Ib and Clade II are similar, and the severity of mpox infection depends heavily on the status of a person’s immune system, the CDC said.
    疾管署表示，第I b分支與第II分支的整體症狀和致死率相似，M痘感染的嚴重程度很大程度上取決於個人免疫系統的狀況。
  • depend heavily on phr. 很大程度上取決於
    The overall symptoms and fatality rates of Clade Ib and Clade II are similar, and the severity of mpox infection depends heavily on the status of a person’s immune system, the CDC said.
    疾管署表示，第I b分支與第II分支的整體症狀和致死率相似，M痘感染的嚴重程度很大程度上取決於個人免疫系統的狀況。
  • effective adj. 有效的
    Mpox vaccines can provide effective protection against Clade Ib infection...
    Mpox疫苗能針對第I b分支感染提供有效的保護力...
  • completion n. 完成（句中為動詞completing的分詞用法）
    Getting the first dose of the standard two-dose mpox vaccine regimen only provides about 40 to 80 percent protection, while completing the full two-dose series can increase effectiveness up to 90 percent, Lin said.
    林明誠表示，接種標準兩劑型M痘疫苗的第一劑，僅能提供約40%至80%的保護力，而完成完整的兩劑療程則可將保護力提升至高達90%。
  • urge v. 強力敦促、力勸
    ...adding that the CDC strongly urges the roughly 29 percent who did not get their second dose to get fully vaccinated as soon as possible.
    ...並補充說疾管署強烈呼籲大約29%尚未接種第二劑的民眾，應盡快完成完整疫苗接種。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Postmodifier)
    The Centers for Disease Control (CDC) yesterday reported two new cases of mpox, including a locally acquired case infected with the newer Mpox Clade Ib strain.
    句中的 "including a locally acquired case..." 使用了現在分詞片語來補充說明前面 "two new cases of mpox" 的細節，意指「包含...」。這種結構在新聞英語中非常常見，用來列舉細項。
  • 非限定關係代間子句補充說明 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    The imported case is a man in his 30s, who traveled to Cambodia last month and engaged in unsafe sexual activity...
    句中的 ", who traveled to..." 是一個帶逗號的非限定關係代間子句，對前面的 "a man in his 30s" 進行補充描述。逗號代表這是一個附加的資訊，如果拿掉也不影響主要句意。
  • 對比連接詞 while 的用法
    While the number of local cases has been declining, the percentage of imported cases has increased, he said...
    在此句中，"While" 作連接詞使用，表示「然而、儘管」，用來連接兩個形成對比的子句（本土病例下降 vs. 境外移入佔比上升）。
  • 被動語態的應用 (Passive Voice)
    ...the 10 imported cases reported this year were double the figure for the same period last year.
    句中的 "reported this year" 是過去分詞片語修飾 "the 10 imported cases"（被通報的10起境外移入病例），省略了 "that were"。主句的謂語動詞則是 "were double"，清楚表達統計數據的比較。
