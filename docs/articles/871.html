Drink chains among businesses fined NT$1.05M for food safety violations

  • violation n. 違反，違規

    當局週四表示，在最近的一次政府稽查中，包括「青山 PeakTea」與「八曜和茶」在內的幾家台灣手搖飲料連鎖店，因違反食品安全而遭到開罰。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Several Taiwanese beverage chains, including PeakTea and Hachiyo Tea, were among businesses fined for food safety violations in recent government inspections, authorities said Thursday.
    句中的「fined for food safety violations in recent government inspections」是一個過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 businesses。其完整概念是「businesses (that) were fined...」，省略了「which/that were」，使句子更精簡，是多益閱讀測驗中極常見的句構。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The Taiwan Food and Drug Administration (TFDA) said the inspections resulted in fines totaling NT$1.05 million (US$32,900).
    新聞報導為了客觀陳述事實，經常大量使用被動語態（如 be + 過去分詞）。例如第 4 段的「were found to be noncompliant」以及第 8 段的「was found to have a caffeine level」，強調動作的承受者（飲料或產品），而非動作的發動者。
  • 過去分詞作為形容詞表「被動與完成」(Past Participle as Adjective)
    A Master of Tea (花茶大師英式紅茶莊園) branch in Tainan's West Central District received the largest fine, NT$130,000, after inspectors found three violations: failure to obtain product liability insurance, use of expired food ingredients and inaccurate caffeine labeling.
    句中的 expired 是動詞 expire（過期）的過去分詞轉化而來的形容詞，用來修飾名詞 ingredients（食材），意指「已經過期的食材」。在多益商業字彙中，常用過去分詞（如 expired, trained, qualified）當作形容詞來形容人事物狀態。
