石虎友善耕作措施與蜂蜜產量增加相得益彰

Leopard cat-safe measures linked to honey boost

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • boost v. 提升，促進，增加
    Leopard cat-friendly farming methods helped boost honey production by 10 percent over the past four years, a Miaoli County-based beekeeper said at an A-Hu Go (阿虎加油) brand event in Taipei yesterday.
    苗栗縣的一名養蜂人在昨天台北的一場「阿虎加油」品牌活動中表示，過去四年來，石虎友善耕作方式幫助蜂蜜產量提升了 10%。
  • launch v. 發布，推出，發起
    The Forestry and Nature Conservation Agency’s Hsinchu branch yesterday launched the brand with a sales exhibition at the city’s Hope Plaza, which runs through today.
    林業及自然保育署新竹分署昨天在該市的希望廣場舉辦特賣展售會來推出這個品牌，活動將持續到今天。
  • visibility n. 能見度，知名度
    The brand was developed using the leopard cat-friendly farming label, which aims to raise the market visibility of products that support leopard cat conservation, it said.
    該分署表示，這個品牌是利用石虎友善耕作標章來開發的，旨在提高支持石虎保育之產品的市場能見度。
  • tailor v. 客製化，量身打造
    A-Hu Go products are packaged in paper boxes bearing a drawing of a leopard cat, it said, adding that the packaging can be tailored to the needs of products.
    該分署表示，「阿虎加油」的產品包裝在印有石虎圖案的紙盒中，並補充說包裝可以根據產品的需求進行客製化。
  • annual adj. 年度的，每年的
    Chiang Kun-hsien (江坤憲), who runs the Chun Tsai Apiary in Miaoli’s Yuanli Township (苑裡) along with his sisters, said their bee yard’s annual honey production has risen at least 10 percent since they began applying leopard cat-friendly farming methods more than three years ago.
    江坤憲與姊妹共同經營位於苗栗苑裡鎮的春在此養蜂場，他表示自從三年多前開始採用石虎友善耕作方式以來，他們蜂場的年蜂蜜產量已經至少成長了 10%。
  • struggle v. 掙扎，苦惱，難以應付
    His sister, Chiang Chia-ching (江佳靜), said they struggled with mysterious bee deaths at the apiary until they attended a leopard cat-friendly farming workshop organized by the branch, at which they learned that pesticides can kill the insects.
    他的姊妹江佳靜表示，他們曾在養蜂場遭遇神祕的蜜蜂死亡問題而苦惱，直到他們參加了由分署舉辦的石虎友善耕作工作坊，並在該活動中得知殺蟲劑會殺死這些昆蟲。
  • apply v. 申請，應用，實施
    All agricultural products with a leopard cat-friendly farming label can apply to use the A-Hu Go brand, the branch said, adding that 16 agricultural products from Miaoli have been approved.
    分署表示，所有擁有石虎友善耕作標章的農產品都可以申請使用「阿虎加油」品牌，並補充說來自苗栗的 16 項農產品已經獲得批准。
  • require v. 要求，規定
    Guidelines for leopard cat-friendly farming requires adoption of grass cover cultivation, and not using herbicides, synthetic pesticides or fertilizers, and any supplies that might harm leopard cats or the environment, it said.
    該分署表示，石虎友善耕作的指導方針要求採取草生栽培，且不得使用除草劑、化學合成農藥或肥料，以及任何可能傷害石虎或環境的資材。
  • approve v. 批准，核准，認可
    All agricultural products with a leopard cat-friendly farming label can apply to use the A-Hu Go brand, the branch said, adding that 16 agricultural products from Miaoli have been approved.
    分署表示，所有擁有石虎友善耕作標章的農產品都可以申請使用「阿虎加油」品牌，並補充說來自苗栗的 16 項農產品已經獲得批准。
  • aim v. 旨在，目的是
    The department aims to increase the county’s total leopard cat-friendly farming area to more than 2,400 hectares, he said.
    他說，農業處的目標是將全縣石虎友善耕作總面積增加到 2,400 公頃以上。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    A-Hu Go products are packaged in paper boxes bearing a drawing of a leopard cat, it said, adding that the packaging can be tailored to the needs of products.
    句中的「bearing a drawing of a leopard cat」是由現在分詞分詞片語構成的後位修飾語，用來修飾前面的名詞 paper boxes（紙盒），相當於形容詞子句「that bear a drawing of a leopard cat」，能讓句子更簡潔。
  • 現在分詞作表結果的對等子句簡化 (Present Participle Indicating Result)
    All agricultural products with a leopard cat-friendly farming label can apply to use the A-Hu Go brand, the branch said, adding that 16 agricultural products from Miaoli have been approved.
    句尾的「adding that...」是由連接詞結合現在分詞構成的附加說明結構，表示前面那句話所延伸出的結果或補充資訊，主詞為前句的主體（the branch），意指「分署並補充表示……」。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    The brand was developed using the leopard cat-friendly farming label, which aims to raise the market visibility of products that support leopard cat conservation, it said.
    句中的「which aims to...」是非限定關係代名詞子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「the leopard cat-friendly farming label」；而子句內部的「that support leopard cat conservation」則是限定關係代名詞子句，用來修飾前面的名詞「products」。
  • 過去分詞作形容詞修飾語 (Past Participle as Adjective)
    They are rarely attracted to nearby pesticide-treated farmlands, he said.
    句中的「pesticide-treated」是由名詞加上過去分詞構成的複合形容詞，用來修飾後面的名詞 farmlands（農田），表示「經過農藥處理的」，這在多益商業閱讀中是非常常見的實用構型。
