乖乖員工達成遷廠補償協議，結束為期五天的罷工

Kuai Kuai workers end 5-day strike with relocation compensation deal

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • relocation n. 搬遷、遷址
    The union representing workers at snack maker Kuai Kuai Co.'s only factory in Taiwan ended a five-day strike on Tuesday after reaching a deal with management on compensation for the company's planned relocation.
    代表零食製造商乖乖股份有限公司全台唯一工廠員工的工會，在與資方就公司計畫遷廠的補償達成協議後，於週二結束了為期五天的罷工。
  • compensation n. 補償、賠償金
    The deal was signed Tuesday afternoon after two days of talks, with the company agreeing to provide more compensation than it had initially offered...
    該協議在經過兩天的談判後於週二下午簽署，公司同意提供比最初提出之方案更多的補償...
  • agreement n. 協議、合約
    Under the agreement reached Tuesday, union members who resign within the next two years after being reassigned within the Zhongli factory in Taoyuan will receive statutory severance pay...
    根據週二達成的協議，在桃園中壢廠內部調職後於未來兩年內辭職的工會成員，將獲得法定資遣費...
  • resign v. 辭職
    Union members who resign by Sept. 30, 2028, because the company's relocation requires them to leave the Zhongli factory will receive statutory severance plus eight months' full wages.
    因公司遷廠必須離開中壢廠而在2028年9月30日以前辭職的工會成員，將獲得法定資遣費加上八個月全薪。
  • severance n. 斷絕、解約（常與 pay 或 allowance 並用指資遣費）
    The company has yet to secure a site for the new factory, and workers who resigned could be left without severance or other compensation.
    公司尚未為新工廠尋得廠址，辭職的員工可能會在沒有資遣費或其他補償的情況下失業。
  • seniority n. 年資、資歷
    In addition, workers with at least 12 years of seniority under Taiwan's new labor pension system will receive an additional NT$100,000 (US$3,148), the statement said.
    此外，聲明表示，在台灣新勞工退休金制度下年資至少滿12年的員工，將獲得額外新台幣10萬元（約合3,148美元）的補償。
  • negotiate v. 談判、協商
    The agreement also contains a 'no-free-rider clause,' meaning the negotiated benefits will apply only to union members.
    該協議還包含一項「禁搭便車條款」，意味著經談判取得的福利將僅適用於工會成員。
  • compromise v. 妥協、折衷
    It said their support enabled union representatives to 'not easily compromise or back down' during negotiations on Monday and Tuesday.
    該會指出，成員們的支持使工會代表在週一和週二的談判中「不易妥協或退讓」。
  • dispute n. 爭端、爭議
    The strike began last Friday amid a dispute over how workers would be treated in the planned relocation of Kuai Kuai's Zhongli factory.
    罷工於上週五開始，起因是對乖乖中壢廠計畫遷廠時如何安置員工的爭議。
  • lay off phr. v. 解雇、裁員
    Kuai Kuai said last Friday that it would not lay off workers for two years and that workers laid off after that period would receive statutory severance plus two months' wages.
    乖乖上週五表示，兩年內不會資遣員工，該期限之後被資遣的員工將獲得法定資遣費加上兩個月的薪資。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作為後位修飾 (Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The union representing workers at snack maker Kuai Kuai Co.'s only factory in Taiwan ended a five-day strike...
    句中的「representing workers at snack maker Kuai Kuai Co.'s only factory in Taiwan」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前方名詞 The union，相當於形容詞子句「which represented workers...」，這種結構在多益閱讀測驗中極為常見，能有效簡化句子。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The deal was signed Tuesday afternoon after two days of talks...
    句型使用「was signed」（be 動詞 + 過去分詞），強調動作的承受者（協定），在商業合約、新聞報導與多益聽力/閱讀測驗中，常用來表達合約、協議或檔案被簽署、批准。
  • 關係代間子句 (Relative Clause with 'who')
    Union members who resign within the next two years after being reassigned within the Zhongli factory in Taoyuan will receive statutory severance pay...
    「who resign within the next two years...」是一個限定用法的關係代間子句，用來補充說明並限制先行詞「Union members」的身份，多益測驗常考 who、which、that 等關係代間詞的格位與選填。
  • 介系詞片語表達背景狀況 (Prepositional Phrase of Circumstance)
    The strike began last Friday amid a dispute over how workers would be treated in the planned relocation of Kuai Kuai's Zhongli factory.
    「amid a dispute over...」中的介系詞 amid 表示「在……之中／伴隨著」，用來引導罷工爆發時的背景情境或衝突原因，常用於商業與新聞英文以精鍊地交代事件起因。
