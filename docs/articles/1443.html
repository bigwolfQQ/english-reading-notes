台北科技論壇展出軍用四足機器人

Military quadbot exhibited at tech forum in Taipei

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • organizer n. 主辦單位，組織者
    A military-use quadruped robot dog was presented at the Taiwan-Japan Future Technology Forum in Taipei yesterday, by the event’s organizer, the Indo-Pacific Strategy Thinktank.
    一款軍用四足機器人狗昨天在台北舉行的台灣日本未來科技論壇上亮相，由該活動的主辦單位「印太戰略智庫」所展示。
  • adopt v. 採納，採用
    The robot is built on the Vision 60 platform developed by US-based Ghost Robotics, which has been adopted by the US Marine Corps, Army and Air Force.
    該機器人是以美國 Ghost Robotics 所開發的 Vision 60 平台打造，已被美國海軍陸戰隊、陸軍和空軍所採用。
  • withstand v. 承受，經受
    The Vision 60 has an IP67 protection rating, allowing it to withstand immersion in one meter of water for 30 minutes.
    Vision 60 具備 IP67 防水防塵等級，能承受在一公尺深的水中浸泡 30 分鐘。
  • payload n. 有效負載，載重
    Powered by an Nvidia Jetson system, the robot can carry a 10kg payload and operate for more than three hours per charge, compared with about 90 minutes for similar products.
    在 Nvidia Jetson 系統的驅動下，該機器人可承載 10 公斤的有效負載，充電後可運作超過三小時，相較之下同類產品約為 90 分鐘。
  • replace v. 替換，更換
    Its modular design allows a malfunctioning leg to be replaced quickly.
    其模組化設計允許快速更換故障的腿部。
  • perform v. 執行，履行
    Wu Jung-tsan (吳榮燦), deputy general manager of Tansimo Robotic Manufacturing, said mission-specific equipment allows the platform to perform tasks ranging from inspections and search and rescue to security and detection.
    騰創製造副總經理吳榮燦表示，任務專屬設備使該平台能夠執行從檢查、搜救到保全與偵測等各種任務。
  • maintenance n. 維護，保養
    With a wireless charging station, it can return to recharge independently, supporting extended monitoring missions while reducing maintenance needs.
    搭配無線充電站，它可以獨立返回充電，支援延長的監控任務同時減少維護需求。
  • handle v. 處理，應付
    A six-degree-of-freedom (6DoF) robotic arm can open doors and handle objects, including ammunition.
    一個六自由度（6DoF）機械臂可以開門並處理物體，包括彈藥。
  • practical adj. 實際的，實用的
    Wu said the Vision 60 took part in a Japanese military exercise in the first quarter of this year, demonstrating that quadruped robots have moved beyond the laboratory into practical use.
    吳榮燦表示，Vision 60 在今年第一季參加了日本的軍事演習，證明了四足機器人已走出實驗室，進入實際應用。
  • deploy v. 部署，調動
    Deploying unmanned systems for some high-risk tasks can improve operational efficiency and reduce risks to personnel, he said.
    他說，為某些高風險任務部署無人系統可以提高營運效率，並降低人員面臨的風險。
  • efficiency n. 效率，效能
    Deploying unmanned systems for some high-risk tasks can improve operational efficiency and reduce risks to personnel, he said.
    他說，為某些高風險任務部署無人系統可以提高營運效率，並降低人員面臨的風險。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The robot is built on the Vision 60 platform developed by US-based Ghost Robotics, which has been adopted by the US Marine Corps, Army and Air Force.
    句中的「developed by US-by Ghost Robotics」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「platform」。這類結構省略了「which was」，能使句子更精簡流暢，是多益閱讀測驗中常見的文法考點。
  • 現在分詞作結果狀語 (Present Participle indicating Result)
    With a wireless charging station, it can return to recharge independently, supporting extended monitoring missions while reducing maintenance needs.
  • 動名詞作主詞 (Gerund as Subject)
    Deploying unmanned systems for some high-risk tasks can improve operational efficiency and reduce risks to personnel, he said.
    句首的「Deploying unmanned systems...」是動名詞片語，作為整個子句的主詞，視為單數因此搭配助動詞 can。動名詞片語當主詞在商業與科技英文中非常常見，用來探討某項行動或措施。
