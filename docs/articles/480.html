補助金旨在協助宗教交流

Subsidies to aid religious exchanges

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • subsidy n. 補助金，津貼
    The Ministry of the Interior has unveiled religious exchange subsidies of up to NT$3 million (US$94,823) to encourage religious groups to diversify their international partnerships.
    內政部已公布高達新台幣300萬元（約合94,823美元）的宗教交流補助金，以鼓勵宗教團體多元化其國際夥伴關係。
