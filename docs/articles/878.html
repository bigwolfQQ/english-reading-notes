文法重點

  • 非限定關係代名詞子句 (Non-defining relative clause)
    The Act, which was sponsored by Kim and Democratic Rep. Al Green, directs the U.S. officials at the Treasury and IMF to advocate for Taiwan's membership...
    句中的「which was sponsored by Kim and Democratic Rep. Al Green」前面帶有逗號，屬於非限定關係代名詞子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「The Act」（該法案），若將此子句拿掉，主要子句的句意依然完整。
  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況或時間 (Participial phrase)
    "As they [Taiwanese officials] get ready to formally submit a request to join the IMF, I hope the U.S. will use our voice to support their petition to join, or encourage them to do so as soon as possible," she said.
    句尾的「ending Taiwan's formal representation」是由現在分詞（ending）引導的分詞構句，用來補充說明前面主句動作所產生的結果（「結束了台灣的正式代表權」），使句子簡潔而不需使用冗長的連接詞。
  • 被動語態 (Passive voice)
    The California Republican asked Bessent about implementation of the Taiwan Non-Discrimination Act, enacted last year as part of the National Defense Authorization Act.
    句中的「enacted last year...」是過去分詞片語當作後位修飾詞，修飾前面的「the Taiwan Non-Discrimination Act」，其概念源自被動語態（which was enacted），用來表達該法案是「被」頒布的。
  • 現在分詞作修飾語 / 分詞片語 (Present participle phrase)
    In response, Bessent said, "We're aware of your bill," referring to the Taiwan Non-Discrimination Act of 2025.
    句尾的「referring to the Taiwan Non-Discrimination Act of 2025」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面整句話或主詞 Bessent 的動作，意思是「指的是……」，在新聞英語中非常常見，用以補充說明前文提及的專有名詞。
