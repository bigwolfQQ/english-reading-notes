文化部長：台灣文化對中國而言是痛苦的

Taiwanese culture painful for China: culture minister

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • existence n. 存在
    Taiwan’s existence and culture are painful for China, Minister of Culture Li Yuan (李遠) said in a radio interview in France
    文化部長李遠在法國接受廣播專訪時表示，台灣的存在與文化對中國而言是痛苦的
  • achieve v. 達成、實現
    Speaking to Radio France Internationale (RFI) on Friday, Li said China has been unable to achieve what Taiwan has demonstrated
    李遠週五接受法國國際廣播電台（RFI）訪問時表示，中國一直無法達成台灣所展現的成就
  • demonstrate v. 展現、示範
    Speaking to Radio France Internationale (RFI) on Friday, Li said China has been unable to achieve what Taiwan has demonstrated
    李遠週五接受法國國際廣播電台（RFI）訪問時表示，中國一直無法達成台灣所展現的成就
  • emphasis n. 強調、重視
    Responding to China’s frequent emphasis on the grandeur and long history of Chinese culture
    針對中國經常強調中華文化的宏大與悠久歷史
  • promote v. 推廣、促進、晉升
    as genuine culture is reflected in people’s everyday lives and does not need to be deliberately defined or promoted.
    因為真正的文化體現在人們的日常生活中，不需要刻意去定義或推廣。
  • perceive v. 看待、感知、察覺
    “A truly strong culture does not need to boast about itself, nor does it need to fear how others perceive or treat it,” Li said in Chinese.
    李遠以中文表示：「一個真正強大的文化不需要自我吹噓，也不需要害怕別人如何看待或對待它。」
  • partnership n. 夥伴關係、合夥關係
    where he visited cultural institutions and witnessed the forging of a partnership between the National Museum of Taiwan Literature and the UK’s National Centre for Writing.
    他在當地參訪文化機構，並見證了國立台灣文學館與英國國家寫作中心之間夥伴關係的締造。
  • schedule v. 預定、安排
    He is scheduled to return to Taiwan on Wednesday.
    他預定於週三返回台灣。
  • cooperation n. 合作
    During the European visit, Li also met with cultural and literary figures and discussed further cooperation in areas including literature, comics and digital arts.
    在歐洲訪問期間，李遠也與文化及文學界人士會面，並討論在文學、漫畫和數位藝術等領域的進一步合作。
  • potential adj. 潛在的
    and discussed potential cooperation with Taiwan in digital art, including joint exhibitions, co-productions and artist development.
    並討論台灣與法國在數位藝術方面的潛在合作，包括聯合展覽、共同製作與藝術家培育。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase)
    Minister of Culture Li Yuan (李遠) said in a radio interview in France, describing Taiwan as “a mirror that reveals the true nature of things.”
    句尾的「describing Taiwan as...」是由現在分詞帶領的片語，用來補充說明前面主句動作的伴隨狀態或結果，在多益閱讀測驗中常考這種分詞構句的用法。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    ...describing Taiwan as “a mirror that reveals the true nature of things.”
    句中的「that reveals the true nature of things」是關係子句，使用「that」來修飾先行詞「a mirror」，說明這面鏡子具備的功能與特性。
  • 地方副詞子句與省略 (Adverb Clause of Place with Omission)
    He left Britain on Thursday for France, where he attended events related to the Taiwan-France Cultural Award...
    句中的「where he attended...」是非限定關係副詞子句，用來補充說明先行詞「France」的地點，並連接兩個相關的事件。
  • 動名詞作受詞 (Gerund as Object)
    ...including the judging of the award and the presentation ceremony.
    介系詞「including」後方接了名詞「the judging」與「the presentation」，「judging」在此為動名詞轉化的名詞，用來列舉活動的內容。
