賽車安全機制失靈，車手向「超級英雄」對手表達感謝

Driver thanks ‘superhero’ rival as race safety fails

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • response n. 反應，回應
    A British racing driver thanked a “superhero” rival for pulling him from the flaming wreckage of his car after a horror crash in Shanghai, saying the quick response saved his life.
    一名英國賽車手在上海發生可怕的撞車事故後，感謝一名「超級英雄」對手將他從著火的車輛殘骸中拉出來，並表示迅速的反應救了他的命。
  • aid n. 援助，幫忙
    Loek Hartog on Saturday stopped and leapt out of his own car to rush to the aid of Ollie Millroy during the China GT Championship race at the Shanghai International Circuit, home of the Chinese Formula One Grand Prix.
    Loek Hartog 週六在上海國際賽車場舉行的中國GT錦標賽期間，停下車並跳出自己的車，趕去協助 Ollie Millroy。
  • instantly adv. 立即，馬上
    “Loek Hartog stopped instantly with no flag marshals or fire crews in sight and risked his own life to get me out of the car,” he added.
    他補充說：「Loek Hartog 在完全沒有看到旗號裁判或消防人員的情況下，立刻停下車，冒著生命危險把我從車裡救出來。」
  • result in phr. 導致，結果是
    The 36-year-old Millroy suffered a huge impact in the multi-car crash during the race, which resulted in his car erupting instantly in flames.
    現年 36 歲的 Millroy 在這場多車相撞的比賽中遭受了巨大的撞擊，導致他的車瞬間起火。
  • participate in phr. 參加，參與
    Millroy’s team, AAI Motorsport, said they would “no longer participate in any races organised by China GT” in a statement that criticized safety standards at the circuit.
    Millroy 的車隊 AAI Motorsport 在一份批評該賽道安全標準的聲明中表示，他們將「不再參加任何由中國GT舉辦的比賽」。
  • statement n. 聲明，陳述
    Millroy’s team, AAI Motorsport, said they would “no longer participate in any races organised by China GT” in a statement that criticized safety standards at the circuit.
    Millroy 的車隊 AAI Motorsport 在一份批評該賽道安全標準的聲明中表示，他們將「不再參加任何由中國GT舉辦的比賽」。
  • effective adj. 有效的
    The team said Millroy’s car “continued to burn for more than a minute” after the crash, and that “no timely and effective firefighting and rescue operations were seen.”
    車隊表示，在撞車事故發生後，Millroy 的車「持續燃燒了超過一分鐘」，且「未見及時且有效的消防與救援行動」。
  • apologize v. 道歉
    The China GT Championship organizing committee said in a statement on Chinese social media: “We sincerely apologize for this accident and for the subsequent shock and distress it has caused.”
    中國GT錦標賽組委會在中國社群媒體上發布的聲明中表示：「我們對這起事故以及造成的後續衝擊與困擾致上最誠摯的歉意。」
  • thorough adj. 徹底的，仔細的
    “We will conduct a thorough review and investigation into this accident, and draw up more comprehensive plans to address any shortcomings,” it said.
    聲明指出：「我們將對這起事故進行徹底的審查與調查，並擬定更全面的計畫來解決任何缺失。」
  • address v. 處理，解決（問題等）
    “We will conduct a thorough review and investigation into this accident, and draw up more comprehensive plans to address any shortcomings,” it said.
    聲明指出：「我們將對這起事故進行徹底的審查與調查，並擬定更全面的計畫來解決任何缺失。」

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾（形容詞片語）
    A British racing driver thanked a “superhero” rival for pulling him from the flaming wreckage of his car after a horror crash in Shanghai, saying the quick response saved his life.
    句中的 "pulling..." 與 "saying..." 都是現在分詞片語。"pulling him from..." 作介詞 for 的受詞補語（或動名詞片語），用來補充說明感謝的原因；而逗點後的 ", saying..." 則是分詞構句，用來補充說明前面主句發生的同時所伴隨的動作（主詞主動發出言論）。
  • 關係代名詞子句（限定與非限定用法）
    Loek Hartog on Saturday stopped and leapt out of his own car to rush to the aid of Ollie Millroy during the China GT Championship race at the Shanghai International Circuit, home of the Chinese Formula One Grand Prix.
    句尾的 ", home of the Chinese Formula One Grand Prix" 是一個同位語，用來補充說明前面 "the Shanghai International Circuit"。而在其他段落中常見由關係代名詞 (如 who, that) 引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾先行詞，例如 "...Loek Hartog, who was the first driver to arrive at the scene..."。
  • 過去完成式的假設語氣（與過去事實相反）
    “It was terrifying, and there were moments when I didn’t know if I’d ever manage to get Ollie out,” he said. “But it’s not about what I did. I’m sure many people in my position would have reacted exactly the same way.”
    雖然此句不是標準的 if 子句假設語氣，但 "many people in my position would have reacted..." 隱含了與過去事實相反的假設（如果其他人處在我的位置，也會做出同樣的反應），因此使用了助動詞過去式 would 加上完成式（此處接原形動詞 have reacted），表達對過去情況的推測。
  • 受詞補語與直接引語（Direct Speech）
    Millroy’s team, AAI Motorsport, said they would “no longer participate in any races organised by China GT” in a statement that criticized safety standards at the circuit.
    新聞報導經常使用直接引語（以引號括起來的原話）或間接引語（如本句中的 "said they would..."）。此句中 "said" 後面接了省略 "that" 的名詞子句作為受詞，子句內部則使用了過去未來式 (would) 來表達在過去時空中對未來的預測。
