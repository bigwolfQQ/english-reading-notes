與中國有關的駭客攻擊斐濟：美國大使館

China-linked hackers hit Fiji: US embassy

單字解析

  • compromise v. 危害、損害；妥協
    Hackers with links to China compromised a regional organization in Fiji...
    與中國有關聯的駭客入侵（危害）了斐濟的一個區域性組織...
  • telecommunication n. 電信
    ...warning that governments and telecommunication networks in the region were also being targeted.
    ...並警告該地區的政府和電信網路也正成為攻擊目標。
  • hub n. 中心、樞紐
    Fiji is emerging as a major telecommunications hub in the Pacific islands...
    斐濟正逐漸成為太平洋島國中的主要電信樞紐...
  • expand v. 擴展、擴充
    ...as Google expands a network of subsea cables...
    ...隨著Google擴建一條海底電纜網路...
  • review v. 審視、審查、複習
    ...Ioane Naivalurua said he was reviewing the situation after the US embassy announced...
    ...伊歐內·奈瓦盧魯阿表示，在美國大使館宣布...後，他正在審視當前情勢。
  • statement n. 聲明、陳述
    ...the embassy said in a statement posted on Facebook...
    ...大使館在一份發布於臉書的聲明中表示...
  • boost v. 提升、增進
    US officials have previously urged cable host Fiji to boost cybersecurity...
    美國官員先前曾敦促電纜主辦國斐濟加強（提升）網路安全...
  • private sector n. phr. 私部門、民間企業
    ...and urged governments, organizations and the private sector to 'take preventative action...'
    ...並敦促政府、組織及私部門『採取預防行動...』
  • investigate v. 調查
    Naivalurua said that police were investigating the reported attack...
    奈瓦盧魯阿表示，警方正在調查這起通報的攻擊事件...
  • evidence n. 證據
    Fiji’s government on Friday asked the US to provide evidence for its claim...
    斐濟政府週五要求美國提供證據，以支持其...的指控
  • bribe n. / v. 賄賂
    ...that a Chinese state agency employee living in Fiji had paid bribes on behalf of Beijing.
    ...即一名住在斐濟的中國國家機構員工曾代表北京行賄。
  • corruption n. 腐敗、貪汙
    The US Department of State accused the man living in Suva of 'significant corruption.'
    美國國務院指控這名住在蘇瓦的男子涉及『重大腐敗』。
  • combat v. 對抗、打擊
    ...pointed to action taken by the FBI in August to combat China-sponsored hackers...
    ...指出聯邦調查局（FBI）在8月採取行動，打擊中國資助的駭客...
  • infrastructure n. 基礎設施
    ...targeting critical infrastructure in the US.
    ...針對美國關鍵基礎設施的攻擊。
  • resilience n. 韌性、恢復力
    ...as the region’s digital security is closely linked to its economic resilience...
    ...因為該地區的數位安全與其經濟韌性...息息相關

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Phrase as Post-modifier）作後置修飾
    Hackers with links to China compromised a regional organization in Fiji, the Pacific nation’s US embassy said yesterday, warning that governments and telecommunication networks in the region were also being targeted.
    句尾的 "warning that..." 是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前面主句所發生的動作同時所伴隨的情況或結果，中文常譯為「同時警告說...」。
  • 現在分詞片語表附帶狀況（伴隨動作）
    Fiji is emerging as a major telecommunications hub in the Pacific islands, as Google expands a network of subsea cables connecting the US military outpost of Guam...
  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾
    ...the embassy said in a statement posted on Facebook, without identifying the organization.
  • 被動語態與進行式結合（Past Continuous Passive）
    Naivalurua said that police were investigating the reported attack...
    句中的 "were investigating" 是過去進行式，表示在過去某一特定時間點正在進行的動作。另外文章中也常看到現在進行式被動 "were also being targeted"，用來強調動作正持續被執行。
  • 使役與感官相關結構及引語中的名詞子句
    Fiji’s government on Friday asked the US to provide evidence for its claim last week that a Chinese state agency employee living in Fiji had paid bribes on behalf of Beijing.
    句中的 "that a Chinese state agency employee..." 是一個同位語子句，用來詳細補充說明前面 "claim"（主張／指控）的具體內容是什麼。
