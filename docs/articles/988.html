氣象署：天氣有利中秋節賞月

Favorable weather to allow Mid-Autumn Festival moon gazing: CWA

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • experience v. 經歷、體驗
    Taiwan will experience partly cloudy to sunny weather in the coming week...
    台灣未來一週將經歷多雲到晴的天氣...
  • condition n. 狀況、條件
    ...with favorable conditions for moon-gazing during the Mid-Autumn Festival on Friday...
    ...週五中秋節的天氣條件有利於賞月...
  • relatively adv. 相對地
    ...generally cloudy to sunny conditions with relatively little moisture are expected across Taiwan through Sept. 26.
    ...預計全台各地直到9月26日大致為多雲到晴、水氣相對較少的天氣。
  • forecast v. / n. 預報、預測
    From Wednesday to Saturday, winds are forecast to shift to an easterly direction...
    從週三到週六，預測風向將轉為偏東風...
  • shift v. 轉移、改變、轉向
    From Wednesday to Saturday, winds are forecast to shift to an easterly direction...
    從週三到週六，預測風向將轉為偏東風...
  • stable adj. 穩定的
    With weather conditions on Friday and Saturday expected to remain generally stable...
    由於週五與週六的天氣狀況預計將維持大致穩定...
  • celebrate v. 慶祝
    Taiwan celebrates the Mid-Autumn Festival, also known as the Moon Festival...
    台灣慶祝中秋節，又稱為月餅節...
  • impact n. 影響、衝擊
    ...with the typhoon's most significant impact expected from Monday afternoon through Tuesday...
    ...颱風最顯著的影響預計將從週一午後持續到週二...
  • located adj. 位於
    ...the storm was located 580 kilometers south-southeast of Osaka...
    ...該風暴位於大阪南南東方580公里處...
  • speed n. 速度
    ...moving north at a speed of 21-27 kilometers per hour.
    ...以每小時21至27公里的速度向北移動。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 / 附屬子句簡化
    ...bringing isolated brief showers to southern Taiwan and mountainous areas in central Taiwan early in the morning.
    句中的 bringing... 是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前面動詞所發生的結果或伴隨的動作（相當於 and it brings...），在新聞英文中非常常見，能讓句子更緊湊。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    From Wednesday to Saturday, winds are forecast to shift to an easterly direction...
    「are forecast」使用了被動語態，用來表達「風向被預測、被預報」，在氣象報導或商業新聞中，當發布預測或統計數據時常使用被動語態以客觀呈現。
  • 過去分詞作形容詞用法
    ...the storm was located 580 kilometers south-southeast of Osaka...
    句中的「located」為過去分詞作形容詞用，用來描述主詞 the storm 的所在位置狀態（被定位在...）。
