在人工智慧熱潮中作為小型經濟體是艱難的

It is tough being a small economy in an AI boom

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • strategy n. 策略、戰略
    Manila would have to just hope it can ride out the current difficulties and, then, take a step back and develop a long-term strategy that resembles the one undertaken by regional peers in less arduous times.
    馬尼拉只能希望自己能度過目前的困境，然後退一步制定一個長期策略，就像區域同儕在較不艱難的時期所採取的那樣。
  • target n. 目標
    Remolona has raised interest rates, mostly to curb inflation that is still well above the central bank’s target because of the Iran war.
    雷莫洛納已經升息，主要是為了抑制由於伊朗戰爭而仍然遠高於央行目標的通膨。
  • priority n. 優先事項、優先權
    The combination of plummeting growth and quick inflation is forcing policymakers to pick their priorities.
    成長暴跌與快速通膨的結合，迫使決策者必須選擇他們的優先事項。
  • source n. 來源
    ...and AI is as much a threat as an opportunity to the Philippines’ outsourcing industry, which has been a crucial source of jobs and growth in recent decades.
    ……而且對於幾十年來一直是就業和成長關鍵來源的菲律賓外包產業來說，人工智慧既是威脅也是機會。
  • procurement n. 採購
    Growth has been set back by a graft scandal involving procurement for flood relief measures.
    經濟成長因一起涉及防洪工程採購的貪腐醜聞而受挫。
  • reserve n. 儲備、外匯存底
    He has told lawmakers that trying to strengthen the peso to 60 per greenback (it traded at 62 on Friday) or more only risks burning through reserves.
    他告訴議員，試圖將披索匯率走強至每美元兌 60 披索（週五的交易價為 62）或更高，只會冒著燒光外匯存底的風險。
  • curb v. 控制、抑制
    The furor has curbed spending on public works.
    這場風波抑制了公共工程的支出。
  • post v. 公佈、發布（數據或成績）
    It has also posted some of the biggest declines among emerging markets, a move that is exacerbating a significant inflation problem.
    它也創下了新興市場中最大的跌幅之一，這一走勢正在加劇嚴重的通膨問題。
  • hover v. 徘徊（在特定數值或水準附近）
    The peso is the worst performer in Asia this quarter, down about 2.3 percent against the US dollar and hovering at record lows.
    披索是本季亞洲表現最差的貨幣，對美元下跌約 2.3%，並在歷史低點附近徘徊。
  • prospect v. 繁榮、成功 (文章中使用 prosper)
    An analysis from Bank of America was bullish on the overall benefits of AI spending for Southeast Asia, but cautioned that not everybody would prosper to the same degree.
    美國銀行的分析對人工智慧支出對東南亞的整體效益保持樂觀，但也警告說並非每個人都會同等程度地繁榮。
  • vulnerable adj. 易受傷的、脆弱的
    The Philippines is especially vulnerable, importing almost all the oil it requires.
    菲律賓特別脆弱，其所需的石油幾乎全部依賴進口。
  • arduous adj. 艱難的、費力的
    Manila would have to just hope it can ride out the current difficulties and, then, take a step back and develop a long-term strategy that resembles the one undertaken by regional peers in less arduous times.
    馬尼拉只能希望自己能度過目前的困境，然後退一步制定一個長期策略，就像區域同儕在較不艱難的時期所採取的那樣。
  • acute adj. 嚴重的、急性的
    That was a time of acute crisis in emerging markets and, while the radical steps put a floor under the ringgit, the country did pay a price...
    那是新興市場處於極度危機的時期，雖然這些激進措施為馬幣提供了支撐，但該國確實付出了代價……

文法重點

  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況或結果
    The peso is the worst performer in Asia this quarter, down about 2.3 percent against the US dollar and hovering at record lows.
    句尾的 "down about..." 與 "hovering at..." 皆為現在分詞或形容詞片語構成的分詞構句，用來補充說明主句主語（The peso）當下的狀態或伴隨發生的情況。
  • 過去分詞作後置定語
    Growth has been set back by a graft scandal involving procurement for flood relief measures.
    句中的 "involving procurement for flood relief measures" 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "a graft scandal"（貪腐醜聞），相當於形容詞子句 "which involves..." 的簡化。
  • 關係代名詞子句（限定與非限定用法）
    ...and AI is as much a threat as an opportunity to the Philippines’ outsourcing industry, which has been a crucial source of jobs and growth in recent decades.
    句尾的 "which has been a crucial source..." 是一個非限定關係子句（前面有逗號），用來補充說明先行詞 "the Philippines’ outsourcing industry" 的背景資訊。
  • 假設語氣（過去式表達與現在事實相反）
    If you want to include counterfactuals, though here it is mostly declarative, consider: 'Imposing capital controls as Malaysia did in 1998 would be unwise.'
    在此句中使用 "would be unwise" 構成假設語氣的結 果子句，表達「若實施資本管制，將會是不明智的」之假設性推論。
