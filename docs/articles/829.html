「健康幣」計畫將於下個月啟動

Health coin program to begin next month

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • launch v. 啟動，發起，推出
    The government’s “health coin” program launches next month, but points would not be redeemable until December...
    政府的「健康幣」計畫將於下個月啟動，但點數要到12月才能兌換...
  • redeemable adj. 可兌換的，可換取現金或商品的
    ...but points would not be redeemable until December, as the agency seeks more partners to expand the program’s redemption options...
    ...但點數要到12月才能兌換，因為該機構正在尋找更多合作夥伴以擴大計畫的兌換選項...
  • partner n. 合作夥伴，合夥人
    ...as the agency seeks more partners to expand the program’s redemption options, Health Promotion Administration (HPA) Director-General Shen Ching-fen (沈靜芬) said yesterday.
    ...因為該機構正在尋找更多合作夥伴以擴大計畫的兌換選項，國民健康署署長沈靜芬昨天表示。
  • expand v. 擴大，擴展
    The HPA is expanding the program to offer more choices across different sectors and venues to avoid a situation in which popular rewards run out, she said.
    她表示，國健署正在擴大該計畫，以便在不同產業和場所提供更多選擇，藉此避免熱門獎勵兌換一空的狀況。
  • option n. 選擇，選項
    The health coin program would have multiple partners offering similar rewards, so people are not limited to a single choice, she said.
    她表示，健康幣計畫將有多個合作夥伴提供類似的獎勵，因此民眾不會侷限於單一選擇。
  • briefing n. 簡報會，說明會
    The HPA yesterday held its first briefing for businesses interested in joining the program, Shen said.
    沈靜芬表示，國健署昨天為有意加入該計畫的企業舉辦了首場說明會。
  • eligible adj. 有資格的，具備條件的
    The briefing would explain how businesses can join the program, who is eligible and what to do to connect to the health coin system, she said...
    她說，該說明會將解釋企業如何加入計畫、誰符合資格以及如何連接至健康幣系統...
  • sector n. 產業，部門
    The HPA is expanding the program to offer more choices across different sectors and venues...
    國健署正在擴大該計畫，以便在不同產業和場所提供更多選擇...
  • available adj. 可取得的，可買到的，有空的
    ...where vouchers for PX Mart were quickly redeemed, but vouchers from other convenience store chains were still available.
    ...當時全聯福利中心的兌換券很快就被兌換完畢，但其他便利商店連鎖店的兌換券仍然可以取得。
  • phase n. 階段，時期
    Items that could be redeemed with the coins have not yet been announced, Shen said, adding that businesses can choose to join in the first or second phase.
    沈靜芬表示，可以用健康幣兌換的物品尚未公布，並補充說企業可以選擇在第一階段或第二階段加入。
  • participating adj. 參與的
    After yesterday’s briefing, the HPA would start checking the products and services offered by participating businesses and convene a panel of experts to review them, she said.
    她表示，在昨天的說明會之後，國健署將開始審查參與企業所提供的產品和服務，並召集專家小組進行評估。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞當後置修飾語（形容詞片語）
    The HPA yesterday held its first briefing for businesses interested in joining the program, Shen said.
    句中的「interested in joining the program」是由過去分詞開頭的形容詞片語，用來後置修飾前面的名詞「businesses」（企業）。完整結構為「businesses (that are) interested in...」，省略了關代與be動詞，是多益閱讀測驗中常見的精簡句構。
  • 表條件或時間的副詞子句（when 子句）
    People can start earning the coins by engaging in healthy behaviors starting next month, when the program launches...
  • 假設語氣（過去未來式 would）
    The health coin program would have multiple partners offering similar rewards, so people are not limited to a single choice, she said.
