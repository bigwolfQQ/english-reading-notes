單字解析

  • perform v. 表演，演出
    Team Taiwan cheerleaders perform during the opening ceremony of the U18 Asian Baseball Championship at Tianmu Baseball Stadium in Taipei yesterday.
    台灣代表隊啦啦隊員昨天在台北天母棒球場舉行的U18亞洲青棒錦標賽開幕典禮上表演。
  • ceremony n. 典禮，儀式
    Team Taiwan cheerleaders perform during the opening ceremony of the U18 Asian Baseball Championship at Tianmu Baseball Stadium in Taipei yesterday.
    台灣代表隊啦啦隊員昨天在台北天母棒球場舉行的U18亞洲青棒錦標賽開幕典禮上表演。
  • lose v. 輸掉，失敗
    Team Taiwan lost 2-0 to South Korea in its opening game.
    台灣代表隊在首場比賽中以0比2不敵南韓。

文法重點

  • 介系詞片語表示時間與地點
    Team Taiwan cheerleaders perform during the opening ceremony of the U18 Asian Baseball Championship at Tianmu Baseball Stadium in Taipei yesterday.
    句中使用「during the opening ceremony...」表達在...期間（時間），以及「at Tianmu Baseball Stadium in Taipei」表達在台北天母棒球場（地點），多益考試常在介系詞題型（如 during vs. while 或 at vs. in）中測驗這類用法。
