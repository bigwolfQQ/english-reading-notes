漫步台灣時光：基隆曾是台灣最大韓國社群聚集地

Taiwan in Time: When Keelung hosted Taiwan’s largest Korean community

單字解析

  • repatriate v. 遣返（將人員遣送回原籍國）
    However, only one ship arrived to repatriate civilians, leaving many, including Hwang’s in-laws, stranded in Keelung.
    然而，只有一艘船抵達來遣返平民，導致許多人（包括黃泰任的公婆）滯留在基隆。
  • stranded adj. 滯留的、受困的
    However, only one ship arrived to repatriate civilians, leaving many, including Hwang’s in-laws, stranded in Keelung.
    然而，只有一艘船抵達來遣返平民，導致許多人（包括黃泰任的公婆）滯留在基隆。
  • account for phr. v. 佔……（比例）、說明、解釋
    A 1947 register shows 197 Koreans in Keelung, accounting for more than half of the 358 recorded in Taiwan.
    一份1947年的戶籍登記顯示，基隆有197名韓國人，佔全台記錄358人的一半以上。
  • surpass v. 超越、勝過
    In 1935, Keelung’s Korean population reached 358, surpassing Taipei and Kaohsiung.
    1935年，基隆的韓國人口達到358人，超越了台北和高雄。
  • recruitment n. 招募、吸收（新成員等）
    This led to the recruitment of more Korean fishermen, especially to Keelung.
    這導致了更多韓國漁民被招募，特別是來到基隆。
  • preferential adj. 優先的、優惠的
    Along with those who chose to stay, many left behind settled in Keelung’s Shengli Alley and found work in the fishing industry, where they initially received preferential treatment from the government because of the pressing need to keep the facilities vacated by the Japanese operating.
    除了那些選擇留下的人之外，許多被遺留下來的人在基隆的勝利巷定居，並在漁業找到工作。由於當時急需保持被日本人騰空設施的運作，他們最初受到政府的優待。
  • be involved with phr. 參與、與……有關聯
    Researcher Yoshihisa Amae writes in “Becoming Korean: Decolonization, repatriation and the everyday lives of ethnic Koreans in postcolonial Taiwan” that 173 out of 197 Korean residents of Keelung were involved with the Taiwan Fishery Corporation...
    研究員天繪義久在《成為韓國人：後殖民台灣的去殖民化、遣返與朝鮮族群的日常生活》一文中寫道，基隆197名韓國居民中有173人參與了台灣水產公司...
  • prospect n. 前景、前途、期望
    Another group that stayed were sex workers who were struggling financially and unsure about their prospects in Korea.
    另一群留下來的人是那些生活財務困難、對在韓國的前景感到不確定的性工作者。
  • institution n. 機構、習俗、體制
    Taiwan’s first Korean Christian church was established in Keelung in 1950, serving as an key community institution.
    台灣第一座韓國基督教會於1950年在基隆成立，成為一個關鍵的社區機構。
  • converge v. 匯聚、集中
    The indoor and outdoor spaces converge in the center channel, which focuses on Hwang’s hair salon and its partially outdoor space.
    室內與室外空間在中央頻道中匯聚，該頻道聚焦於黃泰任的美髮沙龍及其半戶外空間。
  • retain v. 保留、保持
    Hwang has retained her Korean citizenship, meaning she doesn’t receive social welfare or elderly benefits in Taiwan.
    黃泰任保留了韓國國籍，這意味著她在台灣無法領取社會福利或老人津貼。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作後位修飾
    Hwang Tae-im’s in-laws were among the many Korean families that congregated in Keelung after World War II, hoping to board a ship home.
    句尾的 “hoping to board a ship home” 是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明主句主語（Hwang Tae-im’s in-laws）在當時的伴隨狀態或心態（懷著……的希望），相當於副詞子句 “while they hoped to board a ship home” 的簡化。
  • 獨立分詞構句（Absolute Nominative Construction）
    However, only one ship arrived to repatriate civilians, leaving many, including Hwang’s in-laws, stranded in Keelung.
    句尾的 “leaving many... stranded in Keelung” 是分詞構句，用來表達前一個動作所造成的自然結果（連續發生的結果）。“leaving” 的邏輯主語是前面整件事，表示「結果使得許多人滯留」。
  • 關代子句（限定與非限定用法）
    It is the 13th episode of his “Reality Sandwich” documentary series, which has evolved from free-form conversations with artists around the question “What is Reality Sandwich?” to exploring the global Korean diaspora in its past three episodes.
    逗號之後的 “which has evolved from...” 是非限定關係代間子句（Non-defining relative clause），前面加逗號代表補充說明前面的先行詞（his “Reality Sandwich” documentary series），不能省略，且不能用 “that” 代替。
  • 過去分詞作後位修飾
    Along with those who chose to stay, many left behind settled in Keelung’s Shengli Alley...
    片語 “left behind” 是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的代名詞 “many”（指許多被留下的人）。這裡省略了 “who were”，還原原本貌應為 “many (who were) left behind”，過去分詞表示被動與完成的概念。
