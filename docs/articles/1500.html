台灣主要城市舉行國慶升旗典禮

Major cities in Taiwan hold flag-raising ceremonies for National Day

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • ceremony n. 典禮，儀式
    Major cities around Taiwan held flag-raising ceremonies Saturday morning to celebrate the 115th National Day of the Republic of China...
    台灣各地主要城市於週六上午舉行升旗典禮，以慶祝中華民國115年國慶……
  • consecutive adj. 連續的，不間斷的
    Taiwan will hold local elections on Nov. 28, and mayors who have served two consecutive four-year terms cannot run again...
    台灣將於11月28日舉行地方選舉，已連任兩屆四年的市長無法再次參選……
  • successor n. 接任者，繼任者
    ...and their successors take office on Dec. 25.
    ……他們的接任者將於12月25日就職。
  • preside over phr. v. 主持，領導（會議、典禮等）
    In Taichung, Mayor Lu Shiow-yen (盧秀燕) presided over a ceremony at which a drone carrying the national flag flew over the plaza...
    在台中，市長盧秀燕主持了一場典禮，一架懸掛國歌的無人機飛過廣場上空……
  • administration n. 行政機關，管理，政府
    Lu, who is completing her second and final term, said she was proud of her administration's achievements...
    正值第二個也是最後一個任期屆滿的盧秀燕表示，她對其行政團隊的政績感到驕傲……
  • emphasize v. 強調，重視
    As mayor, she said, she had always emphasized cooperation between the city government and the city council to keep public works projects on track.
    她表示，身為市長，她一直強調市政府與市議會之間的合作，以確保公共工程項目順利進行。
  • prosper v. 繁榮，興旺
    Whoever succeeds her should ensure that the city keeps prospering and its residents keep living well, Lu said.
    盧秀燕說，無論誰接替她，都應確保城市持續繁榮、居民安居樂業。
  • attendee n. 出席者，參加者
    In Taipei, Mayor Chiang Wan-an (蔣萬安), who is seeking a second term in the November elections, led attendees in singing the ROC national anthem in front of city hall.
    在台北，正爭取在11月選舉中連任的市長蔣萬安帶領與會者在市政府前合唱中華民國國歌。
  • claim v. 索取，認領，聲稱
    The Taipei City government also set up an entertainment area where visitors who took photos at designated spots... could claim free ice cream.
    台北市政府也設置了一個娛樂區，在指定地點拍照的遊客……可以兌換免費冰淇淋。
  • revenue n. 收益，歲入，營業收入
    Kaohsiung has also benefited from increased revenue from more concerts held in the city, he said...
    他表示，高雄也受惠於該市舉辦更多演唱會所帶來的收益增長……
  • generate v. 產生，引起，發電
    The events attracted more than 5 million concertgoers and generated NT$18.9 billion in revenue.
    這些活動吸引了超過500萬名演唱會觀眾，並產生了新台幣189億元的營收。

文法重點

  • 非限定性關係代名詞子句（使用逗號補充說明）
    In Taipei, Mayor Chiang Wan-an (蔣萬安), who is seeking a second term in the November elections, led attendees in singing the ROC national anthem in front of city hall.
    句中的「who is seeking a second term in the November elections」為非限定性關係子句，前方帶有逗號，用來補充說明主詞 Mayor Chiang Wan-an 的額外資訊（正在尋求連任），若將此子句刪除，主句語意依然完整。
  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾
    In Taichung, Mayor Lu Shiow-yen (盧秀燕) presided over a ceremony at which a drone carrying the national flag flew over the plaza in front of city hall...
    句中的「carrying the national flag」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 drone（無人機），相當於形容詞子句「which carried the national flag」，表達主動意味（無人機攜帶著國旗）。
  • 獨立分詞構句表附帶狀況或結果
    The project is expected to improve transportation by better connecting urban areas on both sides of the railway and to spur the city's development, he said.
    句中的「by better connecting...」使用了介系詞 by 搭配動名詞連接詞組，用來表達「透過……方式」達成某個結果，是商業與新聞英文中非常實用的表達方式。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    Most of the city's government offices and services have been concentrated in Banqiao, in southwestern New Taipei, Hou said.
    句中的「concentrated in Banqiao」是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞短語「Most of the city's government offices and services」，帶有被動意味（辦公室與服務被集中在板橋）。
