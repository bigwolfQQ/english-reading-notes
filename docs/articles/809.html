文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Postmodifier)
    The Chiayi County Agriculture Department on Wednesday made a special pitch for locally raised crocodile meat, as it touted county aquaculture products as suitable Mid-Autumn Festival barbecue fare.
    句中的 "locally raised" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾其後的名詞 "crocodile meat"（被當地飼養的鱷魚肉）。這種結構在英文新聞中非常常見，能簡化子句並使句子更緊湊。
  • 同位語 (Appositive)
    According to Chang, Taiwan's largest concentration of "crocodile," actually spectacled caiman, farms is located in Yizhu Township.
  • 動名詞片語作受詞 (Gerund Phrase as Object)
    Restaurants have devised several ways to prepare crocodile, he said, including grilling, deep-frying, boiling, baking, and stewing with traditional Chinese medicine ingredients.
    介系詞 "including" 後方接了一連串的動名詞（grilling, deep-frying, boiling...）作為其受詞，用來列舉烹調鱷魚的方法。
  • 附帶狀況或原因的連接詞 as (Conjunction 'as')
    ...as it touted county aquaculture products as suitable Mid-Autumn Festival barbecue fare.
    在此處的連接詞 "as" 引導一個原因子句，意思是「因為／隨著它大力宣稱……」，用來連接主句的動作與其背後的理由。
