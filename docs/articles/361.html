魁北克集團黨魁支持臺灣的全球角色與更緊密的聯繫

Bloc Québécois leader backs Taiwan's global role, stronger ties

單字解析

  • participation n. 參與，參加
    Blanchet on Tuesday voiced support for Taiwan's participation in international organizations
    Blanchet 週二表態支持臺灣參與國際組織
  • economic ties n. phr. 經濟聯繫，經貿關係
    and called for closer economic ties during a meeting with President Lai Ching-te (賴清德) in Taipei.
    並在台北與總統賴清德會面時，呼籲建立更緊密的經貿聯繫。
  • advocate v. 主張，擁護，提倡
    The Bloc Québécois is a federal political party in Canada that advocates Quebec sovereignty.
    魁北克集團是加拿大的一個聯邦政黨，主張魁北克主權。
  • status n. 身分，地位
    for Taiwan to participate in international organizations as a full member or under a status determined by Taiwan itself.
    臺灣以正式成員身分或以臺灣自身決定的地位參與國際組織
  • regarding prep. 關於，對於
    Regarding the Montreal-based International Civil Aviation Organization, Blanchet said...
    關於總部位於蒙特婁的國際民用航空組織，Blanchet 表示...
  • cooperation n. 合作
    Turning to broader ties, Blanchet said economic cooperation should be at the "core" of relations...
    談到更廣泛的關係，Blanchet 表示，經濟合作應當成為...關係的「核心」。
  • re-establishment n. 重新建立，復設
    Blanchet also said his party would strongly support the re-establishment of a Quebec government office in Taipei...
    Blanchet 還表示，如果未來魁北克政府考慮重新設立駐台北辦事處，他的政黨將強力支持重建...
  • exchanges n. 交流，交換
    noting the office had previously promoted economic and cultural exchanges.
    並指出該辦事處過去曾促進經濟與文化交流。
  • partners n. 夥伴
    He said Quebec and Canada "have been and will always be partners, allies and friends,"
    他表示，魁北克和加拿大「過去一直是、未來也永遠是夥伴、盟友和朋友」
  • delegation n. 代表團
    the visit marked the first delegation organized by the Bloc Québécois to Taiwan...
    此次訪問是魁北克集團組團訪台的第一個代表團...
  • commitment n. 承諾，投入
    shared a commitment to their land, culture, language and way of life
    對土地、文化、語言和生活方式有著共同的承諾
  • deepen v. 加深，深化
    Looking ahead, Lai said Taiwan and Quebec could deepen cooperation in artificial intelligence...
    展望未來，賴總統表示，臺灣與魁北克可以在...深化合作。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    In remarks delivered in French at the Presidential Office, Blanchet said...
    句中的「delivered in French at the Presidential Office」是由過去分詞片語構成，用來修飾前面的名詞 remarks（談話）。這是一種常見於新聞英語的精簡寫法，相當於形容詞子句「which were delivered...」。
  • 假設語氣（與現在事實相反） (Subjunctive Mood)
    ...saying the latter would remain "incomplete" as long as Taiwan was not a member.
    此處表達在假設情況（臺灣不是成員）下的結果。雖然句型中使用過去式 was，但表達的是對當前現狀的一種條件假設，常搭配情態動詞 would 使用。
  • 現在分詞作附加說明 (Present Participle for Supplementary Information)
    ...noting the office had previously promoted economic and cultural exchanges.
    句尾的「noting the office had...」是以現在分詞開頭的分詞構句，用來補充說明前面子句所提及的事項或提供額外的背景資訊，中文通常可譯為「並指出……」。
  • 過去分詞片語作形容詞用法 (Past Participle as Adjective)
    He referred to a Trade Cooperation Framework Arrangement finalized by Taiwan and Canada in 2025...
    句中的「finalized by Taiwan and Canada in 2025」是過去分詞片語，修飾前方名詞 Trade Cooperation Framework Arrangement（貿易合作架構協定），表示該協定是「被」完成的，省略了「which was」。
