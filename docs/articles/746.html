外交部表示，無人機學院正在推進中

Drone academy advancing, MOFA says

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • register v. 登記、註冊
    The privately funded Global UAV Academy (GUAVA) is to be registered as a juridical person next week and its offices are to be set up before the end of this year, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) said yesterday.
    外交部昨天表示，民間資助的「全球無人機學院」（GUAVA）將於下週登記為法人，其辦公室也將在今年底前設立。
  • establishment n. 建立、創立、機構
    The ministry is assisting in the establishment of GUAVA, which aims to help Taiwan’s diplomatic allies build drone resilience, with the initial phase focusing on pilot training, MOFA said in a policy report submitted to the Legislative Yuan before it starts its next session on Sept. 29.
    外交部在提交給立法院9月29日開議前的政策報告中表示，該部正協助成立GUAVA，旨在協助台灣的友邦建立無人機韌性，初期階段專注於飛行員培訓。
  • phase n. 階段、時期
    The focus of GUAVA in its first phase would be training drone pilots, the report said.
    報告指出，GUAVA第一階段的重點將是培訓無人機飛行員。
  • collaborate v. 合作、協力
    As the ministry’s drone collaboration with like-minded countries needs to go through non-governmental channels, MOFA is assisting in GUAVA’s establishment, said Department of NGO International Affairs Director-General Chiang Zhen-wei (江振瑋), who is head of the ministry’s drone diplomacy task force.
    身兼外交部無人機外交工作小組負責人的非政府組織國際事務會司長江振瑋表示，由於該部與理念相近國家的無人機合作需要透過非官方管道進行，因此外交部正協助GUAVA的成立。
  • personnel n. 人員、員工
    Management personnel would be recommended and elected by the private sector, not designated by the ministry or the Industrial Technology Research Institute, he said.
    他說，管理人員將由民間部門推薦與選出，並非由外交部或工業技術研究院指定。
  • sector n. 部門、行業
    Management personnel would be recommended and elected by the private sector, not designated by the ministry or the Industrial Technology Research Institute, he said.
    他說，管理人員將由民間部門推薦與選出，並非由外交部或工業技術研究院指定。
  • depend on phr. v. 依賴、取決於
    GUAVA is to set up its offices in Taipei and Chiayi County before the end of this year, while the opening of overseas offices would depend on the needs of countries working with the organization, Chiang said.
    江振瑋表示，GUAVA將於今年底前在台北市和嘉義縣設立辦公室，而海外辦公室的開設則將取決于與該組織合作國家的需求。
  • elaborate v. 詳述、詳細說明
    Asked to elaborate, Chiang said that during this year’s Pacific Islands Forum Leaders Meeting, the government signed memoranda of understanding with Palau, the Marshall Islands and Tuvalu, and Taiwan plans to begin training pilots starting with the Pacific allies from late this year or early next year.
    在被要求詳細說明時，江振瑋表示，在今年的太平洋島國論壇領袖會議期間，政府已與帛琉、馬紹爾群島和吐瓦魯簽署了備忘錄，台灣計畫從今年底或明年初開始，率先為太平洋友邦培訓飛行員。
  • estimate v. 估計、估算
    It is estimated that the academy can train up to 30 pilots in Taiwan at the same time, he said.
    他說，據估計，該學院同時最多可在台灣培訓30名飛行員。
  • enhance v. 提高、增加、增強
    Minister of Foreign Affairs Lin Chia-lung (林佳龍) began promoting “drone diplomacy,” a spin on the concept of “chip diplomacy,” and leveraging Taiwan’s strengths in key industries to enhance its diplomatic influence, last year.
    外交部長林佳龍去年開始推動「無人機外交」（這是「晶片外交」概念的衍生），並利用台灣在關鍵產業的優勢來提升外交影響力。
  • confirm v. 確認、證實
    Separately, Lin yesterday confirmed that the ministry is preparing to open a representative office in Cebu, Philippines.
    另一方面，林佳龍昨天證實，外交部正準備在菲律賓宿霧開設代表處。
  • consideration n. 考慮、考量
    With only days remaining before the meeting, any arms sales package for Taiwan would still have to go through the US congressional review process and remains under consideration, he said.
    他說，距離會晤僅剩數日，任何對台軍售包裹案仍必須經過美國國會審查程序，目前仍在考慮中。

文法重點

  • be to + 原形動詞（表示計畫、安排或即將發生的官方行程）
    The privately funded Global UAV Academy (GUAVA) is to be registered as a juridical person next week and its offices are to be set up before the end of this year...
    在新聞英語與正式公文寫作中，「be to + V」常被用來表示已經安排好、預定要執行的官方計畫或命令，相當於 is scheduled to 或 is going to。
  • 分詞構句（表附帶狀況或結果）
    ...with the initial phase focusing on pilot training, MOFA said in a policy report submitted to the Legislative Yuan before it starts its next session on Sept. 29.
    句中的「with + 受詞 + 分詞 (focusing)」形成獨立分詞構句，用來補充說明前面子句的背景或附帶狀況（「隨著/以...為主」）。另外後方的「submitted to...」則是過去分詞片語作後置定語，修飾前面的 policy report。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句（補充說明）
    The office, which would be Taiwan’s second in the country after Manila, would serve travelers and businesspeople in central Philippines...
    句中的「which would be...」前方帶有逗號，屬於非限定關係子句，對前面的先行詞「The office」提供額外的補充資訊。若將此子句拿掉，句子的主要意思依然完整。
  • 虛主詞 It is estimated that...
    It is estimated that the academy can train up to 30 pilots in Taiwan at the same time, he said.
    「It is estimated that...」是多益考試與新聞英文中非常常見的句型，用來表達客觀的推估數據。其中 It 是形式主詞，真正的受詞是 that 引導的名詞子句。
