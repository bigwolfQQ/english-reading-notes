Maduro’s wife seeks release from detention in the US

單字解析

  • release n. / v. 釋放，解除
    ...has requested to be released from New York detention and placed under house arrest...
    ...已要求從紐約拘留所釋放並改為居家軟禁...
  • detention n. 拘留，扣留
    ...has requested to be released from New York detention and placed under house arrest...
    ...已要求從紐約拘留所釋放並改為居家軟禁...
  • attorney n. 律師
    ...her attorney Mark Donnelly said in a document dated Wednesday.
    ...她的律師馬克·唐納利在週三的一份文件中表示。
  • document n. 文件，公文
    ...her attorney Mark Donnelly said in a document dated Wednesday.
    ...她的律師馬克·唐納利在週三的一份文件中表示。
  • defense n. 防禦，辯護
    ...according to her defense team.
    ...根據她的辯護團隊指出。
  • proper adj. 合適的，恰當的
    Arguing that her living conditions in detention do not allow her to receive the proper medical care...
    主張她在拘留所的居住條件無法讓她獲得適當的醫療照顧...
  • condition n. 條件，狀況
    ...the lawyer is requesting she be placed under house arrest in the US under restrictive conditions...
    ...因此要求將她安置在美國進行居家軟禁，並附加限制性條件...
  • confiscation n. 沒收，充公
    ...including 24-hour armed surveillance, an electronic ankle bracelet monitor and confiscation of her passport.
    ...包括24小時武裝監控、電子腳鐐監控以及沒收護照。
  • charge n. 指控，控告
    Both deny the charges...
    兩人均否認這些指控...

文法重點

  • 分詞構句表原因 (Present Participle as Cause)
    Arguing that her living conditions in detention do not allow her to receive the proper medical care, the lawyer is requesting she be placed under house arrest...
    句首的「Arguing...」是由現在分詞構成的分詞構句，用來補充說明律師提出請求的原因（相當於 Because the lawyer argues...）。
  • 要求與建議動詞後的假設性原形動詞 (Subjunctive Mood)
    ...the lawyer is requesting she be placed under house arrest in the US under restrictive conditions...
    當表示要求（request）、建議、命令等動詞出現時，其後的受詞子句（that 子句）通常省略 should，動詞直接使用原形（在此句中為 be placed，而非 is placed）。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    ...have been detained since Jan. 3.
    句型使用「have been + 過去分詞（detained）」構成現在完成式的被動語態，強調主詞（They 指代 Maduro 與 Flores）自 1 月 3 日以來持續處於被拘留的狀態。
