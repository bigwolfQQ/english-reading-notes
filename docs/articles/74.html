文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 / 形容詞化
    Amnesty International Taiwan on Thursday condemned Chinese authorities for questioning a Taiwanese national for six hours over his previous work for the human rights organization, saying support for human rights should not be treated as grounds for restricting a person's freedom.
    句尾的「saying...」是由現在分詞片語引導的補充說明，用來表達主句動作發生時同時進行的行為或結果（相當於 and said...），在新聞英文中非常常見，用以簡潔地帶出相關的言論或聲明。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The statement came a day after China's Taiwan Affairs Office (TAO) confirmed the questioning and described Amnesty International as a "pawn of international anti-China forces."
    句中的「the questioning」是由動詞 question 名詞化而來，而在此之前文章也多次使用被動語態（如 be questioned, be reported, be scrutinized）來強調承受動作的對象，多益考試常考被動語態的動詞搭配及語意理解。
  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense)
    The controversy began last Saturday, when the Taiwanese man's father wrote on social media platform Threads that his son had been questioned for about six hours after arriving in Shanghai earlier that day.
    句中的「had been questioned」使用過去完成式（had + p.p.），用來表示在過去某個時間點（父親發文/爭議開始）之前已經發生的動作（兒子被盤問），清楚交代事件發生的時間先後順序。
  • 連接詞 although 引導的讓步子句
    Taiwan's Mainland Affairs Council (MAC) said Sunday that although the case had not been reported to the government, the account was consistent with similar cases it had previously documented.
    「although the case had not been reported to the government」是一個由 although引導的副詞子句，用來表示「雖然/儘管」的讓步語氣，多益考試常在克漏字中測驗 although 與 despite/in spite of 的詞性差異（although 後接子句，despite 後接名詞）。
