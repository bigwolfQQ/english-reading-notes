英國表示福克蘭群島「屬於英國」，米雷伊則堅持聲索主權

UK says Falklands ‘are British,’ as Milei asserts claim

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • assert v. 堅稱；主張；肯定地說
    The UK yesterday asserted that the Falkland Islands “are British” after Argentine President Javier Milei said the “winds of change” favored his country’s claim to the archipelago, citing Washington’s readiness to review its neutrality in the dispute.
  • archipelago n. 群島；多島的海區
    The UK yesterday asserted that the Falkland Islands “are British” after Argentine President Javier Milei said the “winds of change” favored his country’s claim to the archipelago, citing Washington’s readiness to review its neutrality in the dispute.
  • wade into phr v. 強行干預；強行捲入（爭端、討論）
    The fate of the islands hit headlines again in recent days after US President Donald Trump threatened to wade into the dispute, and a British-Israeli oil project near the islands caused anger in Argentina.
  • stand idly by idiom 袖手旁觀；坐視不管
    In an address on national TV, Milei on Thursday said that Argentina would not “stand idly by” in the face of the oil project and announced sanctions on firms linked to oil extraction “in Argentine territory under British occupation.”
  • allude to phr v. 暗指；間接提到
    “There are winds of change in the world that are favorable to our claim,” he said, alluding to Trump’s threat to withdraw support for Britain’s sovereignty.
  • emotive adj. 激起強烈情感的；敏感的
    Control of the Falklands is a highly emotive issue in Argentina, even though the islanders themselves voted overwhelmingly in 2013 to remain British.
  • unshakeable adj. 不可搖撼的；堅定不移的
    “Our commitment to the Falklands is absolute and unshakeable,” he added.
  • desist from phr v. 停止；中止（某行為）
    Milei said the project violated a UN resolution calling on both sides to desist from unilateral actions in the islands.
  • behind someone's back idiom 背著某人；私底下（通常指不誠實地）
    He added that companies involved in Falklands’ projects “behind the back of the Argentine government” would be barred from operating in Argentine territory.
  • plummet v. 暴跌；急劇下滑
    His address came as his approval ratings have plummeted, just over a year before he seeks re-election.
  • belt-tightening n. 緊縮開支；勒緊腰帶（過苦日子）
    Polls show only about 34 percent of Argentines supporting his drastic belt-tightening and deregulation policies.

文法重點

  • 現在分詞短語作補充說明 (Participle Phrase)
    The UK yesterday asserted that the Falkland Islands “are British” after Argentine President Javier Milei said the “winds of change” favored his country’s claim to the archipelago, citing Washington’s readiness to review its neutrality in the dispute.
    句末的 `citing...` 為現在分詞短語（分詞構句），邏輯主語是前面的發言者 Javier Milei。此處用來補充說明 Milei 作出「改變之風有利於阿根廷」此一口號的依據與具體細節（引述華府的態度）。
  • 同位語與關係子句結合 (Appositive with Relative Clause)
    Britain and Argentina fought a 10-week war in 1982 over the South Atlantic islands, a British overseas territory that Argentines call Las Malvinas.
    句中的 `a British overseas territory` 為同位語，用來直接補充說明前方的受詞 `the South Atlantic islands`（南大西洋群島）。同位語後面再接續以關係代名詞 `that` 引導的限定關係子句（`that Argentines call Las Malvinas`），修飾該領地在阿根廷的稱呼。
  • 分詞構句表示結果 (Participle Phrase Expressing Result)
    The Falklands War began in April 1982 when Argentine forces invaded the islands ruled by Britain since the 19th century, prompting then-British prime minister Margaret Thatcher to send a naval task force to reclaim them.
    句末的 `prompting...` 是由現在分詞引導的分詞構句，修飾前面整句所發生的事件（阿根廷軍隊入侵）。這種結構相當於非限定關係子句 `..., which prompted then-British prime minister...`，用來表達「該事件所帶來的直接結果」。
  • 條件句與未來進行式 (Conditional Sentence with Future Continuous)
    “If we allow it, we will be creating an incentive for the British government to deepen the occupation of the islands and the exploitation of our resources,” he said.
    這是一個第一型條件句（表示對未來的真實假設）。條件子句使用現在式（`If we allow it`），主要子句使用未來進行式（`we will be creating...`），用來強調如果某個行動繼續發生，未來將會「持續帶來某種後果或後續影響」。
  • 時間與情境連接詞 as (Adverbial Clause with 'as')
    His address came as his approval ratings have plummeted, just over a year before he seeks re-election.
    連接詞 `as` 在此引導時間與背景狀語子句（相當於 when 或 at the same time that），說明「發表電視演說」此一主要動作發生的情境背景（即「在其支持率急劇下滑之際」）。
