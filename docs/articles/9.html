英國表示福克蘭群島「屬於英國」，因米雷相重申主權聲索

UK says Falklands ‘are British,’ as Milei asserts claim

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • assert v. 堅稱、主張、斷言
    The UK yesterday asserted that the Falkland Islands “are British” after Argentine President Javier Milei said the “winds of change” favored his country’s claim to the archipelago
    英國昨天堅稱福克蘭群島「屬於英國」，此前阿根廷總統 Javier Milei 表示「改變之風」有利於該國對這群島嶼的主權聲索
  • dispute n. 爭端、爭議
    The fate of the islands hit headlines again in recent days after US President Donald Trump threatened to wade into the dispute
    在美國總統唐納·川普揚言介入此爭端後，這些島嶼的命運近日再次登上新聞頭條
  • sanction n. 制裁
    In an address on national TV, Milei on Thursday said that Argentina would not “stand idly by” in the face of the oil project and announced sanctions on firms linked to oil extraction
    米雷週四在全國電視演講中表示，面對該石油開發案，阿根廷不會「袖手旁觀」，並宣布對涉及石油開採的企業實施制裁
  • sovereignty n. 主權
    There are winds of change in the world that are favorable to our claim, he said, alluding to Trump’s threat to withdraw support for Britain’s sovereignty.
    他說：「世界上的改變之風正有利於我們的主權聲索。」他暗示川普威脅撤回對英國主權的支持。
  • commitment n. 承諾、投入
    Our commitment to the Falklands is absolute and unshakeable, he added.
    他補充說：「我們對福克蘭群島的承諾是絕對且動搖不得的。」
  • reclaim v. 收復、取回、回收
    The Falklands War began in April 1982 when Argentine forces invaded the islands ruled by Britain since the 19th century, prompting then-British prime minister Margaret Thatcher to send a naval task force to reclaim them.
    福克蘭群島戰爭始於 1982 年 4 月，當時阿根廷軍隊入侵了自 19世紀以來由英國統治的群島，促使時任英國首相柴契爾夫人派遣海軍特遣艦隊前去收復。
  • lawsuit n. 訴訟、官司
    On Tuesday, Argentine Falklands War veterans and environmental lawyers filed a lawsuit to halt the oil project led by the British company Rockhopper and the Israeli company Navitas.
    週二，阿根廷福克蘭群島戰爭 veterans 和環境律師提起訴訟，要求阻止由英國公司 Rockhopper 和以色列公司 Navitas 主導的石油開發計畫。
  • incentive n. 誘因、鼓勵、獎勵
    If we allow it, we will be creating an incentive for the British government to deepen the occupation of the islands and the exploitation of our resources
    如果我們允許這樣做，我們將為英國政府加深對島嶼的佔領以及對我們資源的開發創造誘因
  • involve v. 涉及、參與
    He added that companies involved in Falklands’ projects “behind the back of the Argentine government” would be barred from operating in Argentine territory.
    他補充說，那些參與福克蘭群島計畫的公司將被禁止在阿根廷領土上營運。
  • bar v. 禁止、阻攔
    companies involved in Falklands’ projects “behind the back of the Argentine government” would be barred from operating in Argentine territory.
    參與福克蘭群島計畫的公司將被禁止在阿根廷領土上營運。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況或結果
    The UK yesterday asserted that the Falkland Islands “are British” after Argentine President Javier Milei said the “winds of change” favored his country’s claim to the archipelago, citing Washington’s readiness to review its neutrality in the dispute.
    句尾的「citing...」為現在分詞構句，主動修飾前方的整個主句，表示伴隨發生的動作或結果，意指「同時也提及了華盛頓準備……」。這種結構在商業及新聞英文中非常常見，用以簡化子句並串聯相關資訊。
  • 現在分詞作後位修飾語
    The Falklands War began in April 1982 when Argentine forces invaded the islands ruled by Britain since the 19th century...
    句中的「ruled by Britain since the 19th century」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前方的名詞「the islands（群島）」。其完整結構為「which were ruled by Britain...」，省略了關代與be動詞，使句子更為精煉。
  • 過去分詞片語作形容詞修飾
    He added that companies involved in Falklands’ projects “behind the back of the Argentine government” would be barred from operating in Argentine territory.
    句中的「involved in Falklands’ projects」是過去分詞片語，用來後置修飾「companies」，相當於「companies that are involved in...」，在多益考試中常考此類分詞修飾語的用法。
