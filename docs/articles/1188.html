文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Just over 40 percent of those investigated for human trafficking-related crimes in Taiwan between 2021 and 2025 were indicted...
    句中的「investigated for human trafficking-related crimes...」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前方代名詞「those」（指 people），相當於關代子句「who were investigated...」，省略了「who were」以達到精簡新聞語氣的效果。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Of the 3,401 people investigated, 1,914 were alleged to have been involved in labor exploitation...
    新聞英語為了客觀陳述或強調承受動作的對象，經常大量使用被動語態。此處「were alleged to have been...」表示「被指稱...」，將焦點放在受調查的人身上。
  • 對等連接詞 while 表對比 (While Indicating Contrast)
    ...while the number of people indicted in organ-harvesting cases remained below 70 each year.
    連接詞「while」在此處用來連接兩個子句，表示「然而、而」，藉此在同一個句子中對比兩種不同的數據趨勢（例如性販運人數上升，而器官摘除起訴人數維持低檔）。
  • 介系詞片語置於句首作修飾與語意聚焦 (Prepositional Phrase at the Beginning for Focus)
    In the vast majority -- 95.9 percent -- of cases where indictments were not issued, insufficient evidence was the reason cited, the report said.
    將表範圍與情境的介系詞片語「In the vast majority... of cases」放在句首，先建立背景條件，隨後接上由「where」引導的關係子句修飾 cases，使長句的結構層次分明。
