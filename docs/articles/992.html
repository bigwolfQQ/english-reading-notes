Taiwanese man suspected of murdering Thai girlfriend detained

單字解析

  • suspect n. / v. 嫌疑犯；懷疑
    At a bail hearing on Saturday, the Taichung District Court approved prosecutors' request to detain the 43-year-old suspect...
    台中地方法院於週六的羈押庭上，批准檢方聲請收押該名43歲的嫌疑人...
  • prosecutor n. 檢察官
    A Taichung court has approved a request by prosecutors to formally detain a Taiwanese man...
    台中地方法院已批准檢方聲請，正式收押一名台灣男子...
  • detain v. 拘留，扣留，收押
    ...approved prosecutors' request to detain the 43-year-old suspect, surnamed Wu (吳), in light of the severity of the crime...
    ...鑑於犯罪的嚴重性，批准檢方聲請收押該名43歲姓吳的嫌疑人...
  • severity n. 嚴重性，嚴厲
    ...in light of the severity of the crime and the risk that he could flee or destroy evidence.
    ...考量犯罪的嚴重性以及他可能逃亡或滅證的風險。
  • evidence n. 證據，證物
    After gathering evidence, police and prosecutors found the 36-year-old woman's body during a search in Taichung...
    在蒐集證據後，檢警在台中搜索時發現了該名36歲女子的遺體...
  • coordinate v. 協調，配合
    The Taichung District Prosecutors Office said it received a tip-off from police in Taipei's Wanhua District on Friday evening, and coordinated with police to arrest Wu later that night.
    台中地檢署表示，週五傍晚接獲台北市萬華分局警方的線報，並於當天深夜協同警方將吳嫌逮捕歸案。
  • investigation n. 調查，偵查
    ...with initial results from the investigation indicating that she had been strangled to death.
    ...初步調查結果顯示她遭到勒斃。
  • schedule v. / n. 預定，安排；時間表
    The woman had been staying in a hotel in Taipei's Ximending area after arriving in Taiwan on Sept. 2, but failed to return to Thailand as scheduled on Sept. 14.
    該名女子於9月2日抵台後，一直住在台北市西門町地區的一家飯店，但未如期於9月14日返回泰國。
  • identify v. 辨認，識別
    After reviewing surveillance footage from near her hotel, police identified a man seen acting suspiciously as Wu.
    警方調閱該飯店附近的監視器畫面後，認出一名形跡可疑的男子即為吳嫌。
  • administrative adj. 行政的
    He placed the suitcase in the trunk of his car before driving to Taichung, where he worked in an administrative role at a technology company, police said.
    警方指出，他將行李箱放進汽車後車廂，隨後開車前往台中，他在那裡的一家科技公司擔任行政職務。
  • purchase v. / n. 購買，採購
    Local media reports that cited police sources claimed, however, that Wu had purchased the freezer and suitcase in early September...
    然而，當地媒體引述警方消息來源報導稱，吳嫌早在9月初就購買了冰櫃和行李箱...

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle phrase as postmodifier)
    A Taichung court has approved a request by prosecutors to formally detain a Taiwanese man suspected of killing his Thai girlfriend...
    句中的「suspected of killing...」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「a Taiwanese man」，相當於「who is suspected of killing...」的簡化寫法，能讓新聞句子更為緊湊。
  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense)
    ...initial results from the investigation indicating that she had been strangled to death.
    「had been strangled」使用了過去完成式的被動語態。因為「被勒斃」發生在過去調查（investigation, 過去式）更早之前，因此用過去完成式來強調動作先後順序。
  • 關係副詞引導的非限定子句 (Non-defining relative clause with relative adverb)
    ...before driving to Taichung, where he worked in an administrative role at a technology company, police said.
    「where he worked...」是一個非限定子句（前面有逗號），用關係副詞 where 來補充說明先行詞 Taichung（台中）的相關背景，為多益閱讀中常見的長句結構。
