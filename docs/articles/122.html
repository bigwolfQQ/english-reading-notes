美國磨坊重拾傳統做法，因買家尋求更健康的選擇

US millers look to old ways as buyers look for healthier options

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • operate v. 營運、操作、運轉
    Chuck Childers rises before dawn and trudges off to operate one of the US’ oldest functioning gristmills.
    Chuck Childers 在黎明前起床，拖著沉重的步伐去營運美國歷史最悠久仍在運作的水磨坊之一。
  • sustainable adj. 可持續的、永續的
    He and other optimists believe the old ways could fuel a return to healthier, smaller-scale, more sustainable food production across the US.
    他和持樂觀態度的其他人認為，古老的做法可以推動美國全國回歸更健康、小規模、更具可持續性的食品生產。
  • resurgence n. 復甦、再起
    “There’s been a resurgence” of interest in “clean food,” he said in an interview at the mill in Pigeon Forge.
    他在鴿子鍛造鎮的磨坊接受採訪時表示：「對『乾淨食品』的興趣已經出現了復甦。」
  • additives n. 添加劑
    The corn ground at the Old Mill is Tennessee-grown, genetically unmodified and free of harsh chemicals or additives added to the cornmeal and grits.
    在老磨坊研磨的玉米產自田納西州，未經基因改造，且不含添加到玉米粉和大粗粒玉米粉中的強烈化學物質或添加劑。
  • commercial adj. 商業的、營利的
    Retired miller John Lovett estimates that fewer than 100 traditional gristmills still operate at commercial viability across the US.
    退休磨坊主 John Lovett 估計，美國全國目前仍具商業營運可行性的傳統水磨坊不到 100 家。
  • viability n. 生存能力、可行性
    Retired miller John Lovett estimates that fewer than 100 traditional gristmills still operate at commercial viability across the US.
    退休磨坊主 John Lovett 估計，美國全國目前仍具商業營運可行性的傳統水磨坊不到 100 家。
  • alternatives n. 替代方案、替代品
    Even if they want to ditch the industrial food products, healthier alternatives cost more — and in poverty stricken areas of East Tennessee, that’s a non-starter.
    即使他們想拋棄工業食品，更健康的替代品成本也更高——而在田納西州東部的貧困地區，這根本行不通。
  • subsidies n. 補貼、津貼
    Food Tank’s Nierenberg argues that traditional mills should be getting some of the money poured by the US government into agro-industry subsidies.
    Food Tank 的 Nierenberg 認為，傳統磨坊應該獲得美國政府投入農業綜合企業補貼的一部分資金。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    East Tennessee’s water-powered Old Mill first began grinding cornmeal back in 1830 in the deep Appalachian region along the Little Pigeon River.
    句中的「water-powered」是由名詞加上過去分詞構成的複合形容詞，用來修飾後方的名詞「Old Mill」，意指「由水力驅動的老磨坊」，能精簡語句結構。
  • 現在分詞作主詞補語 / 附帶狀況 (Present Participle)
    Chuck Childers rises before dawn and trudges off to operate one of the US’ oldest functioning gristmills.
    句尾的「functioning」是現在分詞（動形容詞），用來修飾「gristmills」，表示「仍在運作中的」水磨坊，強調狀態的持續進行。
  • 附帶狀況與獨立分詞結構 (Absolute Construction / Participial Phrase)
    Heavily processed milling strips away bran, germ and other nutrients, and while federal law requires such flour to be enriched, the end product still lacks key vitamins, minerals and anti-oxidants.
    句中的「while federal law requires such flour to be enriched」是一個由連接詞 while 引導的讓步副詞子句，用來補充說明在聯邦法律有此要求的情況下，實際結果卻仍有不足的對比。
  • 地方副詞片語置於句首的倒裝（此處為部分修飾或結構強調）
    In a nation that invented things such as the frozen TV dinner, Childers is winding back the clock with technology that would be recognizable to farmers two millenia ago.
    句首的「In a nation...」是介詞片語作地方與背景狀語，用來設定全句的語境，突顯主角 Childers 所做的事與該國家主流現象形成強烈對比。
