基隆看守所「罪美味」月餅引發民眾搶購

Civilians clamor for Keelung prison's 'criminally good' mooncakes

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • detention center n. 看守所、拘留所
    Mooncakes made at the Keelung Detention Center have proven a surprise hit ahead of this year's Mid-Autumn Festival.
    基隆看守所製作的月餅在今年中秋節前夕意外大受歡迎。
  • ship out v. phr. 出貨、運送
    with a pair of inmate-bakers preparing to ship out around 10,000 of the traditional pastries over the holiday period.
    兩名收容人烘焙師傅準備在假期期間出貨約1萬顆傳統糕點。
  • batch n. 一批（生產的數量）
    The work is labor-intensive, with each batch of cakes needing to be flipped twice during their 25-30-minute baking time...
    這項工作相當勞動密集，每一批月餅在25至30分鐘的烘烤時間內需要翻面兩次...
  • hire v. 雇用、聘用
    ...each received training from a professional baker hired by the detention center, officials said.
    官員表示，……皆接受了看守所聘請的專業烘焙師的培訓。
  • scale n. 規模
    ...as the scale of production was constrained by the kitchen's small size.
    由於生產規模受限於廚房的小空間。
  • constrain v. 限制、約束
    ...as the scale of production was constrained by the kitchen's small size.
    由於生產規模受限於廚房的小空間。
  • evaluate v. 評估
    ...it will evaluate whether to continue production.
    將會評估是否繼續生產。
  • ability n. 能力
    ...would give the inmates confidence in their ability to reintegrate into society after their release.
    ...能讓收容人對自己刑滿釋放後重返社會的能力建立信心。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附加說明（附帶狀況）
    ...with a pair of inmate-bakers preparing to ship out around 10,000 of the traditional pastries over the holiday period.
    「with + 名詞 + 分詞」結構在句中用來表示附帶狀況或背景。在此句中，preparing 為現在分詞，用來補充說明前面主詞所處的情況。
  • 被動語態與過去分詞作後位修飾
    ...each received training from a professional baker hired by the detention center, officials said.
    句中的「hired by the detention center」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾其前面的名詞「a professional baker」，相當於形容詞子句「who was hired by the detention center」的簡化。
  • whether 引導的名詞子句作受詞
    ...it will evaluate whether to continue production.
    動詞 evaluate 後面接了「whether to continue production」作為受詞，用來表達「評估是否……」，whether 在此表示「是否（二選一或與否）」。
