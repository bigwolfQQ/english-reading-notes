川普就愛爾蘭統一發表評論

Trump comments on Irish unification

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • wading into v. (片語) 涉足、介入（困難或敏感的事物）
    US President Donald Trump on Saturday said that he would “love to see” a united Ireland, wading into a politically sensitive subject as he embarked on a two-day Irish visit that blurs the lines between his presidency and personal business interests.
    美國總統唐納·川普於週六表示，他「很想看到」一個統一的愛爾蘭，在他展開為期兩天、模糊了其總統職權與個人商業利益界線的愛爾蘭訪問行程時，涉足了一個政治上敏感的主題。
  • reunification n. 重新統一
    After meeting Irish Prime Minister Micheal Martin in Dublin, Trump said reunification of the Republic of Ireland and Northern Ireland — split for more than a century — would be “a fantastic thing.”
    在都柏林與愛爾蘭總理米歇爾·馬丁會晤後，川普表示，分裂了逾一世紀的愛爾蘭共和國與北愛爾蘭之統一將會是一件「很棒的事」。
  • referendum n. 公民投票、公投
    Under the 1998 Good Friday Agreement, which ended three decades of sectarian violence in Northern Ireland that killed about 3,000 people, a referendum can be held if the British government determines a majority there is likely to favor joining the Republic of Ireland.
    根據1998年的《貝爾法斯特協議》，如果英國政府認定當地多數民意可能贊成加入愛爾蘭共和國，就可以舉行公民投票。
  • endorse v. 背書、贊同、支援
    Republic of Ireland voters would also have to endorse reunification in a poll for it to happen.
    愛爾蘭共和國的選民也必須在投票中贊成統一，這件事才能實現。
  • neutral adj. 中立的
    The US has previously adopted a neutral position on the issue.
    美國先前對此議題採取中立立場。
  • spokesperson n. 發言人
    Asked about Trump’s comments, a spokesperson for his Downing Street office referred reporters to comments Burnham made in parliament on Wednesday last week when he vowed to “stick to the Good Friday Agreement 100 percent.”
    當被問及川普的評論時，他唐寧街辦公室的發言人引述了伯納姆上週三在國會所作的評論...
  • vow v. 發誓、誓言
    Asked about Trump’s comments, a spokesperson for his Downing Street office referred reporters to comments Burnham made in parliament on Wednesday last week when he vowed to “stick to the Good Friday Agreement 100 percent.”
    ...當時他發誓要「100%堅持《貝爾法斯特協議》」。
  • personnel n. 人員、員工
    At least 4,000 police and other personnel, including snipers, have been mobilized.
    至少有4,000名警察和其他人員（包括狙擊手）已被動員。
  • mobilize v. 動員
    At least 4,000 police and other personnel, including snipers, have been mobilized.
    至少有4,000名警察和其他人員（包括狙擊手）已被動員。
  • convey v. 傳達、表達
    “I conveyed the strong view of the Irish people that the normalization of war and genocide can never be accepted,” Connolly said in a post-meeting statement.
    康諾利在會後聲明中表示：「我傳達了愛爾蘭人民的強烈觀點，即戰爭和種族滅絕的常態化是絕不能被接受的。」
  • agenda n. 議程、待辦事項
    He then met Martin, when the Iran war and US tariffs on EU pharmaceuticals and immigration were on the agenda.
    他隨後與馬丁會面，當時伊朗戰爭、美國對歐盟藥品的關稅以及移民問題都在議程上。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾與附帶結果
    US President Donald Trump on Saturday said that he would “love to see” a united Ireland, wading into a politically sensitive subject as he embarked on a two-day Irish visit that blurs the lines between his presidency and personal business interests.
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    句中的破折號夾署 "split for more than a century" 是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞 "the Republic of Ireland and Northern Ireland"，相當於關代子句 "which have been split for more than a century" 的簡化。
  • 關係代名詞子句（非限定用法）
    Under the 1998 Good Friday Agreement, which ended three decades of sectarian violence in Northern Ireland that killed about 3,000 people, a referendum can be held if the British government determines a majority there is likely to favor joining the Republic of Ireland.
  • 條件句（If 子句）
    Under the 1998 Good Friday Agreement, which ended three decades of sectarian violence in Northern Ireland that killed about 3,000 people, a referendum can be held if the British government determines a majority there is likely to favor joining the Republic of Ireland.
