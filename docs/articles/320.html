道奇隊將在大谷受傷後為其「循序漸進調整狀態」

Dodgers to ‘build up’ Ohtani after injury

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • lineup n. （體育）先發陣容 /（公司等）陣容、系列
    Shohei Ohtani was out of the Los Angeles Dodgers’ lineup on Tuesday night
    大谷翔平於週二晚間缺席了洛杉磯道奇隊的先發打線
  • decision n. 決定、決議
    after talking to the training staff the decision was made to build him up slowly.
    但在與防護團隊討論後，做出讓他緩慢重建狀態的決定。
  • focus v. 聚焦、專注於
    He would not pitch again this season and instead focus on hitting.
    他本季將不再投球，而是專注於打擊。
  • optimum adj. 最佳的、最適宜的
    “It’s about: ‘What can we do to get him to be optimum as we get into the postseason?’”
    「重點在於：『我們能做什麼來讓他在進入季後賽時達到最佳狀態？』」
  • available adj. 可取得的、有空的、可出賽的
    Ohtani would be available to pinch hit against the Reds
    大谷將可隨時待命對紅人隊代打
  • expected v. 預期、期待
    and Roberts expected him to return to the lineup yesterday.
    且 Roberts 預期他昨天會重回先發打線。
  • remaining adj. 剩下的、剩餘的
    Roberts envisions giving Ohtani some days off over the remaining weeks of the regular season.
    Roberts 設想在例行賽剩下的幾週內，給予大谷一些休假天數。
  • manage v. 管理、經營、控制
    He said the situation has taught him to potentially get ahead of managing Ohtani’s workload
    他說，這種情況教會他如何預先著手管理大谷的工作負擔
  • workload n. 工作量
    managing Ohtani’s workload at the plate and on the mound.
    管理大谷在打擊區和投手丘上的工作負擔。
  • rely on phr. v. 依賴、依靠
    At the same time, the team relies on players, including Ohtani, to be honest about their health
    同時，球隊也依賴包括大谷在內的球員，能夠誠實地反映他們的健康狀況
  • in a timely manner phr. 及時地
    to be honest about their health in a timely manner.
    能夠及時且誠實地反映他們的健康狀況。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞當形容詞（修飾名詞）
    The two-way superstar has been dealing with lingering soreness in his left knee and right biceps.
    句中的「lingering」是由動詞 linger（持續、殘留）衍生而來的現在分詞，在此作為形容詞放在名詞 soreness（痠痛）前面，用來修飾「揮之不去的痠痛」。
  • 疑問詞引導的名詞子句
    “It’s about: ‘What can we do to get him to be optimum as we get into the postseason?’”
    引號內的 “What can we do to get him to be optimum as we get into the postseason?” 是一個由疑問詞 What 引導的名詞子句，在句中作為介系詞 about 的受詞。
  • 現在分詞片語作附帶狀況/結果（分詞構句）
    Tommy Edman started in center field and led off in Ohtani’s place, while Freeman was the designated hitter as the NL West-leading Dodgers pursued a fifth consecutive win.
    句尾的「as the NL West-leading Dodgers pursued a fifth consecutive win」中，leading 是現在分詞修飾 Dodgers，表示「在國聯西區領先的」道奇隊，同時整個 as 引導的副詞子句用來表達伴隨的時間與背景狀況。
  • 關係代間子句（限定修飾）
    Ohtani is nearing the end of his third season with the Dodgers, and the first in which he was a full-time two-way player for much of it.
    句中的「in which he was a full-time two-way player for much of it」為「介系詞 + 關代（in which）」引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的先行詞 the first（指第一個球季）。
