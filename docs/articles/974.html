文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作補充說明 (Present Participle Phrase indicating results/supplementary info)
    A man entered a Fubon Life office building in Taipei on Monday and stabbed an employee before turning the knife on himself, leaving both men unconscious, authorities said.
    句尾的「leaving both men unconscious」是由現在分詞ing開頭的片語，用來補充說明前面主句動作所產生的結果（使兩人皆失去意識）。
  • 對等連接詞連接連續動作 (Compound Verbs)
    The attacker then stabbed or slashed himself in the chest and on the right side of his neck before losing consciousness, police said.
    句中動詞被連接詞串聯，描述攻擊者在失去意識前連續完成的動作，呈現出新聞敘事的緊湊感。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The attacker's identity had not been officially confirmed as of press time.
    此句使用過去完成式的被動語態（had not been + 過去分詞），強調「身分」作為受動者，在截稿前尚未被官方證實。
