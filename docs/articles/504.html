中國 13 歲游泳神童

China’s 13-year-old swimming prodigy

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • domination n. 控制，支配，優勢
    Now a teenager, she headlines China’s bid for Asian Games domination and has Olympic glory in her sights.
    如今已是青少年的她，正領銜中國爭奪亞運會的統治地位，並將奧運榮譽視為她的目標。
  • demonstrate v. 展現，證明，示範
    Demonstrate the level of your training and work hard to break past yourself,
    展現出你的訓練水準，努力超越自己，
  • struggle v. / n. 掙扎，奮鬥，努力
    However, the teenager has spoken of struggling to deal with the heavy load placed on her.
    然而，這位青少年也坦言，自己一直在努力應對施加在她身上的沉重負擔。
  • juggle v. 兼顧，同時應付（多項工作或活動）
    She started swimming aged six and since then has juggled schoolwork with training.
    她 6 歲開始學游泳，從此在課業與訓練之間蠟燭兩頭燒。
  • consider v. 考慮
    Yu said she “never considered quitting” and always dreamed of being a national champion.
    于子迪表示她「從未考慮過放棄」，並且一直夢想成為全國冠軍。
  • pressure n. 壓力
    Yet during the tune-up to last year’s National Games she said the pressure of wanting to win the 400m medley nearly became too much.
    然而在去年全運會的備戰期間，她表示想贏得 400 公尺混合式的壓力幾乎讓人喘不過氣。
  • persevere v. 堅持不懈，不屈不撓
    Her coach told her to persevere and “believe in yourself,” she said.
    她說，教練告訴她要堅持下去並「相信自己」。
  • impact n. 衝擊，影響
    Some in the swimming world have questioned the mental and physical impact of high-level training and competing on Yu at such a young age.
    游泳界的某些人士質疑了高強度訓練與競賽對這麼年輕的于子迪在身心上造成的影響。
  • aware adj. 意識到的，知道的
    She is aware that her success for someone so young has made international headlines, but she knows hard work outweighs innate talent.
    她深知自己如此年輕便取得成功已登上國際頭條，但她明白努力勝過天生才華。
  • strength n. 力量，體力，長處
    “I will keep holding fast to my original dream, drawing strength from my team, to stand on the Olympic podium,” she said.
    她說：「我將繼續堅守最初的夢想，從團隊中汲取力量，站上奧運頒獎台。」

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附加說明（補充結果）
    At the last year’s World Aquatics Championships in Singapore she helped China reach the 4x200m women’s freestyle relay final. She did not swim in the final, but China came third, earning Yu a historic bronze for swimming in the heats.
    句尾的「earning Yu a historic bronze...」是由現在分詞（earning）引導的分詞片語，用來補充說明前面主句（中國隊獲得第三名）所產生的自然結果，意思是「因而為于子迪贏得了一面歷史性的銅牌」。
  • 現在分詞短語作時間/條件狀語
    In November last year at China’s National Games, having just turned 13, she clocked a blistering 2:07.41 in the women’s 200m medley for what was then an Asian record.
    句中的「having just turned 13」是完成式的現在分詞短語，置於主句前用以表達時間或原因（也就是「在她剛滿 13 歲之後」），強調動作發生的先後順序。
  • 過去分詞短語作後位修飾
    However, the teenager has spoken of struggling to deal with the heavy load placed on her.
    片語「placed on her」是過去分詞短語，用來修飾前面的名詞「the heavy load（沉重的負擔）」。其完整概念是「which is placed on her（被施加在她身上的）」，關係代名詞與be動詞被省略，使句子更為精簡。
  • 虛擬語氣/假設語氣（與過去事實相反或未來推測）
    In June she won the women’s 400m medley in 4:30.79 at China’s national championships, a time which would easily have earned her silver at the Paris Games in 2024.
    子句中使用「would have + 過去分詞（would easily have earned）」表達的是與過去事實相反的假設或推論。意指「如果她在 2024 年巴黎奧運游出這個成績，那本來可以輕易贏得銀牌」（但她當時並未參加巴黎奧運）。
