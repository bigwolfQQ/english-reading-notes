文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾
    Acer Inc founder Stan Shih poses for a photograph ahead of a musical marking the company’s 50th anniversary in Taipei yesterday.
    句中的 "marking the company’s 50th anniversary" 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "a musical"，相當於形容詞子句 "that marks the company’s 50th anniversary"，在多益閱讀測驗中常用於簡化句子結構。
  • 非限定關係子句
    The musical, which was broadcast live on YouTube, could let more international audiences know how Taiwan’s high-tech industry developed through Acer’s entrepreneurial journey, Shih said.
    句中的 ", which was broadcast live on YouTube," 是一個非限定關係子句（用逗號隔開），用來補充說明前面的名詞 "The musical"。此處同時運用了被動語態 "was broadcast"。
  • 間接問句（名詞子句作受詞）
    The musical, which was broadcast live on YouTube, could let more international audiences know how Taiwan’s high-tech industry developed through Acer’s entrepreneurial journey, Shih said.
    "how Taiwan’s high-tech industry developed through Acer’s entrepreneurial journey" 是個以疑問詞 "how" 引導的名詞子句，在句中擔任動詞 "know" 的受詞。子句內的語序為「主詞 + 動詞」，多益考試常考這種間接問句的語序判斷。
