台股受聯準會升息擔憂影響而大跌

Taiwan shares tumble on Fed rate hike fears

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • turnover n. 營業額、成交量、周轉率
    Turnover totaled NT$721.91 billion (US$22.84 billion).
    成交值達新台幣7,219.1億元（約合228.4億美元）。
  • analyst n. 分析師
    "The inflation worries sent U.S. Treasury yields higher, reinforcing fears over a Fed rate hike," Hua Nan Securities analyst Kevin Su said.
    「通膨 worries 推高了美國公債殖利率，加劇了市場對聯準會升息的恐懼，」華南投顧分析師蘇先生表示。
  • counterpart n. 相對應的人或物、同等級的職位或機構
    "So interest rate-sensitive tech stocks in the United States lost steam overnight and their counterparts in Taiwan simply followed suit."
    「因此，美國對利率敏感的科技股隔夜失去動能，台灣的相應類股也隨之跟進。」
  • supplier n. 供應商
    Smartphone IC designer MediaTek Inc. lost 2.86 percent to close at NT$4,585.00, memory chip supplier Nanya Technology Corp. dropped 4.27 percent to close at NT$493.00...
    智慧型手機IC設計公司聯發科下跌2.86%收在4,585.00元，記憶體晶片供應商南亞科技下跌4.27%收在493.00元...
  • provider n. 供應商、提供者
    ...while power management solution provider Delta Electronics Inc. shed 3.28 percent to end at NT$1,620.00.
    ...而電源管理解決方案供應商台達電子則下跌3.28%收在1,620.00元。
  • downturn n. 經濟衰退、低迷、下滑
    Bucking the downturn, Quanta Computer Inc., another AI server producer, closed up 0.15 percent at NT$336.50.
    逆勢走揚的是另一家AI伺服器生產商廣達電腦，收盤上漲0.15%，收在新台幣336.50元。
  • fund n. 資金、基金
    "However, funds rotated to financial stocks as safe havens, giving some support to the broader market," Su said.
    「然而，資金轉向金融股作為避風港，為大盤提供了部分支撐，」蘇先生表示。
  • profitability n. 獲利能力、盈利能力
    "The current strong demand for liquidity has strengthened their profitability on higher interest income."
    「目前對資金的強勁需求，透過較高的利息收入增強了他們的獲利能力。」
  • unchanged adj. 未變化的、持平的
    ...while Fubon Financial Holding Co. and Cathay Financial Holding Co. ended unchanged at NT$148.50 and NT$110.50, respectively.
    ...而富邦金控與國泰金控則分別收平盤，收在新台幣148.50元和110.50元。
  • barometer n. 氣壓計、風向球、衡量指標
    "It is worth watching the August U.S. consumer price index due later today, as the data serves as an important barometer of the Fed's decision making," Su said.
    「值得關注的是稍後將公布的美國8月消費者物價指數，因為該數據是聯準會決策的重要氣壓計，」蘇先生表示。
  • odds n. 機率、可能性
    Following the PPI data, the odds of the Fed raising rates by 25 basis points next week rose to around 70 percent.
    在PPI數據公布後，聯準會下周升息25個基點的機率上升至約70%。
  • institutional investors n. phr. 機構投資人
    According to the Taiwan Stock Exchange, foreign institutional investors sold a net NT$89.27 billion worth of shares on the main board Friday.
    根據台灣證券交易所的數據，外資機構投資人周五在主板賣超新台幣892.7億元股票。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（用現在分詞表示伴隨或原因）
    Shares in Taiwan moved sharply lower by more than 750 points Friday, led by the electronics sector, amid growing fears over an interest rate hike by the U.S. Federal Reserve after Washington reported the fastest monthly growth in the producer price index in August since May and international crude oil prices surged, dealers said.
    句中的 "led by the electronics sector" 是被動分詞片語（省略了 "which were"），用來補充說明台股大跌是由電子類股所帶動的。這種結構在財經新聞中非常常見，能簡潔地交代事件的主因或背景。
  • 動名詞片語當主詞 / 形容詞化合字
    "The inflation worries sent U.S. Treasury yields higher, reinforcing fears over a Fed rate hike," Hua Nan Securities analyst Kevin Su said.
    句尾的 ", reinforcing fears..." 是現在分詞構句，表示前一句動作所帶來的自然結果（「因而加劇了……的恐懼」）。另外，「interest rate-sensitive」是由「名詞 + 形容詞」組成的複合形容詞，用來修飾 tech stocks，意思是「對利率敏感的」。
  • 獨立分詞構句與附帶狀況
    "However, funds rotated to financial stocks as safe havens, giving some support to the broader market," Su said.
    逗號之後的 ", giving some support to..." 是分詞構句，主詞承接前面的整個子句動作（資金轉向金融股這件事），表示該動作所產生的直接結果——為整體市場提供了支撐。
  • 形容詞片語作後位修飾
    "It is worth watching the August U.S. consumer price index due later today, as the data serves as an important barometer of the Fed's decision making," Su said.
    句中的 "due later today" 是形容詞片語，用來後位修飾前面的名詞短語 "the August U.S. consumer price index"，意思是「預定於今天稍後公布的」。此外，「It is worth + V-ing」是多益常考的高頻句型，表示「……是值得的」。
