文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The Washington Capitals on Tuesday deflected criticism of star Alex Ovechkin’s appearance in a campaign video for Russian President Vladimir Putin, saying the NHL all-time goal-scoring leader had been “asked by his country to participate.”
    句中的「had been asked」為過去完成式的被動語態（had been + 過去分詞），用來表達在過去某個時間點之前，主體（奧vechkin）「被」他的國家要求參與某事。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Ovechkin is seen in the commercial standing behind Russian Minister of Foreign Affairs Sergey Lavrov and was among a group of prominent Russians shown in the campaign video...
    句中的「standing behind...」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面被看見的 Ovechkin 的狀態；而「shown in the campaign video...」則是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的「a group of prominent Russians」，省略了（who were）。
  • 非限定關係代間子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Ovechkin, who turns 41 today, previously voiced support for the Russian president in 2017 when he started a social movement called the “Putin Team.”
    「who turns 41 today」是以逗號隔開的非限定關係子句，對主詞 Ovechkin 提供額外的補充說明，如果拿掉這個子句，主句的意思依然完整。
  • 省略主詞與be動詞的問句結構 (Ellipsis in Participial Clauses)
    When Russia invaded Ukraine in February 2022, Ovechkin said “please, no more war,” when asked about the situation.
    句尾的「when asked about the situation」實際上是「when he was asked about the situation」的簡化形式。當分詞片語的主詞與主要子句主詞一致且含有 be 動詞時，常可省略主詞與 be 動詞，直接保留連接詞與過去分詞。
