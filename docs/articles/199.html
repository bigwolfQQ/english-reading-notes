尼泊爾救援人員因奇蹟般的發現而受到鼓舞

Nepalese rescuers buoyed by miracle finds

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • survivor n. 倖存者，生還者
    Nepalese rescuers faced challenging conditions yesterday as search operations after catastrophic floods entered their 12th day, buoyed by the extraordinary rescues of four survivors.
    隨著災難性洪水的搜救行動進入第 12 天，尼泊爾救援人員昨天面臨著嚴峻的狀況，但由於四名倖存者被奇蹟般地救出，他們受到了鼓舞。
  • collapse n. / v. 崩塌，瓦解
    The glacial and mountain collapse on the China-Nepal border has killed at least 1,372 people and left more than 5,500 missing across the two countries.
    中尼邊境發生的冰川與山體崩塌已造成兩國至少 1,372 人死亡，超過 5,500 人失蹤。
  • infrastructure n. 基礎設施
    The wall of icy water and debris tore through mountain districts on Aug. 26, destroying roads, bridges and other infrastructure.
    8 月 26 日，這股冰冷的洪水與泥石流衝擊了山區縣份，摧毀了道路、橋樑和其他基礎設施。
  • project n. 專案，計畫
    A day earlier, two Nepali hydropower workers were rescued at the Trishuli 3A tunnel, where searches continue alongside multiple other sites, with at least 12 hydropower projects affected.
    前一天，兩名尼泊爾水電工人在特里蘇利 3A 號隧道獲救，那裡的搜救工作與其他多個地點同時進行，至少有 12 個水力發電項目受到影響。
  • restore v. 恢復，修復
    Biraj Bhakta Shrestha said work was continuing to restore electricity in several areas.
    比拉傑·巴克塔·什雷斯塔表示，恢復幾個地區電力的工作正在持續進行。
  • cost n. 成本，費用
    Nepalese Minister of Foreign Affairs Shisir Khanal called the disaster a “Himalayan tsunami” and warned reconstruction costs could run into the billions of dollars.
    尼泊爾外交部長希西爾·卡納爾將這場災難稱為「喜馬拉雅海嘯」，並警告重建成本可能高達數十億美元。
  • magnitude n. 巨大，規模
    The UN has said the magnitude of the destruction makes delivering aid an immense challenge, and has launched a US$50 million appeal to help more than 84,000 people.
    聯合國表示，毀壞的規模使得運送救援物資成為一項巨大的挑戰，並已發起 5000 萬美元的募款呼籲，以幫助超過 84,000 人。
  • identify v. 識別，確認身分
    Only about 100 bodies have been identified and handed over to families.
    僅約有 100 具遺體被辨認身分並交還給家屬。
  • agency n. 機構，局
    In Nepal, rescuers have retrieved 1,341 bodies, while around 4,996 people remain missing, according to the disaster agency.
    根據災難機構的數據，在尼泊爾，救援人員已找回 1,341 具遺體，同時約有 4,996 人失蹤。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況（用過去分詞表被動）
    Nepalese rescuers faced challenging conditions yesterday as search operations after catastrophic floods entered their 12th day, buoyed by the extraordinary rescues of four survivors.
    句尾的「buoyed by...」是由分詞構句簡化而來，原意為「because they were buoyed by...（因為受到……的鼓舞）」。過去分詞 buoyed 在此表示被動，用來補充說明主詞 rescuers 當時的心理狀態。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The glacial and mountain collapse on the China-Nepal border has killed at least 1,372 people and left more than 5,500 missing across the two countries.
    雖然本句前半是主動語態（has killed），但在後文常可看到強調受事者的被動語態結構（如 have been rescued, have been identified），在新聞英語中用以客觀陳述受災與救援事實。
  • 地方副詞置於句首的倒裝（或省略結構）
    “Places that have unreachable, we’ve tried to restore power with solar,” he said.
    此處運用了口語引述中的結構，將地方修飾語置於子句前方，突顯救援地點的困境，隨後接上主詞與動詞進行說明，使語氣更加口語且直接。
  • 現在分詞作後位修飾
    In Nepal, rescuers have retrieved 1,341 bodies, while around 4,996 people remain missing, according to the disaster agency.
    句中的「missing」雖常見於形容詞，但在此作為分詞補語，用來補充說明主詞「4,996 people」持續處於失蹤的狀態。
