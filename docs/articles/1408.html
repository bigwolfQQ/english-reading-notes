日本商工會敦促程序透明化

Japanese chamber urges transparent procedures

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • urge v. 敦促、力勸
    The Japanese Chamber of Commerce and Industry Taipei yesterday urged Taiwan to make its administrative procedures more transparent and predictable...
    台北市日本工商會昨天敦促台灣使行政程序更加透明和具預測性...
  • transparent adj. 透明的、易懂的
    ...urged Taiwan to make its administrative procedures more transparent and predictable to encourage greater investment...
    ...敦促台灣使行政程序更加透明和具預測性，以鼓勵更多投資...
  • predictable adj. 可預測的
    ...make its administrative procedures more transparent and predictable to encourage greater investment...
    ...使行政程序更加透明和具預測性，以鼓勵更多投資...
  • investment n. 投資
    ...to encourage greater investment, as it released its annual white paper.
    ...以鼓勵更多投資，同時該會也發布了年度白皮書。
  • annual adj. 年度的、每年的
    ...as it released its annual white paper.
    ...同時該會也發布了年度白皮書。
  • regulation n. 法規、規章
    ...the chamber called for regulations aligned with international standards and measures to address shortages of skilled workers and electricity.
    ...該商會呼籲法規應與國際標準接軌，以及採取措施解決技術勞工短缺和缺電問題。
  • shortage n. 短缺、不足
    ...measures to address shortages of skilled workers and electricity.
    ...採取措施解決技術勞工短缺和缺電問題。
  • supply chain n. 供應鏈
    Resolving them could encourage larger investments, bring in advanced technology and strengthen supply chains...
    解決這些問題可以鼓勵更大規模的投資、引入先進技術並加強供應鏈...
  • application n. 申請、應徵
    On administrative procedures, the white paper called for a more transparent and efficient process for permit applications, approvals and consultations...
    在行政程序方面，白皮書呼籲為許可證申請、審批和諮詢建立更透明、更高效的流程...
  • approval n. 批准、同意
    ...a more transparent and efficient process for permit applications, approvals and consultations...
    ...為許可證申請、審批和諮詢建立更透明、更高效的流程...
  • recruit v. 招募、招聘
    Respondents rated Taiwan’s living environment and business infrastructure highly, but identified difficulties with administrative practices and with recruiting and assigning staff.
    受訪者對台灣的生活環境和商業基礎設施給予高度評價，但也指出在行政實務以及招募和調派員工方面存在困難。
  • implement v. 實施、執行
    Four have been implemented or are scheduled for implementation...
    其中 4 項已落實或預計落實...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（分詞構句）作附帶結果或補充說明
    ...urged Taiwan to make its administrative procedures more transparent and predictable to encourage greater investment, as it released its annual white paper.
    句尾的「as it released its annual white paper」也可以簡化為分詞構句「releasing its annual white paper」，在此用連接詞 as 引導子句，表達在敦促的同時也發布了白皮書，多益閱讀測驗常考這種帶有同時發生性質的結構。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    ...the chamber called for regulations aligned with international standards and measures to address shortages of skilled workers and electricity.
    句中的「aligned with international standards」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 regulations（法規），等於形容詞子句「which are aligned with international standards」。這種簡化的修飾手法在商業新聞與多益考題中極為常見。
  • 對等連接詞連接多個受詞或動詞片語
    Resolving them could encourage larger investments, bring in advanced technology and strengthen supply chains, he said...
  • 被動語態的使用
    Four have been implemented or are scheduled for implementation...
    句中的「have been implemented」與「are scheduled」皆採用被動語態（be + 過去分詞），因為主詞「Four（指四項訴求）」是動作的承受者，而非發動者。多益測驗常藉此測試考生對動詞語態（主動與被動）的掌握度。
