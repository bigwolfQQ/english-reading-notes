香港法院就天安門事件紀念集會判處三人徒刑

HK court sentences trio over Tiananmen vigils

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • advocate n. 倡導者，擁護者
    Three Hong Kong rights advocates who organized annual vigils in remembrance of China’s 1989 Tiananmen Square crackdown yesterday were sentenced to about five to seven years in prison...
    三名組織每年紀念中國1989年天安門廣場鎮壓集會的香港人權倡議人士，昨天被判處大約五至七年有期徒刑...
  • sentence v. / n. 宣判，判決；徒刑
    ...were sentenced to about five to seven years in prison, with officials in Taiwan calling the punishment “harsh.”
    ...被判處大約五至七年有期徒刑，台灣官方稱此刑罰「嚴苛」。
  • convict v. 宣判有罪，定罪
    Lee Cheuk-yan, Chow Hang-tung and Albert Ho were convicted of inciting subversion earlier this year under a national security law.
    李卓人、鄒幸彤與何俊仁於今年稍早依據國家安全法，被裁定煽動顛覆政權罪名成立。
  • term n. 期間，任期，刑期
    Lee received a seven-year prison term and Chow was sentenced to seven years and three months.
    李卓人被判處七年徒刑，鄒幸彤則被判處七年三個月。
  • plead v. 答辯，認罪
    Both pleaded not guilty.
    兩人皆否認指控。
  • charge v. / n. 控告，指控；費用
    The trio of defendants was charged in September 2021 and has been held in custody for years.
    這三名被告於2021年9月被起訴，已被拘留多年。
  • prosecute v. 起訴，檢訴
    ...authorities reduced public choice in elections and prosecuted leaders of news firms.
    ...當局減少了選舉中的公眾選擇權，並起訴了新聞機構的領導人。
  • appeal v. 上訴，申訴
    Tang said that he would appeal against the results.
    鄧燕娥表示，他將會對判決結果提出上訴。
  • condemn v. 譴責，指責
    In Taipei, the Mainland Affairs Council condemned the “heavy sentences” handed to the three.
    在台北，大陸委員會譴責了對這三人判處的「重刑」。
  • statement n. 聲明，陳述
    “The pursuit of democracy and human rights is not a crime,” the council said in a statement...
    委員會在聲明中表示：「追求民主與人權不是犯罪」...
  • shield v. 庇護，保護，遮蔽
    ...adding that authorities have “fabricated charges and conducted political trials in the name of national security to shield an authoritarian regime.”
    ...並補充說，當局「以國家安全為名捏造罪名並進行政治審判，以庇護威權政權。」

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Three Hong Kong rights advocates who organized annual vigils in remembrance of China’s 1989 Tiananmen Square crackdown yesterday were sentenced to about five to seven years in prison...
    句中的 "who organized..." 是一個形容詞子句，修飾前面的名詞 "advocates"。在英文新聞中，常用這種結構來補充說明主詞的身分或背景，使句子資訊更豐富。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Lee Cheuk-yan, Chow Hang-tung and Albert Ho were convicted of inciting subversion earlier this year under a national security law.
    新聞報導經常使用被動語態（be + 過去分詞，如 were convicted），當動作的承受者（此處為李卓人等人）比動作的執行者更重要，或者執行者眾所皆知/不需明言時，常採用此句型。
  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況 (Participial Phrase indicating accompanying circumstance)
    ...with officials in Taiwan calling the punishment “harsh.”
    此處使用 "with + 受詞 + 分詞 (calling)" 的結構，用來表達附帶的情況或背景，意指「在台灣官方稱此刑罰嚴苛的情況下」。這是多益閱讀與高階英文中常見的表態句型。
  • 對等連接詞連接多個動詞片語 (Coordination of Verb Phrases)
    ...authorities reduced public choice in elections and prosecuted leaders of news firms.
    句中的 and 連接了兩個過去式的動詞片語（reduced... 與 prosecuted...），共用主詞 authorities，用來描述當局在2019年後連續採取的兩項行動。
