  • depart v. 出發，離開

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    US senators on Tuesday urged US President Donald Trump to “stand with Taiwan” and not let China influence US support for Taiwan’s defense, ahead of his planned meeting with Chinese President Xi Jinping (習近平) in Washington.
    句中的 "ahead of his planned meeting with Chinese President..." 透過過去分詞當形容詞用來修飾 meeting；而在後面段落也有許多現在分詞片語（如 urging Trump to end...）用來補充說明主句動作發生時伴隨的情況，或作為結果的補充。
  • 使役動詞 let 的用法 (Causative Verb "let")
    “We can’t let President Xi leave stronger than when he arrived.”
    「let + 受詞 + 原形動詞」表示「讓某人／某物做某事」。在此句中，「let President Xi leave」意指「讓習主席離開」，其中 leave 為不帶 to 的原形動詞。
  • 被動語態的應用 (Passive Voice)
    He is scheduled to depart Friday.
    「is scheduled to」是典型的被動語態結構（be + 過去分詞），用來表達某事是按計畫被安排好的。在新聞英語中經常用來報導官方行程或會議時間。
  • 對等連接詞連接的多重動詞 (Parallel Verbs)
    The People’s Republic of China presents a greater threat to American security and prosperity than ever before, the statement said, urging Trump to end China’s access to advanced chips and manufacturing equipment, press Beijing to stop supporting Russian President Vladimir Putin’s war in Ukraine and confront Xi on supplying resources to Iran.
    句中的分詞片語帶有好幾個平行的動作：urging (敦促)..., press (施壓)... and confront (對質)...，透過對等連接詞 and 連接多個動詞原形變化或分詞，使長句結構保持條理清晰。
