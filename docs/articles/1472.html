奧運冠軍基普喬格贏得墨爾本馬拉松

Olympic champ Kipchoge wins Melbourne marathon

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • victory n. 勝利
    Two-time Olympic champion Eliud Kipchoge won his first marathon in three years yesterday when the Kenyan great cruised to victory at Melbourne.
    兩屆奧運冠軍伊利烏德·基普喬格昨天在墨爾本輕鬆奪冠，贏得他三年來的首場馬拉松勝利。
  • raise funds v. phr. 籌募資金
    It was his first victory over the distance since Berlin in 2023 and part of his “World Tour” to raise funds for causes he believes in.
    這是他自2023年柏林馬拉松以來首次在該項目奪冠，也是他為自己所支持的事業籌集資金的「世界巡迴賽」的一部分。
  • announce v. 宣佈，公佈
    The 41-year-old former world record-holder announced last year that he planned to complete marathons on all seven continents, including Antarctica, over two years.
    這位41歲的前世界紀錄保持者去年宣佈，他計劃在兩年內完成所有七大洲（包括南極洲）的馬拉松比賽。
  • complete v. 完成
    The 41-year-old former world record-holder announced last year that he planned to complete marathons on all seven continents, including Antarctica, over two years.
    這位41歲的前世界紀錄保持者去年宣佈，他計劃在兩年內完成所有七大洲（包括南極洲）的馬拉松比賽。
  • stop n. 停留站，中途站
    Melbourne was the third stop after South Africa and Brazil.
    墨爾本是繼南非和巴西之後的第三站。
  • inspire v. 鼓舞，啟發
    “It makes me proud to run with the younger generation and inspire the running community in Melbourne.”
    「這讓我為能與年輕一代一起奔跑並啟發墨爾本的跑步社群感到驕傲。」
  • runner-up n. 第二名，亞軍
    Australian Thomas Do Canto was runner-up in 2:10:35 while New Zealand’s Camille French came first in the women’s race in 2:28:25.
    澳洲選手托馬斯·多坎托以2小時10分35秒獲得亞軍，而紐西蘭的卡米耶·弗レンチ則以2小時28分25秒贏得女子組冠軍。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作修飾語
    “On the course, it was really wonderful, I enjoyed seeing people standing, cheering and shouting my name,” Kipchoge said, looking fresh at the finish.
    句尾的「looking fresh at the finish」是由現在分詞「looking」引導的分詞片語，用來補充說明主詞 Kipchoge 在抵達終點時的狀態，相當於副詞子句「while he looked fresh at the finish」。
  • 與過去事實相反的假設語氣
    He would have gone faster, but slowed down inside the MCG waving to fans.
    此句使用了假設語氣「would have + 過去分詞」，用來表達「如果沒有放慢速度，他本來可以跑得更快」的過去虛擬情境，但實際上他放慢了速度。
  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    It was his first victory over the distance since Berlin in 2023 and part of his “World Tour” to raise funds for causes he believes in.
    句尾的「he believes in」是一個省略了關係代名詞（which 或 that）的形容詞子句，用來修飾先行詞 causes（事業/原因），in 後面的受詞即為該先行詞。
