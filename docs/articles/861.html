巴西法院就大法官調查案爆發衝突

Brazilian court clashes over probe of judge

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • probe n. 調查、探查
    The Brazilian Supreme Court engaged in a heated, hours-long debate on Tuesday over how to handle a potential probe into one of its top judges...
    巴西最高法院於週二進行了一場長達數小時的激烈辯論，討論如何處理針對其頂尖法官之一的潛在調查...
  • investigation n. 調查、偵查
    ...to decide whether to open an investigation into Alexandre de Moraes, who has been accused of unsavory ties to a jailed banker involved in a major fraud case.
    ...以決定是否對亞歷山大·德·莫賴斯展開調查。莫賴斯被指控與一名涉入重大詐欺案的入獄銀行家有不正當聯繫。
  • fraud n. 詐欺、詐騙
    ...in which the banker sought the justice’s help in the fraud investigation against him.
    ...其中該銀行家尋求大法官協助其面對的詐欺調查。
  • sanction v. 制裁、批准
    Moraes is the bete noire of the Bolsonaro family and was briefly sanctioned by US President Donald Trump’s administration...
    莫賴斯是博爾索納羅家族的死敵，曾遭到美國總統唐納德·川普政府的短暫制裁...
  • postponement n. 延期、延緩
    ...securing a postponement that could last until December.
    ...並爭取將延期推遲至 12 月。
  • corruption n. 貪腐、腐敗
    On Friday, it was revealed that Flavio Bolsonaro is under investigation for suspected corruption and money laundering regarding the financing of a film about his father...
    週五有消息透露，弗拉維奧·博爾索納羅因涉嫌腐敗和洗錢正接受調查，涉及一部關於其父親...的電影的融資問題。
  • money laundering n. 洗錢
    On Friday, it was revealed that Flavio Bolsonaro is under investigation for suspected corruption and money laundering...
    週五有消息透露，弗拉維奧·博爾索納羅因涉嫌腐敗和洗錢正接受調查...
  • financing n. 融資、財務安排
    ...regarding the financing of a film about his father, bankrolled by Vorcaro.
    ...涉及一部關於其父親、由沃爾卡羅資助的電影的融資問題。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後置修飾語 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...in an unprecedented public showdown that has eclipsed a presidential election campaign.
    句中的「that has eclipsed a presidential election campaign」是關係代名詞子句，用來修飾前面的名詞 showdown（對峙）。而分詞也可以用來做類似的形容詞功能，幫助精簡句子並補充說明前方的名詞。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The court’s 10 justices were convened by its president, Edson Fachin...
    句型為「主詞 + be 動詞 + 過去分詞 (ed/en) + by...」。在此句中，10 位大法官是被院長埃德森·法欣所召集，焦點在於法官們「被召集」這項動作與結果，而非發動召集的院長本身。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Absolute Participial Construction)
    ...with justices shouting and trading insults as they remained stuck on procedural issues...
    「with + 名詞 + 現在分詞 (shouting and trading)」構成附帶狀況的分詞構句，用來形容聽證會進行時，法官們一邊叫囂、一邊互相辱罵的同時伴隨狀態。
