研究機構吹捧DNA採樣模組在深海中的運作成效

Institute touts DNA sampler module’s work under the sea

單字解析

  • statement n. 聲明，陳述
    the Taiwan Oceanic Research Institute said in a statement yesterday.
    台灣海洋研究機構昨天在聲明中表示。
  • identify v. 確認，辨識，識別
    A test of the module identified 365 species in waters southwest of Taiwan proper, the institute said.
    該機構表示，該模組的一項測試在台灣本島西南方的水域辨識出了365個物種。
  • boon n. 恩賜，利益，極大的幫助
    is a boon to deep oceanic research, as it could sample trace amounts of genetic material from cells
    是深海研究的一大福音，因為它可以從細胞中採樣微量的遺傳物質
  • amount n. 量，數量
    as it could sample trace amounts of genetic material from cells, tissue fragments and excretions, it said.
    它說，因為它可以從細胞、組織碎片及排泄物中採樣微量的遺傳物質。
  • challenge n. 挑戰
    Deep-sea exploration poses steep challenges stemming from high pressures, low temperatures and low bio-density, the institute said.
    該機構表示，深海探勘面臨來自高壓、低溫以及低生物密度的嚴峻挑戰。
  • inefficient adj. 效率低下的
    Trawling nets could be deployed to collect samples, but they are inefficient and disruptive to the fragile ecology of the deep sea
    雖然可以部署拖網來收集樣本，但它們效率不彰且會破壞深海脆弱的生態
  • consist of phr. v. 由……組成，包含
    The module consists of a pressure-resistant motor, a peristaltic pump — which allows precision and keeps the risk of pollution low — and a filter that can be adjusted to the size of the targeted sample, it said.
    該機構表示，該模組由一個耐壓馬達、一個蠕動幫浦（能提供精確度並保持低汙染風險）以及一個可根據目標樣本大小進行調整的過濾器所組成。
  • precision n. 精確（度），精準
    a peristaltic pump — which allows precision and keeps the risk of pollution low — and a filter that can be adjusted to the size of the targeted sample, it said.
    它說，一個蠕動幫浦（能提供精確度並保持低汙染風險）以及一個可根據目標樣本大小進行調整的過濾器。
  • schedule v. 預定，安排
    it can be programmed to collect samples at scheduled times and can be retrieved with a near-zero environmental footprint, the institute said.
    該研究所表示，它每小時可以過濾超過100公升的海水，可以被編程以在預定時間收集樣本，並且能夠以近乎零環境足跡的方式回收。
  • capability n. 能力，性能
    The module tested its capabilities during the trial
    該模組在試驗期間測試了其性能
  • conduct v. 進行，執行（業務、研究等）
    The institute said that it plans to deploy more of the modules to conduct biodiversity research, ocean environmental conservation, fisheries resource management and long-term ecological observation.
    該機構表示，它計畫部署更多此類模組，以進行生物多樣性研究、海洋環境保育、漁業資源管理以及長期生態觀測。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Postmodifier)
    Scientists have invented a DNA sampler module to collect residual deep-sea genetic material for biological surveys and long-term maritime ecology monitoring...
    句中的 residual 用於修飾 deep-sea genetic material。此外，像是從事名詞補充說明的動名詞/現在分詞用法在多益閱讀測驗中十分常見，用以簡化形容詞子句。
  • 關代子句夾在破折號中作為補充說明 (Relative Clause within Dashes)
    The deep-sea high-flow environmental DNA module — a suitcase-sized machine when deployed with its dedicated platform — is a boon to deep oceanic research...
    破折號中間夾了同位語與省略的主動態分詞片語（when [it is] deployed with...），用來補充說明主詞的特徵，這種結構常出現在多益長篇閱讀的文章中。
  • 非限定關代子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause with 'which')
    The module consists of a pressure-resistant motor, a peristaltic pump — which allows precision and keeps the risk of pollution low — and a filter...
    使用「, which...」對前面的先行詞（peristaltic pump）做補充描述，在多益文法題（尤其是文法選擇題）中常考關代的使用以及動詞單複數一致性。
  • 分詞構句表附帶結果 (Participle Phrase indicating Result)
    ...in which it collected 22 million DNA sequences and enabled the identification of hundreds of species of fish, mollusks, crustaceans, corals, sea stars and sea urchins during the trial, the institute said.
    句中的 and enabled 連接動詞，展現連續發生的動作；而在其他段落中也常運用分詞（如 stemming from）來表達原因或狀態，這是多益測驗中句型改寫與綜合理解的核心文法。
