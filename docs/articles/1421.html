文法重點

  • 關係代間詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    Spain on Tuesday fought back against Croatia to win 2-1 in Split and remain perfect in UEFA Nations League Group A3 while Harry Kane continued his hot form as England beat the Czech Republic 3-0 to stay second.
    句中的「as England beat the Czech Republic 3-0...」以及文章後續出現多處由關係代間詞（如 who, that）引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的名詞或補充說明背景資訊，能讓冗長的句子結構更緊密。
  • 假設語氣（Second Conditional 應用）
    England stayed three points behind Spain with a home win and would advance to the last four if they do not lose their next game at home to Croatia by eight or more goals.
    此句表達對未來可能發生狀況的推測或條件句型，使用了「would + 原形動詞」搭配「if」子句，用來描述在達成特定條件時將會產生的結果。
  • 分詞構句（用現在分詞表達附帶結果或動作）
    On Tuesday, as he equaled the England appearance record held by goalkeeper Peter Shilton with a 125th cap, Kane stretched his national scoring record to 91 with another double.
    句中的「held by goalkeeper Peter Shilton」是過去分詞片語當作後置定語，用來修飾前面的名詞「the England appearance record」，省略了「which was」，使句子更精簡流暢。
  • 被動語態
    England were presented with their opener after 27 minutes when Vladimir Coufal, perhaps imagining an attacker behind him, smashed an England cross into his own net.
    句首使用「were presented with」（被贈予、獲得）的被動語態結構，強調英格蘭隊「被動地」從對手的失誤中獲得了第一顆進球，符合新聞報導客觀敘述事件的語氣。
