文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 / 補充說明
    Taiwan is advancing its drone diplomacy by donating 145 uncrewed aerial vehicles (UAV) to diplomatic allies and establishing overseas branches of the “Global UAV Academy” (GUAVA), the Ministry of Foreign Affairs said.
    句中的 "by donating... and establishing..." 是由介系詞 by 領導動名詞片語，用來表示「透過……方式」達成前面的動作。此外，逗點後的 "the Ministry of Foreign Affairs said" 是新聞英文常見的插入句，用來標明訊息來源。
  • 分詞構句表附帶結果或補充說明
    Drones can be integrated into areas such as smart agriculture, coast guard operations, disaster prevention, medical deliveries, digital governance and sovereign artificial intelligence, making them a key technology to help Taiwan’s diplomatic allies move from simply maintaining ties with Taiwan to achieving greater prosperity, it said.
    逗點後的 "making them..." 是現在分詞 (making) 所引導的分詞構句，用來接續前面子句所引發的結果（使得無人機成為一項關鍵技術）。這種結構在商業與新聞英文中常用來精簡表達因果或接續關係。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    It has purchased about 145 drones in two categories — training and multifunctional models — which would be donated to diplomatic allies in stages, it said.
    句中的 "training and multifunctional models" 是名詞短語的同位語，用破折號夾在中間補充說明 145 架無人機的兩大類別。而後方的 "which would be donated..." 則是非限定關係代間子句，用來補充說明這些無人機將分階段捐贈。
  • 動詞不定詞片語表目的
    The ministry established a Drone Diplomacy Task Force in October last year to bring together resources from the government, industry and academia, it said.
    句尾的 "to bring together resources..." 是不定詞片語（to + 原形動詞），在此處作為副詞用，用來修飾前面的動詞「成立 (established)」，表達成立該工作小組的「目的」是為了整合政府、產業與學術界的資源。
