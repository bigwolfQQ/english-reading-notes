文法重點

  • 分詞片語表附帶狀況（with 分詞 / 形容詞片語）
    Taiwan is expected to retain higher-value server activities such as GPU board production and next-generation product development even as more manufacturing capacity moves overseas, a Taiwanese industry analyst said Tuesday.
    句中的 "even as more manufacturing capacity moves overseas" 是一個由連接詞 "even as"（即使在……的同時）引導的副詞子句，用來表達轉折與時間背景，常出現在多益商業新聞中描述大環境趨勢。
  • 過去分詞片語作後置定修飾語（分詞後置）
    MIC forecasts global server shipments will grow 12.4 percent in 2027, driven by continued investment in AI infrastructure by cloud service providers and AI model developers.
    句尾的 ", driven by..." 是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的整個子句（或其結果），等於 "which is driven by..." 的簡化，表被動與原因關係，是多益閱讀測驗中常見的高級句構。
  • 被動語態（Passive Voice）
    AI server shipments alone are projected to rise 17.8 percent to 5.4 million units that year.
    商業財經新聞為了客觀報導預測數據或官方說法，經常使用被動語態 "are projected to..."（被預估/預計……），這也是多益文法題（動詞語態與字彙搭配）的最愛考點之一。
