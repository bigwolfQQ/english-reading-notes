Taipei Times · 2026-09-17

無能治理的代價

Costs of incompetent governance

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • shirk v. 逃避、推卸（責任、義務等）
    Taipei has seen successive incidents involving the shirking of responsibility in recent years.
    台北市近年來頻頻發生推卸責任的事件。
  • crack down on phr. 取締、嚴厲打擊
    The first involves city police repeatedly failing to crack down on drug trafficking...
    第一起涉及市警局屢次未能取締販毒...
  • administration n. （政府的）行政機關、管理、政府
    ...only to realize that former Taipei mayor Ko Wen-je’s administration had made public detailed data...
    ...直到後來才發現前台北市長柯文哲的政府曾公開詳細數據...
  • conflict of interest n. phr. 利益衝突
    ...causing Chiang to take the blame for a conflict-of-interest scandal.
    ...導致蔣萬安必須為這起利益衝突醜聞背黑鍋。
  • bureaucratic adj. 官僚的、繁文縟節的
    The blame is always placed on a rigid bureaucratic system or lower-level civil servants...
    過錯總是歸咎於僵化的官僚體系或基層公務員...
  • disregard v. 忽視、不理會
    Ko blindly pursued “openness and transparency” at the time, disregarding opposition from the police...
    柯當時盲目追求「公開透明」，無視警察的反對...
  • confidential adj. 機密的、保密的
    ...insisting on publishing confidential vehicle registration data online.
    ...堅持在網路上公布機密的車輛登記數據。
  • allocate v. 分配、撥出（資金、資源等）
    If a mayor is unwilling to allocate funds for the safety of police officers...
    如果一個市長不願意為警察的安全撥款...
  • figurehead n. 有名無實的領導人、魁儡
    ...is that any different from being a mere figurehead?
    ...這跟空有其表的傀儡有什麼兩樣？
  • incompetence n. 無能、不適任
    The root cause of Taipei’s stagnation is the incompetence of leaders who refuse to take political responsibility.
    台北停滯不前的根本原因，是那些拒絕承擔政治責任的領導人的無能。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Phrase of Present Participle）作結果狀語
    ...only to realize that former Taipei mayor Ko Wen-je’s administration had made public detailed data...
    句型「only to + 動詞原形」常用於表示出乎意料的結果（意為「不料卻……」、「結果卻……」），在此處用來描述警方長期抓不到毒販，結果才發現是因為前朝政府公開了車輛詳細數據。
  • 分詞片語作後置修飾（Post-modification by Participle Phrase）
    Detectives risk their lives driving these compromised vehicles.
    句尾的「driving these compromised vehicles」是由現在分詞帶領的分詞片語，用來修飾前面的主詞 Detectives，補充說明他們冒著生命危險時的狀態（一邊開著這些被滲透的車輛）。
  • 假設語氣（Subjunctive Mood / Conditional Sentence）
    If a mayor is unwilling to allocate funds for the safety of police officers and cannot even ensure that departmental forms are properly managed, choosing instead to hide behind city councilors’ scapegoating to seek sympathy, is that any different from being a mere figurehead?
    此句使用條件句（If 子句用現在式表達假設情況，主句用疑問句），探討市長若不作為、只會找藉口時，其職位是否形同虛設，藉此加強對領導階層無能的批判力道。
