機場預估連假期間將迎來 73.8 萬人次旅客

Airport expects 738,000 over holiday period

單字解析

  • average v. / adj. 平均為；平均的
    with daily passenger traffic averaging about 147,000, Taoyuan International Airport Corp said yesterday.
    桃園國際機場公司昨日表示，每日客流量平均約有 147,000 人次。
  • peak v. / n. 達到高峰；頂峰、高峰
    Passenger traffic is expected to peak at more than 150,000 tomorrow and on Friday
    客流量預計將在明天和週五達到 150,000 人次以上的高峰
  • depart v. 出發、離開
    as people depart for the holiday
    隨著民眾為度假而啟程
  • inbound adj. 入境的、返程的
    while inbound traffic is forecast to peak on Sunday
    而入境客流量則預計在週日達到高峰
  • forecast v. / n. 預測、預報
    while inbound traffic is forecast to peak on Sunday
    而入境客流量則預計在週日達到高峰
  • sufficient adj. 充足的、 suficiente
    to allow sufficient time for check-in, baggage drop-off and immigration procedures
    以預留充裕的時間辦理報到、行李託運和出入境通關手續
  • procedure n. 程序、手續
    to allow sufficient time for check-in, baggage drop-off and immigration procedures
    以預留充裕的時間辦理報到、行李託運和出入境通關手續
  • facility n. 設施、設備
    Passengers are also encouraged to use digital and self-service facilities
    旅客也被鼓勵使用數位及自助化設施
  • automate v. 使自動化（句中為 automated 形容詞用法）
    and automated immigration clearance through the e-Gate system
    以及透過 e-Gate 系統進行自動通關
  • accommodate v. 容納、配合、順應
    to accommodate aerial performances for National Day events
    以配合國慶活動的空中表演
  • suspension n. 暫停、中止
    The three suspension periods are from 8:32am to 8:42am
    這三個暫停時段分別為上午 8 時 32 分至 8 時 42 分
  • urge v. 強力敦促、催促
    urging passengers to check their flight status before leaving for the airport.
    並敦促旅客在出發前往機場前先查看航班動態。
  • operational adj. 營運的、操作的
    People can check the latest flight changes and operational information on the airport’s Web site
    民眾可以在機場網站上查詢最新的航班異動與營運資訊

文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    About 738,000 passengers are expected to pass through Taiwan Taoyuan International Airport...
    新聞報導中經常使用被動語態（be + 過去分詞）來客觀地陳述預測、官方說法或統計數據，避免特定主詞，使語氣更具權威與客觀性。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Absolute Participial Phrase)
    ...with daily passenger traffic averaging about 147,000, Taoyuan International Airport Corp said yesterday.
    「with + 名詞 + V-ing」結構用來補充說明主句發生的背景或伴隨狀況，在多益閱讀與商用英文寫作中非常實用，能簡潔地表達附帶細節。
  • 關係副詞 when 引導的形容詞子句
    ...while inbound traffic is forecast to peak on Sunday, when total passenger traffic is expected to reach nearly 152,000...
    句中的「when」引導非限定關係子句，用來補充修飾前面的時間先行詞「Sunday」，並在其中接完整的子句說明當天發生的狀況。
  • 不定詞作副詞用（表目的）
    People should arrive at the airport about three hours before their scheduled departure time to allow sufficient time for check-in...
    句尾的「to allow...」是不定詞片語，置於動詞後方用來表達目的（為了要預留充裕的時間），這是多益測驗中常考句型。
