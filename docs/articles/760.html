警方懸賞緝捕張嫌

Police post reward for the arrest of Chang

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • apprehension n. 逮捕，拘捕
    Police yesterday posted a NT$1 million (US$31,399) cash reward for information leading to the apprehension of Chang Chun-chieh
    警方昨天懸賞新台幣100萬元（合31,399美元）現金，以徵求能協助逮捕張俊傑的線索
  • indict v. 起訴，控告
    Chang was indicted in June along with 23 others in a corruption case
    張俊傑於6月與其他23人一同被起訴，涉及一起貪污案
  • corruption n. 貪污，腐敗
    Chang was indicted in June along with 23 others in a corruption case involving alleged fraud over legislative aide expenses
    張俊傑於6月與其他23人一同被起訴，涉及一起涉嫌詐領立法院助理費的貪污案
  • fraud n. 詐欺，騙局
    a corruption case involving alleged fraud over legislative aide expenses and illegally importing COVID-19 test kits
    一起涉嫌詐領立法院助理費以及非法進口COVID-19快篩試劑的貪污案
  • release v. 釋放，交保
    He was released on NT$10 million bail.
    他當時以新台幣1,000萬元交保。
  • bail n. 保釋金
    He was released on NT$10 million bail.
    他當時以新台幣1,000萬元交保。
  • assistance n. 協助，幫忙
    He has used multiple forms of transportation and received assistance from people in several regions
    他使用了多種交通工具，並獲得多個地區民眾的協助
  • detain v. 扣留，拘留
    adding that police have so far launched six waves of investigations, and detained or questioned seven people suspected of helping him.
    並補充說警方迄今已發動6波搜查，並拘留或訊問了7名涉嫌協助他的人員。
  • warrant n. 令狀，授權令（如逮捕令、搜索票）
    An arrest warrant has also been issued for another suspect, Chen Wei-ta
    針對另一名嫌疑人陳威達也已發布逮捕令
  • accomplice n. 共犯，幫兇
    Investigatiors aim to determine Chang’s full escape route, the roles played by each accomplice and whether others were involved
    調查人員旨在查明張嫌的完整逃亡路線、各共犯所扮演的角色，以及是否還有其他人參與
  • whereabouts n. 行蹤，下落
    The precinct urged anyone who has seen Chang or has information about his whereabouts to contact them immediately.
    分局呼籲任何見過張嫌或掌握其行蹤相關訊息的人士立即與他們聯繫。

文法重點

  • 現在完成進行式 (Present Perfect Continuous)
    Chang Chun-chieh (...) who has been on the run for 10 days after cutting off an electronic bracelet.
    用來表示動作從過去某個時間點開始（逃跑），持續到現在一直在進行，且可能還會持續下去。結構為 has/have + been + V-ing。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    An arrest warrant has also been issued for another suspect, Chen Wei-ta (陳威達), who is suspected of meeting with Chang in a public restroom where the two broke Chang’s monitor and fled separately...
    句中的過去分詞片語 suspected of... 用來修飾前面的 suspect（嫌疑人），相當於完整的形容詞子句 who is suspected of...
  • 使役動詞/感官動詞搭配分詞 (Participial complement after verbs)
    Investigatiors aim to determine Chang’s full escape route, the roles played by each accomplice and whether others were involved in planning, directing or assisting his escape...
    介系詞 in 後面接多個動名詞（planning, directing, assisting）並列，用來補充說明參與的具體細節內容。
