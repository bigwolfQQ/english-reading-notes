社論：太平洋島國論壇有台灣出席是朝正確方向邁出的一步

EDITORIAL: PIF presence step in right direction

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • presence n. 出席，存在
    Unlike last year, Taiwan this time had a strong presence at the event, in a diplomatic setback to China.
    與去年不同，台灣這次在活動中展現了強大的參與度，這對中國來說是一次外交挫敗。
  • participation n. 參與，參加
    Ahead of the event, the Chinese Ministry of Foreign Affairs asked the PIF and Palau to adhere to the “one China” principle in handling Taiwan’s participation.
    在活動前夕，中國外交部要求 PIF 和帛琉在處理台灣的參與時堅持「一個中國」原則。
  • retaliation n. 報復
    Then, in retaliation against Ngerulmud’s decision to invite Taipei, the Chinese Ministry of Culture and Tourism warned its citizens to be wary of traveling to Palau.
    隨後，為了報復恩格魯穆德邀請台北的決定，中國文化和旅遊部警告其公民對前往帛琉旅遊保持警惕。
  • delegation n. 代表團
    As Palauan President Surangel Whipps Jr had insisted on the forum’s autonomous right to invite non-member partners, Taiwan sent a large delegation, led by Minister of Foreign Affairs Lin Chia-lung (林佳龍).
    由於帛琉總統惠恕仁堅持論壇邀請非會員夥伴的自主權，台灣派遣了一個由外交部長林佳龍率領的大型代表團。
  • statement n. 聲明，陳述
    Taiwan and its three diplomatic allies in the region — Palau, Tuvalu and the Marshall Islands — signed a joint statement pledging to work more closely.
    台灣與其在該地區的三個邦交國——帛琉、吐瓦魯和馬紹爾群島——簽署了一份聯合聲明，誓言更緊密地合作。
  • funding n. 資金，經費
    US Deputy Secretary of State Christopher Landau also announced more than US$150 million in funding to support nations in the region.
    美國副國務卿克里斯多福·蘭道也宣布提供超過 1.5 億美元的資金來支持該地區的國家。
  • cooperation n. 合作
    Meanwhile, Australia pledged a A$600 million (US$432.54 million) package to help Pacific island nations combat transnational drug trafficking, and signed defense and cooperation agreements with countries in the region to limit China’s influence.
    同時，澳洲承諾提供 6 億澳元（約 4.3254 億美元）的一攬子計畫，以幫助太平洋島國打擊跨國毒品走私，並與該地區國家簽署了防禦與合作協議，以限制中國的影響力。
  • commit v. 承諾，奉獻
    During the PIF, the US in a statement pledged its commitment to the security and prosperity of Pacific island nations.
    在 PIF 期間，美國在一份聲明中承諾致力於太平洋島國的安全與繁榮。
  • compete v. 競爭
    Taiwan cannot compete with China in terms of financial diplomacy or military coercion.
    台灣在金融外交或軍事脅迫方面無法與中國競爭。
  • partner n. 夥伴
    Taipei should instead offer help that would not cause small countries to lose autonomy, make allies feel the value of cooperation and show the world that Taiwan could be a partner in Pacific governance.
    相反地，台北應該提供不會導致小國失去自主權的協助，讓盟友感受到合作的價值，並向世界證明台灣可以成為太平洋治理的夥伴。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附加說明（補充結果）
    Unlike last year, Taiwan this time had a strong presence at the event, in a diplomatic setback to China.
    句尾的「in a diplomatic setback to China」或類似的分詞/介系詞結構常被用來補充說明主句所帶來的影響或結果，此處用來形容台灣高調出席對中國造成的附帶外交挫敗。
  • 過去分詞作後位修飾
    As Palauan President Surangel Whipps Jr had insisted on the forum’s autonomous right to invite non-member partners, Taiwan sent a large delegation, led by Minister of Foreign Affairs Lin Chia-lung (林佳龍).
  • 關係代名詞子句（限定修飾）
    China’s deepening cooperation with Pacific island nations in policing, security and infrastructure has also alerted Western democracies that the Pacific region — which is home to strategic facilities and resources... — has become the front line of strategic jockeying in the Indo-Pacific region.
    句中的「which is home to...」是一個非限定性關係子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「the Pacific region」，提供關於該地區擁有豐富戰略資源的額外資訊。
