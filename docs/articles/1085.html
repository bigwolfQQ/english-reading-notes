中秋節賞月有望，儘管預期有風暴來襲

Mid-Autum Festival moon to be visible, despite expected storm

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • favorable adj. 有利的、贊同的
    Most of Taiwan should expect partly cloudy to sunny weather over the holiday this week, with favorable conditions to view the moon across Taiwan on the night of Mid-Autumn Festival tomorrow, the Central Weather Administration (CWA) said yesterday.
    中央氣象署（CWA）昨天表示，這週假期台灣大部分地區預期為多雲到晴的天氣，明天中秋節當晚全台各地都有利於賞月。
  • projected adj. 預計的、預測的
    It is projected to continue moving west-northwest to reach waters south of Taiwan before pivoting north on Saturday, the agency said.
    氣象署表示，預測它將持續向西北西移動，到達台灣南方海域，然後在週六轉向北。
  • gradually adv. 逐漸地
    The storm is expected to come closest to Taiwan on Sunday and Monday while remaining at moderate strength, and then gradually weaken as it moves north.
    預期該風暴將在週日和週一最接近台灣，同時維持中等強度，隨著北移而逐漸減弱。
  • scattered adj. 分散的、零星的
    It would bring scattered showers from Taoyuan northward and Yilan County on Monday and Tuesday.
    它將在週一和週二為桃園以北及宜蘭縣帶來零星陣雨。
  • isolated adj. 孤立的、零星的、個別的
    Tomorrow, windward areas including the north coast of Keelung, greater Taipei, eastern Taiwan and the Hengchun Peninsula could see isolated brief showers.
    明天包括基隆北海岸、大台北、台灣東部及恆春半島等迎風面地區可能出現零星短暫陣雨。
  • experience v. 經歷、體驗
    By today, Keelung’s northern coastline, the eastern half of Taiwan proper and mountainous areas around Taipei should experience sporadic showers, with afternoon rains expected throughout Taiwan proper, the CWA said.
    氣象署表示，到了今天，基隆北海岸、台灣本島東半部及台北周邊山區應有零星陣雨，台灣本島各地則預期有午後雷陣雨。
  • exceed v. 超過、勝過
    The week’s midday temperature could exceed 30°C, and parts of Taiwan could experience temperatures of about 33.5°C, the CWA said.
    氣象署表示，這週的午間氣溫可能超過30°C，台灣部分地區氣溫可能達到約33.5°C。
  • period n. 期間、時期
    For Hsinchu and Miaoli counties, a lack of clouds could lead to cool weather, with morning temperatures of less than 20°C, resulting in large temperature swings during the same period, it said.
    氣象署指出，對新竹縣和苗栗縣而言，少雲可能導致天氣轉涼，清晨氣溫低於20°C，從而在同期造成較大的溫差。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Postmodifier)
    It would then move northeast passing over waters east of Taiwan and toward Japan’s Ryukyu Islands, it said.
    句中的 "passing over..." 是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明主詞 "It" 移動時的動作狀態，相當於副詞子句 "and passed over..." 的簡化，常出現在新聞英語中以求簡潔流暢。
  • 對等連接詞連接的附屬子句與現在分詞並用
    The storm is expected to come closest to Taiwan on Sunday and Monday while remaining at moderate strength, and then gradually weaken as it moves north.
    句中的 "while remaining..." 是一個省略了主詞與be動詞的分詞構句（原意為 while it remains），用來表達同時發生的狀態；"as it moves north" 則是表示伴隨時間變化的副詞子句。
  • 現在分詞引導結果狀語 (Present Participle indicating Result)
    At the same time, the highest tide in Penghu County is expected to coincide with the typhoon’s rains and winds, resulting in flooding risks, it said.
    句尾的 ", resulting in..." 是現在分詞作結果狀語，表示前面「滿潮與風雨 coincide（同時發生）」這個事件所自然導致的結果（造成淹水風險），多益考試中非常常考 result in / lead to 這類因果表達。
