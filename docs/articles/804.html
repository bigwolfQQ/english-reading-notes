文法重點

  • be expected to (預期會……/被期待……)
    CTBC Financial Holding Co. subsidiary Taiwan Life Insurance Co. is expected to sign a NT$60 billion (US$1.89 billion) build-operate-transfer (BOT) contract...
    「be expected to + 原形動詞」是多益閱讀與商務新聞中極常見的句構，用來表示客觀預期、官方排程或大眾預估將會發生的事情，相當於 likely to。
  • 非限定關代子句 (逗號後接 which)
    ...Es Con Field Hokkaido, the Fighters' home stadium, which opened in 2023 and combines baseball facilities with hotels, shopping, dining...
    句中的「which opened in 2023 and combines...」前有逗號，屬於非限定關係代名詞子句，用來補充說明前面的專有名詞 Es Con Field Hokkaido，且不可使用 that 代替 which。
  • 分詞當形容詞修飾名詞 (V-ing / p.p.)
    According to the city government, in addition to an international-standard baseball stadium that can also host concerts, the complex... will include two hotels and a luxury shopping mall covering around 33,000 square meters.
    句尾的「covering around 33,000 square meters」是現在分詞片語當作後置形容詞，用來修飾前面的 shopping mall（意指「佔地……的購物商場」），原句等於「...a luxury shopping mall which covers around 33,000 square meters」。
