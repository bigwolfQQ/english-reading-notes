文法重點

  • With + 名詞 + 分詞（獨立分詞構句）
    With the Taiex, the Taiwan Stock Exchange's benchmark index, repeatedly hitting a fresh high, the local bourse ranked as the fourth-largest stock market...
    「With + O. + V-ing/P.P.」結構常用於表達附带狀況，在此句中用來補充說明台股指數屢創新高（hitting）的背景情況，常譯為「隨著...」。
  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 / 補充說明結果
    ...pushing up the market capitalization of the local main board to about US$5.3 trillion, MarketCapWatch's data showed.
    句尾的「pushing up...」是現在分詞片語作結果狀語，用來補充前面股市創新高所帶來的自然結果（推升了市值）。
  • 所有格關代（whose）引導的非限定子句
    As of 5 p.m. Wednesday, Taiwan replaced India, whose market value stood at US$4.94 trillion, to become the fourth-largest stock market in the world...
    「whose market value...」為所有格關係代間詞引導的非限定子句，用來補充說明前面先行詞 India 的市值狀況，逗號後可譯為「其市值為...」。
  • 現在分詞短語表伴隨結果（動態持續）
    The platform also compiles total market capitalization rankings for various countries and major stock markets, allowing users to observe the size of global capital markets...
    句中的「allowing users to...」是由現在分詞構成的結果狀語，表示前面「編製排名」的這個動作，進一步「允許/讓」使用者能夠觀察市場規模。
