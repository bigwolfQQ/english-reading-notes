Three arrested, one at large after fatal drug debt row in Kaohsiung, police say

單字解析

  • suspect n. 嫌疑人
    Three suspects have been arrested and one remains at large after allegedly getting into a fight over drugs
    在據稱因毒品引發衝突後，三名嫌疑人已被逮捕，一人仍然在逃
  • incident n. 事件，事故
    The Kaohsiung Police Department said the incident occurred in the early hours yesterday at an out-of-business health spa
    高雄市政府警察局表示，這起事件發生在昨天凌晨，地點位於一家歇業的養生館
  • conflict n. 衝突，爭端
    when a conflict broke out and they were attacked with clubs and knives, police said
    警方表示，隨後爆發衝突，並遭到棍棒與刀械攻擊
  • multiple adj. 多個的，多重的
    Lin and Chuang also suffered multiple injuries and were taken to an area hospital, police said
    警方表示，林男和莊男也受到多處傷害，被送往地區醫院
  • negotiate v. 談判，協商
    The two groups met to negotiate at about 2am at the spa
    雙方人馬約在凌晨 2 點於養生館碰面談判
  • accomplice n. 幫兇，同夥
    The Tung brothers and their accomplices allegedly came armed with knives and clubs
    童姓兄弟及其同夥據稱帶著刀械和棍棒前來
  • dispute n. 爭端，糾紛
    The incident is believed to have stemmed from a dispute over drug debts
    這起事件據信源於毒品債務糾紛
  • ongoing adj. 進行中的
    adding that investigation was ongoing
    並補充說調查仍在進行中
  • repay v. 償還，還錢
    who were allegedly upset about being pushed to repay about NT$10,000
    他們據稱對於被催促償還約新台幣 10,000 元感到不滿
  • specify v. 具體說明，明確指出
    though it did not specify why that location was chosen
    不過報導並未說明為何選擇該地點

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附加說明（分詞構句）
    Three suspects have been arrested and one remains at large after allegedly getting into a fight over drugs, killing two men and injuring a third, local authorities said.
    句尾的「killing two men and injuring a third」為現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前面主句動作所產生的直接結果（造成兩人死亡、一人受傷）。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    The Kaohsiung Police Department said the incident occurred in the early hours yesterday at an out-of-business health spa on Baotai Road in Fongshan District.
    片語「out-of-business」或過去分詞形成的複合形容詞用來修飾後方的名詞 health spa（養生館），在新聞英語中常簡潔地用來表達狀態。
  • 被動語態的應用
    During the brawl, Lee suffered serious injuries to the head, lost consciousness and was later declared dead.
    句中的「was later declared dead」使用了被動語態（be + 過去分詞），強調李男「被」醫生或相關權威宣告死亡的客觀事實。
  • 連接詞 when 引導的時間子句（意指「當...之際/就在當時」）
    Three male friends... met at the location to discuss a drug debt with another group of men, when a conflict broke out and they were attacked with clubs and knives, police said.
    此處的 when 不僅代表時間，還帶有「就在那時（突然發生另一件事）」的轉折語氣，常出現於敘述突發事件的報導中。
  • 動名詞片語作為介系詞的受詞
    Using surveillance footage, police tracked down four suspects in the attack...
    句首的「Using surveillance footage」是現在分詞形成的片語，作為整句的分詞構句，帶有條件或方法意味（藉由使用監視器畫面）。
