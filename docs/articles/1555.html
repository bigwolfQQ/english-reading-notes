臭蟲肆虐迫使澳洲航空停飛客機

Bedbug infestation forces Qantas to ground airplane

單字解析

  • ground v. 使（飛機）停飛，禁飛
    Qantas has been forced to ground a plane in Rome after bedbugs were discovered on the aircraft on its way from Perth, a source with knowledge of the incident said yesterday.
    根據知情人士昨日透露，在從伯斯飛往羅馬的航班上發現臭蟲後，澳洲航空（Qantas）被迫將一架飛機停飛在羅馬。
  • incident n. 事件，事故
    Qantas has been forced to ground a plane in Rome after bedbugs were discovered on the aircraft on its way from Perth, a source with knowledge of the incident said yesterday.
    根據知情人士昨日透露，在從伯斯飛往羅馬的航班上發現臭蟲後，澳洲航空（Qantas）被迫將一架飛機停飛在羅馬。
  • eradicate v. 根除，消滅
    Bedbugs are small insects that feed on the blood of some mammals, including humans, which can be hard to eradicate.
    臭蟲是小型昆蟲，以包括人類在內的一些哺乳動物的血液為食，且很難根除。
  • cancelation n. 取消
    As first reported by the Sydney Morning Herald, the aircraft had to be taken out of service for cleaning after its arrival in the Italian capital, resulting in the cancelation of the Qantas Rome to Perth service on Saturday.
    正如《雪梨晨鋒報》最先報導的那樣，該飛機抵達義大利首都後必須停飛進行清潔，導致澳洲航空星期六從羅馬飛往伯斯的航班被迫取消。
  • maintenance n. 維護，保養
    A Qantas spokesperson said the flight was canceled to allow its engineers and maintenance teams to carry out a deep clean of the aircraft after “an issue identified on a small number of economy seats.”
    澳洲航空發言人表示，在「在一小部分經濟艙座位上發現問題」後，取消航班是為了讓工程師和維修團隊對飛機進行深度清潔。
  • apologize v. 道歉
    The airline wanted to “sincerely apologize” to customers for the disruption, they said.
    該航空公司表示，他們想就此次航班中斷向顧客「致以誠摯的歉意」。
  • disruption n. 中斷，擾亂
    The airline wanted to “sincerely apologize” to customers for the disruption, they said.
    該航空公司表示，他們想就此次航班中斷向顧客「致以誠摯的歉意」。
  • accommodation n. 住宿
    Affected passengers were given accommodation and rebooked on alternative flights within 48 hours of their original departure, Qantas said.
    澳洲航空表示，受影響的乘客獲提供住宿，並在原定出發時間的 48 小時內被重新安排到替代航班。
  • alternative adj. 替代的，其他的
    Affected passengers were given accommodation and rebooked on alternative flights within 48 hours of their original departure, Qantas said.
    澳洲航空表示，受影響的乘客獲提供住宿，並在原定出發時間的 48 小時內被重新安排到替代航班。
  • precautionary adj. 預防的，警戒的
    Its aircraft cabins were cleaned regularly and to an extremely high standard and treated with disinfectant every 50 days as a standard precautionary cleaning measure, the airline said.
    該航空公司表示，作為一項標準的預防性清潔措施，其飛機客房均按極高標準定期清潔，並每 50 天使用消毒劑進行處理。

文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Qantas has been forced to ground a plane in Rome after bedbugs were discovered on the aircraft...
    句中使用了現在完成式的被動語態「has been forced」與過去式的被動語態「were discovered」，強調動作的承受者（Qantas、bedbugs）而非動作的執行者，在新聞英語中常用來客觀陳述事件。
  • 非限定性關係子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Bedbugs are small insects that feed on the blood of some mammals, including humans, which can be hard to eradicate.
    句尾的「which can be hard to eradicate」前方帶有逗號，是用來補充說明前面整件事（即臭蟲很難根除），which 在此作為關係代名詞指代前文的概念。
  • 分詞構句表結果 (Participle Phrase indicating Result)
    ...the aircraft had to be taken out of service for cleaning after its arrival in the Italian capital, resulting in the cancelation of the Qantas Rome to Perth service on Saturday.
    句尾的「resulting in...」是由現在分詞帶動的分詞構句，用來接續表達前面飛機停飛清理這件事所帶來的自然結果（導致航班取消）。
  • 過去分詞作後置修飾語 (Past Participle as Post-modifier)
    Affected passengers were given accommodation...
    過去分詞「Affected」（受影響的）放在名詞 passengers 前方作形容詞使用，修飾乘客，這在商業與新聞英文中非常常見。
