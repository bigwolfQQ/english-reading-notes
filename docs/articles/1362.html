魁北克分離主義者為總理製造了新考驗

Quebec separatists create new test for PM

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • narrowly adv. 險勝、勉強地
    A separatist party on Monday night narrowly won Quebec’s election...
    一個分離主義政黨在週一晚上的選舉中險勝...
  • provisional adj. 臨時的、暫時的
    Provisional results showed the predominantly French-speaking province of 9 million people had opted for the Parti Quebecois...
    初步結果顯示，這個擁有 900 萬人口、主要說法語的省份選擇了魁北克人黨...
  • referendum n. 全民公投、公民投票
    ...who is promising to hold a referendum on breaking from Canada once Trump leaves office.
    ...並承諾一旦川普卸任，將舉行一場關於脫離加拿大的全民公投。
  • majority n. 多數、過半數
    The PQ only won 59 of the 127 seats in the Quebec National Assembly, or five short of a majority.
    魁北克人黨在魁北克國民議會的 127 席中僅贏得 59 席，距離多數席次還差 5 席。
  • concede v. 承認、認輸
    Plamondon conceded he would need support from opposition parliamentarians to proceed.
    普拉蒙多承認，他需要反對派議員的支持才能推進此事。
  • tackle v. 處理、應對、解決
    Carney, who has stressed the need for a united front to tackle Trump’s tariffs...
    一直強調需要團結一致應對川普關稅的卡尼...
  • tariff n. 關稅
    ...tackle Trump’s tariffs, must now work with a Quebec premier who is determined to break up Canada.
    ...應對川普關稅，現在必須與一位決心分裂加拿大的魁北克省長合作。
  • uncertainty n. 不確定性
    Plamondon, citing the political uncertainty and economic damage done by the tariffs...
    普拉蒙多引述政治不確定性以及關稅造成的經濟損失...
  • prospect n. 前景、預期
    Despite the unclear referendum prospects, some PQ policies look set to boost tension with Ottawa.
    儘管公投前景不明朗，但魁北克人黨的一些政策似乎將加劇與渥太華的緊張關係。
  • vulnerable adj. 易受攻擊的、脆弱的
    “If Quebec becomes independent it would make us politically, economically and militarily vulnerable,” she said.
    她說：「如果魁北克獨立，將使我們在政治、經濟和軍事上變得脆弱。」

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後置修飾語
    ...who is promising to hold a referendum on breaking from Canada once Trump leaves office.
    句中的 "breaking from Canada" 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾介系詞 "on" 後面的受詞 "referendum"，說明公投的具體內容是關於「從加拿大獨立」。
  • 關係代名詞子句（限定用法）
    Carney, who has stressed the need for a united front to tackle Trump’s tariffs, must now work with a Quebec premier who is determined to break up Canada.
    句中包含了兩個由 "who" 引導的關係子句。第一個 "who has stressed..." 用來補充說明前方的主詞 Carney（非限定用法，用逗號隔開）；第二個 "who is determined..." 則用來限定修飾前面的受詞 "a Quebec premier"。
  • 過去分詞作後置修飾語
    Independence votes held by previous PQ governments in 1980 and 1995 failed...
    片語 "held by previous PQ governments in 1980 and 1995" 是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞 "Independence votes"。原句省略了「主格關代 + be動詞」（即 votes which were held by...），用以簡化句子結構。
  • 形容詞片語作主詞補語
    ...some PQ policies look set to boost tension with Ottawa.
    句中的 "set" 是形容詞，意為「準備好的、註定的」，在此與連綴動詞 "look" 結合，構成 "look set to..." 的結構，用來描述某些政策「看來即將」引發與聯邦政府的緊張關係。
