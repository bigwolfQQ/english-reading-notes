中國可能利用國防缺口：馬尼拉

PRC could exploit defense gaps: Manila

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • opportunity n. 機會、時機
    China could see recent cuts to US-South Korean military exercises as an opportunity to reassert itself in the Indo-Pacific region
    中國可能會將最近美韓聯合軍事演習的縮減視為在印太地區重申自身地位的機會
  • commitments n. 承諾、保證
    Washington had assured Manila that its commitments to the Philippines remained unchanged.
    華盛頓已向馬尼拉保證，其對菲律賓的承諾保持不變。
  • cooperation n. 合作
    bilateral and multilateral cooperation involving Manila continued to expand.
    涉及馬尼拉的雙邊與多邊合作持續擴大。
  • scale back v. phr. 縮減、相對減少
    US President Donald Trump’s decision last month to scale back military exercises with South Korea has prompted debate
    美國總統唐納·川普上個月決定縮減與南韓的軍事演習，引發了辯論
  • exploit v. 利用、剝削
    China is very quick to exploit gaps
    中國非常快就能利用漏洞
  • indication n. 跡象、徵兆
    there was “no indication” that the changes in South Korea would affect commitments
    「沒有跡象」表明南韓的變動會影響承諾
  • negotiating v. 談判、協商
    was negotiating a similar pact with Britain, he said.
    他說，並正與英國談判類似的條約。
  • economic adj. 經濟的
    it would be “doubtful” that any economic cooperation could be insulated from longstanding sovereignty disputes
    任何經濟合作都很難不受長期主權爭端的影響，這是「令人懷疑的」
  • suppliers n. 供應商
    South Korea, one of Manila’s largest arms suppliers.
    南韓，馬尼拉最大的武器供應商之一。
  • acquisition n. 收購、取得
    a long-discussed submarine acquisition program remained some way off.
    討論已久的潛艦採購計畫還有一段路要走。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾
    Speaking to Reuters on the sidelines of the Seoul Defense Dialogue, Teodoro said there had been no reduction in US-Philippine military activities...
    句首的「Speaking to Reuters...」是由現在分詞帶領的分詞片語，用來修飾主句的主詞 Teodoro，表示「在...的同時（或一邊...一邊）」，等同於副詞子句 As he spoke to Reuters... 的簡化。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    Teodoro was in Seoul for talks aimed at expanding defense cooperation with South Korea...
    片語「aimed at expanding...」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 talks（會談），相當於「which were aimed at expanding...」的關係子句省略，用以精簡語句。
  • 假設語氣（Could 與 過去式的運用）
    “If we’re talking about the Indo-Pacific as a whole, then it may signal some opportunity for China to reassert itself,” he said.
    此句使用連接詞 if引導的條件句（If we’re talking...），搭配主要子句中的助動詞 may，表達對未來可能性或推測的語氣，符合新聞報導中對地緣政治情勢分析的常見句型。
