懸賞新台幣100萬元徵求線索以逮捕在逃前助理

NT$1M reward offered for information leading to arrest of fugitive aide

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • arrest n. / v. 逮捕
    Police have offered a NT$1 million (US$31,400) reward for information leading to the arrest of Chang Chun-chieh
    警方懸賞新台幣100萬元（合31,400美元），以徵求有助於逮捕張俊傑的線索。
  • reward n. 懸賞金、獎勵
    The NT$1 million reward will be paid if the information leads to Chang's arrest, police said.
    警方表示，若提供的線索能成功逮捕張男，將支付這筆新台幣100萬元的懸賞金。
  • fugitive adj. / n. 逃亡的、逃犯
    NT$1M reward offered for information leading to arrest of fugitive aide
    懸賞新台幣100萬元徵求線索以逮捕在逃前助理
  • investigation n. 調查
    ...or call the Criminal Investigation Bureau at 02-27661919 or 02-27668989.
    ...或撥打刑事警察局的電話02-27661919或02-27668989。
  • accomplice n. 共犯、幫兇
    An arrest warrant has also been issued for a missing alleged accomplice, surnamed Chen (陳)...
    針對一名在逃的陳姓共犯，已發布通緝令...
  • suspect v. / n. 懷疑、嫌疑人
    ...who is suspected of helping Chang damage and remove his electronic monitoring device, police added.
    警方補充說，該名共犯涉嫌協助張男破壞並移除電子監控設備。
  • frequently adv. 頻繁地、經常
    A source familiar with the case told CNA that Chang frequently changed clothes and vehicles to avoid detection.
    一名熟悉此案的消息人士告訴中央社，張男頻繁更換衣服和車子以躲避偵查。
  • attempt n. / v. 嘗試、企圖
    ...in an apparent attempt to blend into crowds, the source said.
    消息人士指出，...顯然是企圖混入人群中。
  • indict v. 起訴、控告
    Kao Chin, Chang and 22 others were indicted June 8 on corruption and fraud-related charges...
    高金素梅、張俊傑等22人於6月8日遭到起訴，罪名包括貪汙及詐欺相關罪嫌...
  • embezzlement n. 挪用公款、侵占
    ...charges involving the embezzlement of public funds and importation of unapproved COVID-19 test kits from China.
    ...涉及侵占公款以及從中國進口未經核準的COVID-19快篩試劑。
  • fraudulently adv. 欺詐地、騙取地
    Kao Chin... was accused of fraudulently obtaining more than NT$7.87 million in public funds...
    高金素梅...被控透過不實申報詐領超過新台幣787萬元的公款...
  • subsidy n. 補助金、津貼
    ...through false claims for legislative assistant salaries, bonuses, overtime pay and child care subsidies.
    ...透過虛報國會助理薪資、獎金、加班費及托育補助。
  • release v. / n. 釋放、交保
    Chang... was released on NT$10 million bail in June.
    張俊傑於6月以新台幣1,000萬元交保。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Police have offered a NT$1 million (US$31,400) reward for information leading to the arrest of Chang Chun-chieh...
    句中的 "leading to the arrest of Chang Chun-chieh" 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "information"（意即「促成...的線索」），相當於形容詞子句 "that leads to the arrest..."。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    An arrest warrant has also been issued for a missing alleged accomplice...
    句型使用現在完成式的被動語態 "has been issued"（已被發布），強調動作的承受者（An arrest warrant，逮捕令），而動作的執行者（警方）在此處省略或不重要。
  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況或結果 (Participial Phrase indicating circumstance or result)
    ...Chang traveled to the city's Wanhua and Ximending areas before visiting night markets in Taoyuan and Taichung in an apparent attempt to blend into crowds, the source said.
    句尾的 "in an apparent attempt to..." 雖然是介係詞片語，但動詞ing開頭的慣用語常用於表達目的或伴隨狀態。在新聞英語中，常透過分詞或介係詞片語精簡冗長的副詞子句，使句子更緊湊。
  • 關係代間詞非限定用法 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Kao Chin, who has denied wrongdoing, was accused of fraudulently obtaining more than NT$7.87 million in public funds...
    逗點後的 ", who has denied wrongdoing, " 是非限定用法的關係子句，對先行詞 Kao Chin 提供額外的補充說明。若將此子句拿掉，主要子句的意思依然完整。
