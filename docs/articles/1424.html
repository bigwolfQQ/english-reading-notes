南韓暗示將縮減運動員兵役特權

S Korea signals military exemption cuts for athletes

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • exemption n. 免除，豁免
    The reward of a military service exemption for gold medal-winning South Korean athletes could be abolished or reduced in future after public backlash to some controversial comments from winners at the Asian Games.
    由於大眾對亞運會獲勝者的一些爭議性言論感到強烈不滿，南韓未來可能會廢除或減少奪金南韓運動員的兵役免除獎勵。
  • abolish v. 廢除，廢止
    The reward of a military service exemption for gold medal-winning South Korean athletes could be abolished or reduced in future after public backlash to some controversial comments from winners at the Asian Games.
    由於大眾對亞運會獲勝者的一些爭議性言論感到強烈不滿，南韓未來可能會廢除或減少奪金南韓運動員的兵役免除獎勵。
  • mandatory adj. 強制的，法定的
    All three apologized, but a broader debate had already reignited over whether athletes should receive special treatment for sporting achievements, particularly as ordinary citizens — and even members of BTS, the globally famous K-pop group — had to complete their mandatory service.
    這三人全都進行了道歉，但關於運動員是否應因體育成就而獲得特殊待遇的更廣泛辯論已經重新燃起，特別是普通公民——甚至連全球知名的 K-pop 團體 BTS 成員——都必須完成他們的強制服役。
  • alternative adj. 替代的，另類的
    Criticism intensified on Tuesday and, yesterday, South Korean Minister of Culture, Sports and Tourism Choi Hwi-young told the National Assembly’s Culture, Sports and Tourism Committee that the alternative military service exemption system for artists and athletes “will inevitably have to be scaled back.”
    週二批評聲浪加劇，昨日南韓文化體育觀光部長崔輝永向國會文化體育觀光委員會表示，針對藝術家和運動員的替代性兵役免除制度「將不可避免地遭到縮減」。
  • inevitably adv. 不可避免地
    Criticism intensified on Tuesday and, yesterday, South Korean Minister of Culture, Sports and Tourism Choi Hwi-young told the National Assembly’s Culture, Sports and Tourism Committee that the alternative military service exemption system for artists and athletes “will inevitably have to be scaled back.”
    週二批評聲浪加劇，昨日南韓文化體育觀光部長崔輝永向國會文化體育觀光委員會表示，針對藝術家和運動員的替代性兵役免除制度「將不可避免地遭到縮減」。
  • contribute to phr. v. 對……有貢獻，促成
    “It is true that the system has contributed to helping outstanding artists and athletes maintain and develop their abilities,” Cho said.
    趙部長表示：「該制度確實有助於協助傑出的藝術家和運動員保持並發展他們的能力。」
  • comprehensively adv. 全面地，徹底地
    “I agree that the system should be comprehensively reviewed once again.”
    「我同意應該再次對該制度進行全面檢討。」
  • qualify v. 取得資格，符合條件
    The soccer players were among many winners from the Asian Games who qualified for the military exemption.
    這些足球員是亞運會眾多獲得兵役豁免資格的得獎者之一。
  • ultimately adv. 最終，歸根結底
    “Ultimately, we have plans to cut back or scrap the supplementary service, including one designed for artists and athletes,” Chung said.
    鄭彬娜表示：「最終，我們計劃削減或廢除補充役，包括專為藝術家和運動員設計的補充役。」

文法重點

  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense)
    ...a broader debate had already reignited over whether athletes should receive special treatment...
    表示在過去某一時間點（球員道歉）之前已經發生並持續影響的動作，用助動詞 had 加上過去分詞 reignited。
  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    ...including Yang Min-hyuk, who assisted the winning goal against Japan, made comments that were viewed as insensitive.
    句中的 assisting 或關係代間子句 who assisted用來修飾前面的陽民赫，而分詞短語也能精簡修飾名詞，此處為關係代名詞子句的運用。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    ...comments that were viewed as insensitive.
    由「be 動詞 (were) + 過去分詞 (viewed)」構成，強調動作承受者（評論），且省略了主動者（被大眾認為）。
  • 地方副詞片語置於句首的倒裝結構 (Inversion with Fronted Adverbial Phrase)
    So are three South Koreans who won gold in e-sports, an event at the Asian Games.
    「So + be動詞/助動詞 + 主詞」結構，表示「……也是如此」，用來承接前一句肯定句的狀態，避免重複敘述。
