中央氣象署：大雨週一將趨緩，迎來多雲到晴的天氣

Heavy rain to make way for mainly dry weather Monday: CWA

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • issue v. 發布、發行
    The CWA issued heavy rain advisories for Taitung County, coastal areas of New Taipei, and mountainous areas of Taipei and Taichung on Sunday...
    中央氣象署於週日針對台東縣、新北市沿海地區以及台北和台中山區發布大雨特報...
  • advisory n. 特報、公告、警報
    The CWA issued heavy rain advisories for Taitung County, coastal areas of New Taipei, and mountainous areas of Taipei and Taichung on Sunday...
    中央氣象署於週日針對台東縣、新北市沿海地區以及台北和台中山區發布大雨特報...
  • indicate v. 顯示、指出、象徵
    ...indicating rainfall of more than 80 millimeters in 24 hours or 40 mm in one hour.
    ...這代表24小時累積雨量將超過80毫米，或1小時雨量超過40毫米。
  • experience v. 經歷、體驗
    The CWA said that these areas could experience sudden heavy downpours...
    氣象署表示，這些地區可能會出現突發性的大雷雨...
  • warn v. 警告、提醒
    ...and warned of landslides, falling rocks and rapidly rising water levels in mountainous areas.
    ...並警告山區應防範山崩、落石及溪水暴漲。
  • persistent adj. 持續的、堅持不懈的
    As persistent rain eases on Monday, most of Taiwan is expected to see cloudy to sunny skies through Tuesday...
    隨著持續性降雨在週一減緩，全台大部分地區預計直到週二都將維持多雲到晴的天氣...
  • ease v. 減緩、減輕、放寬
    As persistent rain eases on Monday, most of Taiwan is expected to see cloudy to sunny skies through Tuesday...
    隨著持續性降雨在週一減緩，全台大部分地區預計直到週二都將維持多雲到晴的天氣...
  • strengthen v. 加強、增強
    On Wednesday, seasonal northeasterly winds will strengthen and bring more humidity to the island, the CWA said.
    氣象署表示，週三起季節性的東北風將增強，並為全台帶來更多水氣。
  • predict v. 預測、預言
    On Thursday and Friday, northern and eastern parts of the country will experience localized showers, the CWA predicted.
    氣象署預測，週四與週五北部及東半部地區將有局部陣雨。

文法重點

  • be set to (do sth)
    Heavy rain across Taiwan is set to give way to prevailing cloudy-to-sunny skies Monday...
    「be set to + 原形動詞」表示「預定、準備好、即將發生某事」，在新聞英文中常用來預告接下來即將發生的事件或天氣轉變。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾
    ...indicating rainfall of more than 80 millimeters in 24 hours or 40 mm in one hour.
    句中的分詞片語「indicating...」作後位修飾，用來補充說明前方「heavy rain advisories（大雨特報）」所代表的具體內容，相當於「which indicated...」的簡化寫法。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    ...most of Taiwan is expected to see cloudy to sunny skies through Tuesday...
    「is expected to」是被動語態的常見結構，意指「被預期、大家預料」，常用於報導中客觀陳述預測或未來的趨勢。
