副總統蕭美琴出席義大利會議

VP Hsiao attends conference in Italy

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • confirm v. 證實、確認
    Vice President Hsiao Bi-khim (蕭美琴) is visiting Italy to attend an international conference on freedom and democracy, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs said yesterday, confirming a previously unannounced trip.
    外交部昨天表示，副總統蕭美琴正在訪問義大利，以出席一場關於自由與民主的國際會議，並證實這是一趟先前未公布的行程。
  • statement n. 聲明、陳述
    The organizers invited Hsiao to attend the Second Ventotene European Conference for Freedom and Democracy, which is being held on the Italian island of Ventotene from Thursday to today, the ministry said in a statement.
    外交部在聲明中表示，主辦單位邀請蕭美琴出席從週四至今天在義大利本托特尼島舉行的第二屆本托特尼自由與民主歐洲會議。
  • accompany v. 陪同、陪伴
    She was accompanied by Minister of Foreign Affairs Lin Chia-lung (林佳龍), it said.
    聲明並指出，她由外交部長林佳龍陪同出席。
  • depart v. 出發、離開
    The Presidential Office would provide more information about the trip after Hsiao returns to Taiwan, it said, without saying when she departed Taiwan or when she would return.
    總統府表示，將在蕭副總統返台後提供更多關於此行程的資訊，但未說明她何時離開台灣或何時返還。
  • attempt n. / v. 嘗試、企圖
    The confirmation came after European Parliament Vice President Pina Picierno, the main initiator of the conference, late on Thursday announced during the event that Hsiao had arrived in Italy, despite Beijing’s attempt to prevent the trip from happening.
    在此項證實之前，身為該會議主要發起人的歐洲議會副議長皮歇爾諾於週四深夜在活動中宣布，儘管北京試圖阻止此行程發生，蕭美琴已抵達義大利。
  • participation n. 參與、參加
    Hsiao’s participation had not been announced beforehand.
    蕭美琴的參與先前並未公布。
  • confidential adj. 機密的、保密的
    “That is why today we welcomed the vice president of Taiwan, Hsiao Bi-khim, to Ventotene — her presence was kept confidential until her arrival to safeguard her participation in the Second European Ventotene Conference for Freedom and Democracy,” she wrote.
    她寫道：「這就是為什麼今天我們在本托特尼歡迎台灣副總統蕭美琴——她的出席直到抵達前都保持機密，以維護她參與第二屆本托特尼自由與民主歐洲會議的安全。」
  • safeguard v. / n. 維護、保護、保障
    “That is why today we welcomed the vice president of Taiwan, Hsiao Bi-khim, to Ventotene — her presence was kept confidential until her arrival to safeguard her participation in the Second European Ventotene Conference for Freedom and Democracy,” she wrote.
    她寫道：「這就是為什麼今天我們在本托特尼歡迎台灣副總統蕭美琴——她的出席直到抵達前都保持機密，以維護她參與第二屆本托特尼自由與民主歐洲會議的安全。」
  • negotiation n. 談判、協商
    “Freedom of dialogue is not up for negotiation with autocracies.
    對話自由不容與獨裁政權妥協。
  • executive director n. phr. 執行長
    Inter-Parliamentary Alliance on China (IPAC) executive director Luke de Pulford backed Hsiao’s participation at the event.
    對華政策跨國議會聯盟（IPAC）執行長德普福德支持蕭美琴參與該活動。
  • response n. 回應、反應
    “The only response to Beijing’s attempt to shrink the world for Taiwanese people is to show that Taiwan is welcome,” De Pulford said.
    德普福德表示：「對北京試圖縮小台灣人民世界生存空間的唯一回應，就是展現台灣是受歡迎的。」
  • national interest n. phr. 國家利益
    This is “not simply because it is in our national interest to do so, but because it is wholly wrong to allow a democracy like Taiwan to be persecuted and outcast on the international stage,” he said.
    他指出，這「不僅僅是因為這樣做符合我們的國家利益，更是因為允許像台灣這樣的民主國家在國際舞台上遭受迫害與排擠是完全錯誤的。」
  • spokesperson n. 發言人
    China has no right to dictate Hsiao’s participation in events she was invited to by the European Parliament, ministry spokesperson Hsiao Kuang-wei (蕭光偉) said.
    外交部發言人蕭光偉表示，中國無權左右蕭美琴參與受歐洲議會邀請的活動。
  • diplomatic relations n. phr. 外交關係
    China consistently opposes any form of official contact between Taiwan and countries that have diplomatic relations with China, Mao said, calling Beijing’s “one China” principle the political foundation of China-Italy and China-EU relations.
    毛寧表示，中國一貫反對台灣與和中國有外交關係的國家進行任何形式的官方接觸，並稱北京的「一個中國」原則是中義、中歐關係的政治基礎。

文法重點

  • 非限定性關係子句 (Non-defining relative clause)
    The organizers invited Hsiao to attend the Second Ventotene European Conference for Freedom and Democracy, which is being held on the Italian island of Ventotene from Thursday to today, the ministry said in a statement.
    句中的「which is being held on the Italian island of Ventotene from Thursday to today」是一個非限定性關係子句，前方帶有逗號，用來補充說明前面的會議（The Second Ventotene European Conference for Freedom and Democracy），提供額外的細節資訊，省略後也不影響主要句子的完整性。
  • 被動語態 (Passive voice)
    She was accompanied by Minister of Foreign Affairs Lin Chia-lung (林佳龍), it said.
    句型「was accompanied by」採用了被動語態。在此強調主詞蕭美琴「被」外交部長林佳龍陪同，焦點在於受動者身上，常應用於新聞報導中客觀陳述事實。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle phrase as post-modifier)
    China has no right to dictate Hsiao’s participation in events she was invited to by the European Parliament, ministry spokesperson Hsiao Kuang-wei (蕭光偉) said.
    在 events 後方省略了關代（such as that/which），直接接上「she was invited to by the European Parliament」作為形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的 events（活動），說明這些活動是她受邀參加的。
