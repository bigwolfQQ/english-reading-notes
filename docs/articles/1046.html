倫敦：英國首次從海上無人載具試射美國魚雷

US torpedo test-fired from UK sea drone for first time: London

單字解析

  • announce v. 宣布，宣告
    Britain and the United States have for the first time test-fired a torpedo from an uncrewed undersea vessel rather than a crewed submarine, the UK government announced Monday.
    英國政府於週一宣布，英國和美國首次從無人水下載具而非有人潛艦試射了魚雷。
  • statement n. 聲明，陳述
    London said in a statement the live firing took place in UK waters last week as part of the AUKUS alliance with Australia.
    倫敦在一份聲明中表示，這次實彈射擊於上週在英國海域進行，是與澳洲的「AUKUS」同盟合作的一部分。
  • alliance n. 同盟，聯盟
    London said in a statement the live firing took place in UK waters last week as part of the AUKUS alliance with Australia.
    倫敦在一份聲明中表示，這次實彈射擊於上週在英國海域進行，是與澳洲的「AUKUS」同盟合作的一部分。
  • advance n. 進步，進展
    The British government said the test “marks a major advance in the future of submarine technology” for the three countries.
    英國政府表示，這次測試「標誌著三國在未來潛艦技術上取得重大進展」.
  • significant adj. 重大的，顯著的
    It “delivers a significant breakthrough for Euro-Atlantic security and NATO’s undersea advantage”, the statement added.
    聲明並補充道，這「為歐洲-大西洋安全以及北約的水下優勢實現了重大突破」.
  • capabilities n. 能力，軍力（在此指作戰能力）
    Western countries are rushing to modernise their war-fighting capabilities with autonomous systems, in the face of threats from Russia and Iran.
    面對來自俄羅斯和伊朗的威脅，西方國家正競相利用自主系統來使其作戰能力現代化。
  • jointly adv. 共同地，聯合地
    London said the heavyweight torpedo -- jointly developed by the US and Australia -- was launched from a 12-metre (40-ft) long undersea drone manufactured in Britain and named Excalibur.
    倫敦表示，該枚由美澳共同開發的重型魚雷，是由一艘在英國製造、名為「神劍」（Excalibur）的12公尺（40英尺）長水下無人機發射的。
  • manufacture v. 製造，生產
    London said the heavyweight torpedo -- jointly developed by the US and Australia -- was launched from a 12-metre (40-ft) long undersea drone manufactured in Britain and named Excalibur.
    倫敦表示，該枚由美澳共同開發的重型魚雷，是由一艘在英國製造、名為「神劍」（Excalibur）的12公尺（40英尺）長水下無人機發射的。
  • employment n. 運用，使用（多益常考：亦可指雇用）
    “The employment of a lethal payload illustrates that uncrewed systems can play roles which extend beyond surveillance,” he told AFP.
    他告訴法新社：「致命酬載的運用說明了無人系統可以扮演超越監視的角色。」
  • partnership n. 夥伴關係，合作關係
    He will announce “a new AI and autonomy partnership” with the US that will “help the UK protect critical national infrastructure, from sea lanes to air space”, the statement added.
    聲明補充說，他將宣布與美國建立「一項新的AI與自主性夥伴關係」，這將「助英國保護從海上航線到空域的關鍵國家基礎設施」.
  • cooperation n. 合作，協力
    “This week I will be making the case for closer international cooperation on AI, a just and lasting peace in Ukraine, and renewed efforts to bring an end to the conflict in the Middle East...
    “本週我將力陳更緊密的國際人工智慧合作、烏克蘭的公正持久和平，以及重新努力結束中東衝突……”
  • confirm v. 確認，證實
    He confirmed that Britain would host the G20 summit in November 2027 in the northwestern English city of Manchester.
    他並證實，英國將於2027年11月在英格蘭西北部城市曼徹斯特主辦G20峰會。

文法重點

  • 現在完成式 (Present Perfect Tense)
    Britain and the United States have for the first time test-fired a torpedo from an uncrewed undersea vessel...
    表示過去發生的動作對現在造成的影響或持續到現在的經驗。「have ... test-fired」強調這是過去到目前為止「第一次」發生這樣的試射行動。
  • 分詞片語作後置修飾 (Participle Phrases as Post-modifiers)
    ...the heavyweight torpedo -- jointly developed by the US and Australia -- was launched from a 12-metre (40-ft) long undersea drone manufactured in Britain and named Excalibur.
    句中的「jointly developed...」與「manufactured in Britain and named...」均為過去分詞片語，分別用來修飾前方的名詞 torpedo 與 drone，省去了「which/that was」的關係代名詞與be動詞，使句子更為精簡。
  • be due to + V (預定要做某事)
    Burnham is due to have his first meeting with US President Donald Trump at the summit since becoming UK leader in July.
    「be due to」常在新聞英語中用來表示「預定、預期」在未來某個時間點發生或進行的官方行程或安排。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    He will announce “a new AI and autonomy partnership” with the US that will “help the UK protect critical national infrastructure...
    「that will help the UK protect...」是由關係代名詞 that 引導的形容詞子句，用來補充說明先行詞 partnership 的功能與特色。
  • 間接問句 / 名詞子句 (Noun Clause as Object)
    ...because what happens abroad impacts each and every one of us at home...
    「what happens abroad」是一個由 what 引導的名詞子句，在連接詞 because 後方作為動詞 impacts 的主詞，指代「海外發生的事情」。
