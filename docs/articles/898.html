研究顯示：多數台灣人覺得自己比實際年齡年輕

Most Taiwanese feel younger than their age: study

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • perceive v. 認為，察覺，感知
    More than half of Taiwanese adults perceive themselves as younger than their actual age...
    超過半數的台灣成年人認為自己比實際年齡年輕...
  • survey n. 調查
    ...a Taiwan Communication Survey (TCS) study showed.
    ...一項台灣傳播調查資料庫（TCS）的研究顯示。
  • conduct v. 實施，執行（調查或研究）
    The survey of more than 2,000 Taiwanese adults, conducted in 2021, showed that...
    這項針對 2,000 多名台灣成年人進行、於 2021 年實施的調查顯示...
  • respondent n. 受訪者
    ...57.6 percent of respondents felt younger than their actual age...
    ...57.6% 的受訪者覺得自己比實際年齡年輕...
  • match v. 相符，相配
    ...while 22.1 percent said their perceived age matched their real age...
    ...而 22.1% 的人表示他們的心理年齡與實際年齡相符...
  • relative to prep. phr. 相對於，與...相比
    ...they tend to perceive themselves as increasingly younger relative to their actual age...
    ...相對於實際年齡，他們往往會覺得自己越來越年輕...
  • assess v. 評估，評價
    ...because they have fewer points of reference for assessing their psychological age...
    ...是因為他們在評估自己心理年齡時的參考基準較少...
  • positive adj. 正面的，積極的
    ...maturity in behavior and speech is often viewed positively...
    ...行為與言談上的成熟通常被視為正面的...
  • stable adj. 穩定的
    ...because their lifestyles and self-identities are more stable...
    ...是因為他們的生活方式與自我認同更為穩定...
  • decline n. 衰退，下降
    ...such as being old-fashioned or experiencing physical decline...
    ...例如老古板或經歷身體退化...
  • maintain v. 保持，維持
    ...that maintaining a youthful mindset helps people stay young...
    ...認為保持年輕心態有助於人們常駐青春...

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾語（分詞構句的應用）
    The survey of more than 2,000 Taiwanese adults, conducted in 2021, showed that...
    句中的「conducted in 2021」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 the survey。原句省略了「which was」，原本結構為「...adults, which was conducted in 2021, showed that...」，能讓句子更簡潔。
  • while引導的對比對等子句
    ...57.6 percent of respondents felt younger than their actual age, while 22.1 percent said their perceived age matched their real age and 20.2 percent felt older than they were.
    在此句中，連接詞「while」用來連接兩個或多個同時發生但具有對比意味的情況，意指「然而、而」，常出現在多益閱讀測驗中用來呈現數據或調查結果的對比。
  • as引導的同時進行子句（隨著...）
    ...and as they grow older, they tend to perceive themselves as increasingly younger relative to their actual age...
    連接詞「as」在此處表示「隨著」，引導一個時間副詞子句（as they grow older），用來描述伴隨著年齡增長而產生的心態變化趨勢。
