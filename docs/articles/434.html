文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    At just six months old, the leaf monkey curled up inside DNP vet Thanawadee Phaichana’s rubber-gloved hand is small indeed.
    句中的「curled up inside DNP vet Thanawadee Phaichana’s rubber-gloved hand」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「the leaf monkey」（葉猴），相當於形容詞子句「that is curled up...」，省略了關代與be動詞，使句子更為精煉。
  • 現在分詞表附帶狀況或結果 (Present Participle indicating Concomitant Circumstance)
    The DNP set up a dedicated Wildlife Crime Intelligence Unit (WCU) in 2023 with three permanent staff charged with tracking and intercepting illegal purchases.
    句尾的「tracking and intercepting...」是現在分詞片語，修飾前面的「staff」，表示這些員工被指派的任務內容（包含追蹤與攔截）。
  • 虛主語 It 句型 (Introductory 'It' Construction)
    What would make a real difference? An end to the exotic pet trade, he said.
    雖然此處為問答對話，但在新聞英文中常使用虛主語句型來對焦重點。此外，句型結構「What would make a real difference?」利用疑問詞引導主詞補語，表達「什麼事情能真正帶來關鍵改變？」。
