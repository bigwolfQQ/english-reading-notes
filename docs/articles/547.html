立委敦促檢視能源供應風險

Lawmaker urges review of risk to energy supply

單字解析

  • assess v. 評估、估價
    Democratic Progressive Party Legislator Chen Kuan-ting (陳冠廷) yesterday urged the government to conduct an early energy risk assessment as Middle East tensions rise.
    民進黨立委陳冠廷昨天敦促政府，隨著中東緊張局勢升高，應及早進行能源風險評估。
  • authority n. 當局、官方；權力
    The Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Energy Administration, CPC Corp, Taiwan and Taiwan Power Co (Taipower) are among the authorities and companies Chen said should prepare.
    經濟部、能源署、台灣中油公司及台灣電力公司（台電）是陳冠廷指出應該做好準備的主管機關與企業。
  • potential adj. 潛在的
    Such preparations are necessary as the risks of shipping through the Strait of Hormuz increase, while Iran-backed Houthi militants are advancing into and around the Bab el-Mandeb Strait, potentially cutting off access to the Red Sea and Suez Canal, Chen said.
    陳冠廷表示，由於荷莫茲海峽的航運風險增加，且獲得伊朗支持的胡塞組織正在曼德海峽及周邊推進，可能切斷通往紅海與蘇伊士運河的通道，因此這些準備工作是必要的。
  • freight n. 貨運、運費
    If the situation in the Middle East continues to worsen, air and sea freight costs would increase, as would costs for the petrochemical industry, driving up domestic commodity prices, he said.
    他說，如果中東情勢持續惡化，空運與海運運費將會上揚，石化產業的成本亦然，進而推升國內物價。
  • commodity n. 商品、物資
    If the situation in the Middle East continues to worsen, air and sea freight costs would increase, as would costs for the petrochemical industry, driving up domestic commodity prices, he said.
    他說，如果中東情勢持續惡化，空運與海運運費將會上揚，石化產業的成本亦然，進而推升國內物價。
  • compile v. 彙編、編製
    CPC should compile a list of the dates, origins and routes of oil and natural gas tankers expected to deliver to Taiwan over the next few months, so it can determine which shipments are expected to cross the Strait of Hormuz or pass through the Bab el-Mandeb Strait, Chen said.
    陳冠廷表示，中油應盤點未來幾個月預計運送至台灣的油輪及天然氣船之日期、產地與航線清單，以便判定哪些貨運預期將穿越荷莫茲海峽或經過曼德海峽。
  • consult v. 諮詢、請教、查閱
    The company should also consult its contacts in the US, Australia and other non-Middle Eastern crude-producing countries to arrange possible alternative sources, he added.
    他補充說，該公司也應諮詢其在美國、澳洲及其他非中東產油國的聯繫管道，以安排可能的替代來源。
  • alternative adj. 替代的、替代方案的
    The company should also consult its contacts in the US, Australia and other non-Middle Eastern crude-producing countries to arrange possible alternative sources, he added.
    他補充說，該公司也應諮詢其在美國、澳洲及其他非中東產油國的聯繫管道，以安排可能的替代來源。
  • priority n. 優先事項、優先權
    The test should assess how long Taiwan’s reserves would last, priorities for supplying power to civilian and industrial users, and the time required to receive deliveries from alternative sources, he said.
    他說，該測試應評估台灣的儲備能維持多久、民生與工業用戶的供電優先順序，以及接收來自替代來源貨運所需的時間。
  • capacity n. 容量、產能、能力
    Being prepared would reduce the costs Taiwan could face if countries around the world began competing for energy supplies and shipping capacity, Chen said.
    陳冠廷表示，預先做好準備將能減少如果世界各國開始爭奪能源供應與運力時，台灣可能面臨的成本。
  • prioritize v. 把……列為優先、優先處理
    Taiwan should also consider how the US is prioritizing its strategic and military resources, as security concerns in the Middle East, Europe and the Indo-Pacific region are simultaneously placing pressure on its production of air-defense missiles, munitions, ships and logistics, he said.
    他說，台灣也應考量美國是如何優先分配其戰略與軍事資源，因為中東、歐洲及印太地區的安全疑慮，正同時對其防空飛彈、彈藥、船艦及後勤的生產造成壓力。
  • diversify v. 多元化
    Taiwan should also diversify its sources of asymmetric-warfare equipment, Chen said, ensuring that production and replenishment capacity is available domestically and at overseas facilities.
    陳冠廷表示，台灣也應多元化其不對稱作戰裝備的來源，確保國內與海外設施皆具備生產與補給能力。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Such preparations are necessary as the risks of shipping through the Strait of Hormuz increase, while Iran-backed Houthi militants are advancing into and around the Bab el-Mandeb Strait, potentially cutting off access to the Red Sea and Suez Canal, Chen said.
    句中的 "potentially cutting off..." 是現在分詞片語，置於子句後方作補充說明，用來表達前面動作所產生的「可能結果」或「伴隨狀況」，相當於副詞子句 "and potentially cut off..."。
  • 倒裝句表達「也一樣」(Inversion for "So do/would...")
    If the situation in the Middle East continues to worsen, air and sea freight costs would increase, as would costs for the petrochemical industry, driving up domestic commodity prices, he said.
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    Chen cited a report by the US think tank Center for Strategic and International Studies, which said that the US military has used large quantities of munitions in its military operations against Iran...
    句中使用非限定用法的關係代名詞 "which" 引導子句（"which said that..."），前面有逗號隔開，用來補充說明前面的先行詞 "a report by the US think tank..."，提供更多額外資訊。
