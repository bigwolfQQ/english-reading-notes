台灣完成66架勇鷹高級教練機生產

Taiwan completes production of 66 Brave Eagle advanced jet trainers

單字解析

  • program n. 計畫、方案
    They landed at 11:36 a.m., marking the completion of a nine-year program to develop and mass-produce the new trainer.
    它們於上午11點36分降落，標誌著為期九年的新教練機研發與量產計畫圓滿完成。
  • replace v. 取代、替代
    The Brave Eagle is designed to replace the decommissioned F-5E/F and AT-3 aircraft...
    勇鷹旨在取代除役的F-5E/F和AT-3機型...
  • efficiently adv. 有效率地
    ...allowing pilots to transition more efficiently to frontline fighters such as the F-16V Block 20 and Block 70 aircraft.
    ...能讓飛行員更有效率地過渡到如F-16V Block 20和Block 70等前線戰機。
  • contract n. 合約、契約
    The advanced jet trainer program was launched in 2017 through contracts involving the Air Force, the government-funded weapons developer National Chung-Shan Institute of Science and Technology and AIDC.
    高級教練機計畫於2017年啟動，透過由空軍、政府資助的武器研發機構國家中山科學研究院以及漢翔共同參與的合約推動。
  • prime contractor n. phr. 主承包商、總承商
    AIDC served as the prime contractor, overseeing aircraft design, component production, assembly and testing...
    漢翔擔任主承包商，負責監督飛機設計、零件生產、組裝與測試...
  • component n. 零件、組件
    Derived from Taiwan's Indigenous Defense Fighter (IDF), the Brave Eagle has more than 80 percent of its components redesigned from those of the IDF...
    衍生自台灣的經國號戰機（IDF），勇鷹有超過80%的零件自IDF重新設計...
  • disrupt v. 打斷、干擾、使中斷
    Mass production began in 2021 but was affected by the COVID-19 pandemic, international conflicts and geopolitical tensions that disrupted global supply chains.
    量產於2021年開始，但受到COVID-19疫情、國際衝突以及干擾全球供應鏈的地緣政治緊張局勢所影響。
  • shortage n. 短缺、不足
    AIDC said it overcame shortages of imported systems and key components by developing some of them domestically...
    漢翔表示，透過在國內自行研發部分系統與關鍵零件，公司克服了進口短缺的問題...
  • deliver v. 交付、運送
    Despite those disruptions, AIDC delivered 14 Brave Eagles in each of 2025 and 2026.
    儘管遭遇這些干擾，漢翔仍在2025年與2026年分別交付了14架勇鷹。
  • integration n. 整合
    AIDC said completing the program has also strengthened its experience and capabilities in aircraft development, systems integration, mass production and supply-chain management...
    漢翔表示，完成該計畫也強化了其在飛機開發、系統整合、量產及供應鏈管理方面的經驗與能力...

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表示附帶狀況或結果）
    Taipei, Sept. 24 (CNA) Taiwan completed production of all 66 domestically developed Brave Eagle advanced jet trainers Thursday, with Aerospace Industrial Development Corp. (AIDC) Chairman Tsao Chin-pin (曹進平) personally piloting one of the final two aircraft to the Air Force Academy in Kaohsiung's Gangshan District.
    句尾使用了「with + 受詞 + 現在分詞 (piloting)」的複合結構，用來補充說明主句動作發生時的附帶狀況，意指「在……的情況下/由……親自駕駛」。
  • 過去分詞作後位修飾（形容詞片語）
    The Brave Eagle is designed to replace the decommissioned F-5E/F and AT-3 aircraft, enabling the Air Force to adopt a "three-stage, two-aircraft" pilot training system that also involves the T-34 trainer aircraft.
    過去分詞「decommissioned」（已除役的）放在名詞 aircraft 前面作修飾，用被動的概念來形容該批飛機已經退役的狀態。
  • 關代子句（形容詞子句）
    Mass production began in 2021 but was affected by the COVID-19 pandemic, international conflicts and geopolitical tensions that disrupted global supply chains.
    句尾使用「that + 動詞 (disrupted)」引導的關係代間子句，用來修飾先行詞「the COVID-19 pandemic, international conflicts and geopolitical tensions」，補充說明這些因素對全球供應鏈造成的干擾。
