Mudslide in China death toll rises to three, nine missing

單字解析

  • death toll n. 死亡人數
    The death toll from a mudslide in eastern China yesterday rose to three after days of torrential rain brought by Typhoon Saudel.
    在沙德爾颱風帶來數日暴雨後，中國東部昨天發生的土石流，死亡人數已上升至三人。
  • relocate v. 遷移、重新安置、撤離
    ...prompting rescuers to rush to the scene and relocate residents to safer ground to prevent further danger, authorities said in a statement.
    官方在一份聲明中表示，這促使救援人員趕赴現場，將居民撤離至較安全的地帶以防範進一步的危險。
  • authorities n. 官方、當局
    ...authorities said in a statement.
    官方在一份聲明中表示。
  • statement n. 聲明、陳述
    ...authorities said in a statement.
    官方在一份聲明中表示。
  • specify v. 具體說明、明確指出
    The report did not specify the cause of the deaths, but said the rain triggered landslides and other disasters.
    該報導未明確指出死亡原因，但表示降雨引發了山崩和其他災難。
  • cause n. 原因
    The report did not specify the cause of the deaths, but said the rain triggered landslides and other disasters.
    該報導未明確指出死亡原因，但表示降雨引發了山崩和其他災難。
  • collapse v. 倒塌、瓦解
    The exact number is unclear, but more than 100 houses collapsed in Huating Town in Putian City, it said.
    報導指出，確切數字尚不清楚，但莆田市華亭鎮有100多棟房屋倒塌。
  • evacuate v. 撤離、疏散
    About 600,000 residents have been evacuated from risky offshore and onshore areas in Fujian as of Friday night, Xinhua reported.
    新華社報導，截至週五晚間，福建已有約60萬名居民從具風險的沿海與內陸地區撤離。
  • deploy v. 部署、調動
    Images CCTV showed that some streets were turned into rivers, while rescuers deployed inflatable boats in their efforts.
    中央電視臺播出的畫面顯示，有些街道變成了河流，同時救援人員在救援行動中部署了充氣艇。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾語
    The death toll from a mudslide in eastern China yesterday rose to three after days of torrential rain brought by Typhoon Saudel.
  • 現在分詞片語表示結果（對等分詞構句）
    The mudslide hit part of a village in Suichuan County in Jiangxi Province early Saturday, damaging a dozen houses, and prompting rescuers to rush to the scene...
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    About 600,000 residents have been evacuated from risky offshore and onshore areas in Fujian as of Friday night...
  • 對比與對等連接詞 while 的用法
    Images CCTV showed that some streets were turned into rivers, while rescuers deployed inflatable boats in their efforts.
