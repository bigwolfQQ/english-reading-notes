台灣外海發現海龜化石連結

Fossil link for sea turtles found off coast of Taiwan

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • confirm v. 確認，證實
    The world’s first confirmed fossil of a modern leatherback sea turtle has been discovered off Taiwan...
    全球第一件經證實的現代革龜化石在台灣外海被發現...
  • discover v. 發現
    The discovery is the first confirmed fossil of a modern leatherback found anywhere in the world and provides important clues about the species’ origins and evolution.
    這項發現是世界各地首次發現經證實的現代革龜化石，並為該物種的起源與演化提供了重要線索。
  • relatively adv. 相對地
    However, because they have a relatively soft shell, their fossils do not generally form well...
    然而，由於牠們的殼相對柔軟，因此化石通常不易完整形成...
  • analysis n. 分析
    That might have changed with the analysis of a fossilized humerus found on the seabed between Taiwan and the Penghu Islands.
    隨著對在台灣與澎湖群島之間海床發現的化石肱骨進行分析，情況可能會有所改變。
  • belong to phr. v. 屬於
    ...confirmed the bone belonged to a leatherback.
    ...確認該骨頭屬於一隻革龜。
  • differ v. 不同，相異
    Leatherbacks differ markedly from other living sea turtles.
    革龜與其他現存海龜有顯著不同。
  • contain v. 包含，容納
    ...while leatherbacks belong to the family Dermochelyidae, which now contains only one living species: the leatherback.
    ...而革龜則屬於棱皮龜科，該科目前僅存一個物種：革龜。
  • donate v. 捐贈，捐獻
    The fossilized humerus studied by Tsai and Liao was donated by Hou Li-jen (侯立仁), a retired professor from Tainan.
    這塊由蔡政修和廖翊如研究的化石肱骨是由台南退休教授侯立仁所捐贈的。
  • publish v. 出版，發表
    Their findings were published in June in the international journal Acta Palaeontologica Polonica.
    他們的研究結果於六月發表在國際期刊《波蘭古生物學報》上。
  • demonstrate v. 證明，展示
    The research team said fossil discoveries in Taiwan have demonstrated the importance and research value of the island’s paleontological record...
    研究團隊表示，台灣的化石發現展現出該島古生物紀錄在理解生物多樣性起源與演化上的重要性與研究價值...

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作後置修飾（形容詞片語）
    A leatherback sea turtle, which can easily grow to more than 2m long, is the largest sea turtle species alive today.
    句中的 "alive today" 是形容詞片語，用來後置修飾前面的名詞片語 "the largest sea turtle species"（現存體型最大的海龜物種）。在英文中，形容詞片語常可放在被修飾的名詞後面，使語氣更流暢並補充說明狀態。
  • 關係代名詞非限定用法（which 子句）
    ...while leatherbacks belong to the family Dermochelyidae, which now contains only one living species: the leatherback.
    逗號後面的 "which now contains..." 是非限定用法的關係子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞 "the family Dermochelyidae"（棱皮龜科）。非限定用法通常用逗號隔開，對先行詞提供額外的補充資訊，且不具限制或篩選作用。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The fossilized humerus studied by Tsai and Liao was donated by Hou Li-jen...
    句中的 "was donated" 採用了被動語態（be 動詞 + 過去分詞），強調動作的承受者（化石肱骨）而非動作的執行者（侯立仁）。句首的 "studied by Tsai and Liao" 則是過去分詞片語作後置修飾，修飾 humerus。
