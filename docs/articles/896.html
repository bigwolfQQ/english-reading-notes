文法重點

  • with + 名詞 + 分詞（獨立分詞構句）
    Minister of Labor Hung Sun-han’s (洪申翰) participation in an APEC ministerial meeting in China went “smoothly” yesterday, with China following APEC practices and rules, the Ministry of Labor (MOL) said.
    「with + 名詞 + 現在分詞(following)」構成附帶狀況的獨立分詞構句，用來補充說明主句動作發生時的背景狀況，意指「在中國遵循APEC慣例與規則的情況下」。
  • 關係代名詞子句（介系詞 + which）
    Photos provided by the ministry showed Hung seated behind a nameplate reading “Chinese Taipei,” the name under which Taiwan participates in APEC, one of the few major international forums in which it is a formal member.
    句中的「in which it is a formal member」是用來修飾先行詞 international forums 的形容詞子句。因為是「在這些論壇之中」，所以介系詞 in 移至關係代名詞 which 的前面。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句（when 引導時間子句）
    Hung is expected to return to Taiwan no later than Thursday, when the Minimum Wage Deliberation Committee is scheduled to meet to discuss next year’s minimum wage level.
    句中的「when the Minimum Wage Deliberation Committee is scheduled to meet...」是非限定用法（前面有逗號），用來補充說明先行詞 Thursday（星期四）發生的事情，翻譯時可譯為「屆時……」。
