文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The US is watching China’s presence in its Arctic zone “very closely” amid concerns regarding the security of the US homeland...
    句中的「regarding the security...」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 concerns（擔憂），相當於形容詞子句「that regard the security...」，在新聞英語中非常常見，用以簡化句型並帶出相關細節。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    One is that China might be involved in scientific research focused on the mineral exploitation of the seabed...
    句中的「focused on the mineral exploitation...」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 research（研究）。因為研究是「被」聚焦於特定主題，所以使用過去分詞 (focused) 而非現在分詞。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    China, which calls itself a “near-Arctic” state and wants to create a “Polar Silk Road” in the Arctic, has stepped up its presence...
    句中的「which calls itself a “near-Arctic” state and wants to...」是一個非限定性關係代名詞子句，用逗號隔開，對先行詞 China 補充說明其背景與企圖，使句子資訊更豐富。
  • 表語子句 (Predicative Clause)
    The concern is two-fold. One is that China might be involved in scientific research...
    句型「One is that...」中的 that引導的是一個名詞子句，在句中擔任主詞 the concern 的「表語」（補充說明主詞的內容）。這種結構在商業簡報或正式文章中經常用於條理分明地陳述重點。
