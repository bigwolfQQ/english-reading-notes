文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表示原因或背景）
    Faced with economic insecurity and anxiety over the region’s prospects, voters can easily become cornered into feeling that without voting for the incumbent faction, welfare and development projects could be cut off.
    句首的「Faced with...」是由過去分詞引導的分詞構句，原句意指「Because voters are faced with economic insecurity...」，省略了主詞與be動詞，直接用過去分詞開頭來表達「面對……情況」，在多益閱讀與商業文章中非常常見。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Innocence is usually not proven by threatening to file a lawsuit.
    句中使用「is usually not proven」的被動語態結構（be + 過去分詞），強調「清白」是被證明的對象，而非主動去證明。多益考試常考被動語態的動詞搭配及時態變化。
  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    The testimony of foreign journalists serves as a reminder to the people of Taiwan: while we might present ourselves as a democracy founded on freedom, the picture is not always so rosy for voters in the peripheries.
    句中的「founded on freedom」是過去分詞片語當作形容詞用，修飾前面的名詞「a democracy」，相當於較長的關係代名詞子句「which is founded on freedom」。這種精簡的修飾手法在新聞英文中極為普遍。
  • 動名詞片語當主詞
    Traditional forms of electoral bribery can be difficult to prosecute, because their discretion makes it challenging to obtain concrete evidence.
    句尾的「to obtain concrete evidence」是不定詞片語，前面搭配虛受詞「it」（makes it challenging to...）。實際的主詞是後面的「obtain concrete evidence」，這是英文中避免主詞過長常見的句型結構。
