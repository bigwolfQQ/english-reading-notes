勞動部將對撤銷官員免職處分提起上訴

Labor ministry to appeal voiding of official’s dismissal

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • appeal n. / v. 上訴
    The Ministry of Labor yesterday said it would appeal an administrative court ruling that voided its dismissal...
    勞動部長昨天表示，將針對一項宣告撤銷其免職處分的行政法院裁決提出上訴...
  • dismissal n. 免職，解雇
    On those grounds, Hsieh’s challenge to her dismissal was well-founded, the court said.
    法院表示，基於這些理由，謝宜容對免職處分提出的挑戰是有理據的。
  • disciplinary adj. 懲戒的，紀律的
    On Sept. 3, the Taipei High Administrative Court ruled that the ministry’s disciplinary proceedings... had violated due process...
    台北高等行政法院於9月3日裁定，勞動部的懲戒程序...違反了正當法律程序...
  • indict v. 起訴，控告
    Prosecutors earlier declined to indict her over the death of the worker...
    檢方先前對她因該名員工死亡而受到的指控做出不起訴處分...
  • subordinate n. 下屬，部屬
    ...following the suicide of a 39-year-old subordinate, surnamed Wu (吳), in his office in New Taipei City on Nov. 4, 2024.
    ...因2024年11月4日一名39歲吳姓下屬在新北市辦公室輕生...
  • investigation n. 調查
    ...distributed as part of its investigation into Hsieh’s conduct.
    ...作為針對謝宜容行為調查的一部分所發放的...
  • legal counsel n. phr. 法律顧問，辯護律師
    ...and had instructed legal counsel to prepare to appeal to the Supreme Administrative Court within the statutory deadline.
    ...並已指示法律顧問準備在法定期限內向最高行政法院提起上訴。
  • deadline n. 截止日期，期限
    ...to appeal to the Supreme Administrative Court within the statutory deadline.
    ...在法定期限內向最高行政法院提起上訴。
  • corruption n. 貪污，腐敗
    Hsieh was also found guilty of corruption by a criminal court...
    謝宜容另被刑事法院判處貪污罪名成立...
  • reinstate v. 使復職，使恢復原狀
    ...Hsieh will remain suspended from duty and will not be reinstated at this time, the ministry said.
    勞動部表示，謝宜容目前仍將停職，不會復職。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...a branch chief who was accused of bullying following a worker’s suicide.
    句中的「who was accused of bullying」為形容詞子句，修飾前面的「a branch chief」（分署長）。在英文中，若被動語態的關係代間詞加上be動詞（who was）常可省略，直接用過去分詞片語（accused of...）作後位修飾，使語氣更為精煉。
  • 現在分詞作附加補充說明 (Present Participle indicating Result or Additional Action)
    The ruling struck down her dismissal. Prosecutors earlier declined to indict her over the death of the worker, finding no causal relationship between her leadership and the suicide.
    句尾的「finding no causal relationship...」是由現在分詞「finding」帶動的分詞構句，用來補充說明檢察官做出「不起訴」決定背後的理由與發現，表達伴隨或結果的狀態。
  • 對等連接詞連接多個動詞結構 (Parallel Verbs connected by Conjunctions)
    Hsieh was also found guilty of corruption by a criminal court, impeached and referred for disciplinary action by the Control Yuan, the ministry said.
    此句展示了主詞在被動語態中連接多個過去分詞（found, impeached, referred），共用同一個主動詞及語態，使句子結構緊湊，避免重複。
