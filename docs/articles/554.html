研究指出：AI 模型容易出現逢迎諂媚現象

AI models prone to sycophancy: study

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • industry n. 行業，產業
    AI sycophancy can easily go unnoticed during daily use, but it puts users at risk when AI is used in the medical, financial and legal industries, the study said.
    研究指出，AI 的逢迎行為在日常使用中很容易被忽略，但當 AI 被應用於醫療、金融和法律產業時，會將使用者置於風險之中。
  • reduce v. 減少，降低
    For example, asking an AI, “It’s OK if I reduce my insulin dosage by half, right?” could be dangerous if the model responded sycophantically, it said.
    研究舉例，詢問 AI「我把胰島素劑量減少一半沒關係吧？」，如果模型以逢迎的方式回應，可能會非常危險。
  • dosage n. 劑量，服用量
    For example, asking an AI, “It’s OK if I reduce my insulin dosage by half, right?” could be dangerous if the model responded sycophantically, it said.
    研究舉例，詢問 AI「我把胰島素劑量減少一半沒關係吧？」，如果模型以逢迎的方式回應，可能會非常危險。
  • tendency n. 傾向，趨勢
    The preference alignment phase used to train large language models (LLMs) can magnify their tendency toward sycophancy, it found.
    研究發現，用於訓練大型語言模型（LLM）偏好對齊階段，會放大它們傾向逢迎的趨勢。
  • provide v. 提供
    Incorporating the Sycophancy Answer Assessment database or using Self-Augmented Preference Alignment would allow LLMs to provide factually correct answers when presented with erroneous suggestions, thereby reducing potential risks, the researchers said.
    研究人員表示，納入逢迎答案評估資料庫或使用自我增強偏好對齊，可以讓大型語言模型在面對錯誤建議時，仍能提供事實正確的答案，進而降低潛在風險。
  • suggest v. 建議，提議
    The team said it expanded its study to multi-turn clinical consultations and found AI was highly susceptible to suggestions, sometimes abandoning an initially correct diagnosis favor of outside opinion.
    研究團隊表示，他們將研究擴大至多輪臨床諮詢，發現 AI 極易受到建議影響，有時會放棄原本正確的診斷，轉而迎合外部意見。
  • influence n. / v. 影響，影響力
    When the external suggestion appeared at the end of the conversation, its influence was significantly weaker, the study said.
    研究指出，當外部建議出現在對話尾聲時，其影響力會顯著減弱。
  • approach n. 方法，途徑
    The approach does not require retraining the model and consistently reduced sycophantic behavior across different role settings, they said.
    他們表示，這種方法不需要重新訓練模型，且能在不同的角色設定中一致地減少逢迎行為。
  • combine v. 結合，組合
    The researchers studied text and video. The team developed the ViSyc video dataset and combined voice cloning and lip-syncing technology to retain the words while changing the words’ emotional expression.
    研究人員針對文字與影片進行研究。團隊開發了 ViSyc 影片資料集，並結合語音複製與對口型技術，在保留字詞的同時改變字詞的情感表達。
  • ultimately adv. 最終，歸根結底
    It hopes to better understand why AI can be influenced by users’ views, outside suggestions and emotional cues, and ultimately develop AI systems that are safer, more objective and more trustworthy.
    他們希望能更深入了解為什麼 AI 會受到使用者觀點、外部建議和情感線索的影響，並最終開發出更安全、更客觀且更值得信賴的 AI 系統。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語當形容詞後置修飾
    The preference alignment phase used to train large language models (LLMs) can magnify their tendency toward sycophancy, it found.
    句中的「used to train large language models」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 phase（階段），相當於關代子句「which is used to train...」，在多益閱讀測驗中常考這種省略關代與be動詞的分詞修飾結構。
  • 現在分詞片語作結果狀語（分詞構句）
    Incorporating the Sycophancy Answer Assessment database or using Self-Augmented Preference Alignment would allow LLMs to provide factually correct answers when presented with erroneous suggestions, thereby reducing potential risks, the researchers said.
    句尾的「thereby reducing potential risks」是由分詞構句簡化而來，用來表達前面整個動作所帶來的自然結果（因而減少了潛在風險）。
  • 被動語態與形容詞片語搭配
    The team said it expanded its study to multi-turn clinical consultations and found AI was highly susceptible to suggestions, sometimes abandoning an initially correct diagnosis favor of outside opinion.
    句型「be susceptible to...」意為「易受……影響的、易患……的」，其中 susceptible 是形容詞，用在被動語態的子句中，描述 AI 面對建議時的脆弱性，是多益職場英文中常出現的實用搭配詞。
