聯合國背書採用「等積地球」地圖以取代麥卡托地圖

UN endorses using ‘Equal Earth’ map over Mercator

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • endorse v. 背書、贊同、支持
    The UN General Assembly on Friday endorsed a new world map that represents Africa’s larger size more accurately...
    聯合國大會於週五通過了一份新世界地圖，能更準確地呈現非洲較大的面積...
  • resolution n. 決議、決議案
    The resolution, which was sponsored by Togo and backed by members of the African Union, was passed with 164 member states voting in favor.
    該決議由多哥發起並獲非洲聯盟成員支持，在 164 個會員國投票贊成下獲得通過。
  • abstain v. 棄權、戒除
    Only the US voted against it while six other countries — Serbia, Estonia, Georgia, Lithuania, Moldova and Ukraine — abstained.
    僅美國投下反對票，另有六個國家（塞爾維亞、愛沙尼亞、喬治亞、立陶宛、摩爾達維亞與烏克蘭）棄權。
  • institution n. 機構、習俗
    The vote encourages governments and institutions around the world to replace the traditional Mercator map...
    這項投票 encourages 全球政府與機構，用傳統麥卡托地圖...
  • replace A with B phr. 用 B 取代 A
    ...to replace the traditional Mercator map, commonly used since the 16th century, with the Equal Earth projection...
    ...用自 16 世紀以來普遍使用的傳統麥卡托地圖，替換成「等積地球」投影法...
  • proportion n. 比例、部分
    ...whereas in the Equal Earth projection that shows continents in their true proportions.
    ...然而在「等積地球」投影法中，各大陸呈現其真實比例。
  • impose v. 強制實行、強加
    The resolution does not ban the Mercator projection or impose a replacement, but only encourages the use of the Equal Earth projection...
    該決議並未禁用麥卡托投影法或強制更換，僅鼓勵使用「等積地球」投影法...
  • adoption n. 採用、採納、領養
    Africa No Filter, one of the lead campaigners for the adoption of the new map, hailed the resolution...
    推動採用新地圖的主要倡議組織之一「非洲無濾鏡」對該決議表示歡迎...
  • urge v. 敦促、力勸
    Several countries hailed the adoption and urged the adoption of the Equal Earth map.
    數個國家對這項採用表示讚賞，並敦促全面改用「等積地球」地圖。
  • distort v. 扭曲、曲解
    But correcting a representation that distorts it is already an act of truth...
    但糾正一個扭曲世界的呈現方式，已經是一種追求真實的行為...
  • application n. 應用、申請
    Outside of that very narrow navigation application, there is no point in using it...
    除了那種非常狹隘的導航應用之外，使用它已經沒有意義了...

文法重點

  • 非限定性關係子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    The resolution, which was sponsored by Togo and backed by members of the African Union, was passed with 164 member states voting in favor.
    句中的「which was sponsored by Togo and backed by members of the African Union」用逗號隔開，是一個非限定性關係子句，用來補充說明主詞 The resolution 的背景資訊，若拿掉不影響主要子句的完整性。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...to replace the traditional Mercator map, commonly used since the 16th century, with the Equal Earth projection...
    句中的「commonly used since the 16th century」是由過去分詞開頭的分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞 the traditional Mercator map，相當於省略了「which is」的關係代名詞子句。
  • 對比連接詞 whereas
    The Mercator projection shows Greenland and Africa as appearing to be about the same size, whereas in the Equal Earth projection that shows continents in their true proportions.
    「whereas」在此用作對比連接詞，意為「然而、反之」，用來連接兩個前後形成強烈對比的子句，突顯麥卡托投影法與等積地球投影法在比例上的差異。
  • 句型：there is no point in + V-ing (做……毫無意義/沒有用)
    Outside of that very narrow navigation application, there is no point in using it...
    「there is no point in (doing sth)」是一個常見實用的句型，用來表示「做某事是沒有意義的或白費力氣的」，後方接動名詞 (V-ing)。
