文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    ...spewing lava and towering plumes of ash, while loud volcanic activity could be heard hundreds of kilometers away, according to foreign news reports.
    句中的 "spewing lava and towering plumes of ash" 是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明主句動作發生時的伴隨狀態（噴發出熔岩與高聳的火山灰）。
  • 過去分詞作形容詞用 (Past Participle as Adjective)
    To assist affected passengers, CAL said it will deploy a larger aircraft...
    句中的 "affected" 是由動詞 affect 轉變而來的過去分詞，在此作形容詞置於名詞 passengers 前方，修飾「受到影響的」旅客。
  • 名詞子句作受詞 (Noun Clause as Object)
    China Airlines (CAL) said its flight CI761... and return flight CI762... were canceled...
    動詞 said 後面接了一個省略了連接詞 that 的名詞子句（its flight CI761... were canceled），用來作 said 的受詞，表達航空公司「說了什麼內容」。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    ...its flights CI763 and CI764, which are scheduled to leave Taoyuan on Tuesday night and return to Taoyuan on Wednesday morning.
    句中的 "which are scheduled to..." 是非限定用法的關係代名詞子句（前面有逗號），對前面的航班 CI763 和 CI764 提供額外的補充說明。
