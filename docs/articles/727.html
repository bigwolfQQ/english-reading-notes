台灣公民參與全球排名第七，司法自由度下滑

Taiwan 7th worldwide for citizen input, slips in judicial freedom

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • release v. 發布，發表
    Taiwan ranked seventh globally in citizen participation in the Global State of Democracy 2026 report released Tuesday by the International Institute for Democracy and Electoral Assistance (International IDEA).
    根據國際民主及選舉支援組織於週二發布的《2026年全球民主現狀》報告，台灣在公民參與度方面全球排名第七。
  • decline v. / n. 下降，衰退
    Taiwan also made gains in legislative oversight although its rating for judicial independence declined, according to the report.
    報告指出，台灣在國會監督方面也有所進步，儘管其司法獨立評等有所下降。
  • category n. 種類，類別
    The Stockholm-based intergovernmental organization said Taiwan maintained its seventh-place ranking in the "Citizen Participation" category in 2025, tying with Finland.
    這個總部設在斯德哥爾摩的政府間組織表示，台灣在2025年的「公民參與」類別中維持第七名的排名，與芬蘭並列。
  • assess v. 評估，評價
    The report assessed 2025 democratic performance in 173 countries across four broad categories -- Representation, Rights, Rule of Law and Participation -- using indicators including judicial independence, electoral credibility, freedom of expression and access to justice.
    該報告評估了173個國家在2025年的民主表現，涵蓋四大廣泛類別——代表性、權利、法治與參與——並使用了司法獨立、選舉公信力、言論自由和取得司法管道等指標。
  • performance n. 表現，績效
    Globally, the report found that about 57 percent of the countries assessed experienced a decline in at least one indicator of democratic performance between 2020 and 2025.
    在全球範圍內，報告發現受評國家中約有57%在2020年至2025年間，在至少一項民主表現指標上出現下滑。
  • highlight v. 強調，突顯
    It also highlighted geopolitical tensions as a growing challenge to democratic governance, citing border disputes and conflicts between India and Pakistan and between Cambodia and Thailand, as well as tensions in the Taiwan Strait, as risks to regional stability and democratic resilience.
    報告也強調地緣政治緊張局勢是對民主治理日益嚴峻的挑戰，並舉出印度與巴基斯坦之間、柬埔寨與泰國之間的邊境爭端與衝突，以及台海緊張局勢，作為對區域穩定和民主韌性的風險。
  • challenge n. 挑戰
    It also highlighted geopolitical tensions as a growing challenge to democratic governance, citing border disputes and conflicts between India and Pakistan and between Cambodia and Thailand, as well as tensions in the Taiwan Strait, as risks to regional stability and democratic resilience.
    報告也強調地緣政治緊張局勢是對民主治理日益嚴峻的挑戰，並舉出印度與巴基斯坦之間、柬埔寨與泰國之間的邊境爭端與衝突，以及台海緊張局勢，作為對區域穩定和民主韌性的風險。
  • promote v. 促進，推廣，晉升
    International IDEA was founded in 1995 by 14 member states to promote democracy and support electoral processes worldwide.
    國際民主及選舉支援組織由14個成員國於1995年創立，旨在推動全球民主並支援選舉程序。
  • withdraw v. 撤回，退出
    The United States, formerly an observer state, withdrew from the organization earlier this year.
    先前為觀察員國的美國已於今年稍早退出該組織。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾（形容詞用）
    ...in the Global State of Democracy 2026 report released Tuesday by the International Institute for Democracy and Electoral Assistance...
    句中的 "released Tuesday by..." 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "report"。完整結構原本是「which was released Tuesday...」，省略了「關係代名詞 + be動詞」後，直接用過去分詞片語作後置定語，在新聞英文中非常常見，能讓句子更簡潔。
  • 分詞構句表附带結果或說明
    ...dropping it from the top-performing tier to the middle tier, alongside countries including the United States, Argentina, Belgium, India and Nigeria.
    句尾的 ", dropping it from..." 是現在分詞構句，主詞為主句所描述的事情（即台灣司法獨立評等的下降）。這個結構用來補充說明前句動作所產生的結果，翻譯時通常譯為「因而使其從……跌至……」。
  • 現在分詞片語作後置修飾
    Taiwan was also among only 13 countries worldwide to record an improvement in the indicator measuring the effectiveness of legislative oversight.
    句中的 "measuring the effectiveness of legislative oversight" 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "indicator"。因為該指標具有「主動測量」的意味，故使用現在分詞（measuring）來引導形容詞片語。
