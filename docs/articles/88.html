賴清德在軍人節向陣亡將士致敬，並承諾給予國軍更多支援

Lai honors fallen soldiers on Armed Forces Day, pledges more support for troops

單字解析

  • pay tribute to phr. 向...致敬、向...表達敬意
    President Lai Ching-te on Thursday paid tribute to Taiwan's fallen military personnel...
    總統賴清德於週四向台灣陣亡的軍事人員致敬...
  • pledge v. 保證、承諾
    ...and pledged that his government would provide better care for the current armed service members...
    ...並承諾其政府將為現役軍人提供更好的照顧...
  • ceremony n. 典禮、儀式
    During a ceremony in Taipei, the president laid flowers at the Martyrs' Shrine...
    在台北的一場儀式中，總統向忠烈祠獻花...
  • descendant n. 後裔、後代
    Other senior government officials and descendants of the fallen soldiers also attended...
    其他政府高層官員及陣亡將士的後代也出席了...
  • administration n. 政府（統治）當局、行政管理
    ...he said that over the past year, his administration has improved salaries and benefits for military personnel...
    ...他表示，過去一年來，他的政府已改善了軍人的薪資與福利...
  • benefit n. 福利、津貼、好處
    ...his administration has improved salaries and benefits for military personnel...
    ...他的政府已改善了軍人的薪資與福利...
  • allowance n. 津貼、補助
    ...including raising combat and professional allowances earlier in 2026.
    ...包括在2026年初調高戰鬥加給與專業加給。
  • increase n. 增加、提高、增長
    Starting next year, military personnel, civil servants, and teachers will receive a 4 percent salary increase...
    從明年開始，軍公教人員將調薪百分之四...
  • mission n. 任務、使命
    ...so that military personnel can focus on their mission of protecting the country...
    ...好讓軍人能夠專心執行保衛國家的任務...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    ...honoring nearly half a million soldiers who died fighting during World War II for the Republic of China...
    句中的「honoring nearly half a million soldiers...」是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前面的地名「the Martyrs' Shrine（忠烈祠）」具有的功能，相當於關代子句「which honors...」。
  • 連接詞 as 引導的時間副詞子句 (Adverbial Clause of Time)
    ...as the country marked its annual Armed Forces Day.
    連接詞「as」在此用來表示「當...時」，引導一個時間副詞子句，用以說明前面主句動作（總統致敬與承諾）發生的背景時間。
  • so that引導的目的副詞子句 (Adverbial Clause of Purpose)
    ...so that military personnel can focus on their mission of protecting the country...
    片語「so that」意為「以便、為了可以」，後面接子句用來表達前面所做措施（改善環境與支援家庭）的目的。
