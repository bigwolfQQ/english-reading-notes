單字解析

  • lure v. 誘惑、引誘
    men young and old are being lured by Moscow with tantalizing job offers
    莫斯科以誘人的工作機會誘騙當地老少男性
  • recruit v. 招募、徵募
    Moscow and Kyiv have recruited thousands of foreign nationals into their armies
    莫斯科和基輔已將數千名外國公民招募進他們的軍隊
  • deploy v. 部署（軍隊、裝備等）
    from where he fears being deployed to the war against Ukraine
    他擔心自己會從那裡被部署到對烏克蘭的戰爭中
  • investigation n. 調查
    adding that the matter was under investigation
    並補充說該事件正在調查中
  • sector n. 部門、行業
    false promises of jobs in security, logistics, construction, information technology and other sectors
    以安全、物流、建築、資訊科技和其他部門的工作為虛假承諾
  • volunteer v. 自願
    However, some of those enlisted volunteered to fight.
    然而，其中一些被徵召的人是自願參戰的。
  • forbid v. 禁止
    We cannot forbid people from engaging in activities.
    我們不能禁止人們從事活動。
  • engage in phr. 從事、參與
    We cannot forbid people from engaging in activities.
    我們不能禁止人們從事活動。
  • victim n. 受害者
    He said up to 100 were the victims of entrapment.
    他說，有多達100人是設局陷害的受害者。
  • professional n. 專業人士
    Maico Enrique Campusano... was offered work as an advertising professional.
    麥科·恩里克·坎帕薩諾...被提供了一份廣告專業人員的工作。

文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    men young and old are being lured by Moscow with tantalizing job offers
    句型使用現在進行式的被動語態（are being + 過去分詞），強調「男性正在被莫斯科誘騙」這個動作的承受者與進行中的狀態。
  • 分詞構句作後位修飾 / 形容詞片語
    A Dominican man, Juan Carlos Savila, has posted videos on Instagram showing himself in action on the front line.
    句尾的「showing himself...」為現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前面的 videos（影片）所包含的內容，相當於關係代間詞子句「which show himself...」的簡化。
  • 現在分詞表附帶狀況或結果 (分詞構句)
    When he arrived in the country at the end of July he was transferred to a Russian military base, from where he fears being deployed to the war against Ukraine.
    句中結合了限定性關係子句與介系詞（from where），用來引導出前一個地方（俄羅斯軍事基地），說明他從這個地方產生了被部署到戰場的恐懼。
