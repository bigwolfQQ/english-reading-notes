中國能發明新藥；但要支付費用則更加困難

China can invent new drugs; paying for them is harder

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • pharmaceutical adj. 製藥的，藥學的
    China now rivals the US as a global center for pharmaceutical innovation
    中國現在已足以與美國匹敵，成為全球製藥創新的中心
  • insurance n. 保險
    but its health insurance system struggles to keep pace with the cost of breakthroughs
    但其醫療保險系統卻難以跟上突破性療法的成本
  • profitability n. 獲利能力，收益性
    a more developed private insurance market could help lift demand and profitability
    一個更成熟的私人保險市場可以透過讓從癌症、糖尿病到失眠等各種新療法的取得變得更容易，來協助提升需求與獲利能力
  • accessible adj. 易接近的，可取得的
    by making new treatments for everything from cancer to diabetes to insomnia more accessible
    透過讓從癌症、糖尿病到失眠等各種新療法的取得變得更容易
  • subsidy n. 補助金，津貼
    a combination of individual payments and government subsidies for everyone else
    一般個人繳費和政府補貼相結合
  • reimbursement n. 費用核銷，償還，報銷
    Expensive treatments added to the reimbursement list of covered drugs
    被加入涵蓋藥物報銷清單的高昂治療費用
  • commercial adj. 商業的
    That gap is what a stronger commercial insurance industry could help fill
    這個差距正是更強大的商業保險產業可以協助填補的
  • burden n. 負擔，重擔
    aims to shift that burden by putting existing policies on a firmer legal footing
    旨在透過將現行政策建立在更穩固的法律基礎上，來轉移這種負擔
  • commercialize v. 使商業化
    which can commercialize the medicines in more lucrative markets
    能夠在更具獲利能力的市場將藥物商業化的
  • lucrative adj. 賺錢的，獲利丰厚的
    commercialize the medicines in more lucrative markets
    在更具獲利能力的市場將藥物商業化

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    China now rivals the US as a global center for pharmaceutical innovation, but its health insurance system struggles to keep pace with the cost of breakthroughs, leaving many patients unable to access top new drugs.
    句尾的 「leaving many patients unable to access...」 是現在分詞「leaving」帶動的分詞構句，用來補充說明前面主句所產生的結果（導致許多病患無法取得……）。
  • 關代 what 引導的名詞子句 (Noun Clause introduced by 'what')
    That gap is what a stronger commercial insurance industry could help fill.
    句中的「what a stronger commercial insurance industry could help fill」是由連接代名詞 what 引導的名詞子句，在句中作主詞補語，意思是「……的東西／所作所為」。
  • 被動語態的應用 (Passive Voice)
    The state insurance scheme is funded mainly by payroll contributions...
    句型使用「is funded」被動語態，強調國家保險計畫是「被」這些薪資扣繳款項所資助，主體是保險計畫本身而非資助者。
