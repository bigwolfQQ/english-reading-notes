高鐵因假日人潮湧現加開北上列車

High-speed rail adds extra northbound trains as holiday crowds swell

單字解析

  • operator n. 經營者，營運商
    The operator said the additional trains, all-stop northbound services with all cars except the business class car designated as non-reserved seating, began running in the afternoon.
    營運單位表示，這些加開列車為全站停靠的北上服務，除了商務車廂外，所有車廂均指定為自由座，並於下午開始發車。
  • additional adj. 額外的，附加的
    The operator said the additional trains, all-stop northbound services with all cars except the business class car designated as non-reserved seating, began running in the afternoon.
    營運單位表示，這些加開列車為全站停靠的北上服務，除了商務車廂外，所有車廂均指定為自由座，並於下午開始發車。
  • designate v. 指定，指派
    The operator said the additional trains, all-stop northbound services with all cars except the business class car designated as non-reserved seating, began running in the afternoon.
    營運單位表示，這些加開列車為全站停靠的北上服務，除了商務車廂外，所有車廂均指定為自由座，並於下午開始發車。
  • forecast n. / v. 預測，預報
    According to THSRC's passenger forecast released at 4:40 p.m., passengers boarding non-reserved cars at stations south of Hsinchu should expect to wait at least 20 minutes for a train.
    根據台灣高鐵於下午4點40分發布的旅客預測，在新竹以南各站搭乘自由座的旅客，預估須等候至少20分鐘才能上車。
  • extend v. 延長，延伸
    During the evening peak between 6 p.m. and 9 p.m., waiting times could extend to 40-60 minutes or even exceed one hour at some stations.
    在傍晚6點至9點的尖峰時段，部分車站的候車時間可能會延長至40至60分鐘，甚至超過一小時。
  • exceed v. 超過，勝過
    During the evening peak between 6 p.m. and 9 p.m., waiting times could extend to 40-60 minutes or even exceed one hour at some stations.

    THSRC's special six-day holiday transportation schedule runs from Sept. 24 to 29.

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況與結果
    ...bringing the two-day total to 663,000.
    句尾的「bringing...」是由對等子句簡化而來的分詞構句，表連續發生的結果（前面兩天的總運量帶到了 663,000 人次），多益閱讀測驗常考這種分詞來測試句意邏輯與動詞型態。
  • 關係副詞 where 引導的形容詞子句
    The longest wait was forecast to be at Changhua Station, where passengers could face delays of 60-80 minutes at around 8 p.m.
    句中的「where」引導非限定關係子句，用來補充說明先行詞「Changhua Station（彰化站）」這個地點。在多益測驗中，常考選關係代間詞（如 which）與關係副詞（如 where）的區別。
  • 被動語態的使用
    ...all cars except the business class car designated as non-reserved seating...
    句中的「designated」實際上是過去分詞（被動概念），修飾前面的 cars（被指定為自由座車廂）。多益商業英文中常用被動語態來描述規定、安排或狀態。
  • 連接詞 as 引導的時間副詞子句
    THSRC added four extra northbound services on Monday to deal with heavy passenger traffic as travelers headed back to their workplaces...
    「as」在此處作連接詞，意指「當……時」，引導時間副詞子句說明加開列車的背景時機。多益常考 as 作為「當」、「因為」或「如同」的多重字義與用法。
