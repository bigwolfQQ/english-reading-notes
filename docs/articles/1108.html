Taipei Times · 2026-09-24

台灣不再只是美中問題

Taiwan no longer just a US-China problem

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • settle v. 解決、結束（爭端等）
    Beijing has for decades insisted that Taiwan is a matter to be settled between China and the US — or, preferably, by China alone.
    數十年來，北京堅稱台灣是中國與美國之間的事情——或者最好由中國獨自解決。
  • prominently adv. 顯著地、重要地
    Taiwan is expected to feature prominently in US-China talks, particularly as Beijing presses Washington over arms sales to Taipei.
    台灣預計將在美中會談中成為核心議題，特別是隨著北京就對台軍售向華盛頓施壓。
  • concede v. （勉強）承認、讓步
    However, focusing only on what Trump might or might not concede misses the larger structural change: Taiwan is increasingly becoming part of Japan’s security geography...
    然而，若只聚焦於川普可能或不可能做出什麼妥協，將會錯失更大的結構性轉變：台灣正日益成為日本安全地理的一部分……
  • contingency n. 意外事故、緊急事件、突發狀況
    A Taiwan Strait contingency would affect Japan’s southwestern islands, US forces based in Japan, major sea lanes and the First Island Chain.
    台海突發事件將會影響日本西南諸島、駐日美軍、主要海上航道以及第一島鏈。
  • dictate v. 命令、支配、決定
    Tokyo cannot dictate the US’ Taiwan policy, nor does it oppose US-China dialogue.
    東京無法對美國的對台政策指手畫腳，也不反對美中對話。
  • deterrence n. 嚇阻、威懾
    Dialogue should not come at the expense of deterrence.
    對話不應以犧牲嚇阻力為代價。
  • implementation n. 實施、執行
    In a system characterized by consensus-building and nemawashi — informal coordination before formal decisions — her choices suggest an emphasis on faster implementation and clearer political control.
    在一個以凝聚共識和「根回し」（即在正式決定前進行的非正式協調）為特徵的體制中，她的選擇暗示了對加速實施政策以及更明確政治控制的強調。
  • commitment n. 承諾、許諾
    Japan has traditionally sought reassurance from the US regarding the Diaoyutais, extended deterrence and its commitment to the alliance.
    日本傳統上尋求美國對釣魚台、擴大嚇阻以及美國對同盟承諾的再保證。
  • coercion n. 強迫、高壓、經濟脅迫
    Taiwan is increasingly discussed alongside the East China Sea, Japan’s southwestern islands, semiconductors, critical minerals and economic coercion.
    台灣正日益與東海、日本西南諸島、半導體、關鍵礦產及經濟脅迫一同被討論。
  • decouple v. 脫鉤、分離
    The world cannot completely decouple from China to avoid its economic coercion, so the US, Japan and their partners need to move from economic resilience to economic deterrence...
    世界無法完全與中國脫鉤以避免其經濟脅迫，因此美國、日本及其夥伴需要從經濟韌性轉向經濟嚇阻……
  • resilience n. 韌性、恢復力
    ...so the US, Japan and their partners need to move from economic resilience to economic deterrence — building an economy that Beijing cannot easily coerce.
    ……因此美國、日本及其夥伴需要從經濟韌性轉向經濟嚇阻——建立一個北京無法輕易脅迫的經濟體。
  • isolate v. 隔離、孤立
    Its security is becoming harder to isolate from the interests of Japan and other US allies.
    台灣的安全正變得更難與日本及其他美國盟友的利益相隔離。
  • guarantee v. 保證、擔保
    However, that does not guarantee particular US decisions, especially under a transactional president.
    然而，這並不保證美國會做出特定的決策，特別是在一位交易型總統的領導下。

文法重點

  • 不定詞作後位修飾 (Infinitive as Postmodifier)
    Beijing has for decades insisted that Taiwan is a matter to be settled between China and the US...
    句中的 "to be settled" 是不定詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "a matter"，意思是「一個『有待被解決』的（問題）」。「be + 過去分詞」表示被動語態，這種結構常用於表達某事即將發生或需要被處理。
  • 否定副詞置於句首的倒裝句 (Negative Adverb Inversion)
    Tokyo cannot dictate the US’ Taiwan policy, nor does it oppose US-China dialogue.
    句中的 "nor does it oppose..." 是倒裝結構。當否定對等連接詞 "nor" 置於句首連接第二個子句時，須將助動詞（在此為 "does"）移至主語（"it"）之前，形成「nor + 助動詞 + 主語 + 動詞」的語序，用來延續前面的否定語氣。
  • 分詞片語作形容詞用法 (Participial Phrase as Adjective)
    In a system characterized by consensus-building and nemawashi... her choices suggest an emphasis on faster implementation...
    句中的 "characterized by..." 是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞 "system"。因為 system 是「被」這些特徵所定義，所以使用過去分詞 (characterized) 而非現在分詞，這種簡化的形容詞子句在商業及新聞英文中非常常見。
  • 動名詞作介系詞的受詞 (Gerund as Object of Preposition)
    The world cannot completely decouple from China to avoid its economic coercion, so the US, Japan and their partners need to move from economic resilience to economic deterrence...
    介系詞（如 "from"、"to"）後面若要接動詞，必須將動詞改為動名詞（V-ing）型態。例如句中的 "from economic resilience to economic deterrence" 是介系詞片語，而文章其他地方如 "from economic resilience to economic deterrence" 展示了從某個狀態移動到另一個狀態的結構。
