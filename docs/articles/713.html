以色列部長指控導演因加薩紀錄片犯下叛國罪

Minister accuses directors of treason over Gaza film

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • accuse v. 指控，控告
    Israeli Minister of Culture Miki Zohar on Sunday called for the award-winning directors of an Israeli-made Gaza documentary to be stripped of citizenship, accusing them of “treason against the state.”
    以色列文化部長米基·佐哈爾於週日呼籲剝奪一部以色列製作的加薩紀錄片之獲獎導演的公民身份，指控他們「對國家叛國」。
  • artificial intelligence n. 人工智慧
    In NAZA, Israeli directors Yuval Abraham and Rachel Szor spoke to 24 different Israeli military or intelligence whistle-blowers who outlined the artificial intelligence-powered targeting systems used to bombard Gaza that have killed tens of thousands of civilians.
    在《NAZA》中，以色列導演尤瓦爾·亞伯拉罕和蕾切爾·索爾與 24 名不同的以色列軍事或情報吹哨人進行了對話，這些吹哨人概述了用於轟炸加薩、已殺害數萬名平民的人工智慧驅動目標定位系統。
  • harm v. / n. 傷害，損害
    Zohar accused the directors of being willing to “harm their homeland in order to receive applause from anti-Semites around the world.”
    佐哈爾指控這些導演甘願「傷害祖國，只為了獲得全世界反猶太主義者的掌聲」。
  • revoke v. 撤銷，吊銷
    “I will act immediately to revoke the Israeli citizenship of these despicable creators for treason against the state,” he added on social media.
    他在社群媒體上補充說：「我將立即採取行動，撤銷這些卑鄙創作者的以色列公民身份，以懲處其叛國罪。」
  • apply v. 適用，應用；申請
    The revocation of nationality is legally possible in Israel, although it is rarely applied.
    在以色列，撤銷國籍在法律上是可行的，儘管很少被實施。
  • commit v. 犯（罪、錯等），承諾
    In 2022, the Israeli Supreme Court confirmed that the state may strip people of their nationality if they have failed to show “loyalty,” for example by committing acts of espionage or treason.
    2022 年，以色列最高法院確認，如果人們未能表現出「忠誠」，例如犯下間諜罪或叛國罪，國家可以剝奪他們的國籍。
  • convict v. 宣判有罪
    The law was also expanded in 2023 to apply to people convicted of “terrorism.”
    該法律還在 2023 年進行了擴大，適用於被判犯有「恐怖主義」罪行的人。
  • submit v. 提交，呈遞
    The law requires the Israeli minister of the interior to submit the revocation request, the justice minister to approve it and judges to validate it.
    該法律要求以色列內政部長提交撤銷請求，司法部長批准，並由法官予以驗證。
  • approve v. 批准，贊成
    The law requires the Israeli minister of the interior to submit the revocation request, the justice minister to approve it and judges to validate it.
    該法律要求以色列內政部長提交撤銷請求，司法部長批准，並由法官予以驗證。
  • deny v. 否定，否認
    Israel denies deliberately targeting civilians.
    以色列否認蓄意針對平民。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...targeting systems used to bombard Gaza that have killed tens of thousands of civilians.
    句中的 "used to bombard Gaza" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "targeting systems"（目標定位系統），相當於關代子句 "that are used to bombard Gaza" 的簡化版，省略了主格關代與be動詞，使語氣更為緊湊精煉。
  • 被動語態的應用 (Passive Voice)
    The revocation of nationality is legally possible in Israel, although it is rarely applied.
  • 條件複句與假設語氣概念 (Conditional Clause)
    ...if they have failed to show “loyalty,” for example by committing acts of espionage or treason.
