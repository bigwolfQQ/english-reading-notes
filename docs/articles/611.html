好色綿羊遭開罰

Amorous sheep get fined

單字解析

  • livestock n. 牲畜，家畜
    A livestock farmer in Changhua County was fined NT$1,000 after his flock of about 95 meat sheep made noises in the night, disturbing nearby residents.
    彰化縣一名畜牧農夫因其飼養的約95隻肉羊在夜間發出噪音、干擾附近居民，而被罰款新台幣1,000元。
  • disturb v. 打擾，妨害，干擾
    A livestock farmer in Changhua County was fined NT$1,000 after his flock of about 95 meat sheep made noises in the night, disturbing nearby residents.
    彰化縣一名畜牧農夫因其飼養的約95隻肉羊在夜間發出噪音、干擾附近居民，而被罰款新台幣1,000元。
  • issue v. 發布，發行，開具
    Police visited the farm in January and issued a citation ordering Chen to address the noise problem, the ruling said.
    裁定指出，警方於1月造訪該農場，並開出告發單命令陳男解決噪音問題。
  • address v. 處理，對付，解決
    Police visited the farm in January and issued a citation ordering Chen to address the noise problem, the ruling said.
    裁定指出，警方於1月造訪該農場，並開出告發單命令陳男解決噪音問題。
  • persistent adj. 持續的，堅持不懈的
    Chen said the bleating was not a persistent problem and mainly occurred during the spring and autumn mating seasons, when rams and young male sheep would call out, the ruling said.
    裁定指出，陳男表示叫聲並非持續性問題，主要發生在春秋兩季的交配期，當時公羊和年輕的公羊會叫出聲。
  • measure n. 措施，方法
    It quoted Chen as saying he had since taken measures to address the problem, including separating the rams and castrating young males that matured early.
    裁定引述陳男說法指出，他自此已採取措施來解決問題，包括將公羊隔開以及對提早成熟的年輕公羊進行結紮。
  • investigate v. 調查，偵查
    Chen said the sheep’s calls were only brief sounds and were “quieter than birdsong,” making them acceptable, and said the authorities had failed to properly investigate the matter.
    陳男表示，羊隻的叫聲僅是短暫的聲音，且「比鳥鳴還要安靜」，因此是可以接受的，並指責當局未妥善調查此事。
  • extend v. 延長，延伸（在此為衍生形容詞 extended「長期的」）
    The judge ruled that noise covered by the Social Order Maintenance Act does not have to exceed a specified decibel level or continue for an extended period, and that noise that is sufficient to disturb others’ peace and quiet can constitute an offense.
    法官裁定，《社會秩序維護法》所涵蓋的噪音不必超過特定的分貝水準或持續很長的時間，只要足以妨害他人安寧即構成違法。
  • constitute v. 構成，組成
    The judge ruled that noise covered by the Social Order Maintenance Act does not have to exceed a specified decibel level or continue for an extended period, and that noise that is sufficient to disturb others’ peace and quiet can constitute an offense.
    法官裁定，《社會秩序維護法》所涵蓋的噪音不必超過特定的分貝水準或持續很長的時間，只要足以妨害他人安寧即構成違法。
  • effectiveness n. 有效性，效力，成效
    Although Chen had separated and castrated some of the sheep, the measures displayed limited effectiveness and had failed to prevent the flock from disturbing neighbors, the ruling said.
    裁定指出，儘管陳男已將部分羊隻隔離並結紮，但這些措施表現出的成效有限，且未能阻止羊群打擾鄰居。
  • appeal n. 上訴，申訴
    The court rejected Chen’s appeal and upheld the NT$1,000 fine.
    法院駁回陳男的上訴，並維持新台幣1,000元的罰款。
  • uphold v. 維持（原判），支持，支撐

    法院駁回陳男的上訴，並維持新台幣1,000元的罰款。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作附帶結果（V-ing）
    A livestock farmer in Changhua County was fined NT$1,000 after his flock of about 95 meat sheep made noises in the night, disturbing nearby residents.
    句尾的「disturbing nearby residents」是由連接詞和主詞簡化而來的分詞構句，用來補充說明前面羊隻發出噪音所產生的直接結果（即「進而打擾了附近居民」）。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    According to the final ruling delivered on Tuesday last week by the Changhua District Court...
    過去分詞片語「delivered on Tuesday last week by the Changhua District Court」修飾其前面的名詞「the ruling」，相當於一個省略了「which was」的關係代名詞子句（...ruling which was delivered on...）。
  • 現在分詞短語作後位修飾
    It quoted Chen as saying he had since taken measures to address the problem, including separating the rams and castrating young males that matured early.
    片語「including separating the rams and castrating young males...」是現在分詞開頭的介系詞性質短語，用來舉例說明前面提到的「measures」（措施），在多益閱讀測驗中常考這種分詞引導的補充說明結構。
  • 讓步副詞子句（Although...）
    Although the sheep’s calls were brief, they were particularly noticeable and piercing at night, and could disturb nearby residents’ sleep, the judge said.
    「Although...」引導一個讓步副詞子句，表示「雖然／儘管……」，用來帶出與主句相反或形成對比的狀況（雖然叫聲短暫，但夜晚特別明顯）。
