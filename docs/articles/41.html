遭指控以「月經」羞辱球員，裁判將面臨南韓足協聽證會

Referee accused of ‘period’ insult to face KFA hearing

單字解析

  • disciplinary hearing n. 懲戒聽證會
    A female referee in South Korea is to face a disciplinary hearing over accusations she insulted former Chelsea midfielder Ji So-yun by saying that she was menstruating, the association said yesterday.
    南韓足協昨天表示，一名南韓女性裁判因被指控羞辱前切爾西中場球員池笑然，稱她正在經期，將面臨紀律聽證會。
  • accusation n. 指控，控告
    A female referee in South Korea is to face a disciplinary hearing over accusations she insulted former Chelsea midfielder Ji So-yun by saying that she was menstruating, the association said yesterday.
    南韓足協昨天表示，一名南韓女性裁判因被指控羞辱前切爾西中場球員池笑然，稱她正在經期，將面臨紀律聽證會。
  • inquiry n. 調查，詢問
    The inquiry would focus on an incident that saw the midfielder booked after confronting the official during a domestic women’s league game between Ji’s Suwon FC Women and Seoul City Hall.
    這起調查將聚焦於一起事件：在池笑然效力的水原FC女足與首爾市廳女足之間的國內女子聯賽比賽中，該名中場球員因找裁判理論而被出示黃牌。
  • confront v. 對質，對抗，面臨
    The inquiry would focus on an incident that saw the midfielder booked after confronting the official during a domestic women’s league game between Ji’s Suwon FC Women and Seoul City Hall.
    這起調查將聚焦於一起事件：在池笑然效力的水原FC女足與首爾市廳女足之間的國內女子聯賽比賽中，該名中場球員因找裁判理論而被出示黃牌。
  • demand v. 要求，請求
    It was sparked when Ji demanded a penalty for her side during the WK League match last month.
    這是在上個月的 WK 聯賽比賽中，池笑然要求為己方判罰十二碼罰球時所引發的。
  • allege v. 指控，宣稱
    After approaching the referee, Ji alleged the official said: “She’s been touchy since the start, she must be on her period.”
    在接近裁判後，池笑然指控該裁判說：「她從一開始就很情緒化，她一定是在經期。」
  • refer v. 提交，轉交，提及
    A Korea Football Association (KFA) official said that they have referred the case to the association’s disciplinary committee.
    韓國足球協會（KFA）的一名官員表示，他們已將此案提交給足協的紀律委員會。
  • take place phr. 發生，舉行
    The KFA did not say when the hearing would take place.
    南韓足協未說明聽證會將於何時舉行。
  • specifically adv. 特定地，明確地
    The KFA’s refereeing department previously said the word “period” was not used and that the remarks did not specifically refer to Ji, broadcaster SBS said.
    據廣播公司 SBS 報導，南韓足協的裁判部門先前表示，並未使用「經期」一詞，且這些言論並非針對池笑然。
  • seriousness n. 嚴重性
    The KFA said their referee council “recognized the seriousness of the allegations” and decided to refer the case to its disciplinary committee.
    南韓足協表示，他們的裁判委員會「意識到指控的嚴重性」，並決定將此案提交給其紀律委員會。

文法重點

  • be to + V (表未來計畫或即將發生的官方安排)
    A female referee in South Korea is to face a disciplinary hearing over accusations she insulted former Chelsea midfielder Ji So-yun...
    「be to V」常用於新聞英語中，表示官方安排、預定或即將發生的正式事件（在此指即將面臨聽證會）。
  • 感官動詞 / 授權動詞的受詞補語 (see + O + p.p.)
    ...an incident that saw the midfielder booked after confronting the official...
    此處的 "saw" 賦予擬人化用法，用來指「某段時間或事件中發生了某事」；"the midfielder" 是受詞，"booked" 是過去分詞作受詞補語，表示中場球員「被」登記警告（出示黃牌），呈現被動語意。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...a euphemism referring to menstruation.
    雖然此處是現在分詞，但句型結構上，名詞片語後方常接分詞片語來修飾前面的名詞。此句中 "referring to menstruation" 用來補充說明前面 "a euphemism"（委婉說法）所指為何。
