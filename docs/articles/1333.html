文法重點

  • be scheduled to do (be V2 的未來安排)
    Karl Deisseroth... is scheduled to speak at Academia Sinica in March next year...
    用來表達「預定於未來某個時間點進行某事」，在商業新聞或行程安排的報導中非常高頻。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (who 引導的形容詞子句)
    Karl Deisseroth, the Stanford University professor who on Monday won the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine, is scheduled to speak...
    句中的「who on Monday won the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine」是用來補充修飾前面的先行詞 Karl Deisseroth，多益閱讀測驗的句型題常考此類主格關代用法。
  • 使役與賦能動詞句構 (enable + O. + to V.)
    The technology enables researchers to better understand the relationship between the brain and behavior...
    「enable + 受詞 + to V.」意為「使……能夠……」，在多益職場與科技文章中極為常見，用來表達某項工具或政策帶來的功能。
  • 被動語態 (be + p.p.)
    ...warned that safety and other issues need to be addressed before the technology can be widely used to treat neurological and psychiatric disorders in humans.
    新聞報導常聚焦於「動作的承受者」，因此大量使用被動語態（如 need to be addressed 與 has been used），強調客觀事實與行動。
