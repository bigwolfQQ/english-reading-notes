平凡的英雄

Everyday heroes

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • member n. 成員，會員
    Members of the Rotary Club District 3490 hold gold-colored shovels as they plant a tree...
    國際扶輪社3490地區的成員們手持金色鏟子，種下一棵樹……
  • in honor of phr. 向……致敬，為紀念……
    ...as they plant a tree in honor of the volunteers who helped clean residents’ homes...
    ……以表彰去年在花蓮縣馬太鞍溪堰塞湖溢流造成水災後，自備鏟子協助清理居民家園的志工們。
  • volunteer n. 志工，義工
    ...in honor of the volunteers who helped clean residents’ homes using shovels they brought themselves...
    ……以表彰……自備鏟子協助清理居民家園的志工們。
  • resident n. 居民
    ...who helped clean residents’ homes using shovels they brought themselves after the flooding...
    ……協助清理居民家園……
  • cause v. 造成，引起
    ...after the flooding caused by the Mataian River barrier lake overflow in Hualien County last year.
    ……在去年花蓮縣馬太鞍溪堰塞湖溢流造成水災後。

文法重點

  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    ...in honor of the volunteers who helped clean residents’ homes using shovels they brought themselves...
    句中的 "who helped clean..." 是用來修飾先行詞 "the volunteers" 的形容詞子句，用以指稱那些提供協助的志工。
  • 過去分詞作後置修飾語 (Past Participle as Postmodifier)
    ...after the flooding caused by the Mataian River barrier lake overflow in Hualien County last year.
    句中的 "caused by..." 是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞 "flooding"，相當於形容詞子句 "which was caused by..." 的簡化用法。
  • 省略關代的名詞子句/形容詞子句 (Omitted Relative Pronoun)
    ...using shovels they brought themselves...
    在 "shovels they brought themselves" 當中，shovels 後方省略了受格關係代名詞 that 或 which，"they brought themselves" 直接修飾 shovels，意指「他們自己帶來的鏟子」。
