台灣以自1990年以來最少金牌數結束2026年亞洲運動會

Taiwan closes out 2026 Asian Games with fewest gold medals since 1990

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • wrap up phr. 圓滿結束、完成
    The 2026 Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games wrapped up its final day of events on Sunday, with Team Taiwan taking home only five gold medals, its lowest total since the 1990 games in Beijing, China.
    2026年愛知-名古屋亞洲運動會於週日結束了最後一天的賽事，台灣代表隊僅帶回5面金牌，創下自1990年中國北京亞運以來的最低紀錄。
  • compete v. 競爭、參賽
    Taiwan, competing under the name Chinese Taipei, also won 17 silvers and 33 bronzes to finish with a total of 55 medals.
    台灣以「中華台北」的名義參賽，另贏得17面銀牌與33面銅牌，總計獲得55面獎牌。
  • total n. 總數、合計
    While the total medal count was only slightly lower than in previous Asian Games -- it won 67 in the 2022 and 2018 editions -- the five golds were Taiwan's fewest since the 1990 games in Beijing, when it won none.
    雖然總獎牌數僅比歷屆亞運略低——在2022年與2018年賽事中分別獲得67面——但5面金牌是台灣自1990年北京亞運（當時未獲金牌）以來最少的一次。
  • edition n. （發行、舉辦的）屆、版次
    While the total medal count was only slightly lower than in previous Asian Games -- it won 67 in the 2022 and 2018 editions -- the five golds were Taiwan's fewest since the 1990 games in Beijing, when it won none.
    雖然總獎牌數僅比歷屆亞運略低——在2022年與2018年賽事中分別獲得67面——但5面金牌是台灣自1990年北京亞運（當時未獲金牌）以來最少的一次。
  • decline n. 下降、衰退
    It was also a steep decline from the 2022 Hangzhou Asian Games, when Taiwan tied its best-ever performance with 19 golds.
    這也比2022年杭州亞運出現了急遽下滑，當時台灣以19面金牌追平了歷史最佳戰績。
  • performance n. 表現、績效
    It was also a steep decline from the 2022 Hangzhou Asian Games, when Taiwan tied its best-ever performance with 19 golds.
    這也比2022年杭州亞運出現了急遽下滑，當時台灣以19面金牌追平了歷史最佳戰績。

    Taiwan's five gold medals included Yu Kai-wen (余凱文) and Huang Shih-yuan's (黃詩媛) win in soft tennis mixed doubles, as well as swimmer Wang Kuan-hung's (王冠閎) first-place finish in the men's 200-meter butterfly.
    台灣的5面金牌包括余凱文與黃詩媛在軟式網球混合雙打中奪冠，以及游泳選手王冠閎在男子200公尺蝶式中拿下第一名。
  • defeat v. 擊敗、戰勝

    同時，奧運冠軍林郁婷在女子60公斤級決賽中，以無異議的5比0裁判判定擊敗中國對手，贏得她個人的連續第二面亞運拳擊金牌。
  • consecutive adj. 連續的

  • opponent n. 對手、敵手

文法重點

  • 獨立分詞構句 (Nominative Absolute Construction)
    The 2026 Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games wrapped up its final day of events on Sunday, with Team Taiwan taking home only five gold medals, its lowest total since the 1990 games in Beijing, China.
    句型「with + 名詞/代名詞 + 分詞 (V-ing / V-ed)」用來表示附帶狀況或背景補充。在此句中，「Team Taiwan」是分詞「taking」的邏輯主詞，兩者為主動關係，用來補充說明台灣代表隊帶回獎牌時的狀態。
  • 現在分詞作形容詞用法 (Present Participle as Adjective)
    Taiwan, competing under the name Chinese Taipei, also won 17 silvers and 33 bronzes to finish with a total of 55 medals.
    句中的「competing under the name Chinese Taipei」是由現在分詞帶領的片語，用來修飾前面的主詞「Taiwan」，相當於一個形容詞子句「which competed under the name Chinese Taipei」，省略了主代名詞與be動詞，使語氣更為精鍊。
  • 關係副詞引導的時間副詞子句 (Adverb of Time Clause with Relative Adverb)
    While the total medal count was only slightly lower than in previous Asian Games -- it won 67 in the 2022 and 2018 editions -- the five golds were Taiwan's fewest since the 1990 games in Beijing, when it won none.
    句尾的「when it won none」是由關係副詞「when」引導的非限定子句，先行詞是前面的「1990 games in Beijing」，用來補充說明該年度沒有獲得任何金牌的背景狀況。
  • 介詞片語表示方法 (Prepositional Phrase of Means)
    Meanwhile, Olympic champion Lin Yu-ting (林郁婷) won her second consecutive Asian Games boxing gold medal by defeating her Chinese opponent in a unanimous 5-0 decision in the women's 60-kilogram final.
    「by + V-ing」結構用來表示達成某個動作的「方法」或「手段」。此處「by defeating her Chinese opponent」說明了林郁婷是透過「擊敗中國對手」的方式來贏得金牌。
