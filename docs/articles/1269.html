文法重點

  • 分詞構句（用作結果或補充說明）
    In addition, all deployment of OCAC overseas staff and use of official vehicles was in compliance with established protocols and limited to official business, thus precluding any private use or overclaiming of funds, the OCAC said.
    句尾的「thus precluding...」是現在分詞形成的結果分詞構句，用來接續前面子句所發生的動作，自然地表達「因此產生了……的結果」，在商業與新聞英文中非常常見。
  • 過去分詞（分詞片語）作後置定語
    ...while reimbursements applied for by Hsu were rigorously audited, with no inaccuracies or violations found, the agency said.
    「applied for by Hsu」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「reimbursements（核銷款項）」，相當於省略了「which were applied for by Hsu」的形容詞子句，使句子更為精煉。
  • 名詞子句（that 引導的受詞子句）
    The Taiwan People's Party (TPP) on Monday accused Overseas Community Affairs Council (OCAC) head Hsu Chia-ching (徐佳青) and her family members of misusing public resources during her trips abroad -- allegations that the OCAC has flatly denied.
