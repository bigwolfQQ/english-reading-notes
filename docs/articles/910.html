消費者信心改善

Consumer confidence improves

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • consumer confidence n. 消費者信心
    Taiwan’s consumer confidence rebounded this month as expectations for economic growth and spending improved...
    根據國泰金控昨天發布的一項調查顯示，由於對經濟成長和消費的預期有所改善，台灣本月的消費者信心有所回升...
  • rebound v. / n. 回升，反彈
    Taiwan’s consumer confidence rebounded this month as expectations for economic growth and spending improved...
    由於對經濟成長和消費的預期有所改善，台灣本月的消費者信心有所回升...
  • expectation n. 預期，期望
    Taiwan’s consumer confidence rebounded this month as expectations for economic growth and spending improved...
    由於對經濟成長和消費的預期有所改善，台灣本月的消費者信心有所回升...
  • forecast n. / v. 預測，預報
    ...even as households continued to expect inflation to run above the government’s forecast...
    ...儘管家庭仍預期通膨率將高於政府的預測...
  • index n. 指數
    The survey of 14,187 people online found that the economic optimism index rose to 14.8 from 3.1 a month earlier...
    這項針對 14,187 名線上民眾進行的調查發現，經濟樂觀指數從一個月前的 3.1 升至 14.8...
  • momentum n. 動能，氣勢
    The improvement comes as Taiwan’s economy maintains solid growth momentum...
    這項改善是在台灣經濟維持穩健成長動能的背景下出現的...
  • respondent n. 受訪者
    Households have become more optimistic about economic growth, with 52 percent of respondents expecting Taiwan’s economy to expand by more than 11 percent this year...
    民眾對經濟成長變得更加樂觀，有 52% 的受訪者預期今年台灣經濟將成長 11% 以上...
  • durable goods n. phr. 耐久財
    ...while easing the decline in spending on durable goods, with the index for big-ticket purchases rising to 18.3 from 15.3 and the index for durable goods increasing to minus-3.9 from minus-6.0.
    ...同時減緩了耐久財支出的跌幅，其中大額消費指數從 15.3 升至 18.3，耐久財指數從負 6.0 增至負 3.9。
  • investment position n. phr. 投資部位，持股部位
    Against this backdrop, 42.7 percent said they planned to increase their investment positions, while 47.5 percent intended to hold steady...
    在此背景下，42.7% 的民眾表示計劃增加投資部位，47.5% 打算維持不變...
  • attribute to phr. v. 將……歸因於
    Cathay Financial attributed the stronger sentiment to continued earnings strength in the technology sector...
    國泰金控將較強的市場情緒歸因於科技產業持續強勁的獲利表現...
  • earnings n. 收益，利潤
    Cathay Financial attributed the stronger sentiment to continued earnings strength in the technology sector and sustained investment in artificial intelligence (AI).
    國泰金控將較強的市場情緒歸因於科技產業持續強勁的獲利表現，以及對人工智慧（AI）的持續投資。
  • capital-spending adj. 資本支出的
    At the same time, major US cloud service providers continued to raise their capital-spending plans...
    與此同時，美國主要雲端服務供應商持續提高資本支出計劃...
  • exceed v. 超過，超出
    About 79 percent of respondents expect inflation to exceed 2 percent in a year...
    約 79% 的受訪者預期一年內的通膨率將超過 2%...
  • target range n. phr. 目標區間
    Only 15 percent expect inflation to remain between 1 percent and 2 percent, the central bank’s target range.
    僅有 15% 預期通膨率將保持在中央銀行目標區間的 1% 至 2% 之間。

文法重點

  • with + 受詞 + 分詞 (附帶狀況說明)
    The improvement comes as Taiwan’s economy maintains solid growth momentum, with the government’s business climate monitor continuing to signal strong expansion, while leading and coincident indicators rose further, Cathay Financial said.
    「with + 名詞 +現在分詞 (continuing)」結構在此處用作附帶狀況，用來補充說明台灣經濟維持成長動能時，政府的景氣對策信號燈也「持續」發出擴張信號的伴隨情形。
  • with + 受詞 + 形容詞/分詞 (補充數據與狀態)
    Households have become more optimistic about economic growth, with 52 percent of respondents expecting Taiwan’s economy to expand by more than 11 percent this year, broadly in line with the government’s forecast of 11.05 percent.
    此處的「with + 52 percent of respondents + expecting...」同樣是 with 的複合結構（附帶狀況），用來具體描述家庭變得樂觀的細節內容（即 52% 的受訪者預期經濟成長率）。
  • 現在分詞當作形容詞修飾名詞
    At the same time, major US cloud service providers continued to raise their capital-spending plans, while their better-than-expected earnings reinforced expectations for sustained AI investment...
    句中的 "sustained" 是由動詞 sustain 變形而來的過去分詞，在此放在名詞 investment 前方，當作形容詞使用，意思是「持續的」投資。
