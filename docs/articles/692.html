平均薪資攀升，但中位數薪資仍低

Average wages climbing, but median wages low

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • average adj. / n. 平均的；平均數
    The average monthly regular wage in July was NT$49,491, a 3.32 percent increase from a year earlier
    7月份的平均每月經常性薪資為新台幣49,491元，較去年同期增加3.32%。
  • regular wage n. 經常性薪資
    The average monthly regular wage in July was NT$49,491, a 3.32 percent increase from a year earlier
    7月份的平均每月經常性薪資為新台幣49,491元，較去年同期增加3.32%。
  • increase n. / v. 增加，提高
    a 3.32 percent increase from a year earlier and up 0.3 percent from the month before
    較去年同期增加3.32%，並較前一個月上升0.3%。
  • bonus n. 獎金，紅利
    Including overtime, bonuses and other commissions, the overall monthly wage averaged NT$71,165
    包含加班費、獎金及其他佣金在內，整體每月總薪資平均為新台幣71,165元。
  • overall adj. 整體的，全面的
    the overall monthly wage averaged NT$71,165, an annual increase of 8.25 percent
    整體每月總薪資平均為新台幣71,165元，年增8.25%。
  • annual adj. 年度的，每年的
    an annual increase of 8.25 percent and a monthly growth of 17.86 percent
    年增8.25%，月增17.86%。
  • median adj. / n. 中位數的；中位數
    In July, the median monthly wage — generally considered a more accurate measure of people’s income
    7月份，每月薪資中位數——通常被認為是對民眾所得更為精準的衡量標準
  • measure n. 衡量標準，措施
    generally considered a more accurate measure of people’s income as extremely high or low wages do not skew it
    通常被認為是對民眾所得更為精準的衡量標準，因為極高或極低的薪資不會扭曲它。
  • survey n. / v. 調查
    The wage data were based on the agency’s survey of employees in the nation’s industrial and service sectors.
    薪資數據是基於該機構對全國工業及服務業受僱員工的調查。
  • sector n. 部門，行業
    employees in the nation’s industrial and service sectors.
    全國工業及服務業的受僱員工。
  • cumulative adj. 累積的
    In the first seven months, cumulative total earnings grew 4.18 percent
    在前七個月中，累計總收益成長了4.18%。
  • indicator n. 指標
    Regarding overtime hours, a key indicator of economic activity, the manufacturing sector averaged 18 hours of overtime
    關於作為經濟活動關鍵指標的加班時數，製造業平均加班18小時。
  • manufacturing adj. / n. 製造業的；製造業
    the manufacturing sector averaged 18 hours of overtime in July
    製造業在7月份平均加班18小時。
  • positive adj. 正向的，積極的
    the overall labor market is moving in a generally positive direction.
    整體勞動市場正朝著大致正向的方向發展。
  • squeeze v. 擠壓，使緊縮
    it could squeeze future real wage growth, she said.
    她表示，這可能會壓縮未來實質薪資的成長。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    In July, the median monthly wage — generally considered a more accurate measure of people’s income as extremely high or low wages do not skew it — increased 3.47 percent...
    句中的「generally considered a more accurate measure...」是一個過去分詞片語，用來補充說明前面的名詞「the median monthly wage」。省略了「which is」，使句子更為簡潔，是新聞英文中常見的寫法。
  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況 (Participial Phrase indicating accompanying circumstance)
    After adjusting for inflation, which increased 1.8 percent annually in the first seven months, the average monthly regular wage was NT$44,113...
    句首的「After adjusting for inflation」是一個介系詞片語搭配動名詞的結構（在統計報導中常簡化為分詞概念使用），用來表示「在調整了……之後」的動作背景，主句的主語與省略的主語一致（即數據本身被調整）。
  • 形容詞子句 (Adjective Clause / Relative Clause)
    ...the median monthly wage — generally considered a more accurate measure of people’s income as extremely high or low wages do not skew it — increased...
    句中「as extremely high or low wages do not skew it」是由連接詞 as 引導的原因副詞子句，用來解釋為什麼它是一個更精準的衡量標準。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The wage data were based on the agency’s survey of employees in the nation’s industrial and service sectors.
