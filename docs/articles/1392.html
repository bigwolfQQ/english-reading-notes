文法重點

  • be expected to / be scheduled to 表預計或預定發生的未來事件
    Two Coast Guard Administration (CGA) patrol vessels have docked at Malakal Harbor in Palau as part of an expedition exercise and diplomatic visit, during which a senior official is expected to meet with Palauan President Surangel Whipps Jr, the agency said yesterday.
    「be expected to + V」意指「預期會……」，用來表達按計畫或推測未來將發生的事情；句中的「be scheduled to + V」也是類似用法，用來表達預定好的行程。
  • 「介系詞 + 分詞」組成的非限定關係子句 (during which)
    The two sides are scheduled to attend a banquet aboard the Chiayi , during which a Double Ten National Day reception and a joint flag-raising ceremony are scheduled, it said.
    句中的「during which」引導一個非限定關係子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞（a banquet）。「which」代指 banquet，「during」表達在該宴會進行的期間內，這種句型在多益閱讀測驗中非常常考。
  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾語 (signed in 2019)
    Afterward, the two vessels are to conduct a joint patrol with Palauan law enforcement units as part of a bilateral coast guard cooperation agreement signed in 2019, it said.
    句尾的「signed in 2019」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「agreement（協定）」。其完整概念是「which was signed in 2019」，省略了「which was」後直接留下過去分詞，能使句子更精簡流暢。
  • 現在分詞片語作附加說明 (adding that...)
    The trip is significant for Taiwan’s international maritime cooperation and “coast guard diplomacy,” it said, adding that the CGA will continue to establish international partnerships.
    逗號之後的「adding that...」是由現在分詞「adding」引導的分詞構句，用來補充說明主句動作發生時的伴隨情況或進一步的結果，意為「並補充說……」。
