中華航空表示，戰爭推升長程航班需求

War pushing demand for long-haul flights, CAL says

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • demand n. 需求
    Although the war between the US and Iran has pushed global oil prices higher, it has also increased demand for direct long-haul flights to Europe as people seek safer routes, China Airlines (CAL) said on Monday.
    中華航空（CAL）週一表示，儘管美伊之間的戰爭推高了全球油價，但也增加了民眾尋求更安全航線、直飛歐洲長程航班的需求。
  • fluctuate v. 波動，起伏
    CAL president Kevin Chen (陳漢銘) said that aviation fuel prices have fluctuated significantly since February amid the ongoing US-Iran war, at one point surging to between US$190 and US$200 per barrel.
    華航總經理陳漢銘表示，自二月以來，隨著持續的美伊戰爭，航空燃油價格大幅波動，一度飆升至每桶 190 美元至 200 美元之間。
  • surge v. 急遽上升，飆升

  • reassess v. 重新評估
    The volatility has prompted the airline to reassess the load factor of each flight and adjust aircraft capacity accordingly, he said.
    他說，這種波動促使該航空公司重新評估每個航班的載客率，並相應調整機型與載運能力。
  • capacity n. 容量，生產力，載客量
    The volatility has prompted the airline to reassess the load factor of each flight and adjust aircraft capacity accordingly, he said.
    他說，這種波動促使該航空公司重新評估每個航班的載客率，並相應調整機型與載運能力。
  • deploy v. 調派，配置（資源、人員等）
    Demand in Southeast Asia for passenger and cargo services is high, prompting CAL to deploy larger aircraft on some routes to maximize cargo space and generate additional revenue, he said.
    他說，東南亞對客運和貨運服務的需求很高，這促使華航在某些航線上調派大型飛機，以最大化貨運空間並創造額外的營收。
  • revenue n. 營收，收益
    Despite high fuel prices, the airline expects revenue this year to surpass last year’s, potentially reaching a record, Chen said.
    陳漢銘表示，儘管油價高企，該航空公司預計今年的營收將超越去年，甚至可能創下歷史新高。
  • surpass v. 超越，勝過

  • optimize v. 使最優化（文章中使用 maximize，為同義多益核心字）
    Demand in Southeast Asia for passenger and cargo services is high, prompting CAL to deploy larger aircraft on some routes to maximize cargo space and generate additional revenue, he said.
    他說，東南亞對客運和貨運服務的需求很高，這促使華航在某些航線上調派大型飛機，以最大化貨運空間並創造額外的營收。
  • benefit v. 受益，獲益

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Phrase as a result / Result clause）表結果
    Although the war between the US and Iran has pushed global oil prices higher, it has also increased demand for direct long-haul flights to Europe as people seek safer routes, China Airlines (CAL) said on Monday.
    句中的「as people seek safer routes」是一個由連接詞 as 引導的原因副詞子句，用來解釋為什麼會增加直飛長程航班的需求。在多益閱讀中常見這種因果關係的子句配置。
  • 分詞構句作後置修飾 / 附帶狀況
    Demand in Southeast Asia for passenger and cargo services is high, prompting CAL to deploy larger aircraft on some routes to maximize cargo space and generate additional revenue, he said.
    逗號之後的「prompting CAL to deploy larger aircraft...」是現在分詞作分詞構句，用來補充說明前面主句所引發的結果（主動語態，原句意指這項高需求『促使』華航調派飛機）。這種結構在商業英文新聞中非常高頻，用以簡潔地表達伴隨發生的動作或結果。
  • 過去分詞作形容詞修飾名詞（被動概念）
    “We have also seen Taiwan emerge as a key hub amid changes in travel patterns triggered by wars, particularly for travelers from Northeast and Southeast Asia heading to North America and Europe,” he said.
    句中的「triggered by wars」是過去分詞片語，用來後置修飾前面的名詞「changes in travel patterns」。因為改變是被戰爭所「觸發」的，所以使用過去分詞表達被動與完成的意味。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞補語 / 分詞片語作形容詞
    Cargo demand has been robust, driven by exports of artificial intelligence and semiconductor-related equipment, the company said last month.
    句首子句的「driven by exports of...」是由過去分詞引導的片語，修飾前面的主詞 Cargo demand（貨運需求是被出口所驅動的）。這展現了多益閱讀中常見的分詞片語倒裝或省略主格be動詞的精簡寫法。
