文法重點

  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    The Taipei District Court on Friday approved a request by prosecutors to detain a taxi driver who rammed his vehicle into the Taipei District Prosecutors Office early Thursday morning.
    句中的 "who rammed his vehicle..." 是一個用來修飾前方先行詞 "a taxi driver" 的主格關係代名詞子句，用以補充說明這位司機做了什麼事情。
  • 分詞構句作補充說明（帶有轉折或結果意味）
    Shao was unable to pay his bond, however, prompting prosecutors to move to detain him instead, a request subsequently approved by the Taipei District Court, leading to the driver's detainment early Friday morning.
    句尾的 ", leading to the driver's detainment..." 是現在分詞 (leading) 引導的分詞構句，用來接續表達前面整個事件所造成的結果（導致該司機遭到羈押）。
  • 非限定關係子句夾入過去分詞片語
    Shao's taxi, which authorities found had slogans such as "the judicial system is dead" painted on the doors, was also seized...
    主詞 "Shao's taxi" 後方接了一個非限定關係子句 "which authorities found had slogans... painted on the doors"，其中又包含了過去分詞片語 "painted on the doors" 作受詞補語，用來形容標語是被漆在車門上。
