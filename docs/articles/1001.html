單字解析

  • injure v. 使受傷，傷害
    Typhoon Dujuan left at least four people dead and 22 injured in Japan...
    杜鵑颱風在日本造成至少四人死亡、二十二人受傷...
  • without power phr. 無電力的，斷電的
    ...while thousands of houses near Tokyo were without power after the storm swept through the region, authorities said yesterday.
    ...官方昨天表示，在暴風掃過該地區後，東京附近的數千戶房屋面臨斷電。
  • authority n. 官方，當局（常用複數）
    ...allowing authorities to lift or downgrade most heavy rain and landslide warnings.
    ...使得官方得以解除或調降大部分的大雨及土石流警報。
  • lift v. 解除（禁令、限制等），撤銷
    ...allowing authorities to lift or downgrade most heavy rain and landslide warnings.
    ...使得官方得以解除或調降大部分的大雨及土石流警報。
  • downgrade v. 調降，降級
    ...allowing authorities to lift or downgrade most heavy rain and landslide warnings.
    ...使得官方得以解除或調降大部分的大雨及土石流警報。
  • disrupt v. 中斷，擾亂
    ...with other storms this season disrupting some events at the Asian Games being held in the country.
    ...本季其他風暴也打亂了在該國舉行的亞洲運動會中的部分賽事。
  • electricity n. 電，電力
    More than 45,000 households in Chiba were still without electricity yesterday afternoon...
    直到昨天下午，千葉縣仍有超過四萬五千戶家庭無電可用...
  • weaken v. 減弱，變弱
    Although Dujuan weakened as it moved northeast, it dumped record rainfall in parts of the Tokyo area.
    儘管杜鵑在向東北移動的過程中減弱，但它在東京地區的部分地方降下破紀錄的雨量。
  • average adj. 平均的
    ...more than double its average rainfall for the whole of September, public broadcaster NHK reported.
    ...超過其整個九月份平均雨量的兩倍，日本公共廣播機構 NHK 報導。
  • intensify v. 加劇，強化
    Scientists say human-driven climate change is intensifying the risk posed by heavy rains...
    科學家表示，人為驅動的氣候變遷正在加劇暴雨所帶來的風險...
  • risk n. 風險，危險
    Scientists say human-driven climate change is intensifying the risk posed by heavy rains...
    科學家表示，人為驅動的氣候變遷正在加劇暴雨所帶來的風險...

文法重點

  • S + V + O + 過去分詞 (使役/狀態補語用法)
    Typhoon Dujuan left at least four people dead and 22 injured in Japan...
    句型「leave + O. + 過去分詞」表示「讓某人／某物呈現某種被動的狀態」。在此句中，22 injured 省略了 be 動詞，用來形容 22 個人「處於受傷的狀態」中。
  • 現在分詞片語作後置修飾語
    ...with other storms this season disrupting some events at the Asian Games being held in the country.
    句尾的 "being held in the country" 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "the Asian Games"，相當於關代子句 "which were being held in the country" 的簡化版，表達「正在該國舉辦的亞運會」。
  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾語
    Search operations were continuing for six people missing after landslides triggered by torrential rain, the agency said.
    句中的 "triggered by torrential rain" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "landslides"，相當於 "which were triggered by torrential rain"，表示「由豪雨所引發的土石流」。
  • Although引導的讓步副詞子句
    Although Dujuan weakened as it moved northeast, it dumped record rainfall in parts of the Tokyo area.
    “Although” 用於引導讓步子句，表示「雖然、儘管」，連接兩個相對立的子句：雖然杜鵑颱風減弱了，但它仍然在東京部分地區帶來破紀錄的降雨量。
  • because 引導的原因副詞子句
    Scientists say human-driven climate change is intensifying the risk posed by heavy rains because a warmer atmosphere holds more water.
    “because” 用於引導原因子句，說明氣候變遷加劇暴雨風險的原因，即「因為較暖的大氣能容納更多水分」。
