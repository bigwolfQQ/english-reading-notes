文法重點

  • 現在分詞作附帶狀況與結果狀語 (while emphasizing...)
    Vice President Hsiao Bi-khim (蕭美琴) praised AmCham's role in identifying problems, proposing solutions and strengthening ties between government and the business community, while emphasizing the expanding partnership between Taiwan and the United States.
    句尾的「while emphasizing...」是由連接詞 while 帶領現在分詞片語，用來補充說明主句動作發生時同時進行的動作，表示「同時強調……」，在多益閱讀測驗中常考這種省略主詞的分詞構句簡化寫法。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (quoted as saying)
    "Our relationship is strong because it is built on shared interests, shared values, and increasingly, shared investments in each other's future," Hsiao was quoted as saying.
    句中的 "was quoted as saying" 是被動語態的結構，用來引用某人的說法（字面意思是「被引述說……」）。在新聞英文中極為常見，用以客觀轉述名人或官員的談話內容。
  • 過去分詞片語作句首修飾語 (Held under the theme...)
    Held under the theme "Honoring the Legacy, Empowering the Future," the celebration featured a history wall displaying rare photographs...
    句首的 "Held under the theme..." 是過去分詞片語（省略了 Being 或 Which was），用來修飾後方的名詞主詞 the celebration，表示該慶祝活動「被舉辦於」某個主題之下。這在商業報導與新聞標題、導言中是極高頻的進階句構。
