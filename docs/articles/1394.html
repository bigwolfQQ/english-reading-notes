團體敦促部會放寬生理假

Groups urge ministry to expand menstrual leave

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • urge v. 敦促，力勸
    Groups yesterday urged the Ministry of Education to allow students two days a month of menstrual leave, up from the one-day limit.
    多個團體於昨日敦促教育部，將學生的生理假從現行的每月一天上限放寬至每月兩天。
  • policy n. 政策，方針
    The one-day policy does not reflect “real bodily experiences,” the groups — including the Taiwan Engagement Association for Youth and the Student Alliance for Gender Equality — told a news conference titled: “Whose period lasts just one day?”
    這些團體——包含台灣青年民主協會與學生性別平等聯盟——在一場名為「誰的月經只有一天？」的記者會上表示，一天的政策並未反映「真實的身體經驗」。
  • implement v. 實施，執行
    Surveys have showed that although the ministry has petitioned education providers to allow menstrual leave, many have yet to implement the guidelines, Student Alliance for Gender Equality proposal team convener Chen Chia-yi (陳佳儀) said.
    學生性別平等聯盟提案組召集人陳佳儀表示，調查顯示，儘管教育部已呼籲教育機構准予生理假，但許多機構尚未落實這些指導方針。
  • documentation n. 文件，證明文件
    Some institutions instead count extra menstrual leave as general sick leave, which requires supplemental documentation, while they do not allow students to take make-up classes or exams, Chen said.
    陳佳儀指出，部分機構反而將額外的生理假算作一般病假，這需要補交證明文件，同時也不允許學生參加補課或考試。
  • increase v. 增加，提高
    The leave allowance should be increased to two days per month without requiring students to provide supplementary documentation, there should be no required interval between leave days and schools should be permitted to offer more days off, she said.
    她表示，請假額度應增加至每月兩天，且無需學生提供補交文件，請假天數之間不應有規定的間隔時間，並應允許學校提供更多休假天數。
  • consider v. 考慮
    The ministry should also consider ensuring that education providers do not deduct attendance points or reduce grades over menstrual leave, she added.
    她補充說，教育部也應考慮確保教育機構不會因生理假而扣減出席分數或降低成績。
  • allowance n. 配給額，津貼，允許量
    The leave allowance should be increased to two days per month without requiring students to provide supplementary documentation...
    請假額度應增加至每月兩天，且無需學生提供補交文件……
  • average adj. 平均的
    The average menstrual cycle varies between 21 and 35 days, and menstruation does not necessarily occur just once per month for shorter cycles...
    平均月經週期落在21至35天之間，對於較短的週期而言，月經不一定每個月只發生一次……

文法重點

  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況或結果 (..., meaning...)
    However, menstrual leave can only be taken once per month, meaning students risk poor attendance and grades, and might not be able to take make-up classes or exams, Wan said.
    句尾的 "meaning..." 是由現在分詞分詞構句簡化而來，原意為 "which means..."，用來補充說明前面子句所造成的結果或後續影響。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句 (which requires...)
    Some institutions instead count extra menstrual leave as general sick leave, which requires supplemental documentation, while they do not allow students to take make-up classes or exams, Chen said.
    逗號後的 "which requires..." 為非限定用法之關係代名詞子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞或整個子句（general sick leave），中間用逗號隔開，不能使用 "that"。
  • 被動語態 (be + 過去分詞)
    Some students have even been reprimanded for requesting additional days, she said...
    "have been reprimanded" 是現在完成式的被動語態，用來強調學生「遭受」訓斥的動作，動作的接受者（Some students）放在主詞位置。
