米利班德打破數十年來對以色列非法定居點的因循苟且

Miliband overturns decades of inertia on illegal Israel settlements

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • extension n. 擴建，延伸
    The indefatigable ability of British Foreign Office ministers over decades to issue boilerplate statements of concern about the extension of illegal settlements in Israel has long been a matter of ridicule.
    幾十年來，英國外交部部長們總是能不知疲倦地針對以色列擴建非法定居點發表標準的關切聲明，這長久以來一直是個笑柄。
  • stance n. 立場，態度
    Duncan described the UK stance toward settlements as weak and naive...
    鄧肯將英國對定居點的立場形容為軟弱且天真...
  • condemnation n. 譴責
    ...while Burt wondered at the ability of the Foreign Office to find new forms of diplomatic condemnation that never amounted to any specific action.
    ...而伯特則對外交部總能找到從未轉化為任何具體行動的新形式外交譴責感到驚奇。
  • preferential adj. 優惠的，優先的
    The UK-Israel trade agreement prevented preferential tariffs applying to goods coming from territories brought under Israeli administration after June 1967.
    英以貿易協定阻止了優惠關稅適用於1967年6月之後被置於以色列管轄之下的領土所產出的貨物。
  • sanctions n. 制裁
    At the same time, the legal advice was that the UK regime could place sanctions on entities only if they were linked to settler violence...
    與此同時，法律意見認為，英國政權只有在實體與定居者暴力有關聯的情況下才能對其實施制裁...
  • entity n. 實體，機構
    ...but could not put a contractor under sanctions simply for working on settlements.
    ...但不能僅僅因為承包商在定居點工作就對其實施制裁。
  • inherit v. 繼承
    It was this unhappy moral and legal mess that Ed Miliband inherited.
    正是這個令人沮喪的道德與法律爛攤子被愛德華·米利班德繼承了。
  • repress v. 壓制，平息 (此處為 reprisals 衍生)
    A group of countries imposing the trade ban together of course makes Israeli reprisals harder, and serves to underline Israel’s isolation.
    多國聯手實施貿易禁令，當然會讓以色列的報復變得更困難，並有助於突顯以色列的孤立狀態。
  • criteria n. 標準，條件
    ...all on the basis the F-35 jets “do not materially support the occupation” — the criteria the foreign secretary has set.
    ——這一切都是基於F-35戰機「並未实质支援佔領」的依據——這是外交大臣設定的標準。
  • exemption n. 豁免，免除
    As to a UK arms sales ban, an exemption continues for the sale of parts for F-35 jet planes to the main US spares pool...
    至於對英國武器銷售的禁令，針對F-35戰機零件銷售給美國主要備件庫的豁免依然存在...

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作後置修飾 (Participle phrase as post-modifier)
    A group of countries imposing the trade ban together of course makes Israeli reprisals harder, and serves to underline Israel’s isolation.
    句中的「imposing the trade ban together」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「A group of countries」，相當於形容詞子句「A group of countries that impose the trade ban together」，用以簡化句型並增添緊湊感。
  • 假設語氣 / 條件句應用 (Conditional structure)
    At the same time, the legal advice was that the UK regime could place sanctions on entities only if they were linked to settler violence...
    句中運用了「only if」引導的條件副詞子句（如果……才……），表達實施制裁的嚴格條件限制，這在商業與法律契約英語中是極為常見的句構。
  • 否定詞置於句首的倒裝句構觀念 (Inversion / Fronting for emphasis)
    However, to focus solely on what is absent is to miss the moment.
    此處雖非傳統的完全倒裝，但使用了不定詞片語「to focus solely on what is absent」置於句首擔任主詞，作為主題前置（Fronting）的一種形式，藉此強調對比與轉折的語氣。
