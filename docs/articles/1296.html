3,000人奔向山林參加埔里山城馬拉松活動

3,000 take to the hills for Puli Power running event

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • take part in phr. 參加，參與
    Nearly 3,000 runners took part in the Puli Power mountain running event in central Taiwan's Nantou County on Sunday...
    週日近3000名跑者在中台灣南投縣參加了埔里山城馬拉松活動……
  • feature v. 以……為特色，特長，由……組成
    The event featured four races -- the 42.195-kilometer full marathon, a 24-kilometer race, 10-kilometer challenge run and 5-kilometer family run.
    該活動設有四個組別——42.195公里的全程馬拉松、24公里組、10公里挑戰組以及5公里親子組。
  • include v. 包含，包括
    Unlike previous editions, which included a section of National Freeway 6, this year's route returned to a course popular with runners...
    與包含國道6號部分路段的往屆不同，今年的路線重返一條深受跑者喜愛的賽道……
  • cover v. 行經（距離），涵蓋，覆蓋
    ...covering the Geographic Center of Taiwan Monument, Hutoushan Mountain, Liyu Lake and Chung Tai Chan Monastery.
    ……涵蓋了台灣地理中心碑、虎頭山、鯉魚潭及中台禪寺。
  • elevation n. 海拔，高度
    Runners in the marathon and 24-kilometer races had to traverse Hutoushan, which tops out at an elevation of 650 meters.
    參加全馬和24公里組的跑者必須穿越海拔最高達650公尺的虎頭山。
  • offer v. 提供
    The 20 food stations offered local specialties including water bamboo, passion fruit, chocolate, curry bread, roasted pork and mullet roe.
    20個補給站提供當地特產，包括茭白筍、百香果、巧克力、咖哩麵包、烤豬肉和烏魚子。
  • venue n. 活動場地，集會地點
    An agricultural products fair was also held at the main event venue, with dozens of stalls selling local produce and food.
    主會場也舉辦了農特產品展售會，有數十個攤位販售當地農產品與食物。
  • combine v. 結合，組合
    Puli Power has been held for 11 years and has become an event combining running with local scenery and cuisine...
    埔里山城馬拉松已舉辦11年，成為一項結合跑步與當地風光及美食的活動……
  • participant n. 參與者，參加者
    ...giving participants a chance to experience Puli through its landscapes and food, Puli Township chief Liao Chi-cheng (廖志城) said.
    埔里鎮長廖志城表示，……讓參與者有機會透過風景和美食來體驗埔里。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    ...passing through scenic spots including Hutoushan Mountain and Liyu Lake while sampling local specialties at 20 food stations along the route.
    句中的 passing 與 sampling 都是現在分詞開頭的片語，用來補充說明主詞 runners 在跑步過程中同時進行的動作，相當於分詞構句，使句子緊湊並省去對等連接詞。
  • 非限定關代子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Unlike previous editions, which included a section of National Freeway 6, this year's route returned to a course popular with runners...
    句中的「which included...」前面有逗號，屬於非限定用法，用來補充說明先行詞 previous editions（往屆活動）的細節，且 which 不能代換成 that。
  • 現在完成被動語態 (Present Perfect Passive Voice)
    Puli Power has been held for 11 years and has become an event combining running with local scenery and cuisine...
    結構為「has/have been + 過去分詞 (V-pp)」，用來表達一個從過去持續到現在的動作（已經舉辦了11年），且主詞 Puli Power 是被舉辦，故使用被動語態。
