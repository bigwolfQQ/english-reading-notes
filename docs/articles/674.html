陸委會提醒旅客注意中國出入境新規

MAC cautions travelers on China entry/exit rules

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • urge v. 敦促，力勸
    The Mainland Affairs Council (MAC) yesterday urged groups traveling to China to exercise extra caution...
    大陸委員會（陸委會）昨天敦促前往中國的旅遊團格外小心...
  • caution n. 謹慎，小心
    ...urged groups traveling to China to exercise extra caution as local officials implementing new border controls...
    ...敦促前往中國的旅遊團格外小心，因當地官員實施的新邊境管制措施...
  • take effect phr. 生效，實施
    ...as local officials implementing new border controls, which take effect today, could be inconsistent or overly strict.
    ...因當地官員實施的新邊境管制措施於今天生效，可能會前後不一或過度嚴格。
  • regulation n. 規章，法規
    The council has reviewed the new regulations and found five main groups at risk...
    該委員會已審視新法規，並發現五大高風險族群...
  • precaution n. 預防措施，防範
    Shen urged Taiwanese travelers to register online before traveling to China as an additional safety precaution.
    沈有忠敦促台灣旅客前往中國前先線上登記，作為額外的安全預防措施。
  • executive n. 高階主管，行政人員
    Businesspeople, executives, high-tech professionals, lower-level government officials and followers of certain religions could face additional risks under the new regulations, he said.
    他說，在新法規下，商界人士、企業高階主管、高科技專業人員、基層政府官員以及特定宗教信仰者可能面臨額外風險。
  • detain v. 扣留，拘留
    Over the past few years, the number of people questioned, detained or reported missing in China has increased...
    過去幾年來，在中國遭盤問、拘留或失蹤的人數有所增加...
  • enforcement n. （法律等的）執行，實施
    ...which could lead to inconsistent or overly strict enforcement, as local officials could take a “better safe than sorry” approach when the rules are first implemented...
    ...這可能導致執法標準不一或過度嚴格，因為地方官員在新規剛實施時可能會採取「寧可謹慎、不要出錯」的做法...
  • implement v. 實施，執行
    ...when the rules are first implemented, Shen said.
    沈有忠說，在新規剛實施時...
  • bar v. 禁止，阻撓
    In addition, people could be barred from leaving China based on authorities’ unilateral determination...
    此外，即使未被正式定罪，當局也可能依據其單方面認定...
  • convict v. 宣判有罪
    ...even without being formally convicted of an offense, he said.
    ...而禁止他們離開中國。
  • commercial adj. 商業的
    ...posing risks to information security, commercial and technological data, and people who have expressed certain political views, he added.
    他並補充說，這對資訊安全、商業和技術數據以及曾表達特定政治觀點的人構成了風險。

文法重點

  • 非限定性關係子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    ...as local officials implementing new border controls, which take effect today, could be inconsistent or overly strict.
    句中的「which take effect today」前後使用逗號隔開，是一個非限定性關係子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「new border controls」。如果把這個子句拿掉，主要句子的意思依然完整。
  • 分詞構句作表語或補充說明 (Participial Phrase)
    ...posing risks to information security, commercial and technological data, and people who have expressed certain political views, he added.
    句尾的「posing risks...」是由現在分詞ing開頭的分詞構句，用來補充說明前面整件事情所造成的結果或影響，相當於 and pose risks...
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    In addition, people could be barred from leaving China based on authorities’ unilateral determination...
    句中的「could be barred」使用了情態助動詞加被動語態 (be + 過去分詞)，強調「人們（被）禁止」離開中國，焦點在於受動作影響的主體。
  • 名詞子句作受詞 (Noun Clause as Object)
    The council has reviewed the new regulations and found five main groups at risk...
    雖然此句省略了連接詞 that，但「found [that] five main groups at risk」結構中，found 後面接了一個名詞子句（或簡化的受詞補語結構）來表達委員會審視法規後的「發現內容」。
