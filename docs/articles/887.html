執政聯盟將繼續推動改革：梅爾茨

Ruling coalition to push on with reforms: Merz

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • coalition n. 聯合，同盟，聯合政府
    Embattled German Chancellor Friedrich Merz yesterday vowed to keep his ruling coalition together for the sake of the country’s “democratic future”
    陷入困境的德國總理佛里德里希·梅爾茨於昨天發誓，為了國家的「民主未來」，他將維繫執政聯盟不散
  • threshold n. 門檻，界線
    where Merz’s Christian Democratic Union (CDU) sank to 4.9 percent, below the threshold to stay in parliament
    梅爾茨的「基督教民主聯盟」（CDU）在该州得票率跌至 4.9%，低於進入議會的門檻
  • intention n. 意圖，打算
    However, Merz, 70, made clear he has no intention of quitting as leader of Europe’s biggest economy
    然而，70 歲的梅爾茨表明，他無意辭去歐洲最大經濟體領導人的職務
  • economy n. 經濟，經濟體
    quitting as leader of Europe’s biggest economy, at the helm of a coalition with the center-left SPD.
    辭去歐洲最大經濟體領導人的職務，他正帶領著一個與中左翼社會民主黨（SPD）組成的聯合政府。
  • pressure n. 壓力
    Merz has faced growing pressure after the latest bruising results
    在經歷了一系列沉重打擊的選舉結果後，梅爾茨正面臨越來越大的壓力
  • criticism n. 批評，指責
    Merz pushed back at criticism that his own low popularity ratings were behind the election defeats.
    梅爾茨反擊了認為他自身低民調支持率是選舉失敗背後的批評聲浪。
  • reasons n. 原因，理由
    “The reasons for these election results go far beyond my own person,” he said.
    他說：「這些選舉結果的原因遠遠超出了我個人。」
  • negotiations n. 談判，協商
    recently agreed on after months of often rancorous negotiations with the CDU.
    這些改革是最近經過數月與基民盟經常充滿爭吵的談判後才達成協議的。
  • minister n. 部長
    “We stand by this coalition,” said German Minister of Finance Lars Klingbeil
    身兼社民黨共同主席的德國財政部長拉爾斯·克林貝爾表示：「我們支持這個執政聯盟。」
  • rumors n. 謠言，傳聞
    Merz has been fighting off rumors he may be ousted for weeks.
    梅爾茨數週來一直在力抗他可能被趕下臺的謠言。
  • revive v. 使復甦，使恢復活力
    trying to revive the conservatives’ fortunes during one of their worst-ever crises.
    試圖重振保守黨運勢的艱鉅任務讓他們感到畏懼。
  • vow v. 發誓，誓言
    Merz admitted that his CDU had suffered a “disaster,” but vowed to stay the course
    梅爾茨承認他的基民盟遭受了「災難」，但誓言要堅守陣地

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（用作補充說明或結果）
    The far-right Alternative for Germany (AfD) scored another win on Sunday, in the northeastern state of Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania, where Merz’s Christian Democratic Union (CDU) sank to 4.9 percent, below the threshold to stay in parliament and its worst result since the party was formed in 1945.
    句中的「below the threshold...」前面省略了分詞和be動詞（which was below...），用來補充說明 CDU 得票率跌破門檻的情況。這類結構在多益閱讀測驗中常用於快速修飾前面的名詞短語。
  • 現在分詞短語作後置修飾語
    Merz pushed back at criticism that his own low popularity ratings were behind the election defeats.
  • 獨立分詞結構（Absolute Phrase）
    Fighting speculation that his CDU/CSU bloc might try to oust him, Merz took the unusual step on Sunday of addressing the nation on TV...
    句首的「Fighting speculation...」是現在分詞片語，其邏輯主詞就是句子的主語 Merz。這表示「在對抗臆測的同時，他採取了......動作」。這種結構常用於新聞英文，用來精鍊地表達兩個同時發生或因果相連的動作。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞與補語
    However, no contender has made an open move for the job — perhaps fearing the act of disloyalty could backfire, or intimidated by the tough task of trying to revive the conservatives’ fortunes...
    句尾使用了「trying to revive...」與「intimidated by...」的對比結構，其中「trying」是動名詞，當作介系詞「of」的受詞；而「intimidated」則是過去分詞片語，用來修飾主詞 contender（或作為形容詞補語），展現多益中常見的情緒分詞用法。
