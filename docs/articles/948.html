勞動部將針對霸凌輕生案中撤銷官員免職令之判決提出上訴

MOL to appeal ruling voiding official's firing in bullying suicide case

單字解析

  • appeal n. / v. 上訴；吸引
    The Ministry of Labor (MOL) on Tuesday confirmed it would appeal an administrative court ruling...
    勞動部於週二證實，將針對一項行政法院判決提出上訴...
  • dismissal n. 免職，解雇
    ...that voided its dismissal of a labor ministry branch chief whose bullying was linked to a worker's suicide.
    ...該判決宣告撤銷其對一名勞動部主管的免職處分，該主管的霸凌行為與一名員工的輕生有關。
  • proceeding n. 訴訟程序，法律程序
    On Sept. 3, the Taipei High Administrative Court ruled that the MOL's disciplinary proceedings against former Workforce Development Agency (WDA) branch head Hsieh Yi-jung... had 'violated due process'
    9月3日，台北高等行政法院裁定，勞動部對勞動力發展署前分署長謝宜容的懲處程序『違反正當法律程序』...
  • challenge n. / v. 質疑，挑戰，異議
    On those grounds, Hsieh's challenge to her dismissal was well-founded, the court said...
    法院表示，基於這些理由，謝宜容對其免職處分提出的異議是有理有據的...
  • subordinate n. / adj. 下屬，部屬；下級的
    ...following the suicide of a 39-year-old subordinate, surnamed Wu (吳), in his office in New Taipei on Nov. 4, 2024.
    ...在2024年11月4日一名39歲的吳姓部屬在新北市辦公室輕生之後...
  • investigation n. 調查
    ...portions of the 205 responses the MOL received to a questionnaire distributed as part of its investigation of Hsieh.
    ...勞動部作為對謝宜容調查的一部分所發放問卷中收到的205份回覆之部分內容。
  • legal counsel n. phr. 法律顧問，辯護律師
    ...and had instructed legal counsel to prepare to appeal the ruling to the Supreme Administrative Court within the statutory deadline.
    ...並已指示法律顧問準備在法定期限內向最高行政法院提起上訴。
  • statutory adj. 法定的，依據法令的
    ...to appeal the ruling to the Supreme Administrative Court within the statutory deadline.
    ...在法定期限內向最高行政法院提起上訴。
  • reinstate v. 使復職，恢復原職
    As those cases are currently pending before the Supreme Court and the Disciplinary Court for civil servants, respectively, Hsieh will remain suspended from duty and will not be reinstated at this time...
    由於這些案件目前分別繫屬最高法院及公務員懲戒法院，謝宜容將維持停職狀態，目前不會復職...

文法重點

  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    The Ministry of Labor (MOL) on Tuesday confirmed it would appeal an administrative court ruling that voided its dismissal of a labor ministry branch chief whose bullying was linked to a worker's suicide.
    句中的 "that voided its..." 是用來修飾先行詞 "an administrative court ruling" 的形容詞子句；而在子句內部，"whose bullying..." 則是所有格關係代名詞子句，用來修飾 "a labor ministry branch chief"，表示「該分署長的霸凌」。
  • 分詞片語作後置修飾語
    Hsieh, who headed the WDA's northern regional branch, was accused of workplace bullying following the suicide of a 39-year-old subordinate, surnamed Wu (吳), in his office in New Taipei on Nov. 4, 2024.
    句中的 "surnamed Wu" 是過去分詞片語，省略了 "who was"，用來後置修飾前面的名詞 "a 39-year-old subordinate"，意為「姓吳的39歲部屬」。
  • 被動語態的運用
    On Tuesday, the Chinese-language tabloid Mirror Media published portions of the 205 responses the MOL received to a questionnaire distributed as part of its investigation of Hsieh.
    句尾的 "distributed as part of its investigation..." 是一個過去分詞片語作後置修飾，而整篇新聞中也大量使用被動語態（如 "was accused of", "was sentenced to"），常用於新聞報導中客觀陳述事實或法律程序。
