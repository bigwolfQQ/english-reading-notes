文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The vice president said the US had a “responsibility” to carry out this week’s strikes because Iran continued to target commercial vessels passing through the Strait of Hormuz.
    句中的 "passing through the Strait of Hormuz" 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "commercial vessels"（商船），相當於形容詞子句 "that passed / are passing through..."，在英文新聞中非常常見，能簡化語句並使資訊更緊湊。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Those killed or wounded in fighting after a brief ceasefire between the US and Iran fell apart were classified in a new category called “Overseas Operations”...
    句首的 "Those killed or wounded in fighting..." 中，"killed or wounded" 是過去分詞片語，修飾代名詞 "Those"（那些人），表示被動（被殺或受傷）。其完整結構為 "Those who were killed or wounded..."，省略了關係代名詞與be動詞。
  • 分詞構句表示附帶狀況 (Participle Clause of Concomitant Circumstance)
    ...even as the unpopular conflict — one the White House said at its outset would last a matter of weeks — has driven up gas prices and left consumers grappling with higher inflation.
  • 不定詞片語作目的狀語 (Infinitive Phrase of Purpose)
    Washington has in recent weeks sought to ratchet up the economic pressure on Tehran, in an effort to extract concessions in any future negotiation...
