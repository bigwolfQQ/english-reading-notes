文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Phrase）作附帶狀況或結果補充
    Taiwan will intensify its initiative to become an Asian asset management center as it seeks to turn its industrial strength into financial clout and enhance the country's competitiveness, President Lai Ching-te (賴清德) said Friday.
    句中的 "as it seeks to..." 是連接詞 "as" 引導的原因副詞子句，用來解釋為什麼台灣要加強這項倡議。另外像句尾的 "seeking to..." 或本篇其他地方的分詞結構，常用於表達伴隨發生的動作或目的。
  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾（形容詞功能）
    The benchmark index of Taiwan's stock market, the Taiex, skyrocketed 59.2 percent in the first six months of 2026, spearheaded by tech stocks with promising positions in the AI sector.
    句尾的 "spearheaded by tech stocks..." 是過去分詞片語，前面省略了 "which were"。用來修飾前面的主詞（台股指數飆漲這件事），表示指數是由科技股所「帶頭/領導」的，是被動關係。
  • 關係代間詞子句（限定修飾）
    Shares of TSMC, the world's largest contract chipmaking company that accounts for over 40 percent of the Taiwan market's value, had risen 306 percent as of the end of June from the beginning of 2024, backed by the AI boom.
    句中的 "that accounts for over 40 percent of the Taiwan market's value" 是由關係代間詞 "that"（此處指代台積電這家公司）引導的限定修飾子句，用來補充說明該公司的市場占比。
  • 過去完成式（Past Perfect Tense）
    Shares of TSMC... had risen 306 percent as of the end of June from the beginning of 2024, backed by the AI boom.
    句型使用過去完成式（had + p.p.），用來強調在過去某一特定時間點（截至今年 6 月底）之前，已經持續發生並完成的動作（從 2024 年初到 6 月底這段期間累積漲了 306%）。
