以色列製作的加薩電影榮獲威尼斯影展評審團獎

Israeli-made Gaza film wins Venice jury prize

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • documentary n. 紀錄片
    A chilling new documentary about Israel’s artificial intelligence (AI)-assisted killing of civilians in Gaza by two Israeli directors on Saturday won a special jury prize at the Venice Film Festival.
    週六，由兩位以色列導演拍攝的一部關於以色列利用人工智慧協助殺害加薩平民令人不寒而栗的新紀錄片，在威尼斯影展上榮獲特別評審團獎。
  • direct v. 導演，指揮
    A chilling new documentary about Israel’s artificial intelligence (AI)-assisted killing of civilians in Gaza by two Israeli directors on Saturday won a special jury prize at the Venice Film Festival.
    週六，由兩位以色列導演拍攝的一部關於以色列利用人工智慧協助殺害加薩平民令人不寒而栗的新紀錄片，在威尼斯影展上榮獲特別評審團獎。
  • award n. / v. 獎項，授予
    Receiving the award, Abraham said that in the 10 days since the festival began, “Israel killed six Palestinian children in Gaza at least.”
    亞伯拉罕在領獎時表示，自影展開始以來的 10 天內，「以色列在加薩至少殺害了六名巴勒斯坦兒童。」
  • policy n. 政策，方針
    “The Israeli intelligence officers we spoke with talked to us how this mass killing is systemic: not by accident, but by design, actions of murder and policies that are based on the total dehumanization of Palestinians,” he told the audience.
    他告訴現場觀眾：「我們交談過的以色列情報軍官向我們講述了這種大規模殺戮是如何系統化進行的：這不是偶然，而是刻意為之，是基於對巴勒斯坦人完全去人性化的謀殺行徑與政策。」
  • recognize v. 表彰，認可，承認
    The special jury prize is given out each year by the jury to recognize an outstanding work, but it is below the Golden and Silver Lions in the pecking order.
    特別評審團獎是評審團每年頒發以表彰傑出作品的獎項，但在等級順序上低於金獅獎和銀獅獎。
  • factory n. 工廠
    The 80-minute film left many audience members stunned, with interviewees describing how they felt like they worked in a killing “factory” or a lethal video game while using Israel’s AI-powered targeting systems to bombard Gaza.
    這部長達 80 分鐘的電影讓許多觀眾感到震驚，受訪者描述了他們在使用以色列的 AI 驅動瞄準系統轟炸加薩時，感覺自己像是在一個殺戮「工廠」或致命電玩遊戲中工作。
  • competition n. 競爭，競賽
    The film was the only documentary in Venice’s main competition.
    這部電影是威尼斯影展主競賽單元中唯一的紀錄片。
  • reliable adj. 可靠的，可信賴的
    Israel’s bombardment of Gaza has left about 73,500 Palestinians dead and 174,500 wounded since October 2023, according to Gaza’s Hamas-run health ministry, whose figures the UN deems reliable.
    根據加薩由哈瑪斯運作的衛生部（聯合國認為其數據可靠）統計，自 2023 年 10 月以來，以色列對加薩的轟炸已造成約 73,500 名巴勒斯坦人死亡、174,500 人受傷。
  • result in phr. v. 導致，結果是
    The pounding of the territory followed an attack on Israel by Hamas militants on Oct. 7, 2023, that resulted in the death of 1,219 people, according to an Agence France-Presse tally based on official figures.
    根據法新社基於官方數據的統計，在此次猛烈轟炸該領土之前，哈瑪斯武裝分子於 2023 年 10 月 7 日對以色列發動了攻擊，造成 1,219 人死亡。
  • verify v. 核實，查證
    The Israeli military has criticized the use of anonymous testimonies in the film, saying on Friday the identities of the whistle-blowers “cannot be verified.”
    以色列軍方批評該片使用匿名證詞，週五表示這些吹哨者的身分「無法被證實」。
  • deny v. 否認
    Israel denies deliberately targeting civilians.
    以色列否認蓄意針對平民。
  • partnership n. 合作關係，合夥
    Abraham defended the film’s reporting, done in partnership with Britain’s The Guardian newspaper, calling it “rigorous” and its findings reported by other mainstream journalists as well.
    亞伯拉罕為該片的報導進行辯護，該報導是與英國《衛報》合作完成的，他稱其「嚴謹」，且其調查結果也被其他主流記者報導。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後置修飾語 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    A chilling new documentary about Israel’s artificial intelligence (AI)-assisted killing of civilians in Gaza by two Israeli directors on Saturday won a special jury prize at the Venice Film Festival.
    句中的「by two Israeli directors」與前面的動詞過去分詞形成了修飾關係，用來補充說明這部紀錄片是由誰執導的。多益閱讀測驗常利用這種結構來測試名詞與修飾語之間的搭配。
  • 過去分詞片語作形容詞用 (Past Participle as Adjective)
    The 80-minute film left many audience members stunned, with interviewees describing how they felt like they worked in a killing “factory” ...
    句中的「stunned」是過去分詞作形容詞用，用來形容受詞「audience members」所感受到的情緒（感到震驚的）。多益常考分詞 -ing（主動/令人感到...）與 -ed（被動/感到...）的用法區別。
  • 所有格關係代名詞 (Possessive Relative Pronoun: whose)
    Israel’s bombardment of Gaza has left about 73,500 Palestinians dead and 174,500 wounded since October 2023, according to Gaza’s Hamas-run health ministry, whose figures the UN deems reliable.
    關係代名詞「whose」用來連接先行詞「health ministry」與後方的子句，表示所有格關係（即「the ministry's figures」）。這在多益文法題（尤其是受詞/格位選擇題）中是高頻考點。
