旅加臺灣人爭取加入聯合國

Taiwanese in Canada push for UN inclusion

單字解析

  • demonstrate v. 展示、表現、示威
    Members of the Taiwanese community in Vancouver have joined this year’s “UN for Taiwan” campaign with a car formation visible from the air to demonstrate their support ahead of the 81st session of the UN General Assembly which begins at the UN headquarters in New York tomorrow.
    溫哥華的臺灣僑民社區加入了今年的「聯合國大會臺灣」運動，以空中清晰可見的車隊排列，展現他們對明日在紐約聯合國總部揭幕的第81屆聯合國大會的支援。
  • campaign n. 活動、運動、競選
    Members of the Taiwanese community in Vancouver have joined this year’s “UN for Taiwan” campaign with a car formation visible from the air to demonstrate their support ahead of the 81st session of the UN General Assembly which begins at the UN headquarters in New York tomorrow.
    溫哥華的臺灣僑民社區加入了今年的「聯合國大會臺灣」運動，以空中清晰可見的車隊排列，展現他們對明日在紐約聯合國總部揭幕的第81屆聯合國大會的支援。
  • display v. 展示、陳列
    Organizers helped 45 vehicles park to spell out “TW” in a parking lot near Vancouver on Saturday, with each vehicle displaying the flag of the Republic of China (ROC).
    主辦單位週六在溫哥華附近的停車場協助45輛汽車停放，拼出「TW」字樣，每輛車都展示了中華民國（ROC）國旗。
  • connection n. 連結、關係
    Event organizer Chang Chien-hung (張建宏) said that vehicles in the rally featured custom license plates including “TAIWAN,” “TAIPEI,” “NEW TPE” and “TW88,” demonstrating the Taiwanese community’s deep connection to their homeland.
    活動組織者張建宏表示，集會中的車輛裝有客製化車牌，包括「TAIWAN」、「TAIPEI」、「NEW TPE」和「TW88」，展現了臺灣僑民與家鄉的深厚連結。
  • contribute v. 貢獻、捐獻
    “In the era of artificial intelligence, Taiwan’s semiconductor industry underpins the world, and Taiwan is eager to contribute more globally,” said Liu Yung-chiang (劉永強), co-chair of the Metro Vancouver Overseas Taiwanese Society.
    溫哥華大都會臺灣協會共同主席劉永強表示：「在人工智慧時代，臺灣的半導體產業支撐了全世界，臺灣渴望在全球做出更多貢獻。」
  • objective n. 目標、目的
    Taipei Economic and Cultural Office in Vancouver Director Liu Li-hsin (劉立欣) said Canada values sustainability, green energy and gender equality — progressive ideals that are strengths of Taiwan and core UN objectives.
    駐溫哥華臺北經濟文化辦事處處長劉立欣表示，加拿大重視永續發展、綠能和性別平權——這些進步的理想正是臺灣的強項，也是聯合國的核心目標。
  • enable v. 使能夠
    Taiwan’s inclusion in the UN would enable the nation to contribute even more to the global community, she added.
    她補充說，臺灣加入聯合國將使該國能夠對全球社群做出更大貢獻。
  • responsible adj. 負責任的
    “Delivering for all” means leaving no one behind, and Taiwan is an important and responsible member of the international community, she said.
    她說，「為所有人服務」意味著不遺漏任何人，而臺灣是國際社會中重要且負責任的成員。
  • fulfill v. 履行、實現、完成
    The 23.5 million people of Taiwan are willing to fulfill their responsibilities as global citizens, and their rights and interests should not be overlooked, she added.
    她並補充，臺灣的2350萬人民願意履行其作為全球公民的責任，他們的權益不應被忽視。
  • status n. 地位、身分
    During his speech at a Lawyers’ Day banquet hosted by the Taiwan Bar Association, Lai said the resolution only addressed the issue of China’s representation at the UN and has no bearing on Taiwan’s status.
    賴總統在出席全國律師聯合會舉辦的律師節慶祝晚宴致詞時表示，該決議僅處理中國在聯合國的代表權問題，與臺灣的地位毫無關係。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    Members of the Taiwanese community in Vancouver have joined this year’s “UN for Taiwan” campaign with a car formation visible from the air to demonstrate their support ahead of the 81st session of the UN General Assembly which begins at the UN headquarters in New York tomorrow.
    句中的 "visible from the air" 是形容詞片語（由過去分詞片語簡化而來，原為 which is visible from the air），用來後位修飾前面的名詞 "a car formation"（車隊排列）。多益閱讀測驗常考這種分詞片語省略代名詞與be動詞的結構。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    Taipei Economic and Cultural Office in Vancouver Director Liu Li-hsin (劉立欣) said Canada values sustainability, green energy and gender equality — progressive ideals that are strengths of Taiwan and core UN objectives.
    句中的 "that are strengths of Taiwan and core UN objectives" 是一個由關係代名詞 "that" 引導的限定修飾子句，用來補充說明前面的名詞片語 "progressive ideals"（進步的理想）。
  • 假設語氣（過去功用）(Subjunctive Mood)
    Taiwan’s inclusion in the UN would enable the nation to contribute even more to the global community, she added.
    此句使用助動詞 "would" 搭配原形動詞 "enable"，表達與現在事實相反或假設性的情境（若臺灣能加入聯合國，將能……）。這是多益文法題中常見的假設語氣表達方式。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The 23.5 million people of Taiwan are willing to fulfill their responsibilities as global citizens, and their rights and interests should not be overlooked, she added.
    句中的 "should not be overlooked" 採用了「助動詞 + be + 過去分詞」的被動語態結構，強調「權益」是不應該「被忽視」的受動者，符合新聞報導中客觀陳述權利保障的語氣。
