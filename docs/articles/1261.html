文法重點

  • 分詞構句作補充說明
    Trade deal talks with the US have reached a “plateau,” Minister of Finance Nirmala Sitharaman said yesterday, after negotiations dragged on for months with the two sides struggling to resolve sticking points.
    句尾的「with the two sides struggling to resolve sticking points」為「with + 名詞 + 現在分詞」的複合結構，用來補充說明談判拖延期間伴隨的狀況（雙方努力解決僵局）。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    “The administration of the national registry [CPR] has found that unauthorized individuals obtained illegal access in particular to the names, addresses and CPR numbers of around 8.8 million registered people,” including those who have died or emigrated, the ministry said in a press release.
    句子中使用了「have died or emigrated」等主動動詞，但在新聞報導中常運用被動語態來強調受事者或客觀事實，例如文末的「Minister for Science... was quoted as saying...」（...被引述說...）。
  • 過去分詞作後置修飾語
    “The administration of the national registry [CPR] has found that unauthorized individuals obtained illegal access in particular to the names, addresses and CPR numbers of around 8.8 million registered people,” including those who have died or emigrated, the ministry said in a press release.
    句中的「registered people」使用了過去分詞「registered」作為形容詞，置於名詞前方修飾「被登記的人」；而「those who have died or emigrated」則使用關係代名詞子句來補充修飾特定族群。
  • 現在分詞片語修飾主詞或帶出結果
    “The longest streak of rainy days continued from Aug. 27 to Oct. 2, making 37 consecutive days of rain,” JMA official Eri Kawanami said.
    句尾的「making 37 consecutive days of rain」是現在分詞（making）引導的分詞片語，用來接續前面主句的動作，表達自然產生的結果（總計形成了連續 37 天的雨勢）。
