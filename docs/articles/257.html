印度的韌性取決於多元化的貿易

India’s resilience depends on diversified trade

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • participation n. 參與
    Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s participation in the Shanghai Cooperation Council meeting in Kyrgyzstan was about more than regional security...
    印度總理納倫德拉·莫迪參與在吉爾吉斯舉行的上海合作組織會議，其意義不僅止於區域安全...
  • proactive adj. 積極的、主動的
    This is only the latest example of India’s proactive pursuit of trade diplomacy.
    這只是印度積極追求貿易外交的最新例子。
  • agreement n. 協議、合約
    Last year’s free trade agreement with the UK was followed this January by the “mother of all deals,” a wide-ranging agreement with the EU.
    去年與英國簽署的自由貿易協定之後，今年 1 月又迎來了「所有協議之母」——一項與歐盟範圍廣泛的協議。
  • tariffs n. 關稅
    US President Donald Trump’s sweeping and erratic tariffs are only the latest body blow to the international trading system.
    美國總統唐納·川普全面且反覆無常的關稅，只是對國際貿易體系的最新沉重打擊。
  • investment n. 投資
    Countries are increasingly viewing trade and investment through a national security lens.
    各國越來越透過國家安全的鏡片來看待貿易與投資。
  • strategy n. 策略、戰略
    How might a middle power such as India anchor its trade strategy in such a tumultuous period?
    像印度這樣的中等強國，如何在如此動盪的時期錨定其貿易策略？
  • diversification n. 多角化、多元化
    In this landscape, the best strategy for a middle power is likely to be diversification: building a network of trade relationships sufficiently dispersed...
    在這種局勢下，中等強國的最佳策略很可能是多元化：建立一個充分分散的貿易關係網路...
  • negotiating v. 談判、協商
    ...while playing a foundational role in the BRICS group and negotiating deals with the US and the EU.
    ...同時在金磚國家集團中發揮基礎性作用，並與美國和歐盟談判協議。
  • policymakers n. 決策者
    Indian policymakers should draw several lessons from these findings.
    印度決策者應從這些發現中汲取幾項教訓。
  • leverage v. 善用、發揮影響力
    First, to leverage its enviable spread of partners, India must trade much more than it does today.
    首先，為了充分利用其令人羨慕的夥伴廣度，印度的貿易量必須比現在多得多。
  • export n. / v. 出口
    As a large net-exporter of digital services, India should not only sign on, but seek a leading role in the coalition.
    作為數位服務的大型淨出口商，印度不僅應該簽署加入，還應該在這個聯盟中尋求主導角色。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句表示附帶狀況或結果
    And high-level trade talks continue with the US, building on a joint statement of intent issued in February.
    句尾的「building on...」是由分詞構句簡化而來，用來補充說明前面高層談判所依據的基礎，省略了主詞與be動詞（which is building on...），使句子更為緊湊流暢。
  • 現在分詞當形容詞修飾名詞
    The rules-based multilateral trade order that the Asian Tigers and China relied on to pursue export-led development has effectively collapsed, with no evident successor in sight.
    句中的「export-led」是由名詞加上過去分詞構成的複合形容詞，用來修飾後方的 development（發展），表示「以...為導向的」。
  • 關係代名詞子句（限定用法）
    A country that has spent decades cultivating strategic autonomy should think long and hard before trading it away for preferential market access...
    「that has spent decades cultivating strategic autonomy」為限制性關係代名詞子句，用來修飾先行詞 A country，限定並精確指出是哪一種類型的國家。
  • 被動語態的運用
    The burden would fall disproportionately on emerging and developing economies, which stand to benefit the most from external market access...
    雖然本句主要使用主動語態，但文章大量穿插被動語態（如 is implemented、has been decapitated 等），用以強調承受動作的對象（如國際貿易體系或協定），在商業與新聞英文中非常常見。
