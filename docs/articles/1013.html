國健署健康幣計畫與企業合作

HPA’s tokens program partners with businesses

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • partner v. 與...合作，結盟
    The Health Promotion Administration’s Health Token program, which is to launch next week, has partnered with major convenience stores and supermarkets
    國健署即將於下週推出的健康幣計畫，已與各大便利商店及超級市場建立合作夥伴關係
  • launch v. 推出，發起，啟動
    The program is to launch on Thursday next week for National Health Insurance cardholders
    該計畫將於下週四對健保卡持有者推出
  • participate v. 參加，參與
    FamilyMart, 7-Eleven and PX Mart, as well as World Gym, Fitness Factory and Curves are participating in the first phase of the program
    全家、7-11、全聯，以及世界健身、健身工廠與 Curves 皆參與了該計畫的第一階段
  • redeem v. 兌換，贖回
    with tokens redeemable from Dec. 1, she said.
    她表示，健康幣自 12 月 1 日起開放兌換。
  • reward v. / n. 獎勵，回報
    would reward healthy behaviors, such as getting medical checkups and vaccinations, with rewards including healthy food and beverages
    將獎勵健康行為（如進行健康檢查與接種疫苗），提供健康食品與飲料等獎勵
  • accumulate v. 累積，積聚
    The HPA estimates that people could accumulate 3,700 to 13,150 tokens in two years
    國健署評估，民眾在兩年內可累積 3,700 至 13,150 枚健康幣
  • collaboration n. 合作，協力
    The program is a collaboration between the public and private sectors
    該計畫是公私部門的合作成果
  • provider n. 供應商，提供者
    Participating life insurance providers include Taiwan Life Insurance Co, Cathay Life Insurance Co
    參與的壽險業者包含台灣人壽、國泰人壽
  • link v. 連結，連接
    people could link their accounts on the NHI app with the Health GO App
    民眾可以將健保 App 的帳戶與健康 GO App 連結
  • verification n. 驗證，核實
    complete identity verification and registration can enter a lottery
    完成身分驗證與註冊即可參加抽獎

文法重點

  • be to + V (表未來的安排或預定計畫)
    The Health Promotion Administration’s Health Token program, which is to launch next week, has partnered with major convenience stores and supermarkets...
    「is to launch」中的 「be to V」句型常用於新聞英語中，用來表示官方或正式排定的計畫、預定將發生或即將執行的動作。
  • 非限定關代子句 (which 引導的補充說明)
    The Health Promotion Administration’s Health Token program, which is to launch next week, has partnered with major...
    句中的「which is to launch next week」前面有逗號，屬於非限定用法，用來補充說明前面的主詞 「Health Token program」，若拿掉這段話，主句的意思依然完整。
  • with + 名詞 + 形容詞/分詞 (附帶狀況)
    ...with tokens redeemable from Dec. 1, she said.
    「with tokens redeemable from Dec. 1」使用了「with + O. + 補語」的結構，用來表達附帶發生的狀況，在此處形容健康幣自 12 月 1 日起可兌換的狀態。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾
    ...including foreign residents, with tokens redeemable from Dec. 1, she said.
    句中的「including foreign residents」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾或補充前面的名詞群組（cardholders），相當於帶有包含關係的形容詞子句。
