衛福部呼籲大眾協助防治自殺

MOHW urges public to help prevent suicide

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • resilient adj. 具韌性的，有彈性的，恢復力強的
    The public should listen to, understand and have empathy with others to build a resilient social safety net together
    大眾應傾聽、理解他人並發揮同理心，以共同建立具韌性的社會安全網
  • annually adv. 每年，一年一度地
    World Suicide Prevention Day is observed on Sept. 10 annually.
    世界自殺防治日於每年9月10日舉行。
  • stigmatization n. 汙名化
    Chuang Jen-hsiang (莊人祥) said “changing the narrative” is about shifting from a culture of silence, lack of understanding and even stigmatization, to one of openness, listening and empathy
    莊人祥表示，「改變自殺敘事」是從沉默、缺乏理解甚至汙名化的文化，轉變為開放、傾聽與同理的文化
  • aware adj. 察覺的，意識到的
    Taiwan Suicide Prevention Center director Huang Min-wei (黃敏偉) said the center’s surveys found that many people are aware that they should try to accompany those who are in need or encourage them to seek help
    台灣自殺防治中心主任黃敏偉表示，該中心的調查發現，許多人意識到他們應該試著陪伴需要協助的人，或鼓勵他們尋求幫助
  • protocol n. 協議，規範，規程
    Only 18.4 percent said they know about the “Question, Persuade and Refer” (QPR) protocol
    僅有18.4 percent 說他們知道「問、說、轉」 (QPR) 協議
  • misconception n. 誤解，錯誤觀念
    People often have the misconception that those who commit suicide do not ask for help
    人們常有誤解認為自殺的人不會尋求幫助
  • launch v. 發起，推出（新計畫、產品等）
    Chuang said the ministry in August 2024 launched a mental health support program for young people and middle-aged adults
    莊人祥表示，該部在2024年8月針對年輕人和中壯年族群推出了心理健康支持方案
  • barrier n. 障礙，屏障
    The program aimed to lower the barrier for those seeking mental health support
    該方案旨在降低尋求心理健康支持的門檻
  • assessment n. 評估，評價
    enable early detection of high-risk cases and provide timely intervention through professional assessments
    實現高風險個案的早期發現，並透過專業評估提供即時介入
  • allocate v. 分配，撥出（預算、資金等）
    Chuang said the ministry plans to continue the program next year and has allocated about NT$430 million (US$13.59 million) in its budget proposal for it.
    莊人祥表示，該部計劃明年繼續推動該方案，並在其預算案中為此編列了大約新台幣4.3億元（約合1359萬美元）。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Phrase）作補充說明（分詞構句的變體）
    Chuang Jen-hsiang (莊人祥) said “changing the narrative” is about shifting from a culture of silence, lack of understanding and even stigmatization, to one of openness, listening and empathy, allowing those who are struggling to feel accepted and supported.
    句尾的逗號後接 "allowing..." 是現在分詞片語引導的結果或附加說明，用來表達前面整件事情所帶來的正面影響（讓那些掙扎的人感到被接納與支持），在商業英文與新聞寫作中非常常見，能讓句型更簡潔流暢。
  • 限定與非限定關係子句修飾先行詞
    Chuang urged the public to ask friends or relatives who are acting differently how they are doing, give them their full attention and listen without judging or offering solutions.
    句中的 "who are acting differently" 是關代 who 引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的先行詞 "friends or relatives"。多益閱讀測驗常考此類結構，用以辨識句子的核心主詞與修飾範圍。
  • be aware that... 名詞子句當受詞
    Taiwan Suicide Prevention Center director Huang Min-wei (黃敏偉) said the center’s surveys found that many people are aware that they should try to accompany those who are in need or encourage them to seek help...
    片語 "are aware that..." 後方接了一個由 that 引導的名詞子句，作為形容詞 aware 的補充說明（表狀態的內容）。在多益考試中，常考 aware 這類形容詞後方應搭配介系詞或連接詞的用法。
