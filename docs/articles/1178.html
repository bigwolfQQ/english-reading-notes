台灣短跑選手破全國紀錄，奪下亞運接力銀牌

Taiwan's sprinters break national record, win Asian Games relay silver

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • relay n. 接力賽
    Taiwan's men's 4x100-meter relay team won silver at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games on Monday night...
    台灣男子 4x100 公尺接力隊周一晚間在愛知-名古屋亞運會上奪得銀牌...
  • break a record phr. 打破紀錄
    ...while breaking the national record in the event with a time of 38.32 seconds.
    ...同時以 38 秒 32 的成績打破了該項目的全國紀錄。
  • previous adj. 先前的、以前的
    Taiwan's previous national record was 38.41, set in June.
    台灣先前的全國紀錄是 38.41，是在 6 月創下的。
  • in contention phr. 有競爭力的、參與競爭的
    Lai kept Taiwan in contention on the opening leg before passing to Chen...
    賴柏翔在第一棒時保持了台灣的競爭力，隨後交棒給陳玟溥...
  • secure v. 獲得、確保
    ...and anchor Yang overtook South Korea to secure second place.
    ...最後一棒的楊俊瀚超越了南韓，確保了第二名的位置。
  • consecutive adj. 連續的
    Yang, 29, was competing at his fourth Asian Games and won a medal for the third consecutive games.
    現年 29 歲的楊俊瀚第四次參加亞運會，連續三屆亞運都有獎牌入帳。
  • within reach phr. 觸手可及的、可以達成的
    He said the team had felt good after Sunday's heats and believed gold was within reach.
    他表示，在周日的預賽之後，團隊感覺狀態良好，並且相信金牌是可以企及的。
  • nevertheless adv. 然而、儘管如此
    He said that the silver -- his first Asian Games relay medal -- was nevertheless special.
    他說，這枚銀牌——他的第一枚亞運接力獎牌——仍然非常特別。
  • tally n. 總數、得分記錄
    With the silver, Taiwan's medal tally rose to three golds, eight silvers and 23 bronzes.
    隨著這枚銀牌的入帳，台灣的獎牌總數上升至 3 金、8 銀、23 銅。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作附加結果的對等子句（分詞構句）
    Taiwan's men's 4x100-meter relay team won silver at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games on Monday night, winning Taiwan's first medal in an Asian Games relay since 2010 while breaking the national record in the event with a time of 38.32 seconds.
    句尾的「winning... while breaking...」是以分詞構句來表達主句動作所產生的自然結果或伴隨動作，省去了連接詞與主詞，直接用分詞來補充說明這場勝利所帶來的成就（贏得獎牌並破紀錄）。
  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense)
    He said he had prayed to her after experiencing a physical problem following the heats...
  • 過去分詞作形容詞修飾名詞
    He said a torn plantar fascia in his left foot, sustained in July, had kept him from training in spikes...
