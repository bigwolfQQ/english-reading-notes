賴清德在亞運表現不佳後讚賞台灣運動員

Lai lauds Taiwan athletes after lackluster Asian Games performance

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • praise v. 讚賞、稱讚
    President Lai Ching-te on Tuesday praised Taiwan's athletes for their performances at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games...
    總統賴清德於週二讚賞台灣運動員在愛知-名古屋亞運會上的表現...
  • delegation n. 代表團
    Meeting members of Taiwan's delegation at the Presidential Office in Taipei, Lai congratulated the medalists...
    賴總統在台北總統府會見台灣代表團成員時，向得獎者表達祝賀...
  • congratulate v. 恭賀、祝賀
    ...Lai congratulated the medalists in his remarks, saying that 'regardless of the color, every medal is an honor for the country.'
    ...賴總統在致詞中向得獎者表達祝賀，並表示『無論顏色為何，每一面獎牌都是國家的榮耀』。
  • ranking n. 排名
    ...ranking 15th among 46 delegations based on the number of gold medals won.
    ...在46個參賽代表團中依金牌數排名第15位。
  • overall adj. 整體的、全面的
    It was Taiwan's lowest gold medal haul and lowest overall ranking since the 1990 Asian Games in Beijing...
    這是台灣自1990年北京亞運以來最低的金牌數和最低的總體排名...
  • highlight v. 突顯、強調
    Lai highlighted the performances of several Taiwanese medalists...
    賴總統特別點出（突顯）幾位台灣得獎者的表現...
  • achievements n. 成就、成績
    ...but stressed that individual achievements were built on the work of an entire support team.
    ...但強調個人的成就是建立在整個後勤支援團隊的努力之上。
  • support team n. phr. 支援團隊、後勤團隊
    ...but stressed that individual achievements were built on the work of an entire support team.
    ...但強調個人的成就是建立在整個後勤支援團隊的努力之上。
  • on behalf of prep. phr. 代表...
    Speaking on behalf of the athletes, Chen said her silver was her best Asian Games result...
    陳代表運動員發言時表示，她的銀牌是她最佳的亞運戰績...
  • stress v. 強調
    ...but stressed that individual achievements were built on the work of an entire support team.
    ...但強調個人的成就是建立在整個後勤支援團隊的努力之上。
  • logistical adj. 後勤的、物流的
    He said the Ministry of Sports would continue improving logistical support, including transportation and meals...
    他表示，體育部將持續改善後勤支援，包括交通和餐飲...
  • ensure v. 確保、保證
    ...to ensure athletes were better cared for at future competitions.
    ...以確保運動員在未來的比賽中獲得更好的照顧。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附加說明 (Present Participle Phrase)
    ...saying that 'regardless of the color, every medal is an honor for the country.'
    句尾的「saying that...」是由現在分詞片語構成的附加說明，用來補充說明前面主句的動作（congratulated）發生的同時所說的話或產生的狀態，常譯為「說：……」。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase)
    ...ranking 15th among 46 delegations based on the number of gold medals won.
    句中的「won」是過去分詞，用來修飾前面的名詞「gold medals」（贏得的金牌），等於形容詞子句「that were won」，省略了「which were」以達到精簡語氣的效果。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    ...but stressed that individual achievements were built on the work of an entire support team.
    子句中使用「were built on」的被動語態（be + 過去分詞），強調個人成就「被建立在」整個團隊的努力之上，著重於承受動作的對象。
  • wh- 關代引導的限定關代子句 (Relative Clause)
    ...and swimmer Wang Kuan-hung (王冠閎), whose victory ended Taiwan's 28-year wait for an Asian Games swimming gold.
    「whose victory ended...」是由所有格關代「whose」引導的關係子句，用來補充修飾前面的選手「Wang Kuan-hung」，「whose」在此代替「Wang Kuan-hung's」。
