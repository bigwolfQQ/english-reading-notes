摩洛哥重新開始受理台灣旅遊簽證

Morocco begins processing Taiwan tourism visas again

單字解析

  • confirmation n. 證實，確認
    The government yesterday said it had received confirmation that Morocco resumed accepting tourism visa applications from Taiwanese passport holders last week, two months after abruptly suspending the process in July.
    政府昨天表示，已接到確幸，摩洛哥上週已恢復接受台灣護照持有者的旅遊簽證申請，此前該國在7月曾突兀地暫停此流程。
  • resume v. 恢復，重新開始
    The government yesterday said it had received confirmation that Morocco resumed accepting tourism visa applications from Taiwanese passport holders last week, two months after abruptly suspending the process in July.
    政府昨天表示，已接到確幸，摩洛哥上週已恢復接受台灣護照持有者的旅遊簽證申請，此前該國在7月曾突兀地暫停此流程。
  • suspend v. 暫停，中止
    The government yesterday said it had received confirmation that Morocco resumed accepting tourism visa applications from Taiwanese passport holders last week, two months after abruptly suspending the process in July.
    政府昨天表示，已接到確幸，摩洛哥上週已恢復接受台灣護照持有者的旅遊簽證申請，此前該國在7月曾突兀地暫停此流程。
  • staffer n. 職員，員工
    The Ministry of Foreign Affairs said a staffer at its office in Tokyo called the Moroccan embassy in Japan on Friday, and was informed that the embassy had officially resumed accepting applications for Taiwanese travelers’ Visa Confirmation Letters (VCLs) beginning Monday last week.
    外交部表示，其駐東京辦事處的一名職員上週五致電摩洛哥駐日本大使館，並被告知該大使館已於上週一正式恢復受理台灣旅客的簽證確認函（VCL）申請。
  • inform v. 通知，告知
    The Ministry of Foreign Affairs said a staffer at its office in Tokyo called the Moroccan embassy in Japan on Friday, and was informed that the embassy had officially resumed accepting applications for Taiwanese travelers’ Visa Confirmation Letters (VCLs) beginning Monday last week.
    外交部表示，其駐東京辦事處的一名職員上週五致電摩洛哥駐日本大使館，並被告知該大使館已於上週一正式恢復受理台灣旅客的簽證確認函（VCL）申請。
  • official adj. 官方的，正式的
    The Ministry of Foreign Affairs said a staffer at its office in Tokyo called the Moroccan embassy in Japan on Friday, and was informed that the embassy had officially resumed accepting applications for Taiwanese travelers’ Visa Confirmation Letters (VCLs) beginning Monday last week.
    外交部表示，其駐東京辦事處的一名職員上週五致電摩洛哥駐日本大使館，並被告知該大使館已於上週一正式恢復受理台灣旅客的簽證確認函（VCL）申請。
  • restriction n. 限制，約束
    However, as the Moroccan embassy has still not resumed VCL applications for non-tourism purposes, the ministry said it would not accept visa applications from Moroccan nationals until such restrictions are lifted.
    然而，由於摩洛哥大使館仍未恢復非旅遊目的之VCL申請，外交部表示，在這些限制解除之前，將不會接受摩洛哥國民的簽證申請。
  • announce v. 宣佈，公佈
    The ministry’s statement was issued after a local travel agency announced over the weekend that the Moroccan embassy in Japan had told the agency it was resuming visa applications from Taiwanese nationals.
    外交部的聲明是在一家當地旅行社於週末宣佈，摩洛哥駐日本大使館已告知該旅行社將恢復受理台灣國民的簽證申請之後發布的。
  • effective adj. 生效的
    Citing the principle of reciprocity, the ministry later announced that it would suspend processing visa applications from Moroccan nationals, effective from July 31, except for special projects approved by the government.
    外交部援引互惠原則，隨後宣佈自7月31日起暫停受理摩洛哥國民的簽證申請，經政府批准的特殊專案則不在此限。
  • eligible adj. 有資格的，具備條件的
    The ministry said at the time that the Moroccan government’s decision had seriously affected two-way exchanges, adding that Taiwanese passport holders can obtain a VCL only through the Moroccan embassy in Japan and are ineligible for a Moroccan e-visa.
    外交部當時表示，摩洛哥政府的決定已嚴重影響雙向交流，並補充說台灣護照持有者只能透過摩洛哥駐日本大使館獲得VCL，且不符合申請摩洛哥電子簽證的資格。

文法重點

  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense)
    The government yesterday said it had received confirmation that Morocco resumed accepting tourism visa applications...
    用來表示在過去某一時間（said）之前已經發生的動作（收到確認）。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The ministry’s statement was issued after a local travel agency announced...
    主詞 statement 是動作 issue 的承受者，故使用「be + 過去分詞 (was issued)」構成被動語態。
  • 現在分詞作附加補充說明 (Present Participle as a Modifier)
    ...adding that Taiwanese passport holders can obtain a VCL only through the Moroccan embassy in Japan...
    句尾的「adding...」是由現在分詞構成的分詞片語，用來補充說明前面子句所發生的事或順帶提及的內容，相當於 and added that...
  • 條件連接詞 until 引導的時間副詞子句
    ...the ministry said it would not accept visa applications from Moroccan nationals until such restrictions are lifted.
    until 引導時間副詞子句，用來表示主句動作持續發生的終點。注意在英文中，表示未來時間的副詞子句常以現在式（are lifted）代替未來式。
