沙烏地阿拉伯調查防禦系統為何失效

Saudis probe how defense systems failed

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • investigation n. 調查
    Saudi Arabia has launched an investigation to determine how its air defenses failed to intercept a barrage of missiles and drones
    沙烏地阿拉伯已展開調查，以判定其防空系統為何未能攔截一連串的飛彈與無人機
  • intercept v. 攔截
    determine how its air defenses failed to intercept a barrage of missiles and drones fired by Houthis.
    判定其防空系統為何未能攔截青年運動所發射的一連串飛彈與無人機。
  • respond v. 回應，回答
    Saudi Arabia did not immediately respond to the claim, but the strikes came hours after air raid sirens blared
    沙烏地阿拉伯並未立即對這項指控做出回應，但這些空襲發生在空襲警報大作的幾小時後
  • authorize v. 授權，批准
    officials, who spoke on condition of anonymity because they were not authorized to comment publicly, said an initial probe showed...
    因未獲授權公開發言而要求匿名並接受採訪的官員表示，初步調查顯示...
  • initial adj. 最初的，初步的
    an initial probe showed that Houthis fired multiple drones to pave the way for a mix of cruise and ballistic missiles
    初步調查顯示，青年運動發射了多架無人機，旨在為巡弋飛彈與彈道飛彈的混合攻擊鋪路
  • aim v. 旨在，目的是
    One of the officials said the investigation also aims to find out why air defense systems did not provide earlier warning
    其中一名官員表示，調查的目標還在於查明，防空系統為何沒有提供更早的預警
  • vulnerability n. 脆弱性，弱點
    has exposed vulnerabilities in Saudi Arabia’s defense system and signaled a dangerous inflection point
    暴露了沙烏地阿拉伯防禦系統中的漏洞，並標誌著一場危險的轉折點
  • stakeholder n. 利害關係人
    to launch a military operation and at the same time try to give confidence to the population and external stakeholders
    一方面發動軍事行動，同時又試圖給予民眾和外部利益相關者信心

文法重點

  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    The Houthis possess ballistic missiles and advanced systems fitted with warheads that can change direction during flight...
    使用關代 "that" 引導形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的先行詞 "warheads"（彈頭），說明這些彈頭具備「能在飛行途中改變方向」的特徵。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾（被動分詞）
    ...advanced systems fitted with warheads that can change direction during flight...
    過去分詞片語 "fitted with warheads" 在句中用來修飾前面的名詞 "advanced systems"，相當於形容詞子句 "which are fitted with warheads" 的簡化版。
  • 名詞子句作受詞
    ...said an initial probe showed that Houthis fired multiple drones to pave the way for a mix of cruise and ballistic missiles...
    連接詞 "that" 引導名詞子句，在句中作為動詞 "showed" 的受詞，用以說明調查所顯示的具體內容。
  • 地方副詞子句與所有格修飾
    ...to reach Riyadh’s King Khalid International Airport, where at least 13 people were killed and more than 300 were wounded in attacks on Monday.
    使用關係副詞 "where" 引導非限定關係子句，補充說明前面的地點「利雅德的哈立德國王國際機場」，並在子句中使用被動語態 "were killed" 與 "were wounded"。
