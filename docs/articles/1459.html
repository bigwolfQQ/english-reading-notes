微軟呼籲為人工智慧設置「緊急煞車」

Microsoft calls for AI ‘emergency brake’

單字解析

  • chief executive officer n. 執行長
    Microsoft Corp chief executive officer Satya Nadella said companies should treat powerful artificial intelligence (AI) models as potential insider threats...
    微軟公司執行長薩蒂亞·納德拉表示，企業應將強大的人工智慧（AI）模型視為潛在的內部威脅……
  • deploy v. 部署，調動
    Nadella said that those deploying advanced AI should not rely on assurances from model makers.
    納德拉表示，那些部署先進AI的人員不應依賴模型製造商的保證。
  • authorized adj. 經過授權的，經批准的
    An authorized person should always be able to pause or shut down a model mid-task.
    授權人員應隨時能夠在任務進行中暫停或關閉模型。
  • disclose v. 揭露，透露
    The statement comes as Anthropic PBC and OpenAI Inc have disclosed a spate of incidents in the past few months...
    這項聲明發布之際，Anthropic PBC和OpenAI Inc已揭露過去幾個月內發生的一連串事件……
  • development n. 發展，開發
    ...place limits on the company’s development of its most advanced models on Sept. 14...
    ……對該公司開發最先進模型的行為施加限制……
  • violate v. 違反，違背
    ...or complete a task that would require violating their governing principles.
    ……也不應完成需要違反其治理原則的任務。
  • critical adj. 關鍵的，至關重要的
    Nadella’s safety tips include not relying on a single AI model for critical decisions...
    納德拉的安全建議包括：不要將重大決策依賴於單一AI模型……
  • enterprise n. 企業，公司
    He also called for disclosures of major AI failures or breaches and for enterprises to share details about what went wrong...
    他還呼籲揭露重大的AI故障或安全漏洞，並要求企業分享出錯細節……
  • authority n. 權力，權威
    “In other words, we need to separate the supply of intelligence from the authority over it,” he added.
    他補充道：「換句話說，我們需要將智慧的供應與對它的控制權分開。」
  • administer / administration n. 政府行政當局，管理
    US President Donald Trump’s administration has so far taken a largely hands-off approach...
    美國總統唐納·川普的政府迄今採取了大致不干預的方法……
  • require v. 要求，規定
    ...but Trump’s newly launched AI task force on Friday said that developers are required to report and resolve security incidents...
    ……但川普新成立的AI工作小組於週五表示，開發人員必須通報並解決安全事件……
  • remedy v. 補救，糾正
    “Companies must immediately disclose incidents involving their models and follow with swift, decisive action to remedy any and all harm,”...
    「企業必須立即揭露涉及其模型的事件，並採取迅速、果斷的行動來補救任何及所有損害」……
  • regulation n. 規章，法規，監管
    Amodei and other AI leaders have called for additional government regulation and industrywide coordination...
    阿莫代伊和其他AI領袖已呼籲加強政府監管與業界協調……

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...a now-viral essay from Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei that warned of the technology’s catastrophic risks.
    句中的「that warned of the technology’s catastrophic risks」是用來修飾前方的名詞 essay（文章）的關係子句；而在前面也有分詞片語作為形容詞用法（如 involving their AI models acting in unintended ways 中的 involving），用來補充說明前面的名詞。此結構在多益閱讀測驗中常考，用以測試考生對名詞與修飾語之間關係的理解。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Delayed notification, inadequate corrective action and a failure to take responsibility will not be tolerated.
    句中的 「will not be tolerated」 採用了助動詞 will 加上 be 動詞與過去分詞 (tolerate 的過去分詞 tolerated) 的被動語態結構。在商業與正式英文（如合約、政策宣告）中，被動語態經常用於強調承受動作的對象（此處為「延遲通報與不當的矯正行動」）或是表現出客觀、正式的語氣。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞 (Gerund Phrase as Subject)
    Delayed notification, inadequate corrective action and a failure to take responsibility will not be tolerated.
    句首的三個主詞：「Delayed notification」（延遲通報）、「inadequate corrective action」（不當的矯正行動）以及「a failure to take responsibility」（未能承擔責任）皆為名詞性質的片語（其中包含動名詞或名詞化的動作）。多益考試常考這類複雜主詞與動詞一致性（在此皆為複數或透過 and 連接形成複數概念搭配 will）的文法題。
  • 使役與要求動詞後的受詞補語 (Verbs of Request/Demand followed by that-clause with subjunctive-like base verb)
    He also called for disclosures of major AI failures or breaches and for enterprises to share details about what went wrong...
    句型「called for [something] and for [enterprise] to share...」展現了使用 call for 後接名詞受詞與不定詞片語 (to share) 來表達要求或訴求的句構。在多益商業英文中，類似的動詞如 request, demand, require 等也常接這樣的結構，用以清楚表達指令或期許。
