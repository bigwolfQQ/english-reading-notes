調查：多數人預期第四季房價將趨於穩定

Most expect housing prices to stabilize in Q4: Survey

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • respondent n. 受訪者
    Over 60 percent of respondents to a survey expect housing prices in Taiwan to stabilize...
    超過60%的調查受訪者預期台灣房價將趨於穩定...
  • stabilize v. 穩定
    ...expect housing prices in Taiwan to stabilize in the fourth quarter of this year...
    ...預期台灣房價在今年第四季將趨於穩定...
  • sentiment n. 情緒，市場氣氛
    ...indicating improving sentiment toward the local housing market, Chinatrust Real Estate Co said yesterday.
    ...中信房屋昨天表示，這顯示出對當地房市的情緒正在改善。
  • poll n. 民意調查
    Citing the poll, Chinatrust said 64.1 percent of respondents expected local home prices to remain stable...
    引述該民調，中信房屋表示，64.1%的受訪者預期當地房價將保持穩定...
  • percentage point n. 百分點
    ...down almost 10 percentage points from a similar poll conducted in June.
    ...與6月進行的類似民調相比，下跌了近10個百分點。
  • property market n. 房地產市場
    ...more potential homebuyers were inclined to enter the property market to hedge against inflation...
    ...更多潛在購屋者傾向進入房地產市場以對沖通膨...
  • hedge against v. phr. 對沖（風險），防範
    ...more potential homebuyers were inclined to enter the property market to hedge against inflation...
    ...更多潛在購屋者傾向進入房地產市場以對沖通膨...
  • mortgage n. 房貸，抵押貸款
    In March, the central bank relaxed its selective credit controls on second-home mortgages...
    3月，央行放寬了第二戶房貸的選擇性信用管制...
  • lending n. 放款，借貸
    ...as the ratio of home mortgages to banks’ total lending has shown signs of falling.
    ...由於房貸占銀行總放款比率已出現下滑跡象。
  • momentum n. 動能，氣勢
    She said further easing could encourage more potential owner-occupier homebuyers to enter the market and boost market momentum.
    她表示，進一步放寬可以鼓勵更多潛在的自住型購屋者進入市場，並提振市場動能。
  • interest rate n. 利率
    The survey found that only 13.7 percent of respondents said the central bank should raise its key interest rates...
    調查發現，僅有13.7%的受訪者表示央行應調升關鍵利率...
  • financial burden n. 財務負擔
    ...as they hoped to avoid an increase in their financial burden by paying higher interest when buying a home.
    ...因為他們希望避免在買房時因支付更高利息而增加財務負擔。
  • prompt v. 促使，激起
    She said that with the local housing market showing positive signs, a rate hike could prompt potential homebuyers to delay purchases...
    她表示，隨著本地房市展現正面訊號，升息可能會促使潛在購屋者延後購屋...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作結果狀語 (Present Participle as Result)
    ...indicating improving sentiment toward the local housing market, Chinatrust Real Estate Co said yesterday.
    句尾的「indicating...」是由對等子句簡化而來的現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前面整件事情所帶來的結果或意義，翻譯時通常譯為「顯示出……」。
  • 被動語態的過去分詞作後置修飾 (Past Participle as Post-modifier)
    ...down almost 10 percentage points from a similar poll conducted in June.
    句中的「conducted in June」是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞「a similar poll（類似民調）」，省略了「which was」，用來形容該民調是在六月被執行的。
  • 複合介係詞與分詞構句 (Compound Preposition & Participle Structure)
    Ahead of the central bank’s quarterly policymaking meeting tomorrow, the survey found that...
    「Ahead of」是複合介係詞，意為「在……之前」。逗號後面接主句「the survey found that...」，用來交代調查進行的時間背景。
  • 名詞子句作受詞 (Noun Clause as Object)
    Chinatrust said 64.1 percent of respondents expected local home prices to remain stable during the October-December period...
    動詞「said」後面接了一個由「that」（此處省略）引導的名詞子句「64.1 percent of respondents expected...」作為其受詞，用以表達中信房屋所說的具體內容。
  • 表原因的As引導副詞子句 (Adclause of Cause introduced by As)
    ...as the ratio of home mortgages to banks’ total lending has shown signs of falling.
    連接詞「as」在此處引導一個表原因的副詞子句，相當於「because」，用來解釋為什麼央行進一步放寬信用管制仍有空間。
