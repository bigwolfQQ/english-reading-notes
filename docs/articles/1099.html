國立故宮博物院與台灣虎航合作推出彩繪機

National Palace Museum takes flight with Tigerair

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • enhance v. 提高，增加，改善
    Taiwanese airlines are turning to culture and cuisine to enhance their flying experience, with Tigerair Taiwan unveiling a National Palace Museum-themed aircraft ahead of its 12th anniversary this weekend.
    台灣的航空公司正轉向結合文化與美食以提升飛行體驗，台灣虎航選在週末迎接成立12週年之前，發表了一架以國立故宮博物院為主題的彩繪機。
  • collaboration n. 合作，協力
    Tigerair yesterday announced a three-year collaboration with the museum to decorate the fuselage of one of its aircraft with renowned Chinese artworks and artifacts.
    虎航昨日宣布與故宮展開為期三年的合作，將在其其中一架飛機的機身上裝飾著名的中國藝術品與文物。
  • incorporate v. 納入，結合，包含
    The design incorporates the mountain contours of the museum’s Dwelling in the Fuchun Mountains painting, and well-known artifacts such as the Jadeite Cabbage, the Meat-shaped Stone and the Mao Gong Ding bronze vessel.
    該設計融合了故宮典藏名畫《富春山居圖》的山水輪廓，以及諸如翠玉白菜、肉形石和毛公鼎等知名文物。
  • exteriors n. 外部，外觀
    The aircraft’s exterior is expected to be completed by mid-November and begin service by the end of the year.
    該飛機的外觀預計於11月中旬完成，並於年底前投入營運。
  • partnership n. 夥伴關係，合作關係
    National Palace Museum Director Hsiao Tsung-huang (蕭宗煌) said the partnership is part of the museum’s effort to bring its collections to international audiences.
    國立故宮博物院院長蕭宗煌表示，這次的夥伴關係是故宮努力將其館藏帶給國際觀眾的一部分。
  • commercial adj. 商業的
    The museum holds about 700,000 artifacts and has been involved in commercial collaborations.
    故宮擁有約70萬件文物，並一直積極參與商業合作。
  • discount n. 折扣
    The airline is offering passengers a 10 percent discount on admission to the museum’s Taipei and southern branches, as well as select museum store products, through the end of next year.
    透過明年底前的優惠，該航空公司正提供乘客參觀故宮台北院區及南部院區門票9折優惠，以及部分故宮商店商品的折扣。
  • differentiate v. 區分，區隔
    The move comes as Taiwan’s carriers increasingly use food and cultural partnerships to differentiate their in-flight offerings.
    此舉正值台灣的航空公司日益利用餐飲與文化合作來區隔其機上產品與服務。
  • departing adj. 出發的，離去的
    China Airlines said it would from next month begin serving dishes from New York-based Taiwanese restaurant WENWEN on flights departing from Los Angeles and Ontario.
    中華航空表示，從下個月開始，將在自洛杉磯和安大略出發的航班上供應來自紐約的台灣餐廳 WENWEN 的餐點。
  • tailored adj. 量身訂製的，專門的
    Starlux Airlines has unveiled its fourth quarter in-flight menu, featuring seasonal ingredients, Taiwanese flavors and dishes tailored to its destinations, including a fall and winter menu from Michelin-starred Taiwanese restaurant Genji.
    星宇航空已公布其第四季機上菜單，融合了季節性食材、台灣風味以及為其航點量身打造的餐點，其中包括來自米其林星級台灣餐廳 Genji 的秋冬菜單。
  • launch v. 發起，推出，啟動
    Starlux also announced that it would launch codeshare flights with American Airlines, connecting its transpacific routes with the latter’s flights to 20 major North American cities, including Philadelphia, New York, Chicago, Miami, Dallas and Las Vegas.
    星宇航空也宣布將與美國航空推出共掛班號航班，將其跨太平洋航線與對方飛往 20 個北美主要城市的航班相連結，包括費城、紐約、芝加哥、邁阿密、達拉斯和拉斯維加斯。

文法重點

  • with + 名詞 + 分詞（獨立分詞結構表達伴隨狀況）
    Taiwanese airlines are turning to culture and cuisine to enhance their flying experience, with Tigerair Taiwan unveiling a National Palace Museum-themed aircraft ahead of its 12th anniversary this weekend.
    「with + 受詞 + 現在分詞 (V-ing)」構成附帶狀況的獨立分詞結構，用來補充說明主句發生的同時，另一個相關的動作正在進行。在此句中，unveiling 的主動語態修飾 Tigerair Taiwan。
  • 過去分詞作後置定語（修飾名詞）
    China Airlines said it would from next month begin serving dishes from New York-based Taiwanese restaurant WENWEN on flights departing from Los Angeles and Ontario.
    句中的「departing from Los Angeles and Ontario」是現在分詞片語，用來後置修飾前面的名詞 flights。此結構相當於一個形容詞子句「flights that depart from...」，能讓句子更簡鍊。
  • 未來過去式 (would + V) 表達轉述未來
    China Airlines said it would from next month begin serving dishes from New York-based Taiwanese restaurant WENWEN on flights departing from Los Angeles and Ontario.
    在間接敘述（報導動詞 said 為過去式）中，若要表達原本說話時的「未來」將會發生的事，時態需往前推一個梯次，用「would + 原形動詞」來表示。
