Taoyuan Airport MRT to add weekday evening train from Sept. 21

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作修飾語 (Present Participle Phrase as Modifier)
    Passengers who board the new 6:04 p.m. train and plan to continue to Taoyuan HSR Station (A18) or toward Zhongli can get off at Taoyuan Airport Terminal 2 (A13)...
    句中的 "who board the new 6:04 p.m. train and plan to continue..." 是一個關係代間子句，用來修飾前面的名詞 Passengers（旅客）。在多益閱讀測驗中，常考由 who/which/that引導的形容詞子句，用以補充說明主詞的特徵或動作。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Aside from travelers, weekday evening trains are frequently used by students and workers commuting from Taipei Main Station to residential areas...
    句型 "are frequently used by..." 採用了被動語態（be動詞 + 過去分詞 p.p.）。當焦點在於承受動作的主體（此處為主詞 weekday evening trains 捷運列車）而非動作的執行者時，會使用被動語態，這在商業與新聞英文中非常常見。
  • 分詞構句表伴隨或條件 (Participle Phrase indicating Condition/Time)
    The Taoyuan Airport MRT will run an additional train from Taipei to Taoyuan Airport starting Sept. 21...
    句尾的 "starting Sept. 21" 是由現在分詞 "starting" 引導的分詞構句，相當於副詞子句 "when it starts on Sept. 21"，用來補充說明加開列車開始實施的時間點。這種用法在商業公告或新聞中經常用來簡潔地表達生效日期。
