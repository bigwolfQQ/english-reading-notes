兄弟象封王拿下下半季冠軍，鎖定中華職棒季後賽席位

Brothers clinch second-half title, secure CPBL playoff berth

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • victory n. 勝利
    The CTBC Brothers clinched the Chinese Professional Baseball League's (CPBL) second-half title Sunday with a 12-1 victory over the TSG Hawks
    中信兄弟隊週日以12比1擊敗台鋼雄鷹，拿下中華職棒（CPBL）下半季冠軍
  • secure v. 獲得、確保、取得
    securing a playoff berth after finishing last in the first half.
    在度過上半季墊底的低潮後，順利取得季後賽門票。
  • record n. 戰績、紀錄
    the Brothers finished the second half with a 36-24 record and celebrated their league-record 21st half-season title
    兄弟隊以36勝24敗的戰績結束下半季，並在黃色彩帶紛飛中慶祝他們締造聯盟紀錄的第21座半季冠軍
  • apologize v. 道歉
    Manager Keiichi Hirano apologized to supporters for the first-half performance
    總教練平野惠一為上半季的表現向支持者道歉
  • credit v. 歸功於
    and credited their backing for the team's recovery.
    並將球隊的復甦歸功於球迷的支持。
  • reclaim v. 奪回、取回
    "We want to reclaim the No. 1 spot in Taiwan," he said
    他說：「我們想要奪回台灣第一的寶座」
  • earn v. 贏得、賺得、獲得
    while striking out five to earn his 12th win of the season.
    並送出五次三振，奪下本季第12勝。
  • advantage n. 優勢
    As second-half champions, the Brothers will begin the playoff series with a one-win advantage.
    身為下半季冠軍，兄弟隊將在季後賽系列賽中帶有一場勝場優勢。
  • host v. 主辦、舉辦
    According to the schedule announced Monday, the Brothers will host the opener at Taichung Intercontinental Baseball Stadium.
    根據週一公布的賽程，兄弟隊將在台中洲際棒球場主辦首戰。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附加說明（補充結果或狀態）
    The CTBC Brothers clinched the Chinese Professional Baseball League's (CPBL) second-half title Sunday with a 12-1 victory over the TSG Hawks, securing a playoff berth after finishing last in the first half.
    句尾的「securing a playoff berth...」是由現在分詞 (securing) 引導的分詞片語，用來補充說明主句動作所產生的直接結果或伴隨狀況，中文常譯為「進而確保了……」或「取得……」。
  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    They will face the Uni-President 7-Eleven Lions, who qualified by finishing second in the full-season standings, in a playoff series beginning Oct. 9.
    句中的「who qualified by finishing second...」是非限定關係子句（逗號後方），用來補充說明統一獅隊是如何取得資格的。who 代替前面的 Uni-President 7-Eleven Lions。
  • 條件句與假設語氣的簡化（if necessary）
    The Lions will host Game 2 at Tainan's Asia-Pacific International Baseball Stadium on Saturday before the series returns to Taichung on Sunday and, if necessary, next Monday.
    句中的「if necessary」是「if it is necessary」的省略形式，意指「如果有必要的話」，常用於商業書信或新聞報導中簡化條件子句。
