文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後置修飾（分詞片語）
    The Financial Supervisory Commission (FSC) is announcing the change for offshore private equity and private credit fund products sold in Taiwan’s dedicated wealth zone by the end of next month...
    句中的 "sold in Taiwan’s dedicated wealth zone" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "offshore private equity and private credit fund products"，相當於形容詞子句 "which are sold in..." 的簡化版，多益閱讀測驗中常考這種分詞省略 "which are" 的結構。
  • 非限定關係子句
    Such funds, which are distributed to Taiwanese investors through licensed banks or asset managers, are capped at 99 investors...
    句中的 ", which are distributed..." 是非限定關係子句（前面有逗號），用來補充說明前面的主詞 "Such funds"。在多益測驗中，常考此處關係代名詞的選擇（例如用 which 而不用 that）。
  • 現在分詞表附帶結果或伴隨動作
    Taiwan plans to ease a limit on the number of investors permitted in offshore private-market funds by October, stepping up efforts to expand the wealth-management sector.
    句尾的 ", stepping up efforts..." 是現在分詞片語作副詞用，表示主句動作所帶來的結果或伴隨行為，意指台灣計畫放寬限制的同時，也「正逐步加大努力」。這是英文新聞報導中非常常見且高效的句型。
  • 動名詞片語作介系詞受詞
    The government is pushing to turn Taiwan into a regional wealth hub, capitalizing on surging private wealth and buoyant capital markets...
    句中的 "capitalizing on..." 是動名詞片語，接在逗號後面作為伴隨狀況或原因的補充說明。介系詞 "on" 後面接動名詞 "capitalizing"，展現了多益商業英文中常用 "capitalize on"（利用／善用……）的動詞片語搭配。
