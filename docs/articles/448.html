台北大巨蛋事件後，老鼠重返該市選戰話題

After Taipei Dome incident, rats return to city's election discourse

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • control n. 控制、管理
    A rodent's interruption of a professional baseball game at Taipei Dome on Wednesday sparked renewed discussion of rat control efforts, weeks ahead of the city's mayoral election.
    週三在大巨蛋舉行的職棒比賽中，一隻老鼠亂入，引發了各界對滅鼠工作的重新討論，此時距離該市市長選舉僅剩數週。
  • spot v. 注意到、看見、發現
    Just before the eighth inning of Wednesday's game between the Fubon Guardians and the Wei Chuan Dragons, a small rat was spotted running out onto the field at Taipei Dome between home plate and the first base dugout.
    在富邦悍將與味全龍週三比賽的第八局開始前不久，一隻小老鼠被發現跑進大巨蛋本壘板與一壘休息區之間的球場上。
  • carry out phr. v. 執行、實施、進行
    Speaking to reporters on Thursday, Taipei Mayor Chiang Wan-an (蔣萬安) said he had already asked Farglory Group, Taipei Dome's owner and operator, to carry out disinfection procedures.
    台北市長蔣萬安週四向記者表示，他已經要求大巨蛋的所有權人兼營運商遠雄集團執行消毒程序。
  • operator n. 經營者、營運商
    Speaking to reporters on Thursday, Taipei Mayor Chiang Wan-an (蔣萬安) said he had already asked Farglory Group, Taipei Dome's owner and operator, to carry out disinfection procedures.
    台北市長蔣萬安週四向記者表示，他已經要求大巨蛋的所有權人兼營運商遠雄集團執行消毒程序。
  • jointly adv. 聯合地、共同地
    Taipei City's sports, environmental and health departments also jointly inspected conditions at the dome, and tasked the sports department with monitoring the situation and imposing penalties if necessary, said Chiang, a member of the Kuomintang (KMT).
    身為國民黨員的蔣萬安表示，台北市政府的體育、環保和衛生部門也聯合視察了大巨蛋的狀況，並指派體育局監控情況並在必要時處以罰款。
  • inspect v. 檢查、視察、審查

    身為國民黨員的蔣萬安表示，台北市政府的體育、環保和衛生部門也聯合視察了大巨蛋的狀況，並指派體育局監控情況並在必要時處以罰款。
  • penalty n. 處罰、罰款、懲罰
    Taipei City's sports, environmental and health departments also jointly inspected conditions at the dome, and tasked the sports department with monitoring the situation and imposing penalties if necessary, said Chiang, a member of the Kuomintang (KMT).
    身為國民黨員的蔣萬安表示，台北市政府的體育、環保和衛生部門也聯合視察了大巨蛋的狀況，並指派體育局監控情況並在必要時處以罰款。
  • feedback n. 回饋、意見
    Based on feedback he has collected, Shen said, many Taipei residents also have strong feelings about the reduced number of public trash cans, which has caused inconvenience while also failing to reduce the number of rat sightings.
    沈伯洋說，根據他所收集到的回饋，許多台北市民對於公共垃圾桶數量減少也有強烈的感受，這不僅造成了不便，也沒有減少老鼠出沒的次數。
  • launch v. 發起、推出、啟動
    The issue receded from the public eye after the city government launched a multi-day cleaning drive and pledged more aggressive control measures.
    在市政府發起為期數天的清潔運動並承諾採取更積極的控制措施後，這個議題便從公眾視野中退去。
  • pledge v. 保證、承諾
    The issue receded from the public eye after the city government launched a multi-day cleaning drive and pledged more aggressive control measures.
    在市政府發起為期數天的清潔運動並承諾採取更積極的控制措施後，這個議題便從公眾視野中退去。
  • confirm v. 確認、證實
    Regarding the issue of public trash cans, Taipei Department of Environmental Protection Director Shyu Shyh-shiun (徐世勲) confirmed last month that the city had been removing bins to stop people from illegally disposing of household garbage.
    關於公共垃圾桶的議題，台北市環保局長徐世勳上個月證實，該市一直在撤除垃圾桶，以阻止民眾非法丟棄家用垃圾。
  • exclude v. 排除、不包括
    Excluding those in tourist areas and business districts, which are being kept in place, the city has decreased the number of public trash cans over several years from around 2,000 to 806, Shyu said.
    徐世勳表示，除了保留在旅遊區和商業區的垃圾桶外，該市多年來已將公共垃圾桶的數量從大約 2,000 個減少到 806 個。

  • 非限定關係子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
