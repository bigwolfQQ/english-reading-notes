文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Phrase of Present Participle）作後置修飾
    A documentary tracing the development of Taiwan's semiconductor industry over the past five decades was screened at a special showing in The Hague Thursday.
    句中的 "tracing the development of Taiwan's semiconductor industry over the past five decades" 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前方名詞 "A documentary"（紀錄片），等於形容詞子句 "which traced..." 的簡化用法，在新聞英文中非常常見，能讓句子更緊湊。
  • 過去分詞（Past Participle）作後置修飾
    Tien said Taiwan's technological advancements were the result of a positive talent cultivation environment that was founded upon the respect for democracy and human rights...
    句中的 "founded upon the respect for democracy..." 是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞短語 "a positive talent cultivation environment"，表被動與完成的概念，相當於關代子句 "that was founded upon..."。
  • 動名詞片語（Gerund Phrase）作主詞補語或介系詞受詞
    Tien echoed Hsiao's explanation, saying that like Odysseus, it took several decades for Taiwan's semiconductor industry to overcome obstacles...
    句尾的 "saying that..." 是分詞構句，表示伴隨的狀況或主詞 Tien 在說話同時所做的動作。此外，句中也運用了虛主詞 "it took... for... to..." 句型來表達「某人花費時間做某事」，這是多益閱讀測驗極高頻的句構。
