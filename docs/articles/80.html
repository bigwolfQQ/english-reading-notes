美國學者：隨著晶片產業海外擴張，台灣應朝多元化發展

Taiwan should diversify as chip sector expands overseas: U.S. scholar

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • dependent adj. 依賴的、從屬的
    Taiwan should avoid becoming overly dependent on semiconductors and preserve resources for other industries while expanding chip production with trusted overseas partners, a U.S. scholar said Thursday.
    一名美國學者週四表示，台灣應避免過度依賴半導體，在與值得信賴的海外夥伴擴大晶片生產的同時，應保留資源給其他產業。
  • industry n. 工業、產業
    The semiconductor industry has been fabulous for Taiwan, but there is the danger that Taiwan devotes too much to being the one-trick pony, as Detroit once did in the United States to automobiles.
    半導體產業對台灣好得不得了，但存在著一種危險，即台灣投入太多心力成為只會單一絕招的庸才，就像美國底特律當年對汽車產業的過度依賴一樣。
  • policy n. 政策、方針
    Government policy could help diversify the economy and leave room for Taiwan to develop robotics, biotechnology and other industries, according to Tiffert.
    蒂费特認為，政府政策有助於經濟多元化，並為台灣發展機器人、生物科技和其他產業保留空間。
  • cooperation n. 合作、配合
    At the same time, he argued, cooperation with Japan, Europe and the United States could distribute semiconductor production more widely without diminishing Taiwan's position.
    同時他主張，與日本、歐洲和美國合作可以更廣泛地分配半導體生產，而不會削弱台灣的地位。
  • profitable adj. 有利潤的、賺錢的
    Tiffert cited TSMC's Arizona operations as evidence, saying Taiwanese partners had helped the United States relearn manufacturing capabilities it had lost, adding that the fabs are also profitable, with yields comparable to those in Taiwan.
    蒂费特以台積電在亞利桑那州的營運為例指出，台灣夥伴協助美國重新學會了其失去的製造能力，並補充說這些晶圓廠也是獲利的，其良率與台灣相當。
  • capacity n. 產能、容量、能力
    Instead of pursuing complete decoupling, governments should establish enough redundant capacity to withstand disasters, conflicts and other disruptions without attempting to replicate entire supply chains domestically, Tiffert argued.
    蒂费特主張，政府不應追求完全脫鉤，而應建立足夠的備用產能，以抵禦災害、衝突和其他干擾，而不是試圖在國內複製整條供應鏈。
  • subsidies n. 補助金、津貼
    Emiko Higashi, a Kioxia board member and member of the Industrial Policy Council at Japan's Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry, cautioned that government subsidies alone cannot make semiconductor projects viable.
    鎧俠（Kioxia）董事會成員兼日本經濟產業省產業政策委員會成員東哲子警告說，單靠政府補貼並無法使半導體專案具備可行性。
  • collaboration n. 合作、協同作業
    She cited Rapidus' collaboration with IBM on 2-nanometer technology as an example of allies combining U.S. research capabilities with Japanese manufacturing expertise.
    她以Rapidus與IBM在2奈米技術上的合作為例，說明盟友如何將美國的研究能力與日本的製造專業結合起來。
  • export n. / v. 出口
    Meanwhile, Marc Hijink, a financial reporter and technology columnist at Dutch newspaper NRC, warned that tighter restrictions on semiconductor equipment exports to China could create opportunities for Chinese competitors, particularly in less advanced lithography systems.
    同時，荷蘭《NRC Handelsblad》報紙的財經記者兼科技專欄作家馬克·希金克（Marc Hijink）警告說，對中國實施更嚴格的半導體裝置出口限制，可能會為中國競爭對手創造機會，特別是在較不先進的光刻系統方面。
  • maintain v. 維持、保持
    Returning to the need for limited but coordinated redundancy, Tiffert said allies should maintain some domestic capacity while continuing to specialize in areas where they are strongest.
    回到對有限但協調之備援能力的需求，蒂费特表示，盟友應維持一定的國內產能，同時繼續專注於他們最強的領域。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作附帶狀況或結果補充
    Taiwan should avoid becoming overly dependent on semiconductors and preserve resources for other industries while expanding chip production with trusted overseas partners, a U.S. scholar said Thursday.
    句中的 "while expanding chip production..." 是「連接詞 + 現在分詞」的簡化結構，用來表達主句動作發生時的同時狀況（在擴展晶片生產的同時）。
  • 過去分詞作後置定語（修飾名詞）
    ...made the remarks during a panel discussion on trusted supply chains at a forum hosted by the Research Institute for Democracy, Society and Emerging Technology (DSET) and SEMI in Taipei.
    句中的 "hosted by..." 是過去分詞片語，置於名詞 "forum" 後方，用來修飾該論壇是由哪些單位所舉辦的，相當於形容詞子句 "which was hosted by..." 的簡化。
  • 現在分詞作補充說明（附加結果或伴隨動作）
    Tiffert cited TSMC's Arizona operations as evidence, saying Taiwanese partners had helped the United States relearn manufacturing capabilities it had lost, adding that the fabs are also profitable, with yields comparable to those in Taiwan.
    逗點後的 "saying..." 與後方的 "adding that..." 都是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明主句動作發生時的連續動作或說話內容，使句型結構更緊湊。
  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    Returning to the need for limited but coordinated redundancy, Tiffert said allies should maintain some domestic capacity while continuing to specialize in areas where they are strongest.
    句尾的 "where they are strongest" 是由關係副詞 where 引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾先行詞 "areas"（領域），說明是在哪一些領域中他們最強。
