公務員流動率、留任率惡化

Turnover, retention rates among public servants worsening

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • turnover n. 人員流動率、離職率
    For the past four years, more than 55 percent of civil servants quit after working less than five years, with the turnover rate reaching a new high last year, a report by the Legislative Yuan’s Budget Center said.
    立法院預算中心的一份報告指出，在過去四年中，超過百分之五十五的公務員在工作不到五年後離職，去年的離職率更創下新高。
  • retention n. 留任、保持
    Turnover, retention rates among public servants worsening
    公務員流動率、留任率惡化
  • quit v. 辭職、離開（工作）
    The turnover rate for civil servants remained high in the past few years, with 18,072 central and local civil servants quitting their jobs from 2022 to last year, the report said.
    報告指出，過去幾年公務員的離職率一直保持在高檔，從 2022 年到去年，共有 18,072 名中央及地方公務員辭職。
  • account for phr. 占（比例）、說明、解釋
    Of all those who quit during the period, 5,965 were first-time civil servants and had less than five years of service, accounting for 55 percent, it said.
    報告表示，在此期間離職的所有人員中，有 5,965 人是首次擔任公務員且服務年資未滿五年，占了百分之五十五。
  • surpass v. 超越、勝過
    They represented the bulk of resignations across the public sector over the period, at rates from 51.53 to 59.43 percent, surpassing 50 percent each year, it said.
    報告說，他們占了該期間整體公部門辭職人數的大宗，比率介於百分之 51.53 至 59.43 之間，每年皆超過百分之五十。
  • subordinate adj. 下級的、次要的
    The High Court and its subordinate courts had the highest number of new-hire resignations for employees with less than five years of experience at 419, the report said.
    報告指出，高等法院及其下級法院在年資未滿五年之新進員工辭職人數中最高，達到 419 人。
  • shortfall n. 赤字、不足額、缺口
    Fewer people are signing up for the national civil service examination, while shortfalls in recruitment are growing year by year, it said, adding the technical categories had the biggest shortfalls.
    報告指出，報名國家公務人員考試的人數減少，而招募缺口逐年擴大，並補充說技術類別的缺口最大。
  • enhance v. 提高、增加、強化
    The authorities should identify the root cause of the shortfalls and enhance their employee assistance measures, the center said.
    該中心表示，主管機關應找出缺口的根本原因，並強化其員工協助措施。
  • efficiency n. 效率、效能
    The goal is to develop strong public service workforces and friendly workspaces, increasing the government’s administrative efficiency, it said.
    報告指出，其目標是打造強健的公務人力團隊與友善的工作環境，藉此提升政府的行政效率。

文法重點

  • 獨立分詞構句 (With + 名詞 + 分詞)
    For the past four years, more than 55 percent of civil servants quit after working less than five years, with the turnover rate reaching a new high last year...
    「with + 名詞 +現在分詞/過去分詞」結構常在新聞英語中用來補充說明背景或伴隨發生的狀況。在此例中，「with the turnover rate reaching a new high」用來補充說明離職率達到新高的狀況。
  • 現在分詞片語作補充說明 (Adding...
    ...while shortfalls in recruitment are growing year by year, it said, adding the technical categories had the biggest shortfalls.
    句尾的「adding...」是由連接詞省略與主詞同構而來的分詞構句，用來接續補充說明前面子句所提及的內容（即報導中進一步補充的細節）。
  • 名詞子句作受詞
    The budget center said that civil service retirements are increasing.
    動詞「said」後面接了一個由「that」引導的名詞子句（that civil service retirements are increasing），作為該動詞的受詞，用來報導或陳述某個事實。
