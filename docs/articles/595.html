丈夫與友人在中國籍配偶未遂槍擊案中遭到逮捕

Husband, friend arrested in non-fatal shooting of Chinese spouse

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • arrest v. / n. 逮捕
    Two people, including the victim's husband, have been arrested in connection with the non-fatal shooting of a Chinese spouse in Taipei on Friday, authorities said.
    當局表示，週五在台北發生一起中國籍配偶遭槍擊但未致死的案件，包含被害人丈夫在內的兩人已遭到逮捕。
  • prosecutor n. 檢察官
    According to Taipei police and prosecutors, the 50-year-old victim, surnamed Chao (趙), had just exited her home near Shuiyuan Road on Friday afternoon when she was ambushed by a masked individual.
    根據台北警方與檢方指出，50歲的被害人姓趙，週五下午剛離開她位於水源路附近的住處，就遭到一名蒙面歹徒埋伏攻擊。
  • fled v. (flee的過去式) 逃跑、逃走
    Following the shooting, the suspect fled, while Chao fell to the ground injured.
    槍擊發生後，嫌犯逃逸，趙女則受傷倒地。
  • custody n. 拘留、監禁、監護權
    Chen was arrested late Friday at the Tai'an Service Area, off of National Freeway No. 1 in Taichung, and was transferred to the custody of prosecutors the following day.
    陳男於週五深夜在台中中山高速公路泰安服務區被捕，並於隔天移送檢方偵辦。
  • financially adv. 財務上、經濟上
    After meeting in an internet chatroom, Yi and Chen began dating, and had recently been living together in New Taipei's Zhonghe District, where Chen was financially supporting her, the report said.
    報導指出，易女與陳男在網路聊天室結識後開始交往，最近一直同居在新北市中和區，且陳男在經濟上一直在支援她。
  • refuse v. 拒絕
    Chen, for his part, was angry that after returning from a recent trip to China, Chao had refused to help him with his waterproofing business, preferring instead to do part-time work at restaurants, Liberty Times reported.
    《自由時報》報導，陳男方面則是因為不滿趙女從中國返台後，拒絕在防水事業上協助他，反而寧願去餐廳打工而感到憤怒。
  • purchase v. / n. 購買、採購
    Citing testimony from Yi, the Liberty Times report claimed that Chen, wary of carrying out the shooting himself, purchased a firearm online and later took Yi to a shooting range in Tainan to practice her marksmanship.
    引述易女的供詞，《自由時報》報導稱，陳男因顧慮自己動手槍擊，便在網路上購買槍械，隨後帶易女前往台南的靶場練習射擊準度。
  • separately adv. 分別地、各自
    Five days before the shooting, Yi and Chen separately staked out the area around Chao's home and workplace, before reporting back to each other, the report said.
    報導指出，在槍擊案發生前五天，易女和陳男分別對趙女的住處和工作場所周邊進行了場勘，隨後互相回報。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表示伴隨狀況或結果）
    Following the shooting, the suspect fled, while Chao fell to the ground injured.
    句尾的「injured」是過去分詞作補語，補充說明 Chao 倒地時的受傷狀態，相當於形容詞用來描述主詞在該動作發生時的狀況。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句
    Yi, who was identified as a "friend" of Chen, was arrested in New Taipei's Sanchong District on Saturday...
    逗號後的「who was identified as a "friend" of Chen」是對主詞 Yi 進行補充說明的非限定關係子句，若將其省略，主句的完整意思仍然成立。
  • 地方副詞片語置於句首之倒裝/強調句構
    According to a Liberty Times report Sunday, Yi had been living in hiding on the mistaken belief that there was a warrant out for her arrest over a drunk driving incident.
  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense)
    Yi had been living in hiding on the mistaken belief that there was a warrant out for her arrest over a drunk driving incident.
    句中的「had been living」使用過去完成進行式，用來表示在過去某一時間點之前就已經持續進行的動作，強調動作的持續性。
