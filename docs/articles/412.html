宏碁審慎囤積庫存

Acer cautiously stocks inventory

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • cautiously adv. 審慎地，小心翼翼地
    Acer Inc is cautiously maintaining inventories of key components...
    宏碁公司正審慎地維持關鍵零組件的庫存...
  • inventory n. 庫存，存貨
    ...is cautiously maintaining inventories of key components...
    ...正審慎維持關鍵零組件的庫存...
  • component n. 零組件，成分
    ...maintaining inventories of key components, including solid-state drives...
    ...維持關鍵零組件的庫存，包含固態硬碟...
  • cushion v. 緩和，減輕（衝擊等）
    ...to cushion the impact of rising prices, the PC brand said on Wednesday.
    該PC品牌於週三表示，此舉是為了緩解價格上漲帶來的衝擊。
  • demand n. 需求
    While the company has seen demand growth for key components begin to slow...
    雖然該公司已看到關鍵零組件的需求成長開始放緩...
  • dynamics n. 動態，動力學
    ...it remains difficult to predict when the supply-demand dynamics would return to a healthy situation...
    ...仍難以預測供需動態何時能回歸健康狀態...
  • source v. 採購，向……取得貨源
    Acer sources most of its memory chips from Samsung Electronics Co...
    宏碁多數的記憶體晶片皆採購自三星電子...
  • strategy n. 策略
    Amid surging memory prices, Acer’s strategy for product designs is allowing consumers...
    在記憶體價格飆漲的情況下，宏碁的產品設計策略是允許消費者...
  • specification n. 規格，說明書
    ...is allowing consumers to choose specifications that meet their immediate needs...
    ...允許消費者選擇符合目前需求的規格...
  • upgrade v. 升級
    ...and upgrade later when memory chip prices fall, the official said.
    該主管表示，並在未來記憶體晶片價格下跌時再進行升級。
  • extend v. 延長，擴大
    ...to extend the overall lifecycle of PCs, the official added.
    該主管補充說，以延長個人電腦的整體生命週期。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾
    Amid surging memory prices, Acer’s strategy for product designs is allowing consumers...
    句首的 「surging」 是現在分詞，在此作為形容詞用，修飾後方的名詞「memory prices」（飆漲的記憶體價格）。這種用法在財經與商業新聞中極常見，能簡潔地表達持續進行的狀態。
  • 關係代名詞子句（限定用法）
    ...is allowing consumers to choose specifications that meet their immediate needs...
    「that meet their immediate needs」是由關係代名詞 「that」 引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾先行詞 「specifications」。在多益閱讀測驗中，常用來測試先行詞與關係子句的搭配及文法一致性。
  • 被動語態的進行式
    Some products are even being designed with upgradable or replaceable motherboards...
    句型 「are being designed」 結合了現在進行式 （are ... -ing） 與被動語態 （be + 過去分詞 designed），用來強調這項設計動作在現階段正持續被執行中。
