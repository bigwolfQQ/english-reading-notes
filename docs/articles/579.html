歐盟面臨關於「變態」AI眼鏡的爭論

European Union eyes battle over ‘pervert’ AI glasses

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • demand v. 要求，強烈要求
    — with a petition in Britain demanding a ban on their sale.
    ——英國的一份請願書更要求禁止販售這些眼鏡。
  • interact v. 互動
    translate text or interact with an artificial intelligence (AI) assistant
    翻譯文字或與人工智慧（AI）助理互動
  • partnership n. 合夥關係，合作
    developed in partnership with Ray-Ban maker EssilorLuxottica, have fared much better.
    與雷朋（Ray-Ban）製造商依視路陸遜梯卡（EssilorLuxottica）合作開發的智慧眼鏡，表現要好得多。
  • exponential adj. 指數級的，急遽的
    However, with exponential growth came the backlash.
    然而，隨著指數級的增長，強烈的反彈也隨之而來。
  • feature n. 功能，特色
    alongside reports that Meta is developing a facial recognition feature to be integrated into the devices.
    同時有報導指出Meta正在開發一項將整合至這些裝置中的臉部辨識功能。
  • prevent v. 預防，防止
    Meta said it has taken multiple steps to prevent tampering with the blinking LED light
    Meta表示，其已採取多項措施來防止篡改閃爍的LED燈
  • invest v. 投資
    “These investments are already having a deterrent effect,” it said
    Meta表示：「這些投資已經產生了遏制效果」
  • regulate v. 規範，管理，調節
    as the government mulls regulating their use and considers a ban on features
    因為政府正在考慮規範其使用，並考慮禁止某些功能
  • comply v. 遵守，符合
    seeking to know if the glasses comply with data protection and AI rules.
    尋求了解這些眼鏡是否符合資料保護和AI規則。
  • enforcement n. 執行，強制實施
    “We need clear EU rules to protect and strong enforcement to make sure new technologies don’t come at the expense of our fundamental rights,” she said.
    她說：「我們需要明確的歐盟規則來保護，以及強有力的執法，以確保新科技不會以犧牲我們的基本權利為代價。」
  • responsible adj. 負責的
    The European Commission said national data protection authorities were “responsible for monitoring and enforcing” privacy rules.
    歐盟委員會表示，國家資料保護機構「負責監督和執行」隱私規則。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Calls are growing louder in Europe for action against “pervert” smart glasses after secretly filmed footage of girls and women appeared online — with a petition in Britain demanding a ban on their sale.
    句中的 "demanding a ban on their sale" 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "a petition"（請願書），相當於關代子句 "which demands a ban on their sale"，使句子更為精簡。
  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況 (Participial Phrase indicating Circumstance)
    Anyone wearing smart glasses can take photos, film videos, listen to music, make phone calls, translate text or interact with an artificial intelligence (AI) assistant, with sales now hitting millions.
    句尾的 "with sales now hitting millions" 形成「with + 名詞 + 分詞/形容詞/介系詞片語」的複合結構，在句中用來補充說明主句發生時的附帶狀況或背景。
  • 過去分詞作後位修飾 (Past Participle as Postmodifier)
    A 2013 push by Google failed to take off, but Meta’s smart spectacles, developed in partnership with Ray-Ban maker EssilorLuxottica, have fared much better.
    句中的 ", developed in partnership with Ray-Ban maker EssilorLuxottica," 是過去分詞片語，夾在主詞與動詞之間，用來補充修飾前面的主詞 "Meta’s smart spectacles"（Meta的智慧眼鏡），相當於省略了 "which were"。
  • 假設語氣/條件句 (Subjunctive/Conditional Construction)
    “As things stand today, we should definitely go for a ban” until they are proven safe, he added.
    句中的 "until they are proven safe" 運用了被動語態的條件子句，表達在眼鏡被官方或客觀證實安全之前，應該持續採取禁止的動作。
