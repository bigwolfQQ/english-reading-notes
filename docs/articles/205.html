PChome halts sales of badger-hair products on animal welfare concerns

單字解析

  • e-commerce n. 電子商務
    Taiwanese e-commerce platform PChome has halted sales of products containing badger hair over animal welfare concerns...
    台灣電子商務平台 PChome 已基於動物福祉考量，暫停販售含有獾毛的產品……
  • halt v. 暫停，停止
    Taiwanese e-commerce platform PChome has halted sales of products containing badger hair over animal welfare concerns...
    台灣電子商務平台 PChome 已基於動物福祉考量，暫停販售含有獾毛的產品……
  • retailer n. 零售商
    ...the company confirmed Monday after an animal rights group praised the online retailer for making the move.
    ……該公司於週一在一個動物權利團體稱讚該線上零售商採取此舉後，證實了這項消息。
  • vendor n. 供應商，小販
    ...PChome told CNA that it announced the measure in July and notified related vendors to "stop products containing badger hair"...
    ……PChome 告訴中央社，該公司已於 7 月宣布這項措施，並通知相關供應商「停止販售含有獾毛的產品」……
  • review v. 審查，審視，複習
    ...while asking them to review product information and remove affected listings.
    ……同時要求他們審查產品資訊並下架受影響的商品。
  • violate v. 違反，違背
    Products found to violate its rules will be handled through standard management mechanisms...
    被發現違反其規定的產品將透過標準管理機制處理……
  • noncompliance n. 不遵守，違規
    ...without elaborating on what action would be taken in cases of noncompliance.
    ……但未詳細說明針對違規情況將採取何種行動。
  • inspect v. 檢查，視察，稽核
    "We will continue to strengthen reviews of product information and platform inspections to fulfill our responsibility for platform management," PChome said.
    PChome 表示：「我們將持續加強產品資訊審查與平台稽核，以履行平台管理的責任。」
  • alternative n. 替代方案，替代品
    ...Jason Baker, the group's president, said high-quality synthetic-fiber alternatives are widely available...
    ……該組織總裁傑森·貝克表示，高品質的合成纖維替代品隨處可得……
  • affordable adj. 價格實惠的，負擔得起的
    ...perform well and are affordable, and that there was no reason to support an industry...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作後位修飾
    Taiwanese e-commerce platform PChome has halted sales of products containing badger hair over animal welfare concerns...
    句中的 "containing badger hair" 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "products"，相當於形容詞子句 "which contain badger hair" 的簡化用法，在英文新聞中非常常見以求精簡。
  • 過去分詞作後位修飾
    ...due to the abuse faced by the animals.
    句中的 "faced by the animals" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "abuse"，相當於關代子句 "which is faced by the animals" 的被動語態簡化形式。
  • 對等連接詞連接多個動詞子句
    ...it announced the measure in July and notified related vendors to "stop selling products containing badger hair," while asking them to review product information and remove affected listings.
    此句運用了對等連接詞 "and" 來連接主詞 "PChome" 的兩個主要動作（announced 與 notified），隨後又用 "while"引導分詞構句 "while asking them..."，表現出複雜但條理分明的句子結構。
