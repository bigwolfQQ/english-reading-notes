紀錄片探討台灣人在蘇聯遭俘虜的歷史

Documentary explores history of Taiwanese POWs in Soviet captivity

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • detain v. 拘留，扣押
    A documentary exploring the little-known history of Taiwanese soldiers detained in remote Soviet prisoner-of-war camps after fighting for Japan in World War II was screened in Tokyo recently.
    一部探索一段鮮為人知歷史的紀錄片，近日在東京放映，內容關於台灣士兵在第二次世界大戰為日本參戰後，被拘留在偏遠的蘇聯戰俘營。
  • surrender n. / v. 投降
    "Memories Frozen in Time," directed by Hsu Ming-chun (許明淳), traces the lives of Taiwanese soldiers held by the Soviet Union after Japan's surrender in 1945.
    由許明淳執導的《記憶凍結的時間》，追溯了台灣士兵在 1945 年日本投降後被蘇聯扣押的人生歷程。
  • research v. / n. 研究，調查
    Hsu spent three years researching records and visiting Taiwan, Japan and Russia, while interviewing former prisoners and their families.
    許明淳花了三年時間研究檔案並走訪台灣、日本和俄羅斯，同時採訪了前戰俘及其家屬。
  • surviving adj. 倖存的，現存的
    Among those featured is 99-year-old Wu Cheng-nan (吳正男), believed to be the only surviving Taiwanese former POW who spent time in the Soviet Union.
    受訪者之一是 99 歲的吳正男，他被認為是唯一在世、曾待在蘇聯的台灣籍前戰俘。
  • fortunate adj. 幸運的
    Wu said he considers himself fortunate to have survived both the war and about two years of captivity.
    吳正男表示，他認為自己很幸運，能在戰爭和約兩年的監禁中存活下來。
  • transport v. 運輸，運送
    He was transported to a detention camp in Kazakhstan, where he was subjected to two years of forced labor.
    他被運送到哈薩克的拘留營，在那裡被迫服了兩年勞役。
  • settle v. 定居，安居
    He later completed high school and university in Japan and eventually settled there.
    他後來在日本完成高中和大學學業，並最終在那裡定居。
  • opportunity n. 機會
    The screening last Saturday was followed by a discussion with Hsu and Wu, who said the film was an opportunity to open a dialogue across generations about memories of war and what it means to be Taiwanese.
    上週六的放映會後，舉行了與許明淳和吳正男的座談會，吳正男表示，這部電影是一個機會，能夠開啟跨世代關於戰爭記憶以及身為台灣人意義的對話。
  • hardship n. 艱難，困苦
    Wu said, adding that despite the hardships he endured, he did not completely reject the experience because it ultimately became part of the life he was able to live.
    吳正男說，並補充說儘管他忍受了艱辛，但他並沒有完全排斥這段經歷，因為它最終成為他能夠過上的人生的一部分。
  • responsibility n. 責任
    Reflecting on Taiwan's complicated historical memories and questions surrounding identity, Hsu said his generation has a responsibility to share different aspects of Taiwan's history with others and with younger generations.
    反思台灣複雜的歷史記憶與身分認同問題時，許明淳表示，他這一代人有責任與其他人及年輕世代分享台灣歷史的不同面向。
  • encourage v. 鼓勵
    He hopes the film will encourage audiences to ask their parents and grandparents about their experiences, saying that without knowing their stories, it would be difficult to pass family history on to the next generation.
    他希望這部電影能鼓勵觀眾向父母和祖父母詢問他們的經歷，並表示如果不了解他們的故事，就很難將家族歷史傳給下一代。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作為後位修飾詞 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    A documentary exploring the little-known history of Taiwanese soldiers detained in remote Soviet prisoner-of-war camps after fighting for Japan in World War II was screened in Tokyo recently.
    句中的「detained in remote Soviet prisoner-of-war camps...」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「Taiwanese soldiers（台灣士兵）」，相當於省略了「who were detained...」。這種結構在多益閱讀測驗中極常見，能有效簡化句子。
  • 現在分詞片語表附帶結果或補充說明 (Present Participle Phrase indicating Result or Action)
    Hsu spent three years researching records and visiting Taiwan, Japan and Russia, while interviewing former prisoners and their families.
    句尾的「while interviewing former prisoners and their families」使用現在分詞「interviewing」，表示在進行上述動作（研究與造訪）的同時所進行的附帶動作，使語意更流暢。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    He was transported to a detention camp in Kazakhstan, where he was subjected to two years of forced labor.
    本句使用了兩個被動語態「was transported（被運送）」與「was subjected to（被迫承受／遭受）」。在商業及新聞英文中，當動作的承受者比發動者更重要時，經常使用被動語態。
  • 使役動詞或促使類動詞的句型 (Verb + Object + to-infinitive)
    He hopes the film will encourage audiences to ask their parents and grandparents about their experiences...
    「encourage」此處遵循「encourage + 受詞 + to V」的句型結構，意思是「鼓勵某人去做某事」。這是多益測驗中考點極高的動詞搭配（Verb Patterns）。
