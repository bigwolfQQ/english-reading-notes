學者：台灣加強國防更能向美國保證，而非嚇阻北京

Taiwan defense buildup may assure US more than deter Beijing: Scholar

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • preparations n. 準備，預備
    Taiwan's growing defense preparations may be sending a stronger signal to the United States than to Beijing...
    台灣不斷增長的國防準備可能正在向美國發出比向北京更強烈的信號……
  • assessment n. 評估，評價
    Oriana Skylar Mastro, a center fellow at Stanford University's Freeman Spogli Institute for International Studies, made the assessment during a Taipei Security Dialogue...
    史丹佛大學佛里曼·史波格國際問題研究所的中心研究員梅惠琳在台北安全對話期間做出了這項評估……
  • impact n. 衝擊，影響
    Economic impact of Taiwan Strait conflict could exceed WWII's: AIT chief
    台灣海峽衝突的經濟影響可能超過二戰：AIT處長
  • spending n. 開支，花費
    Mastro said she was impressed during a recent visit by Taiwan's increased defense spending...
    梅惠琳表示，她在最近的一次訪問中對台灣增加國防支出……印象深刻
  • strategy n. 策略，戰略
    She called that an appropriate strategy because it reassured Washington of Taiwan's determination to defend itself...
    她稱這是一個恰當的策略，因為它向華盛頓保證了台灣保衛自身的決心……
  • investments n. 投資
    Given this situation, Taiwan should communicate its newly acquired capabilities more effectively to Beijing so that its defense investments can both strengthen relations with Washington and deter China...
    她主張，鑑於這種情況，台灣應更有效地向北京傳達其新獲得的能力，以便其國防投資既能加強與華盛頓的關係，又能嚇阻中國。
  • significance n. 重要性，意義
    He also described U.S. arms sales to Taiwan as an important signal of Washington's commitment, arguing that their political significance could be more important than the size of individual packages.
    他也將美國對台軍售描述為華盛頓承諾的重要信號，並認為其政治意義可能比個別包裹的大小更重要。
  • consult v. 諮詢，商議
    That violated a key tenet of the 'Six Assurances' made to Taiwan by the Ronald Reagan administration in 1982 -- that the U.S. would not consult China on arms sales to Taiwan.
    那違反了隆·雷根政府於1982年向台灣做出的『六項保證』中的一項核心原則——即美國不會就對台軍售問題諮詢中國。
  • agreement n. 協議，協定
    'What am I going to say? I don't want to talk to you about it because I have an agreement that was signed in 1982?' he said after the meeting.
    『我要說什麼？我不想跟你談這個，因為我有個1982年簽署的協議？』他在會後表示。
  • functional adj. 起作用的，正常運作的
    Beyond military capabilities and signaling, Taiwan must prepare to remain functional if China seeks to isolate it...
    除了軍事能力和發出信號之外，如果中國企圖孤立台灣，台灣必須做好保持運作的準備……
  • suppliers n. 供應商
    Isolation would not necessarily require a formal blockade, Müller said, because heightened risk alone could alter the behavior of shipping companies, insurers, suppliers and maintenance providers.
    穆勒說，孤立不一定需要 formal 封鎖，因為單單是風險升高就可能改變航運公司、保險公司、供應商和維修服務商的行為。
  • operate v. 運作，營運
    Rather than pursuing complete self-sufficiency, Taiwan should measure how long essential services could continue operating if normal external supplies were interrupted...
    台灣不應追求完全的自給自足，而是應該評估如果正常的外部供應中斷，基本服務可以繼續運作多長時間……

文法重點

  • 現在分詞當形容詞（修飾名詞）
    Taiwan's growing defense preparations may be sending a stronger signal...
    句中的「growing」是現在分詞（由動詞 grow 變化而來），放在名詞 preparations 前面當作形容詞使用，用來修飾「不斷增長的」防衛準備。
  • 過去分詞片語當後置修飾語
    ...a Taipei Security Dialogue panel examining lessons Taiwan could draw from the wars in Ukraine and the Middle East.
    句尾的「examining...」是現在分詞片語，用來補充修飾前面的 panel（座談會小組），相當於一個形容詞子句「...panel that examined...」，簡化後直接用分詞片語來表達。
  • If 假設語氣（與現在或未來事實相反）
    If Washington were to begin discussing proposed arms sales packages for Taiwan with Beijing, it would send 'a very, very bad signal' to Taipei...
    此句使用假設語氣（If + 主詞 + were to + 動詞原形, 主詞 + would + 動詞原形），用來表達對未來可能性極低或假設性情境的臆測（如果華盛頓真的開始與北京討論……那將會發出……）。
  • 動名詞片語當主詞
    Recovery capacity is combat power.
    句首的「Recovery capacity」（恢復能力）是由名詞組合而成的概念，而在其他句子如「Isolating...」或「Pursuing...」中常見動名詞片語作為主詞，此處展示了名詞化的概念，簡潔有力地帶出核心結論。
