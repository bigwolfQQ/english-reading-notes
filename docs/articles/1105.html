台泥集團尋求收購烏克蘭水泥廠

TCC Group seeks purchase of Ukraine cement plant

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • acquire v. 收購、取得
    TCC Group Holdings Co’s (台泥企業) board of directors has approved a plan for the company’s Dutch subsidiary to acquire Ukrainian cement company Ivano-Frankivskcement (IFCEM) and three other companies for about 750 million euros (US$857 million).
    台泥企業集團董事會已批准一項計畫，由該公司的荷蘭子公司以約7.5億歐元（約8.57億美元）收購烏克蘭水泥公司Ivano-Frankivskcement（IFCEM）及其他三家公司。
  • subsidiary n. 子公司
    TCC Group Holdings Co’s (台泥企業) board of directors has approved a plan for the company’s Dutch subsidiary to acquire Ukrainian cement company Ivano-Frankivskcement (IFCEM) and three other companies for about 750 million euros (US$857 million).
    台泥企業集團董事會已批准一項計畫，由該公司的荷蘭子公司以約7.5億歐元（約8.57億美元）收購烏克蘭水泥公司Ivano-Frankivskcement（IFCEM）及其他三家公司。
  • transaction n. 交易
    The transaction includes roofing materials company Ivano-Frankivsk-Dakh, gypsum products maker KRU Gips and dry-mix mortar producer KRU Mix, TCC Group Holdings vice president Shelly Yeh (葉毓君) said at a news conference yesterday.
    台泥企業集團副總經理葉毓君在昨天的記者會上表示，該交易包含屋頂材料公司Ivano-Frankivsk-Dakh、石膏製品製造商KRU Gips及乾拌砂漿生產商KRU Mix。
  • finalize v. 使完成、定案
    The final acquisition price would be adjusted when the deal is finalized based on factors such as net debt and net working capital, Yeh said.
    葉毓君表示，最終的收購價格將在交易定案時，根據淨負債與淨營運資金等因素進行調整。
  • regulatory adj. 監管的、管理規則的
    The plan is still subject to regulatory approval in Taiwan, Ukraine and other jurisdictions before the transaction can be finalized, Yeh cautioned.
    葉毓君警告說，在交易完成之前，該計畫仍需獲得台灣、烏克蘭及其他司法管轄區的監管批准。
  • revenue n. 收益、營收
    According to IFCEM’s 2025 financial report, revenue rose 20.3 percent year-on-year to UAH16.25 billion (US$362.41 million), while gross profit climbed 40.3 percent to UAH6.44 billion.
    根據IFCEM的2025年財務報告，營收年增20.3%至162.5億烏克蘭格里夫納（約3.6241億美元），而毛利則攀升40.3%至64.4億烏克蘭格里夫納。
  • due diligence n. 盡職調查（商業併購前的審查）
    TCC said it has appointed Morgan Stanley as financial adviser for the transaction, while international legal, accounting, tax and environmental consultants have conducted due diligence covering sanctions, labor, operations and foreign exchange controls.
    台泥表示已委任摩根士丹利擔任該交易的財務顧問，同時國際法律、會計、稅務和環境顧問已進行了涵蓋制裁、勞動、營運和外匯管制的盡職調查。
  • reliance n. 依賴、依靠
    The plant has installed 50MW of solar power capacity and 19MW of gas-fired power generation capacity to reduce its reliance on the external grid, the company said.
    該公司表示，該工廠已安裝50MW的太陽能發電容量和19MW的燃氣發電容量，以減少對外部電網的依賴。

文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The final acquisition price would be adjusted when the deal is finalized based on factors such as net debt and net working capital, Yeh said.
    在此句中，「would be adjusted」與「is finalized」皆採用被動語態（be + 過去分詞），強調動作的承受者（價格、交易）而非動作的執行者，這在商業合約與新聞報導中非常常見。
  • 關係代名詞與介系詞片語 (Preposition + Relative Pronoun)
    IFCEM represents a strong vehicle through which to expand its European presence, with a roughly 36 percent market share in Ukraine...
    「through which」引導一個形容詞子句，用來修飾先行詞 a strong vehicle。介系詞 through 放在關係代名詞 which 之前，表示「透過這個載體」，結構嚴謹且正式。
  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況/結果 (Participial Phrase)
    Gross margin surged to 39.6 percent, up from 34 percent in 2024, while net income increased 44.5 percent.
    句中的「up from 34 percent in 2024」是省略了主詞與be動詞的形容詞片語（原形為 which was up from...），用來補充說明毛利率上升後的具體對比數據。
  • 過去分詞作後位修飾 (Past Participle as Postmodifier)
    Severely damaged housing, transportation and energy infrastructure are the most urgent needs...
    過去分詞「damaged」置於名詞 housing 之前作形容詞用，修飾受損的狀態；此外，在第8段「the acquired businesses」中，acquired 也是過去分詞用來修飾 businesses（被收購的企業）。
