文法重點

  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    British ice dance greats Jayne Torvill and Christopher Dean returned to the rink where their remarkable journey began...
    句中的「where their remarkable journey began」是關係副詞 where 引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的先行詞「the rink（溜冰場）」，說明這座溜冰場是他們非凡旅程開始的地方。
  • 分詞構句修飾名詞 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Synchro9, which features nine-skater teams rather than the traditional synchronized skating teams of 16, is to make its debut at the Alpes 2030 Olympics and would also feature at the 2028 Youth Winter Olympics.
    句中使用「which features...」引導的非限定關係代名詞子句來補充說明主詞 Synchro9 的特點。在英文新聞英語中，常用這種結構來補充附加資訊，使句子更緊湊。
  • 對等連接詞與對稱結構 (Correlative structure with the former / the latter)
    Torvill and Dean, who initially combined skating with their day jobs — the former as an insurance clerk and the latter as a police officer — famously won Olympic gold in Sarajevo in 1984...
    句中的「the former... and the latter...」是用來指代前面提過的兩個人（the former 指 Torvill，the latter 指 Dean），表示「前者...而後者...」，常用於句中補充說明各自的身分，避免重複姓名。
