台灣北部部分地區發布大雨特報：氣象署

Parts of northern Taiwan under heavy rain advisories: CWA

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • issue v. 發布、發行
    The Central Weather Administration (CWA) on Saturday issued heavy rain advisories for Taipei and New Taipei, and announced an extremely heavy rain advisory for Yilan County.
    中央氣象署（CWA）於週六針對台北市和新北市發布大雨特報，並針對宜蘭縣發布豪雨特報。
  • announce v. 宣布、公告
    The Central Weather Administration (CWA) on Saturday issued heavy rain advisories for Taipei and New Taipei, and announced an extremely heavy rain advisory for Yilan County.
    中央氣象署（CWA）於週六針對台北市和新北市發布大雨特報，並針對宜蘭縣發布豪雨特報。
  • possibility n. 可能性
    Under the yellow color-coded heavy rain advisory, the CWA warned Taipei and New Taipei of the possibility of more than 80 millimeters of rainfall within 24 hours, or more than 40 mm within one hour.
    在黃色燈號的大雨特報下，氣象署警告台北和新北有24小時降雨量超過80毫米，或1小時降雨量超過40毫米的可能性。
  • exceed v. 超過、超出
    As for Yilan, the higher-tier orange warning indicated that the accumulated rainfall could exceed 200 millimeters within 24 hours or 100 mm within three hours.
    至於宜蘭，較高等級的橙色警戒表明，累積降雨量可能在24小時內超過200毫米，或在3小時內超過100毫米。
  • explain v. 解釋、說明
    The administration explained that the heavy rainfall forecasted for the northern Taiwan locations is due to the effects of a lingering northeasterly wind, which could bring short bursts of heavy precipitation.
    氣象署解釋，預報台灣北部地區出現強降雨，是由於東北風持續影響，可能帶來短時間內的強降雨。
  • forecast v. / n. 預報、預測
    The administration explained that the heavy rainfall forecasted for the northern Taiwan locations is due to the effects of a lingering northeasterly wind, which could bring short bursts of heavy precipitation.
    氣象署解釋，預報台灣北部地區出現強降雨，是由於東北風持續影響，可能帶來短時間內的強降雨。
  • experience v. 經歷、體驗
    Wu said the western half of the island will experience cloudy weather, but mountainous locations could see localized rain on Saturday afternoon.
    吳德榮表示，西半部地區將經歷多雲天氣，但山區在週六下午可能會出現局部降雨。
  • gradually adv. 逐漸地
    Wu said temperatures will gradually climb over the next few days, bringing hot weather in the morning in the west.
    吳德榮表示，未來幾天氣溫將逐漸回升，西部早晚天氣炎熱。
  • reach v. 達到、到達
    He forecasted that highs in northern Taiwan could reach as high as 33 degrees Celsius, while central and southern regions could see highs of 36 degrees.
    他預測，北部地區高溫可達攝氏33度，而中南部地區高溫可達36度。
  • predict v. 預測
    Wu further predicted the weather for next Thursday and Friday, stating that with the weakening of northeasterly winds, the western half of the nation will see cloudy skies while eastern Taiwan could experience sporadic and localized precipitation.
    吳德榮進一步預測下週四和週五的天氣，指出隨著東北風減弱，全台西半部將為多雲天氣，而台灣東部可能出現零星局部降雨。

文法重點

  • 分詞作後置修飾語（分詞片語）
    The administration explained that the heavy rainfall forecasted for the northern Taiwan locations is due to the effects of a lingering northeasterly wind...
    句中的 "forecasted for the northern Taiwan locations" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "the heavy rainfall"。這相當於形容詞子句 "that is forecasted..." 的簡化，能讓句子更為精煉。
  • 關係代名詞非限定用法（which 引導的子句）
    ...due to the effects of a lingering northeasterly wind, which could bring short bursts of heavy precipitation.
    句中的 "which" 前面帶有逗號，屬於非限定用法的關係代名詞，用來補充說明前面的先行詞 "a lingering northeasterly wind"，並為整個句子增加額外的資訊。
  • 現在分詞作形容詞（表持續或狀態）
    Wu said that with the weakening of northeasterly winds, the western half of the nation will see cloudy skies...
    句中的 "weakening" 是動名詞或由現在分詞轉化的名詞片語核心（the weakening of...），用來表達東北風「正在減弱」的狀態或過程。
  • 對等連接詞連接對稱結構（while 表對比）
    ...the western half of the nation will see cloudy skies while eastern Taiwan could experience sporadic and localized precipitation.
    句中的 "while" 在此處作為對等連接詞，用來連接兩個子句，表示前後兩者在同一時間發生的對比情況（西半部多雲，而東半部有零星降雨）。
