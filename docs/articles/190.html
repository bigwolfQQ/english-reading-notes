建構後美國時代的加拿大經濟

Building Canada’s post-US economy

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • substantial adj. 重大的，大量的，實質的
    US President Donald Trump first hit Canada with substantial tariffs in 2018, targeting aluminum and steel.
    美國總統唐納·川普在 2018 年首次對加拿大實施大幅關稅，目標指向鋁和鋼。
  • exempt v. 豁免，免除
    He did so again after returning to office last year, targeting dozens of goods, although he later exempted those compliant with the US-Mexico-Canada Agreement.
    他去年重返白宮後再次採取此舉，將數十種商品納入目標，儘管他後來豁免了符合《美墨加協定》的商品。
  • import n. 進口商品，進口
    Last week, after trade talks broke down, he repeated his favorite trick, imposing 50 percent tariffs on a vast array of Canadian imports, including wine and mittens.
    上週，在貿易談判破裂後，他重施故技，對包括葡萄酒和連指手套在內的大量加拿大進口商品徵收 50% 的關稅。
  • retaliatory adj. 報復性的
    Canada responded with “dollar-for-dollar” retaliatory tariffs.
    加拿大則以「等值」的報復性關稅作出回應。
  • revival n. 復甦，重興
    Trump’s tariffs have come nowhere close to delivering a US manufacturing revival.
    川普的關稅距離實現美國製造業復甦可說相去甚遠。
  • export n. 出口，出口商品
    Its merchandise exports to the US account for one-fifth of its GDP, and nowhere is this dependence more acute than in the auto industry.
    其對美商品出口占了 GDP 的五分之一，而這種依賴性在汽車產業尤為嚴重。
  • supply chain n. 供應鏈
    Given that automobile manufacturing depends on tightly integrated regional supply chains across North America, Europe and Asia, it is difficult to imagine a viable Canadian auto industry without ready access to the Great Lakes-centered manufacturing ecosystem.
    鑑於汽車 manufacturing 依賴北美、歐洲和亞洲各地緊密整合的區域供應鏈，很難想像沒有便捷管道連接以五大湖為中心的製造生態系，加拿大的汽車產業還能如何生存。
  • infrastructure n. 基礎設施
    Investments in new and expanded mines, and in the infrastructure needed to export their output, are also laudable.
    對新建和擴建礦山以及出口其產出所需基礎設施的投資也值得稱讚。
  • robust adj. 強健的，興旺的，強勁的
    Global demand for Canada’s resources is robust, and, crucially, the country does not need access to American supply chains to extract them.
    全球對加拿大資源的需求強勁，而且至關重要的是，該國並不需要依賴美國的供應鏈來開採這些資源。
  • expenditure n. 支出，開支
    In the world’s leading tech economies, by contrast, large firms account for the lion’s share of business R&D expenditures.
    相比之下，在世界領先的科技經濟體中，大型企業占了企業研發支出的絕大部分。
  • constraint n. 限制，約束
    The core constraint is therefore not a systemic inadequacy.
    因此，核心限制並非系統性不足。
  • subsidy n. 補助金，津貼（文中對應 grant/funding 相關概念）
    Canada provides substantial R&D funding through tax credits, but the program, though recently reformed, remains biased toward small firms.
    加拿大透過稅收抵免提供大量的研發資金，但該計畫雖然最近進行了改革，依然偏袒小企業。
  • procurement n. 採購，採購作業
    A more ambitious approach to procurement could foster innovation.
    更具企圖心的採購方法可以培育創新。
  • bankruptcy n. 破產
    For a Canadian pharmaceutical company developing a new drug, such delays can make the difference between profitability and bankruptcy.
    對於開發新藥的加拿大製藥公司而言，這種延誤可能會造成獲利與破產的天壤之別。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    He did so again after returning to office last year, targeting dozens of goods, although he later exempted those compliant with the US-Mexico-Canada Agreement.
    句中的「targeting dozens of goods」是由現在分詞 targeting 引導的分詞片語，用來補充說明主句動作發生時同時伴隨的狀況或結果，相當於附加子句「and he targeted dozens of goods」。這種結構在商業及新聞英文中常用以簡潔地描述伴隨發生的行動。
  • 否定詞片語置於句首的倒裝句 (Negative Adverbial Fronting / Inversion)
    Its merchandise exports to the US account for one-fifth of its GDP, and nowhere is this dependence more acute than in the auto industry.
    當表示否定或強烈語氣的副詞地方片語「nowhere」被擺在句首以加強語氣時，後方的子句必須進行主動詞倒裝（將be動詞 is 移至主語 this dependence 之前），形成「Nowhere is this dependence more acute...」，用來強調「在其他任何地方都沒有比汽車產業更嚴重的依賴性」。
  • 假設語氣（與過去事實相反）的應用 (Subjunctive Mood)
    ...it is difficult to imagine a viable Canadian auto industry without ready access to the Great Lakes-centered manufacturing ecosystem.
    句中的「without ready access to...」隱含了條件句（即 If there were not ready access to...）的假設語氣概念，用介系詞片語精簡地表達「若沒有……的情況下（事實上可能很難獲得）」，表達出若缺乏該生態系，加拿大汽車產業將無法存活的假設情境。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Independent Participial Construction)
    Global demand for Canada’s resources is robust, and, crucially, the country does not need access to American supply chains to extract them.
    句中插入了副詞「crucially」，用以修飾整句話的態度。此外，不定詞片語「to extract them」放在句尾作目的副詞，用以說明不需要美國供應鏈的「目的」是為了「開採這些資源」，這類結構能使長句的邏輯層次更清晰。
