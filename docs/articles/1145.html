孔子紀念儀式

Confucian commemoration

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • perform v. 舉行，表演，執行
    People perform a traditional ritual celebrating Confucius’ birthday, also Teacher’s day, at the Confucius Temple in Taichung yesterday.
    人們昨天在台中孔廟舉行傳統儀式，慶祝至聖先師孔子的誕辰，也就是教師節。
  • traditional adj. 傳統的
    People perform a traditional ritual celebrating Confucius’ birthday, also Teacher’s day, at the Confucius Temple in Taichung yesterday.
    人們昨天在台中孔廟舉行傳統儀式，慶祝至聖先師孔子的誕辰，也就是教師節。
  • ritual n. 儀式
    People perform a traditional ritual celebrating Confucius’ birthday, also Teacher’s day, at the Confucius Temple in Taichung yesterday.
    人們昨天在台中孔廟舉行傳統儀式，慶祝至聖先師孔子的誕辰，也就是教師節。
  • celebrate v. 慶祝
    People perform a traditional ritual celebrating Confucius’ birthday, also Teacher’s day, at the Confucius Temple in Taichung yesterday.
    人們昨天在台中孔廟舉行傳統儀式，慶祝至聖先師孔子的誕辰，也就是教師節。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾
    People perform a traditional ritual celebrating Confucius’ birthday...
    句中的 "celebrating Confucius’ birthday" 為現在分詞片語，置於名詞 "ritual" 後方，用來補充說明該儀式的內容，相當於形容詞子句 "which celebrates Confucius' birthday" 的簡化。
  • 同位語用法
    ...celebrating Confucius’ birthday, also Teacher’s day, at the Confucius Temple...
    句中的 "Teacher’s day" 作為前一個名詞短語 "Confucius' birthday" 的同位語，用來補充說明孔子誕辰同時也是教師節，兩者指的是同一個節日。
