台鐵列車過站不停致歉

Taiwan Railway apologizes after train blows past station

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • apologize v. 道歉
    Taiwan Railway Corp yesterday apologized after a northbound Puyuma Express train failed to stop at Jiaoxi Station in Yilan
    台灣鐵路公司昨天致歉，起因是一列北上普悠瑪自強號列車在宜蘭礁溪車站未依規定停靠
  • fail to phr. 未能……；未達成……
    a northbound Puyuma Express train failed to stop at Jiaoxi Station in Yilan
    一列北上普悠瑪自強號列車在宜蘭礁溪車站未依規定停靠
  • alert n. 警報；警戒；通知
    Taiwan Railway employees at Jiaoxi Station sent out an emergency alert after the No. 219 Puyuma Express train from Hualien to Shulin passed the station
    礁溪站的台鐵員工在花蓮開往樹林的第219次普悠瑪列車通過車站後，發出了一則緊急警報
  • scheduled adj. 預定的；排定時間的
    passed the station without making its scheduled stop at 1:33pm
    在下午1時33分通過車站卻未做預定停靠
  • notification n. 通知；告示
    After receiving the notification, a Taiwan Railway dispatcher ordered the train’s driver to reverse back to Jiaoxi Station
    在接獲通知後，台鐵調度員下令列車駕駛員倒車返回礁溪車站
  • dispatcher n. 調度員；派車員
    a Taiwan Railway dispatcher ordered the train’s driver to reverse back to Jiaoxi Station to let passengers board or disembark
    台鐵調度員下令列車駕駛員倒車返回礁溪車站，讓旅客上車或下車
  • board v. 登機；登船；上車
    to let passengers board or disembark
    讓旅客上車或下車
  • disembark v. 下車；下船；下機
    to let passengers board or disembark
    讓旅客上車或下車
  • incident n. 事件；事故
    Taiwan Railway expressed its “sincere apologies” for the incident
    台鐵對這起事件表達「誠摯的歉意」
  • affect v. 影響
    which it said affected 64 travelers and caused a 21-minute delay to their trip
    並表示該事件影響了64名旅客，並造成他們的行程延誤21分鐘
  • delay n. 延誤；延遲
    caused a 21-minute delay to their trip
    造成他們的行程延誤21分鐘
  • disciplinary action phr. 懲戒處分；紀律處分
    whether they would face any disciplinary action
    是否會面臨任何懲處

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    Taiwan Railway Corp yesterday apologized after a northbound Puyuma Express train failed to stop at Jiaoxi Station in Yilan, forcing it to reverse back to Jiaoxi...
    句中的「forcing it to reverse...」是由現在分詞片語引導的結構，用來補充說明前面整起事件所導致的結果（迫使列車倒車），相當於對等句型「and forced it to reverse...」，在新聞英語中非常常見。
  • without + 動名詞表示否定附帶狀況 (Without + Gerund)
    ...passed the station without making its scheduled stop at 1:33pm, the company said in a statement.
    「without」為介系詞，後面接動名詞「making」形成複合片語，用來表達「在沒有做某事的情況下」發生了前面的動作，在此處用來描述列車「未做預定停靠」就通過了車站。
  • 使役與命令動詞的句型結構 (Order sb. to do sth.)
    After receiving the notification, a Taiwan Railway dispatcher ordered the train’s driver to reverse back to Jiaoxi Station...
    動詞「order（命令、下令）」在此處採用「order + 受詞 + to do sth.（命令某人做某事）」的句型，用來清楚交代調度員對司機員發出的指令內容，多益考試中這類動詞（如 require, urge, ask）的受詞補語常考不定詞（to V）。
  • 名詞子句作受詞 (Noun Clause as Object)
    Taiwan Railway has not said what caused the train’s driver to miss the station or whether they would face any disciplinary action.
    句中的「what caused...」與「whether they would...」是由疑問詞（what）與對等連接詞（whether）所引導的名詞子句，在句中擔任動詞「said」的受詞，表達台鐵尚未說明的事情內容。
