台北市的轉變

The transformation of Taipei City

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • foundations n. 基礎、地基（常用複數）
    Taipei Prefectural Government and Taipei City Hall — today the Presidential Office, Control Yuan and Executive Yuan respectively — along with other colonial institutions laid the foundations for the city’s development into Taiwan’s premier urban area.
    台北州廳與台北市役所——即當今的總統府、監察院和行政院——連同其他殖民機構，為該市發展成為台灣首要都會區奠定了基礎。
  • premier adj. 首要的、第一的
    Taipei Prefectural Government and Taipei City Hall — today the Presidential Office, Control Yuan and Executive Yuan respectively — along with other colonial institutions laid the foundations for the city’s development into Taiwan’s premier urban area.
    台北州廳與台北市役所——即當今的總統府、監察院和行政院——連同其他殖民機構，為該市發展成為台灣首要都會區奠定了基礎。
  • relocated v. 遷移、重新安置
    After the Republic of China (ROC) fled from China — or, as some would put it, relocated to Taiwan and occupied it — Taipei became its capital.
    在中華民國從中國撤退（或者用某些人的說法，是流亡至台灣並佔領了台灣）之後，台北成為了其首都。
  • upgraded v. 升級、提升
    It was insisted that then-Taipei County be upgraded to a special municipality named New Taipei City, ignoring the reality that the area had consisted of several medium-sized cities surrounding Taipei.
    當時堅持將原台北縣升格為名為新北市的直轄市，卻忽略了該地區是由環繞台北的數個中型城市組成的現實。
  • municipal adj. 市的、市立的、市政的
    New Taipei City emerged as an awkward administrative configuration, a product of a local government rushing to secure municipal upgrades.
    新北市成為了一個尷尬的行政配置，是地方政府急於爭取升格直轄市的產物。
  • victory n. 勝利
    The split vote led to the victory of Chen Shui-bian (陳水扁) of the Democratic Progressive Party (DPP).
    分裂的選票導致民主進步黨的陳水扁獲勝。
  • administration n. 管理、行政、政府執政團隊
    His administration’s approval ratings exceeded 70 percent, yet was defeated by the KMT’s Ma Ying-jeou (馬英九) when he sought re-election 1998.
    他的政府施政滿意度曾超過70%，但在1998年尋求連任時被國民黨的馬英九擊敗。
  • approval ratings n. phr. 支持率、滿意度
    His administration’s approval ratings exceeded 70 percent, yet was defeated by the KMT’s Ma Ying-jeou (馬英九) when he sought re-election 1998.
    他的政府施政滿意度曾超過70%，但在1998年尋求連任時被國民黨的馬英九擊敗。
  • defeated v. 擊敗、戰勝
    His administration’s approval ratings exceeded 70 percent, yet was defeated by the KMT’s Ma Ying-jeou (馬英九) when he sought re-election 1998.
    他的政府施政滿意度曾超過70%，但在1998年尋求連任時被國民黨的馬英九擊敗。
  • re-election n. 競選連任
    His administration’s approval ratings exceeded 70 percent, yet was defeated by the KMT’s Ma Ying-jeou (馬英九) when he sought re-election 1998.
    他的政府施政滿意度曾超過70%，但在1998年尋求連任時被國民黨的馬英九擊敗。
  • commercial adj. 商業的（對應文章中的 merchants, business 等概念延伸）
    The second comes from the community of exiled government officials, merchants and businessmen who arrived in Taiwan in 1949...
    第二種來自於1949年抵達台灣的流亡官員、商人和企業家社群...
  • incumbent adj. 現任的
    Whether his surname is Chang or Chiang, the incumbent mayor has certainly leveraged the family name of the former presidents to his advantage.
    無論他的姓氏是章還是蔣，這位現任市長顯然都利用了前總統的家族姓氏來為自己謀取利益。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作補充說明 (Participial Phrase)
    During the Japanese colonial period, Taipei was the seat of Taihoku Prefecture, “taihoku” being the Japanese transliteration of “Taipei.”
    句尾的 “, “taihoku” being...” 是獨立分詞構句（Absolute Phrase），用來補充說明前面的 Taihoku Prefecture 是什麼。這裡沒有用連接詞，而是直接用主詞加上現在分詞 (being) 來連接額外資訊。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    At that point, the mayorship was no longer an elected office, it was appointed by the party.
  • 現在分詞當形容詞修飾 (Present Participle as Modifier)
    My answer would be that this is Chinese Taipei, haunted by the ghost of a consciousness shaped by exile and colonialism.
  • 名詞子句作主詞補語 (Noun Clause as Subject Complement)
    What does this tell us about the civic identity of the people of “Chinese” Taipei?
