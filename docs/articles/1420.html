Lionel Messi 從國家隊退役

Leo Messi retires from national team

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • farewell n. 告別，道別
    Lionel Messi on Tuesday brought the curtain down on his 21-year international career, bidding farewell to adoring Argentina fans in an emotional final appearance for the former world champions in Buenos Aires.
    阿根廷球星 Lionel Messi 於週二為其長達 21 年的國際賽生涯畫上句點，在布宜諾斯艾利斯這場對前世界冠軍的情感豐沛的最後亮相中，向熱愛他的阿根廷球迷道別。
  • victory n. 勝利
    In a perfectly scripted finale, the 39-year-old widely regarded as the greatest soccer player in history signed off with his 126th international goal in front of 85,000 supporters as Argentina cruised to a 3-0 victory over Benin.
    在一場完美編排的劇本尾聲中，這位被普遍認為是足球史上最偉大球員的 39 歲球星，在 85,000 名支持者的面前，以他的第 126 顆國際賽進球為自己完美收官，助阿根廷以 3 比 0 輕取貝南。
  • assist n. (體育) 助攻；協助
    Messi, playing in his record-extending 208th international, also provided the assist for the opening goal scored by veteran Nicolas Otamendi, with Nico Paz also scoring.
    Messi 先是助攻資深隊友 Nicolas Otamendi 攻入首開紀錄的一球，隨後 Nico Paz 也有一球進帳，這場比賽是 Messi 第 208 場出賽，再度刷新由他保持的出場紀錄。
  • campaign n. (政治或商業) 活動、戰役、競選
    Messi though said he had no regrets about his decision to retire, citing Argentina’s grueling FIFA World Cup campaign, which took place as his late father was seriously ill, as a factor.
    不過 Messi 表示，他對自己退役的決定沒有遺憾，並提到阿根廷艱辛的國際足總世界盃征程是一個因素，當時正值他已故的父親重病。
  • factor n. 因素，要素
    Messi though said he had no regrets about his decision to retire, citing Argentina’s grueling FIFA World Cup campaign, which took place as his late father was seriously ill, as a factor.
    不過 Messi 表示，他對自己退役的決定沒有遺憾，並提到阿根廷艱辛的國際足總世界盃征程是一個因素，當時正值他已故的父親重病。
  • halt v. 停止，暫停
    The match was halted in the 10th minute — in tribute to Messi’s famous shirt number — for a minute-long ovation to the former Barcelona and Paris Saint-Germain star.
    比賽在第 10 分鐘暫停——向 Messi 著名的球衣號碼致敬——全場為這位前巴塞隆納和巴黎聖日耳曼星進行了一分鐘的起立鼓掌。
  • defeat n. 失敗，擊敗
    Messi’s farewell came two-and-a-half months after Argentina’s World Cup final defeat to Spain in New Jersey, where Messi exited in tears after making little impression in an ill-tempered 1-0 loss.
    Messi 的告別發生在阿根廷在紐澤西世界盃決賽以 0 比 1 敗給西班牙的兩個半月後，當時 Messi 在一場充滿火氣的敗仗中表現平平，賽後含淚離場。
  • contract n. 合約，契約
    Messi remains under contract with Inter Miami until 2028, although following the death of his father, Jorge, two months ago, he revealed he might hang up his boots for good sooner than anticipated.
    Messi 與國際邁阿密的合約將持續到 2028 年，儘管他的父親喬治兩個月前去世後，他透露自己可能會比預期更早正式掛靴。
  • anticipate v. 預料，預期
    Messi remains under contract with Inter Miami until 2028, although following the death of his father, Jorge, two months ago, he revealed he might hang up his boots for good sooner than anticipated.

  • triumph n. 重大勝利，成就
    Lionel Messi’s departure comes four years after his greatest triumph in international soccer, leading Argentina to victory at the 2022 World Cup to end the country’s 36-year wait for a third title, cementing his status as Diego Maradona’s heir.
    Lionel Messi 的離去距離他在國際足壇取得的最大輝煌——率領阿根廷贏得 2022 年世界盃，結束該國對第三座冠軍長達 36 年的等待，並鞏固了他作為迭戈·马拉多纳傳承者的地位——已經過了四年。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 / 形容詞用
    Lionel Messi on Tuesday brought the curtain down on his 21-year international career, bidding farewell to adoring Argentina fans in an emotional final appearance for the former world champions in Buenos Aires.
    句中的「bidding farewell...」是由現在分詞開頭的片語，用來補充說明主句主語 Lionel Messi 在結束生涯時同時正在做出的動作（或伴隨狀況），相當於副詞子句「while he bid farewell...」的簡化。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    In a perfectly scripted finale, the 39-year-old widely regarded as the greatest soccer player in history signed off with his 126th international goal...
    句中的「widely regarded as...」是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞「the 39-year-old」，相當於一個省略了關代與be動詞的形容詞子句「who is widely regarded as...」，用來補充說明該球員被外界公認的地位。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Absolute Phrase)
    Messi, playing in his record-extending 208th international, also provided the assist for the opening goal scored by veteran Nicolas Otamendi, with Nico Paz also scoring.
    句尾的「with Nico Paz also scoring」是「with + 名詞 + 現在分詞」的結構，在多益閱讀中常見，用來表示伴隨發生的狀況或背景，中文常譯為「同時……」、「在……的情況下」。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (限定修飾)
    Beforehand, he wiped away tears as the bus carrying him and his Argentina teammates made its way through crowds of well-wishers en route to the stadium.
    句中的「carrying him and his Argentina teammates」是現在分詞片語作後位修飾，用來修飾前面的「the bus」。其本質相當於限制性關係代名詞子句「that/which carried him...」，用來精確指出是哪一輛巴士。
