Taipei Times · 2026-10-07

疾管署公布2026年首例本土傷寒病例

CDC announces the first local typhoid case of 2026

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • decline v. 下降，減少
    adding that although cases of flu-like illnesses, including COVID-19, declined slightly last week, the case numbers and deaths were still high.
    並補充表示，上週包括COVID-19在內的類流感疾病病例雖然略有下降，但病例數和死亡人數仍然偏高。
  • relieve v. 緩解，解除（疼痛、症狀等）
    She sought treatment and took medicine to relieve her symptoms, but the fever, as well as headaches and general soreness developed early last month, Lin said.
    林詠青說，她曾就醫並服藥以緩解症狀，但上個月初又出現發燒以及頭痛和全身痠痛等情形。
  • hospitalize v. 使住院
    The woman sought treatment again and was hospitalized, he said, adding that a blood culture test confirmed that she had contracted typhoid fever.
    他說，該名女子再次就醫並住院治療，並補充說血液培養檢測證實她感染了傷寒。
  • recover v. 康復，復原
    The woman recovered after about a week in hospital, he said, adding that she reported having eaten raw seafood — including sashimi and raw oysters — during the incubation period.
    他說，該女子在醫院住院大約一週後康復，並補充說她自述在潛伏期曾食用生海鮮——包括生魚片和生蠔。
  • fatigue n. 疲勞，疲倦
    Common symptoms include persistent fever, headache, fatigue, loss of appetite, abdominal pain, diarrhea, relative bradycardia, and lower chest and abdomen rashes in some cases, he said.
    他說，常見症狀包括持續性發燒、頭痛、疲勞、食慾不振、腹痛、腹瀉、相對脈搏減慢，且某些病例會出現下胸部和腹部皮疹。
  • misdiagnose v. 誤診
    As early symptoms of the disease are highly non-specific, it can often be misdiagnosed as gastroenteritis or other diseases, he added.
    他補充說，由於該疾病的早期症狀高度非特異性，因此經常被誤診為腸胃炎或其他疾病。
  • worsen v. 惡化
    He was hospitalized when his symptoms worsened, including difficulty breathing, and he died 10 days later, Lin said.
    林詠青表示，當他的症狀惡化（包括呼吸困難）時他才住院，並在10天後死亡。
  • urge v. 敦促，力勸
    The CDC urged people with higher risk of developing severe illness or death from flu or COVID-19 to get vaccinated and to seek medical attention when they develop suspected symptoms.
    疾管署敦促感染流感或COVID-19後發展成重症或死亡風險較高的民眾應接種疫苗，並在出現疑似症狀時就醫。
  • fluctuate v. 波動，起伏
    CDC Deputy Director-General Lin Min-cheng (林明誠) said flu activity is expected to slightly fluctuate throughout this month, with the epidemic period possibly ending next month.
    疾管署副署長林明誠表示，預期本月流感活動將略有波動，流行期可能於下個月結束。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Present Participle）作附加補充說明
    The Centers for Disease Control (CDC) yesterday announced this year’s first locally acquired case of typhoid fever, adding that although cases of flu-like illnesses... declined slightly last week, the case numbers and deaths were still high.
    句尾的「adding that...」是由分詞片語構成，用來補充說明主句動作發生時的同時或隨後狀況，意指「並補充說...」，在新聞英語中非常常見，能有效串接引言與額外資訊。
  • 被動語態（Passive Voice）
    The woman sought treatment again and was hospitalized, he said, adding that a blood culture test confirmed that she had contracted typhoid fever.
    句中的「was hospitalized」使用了被動語態（be + 過去分詞），強調主詞（the woman）是接受住院治療這個動作的承受者，而非主動執行者。
  • 複合關係代間質（複合關係代名詞 WH- 家族引導名詞子句）
    The CDC urged people with higher risk of developing severe illness or death from flu or COVID-19 to get vaccinated and to seek medical attention when they develop suspected symptoms.
    句中的「when they develop suspected symptoms」是由連接詞「when」引導的時間副詞子句，用來指明「當他們出現疑似症狀時」應該採取行動，使句子語意更完整。
