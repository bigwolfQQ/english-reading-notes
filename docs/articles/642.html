台灣中部筆記：《華爾街日報》對台灣的盲點

Notes from Central Taiwan: The ‘Wall Street Journal’s’ Taiwan blind spot

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • indignation n. 憤慨，義憤
    The laughter and indignation that essay generated among Taiwan aficionados is an indicator of how far Western journalism on Taiwan has come.
    該篇文章在台灣同好之間引發的笑聲與憤慨，正是西方對台新聞報導進展了多少的指標。
  • outlier n. 局外人，異常值，離群者
    Today, it is an obvious outlier.
    而今天，它顯然是一個異類。
  • annex v. 吞併，併吞
    To claim so is an attempt, in prose, to annex Taiwan to China...
    作此聲稱，是在文字上企圖將台灣併吞給中國...
  • trope n. 修辭，老生常談，刻板印象
    ...appealing to a “preserver” trope that frames Taiwan as a “preserver” of “Chinese tradition”...
    ...迎合了一種「維護者」的刻板印象，將台灣框定為「中國傳統」的「維護者」...
  • resonate v. 產生共鳴，迴響
    In reality, temples carry local social meanings that resonate with the lived experience of locals...
    實際上，寺廟承載著與當地人生活經驗產生共鳴的在地社會意義...
  • interplay n. 相互作用，互動
    A large, rich body of scholarship on the interplay of religion, democracy and identity tells this story...
    大量關於宗教、民主和認同互動的豐富學術研究講述了這個故事...
  • comprehensively adv. 全面地，徹底地
    We have over 200 years of Manchu rule of the western settler part of Taiwan to comprehensively disprove that.
    我們有超過200年滿清統治台灣西部移民地區的歷史，足以徹底駁斥這一點。
  • omit v. 省略，刪除，遺漏
    Because the piece is China-centered, it simply omits things that are not Han Chinese, as if they never existed.
    由於該篇文章以中國為中心，它簡單地省略了非漢人的事物，彷彿牠們從未存在過。
  • interregnum n. 空位期，政權中斷期
    In the real world, it is more like a four-decade interregnum.
    在現實世界中，它更像是一個長達四十年的空位期。
  • convergence n. 收斂，趨同，匯聚
    The lack of convergence between religion in Taiwan and PRC religion since 1987, despite a wealth of exchanges, should have given Wei much food for thought.
    自1987年以來，儘管有豐富的交流，台灣宗教與中國大陸宗教之間卻缺乏趨同，這本該給魏提供許多反思的空間。
  • vector n. 載體，向量，傳播媒介
    Another element gone rogue from Wei’s presentation is the PRC’s use of religion as a vector of influence in Taiwan.
    魏的呈現中走樣的另一個元素，是中國大陸利用宗教作為對台影響力的載體。
  • infiltrate v. 滲透，潛入
    Scholarly and pundit commentary on the use of Mazu worship by the PRC to infiltrate and influence Taiwan is entirely missing...
    學界與名嘴對中國大陸利用媽祖信仰滲透和影響台灣的評論完全付之闕如...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞當後置修飾語（Present Participle as Post-modifier）
    Scholarly and pundit commentary on the use of Mazu worship by the PRC to infiltrate and influence Taiwan is entirely missing...
    句中的「commentary on the use of Mazu worship by the PRC to infiltrate and influence Taiwan」為主詞，其中「to infiltrate and influence Taiwan」是不定式片語，用來修飾前面的名詞，表達目的。而整句運用了現在分詞或形容詞「missing」來作主詞補語，表達評論的缺失。
  • 假設語氣（Subjunctive Mood）
    Assuming they were under the same government, would the lived and practiced religious experience be the same?
    句型使用「假設...（Assuming they were...）」帶出與現實相反的假設狀況，主句則使用「would + 動詞原形」來推論假設情況下的結果。
  • 地方副詞置於句首的倒裝句（Inversion with Fronted Adverbial）
    Missing too are the rich exchanges between religious groups in the People’s Republic of China (PRC) and Taiwan since the end of martial law...
    原句為了強調並與前文語境緊密連接，將形容詞/補語「Missing」置於句首，帶動主詞（the rich exchanges...）與動詞（are）進行倒裝，使句子重心更強烈。
  • 關係代名詞子句（Relative Clause）
    The rich, critical local history of Longshan Temple instead resolves as pious images of people praying.
    關係代名詞子句用來補充說明前面的名詞，使讀者對該名詞有更具體、更詳細的理解，是英文新聞中用來擴充資訊的高頻句構。
