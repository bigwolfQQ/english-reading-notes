台灣在人才推動計畫中，已使400名外國專業人才歸化

Taiwan naturalizes 400 foreign professionals amid talent drive

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • professional n. 專業人才，專家
    A total of 400 foreign “high- level” professionals have been naturalized as Republic of China (Taiwan) citizens over the past decade
    過去十年來，已有總計400名外國「高階」專業人才歸化為中華民國（台灣）公民
  • naturalize v. 使歸化（為公民）
    Over the past 10 years, 170 of the high-level professionals naturalized as ROC citizens were from other Asian countries
    在過去10年中，歸化為中華民國公民的高階專業人才中，有170人來自其他亞洲國家
  • attract v. 吸引
    the government has eased nationality rules to attract talent from a wider range of fields
    政府放寬了國籍法規，以吸引來自更廣泛領域的人才
  • industry n. 產業，工業
    The 400th high-level professional, who works in the electronics industry, was approved for ROC citizenship on Aug. 26, the MOI said.
    內政部表示，這名在電子產業工作的第400名高階專業人才，於8月26日獲准取得中華民國國籍。
  • disclose v. 透露，公開
    The MOI did not disclose the new ROC citizen’s original nationality.
    內政部未透露這位新中華民國公民的原國籍。
  • expert n. 專家
    According to the MOI, high-level professionals are experts in various fields
    根據內政部說法，高階專業人才為各領域之專家
  • amend v. 修正（法律等）
    In December 2016, the act was amended to allow high- level foreign professionals to retain their original nationalities after obtaining ROC citizenship.
    2016年12月，該法修正，允許高階外國專業人才在取得中華民國國籍後保留其原國籍。
  • requirement n. 要求，必要條件
    In May 2024, the act was again amended to reduce the residency requirement for high-level professionals applying for naturalization from five years to two.
    2024年5月，該法再次修正，將高階專業人才申請歸化所需的居留年限從五年縮短為兩年。
  • apply for phr. 申請
    The MOI said Taiwan has various channels through which foreign talent can apply for naturalization.
    內政部表示，台灣有多種管道供外國人才申請歸化。
  • outstanding adj. 傑出的，優秀的
    Taiwan is determined to create a friendly legal and living environment to attract more outstanding foreign professionals
    台灣決心打造友善的法規與生活環境，以吸引更多優秀的外國專業人才

文法重點

  • 現在完成式 (Present Perfect Tense)
    A total of 400 foreign “high- level” professionals have been naturalized as Republic of China (Taiwan) citizens over the past decade
    用來表示「過去十年來 (over the past decade)」一直持續到現在的動作或狀態，句型為「have/has + 過去分詞」，此處搭配被動語態 (have been naturalized) 來表達專業人才「被」歸化。
  • 關係代間詞子句 (Relative Clause with 'who')
    The 400th high-level professional, who works in the electronics industry, was approved for ROC citizenship on Aug. 26
    句中的「who works in the electronics industry」為非限定性關係子句（前面有逗號），用來補充說明前面的主詞「The 400th high-level professional」的背景資訊。
  • 現在分詞表示附带結果或對比 (Present Participle as Result/Contrast)
    Taiwan has built a comprehensive high-tech ecosystem that allows top professionals like him to develop their careers, while the country’s high quality of life also attracted him to become a member of Taiwanese society.
    句尾的「while the country’s high quality of life also attracted him...」引導對比或同時存在的狀況，用連接詞連接兩個獨立觀念，展現多益閱讀常考的長句結構。
  • 介系詞 + 關係代間詞 (Preposition + Relative Pronoun)
    The MOI said Taiwan has various channels through which foreign talent can apply for naturalization.
    「through which」引導形容詞子句修飾前面的「channels (管道)」。因為外國人才可以透過這些管道申請，所以用介系詞 through 搭配關係代間詞 which，等於「through the channels」。
