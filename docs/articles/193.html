南非隊主場擊敗紐西蘭隊，系列賽取得領先

South Africa dominate New Zealand to lead series

單字解析

  • display n. 表現，展示
    South Africa on Saturday produced a brilliant second-half display to earn a 29-24 Test victory over New Zealand in Johannesburg...
    南非隊週六在下半場展現了精彩的表現，在約翰尼斯堡以 29 比 24 的測試賽戰績擊敗紐西蘭隊...
  • advantage n. 優勢
    the Springboks powered to a 12-point advantage in the second half before conceding a try after the full-time hooter.
    跳羚隊在下半場一度取得 12 分的領先優勢，隨後在全場比賽結束的鳴笛聲響起後才被達陣得分。
  • substitute n. / adj. 替補（的）
    Captain and No.8 Ardie Savea scored two tries for the All Blacks, and wing Will Jordan and substitute hooker Samisoni Taukei’aho one each.
    紐西蘭全黑隊的隊長兼 8 號球員阿迪·薩維亞達陣兩次，翼鋒威爾·喬丹和替補勾球手薩米索尼·陶凱阿霍各達陣一次。
  • momentum n. 氣勢，動能
    “We started really well, had momentum, but the Springboks played their game in the second half.
    「我們開局真的很好，掌握了氣勢，但跳羚隊在下半場打出了他們的風格。」
  • injure v. 使受傷
    South Africa suffered a double blow 10 minutes into the first half when full-back Cheslin Kolbe went off injured and Feinberg-Mngomzezulu was yellow-carded.
    南非隊在上半場進行到 10 分鐘時遭受雙重打擊，最後防線切斯林·科爾貝受傷離場，范伯格-姆貢戈姆祖魯則吞下黃牌。
  • sustain v. 遭受，承受（受傷、損失等）
    Kolbe, who converted all 11 goal kicks he took in the first two Tests, sustained a head injury, and Feinberg-Mngomezulu was punished for a deliberate knock down of a Savea pass.
    科爾貝在前兩場測試賽中包辦了全部 11 次踢球得分，此次因頭部受傷退場，而范伯格-姆貢戈姆祖魯則因為蓄意拍落薩維亞的傳球而受到處罰。
  • advantage n. 優勢
    New Zealand immediately took advantage of having an extra man to take a seven-point lead through the first of two first-half tries from Savea.
    紐西蘭隊立刻利用多一人的優勢，由薩維亞在上半場的兩次達陣中的第一次取得領先，超前七分。
  • return n. 回歸，返回
    The return of Feinberg-Mngomezulu coincided with Springbok pressure which culminated in Arendse taking a pass from center Damian de Allende and scoring in the corner.
    范伯格-姆貢戈姆祖魯的回歸恰逢跳羚隊發起攻勢，最終由阿倫斯接獲中衛達米安·德阿連德的傳球，在角落達陣得分。
  • trailing adj. 落後的
    Feinberg-Mngomezulu missed the conversion, leaving the hosts trailing 7-5 midway through the first half at Soccer City stadium.
    范伯格-姆貢戈姆祖魯錯失了轉換得分，使主隊在上半場中段於足球城體育場以 7 比 5 落後。
  • visitors n. 客隊，訪客（此處指客場球隊）
    When South Africa won a scrum close to the New Zealand tryline, and the visitors backpedaled, referee Nika Amashukeli spotted an infringement and awarded a penalty try.
    當南非隊在靠近紐西蘭達陣線的地方贏得爭球，且客隊後退時，裁判尼卡·阿馬舒克利發現了違規行為，並判定一次罰球達陣。
  • retained v. 保持，保留
    Feinberg-Mngomezulu converted and kicked a penalty for a 12-point lead that South Africa retained until a pushover try from Taukei’aho.
    范伯格-姆貢戈姆祖魯罰踢並踢入一次罰球，取得 12 分領先，南非隊一直保持這項優勢，直到陶凱阿霍推進達陣得分為止。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況或結果
    Feinberg-Mngomezulu missed the conversion, leaving the hosts trailing 7-5 midway through the first half at Soccer City stadium.
    句尾的「leaving + 受詞 + 形容詞/分詞」是常見的分詞構句結構，用來表達某個動作所產生的「結果」或「伴隨狀況」，在此處表示范伯格-姆貢戈姆祖魯沒踢進轉換得分，『使得』主隊處於落後的狀態。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    Hooker Asafo Aumua, promoted to the starting lineup in place of the long-serving Codie Taylor, broke from a line-out...
    句中的「promoted to the starting lineup...」是過去分詞片語，用來補充修飾前面的主詞 Hooker Asafo Aumua。原句省略了「who was」，直接使用過去分詞片語作形容詞用，使句子更為精簡流暢。
  • 關係代名詞子句（非限定用法）
    Kolbe, who converted all 11 goal kicks he took in the first two Tests, sustained a head injury...
    「who converted all 11 goal kicks he took in the first two Tests」是一個非限定用法的關係子句，用逗號隔開，對前面的先行詞 Kolbe 提供額外的補充說明，就算拿掉這段話，主要子句的意思依然完整。
  • 獨立分詞構句（Independent Participle Clause）
    Deadlocked 12-12 at halftime, the Springboks powered to a 12-point advantage in the second half...
    句首的「Deadlocked 12-12 at halftime」省略了連接詞與主詞（原意為 When they were deadlocked 12-12 at halftime），保留過去分詞片語來表示時間或原因背景，說明跳羚隊在半場平手的情況下，下半場展開猛攻。
