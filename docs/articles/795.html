中國如何將台海緊張局勢轉化為高收視率的電視間諜劇

How China turned Taiwan tensions into a top-rating TV spy drama

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • double agent n. phr. 雙面間諜
    After shaking off the people trailing her through a crowded market and emerging from a bathroom in disguise, the double agent appears to have escaped.
    在甩掉在擁擠市場中跟蹤她的人並偽裝從洗手間出來後，這名雙面間諜似乎已經逃脫了。
  • co-produced adj. 聯合製作的、共同出品的
    The plot comes from a new Chinese spy drama co-produced by the Ministry of State Security.
    這個情節出自一部由國家安全部聯合製作的中國全新間諜劇。
  • premiere n. 首映、首播
    Since its premiere on Sept. 6, it has become a TV hit, with a fictionalized take on a subject rarely treated so directly...
    自9月6日首播以來，它已成為一部熱門電視劇，對一個鮮少被如此直接處理的主題進行了虛構化的演繹……
  • verify v. 核實、查證
    Yet it remains difficult for analysts to verify how closely the drama follows real events as new episodes continue to be released.
    然而，隨著新劇集的持續播出，分析人士仍然難以核實該劇在多大程度上符合真實事件。
  • adversary n. 對手、敵手
    While Taiwan’s intelligence services are portrayed as an adversary capable of obtaining sensitive missile information...
    雖然台灣的情報機構被描繪成一個有能力獲取敏感飛彈情資的對手……
  • infiltration n. 滲透
    ...the drama casts Chinese state security officers as the heroes defending the country against infiltration and theft.
    ……但該劇將中國國安人員塑造成保衛國家免受滲透與竊密之苦的英雄。
  • surpass v. 超越、勝過
    Online, it surpassed 50 million views across platforms within a week, according to Chinese entertainment data provider Maoyan.
    根據中國娛樂數據提供商貓眼數據顯示，在網路平台上，開播一週內的觀看量就突破了5,000萬次。
  • espionage n. 間諜活動
    Chinese viewers have described the series as unusually “bold” for addressing real political figures and depicting details of espionage operations on both sides.
    中國觀眾形容該劇不同尋常地「大膽」，因為它觸及了真實的政治人物，並描繪了雙方間諜行動的細節。
  • emphasize v. 強調、重視
    Another is that they want to emphasize patriotism...
    另一個原因是他們想要強調愛國主義……
  • betray v. 背叛、出賣
    That contrast helps show who is patriotic and who is betraying the country, Li adds.
    黎補充說，這種對比有助於展現誰是愛國的，誰是在叛國。
  • conduct v. 執行、舉辦、進行
    A poll conducted last month found that only 7.4 percent of Taiwanese respondents had a favorable view of the Chinese Communist party...
    上個月進行的一項民調發現，只有 7.4% 的台灣受訪者對中國共產黨持有好感……

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    A poll conducted last month found that only 7.4 percent of Taiwanese respondents had a favorable view of the Chinese Communist party...
    過去分詞片語「conducted last month」用來修飾前面的名詞「A poll」，相當於形容詞子句「which was conducted last month」的簡化形式，能讓句子更為精簡。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    The series, which will run for 40 episodes, is officially titled The Long Watch in English or Jiaofeng...
    句中的「which will run for 40 episodes」帶有逗號，屬於非限定關係子句，用來補充說明先行詞「The series」的額外資訊（總共有40集），即使拿掉這個子句，主要子句的意思依然完整。
  • 現在分詞作附帶狀況/結果 (Present Participle indicating Result)
    Last year, Taiwan’s own drama about cross-strait security threats performed strongly, becoming the most-watched drama of the year.
    句尾的「becoming the most-watched drama of the year」是由現在分詞「becoming」引導的分詞構句，用來表達前一個動作自然延伸出來的結果（表現強勁，進而成為年度收看人數最多的電視劇）。
  • 條件句與假設語氣的縮寫 (Conditional Subjunctives/Shortened clauses)
    ...nor would she be interested in watching it, given the topic.
    片語「given the topic」在這裡作為先決條件的介詞片語（相當於 considering the topic 或 if we consider the topic），常出現在多益商業英文中，用來表達「考慮到……的情況下」。
