Taipei Times · 2026-09-12

金磚國家二十週年

The BRICS at 20

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • formalize v. 使正式化、使成形
    Since then, the grouping has been formalized — the first BRIC summit took place in June 2009 — and expanded...
    自那時起，這個集團已走向制度化——首次金磚國家峰會於 2009 年 6 月舉行——並不斷擴大...
  • consequential adj. 重大的、影響深遠的
    Today, the BRICS represents a highly consequential experiment in international cooperation...
    今天，金磚國家代表瞭國際合作中一個極具影響力的實驗...
  • encapsulate v. 概括、摘要
    Coined by then-Goldman Sachs investment management division managing director Jim O’Neill in 2001 to encapsulate four fast-growing emerging markets...
    2001 年，時任高盛投資管理部董事總經理吉姆·奧尼爾創造了這個詞，用以概括四個快速增長的新興市場...
  • coalition n. 同盟、聯合體
    ...the “BRICs” grouping has developed into the BRICS, an 11-country political and economic coalition...
    「金磚」集團後來發展成為金磚國家，一個由 11 個國家組成的政治與經濟聯盟...
  • account for phr. v. 佔（比例）、說明、解釋
    ...the BRICS accounts for nearly half of the world’s population and about 40 percent of global GDP...
    金磚國家佔了全球近一半的人口以及約 40% 的全球 GDP...
  • productivity n. 生產力
    And whereas the BRICS’ share of the global economy continues to grow, the pace of productivity and economic growth in advanced economies has been declining steadily since the 1990s.
    而且，儘管金磚國家在全球經濟中的佔比持續增長，但自 1990 年代以來，先進經濟體的生產力和經濟增長步伐一直在穩步下降。
  • hedge n. 避險、防範措施
    For non-Western countries, the BRICS represents a hedge against rising economic and geopolitical uncertainty...
    對非西方國家而言，金磚國家是對沖日益加劇的經濟和地緣政治不確定性的手段...
  • autonomy n. 自主權、自治
    ...and an institutional platform for advancing strategic autonomy and broadening economic cooperation.
    同時也是推進戰略自主和擴大經濟合作的機構平台。
  • bolster v. 支持、加強、鞏固
    This framework helps to expand the BRICS’ network for cooperation and bolster its ability to represent the Global South...
    這一框架有助於擴大金磚國家的合作網絡並增強其代表全球南方能力...
  • multilateral adj. 多邊的
    The New Development Bank (NDB) is the first multilateral development bank with global scope conceived and led solely by emerging economies...
    新開發銀行（NDB）是由新興經濟體獨家構想和主導的第一個具有全球範圍的多邊開發銀行...
  • financing n. 融資、資金
    Unlike these US-led institutions, the NDB does not attach political conditions to its infrastructure financing...
    與這些由美國主導的機構不同，新開發銀行不會在其基礎設施融資附加政治條件...
  • vulnerability n. 脆弱性、易受攻擊性
    The BRICS has also been working to reduce vulnerabilities within the international financial system.
    金磚國家也一直致力於減少國際金融系統內部的脆弱性。
  • interoperability n. 互通性、互操作性
    ...improving interoperability among members’ digital payment systems, linking central bank digital currencies...
    提高成員國數字支付系統之間的互聯互通性、連結央行數位貨幣...
  • prerequisite n. 先決條件
    Diversity brings different ideas and advantages to the table, and ideological uniformity and close strategic alignment are not necessarily prerequisites to cooperation.
    多樣性為檯面上帶來了不同的想法和優勢，意識形態的一致性和緊密的地緣政治結盟不一定是合作的先決條件。
  • consensus n. 共識
    However, a highly diverse grouping, with sharply divergent priorities and geopolitical concerns, would always struggle to forge consensus.
    然而，一個高度多元化、且優先事項和地緣政治關切存在巨大分歧的集團，要在尋求共識上始終面臨掙扎。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況/補充說明
    Since adding Egypt, Ethiopia, Indonesia, Iran, Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates to its ranks in 2024 to last year, the BRICS accounts for nearly half of the world’s population...
    句首的「Since adding...」為分詞構句，省略了主詞（應與主句主詞一致，此處為 the BRICS），用來表示時間或原因的背景，意指「自從（金磚國家）將...加入其行列以來」。
  • 關係代名詞非限定用法（which 引導）
    The BRICS also has 10 “partner countries” — Belarus, Bolivia, Kazakhstan, Cuba, Malaysia, Nigeria, Thailand, Uganda, Uzbekistan and Vietnam — which can participate in summits.
    句尾的「which can participate in summits」使用非限定用法的關係子句（前面有逗號），對先行詞「10 ‘partner countries’」進行補充說明，指出這些夥伴國具有參與峰會的資格。
  • 虛主詞 It 與表語形容詞的句型
    Small wonder there has been such enthusiasm for joining: more than three dozen countries have formally applied or expressed interest in becoming members...
    「Small wonder (that)...」是一個常見的慣用句型（省略了 that），意為「不足為奇、難怪...」。這裡以虛主詞概念帶出後面的實質狀況，說明為什麼加入的熱情會這麼高。
  • 過去分詞作後置修飾語
    For non-Western countries, the BRICS represents a hedge against rising economic and geopolitical uncertainty—generated, not least, by a US seemingly bent on withdrawing from global leadership...
    插入語「generated, not least, by a US...」中的過去分詞「generated」用來修飾前面的名詞「uncertainty」（不確定性），相當於形容詞子句「which is generated...」，表達這些不確定性是由美國所產生的。
