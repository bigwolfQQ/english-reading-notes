讚嘆內閣的預算特技

Admiring Cabinet’s budgetary acrobatics

單字解析

  • criticize v. 批評、指責
    Over the past few days, opposition lawmakers have harshly criticized the Cabinet for “showing contempt for the legislature”...
    在過去幾天裡，在野黨立委嚴厲批評內閣「蔑視立法院」...
  • freeze v. 凍結（資金、資產等）
    ...the lawmakers in January last year responded by voting to freeze NT$500 million in Executive Yuan funding.
    ...立委們隨即在去年一月投票決定凍結行政院的 5 億元經費。
  • allocate / assigned v. 分配、指派（此處用 assign）
    As the legislature had not specified which funds were off-limits, the Cabinet assigned the frozen NT$500 million to personnel expenses...
    由於立法院並未指定哪些資金是禁區，內閣便將遭凍結的 5 億元指派給人事費用...
  • expenses n. 費用、開支
    ...a maneuver that spared operating expenses and construction and procurement spending from bearing the full weight of the freeze.
    ...這項策略使營運開支以及工程採購支出免於承受凍結的全面衝擊。
  • procurement n. 採購、物資籌辦
    ...a maneuver that spared operating expenses and construction and procurement spending from bearing the full weight of the freeze.
    ...這項策略使營運開支以及工程採購支出免於承受凍結的全面衝擊。
  • obligation n. 義務、責任（形容詞為 obligated）
    The Cabinet said that it was legally obligated to make those payments.
    內閣表示其有法律義務支付這些款項。
  • budget n. 預算
    The story began in December 2024, when the Chinese Nationalist Party (KMT) and the Taiwan People’s Party (TPP) were reviewing the central government’s budget.
    這個故事始於 2024 年 12 月，當時中國國民黨與台灣民眾黨正在審查中央政府總預算。
  • proposal n. 提案、建議
    In their proposal, the KMT and TPP’s resolution simply stated that NT$500 million of Executive Yuan funding was to be frozen...
    在他們的提案中，國民黨與民眾黨的提案決議中，僅聲明凍結行政院 5 億元經費...
  • strategy / maneuver n. 策略、調度、花招（此處用 maneuver）
    In the end, the Cabinet effectively put the entire NT$500 million to use, a maneuver that spared operating expenses...
    最終，內閣實際運用了全部 5 億元，這項策略使營運開支...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作修飾語 (Present Participle Phrase as Modifier)
    Angered by then-Cabinet secretary-general Kung Ming-hsin (龔明鑫) leaving that legislative committee meeting without authorization, the lawmakers in January last year responded by voting to freeze NT$500 million in Executive Yuan funding.
    句首的 "Angered by..." 是過去分詞片語作副詞用，表原因，修飾主句的主語 "the lawmakers"，相當於 "Because they were angered by..."。
  • 現在分詞短語作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    However, after combing through the government’s final accounts for last year submitted at the end of last month, the opposition lawmakers were nearly apoplectic.
    句中的 "submitted at the end of last month" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞短語 "the government’s final accounts for last year"，省略了 "which were"。
  • 條件句與假設語態 (Conditional Structure)
    This battle over government accounting not only demonstrates the political resilience of a minority government, but also shows that if legislative oversight is reduced to political score-settling without technical rigor, lawmakers might ultimately find themselves struck by their own boomerang.
    此句運用了 "if + 現在式動詞 (is reduced), 主詞 + might + 原形動詞 (might find)" 的第一條件句結構，用來推論未來可能發生的結果。
