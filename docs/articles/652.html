最高法院維持男子虐待四歲幼童五年六個月徒刑定讞

Supreme Court upholds man's 5 1/2-year sentence for abusing 4-year-old

單字解析

  • uphold v. 維持（判決）、支持、支撐
    The Supreme Court has upheld a five-year-and-six-month prison sentence for a man...
    最高法院維持了一名男子的五年六個月有期徒刑...
  • convict v. 宣判有罪
    ...for a man convicted of severely physically abusing his live-in girlfriend's 4-year-old son...
    ...因一名男子被判嚴重虐待其同居女友的4歲兒子有罪...
  • ruling n. （法官或法庭的）裁決、判決
    The ruling upheld a Taiwan High Court verdict finding the man... guilty...
    該裁定維持了台灣高等法院認定該男子有罪的判決...
  • verdict n. （陪審團的）裁決、判決
    The ruling upheld a Taiwan High Court verdict finding the man, surnamed Wang (王), guilty...
    該裁定維持了台灣高等法院認定王姓男子有罪的判決...
  • intentionally adv. 故意地、有意地
    ...finding the man, surnamed Wang (王), guilty of intentionally causing serious bodily harm to a child.
    ...認定王姓男子犯下故意對兒童身體施以重傷罪。
  • instruction n. 指示、命令、說明
    Wang became angry because he believed the boy was refusing to follow instructions...
    王男變得憤怒，因為他認為該男孩拒絕聽從指令...
  • extended adj. 延長的、長期的
    When the child collapsed after standing for an extended period, Wang lifted him up...
    當男童站立一段延長的時間後倒地時，王男將他拉起...
  • diagnose v. 診斷
    The boy was diagnosed with a right-sided subdural hematoma...

  • accidental adj. 意外的、偶然的
    ...such injuries were highly unlikely to have resulted from an accidental fall at home...
    ...此類傷勢極不可能是由家中的意外跌倒所造成的...
  • impair v. 損害、削弱
    ...as well as impaired function of his left arm, spasticity in both legs...
    ...以及左臂功能受損、雙腿痙攣...

    最高法院最近駁回了王男的上訴，使判決定局。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作為後位修飾語（縮寫自形容詞子句）
    The Supreme Court has upheld a five-year-and-six-month prison sentence for a man convicted of severely physically abusing his live-in girlfriend's 4-year-old son...
    句中的「convicted of...」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「a man」。其完整原貌相當於關代子句「...for a man who was convicted of...」，省略了「who was」使句子更為精簡緊湊，是新聞英文常見的高效表達方式。
  • 現在分詞片語表示附帶結果
    ...leaving the child with permanent brain damage and disabilities.
    句尾的「leaving the child with...」是由現在分詞「leaving」引導的分詞構句，在此處表示前面虐待行為所造成的「自然而然的結果」（結果狀語），翻譯時常譯為「導致……、留下了……」。
  • 動名詞片語作介詞的受詞
    Wang became angry because he believed the boy was refusing to follow instructions and forced him to stand as punishment.
    句中的「refusing」是動名詞，作為「was refusing」的現在進行式動詞構成部分；而在「convicted of severely physically abusing...」中，介詞「of」後面接動名詞「abusing」，並帶有副詞來修飾，展現多益測驗常考的介詞後方必須接動名詞（V-ing）的文法規則。
