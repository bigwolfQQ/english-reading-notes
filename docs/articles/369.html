Candidate probed over gifts of soy sauce, toothpaste to local HOA

單字解析

  • suspect v. / n. 懷疑，涉嫌；嫌疑犯
    A ward chief candidate in New Taipei's Xinzhuang District was ordered held on NT$300,000 (US$9,515) bail on suspicion of vote buying after allegedly giving gifts of soy sauce and toothpaste to a local homeowners' association (HOA).
    新北市新莊區的一名里長候選人因涉嫌賄選，遭檢方諭令以30萬新台幣（約9,515美元）交保，起因是他涉嫌向當地社區管理委員會（HOA）贈送醬油和牙膏等禮品。
  • statement n. 聲明，陳述
    The New Taipei District Prosecutors Office said in a statement Tuesday that it had received a tip-off alleging that the candidate... had given a gift basket... to members of an apartment complex's management committee...
    新北市地檢署週二透過聲明表示，檢方接獲檢舉指稱，這名候選人涉嫌將禮盒送給某公寓大廈管理委員會成員...
  • investigate v. 調查，偵查
    After assigning a task force to jointly investigate the case with the New Taipei police, authorities searched the homes of Yeh and two of his local campaign organizers on Monday...
    檢方指派專案小組與新北市警方共同偵辦此案後，於週一搜索了葉嫌及兩名地方競選幹部的住處...
  • organizer n. 組織者，幹部，籌辦人
    ...authorities searched the homes of Yeh and two of his local campaign organizers on Monday, while summoning 15 people for questioning, the statement said.
    ...聲明指出，於週一搜索了葉嫌及兩名地方競選幹部的住處，並傳喚了15人到案說明。
  • violate v. 違反，違背（法律、規定等）
    Prosecutors said the three are suspected of violating provisions against vote buying in the Public Officials Election and Recall Act...
    檢察官表示，這三人涉嫌違反《公職人員選舉罷免法》中的期約賄選規定...
  • provision n. 條款，規定
    Prosecutors said the three are suspected of violating provisions against vote buying in the Public Officials Election and Recall Act...
    檢察官表示，這三人涉嫌違反《公職人員選舉罷免法》中的期約賄選規定...
  • evidence n. 證據，證物
    ...and asked the court to detain Yeh incommunicado, citing the risk that he might collude with others or destroy evidence.
    ...並以有串證或滅證之虞為由，向法院聲請將葉嫌羈押禁見。
  • ruling n. 裁決，判決
    In a ruling on Monday, however, the New Taipei District Court ordered Yeh and one of the organizers held on NT$300,000 bail...
    不過，新北市地方法院週一裁定，葉嫌與其中一名幹部以新台幣30萬元交保...

    In Taiwan, candidates for elected office frequently distribute low-value promotional gifts, such as pocket tissues and water bottles, worth less than NT$30 to voters.
    在台灣，參選公職的候選人經常向選民分發價值低於30元新台幣、低價的宣傳品，例如隨身包面紙和瓶裝水。
  • standard n. 標準
    ...though it is among the standards used by prosecutors in deciding whether to pursue a case.
    ...儘管它是檢察官決定是否立案偵辦的標準之一。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾（分詞構句簡化）
    ...allegedly giving gifts of soy sauce and toothpaste to a local homeowners' association (HOA).
    句中的 "allegedly giving..." 為現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前面的動作或原因，省略了連接詞與主詞，使句子結構更為精簡流暢。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    A ward chief candidate in New Taipei's Xinzhuang District was ordered held on NT$300,000 bail...
    句型使用「be +過去分詞」(was ordered)，強調動作的承受者（候選人被法院裁定），在新聞英文與法律報導中非常常見，以突顯客觀事實。
  • 現在分詞表附帶結果或補充說明
    ...and asked the court to detain Yeh incommunicado, citing the risk that he might collude with others or destroy evidence.
    句尾的 ", citing the risk that..." 是分詞片語作狀語，用來表示檢察官提出聲請時所依據的理由或原因（cited 的主動意涵）。
  • 非限定關係子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Taiwan's local elections, in which voters will select mayors and county chiefs, city and county councilors... are scheduled for Nov. 28.
    逗號後面的 "in which voters will select..." 是一個非限定關係子句，用來補充說明前面先行詞 "local elections" 的詳細內容，移開也不影響主要子句的完整性。
