文法重點

  • 地方副詞置於句首的完全倒裝句 (Full Inversion)
    Driving the optimism are Taiwan’s deeper linkages across the AI supply chain and a more upbeat earnings outlook.
    本句為了強調與平衡句子結構，將分詞片語「Driving the optimism」置於句首，引起主詞與動詞的完全倒裝。原本的語序應為「Taiwan’s deeper linkages across the AI supply chain and a more upbeat earnings outlook are driving the optimism.」，倒裝後動詞「are」直接放在主詞之前。
  • 現在分詞短語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    About 40 percent of the fund managers surveyed by Bank of America Corp last month said they were overweight Taiwan...
    句中的「surveyed by Bank of America Corp last month」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「fund managers」。雖然此題原句為過去分詞，但文章多處使用現在分詞或過去分詞片語作後位修飾（例如「data compiled by Bloomberg showed」），這是英文新聞精簡句子、避免使用冗長關代子句（who were surveyed...）常見的文法結構。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Absolute Participial Construction)
    Meanwhile, South Korea’s market leadership remains heavily concentrated in chipmakers Samsung Electronics Co and SK Hynix Inc, leaving it more vulnerable as doubts emerge over how much longer the current memory upcycle can run.
    句尾的「leaving it more vulnerable...」是由現在分詞「leaving」引導的分詞構句，用來補充說明前面主句所產生的結果或伴隨狀況。邏輯主詞為前面整句所表達的事情，轉化為子句概念相當於「and this leaves it more vulnerable...」。
  • 動名詞作主詞補語 / 包含關係代名詞的子句
    The next leg of upside for Taiwan stocks is likely to depend less on broad AI enthusiasm and more on local tech companies that remain exposed to genuine bottlenecks in the AI value chain...
    句中的「that remain exposed to genuine bottlenecks in the AI value chain」是一個限定用法的關係代名詞子句（由 that 引導），用來修飾先行詞「local tech companies」。這在商業與科技新聞中非常實用，用以精確界定特定條件的名詞。
