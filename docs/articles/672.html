單字解析

  • candidate n. 候選人
    A candidate backed by Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi’s ruling party on Sunday ousted the incumbent governor...
    日本首相高市早苗執政黨支持的一名候選人在週日罷免了現任知事……
  • incumbent adj. / n. 現任的；在職者
    ...ousted the incumbent governor of Japan’s southern Okinawa Prefecture in an election...
    ……在選舉中擊敗了日本南部沖繩縣的現任知事……
  • boost n. / v. 提升、促進、增加
    ...delivering a boost to Takaichi’s push for a stronger military...
    ……為高市推動建立更強大軍隊的計畫注入了動力（帶來提升）……
  • influence n. / v. 影響力、左右
    ...giving Takaichi greater influence over the strategically important cluster of islands...
    ……這讓高市對這群具有戰略重要性的島嶼獲得了更大的影響力……
  • expand v. 擴大、擴展
    Koja’s victory might also make it easier for Takaichi to expand Japan’s own military footprint...
    古謝的勝利也可能讓高市更容易擴大日本自身的軍事足跡……
  • accept v. 接受
    “I accept the will of the people,” Tamaki said in conceding to Koja...
    「我接受人民的意願，」玉城在向古謝認輸時表示……
  • campaign v. / n. 競選活動、拉票；從事競選
    The former deputy mayor of Naha, Okinawa’s capital city, campaigned on a platform of boosting wages...
    身為沖繩首府那霸市的前副市長，他以提高薪資作為競選政見……
  • industry n. 產業、工業
    ...building a health and wellness industry in Okinawa to help drive growth.
    ……在沖繩建立健康養生產業來推動經濟成長。
  • shift n. / v. 轉變、轉移
    ...the latest election result underscores a shift in the political debate away from that topic...
    ……最近的選舉結果凸顯了政治辯論從該話題轉移的趨勢……
  • interview n. / v. 採訪、面試
    Koja said in an interview a few days before the election.
    古謝在選舉前幾天的一次採訪中說道。
  • maintain v. 維持、保持
    “Our [Japanese] Self-Defense Forces alone are not sufficient to maintain deterrence.”
    「光靠我們的（日本）自衛隊並不足以維持嚇阻力。」
  • necessary adj. 必要的、必需的
    “I also understand that the Japan-US security framework is necessary,” he said.
    「我也理解日美安全體系是必要的，」他說。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    A candidate backed by Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi’s ruling party on Sunday ousted the incumbent governor...
    句中的「backed by Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi’s ruling party」是由過去分詞「backed」開頭的分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「A candidate」（候選人），其還原的完整概念為「A candidate who was backed by...」，省略了「who was」使語氣更為精簡緊湊，是英文新聞報導中極常見的句構。
  • 使役動詞 make 的虛受詞句構 (Clemency / Make + O + adj. / to V)
    Koja’s victory might also make it easier for Takaichi to expand Japan’s own military footprint...
    此句使用了「make + it + 形容詞 + for + 某人 + to V」的句構。因為真正的受詞「to expand Japan’s own military footprint...」過長，若直接放在動詞 make 後面會造成頭重腳輕，因此用虛受詞「it」來代替，而真正的受詞則移至句尾。for Takaichi 是不定詞的語意主詞。
  • 非限定關代子句補充說明 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    ...giving Takaichi greater influence over the strategically important cluster of islands in the East China Sea that host the bulk of US military facilities in Japan.
    雖然句中是以「that host...」作為限定修飾，但在此處用分詞構句（giving...）帶出結果，且句中夾雜了介系詞片語作修飾。在多益考試中，常考關係代名詞（如 that, which, who）引導子句來修飾先行詞的文法題型，應特別注意先行詞與動詞的單複數一致性。
  • 形容詞子句省略關係代名詞與be動詞 (Omission of Relative Pronoun + be)
    ...Koja, who is part of a younger generation of Japanese more supportive of building up military power...
    句中的「more supportive of building up military power」修飾「younger generation」，實際上是省略了「who are」的形容詞片語。多益測驗常會考這種分詞或形容詞片語置於名詞後方的簡化寫法，測試考生對句子結構的拆解能力。
