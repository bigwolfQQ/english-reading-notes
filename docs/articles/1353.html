單字解析

  • market value n.片語 市值
    Lucky Strike Entertainment Corp, the dominant player in the business, has lost almost half of its market value in the past year...
    該產業的龍頭企業 Lucky Strike Entertainment Corp 在過去一年中失去了近半數的市值...
  • consumer sentiment n.片語 消費者信心
    Higher borrowing costs and iffy consumer sentiment are factors, but Americans just do not seem to want to bowl that much anymore.
    更高的借貸成本與不穩定的消費者信心是部分原因，但 Americans 似乎再也不那麼想打保齡球了。
  • on the decline phr. 在下降中，走下坡
    Demand for other social forms of leisure is on the decline, too.
    其他社交形式的休閒需求也同樣在下降。
  • earnings n. 收益，盈餘
    Dave & Buster’s Entertainment Inc’s disappointing earnings and country clubs’ declining membership are cases in point.
    Dave & Buster’s Entertainment Inc 令人失望的獲利以及鄉村俱樂部會員人數的減少就是最好的證明。
  • membership n. 會員身份，會員人數
    Dave & Buster’s Entertainment Inc’s disappointing earnings and country clubs’ declining membership are cases in point.
    Dave & Buster’s Entertainment Inc 令人失望的獲利以及鄉村俱樂部會員人數的減少就是最好的證明。
  • revenue n. 收入，收益
    League membership reached about 10 million people in its heyday in the 1970s.
    在 1970 年代的全盛時期，聯盟會員人數達到約 1000 萬人。
  • budget n. 預算
    Consumers these days have limited budgets that are being squeezed by high energy prices and inflation...
    如今的消費者面臨著高能源價格與通貨膨脹所擠壓的有限預算...
  • inflation n. 通貨膨脹
    ...so they are more price sensitive to the marginal elements of their spending, and bowling is marginal.
    ...因此他們對消費中的邊際項目更加價格敏感，而保齡球就是邊際項目。
  • refinance v. 重新融資，借新債還舊債
    The higher rate environment is forcing them to refinance at higher costs, cutting into their margins and hammering their ratings.
    較高的利率環境迫使它們以更高的成本進行融資，這侵蝕了它們的利潤率並重創了其信用評級。
  • discretionary adj. 可自由支配的
    Americans across income groups still spend lots on discretionary services.
    各收入階層的美國人在可自由支配的服務上仍然花費巨大。
  • productivity n. 生產力
    It can have wider negative externalities because isolation could contribute to worse mental health and loneliness that spills over to less productivity.
    它可能會產生更廣泛的負面外部性，因為孤立可能會導致更糟的心理健康與孤獨感，進而蔓延並降低生產力。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況或結果
    A less social nation not only portends big economic shifts, with “third spaces” closing and businesses running into trouble, but also impacts our mental health and well-being, while robbing us of the connections that in the past led to fresh interests and professional opportunities.
    句中的 "with "third spaces" closing and businesses running into trouble" 是「with + 名詞 + 現在分詞」的複合結構，用來補充說明前面經濟轉變的具體細節。而句尾的 "while robbing us of..." 則是分詞片語表伴隨發生的結果，翻譯時可譯為「同時剝奪了……」。
  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    For most Americans, the places that provide outlets for in-person connection, such as recreation and senior centers or the grocery store, are closing.
    句中 "that provide outlets for in-person connection" 為限定用法的關係代名詞子句，由關係代名詞 "that" 引導，用來修飾前面的先行詞 "the places"（地方），精確界定是哪種地方在關閉。
  • 現在完成進行式
    However, the share of the population that bowls in a day has been trending down since even before the pandemic.
    句型 "has been trending down" 採用現在完成進行式（have/has been + V-ing），用來強調某個動作或趨勢從過去某個時間點（疫情之前）持續進行到現在，且可能還會持續下去。
  • 虛主詞 It 的用法
    It could be that bowling has run its course and is soon to be about as relevant as a croquet tournament.
    句首的 "It" 為虛主詞（無實際意義），真正的的主詞是後面由 "that" 引導的名詞子句 "that bowling has run its course and is soon to be..."。這種句型常用於表達推測或看法。
