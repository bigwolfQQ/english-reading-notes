文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The Cabinet on Thursday approved a supplementary budget request totaling NT$607.6 billion...
    句中的「totaling NT$607.6 billion」為現在分詞片語，用來修飾前方的名詞「a supplementary budget request」，相當於「which totals NT$607.6 billion」的簡化用法，在多益閱讀測驗中非常常考以測試分詞的修飾用法。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...including NT$145.7 billion in spending previously rejected by opposition lawmakers...
    片語「previously rejected by opposition lawmakers」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前方的名詞「spending」，表被動關係（支出遭到在野黨立委否決），相當於關代子句「which was previously rejected...」的省略。
  • 附屬連接詞引導的條件分詞構句 (Subordinate Clause / Participial Construction)
    Asked to clarify the apparent overlap in drone funding, Lt. Gen. Huang Wen-chi... said...
    句首的「Asked to...」是省略了主詞與be動詞的分詞構句（原句形如「When he was asked to...」）。當分詞構句的主詞與主要子句主詞一致時，常省略主詞和連接詞以求精簡，是高級英文寫作與多益文法的重點。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    The procurement plan came after the Legislature, in which the opposition Kuomintang (KMT) and Taiwan People's Party (TPP) hold a majority of seats, passed the Act...
    句中的「in which the opposition Kuomintang... hold a majority of seats」是由「介係詞 + 關係代名詞 (in which)」引導的非限定性關係子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「the Legislature（立法院）」，相當於在裡面（立法院中）擁有過半席次。
