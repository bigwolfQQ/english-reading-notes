文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語當後位修飾語 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The Taipei District Court has issued an arrest warrant for a man indicted in a corruption case involving independent Legislator Kao Chin Su-mei...
    句中的「indicted in a corruption case...」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 a man，相當於形容詞子句「who was indicted in a corruption case...」的簡化形式，能讓句子更為精煉。
  • 分詞構句表附帶結果或狀態 (Participial Phrase indicating Circumstance)
    Police responding to the alert were unable to locate Chang but recovered his wrist monitor, which had been "broken and thrown away," the court said.
    句中的「responding to the alert」是由現在分詞帶領的片語，用來修飾前面的 Police，相當於「Police who responded to the alert」，說明警察當時的動作與狀態。
  • 介系詞片語作讓步狀語 (Prepositional Phrase of Concession)
    This included registering several individuals, including her siblings, as legislative assistants, despite them not actually performing any duties, prosecutors alleged.
    「despite」為介系詞，後面接名詞或動名詞片語（在此為「them not actually performing...」），用來表達「儘管……卻……」的讓步語氣，是多益閱讀與寫作測驗中常見的表對比連接詞/介系詞。
  • 關代非限定用法 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Chang, who was held in incommunicado detention for nearly four months before his indictment, was released on NT$10 million bail in June.
    句中的「who was held in incommunicado detention...」是以逗號隔開的關代非限定用法，對先行詞 Chang 提供補充說明，拿掉這個子句並不影響主要子句（Chang was released...）的完整文意。
