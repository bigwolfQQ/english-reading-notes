AIT處長：台海衝突的經濟衝擊可能超過第二次世界大戰

Economic impact of Taiwan Strait conflict could exceed WWII's: AIT chief

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • conflict n. 衝突
    A conflict across the Taiwan Strait could inflict an economic cost greater than that of World War II
    台海衝突可能造成的經濟成本將大於第二次世界大戰
  • inflict v. 使遭受、施加（痛苦、損害等）
    A conflict across the Taiwan Strait could inflict an economic cost greater than that of World War II
    台海衝突可能造成的經濟成本將大於第二次世界大戰
  • deter v. 嚇阻、阻止
    To deter a conflict, Taiwan doesn't need to demonstrate that it can win a war outright
    為了嚇阻衝突，台灣並不需要證明自己能夠完全打贏一場戰爭
  • adversary n. 對手、敵手
    Instead, Taiwan must convince a potential adversary that landing a large ground force... would be extremely difficult
    相反地，台灣必須讓潛在的對手確信，要在...登陸大批地面部隊將是極其困難的事
  • priority n. 優先事項、當務之急
    Greene said preventing such a conflict was a top priority for the administration of U.S. President Donald Trump
    谷立言表示，預防此類衝突是美國總統唐納·川普政府的當務之急
  • partnership n. 夥伴關係、合作
    Washington is pursuing that goal through high-level diplomacy, a strengthened presence and partnerships in the Indo-Pacific
    華盛頓正透過高層外交、加強在印太地區的存在與夥伴關係來追求這項目標
  • reciprocity n. 互惠、相對
    Greene said an upcoming Sept. 24 U.S.-China summit... represents an opportunity to advance strategic stability based on fairness and reciprocity
    谷立言表示，即將於9月24日舉行的美中峰會代表了一個基於公平與互惠推進戰略穩定的機會
  • procurement n. 採購
    Government drone production and procurement, including civilian procurement, must use fully non-red supply chains
    政府的無人機生產與採購（包含民間採購）必須採用完全去紅色供應鏈
  • allocate v. 分配、撥出（預算、資金等）
    It allocates NT$145.7 billion (US$4.63 billion) to defense
    該預算案撥款新台幣1457億元（約合46.3億美元）用於國防
  • dependency n. 依賴
    to reduce risks and dependencies as Taiwan works toward greater domestic production, she added
    她並補充說，以降低風險與依賴，同時台灣正朝著更高的國內產量邁進

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾
    Hsiao said coastal reconnaissance and attack drones and small unmanned attack vessels included in a proposed supplementary budget would be manufactured domestically.
    句中的 "included in a proposed supplementary budget" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞短語 "coastal reconnaissance and attack drones and small unmanned attack vessels"，相當於關代子句 "which are included in..." 的簡化。
  • 現在分詞作結果狀語
    ...reducing the risk of misunderstandings and misperceptions.
    句尾的 "reducing..." 是現在分詞構句，放在主句後面表示伴隨或自然產生的結果，意思相當於 "and (it) reduces the risk..."。
  • 動名詞片語作受詞
    Greene said preventing such a conflict was a top priority for the administration...
    動名詞片語 "preventing such a conflict" 擔任句子中 say 的受詞子句裡的主詞，動名詞當主詞時視為單數，因此搭配單數動詞 "was"。
  • 關係代名詞子句作修飾語
    ...the 2026 Taipei Security Dialogue, organized by the Institute for National Defense and Security Research, a think tank funded by Taiwan's Ministry of National Defense.
    句尾的 "funded by Taiwan's Ministry of National Defense" 是過去分詞片語作後位修飾，修飾前面的名詞片語 "a think tank"，補充說明該智庫的資金來源。
