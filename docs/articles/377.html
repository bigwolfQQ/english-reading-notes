台灣每週流感就診人次突破 11.5 萬，累計 19 人死亡：疾管署

Taiwan weekly flu visits top 115,000, with 19 deaths: CDC

單字解析

  • outpatient n. / adj. 門診（病人）
    Weekly outpatient and emergency visits for flu-like illness surpassed 115,000 last week...
    上週類流感門急診就診人次突破 11.5 萬...
  • surpass v. 超越、勝過、突破
    Weekly outpatient and emergency visits for flu-like illness surpassed 115,000 last week...
    上週類流感門急診就診人次突破 11.5 萬...
  • upward trend n. phr. 上升趨勢
    ...extending a recent upward trend, the Centers for Disease Control (CDC) said Tuesday.
    ...延續了近期的上升趨勢，疾病管制署（CDC）於週二表示。
  • a total of phr. 總計、共
    A total of 115,136 outpatient and emergency visits for influenza-like illness were recorded...
    類流感門急診總就診人次達 115,136 人次...
  • previous adj. 先前的、以前的
    ...up 11.0 percent from the previous week, CDC Epidemic Intelligence Center Director Kuo Hung-wei (郭宏偉) said.
    ...較前一週上升 11.0%，CDC 疫情中心主任郭宏偉表示。
  • current adj. 當前的、目前的
    ...both highs for the current flu season, which the CDC defines as having begun last Oct. 1...
    ...兩項數據皆創下本流感季的新高，疾管署將其定義為自去年 10月1日開始...
  • indicate v. 指出、顯示
    Laboratory data indicates influenza A viruses remain dominant in Taiwan...
    實驗室數據顯示 A 型流感病毒在台灣依然佔主體...
  • account for phr. v. 佔（比例）、說明、解釋
    ...with the H1N1 subtype accounting for 79.4 percent of detected cases, Kuo said.
    ...其中 H1N1 亞型佔了檢出病例的 79.4%，郭宏偉說。
  • cumulatively adv. 累積地
    Cumulatively this flu season, Taiwan has recorded 1,329 severe influenza cases and 253 deaths...
    累計本流感季以來，台灣已記錄了 1,329 例流感重症及 253 例死亡...
  • involve v. 包含、涉及
    Of the total severe cases, 65.1 percent involved people aged 65 or older...
    在所有重症病例中，65.1% 的年齡在 65 歲或以上...
  • underlying adj. 潛在的、根本的
    ...82.8 percent had underlying chronic illnesses, and 68.6 percent had not received this season's influenza vaccine.
    ...82.8% 具有潛在慢性疾病，且 68.6% 未接種本季流感疫苗。
  • represent v. 代表、相當於、顯示
    ...representing a 9.7 percent drop from a week earlier.
    ...較前一週下降 9.7%。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（附加補充說明結果或狀況）
    ...surpassing 115,000 last week, with 105 severe cases and 19 deaths, extending a recent upward trend...
    句中的 "extending a recent upward trend" 是現在分詞（-ing）引導的分詞構句，用來補充說明前面子句所帶來的整體情況或結果，主詞概念即為前句的整起事件。
  • 關係代間詞子句修飾先行詞
    ...the current flu season, which the CDC defines as having begun last Oct. 1 and running through Sept. 30.
    句中的 "which the CDC defines as..." 是非限定關係子句，用逗號隔開，補充說明先行詞 "the current flu season"（本流感季）的定義與起訖時間。
  • 被動語態的運用
    A total of 115,136 outpatient and emergency visits for influenza-like illness were recorded in the week...
    新聞報導經常使用被動語態（be +過去分詞，如 "were recorded"），因為焦點在於「數據被記錄到／統計到」，而非強調是誰去記錄這些數據，使語氣更具客觀性。
