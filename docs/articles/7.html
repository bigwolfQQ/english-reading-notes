外交部長林佳龍在太平洋島國論壇痛批中國「戰狼」外交

Minister blasts China’s ‘wolf warrior’ diplomacy at PIF

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • condemn v. 譴責
    Minister of Foreign Affairs Lin Chia-lung yesterday condemned China’s “wolf warrior” diplomacy at this week’s Pacific Islands Forum (PIF) Leaders’ Meeting in Palau
    外交部長林佳龍昨天在帛琉舉行的本週太平洋島國論壇（PIF）領導人會議上，譴責中國的「戰狼」外交
  • presence n. 出席，存在
    This year’s meeting was overshadowed by China’s objections to the presence of Taiwan at the summit
    今年的會議因中國反對台灣出席峰會而蒙上陰影
  • qualified adj. 有資格的，勝任的
    saying Taipei was not qualified to attend and that its presence would have “consequences,” said Lin
    林佳龍表示，中國聲稱台北沒有資格參加，且其出席將會產生「後果」
  • comprise v. 包含，組成
    Lin said that the forum comprises 18 Pacific member countries, which form its decisionmaking core.
    林佳龍表示，該論壇由 18 個太平洋成員國組成，這些成員國構成了其決策核心。
  • promote v. 促進，推廣，晉升
    China, dialogue partners, and Taiwan as a development partner, were all invited guests seeking to promote development in the Pacific
    中國、對話夥伴以及作為發展夥伴的台灣，都是尋求促進太平洋發展的受邀嘉賓
  • behavior n. 行為，舉止
    This kind of threatening language and behavior — claiming there will be ‘serious consequences’... — is extremely absurd and ironic
    這種威脅性的言語和行為——聲稱會有『嚴重後果』……——極其荒謬且諷刺
  • destination n. 目的地
    even going so far as to declare Palau an ‘unsafe travel destination’ — is extremely absurd and ironic
    甚至進一步宣佈帛琉為『不安全的旅遊目的地』——極其荒謬且諷刺
  • impact n. 衝擊，影響
    The PIF leaders’ summit subsequently adopted a resolution condemning the missile launch and its impact on peace and unity in the Pacific
    太平洋島國論壇領導人峰會隨後通過了一項決議，譴責該飛彈試射及其對太平洋和平與團結的衝擊
  • approach n. 方法，途徑
    The Chinese Communist Party’s zero-sum, “either you or me” approach is not suited to the Pacific region
    中國共產黨零和的「不是你死就是我活」的方法並不適合太平洋地區
  • respond v. 回應，回答
    The Chinese Ministry of Foreign Affairs did not immediately respond to a request for comment.
    中國外交部未立即回應置評請求。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（-ing）片語作後位修飾 / 形容詞用
    China, dialogue partners, and Taiwan as a development partner, were all invited guests seeking to promote development in the Pacific, he said...
    句中的「seeking to promote development in the Pacific」為現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「invited guests」（受邀嘉賓），相當於關代子句「who were seeking to promote...」，在新聞英語中常用來精簡語句。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    This year’s meeting was overshadowed by China’s objections to the presence of Taiwan at the summit...
    句型使用「was overshadowed by...」，為主動轉被動的結構。在商業與新聞英語中，當強調動作的承受者（在此為 meeting）或為了客觀敘述時，經常會使用被動語態。
  • 形動詞 / 分詞構句表附帶狀況
    ...Lin said, who had just returned from the meeting in Palau.
    非限定性關係代名詞子句「who had just returned from...」用來補充說明主詞 Lin 的背景狀態，說明他剛從帛琉開會回來，這在英文新聞報導引用發言時是非常常見的句構。
