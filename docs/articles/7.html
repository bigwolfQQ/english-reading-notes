外交部長於太平洋島國論壇痛批中國「戰狼」外交

Minister blasts China’s ‘wolf warrior’ diplomacy at PIF

單字解析

  • blast v. 嚴厲批評、痛批
    Minister blasts China’s ‘wolf warrior’ diplomacy at PIF
  • undermine v. 削弱、破壞（信心、團結等）
    Minister of Foreign Affairs Lin Chia-lung (林佳龍) yesterday condemned China’s “wolf warrior” diplomacy at this week’s Pacific Islands Forum (PIF) Leaders’ Meeting in Palau, saying it could undermine unity in the Pacific.
  • overshadow v. 使……蒙上陰影；使相形見絀
    This year’s meeting was overshadowed by China’s objections to the presence of Taiwan at the summit, saying Taipei was not qualified to attend and that its presence would have “consequences,” said Lin, who had just returned from the meeting in Palau.
  • comprise v. 由……組成、包含
    Speaking to reporters in Taipei yesterday, Lin said that the forum comprises 18 Pacific member countries, which form its decisionmaking core.
  • detestable adj. 令人厭惡的、可恨的
    “This kind of Chinese ‘wolf warrior’ diplomacy could easily undermine unity in the Pacific, and is offensive and detestable,” he said.
  • departure n. 偏離、背離（常規或過往做法）
    The expression “wolf warrior” diplomacy takes its name from a popular Chinese movie franchise and refers to strong remarks by Chinese officials in public, marking a departure from the restraint generally seen previously.
  • restraint n. 克制、節制
    The expression “wolf warrior” diplomacy takes its name from a popular Chinese movie franchise and refers to strong remarks by Chinese officials in public, marking a departure from the restraint generally seen previously.
  • absurd adj. 荒謬的、荒唐的
    “This kind of threatening language and behavior — claiming there will be ‘serious consequences,’ even going so far as to declare Palau an ‘unsafe travel destination’ — is extremely absurd and ironic,” he said, referring to a travel warning China issued about Palau last month.
  • intimidation n. 恐嚇、威脅
    China’s threat goes beyond verbal intimidation, Lin added.
  • on the sidelines of phr. 在……的場邊；在（大型會議或活動）期間
    Asked whether he met with US Deputy Secretary of State Christopher Landau on the sidelines of the PIF summit, Lin said it was only natural for attendees to greet and interact with one another at the venue.

文法重點

  • 非限制性關係子句與過去完成式 (Non-restrictive Relative Clause with Past Perfect)
    This year’s meeting was overshadowed by China’s objections to the presence of Taiwan at the summit, saying Taipei was not qualified to attend and that its presence would have “consequences,” said Lin, who had just returned from the meeting in Palau.
    句尾的 `who had just returned from the meeting in Palau` 為非限制性關係子句，用來補充說明專有名詞/特定人名 Lin 的背景資訊。子句內使用了過去完成式 `had returned`，表示「從帛琉返回」這個動作發生在主要動詞「說（said）」之前。
  • 現在分詞作分詞構句（表示伴隨狀況或結果） (Present Participle Construction)
    The expression “wolf warrior” diplomacy takes its name from a popular Chinese movie franchise and refers to strong remarks by Chinese officials in public, marking a departure from the restraint generally seen previously.
    句末的 `marking a departure...` 為現在分詞引導的分詞構句，省略了連接詞與主詞，用來補充說明主要子句敘述的現象所產生的影響與結果（「這標誌著偏離了以往普遍看到的克制」）。
  • 過去分詞簡化句／分詞構句 (Reduced Clause with Past Participle)
    Asked whether he met with US Deputy Secretary of State Christopher Landau on the sidelines of the PIF summit, Lin said it was only natural for attendees to greet and interact with one another at the venue.
    句首的 `Asked whether...` 為被動語態的分詞構句，完整寫法為 "When he was asked whether..."。由於副詞子句的主詞與主要子句主詞相同（皆為 Lin），因此省略連接詞與主詞，並省略被動的 being，僅保留過去分詞 `Asked`。
  • 虛主詞 It 的句型 (Dummy Subject 'It' Construction)
    Lin said it was only natural for attendees to greet and interact with one another at the venue.
    主要子句中的 `it was only natural for attendees to greet and interact...` 採用了「It + be + adj. + for + 人 + to V」的虛主詞句型。其中 `it` 為形式主詞，真正的主詞是後面不定詞短語 `to greet and interact with one another at the venue`。
