林業署在網路惡作劇後重申高山湖泊禁令

Agency reiterates alpine lake ban after online hoax

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • prohibit v. 禁止
    The Forestry and Nature Conservation Agency yesterday reminded people that swimming is prohibited in alpine lakes after a hoax announcement of a Jiaming Lake (嘉明湖) “swimming carnival” was circulated on social media.
    林業及自然保育署昨天提醒民眾，在社群媒體流傳一則關於嘉明湖「游泳嘉年華」的惡作劇公告後，高山湖泊禁止游泳。
  • claim v. 聲稱、主張
    A person posted the event online claiming that a minimum of seven people could join in on Double Ten National Day for NT$81,000 per person.
    有人在網路上發布該活動，聲稱在國慶日當天，至少七人可組團參加，每人費用為新台幣 81,000 元。
  • participant n. 參與者
    They also asked potential participants to complete a 48-hour trek to reach the Taitung County lake, which is at an elevation of more than 3,000m, and then a 300m swim within 10 minutes.
    他們還要求潛在的參加者完成為期 48 小時的徒步健行，才能抵達海拔超過 3,000 公尺的台東縣湖泊，然後在 10 分鐘內完成 300 公尺的游泳。
  • organizer n. 主辦單位、組織者
    Organizers of competitions or events that could cause environmental disruption in natural forests, except regular hiking, existing ecological tours or traditional indigenous activities, must apply to the agency, it said.
    署方表示，可能對自然森林造成環境破壞的競賽或活動主辦單位，除了一般健行、現有生態旅遊或傳統原住民活動外，都必須向該署申請。
  • apply v. 申請
    Organizers of competitions or events that could cause environmental disruption in natural forests, except regular hiking, existing ecological tours or traditional indigenous activities, must apply to the agency, it said.
    署方表示，可能對自然森林造成環境破壞的競賽或活動主辦單位，除了一般健行、現有生態旅遊或傳統原住民活動外，都必須向該署申請。
  • restrict v. 限制
    Such events are restricted to existing hiking trails, forest roads or recreational public space, it added.
    署方補充說，此類活動限於現有的健行步道、林道或休閒公共空間。
  • approve v. 批准、贊成
    The agency would not approve any activity that might affect ecological environments, including operating motor vehicles on trails or closed roads, water activities such as swimming or standup paddleboarding in alpine lakes, or large-scale competitions or events at cultural heritage sites, former indigenous settlements or ecologically sensitive areas, it said.
    署方表示，不會批准任何可能影響生態環境的活動，包括在步道或封閉道路上行駛機動車輛、在高山湖泊進行游泳或立槳等水域活動，或是在文化遺址、前原住民聚落或生態敏感區舉辦大型競賽或活動。
  • reiterate v. 重申
    The agency reiterated that it would not allow such an activity, which is clearly inconsistent with the site’s conservation and management objectives.
    署方重申，絕不允許此類明顯與該地點保育及管理目標不符的活動。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作後置修飾 / 形容詞子句簡化
    A person posted the event online claiming that a minimum of seven people could join in on Double Ten National Day for NT$81,000 per person.
    句中的「claiming that...」是由「who claimed that...」簡化而來的現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前面發布活動的人的行為，使句子更為精簡緊湊。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句
    They also asked potential participants to complete a 48-hour trek to reach the Taitung County lake, which is at an elevation of more than 3,000m, and then a 300m swim within 10 minutes.
    句中的「which is at an elevation of more than 3,000m」前有逗號，屬於非限定用法，用來補充說明「the Taitung County lake」的海拔高度，即便刪除也不影響主要句意。
  • 多重否定與附加條件（except 用法）
    Organizers of competitions or events that could cause environmental disruption in natural forests, except regular hiking, existing ecological tours or traditional indigenous activities, must apply to the agency, it said.
    介系詞片語「except regular hiking, existing ecological tours or traditional indigenous activities」用來排除特定例外狀況，使主句「必須向該署申請」的規範對象更加精確。
