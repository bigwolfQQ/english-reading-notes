中信兄弟奪下中華職棒下半季冠軍

Brothers clinch CPBL second-half championship

單字解析

  • victory n. 勝利
    The CTBC Brothers on Sunday clinched the Chinese Professional Baseball League’s (CPBL) second-half title with a 12-1 victory over the TSG Hawks, securing a playoff berth after finishing last in the first half.
    中信兄弟隊於週日以 12 比 1 擊敗台鋼雄鷹，奪下中華職棒（CPBL）下半季冠軍，在經歷上半季墊底後，順利取得季後賽門票。
  • secure v. 獲得、取得、確保

  • record n. 紀錄、戰績
    Before a sellout crowd of 20,000 at Taichung Intercontinental Baseball Stadium, the Brothers finished the second half with a 36-24 record and celebrated their league-record 21st half-season title under a shower of yellow streamers.
    在台中洲際棒球場 2 萬名滿場球迷的見證下，兄弟隊以 36 勝 24 負的戰績結束下半季，並在黃色彩帶雨中慶祝他們締造聯盟紀錄的第 21 座半季冠軍。
  • turnaround n. 好轉、轉機、營運轉虧為盈

    這座冠軍完成了戲劇性的逆轉，他們在上半季僅拿下 20 勝、37 負外加 2 和，當時在六隊中敬陪末座。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 / 分詞構句
    The CTBC Brothers on Sunday clinched the Chinese Professional Baseball League’s (CPBL) second-half title with a 12-1 victory over the TSG Hawks, securing a playoff berth after finishing last in the first half.
    句尾的「securing a playoff berth...」是由分詞構句簡化而來，表示伴隨發生的結果（主詞 the CTBC Brothers 主動去確保了季後賽門票）。
  • be to + 原形動詞（表示預定、安排好的未來計畫）
    They are to face the Uni-President 7-Eleven Lions, who qualified by finishing second in the full-season standings, in a playoff series beginning on Friday.
    新聞報導中常使用「be to V」結構來表達官方安排、預定要執行的賽程或計畫。
  • 關係代間詞子句（非限定用法）
    They are to face the Uni-President 7-Eleven Lions, who qualified by finishing second in the full-season standings, in a playoff series beginning on Friday.
    逗號後的「who qualified...」是關係子句的非限定用法，用來補充說明 Uni-President 7-Eleven Lions 的額外資訊，逗號前後不可省略。
  • 條件句中的省略與假設用法 (if necessary)
    The Lions are to host Game 2 at Tainan’s Asia-Pacific International Baseball Stadium on Saturday before the series returns to Taichung on Sunday and, if necessary, on Monday next week.
    「if necessary」是「if it is necessary」的省略形式，意指「如果有必要的話」，在商業與新聞英文中非常常見。
