馬拉威的礦業帶來了希望與挑戰

Malawi’s mining offers promises and challenges

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • transform v. 使轉變，改造
    A new generation of mining projects targeting rare earth and critical minerals could transform Malawi’s economy
    一世代瞄準稀土和關鍵礦產的新採礦計畫可能會轉變馬拉威的經濟
  • depend on phr. v. 依賴，依靠
    One of the world’s poorest countries, Malawi has long depended on agriculture, aid and a handful of exports such as tobacco, tea and sugar.
    作為世界上最貧窮的國家之一，馬拉威長期以來一直依賴農業、援助以及少數如菸草、茶葉和糖等出口商品。
  • tangible adj. 有形的，切實的
    Given the large quantities of deposits here, we expect the mining project to bring tangible benefits to our people, particularly in terms of improved social services
    鑑於這裡有大量的礦藏，我們期望採礦計畫能為我們的人民帶來切實的好處，特別是在改善社會服務方面
  • contribute v. 貢獻，促成（此處指佔...比例）
    Mining contributes about 1 percent of Malawi’s GDP, according to figures cited by the Malawian Chamber of Mines and Energy.
    根據馬拉威礦業暨能源商會引用的數據，採礦業約佔馬拉威 GDP 的 1%。
  • estimate v. 估計，估算
    The World Bank estimates the sector could account for about 12 percent of GDP by next year.
    世界銀行估計，到明年該產業可能佔 GDP 的 12% 左右。
  • diversify v. 使多樣化（企業或經濟）
    Mining offers Malawi a realistic chance to diversify the economy, earn foreign exchange, create skilled jobs and build industries beyond agriculture
    採礦業為馬拉威提供了一個實際的機會來多元化經濟、賺取外匯、創造技術工作機會，並建立超越農業的產業
  • participation n. 參與，加入
    The Malawian government has already moved to improve its hand by passing a law in 2023 removing a 10 percent ceiling on state participation in the sector.
    馬拉威政府已經採取行動增強自身的籌碼，在 2023 年通過了一項法律，取消了國家參與該產業 10% 的上限。
  • attract v. 吸引
    It should also shore up its geological mapping to “negotiate from a stronger position, attract credible investors and avoid giving away valuable resources without fully understanding their potential,” Dassu told reporters.
    達蘇告訴記者，它還應該鞏固其地質繪圖，以便「從更有利的立場進行談判、吸引可靠的投資者，並避免在未充分了解其潛力的情況下贈送有價值的資源」。
  • revenue n. （政府或企業的）收益，營收
    Cumulative mineral exports could reach US$30 billion from this year to 2040, with annual revenues approaching US$3 billion by 2034.
    從今年到 2040 年，累積礦產出口額可能達到 30 億美元，年收益到 2034 年將接近 30 億美元。
  • negotiate v. 談判，協商
    It should also shore up its geological mapping to “negotiate from a stronger position, attract credible investors and avoid giving away valuable resources without fully understanding their potential,”
    它還應該鞏固其地質繪圖，以便「從更有利的立場進行談判、吸引可靠的投資者，並避免在未充分了解其潛力的情況下贈送有價值的資源」

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作後置修飾語
    A new generation of mining projects targeting rare earth and critical minerals could transform Malawi’s economy...
    句中的「targeting rare earth and critical minerals」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞短語「mining projects」，相當於形容詞子句「that/which target...」。這種結構在新聞英文中極為常見，能精簡語句表達。
  • 分詞構句表附帶結果或補充說明
    Cumulative mineral exports could reach US$30 billion from this year to 2040, with annual revenues approaching US$3 billion by 2034.
    句尾使用了「with + 受詞 + 現在分詞 (approaching...)」的複合結構，作為分詞構句的一種，用來補充說明主句發生時伴隨的情況或結果，中譯常為「隨著...」。
  • 不定詞片語作目的狀語
    The Malawian government has already moved to improve its hand by passing a law in 2023 removing a 10 percent ceiling on state participation in the sector.
    句中的「to improve its hand」是不定詞片語，放在動詞 moved 後面，用來表達採取行動的「目的」（為了改善其籌碼）。
