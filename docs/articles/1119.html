聖嬰現象帶來的熱浪可能增加 451,000 人死亡

El Nino heat could add 451,000 deaths

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • lead to phr. 導致、引起
    This year’s powerful El Nino and the record global heat it is driving, along with severe droughts, wildfires and shrinking water supplies, could lead to nearly half a million more deaths worldwide by the end of February, a research report released yesterday said.
    一項昨天發布的研究報告指出，今年威力強大的聖嬰現象以及它所推動的創紀錄全球高溫，再加上嚴重的乾旱、野火和不斷萎縮的水資源，可能導致全球在 2 月底前額外增加近 50 萬人死亡。
  • attribute to phr. 歸因於、歸咎於
    The grim warning by the University of Chicago-based Climate Impact Lab said that as many as 451,000 more heat-related deaths between June and the end of February could be attributed to El Nino, compared with heat deaths expected in a typical year.
    總部位於芝加哥大學的「氣候影響實驗室」發出嚴厲警告表示，與常年預期的熱浪死亡人數相比，從 6 月到 2 月底之間，多達 451,000 起與高溫相關的額外死亡人數可歸因於聖嬰現象。
  • persist v. 持續、堅持
    Michael Greenstone, a cofounder of the group and the study’s coauthor, said the heat danger would likely persist beyond February — even though El Nino is expected to end by then.
    該小組的共同創辦人兼研究報告共同作者麥可・格林斯通表示，即便預計聖嬰現象屆時會結束，高溫帶來的危險很可能在 2 月之後持續存在。
  • estimate v. / n. 估計、估價
    The study combined seasonal temperature forecasts with localized estimates of how high temperatures affect mortality across more than 24,000 regions worldwide.
    該研究將季節性溫度預測與全球 24,000 多個地區高溫如何影響死亡率的在地化估計結合起來。
  • project v. 預估、預測
    The projected fatalities are for the entire El Nino period, from June 2026 until the end of February 2027.
    預測的死亡人數涵蓋整個聖嬰現象期間，從 2026 年 6 月直到 2027 年 2 月底。
  • burden n. 負擔、重擔
    The study also warns that developing countries would bear the heaviest burden of the excessive heat.
    該研究還警告說，開發中國家將承受過度高溫最沉重的負擔。
  • measure n. 措施、方法
    With such staggering numbers, there are recommended measures that governments can take, the report said.
    報告指出，面對如此驚人的數字，政府可以採取一些建議的措施。
  • resources n. 資源
    The authors also call for what they describe as anticipatory financing, so governments can move resources before dangerous heat peaks.
    作者也呼籲他們所描述的「預期性融資」，以便政府能夠在危險高溫達到高峰之前調動資源。
  • implement v. 實施、執行
    While it is one thing to have well thought-out plans — as India does — implementing these plans and raising needed funds remains a challenge, he said.
    他說，擁有深思熟慮的計畫是一回事——印度就是如此——但落實這些計畫並籌措所需資金依然是一項挑戰。
  • effectively adv. 有效地
    “The real measure of our preparedness is not how many heat warnings we issue, but how effectively we protect people and reduce preventable deaths,” Tiwari added.
    蒂瓦里補充說：「我們準備就緒的真正衡量標準，不在於我們發布了多少次高溫警報，而在於我們多麼有效地保護了人民並減少了可預防的死亡。」

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    This year’s powerful El Nino and the record global heat it is driving, along with severe droughts, wildfires and shrinking water supplies, could lead to nearly half a million more deaths worldwide by the end of February, a research report released yesterday said.
    句中的「released yesterday」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「a research report（研究報告）」。這相當於形容詞子句「which was released yesterday」的簡化寫法，省略了「which was」，讓長句更加精簡流暢。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The grim warning by the University of Chicago-based Climate Impact Lab said that as many as 451,000 more heat-related deaths between June and the end of February could be attributed to El Nino...
    句中的「could be attributed to」使用了被動語態（情態動詞 + be + 過去分詞）。因為死亡人數是「被歸因於」聖嬰現象，而非主動去歸因，所以使用被動語態來表達因果關係。
  • 假設語氣條件句 (Subjunctive / Conditional Sentence)
    “If you’re already in a hot place, the extra couple of degrees are going to be much more dangerous,” he said.
    這是一個表真實可能的條件句（First Conditional）。「If」引導條件子句使用現在式（are），主要子句則使用「be going to」來預測未來發生的結果，用以表達在特定假設情況下必然產生的影響。
  • 間接問句 / 名詞子句 (Indirect Question / Noun Clause)
    “The real measure of our preparedness is not how many heat warnings we issue, but how effectively we protect people and reduce preventable deaths,” Tiwari added.
    句中的「how many heat warnings we issue」與「how effectively we protect people...」是由疑問詞引導的名詞子句，在句中分別作為對等連接詞「not only... but also...」概念中的對應表語，用來具體說明「準備就緒的衡量標準」是什麼。
