單字解析

  • depart v. 出發，啟程
    Taiwan's Foreign Minister Lin Chia-lung (林佳龍) departed Sunday for Belize and Guatemala with a business delegation...
    台灣外交部長林佳龍週日率領商界代表團啟程前往貝里斯和瓜地馬拉...
  • delegation n. 代表團
    ...departed Sunday for Belize and Guatemala with a business delegation to strengthen economic cooperation...
    ...週日率領商界代表團啟程前往貝里斯和瓜地馬拉，以加強經濟合作...
  • strengthen v. 加強，鞏固
    ...to strengthen economic cooperation with Taiwan's two Central American diplomatic allies, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) said.
    外交部表示，此舉旨在加強與台灣兩個中美洲外交盟友的經濟合作。
  • cooperation n. 合作
    ...to strengthen economic cooperation with Taiwan's two Central American diplomatic allies...
    ...以加強與台灣兩個中美洲外交盟友的經濟合作...
  • press release n. 新聞稿
    ...MOFA said in a press release.
    外交部在新聞稿中表示。
  • anniversary n. 週年紀念
    ...marking the 92nd anniversary of diplomatic relations and Taiwan's National Day, the ministry said.
    該部表示，此活動旨在標誌外交關係建立92週年及台灣國慶。
  • comprise v. 組成，包含
    According to MOFA, the delegation comprises more than 30 representatives from sectors including...
    根據外交部指出，代表團由來自...等領域的30多位代表組成。
  • sector n. 部門，行業，領域
    ...representatives from sectors including green energy and smart transportation, artificial intelligence and space technology...
    ...來自綠能與智慧交通、人工智慧與太空科技等領域的代表...
  • promote v. 促進，推動，宣傳
    The U.S.-Taiwan Business Council, which promotes trade and business ties between the United States and Taiwan, is also taking part...
    促進美國與台灣貿易及商業聯繫的「美國－台灣商務協會」也參與其中...
  • take part in phr. v. 參加，參與
    ...is also taking part in the delegation, MOFA added.
    外交部補充說，該協會也加入了此次代表團。
  • deepen v. 加深，深化
    ...to deepen strategic and economic cooperation and strengthen diplomatic ties under the so-called Diplomatic Allies Prosperity Project.
    ...在所謂的「友邦繁榮計畫」下，深化戰略與經濟合作並鞏固外交關係。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾
    The U.S.-Taiwan Business Council, which promotes trade and business ties between the United States and Taiwan, is also taking part in the delegation, MOFA added.
    句中的「which promotes trade...」為關係代間子句，用來修飾前面的專有名詞「The U.S.-Taiwan Business Council」，補充說明該委員會的功能與宗旨。這在商業新聞中非常常見，用以提供背景資訊。
  • 過去分詞片語作形容詞用（修飾名詞）
    ...and inspect several Taiwan-funded projects in the two countries, MOFA said in a press release.
    句中的「Taiwan-funded」是由名詞加上過去分詞構成的複合形容詞，用來修飾後方的名詞「projects」，意指「由台灣出資贊助的」專案。多益考試常考這類由分詞變化的複合形容詞。
  • 現在分詞引導的時間/附帶狀況狀語
    Since taking office in May 2024, Lin has visited Taiwan's diplomatic allies around the world...
    句首的「Since taking office...」是省略了主詞與be動詞的分詞構句（原句形如 Since he took office...）。「taking office」為現在分詞片語，表達「就職」的這個動作與後方主句的主角（Lin）為主動關係。
