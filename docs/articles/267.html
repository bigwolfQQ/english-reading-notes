美國聯邦航空總署正調查邁阿密致命墜機事故的原因

FAA investigating cause of fatal crash in Miami

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • investigate v. 調查
    US Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) investigators yesterday were detailing their plans for determining what caused an Amazon.com Inc cargo jet to barrel off a runway...
    美國聯邦航空總署（FAA）調查人員昨天正詳細說明他們的計畫，以判定是什麼原因導致亞馬遜公司的一架貨機衝出跑道...
  • cargo n. 貨物
    ...what caused an Amazon.com Inc cargo jet to barrel off a runway at Miami International Airport...
    ...是什麼原因導致亞馬遜公司的一架貨機在邁阿密國際機場衝出跑道...
  • injure v. 傷害、使受傷
    ...killing at least five people and injuring five others as it struck vehicles and was engulfed in flames.
    ...造成至少五人死亡、另外五人受傷，當時它撞上車輛並陷入火海。
  • severe adj. 嚴重的
    Five people were killed, three critically injured and two others hospitalized with less severe injuries, officials said.
    官員表示，造成五人死亡、三人傷勢危急，另有兩人住院治療且傷勢較輕。
  • attempt n. 嘗試、企圖
    Aviation safety expert Steve Arroyo said if it was beyond that area, the plane should have aborted the landing and gone around for another attempt.
    航空安全專家史蒂夫・阿羅約（Steve Arroyo）表示，如果超出該區域，飛機應該放棄降落並繞飛以進行另一次嘗試。
  • contribute v. 促成、導致、貢獻
    Schiavo said that if the plane had a tail wind, that could have contributed to it floating further down the runway.
    希阿沃表示，如果飛機遭遇順風，這可能會助長飛機進一步滑向跑道深處。
  • equipped adj. 配備有...的
    The Miami airport is not equipped with arresting systems that the FAA says have been installed at more than 120 airports nationwide...
    邁阿密機場未配備攔截系統，FAA 表示該系統已安裝在全國 120 多個機場...
  • maintenance n. 維護、保養
    Schiavo said the NTSB will also closely examine the maintenance history of this plane...
    希阿沃表示，國家運輸安全委員會（NTSB）也將密切檢查這架飛機的維護歷史...
  • delayed adj. 延誤的
    More than 160 flights were canceled and nearly 325 delayed by late Sunday...
    截至週日晚間，已有 160 多個航班被取消，近 325 個航班延誤...
  • cooperate v. 合作、配合
    Amazon said in a statement that it would cooperate with any investigation.
    亞馬遜在一份聲明中表示，將全力配合任何調查。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The crash, which sent thick black smoke billowing into the sky, trapped the pilot and copilot in the cockpit and some occupants in cars...
    句中的 "billowing into the sky" 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾其前面的受詞 "thick black smoke"，補充說明濃煙往天空翻騰的動態狀態。
  • 過去分詞作形容詞用 / 被動語態分詞簡化 (Past Participle for State)
    Five people were killed, three critically injured and two others hospitalized with less severe injuries, officials said.
    此處使用被動語態的簡化結構（或形容詞化的過去分詞），"killed"、"injured"、"hospitalized" 分別描述主詞在事故中所處的受傷與就醫狀態。
  • 與過去事實相反的假設語氣 (Subjunctive Mood for Past Unreal Condition)
    Schiavo said that if the plane had a tail wind, that could have contributed to it floating further down the runway.
    此句使用 "if + 過去完成式 (had...)，主詞 + could have + 過去分詞" 的句型，表達對過去某種假設情況（飛機當時若遭遇順風）及其可能造成的結果之推測。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    The Miami airport is not equipped with arresting systems that the FAA says have been installed at more than 120 airports nationwide...
    句中的 "that the FAA says have been installed..." 是限定用法的關係子句，修飾先行詞 "arresting systems"，其中夾雜了插入語 "the FAA says"。
