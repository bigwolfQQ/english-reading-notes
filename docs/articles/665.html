文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    ...the intent of the law in Article 33 "has been incorporated into Article 7 of the guidelines..."
    句型使用現在完成式的被動語態 (has been + 過去分詞 incorporated)，強調「第33條的法律意旨」是承受「被納入」的動作，焦點在於法規內容的改變狀態。
  • be prohibited from + V-ing (被動語態與介系詞片語)
    Instead it says that individuals are prohibited from serving as a member of any group in China...
    prohibit 通常用於 prohibit someone from doing something（禁止某人做某事）。在此改為被動語態 (are prohibited from)，用來強調個人「被禁止」從事後方接續動名词片語 (serving as...) 的行為。
  • 現在分詞作修飾語 (Present Participle as Modifier)
    Invitees should carefully examine organizers' backgrounds, itineraries and promotional materials, and avoid participating in national-level events organized by Chinese authorities that carry potential united front objectives, the ministry said.
    句尾的關係代間詞子句中包含了過去分詞片語 (organized by Chinese authorities) 修飾 events，而開頭則有形容詞性質的 promotional（宣傳用的）及帶有主動意味的 potential（潛在的）來修飾名詞，展現多益英文中常見的豐富名詞修飾結構。
