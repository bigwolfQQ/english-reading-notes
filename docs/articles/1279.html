文法重點

  • 非限定性關係子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Chao told CNA that Tanja Graf, managing director of Literaturhaus München, which organized the event, had confirmed the attempted interference.
    句中的「which organized the event」前面帶有逗號，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「Literaturhaus München（慕尼黑文學之家）」。這種非限定性關係子句不具限制性，拿掉之後不影響主要句意，常出現在新聞英語中以補充額外背景資訊。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    Yang's public reading and discussion about her International Booker Prize-winning novel "Taiwan Travelogue" (臺灣漫遊錄) was also overshadowed by controversy involving her interpreter.
    句中的「International Booker Prize-winning」是由過去分詞（winning）構成的複合形容詞，用來修飾前面的小說「novel」；此外，後面「involving her interpreter」則是現在分詞片語作後位修飾，用來補充說明前面的「controversy（爭議）」，在多益閱讀測驗中是常見的句構題型。
  • 間接引語與時態一致 (Indirect Speech & Sequence of Tenses)
    He said Yang's talk took place during Munich's Oktoberfest and that, to save money, the institute hired a local translator who had been recommended by an association of Taiwanese expatriates in Berlin.
    新聞報導常使用過去式（如 said）來引述他人先前說過的話（間接引語）。在此句中，引述內容裡使用過去式（took place, hired）以及過去完成式（had been recommended），表現出動作發生的先後順序，這是多益測驗考查動詞時態的重要概念。
