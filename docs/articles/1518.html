文法重點

  • 非限定性關係子句 (Non-defining relative clause)
    Lin is leading a delegation to Poland, which visited a site in Miekinia in southwestern Poland where the Taiwan Electrical and Electronic Manufacturers’ Association has proposed building a technology park.
    句中的「which visited a site...」前面有逗號，屬於非限定性關係子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞（即前往波蘭的代表團），在翻譯時順順地接在後面補充即可。
  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 (Present participle as post-modifier)
    Poland is the largest economy in central and eastern Europe, with a strong industrial base, well-developed infrastructure and quality talent, making it an excellent gateway for Taiwanese companies to enter the European market, Lin said.
    句尾的「making it an excellent gateway...」是由現在分詞片語引導的結果狀語，表示前面所提的各項優勢「自然而然促成了」波蘭成為絕佳門戶的結果，多益考試中常考這種分詞構句的用法。
  • 現在完成分詞作形容詞/分詞構句 (Perfect participle structure)
    Having decades of experience in developing science parks, Taiwan aims to establish a high-tech industry cluster in Miekinia, covering fields such as advanced manufacturing, data centers, smart cities, electric vehicles, information and communications technology, and business services, he said.
    句首的「Having decades of experience...」是現在完成式的分詞構句（原意為 Because Taiwan has decades of experience...），強調「擁有數十年經驗」這個動作發生在主要子句的目標確立之前。
