文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The US on Monday reiterated its commitment to the defense of key Asian allies Japan and South Korea, and the three nations expressed support for Taiwan ahead of a summit this week between US President Donald Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping (習近平).
    句中的「ahead of a summit this week between US President Donald Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping」是由介系詞片語構成的修飾語，用來補充說明前面子句發生的時間點。而在新聞英文中，常利用分詞或介系詞片語來交代背景，使句子結構緊湊。
  • 分詞片語作補充說明 (Participle Phrase for Additional Information)
    They issued a joint statement vowing, in apparent reference to China, to stand firm “against economic coercion and non-market policies and practices”...
    句尾的「vowing...」是現在分詞（V-ing）引導的分詞片語，在此作結果或補充說明，等於「and they vowed...」，用來修飾前面的主詞與動詞動作，使語氣流暢並避免使用過多的連接詞。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    They also welcomed the launch of a new trilateral consultation mechanism on economic security, part of broader efforts to strengthen supply chains and cooperation on critical technologies.
    此處使用同位語（部分省略了關代與be動詞，原意為 which is part of...）來補充說明前面的名詞短語「a new trilateral consultation mechanism」，這種結構在商業與新聞英文中非常常見，用來塞入更多背景細節。
  • 形容詞子句 / 關係代名詞引導的修飾 (Adjective Clause)
    “Peace and stability across the Taiwan Strait are not only critical to the security and prosperity of the Indo-Pacific, but also closely tied to the stability of the global economy and supply chains,” Ministry of Foreign Affairs spokesman Hsiao Kuang-wei (蕭光偉) said separately yesterday.
    句中運用了「not only... but also...」的對等連接詞結構，將台海和平穩定對「印太地區的安全與繁榮」以及對「全球經濟與供應鏈的穩定」之雙重重要性做強烈的並列強調。
