拯救印度運動員免於幽閉恐懼症的「獨特點子」

‘Unique idea’ to save Indian athletes from cabin fever

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • accommodation n. 住宿，住所
    India has gone to great lengths to give its athletes a taste of the accommodation at the Asian Games
    印度不遺餘力地讓其運動員提前體驗亞運會的住宿環境
  • temporarily adv. 暫時地
    temporarily housing them in shipping container-style units to replicate the lodgings in Japan.
    暫時將他們安置在貨櫃風格的單間裡，以複製日本當地的住所。
  • cut costs phr. 削減成本，節省開支
    Nagoya and Aichi are to stage the Games from Sept. 19 to Oct. 4, but in a bid to cut costs there would not be an athletes’ village.
    名古屋和愛知縣將於 9 月 19 日至 10 月 4 日舉辦亞運會，但為了削減成本，將不會設立運動員村。
  • participant n. 參與者
    There is a third type of accommodation: temporary units mostly made of wood and expected to be home to about 2,000 participants.
    還有第三種住宿類型：主要由木材製成的臨時組合屋，預計將容納大約 2,000 名參與者。
  • unveil v. 揭幕，公開，展出
    To prepare its competitors, the Sports Authority of India (SAI) on Friday unveiled similar lodgings at athlete headquarters in Patiala and Bengaluru.
    為了讓選手做好準備，印度體育局（SAI）於週五在帕蒂亞拉和班加羅爾的運動員總部揭幕了類似的住所。
  • equipped adj. 配備有...的
    They are equipped with four beds, two air conditioners, a fridge, storage tables, toilet, washroom and washing machine.
    它們配備了四張床、兩台空調、冰箱、置物桌、馬桶、衛浴間以及洗衣機。
  • logistics n. 後勤，物流
    “A player’s psychology is so strong that if there is any problem in such logistics then their level of competition gets affected, and we took care of this,”
    庫馬爾在導覽時表示：「球員的心理素質非常強大，如果在這種後勤安排上有任何問題，他們的競技水準就會受到影響，而我們照顧到了這一點。」
  • experience v. 體驗，經歷
    Kumar, who credited India’s Sports Minister Mansukh Mandaviya with the “unique idea,” said the accommodation was as close as possible to what athletes would experience in Japan.
    庫馬爾將這個「獨特的點子」歸功於印度體育部長曼蘇克·曼達維亞，並表示該住宿盡可能地貼近運動員在日本將體驗到的環境。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 / 補充說明
    The SAI, which arranged for journalists to visit the two cabins in Patiala and speak to athletes, says a roster has been prepared for Indian competitors to stay in the containers before flying to Japan.
    句中 before flying to Japan 省略了主詞與be動詞（before they are/were flying），保留現在分詞作時間副詞子句的簡化寫法，在多益閱讀測驗中相當常見。
  • so ... that ... 結果構句
    “A player’s psychology is so strong that if there is any problem in such logistics then their level of competition gets affected, and we took care of this,”
    「so + 形容詞 + that + 子句」用來表達「如此……以至於……」，用以強調球員心理素質強大的程度，進而影響競技表現。
  • as ... as possible 原級比較
    Kumar, who credited India’s Sports Minister Mansukh Mandaviya with the “unique idea,” said the accommodation was as close as possible to what athletes would experience in Japan.
    「as + 形容詞 + as possible」意為「盡可能……」，在此用來形容住宿條件盡可能貼近日本當地的真實情況。
