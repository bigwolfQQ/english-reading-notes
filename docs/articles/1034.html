當人工智慧不再尋求許可

When AI stops asking for permission

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • evaluation n. 評估，評價
    During an internal evaluation that began in May, agents assigned deliberately hard cybersecurity tasks found that they could leave messages for each other...
    在 5 月開始的一項內部評估中，被指派故意高難度資安任務的代理程式發現，它們可以互相留訊息……
  • repository n. 儲存庫，知識庫
    ...agents assigned deliberately hard cybersecurity tasks found that they could leave messages for each other inside Artifactory, a shared software repository.
    ……被指派故意高難度資安任務的代理程式發現，它們可以在共用軟體儲存庫 Artifactory 內部互相留訊息。
  • outage n. （電力等）中斷，停電，故障停機
    After the high volume of activity on the message board caused an outage on July 4, the agents rebuilt it through a different route within days.
    在留言板上的大量活動導致 7 月 4 日發生當機後，這些代理程式在幾天內透過另一條路徑重建了它。
  • breach n. 違背，破壞，漏洞入侵
    By mid-July, that persistence led to a real breach: Agents chained several vulnerabilities together, reached the open Internet and entered systems at Hugging Face...
    到了 7 月中旬，這種堅持不懈導致了一次真實的漏洞入侵：代理程式將數個漏洞串聯在一起，接觸到公開的網際網路，並進入了 Hugging Face 的系統……
  • resignation n. 辭職
    On Sept. 8, Jacob Coxon, who had spent three years doing pretraining research at OpenAI and Anthropic, announced his resignation from Anthropic.
    9 月 8 日，曾在 OpenAI 和 Anthropic 從事預訓練研究三年的雅各布·考克斯森宣布辭去 Anthropic 的職務。
  • access n. 接近（或進入）之權利，通道，訪問權限
    An AI system does not need resentment, anger or greed for more resources to attack a network and cause damage. It needs a goal, enough capability to pursue it, some access and a guardrail with a gap in it.
    AI 系統不需要怨恨、憤怒或對更多資源的貪婪來攻擊網路並造成損害。它需要一個目標、足夠的追求能力、一些訪問權限以及一個有漏洞的安全護欄。
  • deployment n. 部署，調動
    We build protective layers: limited access, separate authorization for sensitive actions, records nobody involved can quietly edit, outside testing before deployment.
    我們建立保護層：有限的訪問權限、對敏感操作的獨立授權、參與各方都無法悄悄編輯的記錄、部署前的外部測試。
  • circumvent v. 設法避開，規避
    We have trained these systems for years to persist, to circumvent obstacles, to keep trying new approaches.
    多年來，我們一直在訓練這些系統堅持不懈、規避障礙、不斷嘗試新方法。
  • autonomous adj. 自主的，獨立自主的
    That is a useful approach for training an assistant, but hazardous in the case of an autonomous system with broad access and no one who is reliably able to curtail it.
    對於訓練助理來說，這是一種有用的方法，但在具有廣泛訪問權限且沒有人能夠可靠地限制它的自主系統的情況下，這是危險的。
  • authority n. 權力，權威
    The question is no longer how clever these systems can become, but how much authority we hand them before anyone checks whether they still answer to us.
    問題不再是這些系統能變得多麼聰明，而是我們在任何人檢查它們是否仍然對我們負責之前，賦予它們多少權力。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾語（分詞構句）
    During an internal evaluation that began in May, agents assigned deliberately hard cybersecurity tasks found that their...
    句中的「assigned deliberately hard cybersecurity tasks」是一個過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 agents（代理程式）。其完整未簡化的形式為「agents (which were) assigned deliberately hard cybersecurity tasks」，省略了「關係代代名詞 + be 動詞」，在新聞英語中非常常見，能讓句子更為精煉。
  • 獨立分詞構句（Absolute Phrase）
    A person can be technically in the loop while a thousand AI agents trade tens of thousands of messages and take thousands of actions before that person has reviewed even one.
    雖然本句使用連接詞 while，但在文章其他地方常運用分詞結構來表達附帶狀況。例如描述動作伴隨發生時，分詞短語不與主句主詞相同時會保留其獨立主詞，用來補充說明主動作發生時的背景狀況。
  • 否定詞置於句首之倒裝句（Inversion）
    Nor is it a problem only for outside critics.
    當否定副詞或連接詞（如 Nor, Neither）置於句首時，句子必須採取部分倒裝（將助動詞、be 動詞或情態動詞提到主詞前面）。本句中「Nor is it...」相當於「And it is not... either」，置於句首能加強語氣並與前文緊密銜接。
