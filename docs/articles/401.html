氣象署警告可能致命的長浪

CWA warns of potentially deadly waves

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • warn v. 警告，告誡
    The Central Weather Administration (CWA) yesterday warned the public of potentially deadly hazards posed by swells
    中央氣象署（CWA）昨天警告民眾，應防範由長浪所構成的潛在致命威脅
  • record v. 記錄，記載
    From 2000 to November last year, the CWA recorded 445 incidents involving freak waves along Taiwan’s coasts
    從2000年到去年11月，氣象署在台灣沿海記錄到445起異常浪況事件
  • affect v. 影響
    Wave conditions are primarily affected by wind speed, the size of the area over which the wind blows and the duration of the wind, Lin said.
    林芳如表示，海浪狀況主要受風速、風吹拂的面積大小以及風吹持續時間的影響。
  • duration n. 持續時間，期間
    Wave conditions are primarily affected by wind speed, the size of the area over which the wind blows and the duration of the wind, Lin said.
    林芳如表示，海浪狀況主要受風速、風吹拂的面積大小以及風吹持續時間的影響。
  • produce v. 產生，生產
    Stronger winds blowing over a wider area for a longer period produce larger waves with greater energy, she said.
    她表示，更強的風在更廣的區域吹拂更長的時間，會產生能量更大的較大浪潮。
  • by comparison phr. 相較之下
    By comparison, typhoons generally move at about 5kph to 20kph.
    相比之下，颱風通常以每小時約5公里至20公里的速度移動。
  • in cooperation with phr. 與...合作
    In cooperation with National Cheng Kung University’s Coastal Hydraulics Center, the CWA has developed a Coastal Abnormal Wave Warning System
    氣象署與國立成功大學水工試驗所合作，開發了「海岸異常浪況預警系統」
  • develop v. 開發，研發
    In cooperation with National Cheng Kung University’s Coastal Hydraulics Center, the CWA has developed a Coastal Abnormal Wave Warning System to improve warnings of dangerous waves near shore.
    氣象署與國立成功大學水工試驗所合作，開發了「海岸異常浪況預警系統」，以改善對近岸危險浪況的預警。
  • probability n. 可能性，機率
    The Ocean Abnormal Wave Warning System uses nonlinear wave theory and wave models to provide the probability of abnormal waves occurring in the following 24, 36 and 48 hours.
    海洋異常浪況預警系統運用非線性海浪理論與海浪模式，提供未來24、36及48小時內發生異常浪況的機率。
  • monitor v. 監測，監控
    The CWA also uses data from buoys to monitor waves, currents, water temperatures, wind, air temperatures and atmospheric pressure.
    氣象署並利用來自浮標的數據來監測海浪、海流、水溫、風力、氣溫以及大氣壓力。
  • collect v. 收集，採集
    The solar-powered buoys generally collect observations hourly when sufficient power is available
    太陽能浮標在電力充足時通常每小時收集一次觀測數據
  • sufficient adj. 足夠的，充分的
    The solar-powered buoys generally collect observations hourly when sufficient power is available
    太陽能浮標在電力充足時通常每小時收集一次觀測數據

文法重點

  • 過去分詞作後置修飾語 (Past Participle as Postmodifier)
    The Central Weather Administration (CWA) yesterday warned the public of potentially deadly hazards posed by swells...
    句中的「posed by swells」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 hazards（威脅）。原本的概念是「which are posed by swells」，這裡省略了「which are」，直接用過去分詞片語放在被修飾的名詞後面，形成精簡的形容詞功能。
  • 現在分詞作結果狀語 (Present Participle indicating Result)
    ...recording 445 incidents involving freak waves along Taiwan’s coasts, resulting in 778 people falling into the sea.
    句尾的「resulting in...」是現在分詞片語，在此處表自然延伸的結果（相當於 and resulted in...）。這種句構在新聞英文中非常常見，用來表達某個事件所造成的後續影響或數據統計。
  • 對等連接詞引導的現在分詞並列
    ...creating dangerous conditions, she said, adding that unusually large waves despite calm local winds could indicate severe weather systems offshore.
    句中的「adding that...」是現在分詞片語，伴隨主句的動詞 said 一起發生，用來補充說明說話者（she）除了前面那句話之外，還「另外補充了」後面的內容。
  • 副詞子句的省略（條件句簡化）
    The solar-powered buoys generally collect observations hourly when sufficient power is available...
    句中的「when sufficient power is available」是一個由連接詞 when 引導的時間副詞子句，用來修飾主句的動詞 collect，說明浮標收集數據的時間條件。
