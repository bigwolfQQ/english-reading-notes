台灣作家吳明益《複眼人》立陶宛文版發行

Lithuanian edition of Wu Ming-yi's 'The Man with the Compound Eyes' released

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • on the verge of phr. 接近於、瀕臨……
    a woman on the verge of suicide
    一個瀕臨自殺的女人
  • convey v. 傳達、表達（想法、意思等）
    the biggest challenge was not understanding the Chinese text but conveying its nuances and style in his native language.
    最大的挑戰不是理解中文文本，而是在他的母語中傳達其語氣和風格。
  • considerable adj. 相當大的、相當多的
    He said he spent considerable time adjusting the tone and wording
    他說他花了相當多的時間調整語調和用詞
  • preserve v. 維護、保存、保留
    to achieve natural fluency in Lithuanian while preserving the literary texture of the Chinese original.
    以在立陶宛語中實現自然的流利度，同時保留中文原著的文學質感。
  • intentionally adv. 故意地、有意地
    He therefore intentionally rendered some chapters in the present tense
    因此他刻意將某些章節翻譯成現在式
  • earn v. 獲得、賺得（學位、聲譽、錢等）
    after earning a bachelor's degree in theater
    在獲得戲劇學士學位後
  • invitation n. 邀請
    at the invitation of the World Federation of Taiwanese Associations.
    應世界台灣協會聯合會之邀。
  • connect v. 連接、聯繫、建立關係
    to help Chinese readers connect with the literary world of the original work.
    幫助中國讀者與原著的文學世界產生連結。
  • portray v. 描繪、描寫
    To more accurately portray a scene involving the cracking and eating of sunflower seeds
    為了更精確地描繪一個涉及嗑瓜子和吃瓜子的場景
  • diversity n. 多樣性、多元
    Taiwan's diversity within a relatively small geographic area was particularly appealing.
    台灣在相對狹小的地理範圍內展現的多樣性特別吸引人。
  • combination n. 結合、組合
    Its combination of mountainous and coastal landscapes was a new experience
    其山脈與海岸景觀的結合是一種全新的體驗

文法重點

  • 關係代間詞子句 (Relative Clause with whose)
    contrasting it with European languages whose grammatical tenses can constantly remind readers that events took place in the past.
    在此用所有格關係代間詞「whose」來連接先行詞（European languages）與子句，表達「這些歐洲語言的語法時態」，在多益閱讀測驗的文法題中常考 who / whom / whose / which 的格位選填。
  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況 (Participial Phrase indicating circumstance)
    In doing so, he deliberately retained Lithuanian cultural details while adding explanations to help Chinese readers...
    句尾的「while adding explanations...」為分詞構句（省略了主詞與be動詞，還原完整子句應為 while he was adding explanations...），用來表達在進行主要動作的同時伴隨發生的另一個動作。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The Lithuanian translation of Taiwanese writer Wu Ming-yi's 'The Man with the Compound Eyes' has been released in Lithuania.
    句型使用現在完成式的被動語態「has been released」，強調動作的承受者（立陶宛文譯本）已經被發行，是新聞英語與商業書信中極為常見的句型結構。
