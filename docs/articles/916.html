關於移交船隻給菲律賓

On ship transfer to Philippines

單字解析

  • plan v. 計畫，打算
    News that the Coast Guard Administration plans to transfer a decommissioned vessel to the Philippines has raised eyebrows...
    海巡署計劃將一艘除役艦艇移交給菲律賓的消息引發了關注……
  • transfer v. / n. 轉移，移交，調動
    The government should offer a clear explanation on how the transfer aligns with national interests...
    政府應就此移交如何符合國家利益提供明確的解釋……
  • explain v. 解釋，說明
    The government should offer a clear explanation on how the transfer aligns with national interests...
    政府應就此移交如何符合國家利益提供明確的解釋……
  • align with phr. v. 與……一致，符合
    The government should offer a clear explanation on how the transfer aligns with national interests...
    政府應就此移交如何符合國家利益提供明確的解釋……
  • cost n. 成本，費用
    Ships are often decommissioned to make way for a better alternative, due to high costs or because they no longer meet the nation’s needs.
    基於高昂成本或不再符合國家需求，船隻通常會除役以為更好的替代方案騰出空間。
  • alternative n. 替代方案
    Ships are often decommissioned to make way for a better alternative...
    船隻通常會除役以為更好的替代方案騰出空間……
  • impact n. 影響
    Transferring such a vessel has no impact on Taiwan’s ability to patrol the seas.
    移交此類船隻對台灣巡邏海域的能力沒有影響。
  • benefit v. 有益於，受益
    Instead, it benefits the nation by streamlining its fleet.
    相反地，它透過精簡艦隊使國家受益。
  • response n. 回應，反應
    If the Philippines ever needs to activate the vessel Taiwan transferred it in response to incursions...
    如果菲律賓未來需要啟動台灣為回應入侵而移交的艦艇……
  • assistance n. 協助，援助
    Even after receiving the ship, the Philippines would still require significant assistance from Taiwan on the vessel’s operation and maintenance.
    即使在接收該船之後，菲律賓仍需要台灣在該船的運作和維護方面給予重大協助。
  • operation n. 運作，營運，操作
    ...significant assistance from Taiwan on the vessel’s operation and maintenance.
    ……台灣在該船的運作和維護方面給予重大協助。
  • maintenance n. 維護，保養
    ...significant assistance from Taiwan on the vessel’s operation and maintenance.
    ……台灣在該船的運作和維護方面給予重大協助。
  • diplomatic adj. 外交的
    ...and contribute to diplomatic efforts.
    ……並為外交努力做出貢獻。

文法重點

  • 名詞子句（同位語子句）
    News that the Coast Guard Administration plans to transfer a decommissioned vessel to the Philippines has raised eyebrows...
    句中的「that the Coast Guard Administration plans to transfer a decommissioned vessel to the Philippines」是同位語子句，用來補充說明主詞「News」的具體內容。
  • 分詞構句（表原因或條件）
    Ships are often decommissioned to make way for a better alternative, due to high costs or because they no longer meet the nation’s needs.
    句中的「to make way for a better alternative」是不定詞片語作副詞用，用來表達船隻除役的目的（為了騰出空間給更好的替代方案）。
  • 否定副詞置於句首的倒裝句
    Not only does the transfer to the Philippines align with Taiwan’s national interests, it reaffirms Taiwan’s ability to dictate the narrative in the geopolitical space.
    當否定片語「Not only」置於句首時，其後的子句需採用主謂倒裝結構（助動詞 does 提前至主語 the transfer 之前），以加強語氣。
