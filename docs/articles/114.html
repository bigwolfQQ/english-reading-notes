文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle phrase as post-modifier)
    The Federation of Taiwan Pharmacists Associations yesterday said it could revoke the certification of a New Taipei City pharmacist who allegedly drugged a employee on Aug. 26.
    句中 "...a pharmacist who allegedly drugged a employee..." 的關係子句，在分詞與介系詞結構中常被用來精確修飾前方的名詞（此處為 pharmacist）。另外像新聞標題或內文中如 "...a New Taipei City pharmacist allegedly drugging..." 或分詞作為形容詞修飾，能讓句子在不使用完整關係子句的情況下更顯緊湊。
  • 現在分詞表附帶結果或補充說明 (Present participle for accompanying result or description)
    The woman said she found the water bitter, cloudy and containing powder, and that she felt her heart was racing and experienced dizziness after drinking from her bottle.
    句中的 "containing powder" 是現在分詞片語，用來補充形容水（the water）的狀態，等於 "and that contained powder" 的簡化；而 "feeling her heart was racing" 等則展現了感官動詞後的受詞補語用法。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The case was referred to the judiciary.
  • 條件句與假設語氣句構 (Conditional sentence structure)
    If there is evidence showing Chen contravined regulations, the local pharmacists association or local healthcare units can refer his case to the disciplinary authority for penalties...
    此句為第一類條件句（First Conditional），結構為「If + 現在式動詞, 主詞 + 조動詞 (can/will/may) + 原形動詞」，用來表達真實且有可能發生的條件狀況（如果確實有證據顯示違規，就會採取對應的處分動作）。
