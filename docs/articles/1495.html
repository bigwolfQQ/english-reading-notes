在國慶談話中，在野黨領袖批評民進黨與賴清德

Opposition leaders criticize DPP, Lai in National Day remarks

單字解析

  • urge v. 敦促，力勸
    Kuomintang (KMT) Chairperson Cheng Li-wun (鄭麗文) on Saturday urged voters to help her party win local elections in southern Taiwan...
    中國國民黨主席鄭麗文於週六敦促選民協助該黨贏得台灣南部的地方選舉...
  • promulgate v. 公布（法律），頒布
    ...while Taiwan People's Party (TPP) Chairman Huang Kuo-chang (黃國昌) called on President Lai Ching-te (賴清德) to promulgate laws passed by the Legislature.
    ...同時台灣民眾黨主席黃國昌則呼籲總統賴清德公布立法院通過的法律。
  • accuse v. 指責，控告
    Cheng accused the ruling Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) of heightening cross-strait tensions and undermining Taiwan's democracy.
    鄭麗文指責執政的民主進步黨升高兩岸緊張局勢並破壞台灣的民主。
  • essential adj. 必要的，不可或缺的
    Cheng said peace across the Taiwan Strait was essential to safeguarding Taiwan's democracy, freedom and prosperity...
    鄭麗文表示，維護台海和平對於保障台灣的民主、自由與繁榮至關重要...
  • resumption n. 恢復，重啟
    She also criticized the DPP government's energy policies and called for a resumption of nuclear power generation.
    她同時批評民進黨政府的能源政策，並呼籲重啟核能發電。
  • concentrate v. 集中，聚集
    Economic growth should benefit older manufacturing industries, services, agriculture and fisheries, and not be concentrated in the high-tech sector, she said.
    她表示，經濟成長應惠及傳統製造業、服務業、農業與漁業，而不是集中在高科技產業。
  • refuse v. 拒絕
    He said the executive branch had no right to refuse to promulgate, or formally enact, laws...
    他表示，行政部門無權拒絕公布或正式施行法律...
  • implement v. 實施，執行
    ...or to refuse to implement them.
    ...也無權拒絕執行這些法律。
  • deliberately adv. 故意地，蓄意地
    ...accusing Lai of creating the misleading impression that lawmakers were deliberately delaying their passage.
    ...他指責賴清德製造了立委蓄意延宕預算通過的誤導性印象。
  • tolerate v. 容忍，寬容
    The TPP would support a budget that benefited the public but would not tolerate corruption or waste, he said.
    他表示，民眾黨會支持有利於公眾的預算，但絕不容忍腐敗或浪費。
  • pledge n. / v. 保證，承諾
    Huang also urged Lai to honor his campaign pledge to build 130,000 social housing units...
    黃國昌也敦促賴清德兌現其興建13萬戶社會住宅的競選承諾...
  • inequality n. 不平等
    ...and to address high housing costs, the rising cost of living and wealth inequality.
    ...並解決高房價、生活成本上升以及貧富差距等問題。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作修飾語 (Present Participle Phrase as Modifier)
    Speaking at a flag-raising ceremony in Kaohsiung's Guomao community, Cheng accused the ruling Democratic Progressive Party...
    句首的「Speaking at a ...」是由現在分詞片語引導的修飾語，用來補充說明主句主詞 Cheng 在當時正在進行的動作，等同於副詞子句「When she spoke at a ...」。在多益閱讀測驗中常考分詞片語與主詞的邏輯主動關係。
  • 關係代間詞子句修飾先行詞 (Relative Clause)
    He said the executive branch had no right to refuse to promulgate, or formally enact, laws that had cleared a third and final reading in the Legislature...
    句中的「that had cleared a third and final reading in the Legislature」是由關係代間詞 that 引導的限定修飾子句，用來修飾前面的先行詞 laws（法律）。在多益考試中，常測驗關係代間詞（如 that, which, who）的正確選用以及與動詞的單複數一致性。
  • 插入語用法 (Parenthetical Expression)
    The Legislative Yuan, where the KMT and the TPP hold a combined majority, has not yet begun committee review of the budgets...
    逗號之間的「where the KMT and the TPP hold a combined majority」是一個非限定關係子句，在此作為插入語，用來補充說明前面的名詞「The Legislative Yuan（立法院）」。多益寫作與閱讀常利用插入語來測試句子結構的辨識能力。
  • 動名詞片語作受詞 (Gerund Phrase as Object)
    ...accusing Lai of creating the misleading impression that lawmakers were deliberately delaying their passage.
    片語「accusing ... of ...」中，介系詞 of 後面接了動名詞片語「creating the misleading impression ...」作為其受詞。動名詞在多益測驗中經常出現在介系詞後面或是特定動詞（如 avoid, consider, enjoy）之後的填空題。
