單字解析

  • extend v. 延長、延伸
    as Detroit extended their winning streak to six games.
    隨著底特律隊將他們的連勝紀錄延長至六場。
  • batting average n. phr. 打擊率（商業上亦可比喻成功率、表現績效）
    with two stolen bases and a batting average of .258 in 287 at-bats this season.
    本季在287個打數中，有2次盜壘成功以及0.258的打擊率。
  • race n. 競爭、賽跑
    Toronto fell two games behind Cleveland in the race for the final American League wild card
    多倫多隊在爭奪美聯最後一張外卡資格的競爭中落後克里夫蘭隊兩場勝差
  • victory n. 勝利
    sent the Baltimore Orioles to a 7-5 victory over the Mets at Citi Field.
    帶領巴爾的摩金鶯隊在花旗球場以7比5擊敗大都會隊。
  • support n. 支持
    continued an outpouring of support from Mets fans for Alonso over the past two nights
    延續了大都會球迷過去兩個晚上對阿隆索如潮水般的支持
  • underperform v. 表現欠佳、績效不如預期
    As the 2026 Mets, I know we have definitely underperformed
    身為2026年的大都會隊，我知道我們確實表現不佳
  • respect n. 尊敬、尊重
    I’ve got nothing but respect for Pete
    我對皮特只有滿滿的尊重
  • defeat n. 失敗、擊敗
    The Orioles were two outs from a defeat that would have dropped them four games
    金鶯隊距離一場會讓他們落後四場勝差的敗局只差兩個出局數

文法重點

  • 關係代名詞非限定用法 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Lee, who was playing third base, hit the solo homer off a 128kph curve ball from Joe Mantiply in the ninth inning.
    句中的「who was playing third base」用逗號隔開，屬於非限定用法，用來補充說明主詞 Lee（李灝宇）當下的守備位置，拿掉這個子句不影響主要句意。
  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況或結果 (Participial Phrase)
    Chang reached the nine-homer mark while playing for the then-Cleveland Indians in 2021.
    「while playing for...」省略了主詞與be動詞（原句約等於 while he was playing），用現在分詞來簡化時間副詞子句，使句子更精簡流暢。
  • 與過去事實相反的假設語氣 (Subjunctive Mood)
    The Orioles were two outs from a defeat that would have dropped them four games behind Cleveland for the last American League playoff spot...
    「would have dropped」是用於假設語氣的過去完成式助動詞結構，用來表達「如果那場比賽輸了（但實際上沒輸），將會導致……」的假設情境。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    ...a defeat that would have dropped them four games behind Cleveland for the last American League playoff spot when Alonso hit a 126m solo drive off Kodai Senga...
    雖然此句主要為主動，但新聞英文常透過過去分詞（如 dropped, beaten, defeated 等）來表達被動或狀態的轉變，例如「beaten」或「defeated」等字彙隱含的受事者概念。
