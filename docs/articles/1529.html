TAIEX plunges as technology stocks hit

單字解析

  • tumble v. 暴跌、下跌
    Shares in Taiwan tumbled nearly 961 points yesterday...
    台股昨天暴跌近 961 點...
  • sell-off n. 拋售潮
    ...tracking an overnight sell-off in US technology stocks...
    ...跟隨美股科技股隔夜的拋售潮...
  • soar v. 急升、飆升
    ...sent crude oil prices soaring.
    ...使得原油價格飆升。
  • fund n. 資金、基金
    ...as investors shifted funds to the greenback as a safe haven...
    ...隨著投資人將資金轉移至美元以尋求避險...
  • outlook n. 前景、展望
    ...fresh worries about the outlook for the artificial intelligence (AI) investment boom.
    ...人工智慧（AI）投資熱潮的前景引發新疑慮。
  • turnover n. 營業額、成交量
    Turnover on the local main board totaled NT$1.125 trillion...
    台股集中市場成交值總計新台幣 1.125 兆元...
  • institutional investor n. phr. 機構投資人
    The three major institutional investors sold a combined net NT$198.446 billion...
    三大法人合計賣超新台幣 1,984.46 億元...
  • decline n. 下跌、衰退
    ...referring to the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index’s 2.67 percent decline.
    ...指的是費城半導體指數 2.67% 的跌幅。
  • intensify v. 加劇、強化
    “Selling intensified as investors were stunned by falling technology stocks...
    「隨著投資人對科技股下跌感到震驚，賣壓進一步加劇...」
  • trim v. 削減、修剪（此處指減碼持股）
    ...investors appeared to be panicking and scrambling to trim their positions.
    ...投資人似乎陷入恐慌並爭相減碼持股。
  • sustainable adj. 永續的、可持續的
    ...pointing out that the industry had not yet found a sustainable model...
    ...指出該行業尚未找到一個永續的商業模式...
  • revenue n. 收益、營收
    ...except that there is a lot of potential revenue growth out of providing frontier generative AI models.
    ...除了提供前沿生成式 AI 模型具有很大的潛在營收成長空間之外。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句表附帶結果（V-ing）
    ...after a Houthi attack on a Saudi Arabian airport sent crude oil prices soaring.
    句尾的「sent crude oil prices soaring」使用了「主詞 + 動詞 + 受詞 + 現在分詞 (V-ing)」的結構，用來表達某事發生所產生的直接結果或伴隨狀況，表示葉門青年運動的攻擊「促使」原油價格「一路飆升」。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    “The initial losses came after heavy losses among US semiconductor stocks overnight after attacks on a Saudi airport, sending oil prices higher,” Moore Securities Investment Consulting Co analyst Adam Lin said, referring to the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index’s 2.67 percent decline.
    句尾逗號後的「referring to...」是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前面整句話所指的對象（即分析師在談論時，所指的是費城半導體指數的跌幅），為主動修飾用法。
  • 名詞子句作受詞
    “A lot of investors have no clue what they are investing in,” he said...
    句中的「what they are investing in」是一個由疑問代名詞引導的名詞子句，在句中作為介詞「in」的受詞，用來具體說明投資人「沒有頭緒」的內容是什麼。
