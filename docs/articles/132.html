中國的紙老虎同盟顯示美國無法孤立伊朗

China’s paper-tiger alliance shows the US cannot isolate Iran

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • cooperation n. 合作
    Perhaps, but making it so would take a great deal of international cooperation.
    或許如此，但要實現這一點需要大量的國際合作。
  • survive v. 存活，倖存
    Accepting a peace that leaves the regime without the major financial and geopolitical gains it would need to placate a still deeply hostile population is more dangerous to its survival.
    接受一項無法讓政權獲得足以安撫依然高度敵對的人民所需的重大財務和地緣政治利益的和平，對其生存而言更加危險。
  • capitulate v. 投降，屈服
    Trump needs a near-total financial blockade to swiftly force Tehran to capitulate, because the IRGC commanders, who now dominate decisionmaking, are unlikely to oblige otherwise.
    川普需要近乎全面的金融封鎖來迅速迫使德黑蘭投降，因為現在主導決策的革命衛隊指揮官們不太可能以其他方式配合。
  • ally n. 盟友，同盟國
    He could do so also because the US was still the world’s undisputed hegemon, with NATO allies so eager to help that they invoked the organization’s Article 5 collective defense clause on Washington’s behalf, so they could join the fight.
    他之所以能做到這一點，還因為當時美國仍是無可爭議的世界霸主，其北約盟友熱切地協助，以華盛頓的名義啟動了該組織的第五條集體防禦條款，以便加入戰鬥。
  • summit n. 高峰會，首腦會議
    The SCO, which held its 26th annual summit this week, now has a much more global focus and at least as much (or as little, depending on your point of view) substance as the G7 industrialized nations.
    本週舉行第26屆年度峰會的上海合作組織，現在擁有更具全球性的關注點，並且其具體實質（取決於你的觀點，多多少少）至少與七大工業國組織（G7）相當。
  • limitation n. 限制，侷限性
    Much commentary focuses on the group’s limitations as a potential alliance, given that it includes NATO member Turkey, as well as India and that country’s bitter geopolitical rivals, China and Pakistan.
    許多評論聚焦於該集團作為潛在同盟的局限性，鑑於它包括了北約成員國土耳其，以及印度和該國的死敵中國與巴基斯坦。
  • forum n. 論壇，討論會
    It can be a paper tiger and still serve its purpose, which at this point is to provide China and Russia with a forum and a toolkit for balancing US power and influence in Eurasia.
    它可以是一隻紙老虎，卻依然能發揮其作用，現階段其作用是為中國和俄羅斯提供一個論壇和工具箱，以平衡美國在歐亞大陸的影響力。
  • prowess n. 傑出的才能，高超的技藝，實力
    In Asheville, North Carolina, Bessent boasted about US economic prowess, and defended measures — including a trade war with Canada and the sale of US reserves (using euros rather than dollars) to drive up the value of the yen — that damaged supposed allies.
    在北卡羅來納州艾許維爾，貝森特誇耀美國的經濟實力，並為損害所謂盟友的措施辯護——包括與加拿大的貿易戰以及出售美國儲備（使用歐元而非美元）來拉抬日圓價值。
  • sanction n. 制裁
    The common goal of that organization is to displace US hegemony, often now expressed in the form of sanctions, and India is on board for that, too.
    該組織的共同目標是取代美國霸權（通常現在表現為制裁的形式），印度對此也表示贊同。
  • invest v. 投資
    The talk at the SCO summit in Bishkek on Monday and Tuesday was of how to invest more in and trade more with Iran.
    週一和週二在比許凱舉行的上合組織峰會上的談話，都是關於如何加大對伊朗的投資和貿易。

文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The SCO was not designed to be NATO-style military alliance.
    此句使用被動語態「was not designed」，強調動作的承受者（上合組織）而非發動者，在新聞英語中常用於描述制度、計畫或協議的設立與原意。
  • 關係代間詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    The SCO, which held its 26th annual summit this week, now has a much more global focus...
    句中使用非限定性關係子句「which held its 26th annual summit this week」，對前面的先行詞（The SCO）提供額外的補充說明，前後以逗號隔開。
  • 分詞片語作後置修飾 (Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    Much commentary focuses on the group’s limitations as a potential alliance, given that it includes NATO member Turkey, as well as India and that country’s bitter geopolitical rivals, China and Pakistan.
    句中的「given that...」引導一個分詞片語或介系詞性質的連接詞結構（鑑於、考量到），用來補充說明評論聚焦於該集團局限性的原因。
  • 假設語氣/過去式表達難以實現的情況 (Hypothetical Context)
    None of this is imaginable today.
    句中運用了現在式否定結合形容詞「is not imaginable」，強烈對比過去歷史與當前地緣政治現實的巨大差異，突顯出時空背景的轉變。
