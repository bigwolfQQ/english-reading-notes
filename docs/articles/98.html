AI boom sees Hon Hai report record August sales

單字解析

  • manufacturing n. 製造業，製造
    Taiwan-based manufacturing giant Hon Hai Precision Industry Co. reported Saturday its strongest-ever August sales...
    總部位於台灣的製造業巨頭鴻海精密工業股份有限公司於週六公佈了其有史以來最強勁的八月營收...
  • demand n. 需求
    ...amid robust global demand for artificial intelligence applications.
    ...在全 球對人工智慧應用強勁的需求背景下。
  • consolidated adj. 合併的（指財務報表等）
    ...it posted NT$921.77 billion (US$29.14 billion) in consolidated sales in August...
    ...其八月合併營收達到新台幣 9,217 7 億元（約合 291.4 億美元）...
  • soar v. 急升，暴漲
    ...soaring 51.98 percent from a year earlier.
    ...較去年同期飆升 51.98%。
  • division n. 部門，事業群
    Hon Hai said its cloud and networking division enjoyed significant year-on-year revenue growth...
    鴻海表示，其雲端網路部門享有顯著的年營收成長...
  • revenue n. 收益，營收
    ...enjoyed significant year-on-year revenue growth amid accelerating AI momentum...
    ...在 AI 動能加速的推動下，享有顯著的年營收成長...
  • inventories n. 存貨，庫存（inventory的複數）
    ...as clients rebuilt inventories for the upcoming debut of new products...
    ...由於客戶為即將亮相的新產品重建庫存...
  • decline n. 下降，衰退
    ...reporting a sales decline.
    ...營收呈現衰退。
  • peak season n. phr. 旺季
    ...the third quarter is a traditional peak season for the information and communications technology industry...
    ...第三季向來是資訊與通訊科技產業的傳統旺季...
  • supplier n. 供應商

    ...其 20-megapixel（2,000 萬畫素）及以上鏡頭——擁有較高利潤率——占總營收的 10-20%...

    ...accounted for 10-20 percent of total sales...

文法重點

  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況（with / 分詞作修飾）
    Data released by iPhone assembler and AI server maker Hon Hai...
    過去分詞片語「released by...」在此作後位修飾語，用來修飾前面的名詞「Data」，相當於省略了「which was released by...」。多益閱讀測驗常考這種省略關代與be動詞的精簡寫法。
  • 表比較級的最高級寫法 (strongest-ever)
    ...reported Saturday its strongest-ever August sales amid robust global demand for artificial intelligence applications.
    「strongest-ever」是由最高級形容詞（strongest）加上「ever（有史以來）」組成的複合形容詞，放在名詞前用來修飾，意指「有史以來最強的...」。
  • 對等連接詞 while 引導對比狀況
    Hon Hai said its cloud and networking division enjoyed significant year-on-year revenue growth amid accelerating AI momentum, while its smart consumer electronics division saw sales growing significantly...
    連接詞「while」在此處連接兩個句子，用來表達前後兩者情況的對比（一邊是...，而另一邊則是...），在多益長篇閱讀或聽力對話中常用於突顯對比數據。
  • 獨立分詞構句表附帶原因或狀況
    ...adding that traditional peak effects are expected to allow the company to report an increase in sales in September, with clients building up inventories to meet needs for the debut of new products.
    句尾的「with + 名詞 + V-ing（clients building up）」為「with 複合結構」，在句中用來補充說明附帶發生的狀況或原因，相當實用且常出現在多益文法題（介系詞與分詞的搭配）。
