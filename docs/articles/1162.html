關於冰拿鐵的職場大戰根本與咖啡無關

The workplace war over iced lattes is not about coffee

單字解析

  • mundane adj. 世俗的、平凡的、乏味的
    This seemingly mundane question has somehow consumed various corners of the Internet...
    這個看似平凡的問題不知為何席捲了網際網路的各個角落……
  • recruit v. / n. 招募、招聘；新進人員
    What started the so-called iced-coffee discourse was a viral TikTok from corporate recruiter Caitlin Wehniainen...
    引發這場所謂「冰咖啡論戰」的是企業招募人員凱特琳・韋尼艾南的一支爆紅 TikTok 影片……
  • candidate n. 候選人、應徵者、求職者
    In her video, Wehniainen said that younger candidates are showing up at job interviews with iced coffee...
    韋尼艾南在影片中表示，較年輕的求職者帶著冰咖啡出現在面試場合……
  • proxy n. 代理人、代表權
    Iced coffee is just the latest proxy for that tension...
    冰咖啡只是這種緊張關係的最新代理人……
  • stereotype n. 刻板印象、定型化觀念
    Either interpretation reinforces the existing stereotypes that bosses have about Gen Z workers...
    無論哪種解讀，都強化了老闆對 Z 世代員工既有的刻板印象……
  • obsolete adj. 過時的、廢棄的
    An iced coffee takes on outsized weight when many traditional cues of the hiring process have become obsolete.
    當許多傳統的招聘流程線索變得過時時，一杯冰咖啡便承載了過大的分量。
  • applicant n. 申請人、應徵者（文章雖用 candidates，但此為多益核心同義字）
    Applying for a job has turned into an AI doom loop...
    求職已經變成了一個 AI 厄運迴圈……
  • authority n. 權威、官方、職權
    They are reclaiming their authority by quashing dissent, increasing surveillance and cutting benefits.
    他們透過打擊異見、加強監控和削減福利來重新奪回權威。
  • benefit n. 員工福利、津貼、好處
    They are reclaiming their authority by quashing dissent, increasing surveillance and cutting benefits.
    他們透過打擊異見、加強監控和削減福利來重新奪回權威。
  • eliminate v. 消除、淘汰、剔除
    Z Gen’ers are launching careers at time when they are constantly being told AI might eliminate the jobs they desire.
    Z 世代進入職場之際，正值他們不斷被告知 AI 可能會淘汰他們夢寐以求的工作。
  • editorial adj. / n. 編輯的、社論的；社論
    This column reflects the personal views of the author and does not necessarily reflect the opinion of the editorial board...
    本專欄反映作者個人觀點，不代表編輯委員會……的立場。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表達附帶狀況或結果）
    In her video, Wehniainen said that younger candidates are showing up at job interviews with iced coffee, which to her reads as treating the interview like a “stop on your list of errands for the day.”
    句尾的 "treating the interview like..." 是現在分詞片語形成的「分詞構句」，用來補充說明前面的動詞片語（showing up with iced coffee）在主觀上所代表的意義或產生的印象，主詞代名詞 which 代表前面的整件事。
  • 獨立分詞構句（with + 名詞 + 分詞）
    The very nature of work is in flux right now, with artificial intelligence (AI) accelerating the deepening structural shifts.
    此處使用 "with + 名詞 (AI) + 現在分詞 (accelerating)" 的結構，用來表示背景狀況或伴隨發生的動作，常翻譯為「隨著……（事情正在發生）」。
  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    An iced coffee takes on outsized weight when many traditional cues of the hiring process have become obsolete.
    句中的 "that bosses have about Gen Z workers" 或 "that AI might eliminate..." 是限定用法關係子句，用來修飾前面的先行詞（stereotypes 或 jobs），在子句中作受詞時通常可以省略關係代名詞。
  • 假設語氣（與現在事實相反）
    The logic is that if a prospective hire cannot get along and go along when it matters most, what other would they refuse to follow and conform to?
    句中包含了條件句 "if a prospective hire cannot get along..."，探討假設性情境下的推論邏輯，用來表達「如果求職者在關鍵時刻無法配合，未來還會拒絕遵守什麼」的假設情境。
