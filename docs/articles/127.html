上海峰會暴露裂痕

Shanghai summit exposes cracks

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • summit n. 峰會，高峰會
    The 25th anniversary summit of the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO) was held in Bishkek on Monday and Tuesday.
    上海合作組織（上合組織）第二十五週年峰會於週一和週二在比許凱克舉行。
  • arrangement n. 安排，協議
    Twenty-five years ago, the Shanghai Five was a local arrangement aimed at stabilizing post-Soviet frontiers and dealing with the main anxiety around regional extremism.
    二十五年前，「上海五國」是一個地區性安排，旨在穩定後蘇聯邊境並應對圍繞地區極端主義的主要擔憂。
  • institution n. 機構，制度
    It has expanded into a weightier strategic institution, now gathering about 40 percent of the global population in its fold.
    它已經擴大成為一個更有分量的戰略機構，現在將全球約百分之四十的人口納入其中。
  • capacity n. 容量，能力，生產力
    However, China uses economic capacity and its Belt and Road Initiative (BRI) to act as the unrivaled contributor to infrastructure development in Central Asia.
    然而，中國利用其經濟能力和「一帶一路」倡議（BRI），成為中亞基礎設施發展無可匹敵的貢獻者。
  • settlement n. 結算，協議，解決
    Beijing continues to promote trade settlement in local currencies to circumvent the US dollar and make China the primary economic engine for the region.
    北京繼續推動以當地貨幣進行貿易結算，以規避美元，並使中國成為該地區的主要經濟引擎。
  • circumvent v. 規避，繞過
    Beijing continues to promote trade settlement in local currencies to circumvent the US dollar and make China the primary economic engine for the region.
    北京繼續推動以當地貨幣進行貿易結算，以規避美元，並使中國成為該地區的主要經濟引擎。
  • allocate v. 分配，分派
    China seeks another version of the Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank, from which there would be weighted capital allocations to the members.
    中國尋求亞洲基礎設施投資銀行（AIIB）的另一種版本，從中對成員進行加權資本分配。
  • consensus n. 共識
    A political consensus around the necessity of a development financial institution has grown, but the idea is still under discussion and cannot be seen as a functional alternative to global lenders at present.
    圍繞著成立發展金融機構必要性的政治共識已經形成，但這個想法仍在討論中，目前不能被視為全球貸款機構的功能性替代方案。
  • efficiency n. 效率，效能
    However, the efficiency and impact of RATS are crippled by regional rivalries, and the inability to have common basic definitions and accept counterterrorism measures without pointing out national political repression by members.
    然而，RATS 的效率和影響力卻受到地區競爭的削弱，成員國無法建立共同的基本定義，也無法在不指出國家政治壓迫的情況下接受反恐措施。
  • leverage v. 利用，藉助
    Kyrgyzstan, as host, leveraged the summit to seek higher levels of investment, diversify trade and overcome the dominance of larger neighbors such as Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan.
    吉爾吉斯作為主辦國，利用這次峰會尋求更高水準的投資、多元化貿易，並克服哈薩克和烏茲別克等較大鄰國的統治地位。
  • diversify v. 多樣化，多元化
    Kyrgyzstan, as host, leveraged the summit to seek higher levels of investment, diversify trade and overcome the dominance of larger neighbors such as Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan.
    吉爾吉斯作為主辦國，利用這次峰會尋求更高水準的投資、多元化貿易，並克服哈薩克和烏茲別克等較大鄰國的統治地位。
  • dominance n. 優勢，統治地位
    Kyrgyzstan, as host, leveraged the summit to seek higher levels of investment, diversify trade and overcome the dominance of larger neighbors such as Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan.
    吉爾吉斯作為主辦國，利用這次峰會尋求更高水準的投資、多元化貿易，並克服哈薩克和烏茲別克等較大鄰國的統治地位。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    Its commitments are coalescing around important trade corridors such as the China-Kyrgyzstan-Uzbekistan railway.
    句中的 "such as the China-Kyrgyzstan-Uzbekistan railway" 是介系詞片語作後位修飾，用來舉例說明前面提到的 "important trade corridors"（重要的貿易走廊）。在多益閱讀測驗中，常考 such as 引導的名詞或名詞片語作為同位語或舉例。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    China seeks another version of the Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank, from which there would be weighted capital allocations to the members.
    句型使用「介系詞 + 關係代名詞 (from which)」引導形容詞子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞 "the Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank"。這種結構在多益測驗的文法題（Incomplete Sentences）中經常出現，用來測試關係代名詞前介系詞的正確搭配。
  • 假設語氣與條件句概念 (Conditional / Hypothetical Meaning)
    Unless it transforms itself into a development institution, it will not really be able to build the Eurasian identity that it seeks.
    句首使用 "Unless"（除非……，如果不……），等於 "If it does not..."，引導否定條件句，連接未來簡單式 "will not really be able to"。這種表達條件與結果的句型在多益聽力與閱讀中非常實用，常出現在商業談判與合約條件的討論中。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Those issues were discussed in Bishkek as important mechanisms to fund regional infrastructure and support members affected by Western sanctions...
    句中使用了被動語態 "were discussed"，強調動作的承受者（這些議題），而忽略或不需強調動作的執行者。多益測驗中經常藉由主被動語態的選擇來測試動詞時態與語態的正確性。
