賴總統：白埔園區將鞏固晶片生態系

Baipu to bolster chip ecosystem: Lai

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • bolster v. 支持、鞏固、改善
    Kaohsiung’s Baipu Industrial Park (白埔產業園區) would bolster Taiwan’s semiconductor ecosystem, balance north-south development and boost the country’s global competitiveness, President William Lai (賴清德) said at its groundbreaking ceremony yesterday.
    總統賴清德昨天在動土典禮上表示，高雄的白埔產業園區將鞏固台灣的半導體生態系、平衡南北發展，並提升國家的全球競爭力。
  • boost v. 促進、提高、增長
    Kaohsiung’s Baipu Industrial Park (白埔產業園區) would bolster Taiwan’s semiconductor ecosystem, balance north-south development and boost the country’s global competitiveness, President William Lai (賴清德) said at its groundbreaking ceremony yesterday.
    總統賴清德昨天在動土典禮上表示，高雄的白埔產業園區將鞏固台灣的半導體生態系、平衡南北發展，並提升國家的全球競爭力。
  • generate v. 產生、引起、發電
    The nearly 89-hectare park would create more than 4,000 jobs and generate about NT$300 billion (US$9.45 billion) in annual production value, marking a new milestone for Taiwan’s semiconductor industry, Lai said.
    賴總統表示，這個將近 89 公頃的園區將創造 4,000 多個工作機會，並產生約新台幣 3,000 億元（約合 94.5 美元億元）的年產值，為台灣的半導體產業標誌出一個新的里程碑。
  • annual adj. 年度的、每年的
    The nearly 89-hectare park would create more than 4,000 jobs and generate about NT$300 billion (US$9.45 billion) in annual production value, marking a new milestone for Taiwan’s semiconductor industry, Lai said.
    賴總統表示，這個將近 89 公頃的園區將創造 4,000 多個工作機會，並產生約新台幣 3,000 億元（約合 94.5 美元億元）的年產值，為台灣的半導體產業標誌出一個新的里程碑。
  • expand v. 擴大、擴展
    With artificial intelligence (AI) applications and high-performance computing expanding rapidly worldwide, advanced integrated circuit (IC) assembly services have become increasingly important to the semiconductor industry’s competitiveness, he said.
    他說，隨著人工智慧（AI）應用與高效能運算在全球迅速擴展，先進積體電路（IC）封裝服務對半導體產業競爭力的重要性已日益增加。
  • cooperation n. 合作、配合
    The park would draw on cooperation among the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Kaohsiung City Government and major semiconductor companies, including Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co (TSMC, 台積電), he said.
    他說，該園區將結合經濟部、高雄市政府以及包括台灣積體電路製造股份有限公司（台積電，TSMC）在內的主要半導體公司的合作。
  • announce v. 宣布、宣告
    Earlier this month, TSMC announced plans to develop a 3-hectare site in the industrial park, with two buildings housing modular facilities that can be reconfigured as technology evolves.
    台積電表示，該公司於本月稍早宣布計畫在該產業園區開發一處 3 公頃的基地，內含兩棟建築物，配備可隨技術演進重新配置的模組化設施。
  • infrastructure n. 基礎設施、公共建設
    The park’s entire infrastructure is expected to be completed by 2031, it added.
    該局並補充，園區的整體基礎設施預計將於 2031 年前完工。
  • aim v. 旨在、目的是、瞄準
    Lai said the park aims to become Taiwan’s first semiconductor base focused on developing, testing and certifying critical semiconductor equipment and raw materials.
    賴總統表示，該園區旨在成為台灣首座專注於開發、測試及驗證關鍵半導體設備與原物料的半導體基地。
  • establish v. 建立、設立、確立
    The development is expected to shorten the time needed to introduce new technologies and help Taiwanese companies establish a foothold in the global supply chain, he said.
    他說，這項開發預計將縮短引進新技術所需的時間，並幫助台灣企業在全球供應鏈中站穩腳步。
  • ensure v. 確保、保證
    On electricity consumption, Lai said state-owned Taiwan Power Co (台電) has agreed to a plan to build an electricity distribution network in Baipu Industrial Park to ensure stable power supply.
    關於電力消耗，賴總統表示國營的台灣電力公司（台電）已同意在白埔產業園區建立配電網路的計畫，以確保穩定的電力供應。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    With artificial intelligence (AI) applications and high-performance computing expanding rapidly worldwide, advanced integrated circuit (IC) assembly services have become increasingly important...
    句中的 "expanding rapidly worldwide" 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的受詞 "artificial intelligence (AI) applications and high-performance computing"，表示這些應用與運算正在「主動」快速擴展的狀態。
  • 過去分詞作後位修飾 (Past Participle as Post-modifier)
    The site is to include small cleanrooms and laboratories for testing and joint development of advanced assembly technologies, equipment and materials, as well as a training center for highly skilled specialists...
    句中的 "highly skilled" 是由副詞加過去分詞構成的複合形容詞，用來修飾後方的名詞 "specialists"（專家），表示這些專家是「被培訓、具備高技能的」，被動意味濃厚。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The park’s entire infrastructure is expected to be completed by 2031, it added.
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Nominative Absolute Construction)
    With the project nearing completion, the previous practice of using water trucks to maintain supplies at science parks would no longer be necessary, Lai said.
