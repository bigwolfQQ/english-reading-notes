文法重點

  • be to V（表即將發生的計畫或安排）
    Micron Technology Inc is to provide its engineers in Taiwan a cash bonus of NT$1 million (US$31,600) plus equity rewards...
    「is to provide」使用了「be + to V」的句型，在新聞英語中常用來表示官方、正式的計畫、預定或即將發生的重要決定。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    The row over Micron’s payouts coincides with a global debate about who deserves a cut of the sudden wealth generated by booming investment in artificial intelligence (AI)-related data centers and other infrastructure.
    句中的「generated by booming investment...」是一個過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「the sudden wealth」（突然湧現的財富），等於形容詞子句「which was generated by...」，能讓句子更簡潔。
  • 非限定關代子句（which）補充說明
    Micron employs about 15,000 people in Taiwan, which serves as a key manufacturing hub for the chipmaker.
    逗號後面的「which serves as a key manufacturing hub...」是非限定關係代間子句，對前面的先行詞「Taiwan」做補充說明，且前面必須加逗號，不能用 that 代替。
  • 被動語態與分詞片語作後位修飾
    The US memory maker and the Taoyuan union failed to reach an agreement during a mediation meeting last week, with a second round of mediation scheduled.
    句尾的「with a second round of mediation scheduled」使用了「with + 名詞 + 過去分詞 (scheduled)」的附帶狀況構句（Absolute Phrase），用來補充說明調解會議失敗後「接下來已安排了第二輪調解」的伴隨狀態。
