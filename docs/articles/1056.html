台灣男子桌球隊於亞運奪得銅牌

Taiwan men's team take table tennis bronze at Asiad

單字解析

  • consecutive adj. 連續的，不間斷的
    Taiwan's men's table tennis team lost 3-0 to China in the semifinals of the Asian Games Aichi-Nagoya 2026 on Wednesday, settling for bronze for the fourth consecutive time at the quadrennial Games.
    台灣男子桌球代表隊週三在2026年愛知-名古屋亞洲運動會準決賽中，以0比3不敵中國隊，在這個四年一度的運動會上連續第四屆拿下銅牌。
  • secure v. 獲得，確保，取得
    On Wednesday, Taiwan had already secured a place on the podium by reaching the semifinals, but needed to beat China to advance to the gold medal match.
    週三稍早，台灣闖入準決賽已確定站上頒獎台，但必須擊敗中國隊才能晉級金牌戰。
  • advance v. 晉級，前進，推進
    On Wednesday, Taiwan had already secured a place on the podium by reaching the semifinals, but needed to beat China to advance to the gold medal match.
    週三稍早，台灣闖入準決賽已確定站上頒獎台，但必須擊敗中國隊才能晉級金牌戰。
  • opening adj. 開場的，開始的
    In the opening match, world No. 6 Lin Yun-ju (林昀儒) pushed top-ranked Wang Chuqin (王楚欽) to five games before losing 8-11, 9-11, 11-9, 11-8, 13-15.
    在首場比賽中，世界排名第6的林昀儒將世界排名第一的王楚欽逼入第五局，最終以8-11、9-11、11-9、11-8、13-15落敗。
  • rally v. （局勢惡化後）重整旗鼓，恢復，反彈
    After dropping the first two games, Lin rallied to level the match.
    在連失兩局後，林昀儒振作將比數扳平。
  • level v. 使平手，使成為一樣
    After dropping the first two games, Lin rallied to level the match.
    在連失兩局後，林昀儒振作將比數扳平。
  • deciding adj. 決定性的
    He led 7-4 in the deciding game, but Wang won six straight points to reach match point at 10-7.
    他在決勝局一度以7比4領先，但王楚欽連拿6分，以10比7率先聽牌。
  • contest n. 競賽，比賽
    Lin saved three match points and later earned one of his own at 13-12, only for Wang to win the next three points and give China a 1-0 lead in the team contest.
    林昀儒救回了三個賽末點，隨後在13比12時反倒取得自己的局點，可惜王楚欽隨後連得三分，替中國隊在團體賽取得1比0領先。
  • victory n. 勝利
    Seventeen-year-old Kuo Guan-hong (郭冠宏) won the opening game 14-12 against Wen Ruibo (溫瑞博), but lost the next three 7-11, 8-11, 6-11 as China sealed the semifinal victory.
    17歲的郭冠宏在對陣溫瑞博時以14-12拿下首局，但隨後連失三局（7-11、8-11、6-11），中國隊藉此鎖定準決賽勝利。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作結果狀語（分詞構句表結果）
    Taiwan's men's table tennis team lost 3-0 to China in the semifinals of the Asian Games Aichi-Nagoya 2026 on Wednesday, settling for bronze for the fourth consecutive time at the quadrennial Games.
    句尾的「settling for bronze...」是由連接詞和子句簡化而來的現在分詞構句（原意約等於 and settled for...），用來表達前面主句動作所產生的自然結果——即「最終獲得銅牌」。這種結構在多益閱讀測驗中常出現於長句的結尾。
  • 分詞構句表伴隨狀況（By + V-ing）
    On Wednesday, Taiwan had already secured a place on the podium by reaching the semifinals, but needed to beat China to advance to the gold medal match.
    片語「by reaching the semifinals」中的 by 後面接動名词（V-ing），用來表示「透過……方式」達成前面的動作（secured a place）。這是多益測驗中考文法（介系詞後面接動名詞）與閱讀理解的高頻句型。
  • Only for + S + V（表示出乎意料的轉折結果）
    Lin saved three match points and later earned one of his own at 13-12, only for Wang to win the next three points and give China a 1-0 lead in the team contest.
    「only for + 主詞 + 動詞」常用來表示某個行動之後，緊接著發生了令人意外、失望或與預期相反的結果（意指「結果卻……」）。此結構常出現在新聞報導中描述戲劇性的轉折。
