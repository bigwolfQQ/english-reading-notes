文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...including introducing tougher penalties for drivers who fail to yield to pedestrians and legislation aimed at improving pedestrian infrastructure.
    句中的 "aimed at improving pedestrian infrastructure" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "legislation"（立法）。等於關代子句 "which is aimed at..." 的簡化，多益閱讀測驗常考這種分詞片語省略主格與 be 動詞的用法。
  • With 複合結構 (With + Noun + V-ing/Adj)
    Pedestrian safety remains a concern, however, with drivers frequently failing to yield and speeding on narrow streets.
    「with + 名詞 + 分詞/形容詞」結構在英文中用來表示伴隨狀況或原因。在此句中，"with drivers frequently failing to yield and speeding..." 解釋了為什麼行人安全仍然令人擔憂（伴隨著駕駛經常不禮讓與超速的狀況）。
  • 非限定關代子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Her vital signs were subsequently restored as she was taken to China Medical University Hospital, where she remains under treatment.
    句尾的 ", where she remains under treatment" 是非限定關代子句，逗點後方的 "where" 代替前面的地點 "China Medical University Hospital" 並補充說明她在該醫院持續接受治療，多益中常考關代 where 與其他關係代名詞的混淆。
