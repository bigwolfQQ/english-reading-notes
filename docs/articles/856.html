文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作後置修飾語
    Conor Gallagher’s reply, which came with Tottenham trailing 2-0, was their first goal against top-flight opponents this term.
    句中的 "with Tottenham trailing 2-0" 是一個「with + 名詞 + 現在分詞」的附帶狀況構句，用來補充說明主句動作發生時的伴隨情境（熱刺當時正處於落後 2 比 0 的狀態）。
  • 非限定關代子句（補充說明主詞）
    Former Marseille boss De Zerbi, whose appointment late last season saved Tottenham from relegation, is convinced they are still on the right path.
    句中的 ", whose appointment late last season saved Tottenham from relegation," 是一個非限定關係代名詞子句（使用所有格 whose），用來補充說明前方的先行詞 De Zerbi，並在其中夾帶一個完整的子句來說明他的任命案如何拯救了球隊。
  • 分詞構句表結果（動詞-ing）
    Making eight changes from last weekend’s draw with Everton was a gamble that backfired for De Zerbi as his side were beaten by an understrength Liverpool line-up.
    句首的 "Making eight changes..." 是動名词片语作为句子的主词；而在句尾的 "as his side were beaten..." 則是連接詞 as 引導的原因副詞子句，用來解釋為什麼這場賭博會適得其反。
