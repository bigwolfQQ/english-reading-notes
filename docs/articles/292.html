單字解析

  • arrest v. / n. 逮捕，拘留
    Seven members of a suspected human trafficking ring were arrested for attempting to send people to China for organ harvesting...
    7名涉嫌人口販運集團的成員因企圖將人送往中國進行器官摘除而遭到逮捕...
  • investigate v. 調查，偵查
    The case was uncovered when the Criminal Investigation Bureau found texts about a human trafficking ring while investigating a young man surnamed Chang (張).
    此案是在刑事警察局調查一名張姓年輕男子時，發現有關人口販運集團的簡訊而遭揭發。
  • instruction n. 指示，命令，操作說明
    Under the bureau’s instruction, the Taoyuan District Prosecutors’ Office investigated the division of labor within the ring.
    在局方的指示下，桃園地方檢察署深入調查該集團內部的分工。
  • division of labor n. phr. 分工
    Under the bureau’s instruction, the Taoyuan District Prosecutors’ Office investigated the division of labor within the ring.
    在局方的指示下，桃園地方檢察署深入調查該集團內部的分工。
  • recruit v. 招募，吸納（新成員）
    ...while three other members aged 22 to 27 recruited three more young people to join...
    ...另有3名年齡介於22至27歲的成員則招募了另外3名年輕人加入...
  • promise v. / n. 承諾，保證
    ...promising them the equivalent of NT$100,000 to NT$300,000 in Tether for each person they sent, they said.
    ...並承諾每送去一人，就支付相當於新台幣10萬至30萬元的泰達幣。
  • equivalent n. / adj. 相等的價值，等同物；相當的
    ...promising them the equivalent of NT$100,000 to NT$300,000 in Tether for each person they sent, they said.
    ...並承諾每送去一人，就支付相當於新台幣10萬至30萬元的泰達幣。
  • residence n. 住所，住宅

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附帶狀況/結果
    Seven members of a suspected human trafficking ring were arrested for attempting to send people to China for organ harvesting, with prosecutors seeking the maximum seven-year sentence for each defendant.
    句尾使用「with + 受詞 + 現在分詞 (seeking)」的結構，用來補充說明主句動作發生時的伴隨狀況或結果，常譯為「隨著……」或「檢方因而……」，在新聞英語中非常常見。
  • 關係代間代名詞所有格 (Whose / of whom)
    They detained seven suspects, including Chiang, four of whom are being held incommunicado pending trial.
    此處使用「數量詞 + of whom」引導的非限定子句（four of whom...），用來補充說明前面 7 名被拘留的嫌疑人之中，有 4 人處於禁見狀態。這種結構能讓句子更緊湊、具有專業正式的語氣。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    None of the three people held were sent out of Taiwan, they added.
    句中的「held」是過去分詞，修飾前面的名詞「the three people」，相當於省略了「who were held」。這種簡化的分詞片語能讓新聞報導更顯精簡俐落。
