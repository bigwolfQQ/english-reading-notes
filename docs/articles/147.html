奧運獎牌得主名列台灣 2026 年亞運代表團之中

Olympic medalists among Taiwan's delegation to 2026 Asian Games

單字解析

  • delegation n. 代表團
    Taiwan will send 482 athletes, including several renowned Olympic medalists, to compete in 34 sports at the 2026 Asian Games in Japan's Aichi Prefecture later this month, according to the Ministry of Sports.
    根據體育署表示，台灣將派出 482 名運動員，其中包括數名知名的奧運獎牌得主，前往日本愛知縣參加本月稍後舉行的 2026 年亞洲運動會，共競逐 34 項運動。
  • compete v. 競爭，參賽
    She will be representing Taiwan, competing under the name Chinese Taipei, at the Asian Games for the eighth time.
    她將以中華台北的名義代表台灣，第八次參加亞運會。
  • represent v. 代表
    She will be representing Taiwan, competing under the name Chinese Taipei, at the Asian Games for the eighth time.
    她將以中華台北的名義代表台灣，第八次參加亞運會。
  • intensive adj. 密集的，強烈的
    Most of the athletes competing at the Asian Games began training as early as August 2024, with intensive training sessions beginning in July this year.
    大多數參加亞運會的運動員早在 2024 年 8 月就開始訓練，而密集訓練課程則於今年 7 月展開。
  • session n. 期間，會議，場次

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作後置修飾語
    She will be representing Taiwan, competing under the name Chinese Taipei, at the Asian Games for the eighth time.
    句中的 "competing under the name Chinese Taipei" 是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明主詞在代表台灣參賽時的附帶狀況或動作。這種結構在英文新聞中非常常見，能簡潔地表達兩個同時發生或相連的動作。
  • with 複合結構（with + 受詞 + 分詞/形容詞）
    Most of the athletes competing at the Asian Games began training as early as August 2024, with intensive training sessions beginning in July this year.
    此處使用 "with + 名詞 (intensive training sessions) + 現在分詞 (beginning)" 的結構，用來表達附帶狀況或背景原因，意指「隨著密集的訓練課程於今年七月展開……」。在多益閱讀測驗中，這常考成介系詞或分詞形式的文法題。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The 2026 Asian Games, also known as Aichi-Nagoya 2026, will be held from Sept. 19 to Oct. 4 at venues across Aichi Prefecture in Japan.
    句中的 "will be held" 是助動詞 will 加上被動語態（be + 過去分詞），用來表達亞運會是「被」舉辦。在商業與新聞英語中，活動、會議或賽事的預定行程（如 will be held / will be conducted）幾乎都習慣使用被動語態來表達。
