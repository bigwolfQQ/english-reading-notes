單字解析

  • representative n. 代表、眾議員
    Former president Tsai Ing-wen (蔡英文) on Tuesday met with US Representative Nancy Pelosi in San Francisco...
    前總統蔡英文（蔡英文）於週二在舊金山會見了美國眾議員裴洛西...
  • commitment n. 承諾、奉獻
    “The bond between the United States and Taiwan is strong, and Congress’ commitment to the Taiwanese people and their freedom remains steadfast,” she said.
    她表示：「美國與台灣之間的聯繫是牢固的，國會對台灣人民及其自由的承諾依然堅定不移。」
  • steadfast adj. 堅定的、不變的
    “The bond between the United States and Taiwan is strong, and Congress’ commitment to the Taiwanese people and their freedom remains steadfast,” she said.
    她表示：「美國與台灣之間的聯繫是牢固的，國會對台灣人民及其自由的承諾依然堅定不移。」
  • contribution n. 貢獻
    ...in recognition of Pelosi’s contributions to promoting Taiwan-US relations.
    ...以表彰裴洛西對促進台美關係的貢獻。
  • consistently adv. 一致地、一貫地、始終
    “Over the years, regardless of changes in the international situation, Pelosi has consistently been a staunch friend of Taiwan,” Tsai wrote.
    蔡英文寫道：「多年來，無論國際情勢如何變化，裴洛西一直是台灣堅定的友人。」
  • convey v. 傳達、表達
    The scarf’s design was inspired by the ocean and coral, Tsai said, adding that she hoped the gift would convey to Pelosi the beauty and creativity of Taiwan as a maritime nation.
    蔡英文表示，該絲巾的設計靈感來自海洋與珊瑚，並補充說她希望這份禮物能向裴洛西傳達台灣作為海洋國家的美麗與創造力。
  • schedule v. 預定、安排
    She was scheduled to deliver the opening keynote address at Stanford University’s DEFCON’s Technology and National Security Conference yesterday.
    她原定於昨天在史丹佛大學的 DEFCON 技術與國家安全會議上發表開幕主題演說。
  • conduct v. 執行、舉辦、進行
    Pelosi’s visit prompted China to conduct large-scale live-fire military drills around Taiwan, heightening cross-strait tensions.
    裴洛西的訪問促使中國在台灣周邊舉行大規模實彈軍事演習，升高了兩岸緊張局勢。
  • delegation n. 代表團
    Separately, a US congressional delegation is in Taiwan for a four-day visit...
    另一方面，一個美國國會代表團正在台灣進行為期四天的訪問...
  • statement n. 聲明、陳述
    The all-Democratic delegation is led by US Representative Brendan Boyle, the ranking member of the House Budget Committee, the ministry said in a statement...
    該部在聲明中表示，這個全由民主黨組成的代表團由美國眾議員布萊爾率領，他是眾議院預算委員會的資深成員...
  • economic adj. 經濟的
    ...to discuss Taiwan-US relations, economic and trade issues, technology and regional security, the ministry said.
    該部表示，...討論台美關係、經貿議題、科技與區域安全。
  • demonstrate v. 證明、展現、示範
    His work demonstrates his support for closer bilateral ties and Taiwan’s participation in international organizations, it said.
    該部表示，他的工作展現了他對更緊密雙邊關係以及台灣參與國際組織的支持。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（用現在分詞補充說明結果或動作）
    The scarf’s design was inspired by the ocean and coral, Tsai said, adding that she hoped the gift would convey to Pelosi the beauty and creativity of Taiwan as a maritime nation.
    句尾的「adding that...」是由對等子句簡化而來的分詞構句，主詞與主要子句相同（即 Tsai），用來補充說明蔡英文在說這番話的同時，還多表達了什麼內容。
  • 關係子句（形容詞子句修飾先行詞）
    The two met at the San Francisco hotel where Tsai was staying during her visit to the city.
    句中的「where Tsai was staying...」是關係副詞引導的關係子句，用來修飾前面的先行詞「the San Francisco hotel」，說明蔡英文下榻的飯店。
  • 被動語態（強調動作的承受者）
    She was scheduled to deliver the opening keynote address at Stanford University’s DEFCON’s Technology and National Security Conference yesterday.
    句型「was scheduled to」使用了被動語態，用來表達某行程是「被預定、被安排」在某個時間舉行。
  • 獨立分詞片語修飾名詞（過去分詞作後置修飾）
    The all-Democratic delegation is led by US Representative Brendan Boyle, the ranking member of the House Budget Committee, the ministry said in a statement, adding that US representatives Andrea Salinas and Tim Kennedy are the other members.
    「led by US Representative Brendan Boyle」是由過去分詞「led」引導的片語，放在主詞後方修飾「The all-Democratic delegation」，意思是「由……所率領的（代表團）」。
