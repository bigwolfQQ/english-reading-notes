文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Ting-Wei “Willy” Sun (孫廷瑋), a former outside contractor described by US authorities as a “fixer” who allegedly aided in the sales, pleaded guilty on Thursday...
    句中的「described by US authorities as a 'fixer'」是一個過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞短語「a former outside contractor（前外部承包商）」，相當於形容詞子句「who was described by US authorities as a 'fixer'」的簡化形式，多益閱讀測驗常藉此測驗考生對修飾關係與冗長主詞的理解。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    A sentencing date was scheduled for Sept. 8 next year.
    句中的「was scheduled」使用了被動語態（be + 過去分詞），強調「宣判日期」是被安排、排定的，而不是它自己去安排。在多益聽力與閱讀中，商務行程或會議常以被動語態來表達被安排的時間。
  • 現在分詞引導的附帶狀況/手段 (Present Participle Expressing Means)
    The scheme allegedly involved efforts by the defendants and others to evade scrutiny by auditors from Super Micro and the US government — including by falsifying records and setting up “dummy” servers...
    句中的「falsifying records and setting up 'dummy' servers」使用動名詞（由介系詞 by 引導），用來具體說明規避審查的手段與方式。這種結構在商業英文與新聞報導中非常常見，用以補充說明具體的操作細節。
