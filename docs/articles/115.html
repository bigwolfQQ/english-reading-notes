環境部擴大含汞禁用產品清單

Ministry extends mercury-containing banned products list

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • extend v. 擴大、延長
    The Ministry of Environment has announced that it has extended the list of banned products containing mercury to 15 items...
    環境部已宣布將含汞禁用產品清單擴大至 15 項...
  • in line with phr. 與...一致、符合
    ...saying that the move is in line with global standards.
    ...並表示此舉符合全球標準。
  • come into effect phr. 生效、實施
    The Minamata Convention on Mercury came into effect in 2017 with 50 countries’ approval...
    《水俁公約》於 2017 年在 50 個國家的批准下生效...
  • implementation n. 實施、執行
    The Executive Yuan set up a program in 2016 when the convention was under discussion, to promote its implementation in Taiwan...
    行政院在該公約討論期間的 2016 年設立了一項計畫，以推動其在台灣的實施...
  • manufacturing n. 製造業、製造
    The ministry in 2021 banned the use of mercury in manufacturing electrical appliances such as batteries and electrical switches...
    該部於 2021 年禁止在製造電池和電氣開關等電器時使用汞...
  • import n. / v. 進口
    In April, it banned mercury production and the element’s import, sale or use, excluding some industrial applications in which it cannot be substituted...
    今年 4 月，該部禁止了汞的生產以及該元素的進口、銷售或使用，但排除了一些無法被替代的工業應用...
  • consumption n. 消耗量、消費
    Taiwan’s annual mercury consumption was reduced to 0.02 tonnes last year from 1.18 tonnes in 2016...
    台灣的年度汞消耗量已從 2016 年的 1.18 公噸降至去年的 0.02 公噸...
  • decline n. / v. 下降、減少
    The decline was especially sharp following the ministry’s ban on manufacturing of some mercury-added products in 2021...
    在環境部於 2021 年禁止製造部分添汞產品後，降幅尤其劇烈...
  • emissions n. 排放物、排放
    The ministry has also established guidelines for regulating mercury emissions...
    該部也已建立規範汞排放的指引...
  • comply with phr. 遵守、符合
    Most tested items were found to comply with regulations...
    多數受檢項目皆符合法規...

文法重點

  • 現在完成被動語態 (Present Perfect Passive)
    The ministry has extended the ban to six other products...
    由「has/have + been + 過去分詞」構成，強調過去發生的動作對現在造成的影響或結果。在此句中表示環境部「已經將禁令擴大」到其他六項產品，強調這個政策變更目前依然有效。
  • 過去分詞作後位修飾 (Past Participle as Postmodifier)
    ...in addition to the nine products that have been banned since 2021 — such as thermometers, blood pressure monitors or cosmetic products — the ministry has extended the ban to six other products...
    句中的「banned」是過去分詞，用來修飾前面的「products」，相當於形容詞子句「that have been banned」，用簡潔的方式表達「被禁用的產品」。
  • 現在分詞作附帶狀況/補充說明 (Present Participle indicating supplementary action)
    ...it said, adding that mercury management requires long-term, cross-disciplinary commitment.
    句尾的「adding that...」是由現在分詞「adding」引導的片語，用來補充說明主句主詞（it / the ministry）在發言時同時做了「補充說...」的動作，能讓新聞語氣更流暢、緊湊。
