文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Under the law, those convicted of operating scam centers, running cryptocurrency scams, recruiting workers, or trafficking people into scam operations, regardless of nationality, face sentences ranging from 10 years to life imprisonment.
    句中的「convicted of...」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的代名詞 those（指代 people），相當於形容詞子句「who are convicted of...」。多益閱讀測驗常考這種省略 who are 的分詞簡化句構。
  • 被動語態的應用 (Passive Voice)
    Previously, similar offenders would typically be deported to their home countries and face more lenient penalties under Myanmar's immigration laws, Hsiao said.
    句中的「would typically be deported」使用了被動語態（助動詞 would + be + 過去分詞），強調違法者是「被」遣返回國，而非主動執行。商業合規與法律新聞中非常常出現被動語態。
  • 現在分詞表達結果 (Present Participle Indicating Result)
    Along with swift deportation, offenders face permanent blacklisting and bans on reentering Thailand, Hsiao said.
    雖然此句是介系詞片語開頭，但在文章其他地方如第 4 段「if their actions result in death」或第 8 段「that carries a prison sentence」中，常運用子句與分詞來表達條件與結果的因果關係，有助於多益克漏字與閱讀測驗的句意理解。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    According to MOFA, between 2022 and January 2025, it helped 1,533 Taiwanese nationals who had voluntarily or involuntarily become involved in telecommunications fraud rings operating in the region return to Taiwan.
    句中的「who had voluntarily or involuntarily become involved...」是一個限定用法的關係代名詞子句，用來補充說明前面的「1,533 Taiwanese nationals（1,533名台灣公民）」，在多益文法題中常考關代選擇（如 who, whom, whose）。
