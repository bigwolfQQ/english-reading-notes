台灣學生在 2025 年 PISA 評量中：科學全球排名第四、閱讀第三

Taiwan students rank 4th globally in science, 3rd in reading: PISA 2025

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • achieve v. 達到、取得（成就）
    Taiwan's 15-year-old students ranked fourth in the world in science and mathematics and achieved a record-high third place in reading literacy in the 2025 Programme for International Student Assessment (PISA)...
    台灣15歲學生在科學與數學方面排名全球第四，並在2025年國際學生能力評量計畫（PISA）中取得了閱讀素養創紀錄的第三名...
  • maintain v. 維持、保持
    The results showed that Taiwan maintained its strong performance from the 2022 assessment...
    結果顯示，台灣維持了自2022年評量以來的強勁表現...
  • evaluate v. 評估
    PISA, organized by the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD), evaluates the knowledge and skills of 15-year-old students needed to participate effectively in society.
    由經濟合作暨發展組織（OECD）主辦的PISA，旨在評估15歲學生在社會中有效參與所需的知識與技能。
  • participate v. 參與、參加
    More than 760,000 15-year-old students from 91 countries and economic entities participated in the PISA 2025 study.
    來自91個國家與經濟體的76万多名15歲學生參與了2025年PISA研究。
  • score v. / n. 得分、分數
    According to the report, Taiwanese students scored 540 points in science, maintaining the country's fourth-place global ranking...
    根據該報告，台灣學生在科學項目獲得540分，維持該國全球第四的排名...
  • improve v. 改善、進步
    In reading literacy, Taiwan scored 508 points, improving from fifth place in 2022 to a record-high third.
    在閱讀素養方面，台灣獲得508分，從2022年的第五名進步至創紀錄的高居第三。
  • average adj. / n. 平均的、平均數
    Among the 23 OECD countries that participated in both the 2022 and 2025 assessments, average science scores fell by five points...
    在同時參與2022年與2025年評量的23個OECD國家中，平均科學分數下降了5分...
  • trend n. 趨勢、傾向
    ...outperforming the average trend among the 23 OECD participants.
    ...表現優于23個OECD參與國的平均趨勢。
  • ability n. 能力
    Tsai said Taiwan's ability to remain among the world's top-performing education systems despite global declines demonstrates the resilience of its education system...
    蔡今中表示，儘管全球各地成績下滑，台灣仍能保持在全球表現最佳的教育體系之列，這證明了其教育體系...的韌性。
  • demonstrate v. 證明、顯示
    ...demonstrates the resilience of its education system in the face of various challenges.
    ...這證明了其教育體系在面對各種挑戰時的韌性。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾（修飾名詞）
    PISA, organized by the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD), evaluates the knowledge...
    句子中的 "organized by the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD)" 是一個過去分詞片語，用來補充說明主詞 "PISA" 是「被」誰所組織的。這相當於一個被動語態的形容詞子句 "which is organized by..." 的簡化版，能讓商業與新聞英文更精煉。
  • 現在分詞片語作附加說明（補充結果或狀態）
    ...maintaining the country's fourth-place global ranking while matching its best-ever score in the subject, first achieved in 2022.
    句尾的 "maintaining the country's..." 是現在分詞（V-ing）引導的片語，用來表達主句動作發生時的同時伴隨狀況或自然產生的結果，中文常譯為「同時...」或「因而...」。
  • 複合形容詞（最高級用法）
    ...achieved a record-high third place in reading literacy in the 2025 Programme...
    "record-high" 是由「名詞 (record) + 形容詞 (high)」組合而成的複合形容詞，中間加上連字號（hyphen），用來修飾後方的名詞 "third place"，意思是「創紀錄高的」。
