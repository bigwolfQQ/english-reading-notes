單字解析

  • preserve v. 保存、維護、保護
    For three decades, Abha Narain Lambah has pioneered efforts to preserve some of India’s most significant historic sites
    三十年來，阿巴·納賴恩·蘭巴率先努力保存印度一些最重要的歷史古蹟
  • economy n. 經濟、經濟體
    In the world’s most populous nation and one of its fastest-growing major economies, urban development is advancing at dizzying speed.
    在這個世界上人口最多、且是成長最快的主要經濟體之一的國家中，城市發展正以令人眩暈的速度推進。
  • regulation n. 規章、法規、規則
    It’s only in the last 30 years that we’ve managed to have city-wide listings and heritage regulations
    直到過去30年，我們才設法建立全市範圍的名錄和遺產法規
  • fund n. 資金、基金
    Historical buildings are neither protected by government, nor do local communities or owners have the funds to look after them.
    歷史建築既沒有受到政府保護，當地社區或所有者也沒有資金來照顧它們。
  • challenge n. 挑戰
    The challenge was: What do you do about historic buildings, that are not protected under central government rules and have no funding?
    面臨的挑戰是：對於那些不受中央政府規則保護且沒有資金的歷史建築，你該怎麼辦？
  • donor n. 捐贈者、捐助人
    Lambah helped bring together private donors, foundations and community groups to rescue the Victorian heritage site
    蘭巴協助匯聚私人捐助者、基金會和社區團體，以拯救該維多利亞時代的遺產古蹟
  • rescue v. 救援、拯救
    Lambah helped bring together private donors, foundations and community groups to rescue the Victorian heritage site
    蘭巴協助匯聚私人捐助者、基金會和社區團體，以拯救該維多利亞時代的遺產古蹟
  • extend v. 延長、延伸
    Instead, its life has been extended and it has become a cultural space that is actively used by people.
    相反地，它的生命得到了延長，並已成為一個被民眾積極使用的文化空間。
  • active adj. 活躍的、積極的（副詞為 actively）
    Instead, its life has been extended and it has become a cultural space that is actively used by people.
    相反地，它的生命得到了延長，並已成為一個被民眾積極使用的文化空間。
  • growth n. 成長、增長
    That growth is welcome, but the task remains enormous, she said.
    她表示，這種增長是令人欣喜的，但任務依然巨大。

文法重點

  • 分裂句 (Cleft Sentence: It is/was ... that ...)
    It’s only in the last 30 years that we’ve managed to have city-wide listings and heritage regulations...
    用來強調句子的某個部分（此處強調時間副詞片語 in the last 30 years）。結構為「It is/was + 被強調的內容 + that + 句子其他部分」，能讓作者在文章中凸顯特定的時間或重點。
  • Neither... nor... 否定並列句與否定副詞倒裝
    Historical buildings are neither protected by government, nor do local communities or owners have the funds to look after them.
    前半句使用「neither A nor B」連接兩個否定狀況；當「nor」放在句首連接第二個分句時，必須採用部分倒裝（助動詞 do 提前至主語 local communities or owners 之前），藉此強調「兩者皆不」的雙重否定。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    ...historic buildings, that are not protected under central government rules and have no funding?
    用關係代名詞 that 引導形容詞子句，用來修飾先行詞 historic buildings，補充說明這些歷史建築未受到中央政府規則保護且缺乏資金的狀態。
  • 過去分詞片語作形容詞用（被動概念）
    ...once facing demolition, but with its ornate gilt interiors and grand auditorium now intact.
    句中的 once facing demolition 為分詞片語修飾前面的 Royal Opera House，而「with + 名詞 + 形容詞/分詞」的結構（with its ... interiors and ... auditorium now intact）則用來補充附帶狀況，描述歌劇院內裝與禮堂目前完好無缺的狀態。
