不值得被取代的人們

The people not worth replacing

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • supplant v. 取代、代替
    Few ideas have traveled as far as the Great Replacement, which holds that national populations are being deliberately supplanted by immigrants.
    「偉大替代」這個概念流傳深遠，它主張國家的人口正遭到移民蓄意取代。
  • revenue n. 收入、收益
    They bring construction work and tax revenue, but few permanent jobs, and their profits flow to distant investors.
    它們帶來建設工作和稅收，但只有少數永久職工，且其利潤流向遠方的投資者。
  • investor n. 投資者
    They bring construction work and tax revenue, but few permanent jobs, and their profits flow to distant investors.
    它們帶來建設工作和稅收，但只有少數永久職工，且其利潤流向遠方的投資者。
  • infrastructure n. 基礎設施
    The data center is the visible infrastructure of an economy that seems to need a community’s resources, but few of its members.
    資料中心是一個經濟體的具體基礎設施，這個經濟體似乎需要社區的資源，卻不需要其成員。
  • competition n. 競爭
    Immigration politics is a politics of competition.
    移民政治是競爭的政治。
  • employee n. 員工、受雇者
    One person’s rival is another’s neighbor, employee or child.
    一個人的對手是另一個人的鄰居、員工或孩子。
  • demand n. 需求
    For much of US history, immigration was driven not only by war, persecution and family ties, but by employers’ demands for labor.
    在美國歷史的很大一部分時間裡，移民的推動力不僅僅是戰爭、迫害和家庭聯繫，還有雇主對勞動力的需求。
  • contract v. 收縮、縮小
    If demand contracts across a widening range of occupations, Trump’s wall might come to seem unnecessary...
    如果各行各業的需求都在萎縮，川普的圍牆可能會顯得沒有必要……
  • dependent adj. 依賴的
    ...not because Americans have settled their quarrel over belonging, but because the economy has become less dependent on newcomers and natives alike.
    ……這並不是因為美國人已經解決了關於歸屬感的爭端，而是因為經濟已經變得不再那麼依賴新來者和本地人了。

文法重點

  • 受詞子句 (Object Clause)
    Three-quarters of Americans say they would oppose the construction of a data center in their community...
    在此句中，say 後方接了一個省略了連接詞 that 的子句（they would oppose...），作為 say 的受詞，用以表達美國人所說的內容。
  • 關係代間詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    The data center is the visible infrastructure of an economy that seems to need a community’s resources, but few of its members.
    以關係代間詞 that 引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾前方先行詞 an economy，補充說明這個經濟體的特徵。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    National populations are being deliberately supplanted by immigrants.
    此處使用現在進行式的被動語態（are being + 過去分詞 supplanted），強調國家人口「正在被」移民取代這個動態過程。
