中國安全法規增加旅遊與經商風險

China security laws spell travel risks

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • implement v. 實施，執行
    China is set to implement several laws in the coming months that expand the definition of national security...
    中國預計在未來幾個月實施多項擴大國家安全定義的法律...
  • regulation n. 法規，規章
    China’s Regulations of the State Council on Exit and Entry Administration are to take effect on Tuesday next week...
    中國國務院的《出境入境管理法》修正案預計於下週二生效...
  • amendment n. 修正案，修改
    ...while amendments to its National Defense Mobilization Law and the Ministry of Public Security’s Measures for the Supervision and Inspection of Cyberspace Security are to take effect on Oct. 1.
    ...而《國防動員法》修正案以及公安部的《網絡安全監督檢查規定》則將於 10 月 1 日生效。
  • regulate v. 規範，管理，控制
    These laws allow authorities to regulate such activities in the name of national security and national interests, the official said.
    該官員指出，這些法律允許當局以國家安全和國家利益的名義來規範此類活動。
  • discretion n. 裁量權，斟酌決定權
    The Mainland Affairs Council has described such provisions as providing “blanket authorization,” allowing Chinese authorities considerable discretion in determining what constitutes a security or national-interest concern.
    大陸委員會將此類條文形容為提供「概括授權」，允許中國當局在認定何者構成安全或國家利益顧慮時享有相當大的裁量權。
  • investigation n. 調查
    Anyone deemed to have harmed China’s national interests or national security could face investigation or punishment under Chinese law, they said.
    任何被認為危害中國國家利益或國家安全的人，都可能根據中國法律面臨調查或處罰。
  • seize v. 沒收，查扣，佔領
    The revised law would allow the Chinese state to seize private property whenever deemed necessary, they said.
    他們表示，修訂後的法律將允許中國國家在認為有必要時徵用私人財產。
  • resources n. 資源
    ...but also as a mechanism for comprehensively integrating domestic resources and a reflection of Beijing’s growing security concerns amid intensifying competition with the US.
    ...同時也是一項全面整合國內資源的機制，反映出在與美國競爭加劇的背景下，北京日益增長的安全顧慮。
  • mobilization n. 動員
    Third, for the first time, it defines national defense mobilization as activities designed to “ensure a rapid transition between peacetime and wartime,” and convert economic and social strength into national defense capabilities.
    第三，它首次將國防動員定義為旨在「確保平戰轉換順利」，並將經濟與社會實力轉化為國防能力的活動。
  • expropriation n. 徵收，徵用（私有財產）
    Fourth, it changes the legal treatment of civilian resources from “requisition” to “expropriation.”
    第四，它將對民間資源的法律處理從「徵購」改為「徵收」。
  • operations n. 營運，運作，業務
    Taiwanese companies could have their assets expropriated or have their operations classified as resources related to China’s “development” interests...
    台商業者的資產可能會被徵收，或者其營運可能會被歸類為與中國「發展」利益相關的資源...
  • investment n. 投資
    ...raising the risk that investments could be “gone for good,” the official said.
    ...從而提高投資「有去無回」的風險。

文法重點

  • be set to do sth (預定／準備要做某事)
    China is set to implement several laws in the coming months that expand the definition of national security...
    「be set to + 原形動詞」用來表達某事按計畫即將發生或預定要進行，在新聞英語中非常常見，用以報導未來的政策或事件。
  • 分詞構句作附帶說明
    ...creating greater risks for Taiwanese traveling to or doing business in China, Taiwanese officials said yesterday.
    句尾的「creating...」是由對等子句簡化而來的分詞構句，表結果或伴隨狀況，用來補充說明前面整句（中國即將實施多項法律）所帶來的後續影響。
  • 現在分詞當後位修飾語
    An official familiar with cross-strait affairs, speaking on condition of anonymity, said...
    句中的「familiar with cross-strait affairs」是形容詞片語作後位修飾語，修飾前面的名詞「An official」；而「speaking on condition of anonymity」則是現在分詞片語作補充說明，意為「在匿名條件下發言」。
  • 被動語態的應用
    Terms such as “national security” and “national interests” are broadly defined, they said.
    本句使用被動語態「are broadly defined」（被廣泛定義），當文章焦點在於「名詞/概念本身」如何被對待、而非「誰去定義」時，被動語態是新聞英語中經常使用的結構。
  • 不定詞片語作目的狀語
    Third, for the first time, it defines national defense mobilization as activities designed to “ensure a rapid transition between peacetime and wartime,”...
    句中的「to ensure a rapid transition...」是不定詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞片語「activities」，表達這些活動的「目的」是為了確保平戰轉換順利。
