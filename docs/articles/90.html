台北匯市美元走貶

U.S. dollar down in Taipei trading

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • trade v. / n. 交易，買賣
    The U.S. dollar was traded at NT$31.707 at 10 a.m. Thursday on the Taipei Foreign Exchange
    台北外匯市場周四上午10點美元兌新台幣匯率為31.707元
  • foreign exchange n. 外匯
    The U.S. dollar was traded at NT$31.707 at 10 a.m. Thursday on the Taipei Foreign Exchange
    台北外匯市場周四上午10點美元兌新台幣匯率為31.707元
  • down adj. / adv. 下跌，下降
    down NT$0.021 from the previous close
    較前一交易日收盤價下跌0.021元
  • previous adj. 先前的，以前的
    down NT$0.021 from the previous close
    較前一交易日收盤價下跌0.021元
  • close n. 收盤（價）
    down NT$0.021 from the previous close
    較前一交易日收盤價下跌0.021元

文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The U.S. dollar was traded at NT$31.707 at 10 a.m. Thursday on the Taipei Foreign Exchange
    在此句中，主詞「The U.S. dollar（美元）」是動作的承受者，因此使用「be動詞 (was) + 過去分詞 (traded)」構成被動語態，強調匯率被交易的客觀事實。
  • 分詞片語表附帶狀況或結果
    The U.S. dollar was traded at NT$31.707 at 10 a.m. Thursday on the Taipei Foreign Exchange, down NT$0.021 from the previous close.
    句尾的「down NT$0.021 from the previous close」省略了「which was」，直接使用形容詞片語作補充說明，用來描述美元交易價格變動的狀況。
