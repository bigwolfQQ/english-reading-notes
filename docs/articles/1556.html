單字解析

  • investigator n. 調查人員、偵查員
    DNA left on a protective painting cover helped investigators recover two stolen Renoirs...
    留在防護畫套上的 DNA 幫助調查人員找回了兩幅被竊的雷諾瓦畫作……
  • prosecutor n. 檢察官
    ...a French prosecutor said on Monday.
    ……一名法國檢察官於週一表示。
  • estimate v. 估計、估算
    He estimated their combined value at 4.2 million euros (US$4.7 million).
    他估計這兩幅畫的總價值為 420 萬歐元（約合 470 萬美元）。
  • combined adj. 聯合的、總和的、結合的
    He estimated their combined value at 4.2 million euros (US$4.7 million).
    他估計這兩幅畫的總價值為 420 萬歐元（約合 470 萬美元）。
  • abandon v. 拋棄、遺棄
    “As they fled, they were unable to take all four paintings and abandoned two of them...”
    「在他們逃跑時，他們無法帶走所有四幅畫，並遺棄了其中兩幅……」
  • surveillance n. 監視、監控
    Police surveillance also showed that the 29-year-old on Oct. 3 visited the Maeght Foundation...
    警方監視器還顯示，這名 29 歲男子於 10 月 3 日造訪了梅格基金會……
  • imminent adj. 即將發生的、逼近的
    The arrests were prompted by the “very strong suspicion that another heist was imminent,” Bessone said.
    貝松表示，逮捕行動是由於「有非常強烈的懷疑認為另一起竊盜案即將發生」。
  • acknowledge v. 承認
    The third suspect acknowledged receiving the paintings at his home and hiding them...
    第三名嫌疑人承認在他的家中接收了這些畫作，並將其藏匿……
  • funding n. 資金、經費
    ...and renewed calls for better security and funding at the many cultural sites across France.
    ……並再次呼籲加強法國各地眾多文化場所的安全和資金投入。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作為後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    DNA left on a protective painting cover helped investigators recover two stolen Renoirs...
    句中的 "left on a protective painting cover" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "DNA"，相當於形容詞子句 "which was left on a protective painting cover" 的簡化用法，在新聞英文中非常常見，能讓句子更精簡。
  • 非限定關係子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    The Renoir Museum occupies the final home of the leading French Impressionist painter Pierre-Auguste Renoir, who lived there until his death in 1919.
    逗號後面的 "who lived there until his death in 1919" 是一個非限定關係子句，用來補充說明前面的專有名詞 "Pierre-Auguste Renoir"。補充性質的子句前必須加上逗號，且不能用 "that" 代替 "who"。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The arrests were prompted by the “very strong suspicion that another heist was imminent,” Bessone said.
    句中的 "were prompted" 採用了被動語態（be動詞 + 過去分詞），強調動作的承受者（The arrests 逮捕行動）而非施事者。在商業與新聞英語中，當焦點在於事件本身而非誰去執行時，經常會使用被動語態。
