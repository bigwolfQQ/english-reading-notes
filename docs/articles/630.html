台灣在U18擊敗菲律賓奪下銅牌

Taiwan rout Philippines for bronze in U18

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • defeat v. 擊敗，打敗
    Taiwan on Friday similarly defeated the Philippines 10-0 in five innings.
    台灣週五也曾以10比0在五局內同樣擊敗過菲律賓。
  • rule n. 規則，條例
    Under the rule, a game ends early if one team leads by 10 or more runs after five complete innings.
    根據該規則，若在一場比賽完成五局後，其中一隊領先10分或以上，比賽就會提前結束。
  • allow v. 允許，在此處引申為「失分、被敲出（安打）」
    Taiwan’s three pitchers — Yang Yao-cheng, Tsai Cheng-lung and Chen Yu-hsun — combined to allow just two hits throughout the game.
    台灣的三任投手——楊曜丞、蔡承龍與陳睦衡——全場聯手僅被敲出2支安打。
  • award v. 授予，頒發（勝投等）
    Tsai, who struck out two in two innings, was awarded the game.
    投了兩局並三振兩次的蔡承龍獲得勝投。
  • tournament n. 錦標賽，競賽
    Taiwan has finished third six times in the tournament, which has been held every two years since 1994.
    台灣在該賽事中已拿下6次第三名，該賽事自1994年起每兩年舉辦一次。
  • edition n. 版本，此處指「（歷屆的）賽事」
    Taiwan, who won the previous edition in 2024, were seeking their first back-to-back titles and fourth overall championship win...
    在2024年贏得上一屆冠軍的台灣，原本尋求首度衛冕以及隊史第四座冠軍盃...
  • lead n. 領先（地位）
    The hosts were relegated to the third-place playoffs after losing 2-1 to Japan in the Super Round on Saturday.
    地主隊在週六的超級循環賽以1比2敗給日本後，落入季軍爭奪戰。
  • remain v. 保持，剩餘
    Neither team scored again over the remaining six innings.
    雙方在剩下的六局中皆未再得分。
  • secure v. 獲得，確保
    Manya pitched a scoreless seventh to secure the win.
    萬谷投了無失分的第七局以守住勝局。
  • deficit n. 赤字，落後的分數
    Wu Hao-siang held Japan hitless over the final 1-1/3 innings, but Taiwan could not erase the deficit despite finishing with four hits and drawing five walks.
    吳皓翔在最後的1又1/3局讓日本隊無安打，但台灣全場敲出4支安打並獲得5次保送，仍無法抹平落後的差距。
  • advance v. 晉級，前進
    The top three teams from the tournament advance to next year’s World Baseball Softball Confederation U-18 Baseball World Cup...
    本屆賽事的前三名球隊將晉級明年...的世界盃棒球賽。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Tsai, who struck out two in two innings, was awarded the game.
    句中的「who struck out two in two innings」是關係代間詞引導的形容詞子句，用來補充說明主詞 Tsai（蔡承龍）在比賽中的表現。在新聞英語中，常透過這種結構來補充人物的戰績或背景資料。
  • 現在分詞表附帶結果或動作 (Present Participle Indicating Result)
    Yusei Ishida reached on an infield single before Ritora Yamada hit an RBI double and Kenshin Manya followed with a run-scoring single, giving Japan a 2-1 lead.
    句尾的「giving Japan a 2-1 lead」是由現在分詞（present participle）引導的分詞構句，用來表達前面連續安打所自然產生的結果（給了日本隊2比1的領先）。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The top three teams from the tournament advance to next year’s World Baseball Softball Confederation U-18 Baseball World Cup, which is to be held in Pingtan, China, from Sept. 17 to 26.
    句中的「is to be held」使用了被動語態加上「be to V」的結構，用來表達官方排定、預計在未來特定時間與地點舉辦的官方賽事或行程。
