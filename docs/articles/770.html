文法重點

  • 分詞構句作附帶說明 / 結果
    Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co (TSMC, 台積電) remains conservative about utilizing artificial intelligence (AI) to process sensitive data, aiming to mitigate leakage and subsequent damage given the technology’s capabilities...
    句尾的「aiming to...」是由「which aims to...」簡化而來的分詞構句，用來補充說明前面的主句（台積電保持保守態度）其目的為何。在多益閱讀測驗中常考這種分詞片語來測試考生對句子結構與邏輯主詞一致性的理解。
  • Given 複合介詞 / 連接詞表示「鑑於、考量到」
    ...aiming to mitigate leakage and subsequent damage given the technology’s capabilities, TSMC co-chief operating officer Y.J. Mii (米玉傑) said at a forum in Taipei yesterday.
    「given」在此作為介詞使用，等同於 considering 或 taking into account，意思是「鑑於……」、「考量到……」。這在商業英文與多益考試中是非常高頻的用法，常置於句中或句首引導背景條件。
  • 關係代名詞子句（非限定用法）修飾先行詞
    Mii called AI a “three-year-old superman,” which is immensely powerful, yet completely unaware of the damage it can cause and has no inherent sense of right and wrong.
    句中的「which is immensely powerful...」是帶有逗號的非限定關係子句，用來補充說明前面的名詞「a “three-year-old superman”」。多益文法題常考關係代名詞（如 which / who）的選擇，此處先行詞為事物，故使用 which。
  • 被動語態的使用
    ...as microprocessors containing hundreds of billions of transistors are required to be optimized, where AI can be brought into full play, Mii said.
    句中的「are required to be optimized」採用了被動語態，強調微處理器「被要求」必須進行最佳化。多益商業寫作與閱讀中經常使用被動語態來客觀描述規定、程序或商業條件。
