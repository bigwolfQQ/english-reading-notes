文法重點

  • 分詞構句作表補充說明 (with + N + 分詞)
    With US President Donald Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping (習近平) expected to meet in Washington this week, Mainland Affairs Council Deputy Minister Shen Yu-chung (沈有忠) yesterday said...
    「With + 受詞 + 過去分詞 (expected)」構成伴隨或條件的獨立分詞構句，用來補充說明動作發生時的背景狀況，意指「在……預期將會面的情況下」。這是多益閱讀與進階英文常見的結構。
  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase)
    Shen made the remarks at a seminar held by the Cross-Strait Policy Association and Tamkang University’s Center for Cross-Strait Relations Studies in Taipei, adding that he believes there would be “three no changes”...
    句尾的「adding that...」是由現在分詞（-ing）引導的片語，用來修飾主句的動作，補充說明沈有忠在發表談話的同時，還多說了哪些內容。這種用法能使句子緊湊，避免使用冗長的對等子句（and added that...）。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase)
    Shen made the remarks at a seminar held by the Cross-Strait Policy Association and Tamkang University’s Center for Cross-Strait Relations Studies in Taipei...
    句中的「held by...」是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞 a seminar（研討會）。原句省略了「which was」，還原全貌為「a seminar (which was) held by...」，在多益測驗中常考這種分詞修飾名詞的簡化結構。
  • 形容詞片語作受詞補語 (Adjective Phrase as Object Complement)
    Taiwan must bolster its defense and societal resilience, solidify ties with the US Congress and the First Island Chain allies such as Japan, and reinforce the semiconductor industry as a “silicon shield,” he said.
    句中的「reinforce the semiconductor industry as a “silicon shield”」使用了「reinforce + 受詞 (the semiconductor industry) + as + 受詞補語」的結構，用來表達「將半導體產業強化（定位）為矽盾」。
