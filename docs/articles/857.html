障礙賽跑選手薩布爾克服對欄架的恐懼

Steeplechaser Sable conquers fear of hurdles

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • compete v. 競爭、參賽
    Avinash Sable had no idea if he would compete again after a nasty fall...
    阿維納什·薩布爾經歷了一次嚴重的摔傷後，完全不知道自己是否還能再次參賽...
  • sustain v. 遭受、承受（受傷、損失等）
    ...but sustained a serious knee injury at the Monaco Diamond League meet last year...
    ...但在去年摩納哥鑽石聯賽中遭受嚴重的膝蓋受傷...
  • recovery n. 恢復、復原
    ...when psychological issues complicated his recovery.
    ...當心理問題使他的復原過程變得複雜時。
  • confidence n. 信心、自信
    I had no confidence at all, didn’t know how I would be able to recover.
    我一點信心也沒有，不知道自己該如何恢復。
  • support n. / v. 支持、支援
    ...and, with the support of fellow athletes and the staff at the SAI’s Bengaluru center...
    ...在同行運動員和 SAI 班加羅爾中心工作人員的支持下...
  • motivate v. 激勵、促使
    ...when I had pain, they motivated me to get through it.
    ...當我有疼痛感時，他們激勵我撐過去。
  • competitor n. 競爭者、對手
    ...finishing 18 seconds ahead of the second-placed competitor.
    ...領先第二名選手 18 秒衝線。
  • aim v. / n. 旨在、目標
    The aim is to do better than the last Asian Games...
    目標是做得比上屆亞運更好...

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表示附帶狀況或結果）
    India’s Asian Games steeplechase champion Avinash Sable had no idea if he would compete again after a nasty fall resulted in 14 months on the sidelines and crippling self-doubt.
    句中的 after 引導時間副詞子句，子句內部包含了一個主動的動詞短語與結果。在新聞英語中，常運用分詞或動名詞片語（如 resulting in）來緊湊地表達事件帶來的直接後果。
  • 現在分詞當形容詞修飾名詞
    Now back in competitive shape after 14 months on the sidelines, Sable aims to defend his Asian Games steeplechase title...
    句首的 "Now back in competitive shape after 14 months on the sidelines" 是形容詞片語（省略了主詞與be動詞，如 When he is now back...），用來修飾主句的主詞 Sable，表示其當下的狀態。
  • 使役動詞的用法（make + O. + 原形動詞）
    “My coaches made me believe that I would get my fitness back ... when I had pain, they motivated me to get through it.”
    句型為「make + 受詞 + 原形動詞」，在此表示「使某人做某事」。教練讓薩布爾相信他能找回體能，展現了使役動詞強迫或促使某人達成某種心理狀態的語氣。
