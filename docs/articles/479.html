台灣正擬定針對中國大陸的旅遊警示

Taiwan drafting travel advisory on PRC

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • mitigate v. 減輕、緩和
    The government is preparing guidelines to help Taiwanese mitigate risks when traveling to China
    政府正準備相關指引，以協助台灣人在前往中國大陸時降低風險
  • implement v. 實施、執行
    as Beijing prepares to implement new border rules
    隨著北京準備實施新邊境規則
  • inspection n. 檢查、審查
    that could extend inspections to mobile phones and other electronic devices
    這可能會將檢查擴及手機和其他電子設備
  • regulation n. 規章、條例
    Beijing’s revised entry and exit regulations are scheduled to take effect on Tuesday next week
    北京修訂的出入境規定預計於下週二生效
  • advisory n. （政府或機構發布的）警告、公告
    prompting the US to update its travel advisory for China.
    並促使美國更新其對中國的旅遊警示。
  • expose v. 暴露、揭露
    many Taiwanese have expressed concern that business secrets could be exposed
    許多台灣人表達了擔憂，認為商業機密可能會外洩
  • caution n. 謹慎、小心
    urging three groups of people in particular to exercise heightened caution.
    並特別敦促三類群體提高警覺。
  • distribution n. 分發、配送、流通
    Village wardens and local civil affairs officials could also be targeted for information regarding air-raid shelters, emergency supplies and distribution channels
    村里長和地方民政官員也可能成為被鎖定的目標，以獲取有關防空避難所、緊急物資和配送管道的資訊
  • sector n. 部門、行業
    Third, professionals working in sensitive sectors such as semiconductors, quantum science, advanced materials and defense
    第三，在半導體、量子科學、先進材料和國防等敏感產業工作的專業人員
  • sanction v. 制裁
    and firms connected to entities sanctioned by Beijing
    以及與北京制裁實體有關聯的公司
  • slowdown n. 減緩、景氣衰退
    The country’s economic slowdown has created a situation
    該國的經濟放緩造成了……的局面
  • jurisdiction n. 司法管轄權、審判權
    demonstrates Beijing’s efforts to extend its jurisdiction beyond its borders
    展現了北京將其司法管轄權延伸至境外的企圖
  • intellectual property n. phr. 智慧財產權
    have already begun paying closer attention to the risks posed to intellectual property and human resources
    已經開始更密切關注對智慧財產權和人力資源造成的風險
  • vigilant adj. 警戒的、警惕的
    He urged the international community to remain vigilant regarding the CCP’s attempts
    他敦促國際社會對中共的企圖保持警戒

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The government is preparing guidelines to help Taiwanese mitigate risks when traveling to China as Beijing prepares to implement new border rules that could extend inspections to mobile phones and other electronic devices...
    句中的 "when traveling to China" 與 "as Beijing prepares..." 等結構，或者是帶有現在分詞的修飾語，用來補充說明主句動作發生的背景或時間條件。在此處 "traveling" 前省略了主語與be動詞（when [they are] traveling），使句子更為簡鍊。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Beijing’s revised entry and exit regulations are scheduled to take effect on Tuesday next week...
    「are scheduled」為be動詞加上過去分詞構成的被動語態，用來表達預定、安排好的狀態。在新聞英語中，經由官方安排或排程的事件非常常使用被動語態來客觀陳述。
  • 名詞子句作受詞 (Noun Clause as Object)
    many Taiwanese have expressed concern that business secrets could be exposed or that their past social media comments could be used against them...
    句中的 "that business secrets could be exposed..." 是用來補充說明 "concern" 的同位語子句（或作名詞子句處理），用 "that" 引導一個完整的子句，表達具體擔憂的內容。
  • 現在分詞表附帶結果或補充說明 (Present Participle indicating Result/Addition)
    ...adding that global supply chains are accelerating efforts to reduce reliance on China.
    句尾的 ", adding that..." 是由分詞構句簡化而來，原意為 "and he added that..."。用現在分詞 "adding" 可以流暢地在句尾接續補充說明的內容，是新聞報導中常用的寫作技巧。
