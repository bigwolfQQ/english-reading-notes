單字解析

  • reject v. 拒絕、退回
    The Micron Taiwan workers’ union yesterday rejected a one-time payout unveiled by US-based Micron Technology Inc last week...
    美光台灣企業工會昨天拒絕了美國美光科技公司上週公布的一次性發放方案...
  • operating profit n.phr. 營業利潤、營業利益
    ...saying it wants a long-term mechanism under which employees would share 15 percent of the company’s operating profits.
    ...並表示希望建立一個長效機制，讓員工能夠分享公司 15% 的營業利潤。
  • dispute n. 爭端、糾紛
    While the union and the company are still in talks over the dispute, the reward package was proposed unilaterally by the employer...
    雖然工會和公司仍在就爭端進行談判，但這項獎勵方案是資方單方面提出的...
  • compensation n. 補償、薪資報酬
    ...employees in Taiwan would receive compensation equivalent to 35 to 68 months’ pay for this fiscal year...
    ...台灣員工在本會計年度將獲得相當於 35 個月至 68 個月薪資的補償...
  • fiscal year n.phr. 會計年度、財政年度
    ...after the union threatened to strike.
    ...在工會威脅罷工之後。
  • strike v. / n. 罷工
    ...after the union threatened to strike.
    ...在工會威脅罷工之後。
  • transparent adj. 透明的、公開的
    He urged Micron to establish a transparent and verifiable profit-sharing mechanism...
    他敦促美光建立一個透明且可驗證的利潤分享機制...

    Lin said whether the union stages a strike would depend on the outcome of two rounds of negotiations scheduled for this Friday and Monday next week.

文法重點

  • 「介系詞 + 名詞 + which」的關係子句
    ...saying it wants a long-term mechanism under which employees would share 15 percent of the company’s operating profits.
    句中的 "under which" 用來引導形容詞子句，修飾前面的先行詞 "mechanism"（機制）。因為是「在該機制下（under the mechanism）員工共享利潤」，所以將介系詞 "under" 移至關係代間詞 "which" 前方，使語氣更為正式，常出現在商業合約與新聞報導中。
  • 現在分詞片語作結果狀語
    Global demand for memory chips has risen amid the artificial intelligence (AI) boom, tightening supplies and driving up prices.
    逗點後面的 "tightening supplies and driving up prices" 是由現在分詞（tightening / driving）帶動的分詞片語。用來補充說明前面主句所發生的動作所帶來的自然結果（AI 熱潮使需求上升，『進而』導致供應緊縮並推高價格）。
  • 表條件的假設語氣（第一條件句）
    He said that if the company continues to seek to influence the talks, the union would declare that negotiations have broken down...
    此句使用第一條件句來表達未來可能發生的真實狀況。結構為「if + 現在式動詞（continues），主詞 + will / would + 原形動詞（would declare）」。用來假設如果公司持續干預談判，工會將會採取下一步行動。
  • 現在分詞作後位修飾
    Taiwan is Micron’s largest memory production base, accounting for about 60 percent of its total production capacity...
    句中的 "accounting for..." 是現在分詞片語，放在名詞後方用來補充修飾前面的基地（base）。相當於完整子句 "which accounts for about 60 percent..." 的簡化版，能讓新聞行文更為精鍊緊湊。
