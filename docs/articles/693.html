國際清算銀行表示，全球市場人工智慧動能浮現脆弱跡象

BIS says global market AI momentum showing signs of vulnerability

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • vulnerability n. 脆弱性，易受傷害性
    The artificial intelligence (AI)-linked rally that has seen world stock markets soar over the last two years is showing growing signs of vulnerability, global central bank umbrella body, the Bank for International Settlements (BIS), said yesterday.
    國際中央銀行傘形組織「國際清算銀行」（BIS）昨天表示，過去兩年推動全球股市飆升的人工智慧（AI）相關漲勢，正展現出越來越多的脆弱跡象。
  • cautious adj. 謹慎的，小心翼翼的
    Investors were becoming "increasingly cautious" about the profitability of future AI investments, especially as the leverage of major US tech firms continued to rise, the BIS said in a report.
    國際清算銀行在報告中指出，隨著美國主要科技公司的財務槓桿持續上升，投資人對未來 AI 投資獲利能力的態度變得「日益謹慎」。
  • profitability n. 獲利能力，盈利能力

  • leverage n. 財務槓桿，舉債經營；影響力
    Investors were becoming "increasingly cautious" about the profitability of future AI investments, especially as the leverage of major US tech firms continued to rise, the BIS said in a report.
    國際清算銀行在報告中指出，隨著美國主要科技公司的財務槓桿持續上升，投資人對未來 AI 投資獲利能力的態度變得「日益謹慎」。
  • resilience n. 恢復力，韌性
    "The AI momentum, which had propelled equity markets and contributed to the resilience of the global economy of the last year, has begun to show growing signs of vulnerability," BIS economic analysis head Frank Smets told reporters on Friday ahead of the report’s publication.
    國際清算銀行經濟分析主管法蘭克·斯梅茨（Frank Smets）週五在該報告發表前對記者表示：「人工智慧動能曾推動股市上漲，並有助於過去一年全球經濟的韌性，如今已開始顯示出越來越多的脆弱跡象。」
  • down sharply phr. 急跌，大幅下跌
    AI-connected stocks were down sharply yesterday after the heads of a number of top AI firms warned at the weekend that the pace of the technology’s development needed to be slowed to prevent threats to humanity.
    由於多位頂尖 AI 企業的負責人在週末警告稱，必須放慢該技術的發展步伐以防止對人類構成威脅，與 AI 相關的股票昨天急挫。
  • public finances n. phr. 公共財政，國庫財務
    The BIS report also pointed to an uncertain global backdrop of strains on public finances, exacerbated by geopolitical tensions and volatile energy prices.
    國際清算銀行的報告還指出，公共財政承受壓力的全球背景充滿不確定性，地緣政治緊張局勢和動盪的能源價格更使情況惡化。
  • issue v. 發行（債務、股票等），發布
    The hundreds of billions of dollars worth of debt AI firms have been issuing could also be contributing to the rise in government bond market borrowing costs, known as yields, on top of the long-held concerns about the sustainability of debt levels.
    除了長期以來對債務水準永續性的擔憂之外，AI 企業一直在發行的數千億美元債務，也可能促成了政府債券市場借貸成本（即殖利率）的上升。
  • sustainability n. 永續性，持續可能性

  • appetite n. 胃口，強烈的慾望（多益常與 risk 併用指「風險胃納／風險偏好」）

    儘管如此，他補充說，整體而言「沒有出現壓力跡象」，且投資人的風險胃納在近幾個月來依然「非常具有韌性」。

  • aggregate adj. 總計的，合計的

    科技公司的總借款額已從 2010 年的約 220 億美元（佔總私人信貸的 22%）上升到去年的超過 1 兆美元（佔 44%）。

文法重點

  • 現在完成進行式 (Present Perfect Continuous)
    The artificial intelligence (AI)-linked rally that has seen world stock markets soar over the last two years is showing growing signs of vulnerability...
    「has seen」在此為現在完成式，用以表達過去兩年持續到現在的經驗或結果；而句中另一處「has been issuing」則是現在完成進行式（have/has been + V-ing），用來強調某個動作從過去某個時間點開始一直持續到現在，甚至可能繼續下去。這在財經新聞中常用於描述企業持續發行債務或市場趨勢。
  • 非限定關係代間子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    The AI momentum, which had propelled equity markets and contributed to the resilience of the global economy of the last year, has begun to show growing signs of vulnerability...
    句首的「Dubbed...」是過去分詞片語當作分詞構句，而「which had propelled...」則是帶有逗號的非限定關係子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞（The AI momentum），提供額外的背景資訊。若將此子句拿掉，主句的文意依然完整。
  • 過去分詞作為形容詞後置修飾 (Past Participle as Post-modifier)
    The BIS report also pointed to an uncertain global backdrop of strains on public finances, exacerbated by geopolitical tensions and volatile energy prices.
    句尾的「exacerbated by geopolitical tensions and volatile energy prices」是由過去分詞「exacerbated（使惡化）」引出的分詞片語，放在被修飾的名詞片語後面，作後置修飾用，相當於省略了「which are/were exacerbated by...」。這種結構在正式商業與新聞英文中非常常見，能有效精簡句子。
  • 名詞子句作受詞 (Noun Clause as Object)
    “Whether this resilience (in markets) can be sustained, especially if upward pressures on yields continue, remains, however, uncertain,” Smets said.
    引號內的「Whether this resilience... can be sustained」是一個由「Whether（是否）」引導的名詞子句，在整個句子中擔任主詞的位置（整句結構為：[名詞子句] remains uncertain）。名詞子句在多益閱讀測驗的文法題和長篇閱讀中經常出現。
