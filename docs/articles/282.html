文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾語 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    the Ministry of Foreign Affairs said in a statement released yesterday.
    句中的「released yesterday」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「statement」（聲明）。這相當於形容詞子句「that was released yesterday」的簡化寫法，省略了「which/that was」，用來精簡語句。
  • 分詞構句表結果 (Present Participle Indicating Result)
    ..., thereby promoting the well-being of both peoples and fostering mutual benefit and shared prosperity...
    「thereby promoting...」是由連接詞 thereby 帶領的現在分詞構句，用來接續前文的動作，表示自然而然產生的結果（進而促進……）。這在商業與正式英文書信中非常常用，用來表達一項行動所帶來的正面效益。
  • 現在分詞片語作主詞補語／狀況說明 (Present Participle Phrase Indicating Circumstance)
    The itineraries of the delegations, their membership and the lawmakers’ public remarks indicate that Panama has been interested in Taiwan’s technological and economic development, suggesting that parliamentary exchanges between the two sides are increasingly focused on practical cooperation in technology and trade.
    句尾的「suggesting that...」是一個現在分詞片語，補充說明前面整個子句所代表的狀況與其隱含的意義。主詞（行程、成員與發言）主動發出了「暗示」這個動作，因此使用現在分詞 suggesting。
