文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附加說明（補充結果）
    CNN, MS NOW and Politico also went to court after Trump on Friday ordered them to be barred, as the 80-year-old Republican stepped up his years-long assault on the US media.
    句尾的「as the 80-year-old Republican stepped up...」是由連接詞 as 引導的原因副詞子句，用來補充說明前面主句動作發生的背景原因（隨著這名 80 歲的共和黨人加緊攻擊）。
  • 關係代間子句（限定修飾先行詞）
    In their lawsuit, the three organizations asked for a temporary restraining order against the ban on the grounds that it violates the first amendment of the US Constitution, which upholds the right to a free press.
    句尾的「which upholds the right to a free press」是非限定關係子句（帶逗號），以關係代間詞 which 接續，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「the first amendment of the US Constitution（美國憲法第一修正案）」。
  • 被動語態的使用
    Journalists from CNN, MS NOW and Politico were denied any access to the White House grounds on Saturday and had their passes confiscated.
    句中的「were denied」採用了被動語態（be + 過去分詞），強調記者們「被拒絕」進入白宮的受事者立場，突顯事件的衝突性。
  • 複合名詞作修飾語（Noun Adjunct）
    The ribbon-cutting ceremony for the giant granite helipad on the South Lawn was only streamed on the White House’s Web site...
    句中的「ribbon-cutting」是由名詞 ribbon 與現在分詞 cutting 結合而成的複合形容詞，用來修飾後方的名詞「ceremony（典禮）」，意即「剪綵儀式」，在商業與新聞英文中非常常見。
