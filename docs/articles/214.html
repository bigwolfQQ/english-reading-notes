11月地方選舉有26位候選人為陸配：中選會

26 candidates for November local elections are Chinese spouses: CEC

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • register v. 登記，註冊
    A total of 19,695 candidates have registered for 11,051 positions in the nine-in-one local elections on Nov. 28
    共有 19,695 名候選人登記角逐 11 月 28 日九合一地方選舉的 11,051 個職位
  • candidate n. 候選人
    The CEC and local election commissions are scheduled to complete candidate eligibility reviews by Oct. 16
    中選會與地方選舉委員會預計於 10 月 16 日前完成候選人資格審查
  • eligibility n. 資格，合格
    You said the eligibility of all candidates will be determined based on current laws
    游盈隆表示，所有候選人的資格都將依據現行法律來判定
  • compare with v. phr. 與……相比較
    Compared with the 2022 local elections, the total number of candidates decreased slightly by 130
    與 2022 年地方選舉相比，總候選人數量微幅減少了 130 人
  • decrease v. 減少，降低
    the total number of candidates decreased slightly by 130
    總候選人數量微幅減少了 130 人
  • increase v. 增加，提高
    while the number of Chinese-spouse candidates increased by seven, You said.
    而陸配候選人則增加了 7 人，游盈隆表示。
  • in line with prep. phr. 與……一致，符合
    However, the changes are in line with long-term trends observed since the first nine-in-one local elections in 2014
    不過，這些變化符合自 2014 年首次九合一地方選舉以來所觀察到的長期趨勢
  • determine v. 決定，判定
    You said the eligibility of all candidates will be determined based on current laws
    游盈隆表示，所有候選人的資格都將依據現行法律來判定
  • abolish v. 廢除，廢止
    Meanwhile, a national referendum on whether the government should abolish its nuclear-free homeland policy will be held alongside the local elections
    同時，一項關於政府是否應廢除其非核家園政策的全國性公投將與地方選舉同時舉行
  • reject v. 拒絕，駁回
    The CEC rejected two other referendum proposals approved by the Legislature
    中選會駁回了立法院通過的另外兩項公投提案
  • revenue n. 收入，收益
    one seeking to introduce judicial caning and another proposing that revenue from traffic violation fines be earmarked for road safety improvements
    其中一項尋求引進笞刑，另一項則提案將交通違規罰款收入指定專款用於道路安全改善
  • scope n. 範圍
    You previously explained that the two proposals did not fall within the scope of matters that can be put to a referendum
    游盈隆先前解釋，這兩項提案並不屬於可以交付公投的事項範圍

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    including 26 Chinese spouses naturalized as Taiwan nationals, Central Election Commission (CEC) Chairman Michael You (游盈隆) said Monday.
    句中的「naturalized as Taiwan nationals」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「Chinese spouses」（陸配），相當於形容詞子句「who are naturalized as Taiwan nationals」的簡化形式，能讓句子更精簡流暢。
  • 被動語態的未來式 (Future Passive Voice)
    The CEC and local election commissions are scheduled to complete candidate eligibility reviews by Oct. 16 and draw numbers for eligible candidates on Oct. 23
    「are scheduled to complete」使用了 beV + 過去分詞（搭配片語 be scheduled to），表達「預定於何時完成某事」的被動與時程安排語氣，在商業和官方新聞中非常常用。
  • 現在分詞表達附帶結果與說明 (Present Participle indicating Result)
    indicating stable political engagement over the past 12 years, he said.
    句尾的「indicating...」是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前面整句（這些變化符合長期趨勢）所帶出的結果或意義，意思是「這顯示出……」。
  • 被動語態與未來式的結合 (Future Passive Voice with will be)
    Meanwhile, a national referendum on whether the government should abolish its nuclear-free homeland policy will be held alongside the local elections
  • 表建議、要求等動詞後的受詞子句中使用原形動詞（虛擬語氣） (Subjunctive Mood)
    another proposing that revenue from traffic violation fines be earmarked for road safety improvements and public transportation subsidies.
    在表示建議或提案的動詞（如 propose）後面的名詞子句中，used「that 主詞 + (should) + 原形動詞」，因此這裡用「be earmarked」而非「is earmarked」，展現正式英文的文法特點。
