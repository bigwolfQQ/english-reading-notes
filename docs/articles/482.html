文法重點

  • 關係代間代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    President William Lai (賴清德) yesterday promulgated amendments to the Income Tax Act (所得稅法) that would reduce the tax burden on families with children...
    句中的 "that would reduce..." 是用來修飾前面的先行詞 "amendments"（修正案）。關係代間代名詞 "that" 在子句中擔任主詞，用來補充說明這些修正案具體會帶來什麼效果。
  • 非限定性關係子句（補充說明）
    The revised law, which takes effect retroactively from Jan. 1, would also allow social insurance premiums to be fully deducted...
    逗號後的 "which takes effect retroactively from Jan. 1" 是一個非限定性關係子句，對前面的 "The revised law" 進行補充說明。使用非限定性子句時，前面須加上逗號，且不能用 "that" 代替 "which"。
  • 現在分詞作結果狀語（分詞構句）
    ...and are generally mandatory by law, making them similar in nature to NHI, the ministry said.
