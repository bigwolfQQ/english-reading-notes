聯發科發表天璣 9600 Pro 行動晶片

MediaTek releases Dimensity 9600 Pro mobile chip

單字解析

  • unveil v. 發表、推出、揭幕
    MediaTek Inc yesterday unveiled a new smartphone chip made using Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co’s most advanced manufacturing technology.
    聯發科昨天發表了一款使用台積電最先進製造技術的新款智慧型手機晶片。
  • target v. 瞄準、以……為目標
    targeting the premium handset market with semiconductors capable of running more artificial intelligence features directly on the device.
    鎖定高階手機市場，該半導體能夠直接在裝置上執行更多人工智慧功能。
  • margin n. 利潤率、利潤空間
    It is part of an effort to gain ground on US rival Qualcomm Inc in premium phones, where higher-priced devices and AI features offer chipmakers better margins.
    這是為了在高階手機市場上拉近與美國競爭對手高通公司差距的一環，在高階手機市場中，較高價位的裝置與 AI 功能為晶片製造商提供了更好的利潤空間。
  • segment n. 市場區隔、部分
    It also introduced the Dimensity 9600M, made using TSMC’s 3nm process and aimed at a broader segment of the flagship smartphone market.
    它還推出了天璣 9600M，採用台積電 3 奈米製程製造，旨在搶攻更廣泛的旗艦智慧型手機市場。
  • supplier n. 供應商（文章中使用動詞 supply）
    It has supplied chips to Chinese smartphone customers such as Xiaomi Corp, Oppo Mobile Telecommunications Corp and Vivo Communication Technology Co.
    它一直向中國智慧型手機客戶供應晶片，例如小米集團、歐珀移動通信有限公司和維沃移動通信有限公司。
  • surpass v. 超越、勝過
    MediaTek’s market value surpassed that of Qualcomm earlier this year.
    聯發科的市值今年稍早超越了高通。
  • component n. 零件、組件
    MediaTek corporate senior vice president J.C. Hsu said the company was working with handset makers on ways to limit the effect of higher component costs on consumers as the AI boom strains supply chains.
    聯發科公司資深副總經理徐敬全表示，隨著 AI 浪潮使供應鏈吃緊，公司正與手機製造商合作，尋找方法來限制較高的零組件成本對消費者的影響。
  • retail v. 零售價為
    Apple Inc’s new foldable iPhone, announced last week, retails for as much as US$3,199 in the US for the highest-storage model.
    蘋果公司上週宣布推出的新款摺疊式 iPhone，在美國最高儲存容量機型的零售價高達 3,199 美元。
  • expand v. 擴展、擴大
    MediaTek, one of the world’s largest designers of smartphone chips, has been expanding its AI business into datacenter and custom chip products, as technology companies pour billions of dollars into AI infrastructure.
    聯發科是全球最大的智慧型手機晶片設計商之一，隨著科技公司向 AI 基礎設施投入數十億美元，該公司一直在將其 AI 業務擴展到資料中心和客製化晶片產品。
  • mass production n. phr. 大量生產、量產
    Its first AI accelerator designed for a major US cloud service provider is expected to enter mass production in the fourth quarter of this year.
    其專為美國主要雲端服務供應商設計的第一款 AI 加速器，預計於今年第四季進入量產。
  • offering n. 發行（證券等）、提供的產品或服務
    The company raised US$3.9 billion last month through a convertible bond sale, with Nvidia Corp investing US$3.5 billion in the offering, while Alphabet Inc, a long-term MediaTek partner in AI infrastructure, also participated.
    該公司上個月透過可轉換公司債發售籌集了 39 億美元，其中輝達投資了 35 億美元，而作為聯發科在 AI 基礎設施領域長期合作夥伴的字母控股公司也參與其中。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    MediaTek Inc yesterday unveiled a new smartphone chip made using Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co’s most advanced manufacturing technology...
    句中的 "made using..." 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前方的名詞 "a new smartphone chip"，等於形容詞子句 "which was made using..." 的簡化版，能讓新聞英文更簡潔流暢。
  • 現在分詞表達附帶結果或伴隨狀況 (Present Participle indicating Result/Condition)
    ...targeting the premium handset market with semiconductors capable of running more artificial intelligence features directly on the device.
    句尾的 "targeting..." 是現在分詞片語，補充說明主句動作所帶來的目的或伴隨的狀態，表示該晶片的推出「旨在鎖定高階手機市場」。
  • 關係副詞 where 引導的非限定子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause with 'where')
    It is part of an effort to gain ground on US rival Qualcomm Inc in premium phones, where higher-priced devices and AI features offer chipmakers better margins.
    句中的 ", where..." 用來補充說明前面的先行詞 "premium phones"，在先行詞為地點或抽象領域時，可用 where 來引導子句，補充更多背景資訊。
  • 被動語態的應用 (Passive Voice)
    The first smartphones powered by the two chips will be launched soon, the company said.
    句中的 "will be launched" 採用了被動語態（助動詞 will + be + 過去分詞），強調「推出」這個動作的承受者是智慧型手機，而不去強調是誰去執行這個動作。
