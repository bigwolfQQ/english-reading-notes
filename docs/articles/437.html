巴西最高法院採取行動平息法官間的激烈爭端

Brazil Supreme Court moves to end bitter judges’ feud

單字解析

  • suspend v. 暫停、中止、停職
    The Brazilian Supreme Court president on Wednesday attempted to end a crisis in the court by suspending a series of conflicting rulings...
    巴西最高法院院長於週三試圖透過暫停一系列衝突性裁決來結束法院內部危機……
  • ruling n. 裁決、判決
    ...by suspending a series of conflicting rulings by rival judges that have dominated a bitter election campaign.
    ……透過暫停由對立法官做出、且主導了激烈選戰的一系列衝突性裁決。
  • scandal n. 醜聞
    The court is reeling from a scandal involving the alleged links between leading Justice Alexandre de Moraes and a disgraced banker...
    該法院正因一起醜聞而陷入動搖，該醜聞涉及首席大法官亞歷山大·德·莫賴斯與一名聲譽掃地的銀行家之間的疑似聯繫……
  • investigation n. 調查
    ...who famously led the coup investigation into former Brazilian president Jair Bolsonaro...
    ……他因主導針對前巴西總統雅伊爾·波索納洛的政變調查而聞名……
  • dispute n. 爭端、糾紛
    Brazilian Supreme Court President Edson Fachin promptly waded into the dispute and suspended both rulings...
    巴西最高法院院長埃德森·法辛迅速介入這場爭端，並暫停了這兩項裁決……
  • information n. 資訊、情報
    Fachin gave Rodrigues 48 hours to provide information before deciding his fate.
    法辛給予羅德里格斯 48 小時的時間提供資訊，然後再決定他的命運。
  • publication n. 公布、出版
    Mendonca fired the opening salvo in the row by ordering the publication last week of text messages...
    門多薩上週下令公布簡訊，藉此打響了這場爭端的頭陣……
  • confidential adj. 機密的
    Moraes hit back, accusing Mendonca of abuse of power on the basis of confidential police intelligence reports...
    莫賴斯隨即反擊，根據機密的警方情報報告指控門多薩濫用職權……
  • collapse v. 倒閉、崩潰
    The texts from Vorcaro, whose private bank Banco Master collapsed last year...
    沃卡羅的私人銀行馬斯特銀行去年倒閉，他發送的這些簡訊……
  • transparency n. 透明度
    Lula on Wednesday said “greater transparency” was needed in the case...
    盧拉於週三表示，該案需要「更高的透明度」……
  • investigation n. 調查
    “It is urgent to fully lift the secrecy of the investigations into all those involved in the Master case...”
    「當務之急是徹底解除對馬斯特案所有涉案人員調查的保密限制……」
  • arrest n. / v. 逮捕
    In the days leading up to his arrest in November, Vorcaro sent about 30 text messages...
    在 11 月被捕前的幾天裡，沃卡羅發送了大約 30 條簡訊……
  • convicted adj. 被定罪的
    Jair Bolsonaro, an ally of US President Donald Trump, is serving a 27-year jail sentence after he was convicted of trying to cling to power...
    美國總統唐納·川普的盟友雅伊爾·波索納洛在被判罪名成立、試圖抓權不放後，正在服 27 年的有期徒刑……
  • transfer v. 轉帳、轉移
    ...after it emerged that the banker transferred millions of dollars to an intermediary at the senator’s request.
    ……在有報導指出該銀行家應參議員要求將數百萬美元轉帳給一名中間人後……
  • legitimacy n. 合法性
    “If the legitimacy [of the judges] is questioned, everything else becomes uncertain...”
    「如果（法官們的）合法性受到質疑，其他一切都將變得不確定……」

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作後置定語
    The Brazilian Supreme Court president on Wednesday attempted to end a crisis in the court by suspending a series of conflicting rulings by rival judges that have dominated a bitter election campaign.
    句中的 "conflicting"（衝突的）是由動詞 conflict 轉變而來的現在分詞，在此作為形容詞放在名詞 "rulings" 前面修飾其意。此外，"by rival judges that have dominated..." 則是透過關係代名詞子句進一步修飾 rulings，這是多益閱讀測驗中常見的長句結構。
  • 過去分詞（片語）作後置定語
    The court is reeling from a scandal involving the alleged links between leading Justice Alexandre de Moraes and a disgraced banker charged with massive fraud.
    句尾的 "charged with massive fraud" 是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞 "banker"。這相當於一個省略了關代與be動詞的形容詞子句（who was charged with massive fraud），多益常考這種分詞簡化結構來測試對句子主從關係的理解。
  • 現在分詞片語作結果狀語（分詞構句的延伸）
    On Tuesday, the country’s top policeman, Andrei Rodrigues, was swept up in the infighting, when he was suspended by Mendonca, who accused the police of spying on him.
    此句包含了一個非限定關係子句 "who accused the police of spying on him"，用來補充說明 Mendonca 做出的行動。在商業英文與新聞英語中，"who" 引導的非限定子句（帶逗號）非常常考，用來提供額外的背景資訊。
  • 虛主詞 It is + 形容詞 + to V 句型
    “It is urgent to fully lift the secrecy of the investigations into all those involved in the Master case, whoever they may be, no matter who it hurts.”
    句首的 "It" 為虛主詞，真正的受詞/主詞是後面以不定詞開頭的 "to fully lift the secrecy..."。這種結構在多益職場書信與正式公告中極其常見，用來表達「做某事是……的（重要、緊急、必須）」。
  • 動名詞片語作介系詞受詞
    The scandal engulfing the Supreme Court has sown alarm in the Brazilian business community.
    句中的 "engulfing the Supreme Court" 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "The scandal"。而整句主詞動詞搭配為 "The scandal ... has sown alarm"，展現了標準的主動語態表達方式，也是多益聽力與閱讀的核心文法。
