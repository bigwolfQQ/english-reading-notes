  • raise v. 提高，增加
    EVA Airways Corp is to raise excess baggage fees and revise other international baggage charges beginning on Sept. 29
    長榮航空公司將自 9 月 29 日起調高超重行李費並修正其他國際行李收費
  • excess adj. 額外的，過量的

  • revised adj. 修改過的，修正的
    The revised baggage policy covers excess baggage, prepaid excess baggage, overweight and oversized baggage fees.

  • policy n. 政策，規定
    The revised baggage policy covers excess baggage, prepaid excess baggage, overweight and oversized baggage fees.
    修正後的行李政策涵蓋超重行李、預付超重行李、過重與過大行李費。
  • prepaid adj. 預付的
    The revised baggage policy covers excess baggage, prepaid excess baggage, overweight and oversized baggage fees.
    修正後的行李政策涵蓋超重行李、預付超重行李、過重與過大行李費。
  • rate n. 費率，價格
    Under the new rates, excess baggage fees would increase by between US$20 and US$90 per piece, depending on the route.

    在新費率下，超重行李費每件將調高 20 至 90 美元不等，具體取決於航線。

文法重點

  • be to + 原形動詞（預定、即將發生的官方計畫）
    EVA Airways Corp is to raise excess baggage fees and revise other international baggage charges beginning on Sept. 29...
    句型「be to V」常出現在新聞報導中，用來表示官方正式的計畫、安排或即將發生的重要決策，相當於 is scheduled to 或 is going to。
  • 現在分詞當作形容詞/介系詞使用 (citing)
    ...citing higher fuel prices and rising global operating costs amid economic and geopolitical pressures.
    句尾的 citing（源自 cite，指「引述、歸因於」）是以現在分詞分詞片語的形式引導出原因，用來修飾前面的子句，說明調高費用的理由。
  • 對等連接詞 while 表對比
    Zone 3 covers Los Angeles, Seattle, San Francisco, Vancouver, Hawaii and the Middle East, while Zone 4 includes the rest of North America, Europe, including Turkey, Africa and Russia.
    在此句中，while 連接兩個子句，用來表達「然而、而」的對比語氣，常被多益測驗用來考句意理解與連接詞選擇。
