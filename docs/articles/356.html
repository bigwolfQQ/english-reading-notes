文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作為後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...expressed the hope that the partnership would help build a long-term pipeline of translators capable of bringing more Taiwanese literature to British readers.
    句中的「capable of bringing...」是形容詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「translators（譯者）」。其完整概念是「translators who are capable of bringing...」，省略了「who are」後直接以現在分詞片語呈現，使句子更為簡鍊。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    ...a "Literature from Taiwan" track will be added to the National Centre for Writing's Emerging Translator Mentorships, which recruits aspiring translators worldwide and provides mentoring and professional exchanges.
    句中的「which recruits...」是非限定關係子句（前面有逗號），用來補充說明前面的先行詞「Emerging Translator Mentorships（新銳譯者指導計畫）」，為讀者提供更多背景資訊。
  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense)
    ...said the center had begun promoting Taiwanese literature even before Yang Shuang-zi's "Taiwan Travelogue" gained international recognition...
    句中使用了過去完成式「had begun」，用來表示在過去某一時間點（gained international recognition，贏得國際肯定）之前，中心「已經開始」推廣台灣文學，凸顯動作發生的先後順序。
  • 過去分詞作為形容詞修飾名詞 (Past Participle as Adjective)
    He added that the center plans to expand its collaboration with Taiwan in 2027 through further writer and translator residencies, as well as a Taiwan literature festival planned for next spring.
    句尾的「planned for next spring」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「a Taiwan literature festival（台灣文學節）」，意指「被計畫於明年春天舉辦的文學節」，具有被動意味。
