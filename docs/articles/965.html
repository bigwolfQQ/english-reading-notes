《黑神話：悟空》音樂會結束台北兩場演出

'Black Myth: Wukong' concert wraps up 2-night stint in Taipei

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • feature v. 以...為特色；由...主演
    Taiwaneses and Chinese musicians on Sunday wrapped up the Taipei leg of a global concert tour featuring music from the 2024 video game "Black Myth: Wukong".
    台灣與中國音樂家於週日結束了全球巡迴音樂會的台北站演出，該巡演以2024年電玩遊戲《黑神話：悟空》的音樂為特色。
  • conclude v. 結束；結素
    The two-night concert at Taipei's Legacy MAX theater concluded Sunday with an encore performance of the game's main theme...
    這場在台北 Legacy MAX 劇場舉行的兩晚音樂會於週日畫下句點，安可曲為該遊戲的主題曲...
  • in addition to phr. 除了...之外（還）
    In addition to Taipei, the "Black Myth: Wukong Global Concert" tour has stopped in Los Angeles and New York's Carnegie Hall...
    除了台北之外，「黑神話：悟空全球音樂會」巡演已造訪洛杉磯、紐約卡內基音樂廳...
  • organizer n. 主辦單位；組織者
    According to the Taipei concert's organizer, Meow Meow International, conductor Ben Yu and concertmaster Jack Chen led musicians...
    根據台北音樂會主辦單位「喵喵國際」表示，指揮游家輔與樂團首席陳仕杰帶領音樂家...
  • combination n. 結合；組合
    The ensemble performed the game's music using a combination of Western classical and traditional East Asian instruments...
    該樂團結合了西方古典與東亞傳統樂器來演奏遊戲音樂...
  • associated with phr. 與...相關的
    "Celestial Symphony" is one of the best-known pieces associated with the 1986 television series...
    《雲宮迅音》是與1986年電視劇相關的最著名曲目之一...
  • recruit v. 招募；吸收（成員）
    Moreover, Taiwanese singer Robert Hu was recruited to perform alongside Chinese singers Qin Yong and Xiong Zhuying...
    此外，台灣歌手胡家誠獲招募，與中國歌手秦勇和熊竹英同台演出。
  • opportunity n. 機會；時機
    Li said the concert was an opportunity to bring a performance that could resonate culturally with audiences in Taiwan.
    李表示，這場音樂會是一個機會，能帶來一場在台灣能與觀眾產生文化共鳴的演出。
  • be scheduled to phr. 預定...；安排於...
    Following the Taipei concerts, the tour is scheduled to visit Singapore in October, Bangkok in November and Berlin later this year.
    在台北場音樂會之後，巡演預計於10月造訪新加坡、11月造訪曼谷，並於今年稍晚前往柏林。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作為後置修飾語（現在分詞片語）
    Taiwaneses and Chinese musicians on Sunday wrapped up the Taipei leg of a global concert tour featuring music from the 2024 video game "Black Myth: Wukong".
    句中的 "featuring music from..." 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前方的名詞 "a global concert tour"，相當於形容詞子句 "that featured music from..." 的簡化版，用以精簡語句並說明巡演的特色內容。
  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾語（被動概念）
    The concert tour features music from "Black Myth: Wukong," a video game based on characters and stories from the 16th century Chinese novel "Journey to the West"...
    句中的 "based on..." 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "a video game"。因為遊戲是被基於/改編自這些故事，所以使用過去分詞 (based) 表被動與完成的概念，省略了 "which is"。
  • 介詞片語當作附加說明（Alongside...）
    As part of the world tour, local musicians perform alongside Chinese musicians and singers involved in the game's original music...
    句首使用 "As part of..." 作為介詞片語說明身分或角色，而句中的 "alongside..." 則表示「與...並肩/一同」，其後又接了另一個過去分詞片語 "involved in..." 來修飾前面的 "musicians and singers"。
