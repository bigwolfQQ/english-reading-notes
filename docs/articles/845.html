社論：聯合國值得試著加入嗎？

EDITORIAL: Is the UN worth trying to join?

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • participation n. 參與，參加
    Taiwanese-Americans, lawmakers and civic groups marched through Manhattan on Saturday last week in support of Taiwan’s participation in the UN.
    台美人士、國會議員與公民團體上週六在曼哈頓遊行，以支持台灣參與聯合國。
  • delegation n. 代表團
    Democratic Progressive Party Legislator Wang Yi-chuan (王義川) took part while visiting New York City with a delegation.
    民主進步黨立法委員王義川則在隨代表團訪問紐約市期間參與了活動。
  • campaign n. 活動，競選活動，運動
    The campaign comes as the 81st UN General Assembly gets under way.
    這項競選／活動正值第81屆聯合國大會展開之際。
  • sovereignty n. 主權
    President William Lai (賴清德) said China was deliberately misinterpreting UN Resolution 2758 to claim sovereignty over Taiwan...
    總統賴清德表示，中國蓄意扭曲聯合國大會第2758號決議以主張對台灣的主權...
  • practical adj. 實際的，實用的
    What practical benefits would Taiwan gain from participating in the UN and its affiliated organizations?
    台灣從參與聯合國及其附屬組織中能獲得什麼實質（實際）利益？
  • benefit n. 利益，好處
    What practical benefits would Taiwan gain from participating in the UN and its affiliated organizations?
    台灣從參與聯合國及其附屬組織中能獲得什麼實質利益？
  • exclude v. 排除，排斥
    Taiwan already participates in organizations including the WTO, Asian Development Bank and APEC, but remains excluded from the WHO and others.
    台灣已經參與了包括世界貿易組織（WTO）、亞洲開發銀行（ADB）與亞太經濟合作會議（APEC）等組織，但仍然被排除在世界衛生組織（WHO）和其他組織之外。
  • effectiveness n. 效力，有效性
    ...its effectiveness is frequently hobbled by disagreements among member states, particularly in the UN Security Council.
    ...但其效能經常因成員國之間的分歧而受阻，特別是在聯合國安全理事會中。
  • accomplish v. 完成，實現，達成
    The UN’s record on war and security raises questions about what membership could realistically accomplish.
    聯合國在戰爭與安全方面的紀錄，讓人質疑會員身分實際上能達成什麼成果。
  • incentive n. 誘因，動機，鼓勵
    Yet, Taiwan has little incentive to abandon the campaign.
    然而，台灣幾乎沒有動機放棄這場運動。
  • negotiate v. 談判，協商
    It could seek greater participation in specialized international organizations, negotiate bilateral and multilateral agreements, and expand unofficial ties with other countries...
    它可以在不解決其地位爭端的情況下，尋求更深入參與專業國際組織、談判雙邊與多邊協議，並擴大與其他國家的非官方聯繫...
  • objective n. 目標，目的
    ...could some of those objectives be achieved without becoming a UN member?
    ...那麼其中一些目標是否能在不成為聯合國會員的情況下達成？

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附帶狀況／時間複句的簡化
    Democratic Progressive Party Legislator Wang Yi-chuan (王義川) took part while visiting New York City with a delegation.
  • 過去分詞作後置修飾語（被動語態含義）
    What practical benefits would Taiwan gain from participating in the UN and its affiliated organizations?
  • 現在分詞作表「伴隨結果」的獨立分詞構句
    Beijing’s activities in the Taiwan Strait and South China Sea have contributed to tensions with Japan, the Philippines and other countries in the Indo-Pacific region, contributing to... (本句為 ..., while Beijing's activities... have contributed to...) / 類似結構如：...expanding diplomatic relations, participating in international organizations, gaining access to global decisionmaking and establishing wider recognition...
  • 虛主詞 It 的句型（強調不證自明的主語或形式主語）
    Before asking the international community to recognize Taiwan as a nation, Taiwan might have to grapple with what it means by sovereignty itself.
