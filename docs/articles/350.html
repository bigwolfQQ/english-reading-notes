立委林岱樺在貪腐案中被追加起訴

Legislator Lin Tai-hua hit with new charges in corruption case

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • indict v. 起訴，控告
    Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) Legislator Lin Tai-hua (林岱樺), who was indicted last year over false expense claims, was charged on Wednesday with embezzling over NT$13.79 million (US$438,492) in campaign funds.
    民主進步黨籍立法委員林岱樺去年因涉嫌虛報開支遭到起訴，週三又被指控侵占超過1379萬新台幣（合438,492美元）的競選經費。
  • embezzle v. 侵占，挪用（公款等）
    Prosecutors said Lin Tai-hua denied embezzling funds during interrogation, claiming that after the death of her father, she had given full control over reporting of campaign contributions to her brother.
    檢方表示，林岱樺在偵訊時否認侵占資金，並聲稱在父親過世後，她已將競選捐款申報的完整控制權交給了弟弟。
  • campaign funds n. phr. 競選經費
    In total, NT$6.47 million in campaign funds were paid to Lin Tai-jung's company, the indictment said.
    起訴書指出，總計有647萬新台幣的競選經費支付給了林岱融的公司。
  • invoice n. 發票，請款單
    Starting in 2021, one of Lin Tai-jung's companies, Mao Sheng Co., began issuing false invoices for services to Lin Tai-hua's campaign, which his employees, who were not in on the scheme, lawfully reported to the Control Yuan.
    自2021年起，林岱融的其中一家公司「茂盛公司」開始針對林岱樺的競選活動開立不實的服務發票，而該公司的員工並未參與這起計畫，依法向監察院進行了申報。
  • liability n. 債務，負債，責任
    In addition to withdrawing some of the illicit funds in cash, the money was also used to pay tax liabilities for clients of an accounting and bookkeeping firm owned by Lin Tai-jung, as well as the corporate income taxes of three associations.
    除了提領部分不法資金作現金使用外，這筆錢也被用來支付林岱融所經營的會計記帳事務所客戶的稅務債務，以及三個協會的營利事業所得稅。
  • capital increase n. phr. 增資
    NT$7.31 million in campaign funds were paid to Jung Wei Co., which Lin Tai-jung used to conduct a capital increase and pay the company's taxes and building rental costs, while the remainder was kept in a "discretionary slush fund."
    共有731萬新台幣的競選經費支付給了融威公司，林岱融將其用於辦理增資、繳納該公司稅金及大樓租金，其餘款項則存放在一個「自由裁量的小金庫」中。
  • remainder n. 剩餘部分，其餘
    NT$7.31 million in campaign funds were paid to Jung Wei Co., which Lin Tai-jung used to conduct a capital increase and pay the company's taxes and building rental costs, while the remainder was kept in a "discretionary slush fund."
    共有731萬新台幣的競選經費支付給了融威公司，林岱融將其用於辦理增資、繳納該公司稅金及大樓租金，其餘款項則存放在一個「自由裁量的小金庫」中。
  • statement n. 聲明，陳述
    In a statement, Lin Tai-hua said the charges against her were "false," saying she had spent her whole life in politics and was committed to Taiwan's development and the well-being of its people.
    林岱樺在聲明中表示，對她的指控是「不實的」，並稱她一生投入政治，致力於台灣的發展與人民的福祉。
  • commit v. 投入，致力於（常用被動態 be committed to）
    In a statement, Lin Tai-hua said the charges against her were "false," saying she had spent her whole life in politics and was committed to Taiwan's development and the well-being of its people.
    林岱樺在聲明中表示，對她的指控是「不實的」，並稱她一生投入政治，致力於台灣的發展與人民的福祉。
  • asset n. 資產，財產
    Lin added that she had practically no assets, had never engaged in any type of graft or illicit gain, and had no motive to misappropriate campaign contributions.
    林岱樺補充說，她幾乎沒有任何資產，從未從事任何形式的貪汙或不法利益，也沒有動機挪用競選捐款。

文法重點

  • 非限定性關係代名詞子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) Legislator Lin Tai-hua (林岱樺), who was indicted last year over false expense claims, was charged on Wednesday with embezzling over NT$13.79 million (US$438,492) in campaign funds.
    句中的「who was indicted last year over false expense claims」是一個非限定性關係子句，用逗號與主句隔開，用來補充說明主詞「Legislator Lin Tai-hua」的額外資訊，移開後不影響主要句意。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The Kaohsiung District Prosecutors Office filed a supplementary indictment against Lin, her brother Lin Tai-jung (林岱融) and a friend of his, for embezzlement of funds held for official duties or for the public interest.
    句中的「held for official duties or for the public interest」是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞「funds」。完整結構省略了「which were」，用來精簡語句，表達「被持有用於公務或公益的資金」。
  • 對等連接詞連接的多重動詞片語與分詞構句
    NT$7.31 million in campaign funds were paid to Jung Wei Co., which Lin Tai-jung used to conduct a capital increase and pay the company's taxes and building rental costs, while the remainder was kept in a "discretionary slush fund."
    句中使用了關係代名詞「which」引導子句說明用來做什麼，並利用對等連接詞 and 連接兩個原形動詞（conduct 和 pay）；後半段則使用「while」引導對等子句，表達對比的情況（其餘的款項則被存放在...）。
