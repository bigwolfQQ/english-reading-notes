台灣高鐵增開週五列車並提供自由座以緩解擁擠

THSRC adds Friday trains, non-reserved seats to ease crowding

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • peak adj. / n. 尖峰的、高峰的；頂峰
    Taipei, Oct. 5 (CNA) Taiwan High Speed Rail Corp. (THSRC) said Monday it will add southbound trains to Taichung during Friday evening peak hours starting Oct. 8, with all seats except those in business class available on a non-reserved basis.
    台北，10月5日（中央社）— 台灣高鐵公司（THSRC）週一表示，自10月8日起，將於週五傍晚尖峰時段增開南下至台中之列車，除商務車廂外，其餘所有座位皆採自由座方式提供。
  • available adj. 可取得的、可使用的、有空的
    Taipei, Oct. 5 (CNA) Taiwan High Speed Rail Corp. (THSRC) said Monday it will add southbound trains to Taichung during Friday evening peak hours starting Oct. 8, with all seats except those in business class available on a non-reserved basis.
    台北，10月5日（中央社）— 台灣高鐵公司（THSRC）週一表示，自10月8日起，將於週五傍晚尖峰時段增開南下至台中之列車，除商務車廂外，其餘所有座位皆採自由座方式提供。
  • demand n. / v. 需求、要求
    THSRC said in a news release that 5 p.m.-9 p.m. on Fridays is typically a peak travel period, particularly between Taichung and Taipei, as commuters and other travelers returning home increase demand for non-reserved seating.
    台灣高鐵透過新聞稿指出，週五下午5點至9點通常是旅遊尖峰時段，特別是在台中與台北之間，因為通勤族與其他返鄉旅客增加了對自由座的需求。
  • announcement n. 公告、宣布
    The announcement followed severe crowding during the four-day Mid-Autumn Festival and Teachers' Day holiday from Sept. 25-28, when some passengers with reserved seats reportedly struggled to board and passengers from non-reserved cars spilled into business-class cars.
    這項公告是在9月25日至28日為期四天的中秋節與教師節連假期間出現嚴重擁擠之後發布的，當時據報導，部分持對號座車票的旅客費力上車，且自由座車廂的旅客湧入商務車廂。
  • measure n. 措施、方法
    Although it is not a Friday, THSRC said the new measure will first be implemented this Thursday, the final working day before the three-day Republic of China (Taiwan) National Day holiday.
    台灣高鐵表示，儘管這天不是週五，但這項新措施將率先於本週四實施，即三天中華民國（台灣）國慶連假前的最後一個上班日。
  • implement v. 實施、執行
    Although it is not a Friday, THSRC said the new measure will first be implemented this Thursday, the final working day before the three-day Republic of China (Taiwan) National Day holiday.
    台灣高鐵表示，儘管這天不是週五，但這項新措施將率先於本週四實施，即三天中華民國（台灣）國慶連假前的最後一個上班日。
  • capacity n. 容量、產能、載客量
    To increase capacity, THSRC said trains arriving in Taichung will return north from the same platform, allowing them to depart again within 12 minutes of arrival.
    為了增加運能，台灣高鐵表示抵達台中的列車將從同一月台折返北上，使其能夠在抵達後12分鐘內再次發車。
  • arrangement n. 安排、協議
    THSRC said the arrangement will also add northbound capacity and reduce waiting times for passengers using non-reserved seating.
    台灣高鐵表示，這項安排也將增加北上運能，並縮短使用自由座旅客的候車時間。
  • reduce v. 減少、降低
    THSRC said the arrangement will also add northbound capacity and reduce waiting times for passengers using non-reserved seating.
    台灣高鐵表示，這項安排也將增加北上運能，並縮短使用自由座旅客的候車時間。
  • schedule v. / n. 預定、安排；時程表
    The additional Friday services are scheduled to continue until the new N700ST trains enter service on July 1, 2027.
    這些週五的加開服務預計將持續到新型N700ST列車於2027年7月1日投入營運為止。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    THSRC said in a news release that 5 p.m.-9 p.m. on Fridays is typically a peak travel period, particularly between Taichung and Taipei, as commuters and other travelers returning home increase demand for non-reserved seating.
    句中的「returning home」是由現在分詞帶領的片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「travelers（旅客）」，相當於形容詞子句「who return home」。這種寫法在新聞英文中非常常見，能讓句子更精簡流暢。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    The announcement followed severe crowding during the four-day Mid-Autumn Festival and Teachers' Day holiday from Sept. 25-28, when some passengers with reserved seats reportedly struggled to board and passengers from non-reserved cars spilled into business-class cars.
    句中的「when」引導非限定關係子句，先行詞是「Sept. 25-28」這段假期。「when」在此等於「during which」，用來補充說明該段期間所發生的狀況，逗號後方不具限定性，提供額外的背景資訊。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Although it is not a Friday, THSRC said the new measure will first be implemented this Thursday, the final working day before the three-day Republic of China (Taiwan) National Day holiday.
    句型「will be implemented」使用了被動語態（will be + 過去分詞），強調「措施（measure）」本身被執行，而不是它去執行動作。在商業與新聞報導中，當動作的執行者不明顯或不重要時，常會使用被動語態來聚焦於受事者。
  • 現在分詞表達附帶結果 (Present Participle indicating Result)
    To increase capacity, THSRC said trains arriving in Taichung will return north from the same platform, allowing them to depart again within 12 minutes of arrival.
    句尾的「allowing them to depart...」是以現在分詞開頭的片語，用來表達前一個主要動作所自然產生的結果或帶來的效應，相當於「...and this allows them to depart...」，是多益閱讀測驗中常見表達因果或附帶作用的進階句構。
