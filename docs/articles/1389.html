  • anniversary n. 週年紀念日
    Israel yesterday marked the third anniversary of Hamas’ attack with silent tributes...

  • debate n. 辯論、討論
    ...with silent tributes, new strikes on Gaza and intense political debate, weeks before elections...
    ...伴隨默哀致敬、對加薩發動新空襲以及激烈的政治辯論，在選舉前幾週...
  • tribute n. 致敬、悼念
    Israel yesterday marked the third anniversary of Hamas’ attack with silent tributes...
    以色列昨天以默哀致敬……標記了哈瑪斯攻擊事件三週年
  • official adj. 官方的、正式的
    ...despite a ceasefire officially in place for the past year.
    ...儘管過去一年官方已實施停火。
  • retaliation n. 報復
    ...most of which has been reduced to rubble in relentless bombing in retaliation for the worst attack ever against Israel.
    ...該領土大部分已在為報復以色列史上最嚴重攻擊而進行的無情轟炸中化為瓦礫。
  • accountability n. 當責、問責、責任歸屬
    ...to symbolize the Oct. 7 dead and to urge accountability for the failure to prevent the attack.
    ...以象徵 10 月 7 日的死難者，並敦促追究未能防止此次攻擊的責任。

文法重點

  • 非限定關係子句 (Non-defining relative clause)
    At the site of the Nova music festival, where Hamas militants in trucks and paragliders opened fire around dawn on Oct. 7, 2023, family members bowed their heads quietly...
    句中的「where Hamas militants...」引導一個非限定關係子句，前面帶有逗號，用來補充說明前面的地點「the Nova music festival」，提供額外的背景資訊。
  • 分詞片語作後置修飾 (Participle phrase as post-modifier)
    ...family members bowed their heads quietly — although at least one instead put on techno music in tribute to the fallen.
    句尾的「the fallen」是由過去分詞轉化而來的名詞（the + 形容詞/過去分詞表示一類人），意指「陣亡者、死難者」，修飾精簡且符合新聞文體。
  • 被動語態的現在完成式 (Present perfect passive)
    ...most of which has been reduced to rubble in relentless bombing in retaliation for the worst attack ever against Israel.
    「has been reduced」是現在完成式的被動語態，用來強調加薩領土「已經被」無情轟炸變成瓦礫堆的狀態，對現在造成的影響。
  • be to do 句型（表預定、計畫或即將發生的官方行程）
    On Oct. 27, Israel is to hold elections for the first time since the attack...
    「is to hold」中的 be to 用來表達按計畫或安排即將發生的官方重要事件（此處指即將舉行的選舉），常出現在新聞報導中。
