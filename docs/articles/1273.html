文法重點

  • 現在分詞當形容詞（修飾名詞）
    The union representing workers at snack maker Kuai Kuai Co.'s only factory in Taiwan has challenged the company's relocation assurances...
    句中的 representing workers... 是現在分詞片語，用來後置修飾前面的名詞 The union，相當於關代子句 "which represents workers..."。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The dispute arose after Kuai Kuai workers learned in July through the news that Kuai Kuai... sold the Zhongli site to Advanced Semiconductor Engineering, Inc...
    被動語態結構為「be + 過去分詞」，用來強調承受動作的對象。在此文章中也常見諸如 "be allowed to require" 或 "be expected to" 等被動結構。
  • 假設語氣 / 條件句 (Subjunctive/Conditional)
    "How can employees be expected to keep working for two years and believe they will still have jobs afterward?" Peng said.
    此句運用助動詞 can 配合 be expected to 來表達對未來情況的不合理與質疑，展現出強烈的修辭語氣。
