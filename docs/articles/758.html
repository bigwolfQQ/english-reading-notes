學者：沖繩選舉有望促進日本國防建設與台灣安全

Okinawa vote likely to boost Japan’s defense buildup, Taiwan security: Scholars

單字解析

  • governorship n. 知事職位、州長職位
    The anti-Henoko "All Okinawa" camp’s 12-year hold on the Okinawa governorship ended Sunday...
    反邊野古的「全沖繩」陣營對沖繩知事職位長達 12 年的把持於週日劃下句點...
  • incumbent n. 現任者、在職者
    ...when former Naha deputy mayor Genta Koja unseated two-term incumbent Denny Tamaki in a six-candidate race.
    ...前那霸市副市長古謝玄太在一場六人競選中擊敗了兩屆現任知事玉城丹尼。
  • ballot n. 選票、投票
    The conservative-backed 42-year-old won 59.4 percent of valid ballots after campaigning largely on livelihood issues.
    獲得保守派支持、現年 42 歲的古謝玄太在主要打出民生議題的選戰後，贏得了 59.4% 的有效選票。
  • consequence n. 結果、後果
    However, the local election could have consequences far beyond Okinawa.
    然而，這場地方選舉產生的影響可能會遠超出沖繩。
  • favorable adj. 有利的、贊同的
    ...a development both viewed as favorable to Taiwan.
    ——這項發展被雙方視為對台灣有利。
  • friction n. 摩擦、衝突
    ...adding that his victory should ease friction between Okinawa and Tokyo over base relocation and other defense projects.
    ...他並補充說，古謝的勝選應能緩解沖繩與東京之間，關於基地遷移和其他國防專案的摩擦。
  • authority n. 當局、官方
    Prefectural authorities can nevertheless delay projects through administrative and environmental procedures, he added.
    他補充說，地方政府儘管如此，仍然可以透過行政和環境程序來延宕專案。
  • subsidy n. 補助金、津貼
    ...who were also concerned about possible cuts to central government subsidies.
    他們同時也擔憂中央政府的補助金可能會遭到削減。
  • bilateral adj. 雙邊的
    A 2006 bilateral roadmap designated Henoko, a less populated coastal area of Nago, as the replacement site.
    2006 年的雙邊路線圖將名護市人口較少的海濱地區邊野古指定為替代場址。
  • strategy n. 策略、戰略
    "The Ryukyu card will not disappear," Lim said. "But the strategy and approach will change."
    林泉忠說：「琉球牌不會消失。但戰略和手法會改變。」
  • implementation n. 實施、執行
    Koja’s victory would not alter that strategic direction, but could ease its implementation in Okinawa, Kuo said.
    郭育仁表示，古謝的勝選不會改變這種戰略方向，但可以使其實施在沖繩變得更加容易。
  • fiscal year n. 會計年度、財政年度
    With the Defense Buildup Program running through next fiscal year...
    隨著《防衛力整備計畫》持續到下個財政年度...

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    The conservative-backed 42-year-old won 59.4 percent of valid ballots after campaigning largely on livelihood issues.
    句中的「conservative-backed」是由過去分詞 backed 構成的複合形容詞，用來修飾前方的「42-year-old」，意思是「獲得保守派支持的」。這種結構在新聞英語中非常常見，能簡潔地表達被動或完成的概念。
  • 與過去事實相反的假設語氣 (Subjunctive Mood for Past Unreal Conditions)
    Kuo Yu-jen... said Koja could not have won had opposition to relocating Futenma remained decisive...
    此句使用了假設語氣的倒裝句型（省略了 if，將 had 提前至句首）。原句為「if opposition to relocating Futenma had remained decisive」，用來表達「如果當時反對遷移普天間的聲浪依然決定性（事實上沒有維持），古謝就不可能贏（事實上贏了）」。
  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況或結果 (Participial Phrase indicating Concomitant Circumstance)
    Koja’s victory gives Beijing less room to maneuver, Lim said, adding that Beijing may now emphasize trade... while saying less about political status.
    句尾的「adding that...」與「while saying less...」皆為分詞構句。其中 adding 作為主句動作後的順勢補充，說明林泉忠所說的額外內容；while saying less 則表示同時發生的對比狀況（在強調貿易的同時，較少談論政治地位）。
