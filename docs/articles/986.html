台灣在男女團體軟式網球摘下銀牌

Taiwan bags silver medals in men's, women's team soft tennis

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • opponent n. 對手、敵手
    Taiwan took home silver medals after its men's and women's teams lost to their Japanese opponents in the team soft tennis finals at the 2026 Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games on Sunday.
    台灣男子及女子軟式網球代表隊於週日在2026年愛知-名古屋亞洲運動會的團體軟式網球決賽中，敗給日本對手，拿下銀牌。
  • mirror v. 反映、與...相似
    The 2-0 loss to Japan in only 54 minutes mirrored the result of the sides' previous finals match-up at the 2022 Asian Games (played in 2023) in Hangzhou.
    以 2 比 0 不敵日本、歷時僅 54 分鐘的結果，與雙方在 2022 年杭州亞運（於 2023 年舉行）前一次決賽對戰的結果如出一轍。
  • defeat v. 擊敗、打敗
    Taiwan's one win in the match-up was by 22-year-old Asian Games newcomer Chiang Min-yu (江旻育), who defeated Ayaka Iwakura 4-1 in the singles portion of the final.
    台灣在該場對戰中的唯一勝場，是由 22 歲的亞運新秀江旻育拿下，她在決賽的單打項目中以 4 比 1 擊敗岩倉彩香。
  • performance n. 表現、績效
    The silver medal finish tied the women's soft tennis team's best-ever performance at the Asian Games.
    此次獲得銀牌，追平了女子軟式網球代表隊在亞運會上的歷史最佳成績。
  • retirement n. 退休
    Team member Lo Shu-ting (羅舒婷), who previously said this Asian Games would be her "last dance" before retirement, told CNA she was proud of the result.
    先前曾表示本屆亞運將是她在退休前「最後一舞」的代表隊成員羅舒婷，向中央社表示她對這個成果感到驕傲。
  • regret n. 遺憾、懊悔
    "I think we did great. I don't have any regrets in my career," Lo said, adding that going home with a medal "made it all worthwhile."
    羅舒婷說：「我覺得我們表現得很棒。我的運動生涯沒有任何遺憾。」她並補充說，帶著獎牌回家「讓这一切都非常值得」。
  • worthwhile adj. 值得的
    "I think we did great. I don't have any regrets in my career," Lo said, adding that going home with a medal "made it all worthwhile."
    羅舒婷說：「我覺得我們表現得很棒。我的運動生涯沒有任何遺憾。」她並補充說，帶著獎牌回家「讓这一切都非常值得」。
  • format n. 版式、格式、賽制
    In the Asian Games, team soft tennis follows a best-of-three matches format, in which the first national team to win two matches wins.
    在亞運會中，團體軟式網球採用三戰兩勝的賽制，由率先贏得兩場比賽的國家代表隊獲勝。
  • sequence n. 順序、次序
    The event sees two teams face off in a specific sequence: a first match (doubles) played in a best-of-nine games format, followed by a second match (singles) played as a best-of-seven.
    該項目由兩支隊伍按特定順序對決：第一場比賽（雙打）採九局五勝制，接著是第二場比賽（單打）採七局四勝制。
  • advantage n. 優勢
    In cases where a game is tied 3-3 (a "deuce"), one side must gain a two-point advantage to win.
    在比數僵持至 3 比 3（「丟士」）的情況下，其中一方必須取得兩分的領先優勢才能獲勝。

文法重點

  • 非限定關代子句（補充說明前述名詞）
    Team member Lo Shu-ting (羅舒婷), who previously said this Asian Games would be her "last dance" before retirement, told CNA she was proud of the result.
    句中的「who...」前面有逗號，屬於非限定用法（Non-defining relative clause），用來對前面的專有名詞 Lo Shu-ting 進行補充說明，指出她先前曾說過的話，在句中扮演附加資訊的角色。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾
    The event sees two teams face off in a specific sequence: a first match (doubles) played in a best-of-nine games format, followed by a second match (singles) played as a best-of-seven.
    句中的「played in a best-of-nine games format」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 a first match。這相當於省略了「which is played...」，用簡化的分詞結構來使句子更為緊湊精煉。
  • 對稱結構與對比連接詞（while）
    On the men's side, doubles pair and brothers Chen Po-yi (陳柏邑) and Chen Yu-hsun (陳郁勲) were swept 5-0 in the final's first match, while singles player Chang Yu-sung (張祐菘) lost 4-1 in match No. 2.
    連接詞「while」在此處用來表示對比（「然而」、「而」），連接兩個句型結構相似的子句，分別描述男子組雙打與單打在決賽中的不同落敗情況，展現平衡的句法結構。
  • 獨立分詞構句（附帶狀況）
    In terms of scoring, points are counted consecutively 1, 2, 3, 4, with the first player or doubles pair to reach 4 points winning the game.
    句尾的「with + 名詞 + 分詞」結構（with the first player or doubles pair to reach 4 points winning the game）形成獨立分詞構句，用來補充說明計分時伴隨發生的狀況或規則，是英文新聞和商業合約中常見的高級句構。
