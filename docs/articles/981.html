文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The Philippine government on Sunday reaffirmed its long-held stance that it diplomatically recognizes China, rather than Taiwan, but said that position does not prevent Manila from pursuing relations with Taipei.
    句中的 "that it diplomatically recognizes China..." 是同位語子句，用來補充說明 "stance"（立場）的具體內容。而 "diplomatically" 作為副詞修飾動詞 "recognizes"。
  • preclude sb. from doing sth.（預防／阻止某人做某事）
    While that policy remains consistent, clear, and unchanged, it "does not preclude the Philippines from pursuing economic, cultural and functional cooperation with Taiwan,"
    「preclude + 受詞 + from + V-ing」是多益考試常見的句型，意思是「防止、排除、阻礙某人做某事」，與 prevent... from... 或 prohibit... from... 意思相近。
  • 過去分詞作形容詞用 (Past Participle as Adjective)
    Citing a recent comment by Philippines President Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr., the DFA said the Philippines and Taiwan are close neighbors with a long history of doing business together.
    句首的 "Citing a recent comment..." 是現在分詞構句，表示主動的動作（外交部主動引述）；而句中修飾歷史的 "doing business together" 則是現在分詞片語作後位修飾，用來形容 "a long history"。
  • 被動語態的應用 (Passive Voice)
    The DFA statement was released after a Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesperson last week urged Manila to adhere to its one-China stance.
    新聞報導中經常使用被動語態來客觀陳述事實或事件的發生。此處 "was released"（被發布）的主體是聲明本身（The DFA statement），強調動作的承受者。
