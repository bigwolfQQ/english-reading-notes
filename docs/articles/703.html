  • shortage n. 短缺、不足

  • deal with phr. v. 處理、應對

文法重點

  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況（with + 名詞 + 分詞）
    There is no dedicated athletes’ village at the Games, with organizers using a cruise ship, temporary cabins and hotels in a bid to cut costs.
    「with + 受詞 + 現在分詞 (using)」構成附帶狀況的分詞構句，用來補充說明主句發生的同時伴隨的狀況或原因，意指「在主辦單位使用郵輪……的情況下」。
  • 否定對等連接詞 (neither... nor...)
    Local organizers were expecting 15,000 and said last month that they had “neither the manpower nor the budget” to deal with ballooning numbers.
    「neither A nor B」意為「既不 A 也不 B」，在此連接兩個名詞片語（the manpower 與 the budget），用來強調主辦單位兩者皆缺。
  • not only... but (also)... 對等連接詞
    “At the moment there is a serious shortage of accommodation not only for the entire South Korean delegation to the Asian Games, but for delegations from around the world in the Aichi-Nagoya area,” the official said.
    「not only... but (also)...」意為「不僅……而且……」，用來連接兩個介系詞片語（for the entire South Korean delegation... 與 for delegations from around the world...），強調短缺範圍的擴大。
  • 被動語態 (Be + 過去分詞)
    Hundreds of athletes were temporarily evacuated from their accommodation last week as record rainfall lashed Nagoya.
    句中的「were evacuated」為被動語態，表示運動員是被（主辦單位或相關人員）疏散的，強調動作的承受者。
