社論：教育體系亟需改革

EDITORIAL: The education system needs reform

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • shortage n. 短缺、不足
    Taiwan — the shortage of teachers.
    台灣——師資短缺。
  • vacancy n. 職缺、空缺
    schools have struggled to fill substitute teacher vacancies shortly before the start of the school year.
    學校一直在學年開始前夕艱難地填補代理教師的職缺。
  • full-time adj. 專任的、全職的
    Last year, there was a shortage of about 1,700 substitute teachers and 900 full-time teachers.
    去年，代理教師短缺約 1,700 人，專任教師短缺 900 人。
  • position n. 職位、職缺
    The Ministry of Education said 614 substitute teacher positions remained vacant as of last week.
    教育部表示，截至上週，仍有 614 個代理教師職缺懸缺。
  • attract v. 吸引
    The Ministry of Education has encouraged local governments to offer additional incentives to attract recruits
    教育部已鼓勵地方政府提供額外誘因來吸引新血
  • incentive n. 誘因、鼓勵
    The Ministry of Education has encouraged local governments to offer additional incentives to attract recruits
    教育部已鼓勵地方政府提供額外誘因來吸引新血
  • prioritize v. 把...排優先順序、優先考慮
    As a result, a majority said they prioritize procedural legality over educational effectiveness when managing their classrooms.
    結果，多數教師表示，在管理教室時，他們將程序合法性置於教育效能之前。
  • workload n. 工作量
    nor would asking educators to take on greater workloads.
    要求教育工作者承擔更大的工作量亦然。
  • impact n. 衝擊、影響
    Otherwise, the impact would ultimately extend beyond education
    否則，這種衝擊最終將擴大至教育之外
  • ultimately 最終、根本
    Otherwise, the impact would ultimately extend beyond education, affecting the quality of Taiwan’s workforce and the strength of its economy.
    否則，這種衝擊最終將擴大至教育之外，影響台灣勞動力品質與經濟實力。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Postmodifier)
    The first week of the new semester came to a close yesterday, so there is hardly a more suitable time to discuss what is perhaps the most pressing issue facing elementary and secondary education in Taiwan
    句中的「facing elementary and secondary education in Taiwan」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「the most pressing issue」（最迫切的問題），相當於形容詞子句「that faces...」，在多益閱讀測驗中常考簡化子句的用法。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    About 90 percent of teachers have been required to take on extra teaching hours
    此句使用了現在完成式的被動語態「have been required to」，強調教師「被要求」承擔額外授課時數的主體感受，多益商業新聞常出現此結構來描述規定、政策或調查結果。
  • 假設語氣 (Subjunctive Mood)
    If teachers are overworked, overwhelmed by administrative responsibilities and afraid that reasonable professional decisions could result in complaints and lengthy investigations, recruiting more teachers would not solve anything — nor would asking educators to take on greater workloads.
    此句為與現在事實相反的假設語氣（Second Conditional），條件句用現在式表示假設狀況，主要子句則使用「would + 動詞原形」來表達如果發生該情況將會產生的結果。句尾亦包含「nor would...」的倒裝結構，表示「……也一樣不會」。
