社論：教育體系亟需改革

EDITORIAL: The education system needs reform

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • pressing adj. 緊迫的，迫切的
    there is hardly a more suitable time to discuss what is perhaps the most pressing issue facing elementary and secondary education in Taiwan — the shortage of teachers.
  • take on phr. v. 承擔，接受（工作或責任）
    About 90 percent of teachers have been required to take on extra teaching hours, while about 40 percent regularly experience “extreme fatigue,” a National Substitute Teachers Union survey released on Tuesday said.
  • vacanny n. 空缺，職缺
    For years, schools have struggled to fill substitute teacher vacancies shortly before the start of the school year.
  • oversimplification n. 過度簡化
    it would be an oversimplification to attribute the shortage of teachers to demographics alone.
  • attribute to phr. v. 將……歸因於
    it would be an oversimplification to attribute the shortage of teachers to demographics alone.
  • crux n. 關鍵，核心問題
    The crux of the issue is that the increasingly difficult conditions faced by teachers are making the profession less desirable and driving many away.
  • shoulder v. 擔負，承擔
    Teachers are expected to shoulder an increasingly diverse range of responsibilities beyond teaching, from administrative work and paperwork to evaluations and, apparently, even directing traffic.
  • coalition n. 聯合體，同盟
    Last week, a coalition of education unions called for teachers to focus on safety guidance on school grounds...
  • compound v. 使惡化，使加重（常用被動）
    The pressure is compounded by a system in which teachers can feel that they are constantly at risk of complaints or disciplinary proceedings.
  • undermine v. 逐漸削弱，暗中破壞
    ...despite warnings from teacher unions that such changes risk undermining subject-specific education and creating inconsistent professional standards.

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    ...discuss what is perhaps the most pressing issue facing elementary and secondary education in Taiwan — the shortage of teachers.
    句中的「facing elementary and secondary education in Taiwan」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「the *pressing issue*」，相當於形容詞子句「that faces elementary and secondary education in Taiwan」，能讓句子更精簡。
  • 否定副詞置於句首的倒裝結構 (Inversion with Negative Adverb at the Beginning)
    If teachers are overworked, overwhelmed by administrative responsibilities and afraid that reasonable professional decisions could result in complaints and lengthy investigations, recruiting more teachers would not solve anything — nor would asking educators to take on greater workloads.
    句尾的「nor would asking educators to take on greater workloads」使用了否定倒裝句型。當「nor / neither」置於句首表示「……也不……」時，其後需接「助動詞/be動詞/情態動詞 + 主詞」的倒裝語序。
  • 虛主詞 It 的使用 (Dummy Subject 'It')
    It is no surprise, then, that nearly 60 percent of teachers surveyed said they intend to leave their positions within five years.
    句首的「It」為虛主詞（形式主詞），沒有實際意義，真正的的主詞是後面由「that」引導的名詞子句（that nearly 60 percent of teachers surveyed said...）。這種結構常用於表達評論或狀態，避免主詞過長而頭重腳輕。
