街頭舞者與機器人舞者搭檔

Street dancer teams up with robot dancers

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • partner n. 夥伴、搭檔
    Chinese street dancer Wu Yufei (吳宇飛) has spent most of his 13-year career performing solo, but this year, he found some new partners — a crew of robots with sleek moves.
    中國街頭舞者吳宇飛在他 13 年的職業生涯中大多是獨舞，但今年他找到了一些新夥伴——一組動作流暢的機器人。
  • hire v. 僱用、租用
    Businesses and event planners across the country have since rushed to hire robots to perform and attract visitors.
    此后，全國各地的企業和活動策劃人員紛紛搶聘機器人來表演並吸引遊客。
  • impact n. 衝擊、影響
    The impact of AI [artificial intelligence] on dance performances, or the impact of robots on dancing — I was quite against it all at the beginning,” he said.
    他說：「人工智慧對舞蹈表演的影響，或者機器人對跳舞的影響——我一開始其實是非常反對的。」
  • perspective n. 觀點、看法
    However, over time, working with the skilled engineers — who he describes as the “mothers” of the robots — changed his perspective.
    然而，隨著時間的推移，與這些被他形容為機器人「母親」的熟練工程師合作，改變了他的看法。
  • commercialization n. 商業化
    “It takes a lot of fine-tuning,” said Liu Zhiquan (劉志全), commercialization lead at robotics motion-capture firm BridgeDP.
    機器人動作捕捉公司 BridgeDP 的商業化負責人劉志全表示：「這需要大量的微調。」
  • compromise v. 妥協、折衷
    A 30-second dance routine could take days, sometimes weeks, to perfect, and performers need to be willing to compromise, he said.
    他說，一段 30 秒的舞蹈可能需要幾天甚至幾週的時間來完美呈現，而且表演者必須願意妥協。
  • capability n. 能力、性能
    While the general public in China mostly sees robot dancing as entertainment, industry insiders see it as a chance to show off robotic designs, capabilities and reliability.
    雖然中國的廣大民眾主要將機器人舞蹈視為娛樂，但業界業內人士將其視為展示機器人設計、功能和可靠性的機會。
  • forecast v. 預測、預報
    A recent Morgan Stanley report forecast that China’s humanoid robotics market could reach US$2 billion in value this year.
    摩根士丹利最近的一份報告預測，中國人形機器人市場今年價值可能達到 20 億美元。
  • viable adj. 可行的、切實可行的
    Many companies are struggling to find commercially viable uses for their humanoids beyond entertainment.
    許多公司都在努力為其人形機器人尋找娛樂之外的商業可行用途。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Accounting for more than 97 percent of humanoid robot shipments in the first half of this year globally, the Chinese industry also faces increasing scrutiny from investors and policymakers.
    句首的 "Accounting for..." 是由現在分詞領導的片語，用來補充說明主句主語（the Chinese industry）所伴隨發生的狀況，相當於原因或條件狀語子句 (Because it accounts for...)，是多益閱讀測驗中常見的進階句構。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Its market capitalization has since fallen by about half from its peak.
    本句雖然使用的是主動語態完成式 (has fallen)，但在前文與後文中經常穿插被動語態（如：is described as、are struggling、can be replicated），多益考試非常喜歡考主動與被動語態的語意與文法選填題。
  • 假設語氣 / 條件句 (Conditional / Unreal Condition)
    “If the dancers are not tech-savvy, there will be a lot of retakes,” he said.
    此句為第一類條件句 (First Conditional)，用「If + 現在式」來表達真實或可能發生的條件，主句則使用「will + 原形動詞」表示未來可能產生的結果，常用於商業溝通與日常工作情境的假設討論。
