文法重點

  • 過去分詞（片語）作後置定語
    The Taipei City Police Department's Zhongzheng First Precinct set up the guardrails along the sidewalk on the north side of Jinan Road and secured them using steel cables.
    句中的「set up by the police」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「guardrails」（護欄），相當於形容詞子句「which were set up by the police」。這種結構在新聞英語中非常常見，能讓句子更簡潔。
  • 分詞構句表結果
    Chung and Huang broke two of the cables, rendering them unusable, prosecutors said.
    句尾的「rendering them unusable」是由現在分詞「rendering」引導的分詞構句，放在主句之後，用來補充說明前面動作所造成的「結果」（使它們無法使用）。
  • 名詞子句作受詞
    Prosecutors contended that there was no evidence showing that anyone's life, physical safety, freedom or property faced imminent or continuing harm...
    動詞「contended」（主張、認為）後面接了一個由「that」引導的名詞子句（that there was no evidence...），作為該動詞的受詞，用以表達檢方具體的主張內容。
