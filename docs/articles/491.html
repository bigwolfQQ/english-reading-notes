股市熱潮中稅收激增

Tax revenue surges amid boom in stock market

  • revenue n. 稅收、收益、營收
    Tax revenue surged last month as a stock market boom drove a sharp increase in securities transaction taxes...
    由於股市熱潮帶動證券交易稅急遽增加，上個月稅收激增...
  • surge v. / n. 激增、急升
    Tax revenue surged last month as a stock market boom drove a sharp increase in securities transaction taxes...
    由於股市熱潮帶動證券交易稅急遽增加，上個月稅收激增...
  • boom n. 繁榮、熱潮
    Tax revenue surged last month as a stock market boom drove a sharp increase in securities transaction taxes...
    由於股市熱潮帶動證券交易稅急遽增加，上個月稅收激增...
  • consecutive adj. 連續的
    Securities transaction tax revenue jumped 94.5 percent to NT$54 billion, its 13th consecutive month of annual growth.
    證券交易稅收跳增 94.5%，達到新台幣 540 億元，創下連續第 13 個月的年度成長。
  • reflect v. 反映、顯示
    The increase reflects the growing contribution of Taiwan’s buoyant stock market to government coffers...
    這項增長反映了台灣蓬勃發展的股市對政府國庫日益增加的貢獻...
  • contribution n. 貢獻
    The increase reflects the growing contribution of Taiwan’s buoyant stock market to government coffers...
    這項增長反映了台灣蓬勃發展的股市對政府國庫日益增加的貢獻...
  • turnover n. 營業額、成交量、人員流動率
    Average daily stock-market turnover reached NT$1.09 trillion last month, nearly doubling from a year earlier...
    上個月台股日均成交量達到新台幣 1.09 兆元，較去年同期幾乎翻倍...
  • strengthen v. 鞏固、加強
    For the first eight months, average daily turnover climbed 1.7-fold from a year earlier, providing a major boost to securities transaction tax revenue and helping strengthen government finances.
    在前八個月中，日均成交量較去年同期攀升了 1.7 倍，為證券交易稅收提供了強勁的動力，並有助於鞏固政府財政。
  • decline n. / v. 下降、減少
    However, for the first eight months property-related income tax revenue was still down 1.1 percent from a year earlier, although the decline narrowed significantly.
    然而，在前八個月中，房地產相關所得稅收較去年同期仍下降了 1.1%，儘管降幅已明顯縮小。
  • source n. 來源
    The sharp increase underscores how the stock-market rally, fueled in part by Taiwan’s central role in the global AI supply chain, has become an increasingly important source of government revenue.
    這項急遽增長突顯出股市大漲（部分由台灣在全球 AI 供應鏈中的核心角色所推動）如何成為政府稅收日益重要的來源。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附帶結果或原因狀語
    Tax revenue surged last month as a stock market boom drove a sharp increase in securities transaction taxes, helping push government tax collections to a record pace in the first eight months of the year.
    句尾的「helping...」是由對等子句簡化而來的現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前面主句所產生的結果（幫助推升政府稅收）。在商業新聞中非常常見，用以表達某個行動所帶動的後續連鎖反應。
  • 過去分詞片語作後置定語（修飾名詞）
    The sharp increase underscores how the stock-market rally, fueled in part by Taiwan’s central role in the global AI supply chain, has become an increasingly important source of government revenue.
    句中的「fueled in part by...」是一個過去分詞片語，夾在主詞 stock-market rally 與動詞 has become 之間，用來修飾前面股市大漲的原因（部分由台灣在全球 AI 供應鏈中的角色所推動）。這種結構能讓長句更精簡流暢。
  • 間接問句（名詞子句作受詞）
    The sharp increase underscores how the stock-market rally, fueled in part by Taiwan’s central role in the global AI supply chain, has become an increasingly important source of government revenue.
    動詞 underscores 後面接了一個由「how + 主詞 + 動詞」構成的名詞子句（how the stock-market rally... has become...），在句中擔任及物動詞 underscores 的受詞，用來具體說明「突顯了（什麼事情）」。
