文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The New Taipei District Prosecutors Office said in a statement Tuesday that the candidate in the city's Taishan District, surnamed Chen (陳), had held an event offering free haircuts in honor of Taiwan's Aug. 8 Father's Day.
    句中的 "offering free haircuts..." 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞事件 "an event"，相當於形容詞子句 "that offered free haircuts..." 的簡化版，能讓句子更為緊湊精煉。
  • 對等連接詞引導的分詞補充說明 (Participial Phrase indicating accompanying action/result)
    Chen also allegedly treated some local residents to free bowls of douhua, with an estimated value of around NT$50, prosecutors said, adding that these actions were likely intended to influence recipients' voting behavior.
    句尾的 ", adding that..." 是分詞構句，主詞承接前面的 prosecutors。用來補充說明檢察官除了陳述案情之外，還另外做了什麼補充或評論。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    ...prosecutors determined that Chen had likely violated provisions against vote buying in the Public Officials Election and Recall Act, and agreed to release him on NT$100,000 bail and bar him from leaving the country...
    句中的 "be barred from..."（被禁止……）使用了被動語態，強調候選人遭受限制出境的法律處分，在新聞英語及法律相關報導中非常常見。
  • 介系詞加關係代名詞的複合關係子句 (Preposition + Relative Pronoun)
    Taiwan's local elections, in which voters will select mayors and county chiefs, city and county councilors, township mayors and councilors, and village chiefs, are scheduled for Nov. 28.
    句中的 "in which" 用來引導非限定關係子句，先行詞是 "Taiwan's local elections"。介系詞 in 放於關係代名詞 which 之前，表示「在這些地方選舉中」，使語氣更加正式流暢。
