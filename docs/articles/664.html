臺北市立動物園40週年搬遷紀念，歷史照片重現

Taipei Zoo's 40th relocation anniversary marked with historic photos

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • relocation n. 搬遷，遷移
    Taiwan's National Archives Administration on Monday marked the 40th anniversary of Taipei Zoo's move on Sept. 14, 1986, by inviting the public to revisit historic photos of the move on its website.
    臺灣國家發展委員會檔案管理局於週一（14日）紀念臺北市立動物園於1986年9月14日搬遷40週年，邀請大眾在其網站上重溫這場搬遷的歷史照片。
  • press release n. 新聞稿
    In a press release, the administration highlighted photos of the 1986 Taipei Zoo Relocation Parade...
    在新聞稿中，檔案局特別展示了1986年臺北市立動物園搬遷遊行的照片……
  • highlight v. 強調，突顯
    In a press release, the administration highlighted photos of the 1986 Taipei Zoo Relocation Parade...
    在新聞稿中，檔案局特別展示了1986年臺北市立動物園搬遷遊行的照片……
  • accommodate v. 容納，提供方便
    ...the roughly 14-kilometer parade route, which was planned to accommodate animals of different sizes and heights...
    ……大約14公里長的遊行路線；該路線的規劃旨在容納不同體型和高度的動物……
  • original adj. 原本的，最初的
    In addition to the photos, the administration highlighted archival records explaining the decision to relocate the zoo from its original site in Yuanshan.
    除了照片外，檔案局也展示了檔案紀錄，解釋了將動物園從其位於圓山的原始舊址遷走的決定。
  • occupy v. 佔據（空間、土地等）
    According to the records, the original zoo, founded in 1914 during Taiwan's Japanese colonial period as Maruyama Zoo, occupied only 3.49 hectares.
    根據檔案紀錄，這座最初於1914年臺灣日治時期成立、名為圓山動物園的原始動物園，僅佔地3.49公頃。
  • prompt v. 促使，激起
    Pollution from nearby traffic and underground waterways, combined with noise that disturbed the animals from nearby Taipei Songshan Airport, also prompted the Taipei city government to seek a new location for the zoo.
    來自附近交通和地下水道的污染，加上鄰近臺北松山機場的噪音干擾了動物，也促使臺北市政府為動物園尋找新的地點。
  • select v. 挑選，選擇
    The records show that the government spent more than a decade selecting a site and planning and building the new zoo.
    紀錄顯示，政府花費了十多年的時間來選址、規劃並建造新動物園。
  • survey v. 勘測，調查
    Experts surveyed several suburban locations around Taipei before settling on the current 165-hectare site...
    專家在臺北周邊的多個市郊地點進行了勘查，最後才決定選定目前占地165公頃的現址……
  • abandon v. 放棄
    The city government and zoo also decided to abandon traditional cage displays in favor of specially designed habitats...
    市政府與動物園也決定放棄傳統的籠舍展示，改為採用特別設計的棲息地……
  • facility n. 設施，場所
    To celebrate the new zoo and bid farewell to the 72-year-old facility, officials decided to stage a parade...
    為了慶祝新動物園啟用並向這座擁有72年歷史的設施告別，官方決定舉辦一場遊行……
  • commission v. 委託，委任
    Record label Rock Records was also commissioned to release "Happy Paradise" (快樂天堂) ahead of the move...
    滾石唱片公司也受託在搬遷前發行〈快樂天堂〉……

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    In a press release, the administration highlighted photos of the 1986 Taipei Zoo Relocation Parade, including images of student athletes from Taipei's Kainan High School leading vehicles carrying animals through the city as crowds looked on.
    句中的「leading vehicles...」為現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的「student athletes」；而「carrying animals」則是另一個現在分詞片語修飾「vehicles」，用以補充說明帶領與載運的動作，能使句子精煉而不需使用冗長的關代子句。
  • 關係代間子句的補充說明 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    ...the roughly 14-kilometer parade route, which was planned to accommodate animals of different sizes and heights, with the route avoiding as many overpasses as possible to accommodate the giraffes.
    句中的「which was planned to accommodate...」是一個非限定關係子句（前面帶有逗號），用來補充說明「the roughly 14-kilometer parade route」的細節。在多益閱讀測驗中，常考此類結構的句意理解與標點符號用法。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Absolute Participial Construction)
    Pollution from nearby traffic and underground waterways, combined with noise that disturbed the animals from nearby Taipei Songshan Airport, also prompted the Taipei city government to seek a new location for the zoo.
    句中的「combined with noise...」是由過去分詞開頭的獨立結構，用來連接說明前面多種污染因素的結合狀況。這種結構在商業及新聞英文中非常常見，能有效整合多項主詞或原因。
  • 附加目的或結果的介系詞片語 (Prepositional Phrase of Purpose/Result)
    The city government and zoo also decided to abandon traditional cage displays in favor of specially designed habitats intended to improve animal welfare...
    片語「in favor of...」意指「支持、有利於、改用……」，在此處用來連接捨棄傳統籠舍、改採特別設計棲息地的轉折決策。另外，「intended to improve...」則是過去分詞片語作後位修飾，用以說明棲息地的設計目的。
