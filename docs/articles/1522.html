流感病例下降，但死亡人數仍然偏高

Flu cases declining, but deaths still high

單字解析

  • severe adj. 嚴重的
    There were 144 severe flu cases and 46 flu-related deaths last week...
    上週有 144 例流感併發重症及 46 例流感相關死亡病例...
  • purchase v. 採購，購買
    ...extra flu vaccines have been purchased for a government-funded program to meet high demand this season...
    ...為了因應本季的高需求，政府公費計畫已額外採購了流感疫苗...
  • demand n. 需求
    ...extra flu vaccines have been purchased for a government-funded program to meet high demand this season...
    ...為了因應本季的高需求，政府公費計畫已額外採購了流感疫苗...
  • activity n. （疫情）活動，活躍度
    Flu and COVID-19 activity is slowing down, but both are still in an epidemic period...
    流感與新冠肺炎的疫情正在趨緩，但兩者仍處於流行期...
  • account for phr. v. 佔……比例
    ...with influenza A (H1N1) accounting for 91.8 percent of tested cases, she said.
    ...其中 A 型 H1N1 流感占了檢測病例的 91.8%。
  • decline v. 下降，減少
    ...above the epidemic threshold of 11 percent, but the percentage is declining weekly, she said.
    ...但該比例正在逐週下降。
  • risk n. 風險
    ...adding that the risk of developing severe illness should not be overlooked, especially among high-risk groups.
    ...並補充說明，不應忽視發展成重症的風險，特別是在高風險族群之中。
  • peak n. 頂峰，最高峰
    While the recent wave of flu infections has passed its peak, deaths are a lagging indicator...
    雖然近期這波流感 infections 已過高峰，但死亡人數屬於落後指標...
  • significant adj. 顯著的，重大的
    ...dropping a significant 35 percent last week to 5,261, Lee said.
    ...上週大幅下降 35%，降至 5,261 人次。
  • eligible adj. 符合條件的，有資格的
    ...people aged 12 or older and eligible for the first phase of government-funded vaccination...
    ...開放給 12 歲或以上且符合第一階段公費接種資格的民眾...
  • additional adj. 額外的，附加的
    ...while the additional purchases of vaccines are expected to be available from the second half of next month...
    ...而額外採購的疫苗預計從下半個月起陸續供應...
  • encourage v. 鼓勵
    ...the CDC encourages them to get vaccinated as soon as possible, Lin said.
    ...CDC 呼籲（鼓勵）他們盡快前往接種疫苗。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附加說明（分詞構句）
    ...with influenza A (H1N1) accounting for 91.8 percent of tested cases, she said.
    「with + 名詞 + 現在分詞 (accounting)」構成獨立分詞構句，用來補充說明前面「呼吸道病原體」的細節，表示附帶狀況，意指「其中 A 型 H1N1 流感占了……」。
  • 對等連接詞 While 引導的讓步子句
    While the recent wave of flu infections has passed its peak, deaths are a lagging indicator...
    「While」在此處作連接詞，意為「雖然」，用來引導讓步子句，表達「儘管最近這波流感感染已經過了高峰期，但死亡人數卻是一個落後指標」的轉折語氣。
  • 被動語態的應用
    There were 144 severe flu cases and 46 flu-related deaths last week, and extra flu vaccines have been purchased...
    句子後半段使用現在完成式被動語態「have been purchased」，強調「額外的流感疫苗已經被（CDC）採購」這個動作及其對現在造成的影響，受詞成為主句焦點。
  • 關係代名詞子句修飾先行詞
    ...most of whom were older people with chronic conditions who had been vaccinated, she said.
    句中有兩個子句層層修飾：「most of whom」是非限定關係代名詞，用來補充說明前面的 15 位死者；而「who had been vaccinated」則是限定關係代名詞子句，用來修飾前面的「older people」。
