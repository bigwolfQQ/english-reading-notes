台灣尋求吸引更多美國遊客

Taiwan seeks to attract more US tourists

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • surpass v. 超越、勝過
    Taiwan is stepping up efforts to attract US tourists after the number of US visitors surpassed 710,000 last year...
    在去年美國訪台旅客超越71萬人次之後，台灣正加緊努力吸引美國遊客...
  • delegation n. 代表團
    The agency last week led a delegation to New York, Chicago and Los Angeles to promote Taiwan as a travel destination...
    該署上週帶領一個代表團前往紐約、芝加哥和洛杉磯，宣傳台灣作為旅遊目的地...
  • comprise v. 包含、由...組成
    ...with members comprising Taiwanese hoteliers, travel agencies and international flight carriers.
    ...團員包含台灣的旅宿業者、旅行社與國際航空公司。
  • tap into phr. v. 開發、利用、切入
    ...as it seeks to tap into the US Midwest market.
    ...因為它正尋求開發美國中西部市場。
  • facilitate v. 促進、使便利
    This dense flight network has greatly facilitated North American travelers’ visit to Taiwan.
    這個密集的航班網路極大地便利了北美旅客造訪台灣。
  • incentive n. 獎勵、誘因
    To attract more North American travelers, the administration said it is offering incentives for repeat visitors...
    為了吸引更多北美旅客，觀光署表示它正在為重複造訪旅客提供獎勵措施...
  • highlight v. 強調、突顯
    ...by highlighting Taiwan as a safe, convenient and fun travel destination...
    ...藉由強調台灣是一個安全、便利且有趣的旅遊目的地...
  • aspect n. 方面、層面
    The tours we recommended would allow US visitors to explore various aspects of Taiwan...
    我們推薦的行程將允許美國遊客探索台灣的各個面向...
  • operator n. 業者、營運商
    ...adding that Taiwanese tour operators should develop tours allowing tourists exploring Taiwan at night or at dawn.
    ...並補充說台灣的旅遊業者應開發能讓遊客在夜間或清晨探索台灣的行程。

文法重點

  • with + 名詞 + 分詞（獨立分詞構句）
    ...after the number of US visitors surpassed 710,000 last year, with a majority of them visiting for tourism, the Tourism Administration said.
    「with + O. + 分詞」結構用來表示附帶狀況或背景。在此句中，「a majority of them」與「visiting」形成主動關係，用現在分詞表達「其中大多數人是為了觀光而來」的同時狀態。
  • 現在分詞作後位修飾
    ...and watch Taiwanese opera performed by Taiwanese opera singer Tsai Yu-lun...
    過去分詞片語「performed by...」放在名詞「Taiwanese opera」後面，用來修飾該名詞，相當於形容詞子句「which is performed by...」，使句子更為精簡。
  • 不定詞片語作目的狀語
    To attract more North American travelers, the administration said it is offering incentives...
    句首的「To attract more North American travelers」是不定詞片語，置於句首用來表達主要子句動作的目的，意為「為了吸引更多北美旅客」。
