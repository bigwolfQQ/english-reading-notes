氣象署預報：台灣北部與東半部氣溫溫和、有陣雨

Mild temperatures, showers forecast for northern, eastern Taiwan

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • affect v. 影響
    A northeasterly wind system will continue to affect northern and eastern Taiwan over the next few days
    東北季風系統將在未來幾天持續影響台灣北部與東半部
  • weakens v. 減弱
    although the wind system will weaken slightly Thursday, it will continue to affect Taiwan's weather through Saturday.
    雖然該風系在週四將稍微減弱，但它將持續影響台灣的天氣直到週六。
  • period n. 期間、時期
    During that three-day period, brief, scattered showers are forecast for Greater Taipei and eastern Taiwan
    在這為期三天的期間，大台北地區與東半部預計有短暫、零星的陣雨
  • conditions n. 條件、狀況
    Slightly wetter conditions are expected Sunday
    週日預期將出現稍微潮濕的天氣狀況
  • expect v. 預期、預料
    Drier weather is expected as the new workweek begins
    隨著新的工作週開始，預期天氣將轉為乾燥
  • remain v. 保持、維持
    Temperatures are forecast to remain relatively stable through this weekend
    預報顯示這週末氣溫將保持相對穩定
  • stable adj. 穩定的
    Temperatures are forecast to remain relatively stable through this weekend
    預報顯示這週末氣溫將保持相對穩定
  • range v. （在一定範圍內）變動、介於
    Overnight lows are expected to range between 23 and 25 degrees nationwide.
    全台各地夜間低溫預期介於23至25度之間。
  • strengthen v. 增強、強化
    a tropical depression about 3,000 kilometers east of Taiwan could strengthen into a tropical storm overnight
    位於台灣東方約3,000公里處的一個熱帶性低氣壓可能在夜間增強為熱帶風暴

文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    A northeasterly wind system will continue to affect... but is forecast to move northwest toward Japan...
    多益測驗常客。當主詞是動作的承受者時，使用「be動詞 + 過去分詞 (V-pp)」來表達被動。例如「is forecast」意為「被預報／預料」，強調預報這個動作是由氣象署發佈的。
  • 分詞構句簡化時間副詞子句 (Participial Phrase)
    Drier weather is expected as the new workweek begins...
    原句使用連接詞 as 引導時間副詞子句（當...開始時）。在新聞英文中，也常看到將連接詞保留，搭配現在分詞（Ving）或過去分詞（Vpp）來簡化句子，使報導更為精煉。
  • 對等連接詞 while 表對比
    ...brief, scattered showers are forecast for Greater Taipei and eastern Taiwan, while isolated showers are possible in mountainous areas and southern Taiwan...
    while 在此處作為對等連接詞，用來連接兩個對比的子句，意指「而...」、「然而...」，在多益閱讀測驗的句子填空或克漏字中常考用來表示對比或同時發生的情況。
