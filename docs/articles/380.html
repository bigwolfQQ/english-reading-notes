賴清德強調擴大支援台灣選手參與亞運

Lai highlights expanded support for Taiwan athletes at Asian Games

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • highlight v. 強調，突顯
    President Lai Ching-te on Tuesday highlighted expanded medical and rehabilitation services...
    總統賴清德於週二強調了擴大的醫療與復健服務……
  • personnel n. 人員，員工
    ...the delegation headquarters will be staffed by 53 support personnel this year...
    ……今年代表團總部將配備53名後勤支援人員……
  • facility n. 設施，場所
    Staffing at the delegation's support stations -- facilities separate from the headquarters... -- has also increased...
    代表團支援站（與總部獨立的設施）的編制人員也增加了……
  • set v. 設定，制定（目標等）
    Lai also told athletes that he would not set a medal target for the quadrennial Games...
    賴清德也向選手們表示，他不會為這場每四年舉辦一次的賽事設定奪牌目標……
  • target n. 目標，指標
    "I won't set a target for how many gold, silver or bronze medals we should win," he said.
    他說：「我不會設定我們應該贏得多少面金牌、銀牌或銅牌的目標。」
  • urge v. 敦促，力勸
    Instead, Lai urged them to focus on their own performances after earning the opportunity to compete...
    相反地，賴清德敦促他們在爭取到參賽機會後，專注於自己的表現。
  • overall adj. 總體的，全面的
    ...if athletes improve on their previous performances, "the country's overall results will naturally be good."
    ……如果選手們能突破自己以往的表現，「國家的整體成績自然就會好。」
  • achieve v. 取得，實現，達到
    Taiwan's best gold-medal haul at a single Asian Games is 19, achieved at both the 1998 Bangkok Asian Games and the 2022 Hangzhou Asian Games.
    台灣在單屆亞運的最佳金牌紀錄是19面，分別在1998年曼谷亞運及2022年杭州亞運創下。
  • standings n. 排名，名次
    Taiwan finished sixth in the overall medal standings at both editions.
    台灣在這兩屆賽事的總獎牌榜上均名列第六。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作後置修飾 / 補充說明
    Set to open on Sept. 19, the 20th Asian Games will see a 740-member Taiwan delegation supported by the MOS...
    句首的「Set to open on Sept. 19」是由過去分詞片語引導的分詞構句，原句意指「(As it is) Set to open...」，用來修飾並說明隨後的「the 20th Asian Games」（第20屆亞運）。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    ...the 20th Asian Games will see a 740-member Taiwan delegation supported by the MOS, which was established in September 2025 and is headed by inaugural minister Lee Yang...
    句中的「supported by the MOS」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前方的名詞「a 740-member Taiwan delegation」，相當於省略了「which is supported by...」，使句子更為精簡。
  • 條件句（Conditional Clause）
    ...adding that if athletes improve on their previous performances, "the country's overall results will naturally be good."
    此處使用「if + 現在式動詞」來表達真實的條件假設（「如果選手們能突破以往表現」），其主句則使用「will」來表達未來必然發生的結果，符合標準的第一類條件句句構。
