單字解析

  • memoir n. 回憶錄，自傳
    Charles Spencer’s Swan Song, a memoir of his relationship with his sister...
    查爾斯·史賓賽的《天鵝之歌》是一本記錄了他與姊姊關係的回憶錄……
  • serialization n. 連載
    ...the newspaper to whom Spencer sold serialization of a book that includes a line...
    ……史賓賽將一本書的連載權賣給了該報紙，書中有一行字……
  • contribute v. 貢獻，投搞，參與
    ...he kept being asked to contribute to other people’s projects.
    ……他不斷被邀請參與其他人的專案。
  • project n. 專案，計畫
    ...he kept being asked to contribute to other people’s projects.
    ……他不斷被邀請參與其他人的專案。
  • guidance n. 指導，引導（動詞形式為 guide）
    Rather than allow the narrative to be guided by “some TV producer”...
    與其讓敘事被「某個電視製作人」主導……
  • estate n. 地產，莊園，財產
    ...Althorp, the family’s Northamptonshire estate, and one wonders if the present earl is having similar difficulties.
    ……家族位於北安普敦郡的莊園奧爾索普，讓人不禁懷疑當前的伯爵是否正面臨類似的困境。
  • involve v. 涉及，包含
    ...is still harping on about a great many things involving a large cast of characters...
    ……仍在喋喋不休地抱怨許多事情，涉及一大群角色……
  • property n. 房地產，財產
    ...that she couldn’t move into a property on the Althorp estate...
    ……她不能搬進奧爾索普莊園的一處房產……
  • decision n. 決定，決策
    ...the decision he made to substitute the word “power” for the word “strength”...
    ……他決定用「力量」一詞代替「強度」一詞……
  • substitute v. 代替，替換
    ...the decision he made to substitute the word “power” for the word “strength”...
    ……他決定用「力量」一詞代替「強度」一詞……

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Spencer’s Swan Song, a memoir of his relationship with his sister, the late Diana, Princess of Wales, is a fascinating document that tells us a little about Diana and a great deal about Charles Spencer...
    句中的 "a fascinating document" 後方接了關係代間詞子句 "that tells us..."，用來修飾前面的文件。而在主詞部分，"a memoir..." 作為同位語補充說明書籍名稱。這種結構在商業與新聞英文中常用來在一句話中塞入豐富的背景資訊。
  • 假設語氣與過去未實現的推測 (Conditional / Unreal Past)
    If that sounds snarky, after 400 pages of score settling... the reader is left with a head-spinning story...
    使用 "If"引導的條件句 ("If that sounds snarky") 來假設讀者的可能反應，前半句用現在式表達假設情況，後半句則直接描述產生的結果，常用於評論與專欄寫作中與讀者進行互動的語氣。
  • 被動語態的應用 (Passive Voice)
    Rather than allow the narrative to be guided by “some TV producer” from a network “I will never watch”...
    句中的 "to be guided by..." 採用了被動語態，強調敘事動作的承受者（敘事內容 narrative）受到某個製作人的擺布，突顯作者不屑與自主的主張。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Nominative Absolute Construction)
    While telling the story of his and Diana’s relationship, there are drive-bys on Peter Carter-Ruck...
    分詞構句 "While telling..." 省略了主詞（實際上主詞是指作者或書本敘述），用來表達在進行某個動作的同時伴隨著另一個現象發生，能讓句子更緊湊、流暢。
