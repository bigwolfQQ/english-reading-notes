N Korea adds second naval destroyer

單字解析

  • put into service phr. 投入使用，正式服役
    North Korean leader Kim Jong-un put his second naval destroyer into service and called for building a more reliable nuclear deterrent
    北韓領導人金正恩將其第二艘海軍驅逐艦投入服役，並呼籲建立更可靠的核威懾力量
  • deterrent n. 威懾力量，遏制物
    North Korean leader Kim Jong-un put his second naval destroyer into service and called for building a more reliable nuclear deterrent
    北韓領導人金正恩將其第二艘海軍驅逐艦投入服役，並呼籲建立更可靠的核威懾力量
  • trilateral adj. 三邊的，三方參與的
    as the US, South Korea and Japan began a trilateral military exercise he views as a security threat.
    此前美國、南韓和日本開始了一場他視為安全威脅的三邊聯合軍事演習。
  • commissioning n. （軍艦等）正式服役，委任
    The ship’s commissioning comes as North Korea is dialing up pressure on Washington
    這艘軍艦的服役之際，正值北韓正不斷加大對華盛頓的施壓
  • concession n. 讓步，妥協
    Observers say North Korea wants to wrest bigger US concessions.
    觀察人士表示，北韓希望藉此爭取美國更大的讓步。
  • assist v. 協助，援助
    Considered North Korea’s biggest and most advanced warships, the two destroyers were likely built with Russian assistance, experts say.
    專家表示，這兩艘軍艦被認為是北韓最大且最先進的軍艦，很可能是俄羅斯協助建造的。
  • ceremony n. 儀式，典禮
    In a speech at the ceremony, Kim said the back-to-back warship additions instilled fear in the enemies
    金正恩在典禮上的演說中表示，接連新增軍艦讓敵人感到恐懼
  • defense n. 防衛，防守
    and further strengthen our military activities for maritime defense and war deterrence.
    並進一步加強海上防衛和戰爭威懾的軍事活動。
  • enhance v. 提高，增強，提升
    South Korea’s military said the exercise is defensive in nature and meant to enhance a joint response capability
    南韓軍方表示，該演習本質上是防禦性的，旨在提升聯合應對能力
  • preserve v. 維護，保護，保存
    ready to preserve the peace and security we have built and defended for more than eight decades.
    隨時準備維護我們已經建立並捍衛了八十多年的和平與安全。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The destroyer Kang Kon was commissioned into North Korea’s navy in the eastern port city of Wonsan on Sunday at a ceremony attended by Kim and his teenage daughter...
    句中的「attended by Kim and his teenage daughter」是由過去分詞開頭的片語，用來修飾前方名詞 ceremony（典禮），相當於關代子句「which was attended by Kim...」，簡化後能讓商業或新聞英文顯得更精簡流暢。
  • 受詞補語/分詞作受詞補充說明 (Participle as Object Complement)
    North Korean state media photos showed the girl in a white dress and blazer walking alongside her father on a red carpet...
    句型「show + O. + V-ing」用來表達「顯示某人正在做某事」。在此指照片拍到女孩「正在」走路（walking alongside her father），用現在分詞強調動作的進行中。
  • 現在分詞引導附帶狀況/結果 (Present Participle for Circumstance)
    ...state media reported yesterday, as the US, South Korea and Japan began a trilateral military exercise he views as a security threat.
    句尾的「he views as a security threat」是省略了關代（which/that）的形容詞子句，用來修飾前方的 trilateral military exercise，多益閱讀測驗常考這種名詞後方直接接代詞與動詞的限制性修飾結構。
