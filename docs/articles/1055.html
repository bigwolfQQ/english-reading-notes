Focus Taiwan (CNA) · 2026-09-24

預報指出未來幾天台灣天氣溫暖且多數時間乾燥

Warmer, mostly dry weather forecast for Taiwan over next few days

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • forecast n. / v. 預報、預測
    Warmer, mostly dry weather forecast for Taiwan over next few days
    預報指出未來幾天台灣天氣溫暖且多數時間乾燥
  • expect v. 預期、期待
    Taiwan is expected to see mostly sunny skies on Thursday and into the weekend
    台灣預計在週四及週末期間將迎來大多晴朗的天空
  • region n. 地區、區域
    However, scattered showers are possible Thursday in some regions, including the north coast, the Greater Taipei area, and southern and eastern Taiwan, the CWA said.
    不過，氣象署表示，週四部分地區可能有零星陣雨，包括北海岸、大台北地區，以及南部和東部地區。
  • remain v. 保持、維持
    the weather in Taiwan will remain mostly sunny through Sunday.
    台灣的天氣將持續晴朗至週日。
  • experience v. 經歷、體驗
    mountainous areas may experience afternoon showers, he said.
    他說，山區則可能經歷午後陣雨。
  • slightly adv. 稍微、略微
    temperatures will rise slightly over the next few days
    未來幾天氣溫將略有上升
  • affect v. 影響
    a tropical storm located southeast of Taiwan was not expected to affect the country.
    位於台灣東南方的熱帶性低氣壓預計不會對本國造成影響。
  • strengthen v. 加強、增強
    Tropical Storm Surigae is expected to strengthen and could develop into a typhoon as early as Friday
    熱帶風暴舒力基預期將會增強，最快可能在週五發展成颱風
  • unlikely adj. 不太可能的
    it is unlikely to come close to Taiwan
    它不太可能接近台灣

文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Taiwan is expected to see mostly sunny skies on Thursday and into the weekend...
    句型為「主詞 + be動詞 + 過去分詞 (expected) + to V」。用來強調動作的承受者，在新聞英語中經常用於報導預測、官方說法或事實陳述，以保持客觀語氣。
  • 現在分詞片語作修飾語 (Present Participle Phrase)
    ...forecasting daytime highs of 31-34 degrees in western Taiwan and 28-32 degrees in the eastern region.
    句尾的「forecasting...」是由現在分詞引導的片語，用來補充說明主句主語（Cheng）同時發出的動作或帶有的結果，等同於副詞子句「and he forecasted...」。
  • 形容詞片語置於名詞後方（後位修飾）
    As of 8 a.m. Thursday, Surigae was located about 1,500 kilometers east-southeast of Taiwan, according to CWA data.
    「located about 1,500 kilometers east-southeast of Taiwan」是一個過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的主詞 Surigae（舒力基），省略了「which was」，使句子更簡鍊。
