單字解析

  • agency n. 局，署，機構
    the Workforce Development Agency (WDA) said on Sunday.
    勞動力發展署（WDA）於週日表示。
  • category n. 類別，種類
    Lu Zhi-kuan (呂治寬) took gold in the Software Applications Development category
    呂治寬在「軟體應用開發」類別奪金
  • invest v. 投資
    highlighting a government program to invest NT$11.755 billion (US$369.9 million) by 2029 to improve educators’ digital and artificial intelligence (AI) skills.
    強調一項在 2029 年前投資新台幣 117.55 億元（約 3.699 億美元）的政府計畫，以提升教育工作者的數位與人工智慧（AI）技能。
  • role n. 角色，職責
    In a Facebook post, Lai said teachers play an irreplaceable role in guiding students through learning and personal growth
    賴總統在臉書貼文中表示，教師在引導學生學習與個人成長方面扮演著無可取代的角色
  • administrative adj. 行政的
    while acknowledging the pressures they face from teacher shortages, parent-teacher communication, student counseling and administrative workloads.
    同時他也坦承教師們面臨來自師資短缺、親師溝通、學生輔導以及行政工作負擔的壓力。
  • aim v. 旨在，目的是
    A ministry report released in May said the “AI Talent Ark Program” aims to enrich digital teaching materials
    教育部五月份發布的一份報告指出，「AI人才扎根計畫」旨在豐富數位教材
  • efficient adj. 效率高的，有效率的
    While technology can make teaching more efficient, teachers’ understanding of and guidance to students are irreplaceable, he said.
    他表示，雖然科技可以讓教學更有效率，但教師對學生的理解與引導是無可取代的。
  • operator n. 業者，經營者
    The park operator expressed its condolences yesterday over the man’s death
    樂園業者昨天對該男子的死亡表達哀悼
  • investigation n. 調查
    and said the cause of the incident was still under investigation.
    並表示事件原因仍在調查中。
  • decline v. 婉拒，拒絕
    It declined to provide further details, citing the privacy of the visitor and his family.
    基於遊客及其家人的隱私，業者拒絕提供進一步細節。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle phrase as postmodifier)
    Taiwan also won 30 Medallions for Excellence, awarded to competitors who rack up a high number of points in an event...
    過去分詞片語「awarded to...」在此修飾前面的名詞「Medallions for Excellence」，原句省略了「which are」，用來補充說明獎章是頒發給哪些人的。
  • 對等連接詞連接的多重動詞 (Parallel verbs with while)
    ...while acknowledging the pressures they face from teacher shortages, parent-teacher communication, student counseling and administrative workloads.
    「while」引導的現在分詞短語，用來表達同時發生的動作或情況，其中的「acknowledging」承接前文主詞的動作，呈現出總統在發文時同步表達對教師壓力的理解。
  • 現在分詞作結果狀語 (Present participle indicating result)
    A tourist from Myanmar was pronounced dead at a hospital on Sunday after being found unconscious in a shallow-water wave pool at a popular water park in Taichung, but no cause of death had yet been determined.
    句中的「after being found unconscious...」使用了介系詞加上動名詞被動態，說明遊客是在被發現昏迷之後才宣告死亡，表達時間與事件先後的邏輯關係。
  • 分詞片語表示原因 (Participle phrase denoting reason)
    It declined to provide further details, citing the privacy of the visitor and his family.
    句尾的「citing the privacy...」是現在分詞片語，前面省略了「as it cited」，用來補充說明業者拒絕提供細節的原因（即基於隱私考量）。
