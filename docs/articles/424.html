文法重點

  • 非限定關係代間詞子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    More than 17,000 athletes and officials are expected to take part in 43 sports at the Nagoya-Aichi Games — which do not officially start until the opening ceremony on Saturday next week and runs through Oct. 4.
    使用破折號連接一個「which」引導的非限定關係子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「Nagoya-Aichi Games」。這類子句前面通常有逗號或破折號，且去掉後不影響主要子句的完整性。
  • 分詞作形容詞用 (Participle as Adjective)
    Organizers hope the athletes would have a “unique experience” staying in eye-catching accommodation ranging from a cruise liner and wooden containers to standard hotel rooms.
    句中的 "eye-catching" 是由「名詞 + 現在分詞」構成的複合形容詞，用來修飾後方的名詞 "accommodation"（住宿），意思是「引人注目的、吸睛的」。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Nominative Absolute Construction / With + O. + 分詞)
    Weather has already caused disruptions, with record rainfall on Tuesday forcing hundreds of athletes to evacuate their accommodation briefly.
    使用「with + 受詞 (record rainfall) + 現在分詞 (forcing...)」的結構，表示伴隨發生的狀況或原因。在此用來補充說明氣候造成干擾的具體細節（破紀錄降雨迫使運動員撤離）。
