文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Thai police yesterday said that they arrested a senior Buddhist monk on suspicion of embezzling US$2.8 million intended for his monastery...
    句中的「intended for his monastery」為過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「US$2.8 million」（280萬美元），相當於關代子句「which was intended for...」，省略了「which was」以簡化句子結構。
  • 現在分詞片語作後置修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    A 47-year-old woman accused of helping convert and conceal the money was also arrested.
    句中的「accused of helping convert and conceal the money」是過去分詞片語（被動概念），修飾前面的主詞「A 47-year-old woman」。
  • 間接問句 / 名詞子句 (Indirect Question / Noun Clause)
    Police are investigating which were personal property and which belonged to the temple.
    句中的「which were personal property and which belonged to the temple」是由疑問詞引導的名詞子句，在句中擔任動詞「are investigating」的受詞，表達「調查……的事」。
