單字解析

  • advocate v. 主張、擁護、倡導
    Eswatini will continue advocating for Taiwan's meaningful participation in international organizations, the African country's new ambassador to Taiwan said Monday.
    這名非洲國家駐台新大使週一表示，史瓦帝尼將繼續倡導台灣有意義地參與國際組織。
  • diplomatic adj. 外交的
    Eswatini is one of the Republic of China's (Taiwan's official name) 12 diplomatic allies and its only ally in Africa.
    史瓦帝尼是中華民國（台灣官方國名）12個外交盟友之一，也是其在非洲唯一的盟友。
  • supply chain n. 供應鏈
    In his remarks, Vulindlela Simphiwe Kunene said global challenges including pandemics, cybercrime and disruptions to international supply chains could not be effectively addressed...
    庫內內在致詞時表示，包括全球疫情、網路犯罪以及國際供應鏈中斷在內的全球挑戰無法獲得有效解決……
  • contribution n. 貢獻
    "Eswatini will therefore continue to advocate for Taiwan's meaningful participation in international organizations and mechanisms where its contribution can benefit humanity," he said.
    他說：「因此，史瓦帝尼將繼續倡導台灣有意義地參與國際組織與機制，讓台灣的貢獻能造福全人類。」
  • bilateral adj. 雙邊的
    In terms of bilateral ties, Kunene highlighted Taiwan-backed rural electrification as an example of how the partnership had improved people's lives...
    在雙邊關係方面，庫內內以台灣支持的農村電氣化為例，說明夥伴關係如何改善人民的生活……
  • investment n. 投資
    He also pledged to promote closer cooperation in areas including trade and investment, renewable energy, agriculture, healthcare, digital transformation and artificial intelligence.
    他也承諾推動更緊密的合作，領域涵蓋貿易與投資、再生能源、農業、醫療保健、數位轉型及人工智慧。
  • pledge v. 保證、承諾

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    Kunene, who was previously posted to the United Nations, said such support was not only "an expression of friendship," but also "a principled position grounded in fairness, inclusion and the practical requirements of effective international cooperation."
    句中的「grounded in fairness...」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞短語 "a principled position"（一項原則立場），省略了「which is/was」，使句子更為精煉。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Taiwan-Eswatini ties drew international attention earlier this year when Lai's planned visit to the African country in late April was postponed after Seychelles, Mauritius and Madagascar canceled overflight permits for his chartered aircraft...
    句中的 "was postponed"（被延期）使用了被動語態，強調動作的承受者（訪問行程）而非施事者，是新聞英語中非常常見的句構。
  • 關係子句 (Relative Clause)
    Eswatini is one of the Republic of China's (Taiwan's official name) 12 diplomatic allies and its only ally in Africa.
    文章中經常使用關係代名詞（如 who, which, where）引導子句來補充說明先行詞。例如 "where its contribution can benefit humanity" 中的 where 引導地方關係子句，修飾前面的 international organizations and mechanisms。
