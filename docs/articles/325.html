文法重點

  • 關代所有格 (whose)
    ...founder of the People’s Republic, who brought roughly one-quarter of the world’s population under communist rule and whose specter still haunts China’s future.
    句中的 "whose" 是關係代間詞的所有格形式，用來連接先行詞 "Mao Zedong" 與其擁有的事物 ("specter")，在子句中作所有格修飾用。
  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況
    Xi is on course to become the longest-serving paramount leader of the People’s Republic of China, after Mao, having broken with reform-era term limit norms meant to curb excessively concentrated power...
    句尾的 "having broken with..." 是分詞構句（完成式分詞），表示這個動作發生在主句動作之前，補充說明主詞在成為領導人過程中所做的行為。
  • 被動語態與介詞片語後置修飾
    Mao’s death on Sept. 9, 1976, at age 82, mourned by many and welcomed by some who suffered under his nearly three-decade rule...
    句中的 "mourned by many and welcomed by some..." 是過去分詞片語當作形容詞用，後置修飾主詞 "Mao’s death"，省略了 "which was"，展現精鍊的新聞英文寫作風格。
  • 形容詞子句 (限定與非限定用法)
    ...the symbolic center of Chinese political power. His preserved body lies in the Chairman Mao Memorial Hall, where visitors from across the country queue to pay respects.
    句尾的 "where visitors from..." 是地方副詞性質的非限定關代子句，前方有逗號（或在此處接於地點後），用來補充說明 "Chairman Mao Memorial Hall" 這個地點發生的事。
