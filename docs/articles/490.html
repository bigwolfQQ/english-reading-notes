文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    Major cloud service providers are offering incentives to capture this growing demand by encouraging customers to use AI models available on their platforms, Tsai said.
    句中的「encouraging customers to use AI models available on their platforms」是一個由現在分詞（encouraging）引導的分詞片語，用來補充說明前面的動態或方式，等同於副詞子句「by (they) encouraging...」。
  • 受詞補語結構 (Object Complement with Present Participle)
    As a result, eCloudvalley is gaining new customers, while existing customers are also increasing their cloud consumption as they expand AI applications, he added.
    句中的「as they expand AI applications」是引導時間或原因的副詞子句。而在文章其他段落也常見「making the usage limits common in individual subscription services unsuitable」，其中「making + 受詞 + 形容詞」構成受詞補語結構，表達「使得……變得……」。
  • 關係代間詞子句 (Relative Clause with 'that')
    “Nearly all customers that conducted AI agent POC trials with the company over the past two to three years have moved into production environments,” she said.
    句中的「that conducted AI agent POC trials with the company over the past two to three years」是限定性關係子句，用來修飾先行詞「Nearly all customers」，限定說明是哪群客戶。
