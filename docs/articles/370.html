Semiconductor firms boost hiring, broaden talent search: 104 Job Bank

單字解析

  • industry n. 工業，產業
    The semiconductor industry posted an average of 47,000 job openings per month in 2026...
    根據線上求職平臺104人力銀行指出，半導體產業在2026年平均每個月發布47,000個工作職缺...
  • job opening n. phr. 職缺，工作空缺
    The semiconductor industry posted an average of 47,000 job openings per month in 2026, an increase of more than 20 percent from a year earlier...
    半導體產業在2026年平均每個月發布47,000個工作職缺，比前一年同期增加了20%以上...
  • applicant n. 申請人，應徵者
    The share of listings open to applicants from all majors also rose by 2-3 percent during the period...
    該平臺在週二發布的一份人才報告中表示，這段期間對所有科系應徵者開放的職缺比例也上升了2-3%。
  • demand n. 需求
    It said hiring demand in the industry is shifting from manufacturing operations toward factory construction support...
    報告指出，隨著人工智慧（AI）持續推動對更大產能的需求，該產業的招募需求正從製造營運轉向廠房建設支援...
  • capacity n. 產能，容量
    It said hiring demand in the industry is shifting from manufacturing operations toward factory construction support, machinery assembly and yield improvement as artificial intelligence (AI) continues to drive demand for greater production capacity.
    報告指出，隨著人工智慧（AI）持續推動對更大產能的需求，該產業的招募需求正從製造營運轉向廠房建設支援、機械組裝與良率提升。
  • efficiency n. 效率
    The report said workers capable of transferring technology between plants, sharing expertise and integrating systems will be key to improving the efficiency of capacity expansion.
    報告指出，具備跨廠區技術移轉、專業知識分享以及系統整合能力的勞工，將是提升產能擴充效率的關鍵。
  • interdisciplinary adj. 跨領域的
    It also said companies are increasingly seeking interdisciplinary talent with expertise in electronics, optics and materials science...
    報告也指出，企業越來越積極尋找具備電子、光學及材料科學專業的跨領域人才...
  • collaboration n. 合作，協作
    ...making cross-departmental technological integration, communication and collaboration skills increasingly important.
    ...使得跨部門的技術整合、溝通與協作能力變得益發重要。
  • account for v. phr. 佔（比例），說明
    Meanwhile, AI-related job openings in the industry averaged more than 10,000 per month, accounting for one in four openings...
    同時，104表示，該產業中與AI相關的職缺平均每月超過10,000個，佔所有職缺的四分之一...
  • supply chain n. phr. 供應鏈
    ...while the skills sought have expanded from research and development to areas including production, equipment, quality control and supply chain management.
    ...同時尋求的技能範圍已從研發擴大至生產、設備、品質管制以及供應鏈管理等領域。
  • expand v. 擴大，擴展
    As geopolitical risks drive the industry to expand overseas, Asian markets excluding China, Hong Kong and Macau remain the main destinations for overseas job postings.
    隨著地緣政治風險驅動產業向海外擴張，扣除中國大陸、香港和澳門後的亞洲市場依然是海外職缺的主要目的地。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Present Participle）作後置修飾 / 形容詞用
    The share of listings open to applicants from all majors also rose by 2-3 percent during the period, the platform said in a talent report released Tuesday.
    句尾的 "released Tuesday" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "a talent report"（人才報告），相當於關代子句 "which was released Tuesday" 的簡化版，省略了 "which was"。
  • 現在分詞表結果（分詞構句）
    It also said companies are increasingly seeking interdisciplinary talent with expertise in electronics, optics and materials science, making cross-departmental technological integration, communication and collaboration skills increasingly important.
    句尾的 ", making..." 是現在分詞構句，用逗號隔開接在整句後面，表示前面主句動作所自然產生的結果（「使得……變得愈發重要」），主詞概念為前面整件事情。
  • 對比連接詞 while 的用法
    Weber Chung (鍾文雄), a manager at 104 Job Bank, said AI-related openings have increased rapidly since 2025, while the skills sought have expanded from research and development to areas including production, equipment, quality control and supply chain management.
    在此句中，"while" 作為對比連接詞，意思是「然而」、「而」，用來連接兩個並列子句，對比說明兩個不同的現象同時在發生或呈現對比狀態。
  • 現在完成進行式（Present Perfect Continuous）
    ...indicating that while Taiwan's semiconductor supply chain remains deeply rooted in Asia, companies are expanding their operations into key North American markets, according to the report.
    （註：此題對應文章第9句的結構）句中運用了連接詞 "while" 引導讓步子句（「雖然……」），搭配主詞動詞，呈現出轉折與對比的語意，是多益閱讀測驗常考的句構邏輯。
