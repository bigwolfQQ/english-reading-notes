沈舉辦首次台北競選車隊遊行；蔣敦促協調辯論

Shen holds 1st Taipei campaign motorcade; Chiang urges debate coordination

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • campaign n. 競選活動
    Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) Taipei mayoral candidate Puma Shen (沈伯洋) held his first campaign motorcade in the capital on Sunday
    民主進步黨台北市長候選人沈伯洋週日在首都舉辦了他的首次競選車隊遊行
  • incumbent adj. 現任的
    while incumbent Kuomintang (KMT) Mayor Chiang Wan-an (蔣萬安) urged Shen's camp to discuss arrangements for a televised election debate or debates.
    而現任國民黨市長蔣萬安則敦促沈的陣營討論電視選舉辯論的安排。
  • urge v. 敦促、力勸
    Chiang said his campaign team would attend and urged Shen's representatives to do the same.
    蔣表示他的競選團隊將會出席，並敦促沈的代表也照做。
  • support n. 支持
    In Taiwan, campaign motorcades are a common feature of local elections, allowing candidates to greet voters along neighborhood streets while serving as a public show of support for them.
    在台灣，競選車隊是地方選舉的常見特色，能讓候選人在鄰里街道沿途向選民打招呼，同時也是對他們支持的公開展現。
  • reiterate v. 重申
    Speaking to reporters ahead of the motorcade, Shen reiterated his call for four debates, saying a single event would not allow enough time for detailed discussions of individual policy issues.
    車隊出發前接受記者採訪時，沈重申了他舉辦四場辯論的呼吁，表示單場活動將沒有足夠的時間對個別政策議題進行詳細討論。
  • proposal n. 提議、提案
    Earlier Sunday, Chiang was asked about Shen's proposal to hold four debates during an event in Taipei.
    週日稍早，蔣萬安在台北的一場活動中被問及沈關於舉辦四場辯論的提議。
  • focus n. 焦點
    Chiang said the focus should be on actually holding debates, rather than turning them into a campaign talking point
    蔣表示，重點應該放在實際舉辦辯論上，而不是將其變成競選話題
  • agree in principle phr. 原則上同意
    He said both camps have agreed in principle to broadcaster TVBS holding a televised debate or debates and signed consent forms
    他說，雙方陣營原則上已同意由 TVBS 電視台舉辦電視辯論並已簽署同意書
  • representative n. 代表
    with the broadcaster scheduled to meet representatives of both sides on Monday to discuss the details.
    該電視台預定於週一會見雙方代表以討論細節。
  • cast a ballot phr. 投票
    The elections are also known as the "9-in-1" elections because voters in Taiwan's 22 cities and counties will cast ballots for candidates vying for nine different local government posts
    這些選舉也被稱為「九合一」選舉，因為台灣 22 個縣市的選民將為角逐九個不同地方政府職位的候選人投票
  • vie v. 競爭、角逐
    voters in Taiwan's 22 cities and counties will cast ballots for candidates vying for nine different local government posts
    台灣 22 個縣市的選民將為角逐九個不同地方政府職位的候選人投票
  • in total phr. 總計
    In total, elections will be held for 11,051 officials at all levels of local government.
    總計將舉辦涵蓋各級地方政府 11,051 名官員的選舉。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作附帶狀況或結果（分詞構句）
    allowing candidates to greet voters along neighborhood streets while serving as a public show of support for them.
    句尾的 "allowing..." 與 "while serving..." 是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前面的主句（競選車隊）所帶來的結果或功能，主詞一致且省略連接詞，讓句子更簡潔流暢。
  • 被動語態的應用
    Chiang was asked about Shen's proposal to hold four debates during an event in Taipei.
    此句使用了被動語態 (was asked)，當焦點在於接受訪問的人（蔣萬安），而發問者不重要或不需特別指明時，常使用被動語態表達。
  • 動名詞作介詞的受詞
    Chiang said the focus should be on actually holding debates, rather than turning them into a campaign talking point
    介詞 "on" 後面接動名詞 "holding" 與 "turning"，用來表達「在...這件事情上」。這是多益考試非常喜歡考的介系詞後方接 V-ing 的文法結構。
  • 被動語態與分詞片語修飾
    with the broadcaster scheduled to meet representatives of both sides on Monday to discuss the details.
    這裡使用了 "with + 名詞 + 過去分詞 (scheduled)" 的複合結構，用來表示附帶狀況，意指「在電視台預定於週一會見雙方代表的情況下」。
