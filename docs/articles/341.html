遭起訴立委辦公室主任棄保潛逃，助理遭檢方約談

Aide questioned after indicted lawmaker's office manager jumps bail

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • indict v. 起訴，控告
    Taipei prosecutors have questioned an aide who allegedly met with Chang Chun-chieh (張俊傑), the office manager of indicted Legislator Kao Chin Su-mei (高金素梅), on Sunday before Chang jumped bail.
    台北地檢署於周日、即遭起訴之立法委員高金素梅的辦公室主任張俊傑棄保潛逃前，曾約談一名據稱與張俊傑會面的助理。
  • statement n. 聲明，陳述
    In a statement, the Taipei District Prosecutors Office said it had carried out searches with New Taipei Xindian Precinct police Tuesday...
    台北地檢署在聲明中表示，檢察官已於週二會同新北市警察局新店分局...
  • conduct v. 執行，實施，舉辦
    On Wednesday, additional searches were conducted at Chen's office at the Legislative Yuan, prosecutors said...
    檢方表示，週三對陳姓助理在立法院的辦公室進行了追加搜索...
  • subsidy n. 補助金，津貼
    ...fraudulently obtaining more than NT$7.87 million in public funds through fraudulent claims for legislative assistant salaries, bonuses, overtime pay and childcare subsidies.
    ...透過虛報立法院助理薪資、獎金、加班費及托育補助，以欺詐方式獲得超過787萬元的公款。
  • earmark v. 編列，指定（用途）
    Chang, meanwhile, was accused of conspiring with Kao Chin to fraudulently obtain funds earmarked for assistant salaries...
    同時，張俊傑則被指控與高金素梅共謀，詐取指定用於助理薪資的款項...
  • authorize v. 授權，批准
    Prosecutors said that during the COVID-19 pandemic in 2022, Kao Chin also authorized Chang to enlist others to illegally import 74,400 unapproved rapid test kits from China...
    檢察官表示，在2022年COVID-19疫情期間，高金素梅還授權張俊傑招攬他人從中國非法進口74,400劑未經批准的快篩試劑...
  • shortage n. 短缺，不足
    Kao Chin, however, has claimed that the rapid test kits were intended to help Indigenous communities amid a shortage of the devices in Taiwan.
    然而，高金素梅聲稱，這些快篩試劑是為了在台灣相關器材短缺的情況下幫助原住民社群。
  • recommend v. 建議，推薦
    In the indictment, prosecutors recommended a sentence of at least 16 years for Chang and 12.5 years for Kao Chin.
    在起訴書中，檢察官建議對張俊傑判處至少16年有期徒刑，對高金素梅判處12.5年有期徒刑。
  • release v. 釋放，發布
    Chang, who was held incommunicado for nearly four months before his indictment, was released on NT$10 million bail in June.
    張俊傑在被起訴前被禁見近四個月，並於6月以1千萬台幣交保。
  • deny v. 否認
    During the first day of his trial on Sept. 1, Chang denied the corruption charges but admitted to other offenses, such as document forgery.
    在9月1日開庭審理的第一天，張俊傑否認貪污指控，但承認其他罪行，例如偽造文書。

文法重點

  • 關係代名詞非限定用法（Non-defining relative clause）
    Kao Chin, who has denied wrongdoing, was accused of fraudulently obtaining more than NT$7.87 million in public funds...
    句中的「who has denied wrongdoing」用逗號隔開，對先行詞 Kao Chin 進行補充說明（非限定用法）。在多益閱讀測驗中，常考此類子句中格位（主格 who / 受格 whom）或分詞簡化的選擇題。
  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況或結果（Participial phrase）
    ...circumventing medical device regulations.
    句尾的「circumventing...」為現在分詞構句，是由連接詞子句簡化而來，用來補充說明前面主句所發生的動作及其帶來的結果或伴隨狀況（意指「藉此規避醫療器材法規」）。
  • 被動語態（Passive Voice）
    On Wednesday, additional searches were conducted at Chen's office at the Legislative Yuan...
    新聞英語為了客觀報導並強調承受動作的對象，經常大量使用被動語態（be + 過去分詞）。此處「were conducted」強調搜索動作是被檢警執行的。
