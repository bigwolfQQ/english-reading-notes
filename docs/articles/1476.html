颶風西門在墨西哥附近轉為4級風暴

Hurricane Simon turns into a level 4 storm near Mexico

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • strengthen v. 加強，增強
    Hurricane Simon strengthened into a powerful Category 4 storm and was expected to make landfall or skirt the coast of west-central Mexico yesterday, the US National Hurricane Center said.
    美國國家颶風中心表示，颶風西門已增強為威力強大的4級風暴，預計於昨天登陸或擦過墨西哥中西部海岸。
  • expect v. 預期，預料
    Fluctuations in strength are expected until the core of Simon reaches the coast, with a maximum wind speed of 249kph, the center said.
    該中心表示，在西門的中心抵達海岸之前，預期其強度會有所波動，最高風速可達每小時249公里。
  • protect v. 保護
    “Preparations to protect life and property should be rushed to completion shortly in the hurricane warning area,” it said.
    中心表示：「在颶風警告區域內，保護生命與財產的準備工作應趕快在短時間內完成。」
  • damage n. 損害，損失
    It comes as Florida assesses the damage from Isaias, the first hurricane of the Atlantic season, which killed at least four people in the southeastern US.
    在此同時，佛羅里達州正評估大西洋颶風季首場颶風「伊薩亞斯」所造成的損害，該颶風已在美國東南部造成至少4人死亡。
  • evacuate v. 撤離，疏散
    Mexico’s navy had evacuated about 100 people from settlements along the country’s west coast due to the storm.
    由於這場風暴，墨西哥海軍已從該國西海岸沿岸的聚落撤離了大約100人。
  • declare v. 宣布，宣告
    The government of Jalisco state, where Puerto Vallarta is located, declared a red alert for several municipalities.
    巴亞爾塔港所在的哈利斯科州政府已對多個市鎮發布紅色警戒。
  • suspend v. 暫停，中止
    The airport has also suspended operations.
    該機場也已暫停營運。
  • threat n. 威脅
    However, residents of Puerto Vallarta, which sits on a huge horseshoe-shaped bay that helps defuse the power of a hurricane, appeared largely unfazed by the threat.
    然而，巴亞爾塔港的居民似乎對威脅大致感到泰然處之；該市位於一個巨大的馬蹄形海灣上，有助於減弱颶風的威力。
  • entrance n. 入口
    While shop owners sandbagged the entrances to their stores and boarded up the doors and windows, there was little sense of impending disaster.
    雖然店鋪老闆用沙包堆疊店面入口，並用木板封住門窗，但並沒有什麼迫在眉睫的災難感。
  • reference n. 提及，參考
    “What people are most scared of is that the water will reach a tall height... like it did with Hurricane Kenna,” furniture shop owner Paulina Perez said, in reference to the 2002 storm that killed at least four people.
    傢俱行老闆保琳娜·培瑞茲提及2002年造成至少4人死亡的風暴時說：「人們最害怕的是水位會升高到很高的地步……就像颶風肯納那時候一樣。」
  • order v. 命令，下令
    Authorities said they would order residents to take shelter starting at 5pm.
    當局表示，他們將命令居民從下午5點開始尋求庇護。

文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Hurricane Simon strengthened into a powerful Category 4 storm and was expected to make landfall or skirt the coast of west-central Mexico yesterday, the US National Hurricane Center said.
    句中的 "was expected" 使用了「be動詞 + 過去分詞」的被動語態結構，強調西門颶風受到氣象中心的預期，符合新聞報導中客觀陳述預測的常用語法。
  • 非限定關係子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    The government of Jalisco state, where Puerto Vallarta is located, declared a red alert for several municipalities.
    句中的 ", where Puerto Vallarta is located," 是一個非限定關係子句（前面有逗號），用來補充說明 Jalisco 州的地點，"where" 代替 "in which" 在句中作地方副詞。
  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    It comes as Florida assesses the damage from Isaias, the first hurricane of the Atlantic season, which killed at least four people in the southeastern US.
    句中的 "which killed at least four people in the southeastern US" 是關係代間詞子句，用來修飾前面的 Isaias（颶風），詳細說明該颶風造成的傷亡狀況。
  • 地方副詞置於句首的倒裝結構變體 (Introductory Prepositional Phrase)
    In the Pacific tourist resort of Puerto Vallarta, the skies were cloudy on Saturday, but the sea was still relatively calm.
    句首的 "In the Pacific tourist resort of Puerto Vallarta" 是地方介系詞片語，置於句首用來設定背景場景，使讀者先了解事件發生的地點，隨後帶出主句的描述。
