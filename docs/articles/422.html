歐洲中心擔心 AfD 勝利後掀起民族主義浪潮，但這只能怪自己

Europe’s center fears nationalist wave after AfD victory, but only has itself to blame

單字解析

  • score n. 得分、成績
    nearly doubled its score in Sunday’s state election in Saxony-Anhalt to 43.8 percent of the vote.
    在薩克森-安哈特邦週日的州選舉中，其得票率幾乎翻倍，達到 43.8%。
  • collapse v. 暴跌、坍塌
    the center-right Christian Democratic Union (CDU) of German Chancellor Friedrich Merz collapsed to 17.2 percent
    德國總理弗里德里希·梅爾茨的中右翼基督教民主聯盟（CDU）暴跌至 17.2%
  • majority n. 多數
    It has 39 lawmakers in the 83-member parliament, just short of an outright majority.
    它在擁有 83 個席位的議會中擁有 39 名議員，離絕對多數僅一步之遙。
  • support n. 支援、支持率
    support for the AfD there has consistently been double the national average.
    該黨在那裡的支援率一直維持在全國平均水準的兩倍。
  • consultancy n. 顧問公司
    said Mujtaba Rahman of the Eurasia Group risk consultancy.
    歐亞集團（Eurasia Group）風險諮詢公司的穆傑塔巴·拉赫曼（Mujtaba Rahman）表示。
  • conclude v. 得出結論
    Saxony-Anhalt should not be the basis for sweeping conclusions about the electoral sociology of the AfD.
    薩克森-安哈特不應成為對 AfD 選舉社會學做出全面結論的基礎。
  • fluctuate v. 波動、起伏
    Like other parties, their electoral support fluctuates.
    與其他政黨一樣，他們的選民支持率會波動。
  • collaborate v. 合作
    despite a refusal by mainstream parties in countries such as France and Germany to collaborate with far-right parties
    儘管法國和德國等國的主流政黨拒絕與極右翼政黨合作
  • compromise n. 妥協、妥協方案
    The party has been protected from the compromises and failures inherent in government.
    該黨受到了保護，免受執政所固有的妥協和失敗的影響。
  • frustrate v. 使挫折、使沮喪
    further frustrating voters and driving support for the extremes.
    這進一步挫敗了選民並推動了對極端主義的支持。
  • dynamic n. 動態、動力
    There’s a self-reinforcing political dynamic that feeds the AfD’s rise
    存在一種助長 AfD 崛起的自我強化政治動態
  • persistent adj. 持續的、堅持不懈的
    Besides the persistent inability of mainstream governments to deliver for disillusioned voters
    除了主流政府一再無法為心碎選民交出成績單之外
  • attitude n. 態度
    Research suggests voter attitudes toward core far-right themes such as immigration have not changed significantly
    研究表明，選民對移民等核心極右翼主題的態度並沒有顯著改變

文法重點

  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    Alternative for Germany (AfD), a party that dabbles in Nazi imagery, distinguishes between “biological” and “passport” Germans... nearly doubled its score...
    句中使用「that dabbles in...」這個由關係代名詞 that 引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的先行詞 a party（即德國另類選擇黨 AfD），補充說明該政黨的特徵。
  • 現在分詞片語作後置修飾語
    Far-right populist parties are now in government as part of ruling coalitions in Croatia... propping up a right-wing minority government in Sweden...
    句尾的「propping up...」是由現在分詞（prop 的分詞）所引導的分詞片語，用來補充說明前面主詞及其動詞狀態下的伴隨動作或結果。
  • 表假設語氣的過去式助動詞（would）
    It would be wise not to read too much into one election result in one quite particular German state.
  • 被動語態的應用
    The party has been protected from the compromises and failures inherent in government.
    句中的「has been protected」採用了現在完成式的被動語態（have/has been + 過去分詞），用來強調主詞 The party（該黨）承受了保護動作，且狀態持續到現在。
