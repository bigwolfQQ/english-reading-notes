社論：鄭麗文在瓷器店裡的大象（意指魯莽行事、破壞力強）

EDITORIAL: Cheng Li-wun in a China shop

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • campaign trail n. phr. 競選活動期間，競選行程
    Chinese Nationalist Party (KMT) Chairwoman Cheng Li-wun (鄭麗文) has had a tough week on the campaign trail in support of her party’s candidates for November’s local elections.
    中國國民黨（國民黨）主席鄭麗文在為她政黨11月地方選舉候選人助選的競選行程中，度過了艱難的一週。
  • nomination n. 提名，任命
    Trouble has been brewing in Changhua County for the past few months over the KMT’s nomination of Wei Ping-cheng (魏平政) for Changhua County commissioner.
    過去幾個月來，國民黨提名魏平政參選彰化縣長一事，在彰化縣醞釀著麻煩。
  • eligible adj. 有資格的，合乎條件的
    Incumbent Changhua County Commissioner Wang Hui-mei (王惠美) of the KMT is not eligible for re-election, as she has already served two terms...
    現任國民黨彰化縣長王惠美因已任滿兩屆而無法競選連任...
  • endorse v. 背書，公開支持
    Wang added that her loyalties remain with the party and that her reluctance to endorse Wei was due to doubts that he was the best candidate.
    王惠美補充說，她對黨的忠誠依舊，而她不願為魏平政背書，是因為懷疑他是否為最佳人選。
  • consult v. 諮詢，請教，商議
    She expressed frustration over her suspicion that when Cheng had consulted her about the matter, the decision had already been made.
    她表達了她的沮喪，因為她懷疑當鄭麗文就此事諮詢她時，決定其實已經做好了。
  • alliance n. 聯盟，結盟
    ...with accusations that Cheng’s handling of the nomination process there has put the KMT’s alliance with the Taiwan People’s Party (TPP) at risk...
    ...並有指控稱鄭麗文對當地提名過程的處理，使得國民黨與台灣民眾黨（民眾黨）的結盟面臨風險...
  • generate v. 產生，引起
    The mock slap generated heated debate, with Chang Yu-tzu (張育慈), a New Power Party candidate for the Hsinchu County council, calling it a display of violence against women.
    這個假裝掌摑的動作引發了激烈的辯論，時代力量新竹縣議員參選人張育慈稱其為針對女性的暴力表現。
  • divert v. 轉移（注意力等）
    Leaving aside optics — which Cheng laughed off at the time, but should have taken more seriously — the debate diverts attention from what Fan said in the following minutes...
    撇開觀感不談——鄭麗文當時一笑了之，但本應更嚴肅看待——這場辯論轉移了人們對范振宗在隨後幾分鐘內講話內容的注意力...
  • bear responsibility v. phr. 承擔責任
    Fan said that Cheng would bear responsibility if Hsu loses the county race, as she put the KMT-TPP alliance and the KMT’s internal unity at risk.
    范振宗表示，如果徐欣瑩輸掉縣長選戰，鄭麗文將承擔責任，因為她將國眾聯盟與國民黨內部團結置於風險之中。
  • impasse n. 僵局，死胡同
    At an event in Taichung’s Shengang District (神岡) yesterday, a reporter asked Chiang about the political impasse in Changhua between Wang and Cheng.
    昨日在台中神岡的一場活動中，記者向江啟臣詢問了彰化王惠美與鄭麗文之間的政治僵局。
  • safeguard v. 維護，保護
    Asked whether the show of unity with the TPP was her way of telling Cheng how to safeguard the alliance in other localities, she responded in political platitudes...
    當被問及展現與民眾黨的團結是否是她告訴鄭麗文如何在其他地區維護聯盟的方式時，她以政治老生常談做出回應...
  • in jeopardy prep. phr. 處於危險之中，瀕臨危險
    Lu spoke of the importance of unity in the context of the November and 2028 elections, but Cheng is putting both in jeopardy with her bull-in-a-China-shop approach to party leadership.
    盧秀燕在11月和2028年選舉的背景下談到了團結的重要性，但鄭麗文正以她那種魯莽闖入瓷器店的黨內領導方式，將這兩者置於危險之中。

文法重點

  • 現在完成進行式 (Present Perfect Continuous)
    Trouble has been brewing in Changhua County for the past few months over the KMT’s nomination of Wei Ping-cheng...
    句型用「has been + V-ing」，用來強調一個動作從過去某個時間點開始持續發生，並且一直持續到現在，甚至可能還會繼續下去。此處用來描述彰化縣的麻煩「過去幾個月來一直不斷在醞釀中」擾攘至今。
  • 獨立分詞構句 / 伴隨狀況 (Participle Clause)
    ...Wang Hui-mei (王惠美) of the KMT is not eligible for re-election, as she has already served two terms, but she has conspicuously declined to attend campaign events with Wei.
    雖然此句是用對等連接詞 but 連接，但在文章後半段如「..., with accusations that Cheng’s handling... has put... at risk」則使用了「with + 名詞 + 分詞」的結構，用來補充說明伴隨發生的狀況或背景，是新聞英文中非常高頻且能增加句子緊湊度的句型。
  • 假設語氣（以 as if 引導的子句）
    She responded in political platitudes, as if avoiding exacerbating the threat to unity that she was referring to.
  • 關係代間接問句 / 複合關係代名詞子句
    The debate diverts attention from what Fan said in the following minutes as he lambasted Cheng over her handling of the Jhubei City nomination process.
    句中的「what Fan said」是由 what（相當於 the thing which）引導的名詞子句，在介系詞 from 後面當作受詞，用來指稱范振宗所說的話，是多益閱讀與文法測驗中極常見的句型結構。
