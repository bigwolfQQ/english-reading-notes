台灣羽球男雙、女雙組合闖入 BWF 北極公開賽決賽

Taiwanese men's, women's doubles pairs reach BWF Arctic Open finals

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • advance v. 晉級，前進
    One Taiwanese pair each in men's and women's doubles advanced to the finals of the 2026 Clash of Clans Arctic Open...
    台灣的男子及女子雙打組合各有一組人馬晉級 2026 年部落衝突北極公開賽的決賽……
  • tournament n. 錦標賽，競賽
    ...a Badminton World Federation (BWF) Super 500 tournament, in Vantaa, Finland, on Saturday (local time).
    ……於週六（當地時間）在芬蘭萬塔舉行的一場世界羽球聯盟（BWF）超級 500 賽事。
  • defeat v. 擊敗，戰勝
    In the men's doubles semifinals, Lai Po-yu and Tsai Fu-cheng defeated French duo Julien Maio and William Villeger 21-15, 19-21, 21-13.
    在男雙準決賽中，賴柏佑與蔡富丞以 21-15、19-21、21-13 擊敗法國組合朱利安·馬尤與威廉·維利格。
  • lead n. 領先地位
    Lai and Tsai held a narrow lead in the opening game before pulling away from 15-14...
    賴柏佑與蔡富丞在首局取得微弱領先，隨後在 14-14 的比數時拉開差距……
  • commit v. 犯下（錯誤、罪行等）
    After committing several unforced errors in the second game, the Taiwanese pair lost 19-21.
    台灣組合在第二局出現多次非受迫性失誤後，以 19-21 落敗。
  • deciding adj. 決定性的
    They then stepped up their attack in the deciding game after a closely contested start...
    接著雙方在決勝局開局膠著後，兩人加強了進攻……
  • face v. 面臨，對陣
    Lai and Tsai will face Malaysian pair Aaron Tai and Yap Roy King in Sunday's final.
    賴柏佑與蔡富丞將在週日的決賽中對陣馬來西亞組合戴偉欽與葉睿慶。
  • recover v. 恢復，復原
    In the women's doubles, Hsu Yin-hui and Lin Jhih-yun recovered from losing the opening game 17-21 to Chinese pair Bao Lijing and Cao Zihan...
    在女雙方面，許尹鏸與林芝昀在首局以 17-21 敗給中國組合鮑驪婧與曹梓涵後重整旗鼓……
  • secure v. 獲得，確保
    ...winning the next two games 21-14, 21-18 to secure their place in the final.
    ……隨後以 21-14、21-18 連勝兩局，確保了決賽席位。
  • compete v. 競爭，參賽
    ...since they began partnering to compete together in mid-2023.
    ……自她們於 2023 年中開始搭檔參賽以來。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作結果狀語
    Lai and Tsai held a narrow lead in the opening game before pulling away from 15-14, winning six of the next seven points to take the game 21-15.
    句尾的「winning six of the next seven points」是由現在分詞 winning 引導的分詞片語，用來補充說明主句動作所產生的自然結果（連拿七分中的六分）。
  • 介系詞加動名詞片語（After + V-ing）
    After committing several unforced errors in the second game, the Taiwanese pair lost 19-21.
    「After committing...」是介系詞片語作時間副詞用，表達「在犯下……之後」。介系詞後面若接動詞，必須使用動名詞（V-ing）型態。
  • since 引導的時間副詞子句
    It will be their first final at a BWF Super 500 tournament since they began partnering to compete together in mid-2023.
    「since they began partnering...」是一個由連接詞 since 引導的時間子句，用來表示「自從……以來」，主句通常使用完成式或未來式，從屬子句則使用過去式（began）。
