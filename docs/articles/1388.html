美國議員譴責對總統兒子的監視

US lawmaker condemns spying on president’s son

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • surveillance n. 監視，監督
    The surveillance of President William Lai’s (賴清德) son on US soil is “a chilling reminder...”, US Representative John Moolenaar... said on Tuesday.
    美國眾議院議員約翰·穆勒納爾於週二表示，在美國本土對總統賴清德的兒子進行監視，「是一個令人不寒而慄的提醒……」
  • violation n. 違反，侵犯
    Moolenaar said he condemned the surveillance as “another gross violation of our country’s sovereignty” and the CCP’s “dangerous belligerence against Taiwan.”
    穆勒納爾表示，他譴責這種監視是「對我國主權的又一次嚴重侵犯」以及中共對台灣「危險的好戰行為」。
  • statement n. 聲明，陳述
    In a statement published on the committee’s Web site, Moolenaar said he condemned the surveillance...
    穆勒納爾在委員會網站上發布的聲明中表示，他譴責這種監視……
  • suspect v. 懷疑，涉嫌
    He was commenting on the FBI’s arrest on Sunday of Wanying Heather Zhang (張婉瑩) on suspicion of surveilling Lai’s son, Lai Ting-yu (賴廷與).
    他是在評論聯邦調查局（FBI）於週日逮捕張婉瑩一事，她涉嫌監視賴的兒子賴廷與。
  • official n. 官員
    Based on a review of her cellphone and travel records, Holt said that he believed Zhang... met with a Chinese official in China on or about Jan. 25 last year...
    霍爾特表示，根據對她的手機和旅行紀錄的審查，他認為張女……去年1月25日左右在中國與一名中國官員會面……
  • obtain v. 獲得，取得
    ...and that the official tasked her with obtaining information about the residents of an address in Irvine, California.
    ……並且該官員指派她去獲取加州爾灣某地址居民的資訊。
  • associated with phr. 與……相關聯
    Holt determined that the address “was previously associated with a Taiwanese elected official, with vocal anti-CCP views,” the affidavit said.
    宣誓書指出，霍爾特認定該地址「先前與一名具有強烈反共觀點的台灣民選官員有關聯」。
  • board v. 登機，上船
    Zhang was arrested on Sunday at Los Angeles International Airport before boarding a flight to Shanghai...
    張女於週日在洛杉磯國際機場登機飛往上海前被捕……
  • strategic adj. 戰略的
    Former US vice president Mike Pence on Tuesday called China “the greatest economic and strategic threat facing the United States in the twenty-first century”...
    美國前副總統麥克·彭斯於週二將中國稱為「廿一世紀美國面臨的最大經濟與戰略脅迫」……
  • potential adj. 潛在的
    Lai Ting-yu was working in Washington state, where the CCP was collecting information about him for potential blackmail or other actions...
    賴廷與當時在華盛頓州工作，中共一直在那裡收集他的資訊，以便進行潛在的勒索或其他行動……

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    In a statement published on the committee’s Web site, Moolenaar said he condemned the surveillance...
    過去分詞片語「published on the committee’s Web site」用來修飾前面的名詞「statement」，相當於形容詞子句「which was published on the committee’s Web site」，省略了關係代名詞與be動詞，使語氣更為精煉。
  • 現在分詞表達附帶狀況或原因 (Present Participle Indicating Circumstance)
    Zhang was arrested on Sunday at Los Angeles International Airport before boarding a flight to Shanghai on suspicion of acting as an unregistered agent of a foreign government...
    句中的「before boarding a flight...」是由介系詞結合動名詞（現在分詞性質）構成的時間副詞片語，用來補充說明張女被捕的時機。
  • 名詞子句作受詞 (Noun Clause as Object)
    Holt said that he believed Zhang, a California real-estate agent and China-born naturalized US citizen, met with a Chinese official in China on or about Jan. 25 last year...
    動詞「said」後面接了一個由「that」引導的名詞子句（that 可省略），作為該動詞的受詞，用來表達說話者所陳述的內容。
