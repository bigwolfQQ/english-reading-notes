文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Hundreds of thousands of people were readying to vote on animals gorging themselves in rural Alaska.
  • 被動語態的應用 (Passive Voice)
    Only one will be crowned Fat Bear Week champion.
  • 過去分詞片語作修飾語 (Past Participle Phrase)
    The victims, including a pregnant woman, were gathered at a house shortly before 11pm when armed assailants reportedly entered the premises and opened fire...
  • 現在分詞短語表示伴隨狀況或補充說明 (Present Participle Phrase indicating accompanying state)
    The motive for the shooting was not immediately clear, police said, adding that investigators were following a “promising” lead that could assist in the case.
