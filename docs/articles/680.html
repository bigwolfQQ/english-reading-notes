追蹤器顯示：台灣黑熊比想像中更靠近人類聚落

Bears get closer to human settlements than thought, trackers show

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • settlement n. 聚落，定居地
    The Forestry and Nature Conservation Agency yesterday released tracking records of released Formosan black bears for the first time, showing that they get much closer to human settlements than previously thought.
    林業及自然保育署昨天首次發布野放台灣黑熊的追蹤紀錄，顯示牠們比以往認為的還要更靠近人類聚落。
  • capture v. 捕獲，捕捉
    The agency said it had captured two bears, Da Qu-ali (達古阿里) and Aziman (阿里曼), in indigenous villages in Hualien County and began to track them with a camera-mounted satellite collar after releasing them back into the wild.
    該社表示，他們在花蓮縣的原住民部落捕獲了兩隻黑熊「達古阿里」和「阿里曼」，並在將牠們野放回山林後，使用裝有相機的衛星項圈進行追蹤。
  • release v. 釋放，野放，發布
    The Forestry and Nature Conservation Agency yesterday released tracking records of released Formosan black bears for the first time...
    林業及自然保育署昨天首次發布野放台灣黑熊的追蹤紀錄...
  • predict v. 預測，預料
    The agency has introduced artificial intelligence-assisted early warning and monitoring systems in high-risk areas to predict bear intrusions...
    該署已在高風險地區導入人工智慧輔助的早鳥預警與監測系統來預測黑熊入侵...
  • address v. 處理，應對，解決
    To help people address potential bear encounters, the agency would distribute guidelines to communities and schools around hot spots for bear activity, he said.
    他說，為了協助民眾應對潛在的黑熊遭遇，該署將向熊類活動熱點周邊的社區和學校發布指導方針。
  • encounter n. 遭遇，相遇
    According to the agency’s guidelines, human-bear encounters can be categorized into three types: sightings, nuisances and physical attacks.
    根據該署的指導方針，人熊遭遇可分為三種類型：目擊、干擾與實際攻擊。
  • manage v. 管理，經營
    Lin urged people visiting mountainous areas to manage their garbage, especially food scraps and kitchen waste, to prevent attracting bears.
    林呼籲前往山區的民眾管理好垃圾，特別是食物殘渣和廚餘，以避免吸引黑熊。
  • extend v. 延伸，擴大，延長
    Bear sightings used to be concentrated in areas governed by the agency’s Hualien, Taitung and Taichung branches, but they have extended to the Hsinchu branch area, Lin said...
    林說，過去黑熊出沒的目擊事件集中在該署花蓮、台東和台中分署管轄的地區，但現在已擴展至新竹分署轄區...
  • frequency n. 頻率，發生率
    ...adding that the frequency of sightings has also increased.
    ...並補充說目擊頻率也有所增加。
  • conserve v. 保育，保存
    However, indigenous people help conserve the primary and secondary forests that serve as bear habitats, he said.
    不過他說，原住民協助保育了作為黑熊棲息地的原始林與次生林。
  • invest v. 投資，投入
    ...calling on the government to invest more in bear research and conflict prevention.
    ...呼籲政府應在黑熊研究和衝突預防上投入更多資金。
  • estimate v. 估計，估算
    The agency in March estimated that Taiwan has about 1,200 Formosan black bears, but said it would announce a more precise figure by the end of the year.
    該署在 3 月估計台灣約有 1,200 隻台灣黑熊，但表示將在年底前公布更精確的數字。
  • category n. 種類，類別
    According to the agency’s guidelines, human-bear encounters can be categorized into three types...
    根據該署的指導方針，人熊遭遇可分為三種類型...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞當後位修飾語（分詞片語）
    The Forestry and Nature Conservation Agency yesterday released tracking records of released Formosan black bears for the first time, showing that they get much closer to human settlements than previously thought.
    句尾的「showing that...」是由現在分詞片語引導的結果或附帶狀況，用來補充說明前面整句發布追蹤紀錄所產生的結果，省略了「which shows that...」的關代與動詞，使語氣更為緊湊流暢。
  • 過去分詞當形容詞修飾名詞
    The agency said it had captured two bears, Da Qu-ali (達古阿里) and Aziman (阿里曼), in indigenous villages in Hualien County and began to track them with a camera-mounted satellite collar after releasing them back into the wild.
    句中的「released」和「camera-mounted」都是過去分詞，分別放在名詞前或作為複合形容詞，用來修飾「Formosan black bears」與「satellite collar」，表示被動或完成的狀態（被野放的黑熊、裝有相機的項圈）。
  • 使役動詞或感官動詞後的分詞受詞補語
    The system would issue alerts within two minutes after detecting bear activity, he said.
    介系詞「after」後面接動名詞「detecting」，而「bear activity」是 detecting 的受詞。這類結構在英文新聞中非常常見，用來簡潔地表達時間或條件關係（在偵測到……之後）。
  • 假設語氣（若…會…）
    Tunghai University life science professor Lin Liang-kong (林良恭) cited Japanese research as showing that if a bear approaches human settlements, it would take three generations for its descendants to distance themselves from humans again.
    此句使用了第一或第二類型的條件句概念（if 帶現在式 implies 可能性，搭配 would + 原形動詞），用來假設「如果一隻熊接近人類聚落」這種情境發生時，其後代需要花費三代時間才能遠離人類的未來結果。
