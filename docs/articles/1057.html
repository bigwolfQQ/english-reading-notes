美國對台軍售是複雜議題仍在審查中：魯比歐

U.S. arms sales to Taiwan a complex issue, still under review: Rubio

單字解析

  • reiterate v. 重申，反覆說
    United States Secretary of State Marco Rubio reiterated Wednesday that U.S. arms sales to Taiwan remained under review...
    美國國務卿馬可·魯比歐週三重申，美國對台軍售仍在審查中……
  • balance v. 平衡，權衡
    ...saying the U.S. must balance such decisions against its own needs.
    ……並表示美國必須在做出此類決定時權衡自身的需要。
  • concern n. 擔憂，關切
    After four Republican senators raised concerns earlier in the day about stalled U.S. arms sales...
    在四名共和黨參議員稍早在當天對停滯的美國對台軍售……表示擔憂後
  • stalled adj. 停滯的，拋錨的
    After four Republican senators raised concerns earlier in the day about stalled U.S. arms sales and other military assistance to Taiwan...
    在四名共和黨參議員稍早在當天對停滯的美國對台軍售及其他軍事援助給台灣表示擔憂後……
  • capacity n. 容量，生產能力，產能
    A lot of this also involves balancing our industrial capacity and our military industrial base...
    這其中很大一部分還涉及平衡我們的工業產能與我們的軍事工業基礎……
  • afford v. 負擔得起，禁得起
    ...and whether we can afford to sell something, because we may need it for ourselves.
    ……以及我們是否負擔得起出售某物，因為我們可能自己需要它。
  • obligation n. 義務，責任
    ...about Washington's obligation to assist with Taiwan's self-defense capabilities.
    ……內容關於華盛頓協助台灣自衛能力的義務。
  • intend v. 打算，意欲
    ...expressed concerns about whether the State Department intended to fulfill its obligation...
    ……對國務院是否有意履行義務……表示擔憂
  • fulfill v. 履行，實現，執行
    ...whether the State Department intended to fulfill its obligation to provide US$300 million in foreign military financing...
    ……國務院是否有意履行義務，提供 3 億美元的外國軍事融資……
  • negotiating chip n. phr. 談判籌碼
    "We are concerned by President Trump's description of Taiwan as 'a very good negotiating chip' in engagements with [China President] Xi Jinping..."
    這四位參議員寫道：「我們對川普總統在與[中國國家主席]習近平……會談時，將台灣描述為『一個非常好的談判籌碼』感到擔憂。」
  • approve v. 批准，核准
    The official also said that last December, Trump had approved "the largest ever arms package for Taiwan" of US$11.1 billion.
    這位官員還表示，去年 12 月，川普批准了 111 億美元的「有史以來最大規模對台軍售案」。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Present Participle）作後置修飾語 / 分詞片語
    ...when asked about the issue on Wednesday, a senior administration official told CNA that U.S. President Donald Trump had said...
    句中的「when asked about the issue on Wednesday」是由連接詞引導的分詞片語，省略了主語與be動詞（原句應為 when he was asked...）。過去分詞「asked」在此用來被動修飾後面的主詞 a senior administration official，在新聞英文中非常常見，能有效簡化句子結構。
  • 最高級與比較級的特殊句型（...than any other...）
    "In his first term, President Trump approved more arms sales to Taiwan than any other President in history,"
    這句使用了「more... than any other + 單數名詞」的句型來表達最高級的概念。意思是川普總統批准的對台軍售比歷史上「任何其他」一位總統都還要多，透過比較級來突顯其最高級的程度，是多益閱讀與商務寫作中強調極致表現的常用句構。
  • 間接問句（Indirect Question）作為名詞子句
    Senators Mitch McConnell, John Cornyn, Lisa Murkowski, and Thom Tillis expressed concerns about whether the State Department intended to fulfill its obligation...
    句中的「whether the State Department intended to fulfill its obligation...」是由「whether」引導的間接問句，在此擔任介系詞 about 的受詞（名詞子句）。在表達懷疑、擔憂或詢問時，常用 whether 或 if 來連接不確定是否發生的動作或狀態。
