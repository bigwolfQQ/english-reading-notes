颶風逼近墨西哥海岸

Hurricane nears Mexican coast

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • threaten v. 威脅、危及
    Hurricane Nolo swept away from the Hawaiian Islands on Sunday as major Hurricane Polo bore down on Mexico’s Baja California peninsula, threatening storm surge, high winds and heavy rains.
    週日，當強烈颶風波洛逼近墨西哥下加利福尼亞半島，威脅要帶來暴潮、強風與豪雨之際，颶風諾洛則遠離夏威夷群島。
  • sustained adj. 持續的、持久的
    A high-wind warning, wind advisory and high surf advisory remained in effect for parts of the island through Sunday evening as Nolo’s maximum sustained winds increased to near 165kph.
    週日傍晚前，該島部分地區仍維持強風特報、風力諮詢以及高浪諮詢，當時諾洛的最大持續風速增加至約每小時165公里。
  • arrival n. 到達、抵達
    Meanwhile, residents of Mexico’s Baja California braced for the expected arrival yesterday of Category 3 Polo...
    同時，墨西哥下加利福尼亞的居民正嚴陣以待，準備迎接預計於昨日抵達的3級颶風波洛...
  • emergency n. 緊急情況、突發事件
    More than 120 US National Guard members were activated, part of roughly 400 statewide after the governor issued an emergency proclamation.
    在州長發布緊急公告後，超過120名美國國民兵被動員，這大約是全州約400名被動員兵力的一部分。
  • coordinate v. 協調、統籌
    US President Donald Trump approved an emergency declaration, authorizing the Federal Emergency Management Agency to coordinate assistance and provide funding support.
    美國總統唐納·川普批准了緊急宣告，授權聯邦緊急事務管理總署協調救援並提供資金支援。
  • funding n. 資金、經費
    US President Donald Trump approved an emergency declaration, authorizing the Federal Emergency Management Agency to coordinate assistance and provide funding support.
    美國總統唐納·川普批准了緊急宣告，授權聯邦緊急事務管理總署協調救援並提供資金支援。
  • recover v. 恢復、復原
    Hawaii residents are still recovering from Hurricane Lala, which brought heavy rain and winds strong enough to sweep about 100 homes off their foundations on the Big Island.
    夏威夷居民仍在從颶風拉拉的災情中恢復，該颶風帶來的雨勢與強風威力強大，足以將大島上約100棟房屋從地基上吹毀。
  • deploy v. 調動、部署
    With Polo approaching Mexico, the Mexican navy said it had gathered personnel and humanitarian aid in the ports of Guaymas and Mazatlan on the Pacific Coast and deployed three vessels that could deliver aid to coastal communities cut off by the storm.
    隨著波洛逼近墨西哥，墨西哥海軍表示已在太平洋沿岸的瓜伊馬斯和馬薩特蘭港口集結人員與人道救援物資，並部署了三艘船隻，可向被風暴切斷對外交通的沿海社區運送援助。
  • manager n. 經理、管理人
    “Our people are concerned about this event,” said Gloriely Robles Meza, a manager at a small hotel.
    小型飯店經理格洛麗莉·羅布雷斯·梅薩表示：「我們的人民對這次事件感到擔憂。」
  • support n. / v. 支援、支持
    Robles Meza said Sunday that army soldiers arrived in San Juanico to prepare a shelter and provide support for possible emergencies.
    羅布雷斯·梅薩週日表示，陸軍士兵已抵達聖胡安尼科，以準備避難所並為可能的緊急情況提供支援。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Phrase as Adverbial Modifier / 附帶狀況）
    Hurricane Nolo swept away from the Hawaiian Islands on Sunday as major Hurricane Polo bore down on Mexico’s Baja California peninsula, threatening storm surge, high winds and heavy rains.
    句尾的「threatening storm surge...」是由現在分詞片語引導的結構，用來補充說明主句動作發生時所產生的伴隨結果或狀況，相當於「and it threatened...」的簡化。
  • 關係代名詞子句（Restrictive Relative Clause）
    ...residents of Mexico’s Baja California braced for the expected arrival yesterday of Category 3 Polo, which had sustained winds of 205kph as of Sunday evening, the US National Hurricane Center in Miami said.
    「which had sustained winds of 205kph as of Sunday evening」為非限定關係子句，前方使用逗號隔開，用來補充說明先行詞 Category 3 Polo（3級颶風波洛）的詳細風速狀況。
  • 過去分詞作形容詞（Participial Adjective）
    There is concern that washed-out roads could block access to medical facilities.
    句中的「washed-out」是由動詞 wash out 轉變而來的過去分詞，置於名詞前作形容詞用，用來修飾 roads（道路），意指「被大雨沖毀的」。
