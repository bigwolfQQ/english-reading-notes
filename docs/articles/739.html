疾管署：台灣流感疫情預計在中秋節前後達到高峰

Flu cases in Taiwan expected to peak around Mid-Autumn Festival: CDC

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • epidemic n. 疫情，流行病
    Taiwan's flu epidemic continued to rise last week...
    台灣的流感疫情上週持續升溫……
  • outpatient adj. / n. 門診的 / 門診病人
    ...with more than 130,000 outpatient and emergency visits for influenza-like illness recorded...
    ……記錄到超過 13 萬人次的類流感門診與急診就診……
  • peak v. / n. 達到高峰，到達頂峰 / 高峰
    ...and is expected to peak around the Mid-Autumn Festival holiday, the Centers for Disease Control (CDC) said Tuesday.
    疾病管制署（CDC）週二表示，預計該疫情將在中秋節假期前後達到高峰。
  • a total of phr. 總共，合計
    A total of 136,796 outpatient and emergency visits for influenza-like illness were recorded from Sept. 6-12...
    9 月 6 日至 12 日期間，類流感門急診總就診人次達 136,796 人……
  • previous adj. 先前的，以前的
    ...up 16.5 percent from the previous week, CDC Epidemic Intelligence Center Deputy Director Lee Chia-lin (李佳琳) said at a regular briefing.
    CDC 疫情中心副主任李佳琳在例行記者會上表示，較前一週上升 16.5%。
  • briefing n. 簡報會，情況說明會
    ...Lee Chia-lin (李佳琳) said at a regular briefing.
    ……李佳琳在例行記者會上表示。
  • account for phr. v. 占（比例），說明
    ...with the A(H1N1) subtype accounting for 82.2 percent of detected cases, Lee said.
    李佳琳表示，其中 A(H1N1) 亞型占檢出病例的 82.2%。
  • cumulative adj. 累積的，漸增的
    ...bringing the cumulative number of severe cases since Oct. 1 last year to 1,421, Lee said.
    李佳琳表示，使自去年 10 月 1 日以來的累計重症病例達到 1,421 例。
  • forecast v. / n. 預測，預報
    He forecast the outbreak would peak around the Mid-Autumn Festival holiday...
    他預測疫情將在中秋節假期前後達到高峰……
  • involve v. 涉及，包含，（病情）影響到
    Among severe cases, 65 percent involved people aged 65 or older...
    在重症病例中，65 歲或以上長者占 65%（字面：涉及 65 歲或以上長者）……
  • stable adj. 穩定的
    ...was hospitalized after developing fever and cough and is now conscious and in stable condition...
    ……在出現發燒和咳嗽後住院，目前意識清楚且病情穩定……
  • ease v. 減緩，減輕，逐漸放緩
    Meanwhile, Taiwan's COVID-19 epidemic continued to ease, though it remained in the epidemic period.
    另一方面，台灣的新冠疫情持續趨緩，儘管仍處於流行期。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾（分詞構句簡化）
    ...with more than 130,000 outpatient and emergency visits for influenza-like illness recorded...
    句型「with + 受詞 + 過去分詞 (recorded)」表示附帶狀況，意指「在……被記錄的情況下」。過去分詞 recorded 表被動，修飾前面的 visits。
  • 現在分詞短語表附帶結果
    ...bringing the cumulative number of severe cases since Oct. 1 last year to 1,421, Lee said.
    逗號後方的「bringing...」是現在分詞短語作結果狀語，用來補充說明前面通報數據所帶來的連續性結果（即「使累計重症病例達到 1,421 例」）。
  • 形容詞子句（關係代名詞 who 引導）
    The child, who had no underlying conditions and had received two flu vaccine doses last year, was hospitalized...
    逗號夾擠出的「who had no underlying conditions and had received...」是非限定性關係子句，用來補充說明前面主詞 The child 的背景狀況，若拿掉不影響主要句意。
  • 對比連接詞片語（though 引導讓步子句）
    ...Taiwan's COVID-19 epidemic continued to ease, though it remained in the epidemic period.
    「though」在此作為連接詞引導讓步子句（= although），連接兩個相對的情況：新冠疫情持續趨緩，但「儘管如此」它依然處於流行期。
