菲律賓穆斯林自治區選舉可能需要籌組聯合政府

Coalition likely in vote in Philippines’ Bangsamoro region

單字解析

  • coalition n. 聯合，同盟，連立
    Coalition talks might be needed to choose the first elected chief minister...
    為了選出第一位民選首席部長，可能需要進行聯合政府談判……
  • autonomous adj. 自治的，自主的
    ...the Muslim-dominated autonomous region in the southern Philippines...
    ...菲律賓南部穆斯林人口占多數的自治區...
  • negotiate v. 談判，協商
    ...the former separatist movement that negotiated autonomy with the Philippine government.
    ...曾與菲律賓政府談判爭取自治權的前分離主義運動。
  • appoint v. 任命，委派
    ...Abdulraof Macacua, a former MILF commander appointed interim chief by Philippine President Ferdinand Marcos Jr last year.
    ...阿布杜拉羅夫·馬卡庫阿，他去年獲菲律賓總統小費迪南·馬可仕任命為臨時首席部長。
  • align v. 結盟，聯合，對齊
    ...the camp aligned with Macacua led with 35.2 percent of votes...
    ...與馬卡庫阿結盟的陣營以 35.2% 的得票率領先...
  • secure v. 獲得，取得，確保
    ...that neither camp appeared likely to secure the 41 seats needed for a majority...
    ...雙方陣營似乎都不大可能拿下取得多數席次所需的 41 席...
  • stability n. 穩定，穩定性
    ...because it could strengthen peace and political stability, he said.
    ...他表示，因為這能強化和平與政治穩定。
  • turnout n. 投票率，出席人數
    The election drew about an 80 percent turnout...
    這次選舉吸引了約 80% 的投票率...
  • representatives n. 代表
    Voters elected party, district and sectoral representatives...
    選民選出了政黨、選區及產業代表...
  • agreement n. 協議，協定，同意
    The vote is widely viewed as the most significant milestone since a 2014 peace agreement...
    這次投票普遍被視為自 2014 年和平協議以來最重要的里程碑...

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後置修飾（分詞構句/形容詞片語）
    Coalition talks might be needed to choose the first elected chief minister of the Muslim-dominated autonomous region in the southern Philippines, with unofficial results suggesting no single bloc is on course to dominate the new 80-member parliament.
    句中的 "Muslim-dominated"（由穆斯林主導的）是由過去分詞構成的複合形容詞，用來修飾其後的名詞 "autonomous region"。這種結構在商業及新聞英文中非常常見，能簡潔地表達被動或完成的概念。
  • 獨立分詞構句（With + 名詞 + 分詞/形容詞）
    With 98.56 percent of votes counted in the party-list race, the camp aligned with Macacua led with 35.2 percent of votes...
    「With + 名詞 + 過去分詞（如 counted）」形成獨立分詞構句，用來表示附帶狀況或背景原因。在此例中，"98.56 percent of votes" 是被點算的，因此使用過去分詞 "counted"，相當於副詞子句表伴隨狀況。
  • 關係代名詞子句（限定用法）
    Voters elected party, district and sectoral representatives who would make up the 80-member parliament tasked with selecting the region’s first elected chief minister.
    句中的 "who would make up the 80-member parliament..." 是由關係代名詞 "who" 引導的形容詞子句，用前面的先行詞 "representatives"（代表們）進行補充說明。子句中的 "tasked with..." 則是省略了 "who are" 的分詞片語，進一步修飾 "parliament"。
