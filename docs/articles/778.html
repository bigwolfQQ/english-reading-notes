危及美國與盟友的破裂誓言

The broken vows that endanger US, allies

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • institutions n. 機構、制度
    When US President Donald Trump in January urged Iranians to “take over your institutions” and promised that “help is on its way,” he presumably meant American help for the protesters.
    當美國總統唐納·川普在 1 月敦促 Iranians「接管你們的機構」並承諾「救援正在路上」時，他大概是指美國對抗議者的援助。
  • campaign n. 活動、戰役、競選
    However, the ill-timed and uncoordinated US bombing campaign that came only after 50,000 Iranians were murdered in the streets seemed more like a trick to lure them into a death trap.
    然而，這場時機不當且缺乏協調的美國轟炸行動，竟然是在 50,000 名伊朗人在街頭遭謀殺之後才展開，看起來更像是誘捕他們陷入死亡陷阱的詭計。
  • regime n. 政權、當權體制
    Instead, the help that was on the way came from China, not from the US, and it went not to the Iranian people, but to the regime that was killing and otherwise oppressing them.
    相反地，在路上的援助來自中國而非美國，而且它不是給伊朗人民，而是給那個正在殺害並壓迫他們的政權。
  • pressure n. 壓力
    Trump still refuses to apply serious pressure on Beijing to halt its deadly material support for the Iranian regime despite Chinese President Xi Jinping’s (習近平) promise to him to refrain from doing so.
    儘管中國國家主席習近平曾向川普承諾會避免這樣做，川普仍拒絕向北京施加嚴厲壓力，以阻止其對伊朗政權提供致命的物資支援。
  • halt v. 停止、暫停
    Trump still refuses to apply serious pressure on Beijing to halt its deadly material support for the Iranian regime despite Chinese President Xi Jinping’s (習近平) promise to him to refrain from doing so.
    儘管中國國家主席習近平曾向川普承諾會避免這樣做，川普仍拒絕向北京施加嚴厲壓力，以阻止其對伊朗政權提供致命的物資支援。
  • lucrative adj. 賺錢的、獲利豐厚的
    For his part, Trump is more interested in pursuing a supposedly lucrative trade deal with China than in demanding that it stop providing Teheran with life support.
    就川普而言，他對追求一項與中國據稱利潤豐厚貿易協議的興趣，大於要求中國停止為德黑蘭提供維生支援。
  • deal n. 交易、協議
    For his part, Trump is more interested in pursuing a supposedly lucrative trade deal with China than in demanding that it stop providing Teheran with life support.
    就川普而言，他對追求一項與中國據稱利潤豐厚貿易協議的興趣，大於要求中國停止為德黑蘭提供維生支援。
  • jeopardizing v. 危及、損害（動詞ing型態）
    It is not the first time Xi has deceived a US president into believing his empty promises and jeopardizing Washington’s national security interest in the process.
    這並非習近平第一次欺騙美國總統相信其空洞的承諾，並在此過程中危及華盛頓的國家安全利益。
  • commitments n. 承諾、保證（複數形）
    However, even if Xi honors his word more faithfully than he has previous strategic commitments, there are two large loopholes in his pledge of restraint.
    然而，即便習近平比過去履行戰略 commitments 更忠實地遵守諾言，他的克制誓言中仍存在兩個巨大的漏洞。
  • pledge n. 誓言、保證
    However, even if Xi honors his word more faithfully than he has previous strategic commitments, there are two large loopholes in his pledge of restraint.
    然而，即便習近平比過去履行戰略承諾更忠實地遵守諾言，他的克制誓言中仍存在兩個巨大的 loopholes。
  • blockade n. 封鎖
    His second escape hatch, even while Trump remains in office, is that China, instead of openly attacking Taiwan, could impose a crippling blockade on it and put the onus for initiating hostilities on Trump, triggering yet a second war by the president who promised no more such overseas conflicts.
    他的第二個逃生口，即便在川普仍任職期間，就是中國可以不公開攻擊台灣，而是對其實施癱瘓性的 blockade，並將挑起敵對行動的責任推給川普...
  • personnel n. 人員、員工
    Would Trump risk US Navy ships and personnel to break the blockade?
    川普會冒著美國海軍艦艇和 personnel 的風險去突破封鎖嗎？
  • negotiations n. 談判、協商（複數形）
    Xi might be willing to bet that Trump would prefer to engage in protracted negotiations to demonstrate his vaunted deal-making skills, but what he would be trading away for the illusion of peace would be Taiwan’s national security and sovereignty.
    習近平可能會押注川普會更願意進行長期的 negotiations，以展示他吹噓的交易能力...
  • sovereignty n. 主權
    Xi might be willing to bet that Trump would prefer to engage in protracted negotiations to demonstrate his vaunted deal-making skills, but what he would be trading away for the illusion of peace would be Taiwan’s national security and sovereignty.
    ...但他為了和平的幻象而交易出去的將會是台灣的國家安全與 sovereignty。

文法重點

  • 限定與非限定關係子句 (Relative Clauses)
    Instead, the help that was on the way came from China, not from the US, and it went not to the Iranian people, but to the regime that was killing and otherwise oppressing them.
    句中的「that was on the way」是形容詞子句，用來修飾先行詞「the help」；而「that was killing and otherwise oppressing them」則用來修飾「the regime」。關係代名詞「that」在子句中擔任主詞，用以提供更多關於先行詞的細節。
  • 假設語氣條件句 (Conditional Sentences)
    However, even if Xi honors his word more faithfully than he has previous strategic commitments, there are two large loopholes in his pledge of restraint.
    「even if」引導讓步條件句（即使……），表達一種假設的情況。主句則使用現在式「there are」來陳述在這種假設情況下必然存在的客觀事實。
  • 複合關係代名詞引導的名詞子句 (What-clause)
    Xi might be willing to bet that Trump would prefer to engage in protracted negotiations to demonstrate his vaunted deal-making skills, but what he would be trading away for the illusion of peace would be Taiwan’s national security and sovereignty.
    句尾的「what he would be trading away for the illusion of peace」是由 what 引導的名詞子句，在整個對等子句中擔任主詞的角色，意思是「他為了追求和平幻象而將要交易掉的東西」。
  • 地方副詞倒裝與被動語態 (Inversion & Passive Voice)
    It is little wonder that despite Trump’s mystification, his renewed call last week for another attempt at an unarmed overthrow of the regime has so far gone unheeded.
    句中的「has so far gone unheeded」使用了現在完成式的被動語態概念（unheeded 作為主詞補語），表示該呼籲「迄今尚未受到理會、被忽略」。
