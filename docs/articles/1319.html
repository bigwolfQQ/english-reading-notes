十人因走私晶片與詐欺遭到起訴

Ten indicted for smuggling chips, fraud

單字解析

  • indict v. 控告，起訴
    The New Taipei District Prosecutors’ Office has indicted 10 people for allegedly using forged documents...
    新北地檢署已起訴 10 人，指控他們涉嫌使用偽造文件...
  • procure v. 採購，取得
    ...to procure controlled chips for military applications and smuggle them to China and Hong Kong.
    ...來採購軍用等級的管制晶片，並將其走私至中國與香港。
  • smuggle v. 走私
    They allegedly disguised the chips’ country of origin and their status as controlled products to smuggle them abroad...
    他們涉嫌掩飾晶片的產地及管制產品身分以將其走私海外...
  • fraud n. 詐欺，騙局
    The suspects were indicted on charges including aggravated fraud for potentially enhancing China’s military capabilities...
    嫌疑人被依加重詐欺等罪嫌起訴，因為這些行為可能助長中國軍事實力...
  • profit n. 利潤，盈利
    ...making about NT$360 million (US$11.33 million) in illicit profits, the indictment says.
    ...獲取約新台幣 3.6 億元（合 1,133 萬美元）的不法利益，起訴書指出。
  • import v. 進口
    ...ADWELL Technology Inc head Chen Chien-hsiung and other codefendants allegedly imported semiconductors from abroad using forged NCSIST documents...
    「優活創意科技」負責人陳建雄與其他 codefendants 涉嫌使用偽造的中科院文件從海外進口半導體...
  • export v. 出口
    ...while providing assurances that the products would not be exported or resold to entities subject to US sanctions...
    ...同時保證這些產品不會被出口或轉售給受美國制裁的實體...
  • restriction n. 限制
    ...so they would not be subject to export restrictions before selling them to entities in China and Hong Kong...
    ...以規避出口限制，隨後將其出售給中國和香港的實體...
  • transaction n. 交易
    Manufacturers and authorized distributors will refuse transactions involving overseas customers, including those in China, prosecutors said.
    檢方表示，製造商和授權經銷商將拒絕涉及海外客戶（包括中國客戶）的交易。
  • distributor n. 經銷商，分銷商
    Manufacturers and authorized distributors will refuse transactions involving overseas customers...
    製造商和授權經銷商將拒絕涉及海外客戶的交易...
  • penalty n. 懲罰，刑罰 (sentence)
    Prosecutors are seeking a 10-year prison sentence for Chen, eight years for Yuan and six years for Lai.
    檢方對陳建雄具體求刑 10 年、袁同台 8 年、賴金蘭 6 年。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後置修飾 (Present/Past Participle Phrases)
    The suspects were indicted on charges including aggravated fraud for potentially helping enhance China’s military capabilities...
    句中的「including aggravated fraud...」是由現在分詞 including 引導的分詞片語，用來補充說明前面 charges（罪名）包含的具體項目，相當於關代子句「which include...」的簡化。
  • 受詞補語 / 狀態形容詞片語 (Subject to...)
    ...while providing assurances that the products would not be exported or resold to entities subject to US sanctions, prosecutors said.
    「subject to...」是多益常考的高頻片語，在此作為形容詞片語修飾前面的 entities（實體/企業），意指「受...約束的、遭受...的」，在此表示「受美國制裁的」。
  • 對等連接詞連接多個分詞動作 (Coordination of Participles)
    Once in Taiwan, they repackaged and relabeled the chips as made in Taiwan and used other methods to disguise their origin...
    句中的 they 後方接了多個動詞的過去式（repackaged, relabeled, used）構成平行結構，描述主詞在晶片抵台後連續進行的商業造假動作。
  • 條件句與分詞構句的結合 (Conditional / Participial construction)
    Despite the national security implications, the illegal trafficking of semiconductors and advanced electronic equipment... does not currently contravene the National Security Act...
    「Despite + 名詞片語」用來表達讓步語氣（儘管...），連接句意上相對立的概念（雖有國安隱憂，但現行法律未必違法），是新聞英語中非常實用的句構。
