文法重點

  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況與補充說明
    The announcement came two days after South Korea and Japan said Pyongyang launched two short-range ballistic missiles into waters off its east coast, which the countries condemned as threats to regional peace and security.
    句中使用關係代名詞 which 引導非限定關係子句，修飾前面整起事件（發射飛彈），補充說明南韓與日本對此舉的立場與譴責，這在多益閱讀測驗的長篇報導題中非常常見。
  • 介系詞片語作主詞補語 (be of + Noun)
    “To acquire such a technology is of weighty significance in the state’s readiness for war and the future development of the armed forces”.
    句型「be of + 名詞」相當於該名詞的形容詞用法（在此 of weighty significance = weighty / very significant），用來強調主詞（取得此項技術）所具有的高度重要性。
  • 過去分詞作後位修飾 (Past Participle as Post-modifier)
    Last week, Pyongyang rejected a resolution adopted at the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) demanding it halt its nuclear activities...
