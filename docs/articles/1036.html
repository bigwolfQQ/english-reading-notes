文法重點

  • 現在分詞作分詞構句（表附帶結果或狀態）
    ...bringing their medals to nine, leaving them in 10th place overall as of yesterday evening, trailing Uzbekistan and leading Iran.
    句尾的 "bringing..."、"leaving..." 以及 "trailing... / leading..." 都是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前面子句所產生的結果或當下的狀態，省略了連接詞與主詞（原意約等於 and it brings..., and leaves them..., while they trail...）。
  • 關係代名詞非限定用法（補充說明）
    The 34-year-old Chien Hui-hsuan, who won bronze in the women’s individual kata event on Sunday, was joined by...
    句中的 ", who won bronze..." 是帶有逗號的關係代名詞（who）非限定子句，用來對前面的主詞 Chien Hui-hsuan 進行額外的補充說明。如果把這個子句拿掉，主句的文意依然完整。
  • 過去分詞作後置修飾語
    He lost 4-1 to Jordan’s Mohammad al-Jafari in the quarter-finals but remained in medal contention through repechage, which gives athletes defeated by finalists another route to a bronze-medal bout.
    句中的 "defeated by finalists" 是過去分詞片語，用來後置修飾前面的名詞 "athletes"（意即「被決賽選手擊敗的選手」），相當於關代子句 "who are defeated by finalists" 的簡化版。
  • 對等連接詞連接平行結構
    Starter Huang Chieh-han allowed one hit over four innings, striking out two without issuing a walk, while Yu Tsung-ju gave up one hit and struck out two in the fifth.
