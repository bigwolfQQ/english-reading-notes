日本對台灣藝人破壞國寶處以 50 萬日圓罰款

Japan fines Taiwanese entertainer 500,000 yen for national treasure vandalism

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • fine v. / n. 處以罰款；罰金
    Japan's Nara Summary Court on Wednesday fined a Taiwanese entertainer 500,000 yen (US$3,200) for vandalizing the Buddha's Footprint Stone during a visit to Yakushiji Temple in Nara Prefecture in April, according to Japanese national broadcaster NHK on Thursday.
    據日本國家廣播公司 NHK 週四報導，日本奈良簡易裁判所於週三對一名台灣藝人處以 50 萬日圓（約 3,200 美元）罰款，因她在 4 月參訪奈良縣藥師寺時破壞了佛足石。
  • suspect n. / v. 嫌疑犯；懷疑
    NHK reported that a 39-year-old Taiwanese event emcee who was arrested on suspicion of violating Japan's Law for the Protection of Cultural Properties has been released from police custody after paying a 500,000-yen fine following a summary indictment.
    NHK 報導指出，這名 39 歲的台灣活動主持人因涉嫌違反日本《文化財保護法》遭到逮捕，在支付 50 萬日圓罰款並接受簡易起訴後，已從警方拘留所獲釋。
  • custody n. 拘留，監禁；撫養權
    NHK reported that a 39-year-old Taiwanese event emcee who was arrested on suspicion of violating Japan's Law for the Protection of Cultural Properties has been released from police custody after paying a 500,000-yen fine following a summary indictment.
    NHK 報導指出，這名 39 歲的台灣活動主持人因涉嫌違反日本《文化財保護法》遭到逮捕，在支付 50 萬日圓罰款並接受簡易起訴後，已從警方拘留所獲釋。
  • procedure n. 程序，手續
    A summary indictment in Japan's legal system is a simplified criminal procedure for handling minor offenses through written documents rather than a court trial.
    日本法律體系中的簡易起訴，是一種透過書面文件處理輕微犯罪、而非透過法庭審判的簡化刑事程序。
  • conduct n. / v. 行為，舉止；實施，處理
    According to the NHK report, the Taiwanese woman told investigators she deeply regretted her conduct, explaining that she has the habit of sprinkling oil onto items that move her emotionally.
    根據 NHK 的報導，該名台灣女子向調查人員表示她對自己的行為深表後悔，並解釋說她有將油灑在令她情緒激動的物品上的習慣。
  • confirm v. 證實，確認
    Japan's Kyodo News also reported Thursday that the Nara District Public Prosecutors Office confirmed that a 39-year-old Taiwanese woman received a summary indictment and was fined 500,000 yen on Wednesday for staining the temple's hallowed Buddha's Footprint Stone with oil.
    日本共同社週四也報導稱，奈良地方檢察廳證實，一名 39 歲的台灣女性於週三因用油污損該寺廟神聖的佛足石，而收到簡易起訴書並被罰款 50 萬日圓。
  • involvement n. 參與，捲入
    Tsai addressed her involvement in the case on Thursday, posting her first public comments about the incident on Facebook in Chinese and Japanese.
    蔡宜臻於週四對她涉入此案一事做出回應，在臉書上以中日雙語發表了她對該事件的首次公開評論。
  • apologize v. 道歉，認錯
    Tsai said that her behavior was a result of ignorance and apologized to Japan and Yakushiji Temple, adding that there is no excuse for her lack of awareness of the customs and laws of other countries.
    蔡宜臻表示，她的行為是無知造成的，並向日本和藥師寺道歉，同時補充說，她對自己缺乏對其他國家習俗和法律的認知感到毫無藉口。
  • promise v. / n. 承諾，保證
    She then apologized to the Taiwanese public for setting a bad example and promised not to repeat her mistakes.
    她隨後向台灣大眾道歉，為自己做了不良示範致歉，並承諾不會重蹈覆轍。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    A summary indictment in Japan's legal system is a simplified criminal procedure for handling minor offenses through written documents rather than a court trial.
    句中的「simplified」是過去分詞，用來修飾名詞「criminal procedure」，相當於形容詞的功能，意思是「被簡化的刑事程序」。多益閱讀測驗常利用這種分詞做修飾來測試句構分析能力。
  • 分詞片語表示附帶狀況或結果 (Participial Phrase indicating result/accompaniment)
    Tsai said that her behavior was a result of ignorance and apologized to Japan and Yakushiji Temple, adding that there is no excuse for her lack of awareness of the customs and laws of other countries.
    句尾的「adding that...」是由分詞「adding」引導的分詞片語，用來補充說明前面的道歉動作所帶出的進一步言論，等於「and added that...」的簡化，能讓商業書信或新聞報導的語氣更為緊湊流暢。
  • 受詞補語與現在分詞 (Object Complement with Present Participle)
    She then apologized to the Taiwanese public for setting a bad example and promised not to repeat her mistakes.
    片語「set a bad example」中的「setting」是由介系詞「for」所接的動名詞（或分詞）。此外，「promise not to」展現了否定不定詞的用法，用來表達「承諾不做某事」，這是多益文法題（尤其是cloze test克漏字）常考的動詞搭配結構。
