歐洲商會理事長敦促軟體與能源產業應具備靈活性

ECCT chair urges agility in software and energy

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • potential n. 潛力，可能性
    Taiwan is known for its strength in hardware manufacturing, but new European Chamber of Commerce Taiwan (ECCT) chairman Frank Grunert sees considerable potential in software as well.
    台灣以其在硬體製造方面的實力聞名，但新任歐洲商會（ECCT）理事長葛納特（Frank Grunert）也看到了軟體領域相當大的潛力。
  • acquire v. 取得，獲得
    Siemens Taiwan vice president and head of communications Ray Jui (芮家楹) described Grunert as eager to acquire new knowledge and well versed in artificial intelligence and emerging technologies.
    台灣西門子副總裁兼通訊主管芮家楹形容葛納特渴望獲取新知識，並且精通人工智慧與新興技術。
  • capacity n. 容量，能力
    Grunert also advocated a more agile approach to electricity infrastructure projects, saying that Taiwan needs to expand power generation capacity and upgrade its grid to meet rapidly growing demand.
    葛納特還主張對電力基礎設施專案採取更靈活的方法，並表示台灣需要擴大發電容量並升級電網，以滿足快速成長的需求。
  • procurement n. 採購，籌辦
    Once authorities identify the need for a substation, power plant or grid upgrade, budgeting and procurement must precede construction, he said.
    他說，一旦當局確認有變電所、發電廠或電網升級的需求，預算編列和採購必須在施工前進行。
  • precede v. 在...之前，先於
    Once authorities identify the need for a substation, power plant or grid upgrade, budgeting and procurement must precede construction, he said.
    他說，一旦當局確認有變電所、發電廠或電網升級的需求，預算編列和採購必須在施工前進行。
  • implement v. 實施，執行
    He proposed dividing projects into smaller pieces and eliminating unnecessary administrative steps to accelerate implementation.
    他提議將專案拆分成較小的部分，並消除不必要的行政步驟以加速執行。
  • efficient adj. 有效率的
    Public procurement could become more efficient through digitization, he added.
    他補充說，透過數位化，政府採購可以變得更有效率。
  • submit v. 提交，呈遞
    Companies bidding for large contracts still have to print extensive documentation and submit multiple copies.
    參與投標大型合約的公司仍然必須列印大量的文檔並提交多份副本。
  • shortage n. 短缺，不足
    In his inaugural message to ECCT members, Grunert identified energy supply and costs, labor and talent shortages, and deeper European-Taiwanese economic ties as issues that would shape business conditions into the next decade.
    在其對歐洲商會會員的就職致詞中，葛納特指出，能源供應與成本、勞動力與人才短缺，以及更深化的歐台經濟關係，將是塑造未來十年商業環境的議題。
  • complement v. 補足，互補
    During the interview, Grunert said European equipment, chemicals and materials suppliers complement Taiwan’s semiconductor industry.
    在訪談中，葛納特表示歐洲的設備、化學品和材料供應商與台灣的半導體產業互補。
  • withdraw v. 撤回，退出
    On offshore wind, canceled projects, slower development and companies withdrawing from the market were thinning the pipeline of new projects, Grunert said.
    葛納特說，在離岸風電方面，取消的專案、較慢的開發以及退出市場的公司正在縮減新專案的儲備案源。
  • delay v. 延遲，延期
    Overlapping or conflicting responsibilities between central and local authorities could delay permits, he said.
    他說，中央與地方主管機關之間重疊或衝突的權責可能會延誤 permits（許可證）的發放。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    On offshore wind, canceled projects, slower development and companies withdrawing from the market were thinning the pipeline of new projects, Grunert said.
    句中的「withdrawing from the market」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 companies，相當於形容詞子句「companies that were withdrawing from the market」，在新聞英語中常用來精簡句子結構。
  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況或結果 (Participial Phrase indicating circumstance or result)
    Renewable power generation also increases resilience by reducing reliance on imported fuel, while battery storage helps balance fluctuations in wind and solar power, he said.
    句中的「by reducing...」是以介詞加上動名詞（reduction 的動名詞用法）形成的片語，用來表達「透過...方式」達成某事。此外，前面的「increasing」等分詞變化在多益閱讀中也經常作為考點出現。
  • 過去分詞作形容詞修飾名詞 (Past Participle as Adjective)
    He previously served as Siemens AG’s global head of grid control, a role involving software used to manage electricity networks.
