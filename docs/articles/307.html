鎧俠控股表示與 SK 海力士結盟不會發生

Kioxia Holdings says SK Hynix tie-up not happening

單字解析

  • stakeholder n. 利害關係人，股東
    Kioxia Holdings Corp’s top executive brushed aside the likelihood of deeper ties with rival and stakeholder SK Hynix Inc
    鎧俠控股公司的最高主管淡化了與競爭對手兼股東 SK 海力士公司建立更緊密聯繫的可能性
  • demand n. 需求
    while pledging to keep surging memory prices at bay to avoid denting long-term artificial intelligence (AI) demand.
    同時承諾將飆升的記憶體價格控制在合理範圍內，以避免削弱長期的人工智慧 (AI) 需求。
  • capacity n. 產能，容量
    Makers of memory chips are embarking on costly production capacity increases to meet soaring orders from AI service providers
    記憶體晶片製造商正著手進行成本高昂的產能擴充，以滿足人工智慧服務提供商激增的訂單
  • partnership n. 合夥關係，合作關係
    Investors including the chairman of SK Hynix’s parent, SK Inc, have said manufacturing partnerships might help lower the risk of huge capital commitments needed.
    包括 SK 海力士母公司 SK Inc 董事長在內的投資者曾表示，製造業合伙關係或許有助於降低所需龐大資本投入的風險。
  • commitments n. 承諾，投入資金
    Investors including the chairman of SK Hynix’s parent, SK Inc, have said manufacturing partnerships might help lower the risk of huge capital commitments needed.
    包括 SK 海力士母公司 SK Inc 董事長在內的投資者曾表示，製造業合伙關係或許有助於降低所需龐大資本投入的風險。
  • restrain v. 抑制，限制
    Kioxia would need to find other ways to restrain runaway memory prices, Ota said.
    廣田表示，鎧俠需要尋找其他方法來抑制失控的記憶體價格。
  • option n. 選項，選擇
    In an interview with the Asahi newspaper earlier this month, SK Inc chairman Chey Tae-won listed a manufacturing tie-up between Kioxia and SK Hynix as an option.
    在本月稍早接受《朝日新聞》採訪時，SK Inc 董事長崔泰源將鎧俠與 SK 海力士之間的製造結盟列為一個選項。
  • ongoing adj. 進行中的
    An SK Hynix representative said that Chey was speaking in general terms and that no discussions are ongoing.
    SK 海力士的一位代表表示，崔泰源是在泛泛而談，雙方目前並沒有進行任何討論。
  • shortage n. 短缺
    Chronic shortages of an array of memory chips have spurred Kioxia and Sandisk to together plan more than ￥5 trillion in spending
    記憶體晶片系列產品的長期短缺，促使鎧俠與新帝共同計畫斥資超過 5 兆日圓
  • revenue n. 收益（本篇雖用 margin，但延伸學習營收相關）
    Kioxia has been focusing its resources on the high-margin AI data center business
    鎧俠一直將資源集中在高利潤的人工智慧資料中心業務上
  • agreement n. 協議，合約
    Kioxia is getting close to achieving its goal to have 50 percent of its shipment volume covered by long-term agreements, he added.
    他補充說，鎧俠正接近實現其目標，即讓 50% 的出貨量由長期協議所涵蓋。
  • priority n. 優先事項，首要之務
    Ota did not rule out price hikes, but said Kioxia’s priority for now was on keeping chip prices at their current high levels.
    廣田並不排除調漲價格的可能性，但表示鎧俠目前的優先事項是將晶片價格維持在當前的高檔水準。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Phrase as modifier）作後置修飾
    Makers of memory chips are embarking on costly production capacity increases to meet soaring orders from AI service providers that are fueling double or even triple-digit price rises.
    句中的 soaring 和 fueling 都是現在分詞，用來修飾前方或帶領子句說明名詞（soaring orders 指「飆升的訂單」；fueling... 則用關係代間引導子句修飾 orders），在多益閱讀測驗中常見於考驗分詞的形容詞功能。
  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況
    Kioxia Holdings Corp’s top executive brushed aside the likelihood of deeper ties with rival and stakeholder SK Hynix Inc, while pledging to keep surging memory prices at bay...
    句尾的「while pledging...」是省略了主詞與be動詞的分詞構句（原意為 while he was pledging），用來補充說明主句動作同時發生的附帶狀態，這是多益測驗中常用來測試連接詞與分詞用法的句構。
  • 假設語氣（過去式表達與現在事實相反的假設）
    “We would end up hurting our own market and growth if we pushed prices up too much,” Ota said.
    此句使用了第二類假設語氣（If + 過去式動詞, 主詞 + would/could + 原形動詞），用來表達如果「現在」把價格推得太高（而實際上目前正試圖避免），「將會」造成的假設性結果。
  • 被動語態（Passive Voice）
    Kioxia is getting close to achieving its goal to have 50 percent of its shipment volume covered by long-term agreements, he added.
    句中的「covered by long-term agreements」使用了過去分詞當作被動形容詞，修飾 50 percent of its shipment volume，表示出貨量「被」長期合約所涵蓋，多益測驗常考被動語態與分詞搭配。
