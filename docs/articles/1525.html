南港車站為碳排放最大來源：台鐵公司

Nangang Station leading source of emissions: TRC

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • disclose v. 透露，公開，揭露
    Taipei Railway Corp (TRC) has disclosed its latest greenhouse gas emissions report, with Nangang Station — not Taipei Railway Station as many assumed — listed as its largest source of emissions.
    台北鐵路公司（TRC）已公布其最新溫室氣體排放報告，其中南港車站——而非許多人所認為的台北車站——被列為其最大的碳排放來源。
  • launch v. 發起，啟動，推出
    TRC launched its sustainability program in 2024, establishing a greenhouse gas inventory as the first step toward achieving net zero emissions, it said on Tuesday last week.
    該公司於上週二表示，TRC 於 2024 年啟動其永續計畫，建立溫室氣體盤查作為實現淨零排放的第一步。
  • conduct v. 執行，舉辦，進行
    The company conducted an initial inventory of 32 major stations, established an emissions management system and obtained carbon-footprint labels for three of its passenger services
    該公司對 32 個主要車站進行了初步盤查，建立了排放 management system，並為其三項旅客服務取得了碳足跡標籤
  • expand v. 擴大，擴展
    Last year, TRC expanded the inventory to cover all 613 operating sites, maintenance facilities, offices and ancillary facilities.
    去年，TRC 將盤查範圍擴大至所有 613 個營運據點、維修設施、辦公室及附屬設施。
  • account for phr. v. 佔（比例），說明，解釋
    Train operations accounted for 75.1 percent of its total carbon emissions, while station operations contributed 15.6 percent, Sustainability Development Section chief Peng Hsien-fu (彭賢富) said.
    永續發展科科長彭賢富表示，列車營運占其總碳排放量的 75.1%，而車站營運則占 15.6%。
  • facility n. 設施，設備，場所
    Among maintenance facilities, Fugang Maintenance Depot in Taoyuan had the highest emissions at 3,474.97tCO2e
    在維修設施中，桃園的富岡機廠碳排放量最高，達 3,474.97 公噸二氧化碳當量
  • introduce v. 引入，採用，介紹
    TRC is introducing more energy-efficient trains, regenerative braking systems, smart maintenance and digital management to reduce emissions, Peng said.
    彭賢富表示，TRC 正在引入更多節能列車、再生煞車系統、智慧維修及數位管理來減少碳排放。
  • integrate v. 整合，結合
    The company is also integrating operating and equipment data through its SOPHIA cloud platform to improve energy and equipment management, he said.
    他說，該公司也正透過其 SOPHIA 雲端平台整合營運與設備數據，以改善能源與設備管理。
  • issue v. 發行，發布，核發
    From 2019 to last year, TRC issued 55.36 million mobile tickets, reducing emissions by a cumulative 341tCO2e and saving NT$55.36 million
    從 2019 年到去年，TRC 發行了 5,536 萬張行動車票，累計減少 341 公噸二氧化碳當量的排放，並節省了新台幣 5,536 萬元
  • cumulative adj. 累積的，漸增的
    reducing emissions by a cumulative 341tCO2e and saving NT$55.36 million (US$1.73 million) in printing costs, Peng said.
    彭賢富表示，累計減少 341 公噸二氧化碳當量的排放，並節省了新台幣 5,536 萬元（約 173 萬美元）的印刷成本。
  • lease out phr. v. 出租
    It has leased out 73 sites for solar installations, with a combined capacity of 40.6 megawatts.
    該公司已出租 73 個場地用於安裝太陽能，總發電容量達 40.6 百萬瓦。
  • target n. 目標，標的
    The company has set a target of reducing its carbon emissions by 3 percent by 2028.
    該公司已設定目標，要在 2028 年前將碳排放量減少 3%。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞（Phrase as Adjective / 分詞後置修飾）
    Taipei Railway Corp (TRC) has disclosed its latest greenhouse gas emissions report, with Nangang Station — not Taipei Railway Station as many assumed — listed as its largest source of emissions.
    句尾的 "listed as its largest source of emissions" 是過去分詞片語，用來補充形容前面主詞補語所指的 Nangang Station（南港車站被列為...），省略了 "which is"，使句子更精鍊。
  • 現在分詞表達附帶結果（Adverbial Participle Clause）
    From 2019 to last year, TRC issued 55.36 million mobile tickets, reducing emissions by a cumulative 341tCO2e and saving NT$55.36 million...
    逗號後面的 ", reducing... and saving..." 是現在分詞引導的副詞子句，用來表示前面「發行行動車票」這個動作所帶來的自然結果（減少排放並節省成本），在多益閱讀與商務寫作中非常常見。
  • 對等連接詞 while 引導對比子句
    Train operations accounted for 75.1 percent of its total carbon emissions, while station operations contributed 15.6 percent...
    連接詞 "while" 在此處用來表示兩者之間的對比（「然而」或「而」），連接兩個完整的子句，表達列車營運與車站營運在碳排放佔比上的差異。
