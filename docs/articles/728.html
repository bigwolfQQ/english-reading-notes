野生動物保育人士呼籲制定政策應主動而非被動

Wildlife campaigners call for proactive over reactive policymaking

單字解析

  • incorporate v. 納入、結合
    Wildlife conservation groups on Tuesday urged the government to incorporate ecological considerations into public policymaking...
    野生動物保育團體週二敦促政府，在公共政策制定過程中應納入生態考量...
  • policy n. 政策、方針
    ...rather than responding only after environmental harm has occurred, ahead of the 2026 Walk for Wildlife to be held in Taipei on Saturday.
    ...而非僅在環境傷害發生後才做出回應，此呼籲是在2026年「為野生動物而走」大遊行於週六在台北舉行前夕發出的。
  • organize v. 組織、籌辦
    The march, organized by Taiwan Walk for Wildlife (TWW), will be held outside the Legislative Yuan...
    這場遊行由台灣為野生動物而走（TWW）組織籌辦，將於立法院外舉行...
  • schedule v. 預定、安排
    ...and is scheduled to begin at 2 p.m. Saturday under the theme 'Wildlife cannot wait, incorporate ecology into public policy.'
    ...並預定於週六下午2點開始，主題為「野生動物不能等，將生態納入公共政策」。
  • management n. 管理、經營
    ...builds on calls from the previous two editions in 2023 and 2024 for better management of stray dogs and cats...
    ...延續了2023年和2024年前兩屆遊行的訴求，要求更好地管理流浪貓狗...
  • independent adj. 獨立的
    ...but that different policies were operating independently, with ecological concerns only addressed after the damage had already been done...
    ...而是不同的政策各自獨立運作，生態問題往往是在損害已經造成後才得到處理...
  • invest v. 投資、投入
    Taiwan has invested substantial resources in researching the species...
    台灣已投入大量資源研究該物種...
  • impact n. 衝擊、影響
    ...without adequately taking their ecological impact into account.
    ...且未充分將其生態影響納入考量。
  • establishment n. 建立、設立
    Among TWW's demands for the march is the establishment of a biodiversity committee jointly by central and local governments...
    TWW針對此次遊行的訴求之一，是由中央與地方政府聯合成立生物多樣性委員會...
  • authority n. 權力、權威、官方
    ...with authority to coordinate across agencies and track implementation.
    ...賦予其跨部會協調及追蹤執行情形的權力。
  • implement v. 實施、執行
    ...with authority to coordinate across agencies and track implementation.
    ...賦予其跨部會協調及追蹤執行情形的權力。（此處為名詞 implementation）

    任何可能影響環境的政策都應接受審查...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    The march, organized by Taiwan Walk for Wildlife (TWW), will be held outside the Legislative Yuan...
    句中的「organized by Taiwan Walk for Wildlife (TWW)」是由過去分詞片語引導的修飾語，用來補充說明前面的主詞「The march」（這場遊行），相當於形容詞子句「which is organized by...」。在多益閱讀測驗中常考這種分詞省略主格與be動詞的簡化句型。
  • with + 受詞 + 過去分詞 (With-covenant / Absolute Phrase)
    ...with ecological concerns only addressed after the damage had already been done...
    此處使用「with + 名詞 (ecological concerns) + 過去分詞 (addressed)」的複合結構，表示附帶狀況，意指「在...的情況下/隨著...」。這在多益商業英文中常用於描述同時發生的狀態或條件。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    ...along the west coast -- the dolphins' only habitat -- without adequately taking their ecological impact into account.
    雖然此處使用破折號補充說明，但文章中也常見形如「that could affect the environment」的關係子句。關係子句用來修飾前面的先行詞（如 Any policy），在多益文法題中常考關代選擇（如 who/which/that 的區分）。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Any policy that could affect the environment should be subject to review...
    句中的「should be subject to review」以及前段的「ecological concerns only addressed...」展現了新聞英文中常用的被動語態。在報導政策、規定、或商業流程時，被動語態常被用來強調「動作的承受者」或保持客觀語氣。
