華航與維珍航空簽署代號共享協議

CAL, Virgin Atlantic ink codeshare pact

單字解析

  • agreement n. 協議，協定
    China Airlines (CAL) yesterday announced that it has signed a codeshare agreement with fellow SkyTeam member Virgin Atlantic Airways to facilitate travel for passengers connecting through Europe.
    中華航空（CAL）昨天宣佈，已與同為天合聯盟成員的維珍航空簽署代號共享協議，以便利透過歐洲轉機旅客的旅行。
  • facilitate v. 促進，使便利

  • partnership n. 夥伴關係，合作關係
    The partnership is particularly useful, because it offers more convenient connections and could help attract more European passengers to Taiwan, Chen said.
    陳漢銘說，這種合作關係特別有用，因為它提供了更便利的轉機服務，並有助於吸引更多歐洲旅客前往台灣。
  • capacity n. 產能，容量，能力
    “We recognize India as a significant and rapidly growing passenger and cargo market. However, the airline is constrained by its current fleet capacity,” Chen said, adding that new aircraft deliveries over the next two years would provide additional capacity.
    陳漢銘說：「我們認可印度是一個重要且快速成長的客運和貨運市場。然而，該航空公司的現有機隊產能受到限制。」並補充說，未來兩年內新飛機的交付將提供額外的產能。
  • prioritize v. 優先處理，給予優先權
    “Until then, we intend to prioritize strengthening existing routes rather than immediately opening numerous new destinations,” he said.
    他說：「在此之前，我們打算優先鞏固現有航線，而不是立即開闢眾多新航點。」
  • demand n. 需求
    China Airlines said it expects the air freight market to remain strong in the fourth quarter, with cargo capacity already tight and demand being driven by semiconductors, artificial intelligence-related equipment, consumer electronics and other high-value goods.
    華航表示，預期第四季航空貨運市場將保持強勁，目前貨運產能已經吃緊，需求主要由半導體、人工智慧相關設備、消費性電子產品和其他 high-value 貨物所帶動。
  • utilization n. 利用，使用
    The airline said it is maximizing the value and utilization of its limited freighter fleet while also making use of belly cargo capacity on passenger flights.
    該航空公司表示，正在最大化其有限貨機機隊的價值與利用率，同時也充分利用客機的機腹載貨空間。
  • target n. 目標
    With the fourth quarter the peak season for air freight, China Airlines has set a target of more than NT$8 billion (US$250.88 million) in monthly cargo revenue.
    隨著第四季為航空貨運旺季，華航設定了每月貨運營收超過新台幣 80 億元（約合 2.5088 億美元）的目標。
  • revenue n. 收益，營收

文法重點

  • 分詞作後置修飾（Present Participle as Postmodifier）
    The airline made the announcement at a ceremony marking the 15th anniversary of it joining the SkyTeam.
    句中的「marking the 15th anniversary...」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 ceremony（典禮）。這相當於形容詞子句「that marked the 15th anniversary...」，分詞片語能讓句子更精簡，是多益閱讀測驗常見的句構。
  • With + 名詞 + 分詞／形容詞（附帶狀況）
    China Airlines said it expects the air freight market to remain strong in the fourth quarter, with cargo capacity already tight and demand being driven by semiconductors...
    「With + O. + 分詞/形容詞」結構用來表示附帶狀況或背景。在此句中，「with cargo capacity already tight」表示「在貨運產能已經吃緊的情況下」，「and demand being driven by...」則表示「且需求正受到...驅動」。這是多益商業英文中經常用來描述市場現況的高級句型。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞與受詞
    “Until then, we intend to prioritize strengthening existing routes rather than immediately opening numerous new destinations,” he said.
    動詞 prioritize 後方接了動名詞「strengthening existing routes」作為受詞，用來表達「優先進行加強現有航線」這項動作。動名詞在商業英文中經常作為動詞或介系詞的受詞，表達具體的商業行為與計畫。
