Opening of Shengji Tunnel cuts travel time: ministry

單字解析

  • access road n. 聯外道路、進出道路
    A new tunnel connecting Zhongxiao E Road with an access road to the National Biotechnology Research Park in Nangang District opened yesterday...
    連接忠孝東路與南港區國家生技研究園區聯外道路的一條新隧道於昨天開通...
  • significantly adv. 顯著地、重大地
    ...significantly reducing travel time, the Ministry of the Interior said in a statement.
    ...顯著減少了行車時間，內政部在一份聲明中表示。
  • ease v. 緩解、減輕（交通壅塞、負擔等）
    ...which is expected to ease traffic congestion during peak hours on Zhongxiao E Road Sec 7 and Academia Road...
    ...預期該隧道能緩解忠孝東路七段與研究院路在尖峰時段的交通壅塞...
  • congestion n. 擁塞、堵塞
    ...which is expected to ease traffic congestion during peak hours on Zhongxiao E Road Sec 7 and Academia Road...
    ...預期該隧道能緩解忠孝東路七段與研究院路在尖峰時段的交通壅塞...
  • peak hours n. phr. 尖峰時段、上下班高峰期
    ...which is expected to ease traffic congestion during peak hours on Zhongxiao E Road Sec 7 and Academia Road...
    ...預期該隧道能緩解忠孝東路七段與研究院路在尖峰時段的交通壅塞...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Phrase as Modifier / 分詞片語作後位修飾）
    A new tunnel connecting Zhongxiao E Road with an access road to the National Biotechnology Research Park in Nangang District opened yesterday...
    句中的「connecting...」是由現在分詞引導的片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「A new tunnel（一條新隧道）」，相當於形容詞子句「that connects...」。這種結構在新聞英語中非常常見，能讓句子更精簡。
  • 非限定關代子句（Non-defining Relative Clause）
    A ceremony was held yesterday morning for the opening of the Shengji Tunnel, which is expected to ease traffic congestion during peak hours...
    逗號後面接的「which is expected to...」是非限定關代子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「Shengji Tunnel」。非限定子句通常用逗號隔開，如果拿掉該子句，主要子句的意思依然完整。
  • 對等連接詞引導的同時動作（while 表同時發生）
    ...while also cutting travel time between Nangang and New Taipei City’s Sijhih District, Deputy Minister of the Interior Wu Tang-an said in the statement.
    「while also cutting...」中的 while 表示「同時、一邊……」，用來連接另一個伴隨發生的動作，使句子表達兩個同時進行的結果，結構流暢且具備商業或新聞報導常用的精準度。
  • 條件句省略與介系詞片語（if 子句表達假設狀況）
    ...the tunnel provides an alternative route if Academia Road is closed due to flooding or other emergencies, he said...
    「if Academia Road is closed due to flooding...」是一個由 if 引導的條件副詞子句，用來假設當道路因天災淹水封閉時會發生的狀況，說明該替代隧道的實用功能。
