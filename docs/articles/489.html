專訪：人工智慧推動銀行超越傳統處理流程

INTERVIEW: AI pushes banks beyond traditional processing

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • treasury management n. phr. 財務管理、資金調度
    Artificial intelligence (AI), real-time payments and digital assets are pushing corporate treasury management beyond traditional transaction processing...
    人工智慧（AI）、即時支付與數位資產正在推動企業財務管理超越傳統的交易處理...
  • liquidity n. 流動性、現金流
    ...giving companies faster visibility over cash and liquidity while enabling more automated and programmable payments, Standard Chartered Bank said.
    渣打銀行表示，這使企業能更快掌握現金與流動性，同時實現更多自動化與可編程的支付。
  • regulatory framework n. phr. 監管框架、法規架構
    The shift is creating new opportunities for Taiwan as it builds a regulatory framework for digital assets...
    隨著台灣建立數位資產的監管框架，這項轉變正為台灣創造新的機遇...
  • custodian n. 保管人、託管機構
    “The rise of digital assets and tokenized securities is reshaping traditional custodians from passive asset holders into trusted providers of digital market infrastructure,” Mengal said...
    孟格爾表示：「數位資產與代幣化證券的興起，正在將傳統的保管機構從被動的資產持有者，重塑為值得信賴的數位市場基礎設施提供者。」
  • resilience n. 應變能力、韌性
    These technologies are allowing companies to extract more value from payment data while making resilience a competitive advantage, the bank said.
    該銀行表示，這些技術正讓企業從支付數據中提取更多價值，同時將應變能力轉化為競爭優勢。
  • integration n. 整合、結合
    Their integration with real-time payments and new financial infrastructure could further accelerate the move toward more automated and connected financial operations, he said.
    他說，數位資產與即時支付及新金融基礎設施的整合，可以進一步加速朝向更自動化、更互聯的金融營運發展。
  • settlement n. 結算、清算
    The next priority should be regulatory clarity for tokenized securities and stablecoins, including rules governing ownership, custody, settlement, taxation and accounting, Mengal said.
    孟格爾表示，下一個優先事項應該是為代幣化證券和穩定幣提供明確的監管規定，包括管理所有權、保管、結算、稅務和會計的規則。
  • issuance n. 發行
    Taiwan’s established capital-market infrastructure could provide a strong foundation for banks, custodians and securities firms to support the issuance, custody and settlement of tokenized assets, he said.
    他指出，台灣成熟的資本市場基礎設施，能為銀行、保管機構和證券商提供強大基礎，以支援代幣化資產的發行、保管與結算。
  • cross-border adj. 跨境的
    The market could also explore the use of stablecoins and tokenized deposits for cross-border payments, securities settlement and corporate treasury management, Mengal said.
    孟格爾表示，市場也可以探索將穩定幣和代幣化存款應用於跨境支付、證券結算和企業財務管理。
  • capability n. 能力、功能
    Standard Chartered has expanded its digital-asset capabilities in the past few years, covering custody, trading, tokenization and stablecoins, he said.
    他說，過去幾年渣打銀行已擴展了其數位資產能力，涵蓋保管、交易、代幣化與穩定幣。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作附帶狀況（分詞構句）
    ...giving companies faster visibility over cash and liquidity while enabling more automated and programmable payments, Standard Chartered Bank said.
    句尾的「giving...」是現在分詞片語引導的分詞構句，表示伴隨主要子句（Artificial intelligence... are pushing...）而自然發生的結果或動作，翻譯時常譯為「同時……」。
  • 過去分詞作後位修飾語
    The shift is creating new opportunities for Taiwan as it builds a regulatory framework for digital assets, with clearer rules for tokenized securities and stablecoins needed to support the development of the market...
    句中的「needed to support...」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞片語「clearer rules」，省略了「which are」，意思是「（被）需要的規則來支援……」。
  • 不定詞作目的或結果狀語
    The next priority should be regulatory clarity for tokenized securities and stablecoins, including rules governing ownership, custody, settlement, taxation and accounting, Mengal said.
    句中的「governing ownership...」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「rules」，相當於形容詞子句「which govern ownership...」，用以限定說明這些規則所涵蓋的範圍。
  • 否定與對比結構（Rather than）
    Rather than being limited to risk management, resilience is increasingly becoming a strategic advantage as businesses seek to respond more quickly to changes in liquidity, payments and operating conditions, he said.
    「Rather than + V-ing」意為「而不是……」，在此句中用來做強烈的對比，說明應變能力不再侷限於傳統的風險管理，而是轉變為積極的戰略優勢。
