文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    The two Japanese lawmakers made the remarks at a seminar on Japan-Taiwan security cooperation organized by the Taipei-based Indo-Pacific Strategy Thinktank.
    句中的 'organized by the Taipei-based Indo-Pacific Strategy Thinktank' 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 'seminar'，相當於形容詞子句 'which was organized by...'，在新聞英語中非常常見，能有效精簡句子結構。
  • 條件句與假設語氣 (Conditional / Subjunctive)
    Should the three countries form a steadfast and strong alliance, together we can face any form of challenge, he said.
    此句為省略 'if' 的倒裝假設語氣條件句。原句等於 'If the three countries should form...'。將助動詞 'Should' 移至主詞前方以省略 'if'，常用於正式談話或書面英語中，帶有較強的假設與強調意味。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    ...Taiwan, Japan and all countries in the First Island Chain should no longer be regarded as isolated points of defense...
    句中的 'should no longer be regarded as' 採用了被動語態（助動詞 + be + 過去分詞），強調主詞（台灣、日本及第一島鏈國家）是「被」視為或看待的對象，焦點在於客觀的狀態或動作的承受者。
