法國影評人助台灣新電影走向世界：部長

French film critic helped bring Taiwan New Cinema to world: Minister

單字解析

  • seminar n. 研討會
    Taiwan's Minister of Culture Li Yuan (李遠) said Saturday at a seminar in Paris that a landmark article written in the 1980s by French filmmaker and critic Olivier Assayas played a pivotal role...
    台灣文化部長李遠週六在巴黎的一場研討會上表示，法國電影製片兼影評人奧利維耶·阿薩亞斯在 1980 年代所寫的一篇具有里程碑意義的文章，扮演了關鍵角色...
  • pivotal adj. 關鍵的、核心的
    ...played a pivotal role in introducing Taiwan New Cinema to the Western world at a critical moment.
    ...在關鍵時刻對於將台灣新電影引介給西方世界扮演了關鍵角色。
  • retrospective n. 回顧展、回顧
    The event, part of a retrospective on Taiwan New Cinema, a film movement that emerged in the Republic of China (Taiwan) in the 1980s, featured Assayas and French film critic Jean-Michel Frodon.
    這場活動是關於 1980 年代在中華民國（台灣）興起的電影運動「台灣新電影」回顧展的一部分，邀請了阿薩亞斯與法國影評人尚-米歇爾·弗洛東出席。
  • emerge v. 興起、出現
    The event, part of a retrospective on Taiwan New Cinema, a film movement that emerged in the Republic of China (Taiwan) in the 1980s...
    這場活動是關於 1980 年代在中華民國（台灣）興起的電影運動「台灣新電影」回顧展的一部分...
  • influential adj. 有影響力的
    After returning to France, Assayas published an influential article titled 'In the Margin of World Cinema,' introducing a new generation of Taiwanese filmmakers to European audiences.
    回到法國後，阿薩亞斯發表了一篇深具影響力的文章，題為〈世界電影的邊緣〉，向歐洲觀眾介紹了新一代的台灣電影工作者。
  • recognize v. 認可、承認、表彰
    If it hadn't been for that far-reaching article, Taiwan New Cinema might not have survived, Li noted, adding that Assayas' writing helped pave the way for Taiwanese films to gain recognition at major European film festivals.
    李遠指出，如果不是那篇影響深遠的文章，台灣新電影可能無法存活，並補充說阿薩亞斯的文章為台灣電影在歐洲各大影展獲得認可鋪平了道路。
  • recommendation n. 推薦、建議
    Assayas said few international film enthusiasts knew about Taiwanese cinema at the time, but a recommendation from film critic Chen Kuo-fu (陳國富) prompted him to visit Taiwan...
    阿薩亞斯說，當時很少有國際電影愛好者知道台灣電影，但在影評人陳國富的推薦下，促使他造訪台灣...
  • prompt v. 促使、激勵
    ...a recommendation from film critic Chen Kuo-fu (陳國富) prompted him to visit Taiwan, where he met filmmakers including Edward Yang...
    ...但在影評人陳國富的推薦下，促使他造訪台灣，並在那裡結識了包括楊德昌在內的電影人...
  • profound adj. 深遠的、深刻的
    Assayas said he was deeply impressed at the time, adding that Taiwan New Cinema went on to have a profound impact not only on Chinese-language cinema but also on world cinema.
    阿薩亞斯表示，他當時留下深刻印象，並補充說台灣新電影不僅對華語電影，對世界電影也產生了深遠的影響。
  • colleague n. 同事、同僚
    While working at Central Motion Picture Corp. in the 1980s, Li noted that he and his colleagues sought to reexamine Taiwan through film and introduce Taiwanese cinema to the world.
    李遠指出，在 1980 年代於中央電影公司工作期間，他與同事們試圖透過電影重新審視台灣，並將台灣電影推向世界。
  • contemporary adj. 當代的、現代的
    The legacy of Taiwan New Cinema continues today, Li added, with younger directors carrying its spirit while bringing fresh perspectives to history and contemporary life.
    李遠補充說，台灣新電影的傳承延續至今，年輕一代的導演承襲了這種精神，同時為歷史和當代生活帶來了新鮮的視角。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Taiwan's Minister of Culture Li Yuan (李遠) said Saturday at a seminar in Paris that a landmark article written in the 1980s by French filmmaker and critic Olivier Assayas played a pivotal role...
    句中的 "written in the 1980s by French filmmaker..." 是一個過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "a landmark article"。這相當於一個省略了關代與be動詞的形容詞子句（...which was written in the 1980s...），在英文新聞寫作中非常常見，能讓句子更為簡潔流暢。
  • 與過去事實相反的假設語氣 (Subjunctive Mood for Past Events)
    If it hadn't been for that far-reaching article, Taiwan New Cinema might not have survived, Li noted...
    此句使用了 "If it hadn't been for..."（若非……/ 要不是……）的句型，表達與過去事實相反的假設語氣。其主要子句（Main clause）則使用 "might not have survived"（可能無法存活），用來假設過去若沒有該篇文章，台灣新電影可能會有的不同結果。
  • 現在分詞片語表示附帶狀況 (Present Participle Phrase indicating Accompaniment)
    The legacy of Taiwan New Cinema continues today, Li added, with younger directors carrying its spirit while bringing fresh perspectives to history and contemporary life.
    句尾的 "with younger directors carrying its spirit..." 使用了 "with + 名詞 + 現在分詞 (V-ing)" 的複合結構，用來補充說明主句發生時的附帶狀況或背景。在這裡用來描繪「年輕一代導演承襲精神，同時帶來新視角」的畫面。
  • 對等連接詞連接的多重動詞片語 (Compound Verb Phrases with Coordinating Conjunctions)
    While working at Central Motion Picture Corp. in the 1980s, Li noted that he and his colleagues sought to reexamine Taiwan through film and introduce Taiwanese cinema to the world.
    在此句中，that 子句的主詞是 "he and his colleagues"，其後接了兩個由對等連接詞 and 連接的不定詞片語作為動詞 sought 的受詞補語：一是 "to reexamine Taiwan through film"，二是 "introduce Taiwanese cinema to the world"（第二個 introduce 前省略了 to），展現了結構的平行對稱。
