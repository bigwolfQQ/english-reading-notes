台灣人口連續 32 個月下滑

Taiwan's population declines for 32nd consecutive month

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • decline v. 下降，減少
    Taiwan's population declined for the 32nd consecutive month in August, with births totaling 7,492, government data showed Thursday.
    根據政府週四公布的數據，台灣 8 月人口連續第 32 個月下滑，出生人數總計為 7,492 人。
  • consecutive adj. 連續的，連貫的
    Taiwan's population declined for the 32nd consecutive month in August, with births totaling 7,492, government data showed Thursday.
    根據政府週四公布的數據，台灣 8 月人口連續第 32 個月下滑，出生人數總計為 7,492 人。
  • total v. 總計，共計
    Taiwan's population declined for the 32nd consecutive month in August, with births totaling 7,492, government data showed Thursday.
    根據政府週四公布的數據，台灣 8 月人口連續第 32 個月下滑，出生人數總計為 7,492 人。
  • statistics n. 統計數據，統計學
    Household registration statistics released by the Ministry of the Interior showed that Taiwan's population stood at 23,224,721 at the end of August...
    內政部發布的戶口統計數據顯示，截至 8 月底，台灣人口為 23,224,721 人...
  • release v. 發布，公開，釋放
    Household registration statistics released by the Ministry of the Interior showed that Taiwan's population stood at 23,224,721 at the end of August...
    內政部發布的戶口統計數據顯示，截至 8 月底，台灣人口為 23,224,721 人...
  • previous adj. 先前的，以前的
    ...down 103,622 from a year earlier and 10,281 from the previous month.
    ...較去年同期減少 103,622 人，較前一個月減少 10,281 人。
  • record v. 記錄，記載
    A total of 7,492 births were recorded in August, 972 fewer than in the same month last year and 414 fewer than in July.
    8 月總共記錄了 7,492 名新生兒，比去年同期少 972 人，比 7 月少 414 人。
  • comprise v. 包含，組成
    Lienchiang County, which comprises the Matsu Islands, recorded the highest rate at 6.05 per 1,000 people...
    包含馬祖列島的連江縣記錄了最高比率，為每千人 6.05 人...
  • therefore adv. 因此，所以
    Taiwan therefore recorded a natural population decrease of 8,978 in August.
    因此，台灣在 8 月記錄了 8,978 人的自然人口減少。
  • account for phr. 占（比例），說明，解釋
    ...meaning that people aged 65 and older account for at least 20 percent of the population.
    ...意指 65 歲及以上的人口占總人口的至少 20%。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（補充說明伴隨狀況）
    Taiwan's population declined for the 32nd consecutive month in August, with births totaling 7,492, government data showed Thursday.
    句中的 "with births totaling 7,492" 是「with + 名詞 + 現在分詞」的獨立分詞構句結構，用來補充說明主句動作發生時的伴隨狀況或結果，在新聞英語中非常常見。
  • 過去分詞作後位修飾語
    Household registration statistics released by the Ministry of the Interior showed that...
    句中的 "released by the Ministry of the Interior" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "Household registration statistics"（戶口統計數據），相當於形容詞子句 "which were released by..." 的簡化版。
  • 非限定關代子句（補充說明前行詞）
    Lienchiang County, which comprises the Matsu Islands, recorded the highest rate at 6.05 per 1,000 people...
    逗點後的 "which comprises the Matsu Islands" 為非限定關係代名詞子句，對主詞 "Lienchiang County" 進行補充說明，若將其省略，主句的完整性與主要意思仍不受影響。
  • 現在分詞引導的結果或狀態補充
    Taiwan became a super-aged society last year, meaning that people aged 65 and older account for at least 20 percent of the population.
    逗點後的 "meaning that..." 是現在分詞片語作結果狀語，表示前面整件事所產生的含意或結果，等於 "which means that..."。
