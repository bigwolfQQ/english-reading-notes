美國防專家將參與台灣會議與兵棋推演，進行聯合評估計畫

US defense experts to participate in meetings, war games in Taiwan joint assessment program

單字解析

  • participate v. 參與，參加
    US defense liaisons are to take part in 130 meetings and map-based war games in Taiwan under an expanded joint assessment program, the Ministry of National Defense said in a report.
    國防部在一份報告中表示，根據一項擴大的聯合評估計畫，美國國防聯絡官將在台灣參與 130 場會議及地圖兵棋推演。
  • station v. 配置，駐扎，派駐
    Sources have previously said that the Pentagon would station personnel at the ministry under a joint program that is to run from this year through 2028.
    消息人士先前曾表示，五角大廈將根據一項自今年起實施至 2028 年的聯合計畫，派員常駐國防部。
  • involve v. 涉及，包含
    That program would involve officials from the Office of the Undersecretary of Defense for Policy and researchers from the Institute for Defense Analyses, a US nonprofit research organization that conducts national security studies, the ministry report said.
    國防部報告指出，該計畫將涉及美國國防部政策次長辦公室的官員，以及進行國家安全研究的美國非營利研究機構「國防分析研究所」的研究人員。
  • assign v. 指派，分配
    Each organization would assign one representative to work at the ministry’s headquarters in Taiwan.
    每個組織將指派一名代表在台灣的國防部本部工作。
  • conduct v. 執行，舉辦，導電
    The Office of the Undersecretary of Defense for Policy advises US defense leaders on national security and defense policy, while the Institute for Defense Analyses conducts federally funded research and analysis on national security and science policy.
    美國國防部政策次長辦公室就國家安全與國防政策向美國國防領導階層提出建議，而國防分析研究所則針對國家安全與科學政策進行由聯邦資助的研究與分析。
  • allocate v. 撥出，分配（經費、資源）
    The ministry has allocated funding for the initiative since 2022.
    國防部自 2022 年起已為該倡議編列經費。
  • commission v. 委任，委託
    Under the arrangement, the Office of the Undersecretary of Defense for Policy commissions the Institute for Defense Analyses to coordinate experts from the US military and research organizations, including the US Indo-Pacific Command, the Defense Intelligence Agency and the National Ground Intelligence Center.
    根據此安排，國防部政策次長辦公室委託國防分析研究所協調來自美軍及研究組織的專家，其中包括美國印太司令部、國防情報局以及陸軍地面情報中心。
  • coordinate v. 協調，統籌
    Under the arrangement, the Office of the Undersecretary of Defense for Policy commissions the Institute for Defense Analyses to coordinate experts from the US military and research organizations, including the US Indo-Pacific Command, the Defense Intelligence Agency and the National Ground Intelligence Center.
    根據此安排，國防部政策次長辦公室委託國防分析研究所協調來自美軍及研究組織的專家，其中包括美國印太司令部、國防情報局以及陸軍地面情報中心。
  • assess v. 評估，評價
    The US liaisons work with the ministry, General Staff Headquarters and the armed services to assess defense and operational issues and provide policy recommendations.
    美國聯絡官與國防部、參謀本部及各軍種合作，評估防衛與作戰議題，並提供政策建議。
  • recommendation n. 建議，推薦
    The US liaisons work with the ministry, General Staff Headquarters and the armed services to assess defense and operational issues and provide policy recommendations.
    美國聯絡官與國防部、參謀本部及各軍種合作，評估防衛與作戰議題，並提供政策建議。
  • incorporate v. 納入，結合
    Findings from the program’s first three years have been incorporated into the annual Han Kuang military exercises and training assessments conducted by each service, the ministry said.
    國防部表示，該計畫前三年的研究結果已被納入年度漢光演習以及各軍種執行的訓練評估中。
  • frequency n. 頻率，發生次數
    Beginning this year, the US is expanding the scope and depth of the assessments and increasing their frequency, the report said.
    報告指出，從今年開始，美國正在擴大這些評估的範疇與深度，並增加其頻率。
  • budget v. 編列預算，預算
    The ministry said it has budgeted NT$471 million (US$14.82 million) over three years for the program.
    國防部表示，已為該計畫編列了三年 4.71 億新台幣（約 1,482 萬美元）的預算。

文法重點

  • be to V (表預定計畫或安排)
    US defense liaisons are to take part in 130 meetings and map-based war games in Taiwan under an expanded joint assessment program, the Ministry of National Defense said in a report.
    「be + to V」常用於新聞英語中，表示官方或正式的預定計畫、安排（相當於 is/are scheduled to）。
  • 過去分詞片語當後位修飾語 (A past participle phrase modifying a noun)
    Findings from the program’s first three years have been incorporated into the annual Han Kuang military exercises and training assessments conducted by each service, the ministry said.
    句中的「conducted by each service」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「training assessments」，相當於「that are conducted by each service」的縮寫，省略了主格關代與be動詞。
  • 現在分詞片語表附帶結果或動作 (Present participle phrase)
    The meetings would bring US experts together with Taiwan’s joint staff, service headquarters and frontline units for discussions, on-site inspections and map-based war games, as well as qualitative and quantitative assessments using simulation models, it said.
    句尾的「using simulation models」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的「assessments」，表示這些評估是「使用」模擬模型的，帶有主動意味。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The NT$152.5 million allocated for next year would cover an expanded research team, the 130 meetings and the costs of stationing the two US liaisons in Taiwan.
    「allocated for next year」是以過去分詞當作後位修飾語的被動概念，完整結構為「which was allocated for next year」，說明經費是被「編列/分配」給明年的。
