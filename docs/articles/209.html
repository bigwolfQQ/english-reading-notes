太空人在短暫重返大聯盟後，將鄧愷威下放小聯盟

Astros option Teng Kai-wei back to minors after brief majors return

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • strengthen v. 加強，鞏固
    Teng was initially recalled to the majors on Aug. 21 to help strengthen the Astros' bullpen.
    鄧愷威最初是在8月21日被召回大聯盟，以協助強化太空人隊的牛棚戰力。
  • allow v. 允許，在此處指失分、失守
    The Sunday result saw Teng allow six runs in his last three games with the team in the majors...
    周日的結果使得鄧愷威在最近三場大聯盟比賽中共失了六分...
  • announce v. 宣布，宣告
    ...prompting the Astros to announce that the Taiwanese pitcher will be optioned back to the Astros' Triple-A affiliate...
    ...促使太空人隊宣布將這位台灣投手下放至太空人隊的三A附屬球隊...
  • affiliate n. 附屬機構，分公司，（體育）附屬球隊
    ...prompting the Astros to announce that the Taiwanese pitcher will be optioned back to the Astros' Triple-A affiliate, the Sugar Land Space Cowboys.
    ...促使太空人隊宣布將這位台灣投手下放至太空人隊的三A附屬球隊糖城太空牛仔隊。
  • record n. 記錄，戰績
    ...posting a 5-7 record and a 4.76 ERA over 73.2 innings.
    ...在73.2局的投球中繳出5勝7敗的戰績以及4.76的防禦率。
  • active adj. 現役的，活躍的
    ...the only active Taiwanese player in the MLB is Detroit Tigers' 23-year-old Taiwanese infielder Lee Hao-yu.
    ...目前大聯盟中唯一的現役台灣球員是底特律老虎隊23歲的台灣內野手李灝宇。
  • peak n. 頂峰，高峰
    ...down from .378 at the peak of his hot streak.
    ...低於其火燙狀態巔峰時期的0.378。
  • receive v. 收到，得到
    ...though he did receive walks in both.
    儘管他在這兩場比賽中都獲得了保送。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附加說明 (Present Participle Phrase for Additional Information)
    ...prompting the Astros to announce that the Taiwanese pitcher will be optioned back to the Astros' Triple-A affiliate, the Sugar Land Space Cowboys.
    句中的「prompting the Astros to...」是由現在分詞 prompting 引導的片語，用來補充說明前面整句（鄧愷威近期失分過多）所產生的結果或帶來的影響，相當於連續句子的簡化。
  • 分詞構句表伴隨狀況 (Participle Clause of Concomitant Circumstance)
    With Teng and infielder Cheng Tsung-che of the Boston Red Sox back in Triple-A, the only active Taiwanese player in the MLB is Detroit Tigers' 23-year-old Taiwanese infielder Lee Hao-yu.
    此句使用「With + 名詞 + 形容詞片語/副詞」的結構（With Teng and infielder... back in Triple-A），用來表達在某種狀況同時存在的情形下，主句所發生的事情。
  • 關代所有格所有子句 (Relative Clause with Whose/Of which)
    On Sunday, Teng pitched a total of 0.1 innings, in which he struck out one batter but gave up two walks and allowed a run.
    「in which」引導一個非限定的關係代間子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞 innings（局數），代表「在那幾局之中」發生了什麼事。
