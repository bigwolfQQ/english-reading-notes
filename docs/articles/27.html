文法重點

  • 分詞片語作為後置修飾語 (Present/Past Participle Phrases as Post-modifiers)
    Nvidia Corp agreed to acquire artificial intelligence (AI) start-up Hugging Face in a transaction valued at about US$13 billion...
    句中的 "valued at about US$13 billion" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "a transaction"（省略了 "which was"）。這種結構在新聞英文中極為常見，能精煉地補充說明名詞的狀態或細節。
  • 關係代間子句 (Relative Clauses)
    ...protect open-source AI developers, who rely on Hugging Face to share software and data that can be freely used by anyone.
    此句包含兩個關係子句。「, who rely on...」是非限定關係子句，對前面的人形/開發者補充說明；「that can be freely used...」則是限定關係子句，用來修飾「software and data」。
  • 獨立分詞構句與附加說明 (Participial Phrase with 'adding')
    ...in a transaction valued at about US$13 billion, adding a popular software platform to the chip giant’s AI empire.
    句尾的 ", adding..." 是由現在分詞 "adding" 引導的分詞構句，主詞是前面整件事（收購案）。用來表達該行為所帶來的自然結果或附加的動作，使句子更具流暢性與動態感。
