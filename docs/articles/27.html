文法重點

  • 分詞構句作後置修飾（形容詞功能）
    Nvidia Corp agreed to acquire artificial intelligence (AI) start-up Hugging Face in a transaction valued at about US$13 billion...
    句中的「valued at about US$13 billion」是由過去分詞片語構成的後置修飾語，用來修飾前面的名詞「transaction」。其完整概念相當於關代子句「which is valued at about US$13 billion」，在多益閱讀測驗中非常常出現，能有效精簡句子結構。
  • 關係代名詞子句（限定修飾）
    ...for Hugging Face employees who join Nvidia, according to a statement on Thursday.
    「who join Nvidia」是由關係代名詞 who 引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾先行詞「Hugging Face employees」。在多益測驗中，常用來測驗先行詞為人時關係代名詞應使用 who 或是受格 whom。
  • 現在分詞短語表附帶結果或補充說明
    ...adding a popular software platform to the chip giant’s AI empire.
    句尾的「adding a popular software platform...」是現在分詞（-ing）開頭的片語，用來補充說明前面整句收購行動所帶來的結果或伴隨的動作，相當於對等子句「and it adds a popular software platform...」。
  • 虛受格與不定詞片語
    Chief executive officer Jensen Huang has emerged as a strong advocate for open-source AI models as a means of keeping the technology broadly available and preventing it from becoming concentrated in the hands of a few massive players.
    句中的「keeping the technology broadly available and preventing it from...」使用了「keep + 受格 + 形容詞」以及「prevent + 受格 + from + V-ing」的句型。這在多益文法題中（如 Part 5）常被拿來考動詞後的受詞補語型態或是介系詞搭配。
