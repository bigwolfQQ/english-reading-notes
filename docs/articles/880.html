文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Present Participle）作後位修飾
    The document, released Tuesday, identified Taiwan as a like-minded partner that has begun developing principles and frameworks for safeguarding information integrity based on democratic values...
    句中的「safeguarding information integrity」是由現在分詞 safeguarding 引導的分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名词 frameworks（原則與架構），表達「用來維護資訊完整性的」之意。
  • 現在完成進行式（Present Perfect Continuous Tense）
    Taiwan is "a leading partner in terms of situational awareness" and has been organizing civil defense and resilience exercises to counter China's increasing gray-zone warfare tactics...
    「has been organizing」採用了現在完成進行式，強調動作從過去某一時間開始，持續進行到現在，且可能還會持續下去，用來描述臺灣持續舉辦民防與韌性演習的狀態。
  • 分詞片語作附加說明（伴隨狀況）
    The ministry noted that the EUDS Special Committee visited Taiwan in July 2025 on its first trip to Asia, with committee chair Nathalie Loiseau leading the delegation in meetings with President Lai Ching-te...
    此處使用了「with + 名詞 + 現在分詞 (leading)」的複合結構，用來補充說明代表團訪臺時的伴隨狀況，意指「在委員會主席洛瓦索的帶領下」與總統會面。
