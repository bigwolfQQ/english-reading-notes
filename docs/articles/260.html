鄧愷威短暫登大聯盟後重返小聯盟

Teng Kai-wei back to minors after brief spell

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • strengthen v. 加強，鞏固
    Teng was initially recalled to the majors on Aug. 21 to help strengthen the Astros’ bullpen.
    鄧愷威最初是在 8 月 21 日被召回大聯盟，以協助強化太空人隊的牛棚戰力。
  • result n. 結果，成績
    The Sunday result saw Teng allow six runs in his last three games with the team in the majors...
    這場星期日的比賽結果使得鄧愷威在最近三場大聯盟比賽中共失了六分...
  • announce v. 宣布，宣告
    ...prompting the Astros to announce that the Taiwanese pitcher will be optioned back to the Astros’ Triple-A affiliate...
    ...促使太空人隊宣布將這位台灣投手下放回太空人隊的三A附屬球隊...
  • affiliate n. 附屬機構，分公司，（棒球）農場球隊
    ...prompting the Astros to announce that the Taiwanese pitcher will be optioned back to the Astros’ Triple-A affiliate, the Sugar Land Space Cowboys.
    ...促使太空人隊宣布將這位台灣投手下放回太空人隊的三A附屬球隊：糖城太空牛仔隊。
  • record n. 紀錄，戰績
    ...posting a 5-7 record and a 4.76 ERA over 73.2 innings.
    ...繳出 5 勝 7 敗的戰績以及 4.76 的防禦率（ERA）。
  • active adj. 活躍的，現役的
    ...the only active Taiwanese player in the MLB is Detroit Tigers’ 23-year-old Taiwanese infielder Lee Hao-yu.
    ...目前大聯盟中唯一活躍的台灣球員是底特律老虎隊 23 歲的台灣內野手李灝宇。
  • peak n. 頂峰，最高點
    Following the Sunday game, Lee’s batting average fell to around .260, down from .378 at the peak of his hot streak.
    在星期日的比賽之後，李灝宇的打擊率跌至大約 .260，從火燙手感顛峰時期的 .378 下滑。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（現在分詞表結果）
    The Sunday result saw Teng allow six runs in his last three games with the team in the majors, prompting the Astros to announce that the Taiwanese pitcher will be optioned back...
    句尾的「prompting...」為現在分詞構句，用來補充說明前面整句狀況所導致的自然結果（促使太空人隊宣布...），相當於「and this prompted the Astros to announce...」。
  • 關係副詞 where 引用的地方子句（在此處以 in which 代替）
    On Sunday, Teng pitched a total of 0.1 innings, in which he struck out one batter but gave up two walks and allowed a run.
    「in which」等於「where」，用來修飾前面的先行詞「0.1 innings」（局數在此可視為抽象的範圍或情況），補充說明他在這局投球中的具體表現。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞 / 動詞片語搭配
    The eyes of Taiwanese baseball fans were on Lee in July as he went on a 12-game hitting streak, which ended at the end of the month.
    「went on a ... streak」是實用的英文片語，意指「展開／進行了一連串……的活動」；後方接「which」引導的非限定關係子句，補充說明該安打串何時結束。
