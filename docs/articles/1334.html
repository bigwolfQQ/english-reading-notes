台中市以 QR 碼升級智慧停車系統

Taichung upgrades smart parking with QR codes

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • upgrade v. 升級，改善
    Taichung is upgrading its smart parking system, installing high-visibility QR codes at parking spots so people can pay online...
    台中市正在升級其智慧停車系統，在停車格安裝高辨識度的 QR 碼，方便民眾線上繳費...
  • transaction n. 交易，業務
    ...keeping transactions simple and reducing paper waste.
    ...使交易簡便並減少紙張浪費。
  • convenient adj. 方便的，便利的
    To make paying online more convenient, it installed 50cm by 60cm QR codes on surfaces along the Art Museum Parkway area near the museum.
    為了讓線上繳費更便利，交通局在美術館附近的美術園道一帶路面設置了 50 公分乘以 60 公分的 QR 碼。
  • demand n. 需求，要求
    Smart parking was first introduced at the theater and the museum because of the high demand for parking, bureau Director Yeh Chao-fu (葉昭甫) said.
    交通局長葉昭甫表示，由於停車需求高，智慧停車率先在歌劇院和美術館推出。
  • detect v. 偵測，查出
    The system detects when vehicles enter and exit to assess parking fees...
    該系統能偵測車輛進出以計算停車費...
  • assess v. 評估，估算（此處指計算費用）
    The system detects when vehicles enter and exit to assess parking fees, with payments made online...
    該系統能偵測車輛進出以計算停車費並進行線上繳費...
  • staffing n. 人事，員工配置
    ...making parking easier to manage while reducing paper use and staffing costs from parking attendants...
    ...讓停車管理更輕鬆，同時減少紙張使用以及停車開單人員的人事成本...
  • implement v. 實施，執行
    The bureau implemented the QR codes so people can scan them and pay on the spot...
    交通局實施 QR 碼是為了讓民眾可以隨掃隨付...
  • on the spot phr. 當場，立即
    The bureau implemented the QR codes so people can scan them and pay on the spot...
    交通局實施 QR 碼是為了讓民眾可以隨掃隨付...
  • display n. 顯示牌，展覽
    ...and adding that 50 QR code displays have been installed so far.
    ...並補充說明目前已安裝了 50 個 QR 碼顯示牌。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附帶結果或狀態的補充說明 (V-ing)
    Taichung is upgrading its smart parking system, installing high-visibility QR codes at parking spots so people can pay online, keeping transactions simple and reducing paper waste.
    句中的 "installing..." 與 "keeping..." 和 "reducing..." 均為現在分詞作分詞構句，用來補充說明主句動作所產生的連續結果或伴隨狀況，翻譯時常譯為「並...」或「同時...」，在多益閱讀測驗中常考用來連接子句與結果。
  • with + 名詞 + 分詞（獨立分詞構句）
    The system detects when vehicles enter and exit to assess parking fees, with payments made online, making parking easier to manage while reducing paper use and staffing costs from parking attendants, Yeh said.
    "with payments made online" 是一個常見的 "with + O + p.p." 結構，用來表示附帶的狀態。在此處，payments（付款）與 make（進行）之間是被動關係，故使用過去分詞 made。
  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾
    ...adding that 50 QR code displays have been installed so far.
    句中的 "adding that..." 是分詞片語，用來修飾前面主句的動作，補充說明說話者的額外言論，相當於 "and he added that..." 的簡化用法。
