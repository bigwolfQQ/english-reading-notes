台鐵將於車上補票新增行動支付

Taiwan Railway to add mobile payment for onboard fare adjustments

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • fare adjustment n.片語 補票
    Taiwan Railway Corp. (TR) will introduce mobile payment for fare adjustments made onboard trains starting Thursday
    台鐵公司將於週四起，在火車車上補票時引入行動支付。
  • onboard adj. / adv. 在車上、在機上、在船上
    Taiwan Railway Corp. (TR) will introduce mobile payment for fare adjustments made onboard trains starting Thursday
    台鐵公司將於週四起，在火車車上補票時引入行動支付。
  • operator n. 營運商、經營者
    allowing passengers to pay by digital wallet in addition to cash, the railway operator said Tuesday.
    鐵路營運商週二表示，這將允許旅客除了現金之外，還能透過數位錢包付款。
  • in addition to prep.片語 除了……之外（還有）
    allowing passengers to pay by digital wallet in addition to cash, the railway operator said Tuesday.
    鐵路營運商週二表示，這將允許旅客除了現金之外，還能透過數位錢包付款。
  • complaint n. 抱怨、投訴
    The move follows complaints last year from a passenger who had used a credit card to buy a regular train ticket
    此舉源於去年一名旅客的抱怨，該旅客曾用信用卡購買一般火車票
  • additional adj. 額外的、附加的
    but was required to pay an additional amount in cash after boarding a Tze-Chiang Limited Express train.
    但在登上自強號列車後，被要求以現金支付額外款項。
  • inconvenience n. 不便、麻煩
    The passenger said the lack of payment options caused inconvenience.
    該旅客表示，缺乏付款方式造成了不便。
  • accept v. 接受
    Since March 2, TR stations have accepted credit cards and mobile payments for fare adjustments, in addition to cash.
    自3月2日起，台鐵車站除了現金外，已接受信用卡和行動支付進行補票。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後置修飾語 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Taiwan Railway Corp. (TR) will introduce mobile payment for fare adjustments made onboard trains starting Thursday
    句中的「made onboard trains」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「fare adjustments（補票）」，相當於完整子句「which are made onboard trains」。這種結構在商業及新聞英文中非常常見，能有效精簡句子。
  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense)
    The move follows complaints last year from a passenger who had used a credit card to buy a regular train ticket
    句中的「had used」使用過去完成式（had + 過去分詞），用來表達「在過去某個時間點（去年抱怨）之前」已經完成的動作（用信用卡買票），突顯動作發生的先後順序。
  • 對等連接詞與附加片語 (In addition to)
    allowing passengers to pay by digital wallet in addition to cash, the railway operator said Tuesday.
    「in addition to」是一個常考的多益片語介詞，意指「除了……之外（還包含）」，後方通常接名詞或動名詞（在此接了名詞 cash），用來擴充資訊。
