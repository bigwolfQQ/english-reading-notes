川習會後美國對台政策未變：外交部次長

U.S. Taiwan policy unchanged after Trump-Xi summit: Deputy FM

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • summit n. 高峰會、高層會議
    Taiwan's deputy foreign minister said Sunday that the Trump-Xi summit resulted in no change to U.S. policy toward Taiwan
    台灣的外交部副部長週日表示，儘管北京方面極力推動華府明確反對「台獨」，但川習會並未導致美國對台政策發生變化。
  • result in phr. v. 導致、結果為
    Taiwan's deputy foreign minister said Sunday that the Trump-Xi summit resulted in no change to U.S. policy toward Taiwan
    台灣的外交部副部長週日表示，儘管北京方面極力推動華府明確反對「台獨」，但川習會並未導致美國對台政策發生變化。
  • explicitly adv. 明確地、明白地
    despite Beijing's efforts to push Washington toward explicitly opposing 'Taiwan independence.'
    儘管北京方面極力推動華府明確反對「台獨」。
  • state visit n. phr. 國是訪問
    during the Chinese leader's Sept. 23-25 state visit to Washington, D.C.
    當時習近平正於9月23日至25日對華盛頓特區進行國是訪問。
  • in context phr. 在語境中、結合上下文
    Deputy Foreign Minister Chen Ming-chi said Trump's comments should be understood 'in context.'
    外交部政務次長陳明祺表示，川普的言論應「結合上下文」來理解。
  • cite v. 引述、引用
    Chen cited U.S. Ambassador to China David Perdue, who told CNBC on Friday that there had been 'no change' in U.S. policy toward Taiwan
    陳明祺引述美國駐中國大使大衛·普度週五接受CNBC採訪時的說法，普度在會談後表示美國對台政策「沒有改變」。
  • channel n. 管道、途徑
    'We have also received repeated confirmation from our American friends through various channels that U.S. policy toward Taiwan remains unchanged'
    「我們也透過各種管道收到美國友人不斷的確認，美國對台政策維持不變。」
  • assurances n. 保證、承諾
    Chen said, without identifying who had conveyed those assurances.
    陳明祺說，但他沒有透露是誰傳達了這些保證。
  • pending adj. 待定的、未決的、即將發生的
    Chen also addressed a pending US$14 billion arms package for Taiwan.
    陳明祺也談到了外界高度關注、目前尚待批准的對台140億美元軍售包裹案。
  • commitment n. 承諾、投入、奉獻
    and Taipei's commitment to increasing defense investment amid continued Chinese military pressure.
    以及台北在面對中國持續軍事壓力下增加國防投資的承諾。
  • approve v. 批准、核准、贊成
    Asked when the Trump administration might approve the package
    當被問及川普政府何時可能批准該軍售案時
  • weigh against phr. v. 權衡、斟酌（與……相比）
    Rubio said arms sales had to be weighed against several considerations
    魯比歐表示，軍售必須權衡多項考量
  • consistent with adj. phr. 與……一致
    It said this was 'consistent with information we had obtained beforehand and with broader expectations.'
    聲明指出，這「與我們事前獲得的資訊以及更廣泛的預期一致」。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Speaking to reporters ahead of a seminar in Taipei on Sunday, Deputy Foreign Minister Chen Ming-chi said Trump's comments should be understood 'in context.'
    句首的「Speaking to reporters...」是由現在分詞片語引導的修飾語，用來補充說明主詞 Deputy Foreign Minister Chen Ming-chi 在發言時的動作，等同於時間或原因副詞子句（While he was speaking to reporters...）。在新聞英文中非常常見，能讓句子更簡潔。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Chen also addressed a pending US$14 billion arms package for Taiwan.
    單字 pending（源自動詞 pend）在此作為形容詞用，修飾後方的名詞 package。而在第14句開頭的「Asked when...」則是省略了主詞與be動詞（When he was asked...）的過去分詞片語，用被動語態表達「當（他）被問到……時」。
  • 名詞子句作受詞 (Noun Clause as Object)
    He pointed to repeated statements by U.S. officials that Washington's policy toward Taiwan had not changed.
    句中的「that Washington's policy toward Taiwan had not changed」是一個由 that 引導的名詞子句，在此作為同位語，用來補充說明前面 statements（聲明）的具體內容。
  • 關係代間詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    Chen cited U.S. Ambassador to China David Perdue, who told CNBC on Friday that there had been 'no change' in U.S. policy toward Taiwan following the talks.
    句中的「who told CNBC on Friday...」是非限定關係代間詞子句（帶有逗號），用來補充說明先行詞 David Perdue 的身分與其說過的話。在多益閱讀測驗中常考這種關係子句來測試主動詞一致性與連接詞用法。
