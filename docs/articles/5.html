單字解析

  • clout n. （政治、經濟或社會上的）影響力、權力
    Taiwan will intensify its initiative to become an Asian asset management center as it seeks to turn its industrial strength into financial clout and enhance the nation’s competitiveness, President William Lai (賴清德) said yesterday.
  • initiative n. 倡議、新方案、積極行動
    Taiwan will intensify its initiative to become an Asian asset management center as it seeks to turn its industrial strength into financial clout and enhance the nation’s competitiveness, President William Lai (賴清德) said yesterday.
  • assets under management n. phr. 資產管理規模（簡稱 AUM）
    The government’s asset management initiative has already produced results, with the financial sector’s assets under management (AUM) reaching NT$46 trillion as of June, about NT$14 trillion higher than before the plan was launched in 2024.
  • attributable to adj. phr. 歸因於…的、可歸咎於…的
    He did not mention a specific government measure that led to AUM growth, but it could be attributable to soaring share prices as investors chase the artificial intelligence (AI) wave.
  • skyrocket v. 飛漲、急劇上升
    The TAIEX skyrocketed 59.2 percent in the first six months of this year, spearheaded by Ai-related tech stocks.
  • spearhead v. 帶頭、擔任先鋒、率領
    The TAIEX skyrocketed 59.2 percent in the first six months of this year, spearheaded by Ai-related tech stocks.
  • contract chipmaking n. phr. 晶片代工（製造）

文法重點

  • 現在分詞短語作時間/背景狀語（Participle Clause）
    Speaking at a financial development forum in Taipei, Lai said the government would continue adjusting and relaxing financial regulations to give financial institutions greater room to develop the asset management business, but he did not provide any details.
    句首的「Speaking at...」是由「When/As he spoke at...」簡化而來的分詞構句。因為主句主詞（Lai）與動詞 speak 為主動關係，故省略連接詞與主詞，並將動詞改為現在分詞 Speaking，用來交代主句動作發生時的情境或時間。
  • With + 受詞 + 分詞（獨立分詞複合結構）
    The government’s asset management initiative has already produced results, with the financial sector’s assets under management (AUM) reaching NT$46 trillion as of June, about NT$14 trillion higher than before the plan was launched in 2024.
    此句使用「with + 受詞 (the financial sector's AUM) + 分詞 (reaching...)」結構，用來為前面的主句（計畫已見成效）補充說明具體的數據事實與狀況。由於資產規模與 reaching（達到）為主動關係，故採用現在分詞 reaching。
  • 過去分詞短語做後位修飾/補充說明（Past Participle Clause）
    The TAIEX skyrocketed 59.2 percent in the first six months of this year, spearheaded by Ai-related tech stocks.
    「spearheaded by...」為過去分詞短語，簡化自「which was spearheaded by...」。因為 TAIEX（加權指數）與 spearhead（帶頭/領軍）之間是被動關係，故使用過去分詞短語，補充說明股市飆漲是由 AI 科技股所引領的。
  • 同位語與限定關係子句的複合結構
    Shares of Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co (台積電), the world’s largest contract chipmaking company that accounts for more than 40 percent of the local stock market’s value, had risen 306 percent as of the end of June from the beginning of 2024, backed by the AI boom.
    句中的「the world's largest contract chipmaking company」是台積電的同位語，用來補充其身份；同位語之後緊接著「that accounts for...」關係子句修飾該同位語。全句主詞為 Shares，主要動詞為 had risen，句尾再接過去分詞短語 backed by... 說明原因，形成精密的修飾鏈。
  • 動詞不定詞與對等連接詞的平行結構（Parallel Structure with Infinitives）
    Taiwan should build the board into an Asian fundraising platform for start-ups and attract more international companies to list in Taiwan, with the aim of making it an “Asian NASDAQ,” he said.
    在情態助動詞 should 之後，連接了兩個原形動詞片語「build...」與「attract...」，中間以對等連接詞 and 連接，構成平行結構（Taiwan should build... and [should] attract...），使語氣順暢且結構對稱。
