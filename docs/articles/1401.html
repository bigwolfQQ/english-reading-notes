Smoking could worsen facial wrinkles: HPA

文法重點

  • 倍數比較句型 (be + 倍數 + more likely to ... / times greater than ...)
    Women aged 40 or older who smoke are 3.1 times more likely to develop moderate-to-severe facial wrinkles compared with women who have never smoked...
    表示倍數時，常用「數字 + times + 形容詞比較級 (或 more) + 原級形容詞 + than...」或「數字 + times as + 形容詞原級 + as...」的結構。此句中「3.1 times more likely」即用來精確表達「高出3.1倍的機率」。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present/Past Participle as Postmodifier)
    A study was conducted with 299 never-smokers, 551 former smokers and 286 current smokers, aged between 30 and 69, drawn from a health maintenance organization.
    句中的「aged between 30 and 69」與「drawn from a health maintenance organization」皆為過去分詞片語，用來修飾前方的人數清單與受試者。這種結構省略了「who were」，能讓句子更為簡潔緊湊，是英文新聞與商業寫作中常見的句構。
  • 獨立複合結構 / 附帶狀況 (With + 名詞 + 過去分詞)
    The agency said the study concluded that with age, average sun exposure and body mass index controlled, the risk of moderate-to-severe wrinkling for current smokers compared with never-smokers was 2.3 times greater among men...
    「with + 受詞 + 過去分詞 (controlled)」構成獨立片語，用來表示附帶狀況或條件，意指「在……受到控制／的情況下」。這在學術研究或財經分析報導中經常用於描述「在控制特定變數後」的統計結果。
  • 附加子句表達補充說明 (adding that ...)
    WHO research estimates 37 million children aged 13 to 15 use tobacco globally, the agency said, adding that the tobacco industry uses many tactics to target young customers...
    「adding that ...」是由現在分詞「adding」引導的補充子句，用來接續前文並補充說明官員或機構所說的額外內容。這種寫法在新聞報導中非常頻繁，能流暢地串接同一發言者的多段談話。
  • 愈…, 愈… 句型 (The + 比較級 ..., the + 比較級 ...)
    ...the agency said, adding that the earlier one is exposed to nicotine, the higher the risk of addiction and continued use.
    「the earlier one is exposed..., the higher the risk...」使用了多益與英文考試極為常考的「the + 比較級 ..., the + 比較級 ...」句型，用來表示「越早接觸尼古丁，成癮風險就越高」的相對遞進關係。
