國安會顧問針對新規定呼籲謹慎前往中國旅遊

NSC adviser urges caution on travel to China amid new rules

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • adviser n. 顧問
    Alex Huang (黃重諺), an adviser to the Presidential Office's National Security Council (NSC), renewed warnings Friday against traveling to China...
    總統府國家安全會議（NSC）顧問黃重諺週五再度發出警告，提醒民眾避免前往中國……
  • authorities n. 官方，當局
    Huang identified civil servants, members of religious groups, and engineers in sensitive technology firms as the most likely targets of Chinese authorities...
    黃重諺指出，公務員、宗教團體成員以及敏感科技公司的工程師，是中國當局最可能的目標……
  • logistics n. 後勤，物流
    He said Beijing is seeking a wide range of information, from the locations of civil defense shelters and critical supply stockpiles to logistics routes managed by grassroots officials...
    他表示，北京正在尋求廣泛的資訊，從民防避難所的位置、關鍵物資庫存，到基層官員管理的後勤路線……
  • affiliated adj. 附屬的，有關聯的
    There have also been cases of Taiwanese affiliated with religious groups being detained in China while traveling as tourists...
    也有台灣宗教團體相關人員在中國旅遊時遭到拘留的案例……
  • detain v. 扣留，拘留
    There have also been cases of Taiwanese affiliated with religious groups being detained in China while traveling as tourists rather than taking part in religious activities...
    也有台灣宗教團體相關人員在中國旅遊而非參與宗教活動時，在中國遭到拘留的案例……
  • discretion n. 裁量權，斟酌決定權
    ...Taiwan's Mainland Affairs Council (MAC) warned that Beijing's vaguely defined security rules give authorities broad discretion over exit and entry controls.
    ……台灣的大陸委員會（陸委會）警告稱，北京模糊定義的安全規則賦予了當局對出入境管制廣泛的裁量權。
  • violation n. 違反，違規
    ...or indefinitely for technology and export violations.
    ……或因技術與出口違規而無限期禁止。
  • deny v. 拒絕，否認
    They would also allow authorities to deny entry to foreign nationals who submit false applications...
    這些規定也將允許當局拒絕提交虛假申請的外國國民入境……
  • draft v. 草擬，起草
    According to Huang, Taiwan's government is drafting travel guidelines to help citizens reduce risks and protect themselves while in China.
    據黃重諺透露，台灣政府正在擬定旅遊指引，以協助公民在中國期間降低風險並保護自己。
  • employee n. 員工
    ...but pointed to foreign companies that require employees traveling to China to carry burner phones instead of their personal devices.
    ……但指出外國企業會要求前往中國出差的員工攜帶預付免洗手機，而不是他們的個人設備。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Alex Huang (黃重諺), an adviser to the Presidential Office's National Security Council (NSC), renewed warnings Friday against traveling to China ahead of new entry-exit regulations set to take effect there next Tuesday.
    句中的「set to take effect there next Tuesday」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「regulations (規定)」，相當於形容詞子句「which are set to take effect...」，省略了「which are」使句子更簡鍊。
  • 動名詞片語作受詞 (Gerund Phrase as Object)
    ...renewed warnings Friday against traveling to China ahead of new entry-exit regulations...
    介系詞「against」後面接動名詞「traveling」形成動名詞片語「traveling to China」，用以表達反對或警告的具體內容。在多益考試中，介系詞後面務必接名詞或動名詞（V-ing）。
  • 現在分詞表主動與同時發生的動作 (Present Participle)
    Citing examples, Huang said the government is aware of cases in which police officers were questioned about their duties and critical infrastructure...
    句首的「Citing examples」是由現在分詞「Citing」引導的分詞構句，主動修飾主詞 Huang，表示黃顧問在「舉例同時」也說明了接下來的事。這在商業與新聞英語中非常常見，能有效精簡語句。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause with in which)
    ...the government is aware of cases in which police officers were questioned about their duties and critical infrastructure...
    「in which」引導一個限制性關係子句，用來補充修飾先行詞「cases」。介系詞「in」原本放在 questioned 之後（in cases），移至關係代名詞「which」前方，展現正式且道地的英文書寫風格。
