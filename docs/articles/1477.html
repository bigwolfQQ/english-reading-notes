印尼釋放八隻從火災中獲救的紅毛猩猩

Indonesia releases eight orangutans rescued from fires

單字解析

  • authorities n. 當局，官方
    Eight orangutans rescued in over the past weeks from blazes that tore through Borneo Island have been released into a protected forest, Indonesian authorities and rescuers said yesterday.
    印尼當局與救援人員昨天表示，過去幾週從席捲婆羅洲島的大火中救出的八隻紅毛猩猩已被野放到一座保護林中。
  • statement n. 聲明，陳述
    The apes were saved from several locations razed by fires — mostly plantations near their forest homes — in the West Kalimantan Province on the Indonesian side of Borneo last month, the Indonesian Ministry of Forestry and the YIARI wildlife rehabilitation foundation said in a statement.
    印尼林業部與 YIARI 野生動物復育基金會在一份聲明中表示，這些類人猿是上個月在印尼屬婆羅洲西加里曼丹省，從幾個遭大火夷平的地點（大多是其森林家園附近的種植園）被救出的。
  • relocate v. 重新安置，遷移
    The orangutans, three mom and baby pairs as well as two male teenagers, were relocated on Friday to the Batu Nyambu Protected Forest in West Kalimantan, a 23,000-hectare reserve with primary and secondary forest cover.
    這些紅毛猩猩包括三對母子與兩隻雄性青少年，已於週五被轉移到西加里曼丹的巴圖尼安布保護林，這是一座擁有原始林與次生林覆蓋、占地 23,000 公頃的保護區。
  • priority n. 優先事項，首要之務
    “Protection of habitats will always be a top priority to ensure orangutans continue to have safe forests to live in, but as long as their habitat remains threatened, rescue is one of the best options we can take to help them survive,” Sanchez said.
    桑切斯表示：「保護棲息地將永遠是首要任務，以確保紅毛猩猩繼續擁有安全的森林可以棲息，但只要牠們的棲息地仍然受到威脅，救援就是我們能採取幫助牠們生存的最佳選擇之一。」
  • threatened adj. 受到威脅的
    “Protection of habitats will always be a top priority to ensure orangutans continue to have safe forests to live in, but as long as their habitat remains threatened, rescue is one of the best options we can take to help them survive,” Sanchez said.
    桑切斯表示：「保護棲息地將永遠是首要任務，以確保紅毛猩猩繼續擁有安全的森林可以棲息，但只要牠們的棲息地仍然受到威脅，救援就是我們能採取幫助牠們生存的最佳選擇之一。」

    YIARI 營運總監阿吉托埃·蘭廷表示，選擇該保護區是因為當地有食物和水源供應，且與其他森林相連。
  • operational adj. 營運的，運作的

    Indonesia regularly battles wildfires during the dry season, but the fires have intensified this year because of the El Nino, set to be one of the strongest since comparable records began four decades ago.
    印尼在乾季經常與野火搏鬥，但今年的火災因為聖嬰現象而加劇，該現象將成為四十年來有可比紀錄以來最強的一次之一。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Eight orangutans rescued in over the past weeks from blazes that tore through Borneo Island have been released into a protected forest...
    句中的 "rescued in over the past weeks from blazes..." 是一個過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "Eight orangutans"，相當於形容詞子句 "who were rescued..." 被簡化後的結果，能使句子更精煉。
  • 條件副詞連接詞 as long as (只要)
    ...but as long as their habitat remains threatened, rescue is one of the best options we can take to help them survive...
    "as long as" 意為「只要」，用來引導條件副詞子句，表達某個條件成立時，主句的情況就會發生。
  • 複合介詞片語 due to (由於)
    The reserve was chosen due to availability of food and water sources as well as its connectivity to other forests...
    "due to" 後面接名詞或名詞片語（在此為 "availability of food and water sources"），用來表達原因，相當於 because of。
  • 形容詞片語作後位修飾與 be set to 用法
    ...the fires have intensified this year because of the El Nino, set to be one of the strongest since comparable records began four decades ago.
    "set to be..." 是過去分詞片語作後位修飾，用來形容前面的 "El Nino"，意指「預定/即將成為...」；"since" 則引導時間副詞子句，搭配現在完成式或過去式表達「自從……以來」。
