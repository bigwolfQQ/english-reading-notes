Taipei Times · 2026-10-12

台灣敦促中國停止敵對言論

Stop hostile talk, Taiwan urges China

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • commitment n. 承諾、投入
    A US Department of State spokesperson said the US welcomed Lai’s commitment to maintaining peace and stability across the Taiwan Strait in his National Day address.
    美國國務院發言人表示，美國歡迎賴清德在其國慶演說中對維持台海和平與穩定的承諾。
  • cherish v. 珍惜、愛護
    Lai on Saturday described Taiwan as a “city upon a hill” and said that “Taiwan cherishes peace, but we will not give up our freedom.
    賴清德週六將台灣形容為「山巔之城」，並表示「台灣珍惜和平，但我們不會放棄自由。」
  • subordination n. 從屬、隸屬
    We are open to exchanges, but will not accept subordination.
    我們對交流持開放態度，但不接受從屬關係。
  • distort v. 扭曲、曲解
    China’s Taiwan Affairs Office said Lai’s speech “disregards facts, distorts the truth, misleads the public and reveals ulterior motives.”
    中國國務院台灣事務辦公室表示，賴清德的講話「無視事實、歪曲真相、誤導公眾並暴露出其不可告人的動機」。
  • collaborate v. 合作、通力合作
    Taiwan will continue to collaborate with global democratic partners and remain committed to safeguarding national sovereignty...
    台灣將繼續與全球民主夥伴合作，並堅定致力於捍衛國家主權……
  • pragmatic adj. 務實的
    Lai took a pragmatic view of potential threats while remaining open to dialogue, he said.
    他說，賴清德對潛在威脅採取了務實的看法，同時保持對話的開放態度。
  • reiterate v. 重申
    National Taiwan University political science associate professor Chen Shih-min yesterday said Lai’s speech reflects three main principles: maintaining stability, expressing a clear stance and reiterating Taiwan’s commitment to safeguarding peace.
    國立台灣大學政治學系副教授陳世民昨天表示，賴清德的演說反映了三個主要原則：維持穩定、表達明確立場，以及重申台灣守護和平的承諾。
  • dependency n. 依賴、從屬
    The president also detailed Taiwan’s economic achievements, and the figures demonstrated that as the nation reduces its dependency on China, its economy boomed and it became more connected to the world, Chen said.
    陳世民說，總統也詳細列舉了台灣的經濟成就，這些數據證明，隨著我國降低對中國的依賴，經濟反而蓬勃發展，並與世界連結得更緊密。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Taiwan yesterday urged China to stop its hostile rhetoric toward Taiwan as the US welcomed President William Lai’s (賴清德) commitment to Taiwan Strait stability in his Double Ten National Day speech.
  • 對等連接詞連接多個動詞結構 (Parallel Verbs connected by Conjunctions)
    China’s Taiwan Affairs Office said Lai’s speech “disregards facts, distorts the truth, misleads the public and reveals ulterior motives.”
  • 現在分詞表示附帶狀況或結果 (Present Participle indicating Circumstance or Result)
    Lai took a pragmatic view of potential threats while remaining open to dialogue, he said.
  • 表條件或時間的連接詞加現在分詞 (Conjunction + Present Participle)
    The president reiterating that Taiwan is a protector of peace and stability in the Taiwan Strait and that enhancing defense capabilities is not a provocation but to deter war also demonstrated that Taiwan would not be the first to use force...
