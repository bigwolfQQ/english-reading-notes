年輕運動員是未來：奧運得主

Young athletes are future: Olympian

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • trend n. 趨勢，傾向
    China’s 13-year-old sensation Yu Zidi is “exciting for the swimming world” and part of the sport’s trend toward younger athletes, Hong Kong Olympic medallist Siobhan Haughey said on yesterday.
    香港奧運獎牌得主何詩蓓於昨天表示，中國13歲的體壇新星于子迪對「游泳界來說令人振奮」，且這也是該項運動朝向年輕化選手發展的趨勢之一。
  • major adj. 主要的，重大的
    It was the first major title of her nascent career, less than two years out from the Los Angeles Olympics.
    這是她剛起步的職業生涯中所獲得的第一個重大頭銜，距離洛杉磯奧運會還有不到兩年的時間。
  • consensus n. 共識
    “I think the general consensus is we have more and more great young swimmers swimming well,” said Haughey, now 28.
    現年28歲的何詩蓓表示：「我認為大家的普遍共識是，我們有越來越多優秀的年輕游泳選手游得很好。」
  • translate v. 轉化，轉變，翻譯
    “Hopefully, swimming well that young, you can also translate to as you get older and well into your 20s and beyond, so it’s exciting for the swimming world,” she added.
    她補充說：「希望能在這麼年輕的時候就游得好，這種表現也能延續到你年紀漸長、邁入20多歲及以後的時期，所以這對游泳界來說令人興奮。」
  • individual adj. 個人的，單獨的
    She also finished just outside the medals in fourth in three world individual events.
    她還在三個世界個人項目中僅以些微差距與獎牌擦身而過，獲得第四名。
  • evidence n. 證據，跡象
    Haughey said the evidence suggests that Yu is “definitely capable of going to the LA Olympics.”
    何詩蓓表示，跡象顯示于子迪「絕對有能力參加洛杉磯奧運」。
  • capable adj. 有能力的，能夠...的
    Haughey said the evidence suggests that Yu is “definitely capable of going to the LA Olympics.”
    何詩蓓表示，跡象顯示于子迪「絕對有能力參加洛杉磯奧運」。
  • pressure n. 壓力
    “I think she’s doing well under pressure at big meets,” she said.
    她說：「我認為她在大型賽事的壓力下表現得很好。」
  • advantage n. 優勢，有利條件
    “I think being an older swimmer means I have a lot more experience, and I try to use that experience to my advantage, for instance, like how to handle pressure or what to expect at a big meet.”
    「我認為身為一個較年長的泳將意味著我擁有更多經驗，我試著利用這些經驗為自己創造優勢，例如如何應付壓力或在大型賽事中期待什麼。」
  • handle v. 處理，應付
    “I think being an older swimmer means I have a lot more experience, and I try to use that experience to my advantage, for instance, like how to handle pressure or what to expect at a big meet.”
    「我認為身為一個較年長的泳將意味著我擁有更多經驗，我試著利用這些經驗為自己創造優勢，例如如何應付壓力或在大型賽事中期待什麼。」

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase)
    Yu is following in the footsteps of Summer McIntosh, who made Canada’s team for the Tokyo Olympics as a 14-year-old in 2021, finishing fourth in the 400m freestyle.
    句尾的「finishing fourth in the 400m freestyle」是由現在分詞 finishing 引導的分詞片語，用來補充說明主詞在該場競賽中達到的結果或伴隨發生的動作，等於 and finished fourth... 的簡化。
  • 名詞子句作受詞 (Noun Clause as Object)
    “I think the general consensus is we have more and more great young swimmers swimming well,” said Haughey, now 28.
    be 動詞 is 後面接了一個省略了連接詞 that 的名詞子句「we have more and more great young swimmers swimming well」作為主詞補語，用來表達具體的共識內容。
  • 形容詞片語作後位修飾 (Adjective Phrase)
    Yu became the youngest swimming world championships medallist in history last year when she won bronze as a 12-year-old as part of China’s 4x200m freestyle relay team.
    「youngest swimming world championships medallist in history」是由最高級形容詞開頭的長篇名詞片語，精確地修飾主詞 Yu。在多益閱讀測驗中，常考這種由介系詞或形容詞組成的複合名詞結構。
  • 條件句與假設語態 (Conditional Structure)
    “I think if she wants to do it, she definitely can.”
    這是標準的第一類條件句（First Conditional），用來談論可能實現的未來狀況。if 子句使用現在式（wants），主詞的語氣助動詞則用 can 表達確定的能力與可能性。
