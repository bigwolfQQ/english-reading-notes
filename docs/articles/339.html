文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Taiwan and the United States need to prepare for a crisis triggered by China's intensifying gray-zone pressure rather than focus only on deterring a full-scale conflict...
    句中的「triggered by...」與「intensifying...」分別作為分詞片語，用來修飾前方的 crisis 與 pressure。現在分詞 (intensifying) 表主動與進行，過去分詞 (triggered) 表被動，是新聞英語用來精簡句子結構的核心文法。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause with in which)
    He pointed in particular to Chinese Coast Guard operations in which vessels radio commercial ships entering or leaving Taiwan and request information on their cargoes.
    「in which」引導一個形容詞子句，用來修飾先行詞 operations。介系詞 in 帶到前面與關代 which 結合，等於「...operations where vessels radio...」，在正式商業與新聞英語中非常常見。
  • 假設語氣第一與第二型 (Conditionals)
    If the shipping companies comply with this, if the international insurance system says that we will not insure cargoes that don't comply with PRC directives, China can effectively take control...
    這是一句使用「If + 現在式, 主詞 + 조動詞 (can/will)」的條件句，用來表達真實條件下的可能結果，強調若滿足某條件（航運公司配合、保險公司拒保），將會帶來的直接後果。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Nominative Absolute Construction)
    ...with Chinese vessels radioing commercial ships and asking about their routes while asserting jurisdiction over the waters.
    「with + 名詞 + 現在分詞 (vessel radioing / asking / asserting)」結構在新聞寫作中極為實用，用來補充說明主句發生時的伴隨狀態或同時發生的細節。
