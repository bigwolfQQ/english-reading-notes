文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後置修飾語 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The latest duo stars in By Any Means, an opposites-attract movie with real historical heft, set against the racial and political violence of the American South in the late 1960s and told with blood-splashing power and blunt elegance...
    句中的「set against...」與「and told with...」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞片語「an opposites-attract movie」，省略了「which is」，使語氣緊湊，常出現在新聞和商業報導中以精簡句子結構。
  • 分裂句 (Cleft Sentence: It is... that...)
    It’s the interaction between the hitman and the FBI agent that’s at the heart of the movie, along with the lingering question of whether violence can ever be a corrective.
    此句使用了「It is + 強調內容 + that...」的分裂句型，用來特別突顯「the interaction between the hitman and the FBI agent」（殺手與FBI探員之間的互動）是電影的核心，多益閱讀測驗常考此結構以辨識作者強調的重點。
  • 現在分詞表附帶狀況或結果 (Present Participle)
    Unable to crack the 1966 firebombing murder of Mississippi NAACP leader Vernon Dahmer Sr, the FBI turns to Wahlberg’s Gregory Scarpa, letting him loose off the books to solve the case.
    句尾的「letting him loose...」是由現在分詞「letting」引導的分詞片語，用來補充說明前面主句發生後所產生的結果或伴隨的動作，讓動作的連貫性更強。
  • 省略主詞的條件句 (Ellipsis in Conditional Clause)
    He despises his Mafia partner so much that, if forced to choose between him and the KKK, he knows which way he’d lean...
    在「if forced to choose...」中，省略了主詞與be動詞（即 if he is forced to choose），這種結構在商業英語和正式寫作中很常見，能使句子更為簡潔明瞭。
