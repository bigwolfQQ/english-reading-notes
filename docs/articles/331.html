中台灣筆記：在地化台灣的無人機能量

Notes from Central Taiwan: Localize Taiwan’s drone capabilities

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • obstruct verb 阻礙，妨礙
    Because of this, the pro-China Chinese Nationalist Party (KMT) and the Taiwan People’s Party (TPP) appear to be obstructing their development.
    正因如此，親中的中國國民黨和台灣民眾黨似乎在阻撓其發展。
  • circumvent verb 規避，繞過（問題或法規等）
    One approach to handling this obstruction is to circumvent it by building robust drone capabilities across a wide swathe of the population.
    處理這種阻撓的方法之一，是透過在廣大的人口中建立強健的無人機能量來繞過它。
  • robust adjective 強健的，健全的，穩固的
    One approach to handling this obstruction is to circumvent it by building robust drone capabilities across a wide swathe of the population.
    處理這種阻撓的方法之一，是透過在廣大的人口中建立強健的無人機能量來繞過它。
  • provision verb 向……供應糧食（或物資）
    Whether intentional or not, the program mimics the use of drones to provision front line troops and residents in occupied areas, a key function of drones.
    無論是否刻意，該計畫模仿了使用無人機向被佔領區的前線部隊和居民供應物資的做法，這是無人機的一項關鍵功能。
  • routine adjective 例行的，日常的
    Drones for both routine delivery of medicines and emergency treatments are being studied.
    用於日常藥品配送和緊急治療的無人機正在被研究中。
  • repurpose verb 改變……的用途，重新改裝
    It is easy to see how an existing integrated drone-based medical delivery system could be repurposed for wartime use.
    不難看出，現有的整合式無人機醫療配送系統如何能被轉作戰時用途。
  • acquire verb 獲得，取得，採購
    It presently operates 11 drones and plans to acquire 10 more by the year’s end.
    它目前營運 11 架無人機，並計畫在年底前再採購 10 架。
  • implementation noun 實施，執行
    The purpose of such programs is to build drone-based capabilities for use in wartime and create robust expertise in drone use at the local level.
    此類計畫的目的是建立戰時使用的無人機能力，並在地方層面建立強健的無人機使用專業知識。
  • mandatory adjective 強制的，法定的
    For areas controlled by pro-Taiwan politicians from the pan-Green parties, this should be considered mandatory.
    對於由泛綠陣營的親台政治人物控制的地區，這應該被視為強制性的。
  • outstrip verb 超過，勝過
    Demand for drone operators is going to massively outstrip supply.
    對無人機操作員的需求將遠遠超過供應。
  • decentralization noun 去中心化，權力下放
    Another reason to get local governments to operate robust drone programs is decentralization.
    讓地方政府營運強健無人機計畫的另一個原因是去中心化。
  • cultivate verb 培養，培育
    The more local governments can cultivate civil defense capabilities, the better off Taiwan will be...
    地方政府越能培養民防能力，台灣就會越好……
  • suppress verb 鎮壓，壓制
    ...either attempting to suppress local drone training programs — affecting local voters and displaying their pro-China positions for all to see...
    ……要么試圖壓制地方無人機訓練計畫——這會影響地方選民並讓所有人看清他們的親中立場……
  • flourish verb 繁榮，興旺，蓬勃發展
    ...or letting drone programs flourish and angering their friends across the Strait.
    ……要么讓無人機計畫蓬勃發展並惹惱他們海峽對岸的朋友。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句 (Participial Phrase) 作為附加說明
    Whether intentional or not, the program mimics the use of drones to provision front line troops and residents in occupied areas, a key function of drones.
    句尾的「a key function of drones」為名詞片語，前面省略了主詞與 be 動詞（即 which is a key function of drones），用來補充說明前面整件事（即模仿前線補給的做法）是無人機的一項關鍵功能。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Drones for both routine delivery of medicines and emergency treatments are being studied.
    「are being studied」為現在進行式的被動語態（be + being + 過去分詞），強調「用於日常藥品配送和緊急治療的無人機」目前正持續受到研究與關注。
  • 「The + 比較級 ..., the + 比較級 ...」句構（越……越……）
    The more local governments can cultivate civil defense capabilities, the better off Taiwan will be, and the more Taipei can focus on the national situation.
  • 現在分詞 (Present Participle) 作形容詞修飾
    Demand for drone operators is going to massively outstrip supply.
    句中的「massively outstrip supply」為不定詞片語作主詞補語，而「drone operators」中的 drone 雖為名詞，但在多益考試中常見名詞作修飾語（Noun adjunct）的用法，用來限定 operators 的專長領域。
