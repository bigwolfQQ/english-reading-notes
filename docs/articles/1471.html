保羅·賽薩斯以僅 20 歲之齡創下自行車歷史

Paul Seixas makes cycling history at just 20 years old

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • talent n. 人才，天才
    France’s rising cycling talent Paul Seixas made history at the Tour de Lombardy...
    法國新銳自行車好手保羅·賽薩斯週六在倫巴底公路賽中創造了歷史...
  • winner n. 獲勝者，冠軍
    ...when he became its youngest winner ever on Saturday after riders protested safety concerns.
    ...在車手們抗議安全疑慮後，他成為該賽事有史以來最年輕的冠軍。
  • concern n. 擔憂，關切
    ...after riders protested safety concerns.
    ...在車手們抗議安全疑慮後。
  • currently adv. 目前，當前
    ...that had been dominated by the currently injured Tadej Pogacar for the previous five years.
    ...過去五年來，這場賽事一直由目前受傷的塔德伊·波加查所主宰。
  • previous adj. 先前的，以前的
    ...for the previous five years.
    ...過去五年來。
  • overall adj. / adv. 總體的，全面的
    Seixas finished this summer’s Tour de France fourth overall in his debut.
    賽薩斯在今年夏天的環法自行車賽初次登場就獲得總成績第四名。
  • runner-up n. 第二名，亞軍
    ...and finished runner-up to Pogacar, a five-time Tour champion...
    ...並獲得五屆環法冠軍波加查之後的亞軍...
  • delay v. / n. 延誤，延期
    The start of the race was delayed for about five minutes...
    比賽起跑延遲了大約五分鐘...
  • scheduled adj. 預定的，排定行程的
    ...when riders did not begin as scheduled in a protest to denounce unsafe racing conditions...
    ...當時車手們未按原定計劃出發，藉此抗議並譴責不安全的競賽條件...
  • colleague n. 同事，同僚
    “Unfortunately, we lost the life of a brother and of a colleague...”
    「不幸的是，我們失去了一位兄弟和同事的生命...」
  • accept v. 接受
    ...so we want to make this statement to show the world that we cannot keep accepting the fact...
    ...所以我們想要發表這項聲明，向世界展示我們不能繼續接受這樣的事實...
  • incident n. 事件，事故
    British rider Finlay Tarling died following a serious incident in the Volta a Portugal race in August...
    英國車手芬利·塔林在8月的葡萄牙環賽中因一起嚴重事故身亡...

文法重點

  • 過去完成式被動語態 (Past Perfect Passive)
    ...that had been dominated by the currently injured Tadej Pogacar for the previous five years.
    用來表達在過去某個時間點之前，持續了一段時間的被動動作（由 had been + 過去分詞構成）。在此句中用來形容該賽事在過去五年一直被波加查所主宰。
  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    ...the driver reportedly missed the signs from stewards and entered the road going in the opposite direction of the race.
    句尾的「going in...」是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前面主詞 the driver 進入道路時的狀態，相當於副詞子句「while he was going in...」。
  • 關係代間詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    ...to everybody that is following cycling, because we cannot accept the fact that people are dying.
    句中的「that is following cycling」是由關代 that 引導的限定性關係子句，用來修飾先行詞 everybody。
  • 同位語子句 (Appositive Clause)
    ...because we cannot accept the fact that people are dying.
    「that people are dying」是名詞子句，用作 the fact 的同位語，用以詳細說明「這個事實」的具體內容是什麼。
