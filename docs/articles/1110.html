台灣需要醫療韌性

Taiwan needs medical resilience

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • resilience n. 韌性、恢復力
    When discussing Taiwan’s resilience in an increasingly uncertain security environment, policymakers often focus on missiles...
    當討論台灣在日益不確定的安全環境中的韌性時，決策者往往將焦點放在飛彈...
  • disrupt v. 使中斷、擾亂
    Can Taiwan keep hospitals functioning and treat people if supply chains are disrupted?
    如果供應鏈中斷，台灣能否維持醫院運作並治療民眾？
  • vulnerable adj. 脆弱的、易受影響的
    A society might possess advanced technology, strong military capabilities and substantial economic resources, but still become vulnerable if citizens lose confidence in essential services.
    一個社會可能擁有先進技術、強大軍事能力和充裕經濟資源，但如果公民對基本服務失去信心，仍然會變得脆弱。
  • pillar n. 支柱、棟樑
    Medical resilience should therefore be viewed not merely as a healthcare issue, but a pillar of civilian resilience.
    因此，醫療韌性不應僅被視為醫療衛生問題，而應被視為平民韌性的支柱。
  • shortage n. 短缺、不足
    Shortages of essential medicines, dialysis supplies, blood products, antibiotics or temperature-sensitive therapies would affect many Taiwanese.
    基本藥物、透析耗材、血液製品、抗生素或對溫度敏感的療法的短缺，將影響許多台灣人。
  • operational adj. 營運的、運作的
    If people are unable to obtain necessary treatment and healthcare providers face mounting operational challenges, the impact could extend well beyond the healthcare sector.
    如果人們無法獲得必要的治療，且醫療提供者面臨日益嚴重的營運挑戰，其影響可能會擴大到醫療產業之外。
  • component n. 組件、成分、重要部分
    Medical continuity should therefore be considered an important component of national resilience and deterrence by denial...
    因此，應將醫療連續性視為國家韌性和拒止嚇阻的重要組成部分...
  • stockpile v. / n. 儲備、囤積
    Attempting to stockpile everything would be costly and inefficient.
    試圖將所有東西都儲存起來將是昂貴且無效率的。
  • redistribute v. 重新分配
    A reserve can buy time, but it cannot automatically identify shortages across institutions, redistribute supplies, qualify alternative suppliers...
    儲備可以爭取時間，但它無法自動識別各機構間的短缺、重新分配物資、評估替代供應商...
  • allocate v. 分配、分派
    Policymakers need accurate information about where critical products are, how quickly stocks are being consumed and how they can be allocated during emergencies.
    決策者需要準確的資訊，了解關鍵產品的位置、庫存消耗的速度，以及如何在緊急情況下進行分配。
  • diversify v. 多樣化
    Second, it should continue diversifying suppliers and developing distributed reserves for selected essential medicines and healthcare products.
    其次，它應該持續多元化供應商，並為特定的基本藥物和醫療保健產品建立分散式儲備。
  • procurement n. 採購
    Working in advance with like-minded partners on emergency logistics, procurement arrangements, regulatory flexibility and supply-sharing would bolster preparedness before a crisis occurs.
    在危機發生前，提前與理念相近的夥伴在緊急後勤、採購安排、監管靈活性和物資共享方面合作，將能強化備戰。
  • strategic adj. 戰略性的
    Ultimately, the argument for medical resilience is strategic rather than medical.
    歸根究底，支持醫療韌性的理由是戰略性的，而非醫療性的。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句 (Participle Clause)
    What begins as a logistics challenge could evolve into declining public confidence and growing concern about whether normal life can continue.
    在此句中，以現在分詞 declining 與 growing 修飾後方的名詞 public confidence 與 concern，用來形容物流挑戰所演變出來的具體狀況與日益增加的擔憂，使語氣更為緊湊。
  • 假設語氣 (Subjunctive Mood)
    If people are unable to obtain necessary treatment and healthcare providers face mounting operational challenges, the impact could extend well beyond the healthcare sector.
    此句使用現在式的條件句 (If +現在式, 主詞 + could + 原形動詞)，表達對未來可能發生之情況的假設與推論，說明若醫療體系受阻，其衝擊將會如何擴大。
  • 名詞子句當受詞 (Noun Clause as Object)
    The more relevant questions are whether authorities know where critical inventories are located...
    句中的 whether 引導名詞子句作主詞補語，用來具體說明「更相關的問題」是什麼；子句內部又包含了一個由 where 引導的地方副詞子句 (where critical inventories are located)，呈現多層次的句構。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Far less attention is paid to a simpler, but equally important question...
    此處使用被動語態「is paid」，強調受事者「attention (關注)」，因為在新聞英文中，為了突顯被關注的對象或客觀性，常會將動作的承受者放在主詞位置。
