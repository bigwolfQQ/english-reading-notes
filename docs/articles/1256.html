美國從英國空軍基地撤離轟炸機

US removes bombers from UK air base

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • withdraw v. 撤回、撤離
    The US on Sunday confirmed that it has withdrawn all B-1 bombers from the British air base of RAF Fairford...
    美國於週日證實，已從英國皇家空軍費爾福德基地撤出所有 B-1 轟炸機……
  • link v. 連結、與……有關
    ...with officials telling US media that the move was linked to “new threats.”
    ……官員向美國媒體表示，此舉與「新威脅」有關。
  • prompt v. 促使、激起
    The scare prompted a counterterrorism probe and forced evacuations at 85 homes near the air base...
    這次的虛驚一場引發了反恐調查，並迫使該空軍基地附近的 85 戶民宅進行疏散……
  • deploy v. 部署
    A statement from a Pentagon official on Sunday said “we can acknowledge now that all US bombers that were deployed to RAF Fairford have redeployed to their home stations in the United States.”
    五角大廈官員週日發表的聲明表示：「我們現在可以承認，所有部署至皇家空軍費爾福德基地的美國轟炸機都已重新部署回美國本土駐地。」
  • asset n. 資產、有價值的人或物
    “Operational security precluded us from confirming the movement of our assets and forces in real-time,” it said.
    聲明並指出：「作戰安全使我們無法即時確認我們資產與部隊的動向。」
  • unspecified adj. 未明確指定的、未具體的
    An unidentified US official told CNN that the bombers were moved after “an unspecified new threat,”...
    一名未具名的美國官員告訴美國有線電視新聞網（CNN），轟炸機是在接獲「一項未明的新威脅」後轉移的……
  • urgent adj. 緊急的
    One US official said the new information was part of the same threat warnings that surfaced three weeks ago, but the situation became urgent in the past 24 hours, the Times reported.
    據《紐約時報》報導，一名美國官員表示，這項新資訊與三週前浮現的威脅警告是同一批，但在過去 24 小時內情況變得緊急。
  • motive n. 動機
    Speculation about the motives behind the incident ranged from terror threat to fuel thievery...
    外界對於該事件背後動機的揣測從恐怖威脅到偷竊燃料都有……
  • reject v. 拒絕、駁斥
    ...a charge rejected by Tehran.
    ……德黑蘭則駁斥了這項指控。
  • condemn v. 譴責
    Iran’s embassy in London said it “categorically rejects and strongly condemns” speculation that Tehran was involved.
    伊朗駐倫敦大使館表示，該館「堅決拒絕並強烈譴責」有關德黑蘭涉案的揣測。
  • legitimate adj. 合法的、正當的
    ...giving the false impression that it was owned by a legitimate diesel delivery company.
    ……給人一種該車屬於合法的柴油配送公司的假象。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（-ing）作後置修飾語
    The incident came after a farmer living in the village of Whelford, near the air base, called the police after seeing three vans blocking a road and a group of masked men fleeing the scene.
    句中的「living in the village of Whelford」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 a farmer，相當於形容詞子句「who lived in the village of Whelford」。這種結構在新聞英語中非常常見，能簡潔地補充說明人物或事物的背景。
  • 過去分詞（-ed）作後置修飾語
    Aerial footage of the vans showed they appeared to contain barrels, and at least one was marked with a fake logo and phone number...
    句中的「marked with a fake logo and phone number」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞片語 at least one（指代箱型車）。因為箱型車是被標上標誌，所以使用被動語態意味的過去分詞來表達。
  • 名詞子句作受詞（that 子句）
    The US on Sunday confirmed that it has withdrawn all B-1 bombers from the British air base of RAF Fairford...
    動詞 confirmed 後面接了一個由 that 引導的名詞子句「that it has withdrawn all B-1 bombers...」，用來充當 confirmed 的受詞，表達確認的具體內容。
