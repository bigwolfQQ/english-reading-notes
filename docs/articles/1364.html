菲律賓力保「紫山藥王國」地位

Philippines strives to keep ube throne

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • trendsetting adj. 引領潮流的
    The Philippine ube has become a trendsetting sensation from Los Angeles to London to Seoul
    菲律賓的紫山藥已經成為從洛杉磯、倫敦到首爾引領潮流的轟動美食
  • keep pace with phr. 跟上、與...同步
    However, keeping pace with the international boom has proven tricky for the archipelago nation’s overwhelmingly smallholder farmers
    然而，對於這個群島國家的絕大多數小農來說，跟上國際熱潮相當棘手
  • yield n. 產量、收益
    with yields declining and some unable to commit to the plant’s nine-month growing time.
    產量正在下降，且有些農民無法配合該植物長達九個月的生長期。
  • commit v. 投入、承諾
    with yields declining and some unable to commit to the plant’s nine-month growing time.
    產量正在下降，且有些農民無法配合該植物長達九個月的生長期。
  • export n. / v. 出口
    have prompted the government to announce a ban on the export of the raw form of the yam.
    促使政府宣布禁止出口未加工的生山藥。
  • in favor of phr. 支持、贊成
    farmer-exporter Al Bonifacio said he was in favor of doing whatever it took to protect the crop
    農夫兼出口商阿爾·博尼法西奧表示，他贊成採取一切必要措施來保護農作物
  • supply v. / n. 供應
    Our greatest fear is if we are not able to supply ube to the world, someone [else] will step in.
    我們最大的擔憂是，如果我們無法向世界供應紫山藥，其他人將會趁虛而入。
  • extract n. 萃取物
    Sold as powder, extract, paste or frozen puree, ube is easily mixed into coffee
    以粉末、萃取物、泥或冷凍果泥的形式出售，紫山藥很容易混合到咖啡中
  • importer n. 進口商
    At a Tokyo gelato shop, ube importer J.P. Mercado said the Japanese market was expanding rapidly
    在東京的一家義式冰淇淋店，紫山藥進口商 J.P. 梅卡多表示，日本市場正在迅速擴張
  • demand n. / v. 需求
    Demand exploded locally this decade, most memorably during a purple bread mania
    本地的需求在本十年爆炸性成長，最令人難忘的是在紫色麵包熱潮期間
  • facility n. 設施、設備
    Bonifacio said the country’s small farmers widely lacked the facilities for postharvest storage and processing
    博尼法西奧表示，國內的小農普遍缺乏收成後儲存和加工的設施
  • financing n. 融資、財務支援
    while access to marketing and financing lagged.
    同時行銷管道和融資管道也落後。
  • commitments n. 承諾、交貨義務
    Bonifacio said he had been forced to scour the rest of the country for raw ube to meet his growing export commitments
    博尼法西奧表示，為了滿足不斷增長的出口承諾，他被迫在全國各地四處搜羅未加工的紫山藥
  • production n. 生產、產量
    Last year’s production was just 12,483 tonnes
    去年的產量僅有 12,483 噸
  • niche adj. 小眾的、適合特定市場的
    Before the ube craze, it was a niche market
    在紫山藥熱潮之前，它只是一個小眾市場

文法重點

  • 獨立分詞構句 (Absolute Nominative Construction)
    with yields declining and some unable to commit to the plant’s nine-month growing time.
    「with + 名詞/代名詞 + 分詞/形容詞」結構用來表示伴隨狀況或原因。在此句中，with 引導補充說明小農面臨的狀況：產量下降 (yields declining) 且部分農民無法配合生長期 (some unable to commit...)
  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    Standing in his ube field south of Manila, farmer-exporter Al Bonifacio said...
    句首的「Standing in his ube field...」是由現在分詞片語引導的修飾語，用來補充說明主詞 farmer-exporter Al Bonifacio 在動作發生時的狀態，相當於一個時間或條件副詞子句 (While he was standing...)
  • 被動語態的應用 (Passive Voice)
    Ube was a staple of Philippine gardens as long as 5,000 years ago, boiled and eaten by families...
    過去分詞片語「boiled and eaten by families」省略了主格關代與be動詞 (which were)，用來修飾前面的 ube，表達被動且同時進行的動作（被煮熟並被家人食用）。
  • 動名詞作介系詞受詞 (Gerund as Object of Preposition)
    The government is working on how it can boost supply.
    介系詞 on 後方接了一個間接問句「how it can boost supply」作為其受詞，用來具體說明政府正在努力的方向或專注的議題。
