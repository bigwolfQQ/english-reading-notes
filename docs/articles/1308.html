美國誓言在據稱監視賴清德兒子事件後保護個人

U.S. vows to protect individuals after alleged surveillance of Lai's son

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • vow v. 誓言，發誓
    The United States on Monday vowed to protect individuals on its soil from "the overreach of foreign governments"...
    美國於周一誓言保護其領土上的個人免受「外國政府的過度權力伸展」...
  • commit v. 投入，致力於（常用被動態 be committed to）
    "We are committed to safeguarding our sovereignty, keeping our communities safe..."
    「我們致力於維護我們的主權、保障我們社區的安全...」
  • safeguard v. 保護，維護
    "We are committed to safeguarding our sovereignty, keeping our communities safe, and defending individuals..."
    「我們致力於維護我們的主權、保障我們社區的安全，並保護個人...」
  • response n. 回應，答覆
    ...a U.S. State Department spokesperson said in response to CNA's request for comment.
    ...美國國務院發言人在回應中央社的置評請求時表示。
  • agent n. 代理人，經紀人，特工
    ...34-year-old Wanying Zhang, a California real estate agent, was arrested at Los Angeles International Airport...
    ...34 歲的加州房地產經紀人張萬英是在啟程前往中國前於洛杉磯國際機場被捕的。
  • depart v. 出發，離開
    ...Wanying Zhang, a California real estate agent, was arrested at Los Angeles International Airport before she departed for China.
    ...加州房地產經紀人張萬英是在啟程前往中國前於洛杉磯國際機場被捕的。
  • threat n. 威脅
    The arrest of Zhang, a Chinese-born naturalized U.S. citizen, drew comment from U.S. lawmakers on the alleged security threats posed by Beijing.
    張女是一名出生於中國的美國歸化公民，她的被捕引發了美國議員對北京所構成之涉嫌安全威脅的評論。
  • vigilant adj. 警惕的，戒備森嚴的
    ...while Republican Senator Pete Ricketts said the U.S. "must remain vigilant" to the threat posed by "spies and deception on American soil."
    ...而共和黨參議員皮特・里克茨則表示，美國「必須保持警惕」，以防範「美國本土上的間諜和欺騙」所構成的威脅。
  • statement n. 聲明，陳述
    Echoing Essayli, FBI Director Kash Patel said in a statement on X that the Chinese government had allegedly sent an operative...
    聯邦調查局局長卡什・帕特爾在 X 平台的聲明中呼應埃薩利的話說，中國政府據稱曾派遣一名特工...
  • condemn v. 譴責
    In Taipei, the Presidential Office on Monday condemned the alleged surveillance of Lai's son...
    在台北，總統府周臨時於周一譴責這起涉嫌監視賴清德兒子的事件...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作後置定語
    ...defending individuals from the overreach of foreign governments and regimes trying to silence, intimidate, harass, harm or coerce individuals within our borders...
    句中的 "trying to..." 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "governments and regimes"，相當於形容詞子句 "that/which try to..." 的簡化版，能讓新聞英文更為緊湊精煉。
  • 過去分詞片語作後置定語
    The arrest of Zhang, a Chinese-born naturalized U.S. citizen, drew comment from U.S. lawmakers on the alleged security threats posed by Beijing.
    句尾的 "posed by Beijing" 是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞 "security threats"。因為威脅是被北京所「製造/構成」的，所以使用過去分詞 (posed) 表被動與完成的概念。
  • 現在分詞引導附帶狀況（分詞構句）
    Writing on X, Republican Senator Marsha Blackburn accused China of "trying to export its repressive surveillance to America"...
    句首的 "Writing on X" 是一個由現在分詞引導的分詞構句，主詞與後方的主句主詞 (Marsha Blackburn) 一致，表示「一邊在 X 上發文，一邊指責...」的同時發生動作。
  • 引用句中的現在分詞表達伴隨動作
    CBS News, citing an FBI complaint, reported Sunday that Zhang and a man... flew from Las Vegas to Seattle...
    句中的 ", citing an FBI complaint," 是插入語性質的分詞構句，用來補充說明新聞來源是「引用了一份 FBI 的訴狀」，主詞同樣為主句主語 CBS News。
