跳高選手傅兆玄在亞運會奪金，找到自己的節奏

High jumper Fu Chao-hsuan finds his stride with Asian Games gold

單字解析

  • allow v. 允許、使能夠
    This sport lets me fly through the air, and it allows the whole crowd to focus on just me when I'm competing.
    這項運動讓我能在空中飛翔，並在比賽時讓全場觀眾的目光聚焦在我一個人身上。
  • attempt n. 嘗試、試跳
    On Sunday, the crowd watched him clear 2.25 meters on his first attempt at the Asian Games at the Nagoya City Mizuho Park Athletic Stadium in Japan.
    週日，在日本名古屋市瑞穗公園田徑場舉行的亞洲運動會上，觀眾們看著他在第一次試跳時就成功越過了 2.25 公尺。
  • achieve v. 達成、取得（其名詞 achievement 見於文章）
    With that achievement, Fu became the first Taiwanese male competitor in 60 years to win a gold medal in track and field athletics at the Asian Games.
    憑藉著這項成就，傅兆玄成為了 60 年來第一位在亞運會田徑項目中贏得金牌的台灣男子選手。
  • performance n. 表現、績效
    However, the transition to senior competition was tough for Fu as he struggled through a cycle of injuries, recovery, and competition while striving to improve his performance.
    然而，對傅兆玄而言，轉入成年組競賽是艱難的，因為他在努力提升表現的同時，必須掙扎著度過傷病、恢復和比賽的循環。

    In 2024, another Taiwanese high jumper Yeh Po-ting (葉柏廷) cleared 2.30 meters to break the national record, which Fu said added to his frustration.

  • presence n. 出席、存在
    The presence of his mother at the competition made him feel confident, and he was able to keep his rhythm through to the final, Fu said.
    傅兆玄表示，母親在比賽現場的陪伴讓他感到有信心，這使他能夠在整個決賽過程中保持自己的節奏。
  • confident adj. 有信心的、自信的
    The presence of his mother at the competition made him feel confident, and he was able to keep his rhythm through to the final, Fu said.

  • realize v. 實現（夢想等）、意識到
    Having established a record as the first Taiwanese male to win an athletics gold medal at the Asian Games in 60 years, Fu has now set his sights on the world championships and one day realizing his dream of competing in the Olympics.
    隨著創下 60 年來首位在亞運會奪得田徑金牌的台灣男選手紀錄，傅兆玄現在已將目光投向世界錦標賽，並期待有一天能實現參加奧運的夢想。

文法重點

  • 關係代間詞引導的形容詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    The jump matched his season best and put him ahead of India's Sarvesh Anil Kushare, who cleared the same height on his second attempt.
    句中逗號後的「who cleared the same height on his second attempt」是非限定關係子句，用來補充說明先行詞 India's Sarvesh Anil Kushare 的額外資訊。
  • 否定副詞置於句首的倒裝句結構
    Neither athlete made it over 2.28 meters, which gave Fu the gold medal.
    「Neither」作為否定主詞或代名詞開頭，直接否定兩位選手，強調兩者皆無。
  • 分詞構句作時間或條件狀語
    Having established a record as the first Taiwanese male to win an athletics gold medal at the Asian Games in 60 years, Fu has now set his sights on the world championships...
    句首的「Having established...」是由現在分詞完成式構成的分詞構句，表示這個動作發生在主要子句動作（set his sights）之前，意指「在創下紀錄之後」。
  • 強調句型 (Cleft Sentence) 搭配否定副詞倒裝
    He felt he was doing well when he cleared 2.2 meters, and it was only after his successful attempt at 2.25 meters did he start thinking of gold, he said.
    此句包含了強調句型「It was only after... did he...」，將副詞片語提前並透過助動詞倒裝來加強語氣，強調直到那個時刻才開始想金牌的事。
