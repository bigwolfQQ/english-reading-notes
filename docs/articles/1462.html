單字解析

  • unreliable adj. 不可靠的
    The US is unreliable, the Chinese Nationalist Party (KMT) leadership is aligned with the Chinese Communist Party (CCP), and Taiwan does not have the military capability to defend itself in a prolonged war without help.
    美國是不可靠的，中國國民黨（KMT）的領導階層與中國共產黨（CCP）站在同一陣線，且台灣沒有在沒有外援的情況下於長期戰爭中自我防衛的軍事能力。
  • prolonged adj. 長期的、拖延的
    The US is unreliable, the Chinese Nationalist Party (KMT) leadership is aligned with the Chinese Communist Party (CCP), and Taiwan does not have the military capability to defend itself in a prolonged war without help.
    美國是不可靠的，中國國民黨（KMT）的領導階層與中國共產黨（CCP）站在同一陣線，且台灣沒有在沒有外援的情況下於長期戰爭中自我防衛的軍事能力。
  • capability n. 能力、才能
    The US is unreliable, the Chinese Nationalist Party (KMT) leadership is aligned with the Chinese Communist Party (CCP), and Taiwan does not have the military capability to defend itself in a prolonged war without help.
    美國是不可靠的，中國國民黨（KMT）的領導階層與中國共產黨（CCP）站在同一陣線，且台灣沒有在沒有外援的情況下於長期戰爭中自我防衛的軍事能力。
  • bilateral adj. 雙邊的
    Despite the announcement of a record arms sale at the end of 2025 and continuing improvements in bilateral military ties, however, Trump’s second term has given Taiwan more cause for concern.
    然而，儘管在 2025 年底宣佈了一筆創紀錄的對台軍售，且雙邊軍事聯繫持續改善，川普的第二任期仍讓台灣更有理由感到擔憂。
  • defer v. 延期、推遲
    Trump has deferred additional arms sales in order to avoid angering Chinese President Xi Jinping (習近平), openly echoed Xi’s talking points about Taiwan, and publicly implied that Taiwan is too far away for the US to defend.
    川普為了避免激怒中國國家主席習近平，推遲了額外的對台軍售，公開呼應習近平關於台灣的談話要點，並公開暗示台灣距離太遠，美國無法防衛。
  • prioritize v. 優先處理、把...列為優先
    Considering the larger context — for example, Trump’s refusal to prioritize Russia’s defeat in Europe and his threats to invade Greenland, a territory of NATO member Denmark — responsible Taiwanese national security planners must question American dependability.
    考量到更大的背景——例如川普拒絕優先考慮俄羅斯在歐洲的失敗，以及他威脅要入侵格陵蘭（北約成員國丹麥的領土）——負責任的台灣國家安全規畫者必須質疑美國的可靠性。
  • funding n. 資金、經費
    In 2026, the opposition in the Legislative Yuan stripped all of the funding for domestic drone production from a special defense budget and later advanced a separate drone bill that arguably did not prioritize the Ministry of National Defense’s need for unmanned capabilities.
    在 2026 年，立法院的在野黨從一項特別國防預算中刪除了國內無人機生產的所有資金，隨後又推進了一項可爭議的獨立無人機法案，該法案並未優先考慮國防部對無人機戰力的需求。
  • appropriate adj. 合適的、恰當的
    Taipei has been making important investments in its military, but this year’s challenges are emblematic of a decades-long failure to fund a defense budget that is appropriate for the threats Taiwan faces.
    台北一直在其軍事上進行重要投資，但今年的挑戰象徵著數十年來未能資助符合台灣所面臨威脅的國防預算。
  • aggression n. 侵略、攻擊
    Rather, there are good reasons to think Taiwan will defend itself vigorously if Beijing opts for aggression.
    相反地，有充分的理由認為，如果北京選擇侵略，台灣將會強力抵抗。
  • distinct adj. 截然不同的、有別的
    Even those who recognize a shared cultural endowment with People’s Republic of China citizens see Taiwan as distinct from China.
    即使是那些承認與中華人民共和國公民擁有共同文化賦存的人，也將台灣視為與中國截然不同。
  • acquisition n. 獲得、採購

    即使台灣成功讓其軍事採購和改革努力進入高度運轉，它在人力和火力上仍將處于劣勢。
  • resilience n. 韌性、恢復力
    Energy security is a major weakness, as is economic resilience.
    能源安全是一個主要弱點，經濟韌性也是。
  • guarantee n. / v. 保證
    The US may come to its aid, but that’s no guarantee of victory.
    美國可能會前來救援，但這並不能保證勝利。
  • tangible adj. 有形的、確切的
    Fighting on home territory provides both tangible and moral benefits.
    在本土作戰既能帶來有形的利益，也能帶來道德上的好處。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況或結果 (Participle Phrases)
    Trump has deferred additional arms sales in order to avoid angering Chinese President Xi Jinping (習近平), openly echoed Xi’s talking points about Taiwan, and publicly implied that Taiwan is too far away for the US to defend.
    句中的「openly echoed...」與「publicly implied...」為省略了連接詞與主詞的分詞（此處為過去分詞當作動詞平行結構使用，或可視為對應主詞 Trump 的連續動作），用來補充說明主句動作發生時伴隨的情況或結果，使句子簡潔且語氣緊湊。
  • 虛主詞 It 的用法 (Introductory 'It')
    It is far from clear that, as Shuster argues, “voters in Taiwan can feel the balance of power shifting, and many will choose the side that looks more stable, even at the risk of their own liberties.”
    句首的 "It" 是形式主詞（虛主詞），真正的受詞或主詞其實是後面接的由 that 引導的名詞子句（"that, as Shuster argues..."）。使用虛主詞可以避免頭重腳輕，讓長子句移至句尾，保持句子結構的平衡。
  • Even if引導的讓步子句 (Concessive Clauses with 'Even if')
    Taiwan will be outmanned and outgunned, even if it does succeed in kicking its military acquisition and reform efforts into high gear.
