文法重點

  • 現在分詞作附帶狀況/時間狀語的省略句型 (While cooperating with...)
    She said that while cooperating with Japanese authorities and explaining the circumstances truthfully during the investigation, she came to understand that...
    在此句中，「while cooperating...」是由連接詞 while 引導的現在分詞片語，省略了主詞與be動詞（原句結構應為 while she was cooperating）。這在多益閱讀測驗中常考，用以簡化句型並表達「在進行...的同時」之意。
  • 關係代間詞子句作後位修飾 (-ed 分詞當形容詞)
    In 2023, the Spanish national visited Cixian Temple (慈諴宮) — which Taipei has designated as a historic site — in Shilin District (士林), Cheap wrote on Facebook.
    句中的 "designated as a historic site"（被指定為古蹟）以及 "a protected historic site"（受保護的歷史古蹟）皆利用過去分詞具備被動與形容詞化的特質，用來修飾前方或後方的名詞，是多益測驗中測試分詞詞性的經典句構。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (-ing 分詞作補充說明)
    The work was painted in 1960 by Chen Yu-feng (陳玉峰), making restoration particularly difficult, Cheap wrote, adding that the Spanish man was fined just NT$15,000.
    句尾的 ", making restoration particularly difficult" 與 ", adding that..." 是分詞構句，由前句整句或主詞的動作引伸出自然的結果或伴隨動作，不需連接詞就能流暢地連接附加資訊，有助於提升英文寫作與閱讀的流暢度。
  • 表條件或讓步的副詞子句 (regardless of)
    ...she came to understand that regardless of her intentions or motives, she should have made a greater effort to learn about the culture, history and laws of other countries...
    片語 "regardless of"（不管、不顧）後面接名詞片語（her intentions or motives），等同於 "no matter what her intentions or motives were"。此片語在商業書信或多益閱讀中常被用來表達不受某些條件限制。
