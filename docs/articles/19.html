藝人為日本雕像事件致歉

Entertainer apologizes for Japan statue incident

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • deface v. 塗污、損毀（建築物或文物的外觀）
    Taiwanese entertainer Tsai Yi-chen (蔡頤榛) on Thursday apologized for defacing a Japanese national treasure at a temple in Nara in April.
  • stop short of phr. 差一點就…；決定不做/不提到（某件事）
    Prior to the post on Thursday, her manager in a statement had stopped short of confirming whether Tsai, who goes by the stage name Wu-hsiung (五熊), was being investigated by Japanese authorities.
  • distress n. 痛苦、困擾、悲傷
    “For my ignorance and actions in this incident, which offended Yakushiji Temple and caused distress and harm to people in Japan, I would like to offer my sincerest apologies to everyone in Japan, as well as everyone at Yakushiji Temple who was affected,” Tsai wrote on Thursday.
  • indict v. 起訴、控告
    NHK reported that prosecutors said Tsai was indicted on Wednesday, with the Nara Summary Court fining her ￥500,000 (US$3,197).
  • remorse n. 悔恨、深刻的悔意
    “I will continue to offer my sincere apologies to Yakushiji Temple and express my remorse for what I did.”
  • toluene n. 甲苯（有機溶劑）
    The man applied toluene and mineral spirits on the temple doors, and scrubbed them, causing the painted decorations to dissolve and peel off, the influencer wrote.
  • vandalize v. 蓄意破壞（公物、藝術品或古蹟）
    “No wonder so many historic sites in Taiwan are destroyed by fire or vandalized,” he added.
  • contravention n. 違反、違背（法律或規定）
    Prosecutors in the Cixian Temple case declined to charge the Spaniard over contraventions of the Cultural Heritage Preservation Act (文化資產保存法) — which stipulates six months to five years’ imprisonment and a fine of NT$500,000 to NT$20 million for intentionally damaging a monument — due to insufficient evidence that he knew the temple was a protected historic site.
  • stipulate v. （法律、條款）規定、約定
    ...which stipulates six months to five years’ imprisonment and a fine of NT$500,000 to NT$20 million for intentionally damaging a monument...

文法重點

  • 非限定關係子句（Non-defining Relative Clause）
    Prior to the post on Thursday, her manager in a statement had stopped short of confirming whether Tsai, who goes by the stage name Wu-hsiung (五熊), was being investigated by Japanese authorities.
    此處使用「, who goes by the stage name Wu-hsiung (五熊),」作為非限定關係子句，插在專有名詞 Tsai 後面，用來對主詞補充說明補充資訊（藝名為五熊）。這類子句前後會用逗號隔開，若移除該子句，主要句子的文法結構依然完整。
  • 分詞構句表示結果（Participle Phrase Expressing Result）
    The man applied toluene and mineral spirits on the temple doors, and scrubbed them, causing the painted decorations to dissolve and peel off, the influencer wrote.
    句中的「causing the painted decorations to dissolve and peel off」為現在分詞構句。此處省略了連接詞（如 and thereby caused...），直接用 V-ing 表示前面動作（塗抹塗料並洗刷廟門）自然導致的「結果」。
  • 情態助動詞的過去式推論/反省（Should have + V-p.p.）
    ...she should have made a greater effort to learn about the culture, history and laws of other countries, particularly cultural properties of significant historical and cultural value.
    「should have + 過去分詞 (p.p.)」是用於檢討過去事件的文法結構，表示「當時本來應該做某事，但實際上卻沒有做」，帶有遺憾、反省或責備的意味。
  • 「No wonder...」句型（難怪…）
    “No wonder so many historic sites in Taiwan are destroyed by fire or vandalized,” he added.
    「No wonder」是「It is no wonder that...」的口語省略形式，意為「難怪…」或「不足為奇」。後面直接接一個完整的名詞子句（so many historic sites... are destroyed...），用來表達對某個現象或結果的合理化感受。
