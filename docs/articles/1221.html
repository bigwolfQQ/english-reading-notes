魯拉與波索納洛將進入 10 月 27 日的總統決選

Lula, Bolsonaro head to Oct. 27 presidential runoff

單字解析

  • majority n. 多數，過半數
    after neither won a majority in Sunday’s vote.
    在週日的投票中皆未獲得過半數選票後。
  • incumbent n. 現任者，在職者
    said neither the 80-year-old incumbent, seeking his fourth non-consecutive term, nor the 45-year-old son...
    表示這名尋求第四個非連續任期的 80 歲現任總統，以及...
  • term n. 任期，期間
    The next presidential term is set to begin on Jan. 5.
    下一屆總統任期預定於 1 月 5 日開始。
  • endorse v. 背書，公開支持
    Augusto Cury, a writer who finished in third place with 3.4 million votes, said he would not endorse either in the runoff.
    以 340 萬票在第三名作收的作家奧古斯都·庫里表示，他不會在決選中背書任何一方。
  • capitalize on phr. v. 利用……以獲利，從中獲益
    Lula is expected to try to capitalize on the popularity of his social policies and his rival’s many legal woes.
    預計魯拉將試圖利用其社會政策的民氣以及對手許多的法律麻煩來獲取政治利益。
  • rival n. 對手，競爭者
    and his rival’s many legal woes.
    以及對手許多的法律麻煩。
  • approval rating n. 民意支持率，滿意度
    While the once widely popular Lula saw his approval ratings plummet during his third term...
    儘管曾經廣受歡迎的魯拉在第三任期內滿意度直落...
  • plummet v. 暴跌，急速下降
    saw his approval ratings plummet during his third term...
    滿意度直落...
  • affordability n. （價格、成本的）負擔能力
    However, he faces a tough battle because of an affordability crisis...
    然而，由於負擔能力危機...
  • scandal n. 醜聞
    ...and a corruption scandal that ensnared the same justice who led the investigation into his rival.
    以及一場牽連了曾主導調查其對手的同一名法官的貪腐醜聞。
  • strike a deal phr. 達成協議，做成交易
    and insisted that Brazil needs to strike better deals with the Trump administration...
    並堅持認為巴西需要與川普政府達成更好的協議...
  • disapprove v. 不贊同，反對
    Roughly half of Brazil’s population disapproves of the Bolsonaro family...
    大約有一半的巴西人口不贊同波索納洛家族...

文法重點

  • be to + V (表示預定、計畫或即將發生的官方安排)
    Brazilian Senator Flavio Bolsonaro... and Brazilian President Luiz Inacio Lula da Silva are to face off in a runoff on Oct. 25...
    「be to + 原形動詞」常出現在新聞英語中，用來表示官方預定、計畫好或即將發生的重要行程或安排。
  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (分詞構句/形容詞片語)
    ...said neither the 80-year-old incumbent, seeking his fourth non-consecutive term, nor the 45-year-old son... would garner the majority vote...
    句中的「seeking his fourth non-consecutive term」是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前方現任總統的狀態，相當於「who was seeking his fourth non-consecutive term」的簡化形式。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The next presidential term is set to begin on Jan. 5.
    此句使用被動語態「is set to」，強調總統任期被安排、預定於 1 月 5 日開始，在商業與新聞報導中非常常見。
  • 限定性關係代間子句 (Relative Clause with 'that')
    Augusto Cury, a writer who finished in third place with 3.4 million votes, said he would not endorse either in the runoff.
    句中的「who finished in third place with 3.4 million votes」是關係代間子句，用來修飾前面的先行詞 a writer，補充說明該作家的得票名次與票數。
