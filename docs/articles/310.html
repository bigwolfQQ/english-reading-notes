  • commerce n. 商業、貿易
    Taiwan does not need a CIA deputy director to explain that Beijing treats commerce as a battlefield.
    台灣不需要中情局副局長來解釋北京把商業當作戰場。
  • poach v. 挖角、非法捕獵
    It has been living that lesson for decades, watching its chip designers get poached, its retired officers get recruited for cash...
    它體驗這個教訓已經幾十年了，眼睜睜看著自己的晶片設計師被挖角、退休軍官被金錢收買...
  • recruit v. 招募、吸收（人員）
    ...its retired officers get recruited for cash, and its companies get pressured to choose between the Chinese market and their own government’s security priorities.
    ...退休軍官被金錢收買，以及企業被迫在中國市場和自己政府的安全優先事項之間做出選擇。

    ...且當中國共產黨可以隨意吸收或指揮中國企業時，就不能將其視為真正的民營企業。

文法重點

  • What 引導的名詞子句（當作主詞）
    What changed this month is that Washington finally said the quiet part out loud.
    「What changed this month」是由 what 引導的名詞子句，在句中擔任整個句子的主詞，意為「這個月改變的事（是……）」。
  • 被動語態的結構 (get + 過去分詞)
    It has been living that lesson for decades, watching its chip designers get poached, its retired officers get recruited for cash...
    句中使用「get + 過去分詞」（如 get poached、get recruited）來表達被動語態，口語或新聞報導中常用此結構來代替 「be + 過去分詞」，強調動作的發生或遭遇。
  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾
    The same week Ellis spoke, federal agents in Detroit charged a Chinese national, Zeng Weiheng, with lying about why he had spent 20 minutes photographing cargo planes at O’Hare International Airport.
    句尾的「photographing cargo planes at O’Hare International Airport」為現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的動名詞或說明原因，呈現動作的持續狀態。
  • 假設語氣（過去式表示現在的假設）
    If the CIA argues publicly that a Chinese semiconductor firm cannot be assumed independent of the CCP, then the same logic applies with more force...
    此處使用標準的「If + 現在式動詞, 主句 + 現在式助動詞」的真實條件句型，用來推論當前具體情況下的邏輯推演。
