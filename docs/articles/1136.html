軍方尋求將彈藥庫存量增至四倍

Military seeks to quadruple ammo stockpile

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • aim v. 旨在、計畫、企圖
    The military aims to quadruple its stockpile of combat ammunition from 30 days to 120 days under a six-year, NT$27.59 billion procurement program.
    根據一項為期六年、金額275.9億元的採購計畫，軍方旨在將戰鬥彈藥的庫存從30天增加到120天。
  • stockpile n. 儲備、庫存
    The military aims to quadruple its stockpile of combat ammunition from 30 days to 120 days under a six-year procurement program.
    根據一項為期六年的採購計畫，軍方旨在將戰鬥彈藥的庫存從30天增加到120天。
  • procurement n. 採購、取得
    The defense budget documents for next year that the ministry submitted to the legislature showed that it is not a single-year procurement, but a six-year program.
    國防部提交給立法院的明年度國防預算文件顯示，這並非單一年度的採購，而是一項為期六年的計畫。
  • bolster v. 支持、鞏固、增強
    The procurement program [is] to bolster its ability to sustain operations in a blockade.
    該採購計畫旨在增強其在封鎖中維持作戰的能力。
  • allocate v. 撥出、分配（經費、資源等）
    The ministry allocated NT$7.3 billion for the ammunition replenishment program after the Executive Yuan approved it on Sept. 3.
    行政院於9月3日批准該彈藥補充計畫後，國防部已編列（撥款）73億元。
  • replenish v. 補充、把...再裝滿
    Department of Strategic Planning Director Lieutenant General Huang Wen-chi earlier this month said the program must be accelerated to replenish ammunition depleted during military exercises and drills.
    戰略規劃司司長黃文啟中將於本月初表示，必須加速該計畫，以補充在軍事演習和操演中耗盡的彈藥。
  • prioritize v. 把...列為優先、優先處理
    The military plans to prioritize one-off purchases of 120mm and 105mm tank rounds...
    軍方計畫優先一次性採購120公釐與105公釐戰車砲彈...
  • capacity n. 容量、生產能力、產能
    The purpose was to replenish the ammunition stockpile and increase the domestic production capacity for ammunition.
    此目的在於補充彈藥庫存並提高國內彈藥生產能力。
  • exclude v. 排除、排斥
    However, the legislature capped the special defense budget at NT$780 billion, prioritizing the procurement of US military weapons while excluding funding for general-purpose ammunition purchases...
    然而，立法院將特別國防預算上限設為7,800億元，優先採購美軍武器，同時排除通用彈藥採購的資金...

文法重點

  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    The defense budget documents for next year that the ministry submitted to the legislature showed that it is not a single-year procurement, but a six-year program.
    句中的「that the ministry submitted to the legislature」是用來修飾前面的先行詞「The defense budget documents for next year」。關係代名詞 that 在子句中作為受詞（submited 的受詞），在口語或非正式書寫中常可省略。
  • 現在分詞作後位修飾
    Department of Strategic Planning Director Lieutenant General Huang Wen-chi earlier this month said the program must be accelerated to replenish ammunition depleted during military exercises and drills, which have become more frequent in the past few years.
    句中的「depleted during military exercises and drills」是過去分詞片語，用來後位修飾前面的名詞「ammunition」。完整結構相當於「ammunition (which was) depleted...」，省略了「which was」使句子更為精簡。
  • 對等連接詞連接長句與分詞構句
    However, the legislature capped the special defense budget at NT$780 billion, prioritizing the procurement of US military weapons while excluding funding for general-purpose ammunition purchases...
    句尾使用「prioritizing... while excluding...」的現在分詞結構，用來補充說明主句動作發生時的伴隨狀況或結果，使語意更緊湊，展現專業新聞英文的精鍊風格。
