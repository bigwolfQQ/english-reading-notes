  • conclude v. 結束，告一段落

    在結束對史丹佛大學的訪問後，蔡英文連續第二天會見矽谷的科技新創與創投界成員……
  • venture capital n. 風險投資，創投
    After concluding her visit to Stanford University, Tsai met with members of Silicon Valley's tech startup and venture capital communities for a second consecutive day...
    在結束對史丹佛大學的訪問後，蔡英文連續第二天會見矽谷的科技新創與創投界成員……
  • sector n. 部門，行業，領域
    ...she wanted to meet Taiwanese people working, studying or pursuing entrepreneurial ventures in the area, particularly those in the technology sector.
    ……她也希望見見在當地工作、求學或進行創業投資的台灣人，特別是那些身處科技領域的人。
  • invest v. 投資（相關名詞為 investment）
    Tsai urged young Taiwanese in the tech sector to share Taiwan's experience and stories with the world while bringing back valuable insights from Silicon Valley in technology, talent, investment and new ways of working.
    蔡英文敦促在科技領域的台灣年輕人與世界分享台灣的經驗與故事，同時將矽谷在技術、人才、投資以及全新工作方式方面的寶貴見解帶回台灣。
  • opportunity n. 機會，良機
    A country's strength lies not only in its government and businesses, but also in people, especially young people willing to take risks, she said, adding that Taiwanese youths pursuing opportunities in Silicon Valley are an important source of strength for Taiwan.
    她表示，國家的力量不僅在於政府和企業，更在於人民，特別是勇于承擔風險的年輕人，並補充說，在矽谷尋求機會的台灣青年是台灣力量的重要來源。
  • schedule v. 預定，安排（常用被動語態 be scheduled to）

  • invitation n. 邀請

  • enterprise n. 企業，創業（文章中使用 entrepreneurial 形容詞）

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    In addition to accepting an invitation from Stanford University, she wanted to meet Taiwanese people working, studying or pursuing entrepreneurial ventures in the area...
    句中的「working, studying or pursuing...」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「Taiwanese people」，相當於形容詞子句「who were working, studying...」，省略了關代與be動詞，使句子更為簡潔有力。
  • 對稱結構與否定副詞片語 (Correlative Structure & Negative Not Only... But Also...)
    A country's strength lies not only in its government and businesses, but also in people...
    本句使用經典的對稱連接詞「not only... but also...」（不僅……而且……），連接兩個介系詞片語「in its government and businesses」與「in people」，表達範圍的遞進，在多益閱讀與寫作測驗中非常常考。
  • 分詞構句作附加說明 (Participial Phrase for Supplementary Information)
    ...she said, adding that Taiwanese youths pursuing opportunities in Silicon Valley are an important source of strength for Taiwan.
    句尾的「adding that...」是由分詞構句簡化而來，原意為「and she added that...」，用來補充說明主句主詞在說話當下的額外動作或發言內容，能增加新聞敘事的流暢度。
  • 介系詞片語置於句首作時間狀語 (Prepositional Phrase of Time at the Beginning)
    San Francisco, Oct. 10 (CNA) Former President Tsai Ing-wen (蔡英文) called for stronger two-way ties between Taiwan and Silicon Valley at a meeting with young tech entrepreneurs at the Taipei Economic and Cultural Office in San Francisco on Friday.
    新聞報導慣常將時間、地點等介系詞片語（如 on Friday、at a meeting）放在句尾或句首交代背景，幫助讀者快速掌握事件發生的時空脈絡。
