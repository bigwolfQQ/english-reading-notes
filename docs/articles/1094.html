Aircraft survey the route for National Day rehearsals

  • conduct v. 執行、實施、指揮
    Fixed-wing aircraft and helicopters from the three branches of the military yesterday morning conducted a route survey ahead of flight rehearsals for this year’s Double Ten National Day celebrations...
    國軍三軍部隊的固定翼機與直升機昨天上午針對今年雙十國慶慶祝活動的預演進行了航線勘察...
  • rehearsal n. 預演、排練

    ...針對今年雙十國慶慶祝活動的預演進行了航線勘察...
  • celebration n. 慶祝活動、慶典
    ...ahead of flight rehearsals for this year’s Double Ten National Day celebrations...
    ...針對今年雙十國慶慶祝活動的預演...

  • incorporate v. 納入、結合、包含

文法重點

  • be to + V (表預定計畫、官方安排)
    On National Day, each fighter type is to fly in a five-aircraft “V” formation and conduct a fly-by over the Presidential Office in Taipei, the ministry said.
    在新聞英語與正式公告中，「be to + 原形動詞」常用來表示官方已安排好的預定計畫、行程或命令。
  • with + 名詞 + 分詞 (附帶狀況)
    ...with the Ministry of National Defense promising a spectacle similar to previous years while incorporating more challenging formation maneuvers.
    「with + O. + V-ing/P.P.」結構用來補充說明主句動作發生時的伴隨狀態，在此句中用來描述國防部在進行預演的同時，也伴隨著做出承諾。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾
    ...including CH-47SD Chinooks, which are to open the National Day celebrations by flying in formation while carrying giant national flags, the ministry said.
    句中的 "carrying giant national flags" 為現在分詞片語，前面省略了主詞與be動詞 (which are)，用來修飾前面的 flying 動作，表達「一邊飛一邊攜帶巨幅國旗」的同時發生動作。
