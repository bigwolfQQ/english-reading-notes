台灣的主權 AI 悖論：我們究竟所指的主權為何？

Taiwan’s sovereign AI paradox: What exactly do we mean by sovereign?

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • contribute v. 貢獻、捐獻
    On Sept. 15, the Ministry of Digital Affairs (MODA) invited publishers, writers and electronic-book platforms to contribute to Taiwan’s Sovereign AI Training Corpus.
    9月15日，數位發展部（MODA）邀請了出版商、作家和電子書平台為台灣的主權 AI 訓練語料庫做出貢獻。
  • dependence n. 依賴、信賴
    Many countries are reconsidering dependence on US and Chinese technology companies.
    許多國家正在重新考慮對美國和中國科技公司的依賴。
  • dimension n. 面向、維度、規模
    Here, sovereign AI has three dimensions: Knowledge sovereignty means AI understands Taiwan accurately...
    在這裡，主權 AI 具有三個維度：知識主權意指 AI 能夠準確理解台灣……
  • transparency n. 透明度
    Switzerland’s open Apertus model similarly prioritizes transparency and local control rather than competing with frontier models.
    瑞士開源的 Apertus model 同樣優先考慮透明度與本地控制，而非與前沿模型競爭。
  • handle v. 處理、應對
    Taiwan launched the government-backed Trustworthy AI Dialogue Engine, or TAIDE, in 2023 to better handle Traditional Chinese and Taiwanese knowledge.
    台灣於 2023 年推出了政府支持的「可信賴AI對話引擎」（TAIDE），以更好地處理繁體中文與台灣知識。
  • economics n. 經濟效益、經濟學
    Even in 2023, iKala founder Sega Cheng (程世嘉) questioned the economics of building a national foundation model from scratch.
    早在 2023 年，iKala 創辦人程世嘉就對從零開始建立國家基礎模型的經濟效益提出了質疑。
  • purpose n. 目的、意图
    The scales are different because the datasets serve different purposes: foundation models need vast, diverse data...
    規模之所以不同，是因為資料集服務於不同的目的：基礎模型需要龐大且多樣化的資料……
  • improvement n. 改善、進步
    That is a clear improvement, but 58 percent still means getting roughly four questions in 10 wrong.
    這是一個明顯的進步，但 58% 仍然意味著每 10 題中大約有 4 題答錯。
  • strategy n. 策略、戰略
    That compromise helps explain why Taiwan’s strategy has expanded beyond TAIDE.
    這種妥協有助於解釋為什麼台灣的策略已經超越了 TAIDE。
  • guarantee v. 保證、擔保
    Still, while RAG can improve accuracy, it can’t guarantee it.
    儘管如此，雖然 RAG 可以提高準確性，但它無法保證百分之百準確。
  • deploy v. 部署、調動
    As Chang’s comments suggest, complex tasks may benefit from stronger reasoning models, while sensitive tasks may require those models to be deployed locally.
    正如張詠淳的評論所暗示的那樣，複雜的任務可能會受益於更強大的推理模型，而敏感的任務可能需要這些模型在本地部署。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表附帶狀況或結果）
    Chinese models can carry political assumptions shaped by the Chinese Communist Party, while American companies control access, prices and development of the most capable frontier systems.
    句中的 "shaped by the Chinese Communist Party" 是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的 "political assumptions"（政治假設），省略了 "which are"，用來補充說明假設是被中國共產黨所形塑的。
  • 現在分詞作後位修飾
    If Taiwan closes it by relying on proprietary foreign frontier models, it gains capability at the cost of technological sovereignty.
    句中的 "relying on proprietary foreign frontier models" 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前方的介系詞 "by"，說明「依靠專有外國前沿模型」這個動作是如何執行的。
  • 名詞子句作受詞
    Digital Minister Lin Yi-jing (林宜敬) explained that an AI model’s understanding of ideas such as democracy and checks and balances is shaped by its training data.
    句中的 "that an AI model’s understanding... is shaped by its training data" 是由連接詞 "that" 引導的名詞子句，在句中擔任動詞 "explained" 的受詞，表達數位部長解釋的內容。
  • 假設語氣（與現在事實相反）
    “Anyone with some sense of the costs would not rush headlong into training their own foundation model,” he said.
    句型使用了 "would + 原形動詞"，表達在假設情況下（如果有人對成本有概念）會產生的結果，用來提出委婉或強烈的觀點。
