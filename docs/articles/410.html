文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    UG Investment Advisers, a hedge fund overseeing US$3.7 billion, is targeting Taiwan’s wealthy as a new source of capital...
    句中的「overseeing US$3.7 billion」是由現在分詞 overseeing 開頭的分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞片語 a hedge fund，等於形容詞子句「which oversees US$3.7 billion」。這種用法在商業與新聞英語中非常常見，能讓句子更精簡流暢。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Absolute Participial Construction)
    ...betting on a surge in private fortunes fueled by the nation’s artificial intelligence (AI) boom.
    句尾的「fueled by the nation’s artificial intelligence (AI) boom」是過去分詞片語作後位修飾，用來修飾 fortunes。其完整概念是被動語態的省略（which is fueled by...），省略了格位代名詞與be動詞，直接用過去分詞表達被動與完成的附帶狀況。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    The move marks a homecoming of sorts for the hedge fund, which has roots in Taipei, but has largely relied on offshore investors such as Asian and European family offices for almost three decades.
    逗號之後的「which has roots in Taipei...」是非限定關係子句，對前面的先行詞 the hedge fund 提供額外的補充說明。非限定子句不可使用 that，且如果刪除這個子句，主要子句的語意仍然完整。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句補充說明整句或特定名詞 (Non-defining Relative Clause with 'which')
    UG’s largest fund, which it plans to offer in Taiwan, manages more than US$2 billion and focuses on technology stocks across the US and Asia, Chen said.
    夾在主詞和動詞之間的「which it plans to offer in Taiwan」是一個被逗號隔開的非限定關係子句，補充說明 UG’s largest fund 的相關計畫。閱讀時可以先跳過這個子句，優先抓出主幹「UG’s largest fund manages...」。
