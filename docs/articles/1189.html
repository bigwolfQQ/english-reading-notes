台北南港生吉隧道通車，縮短通勤時間

Shengji Tunnel in Taipei's Nangang opens, cutting travel time

單字解析

  • ceremony n. 典禮，儀式
    A ceremony was held Monday to mark the opening of the new Shengji Tunnel...
    週一舉行了一場典禮，標誌著全新生吉隧道的開通...
  • connect v. 連接，連結
    ...which connects Zhongxiao East Road with an access road to the National Biotechnology Research Park...
    ...該隧道將忠孝東路與通往國家生技研究園區的聯絡道路連接起來...
  • significantly adv. 顯著地，重大地
    ...significantly reducing travel time.
    ...顯著縮短了旅行時間。
  • statement n. 聲明，陳述
    In a statement, Deputy Interior Minister Wu Tang-an said...
    內政部次長吳堂安在聲明中表示...
  • congestion n. 堵塞，壅塞
    ...the new route is expected to ease traffic congestion during peak hours...
    ...新路線預期能緩解尖峰時段的交通壅塞...
  • peak hours n. phr. 尖峰時段，交通高峰期
    ...during peak hours on Zhongxiao East Road Section 7 and Academia Road...
    ...在忠孝東路七段與研究院路的尖峰時段...
  • employment n. 就業，雇用
    Wu said the NBRP's development has led to business and employment growth in Nangang...
    吳說，國家生技研究園區的發展帶動了南港的商業與就業成長...
  • infrastructure n. 基礎設施
    ...generating greater demand for transportation infrastructure in the area...
    ...產生了對該地區更大的交通基礎設施需求...
  • alternative adj. / n. 替代的；替代方案
    ...the tunnel provides an alternative route if Academia Road is closed...
    ...若研究院路封閉，該隧道提供了一條替代路線...
  • convenient adj. 方便的，便利的
    The new tunnel provides a more convenient route between the NBRP and the area around Nangang Station...
    新隧道在國家生技研究園區與南港車站周邊之間提供了更便利的路線...

文法重點

  • 非限定性關係子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    A ceremony was held Monday to mark the opening of the new Shengji Tunnel, which connects Zhongxiao East Road with an access road...
    句中的「which connects...」前面帶有逗號，屬於非限定性關係子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞（Shengji Tunnel），不能用 that 代替，且若將此子句拿掉，主要子句的意思依然完整。
  • 現在分詞片語作表結果的補語 (Present Participle Phrase indicating result)
    ...which connects Zhongxiao East Road with an access road to the National Biotechnology Research Park (NBRP) in Nangang District, significantly reducing travel time.
    句尾的「significantly reducing travel time」是由現在分詞（reducing）引導的片語，用來表達前面整個動作所產生的自然結果（「因而顯著縮短了...」），在多益閱讀測驗中常考這種分詞構句的用法。
  • 被動語態的應用 (Passive Voice)
    A ceremony was held Monday to mark the opening of the new Shengji Tunnel...
    「was held」是過去式的被動語態（be動詞 + 過去分詞），因為焦點在於「典禮被舉辦」這個事件本身，而非舉辦典禮的人，新聞英語中常大量使用被動語態來客觀報導事件。
  • 被動語態表「預期」（Be expected to）
    ...the new route is expected to ease traffic congestion during peak hours...
    「is expected to」意為「被預期去做……／預計會……」，這是多益商務英文與新聞英語中非常高頻的片語結構，常 用來表達對未來計畫或預測的看法。
