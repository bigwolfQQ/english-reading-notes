文法重點

  • 非限定關係代名詞子句（補充說明）
    The Taiwanese competitors, who are experts in their respective vocational fields, bagged three gold, three silver, and seven bronze medals...
    句中的「who are experts in their respective vocational fields」前面有逗號，屬於非限定關係代名詞子句，用來補充說明前面的主詞「The Taiwanese competitors（台灣選手）」，若省略不影響主要句意。
  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾（被動關係）
    Additionally, Taiwan also won 30 Medallions for Excellence, awarded to competitors who rack up a high number of points in an event...
    句中的「awarded to competitors...」是由過去分詞片語所構成的後置修飾，相當於「which were awarded to competitors...」，用來修飾前面的「30 Medallions for Excellence（30個優勝獎）」，表達「被頒發」的被動概念。
  • 同位語（Appositive）補充說明
    A total of 1,400 contestants from 68 countries and regions participated in the 48th WorldSkills Competition, a biennial event that ran from Tuesday to Sunday last week...
    逗號後方的「a biennial event that ran from Tuesday to Sunday last week」是同位語，用來補充解釋前面的「the 48th WorldSkills Competition（第48屆世界技能競賽）」是什麼樣的活動。
