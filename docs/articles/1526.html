單字解析

  • attend v. 出席，參加
    Minister of Finance Chuang Tsui-yun (莊翠雲) is to attend a meeting of APEC finance ministers in Hong Kong next week, the Ministry of Finance said yesterday.
    財政部昨天表示，財政部長莊翠雲將於下週前往香港出席亞太經濟合作會議（APEC）財政部長會議。
  • schedule v. / n. 預定，安排 / 時程表
    China is this year’s APEC host, with a leaders’ summit scheduled for next month in Shenzhen.
    中國是今年的 APEC 主辦國，領袖峰會預定於下個月在深圳舉行。
  • announce v. 宣佈，公佈
    Taiwan has yet to announce who would attend the leaders’ summit.
    臺灣尚未宣佈將由誰出席領袖峰會。
  • forecast v. / n. 預測，預報
    From Friday through Monday, cloudy to sunny weather is forecast for most parts of the nation...
    從週五到下週一，全臺大部分地區預測為多雲到晴的天氣...
  • host v. / n. 主辦，主持 / 主辦國，主辦者
    Taipei is to host the Taiwan International Travel Fair from Nov. 6 to 9...
    臺北將於 11 月 6 日至 9 日舉辦臺灣國際旅展...
  • exhibition n. 展覽，展售會
    The exhibition at Taipei Nangang Exhibition Center Hall 1 features 1,600 booths representing 123 countries and cities, the association said in a statement.
    該協會在一份聲明中表示，這場在台北南港展覽館一館舉行的展覽共有 1,600 個攤位，代表 123 個國家和地區。
  • feature v. / n. 以...為特色，特徵
    The exhibition at Taipei Nangang Exhibition Center Hall 1 features 1,600 booths representing 123 countries and cities...
    這場在台北南港展覽館一館舉行的展覽共有 1,600 個攤位，代表 123 個國家和地區...
  • participate v. 參加，參與
    Twelve airlines are participating, including first-time exhibitors Etihad Airways and Sun PhuQuoc Airways...
    共有 12 家航空公司參展，其中包括首次參展的阿提哈德航空和太陽富國航空...
  • regular price n. phr. 正常價格，原價

  • convict v. / n. 宣判有罪 / 囚犯

    該裁決維持了士林地方法院判定宿世新謀殺罪名成立的判決。

文法重點

  • be to + V (表示未來計畫或預定發生的官方行程)
    Minister of Finance Chuang Tsui-yun (莊翠雲) is to attend a meeting of APEC finance ministers in Hong Kong next week...
    新聞英語中常使用「be to V」結構來表達官方預定、即將執行的行程或計畫，此處「is to attend」相當於將要出席。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (with + 名詞 + 分詞)
    China is this year’s APEC host, with a leaders’ summit scheduled for next month in Shenzhen.
    「with + 名詞 + 過去分詞」結構用來補充說明伴隨發生的狀況或背景，此處「scheduled for next month」修飾前方的 leaders’ summit（領袖峰會預定於下個月）。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    From Friday through Monday, cloudy to sunny weather is forecast for most parts of the nation...
    句中「is forecast」為被動語態（forecast 的三態同形），用來表達天氣被預測，強調動作的承受者而非氣象預報員本身。
  • 現在分詞作後置修飾語 (Present Participle as Postmodifier)
    Twelve airlines are participating, including first-time exhibitors Etihad Airways and Sun PhuQuoc Airways...
    「including first-time exhibitors...」是由現在分詞引發的片語，用來補充說明參與的航空公司包含哪些初次參展者。
