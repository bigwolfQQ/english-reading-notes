晶片卓越之道無捷徑：台積電高層表示

No shortcut to excellence in chips: TSMC executive

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • executive n. 主管，高階經理人
    There are “no shortcuts” to excellence in chipmaking, said Y.P. Chyn (秦永沛), a top executive at Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co.
    台積電高階主管秦永沛表示，晶片製造的卓越之路「沒有捷徑」。
  • research and development n. 研發（常縮寫為 R&D）
    “For nearly 40 years, I have always believed there are no shortcuts to excellence in semiconductor manufacturing, and technology research and development,” Chyn said.
    秦永沛說：「近 40 年來，我一直相信，在半導體製造以及技術研發方面，通往卓越的道路沒有捷徑。」
  • operations n. 營運，運作
    He said he had worked with TSMC’s operations teams to overcome “countless technical bottlenecks”
    他說，他曾與台積電的營運團隊合作，克服了「無數的技術瓶頸」
  • mass-production n. 大量生產，量產
    and turn advanced manufacturing technologies into mass-production capabilities that helped drive global technological innovation.
    並將先進製造技術轉化為量產能力，進而推動全球的技術創新。
  • foundation n. 基礎，地基
    and that the four-and-a-half years he spent there “laid the foundation for my semiconductor career.”
    並表示他在那裡度過的四年半時間「為我的半導體職涯奠定了基礎」。
  • critical adj. 關鍵的，決定性的
    Chyn called it a “Taiwan miracle” and said the government-backed ITRI had played a critical role at a pivotal time.
    秦永沛稱其為「台灣奇蹟」，並表示政府支持的工研院在關鍵時刻扮演了至關重要的角色。
  • cultivate v. 培養，培育
    He said the ITRI’s efforts to cultivate talent and transfer personnel “laid the talent foundation” for TSMC
    他說，工研院在培育人才和移轉人員方面的努力，在台積電成立時為其「奠定了人才基礎」
  • personnel n. 人員，員工（總稱）
    He said the ITRI’s efforts to cultivate talent and transfer personnel “laid the talent foundation” for TSMC
    他說，工研院在培育人才和移轉人員方面的努力，在台積電成立時為其「奠定了人才基礎」
  • business model n. 商業模式
    Chyn also thanked TSMC founder Morris Chang (張忠謀) for creating the foundry business model
    秦永沛也感謝台積電創辦人張忠謀創造了晶圓代工商業模式
  • colleagues n. 同事，同僚
    He also thanked TSMC chairman and CEO C.C. Wei (魏哲家), former chairman Mark Liu (劉德音), his colleagues and his family.
    他同時感謝台積電董事長兼總裁魏哲家、前董事長劉德音、他的同仁以及他的家屬。
  • contributions n. 貢獻
    Chyn’s contributions have also helped Taiwan “remain at the core of the global supply chain
    秦永沛的貢獻也協助台灣「持續留在全球供應鏈的核心
  • supply chain n. 供應鏈
    Chyn’s contributions have also helped Taiwan “remain at the core of the global supply chain in the era of AI and high-performance computing,” Lai added.
    賴清德補充說，秦永沛的貢獻也協助台灣「在人工智慧與高效能運算的時代中，持續留在全球供應鏈的核心」。

文法重點

  • 地方副詞片語與介系詞片語置於句首的倒裝結構（地方副詞 + 句型）
    Chyn, TSMC’s executive vice president and co-chief operating officer, made the remarks during a ceremony in Hsinchu County’s Jhudong Township (竹東) at which he was inducted as an Industrial Technology Research Institute (ITRI, 工業技術研究院) laureate.
    句中的 at which 引導一個形容詞子句，修飾前面的 ceremony。原本的介系詞片語 "at the ceremony" 因為關代 which 移到句首，構成 "at which" 的結構，用來連接子句並表達「在該典禮中」，在多益閱讀與關係代名詞測驗中非常常見。
  • 現在分詞（Present Participle）作後位修飾
    Chyn also thanked TSMC founder Morris Chang (張忠謀) for creating the foundry business model, which he described as “a business model that changed the world.”
    句尾的 "which he described as..." 是一個非限定關係代名詞子句，用來補充說明前面的 foundry business model。其中 "changed the world" 也是關係子句（that 引導），修飾前面的 business model，展現多益寫作與閱讀中常見的多層次修飾句型。
  • 現在完成式（Present Perfect Tense）的應用
    Chyn’s contributions have also helped Taiwan “remain at the core of the global supply chain in the era of AI and high-performance computing,” Lai added.
    句型使用 "have helped"（現在完成式），表示過去發生的貢獻（contributions）持續影響到現在，並且協助台灣維持在供應鏈核心的狀態。這在商業新聞報導中經常用於強調過去的成就對現在產生的持續性結果。
