俄羅斯在台放映日本相關電影引發質疑

Russian screening of film on Japan raises questions

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • screening n. 放映（電影等）
    Russian officials hosted an unusual screening of an old Soviet documentary called Smashing Militarist Japan for about 20 Taiwanese guests.
    俄羅斯官員為大約 20 名台灣賓客舉辦了一場罕見的放映會，播放一部名為《粉碎軍國主義日本》的老舊蘇聯紀錄片。
  • alarm v. 使驚恐、使擔憂
    Despite the small audience, the screening of the 81-year-old film has alarmed the Taiwanese government.
    儘管觀眾人數不多，這部已有 81 年歷史的電影放映活動已引起台灣政府的警惕。
  • seek v. 尋求、企圖
    Russia is helping China push its narrative of a rising military threat from Japan, and is seeking to sow discord over Taipei’s relations with Tokyo and other Western partners, it said.
    台灣政府表示，俄羅斯正在協助中國推銷日本構成日增軍事威脅的敘述，並企圖在台北與東京及其他西方夥伴的關係之間挑撥離間。
  • prompt v. 促使、激起
    Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi last year suggested a hypothetical Chinese attack on Taiwan could trigger a Japanese military response, causing a diplomatic crisis between Tokyo and Beijing and prompting repeated warnings from Beijing that Japan is seeking a return to its aggressive past.
    日本首相高市早苗去年曾暗示，中國對台灣的假設性攻擊可能會引發日本的軍事回應，這導致東京與北京之間的外交危機，並促使北京一再警告日本企圖重走其侵略過去的老路。
  • respond v. 回答、回應
    The Russian Ministry of Foreign Affairs did not respond to a request for comment.
    俄羅斯外交部未回應置評請求。
  • comment n. 評論、意見
    The Russian Ministry of Foreign Affairs did not respond to a request for comment.
    俄羅斯外交部未回應置評請求。
  • conduct n. 行為、處理
    While Taiwan respects the “lawful conduct of normal exchanges,” activities held by the Russian office “should also respect historical facts and conform to universal values such as democracy and freedom,” the ministry added.
    外交部並補充說，雖然台灣尊重「正常交流的合法行為」，但俄羅斯辦事處舉辦的活動「也應尊重歷史事實，並符合民主與自由等普世價值」。
  • decline v. 婉拒、拒絕
    Staff from the Russian office, including Deputy Representative Sergey Chudodeev, declined entry to Reuters reporters, saying it was an invitation-only event.
    包括副代表謝爾蓋·丘多德耶夫（Sergey Chudodeev）在內的俄羅斯辦事處工作人員拒絕路透社記者進入，稱這是一場僅限受邀者參加的活動。
  • organize v. 組織、籌辦
    After the screening, Russian Representative Pavel Lapin said Moscow’s Taipei office hoped to organize similar events in the future, a person who attended the event said.
    據一位出席活動的人士透露，放映結束後，俄羅斯代表帕維爾·拉平（Pavel Lapin）表示，莫斯科的台北辦事處希望未來能舉辦類似的活動。
  • issue v. 發布、發行
    Last month, China and Russia issued a joint statement warning against remilitarization in the Asia-Pacific region.
    上個月，中俄發表聯合聲明，警告亞太地區不得重新軍事化。
  • dismiss v. 對……不予理會、駁回
    China’s Taiwan Affairs Office dismissed Taiwan’s concerns about the film as “another ploy to manipulate anti-China sentiment for political gain.”
    中國國務院台灣事務辦公室駁斥台灣對該電影的擔憂，稱其為「出於政治目的操弄反中情緒的又一伎倆」。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Present Participle）作後位修飾
    Russia is helping China push its narrative of a rising military threat from Japan...
    句中的「a rising military threat」使用了現在分詞「rising」置於名詞前作形容詞，用來修飾「military threat（軍事威脅）」，表示「正在上升的、不斷增長的」威脅。這種用法在多益閱讀測驗中經常出現在經濟趨勢或營運狀況的描述中（例如：a growing market, a declining profit）。
  • 過去分詞（Past Participle）作後位修飾
    The film shown was 1945’s black-and-white The Defeat of Militarist Japan...
    句中的「The film shown」裡，過去分詞「shown（被放映的）」被置於名詞「The film」後面，作為後位修飾語。這相當於一個省略了的關係代名詞子句「The film (that was) shown」，多益測驗常考這種分詞簡化句型，用來測試考生對動詞被動語態與修飾關係的理解。
  • 名詞子句作受詞（Noun Clause as Object）
    The Chinese Ministry of Foreign Affairs said it was unaware of the screening.
    動詞「said」後面接了一個省略了「that」的名詞子句「it was unaware of the screening」作為其受詞。在商業英文和新聞英文中，「say / state / report + that 子句」是非常高頻的句型，用來引述他人的說法或官方聲明。
  • 被動語態（Passive Voice）
    Freedom of speech is constitutionally protected, but it also has laws... aimed at preventing hostile foreign forces...
    句中的「is... protected」與「aimed at」皆為被動語態結構（be + 過去分詞）。被動語態在強調法律規範、政策推行或客觀事實的新聞報導中極為常見，多益測驗也經常在文法題中測試被動語態的正確動詞變化與時態搭配。
  • 現在完成式（Present Perfect Tense）
    Taiwan has been on Russia’s “unfriendly” countries and territories list since it joined other Western allies...
    句中使用了「has been」，表示從過去某個時間點（加入對俄制裁）持續到現在的狀態。搭配「since」引導的時間副詞子句是多益考試中測驗完成式時態的經典題型。
