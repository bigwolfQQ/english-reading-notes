賴清德會見日本跨黨派議員團，呼籲深化合作

Lai calls for closer cooperation in meeting with Japan parliamentary group

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • cooperation n. 合作
    President William Lai met with the Japanese cross-party parliamentary group... with both sides calling for closer Taiwan-Japan cooperation, the Presidential Office said yesterday.
    總統府昨天表示，總統賴清德會見了日本跨黨派國會議員團……雙方呼籲進一步深化台日合作。
  • advance v. 推進，促進
    During the meeting on Tuesday, Lai said he believed the group would heighten the Diet’s attention to the Strait and help advance former Japanese prime minister Shinzo Abe’s vision of a free and open Indo-Pacific.
    在周二的會面中，賴總統表示，他相信該團體將提高日本國會對台海的關注，並有助於推進日本前首相安倍晉三「自由且開放的印太地區」願景。
  • security n. 安全
    China’s continued military exercises, “gray zone” tactics and transnational repression pose a compound threat to Taiwan and undermine regional security...
    中國持續的軍演、「灰色地帶」戰術以及跨國鎮壓對台灣構成了複合式威脅並破壞了區域安全……
  • urge v. 敦促，力勸
    ...urging Japan and other democracies to join Taiwan in strengthening their defense and deterrence capabilities to safeguard democracy, peace and prosperity.
    ……敦促日本和其他民主國家與台灣一起加強國防與嚇阻能力，以捍衛民主、和平與繁榮。
  • spending n. 開支，經費支出
    Taiwan’s defense spending has reached 3 percent of GDP this year and would remain above that level next year...
    台灣今年的國防支出已達到國內生產毛額（GDP）的3%，明年將維持在該水準之上……
  • industry n. 產業，工業
    Taiwan is also working to bolster its defense industry and boost artificial intelligence and drone capabilities...
    台灣也正致力於強化國防產業，提升人工智慧與無人機能力……
  • strategy n. 策略，戰略
    Lai said he looked forward to deepening Taiwan-Japan cooperation on national strategy, economic and trade security, and high-tech industries.
    賴清德表示，他期盼深化台日在國家戰略、經貿安全及高科技產業方面的合作。
  • delegation n. 代表團
    The delegation was led by Haruo Kitamura, a member of the House of Councilors from the Conservative Party of Japan.
    該訪團由日本保守黨參議員北村晴男率領。
  • aim v. 旨在，目的是
    He said the delegation’s visit aimed to turn Taiwan-Japan friendship into concrete action and continue moving bilateral relations forward.
    他表示，訪團此行旨在將台日友誼化為具體行動，並持續推動雙邊關係向前邁進。
  • crisis n. 危機
    If Taiwan faces a crisis, Japan would do everything in its power to stand with the nation...
    如果台灣面臨危機，日本將竭盡全力與台灣站在一起……
  • promote v. 推動，促進
    Kitamura said he would promote Japanese versions of the Taiwan Travel Act and Taiwan Relations Act, facilitate senior government exchanges and advance defense cooperation.
    北村晴男表示，他將推動日版《台灣旅行法》與《台灣關係法》，促進高層政府交流，並推進國防合作。
  • lobby v. 向……遊說
    He also said he would rally like-minded lawmakers to lobby the Japanese government for stronger Taiwan-Japan ties and peace and stability across the Taiwan Strait.
    他還表示，他將集結理念相近的議員，向日本政府遊說以建立更強健的台日關係及維護台海和平穩定。
  • establish v. 建立，設立
    Separately, Japanese lawmaker Seki Hei... yesterday said that he supports restoring diplomatic relations between Taiwan and Japan and establishing an alliance, including a trilateral alliance with the US.
    另外，日本學者兼議員石平……昨天表示，他支持恢復台日外交關係並建立同盟，包括與美國建立三邊同盟。
  • compromise n. / v. 妥協
    He also urged Taiwan and Japan to abandon any illusions about the CCP and jointly defend freedom and shared values, warning that “any compromise with the Chinese Communist Party will lead to our own destruction.”
    他還敦促台日放棄對中共的任何幻想，共同捍衛自由與共享價值，並警告說「任何對中國共產黨的妥協都將導致我們自己的毀滅」。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（with + 名詞 + 現在分詞）表示附帶狀況
    President William Lai met with the Japanese cross-party parliamentary group “Diet Members’ Association for Peace and Stability in the Taiwan Strait,” with both sides calling for closer Taiwan-Japan cooperation, the Presidential Office said yesterday.
    結構為「with + 受詞 + 分詞」，在此句中用來補充說明總統會面時同時伴隨的狀況（雙方呼籲更緊密的合作）。
  • 分詞構句作補充說明（adding that...）
    Taiwan is also working to bolster its defense industry and boost artificial intelligence and drone capabilities, he said, adding that the nation is developing a resilient, all-out national defense system.
    句尾的「adding that...」是由連接詞省略、主詞一致所簡化而成的分詞構句，用來接續補充說明前面主句所說的內容。
  • 條件句（第一類條件句：If + 現在式, 主持人 + 助動詞過去式/原形...）
    If Taiwan faces a crisis, Japan would do everything in its power to stand with the nation, he said...
  • 被動語態（be + 過去分詞）
    The delegation was led by Haruo Kitamura, a member of the House of Councilors from the Conservative Party of Japan.
    句中使用「was led by」的被動語態結構，強調代表團「被」北村晴男所率領，焦點在於受事者（delegation）。
