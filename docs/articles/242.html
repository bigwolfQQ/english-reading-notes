單字解析

  • department n. 部門、局、處
    From left, Taipei Department of Cultural Affairs Deputy Commissioner Liu Te-chien...
    左起台北市文化局副局長劉得堅……
  • deputy n. 副職、代理人
    ...Taipei Department of Cultural Affairs Deputy Commissioner Liu Te-chien...
    ……台北市文化局副局長劉得堅……
  • commissioner n. 局長、長官、委員
    ...Taipei Department of Cultural Affairs Deputy Commissioner Liu Te-chien...
    ……台北市文化局副局長劉得堅……
  • announcing v. 宣布、宣告（動詞 announce 的現在分詞）
    ...at a news conference in Taipei yesterday announcing the launch of the 2026 Taipei Heritage Days.
    ……昨日在台北舉行的一場記者會上宣布啟動 2026 台北文化資產日。
  • launch n. 發布、推出、啟動
    ...announcing the launch of the 2026 Taipei Heritage Days.
    ……宣布啟動 2026 台北文化資產日。
  • event n. 活動、事件
    The event marks the centenary of the completion of the former US ambassador’s residence...
    該活動標誌著前美國大使官邸完工滿一百週年……
  • mark v. 標誌著、慶祝、紀念
    The event marks the centenary of the completion of the former US ambassador’s residence...
    該活動標誌著前美國大使官邸完工滿一百週年……
  • completion n. 完成、結束
    ...the centenary of the completion of the former US ambassador’s residence...
    ……前美國大使官邸完工滿一百週年……
  • designated adj. 指定的、特定的
    ...a designated municipal historic site...
    ……一個被指定的市定古蹟……
  • feature v. 以……為特色、由……主演/展出
    ...and would feature walking tours of 40 sites under the theme “American-style strolls in Taipei.”
    ……並將以「美式漫遊台北」為主題，推出涵蓋 40 個地點的走讀導覽。
  • theme n. 主題
    ...under the theme “American-style strolls in Taipei.”
    ……以「美式漫遊台北」為主題。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作後置定語
    ...at a news conference in Taipei yesterday announcing the launch of the 2026 Taipei Heritage Days.
    句中的「announcing the launch of the 2026 Taipei Heritage Days」是由現在分詞 announcing 引導的分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞片語「a news conference」，相當於形容詞子句「which announced the launch...」，在新聞英語中常用來簡化句子，增加緊湊感。
  • 過去分詞作形容詞修飾名詞
    ...a designated municipal historic site...
    句中的 designated（被指定的）是過去分詞作形容詞用，置於名詞 municipal historic site（市定古蹟）之前，用來修飾該名詞，表達該古蹟是經過官方「指定的」。
  • 對等連接詞連接並列動詞
    The event marks the centenary of the completion of the former US ambassador’s residence, a designated municipal historic site, and would feature walking tours of 40 sites under the theme “American-style strolls in Taipei.”
    句中由對等連接詞 and 連接兩個共用主語 The event 的動詞片語：第一個是 marks，第二個是 would feature，展現句子的主從結構與時態變化（表達對未來活動的規劃）。
