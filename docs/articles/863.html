Dolly Parton mazes open

單字解析

  • philanthropist n. 慈善家
    Twenty farms have carved large corn mazes to honor the late country music icon, actor and philanthropist, and raise money for her Imagination Library...
    二十座農場刻出了大型玉米田迷宮，以紀念這位已故的鄉村音樂偶像、演員兼慈善家，並為她的「想像力圖書館」籌募資金...
  • raise money phr. 籌募資金
    ...and raise money for her Imagination Library, which provides free books to children.
    ...並為她的「想像力圖書館」籌募資金，該圖書館為兒童提供免費書籍。
  • tribute n. 致敬、悼念
    They ranged from a drive to rename the country music capital’s airport in Parton’s honor to a surge in online streams of 9 to 5, Islands in the Stream and her many other hit songs.
    這些致敬琳瑯滿目，從推動將這座鄉村音樂之都的機場重新命名以紀念巴頓，到線上串流播放〈9 to 5〉、〈Islands in the Stream〉及她許多其他暢銷歌曲的激增。
  • surge n. 急遽上升、激增
    They ranged from a drive to rename the country music capital’s airport in Parton’s honor to a surge in online streams of 9 to 5, Islands in the Stream and her many other hit songs.
    這些致敬琳瑯滿目，從推動將這座鄉村音樂之都的機場重新命名以紀念巴頓，到線上串流播放〈9 to 5〉、〈Islands in the Stream〉及她許多其他暢銷歌曲的激增。
  • consulting company n. 顧問公司

    設計這些迷宮的猶他州諮詢公司 The Maize Inc 的創辦人布雷特·赫布斯特稱巴頓是「真正的美國寶藏」。
  • impact n. 影響、衝擊

    赫布斯特說：「桃莉對世界的影響力和她對農業的熱愛是無法估量的，我們玉米田迷宮的致敬現在有了新的意義和重要性。」
  • annual adj. 年度的、每年的
    The company said the mazes were in the works months before Parton died and are part of its annual campaign honoring country music’s brightest stars.
    該公司表示，這些迷宮在巴頓去世前數月就已在籌備中，是其每年紀念鄉村音樂最耀眼明星活動的一部分。
  • campaign n. （商業、宣傳）活動、運動
    The company said the mazes were in the works months before Parton died and are part of its annual campaign honoring country music’s brightest stars.

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Twenty farms have carved large corn mazes to honor the late country music icon, actor and philanthropist, and raise money for her Imagination Library, which provides free books to children.
    句中的「which provides free books to children」是一個由關係代名詞帶出的非限定關係子句，用來補充說明前面的「Imagination Library（想像力圖書館）」。在多益閱讀測驗中，常用來測試關係代名詞（which / who / that）的選擇或逗號後方不得使用 that 的文法規則。
  • 現在分詞表達被動與狀態 (Present Participle in Passive context)
    Now she is being immortalized in corn, with the mazes in about a dozen states and two provinces, from Alabama to California and Ohio to Texas.
    句型「is being immortalized」結合了現在進行式與被動語態（be + being + p.v.），用來強調「此時此刻正在被永垂不朽地記錄/呈現」的動作。此外，後方的「with the mazes in about a dozen states...」是「with + 受詞 + 介系詞片語」的附帶狀況句型，常用於描述伴隨發生的狀態。
  • 過去分詞作形容詞用 (Past Participle as Adjective)
    However, she came away impressed.
    句中的「impressed」是過去分詞轉換而來的形容詞，用來修飾主詞 she 的心理狀態（感到印象深刻）。多益常考分詞形容詞的字尾變化，修飾人通常用 -ed（如 impressed），修飾事物則用 -ing（如 impressive）。
