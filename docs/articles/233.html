Taipei Times · 2026-09-07

衛生福利部研議採購更多疫苗

Health ministry moots more vaccines

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • purchase v. 採購，購買
    the ministry would purchase more next year.
    部會將於明年採購更多。
  • promote v. 推廣，促進，宣傳
    The Federation for the Welfare of Taiwan’s Seniors yesterday held a news conference in Taipei to promote flu vaccination and disease prevention among older people
    台灣老人福利聯盟昨天在台北舉辦記者會，向長者推廣流感疫苗接種與疾病預防
  • policy n. 政策
    policies for older people should keep up.
    針對長者的政策也應與時俱進。
  • limited adj. 有限的
    the amount purchased this year is very limited
    今年採購的數量非常有限
  • announce v. 宣布
    The Centers for Disease Control (CDC) last month announced that among the more than 7 million doses...
    疾病管制署（CDC）上個月宣布，在超過 700 萬劑...
  • prioritize v. 優先處理，給予優先權（此處為被動態 prioritized）
    200,000 doses of enhanced flu vaccines are included, which would be prioritized for long-term care facilities.
    包含 20 萬劑加強型流感疫苗，將優先提供給長照機構。
  • available adj. 可取得的，可買到的，可配合的
    a vaccine by TTY Biopharm, which would be available from Oct. 1
    一款為台灣東洋藥品生產，將於 10 月 1 日起供應
  • eligible adj. 有資格的，合乎條件的
    residents and workers of 11 types of long-term care facilities would be eligible for the new vaccines.
    11 類長照機構的住民與工作人員將符合接種新疫苗的資格。
  • performance n. 表現，效能，成效
    the enhanced vaccines can improve the defensive performance by about 20 percent.
    但加強型疫苗可將防禦效力提升約 20%。
  • acquire v. 取得，獲得
    then the ministry would try to acquire up to 600,000 doses for the publicly funded vaccination program next year.
    那麼部會明年將嘗試為公費疫苗計畫取得高達 60 萬劑的疫苗。

文法重點

  • 假設語氣（Conditional Sentence）
    Lin Ching-yi yesterday said if a batch of 200,000 doses of enhanced influenza vaccines... prove to be effective, the ministry would purchase more next year.
    此句使用「if + 現在式動詞 (prove)，主詞 + would + 原形動詞 (would purchase)」的句型來表達對未來可能發生情況的假設與預測，是多益閱讀與商業談判中常見的條件句型。
  • 非限定關係子句（Non-defining Relative Clause）
    There are two brands of enhanced flu vaccines this year, a vaccine by TTY Biopharm, which would be available from Oct. 1, and a vaccine by Sanofi that would be available later in the same month...
    句中的「which would be available from Oct. 1」前面帶有逗號，用來補充說明前面 TTY Biopharm 疫苗的額外資訊，這種用法稱為非限定關係子句，在商業英文書信中常用来補充細節。
  • 分詞片語作附加說明（Present Participle as Adverbial Modifier）
    Flu activity is climbing early this year, with more than 100,000 medical visits for flu-like illnesses already reported in a week, and flu viruses are expected to spread faster with students returning to school.
    句尾的「with students returning to school」使用了「with + 名詞 + 現在分詞」的複合結構，用來表達伴隨的情況或原因，常出現在新聞英語及商業報告中以增加句子的流暢度與資訊密度。
  • 被動語態（Passive Voice）
    Taiwan’s COVID-19 prevention performance received international praise, but disease prevention measures should be practiced every day...
    句中的「should be practiced」採用了「助動詞 + be + 過去分詞」的被動語態結構，強調「防疫措施」是「被」落實執行，多益考試中經常測驗助動詞後方搭配被動語態的正確動詞形式。
