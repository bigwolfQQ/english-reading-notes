  • property owner n. phr. 房產所有人、業主
    The four victims were the wife and two children of the property owner, a man surnamed Yu (游), as well as his father, according to the Yilan County Fire Bureau.
    根據宜蘭縣消防局指出，四名罹難者分別為業主游姓男子的妻子、兩個孩子，以及他的父親。
  • emergency treatment n. phr. 急救、緊急治療
    They were found inside the building by firefighters and taken to a hospital, where they were pronounced dead despite emergency treatment, the bureau said.
    消防局表示，他們被消防人員在建築物內部尋獲並送往醫院，經急救後仍宣告不治。
  • report n. 通報、報告
    The bureau said it received a report at 10:36 p.m. Saturday of a fire at a three-story building on Wenquan Road in Yuanshan Township, opposite Yuan Shan Junior High School.
    消防局指出，他們於週六晚間10時36分接獲通報，指員山鄉溫泉路上一棟三層樓建築物發生火災，地點正對著員山國中。

文法重點

  • 非限定關係代名詞子句（補充說明）
    They were found inside the building by firefighters and taken to a hospital, where they were pronounced dead despite emergency treatment, the bureau said.
    句中的「where」引導非限定關係代名詞子句（前面有逗號），用來補充說明先行詞 a hospital（醫院），意指「在該處（醫院裡）他們被宣告不治」。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾
    Firefighters arriving at the scene found flames shooting from the second floor, while explosions could be heard.
    句首的「arriving at the scene」是現在分詞片語，修飾前面的主詞 Firefighters，相當於形容詞子句「who arrived at the scene」，用來簡潔地表達「抵達現場的消防人員」。
  • 被動語態的應用
    The cause of the fire remains under investigation, according to the fire bureau.
    「is under investigation」採用了被動的概念（調查中），強調火災原因「正在被調查」，在新聞英語中經常用來描述事件的後續處理進度。
