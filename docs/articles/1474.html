Taipei Times · 2026-10-12

巴勒斯坦延期舉行立法選舉

Palestine postpones legislative elections

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • postpone v. 延期，延後
    Palestinian President Mahmoud Abbas postponed what would have been the first Palestinian elections in nearly 20 years...
    巴勒斯坦總統馬哈茂德·阿巴斯延後了本將近 20 年來首次的巴勒斯坦選舉...
  • issue v. 發布，頒布（法令、聲明等）
    ...issuing a decree on Friday to further delay voting for the Palestinian Legislative Council almost another year.
    ...他於週五頒布總統令，將巴勒斯坦立法委員會的投票再延後將近一年。
  • decree n. 法令，政令
    Rather than take place as scheduled next month, the decree pushes the elections back to Sept. 11 next year...
    該政令未按原定計畫於下個月舉行，而是將選舉推遲至明年 9 月 11 日...
  • schedule v. 預定，安排
    Presidential elections, which have not taken place for more than two decades, are scheduled for the same day.
    已超過二十年未曾舉行的總統選舉，也排定在同一天舉行。
  • credibility n. 信用，公信力
    ...and threatens to further erode the Palestinian Authority’s credibility among ordinary Palestinians and donor countries who fund it.
    ...並恐將進一步侵蝕巴勒斯坦權力機構在普通巴勒斯坦人以及資助該機構的捐助國心中的公信力。
  • administer v. 管理，治理
    The internationally backed body is dominated by Abbas’ party, Fatah, and has administered semiautonomous parts of the Israeli-occupied West Bank...
    這個獲得國際支持的機構由阿巴斯領導的法塔赫政黨主導，自 1990 年代達成協議以來，一直管理著以色列佔領的約旦河西岸的部分自治地區...
  • statement n. 聲明，陳述
    The Palestine Liberation Organization’s Executive Committee, which Abbas heads, said in a statement that conditions... were not conducive to holding elections...
    由阿巴斯領導的巴勒斯坦解放組織執行委員會在聲明中表示，條件...不利於舉行選舉...
  • evade v. 逃避，規避
    ...is yet another ridiculous lie designed to evade responsibility and accountability.
    ...又是一個旨在逃避責任與交代的荒謬謊言。
  • majority n. 多數，大多數
    Palestinians have not held a legislative election since 2006, when Hamas won a majority of seats.
    自 2006 年哈馬斯贏得過半席次以來，巴勒斯坦人就未曾舉行過立法選舉。
  • contest v. 競爭，參選
    Before the postponement, leading factions had not registered slates to contest the election...
    在延期之前，主要派系尚未登記參選名單...
  • spokesperson n. 發言人
    “Abbas is terrified by the electoral alliance...” Dimitri Dilliani, a spokesperson for Dahlan’s faction, said in a statement.
    達蘭陣營的發言人迪米特里·迪利亞尼在聲明中表示：「阿巴斯對這個選舉聯盟感到恐懼……」
  • tenure n. 任期，職位任職期間
    Abbas has served as president since 2005 and delayed several presidential and legislative elections throughout his tenure.
    阿巴斯自 2005 年起擔任總統，在其任職期間曾多次延遲總統和 legislative 選舉。

文法重點

  • 關係代名詞非限定用法（Non-defining relative clause）
    Presidential elections, which have not taken place for more than two decades, are scheduled for the same day.
    句中的「which have not taken place for more than two decades」用逗號隔開，作為非限定關係子句，用來補充說明主詞 Presidential elections 的額外資訊（已經超過二十年未曾舉行），若刪除該子句，句子的主要意思依然完整。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾（Participle phrase as postmodifier）
    The internationally backed body is dominated by Abbas’ party...
    句中的「internationally backed」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾後方的名詞 body（機構），相當於形容詞子句「which is backed internationally」，在英文新聞寫作中非常常見，能達到精簡句意的效果。
  • 過去完成式（Past Perfect Tense）搭配 since
    Before the postponement, leading factions had not registered slates to contest the election...
    句中使用了過去完成式「had not registered」，用來表達在過去某個特定時間點（the postponement，延期發生時）之前已經完成或尚未完成的動作，凸顯時間點的先後順序。
  • 現在分詞短語表附帶結果（Present participle expressing result）
    Israel and Western countries then boycotted its government, fueling months of unrest that led to the Hamas takeover of Gaza the following year.
    句尾的「fueling months of unrest...」是由現在分詞 fueling 引導的片語，用來補充說明前面主句所導致的結果（助長了數月的動盪），使兩個動作具有連貫性。
