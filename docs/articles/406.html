文法重點

  • 分詞作後位修飾 (Participle as Post-modifier)
    Incidents involving animal harm by unsafe pet products prompted the city’s Animal Protection and Health Inspection Office to develop a safety labeling system...
    句中的「involving animal harm by unsafe pet products」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「Incidents」，相當於形容詞子句「which involved animal harm...」，在多益閱讀測驗中常用於簡化句子結構。
  • 省略 if 的倒裝假設語氣 (Inverted Conditional Clause)
    ...and comply with measures should a major safety incident occur, Huang said.
    原句中的「should a major safety incident occur」是假設語氣的條件句，省略了連接詞 if，將助動詞 should 移至主詞前面形成倒裝句，原形為「if a major safety incident should occur」。這種寫法在商業合約與正式新聞報導中非常常見。
  • 動詞不定式作目的副詞用 (Infinitive of Purpose)
    ...helps manufacturers to design better products and identify potential flaws to prevent harm...
    句尾的「to prevent harm」是動詞不定式片語，在此處作為副詞用，用來表達前面動作的目的（為了預防傷害）。
