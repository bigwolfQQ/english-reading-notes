旅遊：風格獨具的花東縱谷「山嵐號」火車之旅

Travel: Traveling eastern Taiwan in style

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • consideration n. 考量，考慮
    For most train journeys, where you are going is the main consideration.
    對大多數的火車旅程而言，目的地是主要的考量。
  • aesthetic adj. 美學的，審美的
    On this pricey but meticulously crafted train journey running between Hualien and Taitung County’s Chihshang (池上), what you’re paying for isn’t to get from Point A to Point B, but rather a sensory, aesthetic and cultural experience.
    在這趟價格不菲但精心打造、行駛於花蓮與台東縣池上之間的火車旅程中，你付費買到的不是從A點到B點的移動，而是一種感官、美學與文化的體驗。
  • blend v. 混合，融合
    Its exterior is painted in a yellow-green gradient intended to blend harmoniously with the ripening rice paddies of the East Rift Valley floor and the mountains rising up on either side.
    它的外觀漆上黃綠色漸層，旨在與花蓮縱谷平原上成熟的稻田以及兩側隆起的山脈融為一體。
  • quality n. 品質
    Great effort has been made to make the experience feel like fine dining in a high-end restaurant, from the ambiance to the service to the quality of the ingredients.
    從氛圍、服務到品質，為了讓這場體驗感覺像在高檔餐廳享用精緻美食，官方付出了極大的努力。
  • sturdy adj. 堅固的，結實的
    Every passenger has a wide, sturdy tabletop on which to place their food.
    每位乘客都有一個寬敞、堅固的桌面來放置食物。
  • appetite n. 食慾，胃口
    The carefully selected culinary color palette, accented by delicate garnishes and aesthetic ceramic serving dishes, is sure to spur on your appetite.
    精心挑選的烹飪色彩搭配、精緻的裝飾以及美觀的陶瓷餐具，肯定能激發你的食慾。
  • transform v. 使轉變，改造
    Small holes spaced throughout the ceiling light up and transform the dark journey through the railway tunnel into a whimsical starry-night experience.
    天花板上間隔排列的小燈亮起，將穿越鐵路隧道的黑暗旅程轉化為奇幻的星空體驗。
  • source v. 源自，取得（材料等）
    Ingredients are locally sourced from Hualien and Taitung where possible: taro, seafood, tea, red quinoa and Chihshang rice are all featured on the current menu.
    食材盡可能在地採購自花蓮和台東：芋頭、海鮮、茶葉、紅藜以及池上米都列在當前的菜單上。
  • extend v. 延伸，擴展
    This extends beyond the colorful exterior of the train, the panoramic views and the local cuisine, to the cultural tour at the journey’s longest stop, in Yuli.
    這不僅延伸至火車色彩繽紛的外觀、環景視野和在地美食，還延伸至在旅程最長停留站玉里的文化導覽。
  • departure n. 出發，班機/車班離去
    Passengers are free to leave the tour and explore the town on their own if they wish, with strict instructions to return to the station before the departure time.
    乘客若願意，可以自由離隊並自行探索小鎮，但有嚴格指示必須在發車時間前返回車站。
  • intentional adj. 故意的，有意的
    Whether this was an intentional nod to the trace amounts of gold in Taiwan’s mountains or just a bit of decorative flair, I’m still not sure.
    這究竟是對台灣山區微量黃金的一種有意致敬，還是純粹的裝飾風格，我仍不確定。

文法重點

  • 強調句構 (It is / was ... that ...)
    On the Shanlan train (山嵐號) in eastern Taiwan, however, it’s not the “where” but the “how” that matters.
    此句使用了「It is/was not A but B that...」的強調句型，用來凸顯真正重要的核心（不是去哪裡，而是怎麼去），常出現在商業或新聞英文中以加強語氣。
  • 分詞構句作後置修飾 / 附屬子句簡化
    The carefully selected culinary color palette, accented by delicate garnishes and aesthetic ceramic serving dishes, is sure to spur on your appetite.
    句中的「accented by delicate garnishes...」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的主詞「The carefully selected culinary color palette」，相當於原句省略了「which is accented by...」，使句子更為精煉。
  • where 引導的處所關係副詞子句
    The first stop was at Shoufeng Station (壽豐), where we could get out for a few minutes, cross the road, and get a good look at the train...
    句中的「where we could get out...」是用來補充說明先行詞「Shoufeng Station」的非限定關係子句，在先行詞為地點時，以 where 作為關係副詞連接句子。
  • 假設語氣/條件句表達遺憾或不可能
    ...which I did only reluctantly, as it seemed a pity to destroy these works of art.
    此處運用了連接詞 as 引導原因子句，並透過「it seemed a pity to...」的句型表達吃掉藝術品時內心的惋惜與不捨，為常見的描述性寫作句構。
