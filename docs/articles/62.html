Arieh Warshel 成為首位獲得台灣就業金卡的諾貝爾獎得主

  • receive v. 收到，接受
    Nobel chemistry laureate Arieh Warshel has become the first Nobel Prize winner to receive Taiwan's Employment Gold Card, the National Development Council (NDC) said Friday.
    國家發展委員會（國發會）週五表示，諾貝爾化學獎得主亞利耶·瓦謝爾（Arieh Warshel）已成為第一位獲得台灣「就業金卡」的諾貝爾獎得主。

  • present v. 頒發，授予，呈現
    ...NDC chief Yeh Chun-hsien (葉俊顯) presented the card to Warshel, who received the 2013 Nobel Prize in Chemistry...
    ...國發會主委葉俊顯將金卡頒發給瓦謝爾，他曾獲得2013年諾貝爾化學獎...
  • development n. 發展，開發，培育

    擁有美國和以色列雙重國籍的瓦謝爾，目前是南加州大學的傑出教授...
  • invite v. 邀請

    國發會指出，預期瓦謝爾將與台灣研究人員合作，進行半導體及其他關鍵技術的人才培育與研究。

文法重點

  • 非限定關係子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    According to a statement, NDC chief Yeh Chun-hsien presented the card to Warshel, who received the 2013 Nobel Prize in Chemistry with Martin Karplus and Michael Levitt for their development of multiscale models for complex chemical systems.
    句中的「who received...」前面有逗號，屬於非限定關係子句，用來補充說明先行詞 Warshel 的背景資訊。若將這個子句拿掉，主要子句的意思依然完整。
  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Launched by the NDC in 2018, the Employment Gold Card program aims to attract skilled professionals in 12 fields...
    句首的「Launched by the NDC in 2018」是由過去分詞片語構成的修飾語，等於原句為「Which was launched by the NDC in 2018...」，分詞化簡後置於句首，用來修飾主詞 the Employment Gold Card program，表被動與完成。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    He was invited this year by Chen Kuan-neng, dean of the International College of Semiconductor Technology at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University, to serve as an honorary chair professor, the NDC said.
    此句使用被動語態「was invited」，強調動作的承受者（瓦謝爾）而非發動者（陳冠能）。在新聞報導中，當焦點在於受訪者或受邀者時，經常會使用被動語態。
