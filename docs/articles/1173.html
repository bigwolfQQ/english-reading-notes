紐西蘭警方協助迷途海豹寶寶返回大海

New Zealand police assist wayward seal pup to sea

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • law enforcement n. 執法，警察機關
    New Zealand law enforcement personnel came to the rescue of a seal crossing a busy road...
    紐西蘭執法人員日前出面救援一隻橫過繁忙道路的海豹...
  • assist v. 協助，幫助
    New Zealand police assist wayward seal pup to sea
    紐西蘭警方協助迷途海豹寶寶返回大海
  • attempt v. 嘗試，企圖
    The creature then attempted to get back to the water...
    這隻生物隨後試圖返回水域...
  • assistance n. 協助，幫忙
    “The pup appeared grateful for the assistance,” a police statement added.
    「這隻海豹寶寶似乎對協助感到感激，」一份警方聲明補充道。
  • collaborative adj. 合作的，協同的
    “This was collaborative problem-solving which prevented any further issues for the young seal and for road users.”
    「這是團隊合作解決問題，防止了這隻年輕海豹和用路人遇到進一步的問題。」
  • demonstrate v. 證明，展示
    ...so I guess this demonstrates that’s true...
    ...所以我想這證明了這是真的...
  • increase n. 增加，增長
    The Department of Conservation reported this weekend a 200 percent increase in sightings on land...
    保育部本週末通報陸地上的目擊事件激增 200%...
  • alert v. 向……發出警報，使警覺
    Police urged the public to alert them to more runaway seals...
    警方呼籲大眾，若發現更多四處亂跑的海豹應通報警方...
  • intervene v. 介入，干預
    ...and not attempt to intervene themselves.
    ...切勿試圖自行介入。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（用作形容詞修飾名詞）
    New Zealand law enforcement personnel came to the rescue of a seal crossing a busy road...
    句中的「crossing a busy road」是現在分詞片語，用來後置修飾前面的名詞「a seal」，相當於關代子句「who was crossing a busy road」的簡化，讓句子更簡潔。
  • 過去分詞作形容詞用
    The slightly soggy marine mammal was spotted by locals “dangerously” crossing Hutt Road...
    句中的「soggy」為形容詞，而「spotted」則是過去分詞被動用法，與be動詞構成被動語態（was spotted），用以描述海洋哺乳動物「被」當地居民發現。
  • 不定詞作受詞補語
    Police urged the public to alert them to more runaway seals...
    「urge sb. to do sth.」是多益常見的句型，意指「敦促某人做某事」。在此句中，「to alert...」作為動詞「urged」的受詞（the public）之補語，補充說明大眾被要求做的事。
