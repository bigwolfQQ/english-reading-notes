中油：下週國內汽、柴油價格不調整

CPC to leave domestic gasoline, diesel prices unchanged next week

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • domestic adj. 國內的

    台北，9月5日（中央社）儘管國際原油價格飆漲，國營的台灣中油股份有限公司週六表示，下週將維持國內汽油和柴油價格平穩。

文法重點

  • Despite 介詞用法（表「儘管」）
    Despite a spike in international crude oil prices, state-owned oil supplier CPC Corp., Taiwan said Saturday that it will keep its domestic gasoline and diesel prices steady next week.
    “Despite” 是介詞，後面直接接名詞片語（如本句中的 a spike in international crude oil prices），用來表達「儘管……」，相當於 “in spite of”。注意它後面不可直接接子句（主詞＋動詞）。
  • 非限定關代子句（補充說明）
    A stronger Taiwan dollar, which averaged NT$31.682 this week against the U.S. dollar, compared with NT$31.775 last week, however, helped CPC ease crude oil purchasing cost pressure to some extent.
    句中的 “which averaged NT$31.682 this week against the U.S. dollar” 是用逗號隔開的非限定關係代名詞子句，對前面的先行詞 “A stronger Taiwan dollar” 做額外的補充說明。在多益閱讀測驗中常考這種插入語結構，用來測試句子主幹的辨識能力。
  • 未來完成式（Future Perfect Tense）
    CPC estimated that by Sunday it will have absorbed NT$18.72 billion in accumulated losses since the war in the Middle East started...
    “will have absorbed” 是未來完成式，結構為「will + have + 過去分詞」。用來指在未來某一特定時間點（本句中為 “by Sunday”）之前已經完成的動作。
  • 介詞用法的 by（表「藉由……方式」）
    ...by not fully passing higher crude oil costs onto consumers and businesses, per government price stabilization measures.
    句中的 “by” 後面接動名词短語（not fully passing...），用來表示達成某個結果的手段或方法，譯為「藉由……、透過……」。
