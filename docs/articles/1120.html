美伊舉行會談後，和平進展露出曙光

Hope for progress after US, Iran hold talks

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • adversary n. 對手，敵手
    The first shuttle talks between the US and Iran for months raised hopes yesterday that the adversaries could make some progress toward ending the Middle East war...
    美國與伊朗數月來的首次穿梭外交於昨天燃起希望，雙方對手可能在結束中東戰爭方面取得一些進展...
  • progress n. 進步，進展
    ...raised hopes yesterday that the adversaries could make some progress toward ending the Middle East war...
    ...於昨天燃起希望，雙方對手可能在結束中東戰爭方面取得一些進展...
  • interim adj. 過渡期的，暫時的
    ...the first such contacts since an interim ceasefire agreement collapsed in July.
    ...這是自 7 月臨時停火協議破裂以來首次此類接觸。
  • agreement n. 協議，同意
    ...since an interim ceasefire agreement collapsed in July.
    ...自 7 月臨時停火協議破裂以來首次此類接觸。
  • productive adj. 富有成效的
    “Steve and Jared had a very productive meeting today with mediators of Iran, merely mediators.”
    「史蒂夫和賈德今天與伊朗的調解員進行了一場非常富有成效的會面，僅僅是調解員。」
  • deal n. 協議，交易
    ...I think there’s a lot of momentum for them to make a deal.
    ...但我認為他們達成協議的動態非常強烈。
  • engage v. 參與，互動，接洽
    “...because he believes that engaging with people is important if you’re going to solve problems,”
    「...因為他相信如果你要解決問題，與人接觸是很重要的，」
  • convey v. 傳達，轉達
    Iranian Ministry of Foreign Affairs spokesperson Esmail Baghaei said Tehran had engaged with the US through a Qatari mediator in New York and conveyed conditions...
    伊朗外交部發言人伊斯mail·巴蓋伊表示，德黑蘭已在紐約透過卡達調解員與美國接觸，並傳達了條件...
  • threshold n. 門檻，界線
    ...with Brent crude holding just below the US$100-a-barrel threshold it had shot through this month...
    ...與布蘭特原油剛守住本月曾一度衝破的每桶 100 美元門檻...
  • disrupt v. 擾亂，中斷
    ...killing thousands of people, disrupting global energy supplies and spreading to other countries in the region...
    ...造成數千人死亡、擾亂全球能源供應，並蔓延至該地區的其他國家——
  • negotiating table n. phr. 談判桌
    ...even as he called on the Islamic republic to fully reopen the Strait of Hormuz and return to the negotiating table to find a settlement to end the conflict.
    ...儘管他呼籲伊斯蘭共和國全面重啟荷莫茲海峽並重返談判桌以尋求解決衝突的解決方案。
  • settlement n. 解決，協議，和解
    ...return to the negotiating table to find a settlement to end the conflict.
    ...重返談判桌以尋求結束衝突的解決方案。
  • expand v. 擴大，擴展
    ...hailed a new agreement to expand the US military presence in Greenland...
    ...對擴大美國在格陵蘭島軍事存在的新協議表示讚賞...
  • regulation n. 規章，管理，管制
    ...and pushed back against calls for greater regulation of the artificial intelligence industry.
    ...並反對加強對人工智慧產業管制的呼籲。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The war, launched by Trump in February alongside Israel as “Operation Epic Fury,” has lasted nearly seven months, killing thousands of people, disrupting global energy supplies and spreading to other countries in the region...
    句中的 killing、disrupting、spreading 三個現在分詞開頭的片語，用來補充說明前面「戰爭持續了將近七個月」所帶來的同時發生或接續產生的結果，這種用法在英文新聞中極為常見，能讓句子語意緊湊、前後因果關係更流暢。
  • 假設語氣 (Subjunctive Mood / Conditional)
    “This is a president that will meet with anybody for the most part, because he believes that engaging with people is important if you’re going to solve problems,”
    句中的 if you’re going to solve problems 採用了條件句型（if 子句用現在式表示未來可能發達的情況），用來表達「假設要解決問題，與人接觸就很重要」的條件邏輯。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Iranian Minister of Foreign Affairs Abbas Araqchi and US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner communicated through mediators on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly in New York, the first such contacts since an interim ceasefire agreement collapsed in July.
    句中「an interim ceasefire agreement collapsed in July」前面的 since 引導時間副詞子句，用來標示動作發生的起點；此外，過去分詞短語（如 launched by...）也常出現在句中修飾前方名詞，省略了「which was」，讓新聞文字更為精簡。
  • 名詞子句作受詞 (Noun Clause as Object)
    Trump repeated previous assertions that the war could end around the time of November’s US midterm elections...
    that 引導的名詞子句（that the war could end...）在此作同位語，用來補充說明前面的名詞 assertions（斷言、主張）的具體內容是什麼。
