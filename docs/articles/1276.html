AI 3D 成像系統目標在 2028 年前進駐 29 家醫學中心：智捷生醫

AI 3D imaging system targets 29 medical centers by 2028: IntelliGen

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • deploy v. 部署，調動
    with the goal of deploying it at all 29 medical centers in Taiwan by 2028, the company's chairman said Monday.
    該公司董事長週一表示，目標是在 2028 年前將其部署到台灣全部 29 家醫學中心。
  • collaboration n. 合作，協力
    Developed in collaboration with the MOHW, the platform uses the company's Anatomy Cloud AI technology...
    該平台與衛福部合作開發，使用該公司的「解剖雲」AI 技術...
  • convert v. 轉變，轉換
    ...to convert conventional two-dimensional computed tomography (CT) and magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) scans into high-resolution, full-color 3D anatomical models...
    ...能在 3 到 5 分鐘內將傳統的雙維度電腦斷層掃描（CT）與磁振造影（MRI）掃描，轉換成高解析度、全彩的 3D 解剖模型...
  • access v. 存取，接近，使用
    ...the web-based platform allows doctors to access 3D images on computers, smartphones and tablets...
    ...這個基於網頁的平台允許醫師在電腦、智慧型手機和平板電腦上存取 3D 影像...
  • export n. / v. 出口
    ...with the goal of shifting Taiwan's medical technology industry from contract manufacturing toward higher-value intellectual property exports.
    目標是推動台灣醫療科技產業從代工製造轉型為更高價值的智慧財產權輸出。
  • acquisition n. 收購，取得
    Sun said IntelliGen has completed its acquisition of Japanese medical imaging company Holoeyes.
    孫繼信表示智捷生醫已完成對日本醫療影像公司 Holoeyes 的收購。
  • distribution n. 分銷，配送，經銷
    The acquisition combines IntelliGen's AI-based 3D reconstruction technology with Holoeyes' regulatory approvals and distribution network in Japan...
    此次收購將智捷生醫基於 AI 的 3D 重建技術，與 Holoeyes 在日本的法規核准及經銷網路相結合...
  • commercialize v. 商業化
    Sun said the company plans to commercialize the technology through algorithm licensing, white-label manufacturing...
    孫繼信表示，公司計畫透過演算法授權、貼牌製造...來將該技術商業化
  • subscription n. 訂閱
    ...software subscription services and joint development of surgical robots...
    ...軟體訂閱服務以及手術機器人的共同開發...
  • sector n. 部門，行業
    Sun said he hopes Taiwan's medical technology industry can become another globally competitive sector alongside the country's semiconductor industry...
    孫繼信表示，他希望在政府持續支持下，台灣的醫療科技產業能成為繼該國半導體產業之後，另一個具全球競爭力的產業部門。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（With + 受詞 + 現在分詞）表附帶狀況
    with the goal of deploying it at all 29 medical centers in Taiwan by 2028, the company's chairman said Monday.
    「with + 受詞 + 現在分詞 (V-ing)」結構用來補充說明主要動作發生時的背景或伴隨狀態，在此處表示「帶著在 2028 年前部署至 29 家醫學中心的目的」之意。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    An artificial intelligence-powered 3D medical imaging system developed by Taiwanese startup IntelliGen Technology Co. is being tested at the Ministry of Health and Welfare's (MOHW) data center...
    句中使用現在進行式的被動語態「is being tested」，用來強調系統「正在被測試」的動作，焦點在於受試者本身而非執行測試的人。
  • 過去分詞片語當後位修飾語
    Developed in collaboration with the MOHW, the platform uses the company's Anatomy Cloud AI technology...
    句首的「Developed in collaboration with...」是過去分詞片語，修飾後方的主詞 the platform（該平台被共同開發），原句省略了「Which was」，使句子更為精簡流暢。
  • 動名詞片語作受詞與介系詞受詞
    Sun said the company has spent five to six years developing what he described as Taiwan's first domestically developed AI platform...
    句型「spend + 時間 + V-ing」用來表達「花費時間做某事」，這裡的 developing 是動名詞，用作動詞 spent 的受詞之一。
