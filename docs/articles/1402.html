新冠與流感疫苗應同時施打

COVID and flu vaccines should go together

單字解析

  • eligible adj. 符合條件的，有資格的
    The Taiwan Association of Family Medicine (TAFM) yesterday urged eligible vaccine recipients to get seasonal flu and COVID-19 vaccines...
    台灣家庭醫學醫學會（TAFM）昨天敦促符合資格的疫苗接種對象，應同時接種季節性流感和COVID-19疫苗...
  • program n. 計畫，方案
    As the first phase of the government-funded flu and COVID-19 vaccination program began on Thursday last week...
    隨著政府資助的第一階段流感與COVID-19疫苗接種計畫於上週四開始...
  • awareness n. 意識，認識，關注
    ...the TAFM yesterday invited actor Chen Mei-fen (陳美鳳) and Centers for Disease Control (CDC) Director-General Philip Lo (羅一鈞) to help raise public awareness of the issue.
    ...台灣家醫學會昨天邀請演員陳美鳳與疾病管制署（CDC）署長羅一鈞，共同提升大眾對此議題的關注。
  • doses n. 劑（藥量）
    Lo said more than 1.329 million doses of flu vaccines and 482,000 doses of COVID-19 vaccines have been administered in the past six days...
    羅一鈞表示，在過去六天中，已施打超過132.9萬劑流感疫苗和48.2萬劑COVID-19疫苗...
  • administer v. 施用，給予（藥物、疫苗）
    Lo said more than 1.329 million doses of flu vaccines and 482,000 doses of COVID-19 vaccines have been administered in the past six days...
    羅一鈞表示，在過去六天中，已施打超過132.9萬劑流感疫苗和48.2萬劑COVID-19疫苗...
  • respectively adv. 分別，各自地
    ...an increase of 24 percent and 55 percent respectively compared with the first six days in last year’s program.
    ...與去年計畫的前六天相比，分別增加了24%和55%。
  • purchase v. 購買，採購
    The CDC purchased 2.3 million doses of COVID-19 vaccines for the program this year...
    疾管署今年為該計畫採購了230萬劑COVID-19疫苗...
  • available adj. 可取得的，可買到的，可用的
    ...and should be available before the end of the month, he said.
    ...他表示，預計應可在月底前提供施打。
  • fatality rate n. 死亡率
    The fatality rate of those with severe symptoms from COVID-19 is about one in five people, he said...
    他說，COVID-19重症患者的致死率大約是五分之一...
  • up to date phr. 最新標準的，跟上時代的
    ...adding that more than 80 percent of people who died were not up to date on their vaccines.
    ...並補充說，超過80%的死亡病例並未接種最新疫苗。
  • reduce v. 減少，降低
    ...but the new updated COVID-19 vaccine has a smaller dose, reducing physical discomfort.
    ...但全新升級的COVID-19疫苗劑量較小，能減少身體的不適。
  • risk n. 風險，危險
    Wu said studies have suggested that contracting the flu or COVID-19 can increase the risk of cardiovascular events...
    吳至行表示，研究顯示，感染流感或COVID-19會增加發生心血管事件的風險...

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（現在分詞表補充說明或結果）
    ...adding that more than 80 percent of people who died were not up to date on their vaccines.
    句尾的「adding that...」是由連接詞和主詞簡化而來的分詞構句（原句結構約為 and he added that...），用來補充說明前面「COVID-19重症致死率」背後的相關數據與事實。
  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    ...including 200,000 doses of Novavax’s vaccine, which is undergoing lot release testing, and should be available before the end of the month, he said.
    句中的「which is undergoing lot release testing」是非限定用法（Non-defining relative clause）的關係代名詞子句，前面加上逗號，用來補充說明前方「Novavax’s vaccine（諾瓦瓦克斯疫苗）」目前正在進行的狀態。
  • 被動語態的使用
    As the first phase of the government-funded flu and COVID-19 vaccination program began on Thursday last week...
    句中的「government-funded」是由過去分詞構成的複合形容詞，而文章許多地方也運用被動語態（例如 have been administered、were confirmed、are encouraged 等），在新聞英語中用來強調動作的承受者或客觀事實。
