部長希望所有巴勒斯坦人離開加薩

Minister wants all Palestinians out of Gaza

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • unveil v. 公佈，推出（新計畫、產品等）
    Israeli Minister of National Security Itamar Ben Gvir on Thursday unveiled a plan to remove Palestinians from the Gaza Strip and send them abroad.
    以色列國家安全部長伊塔馬爾·本·吉維爾於週四公佈了一項計畫，旨在將巴勒斯坦人移出加薩走廊並將他們送到國外。
  • departure n. 離開，出發
    he said the plan aimed to see the departure of 250,000 Palestinians from Gaza in the first year
    他表示該計畫旨在第一年讓 25万名巴勒斯坦人離開加薩
  • realistic adj. 切合實際的，務實的
    “It’s realistic,” Ben Gvir said
    「這是切合實際的，」本·吉維爾說
  • emigration n. 移居國外，移民
    “We must encourage the emigration of Gaza’s inhabitants,” he said
    「我們必須鼓勵加薩居民移居海外，」他說
  • civilian n. 平民，百姓
    Forced displacement of civilians in occupied territory is considered a war crime under the Geneva Conventions.
    根據《日內瓦公約》，在被佔領土上強迫平民流離失所被視為戰爭罪。
  • demand v. 要求，強烈要求
    Ben Gvir said his right-wing populist Jewish Power party would demand “a ministry in charge of emigration” during the formation of the next government
    本·吉維爾表示，他的右翼民粹主義政黨「猶太力量黨」將在組建下一屆政府期間要求「一個負責移民事務的部委」
  • concrete adj. 具體的，確切的
    adding that his plan was “the result of a year and a half of work and ... is concrete.”
    並補充說他的計畫是「一年半工作的成果，而且……是具體的」。
  • document n. 文件，公文
    In a 20-page document titled “National Work Plan for Voluntary Emigration from the Gaza Strip,” Ben Gvir further says his plan offers a solution to the Israeli-Palestinian conflict.
    在一份題為《加薩走廊自願移民國家工作計畫》的 20 頁文件中，本·吉維爾進一步表示，他的計畫為以巴衝突提供了解決方案。
  • guarantee n. 保證，擔保
    The Oct. 27 vote is expected to be close, and there is no guarantee that the parties currently in power will remain so after then.
    預計 10 月 27 日的投票將十分膠著，且無法保證目前當權的政黨在此之後能繼續執政。
  • approval n. 批准，同意
    Israeli Minister of Defense Israel Katz on Wednesday said his country was ready with plans to remove Palestinians from Gaza if the US gave its approval.
    以色列國防部長以色列·卡茨於週三表示，如果美國批准，他的國家已準備好計畫將巴勒斯坦人移出加薩。
  • advocate v. 提倡，主張
    Katz has for several months been advocating such a measure, presented as the voluntary emigration of Gaza’s population.
    卡茨幾個月來一直提倡這項措施，該措施被包裝為加薩人口的自願移民。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Trump spoke of developing the Mediterranean territory, devastated by Israeli bombardment since the Oct. 7, 2023, attack by Hamas, into a luxury resort destination.
    句中的「devastated by...」是過去分詞片語，用來補充說明前面的名詞片語「the Mediterranean territory（地中海領土）」，相當於形容詞子句「which was devastated by...」，在新聞英語中非常常見，能讓句子更精簡。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Forced displacement of civilians in occupied territory is considered a war crime under the Geneva Conventions.
    句中的「is considered」為現在式被動語態（be + 過去分詞），強調動作的承受者（Forced displacement）而非動作的執行者，在商業與新聞英語中常用於表達客觀事實、規定或普遍認知。
  • 間接引語與過去分詞作形容詞 (Reported Speech and Participle as Adjective)
    Speaking in the run-up to close-fought Israeli elections, he said the plan aimed to see the departure of 250,000 Palestinians from Gaza in the first year...
    句首的「Speaking...」是由現在分詞構成的附屬子句簡化而來，表示伴隨或時間。而「close-fought」則是由副詞/形容詞與過去分詞結合而成的複合形容詞，用來修飾 elections（選舉），意指「競爭激烈的」。
