北韓舉重選手打破三項世界紀錄

North Korean weightlifter sets three world records

單字解析

  • achievement n. 成就、達成
    Ri’s exceptional achievement was cheered to the rafters in a lively atmosphere at the Nagoya Trade and Industry Center hall...
    李聖琴卓越的成就獲得名古屋貿易展覽中心大廳熱烈氣氛中的如雷掌聲……
  • expectations n. 期望、期待
    The people had huge expectations for me...
    人民對我有很高的期望……
  • governing body n. phr. 管理機構、統籌機構
    From Aug. 1, governing body the International Weightlifting Federation introduced new weight classes...
    從8月1日起，管理機構國際舉重總會引入了新的量級……
  • introduce v. 推出、引進、採用
    ...governing body the International Weightlifting Federation introduced new weight classes leading up to the next Olympics and reset all its world records.
    ……管理機構國際舉重總會為迎接下一屆奧運引入了新的量級，並重置了所有的世界紀錄。
  • absent adj. 缺席的、不在的
    North Korea were absent from Tokyo in 2021 because of COVID-19...
    北韓因COVID-19疫情缺席了2021年的東京奧運……
  • qualify v. 取得資格、合格
    ...and the country did not qualify in weightlifting for Paris in 2024.
    ……且該國未能取得2024年巴黎奧運的舉重參賽資格。
  • consider v. 考慮
    However, when Ri was asked if she would consider moving up to 53kg...
    然而，當李聖琴被問及是否會考慮升到53公斤級……
  • category n. 種類、類別、量級
    ...moving up to 53kg, the lowest women’s category to chase Olympic gold...
    ……升到53公斤級，這是追逐奧運金牌的最低女性量級……
  • support n. 支援、支持
    There were expectations, people who believed in me — my coaches, my support staff and I made sure to win a medal for my country...
    有期望，有相信我的人——我的教練、我的支援團隊，我確保為我的國家贏得一面獎牌……

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    The pocket powerhouse, who stands a mere 140cm tall, joined an elite club of just four weightlifters who have won three Asian Games golds.
    句中的「who stands a mere 140cm tall」為形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的「The pocket powerhouse」（袖珍大力士）。在英文中，若要詳細描述某人或某物的特徵，常使用關係代名詞（如 who）引導子句接在名詞後方進行補充說明。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    Ri’s exceptional achievement was cheered to the rafters in a lively atmosphere at the Nagoya Trade and Industry Center hall, made all the more raucous by a large number of Japanese-North Korean fans waving flags.
    句尾的「made all the more raucous by...」是由過去分詞片語簡化而來的後位修飾，用來補充形容前面大廳的熱鬧氣氛是被大量揮舞旗幟的球迷所營造出來的，帶有被動意味。
  • 條件句與假設語氣 (Conditional Clause)
    If an athlete beats the newly introduced world standard mark, they are deemed to have set a new world record.
    此句為第一條件句（First Conditional），用來表達真實的假設狀況。結構為「If + 現在式動詞, 主詞 + 現在式動詞（或beV）」，用以描述在特定條件達成時，必然會產生的結果或事實。
