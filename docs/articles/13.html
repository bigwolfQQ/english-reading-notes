新書探討因應中國日益加劇威脅之策略

Book discusses strategy to face rising Chinese threat

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • societal resilience n. phr. 社會韌性
    Taiwan’s societal resilience and civil defense efforts must match the multi-domain, structural, persistent national security threat posed by China, international analysts said in a book.
  • anthology n. 選集；論文集
    The Defense of Taiwan and Resilience (台灣防衛與韌性) is an anthology of studies by experts from the Institute of National Defense and Security Research (INDSR), Japan and the Philippines, Horizon Publishing (黑體文化) said earlier this week.
  • gray zone n. phr. / adj. phr. 灰色地帶（介於和平與戰爭之間的模糊衝突狀態）
    The massive expansion in China’s military, economic and “gray zone” actions targeting Taiwan significantly complicated the nation’s security challenges, it cited the book as saying.
  • paradigm n. 典範；模式
    Traditional national defense paradigms offer few answers to Beijing’s leveraging of conventional military means, cognitive warfare, human and data-based infiltration, maritime “gray zone” activities and attacks on digital infrastructure, Horizon said.
  • leverage v. 運用；利用（手段或優勢）
    Traditional national defense paradigms offer few answers to Beijing’s leveraging of conventional military means, cognitive warfare, human and data-based infiltration, maritime “gray zone” activities and attacks on digital infrastructure, Horizon said.
  • encompass v. 包含；涵蓋
    The effort to bolster societal resilience cannot be reduced to the mobilization of the public and industries; it must encompass a comprehensive range of strategic capabilities, Horizon said.
  • deter v. 威懾；嚇阻
    This effort, which requires a comprehensive internal reform, is necessary for deterring China and securing support from the nation’s allies and partners, it added.
  • elicit v. 引出；引起（反應）
    The anthology comprises 10 papers on the theory and practice of resilience, analysis of the Chinese People’s Liberation Army’s (PLA) activities in the region and the responses they elicited in Taiwan, Japan and the Philippines, and countermeasures against “gray zone” activities targeting undersea Internet cables and strategic industries, it said.
  • articulate v. 清晰闡明；明確表達
    The book’s most important contribution is articulating the challenges to Taiwan’s quest for defensive resilience, Horizon cited retired army general first class Huo Shou-ye (霍守業), who now chairs the INDSR, as saying.
  • rest on phr. v. 取決於；依賴
    National defense capabilities and deterrence rest not only on the number of weapon systems and forces, but also the public’s will to resist, he said.
  • apparatus n. 機制；機構；裝置
    Young Taiwanese, grassroots-level civil defense, local governments, key industry sectors, the media’s information environment and the nation’s network of allies would be key parts in Taiwan’s national defense apparatus, he said.
  • hinge on phr. v. 取決於；關鍵在於
    Deputy Minister of the Interior Ma Shih-yuan (馬士元) said effective civil defense hinges on society’s vitality.

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作為後位修飾語 (Past Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    Taiwan’s societal resilience and civil defense efforts must match the multi-domain, structural, persistent national security threat posed by China, international analysts said in a book.
    句中的「posed by China」為過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞「national security threat」。這是省略了關係代名詞與動詞「which is / that is」的簡化結構（that is posed by China），意思是「由中國所造成的」國家安全威脅。
  • 非限定關係子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    This effort, which requires a comprehensive internal reform, is necessary for deterring China and securing support from the nation’s allies and partners, it added.
    由逗號隔開的「which requires a comprehensive internal reform」為非限定關係子句，用來對主詞「This effort」補充說明額外細節。刪除此子句後，主要句子的核心結構「This effort is necessary...」依然完全成立且語意完整。
  • 新聞引用語構「cite + O + as + V-ing」
    The book’s most important contribution is articulating the challenges to Taiwan’s quest for defensive resilience, Horizon cited retired army general first class Huo Shou-ye (霍守業), who now chairs the INDSR, as saying.
    句尾採用了常見的新書或報導引述句構「Horizon cited [人物] as saying [引言內容]」，意為「黑體文化引述……的話指出……」。中間並插入了關係子句「who now chairs the INDSR」來補充說明該人物目前的頭銜與身份。
  • 對稱連接詞「not only... but also...」的平行結構
    National defense capabilities and deterrence rest not only on the number of weapon systems and forces, but also the public’s will to resist, he said.
    「not only A, but also B」為表示「不僅A，還有B」的雙重連接詞。使用時兩端須維持文法對稱，本句中 not only 與 but also 後方皆接介系詞片語「on ...」（but also 後方省略了重複的介系詞 on），用以平行強調國防與威懾力所取決的兩大關鍵要素。
