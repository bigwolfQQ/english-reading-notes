新書探討面對不斷升溫的中國威脅之策略

Book discusses strategy to face rising Chinese threat

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • resilience n. 韌性、恢復力
    Taiwan’s societal resilience and civil defense efforts must match the multi-domain, structural, persistent national security threat posed by China, international analysts said in a book.
    國際分析家在一本書中表示，台灣的社會韌性與民防努力，必須與中國構成的多領域、結構性且持續性的國家安全威脅相匹敵。
  • strategic adj. 戰略性的
    The multi-domain, structural and persistent threat posed by China can only be opposed by an all-domain strategic capability that integrates military, societal, economic and psychological defenses within Taiwan, it said.
    該書表示，中國所構成的多領域、結構性且持續性的威脅，唯有透過整合台灣境內的軍事、社會、經濟與心理防衛的全領域戰略能力來加以對抗。
  • mobilization n. 動員
    The effort to bolster societal resilience cannot be reduced to the mobilization of the public and industries; it must encompass a comprehensive range of strategic capabilities, Horizon said.
    黑體文化表示，強化社會韌性的努力不能簡化為公眾與產業的動員；它必須涵蓋廣泛的戰略能力。
  • comprehensive adj. 全面的、綜合的
    This effort, which requires a comprehensive internal reform, is necessary for deterring China and securing support from the nation’s allies and partners, it added.
    該書補充說，這項需要全面內部改革的努力，對於嚇阻中國以及確保來自該國盟友和夥伴的支持是必要的。
  • deter v. 嚇阻、阻止
    This effort, which requires a comprehensive internal reform, is necessary for deterring China and securing support from the nation’s allies and partners, it added.
    該書補充說，這項需要全面內部改革的努力，對於嚇阻中國以及確保來自該國盟友和夥伴的支持是必要的。
  • secure v. 獲得、確保
    This effort, which requires a comprehensive internal reform, is necessary for deterring China and securing support from the nation’s allies and partners, it added.
    該書補充說，這項需要全面內部改革的努力，對於嚇阻中國以及確保來自該國盟友和夥伴的支持是必要的。
  • comprise v. 包含、組成
    The anthology comprises 10 papers on the theory and practice of resilience, analysis of the Chinese People’s Liberation Army’s (PLA) activities in the region and the responses they elicited in Taiwan, Japan and the Philippines, and countermeasures against “gray zone” activities targeting undersea Internet cables and strategic industries, it said.
    該書指出，這本文集包含 10 篇論文，探討韌性的理論與實踐、中國人民解放軍（PLA）在該地區活動之分析以及台灣、日本和菲律賓所做出的反應，還有針對危害海底網際網路電纜與戰略產業之「灰色地帶」活動的因應對策。
  • countermeasure n. 對策、反制措施
    The anthology comprises 10 papers on the theory and practice of resilience, analysis of the Chinese People’s Liberation Army’s (PLA) activities in the region and the responses they elicited in Taiwan, Japan and the Philippines, and countermeasures against “gray zone” activities targeting undersea Internet cables and strategic industries, it said.
    該書指出，這本文集包含 10 篇論文，探討韌性的理論與實踐、中國人民解放軍（PLA）在該地區活動之分析以及台灣、日本和菲律賓所做出的反應，還有針對危害海底網際網路電纜與戰略產業之「灰色地帶」活動的因應對策。
  • contribute v. 貢獻（名詞形式為 contribution）
    The book’s most important contribution is articulating the challenges to Taiwan’s quest for defensive resilience, Horizon cited retired army general first class Huo Shou-ye (霍守業), who now chairs the INDSR, as saying.
    黑體文化引述現任國防安全研究院董事長、退役的一級上將霍守業的話指出，這本書最重要的貢獻在於明確表達了台灣追求防衛韌性所面臨的挑戰。
  • enterprise n. 企業、公司
    The cooperation among volunteer disaster relief workers, volunteer firefighters, religious groups, enterprises and communities during natural disasters has been a heartening development, he said.
    他說，志工救災人員、義消、宗教團體、企業和社區在自然災害期間的合作，是一個令人鼓舞的發展。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞（片語）作後置修飾語
    Taiwan’s societal resilience and civil defense efforts must match the multi-domain, structural, persistent national security threat posed by China, international analysts said in a book.
    句中的「posed by China」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「national security threat（國家安全威脅）」，相當於形容詞子句「that is posed by China」，意指「由中國所構成的」，省略了關代與be動詞，使句子更為簡鍊。
  • 非限定性關係子句
    This effort, which requires a comprehensive internal reform, is necessary for deterring China and securing support from the nation’s allies and partners, it added.
    句中的「which requires a comprehensive internal reform」是由關係代名詞 which 引導的非限定性關係子句，前方帶有逗號，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「This effort」，為整句話提供額外的附加資訊。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞
    The continued existence of the democratic national order, too, depends on its citizens’ collective ability to recognize hostile attempts to infiltrate and divide Taiwanese society, Huo said.
    句尾的「to infiltrate and divide Taiwanese society」是不定詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「attempts（企圖）」，說明這些敵對企圖的內容是什麼，展現多益閱讀中常見的「名詞 + to V」修飾結構。
