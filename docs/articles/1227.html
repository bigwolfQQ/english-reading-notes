民眾黨要求停職並調查僑委會委員長

TPP calls for suspension and probe of OCAC minister

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • suspend v. 使暫停職務、停職、暫緩
    The Taiwan People’s Party (TPP) caucus yesterday called for the government to suspend Overseas Community Affairs Council (OCAC) Minister Hsu Chia-ching
    台灣民眾黨立法院黨團昨天呼籲政府，將僑務委員會委員長徐佳青停職
  • investigate v. 調查、審查
    and investigate her for allegedly bringing her teenage son on an official overseas trip as a “staff photographer”
    並就她涉嫌安排正值青春期的兒子以「隨行攝影師」身分參與官方海外行程一事進行調查
  • official adj. 官方的、公務的
    and allowing her husband to drive diplomatic vehicles for personal use.
    以及允許丈夫為私人目的駕駛公務外交車輛一事進行調查。
  • in contravention of prep. phr. 違反、抵觸
    in contravention of the Act on Recusal of Public Servants Due to Conflicts of Interest (公職人員利益衝突迴避法).
    違反了《公職人員利益衝突迴避法》。
  • repeatedly adv. 反覆地、屢次地
    Hsu had repeatedly brought her son on official overseas trips
    徐佳青曾多次帶兒子參與官方海外行程
  • privilege n. 特權、優惠
    which they said was a “completely shameless abuse of privilege.”
    他們痛批這是「極度厚顏無恥的特權濫用」。
  • accompany v. 陪伴、伴隨
    she allowed her son to accompany her throughout the trip
    她允許兒子全程隨行
  • disregard v. 無視、不理會
    adding that the minister disregarded security risks.
    並補充說這位委員長無視了安全風險。
  • step down phr. v. 下台、辭職
    calling on Hsu to step down immediately and submit to an investigation.
    她呼籲徐佳青立刻下台並接受調查。
  • expenditure n. 經費支出、開支
    all travel plans, staffing arrangements and budget expenditures strictly adhere to the Directions for the Overseas Travel Allowance Disbursement
    所有出差計畫、人員安排與預算支應均嚴格遵守《國外出差旅費報支要點》
  • adhere to phr. v. 遵守、堅持
    strictly adhere to the Directions for the Overseas Travel Allowance Disbursement
    均嚴格遵守《國外出差旅費報支要點》
  • reimbursement n. 費用核銷、報銷、償還
    All expense declarations and reimbursements are subject to strict supervision by audit institutions
    所有的經費申報與核銷均受到審計機關的嚴格監督
  • compliance n. 遵守、依從（動詞形式為 comply with）
    The OCAC’s dispatch of overseas personnel and official vehicles also complies with operational regulations
    僑委會對於海外人員與公務車輛的派遣亦符合作業規定

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作後置修飾與補充說明
    The OCAC said that all travel plans, staffing arrangements and budget expenditures strictly adhere to the Directions for the Overseas Travel Allowance Disbursement (國外出差旅費報支要點) and budget regulations.
    句中雖然是直接列舉主詞（travel plans, staffing arrangements and budget expenditures），但在新聞與商業英文中常使用分詞與連接詞建構長句。例如在第8段中「calling on Hsu to step down...」，分詞片語「calling on...」用來補充說明前面主詞所發出的進一步動作，中文常譯為「並呼籲……」。
  • 被動語態（Passive Voice）在官方聲明中的應用
    All expense declarations and reimbursements are subject to strict supervision by audit institutions, it added.
    多益商業英文與正式公文常使用被動語態（be + p.p.）或類似被動結構（如 be subject to），用來強調客觀事實、規定或程序，而不特別強調施動者。此句中的「are subject to」意指「受...約束、須接受...」，在合約與財經新聞中極為常見。
  • 間接引語與過去完成式的搭配
    The source also said that when Hsu visited France and Germany in 2022, she allowed her son to accompany her throughout the trip...
    當主要子句使用過去式動詞（said）來報導過去的言論時，引導子句描述過去發生的動作也通常使用過去式（visited, allowed）。這種結構在新聞報導他人談話（reported speech）時非常標準，能精確對應時間軸。
