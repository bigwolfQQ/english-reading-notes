文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Passengers are charged a booking service fee for the use of the global distribution system...
    句型結構為「be + 過去分詞 (charged)」。在此強調動作的承受者（Passengers 旅客），說明旅客是被航空公司收取費用，而非主動去收費。
  • regardless of 引導的讓步片語
    ...and applies regardless of whether passengers purchase tickets through an airline’s official Web site, a travel agency or other sales channels.
    「regardless of」意為「不管、不顧」，後面常接「whether 子句」來表達「無論是否……」，用以說明這項收費不受購票管道影響。
  • 過去分詞作後置修飾語 (Past Participle as Postmodifier)
    The latest fee adjustment applies to tickets issued or reissued from Oct. 7, the airline said.
