VP Hsiao sees growing international interest in Taiwan during Italy trip

單字解析

  • rewarding adj. 值得的、有收穫的
    Vice President Hsiao Bi-khim (蕭美琴) said Monday that her recent visit to the Italian island of Ventotene was rewarding because she saw a growing number of international friends willing to learn about Taiwan and hear its story.
    副總統蕭美琴週一表示，她最近訪問義大利蓬托泰尼島成果豐碩，因為她看到越來越多的國際友人願意了解台灣並傾聽台灣的故事。
  • concern n. 關心、關切、關注
    "Throughout this journey, I have once again felt the international community's concern for Taiwan and encountered a growing number of good friends willing to learn about Taiwan and hear its story," Hsiao said at Taoyuan International Airport on Monday morning upon returning from Italy.
    蕭美琴週一上午自義大利返抵桃園國際機場時表示：「在這趟旅程中，我再次感受到國際社會對台灣的關切，並遇見了越來越多願意了解台灣、傾聽台灣故事的好朋友。」

  • significant adj. 重大的、有意義的
    "For me, the most significant part of the trip lies not only in attending a forum at a specific location, but also in having Taiwan's voice heard by so many more people in a place of such historical significance," Hsiao said, referring to the Second Ventotene European Conference for Freedom and Democracy, which she attended last week.
    蕭美琴在談到她上週出席的第二屆蓬托泰尼自由與民主歐洲會議時表示：「對我來說，這趟行程最重要的意義不僅在於出席特定地點的論壇，更在於讓台灣的聲音在這樣具有歷史意義的地方，被更多人聽見。」
  • address n. 演說、致詞
    Hsiao cited remarks from her Friday address at the Italian conference, saying, "Whether it is Ventotene or Taiwan, no island is too small to have a voice, and no island or society is too small to deserve respect."
    蕭美琴引用她在週五義大利會議上的演說談話表示：「無論是蓬托泰尼還是台灣，沒有任何島嶼小到不能發出聲音，也沒有任何島嶼或社會小到不值得被尊重。」
  • challenge n. 挑戰
    "Authoritarianism, 'gray-zone' threats, and challenges involving coercion and attempts to alter the status quo continue to arise in various forms and locations -- challenges with which Taiwan is all too familiar," she said.
    她表示：「威權主義、『灰色地帶』威脅，以及涉及脅迫和企圖改變現狀的挑戰，正以各種形式和地點不斷出現——這些都是台灣再熟悉不過的挑戰。」

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    According to a Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) press release issued Monday, Hsiao was invited by European Parliament Vice President Pina Picierno, the main initiator of the conference...
    句中的 "issued Monday"（週一發布的）是由過去分詞片語構成，用來修飾前面的名詞 "press release"（新聞稿）。這種結構省略了 "which was"，是英文新聞中為了簡潔、加快節奏而非常常見的句構方式。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    ...referring to the Second Ventotene European Conference for Freedom and Democracy, which she attended last week.
    句中的 ", which she attended last week" 是非限定性關係代名詞子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞 "the Second Ventotene European Conference for Freedom and Democracy"。非限定性子句通常用逗號隔開，提供額外的附加資訊。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    During World War II, the island was used to detain political dissidents, including Altiero Spinelli...
    句中的 "was used" 採用了被動語態（be + 過去分詞），強調「島嶼被用來做某事」這個客觀事實，而動作的執行者在此處並不重要或不需特別指明，這是新聞報導描述歷史背景時常用的句型。
  • 地方副詞置於句首的倒裝構句概念 / 否定與量化結構
    "Whether it is Ventotene or Taiwan, no island is too small to have a voice, and no island or society is too small to deserve respect."
    此處運用了 "no... is too... to..." 的經典句型（沒有任何...小到不能... / 意即：每一個都足夠大/有價值去...）。透過雙重否定或否定主詞搭配 "too... to..."，強烈表達出即使規模再小也具備權利與價值的核心訴求。
