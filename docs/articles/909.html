軟體銀行為 OpenAI 尋求籌集 110 億美元垃圾債券

Softbank seeks US$11bn in junk bonds for OpenAI

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • equivalent n. 相等物，等同
    Softbank Group Corp is seeking the equivalent of more than US$11 billion in what would be one of the biggest junk bond deals ever...
    軟體銀行集團股份有限公司正尋求籌集相當於 110 多億美元的資金，這將是有史以來規模最大的垃圾債券交易之一...
  • conglomerate n. 企業集團
    ...as billionaire Masayoshi Son’s conglomerate ramps up its investments in ChatGPT, creator OpenAI.
    ...此舉正值億萬富翁孫正義的企業集團加大對 ChatGPT 創造者 OpenAI 的投資。
  • ramp up phr. 逐步增加，擴大
    ...as billionaire Masayoshi Son’s conglomerate ramps up its investments in ChatGPT, creator OpenAI.
    ...此舉正值億萬富翁孫正義的企業集團加大對 ChatGPT 創造者 OpenAI 的投資。
  • issue v. 發行（股票、債券等）
    The company is looking to issue US$10 billion of dollar securities across three tenors...
    該公司正尋求發行分屬三種天期的 100 億美元債券...
  • debt n. 債務，負債
    ...and 1 billion euros (US$1.15 billion) of euro debt across two maturities...
    ...以及分屬兩種到期日的 10 億歐元（約 11.5 億美元）歐元債務...
  • fund v. 資助，為……提供資金
    The money would be used in part to fund a follow-on investment in OpenAI expected to close next month...
    這筆資金將部分用於資助預計於下個月完成交割的對 OpenAI 的後續投資。
  • price v. （證券等）定價
    The deal might price on Thursday, they added.
    他們補充說，這筆交易可能會在星期四定價。
  • monetize v. 使獲利，將……變現
    ...Softbank’s fortunes have become increasingly intertwined with its ability to monetize its holding in OpenAI...
    ...軟體銀行在向這家科技先驅投入近 65 億美元後，其財運已與將 OpenAI 持股變現的能力日益交織在一起...
  • decline v. 婉拒，謝絕
    The company declined to comment.
    該公司拒絕置評。
  • borrower n. 借款人，借款企業
    ...making it the biggest junk-rated borrower in bond markets so far this year, Bloomberg-compiled data show.
    ...使其成為今年迄今為止債券市場上規模最大的垃圾級借款人，彭博社彙編的數據顯示。
  • stake n. 股份，持股
    There was also a US$10 billion loan earlier this year backed by its OpenAI stake.
    今年稍早還有一筆由其 OpenAI 持股作擔保的 10 億美元貸款。
  • liquidity n. 流動性，現金變現能力
    ...a move that would increase the liquidity of Softbank’s investments, have been closely watched by investors.
    （此舉本可增加軟體銀行投資的流動性），這些言論一直受到投資者的密切關注。
  • acting as phr. 擔任，充當
    Citigroup Inc is acting as the lead bookrunner and a joint global coordinator...
    花旗集團正擔任主承銷商兼聯合全球協調人...
  • rated adj. 被評級的
    Softbank is rated at “BB+” by S&P Global Ratings and Fitch Ratings...
    軟體銀行被標普全球評級和惠譽評級評為「BB+」...

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後置修飾語（Present/Past Participle Phrases as Post-modifiers）
    ...said people who asked not to be identified as the discussions are private.
    句中的「who asked not to be identified」是用來修飾前方名詞「people」的形容詞子句；而在新聞英文中，也常使用過去分詞或現在分詞片語（例如第 5 段的「expected to close next month」修飾「a follow-on investment」）來簡化句子，達到精鍊、緊湊的報導語氣。
  • 被動語態（Passive Voice）
    The company is looking to issue US$10 billion of dollar securities across three tenors... and have been closely watched by investors.
    財經與商業新聞常探討交易、協商與市場反應，因此會大量運用被動語態（如「have been closely watched by investors」）來強調動作的承受者（即焦點所在），或是當發動動作的行為者不明、不重要時使語氣更客觀。
  • 假設語氣（Subjunctive / Conditional Sentences）
    If Softbank sells about US$11 billion in debt, it would be one of the largest junk bond sales ever by a single firm...
    此句使用第一型條件句（If + 現在式, 主詞 + 조動詞過去式/原形），用來假設未來可能發生的情境（軟體銀行若出售 110 億美元債務），並推論其可能產生的結果，在商業預測與新聞分析中非常常見。
  • 插入語結構（Parenthetical Elements）
    OpenAI chief executive officer Sam Altman’s remarks that the company will not go public this year, a move that would increase the liquidity of Softbank’s investments, have been closely watched by investors.
    句中的「, a move that would increase the liquidity of Softbank’s investments,」夾在主詞與動詞之間，作為同位語性質的插入語，補充說明前一句話提到的事件，有助於在單一句子中塞入更多背景脈絡。
