立委因同名議員的醫院發飆事件而遭無端指責

Lawmaker wrongly blamed for name-twin politician's hospital outburst

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • quell v. 平息，鎮壓
    A Kaohsiung-based legislator spoke out late Monday to quell a wave of internet wrath...
    一名高雄立委於週一深夜發聲以平息一波網路怒火...
  • anonymous adj. 匿名的
    In social media posts over the weekend, an anonymous user accused Taipei City Councilor Lee Po-yi...
    在週末的社群媒體貼文中，一名匿名用戶指控台北市議員李柏毅...
  • accuse v. 指控，指責
    ...who was accused of an angry outburst at a hospital.
    ...被指控在醫院情緒失控大發雷霆。
  • acknowledge v. 承認
    On Monday, Lee acknowledged that he had been "anxious and upset"...
    週一，李柏毅承認，他當時感到「焦慮又沮喪」...
  • admit v. 坦承，承認
    Lee... admitted that he had a "loud discussion" with the attending physician...
    李員坦承，他與主治醫師有過一場「大聲的討論」...
  • deny v. 否認
    ...but denied claims that he had cursed or tried to get special treatment...
    ...但否認了自己曾辱罵或試圖尋求特殊待遇的指控...
  • incident n. 事件
    After news of the incident spread on Monday, many netizens... accidentally vented their rage...
    事件消息傳開後，許多網民不小心將怒火發洩...
  • rectify v. 矯正，糾正，釐清
    Late that evening, Lee -- the legislator -- took to the social media site Threads to try and rectify the situation...
    當天深夜，身為立委的李柏毅利用社群媒體平台 Threads 試圖澄清情況...
  • constituency n. 選區
    ...people planning to leave one-star reviews for Lee Po-yi's constituency office on Google should first check that they have identified the right one...
    計畫在 Google 上給李柏毅的選區辦公室留下負評的人，應該先確認他們找對了地方...

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（用作附加說明或伴隨狀況）
    ...while accompanying a family member for treatment.
    句型中省略了主詞與be動詞（原句應為 while he was accompanying...），用來簡化句子並修飾主句動作發生的背景時間。
  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense)
    ...what he described as a long wait and lackluster care that his infant daughter, suffering from a fever, had experienced in a hospital emergency room.
    句中的「had experienced」使用過去完成式，用來表示在過去某個時間點（李柏毅發表談話）之前，女兒在急診室經歷照顧不周的過去動作。
  • 現在分詞作後位修飾
    ...his infant daughter, suffering from a fever, had experienced in a hospital emergency room.
    「suffering from a fever」是由現在分詞片語構成的形容詞性結構，置於名詞「daughter」後面，用來補充說明女兒當時發燒的狀況。
  • 假設語氣/條件句的應用
    ...people planning to leave one-star reviews for Lee Po-yi's constituency office on Google should first check that they have identified the right one, because "Kaohsiung and Taipei are very far apart."
    此處運用含有情態助動詞 should 的實用句構，表達強烈的建議與邏輯推論，在商業與日常英語溝通中常用於給予對方行動前的提醒與注意事項。
