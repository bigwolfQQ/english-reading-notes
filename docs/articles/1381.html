台灣女子與泰國男子在台中住宅火警中喪生

Taiwanese woman, Thai man die in Taichung residential fire

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • residential adj. 住宅的，居住的
    A Taiwanese woman and a Thai man died in a fire that broke out at a residential building in Waipu District of Taichung early Wednesday morning, according to the city's Fire Bureau.
    根據台中市消防局指出，週三（7日）清晨，台中市外埔區一棟住宅發生火警，造成一名台灣籍女子與一名泰國籍男子死亡。
  • break out phr. v. （火災、戰爭等）爆發，發生
    A Taiwanese woman and a Thai man died in a fire that broke out at a residential building in Waipu District of Taichung early Wednesday morning, according to the city's Fire Bureau.
    根據台中市消防局指出，週三（7日）清晨，台中市外埔區一棟住宅發生火警，造成一名台灣籍女子與一名泰國籍男子死亡。
  • report n. 報告，通報
    The bureau said it received a report at 3:07 a.m. of a fire at a two-story residential building with a tin roof on Tucheng Road in the district.
    消防局表示，他們於凌晨3時7分接獲通報，指該區土城路上一棟兩層樓、頂樓加蓋鐵皮屋的住宅發生火警。
  • arrival n. 到達，抵達
    Upon arrival, firefighters found a woman and a man dead inside the building, while the fire had spread to a nearby bamboo grove, burning an area of about 20 square meters, according to the bureau.
    消防局指出，消防人員抵達現場時，發現屋內有一男一女死亡，而火勢也蔓延至一旁的竹林，燃燒面積約20平方公尺。
  • spread v. 蔓延，擴散
    Upon arrival, firefighters found a woman and a man dead inside the building, while the fire had spread to a nearby bamboo grove, burning an area of about 20 square meters, according to the bureau.
    消防局指出，消防人員抵達現場時，發現屋內有一男一女死亡，而火勢也蔓延至一旁的竹林，燃燒面積約20平方公尺。
  • extinguish v. 撲滅（火災），熄滅
    Firefighters brought the blaze under control at about 4:08 a.m. and extinguished it at 4:12 a.m., the bureau said.
    消防局表示，消防人員於大約清晨4時8分控制火勢，並於4時12分將火熄滅。
  • preliminary adj. 初步的，預備的
    Citing a preliminary investigation, the bureau said the woman was a Taiwanese national and the man was a Thai national.
    消防局引述初步調查表示，該名女子為中華民國國籍，男子則為泰國籍。
  • investigation n. 調查
    The cause of the fire remained under investigation, the bureau said.
    消防局指出，起火原因目前仍在調查中。
  • suspect v. 懷疑，猜想
    According to local media reports, a couple had been living in the building before the fire broke out, and the deceased woman and man were suspected to have been the couple.
    根據當地媒體報導，火災發生前有一對情侶住在該棟建築內，死者男女懷疑即為該對情侶。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle phrase as post-modifier)
    A Taiwanese woman and a Thai man died in a fire that broke out at a residential building in Waipu District of Taichung early Wednesday morning, according to the city's Fire Bureau.
    句中的「that broke out at a residential building...」是形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的名詞 a fire。在新聞英語中，常透過關係代名詞子句來補充說明事件細節，幫助讀者快速掌握背景。
  • 複合介系詞片語簡化 (Upon + Noun)
    Upon arrival, firefighters found a woman and a man dead inside the building...
    「Upon + 名詞」相當於「As soon as + 主詞 + 動詞」（一...就...），用來表達兩個動作緊接著發生。此處 Upon arrival 意即「一抵達（現場）」。
  • 過去完成進行式 (Past Perfect Continuous Tense)
    According to local media reports, a couple had been living in the building before the fire broke out...
    「had been living」由「過去完成式 (had)」加上「現在分詞 (-ing)」組成，用來強調在過去某一時間點（火災發生前）之前，某個動作已經持續進行了一段時間。
  • 被動語態的完成式與不定詞結構 (Passive Voice with Perfect Infinitive)
    ...and the deceased woman and man were suspected to have been the couple.
    「were suspected to have been...」結合了被動語態（were suspected）與不定詞的完成式（to have been）。用完成式不定詞（to have been）是因為「是那對情侶」的這個狀態，發生在「被懷疑」的這個過去時間點之前。
