應對教學挑戰

Navigating challenges of teaching

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • part-time adj. 兼職的，半工半讀的
    after serving as a part-time university lecturer for seven years, she has decided to leave the profession
    在擔任了七年的兼任大學講師後，她決定離開教職
  • profession n. 職業（指需要專業訓練的行業）
    she has decided to leave the profession, saying that being a teacher is exhausting.
    她決定離開教職，並直言當老師令人筋疲力竭。
  • exhausting adj. 令人筋疲力竭的，消耗體力的
    saying that being a teacher is exhausting.
    並直言當老師令人筋疲力竭。
  • vocation n. 職業，行業；天職
    Teaching should be a respected vocation, not one that comes with a high degree of legal jeopardy.
    教學應該是一項受人尊敬的職業，而不是一項伴隨著高度法律風險的工作。
  • protect v. 保護，防護
    Teachers need to practice three major techniques to protect the interests not only of themselves, but also of their students.
    教師需要實踐三大技巧，以維護自身和學生的權益。
  • interest n. 利益，權益
    to protect the interests not only of themselves, but also of their students.
    以維護自身和學生的權益。
  • disadvantaged adj. 弱勢的，處於不利地位的
    If teachers cannot protect themselves in the classroom, how can they protect disadvantaged students?
    如果教師在教室裡無法保護自己，又如何能保護弱勢學生呢？
  • relevant adj. 相關的，切題的
    Teachers should familiarize themselves with educational regulations directly relevant to their work
    教師應熟悉與其工作直接相關的教育法規
  • in accordance with phr. 與……一致，依照
    they can say that their methods are in accordance with the law.
    他們可以表示自己的方法是依法行事的。
  • perspective n. 觀點，看法，角度
    From the perspective of “systems theory,” schools are the “subsystems” of society.
    從「系統論」的觀點來看，學校是社會的「子系統」。
  • effectively adv. 有效地
    Teachers should express themselves effectively, and develop the ability to convey their teaching philosophy clearly and persuasively with parents.
    教師應有效表達自己，並培養向家長清晰且具說服力地傳達其教學理念的能力。
  • implementation n. 實施，執行
    During parent-teacher meetings, teachers should articulate their educational philosophy and its implementation.
    在親師座談會期間，教師應闡明其教育理念及其實施方式。
  • master v. 精通，掌握
    They should also master how to manage interpersonal relationships and conflicts.
    他們也應掌握如何處理人際關係與衝突。
  • differing adj. 不同的，相異的
    Teachers should understand that parents have differing views and identify sources of conflict.
    教師應理解家長有不同的觀點並找出衝突的根源。

文法重點

  • not only ... but also ... 對等連接詞
    Teachers need to practice three major techniques to protect the interests not only of themselves, but also of their students.
    「not only A but also B」用於連接兩個對等的名詞、形容詞或片語，強調「不僅 A，而且 B」。在此句中連接了介系詞片語「of themselves」與「of their students」，用來強調教師保護的對象包含自己與學生雙方。
  • 現在分詞當形容詞使用
    saying that being a teacher is exhausting.
    句中的「exhausting」是由動詞 exhaust 轉變而來的現在分詞，在此作為形容詞，用來修飾前面的主詞概念（當老師這件事），表達「令人感到疲憊的、消耗體力的」。
  • 關係代名詞引導的形容詞子句
    Teaching should be a respected vocation, not one that comes with a high degree of legal jeopardy.
    句中的「that comes with a high degree of legal jeopardy」是由關代 that 引導的子句，用來修飾先行詞「one」（代替前面提過的 vocation）。在多益閱讀測驗中，常需要透過這種句構來釐清修飾關係。
  • 疑問句引導的假設語氣或反問句
    If teachers cannot protect themselves in the classroom, how can they protect disadvantaged students?
    此句使用了「If + 現在式（條件句）, 助動詞 + 主幹（結果句）」的條件句型。前半部提出一個假設狀況（若老師無法保護自己），後半部則用反問句（how can they...）表達否定的強烈語氣（根本無法保護學生）。
  • 動名詞片語當主詞
    Teachers should understand that parents have differing views and identify sources of conflict.
    動名詞片語（在此為 manage interpersonal relationships and conflicts，作為 master 的受詞）展現了英文中動詞轉為名詞以接受動作的用法。多益考試中常考動名詞（Ving）與不定詞（to V）作為動詞受詞的辨別。
