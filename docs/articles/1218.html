單字解析

  • condemn v. 譴責
    The Presidential Office yesterday condemned what it called a “typical case of transnational repression”.
    總統府昨天譴責其所稱的「跨國鎮壓典型案例」。
  • surveil v. 監視，監視...
    Zhang allegedly surveilled Lai Ting-yu and “his family — filming their home, vehicles, and reporting information back to handlers” in China.
    張女涉嫌監視賴廷與及「他的家人——拍攝他們的住家、車輛，並將情資回報給中國的操縱者」。
  • authorities n. pl. 官方，當局
    She thanked US law enforcement authorities for assisting and protecting Taiwanese...
    她感謝美國執法當局協助並保護台灣人...
  • assist v. 協助，援助
    She thanked US law enforcement authorities for assisting and protecting Taiwanese...
    她感謝美國執法當局協助並保護台灣人...
  • combat v. 打擊，對抗
    ...and said Taiwan would continue to work with the US and other democratic allies to jointly combat illegal acts of transnational repression.
    ...並表示台灣將繼續與美國及其他民主盟友合作，共同打擊跨國鎮壓的非法行為。
  • agent n. 代理人，經紀人，特務
    ...on charges of acting as an unregistered agent of a foreign government.
    ...因涉嫌擔任外國政府的未登記代理人而被捕。
  • target n. 目標
    Law enforcement sources told CBS News that the target was Lai Ting-yu
    執法消息人士告訴CBS News，目標是賴廷與
  • coordinate v. 協調，配合
    Zhang, who resides in Irvine, California, “coordinated the trip with an associate and used tradecraft to avoid detection,” Essayli said.
    埃瑟利表示，居住在加州爾灣的張女「與一名同夥協調了這趟行程，並使用了特務手法來規避偵查」。
  • associate n. 同事，夥伴，同夥
    Zhang, who resides in Irvine, California, “coordinated the trip with an associate and used tradecraft to avoid detection,” Essayli said.
    埃瑟利表示，居住在加州爾灣的張女「與一名同夥協調了這趟行程，並使用了特務手法來規避偵查」。
  • reside v. 居住，定居
    Zhang, who resides in Irvine, California, “coordinated the trip with an associate and used tradecraft to avoid detection,” Essayli said.
    埃瑟利表示，居住在加州爾灣的張女「與一名同夥協調了這趟行程，並使用了特務手法來規避偵查」。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作後位修飾
    The Presidential Office’s comments came after CBS News on Sunday reported that the FBI had arrested Wanying “Heather” Zhang, a California real-estate agent, at Los Angeles International Airport that day on charges of acting as an unregistered agent of a foreign government.
    句中的「acting as an unregistered agent of a foreign government」為現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前面的名詞片語「charges」（指控內容），說明具體的罪名為何。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    Lai Ting-yu, an engineer in his 30s who studied at a US college, works for Dutch electronics company Philips and has a young son, said CBS News, citing Taiwanese media reports.
  • 受詞補語（用原形動詞/現在分詞表示狀態）
    “Allegedly surveil a foreign leader’s family on US soil for the PRC — and try to catch a flight back to Shanghai? Not happening,” Patel said...
    此處使用名詞子句與直述語氣交錯，模擬口語的強烈否定語氣。「Not happening」是省略了主語與be動詞（It is not happening）的口語表達，用來強調該行為絕不可能發生。
  • 關代子句（限定與非限定用法）
    Zhang, who resides in Irvine, California, “coordinated the trip with an associate and used tradecraft to avoid detection,” Essayli said.
