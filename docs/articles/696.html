Why CPC, Taipower need subsidies

單字解析

  • constraint n. 限制，約束
    The core principle of economics lies in pursuing the greatest possible collective welfare under the constraint of limited resources.
    經濟學的核心原則在於在資源有限的約束下追求最大的集體福祉。
  • disclose v. 揭露，透露
    When addressing a controversial issue, it is therefore best practice to disclose as much information as possible in an open, fair and impartial manner to all stakeholders.
    因此，在處理具爭議性的議題時，最佳做法是以公開、公平且公正的方式向所有利害關係人揭露盡可能多的資訊。
  • stakeholder n. 利害關係人
    When addressing a controversial issue, it is therefore best practice to disclose as much information as possible in an open, fair and impartial manner to all stakeholders.
    因此，在處理具爭議性的議題時，最佳做法是以公開、公平且公正的方式向所有利害關係人揭露盡可能多的資訊。
  • alleviate v. 減輕，緩和
    This can help alleviate concerns on all sides and minimize unnecessary disputes.
    這有助於減輕各方的擔憂，並將不必要的爭端降到最低。
  • subsidy n. 補助金，津貼
    The following provides answers for common questions to assuage public concerns about government subsidies to CPC Corp, Taiwan and Taiwan Power Co (Taipower).
    以下針對大眾對政府補貼台灣中油公司與台灣電力公司（台電）的擔憂，提供常見問題的解答。
  • expenditure n. 支出，開支
    The expectation is Taipower would be able to make up for its cumulative losses by cutting expenditures and improving efficiency.
    原本的期望是台電能夠透過撙節支出和提升效率來彌補其累計虧損。
  • deteriorate v. 惡化，變壞
    However, the US-Iran war since late February has sent international oil and gas prices soaring, and Taipower’s finances have further deteriorated.
    然而，2月下旬以來的美國-伊朗戰爭導致國際油氣價格飆升，台電的財務狀況進一步惡化。
  • revenue n. 收入，收益
    This in turn could propel the stock market higher and generate additional tax revenue, which could then be returned to the public.
    這反過來又可以推升股市並產生額外的稅收，然後這些稅收可以回饋給大眾。
  • consume v. 消耗，消費（能源等）
    Last year, Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co’s (TSMC) domestic facilities consumed 25.8 terawatt-hours (TWh), or 9.1 percent of Taiwan’s total annual electricity consumption of 283TWh.
    去年，台灣積體電路製造公司（台積電）的國內廠房消耗了 25.8 太瓦時（TWh），占台灣總年用電量 283TWh 的 9.1%。
  • momentum n. 動能，衝力
    Providing appropriate subsidies to CPC and Taipower could ensure stable power supply, giving greater momentum to the semiconductor and ICT supply chains.
    向中油和台電提供適當的補貼可以確保穩定的電力供應，為半導體和 ICT 供應鏈提供更大的動能。
  • priority n. 優先事項，首要之務
    In a rapidly advancing world, our first priority should be to secure and protect Taiwan’s readily available share of the AI boom, making full use of our own comparative advantage.
    在一個快速發展的世界中，我們首要的優先事項應該是確保並保護台灣在 AI 熱潮中隨手可得的份額，充分發揮我們自身的比較利益。
  • strategy n. 策略，謀略
    For now, providing subsidies to CPC and Taipower is still the government’s best strategy.
    目前，提供中油和台電補貼依然是政府的最佳策略。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞當形容詞（修飾名詞）
    However, the US-Iran war since late February has sent international oil and gas prices soaring...
    句中的「soaring」是現在分詞，用來補充說明前面受詞 international oil and gas prices 的狀態（價格不斷飆升），表達主動與持續進行的概念。
  • 假設語氣（與過去事實相反）
    If Taiwan had not phased out nuclear power, CPC and Taipower would not have needed to import so much natural gas and would not have sustained massive losses.
    此句為與過去事實相反的假設語氣。If 子句使用過去完成式（had not phased out），而主要子句則使用「would/could + have + 過去分詞」（would not have needed...），用來假設若過去沒有停用核能，現在的結果會不同。
  • 關係代名詞非限定用法（which 引導的子句）
    This in turn could propel the stock market higher and generate additional tax revenue, which could then be returned to the public.
    句尾的「which」前面有逗號，屬於非限定用法的關係代名詞，用來補充說明前面的整句概念（產生額外的稅收），並接續說明該稅收可以被回饋給大眾。
  • 分詞構句表附帶結果
    ...making full use of our own comparative advantage.
    句尾的「making」是由連接詞和主詞簡化而來的現在分詞構句（原意可能為 and make full use of...），在此用來補充說明主句行動所伴隨的結果或方式。
