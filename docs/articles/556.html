插畫節將以台灣為主題

Illustration Festival to feature Taiwan

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • host v. 主辦，主持
    The Illustration Festival, hosted by French illustration magazine Kiblind , is to feature Taiwan as its theme country on Sept. 26 and 27 in Lyon, France.
    由法國插畫雜誌 Kiblind 主辦的插畫節，將於 9 月 26 日至 27 日在法國里昂以台灣為主題國。
  • feature v. 以...為特色，特別推薦
    The Illustration Festival, hosted by French illustration magazine Kiblind , is to feature Taiwan as its theme country on Sept. 26 and 27 in Lyon, France.

  • issue n. (期刊的) 期，號

    Kiblind 共同創辦人尚・圖雷特表示，該雜誌與台灣有著深厚的淵源，因為該國曾被列入該雜誌 2001 年創刊號的國際單元中。
  • edition n. (發行) 版本，屆次

  • participate v. 參與，參加
    The festival was first held in 2023, with each edition inviting about 30 international artists to participate.

文法重點

  • be to do (預定、即將發生)
    The Illustration Festival, hosted by French illustration magazine Kiblind , is to feature Taiwan as its theme country on Sept. 26 and 27 in Lyon, France.
    「be + to V」結構常用於新聞英語中，表示官方計畫、安排或即將發生的預定行程。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    The Illustration Festival, hosted by French illustration magazine Kiblind , is to feature Taiwan as its theme country on Sept. 26 and 27 in Lyon, France.
    句中的「hosted by French illustration magazine Kiblind」是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞「The Illustration Festival」，相當於「which is hosted by...」的省略寫法。
  • 表原因的連接詞 as
    Kiblind cofounder Jean Tourette said the magazine has a deep connection to Taiwan, as the country was featured in the international section of the magazine’s first issue in 2001.
    在此處的「as」相當於「because」，用來引導原因子句，說明雜誌與台灣有深厚淵源的理由。
  • it is of the utmost importance to... (虛主詞句型)
    Human beings are at the core of creation, and it is of the utmost importance to establish an art festival that emphasizes the artists’ presence on site, Tourette said.
    「it is of the utmost importance to V」是一個正式的句型，其中「it」為形式主詞，真正的受詞是後方的「to establish...」不定詞片語，「of + 名詞」在此相當於形容詞（極為重要的），常出現在多益商業與正式寫作中。
