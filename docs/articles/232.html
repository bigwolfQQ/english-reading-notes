文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾語 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    A total of 19,695 candidates have registered for 11,051 positions in the nine-in-one local elections set for Nov. 28...
    句中的「set for Nov. 28」是由過去分詞「set」開頭的片語，用來修飾前面的名詞短語「the nine-in-one local elections」，相當於關代子句「which are set for Nov. 28」的簡化寫法，在新聞英語中非常常見，能讓句子更緊湊。
  • be scheduled to + 原形動詞 (預定做...)與被動語態
    The CEC and local election commissions are scheduled to complete candidate eligibility reviews by Oct. 16...
    「be scheduled to」是用來表達「預定於……時發生或進行」的常用商務與正式用語。此句同時使用了被動語態，主詞是中選會與地方選委會，說明牠們被排定要完成審查工作。
  • Compared with... (分詞構句的介系詞化用法)
    Compared with the 2022 local elections, the total number of candidates decreased slightly by 130...
    「Compared with...」源自分詞構句「When it is compared with...」，省略了主詞與be動詞後形成的慣用語法，中文常譯為「與……相較之下」，常用於比較數據或情況。
  • whether 引導的名詞子句 (Noun Clause with whether)
    ...a national referendum on whether the government should abolish its nuclear-free homeland policy would be held...
    介系詞「on」後面接了一個由「whether」引導的名詞子句（whether the government should abolish its nuclear-free homeland policy），作為介系詞的受詞，用來表達「是否……」的不確定情況或主題。
