川普的大型石油劫掠

Trump’s great oil heist

單字解析

  • dismantle v. 廢除，拆除，瓦解
    However, removing an autocrat does not necessarily dismantle the authoritarian system that kept him in power...
    然而，移除一個獨裁者並不必然會瓦解維持其權力的威權體系……
  • prospect n. 前景，潛力，希望
    Nearly nine months after Maduro’s capture by US special forces, the prospect of democratic change is dimming by the day.
    在馬杜洛被美國特種部隊俘虜大約九個月後，民主變革的前景正日益黯淡。
  • intact adj. 完整無缺的，原封不動的
    This is happening even as the UN’s fact-finding mission on Venezuela reports that the machinery of repression built under chavismo remains largely intact.
    儘管聯合國委內瑞拉實況調查團報告指出，查維茲主義下建立的鎮壓機器很大程度上仍完好無缺，這種情況卻仍在發生。
  • purge v. 清除，清洗（異己）
    The security services and military high command have not been purged of regime loyalists...
    安全服務部門和軍方最高指揮階層尚未清除政權忠誠派……
  • spin v. 扭曲（事實），對……作有利的詮釋
    No matter how US Secretary of State Marco Rubio tries to spin these facts...
    不管美國國務卿馬可·魯比奧如何試圖美化（扭曲）這些事實……
  • concession n. 特許權，讓步
    ...granting the company a 100-year concession to 17 Venezuelan oil fields containing about 65 billion barrels of proven reserves.
    ……授予該公司對17個委內瑞拉油田為期100年的特許權，這些油田含有約650億桶經證實的儲量。
  • stake n. 股份，重大利益
    The US government also acquired a 35 percent stake in the company and the right to buy 20 percent of its production at cost.
    美國政府還獲得該公司35%的股份，以及以成本價購買其20%產量的權利。
  • interim adj. 臨時的，過渡的
    ...as Venezuela’s interim government expects the deal to deliver as much as US$100 billion in infrastructure investments.
    ……因為委內瑞拉臨時政府預期該協議將帶來高達1000億美元的基礎設施投資。
  • stabilization n. 穩定，安定
    The problem begins when economic stabilization is put first, without a clear plan for a democratic transition...
    問題始於將經濟穩定放在首位，卻沒有明確的民主轉型計畫……
  • vested interest n. phr. 既得利益
    And since the US government has a direct stake in the company, US taxpayers have a vested interest in protecting it.
    而且由於美國政府在該公司擁有直接股份，美國納稅人對保護它具有既得利益。
  • transparency n. 透明度
    ...was negotiated with little transparency and no competitive bidding by the Rodriguez government...
    ……是在羅德里格斯政府缺乏透明度且沒有進行競爭性招標的情況下談判達成的……
  • incentive n. 激勵，誘因，鼓勵
    ...billions of dollars in US and foreign investment create powerful incentives to avoid the uncertainty that free elections and a new government would bring.
    ……數十億美元的美國和外國投資創造了強大的誘因，讓人們去避免自由選舉和新政府將帶來的動盪。
  • materialize v. 成為現實，具體化，實現
    If this deal does materialize, then those incentives would not disappear when Trump leaves office.
    如果這筆交易真的落實，那麼當川普卸任時，這些誘因並不會消失。
  • grapple v. 努力解決，搏鬥，應付
    Before Maduro’s removal, the impoverished chavista government was grappling with collapsing oil production and had few international partners.
    在馬杜洛被推翻之前，貧困的查維茲政府一直疲於應付不斷崩潰的石油產量，且幾乎沒有國際夥伴。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況
    The US government also acquired a 35 percent stake in the company and the right to buy 20 percent of its production at cost.
    此句中並非典型由分詞開頭的分詞構句，但文章多處運用分詞（例如第3段的 'While some political prisoners have been released, and access to...' 或第8段的 'Add Trump’s declaration...'）來串聯動作。以第8段尾端為例：'...and it is easy to see why a future democratically elected Venezuelan government might want to revisit the deal.'，其中的 'democratically elected' 是過去分詞作為形容詞修飾名词政府，展現精煉的修飾手法。
  • 獨立分詞構句 / 獨立結構
    That leaves the chavista regime with a better chance of survival, while the US gets greater energy security and a pliable ally in Caracas — a win-win for everyone but Venezuelans yearning for political freedom.
    句尾的 '...Venezuelans yearning for political freedom' 中，'yearning for political freedom' 是現在分詞片語，後置修飾前面的名詞 'Venezuelans'。這種結構省去了關代與be動詞（即 Venezuelans who are yearning for...），使英文句子更顯緊湊、流暢。
  • 假設語氣（Could 完成式 / 過去式）
    If this deal does materialize, then those incentives would not disappear when Trump leaves office.
    此句使用了第一條件句（First Conditional），條件子句用現在式 'does materialize'（強調可能性），主要子句用 'would + 原形動詞'，用來表達對未來可能發生之情況的預測與其必然結果。
  • 名詞子句作受詞
    No matter how US Secretary of State Marco Rubio tries to spin these facts, or how often Rodriguez promises free elections, a democratic transition is not in sight.
    句中的 'how US Secretary of State Marco Rubio tries to spin these facts' 以及 'how often Rodriguez promises free elections' 是由 'No matter how' 引導的讓步狀語子句，在句中修飾主句，展現複雜句型的建構能力。
