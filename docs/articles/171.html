文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作後位修飾
    Tseng Yu-kang (曾禹康), an attending colorectal surgeon from Taichung Veterans General Hospital who sees patients at the Dajia Branch of Lee General Hospital, said that the woman, who lived alone, believed the boil was not particularly serious.
    句中的 "who sees patients at the Dajia Branch..." 與 "who lived alone" 分別為關係代間子句，用來修飾前方的名詞。此外，文章中也常運用分詞片語（如動詞ing開頭的修飾語）來補充說明主詞的動作或狀態，使句子結構緊湊。
  • 假設語氣（Could have + 過去分詞）
    In a scene that could have been depicted in a historical drama, the woman attempted to lance it using a sewing needle that she had heated over a flame to disinfect it, Tseng said.
    "could have been depicted" 使用了「助動詞過去式 + have + 過去分詞」的結構，用來表達「過去本來可以被描繪（但實際上未必發生）」的假設或推測語氣。
  • By the time 子句引導的時間副詞子句
    By the time she sought treatment, the affected area had swollen to the size of a baseball...
    "By the time + S + 過去式動詞" 用來表示「當……的時候，到……為止」，而主要子句則通常搭配「過去完成式 (had + 過去分詞)」，用來強調在過去某個時間點之前，另一個動作已經完成（在此指患部在此之前就已經腫脹了）。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞
    Squeezing a boil with the hands or tools before it has come to a head can inadvertently spread bacteria and even cause cellulitis, Tseng said.
    句首的 "Squeezing a boil with the hands or tools" 是一個動名詞片語（Squeezing... 為動名詞帶受詞與介系詞片語），整個片語在句中扮演單數主詞的角色，後面接助動詞 can 與原形動詞構成完整句型。
