Taiex surges 1,200+ points to record high as Fed fears ease

單字解析

  • surge v. 急遽上升、激增
    Shares in Taiwan surged more than 1,200 points Monday to a record high...
    台股週一急遽大漲逾 1,200 點，創下歷史新高...
  • turnover n. 營業額、成交量
    Turnover totaled NT$1.15 trillion (US$36.11 billion).
    成交量總計達新台幣 1.15 兆元（約 361.1 億美元）。
  • momentum n. 動能、氣勢
    The index opened higher and continued to gain momentum through the close...
    指數開高走高，收盤前持續維持動能...
  • prompt v. 促使、激勵
    "The poor August nonfarm payroll report prompted investors to see a lower chance of a Fed rate hike in October," ...
    「表現疲弱的 8 月非農就業報告，促使投資人認為聯準會 10 月升息的機率降低。」
  • probability n. 機率、可能性
    ...sending the probability of a Fed rate increase down to 22.7 percent from 64.2 percent a week earlier...
    ...這使得聯準會升息的機率從一週前的 64.2% 大幅降至 22.7%...
  • rally n. （市場）反彈、回升、集會
    ...as buying in AI-related stocks was boosted by a U.S. tech rally Friday...
    ...由於美國週五科技股走揚（反彈）...
  • contribute v. 貢獻、促成
    The stock contributed about 600 points to the Taiex's rise.
    該檔股票為台股的漲幅貢獻了約 600 點。
  • benchmark index n. 基準指數
    The Taiex, the Taiwan Stock Exchange's benchmark index, ended up 1,236.30 points...
    台灣證券交易所的基準指數台股，終場上漲 1,236.30 點...
  • stand on the sidelines phr. 袖手旁觀、保持觀望
    ...so many investors who previously stood on the sidelines returned to the market Monday...
    ...因此許多先前保持觀望的投資人週一重返市場...
  • institutional investor n. 機構投資人（如外資、投信等）
    According to the Taiwan Stock Exchange, foreign institutional investors bought a net NT$71.897 billion worth of shares...
    根據台灣證券交易所的資料，外國機構投資人淨買超...

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 / 被動語態
    ...as buying in AI-related stocks was boosted by a U.S. tech rally Friday amid easing concerns over a Federal Reserve rate hike following weaker-than-expected U.S. jobs data for August, dealers said.
    句中的「was boosted」為被動語態，用來表達「買盤受到美國科技股反彈所激勵」；而句尾的「following weaker-than-expected U.S. jobs data」則是介系詞片語，用來表示時間上的先後與原因（在……數據之後）。
  • 現在分詞（-ing）引導的附帶狀況或結果
    ...sending the probability of a Fed rate increase down to 22.7 percent from 64.2 percent a week earlier, Lin said.
    句中的「sending...」是現在分詞構句，置於句尾表示前面的整件事情所產生的直接結果（「這使得聯準會升息機率下降至……」），在多益閱讀與商業英文中非常常出現以表達因果或結果。
  • 條件句（Conditional Sentence）的假設語氣應用
    "If U.S. gains continue, I expect the local index to hit the target as soon as tomorrow."
    此句為第一類條件句（First Conditional），結構為「If + 現在式動詞, 主詞 + 助動詞（如 expect/will）+ 原形動詞」，用來表達未來很有可能發生的真實條件與預期結果。
