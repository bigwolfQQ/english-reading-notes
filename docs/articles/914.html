競選活動中的中立性

Neutrality on the campaign trail

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • campaign v. / n. 從事競選活動；競選活動
    Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) Legislator Puma Shen (沈伯洋) has been drawing crowds at his appearances at night markets as he campaigns for Taipei mayor.
    民主進步黨立法委員沈伯洋在為參選台北市長進行競選活動、現身夜市時吸引了大量人潮。
  • official adj. / n. 官方的、公務的；官員
    Vendors said they received official notices from the Taipei Market Administration Office ordering the removal of all campaign advertisements.
    攤商表示，他們收到來自台北市市場處的官方通知，命令拆除所有競選廣告。
  • notice n. 通知、告示
    Vendors said they received official notices from the Taipei Market Administration Office ordering the removal of all campaign advertisements.
    攤商表示，他們收到來自台北市市場處的官方通知，命令拆除所有競選廣告。
  • remove v. 移除、拆除
    Vendors said they received official notices from the Taipei Market Administration Office ordering the removal of all campaign advertisements.
    攤商表示，他們收到來自台北市市場處的官方通知，命令拆除所有競選廣告。
  • advertisement n. 廣告
    Vendors said they received official notices from the Taipei Market Administration Office ordering the removal of all campaign advertisements.
    攤商表示，他們收到來自台北市市場處的官方通知，命令拆除所有競選廣告。
  • prohibit v. 禁止、阻止
    The office notice says that under the Civil Servants Election and Recall Act (公職人員選舉罷免法), political parties and individuals are prohibited from hanging or erecting campaign or recall advertisements on roads, bridges, parks, government agencies, schools and other public facilities.
    辦事處的通知指出，根據《公職人員選舉罷免法》，政黨及個人不得在道路、橋樑、公園、政府機關、學校及其他公有公共設施懸掛或豎立競選或罷免廣告。
  • facility n. 設施、設備
    The act restricts campaign advertising on public facilities used for public passage, yet vendors registered with the market administration who display the “ocean current” ad on their own stalls or carts are simply exercising their freedom of speech and political expression.
    該法限制在供公眾通行之公有公共設施上進行競選廣告，然而在自己的攤位或手推車上展示「洋流」廣告、並向市場處登記的攤商，純粹是在行使其言論自由與政治表達權。
  • disrupt v. 擾亂、中斷
    Such displays neither obstruct public passage nor disrupt social order, nor do they have any bearing on administrative neutrality; the vendors are not civil servants.
    此類展示既不阻礙公眾通行，也不擾亂社會秩序，更與行政中立毫無關係；這些攤商並不是公務人員。
  • accountable adj. 負有責任的
    If the department acted on its own, those responsible should be held accountable and disciplined to prevent similar abuses of power.
    如果是該部門擅作主張，相關負責人應被追究責任並懲處，以防止類似的濫權行為再次發生。
  • civil servant n. phr. 公務員
    Tien Feng-wen is a civil servant.
    田逢文是一位公務員。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Present Participle）作後位修飾
    Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) Legislator Puma Shen (沈伯洋) has been drawing crowds at his appearances at night markets as he campaigns for Taipei mayor.
    句中的 "as he campaigns for Taipei mayor" 是一個連接詞引導的時間副詞子句，用來修飾主句的動作背景，說明他在夜市現身時的同時也在進行競選活動。
  • 過去分詞（Past Participle）作被動修飾
    Vendors said they received official notices from the Taipei Market Administration Office ordering the removal of all campaign advertisements.
    句中的 "registered with the market administration" 是分詞片語，當作後位修飾語修飾前面的名詞 vendors，相當於關代子句 "who are registered with..." 的簡化。
  • 對等連接詞 nor 的倒裝句構
    Such displays neither obstruct public passage nor disrupt social order, nor do they have any bearing on administrative neutrality; the vendors are not civil servants.
    當句首或分句使用否定並列連結詞 nor 時，後面必須採取「助動詞 + 主詞 + 主要動詞」的倒裝語序（nor do they have...），用以加強否定語氣。
  • 條件句（Conditional Sentence）的假設語氣應用
    If Chiang ordered the action, he should publicly apologize.
    此句使用第一類條件句（真實條件句），結構為 "If + 現在式動詞, 主持人 + 조動詞 (should) + 原形動詞"，用來表達對未來或過去某種假設情況的處理方式與建議。
