文法重點

  • 關係代詞 whose 引導的關係子句
    Sinlun Borough Warden Lin Jin-long wades through a flooded road to rescue an elderly driver whose car stalled in Keelung after heavy rain yesterday caused the Dawulun River to overflow.
    句中的「whose car stalled in Keelung」為形容詞子句（關係子句），修飾先行詞「an elderly driver」。關係代詞「whose」用作所有格，指代「該駕駛的（汽車）」，用來補充說明駕駛當時遇到的狀況。
  • 不定詞片語表目的（Infinitive of Purpose）
    Sinlun Borough Warden Lin Jin-long wades through a flooded road to rescue an elderly driver whose car stalled in Keelung after heavy rain yesterday caused the Dawulun River to overflow.
    句中的「to rescue...」為不定詞片語，接在主要動詞片語「wades through a flooded road」之後，用來說明主詞里長涉水前進的具體目的（為了救援該名駕駛）。
  • 動詞句型 cause + 受詞 + 不定詞 (to V)
    Sinlun Borough Warden Lin Jin-long wades through a flooded road to rescue an elderly driver whose car stalled in Keelung after heavy rain yesterday caused the Dawulun River to overflow.
    在時間副詞子句「after heavy rain yesterday caused the Dawulun River to overflow」中，使役性質的動詞 cause 採用「cause + 受詞 + to V」結構，受詞為「the Dawulun River」，搭配不定詞「to overflow」，表示「豪雨導致大武崙溪暴漲氾濫」。
