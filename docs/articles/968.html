超過兩千七百人出席以不忠為主題的藝術節歌仔戲表演外燴

Over 2,700 attend banquet for infidelity-themed arts festival opera show

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • banquet n. 宴會、酒宴、辦桌
    More than 2,700 people dined with the Sun Kailin Opera Troupe's outdoor performance of "Justice Bao Beheads Chen Shimei" (包公鍘陳世美), a traditional Taiwanese opera, in Tainan's Anping District on Sunday.
    9月21日，超過2700名民眾週日在台南市安平區，一邊享用辦桌美食，一邊觀賞孫凱琳歌劇團戶外演出的傳統台灣歌仔戲《包公鍘陳世美》。
  • event n. 活動、事件
    The event was part of the infidelity-themed Zhamei Arts Festival (鍘美藝術節), conceived after former President Chen Shui-bian's (陳水扁) daughter Chen Hsing-yu (陳幸妤) revealed her divorce in a scathing one-star review of her ex-husband's orthopedic clinic last month.
    此活動是「鍘美藝術節」的一部分，該藝術節以不忠為主題，其構想源於前總統陳水扁之女陳幸妤上個月在前夫的骨科診所留下辛辣的一星負評、揭露其離婚消息後而萌生。
  • organizer n. 主辦單位、組織者
    Although Chen Hsing-yu's online review served as a catalyst, festival spokesperson Ma Chia-tung (馬嘉彤) told reporters that the event organizers wished to refrain from commenting directly on individuals' private lives out of respect for their privacy.
    儘管陳幸妤的網路評論成了催化劑，但藝術節發言人馬嘉彤告訴記者，基於對個人隱私的尊重，主辦單位希望避免直接評論個人的私生活。
  • refrain v. 克制、忍住、避免（常接 from + V-ing）
    Although Chen Hsing-yu's online review served as a catalyst, festival spokesperson Ma Chia-tung (馬嘉彤) told reporters that the event organizers wished to refrain from commenting directly on individuals' private lives out of respect for their privacy.
    儘管陳幸妤的網路評論成了催化劑，但藝術節發言人馬嘉彤告訴記者，基於對個人隱私的尊重，主辦單位希望避免直接評論個人的私生活。
  • attendee n. 出席者、參加者
    The five-day festival invited attendees to bring their own food for picnics while enjoying free performances on Friday and Saturday nights, followed by paid banquets and free performances from Sunday through Tuesday.
    為期五天的藝術節邀請參與者在週五和週六晚上攜帶自己的食物來野餐，同時享受免費表演，隨後從週日至週二則有付費辦桌和免費表演。
  • estimate v. 估計、估算
    The organizers estimated that Saturday's picnic drew around 50,000 people, as many took advantage of the weekend.
    主辦單位於週六時估計，由於許多人把握了週末假期，野餐活動吸引了大約5萬人參加。
  • take advantage of phr. 利用、善加利用
    The organizers estimated that Saturday's picnic drew around 50,000 people, as many took advantage of the weekend.
    主辦單位於週六時估計，由於許多人把握了週末假期，野餐活動吸引了大約5萬人參加。
  • original adj. 原創的、最初的
    With references to the medical world that were not in the original opera, the Saturday show included not-so-subtle nods to the festival's original inspirations.
    透過融合未見於原版歌仔戲的醫學界元素，週六的演出包含了對該藝術節最初靈感來源的不那麼隱諱的暗示。
  • conceal v. 隱藏、隱瞞
    In the story, Bao defies an imperial pardon and orders the execution of Chen Shimei (陳世美), a prince consort who concealed his marriage from the royal family, committed infidelity, and attempted to murder his first wife and children.
    在故事中，包公無視皇帝特赦，下令處決陳世美——一個向皇室隱瞞婚姻、不忠並企圖謀殺元配妻子及孩子的駙馬。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語當形容詞後置修飾（Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier）
    The event was part of the infidelity-themed Zhamei Arts Festival (鍘美藝術節), conceived after former President Chen Shui-bian's (陳水扁) daughter Chen Hsing-yu (陳幸妤) revealed her divorce in a scathing one-star review of her ex-husband's orthopedic clinic last month.
    句中的「conceived after...」是一個過去分詞片語，用來補充修飾前面的名詞「Zhamei Arts Festival」。其原貌為縮減掉的關係代名詞與be動詞（即 which was conceived after...），多益閱讀測驗常考這種分詞後位修飾的精簡句構。
  • 分詞構句作時間/原因狀語（Participial Phrase as Adverbial Clause Reduction）
    With references to the medical world that were not in the original opera, the Saturday show included not-so-subtle nods to the festival's original inspirations.
    句首的「With references to...」是「With + 名詞 + 形容詞/副詞/分詞」的複合結構，用來表達伴隨狀況或原因，相當於一個副詞子句，在多益商業英文中常用於描述附帶條件或相關細節。
  • 關係代名詞子句修飾先行詞（Relative Clause with 'who'）
    In the story, Bao defies an imperial pardon and orders the execution of Chen Shimei (陳世美), a prince consort who concealed his marriage from the royal family, committed infidelity, and attempted to murder his first wife and children.
    句中的「who concealed his marriage...」是一個由關係代名詞 who 引導的形容詞子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「a prince consort（駙馬）」。子句內串接了三個動詞（concealed, committed, attempted），描述他的連串惡行。
