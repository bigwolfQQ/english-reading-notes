最高法院維持並定讞招待旅遊介入選舉案之判決

Court upholds and finalizes sentences in influence junket

單字解析

  • conviction n. 定罪，判罪
    The Supreme Court has upheld the convictions and prison sentences of a New Taipei City borough warden and an association chairman for election offenses...
    最高法院維持了一名新北市里長及一名協會理事長因涉及選舉犯罪的定罪與徒刑...
  • appeal n. / v. 上訴
    The court rejected appeals by Yao Wei-hua... and Chien An-shih..., confirming the High Court’s guilty verdicts.
    法院駁回了姚韋華與簡安仕的上訴，確認高等法院的有罪判決。
  • overturn v. 推翻，撤銷（判決等）
    In February, the High Court overturned a lower court’s acquittal, finding Yao and Chien guilty...
    今年二月，高等法院推翻了下級法院的無罪判決，認定姚、簡兩人有罪...
  • acquittal n. 無罪釋放，無罪判決
    In February, the High Court overturned a lower court’s acquittal, finding Yao and Chien guilty...
    今年二月，高等法院推翻了下級法院的無罪判決，認定姚、簡兩人有罪...
  • pursuant to prep.phr. 根據，按照
    It increased their sentences pursuant to Article 7 of the Anti-Infiltration Act (反滲透法).
    它依據《反滲透法》第七條加重其刑。
  • commission v. 委託，委任
    ...prosecutors said that Chien, acting under the instruction and financial backing of China’s Taiwan Affairs Office (TAO) in Nanjing, commissioned Yao in September 2023 to invite borough wardens and residents on a trip to China.
    ...檢方表示，簡安仕在中國南京市台灣辦公室的指示與資金贊助下，於 2023 年 9 月委託姚韋華邀請里長和居民前往中國旅遊。
  • participant n. 參加者，參與者
    The participants paid NT$16,500 each for airfare, while meals and lodgings were provided for free, prosecutors said.
    檢方說，參加者每人支付新台幣 16,500 元作為機票費，而食宿則由免費提供。
  • indict v. 起訴，控告
    In 2024, the New Taipei District Prosecutors’ Office indicted Yao and Chien on suspicion of contravening the Anti-Infiltration Act...
    2024 年，新北市地方檢察署以涉嫌違反《反滲透法》起訴姚、簡兩人。
  • evidence n. 證據
    The New Taipei District Court acquitted them in the first trial, citing insufficient evidence to prove that the free lodging and meals were intended as bribes...
    新北市地方法院一審判決兩人無罪，理由是證據不足以證明免費食宿意在作為賄賂...
  • allegation n. 指控，指稱
    Yao also denied the allegations, saying that he “just wanted to go have fun” and had no knowledge of which Chinese officials were attending.
    姚也否認指控，稱他「只是想去玩」，且不知道有哪些中國官員出席。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況/結果
    The Supreme Court has upheld the convictions and prison sentences of a New Taipei City borough warden and an association chairman for election offenses involving a China-funded trip intended to influence Taiwanese voters.
    句中的 "involving a China-funded trip..." 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的 election offenses（選舉犯罪），補充說明該犯罪包含了哪些內容。而在 "intended to influence..." 則是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的 trip，省略了 "which was"，表達「旨在影響」的被動與修飾關係。
  • 分詞構句表示附帶情況（同時發生）
    During a banquet, a red banner with the slogan “One close family on both sides of the Taiwan Strait” was displayed, while TAO officials promoted cooperation between the Chinese Nationalist Party... and opposition to the “separatist” Democratic Progressive Party in an attempt to sway elections, prosecutors said.
    句子中使用了連接詞 while 引導子句，或是常見的分詞構句（如句尾的 "in an attempt to sway elections"），用不定詞片語來表達目的，即「企圖左右選舉」，這在多益閱讀與商業新聞中常用來精簡表達某個行為的目的。
  • 被動語態的運用
    The New Taipei District Court acquitted them in the first trial, citing insufficient evidence to prove that the free lodging and meals were intended as bribes or to influence the participants voting behavior.
    句中的 "were intended as bribes" 採用了被動語態 (be + 過去分詞)，強調免費的食宿是被當作賄賂來意圖。在新聞報導中，當動作的承受者（在此為主詞 the free lodging and meals）是焦點時，經常使用被動語態。
