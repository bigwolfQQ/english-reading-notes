  • operator n. 營運商、業者
    and the bus operator has been fined, authorities said.
    當局表示，該公車業者已被開罰。
  • investigation n. 調查
    The Taipei City Government on Sunday said that after an initial investigation of the incident
    台北市政府周日表示，經過對該事件的初步調查
  • issue v. 發布、發行
    Sindian Bus issued a statement on the same day
    新店客運當天發布聲明
  • statement n. 聲明、陳述
    Sindian Bus issued a statement on the same day
    新店客運當天發布聲明
  • determine v. 決定、查明、確定
    adding that the Taipei District Prosecutors’ Office has ordered an autopsy to determine the cause of death.
    並補充說明台北地方檢察署已下令進行解剖以釐清（確定）死因。
  • follow-up adj. 後續的（多益常考：follow-up appointment 回診 / follow-up meeting 後續會議）
    On Wednesday last week, Kao’s father took him to Taipei’s Wanfang Hospital for a follow-up appointment, they said.
    他們說，上周三高父帶他到台北的萬芳醫院進行回診。
  • cooperate v. 合作、配合

  • enhance v. 提高、增加、加強

  • ensure v. 確保、保證

  • suspend v. 暫停、懸掛（多益常考：停職、停權）

  • regulations n. 規章、法規

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present/Past Participle as Post-modifier)
    The bus, which runs in Taipei and New Taipei City, returned to its terminal in Sindian at about 10:30pm on Thursday, with the man still aboard, police said.
    句中的 "with the man still aboard" 是一個「with + 名詞 + 分詞/形容詞」的複合結構（伴隨狀語），用來補充說明主句動作發生時伴隨的狀態（男子當時人依然在車上）。這在新聞英語和多益考試中非常常見。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    A man was found dead on a bus in New Taipei City on Sunday, several days after he boarded, and the bus operator has been fined, authorities said.
    新聞報導經常使用被動語態（如 "was found"、"has been fined"），當動作的承受者（男子、客運業者）比動作的執行者更重要，或者執行者不明/不需要明說時，就會使用 "be + 過去分詞" 的被動句型。
  • 現在分詞作補充說明 (Present Participle indicating Result or Addition)
    A forensic examination found no signs of trauma and ruled out foul play for the time being, police said, adding that the Taipei District Prosecutors’ Office has ordered an autopsy to determine the cause of death.
    句尾的 ", adding that..." 是由現在分詞 "adding" 引導的分詞片語，用來接續前面警方所說的話，補充說明另一個相關的動作（補充說檢方已下令解剖）。這種用法能讓句子更緊湊，省去連接詞和主詞。
