國防部「網路獵殺部隊」計畫曝光

MND ‘cyber hunt team’ plan revealed

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • establish v. 建立，設立
    The Ministry of National Defense (MND) has proposed a NT$95.93 million (US$3.03 million) budget for next year to establish a new “military cyber hunt team”...
    國防部已為明年編列新台幣9,593萬（約合303萬美元）的預算，用於成立全新的「軍事網路獵殺部隊」...
  • counter v. 反制，對抗
    ...that would counter the Chinese Communist Party’s (CCP) increasing cyberinfiltration and “gray zone” threats.
    ...以反制中國共產黨（中共）日益增加的網路滲透與「灰色地帶」威脅。
  • budget n. 預算
    The plan was revealed in the central government’s general budget proposal for the next fiscal year...
    該計畫是在中央政府下一年度總預算案中曝光...
  • initiative n. 新倡議，新計畫
    The initiative marks a shift in the military’s cyberwarfare mindset...
    此一新倡議標誌著軍方網路作戰思維的轉變...
  • strategy n. 策略，戰略
    ...upgrading from a “defensive cybersecurity” strategy that relies on setting up firewalls to an “integrated offense and defense” strategy...
    ...從依賴設置防火牆的「防禦性網路安全」策略，升級為...「攻防一體」策略...
  • vulnerability n. 漏洞，脆弱性
    Military cybersecurity had mostly relied on alerts and would scramble to patch vulnerabilities after a breach was detected...
    過去軍事資安大多依賴警報，並在偵測到遭突破後才急忙修補漏洞...
  • confidential adj. 機密的，保密的
    ...quietly stealing confidential data or waiting for the suitable time for destruction, they said.
    ...安靜地竊取機密資料或等待破壞的適當時機。
  • align with phr. v. 與...一致，符合
    The initiative also aligns with international standards, and would deepen joint defense between Taiwan and the US.
    這項倡議也符合國際標準，並將深化台美聯合防禦。
  • transformation n. 轉型，轉變
    With the combat experience of elite foreign instructors, the command is expected to speed up its transformation into an “elite cyber tactical force”...
    藉由頂尖外籍教員的實戰經驗，該指揮部預期將加速轉型為「精銳網路戰術部隊」...
  • maintain v. 維持，保持
    ...which would maintain the resilience of key civilian infrastructure and military command systems in peacetime...
    ...在平時維護關鍵民用基礎設施與軍事指揮系統的韌性...
  • guarantee v. 確保，保證（類似用法見 ensure）
    The establishment of the team aims to expose forces hidden in the shadows, and to reclaim dominance in the digital realm, ensuring that military command and communication systems can function reliably...
    成立該團隊的旨在暴露隱藏在陰影中的力量，並奪回數位領域的主導權，確保軍事指揮與通訊系統能可靠運作...

文法重點

  • 關係代間代名词子句 (Relative Clause)
    ...that would counter the Chinese Communist Party’s (CCP) increasing cyberinfiltration and “gray zone” threats.
    句中的「that would counter...」是形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的名詞短語 a new “military cyber hunt team”。關係代間代名词「that」代替先行詞作為子句的主詞，用以補充說明該獵殺部隊的功能。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    State-sponsored hackers are skilled at advanced persistent threat attacks, often remaining dormant for months or even years after infiltrating Taiwan’s military systems...
    句尾的「often remaining dormant...」是現在分詞片語，前面省略了主詞和be動詞（they are），用來補充說明這些駭客在進行攻擊後的狀態，表示伴隨發生的情況。
  • 假設語氣 / 與現在事實相反的條件句 (Subjunctive Mood)
    ...saying that one cannot simply sit back and wait to be attacked, but must instead engage adversaries in the gray zone, the official said.
    此處雖然不是典型的 if 假設語氣，但透過「cannot simply sit back... but must instead...」表達了一種強烈的原則性建議與當前策略的必要性，展現多益閱讀中常見的立場表述句構。
  • 受詞補語結構 (Object Complement)
    Members of the “military cyber hunt team” would act as “scavengers” of the digital domain, completely eliminating these latent ghosts and backdoors before enemy attacks are launched...
    句中的「completely eliminating...」為分詞構句，作副詞用，說明這些成員在扮演清道夫時同時執行的動作，帶有條件或時間（在攻擊發動之前）的意味。
