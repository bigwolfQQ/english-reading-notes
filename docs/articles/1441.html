單字解析

  • responsible adj. 負責任的
    Only Russia is responsible for the war in Ukraine, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskiy said yesterday...
    烏克蘭總統澤倫斯基昨天表示，只有俄羅斯該為烏克蘭戰爭負責...
  • vow v. 發誓，誓言
    ...as he vowed to press ahead with strikes on Russian oil refineries...
    ...他並誓言繼續對俄羅斯煉油廠發動攻擊...
  • press ahead phrasal v. 繼續進行，堅持不懈
    ...as he vowed to press ahead with strikes on Russian oil refineries, despite criticism from US President Donald Trump...
    ...他並誓言繼續對俄羅斯煉油廠發動攻擊，儘管受到美國總統川普的批評...
  • criticism n. 批評，指責
    ...despite criticism from US President Donald Trump, who blamed him for high diesel prices...
    ...儘管遭到美國總統川普的批評，川普將高柴油價格歸咎於他...
  • facility n. 設施，設備
    ...the third strike on the company’s facilities in four days.
    ...這是該公司設施在四天內遭遇的第三次攻擊。
  • reciprocal adj. 相互的，回報的
    ...adding that any de-escalation must be reciprocal and backed by real guarantees.
    ...並補充說，任何降級緊張局勢的舉措都必須是互惠的，且要有實質保證作後盾。
  • comment n. 評論，意見
    Ukrainian Minister of Foreign Affairs Andrii Sybiha pushed back on Trump’s comments...
    烏克蘭外交部長西比哈反擊川普的言論...
  • suggest v. 暗示，建議
    Trump on Saturday said it was time for Ukraine to get a new president, suggesting Zelenskiy has not done enough to end the war...
    川普週六表示，烏克蘭是時候換個新總統了，他暗示澤倫斯基在結束戰爭方麵做得不夠...
  • deal n. 協議，交易
    “I suggest they get a new leader who can make a deal, because he could have made many deals and for some reason he never does,” Trump said.
    川普說：「我建議他們換個能達成協議的新領導人，因為他本可以達成很多協議，但不知為何他從未這麼做。」
  • attempt n. 企圖，嘗試
    ...the US president, who on Friday struck a deal with Russian President Vladimir Putin to buy diesel from Russia in an attempt to address high fuel prices...
    ...這位美國總統週五才剛與俄羅斯總統普丁達成協議，擬向俄羅斯購買柴油，以試圖解決油價高漲問題...
  • urge v. 敦促，力勸
    ...has been urging Zelenskiy for weeks to stop strikes on Russian energy infrastructure.
    ...他數週以來一直敦促澤倫斯基停止攻擊俄羅斯的能源設施。
  • address v. 處理，解決
    ...in an attempt to address high fuel prices ahead of the US midterm elections...
    ...以試圖解決美國期中選舉前的油價高漲問題...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 / 附帶狀況
    Only Russia is responsible for the war in Ukraine, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskiy said yesterday as he vowed to press ahead with strikes on Russian oil refineries, despite criticism from US President Donald Trump, who blamed him for high diesel prices and said he should be replaced.
    句中「as he vowed...」引導時間副詞子句表達同時發生的動作；而在後半段出現的「who blamed him...」則是非限定關係代名詞子句，用來補充說明 US President Donald Trump 的背景與行為。
  • 獨立分詞構句
    The exchange came as Kyiv pressed its campaign against infrastructure deep inside Russia, with a drone attack yesterday shutting down a data center run by Russian technology giant Yandex...
    「with + 名詞 + 現在分詞 (shutting down)」構成附帶狀況的獨立分詞構句，用來補充說明主句發生時伴隨的具體細節，其中 run by... 則是由過去分詞片語作後位修飾，用來修飾 data center。
  • 虛主詞 It 的用法
    “It is possible to stop strikes on energy infrastructure as early as today, but [Russian President Vladimir] Putin does not want to do so,” he said...
    句首的「It」為虛主詞（Dummy subject），真正的真正主詞是後方的不定詞片語「to stop strikes on energy infrastructure as early as today」。這種句型在商業與日常英語中常用於表達「做某事是可能的/困難的」。
  • 與過去事實相反的假設語氣
    “I suggest they get a new leader who can make a deal, because he could have made many deals and for some reason he never does,” Trump said.
    句中的「could have made」使用了「助動詞過去式 + have + 過去分詞」的結構，用來表達過去本來能夠做到、但實際上並未發生的情況，帶有惋惜或批評的語氣。
