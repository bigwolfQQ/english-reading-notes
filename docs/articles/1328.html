77-year-old former air force officer charged with spying

單字解析

  • indict v. 起訴，控告
    The Taoyuan District Prosecutors’ Office yesterday indicted a 77-year-old retired air force captain for allegedly spying for China...
    桃園地檢署昨天起訴了一名77歲的退休空軍上尉，指控他涉嫌為中國擔任間諜……
  • personnel n. 人員，員工
    ...collecting personal information about Taiwanese intelligence personnel, including obtaining property records through his son...
    ……蒐集台灣情治人員的個人資訊，其中包括透過他的兒子取得不動產記錄……
  • in exchange for phr. 作為交換，換取
    ...to collect information on an intelligence officer from a Taiwanese government agency in exchange for an undisclosed sum.
    ……蒐集台灣某政府機關情報官員的資訊，並以此換取未公開的金錢報酬。
  • target n. 目標
    Gao allegedly asked Fai to find out the target’s occupation, vehicle information, home address and workplace...
    高男涉嫌要求費男查出目標的職業、車輛資訊、住家地址與工作地點……
  • hire v. 雇用
    Fai allegedly hired a private investigator to look into the target, prosecutors said...
    檢方表示，費男涉嫌雇用一名私家偵探來調查該目標……
  • access v. 存取，進入，使用
    ...to access the company’s system and obtain records of properties registered to the target, prosecutors said.
    檢方表示，……登入（存取）公司系統並取得登記在目標名下的不動產記錄。
  • warrant n. 搜索票，逮捕令，授權令
    On Aug. 17, investigators got a warrant to search Fai’s residence, subsequently detaining him.
    8月17日，調查人員取得搜索票搜查費男的住所，隨後將其拘留。
  • detain v. 拘留，扣留
    On Aug. 17, investigators got a warrant to search Fai’s residence, subsequently detaining him.
    8月17日，調查人員取得搜索票搜查費男的住所，隨後將其拘留。
  • deny v. 否認
    Fai denied the allegations, saying his personal information and telephone number had been misused...
    費男否認這些指控，供稱是自己的個人資訊和電話號碼遭到不當使用……
  • charge v. 控告，指控
    Fai was charged under the National Intelligence Work Act...
    費男被依《國家情報工作法》起訴（指控）……

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附加說明 (Present Participle Phrase as an Adverbial Modifier)
    On Aug. 17, investigators got a warrant to search Fai’s residence, subsequently detaining him.
    句尾的 “, subsequently detaining him” 是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前面主句動作所產生的後續結果（隨後將其拘留），主詞 investigators 與 detain 之間為主動關係。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Fai was charged under the National Intelligence Work Act...
    本句使用被動語態（was charged），強調費先生是被檢調機關或法律所起訴，焦點在於受到的動作而非發起動作的人。
  • 過去分詞作後位修飾 (Past Participle as Post-modifier)
    ...obtain records of properties registered to the target, prosecutors said.
    句中的 “registered to the target” 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 properties，相當於形容詞子句 “which were registered to the target”，指「登記在目標名下的」不動產。
  • 對等連接詞連接多個動詞片語 (Coordination of Verb Phrases)
    Fai was also charged under the National Security Act... for allegedly collecting and providing documents and electronic records... and under the Personal Data Protection Act with illegally using personal information...
    此句展示了法學報導常見的長句結構，利用 and 連接不同的法條依據與涉案罪名（be charged under... for... and under... with...），使多項罪名在句型架構上保持平行對稱。
