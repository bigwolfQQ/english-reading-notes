文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表示時間或附帶狀況）
    Speaking to reporters yesterday, Taipei Mayor Chiang Wan-an (蔣萬安) said he had already asked Farglory Group, the Taipei Dome’s owner and operator, to carry out disinfection.
    句首的「Speaking to reporters yesterday」是由現在分詞片語構成的分詞構句，省略了連接詞與主詞（原句意近於 As he spoke to reporters yesterday），用來修飾主句的主詞 Taipei Mayor Chiang Wan-an，表示動作同時發生。
  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense)
    ...Taipei Mayor Chiang Wan-an (蔣萬安) said he had already asked Farglory Group, the Taipei Dome’s owner and operator, to carry out disinfection.
    句中使用「had already asked」過去完成式（had + 過去分詞），用來表達在過去某個時間點（said）之前已經完成的動作（要求遠雄集團執行消毒）。
  • 非限定關係子句（補充說明）
    Excluding those in tourist areas and business districts, which are being kept in place, the city has decreased the number of public trash cans...
    句中的「which are being kept in place」是由逗號隔開的非限定關係子句，用來補充說明前面的觀光區與商業區垃圾桶的狀況。非限定子句對先行詞提供額外資訊，若拿掉不影響主要句意。
