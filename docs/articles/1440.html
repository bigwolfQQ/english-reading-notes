文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附加說明 (Participial Phrase indicating accompanying circumstance)
    Saudi Arabia yesterday kept its main international airport closed and moved Riyadh schools online as Yemen’s Houthi rebels threatened new airstrikes across the kingdom, a day after a missile attack killed 12 people and wounded more than 300.
    句尾的「a day after a missile attack killed 12 people...」是一個時間副詞性質的片語，用來補充說明前面主句發生的時間背景。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Independent Participial Phrase)
    The attack was condemned by other countries, with Russia saying hostilities needed to end “through political and diplomatic means,” and German Minister of Foreign Affairs Johann Wadephul describing it as “a terrorist act against Saudi Arabia and civil aviation.”
    「with + 名詞 + 分詞」結構用來表示伴隨狀況或補充說明，這裡用 with Russia saying... 和 German Minister... describing... 來具體說明其他國家是如何譴責的。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Post-modifier)
    The attack struck an airport complex housing terminals 3 and 4 for domestic flights, a regional official said, speaking on condition of anonymity.
    句中的「housing terminals 3 and 4...」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 an airport complex（包含第3和第4航廈的機場綜合設施）。
