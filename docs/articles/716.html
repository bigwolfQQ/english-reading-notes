多納文深入剖析：台灣的主權主張：一個沒有任何行政團隊願意重新劃製的地圖

Donovan’s Deep Dives: Taiwan’s territorial claims: A map no administration is willing to redraw

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • dispute n. 爭端，爭議
    For a small nation, Taiwan has an unusually long list of territorial disputes.
    對於一個小國而言，台灣擁有異常長的一串領土爭端。
  • administration n. （政府的）行政團隊，政府
    This creates considerable headaches politically for the government, especially — but not exclusively — administrations led by the Democratic Progressive Party (DPP).
    這在政治上給政府帶來了相當大的頭痛，特別是——但不限於——由民主進步黨（DPP）領導的行政團隊。
  • sovereignty n. 主權
    The PRC’s current 10-dash line (formerly nine-dash), with a few minor tweaks, is based on the ROC’s claims.
    中華人民共和國現行的「十段線」（前身為「九段線」），經過一些微調後，正是基於中華民國的主張。
  • ideology n. 意識形態
    The KMT’s core ideology is Chinese ethnonationalism, and hardliners take a very absolutist stance on sovereignty.
    國民黨的核心意識形態是中國族群民族主義，強硬派對主權採取非常絕對的立場。
  • pragmatic adj. 務實的
    Yet, as the Thitu Island loss demonstrates, in power the party is more realistic and pragmatic.
    然而，正如失去中業島所證明的那樣，當執政時，該黨會變得更加現實和務實。
  • proposal n. 提案，建議
    Yet, just a few weeks before, he had sent a proposal to the Japanese, and in 2013 signed a joint fisheries agreement allowing Taiwanese access to some of the waters around the Senkakus.
    然而，就在幾週前，他向日方提交了一份提案，並於2013年簽署了一份聯合漁業協議，允許台灣漁民進入釣魚台周邊的部分水域。
  • agreement n. 協議，協定
    Yet, just a few weeks before, he had sent a proposal to the Japanese, and in 2013 signed a joint fisheries agreement allowing Taiwanese access to some of the waters around the Senkakus.
    然而，就在幾週前，他向日方提交了一份提案，並於2013年簽署了一份聯合漁業協議，允許台灣漁民進入釣魚台周邊的部分水域。
  • resolution n. 決議
    Less than two months into president Tsai Ing-wen’s (蔡英文) first term in 2016, the Taipei Times reported that a Presidential Office source stated that the administration had reached an internal resolution on Taiwan’s territorial claims over the South China Sea...
    在蔡英文總統2016年第一任期開始不到兩個月時，《台北時報》報導稱，總統府消息人士表示，行政團隊已就台灣對南海的領土主張達成內部決議...
  • condemn v. 譴責
    The Tsai administration was obliged to condemn the ruling, and Tsai rushed to give a speech on a navy frigate.
    蔡政府被迫譴責該裁決，蔡英文更是匆忙登上軍艦發表講話。
  • budget n. 預算
    If they look weak on sovereignty, this will give the opposition a vector to further question why they need bigger defense budgets.
    如果他們在主權問題上顯得軟弱，這將給反對派提供一個切入點，進一步質疑為什麼他們需要更多的國防預算。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Constitutional reforms beginning in 1991 distinguished the ROC’s “free area” from the mainland and cleared the way for a fully elected legislature.
    句中的 "beginning in 1991" 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "Constitutional reforms"，相當於形容詞子句 "which began in 1991" 的簡化用法，在新聞英語中非常常見，能讓句子更為緊湊精煉。
  • 附屬連接詞引導的省略句 (Ellipsis after Subordinating Conjunctions)
    When out of power, they use it to attack the DPP.
    句中的 "When out of power" 省略了主詞與be動詞（原句結構應為 "When they are out of power"）。在英文中，當副詞子句的主詞與主要子句的主詞一致，且動詞為 be 動詞時，通常可以省略主詞與 be 動詞，使語氣更為明快。
  • 假設語氣 (Subjunctive Mood / Hypothetical Condition)
    If they look weak on sovereignty, this will give the opposition a vector to further question why they need bigger defense budgets.
    此句使用第一類條件句（First Conditional），結構為 「If + 現在式動詞, 主詞 + will + 原形動詞」，用來表達未來可能發生的真實條件與其預期結果。
