    Taiwan High Speed Rail Corp (THSRC) said yesterday that beginning on Thursday, it would operate eight southbound train services...

  • result in phr. v. 導致、造成
    ...resulting in large numbers of non-reserved-seat passengers spilling into reserved-seat cars...
    ...導致大量自由座旅客湧入對號座車廂...
  • statistics n. 統計數據
    Statistics from the Ministry of Transportation and Communications showed the number of high-speed rail passengers reached a record-high...
    交通部統計數據顯示，高鐵旅客量達到歷史新高...
  • capacity n. 容量、運量、產能
    Railway Bureau Director-General Yang Cheng-chun (楊正君) said last week that pressure on the high-speed system’s capacity is unlikely to ease...
    鐵路局長楊正君上週表示，高鐵系統運量的壓力不太可能緩解...

    ...the high-speed rail operator said that it would implement special service deployment plans...

    在雙十國定假日旅遊期間，將提供 23 個加開列車班次，以協助容納增加的旅客需求...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    ...between 5pm and 9pm every Friday consisting solely of non-reserved seat carriages to ease passenger pressure.
    句中的 "consisting solely of..." 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "train services"（列車班次），等於形容詞子句 "which consist solely of..."，在新聞英語中常用來精簡語句。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞補語 / 藉由 after 引發的動名詞複合句
    The announcement came after the high-speed railway operator faced criticism for not limiting sales of non-reserved seats...
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾與被動語態觀念
    ...special service deployment plans designed to maximize its transport capacity during peak or even super peak hours.
    "designed to maximize..." 是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞 "service deployment plans"。這裡省略了關代與be動詞（即 which are designed to...），用過去分詞表達被動與「被設計來做某事」的含意。
  • 副詞子句簡化或對應時態 (When 引導的時間子句)
    ...while the super-peak hours occur when the time coincides with the beginning of a long weekend or a major national holiday, it said.
    "when" 引導一個副詞子句（時間子句）「當……的時候」，用來修飾動詞 "occur"。子句內部的時態使用簡單現在時（coincides）來表達客觀定義與常態規定。
  • 對等連接詞連接並列子句與分詞補充說明
    This would reduce the train deployment time by one hour and increase capacity during peak hours, quickly transporting southbound passengers...
    句中的 "would reduce... and increase..." 是助動詞 would 後面接兩個並列的原形動詞。最後的 ", quickly transporting..." 則是現在分詞構句，用來補充說明前面動作所帶來的結果或伴隨的動作。
