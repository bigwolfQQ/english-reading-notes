萬事達卡表示：旅遊消費模式正在改變

Travel spending changing, Mastercard says

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • spending patterns n. 消費模式
    Taiwanese visiting Japan and South Korea are changing their spending patterns and the places they visit, Mastercard’s data and services team said in a statement yesterday.
    萬事達卡（Mastercard）的數據與服務團隊昨天在聲明中表示，造訪日本和南韓的台灣旅客正在改變他們的消費模式以及造訪的地點。
  • statement n. 聲明，陳述
    Japan, China and South Korea are the top three tourism destinations for Taiwanese, with 14.5 million people traveling abroad in the first eight months of this year, the statement said, citing Tourism Administration data.
    該聲明引觀光署數據指出，日本、中國和南韓是台灣人的前三大旅遊目的地，今年前八個月有 1,450 萬人次出國旅遊。
  • holder n. 持有人
    Last year, Taiwan-issued Mastercard holders’ in-person spending grew 39 percent in Japan and 44 percent in South Korea compared with the previous year, Mastercard Taiwan managing director Eva Chen (陳懿文) said in the statement.
    萬事達卡台灣區總經理陳懿文在聲明中表示，與前一年相比，去年台灣發行的萬事達卡持卡人在日本的實體消費成長了 39%，在南韓則成長了 44%。
  • managing director n. 總經理，常務董事
    Last year, Taiwan-issued Mastercard holders’ in-person spending grew 39 percent in Japan and 44 percent in South Korea compared with the previous year, Mastercard Taiwan managing director Eva Chen (陳懿文) said in the statement.
    萬事達卡台灣區總經理陳懿文在聲明中表示，與前一年相比，去年台灣發行的萬事達卡持卡人在日本的實體消費成長了 39%，在南韓則成長了 44%。
  • outpace v. 超越，跑得比...快
    The figures outpaced the year-on-year growth in Taiwanese tourists’ spending on consumer goods, which were 16 percent in Japan and 35 percent in South Korea during the period, Chen said.
    陳懿文說，這些數字超越了同期台灣旅客在消費財貨上的年增率，該年增率在日本為 16%，在南韓為 35%。
  • year-on-year adj. / adv. 年對年（同期比較）
    The figures outpaced the year-on-year growth in Taiwanese tourists’ spending on consumer goods, which were 16 percent in Japan and 35 percent in South Korea during the period, Chen said.
    陳懿文說，這些數字超越了同期台灣旅客在消費財貨上的年增率，該年增率在日本為 16%，在南韓為 35%。
  • consumer goods n. 消費財，消費品
    The figures outpaced the year-on-year growth in Taiwanese tourists’ spending on consumer goods, which were 16 percent in Japan and 35 percent in South Korea during the period, Chen said.
    陳懿文說，這些數字超越了同期台灣旅客在消費財貨上的年增率，該年增率在日本為 16%，在南韓為 35%。
  • account for v. phr. 佔（比例、總數）
    Tokyo and Osaka accounted for a combined 41 percent of Taiwanese visitors’ spending in Japan, but it increased by 66 percent in Fukuoka, 48 percent in Okinawa and 36 percent in Sapporo, she said.
    她說，東京和大阪合計佔了台灣旅客在日本消費的 41%，但在福岡成長了 66%、沖繩成長了 48%、札幌成長了 36%。
  • by comparison adv. phr. 相較之下
    By comparison, Taiwanese tourism spending in Tokyo increased by just 6 percent, she said.
    她指出，相較之下，台灣人在東京的旅遊消費僅成長了 6%。
  • indicate v. 顯示，指出
    The patterns indicate that Taiwanese increasingly favor experiencing a foreign culture over buying consumer products, she added.
    她補充道，這些模式表明，台灣人越來越偏好體驗外國文化，勝過購買消費性產品。
  • favor v. 偏愛，較喜歡
    The patterns indicate that Taiwanese increasingly favor experiencing a foreign culture over buying consumer products, she added.
    她補充道，這些模式表明，台灣人越來越偏好體驗外國文化，勝過購買消費性產品。

文法重點

  • 分詞作後置修飾語（Present Participle as Post-modifier）
    Data gleaned from Taiwanese travelers in 2024 and last year showed changes in their habits, the team said.
    過去分詞片語「gleaned from Taiwanese travelers in 2024 and last year」用來修飾前面的名詞「Data」，相當於形容詞子句「that was gleaned from...」，省略了「which/that were」，使句子更為精簡緊湊。
  • 獨立分詞構句（Nominative Absolute Construction）
    Japan, China and South Korea are the top three tourism destinations for Taiwanese, with 14.5 million people traveling abroad in the first eight months of this year, the statement said, citing Tourism Administration data.
    句中的「with 14.5 million people traveling abroad...」是由「with + 名詞 + 現在分詞」構成的附帶狀況結構，用來補充說明前句發生時的背景數據或伴隨狀態。
  • 比較級對比結構（Comparative Structure: outpace）
    The figures outpaced the year-on-year growth in Taiwanese tourists’ spending on consumer goods...
    動詞「outpace」意指「在速度或數量上超越」，此處用來直接連接主詞（The figures）與受詞（growth），表達數據成長幅度超過消費品成長，是商業新聞報導中常用來表現成長率高低對比的高效動詞。
  • 數值倍數表達法（Multiplicative Comparison）
    Taiwanese attending South Korean music concerts outspent those who were vacationing in the country four-fold in terms of average daily spending, she said.
    句中的「four-fold」表示「四倍」，搭配動詞「outspend... four-fold」清楚表達音樂會參與者的消費額是普通度假者的四倍，這在多益閱讀測驗的數據比較題中非常常見。
