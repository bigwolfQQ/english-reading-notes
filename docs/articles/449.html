文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作附帶結果或補充說明
    National Tsing Hua University (NTHU) President John Kao (高為元) said Thursday he has donated his May salary to the university and will do so every year in office, apologizing again for pursuing a chancellorship at an American university.
    句尾的「apologizing again...」是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明主句動作發生時的伴隨狀態或結果，意指校長在宣布捐薪水的同時，也一併表達了歉意。
  • 現在分詞引導的原因狀語分詞構句
    The 57-year-old biomedical engineering professor said that he overlooked the unique nature of the presidency by exploring personal career options so early in his second term, failing to live up to the expectations of the Tsing Hua community.
    句尾的「failing to live up to...」是由分詞構句簡化而來，原本可能是「and he failed to...」，改寫為現在分詞後，用來表達前面行為所導致的負面結果（辜負了期望）。
  • 動名詞片語作介系詞的受詞
    At the time, he also attributed his decision to seek the role to his 'insufficient understanding of the cultural differences between Western and Eastern workplaces,' having lived and worked overseas for many years.
    介系詞「to」後面接了動名詞片語「seeking the role」作為受詞；同時句尾的「having lived...」則是完成式分詞構句，用來解釋他產生這種理解不足的原因（因為在海外生活工作多年）。
  • 關係代間詞子句作修飾語
    On Sept. 2, the university's oversight committee passed motions to look into the matter -- including reviewing Kao's leave records and clarifying when senior executives became aware of his job search.
    在此句中，「when senior executives became aware of his job search」是一個由疑問詞當作連接詞的名詞子句，作為「clarifying」的受詞，用以說明需要被釐清的不確定的時間點內容。
