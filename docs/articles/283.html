無人機入侵迫使台中空軍基地暫時關閉

Drone intrusion temporarily shuts Taichung air base

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • unauthorized adj. 未經授權的，擅自的
    An unauthorized drone intrusion over Ching Chuan Kang Air Base (清泉崗基地) at Taichung International Airport on Tuesday night forced the temporary closure of the base...
    週二晚間，一架未經授權的無人機入侵台中航空站的清泉崗空軍基地，迫使該基地暫時關閉...
  • temporary adj. 暫時的，臨時的
    ...forced the temporary closure of the base, diverting eight fighter jets and delaying a commercial flight, authorities said.
    當局表示，此舉迫使該基地暫時關閉，並轉降了 8 架戰鬥機並延誤了一架商業航班。
  • closure n. 停業，關閉
    ...after a drone was spotted entering the airspace, the control tower declared an emergency closure of the airport...
    ...在發現無人機進入領空後，塔台宣布機場緊急關閉...
  • schedule v. 安排，預定
    Air Force Command Headquarters said that clear weather had allowed for scheduled night training exercises...
    空軍司令部表示，良好的天候允許進行既定的夜間飛行訓練...
  • notify v. 通知，告知
    After airport operations personnel spotted the drone, the military immediately notified the Taichung Police Department’s Cingshuei Precinct...
    機場營運人員發現該無人機後，軍方立即通知台中市政府警察局清水分局...
  • dispatch v. 派遣，發送
    ...which dispatched officers to search the affected area in Sishih Borough (西勢), police said.
    警方表示，該分局派遣員警前往西勢里的受影響區域進行搜查。
  • investigation n. 調查
    The air force has handed the case over to the Aviation Police Bureau for further investigation.
    空軍已將此案移交航空警察局進一步調查。
  • penalty n. 處罰，罰款
    The bureau’s Taichung station said that flying a drone within the airport’s 2.6km restricted no-fly zone carries a heavy penalty.
    航警局台中分站表示，在機場 2.6 公里限制禁飛區內飛行無人機將面臨重罰。
  • revoke v. 撤銷，吊銷
    Violators could have their drone operator license revoked and be fined NT$300,000 to NT$1.5 million...
    違規者的無人機操作證可能會被吊銷，並被處以新台幣 30 萬元至 150 萬元罰款...
  • fine v. 處以罰款
    Violators could have their drone operator license revoked and be fined NT$300,000 to NT$1.5 million...
    違規者的無人機操作證可能會被吊銷，並被處以新台幣 30 萬元至 150 萬元罰款...
  • confiscate v. 沒收，充公
    ...in addition to having their equipment confiscated, police said.
    警方表示，除此之外其設備也會被沒收。
  • equip v. 裝備，配備
    ...Taiwan Association for Security and Development chairman Chi Tung-yun (紀東昀) said that while all military airports in Taiwan are equipped with anti-drone systems...
    ...台灣安全發展協會理事長紀東昀表示，雖然台灣所有軍用機場都配備了反無人機系統...
  • deploy v. 部署，調動
    ...the military should expand the deployment of anti-drone systems to include restricted safety zones along surrounding flight paths, he said.
    他說，軍方應擴大部署反無人機系統，將周邊航道的限制安全區納入其中。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 / 形容詞化用法
    The closure forced a passenger flight arriving from Macau to circle the airport before it was allowed to land, Chang said.
    句中的「arriving from Macau」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前方名詞「a passenger flight（客機）」，相當於形容詞子句「that arrived from Macau」。在多益閱讀中常出現在填空題，用以測試分詞的主動修飾概念。
  • 使役動詞 force 的受詞補語用法 (force + O. + to V)
    An unauthorized drone intrusion over Ching Chuan Kang Air Base... forced the temporary closure of the base, diverting eight fighter jets and delaying a commercial flight, authorities said.
    句型「force + 受詞 + to V」意指「強迫某人／某事物去做某事」。雖然在此句中 force 後方直接接名詞受詞 closure，但第二段的「forcing eight Indigenous Defense Fighter jets in the air to divert...」則是典型的 force + 受詞 + to V 結構，多益測驗常考此類動詞的受詞補語型態。
  • 被動語態的應用 (have + O. + p.p.)
    Violators could have their drone operator license revoked and be fined NT$300,000 to NT$1.5 million... in addition to having their equipment confiscated, police said.
    此句包含了「have + 受詞 + p.p.」的被動結構（如 have their drone operator license revoked 與 having their equipment confiscated），用來表達「使某物被……（遭受某種處置）」。這是多益測驗中高級文法的常考句型。
  • 讓步副詞子句縮寫 / 分詞構句
    Responding to criticism that the air force did not employ anti-drone defenses, Taiwan Association for Security and Development chairman Chi Tung-yun said...
    句首的「Responding to criticism...」是由分詞構句簡化而來，原本可能是「While he was responding to criticism...」或「As he responded to criticism...」。分詞構句能讓新聞英文更簡練流暢，也是多益文法題與閱讀測驗的核心考點。
