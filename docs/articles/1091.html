Taipei Times · 2026-09-24

委員會將決定調高薪資

Committee to decide on wage increase

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • decide v. 決定
    The Minimum Wage Review Committee is to meet today to decide whether to increase the minimum wage next year
    最低工資審議委員會預計於今天開會，決定明年是否調高最低工資
  • increase v. / n. 增加，提高
    Business groups have called for an increase of about 3 percent.
    工商團體已呼籲調高約 3%。
  • rate n. 比率，費率，薪資標準
    saying the monthly rate could top NT$30,000 (US$946) and the hourly rate NT$200.
    表示月薪可能會突破新台幣 3 萬元（合 946 美元），時薪則可能突破新台幣 200 元。
  • representatives n. 代表
    The committee — comprising representatives of labor groups, businesses, the government and academia — is required by the Minimum Wage Act (最低工資法) to meet in the third quarter each year.
    該委員會由勞方、資方、政府及學術界代表組成，依據《最低工資法》規定，每年第三季必須開會。
  • take effect phr. 生效
    Changes to the minimum wage that are approved take effect on Jan. 1 the following year unless the Executive Yuan sets a different date.
    經批准的最低工資變動將於次年 1 月 1 日生效，除非行政院另定日期。
  • proposal n. 提案，建議
    The groups said that it had offered a compromise proposal, but business representatives had failed to reach a consensus, prompting it to keep its demand at a 7.77 percent increase.
    各團體表示，他們曾提出妥協方案，但資方代表未能達成共識，因而促使其維持 7.77% 的調幅訴求。
  • forecast n. / v. 預測，預報
    Labor groups are seeking 7.77 percent, calculated by adding the central bank’s forecast of 2.03 percent inflation plus half of its GDP forecast of 11.48 percent.
    勞方團體則爭取調高 7.77%，此數據的計算方式是加上央行預測的 2.03% 通膨率，再加上 GDP 預測值 11.48% 的一半。
  • take into account phr. 考慮到，顧及
    The committee would consider concerns expressed by labor groups that the minimum wage should provide workers with a basic standard of living with dignity, while taking into account the wide differences between industries, he said.
    他表示，委員會將考量勞工團體所表達的關切，即最低工資應提供勞工具有尊嚴的基本生活水準，同時也要兼顧各產業之間的巨大差異。
  • baseline n. 基准，基準線
    However, the committee said that the adjustment should use CPI growth as a baseline while taking industry differences into account.
    然而，委員會表示，調整應以 CPI 成長率為基準，同時考量產業差異。
  • consensus n. 共識
    The groups said that it had offered a compromise proposal, but business representatives had failed to reach a consensus, prompting it to keep its demand at a 7.77 percent increase.
    各團體表示，他們曾提出妥協方案，但資方代表未能達成共識，因而促使其維持 7.77% 的調幅訴求。

文法重點

  • be to do (預定、即將)
    The Minimum Wage Review Committee is to meet today to decide whether to increase the minimum wage next year
    「be + to V」經常用於新聞英語中，表示官方或正式排定的計畫、預定發生的未來事件（相當於 is scheduled to 或 will）。
  • 分詞片語當形容詞後置修飾 (comprising...)
    The committee — comprising representatives of labor groups, businesses, the government and academia — is required by the Minimum Wage Act (最低工資法) to meet in the third quarter each year.
    句中的「comprising representatives...」是現在分詞片語，置於名詞片語 The committee 後方，用來補充說明委員會的組成成員，相當於關代子句「which comprises...」。
  • 被動語態 (be required by... / be approved)
    Changes to the minimum wage that are approved take effect on Jan. 1 the following year unless the Executive Yuan sets a different date.
    在商業與法律新聞中，經常用被動語態來客觀描述規定或程序。例如「Changes... that are approved」表示「被批准的變動」，強調動作的承受者。
  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況 (with + O + V-ing)
    ..., with Minister of Labor Hung Sun-han (洪申翰) saying the monthly rate could top NT$30,000 (US$946) and the hourly rate NT$200.
    「with + 受詞 + 現在分詞(V-ing)」結構在新聞英語中非常實用，用來表示「在……情況下」或同時發生的附帶狀況，此處用來補充說明勞動部長發言的內容。
