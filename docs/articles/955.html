文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present/Past Participle as Postmodifier)
    The 34-year-old Chien Hui-hsuan (簡慧萱), who won bronze in the women's individual kata event Sunday, was joined by 22-year-old Tien Hsin-erh (田欣兒) and 23-year-old Hung Chih-hsuan (洪芷暄) for the team competition.
    句中的「who won bronze...」為關係代間詞子句，用來補充說明主詞 Chien Hui-hsuan 的背景資訊。在新聞英語中，常透過這種結構或分詞片語來補充人物或事件的細節，使句子更緊湊。
  • 現在分詞表結果的附帶子句 (Present Participle Expressing Result)
    The Taiwanese trio defeated Macao by a 5-0 decision in the quarterfinals but lost 5-0 to Vietnam in the semifinals, sending them into a bronze-medal contest against Nepal.
  • 關係代間詞子句補充說明 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    He lost 4-1 to Jordan's Mohammad Al-Jafari in the quarterfinals but remained in medal contention through repechage, which gives athletes defeated by finalists another route to a bronze-medal bout.
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle as Postmodifier)
    ...repechage, which gives athletes defeated by finalists another route to a bronze-medal bout.
    句中的「defeated by finalists」是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞 athletes。完整未簡化的形式為「who were defeated by finalists」，此處省略了「who were」，讓句子更為精簡有力。
