本週將於屏東附近舉行實彈射擊演習

Live-fire test to be held near Pingtung this week

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • involve v. 涉及、包含、參與
    The military is holding live-fire drills featuring missiles, rockets and drones, involving all service branches and the coast guard at Jioupeng Base (九鵬基地) in Pingtung County this week, a source said.
    根據消息人士表示，軍方本週在屏東縣九鵬基地舉行包含飛彈、火箭與無人機的實彈演習，並有三軍部隊與海巡署共同參與。
  • capability n. 能力、才能
    The exercise is expected to involve Patriot, Tian Kung (Sky Bow, 天弓), Hsiung Feng (Brave Wind, 雄風) and Land Sword II (陸射劍二) missiles to practice long-range precision strike capabilities, they said
    他們表示，此次演習預計納入愛國者、天弓、雄風及陸射劍二型飛彈，藉此磨練長程精準打擊能力。
  • joint adj. 聯合的、共同的
    Coast guard vessels would fire anti-ship missiles to drill the joint combat capabilities of the armed forces and the coast guard in wartime.
    海巡署艦艇將發射反艦飛彈，以演練三軍與海巡署在戰時的聯合作戰能力。
  • annually adv. 一年一度地、每年
    With the exercise hosted annually on an rotating basis by the navy and air force, this year’s exercise is hosted by the navy, and a live-fire warning notice for sea and air targets was issued for the exercise area, which covers a large area off the east coast, the source said.
    消息人士指出，這項演習由海軍與空軍每年輪流主辦，今年由海軍主辦，演習區域已發布針對海空目標的實彈射擊警戒區通知，範圍涵蓋東部外海的一大片區域。
  • announce v. 宣布、公告
    The navy’s announcement said three areas are designated for this exercise.
    海軍的公告指出，本次演習劃定了三個區域。
  • designate v. 指定、指派、標出
    The navy’s announcement said three areas are designated for this exercise.
    海軍的公告指出，本次演習劃定了三個區域。
  • restrict v. 限制、限定
    The restricted sea and air zones are the same as those used in drills for the past three years.
    這些受限制的海空域與過去三年演習所使用的區域相同。
  • incorporate v. 納入、結合、包含
    The military is expected to incorporate HIMARS batteries into future drills, they said.
    他們並表示，軍方預計將海馬士砲兵連納入未來的演習中。
  • approve v. 批准、贊成、同意
    The Patriot missile would not be featured in this live-fire exercise unless approved by the US, for two reasons, they said.
    他們指出，除非獲得美國批准，否則愛國者飛彈不會在此次實彈演習中亮相，原因有二。
  • performance n. 表現、性能、績效
    The first is to deny unfriendly observers a chance to see it in flight, as firing missiles could allow potential adversaries to gather performance data with reconnaissance or intelligence-gathering aircraft or vessels.
    第一個原因是為了不讓不友善的觀察者有機會看到它飛行，因為發射飛彈可能會讓潛在對手透過偵察或情資蒐集飛機與船艦來蒐集性能數據。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾
    The military is holding live-fire drills featuring missiles, rockets and drones, involving all service branches and the coast guard at Jioupeng Base (九鵬基地) in Pingtung County this week, a source said.
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The exercise is expected to involve Patriot, Tian Kung (Sky Bow, 天弓), Hsiung Feng (Brave Wind, 雄風) and Land Sword II (陸射劍二) missiles to practice long-range precision strike capabilities, they said
  • 對等連接詞引導的條件句 (unless 句型)
    The Patriot missile would not be featured in this live-fire exercise unless approved by the US, for two reasons, they said.
  • 分詞構句表原因 (as + 分詞/子句)
    The first is to deny unfriendly observers a chance to see it in flight, as firing missiles could allow potential adversaries to gather performance data with reconnaissance or intelligence-gathering aircraft or vessels.
