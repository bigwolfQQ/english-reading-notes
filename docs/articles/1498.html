立法院長韓國瑜為台灣許下三個願望，並呼籲政治妥協

Speaker Han makes 3 wishes for Taiwan, calls for political compromise

單字解析

  • compromise n. / v. 妥協、折衷
    Legislative Speaker Han Kuo-yu (韓國瑜) on Saturday wished for a secure Taiwan, prosperity for its people and a thriving democracy, while urging the government and the opposition to resolve their differences through compromise.
    立法院長韓國瑜週六期許台灣安全、人民安居樂業且民主繁榮，同時敦促朝野透過妥協化解歧見。
  • prosperity n. 繁榮、興旺
    His second wish was for greater prosperity for the people.
    他的第二個願望是人民更加繁榮。
  • thrive v. 繁榮、興旺、蓬勃發展
    so that high-tech industries do not thrive while other sectors struggle.
    以免高科技產業蓬勃發展而其他產業陷入困境。
  • sector n. 部門、行業
    He urged the government to ensure that older manufacturing industries, tourism, the cultural and creative sectors, health care, agriculture and fisheries also benefit from Taiwan's economic growth
    他敦促政府確保傳統製造業、觀光業、文化創意產業、醫療保健、農業及漁業也能從台灣的經濟成長中受益
  • standoff n. 僵局、對峙
    He warned that the standoff between the Executive Yuan and the Legislative Yuan could damage Taiwan's international reputation as a democracy
    他警告說，行政院與立法院之間的僵局可能會損害台灣作為民主國家的國際聲譽
  • reputation n. 名譽、聲望
    He warned that the standoff between the Executive Yuan and the Legislative Yuan could damage Taiwan's international reputation as a democracy
    他警告說，行政院與立法院之間的僵局可能會損害台灣作為民主國家的國際聲譽
  • dialogue n. 對話
    President Lai Ching-te (賴清德) and Premier Cho Jung-tai (卓榮泰) must help resolve the standoff through dialogue and compromise, he said.
    他表示，總統賴清德與行政院長卓榮泰必須協助透過對話與妥協來化解僵局。
  • inevitably adv. 不可避免地
    Quoting former President Chen Shui-bian (陳水扁), he said, "Politics inevitably involves conflict, but compromise leads to progress."
    他引用前總統陳水扁的話表示：「Politics inevitably involves conflict, but compromise leads to progress.」（政治難免有衝突，但妥協帶來進步。）
  • unite v. 團結、聯合
    Chuang Jui-hsiung (莊瑞雄), chief executive of the ruling Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) legislative caucus, said national celebrations should unite the public and not be used as an occasion for political attacks
    執政黨民主進步黨（DPP）立法院黨團總召莊瑞雄表示，國家慶典應該團結大眾，不應被用作政治攻擊的場合
  • overcome v. 克服、戰勝
    The speaker should lead by example if he wants political parties to overcome their differences, Chuang said.
    莊瑞雄說，如果立法院長希望各政黨克服歧見，就應該以身作則。
  • impasse n. 僵局、死胡同
    Lai should rise above party politics and help resolve the impasse between the executive and legislative branches, Hung said
    洪孟楷表示，賴總統應超越黨派政治，協助解決行政與立法部門之間的僵局
  • safeguard v. 維護、保護
    He praised Han's remarks as an important reminder to the government of its responsibility to safeguard Taiwan's security and democracy.
    他讚賞韓國瑜的談話，認為這是對政府肩負維護台灣安全與民主責任的重要提醒。
  • abide by phr. v. 遵守、遵循
    Taiwan People's Party Legislator Hung Yu-hsiang (洪毓祥) also praised the remarks, saying democracy is about more than elections and voting and requires those in power to accept scrutiny and abide by the Constitution.
    台灣民眾黨立委洪毓祥也讚賞這些言論，表示民主不僅僅是選舉和投票，還需要當權者接受監督並遵守憲法。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作附帶狀況（分詞構句）
    Legislative Speaker Han Kuo-yu (韓國瑜) on Saturday wished for a secure Taiwan, prosperity for its people and a thriving democracy, while urging the government and the opposition to resolve their differences through compromise.
    句尾的「while urging...」為分詞構句，省略了主詞與be動詞（while [he was] urging...），用來表達主句動作發生時的同時伴隨動作，意指「一邊期許……，同時敦促……」。
  • 過去分詞作後置定修飾語
    The Cabinet's repeated refusal to countersign bills passed by the Legislature raised serious questions...
    句中的「passed by the Legislature」是過去分詞片語，用來後置修飾前面的名詞「bills」（法案），相當於關代子句「which were passed by the Legislature」，意思是「由立法院通過的法案」。
  • 名詞子句作受詞
    He warned that the standoff between the Executive Yuan and the Legislative Yuan could damage Taiwan's international reputation as a democracy...
    連接詞「that」引導一個名詞子句（that the standoff... as a democracy），在句中作為動詞「warned」的受詞，用來表達警告的具體內容。
