倫敦紅磚巷的資料中心引發抗爭

Data center sparks battle on London’s Brick Lane

單字解析

  • oppose v. 反對
    residents oppose plans for a data center in a disused brewery
    居民們反對在一座荒廢的釀酒廠興建資料中心的計畫
  • focal point n. phr. 核心焦點，中心點
    This has really been the focal point of the British Bangladeshi community
    這裡一直是英國孟加拉裔社群的焦點
  • infrastructure n. 基礎設施
    to help power the British capital’s digital infrastructure boom
    協助供應英國首都數位基礎設施的榮景
  • retail space n. phr. 零售空間
    The 7.5 hectare site would also be transformed by new offices and retail space
    這處佔地 7.5 公頃的場地也將透過新建的辦公室和零售空間進行改造
  • demand n. 需求
    particularly as demand rises for facilities supporting artificial intelligence
    特別是隨著支援人工智慧設施的需求增加
  • affordable adj. 負擔得起的
    about the need for affordable social housing for young people
    關於對年輕人負擔得起的社會住宅的需求
  • capacity n. 容量，生產能力
    gave “significant weight” to the benefits of additional data center capacity
    對額外資料中心容量的效益給予了「高度權衡」
  • decline v. 婉拒，謝絕
    The government’s housing department declined to comment on its decision
    政府的房屋部門拒絕就其決定發表評論
  • project v. 預估，預測
    It projects electricity consumption from centers will double over five years to 2030.
    該機構預測，到 2030 年，資料中心的電力消耗將在五年內翻倍。
  • consultant n. 顧問
    For industry consultant Mark Acton, the infrastructure is increasingly essential
    對產業顧問馬克·阿克頓而言，這項基礎設施日益不可或缺
  • expansion n. 擴張，擴大
    not kept pace with the expansion of the sector
    並未跟上該產業的擴張步伐
  • intention n. 意圖，打算
    Carol Burns, born and raised in the area, has no intention of going anywhere.
    在該地區出生長大的卡羅爾·伯恩斯完全沒有離開的打算。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞短語作後置修飾（形容詞用）
    residents oppose plans for a data center in a disused brewery, citing the erosion of heritage and need for social housing.
    句尾的「citing...」是由現在分詞帶領的分詞片語，用來補充說明前面主句動作產生的理由或伴隨狀況，相當於「and they cite...」的簡化用法，在新聞英語中非常常見。
  • 現在分詞引導附帶狀況與原因
    The East End financial data center is an unlikely addition to the graffiti-lined Brick Lane, where tourists spill between vintage shops and line up for bagels served through the night.
  • 關係副詞 where 引導非限定關係子句
    ...the graffiti-lined Brick Lane, where tourists spill between vintage shops and line up for bagels...
    「where」在此引導一個非限定關係子句（前面有逗號），用來補充說明前面的先行詞「Brick Lane（紅磚巷）」是一個怎樣的地方，在句中作地方副詞用。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞
    The International Energy Agency in April reported that the global electricity demand of data centers grew by 17 percent last year.
    「reported that...」中的「that」引導一個名詞子句，作為「reported」的受詞，表達報告的內容。「that」在非正式語境中常被省略，但在新聞英語中通常會保留以求嚴謹。
  • 假設語氣（條件句）
    If Britain is serious about sovereignty over its AI infrastructure, “you’ve got to have somewhere to put it,” Acton said.
    此句為第一類型條件句（First Conditional），結構為「If + 現在式動詞, 主詞 + 조動詞/have got to + 原形動詞」，用來表達真實或極有可能發生的假設狀況。
