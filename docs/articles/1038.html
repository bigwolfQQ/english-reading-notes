單字解析

  • pursue v. 追求，尋求
    An Afghan cyclist who had to disguise herself as a man to pursue her sporting dreams won a silver medal at the Asian Games yesterday...
    一名阿富汗女單車手昨日在亞運會上奪得銀牌，她曾為了追求運動夢想而不得不女扮男裝...
  • triumph n. 勝利，重大成就
    Italy-based Fariba Hashimi powered home to second in the women’s road race near Nagoya, calling the triumph “something special.”
    常駐義大利的法里巴·哈希米（Fariba Hashimi）在名古屋附近的女子公路賽中奮力奪得第二名，她將這次的勝利稱作是「非常特別的成就」。
  • recount v. 詳細敘述，講述
    “I can’t explain my feelings now,” said the 23-year-old, before recounting how she was attacked with stones and trained disguised as a man...
    「我現在無法用言語形容我的感受，」這位23歲的選手在回顧青少年時期剛接觸這項運動時，曾遭到石頭攻擊，並女扮男裝受訓的經歷前如此表示...
  • represent v. 代表
    “I hope that the next generation will come here and represent their country without any fear.”
    「我希望下一代能來到這裡，毫無畏懼地代表他們的國家。」
  • access n. 接近或使用某物的權利，通道
    The Taliban returned to power in 2021 after the US withdrawal from Afghanistan and severely restricted women’s access to education and public life...
    在美國自阿富汗撤軍後，塔利班於2021年重新掌權，並嚴格限制女性接受教育和參與公共生活的權利...
  • resilience n. 恢復力，韌性
    “This silver medal can show a lot of resilience from Afghan women, and I’m really happy.
    「這面銀牌展現了阿富汗女性強大的韌性，我真的很高興。」
  • relocate v. 搬遷，重新安置
    After fleeing Afghanistan with the help of Italian former world champion Alessandra Cappellotto, Hashimi relocated to Italy, where she lives and trains.
    在義大利前世界冠軍亞歷山德拉·卡佩洛托的協助下逃離阿富汗后，哈希米搬遷至義大利，並在那裡生活與訓練。
  • compete v. 競爭，參賽
    She and her sister competed at the Paris Olympics two years ago, and the 2028 Los Angeles Games are drawing closer.
    她和她的妹妹兩年前曾參加巴黎奧運，而2028年洛杉磯奧運也正步步逼近。
  • give up phr. v. 放棄
    “I never want to give up and hopefully we can see [what can happen] again in Los Angeles,” she said.
    「我絕不想放棄，希望我們能在洛杉磯再次見證奇蹟，」她說。
  • preparation n. 準備工作
    “I’m here without a mechanic, without any preparation,” she said.
    「我來到這裡時沒有技師，也沒有做任何準備，」她說。

文法重點

  • 關係代名詞子句（限定用法）
    An Afghan cyclist who had to disguise herself as a man to pursue her sporting dreams won a silver medal at the Asian Games yesterday...
    句中的 "who had to disguise herself as a man..." 是用來修飾先行詞 "An Afghan cyclist" 的關係子句，提供關於這位單車手的進一步資訊。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾
    Italy-based Fariba Hashimi powered home to second in the women’s road race near Nagoya, calling the triumph “something special.”
    句尾的 "calling the triumph..." 是現在分詞（-ing）片語，用來補充說明主詞在比賽中奪得第二名時的動作與心理狀態，相當於副詞子句 "and she called the triumph..." 的簡化。
  • 地方副詞片語與地方副詞子句
    After fleeing Afghanistan with the help of Italian former world champion Alessandra Cappellotto, Hashimi relocated to Italy, where she lives and trains.
    句尾的 ", where she lives and trains" 是由關副詞 where 引導的非限定關係子句，用來補充說明前面先行詞 "Italy"（義大利）的地點，並說明她在該處進行的生活與訓練。
