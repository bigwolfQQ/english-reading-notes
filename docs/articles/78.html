文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作後置修飾與附帶狀況
    The Ministry of Labor (MOL) on Thursday opened a new round of applications for public welfare organizations to join a pilot program that trains and dispatches part-time migrant caregivers, with the goal of extending the service to all 22 cities and counties across Taiwan.
    句中的 "training and dispatching..." 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的 "pilot program"；而句尾的 "with the goal of extending..." 則是 "with + 受詞 + 分詞/介系詞片語" 的複合結構，用來表達附帶的目的或結果，是多益閱讀與商業寫作中常見的高級句構。
  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾
    Launched on April 27 last year to help address growing demand for caregivers, the program currently authorizes 13 public welfare organizations to recruit, train and dispatch non-live-in migrant caregivers...
    句首的 "Launched on April 27 last year..." 是一個省略了主代名詞與be動詞（原句為 Which was launched...）的過去分詞片語，置於句首用以修飾主句的主語 "the program"，表示被動的動作（計畫被啟動），常用於新聞英語中以精簡篇幅。
  • 過去分詞表被動與狀態（受詞補語結構）
    ...with new providers expected to begin operating as early as the start of next year, according to Hu Hsin-yeh...
    此處使用 "with + 受詞 (new providers) + 過去分詞 (expected)" 的結構，表示「在...的情況下」或是補充說明主句動作發生時的伴隨狀態。因為 provider（供應商）是被預期，所以使用過去分詞表被動。
