文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    ...multiple ballistic missiles launched from the Wonsan area toward the East Sea...
    句中的 "launched from the Wonsan area toward the East Sea" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "multiple short-range ballistic missiles"，相當於形容詞子句 "which were launched..." 被省略了主格關代與be動詞，使語氣更為精簡緊湊。
  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況或時間 (Participial Phrase indicating accompanying circumstance or time)
    That launch from the nuclear-armed North came as South Korea and the US held a shorter edition of their annual Ulchi Freedom Shield military exercises, following an order from Trump to scale them back.
    句尾的 "following an order from Trump..." 是由現在分詞 "following" 引導的分詞構句，在此表達時間上的緊接或原因（在川普下令縮減規模之後/由於川普的命令），常用於新聞英語中串聯前後發生的事件。
  • 形容詞片語作主詞補語 (Adjective Phrase as Subject Complement with 'with')
    Trump’s order alarmed US allies in the region, with the president calling the drills “inappropriate and hostile” toward North Korea.
    此處使用 "with + 名詞 + 現在分詞 (calling...)" 的複合結構（常稱為獨立分詞構句），用來補充說明主句發生時伴隨的情況或原因，在英文新聞中非常常見，用以描述某個動作所帶來的附帶反應或說法。
  • 過去分詞作形容詞修飾名詞 (Past Participle modifying a Noun)
    ...in high-profile summits aimed at securing a denuclearization deal...
    句中的 "aimed at securing a denuclearization deal" 是由過去分詞 "aimed" 開頭的片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "summits"，表達這些高峰會「被旨在」達成某個目標，展現多益測驗常考的被動概念修飾用法。
