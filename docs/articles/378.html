文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Phrase of Present Participle）作後置修飾與附帶狀況
    Taiwan's hiring outlook for the fourth quarter of this year improved with the information sector reporting the strongest hiring prospects during the current AI boom...
    句中的 "reporting the strongest hiring prospects..." 為現在分詞片語，用來補充說明 information sector 的狀態，表示「資訊產業回報了……」。
  • 分詞構句（Participle Construction）表結果或伴隨
    The survey calculates its net hiring index by subtracting the percentage of employers anticipating a decline in the size of their workforce from the percentage expecting an increase.
  • 過去分詞（Past Participle）作後置修飾
    ...according to a survey released by human resources advisory firm ManpowerGroup on Tuesday.
    句中的 "released by..." 是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞 survey，相當於關代子句 "which was released by..."，表示「由……所發布的調查」。
  • 對等連接詞連接的多個子句與對比
    The survey found 37 percent of 651 employers polled in Taiwan said they expected to hire more people in the fourth quarter, while 10 percent said they anticipated a decline in hiring and 52 percent said their hiring plans remain unchanged.
    此句運用了 "while" 來表達對比（然而／而），連接多個子句並列陳述不同比例雇主的預期，使長句結構清晰、層次分明。
