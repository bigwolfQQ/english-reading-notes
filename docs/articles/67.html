中央氣象署針對台灣北部、東北部發布大雨特報

Heavy rain warning issued for northern, northeastern Taiwan

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • issue v. 發布、發行
    The Central Weather Administration (CWA) issued extremely heavy rain advisories Friday for Keelung...
    中央氣象署（CWA）於週五針對基隆市……發布超大豪雨特報。
  • advisory n. 特報、警報、公告
    The Central Weather Administration (CWA) issued extremely heavy rain advisories Friday for Keelung...
    中央氣象署（CWA）於週五針對基隆市……發布超大豪雨特報。
  • indicate v. 顯示、指出、象徵
    The "extremely heavy rain" warning for Taiwan's northern coast indicates at least 200 mm of rainfall in 24 hours...
    台灣北海岸的「超大豪雨」警示代表 24 小時內累積雨量達 200 毫米以上……
  • define v. 定義
    The CWA also issued "heavy rain" advisories, defined as accumulated rainfall of at least 80 millimeters in 24 hours...
    氣象署也對……發布「大雨」特報，定義為 24 小時內累積雨量達 80 毫米以上……
  • accumulate v. 累積、積聚
    ...more than 150 mm of rainfall had accumulated at weather stations in Jinshan and Shimen...
    ……金山與石門的氣象站累積雨量已超過 150 毫米……
  • expert n. 專家
    Meanwhile, independent weather expert Wu Der-rong (吳德榮) said northern and eastern eastern Taiwan will see intermittent rain...
    同時，民間氣象專家吳德榮表示，北部與東部地區將持續有間歇性降雨……
  • improve v. 改善、好轉
    The weather will generally improve from next Monday to Thursday, especially in southern Taiwan...
    下週一至週四天氣將普遍好轉，特別是南部地區……
  • shift v. 轉移、移動
    ...as a large low-pressure system is expected to shift east of Taiwan, leading to the arrival of northeasterly winds...
    ……因為一個大型低壓系統預計將移至台灣東部，導致東北風報到……
  • lead to phr. v. 導致、引發
    ...leading to the arrival of northeasterly winds, Wu said.
    吳說，這將導致東北風報到。
  • arrival n. 到達、到來
    ...leading to the arrival of northeasterly winds, Wu said.
    吳說，這將導致東北風報到。
  • relief n. 解除、緩和、寬慰
    ...a welcome relief after an extended run of rainy weather, he said.
    他說，在經歷了一連串漫長的雨勢後，這無疑是一個令人欣慰的舒緩。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle as Postmodifier)
    ...advisories, defined as accumulated rainfall of at least 80 millimeters in 24 hours...
    句中的 "defined as..." 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "advisories"。其完整子句概念為 "which are defined as..."，在此省略了關係代名詞與be動詞，使句子更為精簡。
  • 被動語態的過去完成式 (Past Perfect Passive Voice)
    ...more than 150 mm of rainfall had accumulated at weather stations in Jinshan and Shimen...
    雖然 accumulate 通常作自動詞或及物動詞，此處使用過去完成式的被動語態（had been accumulated 的省略或表狀態之被動），強調在過去某個特定時間點（上午10點40分）之前，雨量已經被累積達到的狀態。
  • be expected to 用法 (預期會……)
    ...as a large low-pressure system is expected to shift east of Taiwan...
    "be expected to + 原形動詞" 是商業與新聞英文中極常見的句構，用來表示「預期／預料某事將會發生」，比直接用 will 更多了一層客觀預測的語氣。
