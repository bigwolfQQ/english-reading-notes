美國網球公開賽冠軍艾лена·芮巴金娜登上世界之巔

US Open champion Elena Rybakina on top of world

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • celebrate v. 慶祝
    Elena Rybakina is on top of the tennis world, celebrating her first US Open title and her rise to No. 1 barely more than three weeks after she feared injury would force her out of the final Grand Slam of the year.
    艾лена·芮巴金娜正處於網球世界的頂峰，慶祝她生涯首座美國網球公開賽冠軍，以及她重返世界排名第一的榮耀；在此之前僅短短三個多星期，她還曾擔心傷勢會迫使她缺席今年最後一場大滿貫賽事。
  • assure v. 確保、使確信
    She had already assured herself of toppling Sabalenka from the No. 1 spot just by reaching the semi-finals.
    她僅憑晉級準決賽，就已經確定能將莎芭蓮卡從世界第一的寶座上拉下來。
  • deal with phr. v. 處理、應付
    I’ve been dealing with a little bit with an injury, but somehow everything aligned together on my side.
    我一直在處理一點傷勢，但不知怎地，所有事情都在我這邊完美地結合在一起了。
  • manage v. 管理、控制、應付
    Rybakina and her team came up with a plan to manage her training, rest and treatment and give her a chance at her second major title of the year...
    芮巴金娜和她的團隊制定了一個計劃來管理她的訓練、休息和治療，讓她在繼澳洲網球公開賽擊敗莎芭蓮卡之後，有機會贏得她今年的第二座大賽冠軍...
  • victory n. 勝利
    ...after her victory over Sabalenka at the Australian Open.
    ...在澳洲網球公開賽擊敗莎芭蓮卡之後...
  • impressive adj. 令人印象深刻的
    Her run to the final included an impressive fourth-round victory over two-time champion Naomi Osaka before come-from-behind wins over resurgent Olympic gold medalist Zheng Qinwen and former champion Coco Gauff.
    她闖入決賽的征途包括在第四輪以令人印象深刻的表現擊敗兩屆冠軍大坂直美，隨後又逆轉勝了復甦的奧運金牌得主鄭欽文以及前冠軍科科·高夫。
  • rivalry n. 競爭、對抗
    It all led to her 18th showdown with Sabalenka in a rivalry that has emerged as the most dynamic on the WTA tour.
    這一切促成了她與莎芭蓮卡的第 18 次對決，這段競爭關係已成為 WTA 巡迴賽中最具動態、最受矚目的對決。
  • approach n. 方法、作風、態度
    ...whose on-court personality is the perfect foil to her own cool approach.
    ...莎芭蓮卡在球場上的性格剛好與她冷靜的作風形成完美的對比。
  • seize v. 奪取、把握
    Other players — Gauff and Jessica Pegula — also had a chance to seize the number one ranking in New York, so Rybakina knows that Sabalenka is not the only rival she must be wary of.
    其他球員——高夫和潔西卡·皮古拉——在紐約也有機會奪下世界第一的排名，因此芮巴金娜知道，莎芭蓮卡並不是她唯一必須提防的對手。
  • success n. 成功
    I don’t know what future is going to bring, but I hope success and some more wins.
    我不知道未來會帶來什麼，但我希望是成功和更多勝利。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    Elena Rybakina is on top of the tennis world, celebrating her first US Open title and her rise to No. 1...
    句中的「celebrating her first US Open title...」是由現在分詞 celebrating 引導的分詞片語，用來補充說明主句主語 Elena Rybakina 在當下的伴隨動作或狀態，相當於對等句「and she is celebrating...」的簡化。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    Kazakhstan’s Rybakina on Saturday beat two-time defending champion Aryna Sabalenka 6-4, 5-7, 6-2 to claim her third Grand Slam title.
    句中的「two-time defending」由過去分詞與形容詞結合而成，用來修飾名詞 champion（衛冕冠軍）。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    It all led to her 18th showdown with Sabalenka in a rivalry that has emerged as the most dynamic on the WTA tour.
    句中的「that has emerged as the most dynamic on the WTA tour」是由關係代名詞 that 引導的限定性關係子句，用來修飾先行詞 a rivalry（競爭關係），補充說明其在巡迴賽中的地位。
