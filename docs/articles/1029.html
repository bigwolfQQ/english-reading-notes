校園內的威權象徵

Authoritarian symbols on campus

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作附帶狀況或結果（分詞構句）
    Education providers at all levels have been promoting “campus neutrality,” including administrative and educational neutrality, but the presence of symbols of party-state authoritarianism undermines transitional justice and campus democracy, while making political neutrality impossible.
    句尾的 "while making..." 是由連接詞引導的現在分詞片語，用來表達伴隨主句發生的情況或自然產生的結果，翻譯時常譯為「同時也讓……」。
  • be on whether引導的名詞子句（當作介詞的受詞）
    Ten years ago, when I was an undergraduate at National Chung Hsing University (NCHU), there was a referendum on whether to remove a statue of Chiang Kai-shek (蔣介石) from the campus.
    片語 "on whether to..." 中，"whether" 引導名詞子句作介詞 "on" 的受詞，在此處用作表示「是否」進行某項行動的選擇與決策。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞
    Removing the statue was the only way for the campus to achieve genuine political neutrality.
    句首的 "Removing the statue" 是動名詞片語，作為整個句子的主詞，視為單數，因此搭配單數動詞 "was"。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    By separating party and university, a teaching and learning environment free from political oppression is created.
    句尾使用 "is created"（be動詞 + 過去分詞）的被動語態結構，強調「環境是被創造出來的」，而省略了動作的執行者（主動者）。
