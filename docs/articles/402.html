單字解析

  • advocate n. 倡議者，擁護者
    Environmental advocates in Taichung yesterday filed a criminal report accusing 17 current and former officials of forgery...
    台中環保人士昨天提出刑事告發，指控17名現任及前任官員涉嫌偽造文書...
  • file a report phr. 提出告發，提交報告
    The advocates filed the report at the Taichung District Prosecutors’ Office...
    倡議人士前往台中地方檢察署遞交告發狀...
  • halt v. 使停下，暫停
    ...and urged the Executive Yuan to halt the project in line with a 34,000-signature petition...
    ...並敦促行政院配合一份擁有34,000人連署的請願書停工...
  • branch n. 分公司，分處
    Former and current officials of Taiwan International Ports Corp and its Taichung branch... are named in the complaint...
    台灣港務公司及其台中分公司的現任與卸任官員...被點名列入告訴中...
  • approved adj. 經批准的，核准的
    ...which was approved before waters in the region on Aug. 31, 2020, were listed as a “major wildlife habitat”...
    ...該計畫在該海域於2020年8月31日被列為「重要野生動物棲息環境」之前即已獲批准...
  • subject to phr. 受...約束的，須接受...
    That is why the council determined that the project was not subject to a critical habitat assessment review, she said.
    她表示，這就是為什麼委員會認定該工程無須進行關鍵棲息地評估審查的原因。
  • revision n. 修正，修改
    However, the revision of the project was approved on Sept. 21, 2020, after the critical habitat listing, she said.
    然而，她指出，該工程的修正案是在關鍵棲息地公告後的2020年9月21日才獲得批准。
  • budget n. 預算
    ...given that the fill project has a NT$10.4 billion (US$329.66 million) budget, but the wharf project’s was NT$2.8 billion...
    ...考慮到填海造陸工程預算高達104億元新台幣（約合3億2,966萬美元），而碼頭工程預算僅有28億元新台幣...
  • deceive v. 欺騙，瞞騙
    The groups seem to have used incorrect information to deceive the council...
    這些團體似乎使用錯誤資訊來欺騙委員會...
  • pollute v. The materials approved to reclaim land were changed to construction surplus soil and rock, despite the risk of heavy metal pollution, she said.
    污染 (文章中以 pollution 型態出現)
    她指出，儘管存在重金屬汙染的風險，用於填海造陸核准的材料卻被變更為營建剩餘土石方。
  • undergo v. 經歷，承受（審查、評估等）

    然而，她表示，環境部、海委會及海洋保育署卻未下令該填海造陸工程接受額外的環境評估...

    ...indicates that the three central governmental agencies might have facilitated profiteering, Hsu added.
    許心欣補充說，...這顯示這三個中央政府機關可能涉及圖利。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞當作形容詞/後置修飾 (Present Participle as Postmodifier)
    ...urged the Executive Yuan to halt the project in line with a 34,000-signature petition to protect the habitat of critically endangered Taiwanese humpback dolphins.
    句中的 "critically endangered" 是由副詞 critically 修飾現在分詞 endangered（當作形容詞用），用來形容台灣白海豚的保育狀態是「極度瀕危的」。這種結構在多益職場與新聞英文中常用来形容人或事物的狀態。
  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...which was approved before waters in the region on Aug. 31, 2020, were listed as a “major wildlife habitat” of the dolphins, Hsu said.
    關係代間詞子句 "which was approved..." 中使用了被動語態的過去分詞 "approved"，用來修飾前面的名詞計畫 (Plan)。在多益商業合約或新聞報導中，常省略「which + be動詞」，直接用過去分詞片語來修飾名詞（例如：The plan approved yesterday...）。
  • 假設語氣/過去可能發生的推測 (Modal Verbs with Perfect Infinitives: might have + p.p.)
    The council might be guilty of malfeasance if it knew that the habitat listing was issued before the third revision... but did not order an ecological assessment...
    句型 "might be... if it knew... but did not order..." 結合了與過去事實相反或對過去情況的推測。"might have caused" 或 "might be guilty" 用來表達說話者對過去或現在狀況的推測，語氣保留保留了不確定性但具針對性，這是多益商業商業法律或責任追究報導常見的句型。
  • 分詞構句表補充說明 (Participial Phrase for Supplementary Information)
    ...she said, adding the transportation ministry seems to have falsely endorsed the land reclamation project.
    逗點後方的 ", adding..." 是由現在分詞 "adding" 引導的分詞構句，主詞省略（原意為 and she added that...）。這種用法在新聞英文中極為常見，用來在引述完一句話後，流暢地補充說明的另一項重點資訊。
