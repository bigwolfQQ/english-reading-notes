文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附帶狀況或結果狀語 (Present Participle as Adverbial Modifier)
    ...urged Taiwan to make its administrative procedures more transparent and predictable to encourage greater investment, as it released its annual white paper.
    句尾的 "as it released..." 也可以簡化為分詞構句表伴隨狀況。在此例中，原句使用連接詞 as 引導子句，表達發布白皮書的同時也提出了敦促。
  • 過去分詞作形容詞後置修飾 (Past Participle as Postmodifier)
    ...the chamber also called for regulations aligned with international standards and measures to address shortages of skilled workers and electricity.
    句中的 "aligned with international standards" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "regulations"（法規），相當於關係代名詞子句 "which are aligned with international standards" 的簡化版。
  • 對等連接詞連接多個受詞 (Parallel Structure with Conjunctions)
    On administrative procedures, the white paper called for a more transparent and efficient process for permit applications, approvals and consultations, as well as more flexibility in responding to businesses' needs.
    句中 "permit applications, approvals and consultations" 運用了對等連接詞 and 來連接三個並列的名詞，使多益商業文件常見的冗長法律或行政名詞保持結構上的整齊與清晰。
