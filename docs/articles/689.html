文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作為附屬子句的補充說明（分詞構句）
    The upgrade highlights the strength of Taiwan’s AI-driven expansion, with technology exports and investment continuing to outperform expectations and offsetting weakness in other parts of the economy.
    句尾使用「with + 名詞 + 現在分詞 (continuing / offsetting)」的結構，用來補充說明升級背後的伴隨狀況與原因，表達科技出口持續優於預期並同時抵銷了其他領域的疲軟。
  • 被動語態的應用
    Taiwan’s exports reached a record US$82.4 billion last month, driven by strong demand for AI-related hardware and high-performance computing products.
    句中的 "driven by..." 是被動分詞片語簡化而來，用來修飾前面的 exports，強調出口額是「受到...的推動」，在商業財經新聞中非常用來表達原因。
  • 子句中的假設與可能性表達 (is expected to / is likely to)
    The central bank is expected to leave its benchmark interest rate unchanged at its board meeting on Thursday...
    使用 "is expected to + V"（被預期會...）來表達基於目前情勢的合理推測與市場預期，這是財經新聞報導預測未來動向時極為常見的句型。
