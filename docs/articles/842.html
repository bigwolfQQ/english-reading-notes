工商團體希望將薪資調幅限制在3%以內

Trade group wants to limit wage hike to 3 percent

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • minimum wage n. 基本工資
    Taiwan’s minimum wage should not rise more than 3 percent next year to limit the impact of the increase on businesses
    台灣的基本工資明年不應調高超過3%，以限制調薪對企業的衝擊
  • compensation n. 薪酬，補償
    Profitable companies should raise employee compensation, but industries struggling with weaker business conditions should receive tax relief
    獲利良好的公司應該調高員工薪酬，但營運狀況較弱的產業則應獲得租稅減免
  • tax relief n. 租稅減免，稅捐減免
    industries struggling with weaker business conditions should receive tax relief and other government support
    營運狀況較弱的產業則應獲得租稅減免和其他政府支援
  • offset v. 抵銷，補償
    other government support to help offset higher labor costs, Third Wednesday Club chairman Lin Por-fong (林伯豐) said.
    三三會理事長林伯豐表示，其他政府支援有助於抵銷較高的勞動成本。
  • inflation n. 通貨膨脹
    “A 3 percent increase would be more reasonable considering the impact of inflation,” Lin told reporters
    林伯豐告訴記者：「考慮到通膨的影響，3%的調幅會比較合理。」
  • cumulative adj. 累積的
    increasing the monthly rate by a cumulative 47.4 percent and the hourly rate by 63.3 percent, Lin said.
    林伯豐表示，使月薪累計調高了47.4%，時薪則調高了63.3%。
  • mandatory adj. 強制性的，法定的
    The increases have also pushed up mandatory labor costs, including contributions to the national labor and health insurance programs
    這些調幅也推高了強制性的勞動成本，包括對國家勞工與健康保險計畫的提撥
  • personnel expenses n. 人事費用
    adding that they account for about 20 percent of personnel expenses.
    並補充說這些成本約佔人事費用的20%。
  • forecast v. / n. 預測，預報
    even as Taiwan’s economy is forecast to expand 11.05 percent this year on the back of a powerful artificial intelligence (AI) boom
    即使在強勁的人工智慧（AI）熱潮帶動下，台灣今年經濟預測將成長11.05%
  • subsidies n. 補助金，津貼
    Lin urged the government to continue offering incentives and subsidies to companies that raise wages
    林伯豐敦促政府繼續向為員工加薪的公司提供獎勵與補助
  • monetary policy n. 貨幣政策
    He also called for a cautious approach to monetary policy, saying that Taiwan should take global interest-rate trends into account
    他也呼籲對貨幣政策採取審慎態度，並表示台灣應將全球利率趨勢納入考量
  • stating / saying (這裡指對句構的引用) policy stance n. 政策立場
    The central bank’s policy stance has broadly met the expectations of the business community
    央行的政策立場大致符合商界的預期

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ..., particularly traditional industries facing mounting cost pressures, a major business group said yesterday.
    句中的 "facing mounting cost pressures" 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "traditional industries"（傳統產業），相當於形容詞子句 "which face mounting cost pressures" 的簡化用法，在英文新聞中非常常見，用以表達主動且持續的狀態。
  • 分詞構句表附带狀況或結果 (Participial Phrase indicating accompanying circumstance)
    The increases have also pushed up mandatory labor costs, including contributions to the national labor and health insurance programs, Lin said, adding that they account for about 20 percent of personnel expenses.
    句尾的 ", adding that..." 是由連接詞與主詞簡化而來的分詞構句，用來補充說明前面整句所帶來的動作或結果，意指林伯豐在說完前話的同時，也另外補充了這個數據。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Taiwan’s economy is forecast to expand 11.05 percent this year on the back of a powerful artificial intelligence (AI) boom...
    "is forecast" 採用了被動語態，因為經濟成長是由專家或市場「被預測」，而非經濟本身去預測。新聞英文常用被動語態來客觀陳述預測、估計或官方決策。
  • 動名詞片語作受詞 (Gerund Phrase as Object)
    Lin urged the government to continue offering incentives and subsidies to companies that raise wages and share profits with employees, rather than relying solely on across-the-board increases in the minimum wage.
    介系詞 "rather than" 後方接了動名詞片語 "relying solely on..."，用來表達「而不是依賴…」的對比概念。介系詞後面若要接動詞，必須使用動名詞 (-ing) 形式。
