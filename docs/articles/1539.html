文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表附帶狀況或結果）
    Southeast Asian food delivery platform Grab Holdings Ltd in March applied to acquire Delivery Hero SE’s Foodpanda operations in Taiwan, with the Fair Trade Commission (FTC) scheduled to announce its decision on Oct. 27.
    句尾的「with + 名詞 + 過去分詞（scheduled）」構成獨立分詞構句，用來補充說明主句發生時同時存在的狀況或背景條件，意指「在……的情況下」。
  • 被動語態
    It cannot be assessed solely in terms of market share and price competition.
    句型使用情態動詞加上被動語態（can + be + 過去分詞 assessed），強調主詞 It（這筆交易）是承受評估的對象，而非主動去評估。
  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    Through delivery workers’ GPS locations and images, Grab accumulates detailed, real-time local data that are far more valuable than static maps.
    句中的 "that are far more valuable than static maps" 是由關係代名詞 that 引導的限定用法的形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的先行詞 "detailed, real-time local data"。
  • 假設語氣（與現在事實相反）
    Even if Grab claims that its partnership with Huawei does not cover Taiwan, the nation has no way to verify whether data could be fully isolated from China following the acquisition.
    句中的 could 助動詞（could be fully isolated）表達在特定假設情境（併購後）下能力或可能性的推測，帶有假設語氣的語感。
