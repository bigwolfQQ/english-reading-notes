台灣不應針對媒體

Taiwan should not target media

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • thrive v. 繁榮，興旺
    Taiwan has demonstrated that a free press can thrive in a democratic society.
    台灣證明了新聞自由可以在民主社會中蓬勃發展。
  • achievement n. 成就，達成
    However, that achievement risks being overshadowed by moves in the legislature to cut the budget and freeze funding...
    然而，這項成就卻面臨被立法院削減預算及凍結資金的舉動所蒙陰影的風險。
  • recognize v. 體認到，承認，認出
    Other countries in the region recognized the value of English-language broadcasting much earlier.
    該地區的其他國家很早就體認到英語廣播的價值。
  • platform n. 平台
    An English-language media platform allows international audiences to access Taiwanese news...
    英語媒體平台讓國際受眾能夠獲取台灣的新聞...
  • access v. 存取，獲取，接近
    ...a broader English-language media ecosystem would give international audiences more opportunities to access news and perspectives directly from Taiwan.
    ...更廣泛的英語媒體生態系統將使國際受眾有更多機會直接從台灣獲取新聞與觀點。
  • aspect n. 方面，層面
    TaiwanPlus’ coverage spans politics, society, culture and other aspects of life in Taiwan.
    TaiwanPlus 的報導涵蓋台灣的政治、社會、文化及其他生活層面。
  • controversial adj. 具爭議性的（名詞形式為 controversy）
    TaiwanPlus has become entangled in political controversy and faced criticism from the opposition.
    TaiwanPlus 已經陷入政治爭議並面臨來自反對黨的批評。
  • scrutiny n. 詳細審查，仔細檢查
    Being a public media organization does not place it beyond scrutiny.
    身為公共媒體機構並不代表它超然於審查之外。
  • indispensable adj. 不可或缺的
    TaiwanPlus does not need to be treated as indispensable.
    TaiwanPlus 不需要被視為不可或缺的。
  • justify v. 證明…是合理的，為…辯護
    It should be expected to justify its funding, maintain journalistic standards and respond to legitimate criticism.
    它應該被要求證明其資金的合理性、維持新聞標準並回應合理的批評。
  • criteria n. 標準，準則（criterion的複數形）
    Funding decisions concerning public media should be based on transparent criteria, public interest and responsible use of public money.
    關於公共媒體的資金決策應基於透明的標準、公眾利益以及對公眾資金的負責任使用。
  • ongoing adj. 進行中的
    At a time when wars are ongoing and Taiwan’s international space is curtailed...
    在戰爭持續且台灣的國際空間受到壓縮的時刻...

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表示伴隨狀況或時間）
    At a time when censorship remains a reality in many parts of the world, Taiwan has demonstrated that a free press can thrive in a democratic society.
    「At a time when...」引導一個形容詞子句修飾 time，用來設定背景時間。而在整篇文中亦常使用分詞或介詞片語來補充說明背景，例如「At a time when wars are ongoing and Taiwan’s international space is curtailed...」，用以交代事件發生的時空背景。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    However, that achievement risks being overshadowed by moves in the legislature to cut the budget and freeze funding...
    此句中的「risks being overshadowed by...」使用了動名詞的被動語態（being + 過去分詞），用來表達「冒著被…掩蓋的風險」，強調成就本身是承受動作的對受者。
  • 虛主詞 It 的用法
    ...one thing has always stood out to me: how unusual it is that Taiwan... had to wait until 2021 for its first dedicated English-language news channel.
    英文常用「It is + 形容詞 + that 子句」或「It takes + 時間 + to V」的句型來避免主詞過長。第一句中，it 是虛主詞，真正的受詞子句是後面接的「that Taiwan...」；第二句則是「It took (someone) time to do something」的結構變體，用來表達花費了多少時間。
  • 否定句型與並列結構 (Parallelism)
    In a democracy, concerns about the media should be met with better journalism, oversight and transparency, not pressure or retaliation.
    此句運用了「not A, but B」（或在此處寫成「..., not pressure or retaliation」）的對比並列結構，明確指出面對媒體疑慮時「應該」採取的方法（報導、監督、透明度）與「不應該」採取的方法（壓力、報復），讓論述邏輯非常清晰。
