Taipei Times · 2026-09-04

台灣不應針對媒體

Taiwan should not target media

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • no small feat 片語 不小的成就、非凡的壯舉
    That is no small feat.
  • censorship 名詞 審查制度、檢查
    At a time when censorship remains a reality in many parts of the world, Taiwan has demonstrated that a free press can thrive in a democratic society.
  • overshadow 動詞 使陰暗、使失色、相形見絀
    However, that achievement risks being overshadowed by moves in the legislature to cut the budget and freeze funding for TaiwanPlus...
  • dedicated 形容詞 專用的、專門的、奉獻的
    ...Taiwan, a place with so much to tell the world, had to wait until 2021 for its first dedicated English-language news channel.
  • ecosystem 名詞 生態系統
    ...a broader English-language media ecosystem would give international audiences more opportunities to access news and perspectives directly from Taiwan.
  • entangled 形容詞 陷入糾紛的、糾纏不清的
    TaiwanPlus has become entangled in political controversy and faced criticism from the opposition.
  • indispensable 形容詞 不可或缺的、絕對必要的
    TaiwanPlus does not need to be treated as indispensable.
  • scrutiny 名詞 詳細審查、仔細檢查
    Being a public media organization does not place it beyond scrutiny.
  • retaliation 名詞 報復、反擊
    In a democracy, concerns about the media should be met with better journalism, oversight and transparency, not pressure or retaliation.
  • accountability 名詞 當責、問責性、負有責任
    Accountability is not the same as political punishment.
  • curtailed 形容詞 受削減的、受限制的
    At a time when wars are ongoing and Taiwan’s international space is curtailed, it needs more avenues — not fewer — to communicate with the world.

文法重點

  • At a time when... 引導的時間副詞子句
    At a time when censorship remains a reality in many parts of the world, Taiwan has demonstrated that a free press can thrive in a democratic society.
    「At a time when + 子句」常出現在新聞英語中，用來營造特定的時代背景或情境。「when」在此為關係副詞，用修飾前面的片語 time，指「在...的時期」。
  • 現在分詞當形容詞修飾名詞
    In a contested information environment, who tells Taiwan’s story and where that information comes from matter.
    句中的「contested」是由動詞 contest 轉換而來的過去分詞（在此作形容詞用），修飾後方的「information environment」，意指「充滿爭議的／遭到爭奪的資訊環境」。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    However, that achievement risks being overshadowed by moves in the legislature to cut the budget...
    文章中使用了動名词的被動語態「being overshadowed」，用來表達「某事面臨『被』…所蒙上陰影的風險」，強調動作的承受者。
