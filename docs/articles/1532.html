星宇航空九月營收年增近百分之七十

Starlux Airlines posts almost 70 percent year-on-year sales growth in September

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • revenue n. 營收、收益
    Starlux Airlines Co’s (星宇航空) revenue last month rose almost 70 percent from a year earlier
    星宇航空股份有限公司上個月的營收較去年同期成長近百分之七十
  • bolster v. 支持、增強、鼓舞
    bolstered by strong demand for passenger travel and for cargo shipments of tech devices amid the artificial intelligence (AI) boom.
    主要受到旅客運輸的強勁需求，以及在人工智慧（AI）熱潮下科技設備貨運需求的推動。
  • consolidated sales n. phr. 合併營收、綜合銷售額
    The carrier generated NT$4.98 billion (US$155.21 million) in consolidated sales last month
    該航空公司上個月創造了 4.98億新台幣（約 1.5521 億美元）的合併營收
  • carrier n. 航空公司、運輸業者
    The carrier generated NT$4.98 billion (US$155.21 million) in consolidated sales last month, up 69 percent from a year earlier, it said on Monday.
    該航空公司週一表示，上個月創造了 4.98億新台幣（約 1.5521 億美元）的合併營收，較去年同期成長百分之六十九。
  • peak season n. phr. 旺季
    Fourth quarter is a peak season for international trade
    第四季是國際貿易的旺季
  • inventory n. 存貨、庫存
    foreign buyers are expected to keep building inventories of AI servers, semiconductor equipment and electronic components
    預期國外買家將持續建立 AI 伺服器、半導體設備與電子零組件的庫存
  • optimistic adj. 樂觀的
    the carrier said it is optimistic about its cargo business this quarter.
    該航空公司表示對本季的貨運業務保持樂觀。
  • deploy v. 調度、部署（設備或人員）
    Starlux said it would increase flights and deploy larger planes in its winter schedule
    星宇航空表示將在冬季班表中增加航班並調派大型飛機
  • total v. 總計達
    In the first nine months of this year, Starlux’s consolidated sales totaled NT$44.84 billion
    今年前九個月，星宇航空的合併營收總計達 44.84億新台幣
  • load factor n. phr. 載客率、載重率
    Tigerair Taiwan Ltd (台灣虎航), the country’s only low-cost carrier, said its average load factor — the percentage of seats filled — topped 90 percent last month
    台灣虎航，台灣唯一的低成本航空公司，表示其平均載客率——即座位售出的百分比——上個月突破百分之九十

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 / 分詞構句 (Past Participle Phrase)
    Starlux Airlines Co’s (星宇航空) revenue last month rose almost 70 percent from a year earlier, bolstered by strong demand for passenger travel...
    句中的「bolstered by...」是由過去分詞片語構成的被動分詞構句，用來補充說明主句營收成長的原因，相當於「which was bolstered by...」，在多益閱讀測驗中常用於簡化句子結構。
  • 對等連接詞 while 連接對比子句 (While for Contrast)
    Passenger revenue rose 70 percent from a year earlier to NT$3.72 billion, while cargo revenue rose 59 percent to NT$706 million, the carrier said.
    連接詞 「while」在此處用來連接兩個同時發生但數值不同的情況，表達「然而、而」的對比語氣，是商務與財經報導中描述各項數據對比時非常常見的句型。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    ...foreign buyers are expected to keep building inventories of AI servers...
    句型「are expected to」使用了被動語態（be + p.p.），用來表達客觀的預期或市場普遍看法，常出現於新聞英語與商業預測中以避免直接指稱特定消息來源。
  • 副詞子句簡化為分詞片語 (Participial Phrase indicating Cause)
    Citing that trend and year-end shopping demand, the carrier said it is optimistic about its cargo business this quarter.
    句首的「Citing that trend...」是由現在分詞「Citing」引導的片語，其主詞與主句主語（the carrier）一致，原意為「Because it cited that trend...」，簡化後可讓財經新聞的句子更緊湊、專業。
