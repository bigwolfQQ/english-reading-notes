文法重點

  • 關係代名詞 what 引導的名詞子句
    Anthropic PBC is in talks to bring Nvidia Corp as an anchor investor into what could be the largest initial public offering (IPO) in history...
    「what」在此作為關係代名詞，等於「the thing(s) which」，用來引導一個名詞子句（what could be the largest initial public offering...），在介系詞 into 後面作為受詞，意指「……的那個（可能成為歷史上最大 IPO 的）發行案」。
  • 分詞構句作後位修飾
    ...under which Anthropic committed to buy US$30 billion of Microsoft Azure computing capacity powered by Nvidia chips.
    過去分詞片語「powered by Nvidia chips」在此作為形容詞用，修飾其前的名詞「computing capacity（運算能力）」。被動意味濃厚，原意為「which is powered by Nvidia chips」，簡化後省略了關係代名詞與be動詞，使句子更為精鍊。
  • 現在分詞表附帶狀況或發展
    Bringing a deep-pocketed anchor investor such as Nvidia onboard could boost investor confidence in the listing, which is shaping up to be a major test...
    動名詞片語「Bringing a deep-pocketed anchor investor...」作為句子的主詞。此外，句尾的「which is shaping up to be...」是非限定關係子句，用來補充說明前面的 listing（上市案），其中片語「shape up to be」常用於新聞英文中，表示「逐漸發展成為……」。
