台船與 Havoc 合作開發無人水面載具

CSBC partners with Havoc on autonomous surface vessel development

單字解析

  • partner v. 合作，結盟
    CSBC partners with Havoc on autonomous surface vessel development
    台船與 Havoc 合作開發無人水面載具
  • announce v. 宣布，宣告
    CSBC Corp., Taiwan's largest shipbuilder, announced Friday that it has teamed up with U.S.-based all-domain collaborative autonomy firm Havoc to develop autonomous surface vessels (ASVs) in Taiwan.
    台灣最大造船廠台灣國際造船公司（台船）週五宣布，已與美國全域協同自主性公司 Havoc 結盟，在台灣共同開發無人水面載具（ASV）。
  • agreement n. 協議，協定
    In a statement, CSBC said it signed a partnership agreement with Havoc at the U.S. firm's headquarters in Rhode Island on Thursday, U.S. time, to pursue licensed co-production of ASVs for Taiwan's maritime security.
    台船在聲明中表示，美東時間週四於該美國企業位於羅德島州的總部簽署了合作協議，將推動無人水面載具的授權共同生產，以維護台灣的海上安全。
  • leverage v. 善用，發揮（資源、優勢等）
    Under the agreement, the two companies will leverage their respective capabilities to integrate Havoc's industry-leading autonomy software into CSBC-built platforms produced and maintained in Taiwan, CSBC said.
    台船表示，根據該協議，兩家公司將發揮各自的優勢，將 Havoc 業界領先的自主軟體整合至在台灣製造與維護的台船平台中。
  • participate v. 參與，參加
    The partnership is expected not only to strengthen CSBC's ability to produce ASVs in Taiwan, but also to create opportunities for local suppliers to participate in the project, the shipbuilder said.
    這家造船廠指出，此項夥伴關係預期不僅能強化台船在台灣生產無人水面載具的能力，也將為本地供應商創造參與該專案的機會。
  • supplier n. 供應商
    The partnership is expected not only to strengthen CSBC's ability to produce ASVs in Taiwan, but also to create opportunities for local suppliers to participate in the project, the shipbuilder said.

文法重點

  • 現在完成被動語態 (Present Perfect Passive)
    CSBC Corp., Taiwan's largest shipbuilder, announced Friday that it has teamed up with U.S.-based all-domain collaborative autonomy firm Havoc...
    句型「has teamed up」由「have/has + 過去分詞」組成，用來表示一個過去發生的動作對現在造成的影響或結果（即「已經結盟」的狀態）。
  • 不僅……而且…… (not only ... but also ...)
    The partnership is expected not only to strengthen CSBC's ability to produce ASVs in Taiwan, but also to create opportunities for local suppliers to participate in the project...
    此結構用於連接兩個對等的動詞片語（strengthen... 與 create...），強調夥伴關係帶來的雙重效益。注意句首使用被動語態「is expected」，且「not only」置於句中時，常配合對稱的對等連接詞使用。
  • 被動語態的分詞片語作後置修飾 (Past Participle as Post-modifier)
    ...integrate Havoc's industry-leading autonomy software into CSBC-built platforms produced and maintained in Taiwan...
    句中的「produced and maintained in Taiwan」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「platforms（平台）」。原意相當於形容詞子句「which are produced and maintained in Taiwan」，簡化後能讓商業新聞的句子更為精煉。
  • 介系詞片語表示符合規定 (in line with)
    CSBC said the cooperation with Havoc is in line with Section 1266 of the U.S. National Defense Authorization Act...
    片語「in line with」在多益商業與法律文章中非常常見，意思是「符合、與……一致」，用來描述某項商業行動或政策合乎特定的法規或標準。
