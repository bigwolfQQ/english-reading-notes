微軟將加倍擴建其在台灣的資料中心

Microsoft to double data centers in Taiwan

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • facility n. 設施，場所
    Microsoft Corp plans to double its data centers in Taiwan from two facilities to four, Microsoft Taiwan general manager Sean Pien (卞志祥) said yesterday.
    微軟台灣區總經理卞志祥昨天表示，微軟公司計劃將其在台灣的資料中心從兩座設施增加一倍至四座。
  • demand n. / v. 需求，要求
    “Over the past two to three years, we have consistently seen demand outstrip supply, and we expect that situation to continue over the next two to three years,” Pien said on the sidelines of a news conference in Taipei.
    卞志祥在台北的一場記者會場邊表示：「在過去兩到三年裡，我們一直看到需求大於供給，我們預期這種情況在未來兩到三年內將持續下去。」
  • operational adj. 營運的，運作的
    The data center sites would mainly be in northern Taiwan to meet customer demand and reduce the risk of operational disruptions caused by a single event, he said, without saying their exact locations.
    他說，資料中心選址將主要位於台灣北部，以滿足客戶需求並降低單一事件所造成的營運中斷風險，但他沒有透露確切的地點。
  • allocation n. 分配，配置
    The computing capacity shortage extends beyond graphics processing units and central processing units to power supply and space allocation for additional server racks, Pien said.
    卞志祥表示，運算能力的短缺不僅限於圖形處理器和中央處理器，還擴及供電以及用於容納更多伺服器機架的空間配置。
  • revenue n. 收益，營收
    Microsoft aims to balance revenue growth, profitability and customer demand going forward, making continued expansion of its global computing capacity a clear priority, he said.
    他說，微軟的目標是在未來平衡營收成長、獲利能力與客戶需求，使全球運算能力的持續擴充成為明確的優先事項。
  • profitability n. 獲利能力，盈利能力
    Microsoft aims to balance revenue growth, profitability and customer demand going forward, making continued expansion of its global computing capacity a clear priority, he said.
    他說，微軟的目標是在未來平衡營收成長、獲利能力與客戶需求，使全球運算能力的持續擴充成為明確的優先事項。
  • priority n. 優先事項，優先權
    Microsoft aims to balance revenue growth, profitability and customer demand going forward, making continued expansion of its global computing capacity a clear priority, he said.
    他說，微軟的目標是在未來平衡營收成長、獲利能力與客戶需求，使全球運算能力的持續擴充成為明確的優先事項。
  • beneficiary n. 受益者，受惠者
    Taiwan’s supply chain would “undoubtedly” remain a key beneficiary and play a critical role in the global race to expand computing capacity, Pien said.
    卞志祥表示，台灣的供應鏈「無疑地」將繼續成為主要受益者，並在擴充運算能力的全球競賽中扮演關鍵角色。
  • constraint n. 限制，約束
    To ease computing capacity constraints and meet local regulatory requirements, Microsoft has expanded the number of services available through its Taiwan data centers to more than 200 items from more than 70 initially, Pien said.
    卞志祥表示，為了緩解運算能力的限制並滿足當地法規要求，微軟已將其台灣資料中心提供的服務數量從最初的 70 多項擴大到 200 多項。
  • regulatory adj. 監管的，規章的
    To ease computing capacity constraints and meet local regulatory requirements, Microsoft has expanded the number of services available through its Taiwan data centers to more than 200 items from more than 70 initially, Pien said.
    卞志祥表示，為了緩解運算能力的限制並滿足當地法規要求，微軟已將其台灣資料中心提供的服務數量從最初的 70 多項擴大到 200 多項。
  • deployment n. 部署，調度
    Regarding the company’s custom chip development, Pien said Microsoft’s development, production capacity and future deployment of application-specific integrated circuits (ASICs) are all progressing smoothly.
    關於該公司的自研晶片開發，卞志祥表示，微軟特殊應用積體電路（ASIC）的開發、產能以及未來部署，目前都進展順利。

文法重點

  • 現在完成式 (Present Perfect Tense)
    “Over the past two to three years, we have consistently seen demand outstrip supply, and we expect that situation to continue over the next two to three years,” Pien said on the sidelines of a news conference in Taipei.
    句型使用「have + 過去分詞」(have seen)，用來表達從過去某個時間點（過去兩到三年）開始一直持續到現在的動作或狀態，常與 over the past / last [時間] 連用。
  • 分詞構句作副詞子句簡化 (Participial Phrase)
    Addressing the shortage requires building new data centers and reconfiguring facilities to accommodate additional capacity, he said.
    句首的「Addressing the shortage」是現在分詞（V-ing）開頭的分詞構句，原本的主動語態子句（如：If we address the shortage...）省略了主語並將動詞改為 V-ing，用來修飾主句，表示條件或伴隨狀況。
  • 使役動詞用法 (Causative Verb)
    Such workloads are highly predictable and controllable, making them well suited to ASICs, which can improve efficiency and lower computing costs, he added.
    句中的「making them well suited to ASICs」使用了使役動詞 make，後面接受詞 (them) 以及形容詞 (well suited) 作為受詞補語，用來描述使受詞產生的狀態。
