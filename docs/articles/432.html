Taipei Times · 2026-09-11

八月成為有記錄以來最熱的月份

August hottest month ever recorded

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • assessment n. 評估，評價
    In response to the assessment by the Copernicus Climate Change Service, the UN said that until humanity weaned off fossil fuels, the planet would only push deeper into uncharted territory.
    針對哥白尼氣候變化服務局的評估，聯合國表示，除非人類戒除化石燃料，否則地球將只會進一步陷入未知的領域。
  • surpass v. 超越，勝過
    Global average air temperatures last month were 16.96°C, Copernicus said — surpassing by 0.01°C the previous all-time monthly record set in July 2023.
    哥白尼表示，上個月全球平均氣溫為 16.96°C，超越了 2023 年 7 月創下的前歷史單月最高紀錄 0.01°C。
  • severity n. 嚴重性，嚴厲
    Scientists say that human activity — primarily the burning of coal, oil and gas — has warmed the planet by about 1.4°C since preindustrial times and increased the severity and likelihood of extreme weather.
    科學家表示，人類活動——主要是燃燒煤炭、石油和天然氣——自前工業化時代以來已使地球暖化了約 1.4°C，並增加了極端天氣的嚴重性與發生機率。
  • emission n. 排放，排放物
    Yet heat-trapping greenhouse gas emissions rose to new highs last year.
    然而，會鎖住熱能的溫室氣體排放量去年卻升至新高。
  • executive n. 主管，行政人員
    “The evidence is irrefutable: This is the spiraling price of humanity’s fossil fuel addiction, and the global climate crisis it is fueling,” UN Framework Convention on Climate Change Executive Secretary Simon Stiell said of the Copernicus report.
    聯合國氣候變化綱要公約執行秘書西蒙·斯蒂爾在談到哥白尼的報告時表示：「證據不容辯駁：這是人類對化石燃料成癮所付出的螺旋式代價，以及它正在助長的全球氣候危機。」
  • extend v. 延長，延伸
    Global sea surface temperatures also set a new daily high and tied equal-hottest for August, extending a three-month streak of record-breaking warmth across the world’s oceans.
    全球海面溫度也創下新的每日高點，並追平了 8 月份的最高紀錄，延續了全球海洋長達三個月的破紀錄暖化趨勢。
  • absorb v. 吸收
    More than 90 percent of excess heat trapped by greenhouse gas emissions has been absorbed by the oceans, sparing land from warming, but at severe cost to marine life, reefs and coastal communities.
    溫室氣體排放所吸收並鎖住的過剩熱量中，有超過 90% 被海洋吸收，使陸地免於暖化，但代價卻是海洋生物、珊瑚礁和沿海社區遭受嚴重的損害。
  • accumulate v. 累積，積聚
    Records tumbled in overheated basins such as the Atlantic and Mediterranean and also the tropical Pacific, where the strongest El Nino weather pattern in the modern era is fast accumulating strength.
    在大西洋、地中海以及熱帶太平洋等過熱的海域中，各項紀錄紛紛被打破；現代史上最強的聖嬰氣候模式正在該處迅速累積強度。
  • peak n. / v. 頂峰，達到高峰
    Scientists expect records to keep falling in the months ahead as this “super” El Nino approaches peak intensity around December and for next year — and maybe even this year — to become the hottest years ever observed.
    科學家預期隨著這個「超級」聖嬰現象在 12 月左右達到頂峰強度，各項紀錄將在未來幾個月內不斷被打破，而明年——甚至可能今年——將成為有史以來最熱的年份。
  • analysis n. 分析
    Regions home to about 900 million people lived through the hottest July ever observed, an analysis of Copernicus data showed.
    哥白尼數據的分析顯示，約有 9 億人口居住的地區經歷了有史以來最熱的 7 月。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    Yet heat-trapping greenhouse gas emissions rose to new highs last year.
    句中的 "heat-trapping" 是由現在分詞與名詞複合而成的形容詞，放在名詞 greenhouse gas emissions 前面，用來修飾「會鎖住熱能的」溫室氣體排放。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    More than 90 percent of excess heat trapped by greenhouse gas emissions has been absorbed by the oceans...
    句中的 "trapped by greenhouse gas emissions" 是過去分詞片語，放在名詞 excess heat 的後面，用來修飾被溫室氣體「所鎖住的」過剩熱量，相當於形容詞子句 "which is trapped by..." 的簡化。
  • 關係副詞 where 引導的非限定性關係子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause with 'where')
    ...and also the tropical Pacific, where the strongest El Nino weather pattern in the modern era is fast accumulating strength.
    句中的 "where" 引導一個非限定性關係子句（前面有逗號），用來補充說明先行詞「熱帶太平洋 (the tropical Pacific)」，並在子句中作地方副詞，描述該氣候模式正在該處累積強度。
  • 條件句中的省略與假設 (Conditionals with 'unless')
    The climate would “continue to get more extreme unless we turn off the tap to global emissions,” she added.
    句中使用了 "unless"（除非……，如果不……）來引導條件子句，表達否定條件。主句則使用了過去未來的助動詞 "would" 搭配原形動詞，表達在該條件未達成時的必然結果。
