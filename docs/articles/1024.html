Meta’s new AI app downloaded in great numbers

單字解析

  • traction n. 牽引力、關注度、市場接受度
    a sign that the social media company is gaining traction in the increasingly crowded market for consumer AI assistants.
    這顯示出這家社群媒體公司在日益擁擠的消費型 AI 助理市場中正逐漸獲得關注。
  • available adj. 可取得的、可使用的、有空的
    The app, which is available for people 18 years and older, was downloaded more than 902,000 times since Meta introduced it on Sept. 8...
    這款供 18 歲及以上人群使用的 App 自 Meta 於 9 月 8 日推出以來，下載量已超過 902,000 次...
  • predecessor n. 前身、前輩、前任
    That is more than the 773,000 downloads of its predecessor, the Meta AI app, in the same post-launch period, he added.
    他補充說，這超過了其前身 Meta AI 應用程式在相同發布後期間的 773,000 次下載量。
  • indicator v. (indicating) 表明、顯示、指出
    Muse is available within Meta-owned WhatsApp, indicating it might have even more users beyond the number of standalone app downloads.
    Muse 可在 Meta 旗下的 WhatsApp 中使用，這表明除了獨立應用程式的下載量之外，它可能擁有更多的用戶。
  • customized adj. 客製化的、訂製的
    Muse lets people create customized AI agents, tools that can be sent out to the Web to complete digital tasks on a user’s behalf.
    Muse 讓使用者能夠建立客製化的 AI 代理，這些工具可以被派送到網路上代表使用者完成數位任務。
  • third-party adj. 第三方的
    ...as well as to connect to third-party services such as Gmail, Apple and Google calendars, OpenTable or other Web sites via a browser.
    ...還能透過瀏覽器連接到第三方服務，例如 Gmail、Apple 和 Google 日曆、OpenTable 或其他網站。
  • subscription n. 訂閱、訂閱費
    ...but a US$20 or US$100 per month subscription comes with more weekly tokens.
    ...但每月 20 美元或 100 美元的訂閱方案則提供更多的每週代幣。
  • innovation n. 創新、革新
    “It amounts to a very tangible sign of successful product innovation” and is evidence that Meta’s US$200 billion AI investments “have not been in vain.”
    「這代表了產品創新成功的非常具體跡象」，並且證明了 Meta 高達 2,000 億美元的 AI 投資「並非白費。」
  • investment n. 投資、投資額
    ...and is evidence that Meta’s US$200 billion AI investments “have not been in vain.”
    ...並且證明了 Meta 高達 2,000 億美元的 AI 投資「並非白費。」
  • monetization n. 獲利化、營利
    ...and could bring “substantial new monetization opportunities for Meta in the form of advertising, subscriptions and transaction revenue-share,” the analysts added.
    分析師補充說，並可能「以廣告、訂閱和交易營收分潤的形式，為 Meta 帶來龐大的新獲利機會」。
  • supplier n. 供應商
    The early success of Muse has helped lift the stocks of some of Meta’s key suppliers, particularly makers of microprocessors.
    Muse 的早期成功推動了 Meta 一些主要供應商的股價上漲，特別是微處理器製造商。

文法重點

  • 關係代名詞非限定用法（Which 導出的補充說明子句）
    The app, which is available for people 18 years and older, was downloaded more than 902,000 times since Meta introduced it on Sept. 8...
    句中的「which is available for people 18 years and older」帶有逗號，屬於非限定用法的關係子句，用來補充說明前面主詞 the app 的額外資訊。若拿掉這個子句，主要子句「The app was downloaded more than 902,000 times...」文意依然完整。
  • 分詞片語作後置修飾（過去分詞修飾名詞）
    By contrast, the free Meta AI app, released in April, focuses on image and short-video generation...
    句中的「released in April」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「the free Meta AI app」。完整結構原本是「which was released in April」，省略了「which was」後直接留下過去分詞，使句子更為精簡。
  • 現在分詞作結果或附帶狀況的補語
    Advanced Micro Devices Inc surged almost 10 percent, pushing its market value above US$1 trillion.
    句尾的「pushing its market value above US$1 trillion」是由現在分詞（pushing）帶領的分詞構句，用來表達前一個動作（股價飆升）所自然產生的連續結果（將市值推升至 1 兆美元以上）。
  • 獨立分詞構句（With + 名詞 + 分詞）
    ...as oil resumed its role in setting the tone for markets, with Brent heading for its first gain in a week.
    句尾使用了「with + 名詞 (Brent) + 現在分詞 (heading for...)」的結構，這在英文新聞中非常常見，用來補充說明主要的事件發生時，同時存在的背景狀況或附帶動作。
