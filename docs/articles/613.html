單字解析

  • expand v. 擴大，擴展
    marking the aircraft’s debut as the China Airlines (CAL) Group expands its operations in southern Taiwan.
    隨著中華航空（華航）集團擴大其在南台灣的營運，這標誌著該款飛機的首次亮相。
  • deploy v. 配置，部署
    The A321neo, registered B-18135, was recently delivered to the CAL Group and would primarily be deployed on CAL’s international routes from Kaohsiung
    這架註冊編號為 B-18135 的 A321neo 最近交付給華航集團，主要將部署於華航自高雄出發的國際航線
  • international adj. 國際的
    would primarily be deployed on CAL’s international routes from Kaohsiung
    主要將部署於華航自高雄出發的國際航線
  • operate v. 營運，運作
    would begin operating international routes from Kaohsiung, CAL president Kevin Chen (陳漢銘) said.
    華航總經理陳漢銘表示，將開始營運自高雄出發的國際航線。
  • passenger n. 乘客，旅客
    said the carrier had prepared limited-edition inaugural flight certificates for passengers on the maiden voyage
    表示航空公司為首航乘客準備了限量版的首航證書
  • fleet n. 機隊，車隊
    Mandarin Airlines operates a fleet of 13 ATR 72-600 turboprop aircraft on domestic routes
    華信航空在國內線上營運一支由 13 架 ATR 72-600 渦輪螺旋槳飛機組成的機隊
  • strategy n. 策略，戰略
    As part of the CAL Group’s strategy to enhance its presence in southern Taiwan
    作為華航集團強化其在南台灣佈局策略的一部分
  • enhance v. 提高，增加，強化
    As part of the CAL Group’s strategy to enhance its presence in southern Taiwan
    作為華航集團強化其在南台灣佈局策略的一部分
  • convenience n. 便利，方便
    improving travel convenience for passengers in southern Taiwan
    提升南台灣旅客的旅行便利性
  • competitiveness n. 競爭力
    while enhancing the airline’s competitiveness, he added.
    他補充說，同時增強航空公司的競爭力。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附帶狀況/結果分句
    marking the aircraft’s debut as the China Airlines (CAL) Group expands its operations in southern Taiwan.
    句尾的「marking...」是由現在分詞構成的補充說明子句，用來接續前面子句所發生的動作，表示自然帶出的結果或狀況，翻譯時常譯為「標誌著...」或「藉此...」。
  • 被動語態的運用
    The A321neo, registered B-18135, was recently delivered to the CAL Group and would primarily be deployed on CAL’s international routes...
    新聞英語中經常使用被動語態（如 was delivered 與 would be deployed）來強調動作的承受者（飛機），而不是動作的執行者，使語氣顯得客觀且符合商務與新聞報導的慣例。
  • 不定詞片語作目的狀語
    To mark the aircraft’s inaugural Kaohsiung-Penghu flight, airports in Kaohsiung and Penghu held water-cannon salute ceremonies...
    句首的「To mark...」是「In order to」的簡寫，為不定詞片語置於句首作副詞用，用來表達後方主要子句動作（舉辦水門禮）的目的（為了慶祝...）。
  • 關係代間接引語（Reported Speech）
    The group’s newest A321neo had officially entered service in southern Taiwan and... would begin operating international routes from Kaohsiung, CAL president Kevin Chen (陳漢銘) said.
    報導新聞時常會引用說話者的話語。此句將引言置於前方，主詞與動詞（CAL president... said）置於後方，且引言中的時態通常會配合主句的過去式（said）作相應調整（例如使用 had entered 與 would begin）。
