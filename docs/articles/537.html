  • distribute v. 分發，分配

  • equivalent adj. 相等的，相當於
    ...it will distribute rewards equivalent to 35-68 months' pay to its workers in Taiwan...
    ...將發放相當於35至68個月薪資的獎勵給其台灣員工...
  • statement n. 聲明，陳述
    In a statement, Micron said the fiscal 2026 rewards will be distributed to more than 60,000 Micron workers worldwide...
    美光在聲明中表示，2026會計年度的獎勵將發放給全球超過6萬名美光員工...

    The U.S. company said the record rewards demonstrated its long-term commitment to allowing employees to share in its success.
    這家美國公司表示，這筆創紀錄的獎勵展現了其長期承諾，即讓員工共享公司的成功。

  • demand n. / v. 需求，要求
    ...industry sources said a potential strike at Micron Taiwan could further constrain supplies and exacerbate the existing supply-demand imbalance...
    ...產業消息人士表示，美光台灣分公司的潛在罷工可能會進一步緊縮供應，並加劇現有的供需失衡...
  • capacity n. 產能，容量
    ...accounting for about 60 percent of its total production capacity and largely producing DRAM and high-bandwidth memory chips.

  • shortage n. 短缺，不足
    ...as a supply shortage pushed up memory chip prices.
    ...主因是供給短缺推高了記憶體晶片價格。
