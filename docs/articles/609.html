行政院長鼓勵生育

Premier encourages having kids

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • attend v. 參加，出席
    Premier Cho Jung-tai (卓榮泰) yesterday encouraged newlyweds to have children while attending a mass wedding held by Chunghwa Telecom in Taipei...
    行政院長卓榮泰昨天在台北出席中華電信舉辦的集團結婚典禮時，鼓勵新婚夫妻生育子女...
  • demographic adj. 人口統計的，人口的
    ...thanked the newlyweds for taking a step that could help Taiwan “address its demographic challenges,” referring to the country’s low birthrate.
    ...感謝新婚夫妻跨出這一步，有助於台灣「應對人口挑戰」，他指的是該國的低生育率。
  • consecutive adj. 連續的，連貫的
    ...while the number of births dropped for a 10th consecutive year to a record-low 107,812.
    ...而出生人數則連續第 10 年下降，創下 107,812 人的歷史新低。
  • obligation n. 義務，責任
    “Marriage is everyone’s right, and having children is everyone’s choice. It is definitely not a responsibility or obligation,” he said.
    他說：「結婚是每個人的權利，生孩子則是每個人的選擇。這絕不是一種責任或義務。」
  • allowance n. 津貼，補助
    ...highlighting plans for a NT$5,000 monthly child-rearing allowance starting next year, expanded childcare services and measures aimed at easing housing and education costs.
    ...並強調了明年生效的每月 5,000 元育兒津貼計畫、擴大托育服務，以及旨在減輕住房和教育成本的措施。
  • state-owned enterprise n. phr. 國營企業
    Cho said he hoped state-owned enterprises would organize more large-scale mass weddings...
    卓榮泰表示，他希望國營企業能舉辦更多大型集團結婚...
  • subsidy n. 補助金，津貼
    ...including an NT$80,000 childbirth subsidy, a seven-hour workday with eight hours’ pay for employees with children younger than six...
    ...包括 8 萬元生育補助、育有 6 歲以下子女員工享有 7 小時工作時數領 8 小時薪資的福利...
  • appeal n. 吸引力，申訴
    A 19-year Chunghwa Telecom employee, Lin said the planned NT$5,000 monthly allowance had “some appeal,” but added that the amount would “definitely not be enough” amid rising living costs.
    身為中華電信 19 年老員工，林男表示計畫中的每月 5,000 元津貼「有點吸引力」，但補充說在生活成本上升的情況下，這筆金額「絕對不夠」。
  • insufficient adj. 不足的，不夠的
    ...added that while the government’s available support showed “sincerity,” it remained insufficient.
    ...但也補充說，雖然政府現有的支持展現了「誠意」，但仍然不足。
  • mortgage n. 房貸，抵押貸款
    “Once you take on a mortgage, you might have to pay NT$20,000 to NT$30,000 a month,” the 30-year-old said...
    這位 30 歲的年輕人表示：「一旦你扛起房貸，每個月可能就得繳 2 萬元到 3 萬元」...
  • burden n. 負擔
    ...he mentioned that his wife works too and that they share the mortgage payments, easing the financial burden.
    ...他提到妻子也有工作，且兩人分擔房貸，減輕了財務負擔。

文法重點

  • 非限定關代子句 (Non-defining relative clause)
    Cho, who served as a witness at the ceremony at the Grand Hotel, thanked the newlyweds for taking a step that could help Taiwan...
    句中的「who served as a witness at the ceremony at the Grand Hotel」是用逗號隔開的非限定關係代名詞子句，用來補充說明主詞 Cho（卓榮泰）的身份，若刪除該子句，主句的意思依然完整。
  • 現在分詞作附帶狀況/結果 (Present participle expressing accompanying circumstance)
    ...thanked the newlyweds for taking a step that could help Taiwan “address its demographic challenges,” referring to the country’s low birthrate.
    句尾的「referring to the country’s low birthrate」是由現在分詞片語構成的結構，用來補充說明前面整句話所指的內容（主動語態，表伴隨發生的動作）。
  • 過去分詞作後置修飾語 (Past participle as postmodifier)
    ...while attending a mass wedding held by Chunghwa Telecom in Taipei, where 72 couples were married.
    句中的「held by Chunghwa Telecom in Taipei」是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞 a mass wedding。因為 wedding（婚禮）是被舉辦，所以使用過去分詞 held，省略了「which was」。
  • 對比連接詞 while 引導的子句 (Conjunction 'while' indicating contrast/simultaneous action)
    While encouraging the couples to have children, Cho added that childbirth remains a personal choice.
    「While encouraging...」省略了主詞與be動詞（原貌為 While he was encouraging...），用來表達「在...的同時」，連接兩個同時發生或形成對比的動作。
  • 使役與修飾語複合結構 (Complex noun modifier with with/without)
    ...including an NT$80,000 childbirth subsidy, a seven-hour workday with eight hours’ pay for employees with children younger than six...
    句中使用「with eight hours’ pay」這個介系詞片語作為後置修飾語，用來形容「seven-hour workday」（七小時工作日）附帶的條件（領八小時薪資），使商業和福利政策的描述更為精準。
