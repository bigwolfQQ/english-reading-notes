台灣作家作品入圍美國國家圖書獎長名單

Taiwanese book longlisted for National Book Award

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • recognition n. 讚賞、認可、承認
    marking the second such recognition for a Taiwanese writer after Yang Shuang-zi
    這標誌著繼楊雙子之後，台灣作家第二次獲得此殊榮
  • nonprofit n. / adj. 非營利組織、非營利的
    The nonprofit announced the longlist on Tuesday.
    該非營利組織於週二公布了長名單。
  • publish v. 出版、發行
    To the East of the East was one of 10 titles — originally published in Arabic, Chinese...
    《東方之東》是從 159 本書中選出的 10 本作品之一，這些原著最初分別以阿拉伯文、中文……出版
  • attribute to phr. v. 將……歸因於
    Ping Lu, whose real name is Lu Ping, attributed the recognition to Tiang’s translation
    本名路平的平路在接受記者採訪時將此殊榮歸功於程異的翻譯
  • contribution n. 貢獻
    and that “my contribution may only be about 10 percent.”
    並表示「我的貢獻可能只有 10% 左右」
  • director n. 主任、董事、主管
    after working in Hong Kong as director of the Kwang Hwa Information and Culture Center
    在香港擔任光華新聞文化中心主任後返回台灣
  • circumstance n. 境況、情況、情勢
    The novel reflected her circumstances at the time
    這部小說反映了她當時的境況
  • examine v. 檢查、審視、探討
    exploring how a person examines their inner emotions from an outside perspective.
    探討了個人如何從外部視角審視自己的內心情感。
  • relationship n. 關係、關聯
    The novel is also about the relationship between an island and the mainland
    這部小說也關於島嶼與大陸之間的關係
  • pursuit n. 追求
    that one’s ideal life is often linked to the pursuit of freedom
    理想生活通常與追求自由息息相關

文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The English-language translation of Taiwanese writer Ping Lu’s To the East of the East has been longlisted for the US’ National Book Award...
    句型為「has been + 過去分詞 (longlisted)」，用來強調動作的承受者（即翻譯作品），且動作由外部的美國國家圖書獎執行，不需要特別說出施事者是誰。
  • 分詞片語作後置修飾 (Participial Phrase as Post-modifier)
    Translated by Jeremy Tiang (程異), the novel follows a Taiwanese writer’s trip to Beijing...
    句首的「Translated by Jeremy Tiang」是由過去分詞引導的片語，修飾主句的主語 the novel（小說是被翻譯的，所以用過去分詞表被動），等同於副詞子句「Because the novel was translated by Jeremy Tiang...」的簡化。
  • 關係代間詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    Ping Lu, whose real name is Lu Ping (路平), attributed the recognition to Tiang’s translation...
    「whose real name is Lu Ping」是非限定修飾的所有格關係子句，用代間詞 whose 來代替 Ping Lu 的所有格，補充說明平路的本名是什麼，並用逗號隔開。
  • 假設語氣 / 條件句 (Conditional Clause)
    “Sometimes if you go far enough, you can hear the call from within your heart,” she said.
    使用「if + 現在式動詞 (go)，主句用情態動詞 + 原形動詞 (can hear)」的第一類條件句，表達真實的假設狀況（如果走得夠遠，就能聽見呼喚）。
