    Chinese Taipei Gymnastics Association (CTGA) Secretary-General Chiang Chien-tung has stepped down after unilaterally rejecting an invitation for two gymnasts to compete...

  • decline v. 婉拒，拒絕

  • consult v. 諮詢，與…商量

  • deprive v. 剝奪，使喪失

    ...因為在未與他們商量的情況下婉拒邀請，並剝奪了他們參賽的機會。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況/結果
    ...for declining the invitation without consulting them and depriving them of a chance to compete.
    句中的 "depriving them of a chance to compete" 為現在分詞片語引導的分詞構句，補充說明前面動作伴隨發生的結果或狀況，主詞概念與主句保持一致。
  • 過去分詞作後置修飾語
    According to the CTGA, the pair had earned reallocated spots to compete at the Artistic Gymnastics World Championships...
    句中的 "reallocated" 是過去分詞，用作形容詞放在名詞 "spots" 前方，修飾被重新分配的名額。
  • 使役動詞的被動語態
    Chiang stepped down as secretary-general after the Sports Ministry invoked Article 43 of the National Sports Act last Friday to remove him...
    句尾的 "to remove him" 是一個不定詞片語，用來表達目的（為了將他撤職）。在多益考試中常考此類表達目的或結果的不定詞結構。
