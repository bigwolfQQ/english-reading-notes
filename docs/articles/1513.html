行政院長：台灣正密切監控鼠疫謠言

Taiwan closely monitoring plague rumors: premier

單字解析

  • monitor v. 監控，監測
    The government is monitoring online rumors that a pneumonic plague outbreak has spread from Russia into China...
    政府正在監控網路上有關肺鼠疫疫情從俄羅斯蔓延至中國的謠言...
  • enhance v. 提高，增加，加強
    ...and it might enhance quarantine measures, Premier Cho Jung-tai (卓榮泰) said yesterday.
    ...並可能加強檢疫措施，行政院長卓榮泰昨天表示。
  • measure n. 措施，方法
    The Ministry of Health and Welfare had implemented border quarantine measures after briefing the Executive Yuan on the issue two weeks ago...
    衛生福利部在兩週前向行政院簡報此議題後，已實施邊境檢疫措施...
  • implement v. 實施，執行
    The Ministry of Health and Welfare had implemented border quarantine measures after briefing the Executive Yuan on the issue two weeks ago...
    衛生福利部在兩週前向行政院簡報此議題後，已實施邊境檢疫措施...
  • brief v. 向...簡報，彙報
    The Ministry of Health and Welfare had implemented border quarantine measures after briefing the Executive Yuan on the issue two weeks ago...
    衛生福利部在兩週前向行政院簡報此議題後，已實施邊境檢疫措施...
  • authorities n. pl. 當局，官方
    Authorities would continue monitoring online information from China about the disease...
    相關單位將繼續監控來自中國有關該疾病的網路資訊...
  • determine v. 決定，確定
    ...to identify any “suspicious” developments and determine whether quarantine measures should be bolstered accordingly...
    ...以識別任何「可疑」發展，並決定是否應相應地強化檢疫措施...
  • accordingly adv. 因此，相應地
    ...and determine whether quarantine measures should be bolstered accordingly, he said.
    ...並決定是否應相應地強化檢疫措施，他表示。
  • contact n. / v. 接觸，聯繫
    The council would remain in close contact with the health ministry to keep Taiwanese working in China informed of the latest developments...
    陸委會將與衛福部保持密切聯繫，讓在中國工作的台灣人隨時掌握最新發展...
  • verify v. 核實，查證
    Minister of Health and Welfare Shih Chung-liang (石崇良) said his ministry had contacted the WHO to verify whether the Oct. 2 death... was due to pneumonic plague.
    衛生福利部長石崇良表示，衛福部已聯繫世界衛生組織（WHO），以核實10月2日的死亡是否為肺鼠疫。

文法重點

  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense)
    The Ministry of Health and Welfare had implemented border quarantine measures after briefing the Executive Yuan on the issue two weeks ago...
    表示在過去某一時間點（行政院長說明時）之前已經完成的動作。had implemented 用於強調「部會先前已經實施了檢疫措施」這個先後順序。
  • 現在完成式 (Present Perfect Tense)
    However, so far, no tests of pathogens or other samples have returned positive results...
    片語 so far（到目前為止）常搭配現在完成式 (have returned) 使用，用來描述從過去持續到現在的一段時間內所發生的狀況或結果。
  • 間接問句 (Indirect Questions)
    ...and how concerned the government was about the alleged outbreak.
    當疑問句成為句中的一部分（作受詞）時，語序需改為「疑問詞 + 主詞 + 動詞」。在此句中，how concerned 為疑問詞組，the government 為主詞，was 為動詞，維持直述句語序。
  • 過去分詞作形容詞 (Past Participle as Adjective)
    ...to identify any “suspicious” developments and determine whether quarantine measures should be bolstered accordingly...
    在此句中，should be bolstered 採用了被動語態（助動詞 should + be + 過去分詞 bolstered），用來表達檢疫措施「應該被」強化，強調動作的承受者。
