中央氣象署：東北風系統週三起帶來降雨

Northeasterly wind system to bring rain from Wednesday: CWA

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • pass over phr. 掠過、從上方經過
    This autumn's first northeasterly wind system will pass over Taiwan from Wednesday through Friday, bringing scattered rain showers to much of the country, the Central Weather Administration (CWA) said Monday.
    中央氣象署（CWA）週一表示，今年秋天的首波東北風系統將於週三至週五掠過台灣，為全台大部地區帶來零星陣雨。
  • with the exception of phr. 除了……之外
    CWA forecaster Wang Pin-hsiang (王品翔) told CNA Taiwan would continue to enjoy mostly cloudy to clear weather on Tuesday, with the exception of possible localized showers in some mountainous and coastal areas.
    氣象署預報員王品翔告訴中央社，台灣週二將持續享有大多多雲到晴的天氣，不過部分山區和沿海地區可能會有局部陣雨。
  • arrival n. 到達、來臨
    From Wednesday through Thursday, however, the arrival of a northeasterly wind system will bring scattered showers to the east and areas above Taoyuan in the north, Wang said.
    不過，王品翔表示，隨著週三至週四東北風系統的到來，將為東部及北部桃園以北地區帶來零星陣雨。
  • moisture n. 水分、濕氣
    On Friday, increased atmospheric moisture will bring a chance of heavy rains in Keelung and along the north coast, as well as in Yilan and Hualien, Wang said.
    王品翔說，週五水氣增加，將為基隆、北海岸以及宜蘭和花蓮帶來降下大雨的機會。
  • weaken v. 減弱、衰退
    While the wind system is forecast to weaken on Saturday, moist air from the south is expected to move northward, bringing rain to a wide swathe of the country, according to the CWA.
    根據氣象署指出，雖然預估該風系統將於週六減弱，但預期南方的水氣將向北移動，為全台廣大地區帶來降雨。
  • forecast v. / n. 預測、預報
    Throughout this week, slightly cooler overnight and early morning temperatures are forecast for Taiwan, with lows ranging from 22-25 degrees Celsius nationwide.
    氣象署預報，本週全台各地夜間與清晨氣溫將略微偏涼，全台各地低溫落在攝氏 22 至 25 度之間。
  • range v. （在一定範圍內）變動、介於
    Throughout this week, slightly cooler overnight and early morning temperatures are forecast for Taiwan, with lows ranging from 22-25 degrees Celsius nationwide.
    氣象署預報，本週全台各地夜間與清晨氣溫將略微偏涼，全台各地低溫落在攝氏 22 至 25 度之間。
  • nationwide adj. / adv. 全國性的、全國各地
    Throughout this week, slightly cooler overnight and early morning temperatures are forecast for Taiwan, with lows ranging from 22-25 degrees Celsius nationwide.
    氣象署預報，本週全台各地夜間與清晨氣溫將略微偏涼，全台各地低溫落在攝氏 22 至 25 度之間。
  • meanwhile adv. 同時、在此期間
    Daytime highs, meanwhile, will rise to 30-33 degrees in the western half of the country, and 28-30 degrees in the east, Wang said.
    同時，王品翔說，西半部地區白天高溫將升至 30 至 33 度，東半部則為 28 至 30 度。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作表結果的附帶說明（分詞構句）
    This autumn's first northeasterly wind system will pass over Taiwan from Wednesday through Friday, bringing scattered rain showers to much of the country, the Central Weather Administration (CWA) said Monday.
    句尾的「bringing ...」是由連接詞和子句簡化而來的現在分詞片語（and bring...），用來補充說明前面主句動作所產生的自然結果或伴隨狀況，這是新聞英文中非常常見且高效的表達方式。
  • 被動語態的運用
    While the wind system is forecast to weaken on Saturday, moist air from the south is expected to move northward...
    句中的「is forecast」與「is expected」皆為被動語態（be + 過去分詞），在氣象新聞或商業報導中，當說話者不需特別強調發布預測或期待的人，或者強調客觀預估時，常會使用被動語態來呈現。
  • 對等連接詞引導的子句與省略
    On Friday, increased atmospheric moisture will bring a chance of heavy rains in Keelung and along the north coast, as well as in Yilan and Hualien, Wang said.
    句中的「as well as in Yilan and Hualien」連接並列的介詞片語，省略了重複出現的動詞與名詞結構，使句子更為精煉緊湊。
