Taiwan shares close sharply up, led by electronics sector after U.S. rally

單字解析

  • benchmark index n. phr. 基準指數、指標指數
    The Taiex, the Taiwan Stock Exchange's benchmark index, ended up 538.09 points...
    台灣證券交易所發行量加權股價指數（台股指數）終場上漲 538.09 點...
  • turnover n. 成交量、營業額
    Turnover totaled 820.22 billion (US$25.79 billion).
    成交值總計新台幣 8,202.2 億元（約合 257.9 億美元）。
  • rate hike n. phr. 升息、利率調升
    Although the U.S. Federal Reserve hinted at another rate hike by the end of this year...
    儘管美國聯準會暗示今年底前可能再次升息...
  • basis point n. phr. 基點（金融術語，等於 0.01 個百分點）
    ...after last week's 25 basis point increase, major cloud service providers continued to pump funds into AI infrastructure...
    ...繼上週升息 25 個基點後，主要的雲端服務供應商持續將資金投入 AI 基礎設施...
  • pump v. 大量注入（資金等）
    ...major cloud service providers continued to pump funds into AI infrastructure...
    ...主要的雲端服務供應商持續將資金投入 AI 基礎設施...
  • investor n. 投資人
    ...prompting investors to pick up tech stocks at home and abroad...
    ...促使國內外投資人進場承接科技股...
  • benefit v. 使受益、得益
    In Taipei, strong buying continued to benefit semiconductor stocks...
    在台北，強勁的買盤持續讓半導體類股受益...
  • upbeat adj. 樂觀的
    ...as investors appeared upbeat about its efforts in application-specific integrated circuit (ASIC) development...
    ...因為投資人對其在應用特定積體電路（ASIC）開發上的努力抱持樂觀態度...
  • market value n. phr. 市值
    ...TSMC, which accounts for over 40 percent of total market value, added 0.81 percent...
    ...占總市值四成以上的台積電上漲 0.81%...
  • supplier n. 供應商
    ...but memory chip supplier Nanya Technology Corp. ended down 1.71 percent at NT$516.00.
    ...但記憶體晶片供應商南亞科技股份有限公司則下跌 1.71%，收在新台幣 516.00 元。
  • cooperation n. 合作
    ...on reports that Intel Corp. planned cooperation in advanced IC assembly services.
    ...市場傳出英特爾公司計畫在先進 IC 封裝服務上展開合作。
  • income n. 收入、收益
    ...on hopes that the Fed's rate hike will boost interest income, dealers said.
    交易員表示，主要受到聯準會升息將提升利息收入的期望所帶動。
  • sentiment n. 市場情緒、心理
    A fall in crude oil prices also improved market sentiment today...
    原油價格下跌今日也改善了市場情緒...
  • institutional investor n. phr. 機構投資人（如外資、投信等）
    According to the Taiwan Stock Exchange, foreign institutional investors bought a net NT$20.38 billion of shares...
    根據台灣證券交易所資料，外國機構投資人淨買超新台幣 203.8 億元...

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表示伴隨狀況或結果）
    Shares in Taiwan closed sharply higher Monday, with buying sparked by a U.S. tech rally at the end of last week, according to dealers.
    句中的 "with buying sparked by..." 為「with + 名詞 + 過去分詞」的複合結構，在句中作分詞構句，用來補充說明主句發生時的伴隨狀況，意指「在……買盤被激勵的情況下」。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句（補充說明）
    After its American depositary receipts rose 1.02 percent Friday, TSMC, which accounts for over 40 percent of total market value, added 0.81 percent...
    逗號之後的 "which accounts for over 40 percent of total market value" 是非限定關係代名詞子句，前面加逗號，用來補充說明前面的主詞 TSMC（台積電）占了總市值的多少比例，若刪除不影響主要句意。
  • 現在分詞作後位修飾
    Among other electronics component stocks, power management solution provider Delta Electronics Inc. surged 8.07 percent... and flat panel maker AUO Corp. jumped 10 percent...
    句首的 "Bucking the upturn" 是由現在分詞 "Bucking"（對抗、逆勢）開頭的分詞片語，用來修飾後方的主詞 Hon Hai Precision Industry Co.（鴻海），表示「逆勢著上升趨勢而行」的動作。
  • 形容詞片語作表語（be + 形容詞 + about...）
    IC designer MediaTek also attracted interest as investors appeared upbeat about its efforts in application-specific integrated circuit (ASIC) development...
    句中的 "appeared upbeat about..." 使用連綴動詞 appear 搭配形容詞 upbeat（樂觀的、看好的），並以介系詞片語 about... 接受詞，用來描述投資人當時表現出來的心理狀態。
