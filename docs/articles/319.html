賽車隊指責中國主辦方「疏忽」

Racing team calls Chinese organizers ‘negligent’

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • negligent adj. 疏忽的、怠忽職守的
    A car racing team whose injured driver was rescued by another competitor after a horrific crash in Shanghai on Saturday has called the organizers “grossly negligent in the safety response” and said it is withdrawing from future events in China.
    週六在上海發生了一場駭人的車禍，一名受傷的車手被另一位參賽者救出，該賽車隊隨後痛批主辦單位「安全應變嚴重疏忽」，並表示將退出未來在中國舉辦的賽事。
  • withdraw v. 退出、撤回
    It said it would withdraw from all GT events in China until its demands were met.
    該車隊表示，在訴求得到滿足之前，將退出所有在中國舉辦的 GT 賽事。
  • sustain v. 遭受、承受（受傷、損失等）
    According to his Instagram account, Millroy sustained six broken ribs, a broken collarbone, a broken hand and a punctured lung.
    根據他的 Instagram 帳號，米爾羅伊受了六根肋骨骨折、一處鎖骨骨折、一處手部骨折以及肺挫裂傷。
  • conduct n. 行為、處理方式
    Millroy’s team, AAI Motorsports, in a long statement was scathing about the conduct of Chinese organizers.
    米爾羅伊的車隊 AAI Motorsports 在一份長篇聲明中，對中國主辦方的行為表示嚴厲斥責。
  • comprehensive adj. 全面的、綜合的
    AAI Motorsports called for a “comprehensive investigation” and public disclosure around the accident, track safety and the emergency response.
    AAI Motorsports 呼籲對事故、賽道安全和緊急應變進行「全面調查」並公開披露。
  • independent adj. 獨立的
    “We will not return until the event organizing committee conducts a comprehensive, independent and transparent investigation into every aspect of the incident...
    「在賽事組織委員會對...事故各個方面進行全面、獨立和透明的調查...之前，我們不會返回。」
  • apologize v. 道歉
    In a statement, event organizers apologized for the “incident and the distress” it caused and offered “sincere sympathy” to the injured driver.
    賽事主辦方在聲明中為「此次事故及其造成的困擾」道歉，並向受傷車手表達「誠摯的慰問」。
  • preliminary adj. 初步的、預備的
    “According to our preliminary review, the event’s overall staffing and resources met the standards required for a national-level motorsport event...
    「根據我們的初步審查，賽事的整體人員配置和資源達到了國家級賽車運動賽事所要求的標準...」
  • overall adj. 總體的、全面的
    “According to our preliminary review, the event’s overall staffing and resources met the standards required for a national-level motorsport event...
    「根據我們的初步審查，賽事的整體人員配置和資源達到了國家級賽車運動賽事所要求的標準...」
  • operational adj. 營運的、操作的
    ...although there remains room for improvement in detailed operational management,” the organizers wrote.
    「...儘管在詳細的營運管理方面仍有改進的空間，」主辦方寫道。

文法重點

  • 非限定性關係代名詞子句（Whose 表所有格）
    A car racing team whose injured driver was rescued by another competitor after a horrific crash in Shanghai on Saturday has called the organizers “grossly negligent in the safety response”...
    句中的「whose injured driver」是用來修飾先行詞「A car racing team」的非限定性關係子句，用 whose 來表示「其（車隊）受傷的車手」，在多益閱讀測驗中常考所有格關係代名詞的用法。
  • 強調句構 (It was... who...)
    And it was another driver, Loek Hartog, who stopped his car and, armed with a fire extinguisher, pulled Millroy out of the burning wreckage.
    此處使用「It was + 被強調的對象 + who...」的強調句型，用來突顯是「另一個車手 Loek Hartog」做出救援舉動，加強語氣並吸引讀者注意。
  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況（Armed with...）
    ...it was another driver, Loek Hartog, who stopped his car and, armed with a fire extinguisher, pulled Millroy out of the burning wreckage.
    句中的「armed with a fire extinguisher」是由過去分詞片語引導的分詞構句，修飾主詞 Loek Hartog，補充說明他當時「手持滅火器」的狀態，相當於放在句中的附加形容詞片語。
  • 連接詞 until 引導的時間副詞子句
    It said it would withdraw from all GT events in China until its demands were met.
    「until its demands were met」是由 until 引導的時間子句，用來表示「直到……為止」，主句使用過去未來式 (would withdraw)，子句則用過去式被動語態 (were met) 表達條件的時態一致。
