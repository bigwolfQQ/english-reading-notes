台灣美光工會拒絕一次性獎勵方案

Micron Taiwan union rejects one-time reward package

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • reject v. 拒絕
    The Micron Taiwan workers' union on Tuesday rejected a reward package unveiled by U.S.-based Micron Technology Inc. last week
    美光台灣企業工會於週二拒絕了美國美光科技公司上週公布的獎勵方案
  • dispute n. 爭議，糾紛
    while the union and the company are still in talks over the current bonus dispute
    雖然工會與公司目前仍在就當前的獎金爭議進行談判
  • compensation n. 補償，薪酬
    employees in Taiwan would receive compensation equivalent to 35-68 months' pay for fiscal 2026
    台灣員工在2026會計年度將獲得相當於35至68個月薪資的補償
  • transparent adj. 透明的
    He urged Micron to establish a transparent and verifiable profit-sharing mechanism
    他敦促美光建立一個透明且可核實的利潤分享機制
  • negotiation n. 談判，協商
    Lin said whether the union stages a strike will depend on the outcome of two rounds of negotiations scheduled for Friday and Sept. 21.
    林哲瑞表示，工會是否發動罷工將取決於預定於週五和9月21日舉行的兩輪談判結果。
  • demand n. 需求
    Global demand for memory chips has risen amid the artificial intelligence (AI) boom
    全球對記憶體晶片的需求已經上升，在人工智慧（AI）熱潮的推動下
  • dissatisfaction n. 不滿
    However, Micron workers in Taiwan have expressed dissatisfaction with bonuses they previously received
    然而，台灣的美光員工對先前收到的獎金表示不滿
  • exacerbate v. 使惡化，加劇
    Industry sources said a strike at Micron Taiwan could further constrain supplies and exacerbate the existing supply-demand imbalance amid the AI boom.
    業界人士表示，台灣美光若發生罷工，可能會進一步壓縮供應，並在AI熱潮中加劇現有的供需失衡。
  • mediation n. 調解，斡旋
    In response, Micron said it would continue to listen to its employees and participate in the mediation process in good faith.
    對此，美光表示將繼續聆聽員工的心聲，並以誠意參與調解程序。
  • employ v. 僱用
    Micron employs about 15,000 people in Taiwan and has invested more than NT$1.6 trillion in the country
    美光在台灣僱用約15,000名員工，並在台灣投資超過1.6兆新台幣

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 / 伴隨狀況
    The trend has allowed companies including Micron, South Korea's Samsung Electronics Co. and SK hynix Inc. to post record sales and profits.
    句中的 including 引導分詞片語作為介系詞使用，用來補充說明前面 companies 包含哪些具體企業。
  • 關係代間接引述與條件子句
    He said that if the company continues to seek to influence the talks, the union will declare that negotiations have broken down and hold a vote among its members on whether to strike.
    此句包含典型的「if 條件句」表達未來可能發生的情境（if the company continues...），以及用「that」引導的名詞子句作為受詞，結構層次分明，是多益閱讀與商業英文中常見的複雜句型。
  • 分詞構句表附帶結果
    Global demand for memory chips has risen amid the artificial intelligence (AI) boom, tightening supplies and driving up prices.
    句尾的 "tightening supplies and driving up prices" 是由對等連接詞 and 連接的現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前面需求上升所帶來的直接連帶結果（使供應緊繃並推高價格）。
  • 過去分詞作後位修飾
    as a supply shortage drove up memory chip prices.
    雖然本句主要示範的是 as 引導的原因副詞子句，但在文章前段如 "negotiations scheduled for Friday" 中，也頻繁使用了過去分詞片語（scheduled for Friday）來修飾前面的名詞（negotiations），省略了「which are」，是多益測驗中極高頻的文法考點。
