中央氣象署：週四起多雲到晴的天氣將轉為降雨

Mostly dry weather to give way to showers Thursday: CWA

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • strengthen v. 加強，增強
    before strengthening seasonal northeasterly winds bring rain to northern and eastern Taiwan starting Thursday, the Central Weather Administration (CWA) said Sunday.
    中央氣象署（CWA）周日表示，在增強的季節性東北季風自周四起為台灣北部和東部帶來降雨之前...
  • conditions n. 條件，狀況，情況
    CWA forecaster Liu Pei-teng (劉沛滕) told CNA that conditions will range from cloudy to sunny from Monday through Wednesday...
    氣象署預報員劉沛滕告訴中央社，周一至周三的天氣狀況將介於多雲到晴之間...
  • range v. （在一定範圍內）變動，介於...之間
    with daytime highs ranging from 32-35 degrees Celsius and potentially reaching 36 degrees in Taipei and inland areas of western Taiwan, Liu said.
    劉沛滕表示，白天高溫約在攝氏32至35度之間，台北及台灣西部內陸地區可能達到36度。
  • remain v. 保持，維持
    Temperatures will remain largely unchanged during that period...
    在此期間氣溫將大致維持不變...
  • potential adj. 潛在的，可能的
    and potentially reaching 36 degrees in Taipei and inland areas of western Taiwan, Liu said.
    劉沛滕表示，台北及台灣西部內陸地區可能達到36度。
  • data n. 數據，資料
    Nighttime lows will range from 23-26 degrees across the island, according to CWA data.
    根據氣象署資料，全台各地夜間低溫將落在23至26度之間。
  • localized adj. 局部的，地方性的
    From Thursday through next Tuesday, stronger northeasterly winds will bring localized showers in areas north of Taoyuan and eastern Taiwan...
    從周四到下周二，較強的東北季風將為桃園以北及東部地區帶來局部陣雨...
  • forecast v. 預報，預測
    Nighttime temperatures will also fall to around 22-24 degrees across Taiwan, Liu forecast.
    劉沛滕預測，全台各地夜間氣溫也將下降至22至24度左右。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞當形容詞修飾名詞
    before strengthening seasonal northeasterly winds bring rain...
    句中的「strengthening」是現在分詞，在此作為形容詞放在名詞片語「seasonal northeasterly winds」的前面，用來修飾風正在「增強」的狀態。
  • 連接詞 before引導的時間副詞子句
    Most of Taiwan will see cloudy to sunny skies through Wednesday before strengthening seasonal northeasterly winds bring rain to northern and eastern Taiwan starting Thursday...
    「before」在此作為從屬連接詞，引導一個時間副詞子句（before strengthening seasonal northeasterly winds bring...），用來表達主句動作發生的先後順序。
  • 分詞片語表附帶狀況或結果（with + 名詞 + 分詞）
    with daytime highs ranging from 32-35 degrees Celsius and potentially reaching 36 degrees...
    這個結構為「with + 名詞 (daytime highs) + 現在分詞 (ranging / reaching)」，用來補充說明伴隨發生的狀態，常被翻譯為「隨著...」或「...達...度」。
