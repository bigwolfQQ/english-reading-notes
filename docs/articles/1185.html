文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    NDC head Yeh Chun-hsien (葉俊顯) was cited in the statement as saying that...
    句型為「be + 過去分詞 (cited)」。在此處使用被動語態，強調「言論被引用」這個客觀事實，而不需要特別強調是誰引用了他說的話。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Launched by the NDC in 2018, the Employment Gold Card program aims to attract skilled professionals...
    句首的「Launched by the NDC in 2018」是由過去分詞引導的片語，用來修飾後方的主詞「the Employment Gold Card program」，相當於完整的副詞子句「Since it was launched by the NDC in 2018...」，能讓句子更精簡流暢。
  • 現在分詞引導名詞子句的補語 (Present Participle in Noun Clause)
    NDC head Yeh Chun-hsien (葉俊顯) was cited in the statement as saying that locating this overseas talent recruitment platform in Silicon Valley was critical given that...
    句中「as saying that...」裡的「saying」是現在分詞，用來補充說明前面主詞的動作內容。「that」引導一個大的名詞子句作為受詞，而子句內部又包含一個由「given that」引導表條件的副詞子句。
