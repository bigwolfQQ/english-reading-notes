Taipei Times · 2026-09-09

關懷外籍家庭看護工

Caring for migrant care workers

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • proportion n. 比例、部分
    Taiwan has nearly 1 million people in need of long-term care, yet the proportion of people with severe disabilities receiving long-term care benefits has been declining year by year.
    台灣有近 100 萬人需要長期照顧，然而領取長期照顧給付的重度失能者比例卻逐年下降。
  • compete v. 競爭
    Meanwhile, Japan, South Korea and Hong Kong are competing to attract those workers.
    與此同時，日本、南韓和香港正競相吸引這些勞動力。
  • afford v. 負擔得起
    The question is not whether families can afford carers, but whether any will still be willing to move to Taiwan three years from now.
    問題不在於家庭是否負擔得起看護，而在於三年後是否還有人願意來台灣。
  • launch v. 發起、推出（計畫、產品等）
    Under the program — which was launched last year — nonprofit organizations hire care workers who are covered by the Labor Standards Act.
    在該計畫下——於去年推出——非營利組織僱用受《勞動基準法》保障的看護工。
  • demand n. 需求
    However, with just 13 participating organizations and 41 foreign care workers, the program is nowhere near large enough to meet the demands of even a single district of Taipei.
    然而，該計畫僅有 13 個參與組織和 41 名外籍看護工，遠遠不足以滿足台北市哪怕一個行政區的需求。
  • subsidy n. 補助金、津貼
    If the services provided by migrant domestic care workers for people with severe disabilities were incorporated into the long-term care benefits system — with partial subsidies based on the individual’s level of disability and the household’s financial circumstances — that could ease the burden on families, and improve migrant workers’ pay and access to days off.
    如果外籍家庭看護工為重度失能者提供的服務能被納入長照給付系統——根據個人的失能程度和家庭財務狀況給予部分補助——這就能減輕家庭的負擔，並改善外籍移工的薪資與休假權利。
  • burden n. 負擔
    That could ease the burden on families, and improve migrant workers’ pay and access to days off.
    這就能減輕家庭的負擔，並改善外籍移工的薪資與休假權利。
  • exceed v. 超過、超出
    The long-term care budget for this year has already exceeded NT$100 billion, yet people with severe disabilities have among the lowest takeup rates.
    今年的長照預算已經突破 1,000 億新台幣，然而重度失能者的使用率卻名列前茅中最低的族群之一。
  • transition v. 轉變、過渡
    Japan, by contrast, allows skilled care workers pathways to extend their work visas and transition to other forms of residency.
    相比之下，日本允許熟練的看護工有管道延長工作簽證並轉為其他形式的居留權。
  • flexible adj. 具彈性的、靈活的
    People with mild or moderate care needs could receive services through institutional staffing agencies, using multiple caregivers working in shifts and flexible hours.
    輕度或中度照顧需求的民眾可以透過機構派遣人力獲得服務，採用多名照護員輪班及彈性工時。
  • shoulder v. 扛起、承擔
    Neither employers nor the government wants to shoulder the additional costs, but the cost of care does not disappear simply because no one is willing to bear it...
    雇主和政府都不想承擔額外成本，但照顧的成本並不會因為沒有人願意承擔而消失……

文法重點

  • 關係代名詞非限定用法（which 引导的補述子句）
    Under the program — which was launched last year — nonprofit organizations hire care workers who are covered by the Labor Standards Act.
    句中的「— which was launched last year —」使用破折號夾入一個非限定用法的關係子句，用來補充說明前面的 the program（該計畫）。which 代替前面整個名詞片語，並在子句中作主詞。
  • 假設語氣（過去式/過去完成式，用來表達與現在事實相反的假設）
    If the services provided by migrant domestic care workers for people with severe disabilities were incorporated into the long-term care benefits system... that could ease the burden on families...
    此句為與現在事實相反的假設語氣。If 子句中使用過去式動詞（were incorporated），主要子句則使用助動詞的過去式加上原形動詞（could ease），表示「如果……（現在事實上沒有發生），那麼就能……」。
  • 否定副詞置於句首之倒裝句型（結合強調與分詞構句概念）
    Taiwan is using the silence of more than 200,000 foreign care workers to subsidize a society that refuses to confront the true cost of care.
    雖然此句並非標準的倒裝句，但句中包含了一個修飾主詞的現在分詞片語（refuses to confront...）所引導的關係子句。用以修飾 a society，精準傳達「拒絕面對照顧真實成本的社會」這樣的動態描述。
