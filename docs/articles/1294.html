台灣監獄人口中，毒品犯佔比下降

Drug offenders as a share of Taiwan's prison population falls

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • offender n. 罪犯，違法者
    Drug offenders as a share of Taiwan's prison population falls
    台灣監獄人口中，毒品犯佔比下降
  • serve a sentence phr. 服刑
    The share of inmates serving sentences for drug-related offenses has fallen from 35.1 percent to 24.8 percent over the past five years...
    過去五年來，因毒品相關罪行服刑的受刑人比例已從35.1%降至24.8%...
  • strategy n. 策略，行動方針
    According to the agency, the government's third-phase New Generation Anti-Drug Strategy Action Plan, which runs from 2025 to 2028, emphasizes tiered diversion and diversified treatment.
    該署表示，政府從2025年至2028年執行的第三期「新世代反毒策略行動綱領」，強調分級轉介與多元治療。
  • approach n. 方法，作法
    The agency said the approach reduces unnecessary incarceration, eases prison overcrowding and limits contact between drug users and drug traffickers while ensuring offenders receive treatment suited to their needs.
    該署表示，這種作法減少了不必要的監禁、緩解了監獄擁擠問題，並限制了吸毒者與販毒者之間的接觸，同時確保違法者能接受符合其需求的治療。
  • account for phr. v. 佔（比例），說明
    According to agency statistics, inmates imprisoned for drug use offenses accounted for 35.1 percent of Taiwan's prison population in 2022...
    根據該署統計，2022年因吸毒罪行入獄的受刑人佔台灣監獄總人口的35.1%...
  • statistics n. 統計數據
    According to agency statistics, inmates imprisoned for drug use offenses accounted for 35.1 percent of Taiwan's prison population in 2022...
    根據該署統計，2022年因吸毒罪行入獄的受刑人佔台灣監獄總人口的35.1%...
  • represent v. 代表，佔……比例
    As of the end of June this year, 14,521 inmates were serving sentences for drug related offenses, representing 24.8 percent of the total prison population of 58,455.
    截至今年6月底，有14,521名受刑人因毒品相關罪行正在服刑，佔總監獄人口58,455人的24.8%。
  • integrate v. 整合，結合
    Therefore, the agency said, it is continuing to promote a science-based treatment model for drug offenders, while also working with the Ministry of Health and Welfare on a program to integrate and improve the quality of drug addiction treatment services in correctional facilities.
    因此，該署表示，其正持續推動針對毒品犯的科學化治療模式，同時也與衛生福利部合作推動一項計劃，以整合並提升矯正機構中戒癮治療服務的品質。
  • findings n. 研究結果，調查結果
    It said the findings support its policy of treating eligible offenders in the community while reserving imprisonment for more serious cases.
    該署表示，這些研究結果支持了其政策：在社區中治療符合資格的犯罪者，同時將監禁保留給更嚴重的案件。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞當形容詞或作後置修飾
    The share of inmates serving sentences for drug-related offenses has fallen...
    句中的「serving sentences...」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「inmates（受刑人）」，相當於形容詞子句「who are serving sentences...」。這種結構在商業英文和新聞報導中非常常見，能讓句子更精簡。
  • 非限定性關係子句
    According to the agency, the government's third-phase New Generation Anti-Drug Strategy Action Plan, which runs from 2025 to 2028, emphasizes tiered diversion and diversified treatment.
    句中的「which runs from 2025 to 2028」被逗號包圍，是一個非限定性關係子句，用來補充說明前面的「New Generation Anti-Drug Strategy Action Plan（新世代反毒策略行動綱領）」的執行期間。去掉這個子句不影響主要句意。
  • 對等連接詞連接的多個受詞或動作
    The agency said the approach reduces unnecessary incarceration, eases prison overcrowding and limits contact between drug users and drug traffickers while ensuring offenders receive treatment suited to their needs.
    句中的「reduces」、「eases」和「limits」是由對等連接詞 and 連接的三個動詞，共同作 said 後面省略 that 的名詞子句之謂語。最後的「while ensuring...」則是用來表達「同時確保……」的附帶狀況。
