分析師警告：空軍可能因提前退休流失更多飛行員

Analyst warns Air Force may be losing more pilots to early retirement

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • personnel n. 人員、員工（多益常考集合名詞，通常作單數但視為複數或不可數）
    A military analyst said Friday, warning that the trend could worsen a shortage of experienced personnel.
    一名軍事分析師週五表示，並警告此趨勢可能會惡化資深人員短缺的問題。
  • budget v. / n. 編列預算、預算
    The budgeted headcount for volunteer military personnel... is set at 158,223 in 2027.
    志願役軍人的預算員額在2027年設定為158,223人。
  • increase v. / n. 增加、增長
    It marks the first increase in the number of volunteer service members since 2024...
    這標誌著自2024年以來志願役服務人員人數的首次增加...
  • attribute to phr. v. 歸因於、歸咎於（多益閱讀與聽力極高頻片語）
    ...which Chieh attributed to increases in duty and combat allowances that took effect in April 2025...
    ...揭仲將此歸因於2025年4月生效的值勤與戰鬥加給調高...
  • reduction n. 減少、降低
    ...as well as a reduction in nonessential duties.
    ...以及非必要勤務的減少。
  • reflect v. 反映、顯示
    ...Chieh said the sharp increase likely reflects a rise in premature retirements among pilots.
    ...揭仲表示，這項急劇增加可能反映出飛行員提前退休人數的上升。
  • decline v. / n. 下降、衰退
    ...have declined for four consecutive years, falling from NT$838.28 million in 2023 to NT$755.78 million in 2027...
    ...已連續四年下降，從2023年的8.3828億台幣降至2027年的7.5578億台幣...
  • intend v. 打算、旨在（常以 be intended to do 形式出現）
    The incentive is intended to retain majors, lieutenant colonels and colonels...
    這項獎勵金旨在留住少校、中校和上校...
  • retain v. 保留、留住（人才、員工）
    The incentive is intended to retain majors, lieutenant colonels and colonels who have completed their minimum required service period...
    這項獎勵金旨在留住已完成最低服役期限的少校、中校和上校...
  • departure n. 離開、辭職、班機出發
    ...adding that the decline suggests the departure of senior pilots in key leadership roles has not slowed.
    ...並補充說，金額下降表明擔任關鍵領導職務的資深飛行員離職情況並未放緩。
  • imbalance n. 不平衡、失衡
    "This could lead to an imbalance in the ratio between senior and junior pilots," Chieh said...
    揭仲說：「這可能導致資深與資淺飛行員比例失衡」...
  • concerning adj. 令人擔憂的
    The issue is particularly concerning given the Air Force's plan to add 107 pilot officers...
    鑑於空軍計劃增加107名飛行軍官，這個問題尤其令人擔憂。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作後置修飾 / 補充說明 (Present Participle / Past Participle)
    A military analyst said Friday, warning that the trend could worsen a shortage of experienced personnel.
    句尾的「warning...」是由現在分詞帶領的分詞構句，用來補充說明前面「分析師發言」同時所帶有的動作或結果，相當於「and warned that...」的簡化寫法，在新聞英文中極為常見。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    The incentive is intended to retain majors, lieutenant colonels and colonels who have completed their minimum required service period, Chieh said...
    句中的「who have completed...」是用來修飾前方先行詞（majors, lieutenant colonels and colonels）的限定性關係子句，用來精準界定是哪幾種階級的軍官。
  • 獨立分詞構句 / 介系詞慣用語 (Given that...) / 獨立結構
    The issue is particularly concerning given the Air Force's plan to add 107 pilot officers...
    「given」在此作為介系詞使用（源自過去分詞分化），意為「鑑於、考慮到」，後面直接接名詞片語。這是在多益測驗及商務寫作中非常實用的句型，用來引出評估某情況時的前提條件。
