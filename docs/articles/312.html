文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Present Participle）作後置修飾 / 形容詞用
    A subsequent discussion on the social media app Threads contained floods of accounts claiming to be “classmates” of Chiang’s son, posting lengthy statements in his defense.
    句中的 "claiming to be..." 與 "posting lengthy statements..." 均為現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 accounts，相當於關代子句 "that claimed to be... and posted..."，在新聞英語中非常常見，能讓句子更緊湊。
  • what引導的名詞子句（Noun Clause as Subject）
    What appeared to be a carefully orchestrated crisis-management effort was ultimately exposed by what appeared to be an instruction accidentally sent along with one of the comments...
    句首的 "What appeared to be..." 是由 what 引導的名詞子句，在句中擔任主語（主詞）。第二個 "what appeared to be..." 則是介系詞 by 的受詞。結構上相當於 "The thing that appeared to be..."。
  • 虛主詞 It 與被動語態的搭配
    Although those memes might be amusing, the technological cognitive warfare they conceal must be taken seriously.
    句尾的 "must be taken seriously" 使用了被動語態（情態助動詞 must + be + 過去分詞 taken），用來強調「科技認知作戰」這個受事者必須被嚴肅看待，符合新聞客觀且嚴謹的語氣。
  • 假設語氣（Subjunctive / Conditional Structure）
    If the low-cost, AI-controlled public opinion manipulation model proves effective in Taiwan, CCP cyber units could replicate the model and launch cognitive warfare operations on a much larger scale...
    此句為第一類條件句（First Conditional），用來表達對未來可能發生情況的假設。條件子句使用現在式 (proves effective)，主要子句則使用助動詞過去式加原形動詞 (could replicate / launch)，表達「如果……，將能夠……」的假設推論。
