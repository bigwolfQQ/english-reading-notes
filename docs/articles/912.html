社論：央行逆勢而行

EDITORIAL: Central bank bucks the trend

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • interest rate n. 利率
    The central bank on Thursday kept interest rates unchanged for a 10th consecutive quarter...
    中央銀行週四宣佈維持利率不變，連續第十季未作調整……
  • forecast n. / v. 預測，預報
    The bank also raised its GDP growth forecast for this year to 11.48 percent...
    央行並將今年經濟成長率（GDP）預測值由上調至百分之十一點四八……
  • inflation n. 通貨膨脹
    ...and revised its full-year inflation forecasts upward to 2.03 percent for the consumer price index (CPI)...
    ……同時將全年通膨預測值向上修正，消費者物價指數（CPI）調高至百分之二點零三……
  • hike n. / v. 調高，增加（尤指價格、費率等）
    Last week’s decision was not unanimous, with two board members voting for a rate hike.
    上週的決議並未達成一致，有兩位理事投票贊成升息。
  • stance n. 立場，態度
    ...Governor Yang Chin-long (楊金龍) reiterated that the bank had adopted a more hawkish stance since March 2024...
    ……總裁楊金龍重申，央行自二○二四年三月以來採取了較為鷹派的立場……
  • domestic consumption n. 國內消費
    ...while domestic consumption and wage growth remain moderate.
    ……而國內消費與薪資成長則維持溫和。
  • mortgage n. 房貸，抵押貸款
    A rate hike would increase mortgage burdens for first-time homebuyers and harm traditional industries and SMEs...
    升息會增加首購族的房貸負擔，並傷害傳產與中小企業……
  • outlook n. 前景，展望
    However, economists at DBS Bank Ltd and Capital Economics Ltd said that the central bank’s inflation outlook seemed a bit sanguine.
    然而，星展銀行與凱投宏觀的經濟學家表示，央行對通膨前景的看法似乎有些過於樂觀。
  • allocation n. 配置，分配
    This uneven capital allocation is a structural issue the central bank cannot solve through monetary policy alone.
    這種不均衡的資本分配，是央行無法僅透過貨幣政策解決的結構性問題。
  • coordination n. 協調，配合
    Yang called for interagency coordination to address this problem.
    楊金龍呼籲進行跨部會協調以解決此一問題。
  • ample adj. 充裕的，豐富的
    As funding demand from tech companies and stock market transactions has risen, even though liquidity in the overall banking system remains ample...
    隨著科技公司與股市交易的資金需求增加，儘管整體銀行體系的流動性依舊充裕……

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作附帶狀況或結果（分詞構句）
    The central bank on Thursday kept interest rates unchanged for a 10th consecutive quarter, while further easing housing credit controls by raising the loan-to-value ratio cap on loans for a second home to 70 percent from 60 percent.
    句中的 "while further easing..." 是一種分詞構句的變體（保留了連接詞 while），用來表達主句動作發生時的同時伴隨狀況，翻譯為「同時進一步鬆綁……」。
  • 過去分詞作形容詞用（修飾名詞）
    The bank also raised its GDP growth forecast for this year to 11.48 percent, up from 9.45 percent estimated in June...
    句中的 "estimated in June" 是過去分詞片語，置於名詞片語後面用來修飾 9.45 percent（即「在六月被預估的」9.45%），省略了 "which was"。
  • 關係子句（形容詞子句）修飾先行詞
    The move surprised market observers who had expected the bank to follow the global tightening trend after the US Federal Reserve raised rates by 0.25 percentage points last week.
    句中的 "who had expected the bank to follow..." 是由關係代名詞 who 引導的限定用關係子句，用來修飾前面的先行詞 "market observers"（市場觀察家），補充說明這些觀察家先前抱持的預期。
  • 名詞子句作受詞
    Before the board meeting, it faced considerable pressure over whether to raise rates, as CPI had remained above its 2 percent alert threshold for four consecutive months.
    句中的 "whether to raise rates" 是由連接詞 whether 引導的名詞片語（源自名詞子句簡化），在介系詞 over 後面作為其受詞，用來具體說明面臨壓力的內容為何。
