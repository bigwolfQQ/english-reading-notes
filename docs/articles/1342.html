    ...accelerate artificial intelligence (AI) adoption in the coming years amid a growing consensus in the sector over the value of the technology...
    ...在產界對該技術價值的共識日益增加的情況下，加速導入人工智慧（AI）...
  • consensus n. 共識
    ...amid a growing consensus in the sector over the value of the technology, chairman Allen Lien said...

  • subsidiary n. 子公司
    Acer Medical, a subsidiary of Acer Inc, develops AI-powered medical software and platforms...
    宏碁智醫是宏碁的子公司，專門開發AI醫療軟體及平台...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Another product, VeriOsteo OP, launched in 2023, is osteoporosis screening software that can analyze chest X-rays taken for other purposes to identify previously undetected osteoporosis risks, Lien said.
    句中的「taken for other purposes」是由過去分詞開頭的片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「chest X-rays（胸部X光片）」，相當於關代子句「which were taken for other purposes」，在新聞英語中為了簡潔常省略關代與be動詞。
  • 現在分詞作附帶狀況/結果 (Present Participle indicating Result or Simultaneous Action)
    Initially focusing on post-stroke care, the system allows AI to flag issues for nurses to review before sending reminders to users, he said.
    句首的「Initially focusing on...」是分詞構句，表示主詞「the system」在當下的狀態或附帶情況，表示該系統一開始聚焦於中風後照護。
  • 限定性關係代名詞子句 (Restrictive Relative Clause)
    Acer Medical Inc expects Taiwan’s healthcare providers to accelerate artificial intelligence (AI) adoption in the coming years amid a growing consensus in the sector over the value of the technology...
    雖然此句主體沒有使用關代，但在後文如「...healthcare institutions using its products and services...」中，利用「using」開頭的修飾語（源自分詞或省略關代）來限定前面的名詞，是商業與新聞英文中常見用來精簡句構的高頻寫法。
  • 對等連接詞連接平行結構 (Parallel Structure with Coordinating Conjunctions)
    As LLMs advance, Acer Medical is also expanding into data processing, system integration and model optimization, while using Acer Group’s (宏碁集團) hardware resources to provide on-premises computing capacity tailored to hospitals’ needs, Lien said.
    句中的「data processing, system integration and model optimization」使用了 A, B and C 的平行結構，連接三個同性質的名詞詞組，讓冗長的商業句子保持清晰與閱讀流暢度。
