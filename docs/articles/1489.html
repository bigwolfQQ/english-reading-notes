高雄夫妻因2歲女兒死亡涉嫌虐童遭羈押

Kaohsiung couple detained for abuse after 2-year-old daughter's death

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • suspect n. / v. 嫌疑犯 / 懷疑，涉嫌
    A Kaohsiung court on Saturday approved a request by prosecutors to detain a couple on suspicion of fatally abusing their 2-year-old daughter.
    高雄地方法院於週六批准了檢察官的聲請，裁定羈押涉嫌虐死2歲女兒的一對夫妻。
  • respond v. 回應，反應，出動（救援）
    The Kaohsiung City Fire Department said it responded to a call for an ambulance at the family's Sanmin District home at 8:28 p.m. Thursday...
    高雄市政府消防局表示，他們於週四晚間8時28分接獲通報，前往該家庭位於三民區的住所派遣救護車...
  • investigate v. 調查，審查
    ...and the case was transferred to prosecutors to investigate the circumstances.
    ...且全案已移送檢察官以調查案發情況。
  • statement n. 聲明，陳述
    In a statement on Sunday, the Kaohsiung District Prosecutors' office said it conducted an examination and autopsy on the child Friday...
    高雄地檢署於週日發布聲明指出，檢方已於週五對孩童進行相驗與解剖...
  • conduct v. 執行，實施，舉辦
    ...said it conducted an examination and autopsy on the child Friday to establish whether a crime had been committed.
    ...指出檢方已於週五對孩童進行相驗與解剖，以釐清是否涉及刑事犯罪。
  • establish v. 確定，確立，查明
    ...to establish whether a crime had been committed.
    ...以釐清是否涉及刑事犯罪。
  • in addition to prep. phr. 除了…之外（還）
    In addition to the 2-year-old's suspicious death, the family's 1-year-old boy was also found to have likely suffered abuse, prosecutors said.
    檢察官表示，除了2歲女童的可疑死因外，該家庭1歲的男童也被發現可能遭受虐待。
  • determine v. 決定，查明，判定
    ...and determined that both had likely committed the crimes of causing intentional harm to a child...
    ...並認定兩人皆涉嫌對兒童故意傷害...
  • request n. / v. 請求，要求
    A request to hold both Hsiung and Deng in incommunicado detention was approved by the Kaohsiung District Court on Saturday...
    高雄地方法院於週六批准了對熊、鄧二人實施禁見羈押的聲請...
  • remain v. 保持，維持（於某狀態）
    As of Sunday, the family's 1-year-old son remained in hospital under observation...
    截至週日，該家庭1歲的兒子仍留院觀察...

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    A Kaohsiung court on Saturday approved a request by prosecutors to detain a couple on suspicion of fatally abusing their 2-year-old daughter.
    句中的 "fatally abusing..." 是分詞片語，用來修飾前面的動名词短語或補充說明原因，在此表達「因涉嫌致死虐待其2歲女兒」。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The child, a 2-year-old girl, was later pronounced dead, and the case was transferred to prosecutors to investigate the circumstances.
    句中使用了 "was pronounced" 與 "was transferred" 兩個被動語態結構（be動詞 + 過去分詞），強調動作的承受者（女童、案件），而非動作的執行者。
  • 連接詞 while 引導對比子句 (While indicating contrast)
    As of Sunday, the family's 1-year-old son remained in hospital under observation, while the eldest sister, who is under age 7, was placed in protective care, prosecutors said.
    這裡的 "while" 用於連接兩個同時發生但形成對比的情況，意指「然而」或「與此同時」，連接弟弟留院觀察與姐姐被安置保護的兩種不同狀況。
