節奏明快的舞蹈在象牙海岸街頭迅速竄紅

Fast-paced dance takes off on streets in Ivory Coast

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • gather v. 聚集、集合
    On the streets of a working-class Abidjan neighborhood, hundreds of young people gather every week after class to dance the biama...
    在阿比讓一個工人階級社區的街道上，數百名年輕人在放學後每週聚會一次，跳起 biama...
  • population n. 人口、居民人數
    The craze has gripped Ivory Coast — where nearly two-thirds of the population is aged under 25 — since it evolved from the country’s popular coupe-decale dance music a few years ago.
    這種狂熱已經席捲了象牙海岸——該國近三分之二的人口年齡在 25 歲以下——自從它幾年前從該國流行的 coupe-decale 舞曲演變而來以來。
  • organize v. 組織、籌辦
    In Yopougon, the district where it all began, so-called biamasseurs organize the outdoor events where one DJ after another takes to the stage...
    在一切開始的地區約波貢（Yopougon），被稱為 biamasseurs 的組織者舉辦戶外活動，一個接一個的 DJ 登上舞台...
  • cost v. 花費（金錢）
    Dancers take part for free, while it costs between 1,000 and 2,000 CFA francs (between US$1.70 and US$3.50) to watch.
    舞者免費參加，而觀看則需要支付 1,000 到 2,000 非洲金融共同體法郎（約合 1.70 至 3.50 美元）。
  • create v. 創造、創作
    Young people “let their imagination run free to create their moves, dance with their eyes, pull faces,” he said.
    他說，年輕人「任由他們的想像力自由馳騁來創造他們的舞步，用眼睛跳舞，做鬼臉」。
  • cost of living n. phr. 生活成本、生活費
    With the tips they earn dancing in the evening they buy clothes and school supplies in a city with a cost of living that is one of the highest in Africa.
    他們用晚上跳舞賺取的小費購買衣服和學習用品，而在這個城市，生活成本是非洲最高的地方之一。
  • economic adj. 經濟的
    Since then, the dance has taken Abidjan, the country’s economic capital, and other cities by storm...
    從那時起，這種舞蹈以迅雷不及掩耳之勢席捲了該國的經濟首都阿比讓和其他城市...
  • risk n. 風險、危險
    ...with weekly events undeterred by the risk of the late-night noise disturbing nearby residents, who sometimes do complain.
    ...每週的活動絲毫沒有受到深夜噪音干擾附近居民（他們有時確實會抱怨）風險的阻撓。
  • popularity n. 普及、流行、聲望
    They serve as a springboard for beginners and a showcase for the more accomplished whose rising fame and popularity is amplified by social media.
    它們成為初學者的跳板，也是更有成就者的展示舞台，他們日漸上升的名氣和人氣透過社群媒體被放大。
  • price n. 價格、代價
    ...before going on stage in front of thousands at a festival in the fashionable neighborhood of Cocody, where entry prices were considerably higher at between 10,000 and 15,000 CFA francs.
    ...在科科迪時尚社區的一個節日上登台表演之前，當時台下有數千名觀眾，門票價格要高得多，在 10,000 到 15,000 非洲金融共同體法郎之間。
  • market n. 市場、商機
    Arranger Elie-Michel Assie Diou sees “a market” in the biama movement’s success.
    編曲者埃利-米歇爾·阿西·迪烏（Elie-Michel Assie Diou）在 biama 運動的成功中看到了「一個市場」。

文法重點

  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    In Yopougon, the district where it all began, so-called biamasseurs organize the outdoor events where one DJ after another takes to the stage...
    句中的 "where it all began" 以及後面的 "where one DJ after another takes to the stage" 皆為關係副詞 where 引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的地方先行詞 "the district" 與 "the outdoor events"，提供更詳細的背景資訊。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Biama was created by accident in 2021 in a video gaming room when a jubilant young man jumped up and started improvising dance moves...
    句型使用 "was created"（be 動詞 + 過去分詞），強調動作的承受者 Biama（被創造），而由誰創造則用 "by accident" 或省略，這是新聞英語中常用來客觀陳述事件發生經過的句構。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Today, Karl Guei, whose nickname is “Kalomaman,” has 2 million TikTok subscribers, according to culture journalist Toussaint Konan.
    句尾的 "according to culture journalist Toussaint Konan"（根據文化記者圖桑·Konan）是一個介系詞片語，而文章中也常使用現在分詞或過去分詞片語來修飾名詞（例如被動分詞形容詞），用以精鍊句型，省去冗長的子句。
