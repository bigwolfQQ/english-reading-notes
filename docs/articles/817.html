文法重點

  • 現在分詞作附帶狀況或結果（分詞構句）
    Taiwanese carrier China Airlines (CAL) will launch a codeshare partnership with Britain's Virgin Atlantic Airways, the company announced Wednesday at an event in Amsterdam marking the 15th anniversary of its accession to the SkyTeam airline alliance.
    句尾的 "marking the 15th anniversary..." 是現在分詞片語作後位修飾，用來補充說明前面的 event（活動），表達該活動「標誌著」天合聯盟成立 15 週年。這類結構在新聞英語中非常常見，能簡潔地補充背景資訊。
  • 獨立分詞構句（Given 引導的條件/假設）
    airlines cannot operate alone given limited fleet resources and capacity constraints and must rely on alliances...
    句中的 "given..." 相當於 "considering..." 或 "if we consider..."（鑑於／考量到），在此作為介系詞使用，用來引出航空公司無法單獨營運的原因（即機隊資源與運力限制）。
  • 現在分詞片語表示補充說明
    Chen said 15 years of membership has enabled CAL to offer passengers seamless transfer services through codeshare and interline agreements, including through check-in, baggage transfers, mileage accrual, priority boarding and access to airport lounges worldwide.
  • 現在分詞表示原因或伴隨狀況
    He said CAL aims to further expand its global presence through SkyTeam and attract more transit passengers via Taiwan by capitalizing on the alliance's extensive worldwide network.
  • 分詞構句作後位修飾
    CAL currently operates nearly 30 weekly nonstop flights to six European destinations -- Amsterdam, Frankfurt, London, Rome, Prague and Vienna -- making it Taiwan's largest carrier by both the number of direct European destinations served and flight frequency.
