文法重點

  • 分詞片語作為後置定語（修飾名詞）
    Taiwan is expanding semiconductor research infrastructure and developing homegrown chip-design software under a government initiative aimed at maintaining the nation's technological edge, the Executive Yuan said yesterday.
    句中的「aimed at maintaining...」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「a government initiative」。完整結構是「...initiative (which is) aimed at...」，省略了「which is」後直接用過去分詞片語來表達「旨在......的（計畫）」，是新聞英文中非常常見且簡潔的句構。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Two projects under the Taiwan Chip-based Industrial Innovation Program are being carried out from 2024 to 2028, focusing on research infrastructure and chip-design software, said Peter Hong...
    句型「are being carried out」使用了現在進行式的被動語態（be + being + p.p.）。用來強調這兩項計畫在 2024 到 2028 年這段期間「正在被執行中」，突顯政府政策持續進行的狀態。
  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    Under the hardware project, seven university semiconductor institutes would receive subsidies for equipment that would be linked through a single service platform, Hong said.
    句中的「that would be linked through a single service platform」是由關係代名詞 that 引導的限定性關係子句，用來修飾前面的先行詞 equipment（設備）。這有助於精確補充說明這些設備將會具備什麼樣的串聯功能。
  • 對比連接詞 while 的用法
    Universities would develop key EDA tools for advanced packaging, while ITRI would build an intelligent EDA cloud platform and integrate tools developed by industry and research institutions into a complete chip-design toolchain, Hong said.
    在此句中，「while」用來連接兩個子句，表示「而」或「然而」的對比語氣，同時呈現大學與工研院（ITRI）在開發軟體計畫上各自負責的不同任務分工。
