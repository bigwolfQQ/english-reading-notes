文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Present Participle）作後置修飾語
    A delegation of opposition Kuomintang (KMT) lawmakers visiting the United States said Monday that it had met with San Francisco city officials...
    句中的 "visiting the United States" 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "A delegation of opposition Kuomintang (KMT) lawmakers"，相當於形容詞子句 "who are visiting the United States"（或是過去進行式的簡化），在新聞英文中非常常見，用以精簡語句。
  • 過去分詞（Past Participle）作後置修飾語
    In a statement issued Monday, the delegation said it met with San Francisco officials...
    句中的 "issued Monday" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "statement"。因為 statement（聲明）是被「發布」的，所以使用過去分詞表示被動關係，還原完整子句為 "which was issued Monday"。
  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況（Present Participle as Circumstance）
    The delegation said San Francisco's open regulatory environment and innovation-friendly policies have helped make the city a global technology hub, adding that its experience could provide a useful reference for Taiwan...
    逗號後面的 "adding that..." 是一個現在分詞構句，主詞與前句主詞一致（指代 delegation），用來補充說明前面主句所發生的事，意指「並補充說...」。這在新聞報導中常被用來延續引述或補充相關發言。
  • 疑問詞引導的名詞子句（Noun Clause as Object）
    They said the trip had provided firsthand insight into how advanced technologies are used in urban governance...
    片語 "into" 後面的 "how advanced technologies are used in urban governance" 是一個由疑問詞 "how" 引導的名詞子句，在句中擔任介係詞 "into" 的受詞。子句內部維持「主詞 + 動詞」的正常語序（advanced technologies are used）。
