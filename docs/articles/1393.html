教育部公布教師申訴規定修正案

Ministry promulgates teacher complaint rule changes

單字解析

  • promulgate v. 公布，頒布（法規等）
    The Ministry of Education yesterday promulgated amendments to regulations governing the handling of complaints against teachers...
    教育部昨天公布了教師申訴處理相關辦法的修正案...
  • amendment n. 修正案，修改
    The amendments, which are expected to take effect tomorrow, are centered on simplifying the complaints process...
    這些修正案預計明天生效，其核心在於簡化申訴流程...
  • take effect phr. 生效，實施
    The amendments, which are expected to take effect tomorrow, are centered on simplifying the complaints process...
    這些修正案預計明天生效，其核心在於簡化申訴流程...
  • stipulate v. 規定，約定
    The new rules stipulate that schools must close the case without convening a campus incident resolution council...
    新規則規定，學校必須結案，無需召開校園事件處理會議...
  • appeal v. / n. 上訴，申訴
    The party filing the report could appeal the school’s decision to a higher educational authority...
    提出檢舉的一方可以向更高層級的教育主管機關對學校的決定提出申訴...
  • authority n. 官方，主管機關，權力
    The party filing the report could appeal the school’s decision to a higher educational authority...
    提出檢舉的一方可以向更高層級的教育主管機關對學校的決定提出申訴...
  • assess v. 評估，評價
    The amended regulations would curb the role of independent evaluators, who would be called upon to investigate a teacher’s performance in ordinary performance assessment and disciplinary cases...
    修訂後的法規將限制獨立評鑑人員的角色，只有在學校缺乏工作人員或在特殊情況下，才會請他們在一般績效評估和懲戒案件中調查教師的表現...
  • cooperate v. 合作，配合
    Parents who fail to cooperate with student guidance could be referred to family education centers...
    未能配合學生輔導的家長可能會被轉介至家庭教育中心...
  • compensate v. 補償，賠償
    Compensation should be provided to educators for missing out on promotion opportunities and lost income during suspension over unfounded accusations...
    對於因無端指控而停職期間錯失升遷機會及損失收入的教育人員，應給予補償...
  • suspend v. 暫停，停職，懸掛

文法重點

  • 非限定關係子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    The amendments, which are expected to take effect tomorrow, are centered on simplifying the complaints process...
    句中的「which are expected to take effect tomorrow」為非限定關係子句，用逗號隔開，用來補充說明前面的主詞「The amendments」（這些修正案），拿掉這個子句不影響主要句意。
  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The party filing the report could appeal the school’s decision to a higher educational authority, which would also be authorized to decline taking up the case should it find the complaint to concern conduct clearly exempt from punishment, it said.
    句中的「filing the report」是現在分詞片語修飾「The party」；而「exempt from punishment」則是過去分詞片語作為後置修飾，用來修飾「conduct」（行為），意思是「免受處罰的」。
  • 省略 if 的倒裝假設語氣 (Inverted Conditional Clause)
    ...which would also be authorized to decline taking up the case should it find the complaint to concern conduct clearly exempt from punishment...
    句中的「should it find...」原本是條件句「if it should find...」的省略 if 倒裝句型。在正式書體中，若假設語氣包含 should、were、had 等助動詞，常可省略 if 並將助動詞移至主詞前方。
