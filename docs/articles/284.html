印尼搜救火山附近海域失蹤的8人

Indonesia searches for 8 missing at sea near volcano

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • official n. 官員、高級職員
    Indonesian rescuers searched for eight people who went missing at sea while heading to a volcanic island to report on an eruption there, officials said yesterday.
    印尼官員昨天表示，印尼搜救人員正在尋找在前往一座火山島報導當地噴發情況時，在海上失蹤的八人。
  • report on phr. 報導、匯報
    Indonesian rescuers searched for eight people who went missing at sea while heading to a volcanic island to report on an eruption there, officials said yesterday.
    印尼官員昨天表示，印尼搜救人員正在尋找在前往一座火山島報導當地噴發情況時，在海上失蹤的八人。
  • statement n. 聲明、陳述
    Anak Krakatau initially erupted for about 25 hours over the weekend, and it has since continued to erupt intermittently with explosions ejecting lava, ash and incandescent volcanic material from the crater, the Indonesian Geology Agency said in a statement.
    印尼地質局在一份聲明中表示，喀拉喀托之子火山週末首先噴發了大約25小時，此後斷斷續續持續噴發，爆炸從火山口噴出熔岩、火山灰和白熱的火山物質。
  • crew n. 全體船員、機組人員、工作人員
    A speedboat carrying five photojournalists, two crew members and a route guide lost contact after leaving Carita, a coastal town in Banten Province, for the volcanic island, Banten Province Search and Rescue Office Chief Al Amrad said.
    萬丹省搜救局長阿爾·阿姆拉德表示，一艘載有五名攝影記者、兩名船員和一名領航員的快艇，在離開萬丹省沿海城鎮卡里塔前往該火山島後失聯。
  • relatives n. 親屬、親戚
    He said the last message from the boat was received by relatives of a journalist on board at 6:13pm on Monday, saying the group had arrived in the waters around Anak Krakatau.
    他說，船上最後一條訊息是週一晚上6點13分由船上一名記者的親屬接收到的，內容是該小組已抵達喀拉喀托之子火山周圍水域。
  • agency n. 局、機構、經銷處
    The five Indonesian journalists work for Indonesia’s state news agency Antara, Turkey’s Anadolu Agency, Indonesia’s Merdeka and iNews, while the fifth is a freelance photographer.
    這五名印尼記者分別任職於印尼國家通訊社安塔拉、土耳其安納杜魯新聞社、印尼的 Merdeka 和 iNews，第五位則是一名自由攝影師。
  • operation n. 行動、運作、業務
    Five vessels, including two navy ships, were deployed in the search operation, with personnel from the National Search and Rescue Agency, the military and the police involved.
    包括兩艘海軍艦艇在內的五艘船隻被部署到搜救行動中，國家搜救局、軍方和警察的人員均參與其中。
  • probability n. 可能性、機率
    The Geology Agency cautioned that volcanic activity remains elevated and the probability of further eruptions is still high.
    地質局警告說，火山活動仍然升高，再次噴發的可能性仍然很高。
  • disrupt v. 使中斷、打亂、擾亂
    The eruption caused airport closures on Sunday and Monday that disrupted 2,961 flights, including 622 international services, and affected about 341,000 passengers.
    這次噴發導致機場在週日和週一關閉，打亂了2,961個航班，並影響了約341,000名旅客。
  • authorize v. 授權、批准
    The government authorized schools in areas affected by volcanic ash to shift temporarily to online learning to reduce students’ exposure to ash.
    政府授權受火山灰影響地區的學校暫時轉為線上教學，以減少學生接觸火山灰。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後置修飾 (Present/Past Participle as Post-modifier)
    Indonesian rescuers searched for eight people who went missing at sea while heading to a volcanic island to report on an eruption there...
    句中的「while heading to...」是由連接詞引導的分詞片語（原句為 while they were heading to），省略了主詞與be動詞，直接用現在分詞 heading 表主動進行的動作，用來修飾前方主詞或補充說明背景。
  • 過去分詞作後置修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Adjective)
    Videos and photos released by the agency showed rescuers searching the waters.
    句中的「released by the agency」是過去分詞片語，相當於形容詞子句「which were released by the agency」，放在被修飾的名詞 Videos and photos 後方，用來表達被動的修飾關係（被該機構發布的）。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Nominative Absolute Construction)
    Five vessels, including two navy ships, were deployed in the search operation, with personnel from the National Search and Rescue Agency, the military and the police involved.
    句尾的「with personnel... involved」為「with + 名詞 + 過去分詞」的附帶狀況構句，用來補充說明在搜救行動中同時有哪些人員參與，屬於多益閱讀常考的進階文法結構。
