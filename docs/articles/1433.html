美國、中國與歐盟：規範人工智慧的三種路徑

US, China, EU: three paths to regulating AI

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • govern v. 管理、治理、控制
    The US, China and the EU are taking different paths to govern artificial intelligence...
    美國、中國與歐盟正採取不同的路徑來治理人工智慧……
  • pledge n. / v. 保證、承諾
    ...President Donald Trump has built his AI policy almost entirely from voluntary industry pledges and executive orders...
    ……總統唐納·川普幾乎完全憑藉著自願性業界承諾與行政命令來建立其 AI 政策……
  • scrap v. 廢棄、取消
    On his first day back in office, Trump scrapped his predecessor Joe Biden’s 2023 order...
    在他重返白宮的第一天，川普便廢除了其前任喬·拜登於 2023 年簽署的行政命令……
  • unveil v. 公布、推出、揭幕
    The White House in July last year unveiled an “AI Action Plan”...
    白宮在去年 7 月公布了一份「AI 行動計畫」……
  • penalty n. 處罰、罰款
    The pact carries no penalties for companies that fall short...
    對於未達標的公司，該協議並未包含任何罰則……
  • consent n. 同意、許可
    ...banning the creation of digital clones of real people without their consent.
    ……並禁止在未經同意的情況下創建真實人物的數位分身。
  • comprehensive adj. 全面的、綜合的
    The European Union rushed to adopt its AI Act in 2024, which Brussels touts as the world’s most comprehensive rules on the technology.
    歐盟於 2024 年匆忙通過了《人工智慧法》，布魯塞爾吹捧該法為全球針對該技術最全面的法規。
  • stifle v. 扼殺、壓制
    But European companies, fearing the rules would stifle innovation as they race to catch up with US and Chinese rivals...
    但歐洲企業擔憂，在競相追趕美國與中國競爭對手的同時，這些法規會扼殺創新……
  • rival n. 競爭對手
    ...as they race to catch up with US and Chinese rivals, later won a delay on rules covering high-risk systems.
    ……在競相追趕美國與中國競爭對手的同時，隨後爭取到了將涵蓋高風險系統的規則予以延後實施的機會。
  • dominance n. 優勢、主導地位
    European firms have been more wary of slowing down, fearing it could be an attempt by US giants to lock in their dominance.
    歐洲企業一直對放慢速度抱持較為戒慎的態度，擔憂這可能是美國巨頭試圖鞏固其主導地位的手段。
  • scrutiny n. 詳細審查、監視
    ...the first AI chatbot added to the EU’s list of digital services subject to greater legal scrutiny under its online content rules.
    ……成為第一個被納入歐盟數位服務清單的 AI 聊天機器人，根據其線上內容規則，該聊天機器人將受到更嚴格的法律審查。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附帶狀況/結果 (Present Participle Phrase)
    The US, China and the EU are taking different paths to govern artificial intelligence, as calls grow worldwide to rein in its most powerful systems.
    句尾的 “as calls grow worldwide...” 引導原因子句，說明各國採取不同路徑的背景。此外，文章中常見如 “fearing the rules would stifle innovation...” 等分詞片語，用來簡化副詞子句，表達「由於擔心……」的心理狀態。
  • 分詞後置修飾名詞 (Past Participle as Post-modifier)
    An accompanying order titled “Preventing Woke AI in the Federal Government” requires federal agencies to buy only models deemed ideologically neutral.
  • 形容詞片語作後置修飾 (Adjective Phrase as Post-modifier)
    This year, the release of AI models capable of powerful cyberattacks shifted the focus to security.
  • 被動語態與分詞構句的結合
    ...banning the creation of digital clones of real people without their consent.
