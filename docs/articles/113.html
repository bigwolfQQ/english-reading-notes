台灣與拿坡里大學簽署協議

Taiwan, Naples university ink deal

單字解析

  • sign an agreement phr. 簽署協議
    The National Central Library on Friday signed an agreement with the University of Naples “L’Orientale,” increasing its network of Taiwan Research Centers for Chinese Studies in Italy to three.
    國家圖書館於周五與拿坡里東方大學簽署了一項協議，使其在義大利的「漢學研究中心臺灣資源中心」網路擴增至三個。
  • network n. 網路、網脈、體系
    The National Central Library on Friday signed an agreement with the University of Naples “L’Orientale,” increasing its network of Taiwan Research Centers for Chinese Studies in Italy to three.
    國家圖書館於周五與拿坡里東方大學簽署了一項協議，使其在義大利的「漢學研究中心臺灣資源中心」網路擴增至三個。
  • institution n. 機構、協會
    Italian Sinology is internationally renowned, with the three universities regarded as the country’s leading institutions for Chinese studies, National Central Library Acting Director-General Weng Chih-tsung (翁誌聰) said.
    國家圖書館代理館長翁誌聰表示，義大利的漢學研究在國際間享譽盛名，這三所大學被視為該國中文研究的領導機構。
  • result n. 結果、成果
    The universities in Venice and Rome became the library’s Taiwan Resource Center for Chinese Studies partners in 2018, and the addition of the Naples university on Friday is a result of eight years of efforts, Weng said.
    翁誌聰表示，威尼斯與羅馬的大學於2018年成為該館「漢學研究中心臺灣資源中心」的合作夥伴，而周五拿坡里大學的加入則是八年努力的成果。
  • ceremony n. 典禮、儀式
    The signing ceremony took place at Palazzo Corigliano’s Cabinet of Mirrors and the agreement was signed by Weng and the university’s rector, Roberto Tottoli.
    簽約儀式在科里格里亞諾宮的鏡廳舉行，協議由翁誌聰與該校校長羅伯托・托托利共同簽署。
  • worldwide adv. / adj. 全球性地、世界各地
    The Naples university has become the library’s 58th partner worldwide, Weng said.
    翁誌聰表示，拿坡里大學已成為該館在全球第58個合作夥伴。
  • database n. 資料庫
    The library provided the university with 248 books on Chinese and Taiwan studies, and would add more every year, he said, adding that it would also provide digital collections and electronic databases.
    他說，圖書館向該大學提供了248本關於中國與台灣研究的書籍，並且每年都會增添新書，同時也將提供數位館藏與電子資料庫。
  • commitment n. 承諾、奉獻
    The gifts symbolize Taiwan’s and Italy’s shared commitment to preserving knowledge and promoting cultural exchange, the library said.
    圖書館表示，這些贈禮象徵著台灣與義大利對於保存知識及促進文化交流的共同承諾。
  • partnership n. 夥伴關係、合夥關係
    We hope the new Italian partnership would raise the visibility of Taiwan’s academic resources in Europe, Weng said.
    翁誌聰表示，我們希望這項新的義大利夥伴關係能提升台灣學術資源在歐洲的能見度。
  • visibility n. 能見度、明顯性
    We hope the new Italian partnership would raise the visibility of Taiwan’s academic resources in Europe, Weng said.
    翁誌聰表示，我們希望這項新的義大利夥伴關係能提升台灣學術資源在歐洲的能見度。

文法重點

  • 獨立分詞構句 (Absolute Phrase)
    Italian Sinology is internationally renowned, with the three universities regarded as the country’s leading institutions for Chinese studies...
    「with + 名詞 + 過去分詞 (regarded)」形成獨立分詞構句，用來補充說明主句的背景或附帶狀況，表示「在三所大學被視為……的情況下」。
  • 現在分詞作補充說明 (Present Participle as a Clause Reducer)
    The National Central Library on Friday signed an agreement with the University of Naples “L’Orientale,” increasing its network of Taiwan Research Centers for Chinese Studies in Italy to three.
    句尾的「increasing...」是由對等子句簡化而來的分詞構句，表示前面簽署協議這個動作所帶來的自然結果（「進而增加至三個」）。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The signing ceremony took place at Palazzo Corigliano’s Cabinet of Mirrors and the agreement was signed by Weng and the university’s rector, Roberto Tottoli.
    句子的後半部使用了被動語態「was signed by」，強調動作的承受者（the agreement 協議），並用「by」引出動作的執行者。
  • 動名詞片語作受詞 (Gerund Phrase as Object)
    The gifts symbolize Taiwan’s and Italy’s shared commitment to preserving knowledge and promoting cultural exchange, the library said.
    介系詞「to」後面接動名詞「preserving」與「promoting」，構成動名詞片語，作為名詞「commitment」的受詞，表達對……的承諾。
