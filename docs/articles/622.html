雷恩·哈斯看台灣：台灣的內外矛盾

Ryan Hass On Taiwan: Taiwan’s Inside-Out Paradox

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • budget n. 預算
    Within Taiwan, there are ongoing battles between the legislative and executive branches over the budget...
    在台灣內部，立法部門與行政部門之間正就預算問題進行持續的鬥爭...
  • resources n. 資源
    ...and between cities and the central government over resources.
    ...以及地方政府與中央政權之間為了資源分配而爭執不下。
  • economy n. 經濟體；經濟
    It is one of the world’s strongest-performing advanced economies...
    它是全球表現最強勁的先進經濟體之一...
  • supply chain n. 供應鏈
    ...and occupies a central position in critical technology supply chains.
    ...並在關鍵技術供應鏈中佔據核心地位。
  • fiscal adj. 財政的；會計的
    This is a powerful contrast with virtually every other major economy in the world today, all of which face fiscal constraints.
    這與當今世界上幾乎所有面臨財政約束的其他主要經濟體形成了強烈的對比。
  • constraint n. 限制；約束
    ...all of which face fiscal constraints.
    ...所有這些都面臨財政約束。
  • enterprise n. 企業；公司
    The benefits of the AI boom are not being felt evenly across society, particularly among small and medium-sized enterprises...
    人工智慧熱潮帶來的紅利並未在全社會均勻感受到，特別是在中小企業...
  • employee n. 員工
    ...and the employees who power them.
    ...以及推動它們的員工。
  • progress n. 進展；進步
    Partisan gridlock continues to constrain progress in strengthening Taiwan’s self-defense.
    黨派僵局持續制約著台灣加強自我防禦的進展。
  • participate v. 參與
    Multipolarity could create more opportunities for Taiwan to participate in different issue-based coalitions...
    多極化可能會為台灣創造更多機會參與不同的議題導向聯盟...
  • afford v. 負擔得起
    As AI-enabled weapons systems become more affordable and accessible...
    隨著具備AI功能的武器系統變得更加負擔得起且易於取得...
  • prosperous adj. 繁榮的
    It is how to make Taiwan more secure and prosperous in a transforming world.
    而是如何在轉變中的世界讓台灣更安全、更繁榮。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表同時發生的動作或附帶狀況）
    Jockeying is underway for local elections this November and national elections in 2028.
    句中的 jockeying 為現在分詞片語作為主詞補語或進行式結構，用來描述地方選舉與大選的卡位競爭正在進行中。此類結構在新聞英文中極為常見，用以精煉地表達動態。另外如「As AI diffuses and gains adoption...」等處也常運用副詞子句簡化或分詞來串連動作。
  • 所有格與複合關係代名詞子句（all of which）
    This is a powerful contrast with virtually every other major economy in the world today, all of which face fiscal constraints.
  • 被動語態的運用（Passive Voice）
    The benefits of the AI boom are not being felt evenly across society...
  • 現在分詞當作形容詞修飾名詞
    ...making it more accessible to a wider array of actors.
