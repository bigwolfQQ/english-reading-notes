發展放緩的呼籲壓低了人工智慧股

Slowdown calls deflate AI stocks

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • pace n. 步調、速度
    the pace of development must slow to prevent threats to humanity.
    開發的步調必須放慢，以防止對人類構成威脅。
  • call on phr. 呼籲、要求
    called on AI companies to slow the rate at which they advance model capabilities
    呼籲 AI 公司放慢提升模型能力的速度
  • misuse n. / v. 誤用、濫用
    amid mounting fears of AI misuse.
    在外界對 AI 遭到濫用的恐懼日增之際。
  • investor n. 投資者、投資人
    shares in ChatGPT maker OpenAI’s investor Softbank Group Corp tumbled
    ChatGPT 製造商 OpenAI 的投資者軟銀集團公司股價暴跌
  • supply chain n. 供應鏈
    while chip supply chain company Tokyo Electron Ltd fell 3.7 percent.
    而晶片供應鏈企業東京威力科創則下跌 3.7%。
  • selling pressure n. 賣壓
    “Selling pressure is likely to hit AI and semiconductor-related stocks
    「賣壓可能會襲擊 AI 及半導體相關股票」
  • uncertainty n. 不確定性
    “Additionally, uncertainty surrounding the situation in the Middle East continues to weigh on sentiment.”
    「此外，中東局勢的不確定性持續對市場情緒造成壓力。」
  • discounted adj. 打折的、折價的
    tumbled as much as 10.5 percent after making a discounted share placement.
    在進行折價配股後，股價一度暴跌達 10.5%。
  • dismiss v. 對……不予理會、摒棄、駁回
    Some investors dismissed the warnings from Anthropic and OpenAI.
    一些投資者對來自 Anthropic 和 OpenAI 的警告不以為然。
  • strategy n. 策略、戰略
    Saxo Bank A/S chief investment strategist Charu Chanana said.
    盛寶銀行集團首席投資策略師查魯·查納納表示。
  • valuation n. 估值、評價
    “Their valuations assume both strong demand and a relentless pace of technological progress,”
    「他們的估值假設了強勁的需求以及科技進步的不懈步伐，」
  • generate v. 產生、引起
    “But that does not necessarily mean every investment being made today will generate an attractive return.”
    「但這並不一定意味著今天進行的每一項投資都會產生有吸引力的回報。」

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    Stocks related to artificial intelligence (AI) fell sharply yesterday after the CEOs of the US companies developing the most advanced AI models warned...
    句中的「developing the most advanced AI models」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前方名詞「the US companies」，相當於形容詞子句「who are developing the most advanced AI models」，能使句子更為精簡。
  • 關係子句省略受格關代 (Omitted Relative Pronoun)
    called on AI companies to slow the rate at which they advance model capabilities...
    在「the rate (that) they advance model capabilities」中，先行詞為 the rate，後面接一個受格關係子句，用來修飾速度。句中的關係代名詞（在此處用 at which 引導）指代前面的 rate。
  • 現在分詞短語作附帶狀況/結果 (Present Participle Phrase indicating result)
    Shares of Z.AI Co (智譜), the developer of the GLM AI series, tumbled as much as 10.5 percent after making a discounted share placement.
    句尾的「after making a discounted share placement」使用了介系詞加動名詞片語（making...），用來表達在進行某個動作之後發生的狀況，是新聞英語中非常常見且簡潔的時間與動作表達方式。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Michael Burry, whose prescient bets against the US housing market before the 2008 financial crisis were chronicled in the movie The Big Short...
    句中的「were chronicled」使用了被動語態（be動詞 + 過去分詞），強調貝瑞的押注事蹟「被記錄/被寫進」電影《大賣空》中，主體是事蹟本身而不是記錄的人。
