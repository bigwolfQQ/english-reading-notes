文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    A 50-year-old woman surnamed Chao (趙) was shot in the chest and hand at about 2pm yesterday while walking in an alley in Taipei.
    句中的「while walking in an alley」省略了主詞與be動詞（原句形如 while she was walking），用現在分詞片語來修飾主句的主語，表示「在...的同時」，在新聞英文中非常常見。
  • 過去分詞作後位修飾 (Past Participle as Post-modifier)
    Vehicle registration data identified a man surnamed Chen (陳), Chao’s estranged husband, as the owner of the car...
    句中的「surnamed Chen」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的「a man」，相當於形容詞子句「who was surnamed Chen」，用以簡化句子並精鍊資訊。
  • be to + 原形動詞（表示預定、計畫的未來）
    Under the agreement, the two companies are to leverage their respective capabilities to integrate Havoc’s industry-leading autonomy software...
    「are to leverage」中的「be to」結構常用於新聞報導或正式公文、合約中，用來表達按照計畫、協議「預定要執行」或「必須達成」的未來動作。
  • 非限定關係子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    ...as the owner of the car, which police believe he drove as a getaway vehicle for the shooter.
    逗號後面的「which police believe he drove...」是一個非限定關係子句，用來補充說明前面的「the car」，其中夾帶了一個插入子句「police believe」，用來表達警方的推測。
