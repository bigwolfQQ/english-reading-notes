台灣在太平洋島國論壇聚會中承諾與太平洋友邦更緊密合作

Taiwan pledges closer cooperation with Pacific allies at PIF gathering

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • pledge v. / n. 承諾，保證
    Taiwan and its three diplomatic allies in the South Pacific -- Palau, Tuvalu and the Marshall Islands -- signed a joint statement Wednesday pledging to work more closely for the betterment of the region
    台灣與其在南太平洋的三個外交盟友——帛琉、吐瓦魯和馬紹爾群島——於週三簽署了一份聯合聲明，承諾為該地區的福祉更緊密地合作
  • cooperation n. 合作
    Taiwan pledges closer cooperation with Pacific allies at PIF gathering
    台灣在太平洋島國論壇聚會中承諾與太平洋友邦更緊密合作
  • conclude v. 結束，告一段落
    as Foreign Minister Lin Chia-lung (林佳龍) concluded a visit to Palau to attend a major annual gathering of Pacific nations
    隨著外交部長林佳龍結束對帛琉的訪問並出席太平洋國家年度重要聚會
  • strengthen v. 加強，鞏固
    Lin held talks with Palau President Surangel Whipps Jr., Marshall Islands President Hilda Heine and Tuvalu Prime Minister Feleti Teo on strengthening Taiwan's relations with the three countries
    林佳龍與帛琉總統惠恕仁、馬紹爾群島總統海妮及吐瓦魯總理戴斐立舉行會談，就加強台灣與這三國的關係進行討論
  • safeguard v. 維護，保護
    and jointly safeguarding peace, stability and prosperity in the region
    以及共同維護該地區的和平、穩定與繁榮
  • facility n. 設施，設備
    the U.S. State Department announced that it is working with Taiwan and other partners to support the development and operation of a new hospital facility in Palau
    美國國務院宣布正與台灣及其他夥伴合作，以支持帛琉新醫院設施的開發與營運
  • comprehensive adj. 全面的，綜合的
    and to conduct a comprehensive drinking water sampling project covering drinking water sources and wells across the island
    並進行一項涵蓋全島飲用水源與水井的全面性飲用水採樣計畫
  • exclude v. 排除，排斥
    Lin's latest participation in PIF-related events in Palau followed Taiwan's and other non-members' exclusion from last year's event in the Solomon Islands.
    林佳龍最近參與帛琉與論壇相關的活動，是在台灣及其他非成員國被排除在去年索羅門群島舉辦的活動之後。
  • maintain v. 維持，保持
    Currently, only three of the 18 PIF members -- the Marshall Islands, Palau and Tuvalu -- maintain formal diplomatic relations with Taiwan.
    目前，太平洋島國論壇的18個成員中，僅有3個——馬紹爾群島、帛琉和吐瓦魯——與台灣維持正式外交關係。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附帶狀況/結果補語
    Taiwan and its three diplomatic allies... signed a joint statement Wednesday pledging to work more closely for the betterment of the region...
    句尾的「pledging...」是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前面主句簽署聯合聲明的同時所帶有的承諾內容，相當於 and pledged... 的簡化寫法，在新聞英文中非常常見。
  • 分詞構句作時間/原因狀語
    ...as Foreign Minister Lin Chia-lung concluded a visit to Palau to attend a major annual gathering of Pacific nations, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) said Thursday.
    句中的「to attend a major annual gathering...」是不定詞片語作副詞用，用來修飾動詞 concluded，表示造訪帛琉的「目的」是為了出席重要年度聚會。
  • 被動語態的應用
    Media reports suggested that the Solomon Islands... sought to block Taiwan's participation under pressure from Beijing.
    句尾的「under pressure from Beijing」雖然不是標準的動詞被動語態，但名詞 pressure 隱含了被動的概念（受到來自北京的施壓），在多益商業閱讀中經常用來表達受制於某方的情況。
