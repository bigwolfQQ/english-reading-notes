單字解析

  • expansion n. 擴張、擴大
    Philippines opens largest OFW service center in Taipei, eyes expansion
    菲律賓在台北開設最大海外移工服務中心，並著眼於擴點
  • establish v. 建立、設立
    The Philippines opened its largest overseas Filipino worker (OFW) service center in Taipei on Sunday, with hopes of establishing two more in central and southern Taiwan over the next two years
    據該國海外勞工福利署（OWWA）表示，菲律賓於週日在台北開設了其規模最大的海外菲律賓移工（OFW）服務中心，並希望在未來兩年內於台灣中部和南部再建立兩個服務中心
  • facility n. 設施、場所
    The OFW Global Center-Taipei is the third such facility worldwide, following centers in Hong Kong and Kuala Lumpur.
    台北海外菲律賓人全球中心是繼香港和吉隆坡中心之後，全球第三個此類設施。
  • access v. / n. 使用、接近；通道、管道
    It is designed as a one-stop hub where Filipino migrant workers in Taiwan can access government services, welfare assistance and training without returning to the Philippines.
    它被設計成一個一站式服務中心，在台的菲律賓移工無需返回菲律賓，即可在此獲得政府服務、福利協助和培訓。
  • entrepreneurship n. 企業家精神、創業
    Planned activities include Mandarin classes, financial literacy programs, computer and artificial intelligence training, and courses on content creation and entrepreneurship, according to Caunan.
    據考南表示，計劃中的活動包括中文課程、財務識字計畫、電腦與人工智慧培訓，以及內容創作和創業課程。
  • pursue v. 追求、尋求
    the training can help workers pursue new career opportunities or start businesses after returning to the Philippines.
    這些培訓可以幫助工人在返回菲律賓後追求新的職涯機會或創業。
  • handle v. 處理、應付
    She said migrant workers often have to take time away from their jobs to handle administrative procedures and hoped the center would offer faster document processing and additional services.
    她表示，移工經常必須從工作中分出時間來處理行政程序，並希望該中心能提供更快的文件處理和額外服務。
  • commitment n. 承諾、投入
    MECO Chairperson Corazon Avecilla-Padiernos said the new center in Taipei reflected the Philippine government's commitment to supporting its citizens overseas.
    MECO主席科拉松·阿瓦西拉-帕迪埃爾諾表示，台北的新中心反映了菲律賓政府對支持其海外公民的承諾。
  • convenient adj. 方便的、便利的
    "This is very convenient now because everything is here already, what a Filipino overseas worker needs," Domingo said.
    多明戈說：「現在非常方便，因為這裡應有盡有，這正是海外菲律賓移工所需要的。」
  • administrative adj. 行政的
    She said migrant workers often have to take time away from their jobs to handle administrative procedures
    她表示，移工經常必須從工作中分出時間來處理行政程序

文法重點

  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況 (with + 名詞 + 分詞)
    The Philippines opened its largest overseas Filipino worker (OFW) service center in Taipei on Sunday, with hopes of establishing two more in central and southern Taiwan over the next two years...
    「with + 名詞 + 分詞」結構用來補充說明主要的動作發生時伴隨的狀況或結果。在此句中，with hopes of... 表示「帶著……的希望／懷抱著……的期望」，用來修飾前面的主句。
  • 關係副詞 where 引導的形容詞子句
    It is designed as a one-stop hub where Filipino migrant workers in Taiwan can access government services...
    句中的 where 引導一個關係子句，用來修飾前面的先行詞 a one-stop hub（一個樞紐／中心）。where 在子句中扮演地方副詞的角色，相當於 in which。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (名詞 + 現在分詞)
    ...with feasibility studies underway.
    此結構為「with + 名詞 (feasibility studies) + 形容詞片語/副詞 (underway)」，用來補充說明主句動作進行時的背景情況，意指「在可行性研究進行中的情況下」。
  • 地方副詞置於句首的倒裝句
    Among those welcoming the center was Geraldine G. Domingo, 54, a Taipei-based caregiver...
    為了強調並平衡句構，當地方或介系詞片語（Among those welcoming the center）移到句首時，主詞（Geraldine G. Domingo...）與動詞（was）位置倒裝，讓語氣更流暢。
