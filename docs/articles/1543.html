The journalists’ revolt against AI scraping

  • shareholder n. 股東

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    That is why Editorial Perfil, a major Latin American publisher headquartered in Argentina (and the owner of Perfil, the newspaper where I am digital director), recently sued ChatGPT’s owner...
    句中的「headquartered in Argentina」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞短語「a major Latin American publisher」，相當於關代子句「which is headquartered in Argentina」，藉此精簡句子結構。
  • 讓步副詞子句搭配省略句型 (Concessive Clause with Ellipsis)
    The companies behind these innovative products tout their immense potential benefit for humanity, but this narrative, while not necessarily wrong, brushes aside that these models are built on the wholesale theft of copyrighted work...
    「while not necessarily wrong」是一個省略了主詞與be動詞的讓步子句（原句形為 while it is not necessarily wrong），在英文新聞寫作中常用來在承認對手某個觀點的同時，迅速轉入主要的批評論點。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞 (Gerund Phrase as Subject)
    AI firms are not only stealing content, but also encouraging users to stay within their closed ecosystems, turning the Internet into an oligopolistic market.
    句尾的「turning the Internet into an oligopolistic market」是現在分詞片語引導的分詞構句，用來補充說明前面主句所發生的結果（AI 企業不只偷內容還鼓勵使用者留在生態系，『因而把網際網路變成了寡頭市場』）。
  • 假設語氣（與現在事實相反） (Subjunctive Mood for Unreal Present)
    If they cannot be bothered to act in the public’s interest, they should at least look out for their own.
