Taipei Times · 2026-09-15

蕭美琴之行開創全新外交模式：官員

Hsiao’s trip creates new diplomatic model: official

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • relations n. 關係、往來
    Taiwan-Europe relations continue to deepen as Vice President Hsiao Bi-khim (蕭美琴) frequently visits non-allied European countries under a “Team Taiwan Diplomacy” working model, a foreign affairs official said yesterday.
    一名外交官員昨天表示，隨著副總統蕭美琴以「台灣隊外交」的工作模式頻繁訪問非邦交歐洲國家，台灣與歐洲的關係持續深化。
  • invite v. 邀請
    Hsiao was invited to the Ventotene European Conference for Freedom and Democracy in Italy last week after she attended the annual summit of the Inter-Parliamentary Alliance on China in Brussels in November last year.
    蕭美琴去年11月出席在布魯塞爾舉行的「對華政策跨國議會聯盟」年度峰會後，上週應邀前往義大利參加范托內內自由與民主歐洲會議。
  • anonymity n. 匿名、化名
    An official speaking on condition of anonymity said that this shows that high-level interactions between Taiwan and Europe are deepening, shifting from “highly sensitive” exceptions to a new interactive mode.
    一位匿名發言的官員表示，這顯示台灣與歐洲之間的高層互動正在加深，從「高度敏感」的特例轉變為一種新的互動模式。
  • personnel n. 人員、員工
    Frontline diplomatic and security teams were highly discreet about Hsiao’s visit to Italy in terms of itinerary planning, communication with European personnel and emergency response, given that it was a vice president-level visit to a non-allied nation, the official said.
    該官員指出，鑑於這是一次副總統級別對非邦交國的訪問，第一線外交與安全團隊在行程規畫、與歐洲人員的溝通以及緊急應變方面，對蕭美琴的義大利之行保持高度謹慎。
  • disrupt v. 擾亂、中斷
    Alert levels were also raised after President William Lai’s (賴清德) visit to Eswatini in April was disrupted by China’s weaponization of flight information regions, they said.
    他們並表示，在總統賴清德4月訪問史瓦帝尼因中國武器化飛航情報區而受干擾後，警戒層級也隨之提高。
  • opportunity n. 機會、時機
    The official cited Lai as saying that Taiwan should seize every opportunity that helps expand its international space and deepen partnerships with like-minded countries, the official said.
    該官員引述賴總統的話表示，台灣應把握每一個有助於拓展國際空間以及深化與理念相近國家夥伴關係的機會。
  • facilitate v. 促進、促成、協助
    Lai ordered the ministry and relevant teams to facilitate and asked Minister of Foreign Affairs Lin Chia-lung (林佳龍) to communicate with involved parties, they said.
    他們表示，賴總統下令外交部及相關團隊全力促成，並要求外交部長林佳龍與相關各方進行溝通。
  • departure n. 出發、離開
    Lai also hosted the itinerary and contingency meeting before Hsiao’s departure and ordered officials to “follow the host country’s lead,” the official said.
    官員表示，賴總統並在蕭美琴出發前親自主持行程與應變會議，並下令官員「遵循主辦國的引導」。
  • transform v. 轉變、轉換
    Lai provides strategic direction and political authorization, while Lin and the diplomatic team continue to expand Taiwan’s political credit and activities in Europe, the official said, adding that Hsiao can transform the diplomatic gains into higher-level political interactions based on her extensive diplomatic experience and strong connections in Western countries.
    官員表示，賴總統提供戰略方向與政治授權，而林佳龍與外交團隊則持續擴大台灣在歐洲的政治信用與活動，並補充說蕭美琴能憑藉其豐富的外交經驗與在西方國家深厚的人脈，將外交成果轉化為更高層級的政治互動。
  • announce v. 宣布、公佈
    Hsiao’s schedule was not announced prior to the visit to ensure her safety, they said, adding that it took the delegation 27 hours to travel by plane, vehicle and ferry to Italy.
    他們說，為確保安全，蕭美琴的行程在訪前並未公佈，並補充指出該代表團透過飛機、汽車和渡輪輾轉花了27小時才抵達義大利。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作表語/補充說明 (indicating/shifting)
    An official speaking on condition of anonymity said that this shows that high-level interactions between Taiwan and Europe are deepening, shifting from “highly sensitive” exceptions to a new interactive mode.
    句尾的 "shifting..." 是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前面動詞片語 "are deepening" 所帶來的轉變結果，相當於 "and they are shifting..." 的簡化結構。
  • 分詞構句表條件 (given that...)
    Frontline diplomatic and security teams were highly discreet about Hsiao’s visit to Italy in terms of itinerary planning, communication with European personnel and emergency response, given that it was a vice president-level visit to a non-allied nation, the official said.
    "given that" 是常用多益商業寫作的複合片語連接詞，意指「鑑於、考量到」，後面接子句作為前句行為的原因或前提條件。
  • 被動語態 (was disrupted / were raised)
    Alert levels were also raised after President William Lai’s (賴清德) visit to Eswatini in April was disrupted by China’s weaponization of flight information regions, they said.
    新聞英語常使用被動語態來強調事件的承受者或客觀性。此處 "was disrupted" 與 "were raised" 皆為被動語態，分別強調訪問受到干擾以及警戒層級被提高。
  • 對等連接詞連接對稱結構 (while...)
    Lai provides strategic direction and political authorization, while Lin and the diplomatic team continue to expand Taiwan’s political credit and activities in Europe...
    "while" 在此作對等連接詞使用，表示「而、然而」，用來對比說明賴總統與林部長及外交團隊各自負責的不同任務與角色。
