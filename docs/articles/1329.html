沈、蔣兩人對台北市長選戰中的毒品政策辯論持開放態度

Shen, Chiang open to debate over drug policy in Taipei mayoral race

單字解析

  • proposed v. 提議，提案
    Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) Taipei mayoral candidate Puma Shen (沈伯洋) proposed a debate on drug policy yesterday...
    民主進步黨（DPP）台北市長候選人沈伯洋昨天就毒品政策提出辯論...
  • controversy n. 爭議，辯論
    ...while Taipei Mayor Chiang Wan-an (蔣萬安) of the Chinese Nationalist Party (KMT) said the issue could be discussed at a formal debate as controversy over Shen’s past writings continued.
    ...而中國國民黨（KMT）的台北市長蔣萬安則表示，隨著對沈伯洋過去著作的爭議持續，該議題可以在正式辯論中進行討論。
  • agreement n. 協議，同意
    Chiang, seeking re-election, said he and Shen have reached a preliminary agreement to take part in a TVBS-organized debate...
    尋求連任的蔣萬安表示，他和沈伯洋已達成初步協議，將參加一場由TVBS主辦的辯論會...
  • negotiated v. 談判，協商
    ...with details to be negotiated by their aides.
    ...細節將由雙方幕僚進行協商。
  • positions n. 立場，職位
    Chiang said all municipal issues, including drug policy, as well as the candidates’ positions on them, were open for discussion.
    蔣萬安表示，所有市政議題，包括毒品政策以及候選人對這些議題的立場，都是可以公開討論的。
  • dispute n. 爭端，糾紛
    The dispute centers on a September 2014 article Shen wrote for Thinking Taiwan...
    這場爭議的焦點在於沈伯洋於2014年9月為《思想坦克》所寫的一篇文章...
  • implemented v. 實施，執行
    ...citing methadone treatment and needle-exchange programs already implemented in Taipei.
    ...並舉出台北市已經實施的美沙酮治療和針頭交換計畫為例。
  • classified v. 分類，歸類
    Methadone is classified as a Category 2 narcotic in Taiwan but also has lawful medical uses.
    美沙酮在台灣被列為第二級管制藥品，但也有合法的醫療用途。
  • separate adj. 單獨的，各自的
    ...while needle-exchange programs are a separate harm-reduction measure.
    ...而針頭交換計畫則是一項獨立的減害措施。
  • explanation n. 解釋，說明
    Chiang questioned Shen’s explanation that his 2014 proposals concerned policies already in place...
    蔣萬安質疑沈伯洋關於其2014年提案涉及已實施政策的解釋...
  • responding v. 回應，回答
    Responding later yesterday, Shen campaign spokesperson Tsai I-kae (蔡一愷) said studying a policy and advocating its implementation were different things.
    沈伯洋競選發言人蔡一愷昨天稍晚回應表示，研究一項政策與倡導其實施是兩碼子事。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Present Participle）作形容詞或表附帶狀況
    Chiang, seeking re-election, said he and Shen have reached a preliminary agreement...
    句中的「seeking re-election」是現在分詞片語，置於主詞 Chiang 後方作補充說明，相當於形容詞子句「who is seeking re-election」，用來修飾主詞 Chiang，在新聞英語中非常常見以求精簡。
  • 獨立分詞構句（Absolute Nominative Construction）
    ...with details to be negotiated by their aides.
    此處使用「with + 名詞 + to be p.p.」的結構，用來表達伴隨發生的狀況或未完成的動作。「details」是被動接受「negotiated」的對象，用不定詞被動態（to be negotiated）來表示「有待協商的細節」。
  • 假設語氣（Subjunctive / Conditional）
    If decriminalization was considered too radical, he suggested Taiwan could follow other countries by establishing community centers...
    本句為與現在事實相反的假設語氣（或過去的條件句），用「If + 過去式動詞 (was considered)」搭配主句中的助動詞過去式 (could)，表達如果除罪化被認為過於激進（假設情境），他便會建議……。
  • 被動語態（Passive Voice）
    Methadone is classified as a Category 2 narcotic in Taiwan but also has lawful medical uses.
    句中的「is classified」為主詞 Methadone（美沙酮）搭配be動詞與過去分詞構成的被動語態，強調「美沙酮被分類為第二級管制藥品」這個客觀事實，而不需強調是誰去分類它。
