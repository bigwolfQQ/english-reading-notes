AIT處長表示：選舉將至，應戒備新型AI威脅

Brace for new AI threats ahead of elections, AIT head says

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • guard against phr. 防範、戒備
    Taiwan must guard against artificial intelligence (AI)-enabled information manipulation ahead of local elections on Nov. 28, American Institute in Taiwan (AIT) Director Raymond Greene said yesterday.
    美國在台協會（AIT）處長谷立言昨天表示，台灣必須防範人工智慧（AI）生成的資訊操弄，以因應11月28日舉行的地方選舉。
  • disinformation n. 假訊息、造假資訊
    Although Taiwan has earned acclaim for its efforts to counter disinformation, it must still brace for a new wave of sophisticated, AI-powered cognitive warfare...
    儘管台灣因反制假訊息的努力而獲得讚譽，但仍必須為新一波精密的、由AI驅動的認知戰做好準備...
  • brace for phr. 為……做好準備、嚴陣以待
    ...it must still brace for a new wave of sophisticated, AI-powered cognitive warfare, Greene said during his keynote speech...
    ...但仍必須為新一波精密的、由AI驅動的認知戰做好準備，谷立言在發表專題演說時如此表示...
  • infrastructure n. 基礎設施
    Taiwan also urgently needs to defend financial institutions and critical infrastructure against AI-powered cyberattacks, he said.
    他說，台灣也迫切需要防範金融機構與關鍵基礎設施遭受AI驅動的網路攻擊。
  • resilience n. 韌性、恢復力
    Taiwan has made great progress in energy resilience by increasing the importation and storage of liquefied natural gas...
    台灣透過增加液化天然氣的進口與儲存，在能源韌性方面取得了重大進展...
  • allocation n. 配置、分配
    The nation has also made less visible, but equally impressive, improvements in its electricity allocation policy, power-grid decentralization...
    他說，台灣在電力分配政策、電網去中心化……等方面，也做出了雖較不顯眼、但同樣令人印象深刻的改進。
  • commitment n. 承諾、投入
    Washington welcomes President William Lai’s (賴清德) commitment to increase defense spending to 5 percent of GDP by 2030...
    華府歡迎總統賴清德承諾在2030年前將國防開支提高至GDP的5%...
  • boost v. 提升、促進、增加
    Compared with many other US partners, Taiwan is far better placed financially to boost its defense, Greene said.
    谷立言表示，與許多其他美國夥伴相比，台灣在財務上更有能力提升國防。
  • deter v. 嚇阻、阻止
    Deterring Chinese aggression is paramount for Taiwan and the world, he said.
    他說，嚇阻中國的侵略對台灣和世界至關重要。
  • impact n. 衝擊、影響
    “Beyond the human toll, any conflict across the Taiwan Strait would have a bigger impact on the global economy than the Second World War,” Greene said.
    谷立言表示：「除了生命損失之外，台海兩岸發生的任何衝突，對全球經濟的衝擊都將大於第二次世界大戰。」
  • priority n. 優先事項、首要任務
    The Trump administration has made preserving peace in the Indo-Pacific region one of its top priorities, he said.
    他說，川普政府已將維護印太地區的和平列為首要任務之一。
  • reciprocity n. 互惠、相互對待
    “The upcoming US-China summit is an opportunity to further advance a relationship of strategic stability, on the basis of fairness and reciprocity...”
    「即將舉行的美中峰會是一個機會，能在公平和互惠的基礎上，進一步推進戰略穩定關係……」
  • strategic adj. 戰略性的
    Taiwan has many strategic advantages Ukraine and many other countries do not have...
    他說，台灣擁有許多烏克蘭及許多其他國家所沒有的戰略優勢……
  • safeguard v. 維護、保護
    Taiwan and the US must constantly improve their defensive capabilities to safeguard peace, he said.
    他說，台灣和美國必須不斷提升防衛能力以維護和平。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞當形容詞（修飾名詞）
    Taiwan must guard against artificial intelligence (AI)-enabled information manipulation ahead of local elections on Nov. 28, American Institute in Taiwan (AIT) Director Raymond Greene said yesterday.
    句中的「AI-enabled」（由AI啟用的/生成的）是由現在分詞/過去分詞構成的複合形容詞，用來修飾後方的名詞「information manipulation」（資訊操弄），在多益閱讀測驗中常考這種用分詞當形容詞的結構。
  • 分詞構句表附帶結果或狀態
    US President Donald Trump has been calling on European countries to take on greater responsibilities for their self-defense, he said, adding that Ukraine has shown that sustained military spending can improve a nation’s military capabilities.
    句尾的「adding that...」是分詞構句，由主動動詞加入ing形成，用來補充說明前面主句所說的話，省去了連接詞和主詞（原句意為 and he added that...），這是商業與新聞英語中串聯句意的常見高頻寫法。
  • 比較級修飾語（far better）
    Compared with many other US partners, Taiwan is far better placed financially to boost its defense, Greene said.
    在形容詞比較級「better」前方加上「far」，用來加強語氣，表示「遠比……更好、更有利」。類似的加強語氣副詞還有 much, even, a lot 等，多益測驗常在文法選填題中測試這些修飾比較級的用法。
  • 被動語態的運用
    ...Ukraine’s military, which has been hobbled by its rigid and hierarchical command structure, Greene said.
    句中的「has been hobbled by」是現在完成式的被動語態（has been + 過去分詞），用來強調烏克蘭的軍隊「受到……的嚴重牽制與阻礙」。在著重事實與客觀報導的新聞英語中，被動語態非常常見。
