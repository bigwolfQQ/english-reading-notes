Paraguay, Eswatini, Palau advocate for Taiwan's inclusion in UN

單字解析

  • inclusion n. 納入，加入
    Three of Taiwan's diplomatic allies -- Paraguay, Eswatini, and Palau -- made a push Wednesday for Taiwan's inclusion in the United Nations...
    台灣的三個友邦——巴拉圭、史瓦帝尼與帛琉——週三大力爭取台灣加入聯合國...
  • ongoing adj. 進行中的，不間斷的
    Paraguayan President Santiago Peña said in his speech that the ongoing crisis in the Middle East, including the closure of the Strait of Hormuz, was already troubling.
    巴拉圭總統貝尼亞在演說中表示，中東正在進行中的危機，包括荷姆茲海峽的關閉，已經令人感到憂心。
  • impact n. 影響，衝擊
    ...adding that a potential cross-strait war could have a global impact because of the chips Taiwan produces.
    ...並補充說，由於台灣生產晶片，潛在的兩岸戰爭可能會對全球產生影響。
  • conflict n. 衝突，爭端
    What is at stake in this potential conflict is significant: the very future of humanity.
    在這場潛在衝突中攸關的事項非同小可：那是人類的未來本身。
  • participation n. 參與，參加
    ...reaffirming Paraguay's support for the full and meaningful participation of Taiwan in the U.N. and its specialized agencies.
    ...重申巴拉圭支持台灣全面且有意義地參與聯合國及其專門機構。
  • partner n. 合作夥伴
    ...his country chooses its partners "based on shared values of democracy, human rights, and a rules-based international order."
    ...他的國家選擇夥伴是「基於民主、人權以及以規則為基礎的國際秩序之共享價值」。
  • contribute v. 貢獻，促成
    ...a trusted partner that can contribute even more fully if given space to do so...
    ...一個值得信賴的夥伴，若給予其空間，將能做出更充分的貢獻...
  • role n. 角色，職責
    Meanwhile, King Mswati III of the African kingdom of Eswatini also expressed support for Taiwan, which he said plays a vital role in global supply chains.
    同時，非洲王國史瓦帝尼國王恩史瓦帝三世也表達了對台灣的支持，他表示台灣在全球供應鏈中扮演著至關重要的角色。
  • facilitate v. 促進，協助，使容易
    The United Nations should seek appropriate ways to facilitate Taiwan's substantive participation in U.N. meetings and activities...
    聯合國應尋求適當的方法，促進台灣實質參與聯合國的會議與活動...
  • campaign v. 發起運動，競選，爭取
    ...the country has been campaigning during the annual U.N. General Assembly for its inclusion in the U.N. system and related specialized agencies.
    ...我國每年在聯合國大會期間都會積極爭取加入聯合國體系及相關專門機構。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 / 附加補充說明
    Noting that the U.N. Charter begins with the commitment, "We the peoples," Whipps said the U.N. "marginalizes Taiwan's 23 million people, disregarding that foundational purpose."
    句首的「Noting that...」為現在分詞（V-ing）引導的分詞片語，用來補充說明主句主語 Whipps（帛琉總統）在發言時同時伴隨的動作或心理狀態，相當於副詞子句「As he noted that...」的簡化。
  • 關係代名詞子句（非限定用法）
    Meanwhile, King Mswati III of the African kingdom of Eswatini also expressed support for Taiwan, which he said plays a vital role in global supply chains.
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    Since 1971 when the Republic of China (Taiwan) was expelled from the U.N., the country has been campaigning during the annual U.N. General Assembly...
  • 條件句的省略（省略 if）
    ...a trusted partner that can contribute even more fully if given space to do so...
