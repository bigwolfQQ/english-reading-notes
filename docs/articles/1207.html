日本大學生在東京旅遊展推廣台灣茶

Japanese university students promote Taiwan tea at Tokyo tourism expo

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • seminar n. 研討會
    Students from a Japanese university held a Taiwanese tea tasting and seminar at the 2026 Tourism Expo Japan on Saturday...
    來自一所日本大學的學生週六在2026年日本旅遊博覽會上舉辦了台灣茶品茗會與研討會...
  • exhibition n. 展覽
    ...held from Friday through Sunday at the Tokyo Big Sight international exhibition center.
    ...舉辦時間從週五到週日，地點位於東京國際展示場。
  • organize v. 組織、主辦
    The experience was organized by Toyo University's Department of International Tourism Management...
    這次體驗活動由東洋大學國際觀光學系主辦...
  • assistance n. 協助、幫助
    ...with assistance from the Japanese branch of Taiwanese beverage company Happy Lemon.
    ...並獲得台灣手搖飲品牌快樂檸檬日本分公司的協助。
  • representative n. 代表
    Happy Lemon representative Lin Tai-yi (林太一) served as the presenter of the seminar.
    快樂檸檬代表林太一擔任該研討會的主講人。
  • originate v. 起源、發源
    Students from Toyo University's tourism department said the idea originated during a trip to Taiwan...
    東洋大學觀光學系的學生表示，這個構想源於一次台灣之旅...
  • conduct v. 進行、執行
    ...leading them to conduct further research after returning to Japan.
    ...促使他們返回日本後進行了進一步的研究。
  • select v. 挑選、選擇
    For the event, the students selected Taiwanese teas they considered well suited to Japanese tastes...
    為了這次活動，學生們挑選了他們認為非常適合日本口味的台灣茶...
  • feature n. 特色、特徵
    He told CNA that one feature distinguishing Taiwanese tea is the different ways in which the leaves are oxidized and roasted.
    他告訴中央社，台灣茶的一個特色在於茶葉經過不同的氧化與烘焙方式。
  • potential n. 潛力
    ...and highlight its potential to promote cultural and tourism exchanges, according to the organizers.
    ...並突顯其促進文化與旅遊交流的潛力，主辦單位表示。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...held from Friday through Sunday at the Tokyo Big Sight international exhibition center.
    句中的 "held from..." 為過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "the three-day annual expo"（為期三年的年度博覽會），省略了「which was」，使句子更精煉。
  • 非限定關代子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    ...organized by Toyo University's Department of International Tourism Management, which was participating in the expo...
    關係代間詞 "which" 前面有逗號，引導非限定關代子句，用來補充說明先行詞 "Toyo University's Department of International Tourism Management" 的額外資訊。
  • 現在分詞片語作結果狀語 (Present Participle Phrase of Result)
    ...leading them to conduct further research after returning to Japan.
    句尾的 "leading them to..." 是現在分詞片語，用來表達前一個動作（對茶飲文化產生興趣）所自然導致的結果（促使他們進行進一步研究）。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The pairings were intended to show how Taiwanese tea can be enjoyed in everyday settings...
    句中使用了被動語態 "can be enjoyed"（可以被享用），強調茶作為動作的承受者，聚焦於茶本身在日常情境中的呈現方式。
