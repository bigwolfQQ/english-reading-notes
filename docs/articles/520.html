修改憲法無法解決台灣的政治僵局：座談會表示

Amending constitution won't solve Taiwan's political deadlock: Panel

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • amend v. 修正，修改（法案、憲法等）
    Amending the Constitution is not the answer to Taiwan's political deadlock over disputed legislation and judicial appointments, panelists at a Taipei seminar said Saturday...
    台北一場研討會的與會專家於週六表示，修改憲法並不是解決台灣因爭議法案和司法任命而陷入政治僵局的答案...
  • deadlock n. 僵局，僵持
    Amending the Constitution is not the answer to Taiwan's political deadlock over disputed legislation and judicial appointments...
    修改憲法並不是解決台灣因爭議法案和司法任命而陷入政治僵局的答案...
  • legislation n. 法律，法規，立法
    ...disputed legislation and judicial appointments...
    ...爭議法案和司法任命...
  • appointment n. 任命，委任；約會
    ...disputed legislation and judicial appointments...
    ...爭議法案和司法任命...
  • consensus n. 共識
    ...while existing institutional shortcomings could be addressed through political consensus and constitutional practice.
    ...而現有的體制缺陷可以透過政治共識和憲政慣例來解決。
  • nominee n. 被提名人
    ...as well as the Legislature's repeated rejection of presidential nominees to the Constitutional Court.
    ...以及立法院一再否決總統對司法院大法官的提名人選。
  • expenditure n. 支出，開支
    He cited France's restrictions on legislation that increases government expenditure...
    他舉例指出，法國對增加政府支出的立法限制...
  • concession n. 讓步
    "Without reconciliation, compromise and concessions, democratic politics cannot work..."
    「沒有和解、妥協與讓步，民主政治就無法運作……」
  • convene v. 召集，召開（會議等）
    Then-President Lee Teng-hui (李登輝) of the Kuomintang agreed to convene the National Affairs Conference in the summer of 1990...
    當時的國民黨籍總統李登輝同意於1990年夏天召開國是會議...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...panelists at a Taipei seminar said Saturday, calling instead for dialogue between the ruling and opposition parties.
    句尾的「calling instead for...」是由現在分詞片語引導的補充說明，用來描述主詞 panelists 在說話時同時伴隨的動作或採取的立場，相當於 and called instead for...
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    He was referring to the stalemate between the Cabinet and the opposition-controlled Legislature over bills...
    句中的「opposition-controlled」是由過去分詞構成的複合形容詞，用來修飾前方的名詞 Legislature，表示該立法機構「被」在野黨所控制。
  • 假設語氣（與過去事實相反） (Subjunctive Mood)
    "Without reconciliation, compromise and concessions, democratic politics cannot work, regardless of how the Constitution is designed," Jiang said.
    句首的「Without...」在此表假設條件（等同於 If there were without... 或 If it were not for...），用以表達「若沒有（這些條件），民主政治就無法運作」的假設語氣概念。
