文法重點

  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況
    Two new gas-fired units at the Taichung Power Plant reached full-load capacity on Friday, keeping them on track for commercial operation as Premier Cho Jung-tai (卓榮泰) reiterated a pledge to phase out coal-fired units at the facility by 2034.
    句尾的「keeping them on track...」是由分詞構句構成，主詞為前句整件事或主句主語，用來補充說明主句動作同時伴隨發生的結果或狀況。
  • 關係代間代名詞子句（非限定用法）
    Cho reaffirmed the 2034 pledge during an inspection of the power plant, which operates 10 coal-fired units and has long been criticized as a major source of pollution in Taiwan.
    句中的「which operates...」是具備逗號的非限定關係子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「the power plant」，為多益閱讀測驗中常見的長句修飾結構。
  • With 加受動詞分詞的複合結構（獨立分詞構句）
    During the transition's second phase from 2031 to 2034, four additional gas units will come online in stages, with coal units 3 and 4 being demolished, and the remaining six decommissioned and placed on emergency standby, according to Taipower.
    「with + 受詞 + 分詞」結構（with coal units 3 and 4 being demolished）用來表示在某個狀況同時存在下發生主要動作，是商業與新聞英文中表現因果或附帶條件的高階句構。
