文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附帶說明（補充說明結果）
    ...he said, adding that the company plans to ship hundreds of units from the second half of this year through early next year.
    句尾的「adding that...」是由現在分詞（adding）引導的分詞片語，用來補充說明主句主語（he）在說話的同時，還順便補充了什麼內容，相當於 and he added that... 的簡化。
  • 被動語態（Passive Voice）表達預期與計畫
    AI controller shipments are expected to exceed 1,000 units and account for about 20 percent of NexCOBOT’s controller sales next year...
    句型使用「are expected to...」（被預期去...），這是新聞英語中常用來報導未來預測、市場預期或官方計畫的經典被動語態結構，能讓語氣顯得客觀。
  • 插入語結構（Parenthetical Elements）
    Controller sales — including those for computer numerical control equipment, industrial robotic arms, collaborative robots and AI robotics — are expected to account for more than 20 percent of NexCOBOT’s total revenue next year, he said.
    破折號夾住的「— including those for... —」作為插入語，用來補充解釋 Controller sales 包含哪些項目，讓主句的結構雖然拉長但依然清晰。
  • 不定詞片語作目的狀語（Infinitive Phrase of Purpose）
    To address the complexity of humanoid robots’ more than 20 joints, as well as the demands that heat dissipation, wiring and real-time synchronization place on AI controllers, NexCOBOT earlier this year introduced a “four-in-one” AI control platform...
    句首的「To address...」是不定詞片語，放在句首用來表示目的，意思是「為了處理／因應……」，逗號後面接主要的動作發生主體（NexCOBOT introduced...）。
