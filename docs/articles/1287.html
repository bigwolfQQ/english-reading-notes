台灣藝術家在西班牙「She Makes Noise」音樂節登台演出

Taiwanese artists featured at 'She Makes Noise' festival in Spain

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • annual adj. 年度的，每年的
    Three Taiwanese female artists performed at the annual Spanish electronic music festival "She Makes Noise" in Spain on Saturday as part of a special program highlighting Taiwan.
    三位台灣女性藝術家週六在西班牙舉辦的年度西班牙電子音樂節「She Makes Noise」中演出，作為突顯台灣特別計畫的一部分。
  • feature v. 以……為特色，由……主演/擔任主角
    The "She Makes Noise x Taiwan" show, being held Oct. 2-9, features Taiwanese artists -- Sabiwa, Scintii and Sonia Calico...
    於 10 月 2 日至 9 日舉行的「She Makes Noise x Taiwan」演出以台灣藝術家 Sabiwa、Scintii 和 Sonia Calico 為特色……
  • distinctive adj. 獨特的，有特色的
    ...three women who each have distinctive styles in both their creative work and performances, according to a Taiwanese diplomat in Spain.
    ……據西班牙的一名台灣外交官表示，這三位女性在創作工作與表演中皆擁有獨特的風格。
  • diverse adj. 多樣化的，不同的
    The festival this year showcases the diverse and interdisciplinary nature of Taiwan's electronic music scene...
    今年的音樂節展現了台灣電子音樂界多元且跨領域的特質……
  • organize v. 組織，籌辦
    For the first time, TECO Madrid is working with the festival's founder and director Natalia Piñuel Martín to help organize the program...
    駐馬德里辦事處首次與該音樂節的創辦人兼總監娜塔莉亞・皮涅爾・馬丁合作協助組織這項計畫……
  • promote v. 促進，推廣，宣傳
    ...including activities to promote exchanges between Taiwanese artists and the Spanish music community, Chang said.
    張表示，這包括促進台灣藝術家與西班牙音樂界之間交流的活動。
  • reflect v. 反映，顯示
    This development reflects strong interest in Taiwan's electronic music scene and artists, Chang said.
    張指出，這項發展反映出外界對台灣電子音樂界和藝術家的高度興趣。
  • aim n. 目標，目的
    On Saturday, she told CNA that her aim was to get the entire audience "jumping," and the Spanish crowd did not disappoint...
    週六她告訴中央社，她的目標是讓全場觀眾「跳起來」，而西班牙群眾也沒有讓人失望……
  • ensure v. 確保，保證
    Scintii said she usually clears her mind before each performance and focuses on her outfit and sound to ensure she can present the show as planned.
    Scintii 表示，她在每次演出前通常會放空思緒，專注於自己的服裝和聲音，以確保能按計劃呈現演出。
  • opportunity n. 機會，時機
    ...she hopes it will give Spanish audiences an opportunity to discover Asian electronic music culture...
    ……她希望這能讓西班牙觀眾有機會發現亞洲電子音樂文化……
  • strengthen v. 加強，鞏固
    ...and will help strengthen the cultural ties between Taiwan and Spain.
    ……並有助於加強台灣與西班牙之間的文化聯繫。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 / 形容詞用
    The "She Makes Noise x Taiwan" show, being held Oct. 2-9, features Taiwanese artists...
    句中的 "being held Oct. 2-9" 是現在分詞被動語態的片語，用來補充說明前面的主詞 "The 'She Makes Noise x Taiwan' show"（正在於10月2日至9日舉辦的……），相當於一個被動的形容詞子句 "which is being held Oct. 2-9"。
  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況（含有完成式的分詞構句）
    Sonia Calico said she was no stranger to Spain, having toured several Spanish cities with the electronic rock band Go Chic before launching her solo career.
    句尾的 "having toured..." 是由完成式分詞構句（Having + p.p.）所構成的，用來表示這個動作發生在主要子句動作（was no stranger）之前，具有「在……之前已經……」的時間先後關係。
  • 關係副詞 where 引導的形容詞子句
    It is quite different in Berlin, where her audiences tend to be more reserved...
    句中的 "where" 是關係副詞，用來引導非限定用法的形容詞子句，修飾先行詞 "Berlin"，補充說明柏林這個地方「當地的觀眾往往比較保守」。
  • 介系詞片語置於句首的倒裝結構（主詞動詞對調）
    With the focus of the "She Makes Noise" festival this year on creators from Taiwan, Piñuel said, she hopes it will give Spanish audiences an opportunity...
    句首的 "With the focus... on creators from Taiwan" 是一個帶有附帶狀況的介系詞片語，用來為後面的主句鋪陳背景情境，展現多益閱讀測驗中常見的精煉商業與新聞寫作句構。
