台灣橙成為台灣第一個受專利保護的原住民作物

Citrus taiwanica becomes Taiwan's first patent-protected Indigenous crop

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • patent n. / v. 專利，專利權
    Citrus taiwanica, a citrus species traditionally used by the Indigenous Saisiyat people, has received patent protection covering products derived from the plant...
    一種傳統上由賽夏族人使用的柑橘物種「台灣橙」，已獲得涵蓋該植物衍生產品的專利保護……
  • derive v. 衍生，源自
    ...products derived from the plant and been licensed to a Saisiyat cooperative in Miaoli County...
    ……該植物衍生產品並已授權給苗栗縣的一家賽夏族合作社……
  • license v. / n. 授權，許可證
    ...and been licensed to a Saisiyat cooperative in Miaoli County, the Forestry and Nature Conservation Agency (FANCA) said Wednesday.
    ……並已授權給苗栗縣的一家賽夏族合作社，林業及自然保育署（FANCA）於週三表示。
  • authorization n. 授權，批准
    The patent covers applications of the plant and products derived from it, with authorization from the Saisiyat tribe required for their use...
    該專利涵蓋了該植物及其衍生產品的應用，其使用需獲得賽夏族的授權……
  • benefit v. / n. 受益，利益，好處
    ...and that technologies and products developed through modern research based on that knowledge should benefit its original holders.
    ……而基於該知識透過現代研究開發的技術和製品，應當讓其最初的擁有者受益。
  • monopolize v. 壟斷，獨佔
    Speaking at a press event, Lin said the license was not intended to monopolize the plant, but to protect and preserve the traditional knowledge of the Saisiyat people.
    林華慶在記者會上發言表示，此授權並非意在壟斷該植物，而是為了保護和保存賽夏族人的傳統知識。
  • economic value n. phr. 經濟價值
    However, land development and the plant's perceived lack of economic value caused its wild population to decline sharply.
    然而，土地開發以及該植物被認為缺乏經濟價值，導致其野生族群數量急劇下降。
  • launch v. 發起，推出，啟動
    FANCA subsequently launched a restoration project with the Saisiyat in Nanzhuang and began co-managing the forest with the tribe in 2018, Lin said.
    林華慶表示，林業保育署隨後與南庄的賽夏族人展開復育計畫，並於2018年開始與該部落共同管理森林。
  • yield n. 產量，收益
    As the trees typically begin bearing fruit five to six years after planting, yields are expected to increase toward the end of this year, he added.
    他補充說，由於樹木通常在種植後五到六年開始結果，預計產量將在今年底左右增加。
  • commission v. 委託，委任
    After learning that the Saisiyat traditionally dried the citrus and ground it into powder to treat colds, FANCA commissioned National Taiwan University horticulture associate professor Lin Shu-yen (林書妍) to conduct further research.
    在得知賽夏族傳統上會將該柑橘曬乾並磨成粉來治療感冒後，林業保育署委託國立台灣大學園藝系副教授林書妍進行進一步研究。
  • potential adj. / n. 潛在的，可能的 / 潛力
    The research team found that extracts from the fruit could significantly reduce pneumonia severity indices in animals, suggesting potential applications in the medical and health fields, Lin said.
    林華慶說，研究團隊發現該水果的萃取物能顯著降低動物的肺炎嚴重度指數，這表明其在醫療和健康領域具有潛在應用價值。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾語 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Citrus taiwanica, a citrus species traditionally used by the Indigenous Saisiyat people, has received patent protection...
    句中的「traditionally used by the Indigenous Saisiyat people」是由過去分詞 used 開頭的片語，用來修飾前面的名詞短語「a citrus species」，相當於「which is traditionally used by...」的簡化形式，能讓句子更簡潔。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Absolute Participial Phrase)
    The patent covers applications of the plant and products derived from it, with authorization from the Saisiyat tribe required for their use...
    「with + 名詞 + 過去分詞 (authorization ... required)」構成獨立分詞構句，用來補充說明主句發生的背景或伴隨狀況，翻譯為「在……的情況下」或「需獲得……」。
  • 現在分詞短語作結果狀語 (Present Participle Phrase indicating result)
    ...suggesting potential applications in the medical and health fields, Lin said.
    句尾的「suggesting potential applications...」是以現在分詞開頭的片語，用來表達前面研究所帶來的自然結果或含意，主詞「The research team found that...」即為該動作的發起者。
