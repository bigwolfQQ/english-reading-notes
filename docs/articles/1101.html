Taipei Times · 2026-09-24

生產指數創下新高

Production indices hit new high

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • index n. 指數
    Taiwan’s industrial production last month set a new record, with the index soaring more than 23 percent from a year earlier
    台灣上個月的工業生產創下新紀錄，該指數較去年同期飆升超過 23%
  • demand n. 需求
    due to robust global demand for artificial intelligence (AI) applications
    由於全球對人工智慧（AI）應用程式的需求強勁
  • sector n. 產業、部門
    while the subindex of the manufacturing sector, which accounts for more than 90 percent of the country’s production, rose 24.61 percent to 148.42.
    同時，製造業次指數（佔該國生產總值 90% 以上）則上升 24.61%，達到 148.42。
  • consecutive adj. 連續的
    Last month was the 30th consecutive month that both indices moved higher on a year-on-year basis, the data showed.
    數據顯示，上個月是兩個指數連續第 30 個月按年增長。
  • foundry n. 鑄造廠、晶圓代工廠
    pushed up production by Taiwanese 12-inch pure play foundry operators, DRAM suppliers, IC assembly and testing firms, and IC designers
    推動了台灣 12 吋純晶圓代工廠、DRAM 供應商、IC 封測公司與 IC 設計公司的產量
  • capacity n. 產能、容量
    largely because semiconductor suppliers were keen to expand capacity through equipment purchases to meet global AI demand
    主要是因為半導體供應商積極透過採購設備來擴充產能，以滿足全球 AI 需求
  • output n. 產量、輸出
    The base metal industry’s output slid 0.36 percent year-on-year last month
    金屬基本工業的產出上個月較去年同期下滑 0.36%
  • deputy n. 副主管、副手
    Chen Yu-fang (陳玉芳), deputy head of the ministry’s Department of Statistics, said
    經濟部統計處副處長陳玉芳表示
  • exports n. 出口（商品）
    Taiwan’s exports are expected to continue to benefit from the AI boom
    台灣的出口預期將持續受惠於 AI 熱潮

文法重點

  • with + 名詞 + 分詞（獨立分詞結構）
    Taiwan’s industrial production last month set a new record, with the index soaring more than 23 percent from a year earlier due to robust global demand...
    「with + O. + 分詞」結構用來表示附帶狀況。在此句中，the index 與 soar（飆升）為主動關係，故使用現在分詞 soaring，翻譯為「隨著指數較去年同期飆升超過 23%」。
  • 非限定性關係子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    ...while the subindex of the manufacturing sector, which accounts for more than 90 percent of the country’s production, rose 24.61 percent to 148.42.
    句中的「which accounts for...」前方有逗號，屬於非限定性關係子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「manufacturing sector（製造業）」的佔比狀況，不可省略逗號或用 that 代替。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The ministry said that while the global economy remains impacted by various trade policies and geopolitical unease, Taiwan’s exports are expected to continue to benefit...
    句中的「remains impacted by...」與「are expected to...」皆使用了被動語態（be + 過去分詞），強調動作的承受者（global economy 與 Taiwan’s exports），在財經與官方新聞報導中非常常見。
