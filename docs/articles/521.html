文法重點

  • 過去分詞（片語）作後置修飾語
    The IF Illustration Festival, hosted by French illustration magazine Kiblind, will feature Taiwan as its theme country on Sept. 26-27 in Lyon, France.
    句中的「hosted by French illustration magazine Kiblind」是由過去分詞開頭的片語，用來修飾前面的主詞「The IF Illustration Festival」。這相當於一個被動語態的形容詞子句（which is hosted by...）的簡化版，能讓句子更緊湊、專業。
  • 「with + 受詞 + 分詞/形容詞」附帶狀況複合結構
    The art festival, titled "Focus Taiwan," invited three Taiwanese artists to take part in a month-long artist-in-residence program, with their works to be displayed at the festival in Lyon.
    「with + 受詞 + 補語」結構在英文中用來表達「在...的情況下」或伴隨發生的狀態。在此句中，「with their works to be displayed」說明了藝術家參與駐村計畫的同時，其作品也將隨之展出的附帶狀況。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句（補充說明）
    Yang, who studied in Brussels and is experienced in using vivid blocks of color to simplify lines and compositions, said she looks forward to working in Lyon, a city she has never visited.
    句中的「who studied in Brussels and is experienced in...」前方有逗號隔開，屬於「非限定用法」的關係子句。它對先行詞 Yang 提供額外補充資訊，如果拿掉這個子句，主要子句的核心意思仍然完整。
  • 虛主詞 It 與直述句構（強調重要性）
    Human beings are at the core of creation, and it is of the utmost importance to establish an art festival that emphasizes the artists' presence on site, Tourette said.
    句型「It is of the utmost importance to...」中，it 是代替後方不定詞片語「to establish...」的虛主詞。「of + 名詞」等同於形容詞（of the utmost importance = extremely important），常用於正式書信、新聞或商業談話中強調某事的關鍵性。
