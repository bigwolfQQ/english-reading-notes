文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle phrase as post-modifier)
    At the G20 finance ministers' summit that concluded on Sept. 1, China faced pressure to reduce the massive trade surplus that is an important component of its economy.
    句中的 "concluded on Sept. 1" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "summit"（峰會），相當於形容詞子句 "that concluded on Sept. 1"。這種結構在英文新聞中非常常見，能讓句子更簡潔。
  • Given that 引導的原因狀語從句 (Given that meaning 'considering that')
    That will be in part because the U.S. will have more influence over the agenda this time, given that the meeting is being held in Washington, Wang said.
    "given that" 意為「鑑於……」、「考慮到……」，用來引導一個表原因的子句。在多益閱讀測驗中常作為連接詞片語考驗邏輯語意。
  • 被動語態的進行式 (Present continuous passive voice)
    That will be in part because the U.S. will have more influence over the agenda this time, given that the meeting is being held in Washington, Wang said.
    句中的 "is being held" 採用了「現在進行式的被動語態」（is/am/are + being + 過去分詞），用來強調這場會議「目前正在被籌備／舉行中」，凸顯當下的動作狀態。
  • 虛主詞 It 句型配合名詞子句 (It is ... whether/if ...)
    Given Trump's low poll numbers and possible loss of control of the House of Representatives and the Senate in November, it is not clear if anything he can get from the September meeting would reverse his party's fortunes.
    句首的 "it" 為形式主詞（無實際意義），真正的的主詞是後面由 "if"（是否）所引導的名詞子句，用來探討不確定的狀況。這種句型在商業英文與新聞英語中常用於表達不確定性或評估。
