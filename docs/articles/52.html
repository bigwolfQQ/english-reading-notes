文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Postmodifier)
    A UK clinical trial known as “Sentinel” is testing whether a small patch of skin from an organ donor can help detect lung transplant rejection at an earlier stage.
    句中的 "known as 'Sentinel'" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞短語 "A UK clinical trial"，相當於形容詞子句 "which is known as 'Sentinel'" 的簡化版，省略了主詞與be動詞，使句子更精煉。
  • 分詞構句表結果 (Participial Phrase Expressing Result)
    The patch is grafted onto the patient’s forearm, making it possible to monitor visible changes such as rashes that may signal rejection before standard tests do.
    句尾的 ", making it..." 是現在分詞構句，置於主要子句之後，用來表達前面動作自然產生的結果。其中 it 為虛受詞，真正的受詞是後面的不定詞片語 "to monitor..."。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    In one case, a patient who received a lung transplant in 2024 noticed a purple rash on the patch...
    句中的 "who received a lung transplant in 2024" 是由關係代名詞 who 引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾先行詞 "a patient"，補充說明該病患的背景資訊。
