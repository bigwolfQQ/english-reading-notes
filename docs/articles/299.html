文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Taipei prosecutors have questioned an aide who allegedly met with Chang Chun-chieh (張俊傑), the office manager of indicted Legislator Kao Chin Su-mei (高金素梅), on Sunday before Chang jumped bail.
    句中的「indicted Legislator Kao Chin Su-mei」使用了過去分詞當作形容詞來修飾後方的名詞 Legislator Kao Chin Su-mei，表示「被起訴的立委」。這種結構在新聞英語中非常常見，用來簡潔地補充說明人物或事物的背景身分。
  • 分詞構句表附帶結果或補充說明 (Participial Phrase indicating Result/Circumstance)
    ...adding that she was later granted bail of NT$50,000 (US $1,589) with restrictions on her residence.
    句尾的「adding that...」是現在分詞構句，主詞承接前面的主句主旨，表示「補充說……」。這種用法省去了連接詞和主詞，讓兩個動作的發生具有連貫性，常用於報導中引述說法或補充後續發展。
  • 關係代間詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    The alleged contact came hours before Chang’s electronic wrist monitor sent out a low-battery alert on Sunday night, which led responding police to discover that Chang had gone missing...
    句中的「which led responding police to discover...」是一個非限定用法（逗號隔開）的關係代間詞子句，用來補充說明前面整子句發生的事情（電子腳鐐發出警報），並帶出其造成的結果。which 在子句中作主詞。
  • 儘管子句與介詞片語的對比 (Concessive Prepositional Phrase / Despite)
    This included registering several individuals, including her siblings, as legislative assistants, despite them not actually performing any duties, prosecutors alleged.
    「despite them not actually performing any duties」使用了「despite + 名詞/動名詞」的結構來表達「儘管……卻依然……」的讓步語氣。despite 後方接了代名詞所有格或受格（them）及其動名詞短語，表達不合常理的對比狀況。
