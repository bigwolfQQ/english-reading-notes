向東看齊，學習民主抵抗的課堂

Look east for lessons in democratic resistance

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • oust v. 罷免、驅逐、趕下臺
    joining forces to oust a would-be dictator.
    齊心協力將一位意圖成為獨裁者的人趕下臺。
  • trajectory n. 軌跡、發展方向
    central and eastern Europe’s trajectory from the Cold War to the recent election defeat
    中東歐從冷戰到最近選舉失敗的發展軌跡
  • prevail v. 盛行、佔優勢、戰勝
    autocratic rule prevailed in Taiwan, the Philippines and South Korea
    專制統治在臺灣、菲律賓和韓國佔據主導地位
  • crackdown n. 鎮壓、嚴厲取締
    They endured brutal crackdowns
    他們忍受了殘酷的鎮壓
  • breakthrough n. 突破
    Then came the breakthroughs.
    隨後迎來了突破。
  • resilience n. 韌性、恢復力
    East Asia has again produced a striking example of democratic resilience.
    東亞再次產生了一個令人矚目的民主韌性範例。
  • rally v. 集會、團結、聯合起來
    protesters, legislators and ordinary citizens in South Korea rallied against then-South Korean president Yoon Suk-yeol’s declaration of martial law
    韓國的抗議者、議員和普通公民挺身反抗時任韓國總統尹錫悅的戒嚴令宣佈
  • removal n. 罷免、去除、移除
    the process that removed him from office and led to his conviction
    將他罷免並導致其被定罪的進程
  • attribute v. 把……歸因於
    It is tempting to attribute the imbalance to enduring Eurocentrism.
    人們很容易將這種不平衡歸咎於根深蒂固的歐洲中心主義。
  • undermine v. 逐漸削弱、破壞
    those seeking to undermine democratic institutions increasingly learn from one another
    那些企圖破壞民主制度的人越來越多地互相學習
  • strategy n. 策略、戰略
    surveying the world for usable strategies.
    在全球範圍內考察可用的策略。
  • collaborate v. 合作
    collaborated with two academics on a Thai translation
    與兩位學者合作進行泰語翻譯

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表附帶狀況或結果）
    joining forces to oust a would-be dictator.
    句尾的現在分詞片語「joining forces...」用來補充說明主句主語（voters）同時進行的動作或自然產生的結果，中文常譯為「齊心協力……」。
  • 過去分詞作後置定語
    a country long marked by military coups and prolonged periods of military rule.
  • 現在分詞修飾名詞
    exuberant crowds taking sledgehammers to the Berlin Wall later that year.
    分詞片語「taking sledgehammers to...」在句中作後置定語，用來修飾 crowds，描述這些群體當下的動作，使畫面更具動態感。
