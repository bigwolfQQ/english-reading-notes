單字解析

  • comeback n. 復出、東山再起
    Although we can’t fly to Paris for singer Celine Dion’s comeback shows, we can still enjoy K-pop girl group IVE’s concerts in Taipei...
    雖然我們無法飛去巴黎聽席琳狄翁的復出演唱會，我們還可以去台北的女團IVE演唱會...
  • enjoy v. 享受、欣賞、享有
    ...we can still enjoy K-pop girl group IVE’s concerts in Taipei, or the hit “Tung! Take Me Away” concert in Taitung this weekend.
    ...我們還可以去台北的女團IVE演唱會、台東的《東！帶我走》系列演唱會。
  • hit adj. 受歡迎的、轟動的
    ...or the hit “Tung! Take Me Away” concert in Taitung this weekend.
    ...或是台東熱門的《東！帶我走》系列演唱會。
  • discussed adj. 被討論的
    Singer Post Malone’s show in Kaohsiung, and even the widely discussed “Beheading Heartbreaker Chen Shimei Arts Festival” in Tainan are also coming next weekend.
    歌手巨星馬龍的高雄演唱會，甚至轟動全國的台南《鍘美藝術節》也將登場。
  • feature v. 以...為特色、由...主演、包含
    I heard that Waterbomb Kaohsiung not only features top artists from Taiwan and South Korea, but also activities like the Blue Team and Yellow Team’s water fights.
    聽說水炸彈不僅有台灣與韓國的頂尖藝人，還有藍隊和黃隊的水槍大戰等活動。
  • activity n. 活動
    ...but also activities like the Blue Team and Yellow Team’s water fights.
    ...還有藍隊和黃隊的水槍大戰等活動。
  • include v. 包含、包括
    Saturday’s lineup includes “Sister” Jeannie Hsieh, OSN Kao, Twice’s Park Ji-hyo, Wonho and Winner.
    週六有︰「姐姐」謝金燕、高爾宣、Twice朴志效、元虎、Winner……等。
  • lineup n. 陣容、節目安排
    Sunday’s lineup includes EXO’s Kai, Gray, Loco, BTOB’s Huta as well as Kiss of Life.
    週日有︰EXO Kai、Gray、Loco、BTOB 李旼赫、Kiss of Life……等。

文法重點

  • 譲步副詞子句 (Although...)
    Although we can’t fly to Paris for singer Celine Dion’s comeback shows, we can still enjoy K-pop girl group IVE’s concerts in Taipei...
    使用 「Although + 子句」來表達「雖然...」，連接兩個對比的狀況，語句後半部用逗號隔開接主句。
  • 對等連接詞片語 not only... but also...
    I heard that Waterbomb Kaohsiung not only features top artists from Taiwan and South Korea, but also activities like the Blue Team and Yellow Team’s water fights.
    用來連接兩個對等的成分（此處連接兩個動詞片語 features 與 activities），強調「不僅 A，而且 B」。
  • 地方副詞置於句首的倒裝（此篇為直接列舉）
    Saturday’s lineup includes “Sister” Jeannie Hsieh... Sunday’s lineup includes EXO’s Kai...
    運用主詞 + includes + 受詞的句型結構，透過並列的句子（Saturday's lineup... / Sunday's lineup...）讓文章脈絡更清晰，在多益閱讀中常出現在商業行程或活動時間表的描述。
