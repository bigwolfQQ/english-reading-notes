副總統訪義結束返國

Vice president returns home after Italian visit

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • attend v. 出席，參加
    Vice President Hsiao Bi-khim (蕭美琴) returned to Taiwan Monday after being invited to attend the second Ventotene European Conference for Freedom and Democracy in Italy.
    副總統蕭美琴應邀前往義大利出席第二屆凡托內特歐洲自由與民主會議後，於週一返抵台灣。
  • deliver v. 發表（演說），遞送
    Upon her arrival at Taoyuan International Airport, Hsiao delivered remarks via livestream about her visit to the Italian island of Ventotene...
    抵達桃園國際機場後，蕭美琴透過直播就她對義大利凡托內特島的訪問發表談話……
  • arrival n. 到達，抵達
    Upon her arrival at Taoyuan International Airport, Hsiao delivered remarks via livestream...
    抵達桃園國際機場後，蕭美琴透過直播發表談話……
  • take part in phr. 參與，參加
    "Taking part in discussions on freedom, democracy and peace on this island is not a coincidence," Hsiao said.
    蕭美琴表示：「在這座島上參與關於自由、民主與和平的討論並非巧合。」
  • aspire v. 渴望，追求
    "Even though we are half a world away and come from different cultures, freedom is our common language, while peace is what we all aspire to," she said...
    她說：「儘管我們相隔半個地球且來自不同的文化，但自由是我們的共同語言，而和平則是我們眾人所渴望的……
  • circumstances n. 情況，環境，境況
    ...emphasizing that no matter how difficult the circumstances, Taiwan will bravely continue on its path of walking alongside the world.
    ……並強調無論環境多麼艱難，台灣都將勇敢地繼續與世界並肩同行的道路。
  • guest of honor n. phr. 特邀貴賓，榮譽嘉賓
    Hsiao attended the conference on Friday as a guest of honor, accompanied by Foreign Minister Lin Chia-lung (林佳龍).
    蕭美琴週五以特邀貴賓身分出席會議，由外交部長林佳龍陪同。
  • accompany v. 陪伴，陪同
    Hsiao attended the conference on Friday as a guest of honor, accompanied by Foreign Minister Lin Chia-lung (林佳龍).
    蕭美琴週五以特邀貴賓身分出席會議，由外交部長林佳龍陪同。
  • participation n. 參與，參加
    Her participation was not announced until after she arrived in Italy to prevent interference from Beijing.
    她的行程直到抵達義大利後才公布，以防止來自北京的干擾。
  • prevent v. 預防，防止，阻止
    Her participation was not announced until after she arrived in Italy to prevent interference from Beijing.
    她的行程直到抵達義大利後才公布，以防止來自北京的干擾。
  • take office phr. 就職，上任
    The trip was Hsiao's second to Europe since she took office in May 2024...
    這是蕭美琴自2024年5月上任以來第二次訪問歐洲……

文法重點

  • 介系詞加動名詞 (Upon + V-ing)
    Upon her arrival at Taoyuan International Airport, Hsiao delivered remarks...
    「Upon」或「On」後面接動名詞（V-ing）或名詞時，表示「一……就……」的時間先後順序。在此句中，「Upon her arrival」相當於「As soon as she arrived」，用來簡潔地表達她一抵達機場就立刻進行了直播談話。
  • 被動語態的分詞片語修飾 (Past Participle as Modifier)
    ...accompanied by Foreign Minister Lin Chia-lung (林佳龍).
    過去分詞片語「accompanied by...」在此作為後位修飾語（省略了主格關代與be動詞，原句形如 who was accompanied by...），用來補充說明主詞 Hsiao 在出席會議時是被外交部長林佳龍所陪同的。
  • Not... until... 句型
    Her participation was not announced until after she arrived in Italy to prevent interference from Beijing.
    「not... until...」意為「直到……才……」。句中強調蕭美琴的行程保密，直到她人已經抵達義大利之後，官方才將此消息公佈，藉此達到防範干擾的目的。
