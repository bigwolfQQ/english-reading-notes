副總統：台灣絕不屈服於自由

Taiwan will not yield freedom: VP

單字解析

  • compromise v. 妥協、危及、損害
    Taiwan will not compromise its freedoms or democracy in the face of intensifying Chinese military threats and “gray zone” pressure...
    面對日益加劇的中國軍事威脅與「灰色地帶」壓力，台灣不會妥協其自由或民主...
  • in the face of phr. 面對、在……面前
    Taiwan will not compromise its freedoms or democracy in the face of intensifying Chinese military threats and “gray zone” pressure...
    面對日益加劇的中國軍事威脅與「灰色地帶」壓力，台灣不會妥協其自由或民主...
  • coercion n. 脅迫、強迫
    “The reality is also that the Communist Party of China claims Taiwan and is coercing us into submission,” Hsiao said...
    蕭美琴表示：「現實情況也是，中國共產黨對台灣提出主權聲索，並正強迫我們屈服。」
  • submission n. 屈服、服從
    “The reality is also that the Communist Party of China claims Taiwan and is coercing us into submission,” Hsiao said...
    蕭美琴表示：「現實情況也是，中國共產黨對台灣提出主權聲索，並正強迫我們屈服。」
  • respond v. 回應、反應
    Asked whether Taiwan should draw a line beyond which it would respond to Chinese “gray zone” incursions, Hsiao said that “the threat to use force has never been acceptable.”
    被問及台灣是否應該畫出底線，一旦跨越便將對中國的「灰色地帶」入侵做出回應時，蕭美琴表示：「動武的威脅是絕對不能接受的。」
  • provoke v. 挑釁、激怒
    “We will not provoke, but at the same time, we will not yield by compromising our basic freedoms and our democracy,” she said.
    她說：「我們不會挑釁，但與此同時，我們也不會透過妥協我們基本的自由與民主來屈服。」
  • yield v. 屈服、產出、讓步
    “We will not provoke, but at the same time, we will not yield by compromising our basic freedoms and our democracy,” she said.
    她說：「我們不會挑釁，但與此同時，我們也不會透過妥協我們基本的自由與民主來屈服。」
  • approach n. 方法、途徑
    Taiwan must respond to growing coercion and threats by taking a more dynamic approach to strengthening and fortifying itself, Hsiao added.
    蕭美琴補充道，台灣必須透過採取更具動態的做法來自我強化與鞏固，以應對日益增加的脅迫與脅迫。
  • conduct n. 行為、舉止、處理方式
    The interview aired at about 1:30pm Taiwan time... regarding the growing presence and increasingly aggressive conduct of China Coast Guard vessels near Taiwan.
    該專訪於台灣時間下午約 1 時 30 分播出……內容談及中國海警船在台灣周遭日益增加的存在與愈發具侵略性的作為。
  • estimate v. 估計、估算
    The estimated daily average number of vessels rose 37.5 percent from summer last year.
    估計每日平均船隻數量比去年夏天上升了 3.75%。
  • surge v. 急遽上升、飆升
    East of Taiwan, where China Coast Guard vessels began patrolling for the first time last summer, the figure surged 3,800 percent over the same period.
    在台灣東部——中國海警船去年夏天首次開始巡邏的地方——該數字在同期飆升了 3,800%。
  • maintain v. 維持、保持、保養
    The Chinese ministry described the patrols as legitimate exercises of jurisdiction intended to maintain maritime order and regional stability.
    中國外交部將這些巡邏形容為旨在維持海上秩序與區域穩定的正當管轄行使。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況/結果
    Taiwan must respond to growing coercion and threats by taking a more dynamic approach to strengthening and fortifying itself, Hsiao added.
    句尾的「by taking...」是介系詞片語，運用動名詞帶出具體的方法或手段（藉由採取……的做法），在多益閱讀與聽力中常用於描述商業策略或解決方案的實施方式。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    An analysis conducted with the Futures Lab at the Center for Strategic and International Studies showed a record China Coast Guard presence in waters near Taiwan proper last summer.
  • 限定與非限定關係子句
    East of Taiwan, where China Coast Guard vessels began patrolling for the first time last summer, the figure surged 3,800 percent over the same period.
  • 現在分詞（動名詞）作主詞補語或形容詞用法
    “The reality is also that the Communist Party of China claims Taiwan and is coercing us into submission,” Hsiao said...
