文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The possibility that the company dismissed Cheng as part of a genuine corporate restructuring could not be ruled out beyond a reasonable doubt, resulting in an acquittal on the second count, Cheung said.
    句尾的「resulting in an acquittal...」是由現在分詞（resulting）引導的分詞片語，用來補充說明前面整件事情所導致的結果（即「因而導致第二項指控獲得無罪釋放」），在多益閱讀測驗的句意理解與文法題中非常常考。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The company was found not guilty of a second charge that alleged it unlawfully terminated Cheng’s employment contract on July 17, 2024...
    句中的「was found」為被動語態（be + 過去分詞），用來表達公司被法院裁定（判定）為無罪。商業與法律新聞常使用被動語態來客觀陳述判決、調查結果或合約執行情況。
  • 同位語子句 (Appositive Clause)
    The possibility that the company dismissed Cheng as part of a genuine corporate restructuring could not be ruled out...
    句中的「that the company dismissed...」是以 that 引導的同位語子句，用來詳細說明前面抽象名詞「possibility（可能性）」的具體內容。多益閱讀常在此處測試名詞與子句的搭配。
