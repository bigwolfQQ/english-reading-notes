醫生警告：過勞與壓力會損害視力

Overwork, stress can damage vision, doctor warns

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • prolonged adj. 長期的、拖延的
    Overwork and prolonged stress could damage vision, a doctor warned...
    醫生警告說，過度和長期的壓力可能會損害視力……
  • deterioration n. 惡化、退化
    ...experienced a sudden deterioration of the vision in her left eye.
    ……經歷了左眼視力的突然惡化。
  • office worker n. phr. 辦公室職員、上班族
    The office worker, surnamed Yang (楊), experienced the sudden drop of visual acuity...
    這名姓楊的上班族經歷了視力突然下降……
  • acuity n. （尤指視力或聽力的）敏銳度
    The office worker, surnamed Yang (楊), experienced the sudden drop of visual acuity in her left eye...
    這名姓楊的上班族經歷了左眼視力突然下降至 0.3……
  • diagnose v. 診斷
    She was diagnosed with central serous chorioretinopathy...
    她被診斷出罹患中心性漿液性脈絡膜視網膜病變……
  • treatment regimen n. phr. 治療方案、療程
    ...after undergoing an integrated treatment regimen combining traditional Chinese and Western medicine...
    ……在接受結合中醫和西醫的綜合治療方案後……
  • impair v. 損害、削弱
    Excessive eye use can impair blood and lymphatic circulation around the eyes...
    過度用眼會妨礙眼睛周圍的血液和淋巴循環……
  • symptom n. 症狀
    Typical symptoms include a dark spot in the center of the field of vision...
    典型症狀包括視野中心有一個黑點……
  • recurrent adj. 反覆發作的、復發的
    ...but recurrent episodes should not be taken lightly...
    ……但不應輕忽復發的情況……
  • regulate v. 調節、控制
    The treatment can stimulate microcirculation, help the accumulated fluid be naturally reabsorbed, and regulate the nerves around the eyes and intraocular pressure...
    這種治療可以刺激微循環、幫助積聚的液體自然吸收，並調節眼睛周圍的神經和眼壓……
  • approach n. 方法、途徑
    Combined with acupuncture at points farther from the eyes... the approach aims to address an underlying tendency toward inflammation and fluid retention...
    結合距離眼睛較遠穴位的針灸……這種方法旨在解決潛在的發炎和水分滯留傾向……
  • out of pocket idiom 自付費用的、實付的（現金支出）
    An ophthalmic acupuncture session costs about NT$200 out of pocket...
    一次眼針治療的自費費用大約是新台幣 200 元……
  • promptly adv. 迅速地、立即地
    ...should seek medical attention promptly...
    ……應該立即就醫……

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後置修飾（形容詞功能）
    The treatment can stimulate microcirculation, help the accumulated fluid be naturally reabsorbed, and regulate the nerves around the eyes and intraocular pressure, he said.
    句中的「accumulated」是由動詞 accumulate 轉變而來的過去分詞，用來修飾其後方的名詞「fluid（液體）」，相當於形容詞的功能，代表「被累積的液體」。
  • 受詞補語原形動詞用法（help + O + 原形V）
    The treatment can stimulate microcirculation, help the accumulated fluid be naturally reabsorbed, and regulate the nerves around the eyes and intraocular pressure, he said.
    動詞 help 後面接受詞「the accumulated fluid」時，其後的補語動詞可以使用原形動詞（reabsorbed）或帶 to 的不定詞（to be reabsorbed），此處省略了 to，用以表達幫助該液體被自然吸收。
  • 現在分詞片語表附帶狀況或原因
    Excessive eye use can impair blood and lymphatic circulation around the eyes, causing fluid to accumulate and resulting in localized swelling and increased intraocular pressure...
    句尾的「causing... and resulting in...」是由現在分詞構成的片語，用來補充說明前面「過度用眼會妨礙循環」這件事所帶來的自然結果，相當於「and it causes... and results in...」的簡化。
  • 被動語態的應用
    She was diagnosed with central serous chorioretinopathy, a condition in which fluid leaks under the retina...
    句中的「was diagnosed」使用了「be + 過去分詞」的被動語態結構，強調主角（她）是接受診斷的對象，而非主動去診斷別人。
  • 關係代間詞結合介系詞（in which）引導形容詞子句
    She was diagnosed with central serous chorioretinopathy, a condition in which fluid leaks under the retina...
    「in which」用來代替前面提到的先行詞「a condition」，在子句中作介系詞 in 的受詞，等於「where」，用來補充說明這種病症發生的狀況（液體在視網膜下滲漏）。
