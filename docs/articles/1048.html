超越文化握手

Beyond the cultural handshake

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • diplomacy n. 外交，外交手腕
    Serving as interpreter during Culture Minister Li Yuan’s visit to Britain offered an unusual view of cultural diplomacy.
    在文化部長李遠訪英期間擔任口譯，讓我對文化外交有了很不一樣的見解。
  • delegation n. 代表團
    During four intensive days, from Sept. 6 to Sept. 9, the delegation moved between museums, arts centers, universities and literary organizations.
    在從 9 月 6 日到 9 月 9 日這緊湊的四天裡，代表團穿梭於博物館、藝術中心、大學和文學機構之間。
  • intensive adj. 密集的，強集的
    During four intensive days, from Sept. 6 to Sept. 9, the delegation moved between museums, arts centers, universities and literary organizations.
    在從 9 月 6 日到 9 月 9 日這緊湊的四天裡，代表團穿梭於博物館、藝術中心、大學和文學機構之間。
  • distribution n. 分發，分配
    In Taiwan, debates about cultural equality often concern the uneven distribution of resources between regions.
    在台灣，關於文化平權的辯論往往涉及地區之間資源分佈不均的問題。
  • storage n. 儲存，庫存
    The Ministry of Culture is considering converting a former university campus in Tainan into a joint storage center for collections that existing museums cannot accommodate.
    文化部正在考慮將台南的一處前大學校園改建為現有博物館無法容納之館藏的聯合典藏中心。
  • accommodate v. 容納，為...提供住宿
    The Ministry of Culture is considering converting a former university campus in Tainan into a joint storage center for collections that existing museums cannot accommodate.
    文化部正在考慮將台南的一處前大學校園改建為現有博物館無法容納之館藏的聯合典藏中心。
  • generate v. 產生，引起
    The British model cannot simply be transplanted, but it shows how practical constraints can generate cultural innovation.
    英國的模式不能直接移植，但它展示了實務限制如何能激發文化創新。
  • infrastructure n. 基礎建設
    Literary quality alone does not carry a book across borders; it needs an infrastructure of selection, translation, advocacy and trust.
    僅憑文學品質並無法讓一本書跨越國界；它需要選書、翻譯、推廣與信任的基礎建設。
  • attract v. 吸引
    The program has already attracted 16 applicants, a notable response for a specialized mentorship.
    該計畫已經吸引了 16 名申請者，對一個專業的導師計畫來說，這是一個顯著的迴響。
  • professional adj. 專業的
    Translation requires years of language learning, cultural immersion and professional development.
    翻譯需要多年的語言學習、文化沉浸和專業發展。
  • cooperation n. 合作
    Its Festival of Korean Dance demonstrates what can happen when cooperation grows from individual appearances into a recognizable platform encompassing residencies, performances, touring and audience development.
    其韓國舞蹈節展示了當合作從個別的演出發展成為涵蓋駐村、表演、巡演和觀眾開發的知名平台時，會發生什麼樣的成果。
  • expertise n. 專業知識，專長
    Their value emerges later, if those who met continue writing to one another, train translators, invite artists, share expertise and build programs together.
    它們的價值會在後來顯現，如果那些相遇的人繼續保持書信往來、培訓譯者、邀請藝術家、分享專業知識並共同建立計畫。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Serving as interpreter during Culture Minister Li Yuan’s visit to Britain offered an unusual view of cultural diplomacy — not as ceremony, but as a series of conversations capable of becoming lasting relationships.
    句尾的 "capable of becoming lasting relationships" 是形容詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "conversations"。這種結構省略了 "which are"，能讓句子更緊湊、具有新聞英文的流暢感。
  • 疑問詞引導的名詞子句作受詞 (Wh- clause as Object)
    Yet one question connected these encounters: How can an initial meeting become a relationship that lasts?
    冒號後方的 "How can an initial meeting become a relationship that lasts?" 雖然保留了問句形式，但在整句話中扮演說明冒號前「一個問題」的內容。在寫作與多益閱讀中常作為重點訊息的帶入方式。
  • 假設語氣/條件句的應用 (Conditional structure)
    If Taiwan wants more of its literature to reach English-language readers, it must cultivate translators before their names are known.
    使用 "If + 現在式動詞, 主詞 + 助動詞(must/will) + 原形動詞" 的真實條件句（First Conditional），用來表達如果達成某個條件（台灣希望文學觸及英語讀者），就必須採取對應的行動（培養譯者）。這是多益商業與新聞英文中極常見的表達邏輯與規則。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    The most promising conversations were those in which both sides began to imagine something neither could create separately.
    句中的 "in which" 引導關係子句修飾先行詞 "those"（即 conversations），其中 "neither could create separately" 又是省略了關係代名詞 (that/which) 的形容詞子句，用來修飾 "something"。多益閱讀測驗常藉此測試應試者對複雜修飾語與子句結構的理解。
