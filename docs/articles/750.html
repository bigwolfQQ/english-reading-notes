沙烏地阿拉伯矢言對攻擊做出堅決回應

Saudi Arabia vows firm response to attacks

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • vow v. 發誓、矢言
    Saudi Arabia yesterday vowed to respond “firmly” to attacks by Yemen’s Houthis...
    沙烏地阿拉伯昨天矢言，將對葉門「青年運動」的攻擊做出「堅決」回應……
  • respond v. 回應、反應
    Saudi Arabia responded with more than 50 strikes, as it had the day before, the Houthis said.
    青年運動表示，沙烏地阿拉伯隨後發動了 50 多次空襲進行回應，正如前一天一樣。
  • blockade n. 封鎖
    ...when the group declared a maritime blockade on Riyadh and began targeting its ships in the Red Sea.
    ……當時該團體宣布對利雅德實施海上封鎖，並開始將其在紅海的船隻作為攻擊目標。
  • target v. 把……作為攻擊目標、瞄準
    The Houthis have also targeted the oil infrastructure of Saudi Arabia...
    青年運動還將沙烏地阿拉伯的石油基礎設施作為攻擊目標……
  • infrastructure n. 基礎設施
    The Houthis have also targeted the oil infrastructure of Saudi Arabia...
    青年運動還將沙烏地阿拉伯的石油基礎設施作為攻擊目標……
  • exporter n. 出口商
    ...Saudi Arabia, the world’s leading crude exporter, pushing global oil prices above the US$100 threshold.
    ……沙烏地阿拉伯——這個全球主要的原油出口國——推動全球油價突破每桶 100 美元的門檻。
  • threshold n. 門檻、開端
    ...pushing global oil prices above the US$100 threshold.
    ……推動全球油價突破每桶 100 美元的門檻。
  • confirm v. 確認、證實
    The Riyadh-led coalition yesterday confirmed there had been missile and drone attacks...
    由利雅德領導的聯軍昨天證實曾發生飛彈與無人機攻擊……
  • damage n. 損害、損失
    ...reporting 13 civilians wounded and damage to seven houses and two vehicles.
    ……並通報有 13 名平民受傷、7 棟房屋及 2 輛車受損。
  • measure n. 措施、方法
    ...and would take “all necessary operational measures to deter this ... hostile and extremist approach.”
    ……並將採取「所有必要的營運措施來遏制這種……敵對且極端主義的做法」。
  • shortfall n. 短缺、不足
    Meanwhile, the US is facing a shortfall in ammunition as a result of its war against Iran...
    同時，由於對伊朗的戰爭，美國正面臨彈藥短缺的問題……
  • cost n. 成本、費用
    ...Operation Epic Fury (OEF) had an estimated cost of US$33.4 billion...
    ……「史詩之怒行動」（OEF）的估計成本為 334 億美元……

文法重點

  • 現在完成進行式 (Present Perfect Continuous Tense)
    The Houthis have been engaged in renewed fighting with Saudi-backed government forces since Yemen’s civil war reignited in July...
    用來表示一個動作從過去某個時間點開始，持續進行到現在（且可能持續下去）。在此句中，「have been engaged」強調青年運動與政府軍自七月內戰再起以來，持續處於交戰狀態。
  • 關係代間子句（非限定用法）
    They seized control of Yemen’s Red Sea coast and the Bab al-Mandab Strait last week, which has become vital as the wider US-Iran war chokes the Strait of Hormuz.
    句尾的「which has become vital...」是由關係代間詞 which 引導的非限定子句，前面通常加逗號，用來補充說明前面的先行詞（指前面提到的紅海海岸與海峽），不能省略。
  • 分詞作為形容詞（過去分詞修飾名詞）
    ...the munitions expenditure on OEF has resulted in strategic inventory shortfalls and revealed industrial base bottlenecks for munitions resupply...
    句中的「expended munitions」意指「被消耗的彈藥」，其中 expended 是過去分詞（分詞構形的形容詞化），用來修飾名詞 munitions，表示被動或已完成的狀態。
