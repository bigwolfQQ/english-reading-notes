中國能縮小與美國的技術差距嗎？

Will China close the technology gap with the US?

單字解析

  • development n. 發展
    As the rapid development of artificial intelligence triggers a renewed debate about its risks, the US and China keep racing...
    隨著人工智慧的快速發展引發了對其風險的新一輪辯論，美國和中國持續競相...
  • capability n. 能力、性能
    The US has outpaced China and other countries in AI model capabilities, research, citations and other areas.
    美國在人工智慧模型的效能、研究、引用次數及其他領域一直領先中國和其他國家。
  • restrict v. 限制、約束
    ...even as US-led restrictions have blocked China from accessing some of the most advanced technologies...
    ...儘管美國主導的限制措施阻礙中國取得某些最先進的技術...
  • access v./n. 使用權、存取、接近
    ...blocked China from accessing some of the most advanced technologies, including the cutting-edge chips...
    ...阻礙中國取得某些最先進的技術，包括尖端晶片...
  • startup n. 新創公司
    Beijing-based startup Moonshot AI made a splash with the release of Kimi K3...
    總部位於北京的新創公司 Moonshot AI 隨著 Kimi K3 的發布而引起轟動...
  • cost-effective adj. 具成本效益的、划算的
    The company’s R1 model, released early last year, was more cost-effective than similar US models.
    該公司於去年初發布的 R1 模型比類似的美國模型更具成本效益。
  • accelerate v. 加速、促進
    Top Chinese leaders have said China should accelerate AI development and pursue technological self-reliance.
    中國高層領導人曾表示，中國應加速人工智慧發展並追求技術自立。
  • maintain v. 維持、保持

    在美國，川普及其政府一再強調美國保持對中國人工智慧優勢的重要性。
  • accuse v. 指控、控告
    The V has accused China’s AI developers of engaging in what it has called “industrial-scale distillation”...

  • comprehensive adj. 全面的、綜合的

  • transfer n./v. 轉移、調動
    But now “it may also have to think about the outputs of frontier AI as a form of technology transfer,” she said.
    但她說，現在「它可能也必須將前沿人工智慧的輸出端視為一種技術移轉的形式來思考」。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作修飾語 / 分詞構句
    The company’s R1 model, released early last year, was more cost-effective than similar US models.
    句中的「released early last year」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「The company’s R1 model」，等於形容詞子句「which was released early last year」的簡化，讓句子更精鍊。
  • Even as 引導讓步子句
    China has been rapidly catching up as an AI superpower even as US-led restrictions have blocked China from accessing some of the most advanced technologies...
    「even as」意指「甚至在……的時候 / 儘管」，用來連接兩個同時發生但帶有對比或轉折意味的子句，強調即使在美國有限制的情況下，中國仍快速迎頭趕上。
  • What 引導的名詞子句（作為介詞受詞）
    ...experts say the future of AI will depend significantly on what China and the US do and don’t do.
    片語「on」後面接了一個由「what」引導的名詞子句「what China and the US do and don’t do」，在句中作為介詞「on」的受詞，用來具體說明未來將取決於什麼內容。
  • 不定詞片語作主詞補語 / 目的
    Top Chinese leaders have said China should accelerate AI development and pursue technological self-reliance.
    動詞「should」後接原形動詞「accelerate」與「pursue」，構成平行結構的受詞，表達中國應當採取的具體行動方針。
