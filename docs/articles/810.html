台灣作品入圍美國國家圖書獎翻譯文學長名單

Taiwan work makes National Book Award for Translated Literature longlist

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • recognition n. 讚賞、認可、承認
    marking the second such recognition for a Taiwanese writer after Yang Shuang-zi
    標誌著繼楊双子之後，台灣作家獲得的第二項此類肯定
  • nonprofit n. / adj. 非營利組織、非營利性的
    according to the National Book Foundation, an American nonprofit, which announced the longlist Tuesday.
    根據於週二公布長名單的美國非營利組織國家圖書基金會指出。
  • announce v. 宣布、公布
    The foundation said it will announce the finalists on Oct. 6
    基金會表示將於 10 月 6 日公布決賽入圍名單
  • reveal v. 透露、揭曉
    with the winner revealed live at the 77th National Book Awards Ceremony on Nov. 18.
    得獎者將於 11 月 18 日舉行的第 77 屆國家圖書獎頒獎典禮上現場揭曉。
  • attribute to phr. v. 將……歸因於
    Ping Lu, whose real name is Lu Ping (路平), attributed the recognition to Tiang's translation
    本名路平的平路將這項肯定歸功於程異的翻譯
  • contribution n. 貢獻
    saying he writes well in English and that "my contribution may only be about 10 percent."
    表示他英文寫得很好，並說「我的貢獻可能只有 10% 左右。」
  • director n. 主任、主管、董事
    after working in Hong Kong as director of the Kwang Hwa Information and Culture Center
    在香港擔任光華新聞文化中心主任之後
  • circumstances n. 處境、情況（常用複數）
    The novel reflected her circumstances at the time
    這部小說反映了他當時的處境
  • relationship n. 關係
    To some extent, the novel is also about the relationship between an island and the mainland
    從某種程度上來說，這部小說也關於島嶼與大陸之間的關係
  • pursuit n. 追求
    one's ideal life is often linked to the pursuit of freedom
    一個人的理想生活通常與對自由的追求息息相關

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表示伴隨狀況或結果）
    marking the second such recognition for a Taiwanese writer after Yang Shuang-zi
    句尾的「marking...」是由連接詞和子句簡化而來的分詞構句（原句概念約為「which marks...」），用來補充說明前面整件事情所帶來的結果或意義。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    Translated from Chinese by Jeremy Tiang (程異), the novel follows a Taiwanese writer's trip...
    句首的「Translated from Chinese by Jeremy Tiang」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾後面的主詞 the novel（小說是被翻譯的，故用過去分詞 Translated），相當於「The novel, which was translated from Chinese by Jeremy Tiang...」。
  • 關係代間詞非限定用法（Non-defining relative clause）
    ...according to the National Book Foundation, an American nonprofit, which announced the longlist Tuesday.
  • 動名詞片語作主詞
    ...exploring how a person examines their inner emotions from an outside perspective.
