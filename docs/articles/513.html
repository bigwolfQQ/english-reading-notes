多納文深入剖析：台灣、日本與菲律賓需要共同面對來自北京的威脅

Donovan’s Deep Dives: Taiwan, Japan and the Philippines need to face the common threat from Beijing

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • encroach v. 侵佔、侵犯、漸進干擾
    For a time, it became popular to describe the tactics of the People’s Republic of China (PRC) in encroaching on sovereignty and militarizing islands in the South China Sea as “salami slicing.”
    曾有一陣子，大家流行將中華人民共和國（PRC）侵犯主權以及將南海島嶼軍事化的戰術形容為「切香腸」。
  • ramp up phr. v. 逐步增加、提高、擴大
    The Chinese Coast Guard has likewise been ramping up their activities, and the civilian paramilitary People’s Armed Forces Maritime Militia has been backing up the navy and coast guard with alarming swarming maneuvers.
    中國海警也同樣在加強他們的活動，而民間準軍事組織中國海上民兵則以令人擔憂的蜂擁戰術支援海軍和海警。
  • arbitral tribunal n. phr. 仲裁庭
    In July 2016, an UNCLOS arbitral tribunal ruled that China had no legal basis for claiming historic resource rights beyond those provided by the convention within the nine-dash line.
    2016年7月，《聯合國海洋法公約》仲裁庭裁定，中國在九段線內主張歷史資源權利的法律依據，超出了公約所提供的範圍。
  • presage v. 預示、預兆
    The upgrading of the nine-dash line to 10 included a dash off the east coast of Taiwan, which presaged the recent regular “law enforcement” activities by the Chinese coast guard.
    九段線升級為十段線包括了台灣東海岸外的一段，這預示著中國海警最近經常性的「執法」活動。
  • objective n. 目標、目的
    For the moment, the manufactured claim may serve only as a flexible diplomatic and legal pressure point to advance immediate objectives: disrupting Japan–Philippines maritime boundary talks and escalating cognitive warfare against Taiwan.
    目前，製造出來的主張可能僅僅作為一個靈活的外交和法律施壓點，以推進眼前目標：破壞日菲海上邊界談判，並升級針對台灣的認知作戰。
  • appeasement n. 綏靖政策、姑息
    Appeasement has always failed over the long term with hegemonic, dictatorial regimes like the PRC’s, but fear remains a powerful motivator for some in closing one’s eyes to historical precedent and hoping somehow this time things will be different.
    對於像中華人民共和國這樣霸權、獨裁的政權，綏靖政策長期以來總是失敗，但對於某些人來說，恐懼仍然是一個強大的動機，讓他們選擇視而不見歷史先例，並希望這次事情不知為何會有所不同。
  • eventuality n. 可能發生之事、不測事件
    They are laying the groundwork for such an eventuality, such as a withdrawal of US forces, or the calculation in Beijing that their relative military advantage is worth the risk.
    他們正在為這種可能性鋪路，例如美軍撤離，或是北京評估其相對軍事優勢足以承擔風險。
  • inalienable adj. 不可剝奪的、不能讓渡的
    Recently, in joint diplomatic statements, Beijing has been increasingly pressuring other countries to strongly reaffirm their support for China’s “one China principle” that Taiwan is an “inalienable” part of China’s territory.
    最近，在聯合外交聲明中，北京一直在施壓其他國家，要求其強烈重申支持中國的「一個中國原則」，即台灣是中國領土「不可分割」的一部分。
  • traverse v. 橫越、穿過
    Both nations are treaty allies of the US, and those key geographical positions allow careful monitoring of Chinese navy activities as they traverse through the first island chain.
    這兩個國家都是美國的條約盟友，這些關鍵的地理位置使得各方能夠仔細監控中國海軍穿過第一島鏈時的活動。
  • dovetail v. 吻合、契合、密合
    It would also dovetail with the recent Chinese propaganda on the sovereign status of the Batanes.
    這也與近期中國關於巴坦群島主權地位的宣傳不謀而合。
  • uptick n. 小幅上升、增加
    The recent uptick in aggressiveness may be related to Washington being distracted by its war in Iran, and US President Donald Trump’s failure to stand against Beijing’s hostility towards Japan and its Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi.
    近期激進態勢的激增，可能與華盛頓因伊朗戰爭而分心，以及美國總統唐納·川普未能站出來反對北京對日本及其首相高市早苗的敵意有關。
  • interoperability n. 互通性、協同運作能力
    A bigger factor is likely recent moves by Japan and the Philippines to both boost their individual military capabilities while simultaneously deepening their mutual cooperation and interoperability, which Beijing views with alarm, judging by their party-state media going into hyperbolic overdrive.
    一個更大的因素可能是日本和菲律賓最近採取行動，既提升各自的軍事能力，同時深化彼此的合作與互通性，這引起了北京的驚慌，從其黨政媒體進入激進的過熱狀態即可看出端倪。
  • imperative n. 當務之急、必須的事項
    Though Washington remains a key partner, all of these recent moves by Beijing increase the imperative for Taiwan to closely coordinate with Japan and the Philippines.
    儘管華盛頓依然是一個關鍵夥伴，但北京最近的所有這些舉動，都增加了台灣與日本及菲律賓緊密協調的迫切性。

文法重點

  • 現在完成進行式 (Present Perfect Continuous Tense)
    The People’s Liberation Army has been increasing its navy and air force activities in the region.
  • 分詞片語作後置修飾 (Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    Beijing has not formally laid claim to Okinawa, but there is the strong implication they might, or press for the restoration of the Ryukyu Kingdom, which had once been a Chinese tributary state.
  • 假設語氣 (Subjunctive / Conditional Structure)
    If true, this is alarming.
