台灣與美國必須反制中國灰色地帶壓力：史丹佛學者

Taiwan, U.S. must counter China gray-zone pressure: Stanford scholar

單字解析

  • deter v. 嚇阻，阻止
    Taiwan and the United States need to prepare for a crisis triggered by China’s intensifying gray-zone pressure rather than focus only on deterring a full-scale conflict
    台灣和美國需要為中國不斷加劇的灰色地帶壓力所引發的危機做好準備，而不是只把焦點放在嚇阻全面性衝突上
  • comply v. 遵守，順從
    If the shipping companies comply with this, if the international insurance system says that we will not insure cargoes that don’t comply with PRC directives...
    如果航運公司順從這一點，如果國際保險系統表示我們將不為不遵守中國共產黨指令的貨物承保...
  • jurisdiction n. 管轄權，司法權
    In June, Beijing began what it called a “special maritime law-enforcement operation” in the area, with Chinese vessels radioing commercial ships and asking about their routes while asserting jurisdiction over the waters.
    今年六月，北京在該地區展開了他所謂的「特別海上執法行動」，中國船隻透過無線電聯繫商船並詢問其航線，同時對這些水域主張管轄權。
  • disruption n. 中斷，擾亂
    Even the prospect of financial markets beginning to price in disruptions to Taiwan’s chip supplies could cause a sharp fall in major U.S. technology stocks...
    即使是金融市場開始將台灣晶片供應中斷的預期納入定價的前景，也可能導致美國主要科技股急劇下跌...
  • export n. / v. 出口
    He argued that China itself would have little incentive to directly cut off Taiwan’s semiconductor exports because doing so would make Beijing clearly responsible for any resulting global economic shock.
    他主張，中國本身幾乎沒有動機直接切斷台灣的半導體出口，因為這麼做會讓北京明確為任何隨之而來的全球經濟震撼負起責任。
  • sanction n. 制裁
    If Washington then sent forces to the region, imposed sanctions or took other punitive measures, “politically it looks like it’s the United States initiating the charge,” Freymann said.
    弗雷曼表示，如果華盛頓隨後向該地區派遣兵力、實施制裁或採取其他懲罰性措施，「在政治上看起來就像是美國在挑起這場衝突」。
  • disclose v. 揭露，公開
    Taiwan could also require shipping companies to publicly disclose whether they are complying with Chinese directives, Freymann said...
    弗雷曼表示，台灣也可以要求航運公司公開披露他們是否遵守中國的指令...
  • urgency n. 緊急，急迫性
    The urgency, Freymann said, stems from the Chinese Communist Party’s national congress expected next autumn...
    弗雷曼表示，這種急迫性源於預計於明年秋天舉行的中國共產黨全國代表大會...
  • incentive n. 動機，誘因，鼓勵
    He argued that China itself would have little incentive to directly cut off Taiwan’s semiconductor exports...
    他主張，中國本身幾乎沒有動機直接切斷台灣的半導體出口...

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Taiwan and the United States need to prepare for a crisis triggered by China’s intensifying gray-zone pressure rather than focus only on deterring a full-scale conflict...
    句中的 "triggered by..." 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "a crisis"，相當於形容詞子句 "which is triggered by..." 的簡化版，能讓句子更精簡緊湊。
  • 虛主詞 It 的用法 (Introductory 'It')
    “Taiwan’s fate is more likely to be determined by a crisis than by a war,” Eyck Freymann, a Hoover Fellow at Stanford’s Hoover Institution, said during a panel on democratic resilience and national security.
    句型中 "be likely to..." 用於表達很有可能發生的事。主詞較長時常搭配 be 動詞與形容詞，而這裡是以 "Taiwan’s fate" 作為主詞，結構清楚明瞭。
  • 現在分詞表主動與背景說明 (Present Participle)
    In June, Beijing began what it called a “special maritime law-enforcement operation” in the area, with Chinese vessels radioing commercial ships and asking about their routes while asserting jurisdiction over the waters.
    句尾的 "with Chinese vessels radioing... and asking... while asserting..." 是 「with + 名詞 + 分詞」的獨立分詞結構，用來補充說明主句發生時的伴隨狀況或背景細節。
  • 假設語氣/條件句 (Conditional Clause)
    “If the shipping companies comply with this, if the international insurance system says that we will not insure cargoes that don’t comply with PRC directives, China can effectively take control of the maritime space around Taiwan without firing a shot,” Freymann said.
    此處使用 "If + 現在式動詞..., 主詞 + 조動詞 (can) + 原形動詞" 的第一類真實條件句，用來推論在特定假設情況下必然或可能產生的結果。
