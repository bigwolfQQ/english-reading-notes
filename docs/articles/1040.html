文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Present Participle）作後位修飾
    ...protests by players and fans over the killings of Palestinians during the genocide Israel is committing in Gaza.
    句中的「is committing in Gaza」是現在分詞片語（省略了受格關代 which/that），用來修飾前面的名詞「genocide（種族滅絕）」，說明這個種族滅絕是誰在進行的、在哪裡進行的。
  • 過去分詞（Past Participle）作後位修飾
    The matches are set to be staged at neutral venues and behind closed doors after protests by players and fans...
    句中的「staged at neutral venues」是由過去分詞開頭的片語，用來修飾主詞「The matches（比賽）」，表達「被舉辦、被安排」的被動概念。
  • 現在分詞短語表附帶狀況或結果（分詞構句）
    The FAI later committed to playing the fixtures, citing sanctions that could affect the national team and the association.
    句尾的「citing sanctions...」是由分詞構句簡化而來，原本可能是「and they cited sanctions...」，用來補充說明足協承諾出賽背後所持的理由。
  • 假設語氣（Subjunctive）用於 demand/request 等動詞後面的名詞子句
    ...voted overwhelmingly late last year for its board to request that UEFA immediately suspend the Israel Football Association from European competitions.
    在表示要求（request）的動詞後面的 that 子句中，動詞通常使用原形動詞（在此為 suspend，省略了助動詞 should），展現多益測驗非常喜歡考的文法考點。
