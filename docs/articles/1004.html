文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The US would not cancel a proposed US$14 billion arms sale package to Taiwan after US President Donald Trump’s meeting with Chinese President Xi Jinping (習近平) this week, National Security Council adviser Alex Huang (黃重諺) said in a radio interview broadcast yesterday.
    句尾的「broadcast yesterday」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「a radio interview」，省略了「which was」，使句子更為精簡。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Nominative Absolute Construction)
    Xi is slated to arrive in Washington today, with a planned summit with Trump tomorrow.
    「with + 名詞 + 過去分詞片語（planned summit）」形成獨立分詞構句，用來補充說明主句發生時的伴隨狀況或背景條件。
  • 現在分詞作補充說明 (Present Participle Indicating Result/Action)
    For example, a think tank article published this month argued that the US should pull back from the First Island Chain, he said, appearing to reference a Sept. 10 article by Jennifer Kavanagh published by Defense Priorities.
    句尾的「appearing to reference...」是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明主句中說話者的狀態或行為（似乎在指稱...），作結果或狀態的補充描述。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞 / 名詞子句當受詞 (Gerund Phrase & Noun Clause)
    Fears Taiwan would become a bargaining chip during a US-Chinese leader’s summit is a recurring theme in Taiwanese politics, but nothing like that has happened in the past few decades, Huang said.
    「Fears [that] Taiwan would become...」中包含了省略連接詞 that 的名詞子句，用來補充說明 fears 的具體內容；整個片語作為主詞，搭配單數動詞 is。
