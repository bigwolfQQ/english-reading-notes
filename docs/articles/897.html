環境部將修正室內空氣品質法規

Ministry to revise indoor air quality regulations

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • revise v. 修訂，修改（法規、計畫等）
    The Ministry of Environment yesterday said it is proposing revisions to the Indoor Air Quality Management Act...
    環境部昨天表示，正擬議修正《室內空氣品質管理法》...
  • regulation n. 規章，法規
    ...including stricter regulations for facilities serving young children and older adults...
    ...其中包括對服務幼童及高齡者的設施實施更嚴格的規範...
  • facility n. 設施，場所
    ...adjusted requirements for dedicated indoor air quality personnel according to the size of a facility.
    ...以及根據設施規模調整專責室內空氣品質人員的規定。
  • consult v. 諮詢，請教
    The ministry held three meetings with experts and academics last month and plans to consult local environment protection bureaus with practical enforcement experience next month.
    環境部上個月與專家學者舉行了三次會議，並計畫於下個月諮詢具備實際執法經驗的地方環境保護局。
  • draft n. 草案，草稿
    The timeline for completing the draft amendments remains uncertain, Huang said.
    黃偉鳴表示，完成草案修訂的時間表目前仍不確定。
  • aim v. 旨在，以……為目標
    The ministry aims to establish standards by the end of this year for simplified continuous automatic devices to help monitor air quality in indoor facilities, he said.
    他說，環境部目標在今年底前建立簡化式連續自動設備的標準，以協助監測室內設施的空氣品質。
  • appoint v. 指派，任命
    The thresholds for requiring facilities to appoint dedicated indoor air quality management personnel would also be revised and adjusted based on the characteristics and size of each facility, he added.
    他並補充說，要求設施指派專責室內空氣品質管理人員的門檻，也將根據各設施的特性與規模進行修正與調整。
  • initiative n. 倡議，新計畫
    The ministry has finished an initiative started last year to help kindergartens improve indoor air quality, Huang said...
    環境部表示已完成去年啟動、旨在協助幼兒園改善室內空氣品質的倡議...
  • comprehensive adj. 全面的，綜合的
    The UN has discussed epidemic-prevention issues, saying that following the COVID-19 pandemic, disease prevention should be considered comprehensively, she said.
    她表示，聯合國已討論過防疫議題，並指出在COVID-19大流行之後，疾病預防應該被全面考量。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The eighth annual Taiwan Indoor Air Quality Week, organized by the Taiwan Indoor Environment Quality Management Association, opened yesterday.
    句中用過去分詞片語「organized by...」來修飾前面的專有名詞「Taiwan Indoor Air Quality Week」，相當於形容詞子句「which was organized by...」的簡化，能讓句子更精簡流暢。
  • 現在分詞表達附帶結果或補充說明 (Present Participle)
    ...adding that the results would be announced as soon as possible.
    動詞後面接「adding that...」的現在分詞結構，用來表達主句動作發生時的同時狀態或進一步的補充說明（即「補充說……」），在新聞英語中非常常見。
  • 地方副詞子句與條件句運用 (Adverbial Clause of Place & Condition)
    In France, where air-conditioning penetration is only about 20 percent, vulnerable groups were exposed to heat-related harm amid heat waves in Europe this summer...
    句中的「where air-conditioning penetration is only about 20 percent」為關係副詞 where 引導的非限定子句，用來補充修飾先行詞「France」，提供額外的背景資訊。
