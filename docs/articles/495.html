文法重點

  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況或結果 (Participial Phrase)
    However, the preceding line, being concerned with selecting the worthy and capable, and fostering trust and harmony, might prove to be even more difficult.
    句中的 "being concerned with..." 是由現在分詞 "being" 引導的分詞構句，用來修飾主詞 "the preceding line"（前一句），補充說明其所涉及的事物，等同於形容詞子句 "which is concerned with..." 的簡化。
  • 分裂句強調語氣 (Cleft Sentence)
    ...it was only after excavation began that fire mains from 1988, unidentified power lines and Chunghwa Telecom Co cables were discovered on site.
  • 虛主詞 It 的用法 (Dummy Subject)
    Perhaps not all of these issues were foreseeable, but the question of how many among them could have been caught in advance is still worth asking.
    句尾的 "it is still worth asking" 中，"It" 為虛主詞，真正的主詞是前面的整段討論或省略掉的事實。此外，"be worth + V-ing" 也是多益常見的實用句型，表示「值得做某事」。
  • 對等連接詞連接平行結構 (Parallel Structure)
    True virtue is not loyalty to one’s leader, but responsibility to the people. True capability is not about giving good presentations, but solving people’s problems.
