數據顯示：今年旅宿業罰鍰總計達新台幣 1.43389 億元

Lodging fines total NT$143.389m this year, data show

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • total v. 總計，合計
    accommodation-sector fines totaled NT$143.389 million (US$4.5 million), Tourism Administration data showed.
    觀光署數據顯示，住宿業的罰鍰總計達新台幣 1.43389 億元（約合 450 萬美元）。
  • decline n. 下降，減少
    a slight decline from the same period last year, the administration said, attributing the decline to individual business decisions regarding the market.
    該署表示，這比去年同期略有下滑，並將此降幅歸因於個別業者對市場的商業決策。
  • attribute to phr. v. 將……歸因於……
    attributing the decline to individual business decisions regarding the market.
    將此降幅歸因於個別業者對市場的商業決策。
  • remaining adj. 剩餘的
    The remaining 532 unlicensed lodging establishments were guesthouses.
    其餘 532 家非法旅宿則是民宿。
  • establishment n. 機構，營業場所
    The remaining 532 unlicensed lodging establishments were guesthouses.
    其餘 532 家非法旅宿則是民宿。
  • due to prep. 由於，因為
    The number of unlicensed hotels and short-term rental suites rose compared with the same period last year, mainly due to an increase in short-term rental suites in urban areas
    非法旅館與短期租賃套房的數量較去年同期上升，主要是因為都會區短期租賃套房增加
  • conduct v. 執行，實施（調查、檢查等）
    authorities had conducted 4,054 inspections of licensed hotels and daily rental suites
    官方已對合法旅館及日租套房進行了 4,054 次稽查
  • issue v. 發布，開立（證照、罰單等）
    Of the 733 fines issued, Taipei topped the list with 204
    在已開出的 733 張罰單中，台北市以 204 張高居榜首
  • account for phr. v. 佔（比例、數量等）
    Tainan accounted for the most at NT$29.75 million
    台南市以新台幣 2,975 萬元占居最高
  • operate v. 營運，經營
    As guesthouses are typically operated as side businesses using spare rooms in private residences
    由於民宿通常是由私人住宅中的閒置房間作為副業經營
  • cease v. 停止，終止
    they are less likely than hotels to suspend or cease operations due to business costs
    牠們因營業成本而暫停或停止營業的可能性，小於一般旅館。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作為後置修飾語（現在分詞片語）
    attributing the decline to individual business decisions regarding the market.
    句尾的 "attributing..." 是現在分詞引導的分詞片語，用來補充說明前面子句所產生的結果或伴隨的動作（主詞 the administration 主動發出將衰退歸因於決策的動作）。
  • 複合形容詞（數字-名詞-形容詞）
    The number of unlicensed hotels and short-term rental suites rose compared with the same period last year...
    "short-term" 是由形容詞加名詞構成的複合形容詞，中間加上連字號（hyphen），用來修飾後方的名詞 "rental suites"。
  • 形容詞比較級搭配否定句型（less likely than）
    ...they are less likely than hotels to suspend or cease operations due to business costs, it said.
    此處使用「less + 原級形容詞 + than」的句型來表達「較不……比……」。用來比較民宿與飯店在面對營業成本時，停止營業的可能性較低。
