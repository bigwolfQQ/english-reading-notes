曼城與拜仁在賽季開幕戰握手言和

Man City, Bayern draw in season opener

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • opener n. 首場比賽，開幕戰
    Manchester City on Tuesday fought from two goals down to grab a 2-2 draw at Bayern Munich to start their UEFA Women’s Champions League season, while Mariona Caldentey was the hero as Arsenal left it late in their opener.
    曼徹斯特城隊於週二從落後兩球的劣勢中急起直追，以 2 比 2 逼平拜仁慕尼黑，為他們的歐洲女子冠軍聯賽賽季揭開序幕，而馬里奧娜·卡爾登泰則成為英雄，幫助兵工廠在開幕戰中驚險險勝。
  • thanks to phr. 由於，幸虧
    Bayern raced into a 2-0 lead thanks to Giulia Gwinn’s early strike, and a Kerstin Casparij own goal inside the first 30 minutes.
    拜仁憑藉朱莉婭·格溫的閃電進球，以及科斯蒂娜·卡斯帕里傑在比賽前 30 分鐘內的烏龍球，迅猛取得 2 比 0 的領先。
  • ensure v. 確保，保證
    However, German forward Carlotta Wamser popped up with four minutes remaining to ensure the spoils were shared in Bavaria.
    然而，德國前鋒卡洛塔·瓦姆瑟在比賽還剩四分鐘時現身進球，確保雙方在巴伐利亞平分秋色。
  • standard n. 標準，水準
    “[The first half] was probably not good enough for our standards,” Mead told UEFA.
    「[上半場]對我們的標準來說可能還不夠好，」米德告訴歐足聯。
  • character n. 性格，特質，品格
    “But again, we showed what the team is about, showed our character.”
    「但話說回來，我們展現了這支球隊的本色，展現了我們的性格。」
  • bid n. 企圖，努力，爭取
    Arsenal, the 2024-2025 season’s Champions League winners, made a faltering start to their bid to recover the continent’s premier club title.
    2024-2025 賽季的歐冠得主兵工廠隊，在其重奪歐洲頂級球會頭銜的征途上開局跌跌撞撞。
  • sign v. 簽約
    The Gunners looked certain to be heading for a goalless stalemate until new signing Selina Cerci went down in the box under Yukina Sakabe’s 92nd-minute challenge.
    槍手們本看似將走向無進球的僵局，直到新援塞利納·切爾西在禁區內遭受日下部雪菜第 92 分鐘的犯規而倒地。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況或結果
    Manchester City on Tuesday fought from two goals down to grab a 2-2 draw at Bayern Munich to start their UEFA Women’s Champions League season, while Mariona Caldentey was the hero as Arsenal left it late in their opener.
    句中的 "as Arsenal left it late..." 引導時間副詞子句，而「left it late」是一種慣用語，用來形容球隊在比賽尾聲才驚險取得進球或勝利。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    City looked set to begin the league phase with a loss, as Khadija Shaw was denied from the spot by Bayern goalkeeper Ena Mahmutovic in the second period.
    「was denied」為被動語態，用來表達主詞 Khadija Shaw「被」拜仁門將從十二碼罰球點（from the spot）拒絕（即撲出進球）。
  • 過去分詞片語當形容詞後置修飾
    A Sakina Karchaoui penalty inside the final 20 minutes ensured Paris Saint-Germain left the Estadio Alfredo Di Stefano with a 1-1 draw after Felicia Schroeder’s second-minute opener for Real Madrid.
    「Felicia Schroeder’s second-minute opener」中的 second-minute 為複合形容詞，用來修飾名詞 opener，精確指出該進球發生在比賽第幾分鐘。
