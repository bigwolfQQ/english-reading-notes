文法重點

  • 現在完成被動語態 (Present Perfect Passive)
    The 29th Taiwan-France Cultural Award has been presented to Tamkang University's Wu Hsi-deh...
    結構為「have/has + been + 過去分詞 (V-pp)」。用來強調過去發生的動作對現在造成的影響或結果，且主詞是接受動作的對象（在此為主詞 Award 接收頒獎的動作）。
  • 非限定關代子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    According to Taiwan's Ministry of Culture (MOC), which jointly organizes the award with the Académie des Sciences Morales et Politiques of the Institut de France, Wu founded...
    句中的「which...」前面有逗號，用來補充說明前面的先行詞（Ministry of Culture），提供額外的附加資訊。如果把這個子句拿掉，主要子句的語意依然完整。
  • 分詞構句作補充說明 (Participial Phrase for Additional Info)
    Established in 1996 and held annually since then, the Taiwan-France Cultural Award recognizes institutions...
    句首的「Established in 1996 and held annually since then」是由過去分詞片語引導的分詞構句，原句可還原為「As it was established in 1996 and has been held annually since then,...」，用來簡潔地表達時間與原因背景。
