美國誓言保護（境內民眾）免受鎮壓

US vows to protect from repression

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • vow v. / n. 誓言，發誓
    The US yesterday vowed to protect people on its soil from “the overreach of foreign governments”.
    美國昨天誓言保護其領土上的民眾免受「外國政府的過度權力伸展」之害。
  • sovereignty n. 主權
    We are committed to safeguarding our sovereignty, keeping our communities safe...
    我們致力於維護我們的主權、確保我們的社區安全……
  • spokesperson n. 發言人
    ...a US Department of State spokesperson said in response to a request for comment.
    ……美國國務院發言人在回應置評請求時表示。
  • agent n. 代理人，特務，經紀人
    Essayli said in a post on X that Zhang was arrested for acting as an unregistered agent of the Chinese government...
    埃薩利在 X 上的一篇貼文中表示，張女因擔任中國政府的未登記代理人……而被捕
  • vigilant adj. 警戒的，警惕的
    ...while US Senator Pete Ricketts said the US “must remain vigilant” to the threat posed by “spies and deception on American soil.”
    ……而美國參議員皮特·里基茨則表示，美國「必須保持警惕」，以防範「美國本土上的間諜與欺騙」所帶來的威脅。
  • deception n. 欺騙，詭計
    ...the US “must remain vigilant” to the threat posed by “spies and deception on American soil.”
    ……美國「必須保持警惕」，以防範「美國本土上的間諜與欺騙」所帶來的威脅。
  • relative n. 親屬，親戚
    ...spying on a close relative of a high-ranking Taiwanese government official.
    ……並對一名台灣高層政府官員的近親進行間諜活動
  • complaint n. 告訴狀，投訴，抱怨
    CBS News, citing an FBI complaint, reported on Sunday that Zhang and a man... flew from Las Vegas to Seattle...
    哥倫比亞廣播公司新聞網引述一份聯邦調查局的告訴指出，張女與一名男子……從拉斯維加斯飛往西雅圖……
  • warrant n. 令狀，授權令
    It added that Zhang allegedly messaged a Chinese government handler via WeChat, citing a search warrant for her iCloud account.
    報導並補充說，根據對其 iCloud 帳戶的搜查令顯示，張女據稱透過微信向一名中國政府的接頭人發送了訊息。
  • indict v. 起訴，控告
    Zhang appeared in court on Monday for a preliminary hearing, but has yet to be formally indicted...
    張女於週一出庭進行初步聽審，但尚未遭到正式起訴……

文法重點

  • 現在分詞短語作後置修飾（減化關係子句）
    ...after a woman was arrested on Sunday by the FBI for allegedly surveilling President William Lai’s (賴清德) son for China.
    句中的「allegedly surveilling...」為現在分詞短語，用來補充說明或修飾前面動詞所涉及的動作，相當於副詞子句的簡化，表達「涉嫌監視」的意思。
  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況或原因
    The arrest of Zhang, a Chinese-born naturalized US citizen, drew comment from US lawmakers on the security threats posed by Beijing.
    句尾的「posed by Beijing」是過去分詞片語當作形容詞後置修飾 threats（威脅），原句隱含了「which are posed by Beijing」的關代主格加上be動詞的省略，用以精鍊句型結構。
  • 被動語態的應用
    ...Zhang was arrested at Los Angeles International Airport before she departed for China.
    新聞報導經常使用被動語態（was arrested）來客觀陳述事件結果，強調承受動作的主體（張女），而不一定需要強調施事者。
