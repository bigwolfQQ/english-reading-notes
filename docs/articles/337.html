勞動部警告 foodpanda：不得因外送延遲而違法扣減外送員薪資

MOL warns foodpanda over docking couriers' pay for late deliveries

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • dock v. 扣發（薪資等）
    Delivery platform foodpanda could face fines up to NT$100,000 (US$3,178) per affected worker for illegally docking couriers' pay for late deliveries based on unilaterally imposed time limits, the Ministry of Labor (MOL) said Wednesday.
    勞動部於週三表示，外送平台 foodpanda 因根據單方強加的時間限制，違法扣減外送員因送餐延遲的薪資，每位受影響的勞工可能面臨最高新台幣 10 萬元的罰款。
  • fine n. 罰款、罰金
    Delivery platform foodpanda could face fines up to NT$100,000 (US$3,178) per affected worker for illegally docking couriers' pay for late deliveries based on unilaterally imposed time limits, the Ministry of Labor (MOL) said Wednesday.
    勞動部於週三表示，外送平台 foodpanda 因根據單方強加的時間限制，違法扣減外送員因送餐延遲的薪資，每位受影響的勞工可能面臨最高新台幣 10 萬元的罰款。
  • violate v. 違反、違背（法律、規定等）
    At multiple recent meetings with local labor officials, the ministry declared that such practices by foodpanda violate the Delivery Workers' Rights Protection and Delivery Platform Management Act, according to a statement issued by the ministry.
    根據該部發布的聲明，勞動部在近期與地方勞動官員的多場會議中宣告，foodpanda 的此類作法已違反《外送員權益保障及外送平台管理法》。
  • validity n. 有效性、合法性
    Local labor bureaus can impose fines ranging from NT$20,000 to NT$100,000 per affected courier once they determine the validity of the complaints filed by workers, MOL Department of Labor Relations Director-General Wang Hou-wei (王厚偉) told CNA.
    勞動部勞動關係司司長王厚偉告訴中央社，地方勞動局一旦認定勞工所提申訴的有效性，即可對每位受影響的外送員處以新台幣 2 萬元至 10 萬元的罰款。
  • disclose v. 透露、公開、揭露
    Wang did not disclose how many complaints had been filed with local authorities, citing a lack of complete data, but said all reported cases involved foodpanda.
    王厚偉未透露已向地方主管機關提出多少起申訴，理由是缺乏完整數據，但表示所有被通報的案件都指向 foodpanda。
  • deduction n. 扣除、扣款
    According to Wang, delivery couriers reported facing pay deductions for exceeding estimated delivery times by as little as one to five minutes, calling the policy "unfair" due to "numerous unforeseen and uncontrollable factors on the road."
    王厚偉指出，外送員回報稱，他們因超過預估送餐時間短短一到五分鐘而面臨薪資扣減，並因「路上無數無法預見且無法控制的因素」而痛批這項政策「不公平」。
  • unforeseen adj. 未預見的、意料之外的
    According to Wang, delivery couriers reported facing pay deductions for exceeding estimated delivery times by as little as one to five minutes, calling the policy "unfair" due to "numerous unforeseen and uncontrollable factors on the road."
    王厚偉指出，外送員回報稱，他們因超過預估送餐時間短短一到五分鐘而面臨薪資扣減，並因「路上無數無法預見且無法控制的因素」而痛批這項政策「不公平」。
  • mechanism n. 機制、體制
    When delivery delays occur, platforms should look into the causes and handle them via proper mechanisms instead of making unilateral pay deductions, the ministry said.
    勞動部表示，當送餐延遲發生時，平台應該調查原因並透過適當機制處理，而不是進行單方面的薪資扣減。
  • force n. 效力、效力實施（come into force 意為「生效」）
    Under the law, which came into force on July 21, platforms must pay riders based on the exact time a delivery takes, using a rate of 1.25 times the minimum wage, and no order can pay less than NT$45.
    根據 7 月 21 日生效的法律，平台必須根據外送所花費的確切時間向外送員支付薪資，費率為基本工資的 1.25 倍，且任何訂單的報酬不得低於新台幣 45 元。
  • submit v. 提交、呈遞
    The union coalition called on couriers to submit formal complaints to local labor departments if they experience illegal pay deductions.
    工會聯盟呼籲外送員若遭遇違法扣薪，應向地方勞動局提交正式申訴。
  • disburse v. 支付、撥款、發放
    In response, Emma Kuo (郭昕宜), foodpanda's director of public affairs and public relations, told reporters that the platform has never refused to disburse rider pay due to delivery delays.
    針對此事，foodpanda 公共事務與公共關係總監郭昕宜告訴記者，該平台從未因送餐延遲而拒絕發放外送員薪資。
  • clarify v. 澄清、說明
    Kuo said the company only asked delivery couriers to clarify unusually long trips, adding that full pay will be disbursed as long as a valid explanation is provided.
    郭昕宜表示，公司僅要求外送員澄清異常長的行程，並補充說只要提供有效的解釋，就會全額發放薪資。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Delivery platform foodpanda could face fines up to NT$100,000 (US$3,178) per affected worker for illegally docking couriers' pay for late deliveries based on unilaterally imposed time limits, the Ministry of Labor (MOL) said Wednesday.
    句中的 "unilaterally imposed time limits" 是由過去分詞 "imposed" 所引導的分詞片語，用來修飾名詞 "time limits"（時間限制），意思是「單方強加的時間限制」。過去分詞表被動與完成的概念，常精簡地用來修飾前面的名詞。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Under the law, which came into force on July 21, platforms must pay riders based on the exact time a delivery takes...
    句中的 ", which came into force on July 21," 是一個非限定關係代名詞子句，前面帶有逗號，用來補充說明前面的先行詞 "the law"。這類子句即使拿掉，也不會影響主要子句的完整語意。
  • 現在分詞表伴隨或原因 (Present Participle Phrase)
    Wang did not disclose how many complaints had been filed with local authorities, citing a lack of complete data, but said all reported cases involved foodpanda.
    句尾的 ", citing a lack of complete data" 是由現在分詞 "citing" 引導的分詞片語，用來補充說明前面動作發生時的理由（因為引用了缺乏完整數據的狀況）。這種用法能讓句子更簡潔流暢，避免使用冗長的連接詞子句。
  • 條件副詞子句省略句型 (Conditional Clause with 'as long as')
    Kuo said the company only asked delivery couriers to clarify unusually long trips, adding that full pay will be disbursed as long as a valid explanation is provided.
    句中的 "as long as a valid explanation is provided" 是一個條件副詞子句，用來表達「只要……就……」的條件。此處子句中使用了被動語態 "is provided"，讓語氣更具客觀與正式感。
