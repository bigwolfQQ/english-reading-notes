Flu epidemic continues as deaths and cases hit new high

單字解析

  • precautions n. 預防措施，防範
    urging people to take precautions.
    敦促民眾採取預防措施。
  • confirmed adj. 確診的，已證實的
    165 severe flu cases and 30 flu-related deaths were confirmed
    165 起流感重症病例和 30 起流感相關死亡病例獲得確診
  • accumulated adj. 累積的，積聚的
    Of the accumulated 1,586 severe cases
    在累計的 1,586 起重症病例中
  • underlying adj. 潛在的，根本的
    elderly people with underlying conditions have the highest risk
    具備潛在健康狀況的老年人風險最高
  • infected adj. 受感染的
    At the hospital he was confirmed to be infected with influenza A virus
    在醫院他被確診感染 A 型流感病毒
  • combined adj. 結合的，聯合的
    died of influenza-induced acute necrotizing encephalopathy combined with sepsis
    死於流感引發的急性壞死性腦包炎合併敗血症
  • increase v. 增加，提高
    Medical visits for COVID-19 increased 20.6 percent
    COVID-19 的就醫人次增加了 20.6%
  • hospitalization n. 住院治療
    about two weeks after hospitalization
    住院大約兩週後
  • active adj. 活躍的
    respiratory diseases typically become more active when temperatures drop
    呼吸道疾病通常在氣溫下降時變得更活躍
  • frequently adv. 頻繁地
    seek medical attention as soon as possible
    盡快尋求醫療協助（註：選用文章中具有商業與日常實用性的片語/動詞片語架構相關字彙）
  • expected adj. 預期的，預料的
    Flu cases are expected to rise to a peak
    預期流感病例將上升至巔峰
  • congregate v. 聚集，集合
    as people congregate during the upcoming Mid-Autumn Festival
    隨著民眾在即將到來的中秋節期間聚集
  • notified adj. 接到通知的
    and were notified that they could get a free blood test.
    並接獲通知他們可以接受免費抽血檢驗。

    36 人拒絕接受檢驗
  • determine v. 決定，判定，查明
    to determine if the deaths were connected with hepatitis C infection
    以判定這些死亡是否與 C 型肝炎感染有關

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    The Centers for Disease Control (CDC) yesterday said that 165 severe flu cases and 30 flu-related deaths were reported last week, the highest weekly numbers in this flu season, and that cases might continue to increase, urging people to take precautions.
    句尾的「urging people to take precautions」是由現在分詞 urging 引導的分詞片語，用來補充說明前面子句所發生的動作及其帶有的伴隨結果（主動引導或促使），相當於 and urged people... 的簡化結構。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Absolute Nominative Construction)
    Medical visits for flu-like illnesses increased by 1.4 percent to 141,374 last week, with 11.8 percent being emergency visits, showing that the seasonal flu is still in its epidemic period...
    「with 11.8 percent being emergency visits」為「with + 名詞/代名詞 + 現在分詞」的獨立分詞構句，用來補充主句動作發生時的伴隨狀況，其中 percent 與 be 動詞形成主動語態的邏輯主謂關係。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    At the hospital he was confirmed to be infected with influenza A virus and showed signs of encephalitis, he said.
    「he was confirmed to be infected...」使用了被動語態（was confirmed），強調主詞「he（男童）」是動作的承受者，在新聞英語中經常用於報導官方證實或醫學檢驗結果。
  • 過去分詞作後位修飾 (Past Participle as Post-modifier)
    Of the 418 people who have been contacted but have not received the free blood test, 36 refused to get tested, four had died, and 32 reported receiving a blood test on their own...
    句中的「tested」或關係代名詞子句「who have been contacted」都是修飾前方名詞的方法。其中「tested people」或是「the tested people」將過去分詞當作形容詞使用，用來修飾被檢測的人，呈現被動意味。
