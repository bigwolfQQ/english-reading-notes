梅德維夫因球擊中球迷而被取消資格

Medvedev disqualified after hitting fan with ball

單字解析

  • disqualify v. 取消資格
    Novak Djokovic on Monday advanced to the China Open final after Daniil Medvedev was disqualified late in the second set
    諾瓦·喬科維奇於週一晉級中國公開賽決賽，此前丹尼爾·梅德維夫在第二盤末段因故被取消資格
  • advance v. 晉級、前進
    Taiwanese player Hsieh Su-wei and her partner... reached women’s doubles round of 16. Hsieh was the only Taiwanese player to advance
    台灣選手謝淑薇與她的搭檔……晉級女子雙打16強。謝淑薇是唯一晉級的台灣選手
  • eliminate v. 淘汰
    compatriots Wu Fang-hsien, Chan Hao-ching and Liang En-shuo were eliminated in round of 32.
    同胞吳芳嫺、詹皓晴和梁恩碩都在 32 強遭到淘汰。
  • retire v. 退賽、退休
    The Australian had advanced over Hubert Hurkacz after the 29-year-old from Poland retired from their semi-final with an adductor muscle strain.
    這名澳大利亞選手在胡貝爾·胡爾卡奇因內收肌拉傷退出他們的準決賽後晉級。
  • apologize v. 道歉
    adding that it was “100 percent an accident” and that he wished to apologize to the fan in person.
    並補充說這「百分之百是一場意外」，他希望能親面向該球迷道歉。
  • control n. / v. 控制、掌控
    until the Serbian took control in the second set.
    直到塞爾維亞人在第二盤掌握主動權。
  • intentionally adv. 故意地
    “I’m sure that Daniil didn’t ... do it intentionally.
    「我確信丹尼爾不是……故意這樣做的。
  • pressure n. 壓力
    “Truthfully speaking, I felt a lot of pressure today,” the 23-year-old told her home crowd
    「老實說，我今天感到很大的壓力，」這位 23 歲的選手告訴她的家鄉觀眾
  • support n. / v. 支持
    thanking fans for their support.
    感謝球迷的支持。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表示伴隨狀況或結果）
    A man sitting in the stands behind Medvedev’s side of the court was seen holding his face and escorted from his seat after being hit, as Djokovic was serving for the match with the score at 7-5, 5-3.
    句中的「sitting in the stands...」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「A man」，相當於形容詞子句「A man who was sitting in the stands...」。此外，「holding his face and escorted...」則是用來補充說明該名男子被看到時的伴隨動作。
  • 過去未來式 (be to + V)
    Eleventh-ranked Djokovic was to play world No. 9 Alex de Minaur for the title last night after press time in Beijing.
    「was to play」使用了「be to + 原形動詞」的結構，在過去時態中用來表示按計劃或預定將要發生的事情。
  • 附加引述與補充分詞構句 (V-ing)
    “Terribly sorry to the spectator, first of all,” he told reporters afterward, adding that it was “100 percent an accident” and that he wished to apologize to the fan in person.
    句尾的「adding that...」是現在分詞作分詞構句，用來補充說明主詞在說話時額外陳述的內容，使句子結構更緊湊，避免重複使用主詞和動詞。
