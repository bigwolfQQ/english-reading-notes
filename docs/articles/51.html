石東文深入探討：民主如何改變台灣的交通

Donovan’s Deep Dives: Democracy changed how Taiwan moves

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • frustration n. 挫折感、沮喪
    there has been noticeable frustration with the way democratic institutions are functioning.
    對於民主體制運作的方式，人們已經產生了明顯的挫折感。
  • circumvent v. 規避、繞過（法律或規則）
    Attempts to circumvent political opposition and institutional checks can begin a slide toward authoritarianism
    試圖規避政治反對勢力和體制制衡，可能會開啟滑向威權主義的道路
  • inevitably adv. 不可避免地
    While that might solve problems in the short term, it inevitably evolves into a system that serves the rulers
    雖然這可能在短期內解決問題，但它不可避免地會演變成一個服務統治者的體系
  • oversight n. 監督、監管
    Authorities tightened oversight of bus operators, while the central government increased penalties
    當局加強對公車客運業者的監督，而中央政府則提高罰則
  • penalties n. 處罰、罰款、刑罰
    while the central government increased penalties and licence sanctions for drivers who failed to yield to pedestrians.
    而中央政府則提高對未能禮讓行人駕駛的罰則與吊扣駕照處分。
  • voluntarily adv. 自願地
    People did this voluntarily, not out of fear of reprisal, but out of a sense of personal responsibility
    人們這樣做是出於自願，不是出於對報復的恐懼，而是出於個人責任感
  • accountability n. 問責制、責任歸屬
    Authoritarian systems, on the other hand, weaken accountability and can encourage people to rely on status
    另一方面，威權體系則會削弱問責制，並鼓勵人們依賴地位
  • impartial adj. 公正的、無偏見的
    and informal power rather than impartial rules.
    而不是公正的規則。
  • corruption n. 貪腐、腐敗
    Corruption affected officialdom, law enforcement and parts of the judiciary
    貪腐影響了官場、執法部門和部分司法界
  • gauge v. 衡量、評估
    In traffic, one had to gauge the relative power of others and one’s relative place in the pecking order.
    在交通方面，人們必須衡量其他人相對的權力以及自己在階級順序中的相對位置。
  • perpetrator n. 加害者、犯罪者
    the blame would be pinned on the good samaritan because they could be identified, not the actual perpetrator.
    好心人可能會被指認為加害者，而不是真正的加害者，因為他們是可以被找到的目標。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞當形容詞修飾名詞
    A number of gruesome traffic deaths helped produce stronger rules, while open debate contributed to changing social expectations.
    句中的 changing 作為現在分詞，用來修飾名詞 expectations（社會期望），表示「正在改變的、不斷轉變的社會期望」。
  • 附帶狀況的 with 複合結構 (with + 名詞 + 分詞)
    Citizens in a democracy broadly trust that their rights are protected and understand that with freedom comes responsibilities.
    句中的 with freedom comes responsibilities 運用了倒裝（將介系詞片語置於句首以強調），同時表達了伴隨狀態，意指「伴隨著自由而來的是責任」。
  • 條件句與假設語氣的應用 (even when)
    Attempts to circumvent political opposition and institutional checks can begin a slide toward authoritarianism, even when presented as short-term solutions to gridlock.
    even when 引導讓步子句，其中省略了主詞與be動詞（原句應為 even when they are presented as...），用來加強語氣，表示「即使被當作是……的時候也是如此」。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    Below them were people in expensive cars who were likely well-connected and gravel truck drivers, who were often ex-convicts hired by the gangster-run construction companies.
    句尾的 hired by the gangster-run construction companies 是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的 ex-convicts（前科犯），等於形容詞子句 who were hired by... 的簡化版，意指「被黑幫經營的營造公司所僱用的」。
