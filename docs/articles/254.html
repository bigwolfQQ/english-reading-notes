Taipei Times · 2026-09-07

擁抱結構化移民

Embrace structured immigration

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • incentive n. 獎勵，誘因
    The government has rightfully, and fairly, attempted to reverse the trend by offering financial incentives to young people to have children.
    政府透過提供年輕人生育的財務誘因，合情合理且公正地試圖扭轉這項趨勢。
  • option n. 選擇，選項
    Taiwanese society must accept that managed, large-scale immigration is no longer just an option — it is a necessity.
    台灣社會必須接受，有管理的、大規模的移民不再只是一個選項——而是一項必要措施。
  • strategy n. 策略，計畫
    Taiwan should provide free Mandarin language courses to lower-income migrant groups.
    台灣應該向低收入移工群體提供免費的華語課程。
  • contract n. 合約，契約
    To prove this, presenting a clean criminal record and a valid employment contract paying an industry-standard average wage should suffice.
    為了證明這一點，出示良民證以及一份支付產業標準平均薪資的有效僱傭合約應該就足夠了。
  • terminate v. 終止，解雇
    If a caregiver’s employment is terminated, they should be granted a realistic five-month grace period to find a new position.
    如果看護的僱傭關係終止，他們應該獲得切合實際的五個月緩衝期來尋找新職位。
  • enterprise n. 企業，公司
    Private enterprises are far better suited than bureaucrats to decide whether an employee brings economic value.
    私人企業遠比官僚機構更適合決定員工是否帶來經濟價值。
  • contribute v. 貢獻，捐獻
    Ultimately, Taiwan’s immigration system should pivot around one fundamental question: Does the migrant contribute to society?
    歸根究底，台灣的移民系統應該繞著一個根本問題打轉：該移工是否對社會有貢獻？
  • adequate adj. 足夠的，適當的
    Taiwan must also broadly accept dual citizenship and grant all graduating foreign students an adequate grace period to secure employment.
    台灣也必須全面接受雙重國籍，並授予所有畢業的外國學生充足的緩衝期以確保就業。
  • globalization n. 全球化
    The demographic shift is so deep that even in the unthinkable scenario...
    人口結構的轉變是如此劇烈，以至於在一個連想都不敢想的情形下...
  • shift n. 轉變，移動
    The demographic shift is so deep that even in the unthinkable scenario that every Taiwanese woman of childbearing age begins having three children by next year, it would not be enough.
    人口結構的轉變是如此劇烈，以至於在一個連想都不敢想的情形下——即明年開始台灣每一位育齡女性都生三個孩子——也仍然不夠。
  • standardized adj. 標準化的
    First, the path to permanent residency must be clear and standardized.
    首先，獲得永久居留權的途徑必須清晰且標準化。
  • economic adj. 經濟的
    While integration carries upfront costs, they are rapidly offset by the long-term economic returns.
    雖然融合承擔了前期的成本，但它們很快就會被長期的經濟回報所抵消。

文法重點

  • 假設語氣（Subjunctive Mood / Unreal Conditional）
    The demographic shift is so deep that even in the unthinkable scenario that every Taiwanese woman of childbearing age begins having three children by next year, it would not be enough.
    此句使用了假設語氣的概念，表達一種極端且不太可能發生的假設情境（即全台灣育齡女性明年起都生三個小孩），主句使用「would not be enough」來呈現該假設若發生時的結果。
  • 現在分詞當形容詞（Present Participle as Adjective）
    Taiwanese society must accept that managed, large-scale immigration is no longer just an option — it is a necessity.
    句中的「managed」是由過去分詞轉化而來的形容詞，用來修飾「immigration」，表示「被管理的、受控管的」移民政策。
  • 副詞子句簡化 / 分詞構句（Participle Phrase）
    Having already benefited from and contributed to the Taiwanese system, it makes zero economic sense to discard these educated resources.
    句首的「Having already benefited...」是由原因副詞子句簡化而來的分詞構句，表示這些外籍學生「已經從中受益並做出貢獻」，分詞的主詞與主要子句的主意涵相符。
  • so... that... 構句（如此……以至於……）
    The demographic shift is so deep that even in the unthinkable scenario... it would not be enough.
    「so + 形容詞/副詞 + that + 子句」是用來表達「程度深到產生某種結果」的常用句型，此處用來強調台灣人口結構轉變的嚴重程度。
