福斯董事會支持再裁員 5,0000 人

Volkswagen board backs 50,000 further job cuts

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • restructuring n. 重組，改組
    Volkswagen AG’s supervisory board backed a sweeping restructuring that calls for 50,000 additional job cuts...
    福斯汽車（Volkswagen AG）的監事會支持一項大規模重組計畫，該計畫要求額外裁員 50,000 人...
  • mandate n. 授權，委任
    ...giving chief executive officer Oliver Blume his clearest mandate yet to overhaul Europe’s biggest carmaker.
    ...這賦予了執行長奧利弗·布鲁姆（Oliver Blume）迄今為止最明確的授權，得以全面改革歐洲最大的汽車製造商。
  • workforce n. 勞動力，全體員工
    The new staff cuts represent about 8 percent of Volkswagen’s global workforce as of the end of last year.
    新的裁員人數約占福斯截至去年底全球員工總數的 8%。
  • capital expenditure n. 資本支出
    It is also tightening investment, planning 135 billion euros (US$157 billion) of capital expenditure and research and development spending...
    該公司還在收緊投資，計畫在明年至 2031 年期間進行 1350 億歐元（約 1570 億美元）的資本支出以及研發支出...
  • margin n. 利潤率，差額
    ...saying the 50,000 figure is a planning assumption derived from Volkswagen’s target to achieve a 9 percent margin by 2030...
    ...表示 50,000 這個數字是根據福斯在 2030 年實現 9% 利潤率的目標而得出的規劃假設...
  • unanimous adj. 一致同意的，全體一致的
    That distinction reflects the broader compromise behind Thursday’s unanimous board vote...
    這種區別反映了週四董事會全票通過背後的廣泛妥協...
  • operating margin n. 營業利潤率
    The overhaul targets an operating margin of 9 percent by 2030 on annual sales of about 9 million vehicles.
    該改革旨在到 2030 年實現約 900 万輛年銷量的 9% 營業利潤率。
  • assurances n. 保證，承諾
    Workers secured assurances that no factory would be immediately abandoned...
    工人們獲得了保證，即短期內不會放棄任何工廠...
  • capacity n. 產能，容量
    Volkswagen acknowledged that it has about 500,000 vehicles-worth of excess annual capacity in Europe.
    福斯坦承其在歐洲擁有約當每年 50 万輛汽車的過剩產能。
  • dividend n. 股利，紅利
    The Porsche-Piech family... has pushed for faster action as returns and dividend flows come under pressure.
    保時捷-皮耶希家族...隨著回報率和股息流面臨壓力，一直敦促採取更快的行動。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作附帶說明 / 結果
    The measures approved on Thursday at a meeting in Wolfsburg double workforce reductions across Volkswagen Group brands since late 2024, while also paving the way for the company to slim its vehicle lineup by as much as half by 2035.
    句中的「while also paving the way...」為分詞構句（省略了主詞與be動詞，還原完整子句應為 while it is also paving...），用來表達在主要子句動作發生同時所產生的並列結果或伴隨動作。
  • 現在分詞作後位修飾
    The spending reductions come as Chinese rivals led by BYD Co (比亞迪) expand in Europe, adding to pressure direct investment toward its strongest businesses.
    句尾的「adding to pressure...」是現在分詞片語作為補充說明，修飾前面整個子句所帶來的影響或結果，相當於「and this adds to pressure...」。
  • 過去分詞作後位修飾
    In the final package, dubbed “Future Plan 2030,” Blume won backing for the extra job cuts and broad efficiency measures.
    句中的「dubbed “Future Plan 2030”」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「package」（方案），省略了「which was」，意指「被稱為『2030未來計畫』的最終方案」。
