油價將維持穩定

Fuel prices to remain stable

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • announce v. 宣布、宣告
    CPC Corp, Taiwan (CPC, 台灣中油) on Saturday announced that it would keep its domestic gasoline and diesel prices steady this week...
    台灣中油公司於週六宣布，本週將維持國內汽柴油價格穩定...
  • steady adj. 穩定的、平穩的
    ...announced that it would keep its domestic gasoline and diesel prices steady this week...
    ...宣布本週將維持國內汽柴油價格穩定...
  • follow suit phr. 照著做、跟進
    ...and Formosa Petrochemical Corp (台塑石化) followed suit yesterday, despite a spike in international crude oil prices.
    ...台塑石化公司隨後也在昨天跟進，儘管國際原油價格出現飆升。
  • despite prep. 儘管、雖然
    ...despite a spike in international crude oil prices.
    ...儘管國際原油價格出現飆升。
  • consecutive adj. 連續的
    This week would be the sixth consecutive week that CPC and Formosa have maintained domestic crude oil prices...
    這將是中油與台塑連續第六週維持國內原油價格不變...
  • maintain v. 維持、保持
    ...that CPC and Formosa have maintained domestic crude oil prices...
    ...中油與台塑連續第六週維持國內原油價格不變...
  • average adj. / n. 平均的 / 平均
    ...based on a weighted average of 70 percent Dubai and 30 percent Brent crude...
    ...根據 70% 杜拜原油與 30% 布蘭特原油加權平均計算...
  • cost n. 成本、費用
    ...helped CPC slightly ease crude oil purchasing cost pressure, it said.
    ...有助於中油稍微緩解原油採購成本的壓力。
  • pressure n. 壓力
    ...helped CPC slightly ease crude oil purchasing cost pressure, it said.
    ...有助於中油稍微緩解原油採購成本的壓力。
  • retail adj. 零售的
    Retail gasoline prices this week would remain at NT$30.5, NT$32 and NT$34 per liter...
    本週汽油零售價將維持在每公升 30.5 元、32 元及 34 元...

文法重點

  • Despite 介詞用法
    ...and Formosa Petrochemical Corp (台塑石化) followed suit yesterday, despite a spike in international crude oil prices.
    despite 是介系詞，意為「儘管」，後面直接接名詞或名詞片語（如本句的 a spike in international crude oil prices），用來表達兩件事之間的對比或讓步關係。注意它後面不能直接接主詞和動詞的子句。
  • 現在完成式 (Present Perfect Tense)
    This week would be the sixth consecutive week that CPC and Formosa have maintained domestic crude oil prices...
    have maintained 為現在完成式，用來表示一個動作從過去某個時間點開始（中油和台塑過去幾週持續凍漲或維持價格），一直持續到現在的狀況。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    A stronger New Taiwan dollar, which averaged NT$31.682 last week against the US dollar... helped CPC slightly ease crude oil purchasing cost pressure...
    句中的 which averaged NT$31.682 last week against the US dollar 是非限定關係子句，用逗號隔開，用來補充說明前面的主詞「走勢較強的新台幣」的平均匯率細節。
