文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    At least 4,000 police and other personnel, including snipers, were mobilized for the visit, in what has been called one of the biggest security operations ever mounted in Ireland.
    句中的「were mobilized」與「has been called」皆為被動語態（be 動詞 + 過去分詞），用來強調動作的承受者（警察與人員、此次維安行動），而不需要特別指明是誰去動員或稱呼牠們，在新聞英語中非常常見。
  • 非限定關係子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Trump’s trip to Doonbeg — a remote, coastal site in County Clare that the Trump Organization purchased in 2014 — adds an extra layer of complexity to the security effort.
    句中的「that the Trump Organization purchased in 2014」是一個限定關係子句，用來修飾先行詞「a remote, coastal site」，補充說明該地點的來源。此結構在多益閱讀測驗的長句中經常出現，用以提供額外細節。
  • 分詞片語作後置修飾 (Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    About 40,000 people are expected at the tournament over the weekend, many to see six-time major winner and world number two Rory McIlroy.
    句中「six-time major winner and world number two」是由名詞與數字組成的複合形容詞結構，用來修飾後方的專有名詞「Rory McIlroy」，提供其身份背景，讓句子更為精簡。
