文法重點

  • 非限定關係子句補充說明（用逗號隔開的 which 子句）
    After its American depositary receipts rose 2.06 percent Friday, contract chipmaker TSMC, which accounts for over 40 percent of the local market's total value, rose 2.07 percent to close at NT$2,460.00...
    句中的「which accounts for over 40 percent of the local market's total value」前面有逗號，屬於非限定關係子句。用來補充說明前面的主詞「contract chipmaker TSMC（晶圓代工廠台積電）」的市值佔比，就算拿掉這個子句，主句的意思依然完整。
  • 分詞構句（表附帶狀況或原因）
    "Although the U.S. jobs data for August raised worries over a rate hike by the Federal Reserve, investors here still rushed to pick up semiconductor stocks following gains in the U.S. amid the current AI boom," Concord Securities analyst Kerry Huang said, citing a 3.38 percent increase in the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index.
    句尾的「citing a 3.38 percent increase...」是現在分詞（-ing）引導的分詞構句，主詞是前面說話的分析師 Kerry Huang。這裡表示「一邊引用……的數據」或「藉此引用……」，用來精簡連接詞和主詞，使句子更緊湊。
  • 過去分詞作後位修飾語
    ..., but smartphone camera lens supplier Largan Precision Co. plunged 10 percent, the maximum daily decline, to end at NT$6,660.00 after a European brokerage issued a "sell" recommendation.
    雖然本句沒有直接使用過去分詞作後位修飾，但我們可以觀察「due later this week」（在第14段）中的形容詞片語用法。在此句中，「issued a 'sell' recommendation」是一個主動的子句，而多益常考如「data due later this week」中的 due 則是由被動或預定概念演變而來的形容詞用法，修飾前面的 data（預定於本週稍後公布的數據）。
  • 對等連接詞 while 表對比
    Eclat Textile Co. shed 2.24 percent to close at NT$306.00, while rival Makalot Industrial Co. ended down 0.76 percent at NT$195.00.
    連接詞 while 在此處用來表示「然而、而」，連接兩個結構相似的主谓子句，藉此對比兩家紡織競爭對手（儒鴻與聚陽）在股市中同步下跌的表現或不同幅度的股價走勢。
