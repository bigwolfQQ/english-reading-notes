曼城崛起蒙上陰影

Man City’s rise clouded by dark shadows

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • impropriety n. 不當行為、不規矩
    Manchester City’s fate is yet to be sealed, but reports they have been found guilty of widespread financial impropriety cast a new light on a meteoric rise...
    曼徹斯特城隊的命運尚未底定，但有關他們被判犯下廣泛財務違規行為的報導，讓他們......的過程，蒙上了全新且質疑的眼光。
  • meteoric adj. 迅猛的、流星般的
    ...reports they have been found guilty of widespread financial impropriety cast a new light on a meteoric rise from mid-table mediocrity to English Premier League domination...
    ...有關他們被判犯下廣泛財務違規行為的報導，讓他們從中游平庸球隊迅速崛起成為英格蘭超級聯賽霸主的過程，蒙上了全新且質疑的眼光。
  • takeover n. 併購、接收
    ...since a transformative Abu Dhabi takeover in 2008.
    自 2008 年經歷阿布達比改造性併購以來。
  • invest v. 投資
    However, the billions of pounds invested into east Manchester from the Middle East have yielded eight Premier League crowns...
    然而，來自中東投資到東曼徹斯特的數十億英鎊，已為球會帶來了 8 座英超冠軍......
  • yield v. 產生、帶來（利潤或成果）
    ...have yielded eight Premier League crowns, four FA Cups, seven League Cups and the club’s first Champions League title.
    ......已為球會帶來了 8 座英超冠軍、4 座足總盃、7 座聯賽盃以及球隊的第一座歐冠冠軍。
  • flout v. 公然蔑視、違抗、違反
    ...German news magazine Der Spiegel published a series of bombshell articles accusing City of flouting financial fair play rules.
    ......德國新聞雜誌《明鏡周刊》發表了一系列爆炸性報導，指控曼城違反財務公平競賽規則。
  • breach n. 違背、違反、違約
    In February 2023, the Premier League charged City with 115 breaches of FFP play rules from 2009 to 2018.
    2023 年 2 月，英超聯賽指控曼城在 2009 年至 2018 年期間違反 FFP 規則 115 項。
  • inflate v. 膨脹、誇大、拉抬（價格）
    The Premier League charges included artificially inflating the value of sponsorship deals from companies in Abu Dhabi linked to City’s ownership...
    英超聯賽的指控包括：人工提高與曼城所有權相關的阿布達比公司的贊助合約價值......
  • revenue n. 收益、營業收入
    In 2022, City topped the Deloitte Money League for the first time as the world’s highest revenue-generating club...
    2022 年，曼城首次榮登德勤足球財富聯賽榜首，成為全球營收最高的球會......
  • transfer n. （球員等）轉會、調動
    ...and they have just splashed out a record ￡460 million (US$523 million) on new players in one transfer window.
    ......他們最近才在一個轉會窗口中創紀錄地砸下 4.6 億英鎊（5.23 億美元）引進新球員。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    However, the billions of pounds invested into east Manchester from the Middle East have yielded eight Premier League crowns...
    句中的 "invested into east Manchester from the Middle East" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "billions of pounds"。原句等於 "the billions of pounds that were invested..."，省略了關係代名詞與be動詞，使句子更為精簡緊湊。
  • 現在分詞片語作結果狀語 (Present Participle Phrase of Result)
    In 2022, City topped the Deloitte Money League for the first time as the world’s highest revenue-generating club, and they have just splashed out a record ￡460 million (US$523 million) on new players in one transfer window.
    句中的 "revenue-generating" 是複合形容詞（由名詞加現在分詞組成），用來修飾 club，表達「產生營收的」。這類結構在商業及新聞英文中非常常見，用來形容具有特定產出能力的事物。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    City were found guilty by UEFA in February 2020 of breaking financial fair play (FFP) rules and given a two-year European ban and a fine.
    此句使用了被動語態 "were found guilty" 與 "were given"（句中省略了第二個 were），強調動作的承受者（曼城）而非執行者。在法律、紀律處分或官方裁決的新聞報導中，頻繁使用被動語態以維持客觀語氣。
  • 獨立分詞構句 / 伴隨狀況 (Absolute Participial Construction / Accompanying Circumstance)
    In the past few years, the financial charges have cast a shadow over the Etihad, even while trophies continued to be won.
    句尾使用了 "while trophies continued to be won"（雖然獎盃持續被贏得），此處的 while 引導一個副詞子句，表達同時發生的背景狀況，突顯曼城在面臨場外法律與財務指控的同時，場上依舊奪冠的對比。
