社論：蔣萬安的船正在進水

EDITORIAL: Chiang’s ship is taking on water

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • campaign n. 競選活動；運動
    Taipei Mayor Chiang Wan-an’s campaign team has finally started to get serious this month
    台北市長蔣萬安的競選團隊本月終於開始嚴肅以對
  • criticism n. 批評，指責
    drawn a larger wave of public criticism
    引發了更大一波公眾批評
  • candidate n. 候選人
    no major third-party candidates this year
    今年沒有主要的大黨候選人
  • challenger n. 挑戰者
    However, his main challenger, Democratic Progressive Party Legislator and Taipei mayoral candidate Puma Shen
    然而，他的主要挑戰者、民主進步黨立法委員暨台北市長候選人沈伯洋
  • performance n. 表現，績效
    with his performance being scrutinized by a nationwide audience
    其表現正受到全國觀眾的嚴格檢視
  • preferential treatment n. phr. 優惠待遇，特權
    as well as being suspected of giving preferential treatment to the mayor’s family.
    以及被懷疑給予市長家屬特權。
  • poll n. 民意調查
    Opinion polls, even from generally pro-KMT organizations, show Shen’s approval rating quickly catching up to Chiang’s
    民意調查，甚至是普遍親國民黨的機構所做的民調，都顯示沈伯洋的支持率迅速追上蔣萬安
  • tactics n. 策略，戰術
    as it shifts tactics from ignoring Shen to aggressive mudslinging
    將戰術從忽略沈伯洋轉變為具侵略性的泥巴戰
  • spokesperson n. 發言人
    that five of the KMT’s Taipei legislators would become spokespersons.
    五名國民黨台北立委將擔任發言人。
  • clarify v. 澄清，說明
    Shen quickly clarified that his article was discussing drug prevention policies in many countries
    沈伯洋迅速澄清，他的文章是在討論許多國家的毒品防制政策
  • backlash n. 強烈反彈
    Chiang’s Facebook post about Shen’s drug stance triggered a record-breaking backlash
    蔣萬安關於沈伯洋毒品立場的臉書貼文引發了破紀錄的反彈
  • competent adj. 有能力的，勝任的
    wondering if Chiang is even competent enough to steer the loose ship
    懷疑蔣萬安是否有能力掌舵這艘鬆散的船

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Phrase with Present Participle）作附加說明或結果
    raising concerns about whether the city government’s wheels are coming off.
    句尾的「raising...」是由對等子句簡化而來的現在分詞構句，用來補充說明前面整件事所帶來的結果或影響，譯為「引發了……」。
  • 被動語態的進行式（Present Continuous Passive Voice）
    with his performance being scrutinized by a nationwide audience
    「being + 過去分詞 (scrutinized)」構成現在進行式的被動語態，放在介系詞 with 的複合結構中，用來形容沈伯洋的表現「正在被全國觀眾嚴格檢視中」。
  • 複合形容詞（Compound Adjectives）
    Chiang’s Facebook post about Shen’s drug stance triggered a record-breaking backlash
    「record-breaking」是由名詞 (record) 加上現在分詞 (breaking) 組成的複合形容詞，用來修飾名詞 backlash，意思是「破紀錄的」。
  • 間接問句（Indirect Question）
    raising concerns about whether the city government’s wheels are coming off.
    「whether the city government’s wheels are coming off」是名詞子句，作為介系詞 about 的受詞。在間接問句中，語序為「連接詞 + 主詞 + 動詞」（whether + 主詞 + are coming off），不使用疑問句的倒裝語序。
