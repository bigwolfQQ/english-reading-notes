前總統蔡英文訪美期間與裴洛西會面

Former President Tsai reunites with Pelosi during U.S. visit

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • meet with phr. 會面、會晤
    Former Taiwan President Tsai Ing-wen (蔡英文) met with former United States House Speaker Nancy Pelosi in San Francisco on Tuesday...
    台灣前總統蔡英文週二在舊金山與美國前眾議院議長裴洛西會面...
  • following prep. 在……之後
    Following the meeting, Pelosi described Tsai in a social media post as a "courageous champion of democracy"...
    會面後，裴洛西在社群媒體貼文中將蔡英文形容為「勇敢的民主捍衛者」...
  • commitment n. 承諾、奉獻
    ...and Congress' commitment to the Taiwanese people and their freedom remains steadfast...
    ...且國會對台灣人民及其自由的承諾依然堅定不移...
  • steadfast adj. 堅定的、不變的
    ...and Congress' commitment to the Taiwanese people and their freedom remains steadfast, she added.
    她補充說，國會對台灣人民及其自由的承諾依然堅定不移。
  • contribution n. 貢獻
    ...in recognition of Pelosi's contributions to promoting Taiwan-U.S. relations.
    ...以表彰裴洛西對促進台美關係的貢獻。
  • regardless of phr. 不管、不顧
    "Over the years, regardless of changes in the international situation, Pelosi has consistently been a staunch friend of Taiwan," Tsai wrote.
    蔡英文寫道：「多年來，無論國際情勢如何變化，裴洛西始終是台灣堅定的朋友。」
  • consistently adv. 一貫地、始終如一地
    ...Pelosi has consistently been a staunch friend of Taiwan...
    ……裴洛西始終是台灣堅定的朋友……
  • convey v. 傳達、表達
    ...adding that she hoped the gift would convey to Pelosi the beauty and creativity of Taiwan as a maritime nation.
    ...並補充說她希望這份禮物能向裴洛西傳達台灣作為海洋國家的美麗與創意。
  • schedule v. 預定、安排（常用被動態 be scheduled to）
    She is scheduled to deliver the opening keynote address at Stanford DEFCON's 2026 Technology & National Security Conference on Wednesday.
    她預定於週三在史丹福大學 DEFCON 2026 科技與國家安全會議上發表開幕主題演說。
  • conduct v. 執行、舉辦、進行
    Pelosi's visit prompted China to conduct large-scale live-fire military drills around Taiwan...
    裴洛西的訪問促使中國在台灣周邊進行大規模實彈軍事演習……
  • achieve v. 實現、達成
    China claims Taiwan as part of its territory and has not renounced the use of force to achieve unification.
    中國聲稱台灣是其領土的一部分，且未放棄使用武力來實現統一。
  • term n. 任期、期間
    The current trip is Tsai's first visit to the United States since leaving office in May 2024 after serving two four-year terms as president.
    此行是蔡英文自2024年5月卸任（擔任兩屆四年任期的總統）以來首次訪問美國。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 / 形容詞用
    The two met at the San Francisco hotel where Tsai was staying during her visit to the city.
    句中的 "where Tsai was staying..." 是關係副詞引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的地方名詞 "hotel"，補充說明蔡英文停留的飯店。
  • 現在分詞作附帶狀況（分詞構句）
    ...adding that she hoped the gift would convey to Pelosi the beauty and creativity of Taiwan as a maritime nation.
    句尾的 ", adding that..." 是現在分詞作分詞構句，表示主詞在進行主要動作的同時，伴隨發出的另一個動作，相當於 "and she added that..."。
  • 被動語態的應用
    She is scheduled to deliver the opening keynote address at Stanford DEFCON's 2026 Technology & National Security Conference on Wednesday.
    句型 "be scheduled to V" 採用了被動語態的概念，用來表達某項行程或活動是「被預定、被安排」在某個時間舉行。
