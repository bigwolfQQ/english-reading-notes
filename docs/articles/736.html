文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作附帶說明／原因
    Hsiao visited the Italian island of Ventotene last week to attend the Second Ventotene European Conference for Freedom and Democracy at the invitation of European Parliament Vice President Pina Picierno, the conference's main initiator, and Carlo Corazza, head of the European Parliament Liaison Office in Italy.
    句中夾敘夾帶著同位語（the conference's main initiator... 等），用來補充說明前面人物的身分背景，讓新聞在單句中提供豐富的背景資訊。
  • 不定詞片語（To-infinitive）表達目的
    The trip was not announced in advance to avoid "unnecessary interference" by China...
    句尾的「to avoid ...」是不定詞片語作副詞用，用來修飾前面的動詞片語「was not announced」，表示「為了……目的而未事先公布」。這在多益閱讀與商務寫程中是非常常見的句構。
  • 關係代間詞子句（介系詞 + which）
    The EU reiterated its adherence to its "One-China Policy," under which it recognizes the People's Republic of China rather than the Republic of China (Taiwan).
    「under which」引導一個非限定關係子句，用來補充修飾前面的「One-China Policy」。其中「under」搭配政策（policy），意指「在……政策規範／情況下」，是正式商業與新聞英語中常見的高級文法結構。
