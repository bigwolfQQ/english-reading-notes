台北巨型機械大象遊行首晚吸引10萬人次

1st night of giant mechanical elephant parade in Taipei draws 100,000

單字解析

  • spectator n. 觀眾（尤指體育賽事或戶外活動）
    The first night of the "Trois éléphants passent ..." street performance drew around 100,000 Taiwanese and foreign spectators to the streets of Taipei's Shilin District on Saturday...
    「大象行過……」街頭表演的首場演出於週六吸引了約10萬名台灣及外國觀眾來到台北市士林區的街頭...
  • centerpiece n. 核心亮點、最重要的部分
    ...the parade, which featured three mechanical elephants, each 4.5 meters tall and 8 meters long, as its centerpiece of the performance, the city government said in a statement Saturday.
    市政府於週六的一份聲明中表示，該遊行以三隻高4.5公尺、長8公尺的機械大象作為表演的核心亮點。
  • collaboration n. 合作、協力
    The statement quoted Compagnie Oposito Artistic Director Jean-Raymond Jacob as describing the collaboration as one that seemed "impossible to play together."
    聲明引述歐波西托劇團藝術總監尚-雷蒙・雅各的說法，形容這次的合作似乎是「一場不可能一起演奏的表演」。
  • deploy v. 部署、調動（人員或裝備）
    To prepare for Sunday's performance, the TPAC said it would deploy dozens more police officers than the more than 400 stationed on Saturday to enhance security.
    為了準備週日的演出，北藝中心表示將部署比週六超過400名更多的警力，以加強維安。
  • enhance v. 提高、增加、強化
    To prepare for Sunday's performance, the TPAC said it would deploy dozens more police officers than the more than 400 stationed on Saturday to enhance security.
    為了準備週日的演出，北藝中心表示將部署比週六超過400名更多的警力，以加強維安。
  • restriction n. 限制、約束
    Traffic restrictions will also be imposed on Jihe Road between Zhongzheng Road and Wenlin Road from 5:30 p.m. Sunday.
    自週日傍晚5點30分起，基河路（中正路與文林路之間）也將實施交通管制。
  • impose v. 實施、強制推行（規定或稅收等）
    Traffic restrictions will also be imposed on Jihe Road between Zhongzheng Road and Wenlin Road from 5:30 p.m. Sunday.
    自週日傍晚5點30分起，基河路（中正路與文林路之間）也將實施交通管制。
  • congestion n. 擁擠、塞車
    The TPAC said congestion on nearby roads was observed Saturday night and urged road users to avoid the area if possible.
    北藝中心表示，週六晚間觀察到附近道路出現交通壅塞，並呼籲用路人如非必要盡量避開該區域。
  • accommodate v. 容納、順應、配合（需求）
    The Taipei Metro will adjust its train schedules to accommodate the large event, similar to its arrangements for the annual New Year's Eve celebrations, it added.
    台北捷運將調整列車班次以因應這場大型活動，這與跨年夜大型慶祝活動的安排類似，它補充道。
  • conspicuous adj. 顯眼的、明顯的
    Meanwhile, Puma Shen (沈伯洋), the ruling Democratic Progressive Party's candidate for Taipei mayor in November, told reporters that conspicuous signs indicating viewing areas and electronic signage to help direct pedestrian flows could perhaps help.
    同時，民進黨11月台北市長參選人沈伯洋向記者表示，醒目的觀賞區標示以及有助於引導人流的電子標示或許能有所幫助。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present/Past Participle Phrases as Post-modifiers)
    ...the parade, which featured three mechanical elephants, each 4.5 meters tall and 8 meters long, as its centerpiece of the performance, the city government said in a statement Saturday.
    句中的「each 4.5 meters tall and 8 meters long」為形容詞片語，用來補充說明前面的三隻機械大象（three mechanical elephants）的尺寸。這類結構在多益閱讀測驗中常用於細節題，用以描述產品規格或尺寸。
  • 現在分詞引導附帶狀況或結果 (Present Participle indicating Result or Concurrent Action)
    Some attendees also complained that crowd movement at the event was chaotic, resulting in bottlenecks and blocked pedestrian flows.
    句尾的「resulting in...」是現在分詞作分詞構句，表達前一個子句（人潮動線混亂）所自然導致的結果（造成瓶頸與行人動線受阻）。在多益商業英文中，常用 "resulting in + N." 來表達「因而導致……」的因果關係。
  • 被動語態的應用 (Passive Voice)
    Traffic restrictions will also be imposed on Jihe Road between Zhongzheng Road and Wenlin Road from 5:30 p.m. Sunday.
    句型「will also be imposed」採用了未來式的被動語態（will be + 過去分詞）。在新聞英文與公文、合約等商業書信中，當強調動作的承受者（此處為主詞 Traffic restrictions 交通管制）而非執行者時，經常會大量使用被動語態。
