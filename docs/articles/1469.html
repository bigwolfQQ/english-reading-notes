拜仁慕尼黑在八秒內失球

Bayern Munich concede goal after eight seconds

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • priority n. 優先事項、優先權
    Bayern coach Vincent Kompany said his team’s visit to Augsburg was a “priority game” after their only league loss against Augsburg in Munich last season
    拜仁教練文森特·孔帕尼表示，由於上賽季在慕尼黑對陣奧格斯堡吞下了他們唯一的聯賽敗仗，因此球隊此次客場對陣奧格斯堡是一場「優先級別的比賽」
  • ultimately 最終、最後
    he cannot have imagined making a worse start to what ultimately ended in a 2-2 draw
    他絕對無法想像這場最終以 2-2 平局收場的比賽會有如此糟糕的開局
  • attempt n. 嘗試、企圖
    who botched his attempt at a chipped clearance and allowed Robin Fellhauer to score after just eight seconds.
    他處理吊傳解圍時失誤，讓羅賓·費爾豪爾僅僅在八秒鐘後就得分。
  • equalize v. 使平手、扳平比分
    Nathaniel Brown finally equalized in the 81st.
    內森·布朗終於在第 81 分鐘將比分扳平。
  • dominance n. 優勢、統治地位
    Bayer Leverkusen, the only team to break Bayern’s dominance in any of the last 14 seasons, held on for a 4-3 win in Mainz
    勒沃庫森作為過去 14 個賽季中唯一打破拜仁統治地位的球隊，在美因茨以 4-3 險勝
  • suspension n. 暫停、中止
    Rayo fans forced a suspension of their team’s match against Athletic Bilbao
    巴列卡諾球迷迫使他們與畢爾巴鄂競技隊的比賽暫停
  • concern n. 擔憂、憂慮
    It was Rayo’s first league game of the season at home since city authorities initially closed the stadium over safety concerns.
    這是巴列卡諾本賽季的第一個主場聯賽，此前市政府因安全考量最初關閉了球場。
  • defending adj. 衛冕的
    Gabriel Jesus scored in his first start and the defending champions eased to a 3-0 win over Getafe.
    加布里埃爾·熱蘇斯在其首次首發中破門，衛冕冠軍以 3-0 輕取赫塔菲。
  • recover v. 恢復、復原、扭轉局勢
    Bruno Guimaraes came off the bench to score on his home debut as defending champions Arsenal recovered to beat Leeds 2-1 in the Premier League.
    布魯諾·吉馬良斯替補登場並在主場首秀中進球，衛冕冠軍阿森納在英超聯賽中逆轉以 2-1 擊敗利茲聯。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    Vinicius was celebrating after scoring when referee Isidro Diaz de Mera was called by the video assistant to review the moments before his goal.
    句中的「celebrating after scoring」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾主詞 Vinicius，表達他在進球後「正在進行」的動作。這種結構在新聞英語中常用於描述事件發生時的背景動作。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾與表狀態 (Past Participle Phrase)
    Bayern coach Vincent Kompany said his team’s visit to Augsburg was a “priority game” after their only league loss against Augsburg in Munich last season...
    「lost against Augsburg in Munich last season」為過去分詞片語當作形容詞用，修飾前面的名詞 loss（敗仗），帶有被動與完成的意味（即「在上賽季對陣奧格斯堡時吞下的」敗仗）。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause with 'who')
    It began with Bayern playing the ball back from the kickoff to Neuer, who botched his attempt at a chipped clearance...
    句中的「who botched his attempt at a chipped clearance」是非限定用法（帶逗號）的關係子句，先行詞為前面的專有名詞 Neuer。用 who 引導來補充說明諾伊爾做了什麼失誤，使句子結構緊湊而不需拆成兩句話。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Independent Participial Construction)
    ...leaving Real Madrid needing to hang on for the final 20 minutes to earn a 1-0 win.
    「leaving Real Madrid needing...」是由現在分詞引起的結果分詞構句。前面的主句動作（Vinicius 被罰下）導致了後方接續的狀況，省略了連接詞而直接用分詞表達自然的因果或附帶結果。
