部長表示：台灣面臨每天 260 萬次網路攻擊

Taiwan faces 2.6m daily cyberattacks, minister says

單字解析

  • frequently adv. 頻繁地、經常
    Minister of Digital Affairs Lin Yin-ching (林宜敬) said yesterday, adding that hackers from China, North Korea, Iran and Russia frequently target the nation.
    數位發展部長林宜敬昨天表示，並補充說來自中國、北韓、伊朗和俄羅斯的駭客經常將我國作為目標。
  • target v. 把...作為攻擊目標、瞄準
    Aside from cybersecurity threats, Taiwan is targeted by scammers who use hybrid tactics...
    除了資安威脅外，台灣也成為使用混合戰術的詐騙集團的目標...
  • initiative n. 倡議、新措施
    Lin made the remarks at an event hosted by the Digital Trust Association to launch the initiative designating Sept. 21 as Taiwan Cyber Day.
    林宜敬是在數位信任協會主辦的一場活動中做出上述表示，該活動旨在發起將 9 月 21 日指定為「台灣資安日」的倡議。
  • resilient adj. 具韌性的、有彈性的、恢復力強的
    Cybersecurity needs to be guarded not only by the government but also by a resilient society, Lin said.
    林宜敬說，資安不僅需要政府守護，也需要具備韌性的社會來共同防範。
  • on average phr. 平均
    On average, Taiwan counters more than 2.6 million cyberattacks per day, more than other Asia-Pacific nations.
    平均而言，台灣每天抵禦超過 260 萬次網路攻擊，多於其他亞太國家。
  • counter v. 抵禦、反擊、反對
    On average, Taiwan counters more than 2.6 million cyberattacks per day, more than other Asia-Pacific nations.
    平均而言，台灣每天抵禦超過 260 萬次網路攻擊，多於其他亞太國家。
  • bolster v. 強化、改善、支撐
    However, this also helped bolster Taiwan’s cybersecurity industry,” he said...
    然而，這也協助強化了台灣的資安產業...
  • opportunity n. 機會、時機
    ...adding that he hoped that the nation would turn the threat into a business opportunity, exporting the experience of countering cybersecurity threats overseas.
    ...並補充說他希望國家能將威脅轉化為商機，將抵禦資安威脅的經驗輸出海外。
  • export v. 輸出、出口
    ...exporting the experience of countering cybersecurity threats overseas.
    ...將抵禦資安威脅的經驗輸出海外。
  • statistics n. 統計數據、統計學
    Aside from cybersecurity threats, Taiwan is targeted by scammers who use hybrid tactics, combining deepfake videos, fraudulent billing information and text messages to trick online users, National Security Bureau statistics show.
    國家安全局統計顯示，除了資安威脅外，台灣也成為使用混合戰術的詐騙集團的目標，這些詐騙集團結合了深偽影片、詐欺帳單資訊與簡訊來欺騙網路用戶。
  • distinguish v. 區分、辨別
    Although 70 percent of respondents said they could distinguish between genuine and artificial intelligence-generated content...
    雖然 70% 的受訪者表示他們能分辨真實與人工智慧生成的內容...
  • misconception n. 誤解、錯誤觀念
    People should discard the misconception that cybersecurity has nothing to do with them, Chan said...
    詹婷怡說，人們應該摒棄資安與自己無關的錯誤觀念...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（V-ing）作附加補充說明
    Taiwan faces about 2.6 million cybersecurity attacks every day, Minister of Digital Affairs Lin Yin-ching (林宜敬) said yesterday, adding that hackers from China, North Korea, Iran and Russia frequently target the nation.
    句尾的「adding that...」是由現在分詞構成的分詞片語，用來補充說明主句動作發生時同時發生的事或進一步的結果，在新聞英文中非常常見，相當於「and added that...」。
  • 不僅...而且... (Not only... but also...)
    Cybersecurity needs to be guarded not only by the government but also by a resilient society, Lin said.
    「not only A but also B」用於連接兩個並列的主體（此處為受動詞 guard 支配的受詞片語），強調兩者同樣重要。當連接主詞時需注意主謂一致，連接其他成分則保持對等詞性。
  • 關係代名詞子句（who引導）修飾先行詞
    Aside from cybersecurity threats, Taiwan is targeted by scammers who use hybrid tactics, combining deepfake videos, fraudulent billing information and text messages to trick online users, National Security Bureau statistics show.
    句中的「who use hybrid tactics...」是用來修飾前方先行詞「scammers（詐騙份子）」的限定修飾關係子句，用以補充說明這些詐騙份子的特徵與作案手法。
  • 連接詞 although 引導的讓步副詞子句
    Although 70 percent of respondents said they could distinguish between genuine and artificial intelligence-generated content, only about 60 percent were able to accurately identify such content in a test.
    「Although」用來引導讓步子句，表示「雖然、儘管」，帶出一個與主要子句形成對比或轉折的背景條件（雖然70%的人自信能分辨，但實際測試只有60%辦得到）。
