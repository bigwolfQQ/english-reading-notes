解析英語（常春藤）

ANALYTICAL ENGLISH 解析英語(常春藤)

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • interaction n. 互動
    The concept of a “social battery” is a metaphor used to describe the amount of energy an individual has for social interaction.
    「社交電池」這個概念是一種隱喻，用來描述一個人從事社交互動時所擁有的精力多寡。
  • periodic a. 定期的，週期的
    Like a physical battery, this energy runs low over time and requires periodic recharging.
    就像實體電池一樣，這種能量會隨著時間逐漸耗盡，且需要定期充電。
  • recharge vi. & vt. (使自己)恢復精力；充電
    Like a physical battery, this energy runs low over time and requires periodic recharging.
    就像實體電池一樣，這種能量會隨著時間逐漸耗盡，且需要定期充電。
  • individual n. 個人
    Understanding these patterns helps individuals manage their social lives more effectively.
    了解這些模式有助於人們更有效地管理自己的社交生活。
  • effectively adv. 有效地
    Understanding these patterns helps individuals manage their social lives more effectively.
    了解這些模式有助於人們更有效地管理自己的社交生活。
  • differ vi. 不同，相異
    In general, a social battery functions differently between introverts and extroverts.
    一般而言，社交電池在內向者與外向者之間的運作方式有所不同。
  • variation n. 變化，差異
    This difference reflects a variation in temperament rather than a lack of social skills.
    這種差別反映的是性格上的差異，而非缺乏社交能力。
  • respond vi. 反應，回應
    It shows how people respond to social environments in different ways.
    這顯示出人們對社交環境的反應方式各不相同。
  • factor n. 因素，要素
    Several factors can drain a person’s energy.
    有幾個因素會消耗一個人的能量。
  • quality n. 品質
    These include the people they interact with, the type and quality of interaction, and the size of the group.
    這些包括他們互動的對象、互動的類型與品質，以及群體的規模。
  • affect vt. 影響
    Internal stressors like anxiety can also affect how quickly energy is depleted.
    如焦慮等內在壓力源也可能會影響能量消耗的速度。
  • maintain vt. 維持，保持
    Recognizing personal limits and taking breaks are essential to maintaining a healthy balance between social life and personal well-being.
    認識個人的極限和休息對維持社交生活與個人身心健康之間的良好平衡不可或缺。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The concept of a “social battery” is a metaphor used to describe the amount of energy an individual has for social interaction.
    句中的「used to describe...」是由過去分詞開頭的片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 metaphor（隱喻），等於形容詞子句「which is used to describe...」，省略了關代與be動詞，使語氣更為精簡。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞 (Gerund Phrase as Subject)
    Understanding these patterns helps individuals manage their social lives more effectively.
    句首的「Understanding these patterns」是由動名詞（Understanding）帶領的動名詞片語，在句中扮演主詞的角色，動名詞片語作主詞時視為單數，因此後方接單數動詞 helps。
  • 間接問句 (Indirect Question)
    It shows how people respond to social environments in different ways.
    「how people respond to social environments...」是由疑問詞 how 引導的名詞子句，在句中作動詞 shows 的受詞。間接問句的語序為「疑問詞 + 主詞 + 動詞」，不採用倒裝句型。
  • 對等連接詞連接平行結構 (Parallel Structure with Conjunctions)
    When a person’s social battery is low, they may feel tired, stressed, irritable, or less interested in talking.
    句中的「tired, stressed, irritable, or less interested in talking」是一連串由對等連接詞 or 連接的形容詞與形容詞片語，共同構成並列結構，修飾主詞 they 在該狀態下的感受。
