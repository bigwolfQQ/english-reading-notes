瑞典大選選情膠着難分勝負

Sweden’s election too close to call

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • incumbent adj. 現任的
    Sweden’s general election was too close to call early yesterday, as the incumbent prime minister and the left-wing opposition leader both said they were ready to govern...
    隨着現任總理與左翼反對派領袖皆表示已準備好執政……瑞典大選在昨天清晨陷入選情膠着、難分勝負的局面。
  • opposition n. 反對黨，對立
    With more than 90 percent of votes counted, four opposition parties led by Swedish Social Democratic Party Chairwoman Magdalena Andersson, held a three-seat majority...
    在已點算的90%以上選票中，由瑞典社會民主黨主席安德森領導的四個反對黨，取得了三席的優勢……
  • majority n. 多數，多數黨
    ...held a three-seat majority in the 349-seat parliament over Swedish Prime Minister Ulf Kristersson’s right-wing alliance...
    ……在349席的國會中，擊敗瑞典總理克里斯特松的右翼聯盟，取得三席的優勢……
  • alliance n. 聯盟，同盟
    ...over Swedish Prime Minister Ulf Kristersson’s right-wing alliance, which includes the anti-immigration Sweden Democrats.
    ……擊敗瑞典總理克里斯特松的右翼聯盟（其中包括反移民的瑞典民主黨）。
  • confirm v. 證實，確認
    “For now, we are in the lead, and if that is confirmed, Sweden will have a new government,” 59-year-old Andersson... told her supporters.
    現年59歲的安德森……對其支持者表示：「目前我們領先，若此結果獲得證實，瑞典將迎來新政府。」
  • attempt n. / v. 嘗試，企圖
    Kristersson, whose Moderate Party was credited with 19.9 percent of the counted votes, said that if the result stood, he would respect Andersson’s attempts to form a government...
    克里斯特松的溫和黨獲得已點算選票的19.9%，他表示若最終結果不變，他將尊重安德森組閣的嘗試……
  • differ v. 意見分歧，不同 (源自 article 中的 differences)
    However, he said that the four had irreconcilable differences, primarily between the Left Party and the Centre Party, and were unlikely to agree on a government program.
    不過，他指出這四黨之間存在着無法調和的分歧，主要在左翼黨與中間黨之間，雙方不太可能就施政綱領達成共識。
  • examine v. 檢視，審查，檢查
    “I will therefore, even if tonight’s result stands, examine whether there are any possibilities within this non-socialist parliamentary majority to find some workable government constellation,” Kristersson told his supporters.
    「因此，即便今晚的結果維持不變，我仍將檢視在這個非社會主義國會多數中，是否存在任何可能性來找出可行的政府組合，」克里斯特松對支持者說道。
  • workable adj. 可行的，行得通的
    ...to find some workable government constellation,” Kristersson told his supporters.
    ……來找出可行的政府組合，」克里斯特松對支持者說道。
  • support n. / v. 支持，贊助
    In a big surprise, the anti-immigration Sweden Democrats, which entered parliament in 2010, lost support for the first time...
    在一個重大驚奇中，於2010年進入國會的反移民瑞典民主黨首次失去支持……
  • outcome n. 結果，結局
    The final outcome might not be clear for a few days, several party leaders said.
    幾位黨領袖表示，最終結果可能需要幾天時間才會明朗。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句 (Present Participle as Adverbial Clause)
    Sweden’s general election was too close to call early yesterday, as the incumbent prime minister and the left-wing opposition leader both said they were ready to govern and the far right was seen losing ground.
    句尾的 "and the far right was seen losing ground" 或文章其他段落中的 "With more than 90 percent of votes counted..." 運用了分詞來補充說明背景或伴隨狀況。「With + 名詞 + 過去分詞 (counted)」形成獨立分詞構句，用來表示「在……的情況下」或「隨着……」，是多益閱讀與商務英文中非常常見的結構。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    However, her party — which ruled the Scandinavian country for most of the 20th century and remains Sweden’s largest — also looked to be heading for its worst score in more than a century with 28 percent of the vote.
    句中的破折號內夾了一個由 "which" 引導的非限定關係子句（"which ruled... and remains..."），用來補充說明 "her party"（她的政黨）的額外背景資訊。非限定子句通常用逗號或破折號隔開，若刪除該子句，主要子句的文法結構依然完整。
  • 假設語氣/條件句 (Conditional Sentences)
    “For now, we are in the lead, and if that is confirmed, Sweden will have a new government,” 59-year-old Andersson... told her supporters.
    此句使用第一類條件句（First Conditional），結構為「If + 現在式動詞, 主持人 + will + 原形動詞」。用來表達未來可能實現的真實條件與其結果（「如果這項結果獲得證實，瑞典將會擁有一個新政府」）。
