希臘爆冷擊敗尤爾根·克洛普率領的德國隊

Greece stun Juergen Klopp’s Germany

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • substitute n. / v. 替補球員 / 代替
    Substitute Dimitrios Kourbelis on Sunday gave Greece a shock 1-0 UEFA Nations League win
    替補球員迪米特里奧斯·庫爾貝利斯周日助希臘在歐國聯爆冷以 1 比 0 獲勝
  • replacement n. 替代人選、接替者
    Portugal coach Jorge Jesus dropped Cristiano Ronaldo and was rewarded as his replacement Goncalo Ramos scored the winner
    葡萄牙教練若熱·熱蘇斯未讓克里斯蒂亞諾·羅納爾多先發，其替代人選貢薩洛·拉莫斯攻入致勝球，此調度獲得回報
  • possession n. 擁有、佔有（在體育中指控球權）
    Although midfielder Bambase Conte had an impressive debut and Germany dominated possession, they rarely threatened Konstantinos Tzolakis’ goal.
    儘管中場球員班巴斯·孔特上演了令人印象深刻的處子秀，且德國隊掌控了控球權，但他們很少威脅到球門。
  • boycott v. / n. 聯合抵制、拒絕參與
    which almost did not take place due to heavy pressure back in Ireland on the team to boycott the game over the war in Gaza.
    由於愛爾蘭國內對球隊就加薩戰爭抵制比賽施加了巨大壓力，這場比賽差點無法舉行。
  • contribute v. 貢獻、促成
    “The most important thing is to show what I can do whenever I’m called upon and to contribute to the team,” Ramos told Sport TV
    「最重要的是在獲得徵召時展現出我能做的事，並為球隊做出貢獻，」拉莫斯告訴體育電視台
  • advantage n. 優勢、有利條件
    Denmark doubled their advantage 13 minutes later when Gustav Isaksen’s deflected shot spun in off the crossbar.
    13 分鐘後，古斯塔夫·伊薩克森的折射射門擊中橫樑彈入網內，丹麥將領先優勢擴大一倍。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況或結果 (Present Participle as Adverbial Clause)
    Denmark doubled their advantage 13 minutes later when Gustav Isaksen’s deflected shot spun in off the crossbar.
    句中的「when Gustav Isaksen’s deflected shot spun in off the crossbar」用以引導時間子句，而過去分詞「deflected」則作為形容詞修飾前面的名詞「shot」，表示「被折射的」射門。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Juergen Klopp was made to wait for his maiden victory in charge of an ineffective Germany
    句型「was made to wait」使用了被動語態（be + 過去分詞），強調尤爾根·克洛普「被迫」等待他在德國隊執教生涯的首場勝利。
