勞工團體敦促對違反基本工資規定處以統一罰則

Labor groups urge uniform penalties for minimum wage violations

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • adopt v. 採行，採用
    Labor groups on Wednesday called on the government to adopt uniform nationwide penalty standards for employers that violate minimum wage rules...
    勞工團體於週三呼籲政府，針對違反基本工資規定的雇主採行全國一致的裁罰標準...
  • violate v. 違反，違背
    ...employers that violate minimum wage rules, arguing that wide disparities among local authorities weaken enforcement of the law.
    ...違反基本工資規定的雇主，並主張地方主管機關之間的巨大落差削弱了法律的執行力。
  • enforcement n. 執行，實施
    ...arguing that wide disparities among local authorities weaken enforcement of the law.
    ...並主張地方主管機關之間的巨大落差削弱了法律的執行力。
  • specific adj. 明確的，特定的
    ...while others have no specific standards for minimum wage violations.
    ...而其他政府則沒有針對違反基本工資的具體標準。
  • maximum adj. 最高限度的，最大值的
    Thresholds for imposing the statutory maximum fine of NT$1 million range from six to 17 violations across jurisdictions...
    各管轄區對處以法定最高新台幣100萬元罰鍰的門檻，從6次到17次違規不等...
  • advantage n. 優勢
    Yang warned that such disparities could undermine the effectiveness of minimum wage legislation and give noncompliant employers an unfair advantage over those that follow the law.
    楊書瑋警告，這種落差可能會破壞最低工資立法的有效性，並使不合規的雇主在面對遵守法律的雇主時享有不公平的優勢。
  • establish v. 建立，設立
    He called on the Ministry of Labor (MOL) to establish a nationwide standard under which employers found in violation five or more times would face the statutory maximum fine of NT$1 million...
    他呼籲勞動部（MOL）建立一項全國一致的標準：不論公司規模大小，被發現違規達5次或以上的雇主，將面臨新台幣100萬元的法定最高罰鍰...
  • deliberately adv. 故意地，蓄意地
    "Five violations are already a lot, which suggests the employer is deliberately breaking the law," he said.
    他說：「違規5次已經很多了，這暗示該雇主是故意知法犯法。」
  • account for phr. v. 占（比例），說明
    The six special municipalities accounted for 62.1 percent of the cases, while the support services sector accounted for the largest share by industry, at 33.33 percent.
    六都占了案件總數的6.21%，而以行業別來看，支援服務業占了最大比例，占了33.33%。
  • amendment n. 修正，修正案
    Once the amendment is completed, minimum wage violation cases nationwide "will be handled according to a uniform set of principles," MOL said...
    勞動部指出，一旦修法完成，全國違反基本工資的案件「都將根據一套統一的原則來處理」...
  • performance n. 表現，績效
    ...citing Taiwan's strong economic performance and continued price increases.
    ...鑑於台灣強勁的經濟表現與持續上漲的物價。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作為後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Labor groups on Wednesday called on the government to adopt uniform nationwide penalty standards for employers that violate minimum wage rules, arguing that wide disparities among local authorities weaken enforcement of the law.
    句尾的「arguing that...」是由現在分詞開頭的片語，用來補充說明主句所發生的動作及其背後的原因或理由，相當於「and they argued that...」的簡化用法，在新聞英文中非常常見。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    He called on the Ministry of Labor (MOL) to establish a nationwide standard under which employers found in violation five or more times would face the statutory maximum fine of NT$1 million...
    句中的「under which...」是「介系詞 + 關係代名詞」引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾先行詞「a nationwide standard」，在此表達「在該標準之下」的意思，使句子結構更緊湊、正式。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Once the amendment is completed, minimum wage violation cases nationwide "will be handled according to a uniform set of principles," MOL said...
    句中的「is completed」與「will be handled」皆採用被動語態（be + 過去分詞），強調動作的承受者（修正案、違規案件），這在商業與法律新聞中經常用來客觀陳述程序或規定。
