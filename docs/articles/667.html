文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾（形容詞用）
    Average monthly regular wages, adjusted for inflation, rose 1.12 percent from a year earlier to NT$44,113...
    句中的「adjusted for inflation」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞短語「Average monthly regular wages」。其原貌相當於形狀為「which were adjusted for inflation」的形容詞子句，經簡化後直接保留過去分詞，用以表達「經通膨調整後的」薪資。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句（補充說明）
    ...marking the largest gain for the same period since 2020, when they increased 1.60 percent, the Directorate General of Budget, Accounting and Statistics (DGBAS) said.
  • 現在分詞短語作結果狀語
    ...with real aggregate earnings recording their strongest growth for the period in 11 years, according to government data released Monday.
    句中的「recording their strongest growth...」是以「with + 名詞 + 現在分詞」的附帶狀況結構呈現，用來補充說明主句動作發生時伴隨的具體情況，在此處精準描述實質總薪資所創下的紀錄。
  • 形容詞最高級的用法
    Real average aggregate earnings -- including regular wages as well as non-regular income such as overtime pay and bonuses -- increased 2.33 percent year-on-year to NT$434,420, the fastest growth for the January-July period since 2015...
    句中的「the fastest growth」使用了形容詞最高級（fastest），用來強調該段期間的成長速度是自 2015 年以來最快的，常在商業與經濟新聞中用來凸顯數據的突破性表現。
  • 表身分或特徵的限定關係代名詞子句
    Under the OECD definition, low-paid workers are those whose annual earnings are below two-thirds of the median annual wage.
    句中的「whose annual earnings are below...」是由所有格關係代名詞 whose 引導的限定關係子句，用來修飾先行詞「those（那些人）」，指稱符合特定年收入條件的低薪勞工群體。
