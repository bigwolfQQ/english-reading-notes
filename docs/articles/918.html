日本提高永居門檻，引發憤慨

Japan raises the bar on residency. Cue the outrage

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • residency n. 居住權、居留
    permanent residency in a country that is not your own can be a prickly issue.
    在一個不屬於你的國家獲得永久居留權可能是一個棘手且敏感的問題。
  • enforcement n. 執行、實施、取締
    including higher renewal fees for visas and stricter enforcement of overstayers.
    包括提高簽證的續簽費用以及更嚴格地執行逾期居留者的取締。
  • proposal n. 提案、建議
    The latest proposal is to raise the bar on permanent residency
    最新的提案是提高永久居留權的門檻
  • proficiency n. 精通、熟練程度
    envisioning taking into account language proficiency
    預計將把語言能力納入考量
  • solvent adj. 有償付能力的、有資力償債的
    and examining applicants’ ability to remain solvent after retirement.
    並檢視申請人在退休後保持償付能力（財務獨立）的能力。
  • entitlement n. （享有某事的）權利、資格
    By further separating permission to work from an entitlement to remain permanently
    透過進一步將工作許可與永久留下來的權利區分開來
  • onerous adj. 繁重的、費力的、艱鉅的
    Conditions would nonetheless be far less onerous than in many states
    儘管如此，這些條件仍遠不及許多國家的條件那麼繁重
  • incentive n. 誘因、鼓勵
    economic incentives would almost always trump other concerns.
    經濟誘機幾乎總是能勝過其他顧慮。
  • proxy n. 代理、替代指標
    Income is only a rough proxy for economic value
    收入只是衡量經濟價值的一個粗略指標
  • sector n. 產業、部門
    Japan would in future likely have to make exceptions for sectors where workers are needed
    未來日本可能不得不為那些需要勞動的產業做出特例

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The latest proposal is to raise the bar on permanent residency, adding requirements for minimum salary and Japanese language ability.
    句尾的「adding requirements for...」是由現在分詞短語引導的補充說明，用來描述前面提案所產生的具體內容或伴隨結果，相當於 and adds requirements... 的簡化。
  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況 (Participle Clause of Concomitant Circumstance)
    By further separating permission to work from an entitlement to remain permanently, the latest changes merely advance this policy.
    「By + 動名詞短語」用來表達手段或方法（藉由……），而逗號之後的主句則接續說明所達成的結果或動作，是商業與新聞英文中非常高頻且正式的句構。
  • 假設語氣（與現在事實相反）(Subjunctive Mood)
    Critics often fail to appreciate that the Japan of today is no longer a country that needs to bend over backward to advertise itself as a destination.
    雖然此句並未使用典型的 if 假設句，但「is no longer a country that needs to...」以直述語氣表達出現實狀態的轉變，而句中的否定與比較意味（no longer）常與假設情境對比，是多益閱讀常考的語意轉折點。
  • 關係代間詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    Japan was long an outlier in requiring neither language ability nor an explicit minimum salary, instead largely gatekeeping permanent residency behind the number of years spent in the country — seemingly assuming that only the very dedicated would stay so long anyway.
    句中的「spent in the country」是省略了「which were」的過去分詞片語，修飾前面的「the number of years」；而結尾的「that only the very dedicated would stay...」則是名詞子句，作為 assuming 的受詞。
