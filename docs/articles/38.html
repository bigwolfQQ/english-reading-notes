茲維列夫在美國網球公開賽驚險擊敗海利斯晉級

Zverev survives Halys scare at US Open

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • survive v. 挺過，倖存，艱難度過
    Alexander Zverev survived another almighty scare at the US Open...
    亞歷山大·茲維列夫在美國網球公開賽中再次挺過了一場極大的驚嚇...
  • secure v. 獲得，取得，確保
    ...to secure a place in the third round of the Grand Slam.
    ...以取得晉級該大滿貫第三輪的席位。
  • resilience n. 恢復力，韌性，應變能力
    ...forcing him to summon his resilience again and stay on track for a maiden title in New York.
    ...這迫使他再次展現韌性，以保持在紐約爭奪首座冠軍的正軌上。
  • performance n. 表現，績效，演出
    The performance was quite shit to be honest...
    老實說，今天的表現實在有夠爛...
  • dominant adj. 主導的，佔優勢的，宰制的
    ...Elena Rybakina put on a dominant performance to beat Spain’s Jessica Bouzas Maneiro...
    ...埃лена·萊巴金娜則繳出宰制全場的表現擊敗西班牙的潔西卡·布薩斯·馬內羅...
  • expectations n. 期待，預期
    ...delivered on expectations in the night’s final session...
    ...在當晚的最後一場賽事中不負眾望...
  • pursuit n. 追求，尋求
    ...extending her pursuit of a third Grand Slam crown.
    ...延續她對第三座大滿貫金杯的追逐。
  • manage v. 設法完成，管理，應付
    ...but I’m happy I managed to close it out...
    ...但我很高興我設法拿下了比賽...
  • preparation n. 準備，籌備
    “It’s true it wasn’t perfect preparation at all,” Rybakina said.
    萊巴金娜說：「沒錯，這絕對不是完美的備戰。」

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Participial Phrase）作附加說明/結果
    ...forcing him to summon his resilience again and stay on track for a maiden title in New York.
    句尾的「forcing...」是由對等子句簡化而來的現在分詞構句，用來補充說明前面整件事所產生的結果（迫使他……）。
  • 分裂句（Cleft Sentence: It is/was... that...）
    “That’s what I go to the gym for, that’s what I go to the track for, what I train for, to be able to come out on top in these difficult moments,” he said.
    句中的「That's what...」是用來強調特定原因或目的的句型，將想要強調的受詞部分（what I go to the gym for）移到後方透過代名詞 That 來凸顯。
  • 關係代名詞子句（Relative Clause）修飾先行詞
    The Kazakh, who is looking to climb to the top of the WTA rankings with a title in New York, delivered on expectations...
    「who is looking to...」是一個非限定性關係代名詞子句，置於主詞 The Kazakh 後方，用來補充說明這位選手目前的動向與目標。
  • 分詞片語修飾主詞（Past Participle as Adjective/Modifier）
    Rybakina had dispelled doubts over her fitness after retiring from the Cincinnati Open in the build-up to the tournament...
    句中的「retiring from...」是現在分詞片語置於介系詞 after 之後，當作動名词使用，表達「在……之後退賽」的時間點。
