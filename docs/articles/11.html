文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作後置修飾語
    ...after a rights group reported that a lawyer representing detained leaders of an underground church in Beijing had fled to the nation.
    句中的「representing detained leaders...」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 a lawyer，相當於形容詞子句「who represented...」。這在英文新聞中非常常見，能有效精簡句子結構。
  • 過去分詞作形容詞修飾名詞
    ...a lawyer representing detained leaders of an underground church in Beijing had fled to the nation.
    句中的 detained 是過去分詞，用作形容詞放在名詞 leaders 前面，表示「被拘留的」領袖。過去分詞表被動或完成的狀態。
  • Because of 複合介系詞片語表示原因
    “Wang and her family left China because of concerns about increasing government pressure related to her legal work...
    「because of」後面接名詞片語（如 concerns about...），用來表達行為發生的原因。需注意與連接詞 because 的區別，because 後面必須接完整的子句（主詞加動詞）。
