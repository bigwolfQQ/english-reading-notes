單字解析

  • accommodation n. 臨時住宿；安置
    Taiwan yesterday said it has provided “temporary accommodation” to a Chinese citizen, after a rights group reported that a lawyer representing detained leaders of an underground church in Beijing had fled to the nation.
  • detain v. 拘留；扣押
    Members of the Zion Church, including the founder Ezra Jin (金明日), were detained in October last year on “suspicion of the illegal use of information networks.”
  • representation n. 法律代理；辯護
    “Wang and her family left China because of concerns about increasing government pressure related to her legal work and representation of detained Beijing Zion Church leaders,” ChinaAid said on Thursday.
  • humanitarian grounds n. phr. 人道考量；人道立場
    Responding to reports about a “Chinese lawyer seeking assistance in Taiwan,” the MAC said the government has provided a “Chinese national transferring through a Taiwanese airport” with “temporary accommodation on humanitarian grounds and is following up on the matter.”
  • follow up on phr. v. 持續跟進；後續處理
    Responding to reports about a “Chinese lawyer seeking assistance in Taiwan,” the MAC said the government has provided a “Chinese national transferring through a Taiwanese airport” with “temporary accommodation on humanitarian grounds and is following up on the matter.”

    Taiwan has no law for handling political asylum.
  • suspicion n. 嫌疑；懷疑

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾
    Taiwan yesterday said it has provided “temporary accommodation” to a Chinese citizen, after a rights group reported that a lawyer representing detained leaders of an underground church in Beijing had fled to the nation.
    「representing detained leaders...」為現在分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞「a lawyer」，相當於形容詞子句「who represented...」，用來精簡句子結構。
  • 分詞構句（現在分詞置於句首表示背景或回應）
    Responding to reports about a “Chinese lawyer seeking assistance in Taiwan,” the MAC said the government has provided a “Chinese national transferring through a Taiwanese airport” with “temporary accommodation on humanitarian grounds and is following up on the matter.”
    句首的「Responding to...」為分詞構句，省略了主詞「the MAC」（陸委會），用來表示主要動作發生時的背景與動機：「（陸委會）在回應相關報導時表示……」。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾（被動含意）
    Eight of the Zion Church members detained in October are still being held, ChinaAid said.
    「detained in October」為過去分詞片語，放在名詞「members」之後做後位修飾，修飾「在十月份被拘留的（成員）」，相當於形容詞子句「who were detained in October」。
  • 同位語（補充說明前方名詞）
    Wang was a lawyer for detained leaders of Zion Church, one of China’s unregistered churches, ChinaAid said.
    名詞片語「one of China’s unregistered churches」紧跟在專有名詞「Zion Church」後方，作為同位語，用來補充說明該教會的屬性（中國未登記教會之一）。
