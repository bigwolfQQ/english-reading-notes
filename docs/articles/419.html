北京正在失去其外交沈著

Beijing is losing its diplomatic composure

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • reiterate v. 重申，反覆說
    The note reiterated China’s position on the 2016 arbitration ruling
    該便條重申了中國對 2016 年仲裁裁決的立場
  • arbitration n. 仲裁
    The note reiterated China’s position on the 2016 arbitration ruling
    該便條重申了中國對 2016 年仲裁裁決的立場
  • binding adj. 有約束力的，具法律效力的
    that it is “illegal,” “invalid” and nonbinding.
    該裁決是「非法的」、「無效的」且無拘束力。
  • rebut v. 反駁，駁斥
    an exasperated-looking Teodoro read it aloud line-by-line to the audience and rebutted each point.
    一臉憤慨的特奧多羅不但沒有把便條收進口袋，反而向聽眾逐行朗讀並反駁了每一個觀點。
  • display n. / v. 陳列，展示，表現
    Instead, Teodoro put it on display and criticized the move as indecent.
    相反地，特奧多羅將其公諸於世，並批評此舉不得體。
  • coercion n. 強迫，高壓手段
    “This is not gray zone anymore,” but rather “coercion, bullying and aggression,” he said.
    他表示：「這已經不再是灰色地帶了」，而是「脅迫、霸凌與侵略」。
  • influence n. 影響力
    If a country that calls itself a “great power” truly had sufficient confidence and international influence, why would it feel the need to interrupt someone’s remarks with a memo at a multilateral security forum held in another country?
    如果一個自稱「大國」的國家真的擁有充足的自信和國際影響力，為什麼會覺得有必要在其他國家舉行的多邊安全論壇上，用便條打斷別人的發言呢？
  • persuasion n. 說服，說服力
    A truly great power relies on institutions, arguments and the power of persuasion;
    真正的大國依靠的是制度、論點和說服力；
  • strategy n. 策略（文中用 tactics）
    a nation with true substance has no need for petty tactics to prove its strength.
    一個真正有實力的國家不需要用卑劣的策略來證明自己的實力。
  • concern n. 擔憂，關切
    Viewed from this perspective, what that memo revealed to the world was not China’s power, but its deep concern and anxiety over others discussing the South China Sea.
    從這個角度來看，那份便條向世界揭示的不是中國的力量，而是它對其他人討論南海問題的深深擔憂與焦慮。
  • restraint n. 克制，自制
    Particularly in multilateral settings, true strength often lies in exercising restraint.
    特別是在多邊場合中，真正的力量往往在於行使克制。
  • appropriate adj. 合適的，恰當的
    if you cannot even exercise basic judgement over what is appropriate in a given setting, what would ultimately suffer is your own image.
    但如果你連在特定場合下什麼是合適的都無法做出基本判斷，最終受損的將是你自己的形象。
  • possess v. 擁有
    it should understand that true greatness lies not in the number of warships a state possesses, the size of its economy or its ability to silence others
    它就應該明白，真正的偉大不在於一個國家擁有多少軍艦、經濟規模有多大，或是讓別人噤聲的能力

文法重點

  • 現在分詞當作形容詞（修飾名詞）
    an exasperated-looking Teodoro read it aloud line-by-line to the audience and rebutted each point.
    句中的「exasperated-looking」是由過去分詞衍生而來的複合形容詞，用來修飾名詞 Teodoro，意思是「看起來感到憤慨的」，在英文新聞中常用來生動描述人物的外貌或神情。
  • 假設語氣（與現在事實相反）
    If a country that calls itself a “great power” truly had sufficient confidence and international influence, why would it feel the need to interrupt someone’s remarks with a memo at a multilateral security forum held in another country?
    此句使用與現在事實相反的假設語氣。條件句（if 子句）中使用過去式動詞 (had)，而主要子句則使用「would + 原形動詞 (would feel)」，用來假設一個實際上並不存在的情況並提出質疑。
  • Not A, but B 對等連接詞結構
    Viewed from this perspective, what that memo revealed to the world was not China’s power, but its deep concern and anxiety over others discussing the South China Sea.
    句型「not A, but B」意為「不是 A，而是 B」。用來強烈對比並排除錯誤的觀點（China’s power），帶出真正的核心事實（its deep concern and anxiety），是商業與新聞英文中強調重點的常見結構。
  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    this time, China might have intended to put the Philippines in its place, but it instead put Teodoro in the international spotlight and allowed him to turn the memo into a “souvenir” of China’s diplomatic posture, for all to refer to.
    句尾的「for all to refer to」包含了不定詞片語的語意主語 (for all)，用來修飾前面的名詞 souvenir，說明這個「紀念品」是供所有人（all）去參照用的，屬於進階的修飾語法結構。
