文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語當形容詞後置修飾
    The Aotearoa was tracked and followed by Chinese forces when it last transited the Strait in November last year, one source with knowledge of the situation said.
    片語「with knowledge of the situation」是由介系詞 with 開頭的複合結構（with + 名詞 + 介系詞片語），在此作為後置修飾語，用來形容前面的名詞 source（消息來源），意指「一位知情的人士」。
  • 對等連接詞 as 的原因子句
    Minister of National Defense Wellington Koo (顧立雄) said that as the Taiwan Strait was an international waterway, all countries enjoyed freedom of navigation.
    在此句中，連接詞 as 用來引導原因子句（as the Taiwan Strait was an international waterway），意思相當於 because 或 since，用以解釋所有國家享有航行自由的原因。
  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況
    During that transit, Chinese ships and aircraft monitored the Aotearoa, with Chinese jets carrying out simulated attacks, the source added.
    此處使用「with + 名詞 + 現在分詞（carrying out）」的附帶狀況分詞構句，用來補充說明中國船艦與飛機監控奧特阿羅亞號的同時，伴隨著中國戰機進行模擬攻擊的動作。
