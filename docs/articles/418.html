超越 2030 雙語政策

Building beyond Bilingual 2030

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • perspective n. 觀點、想法、角度
    More interesting is what happens when we look beyond those disagreements and consider what each perspective adds to the conversation.
    更有趣的是，當我們超越這些分歧，思考各個觀點為這場對話增添了什麼時，會發現更值得探討的面向。
  • assessment n. 評量、評估
    Nigel Daly (...) questioned whether assessment reflects the capabilities students increasingly need.
    奈傑爾·戴利 (...) 質疑了評量是否能反映學生日益所需的能力。
  • evidence n. 證據、證明
    Assessment can help signal those priorities while providing evidence of whether students are developing them.
    評量有助於標示出這些優先順序，同時提供證據來證明學生是否正在培養這些能力。
  • expertise n. 專門技術、專業知識
    Teaching academic content using English requires more than subject expertise or English-language proficiency.
    使用英語教授學術內容，不僅需要學科專業知識或英語能力。
  • proficiency n. 熟練、精通、能力
    Teaching academic content using English requires more than subject expertise or English-language proficiency.
    使用英語教授學術內容，不僅需要學科專業知識或英語能力。
  • feedback n. 回饋、反應意見
    Professional development can connect broader education goals with the daily decisions teachers make about tasks, interaction, feedback and learning.
    專業發展可以將更廣泛的教育目標，與老師在日常中對作業、互動、回饋和學習所做出的決定連結起來。
  • investment n. 投資
    The year 2030 is a useful milestone for setting goals, focusing investment and evaluating progress, but learning would continue long after it passes.
    2030 年是設定目標、集中投資和評估進展的有用里程碑，但學習並不會在它過去之後就結束。
  • evaluate v. 評估、評價
    The year 2030 is a useful milestone for setting goals, focusing investment and evaluating progress, but learning would continue long after it passes.
    2030 年是設定目標、集中投資和評估進展的有用里程碑，但學習並不會在它過去之後就結束。
  • progress n. 進步、進展
    The year 2030 is a useful milestone for setting goals, focusing investment and evaluating progress, but learning would continue long after it passes.
    2030 年是設定目標、集中投資和評估進展的有用里程碑，但學習並不會在它過去之後就結束。
  • legacy n. 遺產、留下的成果
    Perhaps the most important legacy of Bilingual 2030 is not a final verdict on whether every ambition was achieved by a particular date.
    或許 2030 雙語政策最重要的遺產，並不是關於在特定日期前是否達成所有雄心壯志的最終裁決。
  • substantially adv. 相當大地、顯著地
    The nation has already invested substantially in many of these areas.
    國家已經在其中許多領域進行了巨額投資。

文法重點

  • What 引導的名詞子句當主詞
    More interesting is what happens when we look beyond those disagreements and consider what each perspective adds to the conversation.
    句首的「More interesting is...」是為了強調與倒裝，其原本的主詞是「what happens when...（當我們……時所發生的事）」。句中的「what happens...」和「what each perspective adds...」都是由 what 引導的名詞子句，在句中分別擔任主詞和受詞。
  • 現在分詞（Present Participle）作後置修飾與表附帶狀況
    Assessment can help signal those priorities while providing evidence of whether students are developing them.
    句尾的「while providing evidence...」是省略了主詞與be動詞的連接詞分詞構句（while they are providing...），用來補充說明評量在標示優先順序的同時，也「伴隨」提供了什麼樣的證據。
  • 條件句（Conditional Sentence）的假設用法
    If we expect classroom practice to evolve, teachers need opportunities to develop alongside the expectations placed upon them.
    此句使用第一類條件句（First Conditional），結構為「If + 現在式動詞, 主持人 + 助動詞/原形動詞（或現在式）」。用來表達未來可能實現的條件（如果我們期望課堂實踐演進），以及在該條件下產生的必然結果或需求。
  • 過去分詞（Past Participle）作後置修飾語
    Professional development can connect broader education goals with the daily decisions teachers make about tasks, interaction, feedback and learning.
    句中的「placed upon them」是過去分詞片語，用來後置修飾前面的名詞「expectations（期望）」。這相當於形容詞子句「that are placed upon them」，用被動的概念來表達「被加諸在他們身上的期望」。
  • 地方副詞置於句首的倒裝句型（或強調句型）
    Their value lies precisely in what becomes possible when we put them together.
    雖然此句並非嚴格的不及物動詞完全倒裝，但「lies precisely in...」將核心價值（value）的所在之處具體描繪出來。介系詞片語「in what becomes possible...」作為表語，說明了價值「在於」什麼事情上，使句意表達更為精煉有力。
