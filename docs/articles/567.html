文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    At a security conference in Seoul this week, Philippine Defense Secretary Gilberto Teodoro Jr was handed a note, apparently from a Chinese military attache, restating Beijing’s position on the 2016 South China Sea arbitration.
    句中的 "restating Beijing’s position..." 是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前面的 a note。而前面的 "apparently from a Chinese military attache" 則是省略了關代與be動詞（which was apparently from...）的形容詞片語，用來修飾 a note。多益閱讀測驗常考這種分詞片語修飾前方名詞的句構。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Absolute Construction)
    The note did not contest this, because no counterargument exists in the convention. Instead it called the ruling a violation of “fundamental principles of international law,” a phrase doing the work a citation would do.
    句尾的 ", a phrase doing the work a citation would do" 是獨立分詞片語，其中 "a phrase" 是動名词 "doing" 的語意上的主詞，補充說明前面整句提到的說法。這種結構在多益商務英文的高階報導中常見，用以提供額外的評論或補充細節。
  • 否定倒裝句 / 地方副詞置於句首 (Negative Inversion / Fronting)
    In each case Beijing behaves as though it alone gets to certify which entities qualify as states, where their borders run, and how much sovereignty they may exercise before that exercise becomes an insult.
    句首的 "In each case" 雖非嚴格意義上的否定詞倒裝，但屬於將介系詞片語置於句首以強調範圍或情境的句構，主詞 "Beijing" 與動詞 "behaves" 正常排列。而其後的複合名詞子句（which entities qualify..., where their borders run...）則展示了多益常考的間接問句語序（疑問詞 + 主詞 + 動詞）。
