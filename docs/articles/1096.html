衛福部要求對台北市社工涉詐案進行調查

Ministry calls for Taipei social worker fraud probe

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • investigation n. 調查
    The Ministry of Health and Welfare yesterday said it had asked the Taipei City Government to launch an administrative investigation...
    衛生福利部昨天表示，已要求台北市政府發動行政調查...
  • launch v. 發起，啟動
    ...after a former social worker supervisor was on Tuesday detained for allegedly embezzling NT$12 million...
    ...在此前一天，一名社工前督導於週二因涉嫌侵占 1,200 萬元遭羈押...
  • embezzle v. 挪用，侵占（公款或他人財產）
    ...used her position to embezzle the savings of five elderly people under guardianship in Taipei’s Wenshan District.
    ...利用職務之便，侵占台北市文山區 5 名受監護宣告老人的積蓄。
  • transfer v. 調動，轉移
    After Chiang resigned and transferred to the New Taipei City Government, the department noticed funds and properties were missing...
    江女辭職並調至新北市政府後，社會局發現資金與財產不翼而飛...
  • inventory n. 清點，存貨，庫存清單
    ...when it was conducting a handover inventory check, so it reported the case to the police...
    ...在進行交接盤點時，因此向警方報案...
  • seize v. 查扣，沒收，扣押
    Police searched Chiang’s residence on Tuesday and seized several handbags and goods from luxury brands including Chanel and Hermes.
    警方於週二搜查江女住處，並查扣香奈兒、愛馬仕等多個精品品牌的皮包與商品。
  • revocation n. 撤銷，廢止
    ...but Chiang faces a professional license revocation if found guilty.
    ...但江女若被判有罪，將面臨吊銷專業執照的處分。
  • in accordance with phr. 依據，按照
    The ministry has requested the Taipei City Government to promptly conduct an administrative investigation and take subsequent measures in accordance with investigation results and the law...
    衛福部已要求台北市政府儘速進行行政調查，並根據調查結果及法律採取後續措施...
  • eligible adj. 有資格的，合乎條件的
    ...adding that the social worker would not be eligible to retake the licensing examination.
    ...並補充說明該名社工將不得再次參加專門職業及技術人員高等考試社會工作師考試。
  • cooperate v. 合作，配合
    The department will fully cooperate with the judicial investigation and hold the person accountable in accordance with the law without leniency...
    社會局將全力配合司法調查，依法究辦、絕不寬貸...

文法重點

  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense)
    The Ministry of Health and Welfare yesterday said it had asked the Taipei City Government to launch an administrative investigation...
    句子中的主動作「said」（過去式）發生在過去，而衛福部「要求台北市政府調查」這個動作發生在說話之前更早的時間點，因此使用過去完成式 (had asked) 來表達「過去某個時間點之前已經完成的動作」。
  • 現在分詞當作形容詞或後置修飾 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    ...used her position to embezzle the savings of five elderly people under guardianship in Taipei’s Wenshan District.
    句中的「under guardianship」（受監護的）為分詞片語或介係詞片語，用來修飾前方的名詞「five elderly people」（五位老人），使句意更精確，省去了完整形容詞子句（如 who were under guardianship）的冗長。
  • 假設語氣（條件句）與被動語態結合 (Conditional with Passive Voice)
    ...but Chiang faces a professional license revocation if found guilty.
    「if found guilty」是省略了主詞與be動詞的條件副詞子句（原句為 if she is found guilty）。此處結合了條件句與被動語態（found guilty 意為「若被判有罪」），常用於新聞報導中簡化冗長句型。
