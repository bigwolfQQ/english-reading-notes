國際滑冰總會撤銷俄羅斯花滑選手的中立身分

ISU withdraws Russian skater’s neutral status

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • withdraw v. 撤回，撤銷
    ISU withdraws Russian skater’s neutral status
    國際滑冰總會撤銷俄羅斯花滑選手的中立身分
  • revoke v. 吊銷，撤銷，廢除
    ...after the International Skating Union (ISU) revoked her neutral athlete status...
    ...在國際滑冰總會（ISU）撤銷她的中立運動員身分後...
  • disqualify v. 使失格，取消資格
    ...a result from which she was later disqualified, elevating the US to the top of the podium.
    ...她隨後被取消了該成績資格，使得美國隊遞補登上頒獎台榜首。
  • ineligible adj. 無資格的，不合格的
    ...but remained ineligible for the Milano Cortina Winter Olympics.
    ...但仍不具參加米蘭-科爾蒂納丹佩佐冬季奧運會的資格。
  • appeal v. 上訴，申訴
    ...that all the skaters affected by the ruling would appeal to the Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS).
    ...所有受該裁決影響的選手都將向國際體育仲裁法庭（CAS）提起上訴。
  • claim n. 索賠，起訴，聲稱
    ...will soon file a claim with the CAS against the ISU...
    ...很快將針對國際滑冰總會向國際體育仲裁法庭提起訴訟
  • bar v. 禁止，阻撓
    Russian athletes have been barred from competing under their national flag since Russia’s invasion of Ukraine in 2022.
    自2022年俄羅斯入侵烏克蘭以來，俄羅斯運動員一直被禁止以國家國旗名義參賽。
  • participate v. 參與，參加
    ...to participate as Individual Neutral Athletes, subject to a rigorous vetting process.
    ...以個人中立運動員身分參賽，但前提是必須通過嚴格的審查程序。
  • subject to phr. 受...約束，視...而定，取決於
    ...subject to a rigorous vetting process.
    ...但前提是必須通過嚴格的審查程序。
  • determination n. 決定，判定
    “Each determination is subject to the applicable appeal rights...
    「每一項決定均須遵守適用上訴權利...」
  • specify v. 具體說明，明確指定
    The ISU did not specify which criteria Valieva had not met.
    國際滑冰總會並未具體說明瓦莉娃未符合哪一項標準。
  • affiliate v. 使隸屬於，使加入
    ...234 individuals affiliated with member federations in Russia and Belarus...
    ...隸屬於俄羅斯和白俄羅斯成員聯合會的234名個人...

文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Russian athletes have been barred from competing under their national flag since Russia’s invasion of Ukraine in 2022.
    句型採用現在完成式的被動語態（have/has been + 過去分詞），強調「俄羅斯運動員」是遭受禁止的對象，且此狀態從過去持續至今。
  • 分詞片語作後置修飾 (Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    ...234 individuals affiliated with member federations in Russia and Belarus had submitted neutrality declarations...
    過去分詞片語「affiliated with member federations in Russia and Belarus」用來修飾前面的名詞 individuals，相當於形容詞子句「who are affiliated with...」的簡化用法。
  • 複合關係代名詞與介系詞提前 (Preposition + Relative Pronoun)
    ...a result from which she was later disqualified, elevating the US to the top of the podium.
    關係代名詞「which」前方搭配介系詞「from」，用來連接先行詞 a result 與後方的子句，表示「從該結果中被取消資格」。後面接分詞構句「elevating...」表達伴隨發生的結果。
