「烈火-4」型飛彈與印度的戰略自主

The Agni-IV and India’s strategic autonomy

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • capacity n. 能力、容量
    What the test shows more clearly is that India has a much greater capacity to deter China well beyond their disputed border.
    這次試射更清楚顯示出，印度具備了在雙方爭議邊界之外、更強大嚇阻中國的能力。
  • capability n. 能力、軍事能力
    More important is what it tells us about India’s national capabilities, and how those capabilities affect the way New Delhi deals with Beijing and the regional order.
    更重要的是，它向我們揭示了印度的國家實力，以及這些實力如何影響新德里與北京打交道及應對區域秩序的方式。
  • supplier n. 供應商
    A country might stay outside an alliance and have little autonomy if its security depends heavily on outside commitments or if critical military systems come from a single foreign supplier.
    如果一個國家的安全高度依賴外部承諾，或者關鍵軍事系統來自單一外國供應商，那麼即使該國保持在同盟之外，也幾乎沒有什麼自主權。
  • indigenous adj. 本土的、國產的
    In the process, it has also accumulated experience in indigenous research and development, systems integration and the operation of strategic forces.
    在此過程中，它也累積了本土研發、系統整合以及戰略武力運作的經驗。
  • disengage v. 脫離、退出、解除交往
    A similar pattern can be seen in India’s approach to Beijing, a neighbor it cannot simply disengage from, and a major power that can narrow India’s security and development space.
    類似的模式也可以在印度對待北京的方法中看見，北京是一個無法輕易與之脫鉤的鄰居，也是一個能夠壓縮印度安全與發展空間的大國。
  • bolster v. 加強、改善、支撐
    New Delhi is still bolstering its military capabilities, paying greater attention to the Indian Ocean and investing in technologies where dependence could become a liability.
    新德里仍在強化其軍事能力、更關注印度洋，並投資於那些依賴可能成為負債的技術。
  • liability n. 負債、不利因素、責任
    New Delhi is still bolstering its military capabilities, paying greater attention to the Indian Ocean and investing in technologies where dependence could become a liability.
    新德里仍在強化其軍事能力、更關注印度洋，並投資於那些依賴可能成為負債的技術。
  • sideline v. 排擠、使靠邊站
    China remains too important for India to sideline.
    對印度來說，中國依然過於重要，無法被邊緣化。
  • resume v. 恢復、重新開始
    That helps explain why New Delhi has resumed political contact with Beijing.
    這有助於解釋為什麼新德里恢復了與北京的政治接觸。
  • rely on phr. v. 依賴、依靠
    It matters more to look at the areas in which India continues to rely on China, where it is searching for alternatives and where New Delhi increasingly treats dependence as a strategic risk.
    更重要的是去檢視印度繼續依賴中國的領域、它正在尋找替代方案的領域，以及新德里日益將依賴視為戰略風險的領域。
  • alternative n. 替代方案
    On the Indian Ocean, technology and supply chains, India has been building alternatives and reducing dependence on any single major power.
    在印度洋、科技和供應鏈方面，印度一直在建立替代方案，並減少對任何單一大國的依賴。

文法重點

  • What 引導的名詞子句（主詞補語／主詞）
    What the test shows more clearly is that India has a much greater capacity to deter China well beyond their disputed border.
    句首的「What the test shows more clearly」是由 what 引導的名詞子句，在句中擔任主語（What 等於 the thing which），用來強調試圖表達的核心重點。
  • 形容詞比較級 + than 引導的比較結構
    They are better understood as policy choices than as the meaning of strategic autonomy itself.
    此句運用了「are better understood as A than as B」的比較結構，用來精確對比並否定某種舊有理解，突顯作者認為更貼切的觀點。
  • 表條件與結果的假設意味句型（Conditional meaning with if）
    A country might stay outside an alliance and have little autonomy if its security depends heavily on outside commitments...
    句中運用了「if 條件句」來設定特定情境（安全過度依賴外部承諾），藉此推導出即使表面上不結盟也可能缺乏實質自主權的結果。
  • 關係代名詞子句修飾先行詞 (where 引導地方／抽象領域關係子句)
    It matters more to look at the areas in which India continues to rely on China, where it is searching for alternatives and where New Delhi increasingly treats dependence as a strategic risk.
    句中的「where...」連續使用了兩個地方／狀況副詞關係子句，用來補充修飾前面提到的「areas（領域）」，詳細說明印度在這些領域中正在做的事。
