皇家馬德里與巴黎聖日耳曼吞下聯賽主場首敗

Real Madrid and PSG lose first home league games

單字解析

  • domestic adj. 國內的
    Real Madrid and Paris Saint-Germain (PSG) were dealt their first losses in their domestic leagues on Friday
    皇家馬德里與巴黎聖日耳曼（PSG）週五在國內聯賽中吞下了各自的首場敗仗
  • defeat n. 失敗，戰敗
    Madrid fell at Real Betis 1-0 for its first defeat since the return of coach Jose Mourinho.
    皇馬以 0 比 1 不敵皇家貝提斯，吞下了自教練荷西·穆里尼奧回歸以來的首場敗仗。
  • newcomer n. 新加入者，新手
    Betis stunned Madrid when newcomer Troy Parrott, an Irish striker, knocked in a ball
    貝提斯爆冷擊敗了皇馬，當時新加盟的愛爾蘭前鋒特洛伊·帕洛特補射破門
  • performance n. 表現，績效
    Alvaro Valles smothered Mbappe’s spot kick, adding to his great performance.
    阿爾瓦羅·瓦列斯撲掉了姆巴佩的罰球，為他本就出色的表現錦上添花。
  • previous adj. 先前的，以前的
    Mbappe scored four goals in his previous two games, but the French star was off at La Cartuja Stadium.
    姆巴佩在過去兩場比賽中攻入四球，但這位法國球星在卡圖哈體育場狀態全無。
  • dominate v. 主導，稱霸
    We dominated, controlling everything, lots of attacks, lots of chances.
    我們佔據了優勢，掌控了一切，有大量的進攻與機會。
  • frustrated adj. 沮喪的，感到挫折的
    If we had drawn we would have been left frustrated, so imagine how we feel after losing.
    如果我們踢平會感到沮喪，所以試想一下輸球後我們的感受。
  • opening adj. 開場的，首場的
    Next up for Madrid is a home game against Inter Milan in the opening round of the Champions League on Tuesday.
    皇馬接下來的賽程是週二在歐冠聯賽首輪主場迎戰國際米蘭。
  • announcement n. 宣布，公告（文中為動詞 announce）
    Its first league defeat came hours after the French powerhouse announced contract extensions for coach Luis Enrique.
    這場聯賽首敗發生在法國豪門宣布教練路易斯·恩里克續約數小時後。
  • extension n. 延期，延長
    Its first league defeat came hours after the French powerhouse announced contract extensions for coach Luis Enrique.
    這場聯賽首敗發生在法國豪門宣布教練路易斯·恩里克續約數小時後。
  • transfer n. 轉調，轉會
    US striker Folarin Balogun was left out of Monaco’s squad after the collapse of his proposed transfer to Everton this week.
    在傳出他轉會埃弗頓的交易破局後，美國前鋒弗洛里安·巴洛貢被排除在摩納哥的球隊大名單之外。

文法重點

  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense)
    Betis was coming off a shock 5-2 loss to Levante, after it had won twice.
    表示在過去某一時間點（Betis 吞下 5 比 2 敗仗時）之前已經發生的動作（贏了兩場比賽），用 「had + 過去分詞」來表達先後順序。
  • 與過去事實相反的假設語氣 (Subjunctive Conditional)
    If we had drawn we would have been left frustrated, so imagine how we feel after losing.
    用來假設過去並未發生 A 卻會有 B 結果的狀況。結構為「If + 主詞 + had + 過去分詞, 主詞 + would/could/might + have + 過去分詞」。句中表達「如果我們踢平了（實際上輸了），我們就會感到沮喪」。
  • 關係代名詞子句當形容詞用 (Relative Clause)
    Monaco leads Ligue 1 as the only team to win its first three games.
    在此句中，不定詞片語「to win its first three games」用來修飾前面的名詞「the only team」，具有形容詞的功能，說明這支球隊的特徵。
