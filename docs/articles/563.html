塞內加爾在非洲太空競賽中放眼自製衛星

Senegal eyes homemade satellites amid African space race

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • assemble v. 組裝、聚集
    Senegalese engineers in hairnets and white gloves are carefully assembling a nanosatellite at a French lab — another step toward fostering their own space industry at home.
    身穿網帽和白色手套的塞內加爾工程師正在法國實驗室裡小心翼翼地組裝奈米衛星——這是他們在國內發展自身太空工業的又一步驟。
  • collaborate v. 合作、協力
    The engineers from Sensat built their first satellite — Gaindesat-1A — in collaboration with the Montpellier scientists, launching it in 2024.
    來自森薩特的工程師與蒙pellier的科學家合作製造了他們的第一顆衛星「加因德衛星一號A」（Gaindesat-1A），並於2024年發射。
  • independent adj. 獨立的
    It is part of a drive for technological independence by Senegal, mirrored in many parts of Africa.
    這是塞內加爾追求技術獨立行動的一部分，這在非洲許多地區都有所呼應。
  • consultancy n. 諮詢公司、顧問公司
    Across Africa, national space budgets have grown to US$800 million, said Temidayo Oniosun, founder of Lagos-based consultancy Space in Africa, up from less than US$300 million in 2018.
    總部位於拉agos的諮詢公司「非洲太空」（Space in Africa）創辦人特米達約・奧尼奧松（Temidayo Oniosun）表示，全非洲的國家太空預算已成長至8億美元，高於2018年的不到3億美元。
  • run v. 經營、運作、營運
    Nigeria’s national space agency was founded the following year, sent its first device space-bound in 2003 and runs two satellites in orbit — one for communications and the other run by the military’s separate space agency, Oniosun said.
    奧尼奧松表示，奈及利亞國家太空機構成立於次年，於2003年將其第一台裝置送入太空軌道，並營運著兩顆衛星——一顆用於通訊，另一顆由軍方的獨立太空機構營運。
  • partnership n. 夥伴關係、合作關係
    Even restive Somalia could have its own space port as part of a US$6 billion partnership with Turkey announced last year.
    甚至動盪不安的索馬利亞也可能擁有這是去年宣布與土耳其達成60億美元夥伴關係的一部分。
  • host v. 主辦、主持
    New players continue to join the rush: Ivory Coast named its first space agency director last month and is to host the African Space Expo later this month.
    新玩家持續加入這股熱潮：象牙海岸上個月任命了其第一位太空機構主任，並將於本月稍後主辦非洲太空博覽會。
  • infrastructure n. 基礎設施
    Then, if the infrastructure is ready, “the fourth satellite can be built entirely in Senegal.”
    然後，如果基礎設施準備就緒，「第四顆衛星就可以完全在塞內加爾建造。」

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    For the follow-up, Gaindesat-1B, which is designed to collect environmental and climate data, “we’ve removed the coaching component,” Faye said.
    句中「designed to collect environmental and climate data」是由關代子句簡化而來的分詞片語（原為 which is designed...），用來修飾其前的名詞 Gaindesat-1B，說明該衛星的功能與設計目的。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Even restive Somalia could have its own space port as part of a US$6 billion partnership with Turkey announced last year.
    句尾的「announced last year」是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞片語 a US$6 billion partnership with Turkey，表示「去年被宣布的」合作夥伴關係，具有被動意味。
  • 條件句與假設語氣 (Conditional Clause)
    Then, if the infrastructure is ready, “the fourth satellite can be built entirely in Senegal.”
    此句使用「if + 現在式動詞」的真實條件句型（if the infrastructure is ready），主句則搭配助動詞 can 加原形動詞（can be built），表達在特定條件達成時，未來的可能性與結果。
