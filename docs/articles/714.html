文法重點

  • Not only... but also... 倒裝句型
    Not only is it a kick in the face to every woman who had dedicated her life to perfecting her craft, but every women who enjoys sports for what it really is...
    當「Not only」置於句首以加強語氣時，其後的子句須採用部分倒裝（將be動詞、助動詞或情態助動詞移至主詞前方）。本句中將 is 移至代名詞 it 前方，形成「Not only is it...」。
  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾
    Olympic athletes have come out in force to condemn a sports betting advert featuring actress Sydney Sweeney in various states of undress...
    句中的「featuring actress Sydney Sweeney...」是由現在分詞帶領的片語，用來修飾前方的名詞「a sports betting advert」。這相當於形容詞子句「which featured actress Sydney Sweeney...」的簡化寫法。
  • 疑問詞引導的名詞子句
    “How do we live in a world where monetising a women’s body and sexualising women’s sport is still a thing?”
    「How do we live in a world...」在此作為直接引語的問句，但在複雜句中，「how」常引導名詞子句作受詞或主詞補語，此處直接作為直述句中的疑問句型表達強烈的反問語氣。
  • 關係代名詞子句（限定修飾）
    ...every woman who had dedicated her life to perfecting her craft...
    「who had dedicated her life to perfecting her craft」是關係代名詞子句，用來修飾先行詞「every woman」，限定並補充說明這位女性所做過的努力。
