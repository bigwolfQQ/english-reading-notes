中國因對台軍售威脅取消美中峰會

China threatens US meeting over arms sales

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • cancel v. 取消
    China has warned that it could cancel a planned US-China leaders’ summit if Washington approves new arms sales to Taiwan...
    中國已警告稱，如果華府批准對台灣的新軍售，北京可能會取消一項計畫中的美中領導人高峰會...
  • approve v. 批准、贊成
    ...if Washington approves new arms sales to Taiwan, a Japanese media outlet quoted sources as saying yesterday.
    ...如果華府批准對台灣的新軍售，一家日本媒體昨天引述消息人士的話報導。
  • summit n. 高峰會、頂峰
    China has warned that it could cancel a planned US-China leaders’ summit if Washington approves new arms sales to Taiwan...
    中國已警告稱，如果華府批准對台灣的新軍售，北京可能會取消一項計畫中的美中領導人高峰會...
  • negotiation n. 談判、協商
    Trump later told reporters that arms sales to Taiwan could be a “very good bargaining chip” in his negotiations with China...
    川普後來告訴記者，對台軍售可能是他與中國談判中「非常好的籌碼」...
  • commitment n. 承諾、投入
    ...raising concerns about the US’ long-term security commitments to its allies in Asia, the report said.
    報導稱，這引發了人們對美國對亞洲盟友長期安全承諾的擔憂。
  • capability n. 能力、才能
    The report said that under the Taiwan Relations Act, the US provides Taiwan with weapons to help it maintain a sufficient self-defense capability.
    報導指出，根據《台灣關係法》，美國向台灣提供武器以協助其維持足夠的自衛能力。
  • announce v. 宣布、宣告
    In December last year, the Trump administration announced an arms sales package to Taiwan worth about US$11 billion...
    去年12月，川普政府宣布了一筆價值約11億美元的對台軍售案...
  • economic adj. 經濟的
    Meanwhile, the US is stepping up economic sanctions aimed at isolating Iran...
    與此同時，美國正在加強旨在孤立伊朗的經濟制裁...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    Meanwhile, the US is stepping up economic sanctions aimed at isolating Iran, and attention is turning to how China, a longtime supporter of Iran, would respond, the Kyodo News report said, quoting sources as saying that Beijing is willing to coordinate with Washington on the issue of Iran.
    句中的 "quoting sources as saying..." 是現在分詞引導的片語，用來補充說明前面報導所做的動作與來源，相當於副詞子句的簡化，常用於新聞英語中以增加敘事的流暢度與真實性。
  • 條件句中的假設用法 (Conditional Clause with Subordinating Conjunction)
    China has warned that it could cancel a planned US-China leaders’ summit if Washington approves new arms sales to Taiwan...
    此句使用 "if" 引導的條件句 ("if Washington approves...")，主句則使用情態助動詞 could 表「有可能發生成真」的推測，這是表達國際政治外交警告與談判條件時非常經典的句構。
  • 間接問句/名詞子句作受詞 (Indirect Question / Noun Clause as Object)
    ...and attention is turning to how China, a longtime supporter of Iran, would respond, the Kyodo News report said...
    介系詞 "to" 後方接了 "how China... would respond" 作為受詞。這是由疑問詞 "how" 引導的名詞子句，在句中扮演受詞的角色，用來表達「……的情況將如何轉變/如何應對」。
