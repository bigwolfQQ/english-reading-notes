中國的超級水壩正在成為上游的水炸彈

China’s superdam is becoming an upstream water bomb

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • construct v. 建造、建設
    China is constructing an unprecedented hydropower project in Tibet on the Yarlung Tsangpo...
    中國正在西藏雅魯藏布江上建造一項空前的水力發電工程……
  • capacity n. 容量、能力、發電量
    The superdam will dwarf China’s Three Gorges Dam, the world’s largest, in power-generating capacity.
    這座超級水壩在發電能力上將使中國世界第一的三峽大壩相形見絀。
  • construction n. 建設、施工
    A recent paper published in the state-linked, Mandarin-language journal Sedimentary Geology and Tethyan Geology identifies serious geological hazards at the construction site.
    一篇近期發表在與官方有關聯的中文期刊《沉積學與特提斯地質》上的論文，指出了施工現場嚴重的地質災害。
  • infrastructure n. 基礎設施
    The geologists conclude that the terrain has a “loose structure and weak cohesion,” and warn that fault movement or an earthquake could easily trigger large-scale instability, threatening the reservoir and surrounding infrastructure.
    地質學家得出結論，該地形具有「結構疏鬆且內聚力弱」的特點，並警告斷層運動或地震可能會輕易引發大規模的不穩定性，從而威脅水庫及周邊基礎設施。
  • recommend v. 建議、推薦
    The authors recommend reinforcing the reservoir slopes, installing retaining structures and redesigning key elements to reduce the risk of catastrophe.
    作者建議加固水庫邊坡、安裝擋土結構並重新設計關鍵元件，以降低災難風險。
  • relationship n. 關係、關聯
    While scientists continue debating the precise relationship, influential research concluded that the immense weight of water stored in the reservoir of the new Zipingpu Dam might have increased pressure along an underlying fault...
    儘管科學家們仍在爭論確切的關係，但一項具有影響力的研究得出結論，新紫坪鋪水庫中儲存的海量水重可能增加了底層斷層沿線的壓力……
  • consequence n. 後果、結果
    However, the Himalayan fault system does not respect national borders; nor would the consequences of a major dam failure.
    然而，喜馬拉雅斷層系統不尊重國界；重大水壩潰決的後果也不會。
  • operation n. 運作、營運、操作
    Even routine reservoir operations can affect downstream ecosystems, agriculture, sediment transport and flood patterns.
    即使是例行的水庫運作也會影響下游生態系統、農業、沉積物輸運和洪水模式。
  • transparency n. 透明度
    There has been no meaningful environmental transparency, no independent geological review and no credible consultation with the countries facing the greatest downstream risks.
    沒有有意義的環境透明度，沒有獨立的地質審查，也沒有與面臨最大下游風險的國家進行可信的協商。
  • assess v. 評估、評價
    China should immediately invite an independent panel of international seismologists, geologists and dam-safety experts to inspect the site, review the project’s geological assumptions and assess whether the recommended design modifications are sufficient.
    中國應立即邀請由國際地震學家、地質學家和大壩安全專家組成的獨立小組來檢查現場、審查專案的地質假設，並評估建議的設計修改是否足夠。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句 (Participle Clause)
    ...before flowing into Bangladesh, where it is the single largest source of fresh water.
    句中的 "flowing into..." 是現在分詞片語引導的分詞構句，用來補充說明主詞在主流動過程中的伴隨動作或接續狀況，相當於 "...before it flows into Bangladesh" 的簡化。
  • 否定副詞置於句首的倒裝句 (Inversion with Negative Adverb)
    However, the Himalayan fault system does not respect national borders; nor would the consequences of a major dam failure.
  • 現在分詞作後置修飾語 (Present Participle as Postmodifier)
    The authors recommend reinforcing the reservoir slopes, installing retaining structures and redesigning key elements to reduce the risk of catastrophe.
  • 關係代間詞引導的形容詞子句 (Adjective Clause)
    Its reservoir will impound a gargantuan volume of water above millions of people downstream in India’s northeast and deltaic Bangladesh, much of which lies barely above sea level.
