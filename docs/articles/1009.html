文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 / 形容詞化用法
    A Chiayi County-based drone company on Friday last week signed a memorandum of understanding (MOU) with a consortium of Taiwanese uncrewed systems industry groups to promote Taiwanese drones in the North American market.
    句中的「Chiayi County-based」是由名詞加上過去分詞構成的複合形容詞，用來修飾前面的 drone company（總部設於嘉義縣的無人機公司）。這種結構在商業與新聞英文中非常常見，用來簡潔地表達人或物的所在地點、狀態或由誰所屬。
  • 分詞片語作附加補充說明（分詞構句）
    Taiwan has strong capabilities in small and medium-sized drones, Chiayi County Commissioner Weng Chang-liang (翁章梁) said, adding that expanding UAV exports could lead to a new strategic industry for the nation.
    句尾的「adding that...」是由現在分詞（adding）開頭的分詞片語，用來補充說明前面主句所說的話之延伸結果或額外補充，主詞隱含為前面整句話的動作發起者（Weng Chang-liang）。
  • that 引導的名詞子句（受詞子句）
    7A Drones has good working relationships with high-quality Taiwanese manufacturers, and is confident it can provide drones made outside Chinese supply chains in collaboration with NATUAVA, he said.
    形容詞 confident 後方接了一個省略了連接詞 that 的名詞子句「it can provide drones...」，用來補充說明「對什麼事情感到有信心」。在多益考試中常考 that 的省略以及子句內動詞時態的搭配。
  • 介系詞片語作表態與條件（Under the agreement）
    Under the agreement, the association would help Taiwanese companies connect with North American governments, industry experts, system integrators and investors.
    句首的「Under the agreement」是一個介系詞片語，意思是「根據該協議」，用來修飾整句話發生的依據或背景條件。這種用法在商業合約與新聞報導中極為高頻。
