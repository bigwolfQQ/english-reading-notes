劉菖閔為台灣奪下亞運第9面武術銅牌

Liu wins wushu bronze for Taiwan's 9th Asian Games medal

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • event n. （比賽）項目、活動
    Taiwanese wushu athlete Liu Chang-min (劉菖閔) won bronze in the men's nanquan-nangun event at the 2026 Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games on Tuesday, securing Taiwan's ninth medal of the Games.
    台灣武術選手劉菖閔週二在2026年愛知-名古屋亞運男子南拳南棍全能項目中奪得銅牌，為台灣拿下本屆亞運的第九面獎牌。
  • secure v. 獲得、取得、確保
    ...securing Taiwan's ninth medal of the Games.
    ……為台灣拿下本屆亞運的第九面獎牌。
  • score v. / n. 得分、分數
    Liu scored 9.720 points in the nanquan portion during the morning session and 9.710 in the afternoon's nangun routine.
    劉菖閔在上午場的南拳項目拿下9.720分，並在下午的南棍套路中獲得9.710分。
  • overall adv. / adj. 總體上、全面地、總共的
    His combined score of 19.430 placed him third overall.
    他總計19.430分，名列總成績第三名。
  • miss v. 錯過、未達到
    Taiwan's Huang Wen-sheng (黃文聖) narrowly missed the podium.
    台灣另一選手黃文聖以極小差距錯失頒獎台。
  • finish v. 完成、獲得（名次）
    Huang placed seventh in nanquan with 9.703 points before finishing second in nangun with 9.726...
    黃文聖在南拳以9.703分名列第七，隨後在南棍獲得9.726分名列第二……
  • consistent adj. 持續穩定的、一致的
    ...his consistent performances across the two routines were enough to secure bronze.
    ……他兩套動作的穩定發揮足以確保銅牌。
  • determine v. 決定、查明
    The event's final standings were determined by the athletes' combined nanquan and nangun scores.
    該項目的最終排名是由選手的南拳和南棍總分來決定。
  • following prep. 在……之後
    Liu's bronze was Taiwan's second wushu medal at this year's Asian Games, following Johnny Sun's (孫家閎) silver in the men's taijiquan and taijijian event on Monday.
    劉菖閔的銅牌是台灣在本屆亞運會上的第二面武術獎牌，在此之前，孫家閎於週一在男子太極拳太極劍項目中奪得銀牌。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作結果狀語
    ...winning bronze in the men's nanquan-nangun event at the 2026 Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games on Tuesday, securing Taiwan's ninth medal of the Games.
    句尾的逗號加上 V-ing（securing），用來補充說明前面主句動作所產生的自然結果，意指「奪得銅牌，（進而）為台灣確保了第九面獎牌」。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The event's final standings were determined by the athletes' combined nanquan and nangun scores.
    句型採用「be動詞 + 過去分詞 (were determined)」，強調「最終排名是由……所決定的」，凸顯動作的承受者（standings）。
  • 介系詞片語表示時間與地點
    Taipei, Sept. 22 (CNA) Taiwanese wushu athlete Liu Chang-min won bronze in the men's nanquan-nangun event at the 2026 Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games on Tuesday...
    句中運用多個介系詞來建構完整資訊：in 用於較大範圍的項目與賽事 (in the event, in the Asian Games)、at 用於特定場館 (at the 2026 Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games 或比賽地點)、on 用於具體日期 (on Tuesday)，這是多益測驗常考的時間與地點介系詞用法。
