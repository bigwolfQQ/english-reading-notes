Ex-Colombian president urges peace push with FARC

  • implement v. 實施，執行

文法重點

  • 非限定性關係子句 (Non-defining relative clause)
    Santos — who led the Latin American country from 2010 to 2018 — negotiated the peace deal with the FARC and won the 2016 Nobel Peace Prize for his efforts.
    句子中使用破折號夾入一個由「who」引導的非限定性關係子句（who led the Latin American country from 2010 to 2018），用來補充說明主詞 Santos 的身分背景。這類子句前後通常有逗號或破折號隔開，若將其拿掉，主要子句的句意依然完整。
  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾語 (Past participle phrase as postmodifier)
    Colombian President Abelardo de la Espriella should fully implement the peace accord signed with Revolutionary Armed Forces of Colombia (FARC) guerrillas a decade ago...
    句中的「signed with Revolutionary Armed Forces of Colombia (FARC) guerrillas a decade ago」是一個過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 the peace accord。這相當於一個省略了「which was」的被動語態關係子句（which was signed with...），能讓句子結構更為精簡緊湊。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The wide-ranging deal with FARC was built into the country’s constitution...
    此句使用了被動語態「was built」，強調動作的承受者（The wide-ranging deal with FARC），說明該協議是被納入憲法之中，而不必強調是誰將其納入，這在新聞英語中常用於客觀陳述事實或政策。
  • 現在分詞片語作附帶狀況/結果 (Present participle phrase indicating circumstance)
    Petro “concentrated on what he called ‘total peace,’ that is, trying to negotiate with criminal groups dispersed throughout the country, at the expense of the deal with the FARC,” Santos said.
    句中的「trying to negotiate...」是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前文「total peace」的做法細節，表示伴隨發生的動作或具體內容。此外，句尾的「dispersed throughout the country」則是過去分詞片語，用來修飾 criminal groups（指被分散在全國各地的犯罪集團）。
