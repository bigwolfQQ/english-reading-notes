文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾
    Lin urged the mayor not to be “deaf and blind” to the city’s problems — a play on the Mandarin pronunciation of “lobster” (“longxia”), which sounds similar to “deaf and blind.”
    句尾的 “sounds similar to...” 為現在分詞子句（省略了which is），用來補充說明前面的名詞 pronunciation，表示「聽起來類似於……」的狀態。
