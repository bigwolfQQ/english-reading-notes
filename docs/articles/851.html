西方衰落論的虛假政治

The false politics of Western declinism

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • trade war n. 貿易戰
    As US President Donald Trump plunges the US into another reckless trade war — this time with neighboring Canada...
    正當美國總統唐納·川普將美國拖入另一場輕率的貿易戰——這次是對象是鄰國加拿大...
  • beneficiary n. 受益人，受惠者
    Despite being the main architect and beneficiary of the multilateral trading system since 1945...
    儘管自 1945 年以來一直是多邊貿易體系的主要締造者與受益者...
  • strategy n. 策略，戰略
    ...praised the Trump administration’s National Security Strategy for recognizing Europe’s “civilizational-scale decline.”
    ...讚揚川普政府的國家安全戰略承認了歐洲的「文明規模衰落」。
  • competitiveness n. 競爭力
    Presenting his 2024 report on European competitiveness, former Italian prime minister Mario Draghi called for an EU turnaround...
    前義大利總理馬里奧·德拉吉在發表其 2024 年關於歐洲競爭力的報告時，呼籲歐盟進行大翻轉...
  • average income n. phr. 平均收入
    Are Americans and Europeans getting poorer in terms of GDP per capita, average income or life expectancy?
    美國人和 Europeans 在人均 GDP、平均收入或預期壽命方面變得更貧窮了嗎？
  • distributed adj. 分配的，分佈的
    While aggregate living standards across the West have risen, the gains have been unevenly distributed.
    雖然整個西方的整體生活水準有所提高，但收益分配卻不均勻。
  • negotiating table n. phr. 談判桌
    ...triggered a major crisis, and forced Western governments back to the negotiating table.
    ...引發重大危機並迫使西方政府重返談判桌。
  • objective n. 目標，目的
    At least that is a coherent objective, though not the one Europe thinks it is negotiating over...
    至少這是一個連貫的目標，儘管不是歐洲以為自己正在談判的目標...
  • negotiate v. 談判，磋商
    ...which helps explain why Europeans have found trade talks with the Trump administration so confounding.
    ...這有助於解釋為什麼歐洲人會發現與川普政府的貿易談判如此令人困惑。
  • economic growth n. phr. 經濟成長
    Declinist rhetoric nurtures political consent for a zero-sum approach to trade and economic growth.
    衰落主義的言辭為貿易和經濟成長的零和方法培養了政治共識。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況或結果
    Presenting his 2024 report on European competitiveness, former Italian prime minister Mario Draghi called for an EU turnaround in order to avoid the “slow agony” of decline.
    句首的「Presenting his 2024 report on European competitiveness」是由現在分詞主持的片語，用來補充說明主句主語 Mario Draghi 在發表報告的同時所進行的動作，相當於副詞子句「While he was presenting...」。
  • 假設語氣（過去式表達與現在事實相反）
    If Europe responds to Trump’s bullying tactics in kind, the result would be a standoff that leaves both parties worse off.
    此句為第一/第二混合型條件句結構，用 「If + 現在式動詞」表達未來可能發生的條件，主句則用「would + 原形動詞」來推測假設情境下會產生的結果。
  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    A country with a growing workforce can increase its share of global GDP even if no one gets a dollar richer.
    句中的「with a growing workforce」是介係詞片語修飾前面的名詞 country，而後面的「even if no one gets a dollar richer」則是讓步副詞子句，用來加強語氣表達「即使...也...」。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞
    Conflating absolute and relative decline is more than just sloppy thinking.
    句首的「Conflating absolute and relative decline」是動名詞片語（動名詞 + 受詞），作為整個句子的主語，中文翻譯為「將絕對衰落與相對衰落混為一談」。
