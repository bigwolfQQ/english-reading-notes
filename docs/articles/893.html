台灣製造潛艦最快可能於下個月交付海軍

Taiwan-built sub could reach navy next month

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • deliver v. 交付、遞送
    Taiwan’s first domestically built submarine, the Hai Kun (Narwhal, 海鯤), could be delivered to the navy as early as next month...
    台灣第一艘國造潛艦「海鯤號」最快可能在下個月交付海軍...
  • deadline n. 截止期限
    The vessel missed the initial delivery deadline of November last year due to “testing delays,” its primary contractor...
    由於「測試延誤」，該艦錯過了去年11月的最初交船期限...
  • contractor n. 承包商
    ...its primary contractor, shipbuilder CSBC Corp, Taiwan, said in June.
    ...其主要承包商台灣國際造船公司於6月表示。
  • procure v. 採購、取得
    The contractor also cited “political pressure from China” in procuring critical equipment.
    該承包商還提到在採購關鍵設備時受到「來自中國的政治壓力」。
  • critical adj. 關鍵的、至關重要的
    The contractor also cited “political pressure from China” in procuring critical equipment.
    該承包商還提到在採購關鍵設備時受到「來自中國的政治壓力」。
  • schedule n. 時程表、計畫
    All testing operations are in the final stages, with the vessel likely to depart on a further round of sea trials this week, based on its prior testing schedule, Chi said.
    紀東昀表示，根據先前的測試時程，所有測試作業已進入最後階段，該艦本週可能再次出海進行新一輪的海試。
  • assistance n. 協助、幫忙
    The submarine has spent about a month in dry dock undergoing adjustments, as many of its systems and equipment are supplied from overseas and require optimization, often needing assistance from the original manufacturer, he said.
    他說，該潛艦已在乾塢中進行了大約一個月的調整，因為其許多系統與設備都是從海外供應的，需要進行優化，且經常需要原廠的協助。
  • prioritize v. 把...列為優先、優先處理
    Provided the navy and CSBC Corp, Taiwan continue prioritizing safety, there is a high likelihood of delivery by next month...
    只要海軍和台船繼續將安全列為優先考量，下個月交付的高度可能性將...
  • likelihood n. 可能性
    Provided the navy and CSBC Corp, Taiwan continue prioritizing safety, there is a high likelihood of delivery by next month...
    只要海軍和台船繼續將安全列為優先考量，下個月交付的高度可能性將...
  • personnel n. 人員、員工
    The vessel would still need to undergo combined systems testing and crew training for navy personnel before it is ready for mission...
    在準備好執行任務之前，該艦仍需進行聯合系統測試以及海軍人員的乘員訓練...

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表示原因或背景）
    The submarine has spent about a month in dry dock undergoing adjustments, as many of its systems and equipment are supplied from overseas and require optimization...
  • 複合形容詞（名詞 + 過去分詞）
    Taiwan’s first domestically built submarine, the Hai Kun (Narwhal, 海鯤), could be delivered to the navy as early as next month...
  • 假設語氣（Provided 引導條件句）
    Provided the navy and CSBC Corp, Taiwan continue prioritizing safety, there is a high likelihood of delivery by next month...
  • with 複合結構（with + 受詞 + 形容詞/分詞）
    All testing operations are in the final stages, with the vessel likely to depart on a further round of sea trials this week...
