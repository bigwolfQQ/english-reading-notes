央行報告指出：外匯存底下滑

Foreign exchange reserves drop, central bank reports

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • foreign exchange reserves n. phr. 外匯存底
    The nation’s foreign exchange reserves decreased by US$1.159 billion to US$600.745 billion last month from the previous month, the central bank said in a statement yesterday.
    中央銀行昨天在聲明中表示，我國上個月外匯存底減少11.59億美元，降至6,007.45億美元。
  • intervention n. 介入，干預
    The decline in foreign exchange reserves was mainly due to the strengthening US dollar and the central bank’s intervention in the foreign exchange market by selling US dollars and buying New Taiwan dollars to cap the local currency’s depreciation, it said.
    央行表示，外匯存底減少主要是因為美元走強，以及央行進場干預外匯市場，透過賣出美元、買進新台幣來抑制本地貨幣的貶值。
  • depreciation n. 貶值
    The decline in foreign exchange reserves was mainly due to the strengthening US dollar and the central bank’s intervention in the foreign exchange market by selling US dollars and buying New Taiwan dollars to cap the local currency’s depreciation, it said.
    央行表示，外匯存底減少主要是因為美元走強，以及央行進場干預外匯市場，透過賣出美元、買進新台幣來抑制本地貨幣的貶值。
  • portfolio n. 投資組合
    Last month, the US dollar index rose 2.03 percent, while other major non-greenback currencies except the yen declined, weighing on the central bank’s portfolio when the amount was converted into US dollars, an online report by the Chinese-language Liberty Times ... quoted Department of Foreign Exchange Director-General Eugene Tsai ... as saying.
    《自由時報》...的一篇網路報導引述外匯局局長蔡炯民的話指出，上個月美元指數上漲2.03%，除了日圓以外的其他主要非美元貨幣皆下跌，當這些金額轉換成美元時，對央行的投資組合造成了下行壓力。
  • convert v. 兌換，轉換
    Last month, the US dollar index rose 2.03 percent, while other major non-greenback currencies except the yen declined, weighing on the central bank’s portfolio when the amount was converted into US dollars, an online report by the Chinese-language Liberty Times ... quoted Department of Foreign Exchange Director-General Eugene Tsai ... as saying.
    《自由時報》...的一篇網路報導引述外匯局局長蔡炯民的話指出，上個月美元指數上漲2.03%，除了日圓以外的其他主要非美元貨幣皆下跌，當這些金額轉換成美元時，對央行的投資組合造成了下行壓力。
  • outflows n. 資金外流
    The NT dollar fell 0.69 percent against the US dollar last month, as foreign investors posted net outflows of about US$2.3 billion, Tsai said.
    蔡炯民表示，由於外資呈現約23億美元的淨流出，上個月新台幣兌美元匯率下跌0.69%。
  • fluctuation n. 波動，起伏
    Looking ahead, foreign fund flows and local exporters’ US dollar holdings, as well as overseas investments by Taiwanese individuals and businesses, all have an impact on the movements of the NT dollar, he said, adding that the central bank would act according to market conditions to avoid excessive fluctuations in the NT dollar.
    他指出，展望未來，外資資金流向、本地出口商的美元持倉，以及台灣個人與企業的海外投資，都會對新台幣的走勢產生影響，並補充說央行將視市場情況採取行動，以避免新台幣過度波動。
  • allocation n. 配置，分配
    It also depends on global capital flows and overseas asset allocation by Japanese investors, he added.
    他補充說，這也取決於全球資本流動以及日本投資者的海外資產配置。
  • institutional investors n. phr. 機構投資人
    Meanwhile, the market value of securities investments and NT dollar-denominated deposits held by foreign institutional investors rose to US$1.927 trillion last month, equivalent to 321 percent of foreign exchange reserves, compared with US$1.861 trillion and a ratio of 309 percent the previous month, central bank data showed.
    同時，央行數據顯示，外國機構投資人持有的有價證券投資市值及新台幣存款上個月增加至1.927兆美元，相當於外匯存底的321%，高於前一個月的1.861兆美元及309%的比例。
  • equivalent adj. 相等的，相當於
    Meanwhile, the market value of securities investments and NT dollar-denominated deposits held by foreign institutional investors rose to US$1.927 trillion last month, equivalent to 321 percent of foreign exchange reserves, compared with US$1.861 trillion and a ratio of 309 percent the previous month, central bank data showed.

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作附帶狀況（結果）狀語
    The decline in foreign exchange reserves was mainly due to the strengthening US dollar and the central bank’s intervention in the foreign exchange market by selling US dollars and buying New Taiwan dollars to cap the local currency’s depreciation, it said.
    句中的「by selling US dollars and buying New Taiwan dollars」是用現在分詞帶出具體的動作手段或方式，解釋央行是如何進行干預的。
  • 非限定關係子句修飾時間
    It aligned with the situation in Japan in August, when the nation’s foreign exchange reserves fell below the US$1 trillion mark, down US$94.6 billion month-on-month to US$995 billion, as Japanese authorities intervened in the foreign exchange market to support the yen, the central bank said.
    句中的「when the nation’s foreign exchange reserves fell...」是非限定關係子句（前面有逗號），用來補充說明前面提到的時間點「August（八月）」當時發生了什麼狀況。
  • 分詞構句作表述補充
    Last month, the US dollar index rose 2.03 percent, while other major non-greenback currencies except the yen declined, weighing on the central bank’s portfolio when the amount was converted into US dollars...
    句尾的「weighing on the central bank’s portfolio...」是由現在分詞片語構成的分詞構句，補充說明前面子句所發生的動作（美元指數上升、其他貨幣下跌）所帶來的結果或影響（對央行投資組合造成沉重壓力）。
  • 名詞子句作受詞
    As for the yen exchange rate, whether the Bank of Japan would raise rates further and if the Japanese authorities would intervene in the market again are key factors to the currency’s future movements, he said.
    句中的「whether... and if...」引導兩個並列的名詞子句，在句中共同擔任核心動詞「are」的主詞，用來探討日圓未來走勢的未知變數。
