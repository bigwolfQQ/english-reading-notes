輝達與鴻海運用機器人提高 GB300 組裝成功率

Nvidia, Hon Hai use robots to improve GB300 assembly success rates

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • operations n. 營運，業務運作
    American AI chip designer Nvidia Corp. and Taiwanese manufacturing giant Hon Hai Precision Industry Co. have improved the success rates of critical assembly steps for Nvidia's GB300 NVL72 rack-scale AI platform by using robots, the companies said.
    美國人工智慧晶片設計商輝達與台灣製造業巨頭鴻海精密工業股份有限公司表示，雙方透過使用機器人，提高了輝達 GB300 NVL72 機櫃級 AI 平台關鍵組裝步驟的成功率。
  • components n. 零件，組件
    In an Oct. 2 report on Nvidia's technical blog titled 'The Machines that Make the Machines,' the U.S. company detailed how its operations team and Hon Hai trained robots to assemble critical GB300 NVL72 components, improving manufacturing stability and efficiency.
    在輝達技術部落格於 10 月 2 日發布題為「打造機器的機器」的報告中，這家美國公司詳細說明了其營運團隊與鴻海如何訓練機器人來組裝關鍵的 GB300 NVL72 組件，從而提高了製造穩定性與效率。
  • verify v. 驗證，查證
    Nvidia said the two companies focused on assembling the GB300's tester tray, which is used to verify the functionality and performance of GB300 computing modules before shipment and deployment.
    輝達表示，兩家公司聚焦於組裝 GB300 的測試托盤，該托盤用於在出貨與部署前驗證 GB300 計算模組的功能與效能。
  • shipment n. 出貨，運送
    At an investor conference in mid-August, Hon Hai forecast that its AI rack shipments would more than double from a year earlier in 2026.
    在 8 月中旬舉行的法人說明會上，鴻海預估其 AI 機櫃出貨量在 2026 年將比前一年成長超過一倍。
  • deployment n. 部署，配置
    Nvidia said the two companies focused on assembling the GB300's tester tray, which is used to verify the functionality and performance of GB300 computing modules before shipment and deployment.

  • challenge n. 挑戰
    For busbar assembly, Nvidia said a major challenge was getting robots to grasp and transport a metal bar that carries high electrical currents to the tray, insert it and secure it with screws at 16 locations.
    針對匯流排組裝，輝達指出，一項主要的挑戰在於讓機器人抓取並搬運一條將高電流傳輸至托盤的金屬棒，將其插入並在 16 個位置用螺絲固定。

文法重點

  • 現在完成式 (Present Perfect Tense)
    American AI chip designer Nvidia Corp. and Taiwanese manufacturing giant Hon Hai Precision Industry Co. have improved the success rates of critical assembly steps...
    「have improved」為現在完成式，用來表達過去發生的動作（提高成功率）一直持續到現在，且對目前狀況產生成果與影響。
  • 關係代間代名詞子句 (Relative Clause with 'which')
    Nvidia said the two companies focused on assembling the GB300's tester tray, which is used to verify the functionality and performance of GB300 computing modules before shipment and deployment.
    句中的「which is used to...」是非限定關係子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「the tester tray」（測試托盤）的用途，逗號後方不可用 that。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    For multi-connector insertion, involving four cable connectors of different sizes, the success rate ranged from 90 percent to 95 percent...
    「involving four cable connectors...」是由現在分詞 involving 引導的分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞詞組「multi-connector insertion」（多連接器插入），相當於主動語態的形容詞子句「which involves...」。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The initiative covers three emerging industries -- electric vehicles, robots and digital health care -- which the company is developing using AI, semiconductor and communications technologies...
    句中的「is developing」雖然是現在進行式主動，但若看被動結構在文章中也常態性出現（如 are needed, is used），此處帶出企業營運發展的動作進行中。另如「developed with Nvidia」則是過去分詞作後位修飾被動概念。
