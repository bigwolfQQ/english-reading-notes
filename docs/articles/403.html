畜產試驗所推廣蛋白加工技術

Livestock institute touts egg white processing method

單字解析

  • repurpose v. 改變用途，重新利用
    The Taiwan Livestock Research Institute has developed a technique to repurpose salted egg white as food ingredients...
    台灣農業部畜產試驗所開發了一項將鹹蛋白重新利用為食品原料的技術...
  • annual output n. phr. 年產量，年產值
    ...with projections showing it could add more than NT$10 million (US$316,977) to the annual output of the domestic salted egg yolk processing industry.
    ...預估可為國內鹹蛋黃加工產業的年產值增加超過新台幣1,000萬元（合316,977美元）。
  • by-product n. 副產品
    The industry produces more than 4,300 tonnes of salted egg white each year as a by-product.
    該產業每年產生超過4,300公噸的鹹蛋白作為副產品。
  • discard v. 拋棄，丟棄
    While salted egg yolk is commonly used in pastries, including mooncakes, most salted egg whites are discarded due to their high salt content of up to 9 percent...
    雖然鹹蛋黃普遍用於糕餅中，包含月餅，但大多數鹹蛋白因其高達9%的高鹽分而被丟棄...
  • boost v. 促進，提高，增強
    ...with dietary fiber added to boost the processed nutritional value of egg whites, Huang said.
    黃振芳說，並添加膳食纖維以提升蛋白加工後的營養價值。
  • bring down phr. v. 降低，減少
    It took the research team five years to develop the technique, which brought down the sodium content to 453mg per 100g...
    研究團隊花了五年時間開發這項技術，將鈉含量降至每100公克453毫克...
  • in quantities n. phr. 大量地
    The salted egg whites were used in large quantities to overcome konjac powder’s characteristic of being highly incompatible with proteins, Wu said.
    吳鈴彩表示，鹹蛋白被大量使用，以克服魔芋粉高度不相容於蛋白質的特性。
  • adjust v. 調整，調節
    Operational details of the technique can be adjusted to create different shapes and mouth sensations for different food products tailored to food processors’ needs, she said.
    她說，該技術的操作細節可以進行調整，以針對食品加工業者的需求，為不同的食品創造不同的形狀和口感。
  • ratio n. 比率，比例
    The ratio of salted egg white by-products and their processed egg white output is about one to one, she said.
    她說，鹹蛋白副產品與其加工後蛋白產出的比例大約是一比一。
  • operator n. 經營者，業者
    The institute’s technique benefits food processing operators and has the potential to advance high-protein fitness foods for gym-goers, he said.
    他說，該技術惠及食品加工業者，並具有推進健身族群高蛋白健身食品的潛力。
  • patent v. 取得專利
    The technique was patented last year and won a silver prize at the second Taiwan Agricultural Sustainability Awards in Taipei on Wednesday.
    該技術已於去年獲得專利，並於週三在台北舉行的第二屆台灣農業永續獎中贏得銀獎。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況（with + 名詞 + 分詞）
    The Taiwan Livestock Research Institute has developed a technique to repurpose salted egg white as food ingredients, with projections showing it could add more than NT$10 million...
    「with + 名詞 +現在分詞 (showing)」用來補充說明主句動作發生時的伴隨狀況或結果，表示「在預測顯示……的情況下」，在商業與新聞英文中非常常見。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    ...most salted egg whites are discarded due to their high salt content of up to 9 percent...
    句型為「be + 過去分詞 (are discarded)」，強調動作的承受者（大多數的鹹蛋白）而不是動作的執行者，新聞報導中常用來客觀描述事實或過程。
  • 非限定關係子句 / 關係代間代名詞片語
    Wu Ling-tsai, an assistant researcher at the institute who led technical development of the project, said konjac powder was integrated into the egg white...
    句中的「who led technical development of the project」是用來補充修飾先行詞「an assistant researcher」，提供關於吳鈴彩的額外背景資訊。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞 (It takes + 時間 + to V)
    It took the research team five years to develop the technique, which brought down the sodium content to 453mg per 100g...
    使用虛主詞 「It」代表真正的主詞「to develop the technique」，句型「It takes (took) + 某人 + 時間 + to V」用來表達「花費某人多少時間去做某事」。
