Taipei Times · 2026-10-14

山本在密爾瓦基吞敗

Yamamoto flounders in Milwaukee

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • jeopardy n. 危險，威脅
    Yoshinobu Yamamoto finally flopped in a playoff game, and the Los Angeles Dodgers are heading back home with their bid for a third straight championship in serious jeopardy.
    吉本由伸在季後賽中終於慘遭滑鐵盧，洛杉磯道奇隊帶著他們爭取三連霸的美夢陷入嚴重危機的劣勢返回主場。
  • postseason n. 季後賽
    Yamamoto lasted just 2-2/3 innings for his shortest postseason start as the Dodgers lost 7-4 to Milwaukee on Monday to fall behind 2-0 in the National League Championship Series (NLCS).
    山本僅投了 2 又 2/3 局，創下他最短的季後賽先發紀錄，道奇隊週一以 4 比 7 不敵密爾瓦基，在國家聯盟冠軍賽（NLCS）以 0 比 2 落後。
  • departure n. 背離，偏離，出發
    It was quite a departure for a pitcher who was virtually unhittable last October.
    對於這位去年 10 月幾乎無法被擊安打的投手來說，這可說是一大反常。
  • appearances n. （運動員的）出賽，登場
    Yamamoto, last year’s World Series MVP, entered 8-1 with a 2.14 ERA in 11 career postseason appearances.
    去年世界大賽 MVP 山本，在生涯 11 場季後賽出賽中原本拿下 8 勝 1 敗、防禦率 2.14 的成績。
  • quest n. 追求，探索
    The Dodgers were banking on him to deliver another gem to tie the NLCS as they continue their quest to become the first NL team to win three straight World Series.
    道奇隊指望他能再次繳出精彩表現以扳平國聯冠軍賽，因為他們持續追求成為第一支贏得三次世界大賽連霸的國聯球隊。
  • protect v. 保護，守住（領先優勢）
    Yamamoto was unable to protect two Los Angeles leads in the first three innings.
    山本在前三局未能守住洛杉磯隊的兩次領先。
  • turning point n. phr. 轉折點
    The turning point of Yamamoto’s night came in the third.
    山本今晚的轉折點出現在第三局。
  • maintain v. 維持，保持
    The Dodgers pulled back ahead 4-3 in the fifth, but they could not maintain the lead and then Los Angeles’ lineup had no answers for Milwaukee’s bullpen.
    道奇隊在第五局以 4 比 3 反超，但他們無法保持領先，隨後洛杉磯的打線對釀酒人的牛棚毫無辦法。
  • opportunity n. 機會
    If the Dodgers do not start hitting, Yamamoto might not get another opportunity to pitch this postseason.
    如果道奇隊再不開始安打，山本可能在這個季後賽再也沒有機會先發投球了。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作附帶狀況或結果（分詞構句）
    Yamamoto lasted just 2-2/3 innings for his shortest postseason start as the Dodgers lost 7-4 to Milwaukee on Monday to fall behind 2-0 in the National League Championship Series (NLCS).
    句尾的「to fall behind 2-0...」是不定詞片語作結果副詞用，表示前面道奇隊輸球所導致的結果，即「因而落後 0 比 2」。
  • 關係代間詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    It was quite a departure for a pitcher who was virtually unhittable last October.
    句中的「who was virtually unhittable last October」是由關係代間詞 who 引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的先行詞「a pitcher（一位投手）」。
  • 條件句（Conditional Sentence）
    If the Dodgers do not start hitting, Yamamoto might not get another opportunity to pitch this postseason.
    此句為第一類條件句（First Conditional），用來表達如果現在或未來某個條件成立（道奇隊沒有開始安打），則可能會發生某個結果（山本可能不會再有投球機會）。結構為：If + 現在式動詞, 主詞 + 조動詞（may/might/will） + 原形動詞。
