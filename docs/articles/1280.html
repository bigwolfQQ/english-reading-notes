文法重點

  • 分詞構句（現在分詞作附帶狀況/結果）
    "This is another example of Taiwan combining its ICT capabilities with high-quality healthcare," he said, adding that the technology could further raise Taiwan's international profile and competitiveness in healthcare.
    句尾的「adding that...」是由對等子句簡化而來的分詞構句，主詞 he（他）主動發出「補充說明 (add)」的動作，因此使用現在分詞V-ing，用來補充說明前面那句話之後所延伸出的後續情形或言論。
  • 使役動詞 enable 的句型 (enable + O. + to V)
    Meanwhile, Ho said advances in medical imaging have enabled doctors to move beyond relying primarily on experience when assessing patients...
    「enable + 受詞 + to V」是多益測驗極常見的高頻句型，意指「使……能夠……」。在此句中，advances（進步）作為主詞，enable 使 doctors（醫師）能夠 move beyond（超越）……。
  • 非限定性關係代名詞子句 (who...
    Belize's Ambassador to Taiwan Katherine Meighan, who also attended the forum, said the AI 3D medical imaging technology could enable faster, more efficient and accurate diagnoses...
    逗點後面接的「who also attended the forum」是非限定性關係代名詞子句，用來補充說明前面主詞 Katherine Meighan 的身分與額外資訊。若將此子句拿掉，主句的文意依然完整。
  • 被動語態 (Be + 過去分詞)
    The conference, organized by the Ministry of Health and Welfare (MOHW), showcased applications of 3D medical imaging...
    句中的「organized by...」是過去分詞片語當作形容詞修飾前面的主詞 The conference（會議）。因為會議是被衛生福利部所主辦，所以使用被動概念（organize 轉為 organized），這在商業新聞與合約文書的標題或修飾語中非常常見。
