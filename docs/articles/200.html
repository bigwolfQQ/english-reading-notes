Taipei Times · 2026-09-06

一位校長如何從洪水中拯救了900名學生

How a principal saved 900 students from the flood

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • urgent adj. 緊急的、緊迫的
    School principal Rajendra Dawadi was inside his school near Nepal’s Trishuli River with about 900 students last week when a colleague delivered an urgent warning.
    上週，尼泊爾崔蘇里河附近的學校內，校長拉真德拉・達瓦迪正與大約 900 名學生待在一起，此時一名同事傳達了緊急警告。
  • accountant n. 會計師、會計人員
    “My accountant came to the school and said, ‘The flood is coming, the flood is coming,’” Dawadi said.
    「我的會計跑來學校說：『洪水來了，洪水來了，』」達瓦迪說。
  • evacuation n. 疏散、撤離
    He quickly ordered an evacuation, and teachers hurried hundreds of students from classrooms to higher ground.
    他迅速下達疏散命令，老師們催促數百名學生從教室移往高處。
  • collapse v. 倒塌、瓦解、崩潰
    Minutes later, he watched the Trishuli Bazaar Secondary School he had run for years collapse into a raging torrent.
    幾分鐘後，他眼睜睜看著自己經營多年的崔蘇里巴扎中學倒塌並墜入滾滾洪流之中。
  • authorities n. 當局、官方
    Authorities in Trishuli Bazaar had not provided an evacuation plan, Dawadi said.
    達瓦迪表示，崔蘇里巴扎當局並未提供疏散計畫。
  • refuge n. 避難、庇護、避難所
    Others took refuge on higher ground.
    其他人則在高等處避難。
  • comprehend v. 理解、領悟
    At first, she struggled to comprehend the danger.
    起初，她很難理解危險性。
  • scale n. 規模、範圍
    “We never would have thought the flood could reach such a scale,” she said.
    「我們絕不會想到洪水會達到這種規模，」她說。
  • reunite v. 使團聚、重聚
    It was only four days later that a helicopter finally reunited her with her family.
    直到四天後，一架直升機才終於讓她與家人團聚。
  • figures n. 數字、數據
    According to figures cited by Save the Children, at least 69 schools were destroyed or damaged, disrupting the education of nearly 19,000 children.
    根據「救助兒童會」引用的數據，至少有 69 所學校被毀或受損，打亂了近 19,000 名兒童的教育。
  • disrupt v. 打斷、中斷、擾亂
    According to figures cited by Save the Children, at least 69 schools were destroyed or damaged, disrupting the education of nearly 19,000 children.
    根據「救助兒童會」引用的數據，至少有 69 所學校被毀或受損，打亂了近 19,000 名兒童的教育。

文法重點

  • 與現在事實相反的假設語氣 (Second Conditional)
    “If the information was wrong, we would lose a day of schooling,” Dawadi said. “But if it was true, we were going to lose our lives.”
    句型結構為「If + 過去式動詞, 主詞 + would/could/might + 原形動詞」。用來假設與現在或未來事實相反的情境。此處表達「如果情報是錯的（事實上不是），我們會損失一天學業；如果是真的（事實上是真的），我們就會失去生命。」
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    Minutes later, he watched the Trishuli Bazaar Secondary School he had run for years collapse into a raging torrent.
  • 強調句構 (It was... that... cleft sentence)
    It was only four days later that a helicopter finally reunited her with her family.
