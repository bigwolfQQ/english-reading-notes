簡化碳費制度

Streamlining the carbon fee system

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • emits v. 排放（氣體、熱等）
    The scheme is to be gradually expanded to encompass small and medium-sized manufacturers and commercial service providers that emit less carbon individually but exist in large numbers...
    該方案將逐步擴大，涵蓋個別碳排放量較小但數量龐大的中小型製造商和商業服務業...
  • competitiveness n. 競爭力
    This climate policy, which has significant implications for the green transformation of Taiwan’s industrial structure and its competitiveness in global supply chains...
    這項氣候政策對台灣產業結構的綠色轉型及在全球供應鏈中的競爭力具有重大意義...
  • implementation n. 實施、執行
    However, implementation faces a number of challenges, including carbon fee rate disputes, stringent approval requirements...
    然而，政策執行面臨諸多挑戰，包含碳費費率爭議、嚴格的審查要求...
  • subsidies n. 補助金、津貼
    Central government authorities should establish a legal requirement that 100 percent of carbon fee revenues be allocated to a specific greenhouse gas reduction fund and be earmarked for subsidies that help businesses upgrade low-carbon equipment...
    中央政府主管機關應建立法定要求，將百分之百的碳費收入分配至特定的溫室氣體減量基金，並指定用於補助企業升級低碳設備...
  • streamline v. 使簡化、使效率化
    To streamline Taiwan’s carbon pricing framework and help industries make a smooth transition, central government authorities should amend the Regulations Governing Self-determined Reduction Plans...
    為了簡化台灣的碳定價框架並協助產業順利轉型，中央政府主管機關應修正《自主減量計畫管理辦法》...
  • deductions n. 扣除額、減免
    The ministry should revise regulations to grant substantial carbon fee deductions directly to companies that adopt renewable electricity and meet statutory benchmarks for improving energy efficiency.
    部會應修訂法規，直接給予採用再生電力並達到提高能源效率法定基準的企業實質碳費扣減。
  • partnership n. 夥伴關係、合夥企業
    Most importantly, the government should establish a public-private partnership to help small and medium enterprises measure their carbon emissions and transition to lower-carbon operations.
    最重要的是，政府應建立公私協力夥伴關係，協助中小企業測量其碳排放並轉型至低碳營運。
  • compliant adj. 合規的、遵從的
    This would help traditional industries with limited resources establish compliant carbon footprint data and safeguard their ability to compete fairly within supply chains.
    這將有助於資源有限的傳統產業建立合規的碳足跡數據，並保障其在供應鏈中公平競爭的能力。
  • safeguard v. 維護、保護
    This would help traditional industries with limited resources establish compliant carbon footprint data and safeguard their ability to compete fairly within supply chains.
    這將有助於資源有限的傳統產業建立合規的碳足跡數據，並保障其在供應鏈中公平競爭的能力。
  • allocate v. 分配、分派
    Central government authorities should establish a legal requirement that 100 percent of carbon fee revenues be allocated to a specific greenhouse gas reduction fund...
    中央政府主管機關應建立法定要求，將百分之百的碳費收入分配至特定的溫室氣體減量基金...

文法重點

  • 非限定性關係子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    This climate policy, which has significant implications for the green transformation of Taiwan’s industrial structure and its competitiveness in global supply chains, demonstrates the government’s commitment...
    句中的「which has significant implications for...」前方帶有逗號，屬於非限定性關係子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「This climate policy」，若拿掉這個子句，主句的完整意思仍然成立。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice) 的運用
    Central government authorities should establish a legal requirement that 100 percent of carbon fee revenues be allocated to a specific greenhouse gas reduction fund and be earmarked for subsidies...
    句中的「be allocated」與「be earmarked」採用被動語態，強調碳費收入是被分配與指定用途的，動作的承受者（revenues）是主體。此外，that 子句中的動詞使用原形（(should) be allocated），帶有強烈的建議或法規要求意味。
  • 分詞片語作後置修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The scheme is to be gradually expanded to encompass small and medium-sized manufacturers and commercial service providers that emit less carbon individually but exist in large numbers, with established carbon pricing mechanisms and the option of voluntary reduction plans that qualify companies for reduced rates.
    句尾的「with established carbon pricing mechanisms...」是由介系詞 with 引導的複合結構，其中「established」為過去分詞作形容詞用，用來修飾後方的名詞「carbon pricing mechanisms」，表示「被建立的碳定價機制」。
