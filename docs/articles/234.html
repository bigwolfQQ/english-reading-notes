捷運補助款編列創紀錄

Record MRT funding earmarked

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • earmark v. 編列（預算）、指定（用途）
    The government has earmarked a record NT$49.9 billion in subsidies for local MRT construction next year.
    政府已在明年的地方捷運建設補助款中編列了創紀錄的 499 億新台幣。
  • subsidy n. 補助金、津貼
    The government has earmarked a record NT$49.9 billion (US$1.58 billion) in subsidies for local MRT construction next year.
    政府已在明年的地方捷運建設補助款中編列了創紀錄的 499 億新台幣（約 15.8 億美元）。
  • allocation n. 撥款、分配額
    ..., and the highest annual allocation for local MRT projects to date, the Ministry of Transportation and Communications said yesterday.
    ……交通部昨天表示，這也是歷年来地方捷運專案最高年度撥款額。
  • statistics n. 統計數據、統計學
    The ministry hosted a news conference to present statistics after local governments in Taipei, New Taipei City, Taoyuan and Taichung accused the central government of curtailing the budget for the MRT projects in their localities and sought additional funding.
    在台北、新北、桃園和台中等地方政府指控中央政府刪減其轄區內捷運專案的預算並尋求額外資金後，交通部舉辦了一場記者會來公布統計數據。
  • curtail v. 縮減、削減
    ...after local governments in Taipei, New Taipei City, Taoyuan and Taichung accused the central government of curtailing the budget for the MRT projects in their localities and sought additional funding.
    ……在台北、新北、桃園和台中等地方政府指控中央政府刪減其轄區內捷運專案的預算並尋求額外資金後……
  • tender n. 投標、招標
    ...taking into account factors such as construction schedules, tender awards and budget execution.
    ……並納入施工進度、招標決標與預算執行率等因素進行考量。
  • execution n. 執行、實施
    ...taking into account factors such as construction schedules, tender awards and budget execution.
    ……並納入施工進度、招標決標與預算執行率等因素進行考量。
  • account for v. phr. 占（比例）、說明
    ...with Taipei, New Taipei City and Keelung accounting for NT$488.2 billion, or about 68 percent, Wu said.
    伍勝園指出，其中台北、新北和基隆占了 4,882 億新台幣，約占 68%。
  • shortfall n. 赤字、短缺
    Taipei said it is short NT$6 billion in central government funding, while Kaohsiung says it has a NT$13.6 billion shortfall.
    台北市表示中央政府資金短缺 6 億新台幣，而高雄市則表示有 136 億新台幣的資金缺口。
  • assess v. 評估、評定
    Local governments submit their funding needs, after which the ministry reviews the proposed projects and assesses whether the funding is warranted, he said...
    他說，地方政府提交資金需求，隨後交通部會審查擬議中的專案並評估資金是否合理……
  • warranted adj. 有正當理由的、必要的
    ...and assesses whether the funding is warranted, he said, adding that projects with unclear plans would not be approved.
    ……並評估資金是否合理，他並補充說，計畫不明確的專案將不會獲得批准。
  • scrutiny n. 詳細審查、仔細檢查
    All projects are subject to rigorous scrutiny at every stage...
    所有專案在每個階段都須接受嚴格的審查……
  • amendment n. 修正案、修改
    The ministry said that an amendment to the Act Governing the Allocation of Government Revenues and Expenditures (財政收支劃分法) last year has reduced the central government’s financial resources available for local infrastructure.
    交通部表示，去年對《財政收支劃分法》的一項修正案，已經減少了中央政府可用於地方基礎設施的財政資源。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（附加補充說明）
    ...adding the ministry has made similar fiscal arrangements in the same way in the past few years.
    句尾的「adding...」為現在分詞構句，省略了連接詞與主語（原句意為 and added that...），用以補充說明前面子句所發生的動作或說法。
  • be subject to + 名詞（受制於／須接受……）
    All projects are subject to rigorous scrutiny at every stage...
    「be subject to」是多益常考的重要片語，意指「易受……影響的」或「須經由……審查的」，在此句中用來表達所有專案都必須經過嚴格檢視。
  • 現在分詞作後位修飾
    Some are still in the planning stage, while others are already under construction, he said.
    句中的「are still in the planning stage」與「are already under construction」運用了介系詞片語作表語，而前面提到補助款決定過程時，也常使用現在分詞來修飾名詞或補充狀態。
