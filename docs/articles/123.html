Venezuelans skeptical of US oil deal reshaping their future

單字解析

  • bountiful adj. 豐富的、慷慨的
    Venezuelans’ identity has been built on the belief that their country’s bountiful oil resources belong to the people...
    委內瑞拉人的身分認同一直建立在這樣的信念上：他們國家豐富的石油資源屬於人民...
  • generate v. 產生、引起（營收、利潤等）
    While the deal could eventually generate billions of dollars for an oil industry and economy...
    雖然這筆交易最終可能會為石油產業和經濟帶來數十億美元的收入...
  • objective n. 目標、目的
    ...they believe that gaining control of Venezuelan oil was the ultimate objective when US President Donald Trump ordered US forces to abduct then-Venezuelan President Nicolas Maduro...
    ...他們相信，控制委內瑞拉的石油才是美國總統唐納·川普下令美國軍隊綁架時任委內瑞拉總統尼古拉斯·馬杜洛時的最終目標...
  • revenues n. 收益、歲入（常用複數）
    ...Venezuela got most of its revenues from oil exports rather than from taxing local citizens and businesses...
    ...委內瑞拉的大部分收入來自石油出口，而不是對當地公民和企業徵稅...
  • subsidy n. 補助金、津貼
    The state ended key subsidies, and social unrest followed.
    政府取消了關鍵補貼，隨後引發了社會動盪。
  • private sector n. phr. 私營部門、民間企業
    ...many families benefited from well-paid jobs in the public and private sectors.
    ...許多家庭從公共和私營部門的高薪工作中受益。
  • sanctions n. 制裁（常用複數）
    Sanctions imposed by the US over democratic and human rights concerns crippled the industry and the economy further.
    美國因民主和人權問題而實施的制裁進一步削弱了該產業和經濟。
  • inflation n. 通貨膨脹
    Even though markets and stores are well-stocked, people struggle to afford the basics because of soaring inflation.
    儘管市場和商店貨源充足，但由於通貨膨脹飆升，人們仍然難以負擔基本生活用品。
  • investment n. 投資
    In February, a legal overhaul opened the path to private investment in the oil industry...
    2月的一項法律改革為石油產業的私人投資開闢了道路...
  • infrastructure n. 基礎設施
    Because Venezuela’s energy infrastructure has been severely degraded, it could take years before new investments lead to significantly higher oil production...
    專家表示，由於委內瑞拉的能源基礎設施已經嚴重退化，新投資需要數年時間才能顯著提高石油產量...

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表附帶狀況或結果）
    ...leaving Rodriguez in charge.
    句尾的「leaving Rodriguez in charge」為現在分詞構句，由連接詞和子句簡化而來（原意為 and left Rodriguez in charge），在此用來補充說明前面主句動作所產生的直接結果。
  • 現在分詞當形容詞修飾名詞
    Sanctions imposed by the US over democratic and human rights concerns crippled the industry and the economy further.
    句中的「imposed by the US」是過去分詞片語，置於名詞 Sanctions 後方，用來修飾說明這些制裁是由美國所施加的。
  • 假設語氣（與現在事實相反）
    If we didn’t have it, we would be fried.
    此句使用與現在事實相反的假設語氣。條件句（If 子句）使用過去式動詞 didn’t have，而主要子句則使用「would + 原形動詞」（would be fried），表達如果目前沒有石油，我們就會完蛋了。
