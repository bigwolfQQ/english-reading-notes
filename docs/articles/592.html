卓榮泰在中華電信集團結婚典禮上敦促新人孕育下一代

Cho urges newlyweds to have children at Chunghwa Telecom mass wedding

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • address v. 處理、對付、解決（問題等）
    Cho, who served as a witness at the ceremony held at the Grand Hotel, thanked the newlyweds for taking a step that could help Taiwan "effectively address its demographic challenges,"
    卓榮泰在圓山大飯店舉行的儀式中擔任證婚人，他感謝新人們採取了有助於台灣「有效應對人口挑戰」的行動，
  • obligation n. 義務、責任
    "Marriage is everyone's right, and having children is everyone's choice. It is definitely not a responsibility or obligation,"
    他說：「結婚是每個人的權利，生孩子是每個人的選擇。這絕對不是責任或義務。」
  • allowance n. 津貼、補助
    For couples planning to start families, Cho urged them to make use of government support, highlighting plans for a NT$5,000 (US$158) monthly child-rearing allowance starting next year,
    對於計劃組建家庭的夫婦，卓榮泰敦促他們善用政府補助，並強調了明起實施每月新台幣5,000元（約158美元）育兒津貼的計劃、
  • subsidy n. 補助金、津貼
    "Of course, the more the better," he said, adding that he hoped the government could provide additional subsidies.
    「當然，越多越好，」他說，並補充希望政府能提供額外補助。
  • insufficient adj. 不足的、不夠的
    Dino Chen, a senior engineer who has worked at Chunghwa Telecom for five years, said he and his wife plan to have a child in one or two years, but added that the government's current support showed "sincerity" but remained insufficient.
    在中華電信工作五年的資深工程師Dino Chen表示，他和妻子計劃在一、兩年內生一個孩子，但補充說政府目前的支持表現出「誠意」但仍然不足。
  • burden n. 負擔
    While Chen acknowledged that "there is definitely pressure," he said his wife also works and that they share the mortgage payments, easing the financial burden.
    雖然Chen承認「確實有壓力」，但他說妻子也有工作，他們共同分擔房貸，減輕了財務負擔。
  • highlight v. 強調、突顯
    He highlighted company measures to support employees starting families, including an NT$80,000 childbirth subsidy,
    他強調了公司支持員工組建家庭的措施，包括新台幣8,000元的生育補助、
  • concern n. 擔憂、關注的事
    "Once you take on a mortgage, you might have to pay NT$20,000 to NT$30,000 a month," the 30-year-old said, identifying housing as their "main" concern.
    「一旦你扛起房貸，每個月可能得繳2萬到3萬元，」這位30歲的男子說，並將住房視為他們的「主要」顧慮。
  • appeal n. 吸引力、感染力
    A 19-year employee of Chunghwa Telecom, Lin said the planned NT$5,000 monthly allowance had "some appeal," but added that the amount would "definitely not be enough" amid rising living costs.
    身為中華電信19年的員工，林冠男表示，計劃中的每月5,000元津貼「有一點吸引力」，但補充說，在生活成本不斷上漲的情況下，這筆金額「絕對不夠」。
  • acknowledge v. 承認
    While Chen acknowledged that "there is definitely pressure," he said his wife also works and that they share the mortgage payments,
    雖然Chen承認「確實有壓力」，但他說妻子也有工作，他們共同分擔房貸，

文法重點

  • 非限定關代子句（補充說明前述名詞）
    Cho, who served as a witness at the ceremony held at the Grand Hotel, thanked the newlyweds...
    句中的「who served as a witness at the ceremony...」前方有逗號，屬於非限定用法，用來補充說明主詞 Cho 的身分，拿掉這段話並不影響主要句意。
  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾
    ...highlighting plans for a NT$5,000 (US$158) monthly child-rearing allowance starting next year...
    「starting next year」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前方的名詞 allowance，相當於「that will start next year」的簡化用法。
  • 對比與讓步副詞子句（While 引導）
    While encouraging the couples to have children, Cho stressed that childbirth remains a personal choice.
    「While encouraging...」是省略了主詞與be動詞的副詞子句（原句形狀應為 While he was encouraging...），用來表達「在...同時」的背景情況。
