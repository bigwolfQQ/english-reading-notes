Why a journalist is exposing the world’s biggest porn bosses

單字解析

  • anonymous adj. 匿名的，不具名的
    The owners of the industry’s biggest companies are so anxious to remain anonymous that we often don’t even know their names.
    該產業最大公司的老闆們極度渴望保持匿名，以至於我們經常連他們的名字都不知道。
  • investigate v. 調查，研究
    So Nilsson’s decision to start investigating pornography for the Financial Times will no doubt have come as a very unwelcome development...
    因此，尼爾森決定為《金融時報》開始調查色情產業，毫無疑問會是一個非常不受歡迎的發展……
  • pragmatic adj. 務實的，講求實效的
    This perspective has shaped her calmly pragmatic approach to the subject.
    這種視角塑造了她對這個主題冷靜而務實的方法。
  • strategy n. 策略，對策
    Bergmair had written his undergraduate thesis on “financing strategies for corporate acquisitions,” and knew so little about pornography...
    貝格邁爾曾以「企業併購的融資策略」為題撰寫大學畢業論文，對色情產業所知甚少……
  • profitable adj. 有利可圖的，賺錢的
    “He knew it was a highly profitable business and he just cared about ensuring that the structure was safe enough so that he could keep taking his dividends out.”
    「他知道這是一項利潤極高的業務，他只關心確保結構足夠安全，以便他能持續領取股息。」
  • dividend n. 股息，紅利
    “He knew it was a highly profitable business and he just cared about ensuring that the structure was safe enough so that he could keep taking his dividends out.”
    「他知道這是一項利潤極高的業務，他只關心確保結構足夠安全，以便他能持續領取股息。」
  • distribute v. 分發，分配，散布
    We’re already familiar with the way pornography companies drove early improvements in the power of the Internet from the 1990s onwards, to enable large quantities of video to be distributed to the maximum number of people at maximum speed.
    我們已經熟悉色情公司推動1990年代以來網際網路功能早期改進的方式，以便將大量影片以最快速度分發給最多的人。
  • lucrative adj. 賺錢的，利潤丰厚的
    And long before influencers started working out what they needed to do to grow and maintain a lucrative social media following...
    早在網紅們開始摸索如何發展和維持有利可圖的社群媒體粉絲之前……
  • consumer v. 消費，消耗，吃喝（文章中作 consume 的動詞變化）
    ...the way “all this lovely, worthwhile content” is transmitted to her phone, and the way she consumes it, is closely based on a model developed by online pornography tech developers.
    ……所有這些『可愛、有價值的內容』傳輸到她手機上的方式，以及她消費它的方式，都是密切基於線上色情科技開發人員所開發的模式。
  • innovation n. 創新，革新
    Social media sites have learned from innovations developed by pornography companies — how to make the stream of content addictive, how to ensure people stay browsing for a little longer.
    社群媒體網站從色情公司開發的創新中學到了東西——如何使內容流上癮，如何確保人們多瀏覽一會兒。
  • asset n. 資產，財產
    She wonders what we can learn “about the state of capitalism when so many people are saying: ‘Actually, it’s not going to work for me to get a degree and try to get a job and work my way up because just earning an income, if you don’t have previous assets, is not working any more...’
    她納悶當這麼多人說：『實際上，拿學位、試圖找工作並一步步往上爬對我來說行不通，因為如果你沒有先前的資產，單靠賺取收入已經不管用了……』時，我們可以從中學到關於資本主義現狀的什麼。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後置修飾語 (Present Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    “These days, the men calling the shots are nowhere to be seen.”
    句中的 "calling the shots" 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "the men"（那些掌權發號施令的人）。這種結構相當於形容詞子句 "who are calling the shots" 的簡化，能讓句子更為緊湊流暢。
  • so ... that ... 結果構句 (Result Clause)
    The owners of the industry’s biggest companies are so anxious to remain anonymous that we often don’t even know their names.
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Independent Participial Construction)
    ...she is approaching the end of maternity leave (she finished writing her book in the exhausting first weeks after giving birth).
  • 強調虛主詞 It is / was ... (Cleft Sentence)
    It turned out he was an Austrian former Goldman Sachs investment banker...
  • 間接問句 (Indirect Question)
    She wondered why, unlike most businesses of a similar scale, pornography companies rarely made the headlines.
