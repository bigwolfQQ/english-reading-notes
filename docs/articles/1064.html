台灣各地連假期間氣象預報大致穩定

Stable weather forecast for most of Taiwan over holiday weekend

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • upcoming adj. 即將到來的
    Taiwan will see mostly stable weather over the upcoming four-day holiday weekend...
    台灣在即將到來的四天連假期間將迎來大致穩定的天氣...
  • favorable adj. 有利的、順心的
    ...with favorable conditions for moon-gazing during the Mid-Autumn Festival on Friday, according to the Central Weather Administration (CWA).
    ...根據中央氣象署（CWA）表示，週五中秋節當天有著適合賞月的良好天候狀況。
  • range from ... to ... phr. 範圍從……到……
    ...while conditions on Friday would range from cloudy to clear, most cloud cover in the afternoon will gradually clear up by evening.
    ...雖然週五的天氣狀況將介於多雲到晴之間，但下午的大多數雲層將在傍晚前逐漸散去。
  • gradually adv. 逐漸地
    ...most cloud cover in the afternoon will gradually clear up by evening.
    ...下午的大多數雲層將在傍晚前逐漸散去。
  • limited to phr. 受限於、僅限於
    ...with a chance of afternoon showers limited to mountainous areas.
    ...午後短暫陣雨的機率僅限於山區。
  • strengthen v. 增強、鞏固
    The system is expected to strengthen into a tropical storm as early as Thursday...
    該系統最快可能在週四增強為熱帶風暴...
  • as early as phr. 早在……；最快在……
    The system is expected to strengthen into a tropical storm as early as Thursday...
    該系統最快可能在週四增強為熱帶風暴...
  • expect v. 預期、期待
    The system is expected to strengthen into a tropical storm as early as Thursday...
    該系統預期最快在週四增強為熱帶風暴...
  • remain v. 保持、維持
    Temperatures will remain warm over the holiday weekend...
    連假期間氣溫將維持溫暖...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾（形容詞用）
    On Monday, which is celebrated as Teacher's Day in Taiwan, as well as Tuesday, the outer bands of a passing tropical storm or typhoon may bring scattered showers to Yilan and areas above Taoyuan in the north, Lai said.
    句中的「a passing tropical storm」使用了現在分詞「passing」置於名詞前作修飾，意指「正在經過的、路過的」熱帶風暴。分詞（現在分詞表主動或進行，過去分詞表被動或完成）在多益閱讀測驗中常考用來修飾名詞的精確用法。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The system is expected to strengthen into a tropical storm as early as Thursday...
    句型「is expected to」為典型的被動語態結構（be + 過去分詞），用來表達「被預期……」。在商業英文與新聞報導中，當說話者不需強調發動者或為了保持客觀語氣時，經常使用被動語態。
  • 關係代間詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    ...while those in the east, where it will be a bit cloudier, will have a fair chance of seeing the moon, Lai said.
    句中的「where it will be a bit cloudier」是由關係副詞 where 引導的非限定關係子句，用來補充說明先行詞「those in the east」（東部的民眾）當地的氣象狀況。非限定子句通常用逗號隔開，多益測驗常考關係代間詞與關係副詞的選擇。
