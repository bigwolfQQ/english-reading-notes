花蓮卡車跨越對向車道相撞，造成母子死亡

Mother, son killed in Hualien crash after truck crosses into oncoming lane

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • crash n. 車禍、碰撞
    A mother and her son died in a car crash in Hualien County on Friday after an oncoming truck crossed into their lane and rammed into their vehicle.
    花蓮縣週五發生一起車禍，一輛對向卡車跨越車道撞上自小客車，導致車內一對母子死亡。
  • injured adj. 受傷的
    The truck driver was seriously injured.
    卡車司機受到重傷。
  • reason n. 原因、理由
    The northbound truck veered into the southbound lane for some unknown reason and struck the car near the 238.5-kilometer marker on Provincial Highway 9 in Guangfu Township at around 5 p.m., leaving the mother, surnamed Lin (林), and her son trapped inside the severely damaged vehicle.
    這輛北向行駛的卡車因不明原因駛入南向車道，於下午 5 點左右在光復鄉台 9 線 238.5 公里處附近撞上一輛轎車，導致姓林的母親與其兒子受困在嚴重毀損的車內。
  • damaged adj. 受損的
    The northbound truck veered into the southbound lane for some unknown reason and struck the car near the 238.5-kilometer marker on Provincial Highway 9 in Guangfu Township at around 5 p.m., leaving the mother, surnamed Lin (林), and her son trapped inside the severely damaged vehicle.
    這輛北向行駛的卡車因不明原因駛入南向車道，於下午 5 點左右在光復鄉台 9 線 238.5 公里處附近撞上一輛轎車，導致姓林的母親與其兒子受困在嚴重毀損的車內。
  • reach v. 到達、抵達
    However, both were without signs of life and were pronounced dead upon reaching the hospital.
    然而，兩人被救出時皆已無生命跡象，送達醫院後宣告死亡。
  • hospitalized adj. 住院治療的
    The truck driver, a 37-year-old man surnamed Lo (羅), had a collapsed lung, cerebral hemorrhage and rib fractures, and was hospitalized.
    卡車司機為一名 37 歲的羅姓男子，有肺塌陷、腦出血與肋骨骨折等傷勢，目前住院治療中。
  • notify v. 通知、告知
    Police said Lin and her son were not local residents, and have notified their family.
    警方表示，林姓女子與其兒子並非當地居民，已通知其家屬。
  • cause n. 原因
    The cause of the crash remains under investigation, police said.
    警方表示，車禍發生的原因仍在調查中。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句表示結果 (S + V..., 分詞片語)
    The northbound truck veered into the southbound lane for some unknown reason and struck the car near the 238.5-kilometer marker on Provincial Highway 9 in Guangfu Township at around 5 p.m., leaving the mother, surnamed Lin (林), and her son trapped inside the severely damaged vehicle.
    句尾的「leaving the mother... trapped...」是由連接詞和子句簡化而來的分詞構句，用來補充說明前面主句所引發的後續結果（留下母子受困在車內的情況）。
  • 過去分詞作後位修飾語
    However, both were without signs of life and were pronounced dead upon reaching the hospital.
  • 介系詞片語簡化時間子句 (upon + V-ing)
    However, both were without signs of life and were pronounced dead upon reaching the hospital.
  • 被動語態 (Be + 過去分詞)
    The cause of the crash remains under investigation, police said.
