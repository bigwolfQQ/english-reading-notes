製造業固定資產採購額增加

Manufacturing sector’s fixed-asset purchases increase

單字解析

  • sector n. 部門、產業、領域
    The manufacturing sector’s fixed-asset purchases, excluding land, increased for the second straight quarter, as firms in the advanced semiconductor processing...
    製造業不含土地的固定資產採購額連續第二季增加，由於先進半導體製程的廠商...
  • exclude v. 排除、不包括
    The manufacturing sector’s fixed-asset purchases, excluding land, increased for the second straight quarter...
    製造業不含土地的固定資產採購額連續第二季增加...
  • expand v. 擴大、擴充
    ...as firms in the advanced semiconductor processing, memory, packaging and testing, and artificial intelligence (AI) server segments continued to expand production...
    ...由於先進半導體製程、記憶體、封裝與測試，以及人工智慧（AI）伺服器等領域的廠商持續擴大產能...
  • equipment n. 設備、裝備
    Fixed assets include machinery, equipment, buildings and construction projects, as well as furniture, fixtures and vehicles.
    固定資產包括機械、設備、建物與營建工程，以及家具、固定裝置與車輛。
  • figure n. 數字、數據
    Last quarter’s figure was up 36.4 percent from the previous quarter, it said.
    報告指出，上季的數據較前一季成長了 36.4%。
  • sales n. 銷售額、營業額
    Meanwhile, total sales by the manufacturing sector, including overseas production, hit NT$1.1 trillion in the second quarter...
    同時，第二季製造業總營業額（包含海外生產）達到新台幣 1.1 兆元...
  • post v. 發布、公布（數據或業績）
    Last quarter, the electronic components industry posted the largest purchases in fixed assets, at NT$775.1 billion.
    上季，電子零組件業公布了最高固定資產採購額，達新台幣 7,751 億元。
  • account for phr. 占（比例）、說明
    The amount is a 71.5 percent increase from a year earlier and accounted for 81.1 percent of total purchases by local manufacturers in the quarter, it said.
    該金額較去年同期增加 71.5%，並占該季本地製造商總採購額的 81.1%。
  • rank v. 排名、列於
    The computer and optoelectronics industry ranked second in purchases, with firms spending NT$25.8 billion...
    電腦及光電產業的採購額排名第二，廠商支出新台幣 258 億元...
  • total v. 總計、總額達
    Overall, the manufacturing sector’s fixed-asset purchases in the first half of the year totaled NT$1.66 trillion...
    總體而言，今年上半年製造業的固定資產採購額總計達新台幣 1.66 兆元...
  • expenditure n. 支出、花費
    For the second half of the year, driven by continued global AI infrastructure development and high capital expenditures by major cloud service providers...
    展望下半年，在持續的全球 AI 基礎設施發展以及主要雲端服務供應商的高額資本支出帶動下...
  • capacity n. 產能、容量
    ...domestic semiconductor, server, related supply chain companies and some traditional industries should continue to expand production capacity, the ministry said.
    經濟部表示，國內半導體、伺服器、相關供應鏈企業及部分傳統產業應會持續擴大產能。
  • investment n. 投資
    Furthermore, the continued net zero transition and energy infrastructure investment trends are also expected to sustain investment momentum in the manufacturing sector, it said.
    該部並表示，此外，持續的淨零轉型與能源 infrastructure 投資趨勢，預期也將維持製造業的投資動能。
  • cautious adj. 謹慎的、小心翼翼的
    However, as geopolitical and trade policy uncertainties continue to affect global economic growth momentum, manufacturers should remain cautious in making investments and expanding production, the ministry added.
    經濟部補充說，不過，由於地緣政治與貿易政策的不確定性持續影響全球經濟成長動能，製造商在進行投資與擴產時應保持謹慎。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後置修飾語（用現在分詞）
    The manufacturing sector’s fixed-asset purchases, excluding land, increased for the second straight quarter, as firms in the advanced semiconductor processing... continued to expand production...
    句中的 "excluding land" 是由現在分詞 "excluding" 引導的分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞片語 "fixed-asset purchases"，相當於形容詞子句 "which exclude land" 的簡化用法，在多益閱讀測驗中非常常見。
  • 表示原因的連接詞 as引導原因子句
    ...increased for the second straight quarter, as firms in the advanced semiconductor processing, memory, packaging and testing, and artificial intelligence (AI) server segments continued to expand production, the ministry said...
    連接詞 "as" 在此用來引導原因子句（意同 because 或 since），說明採購額增加的背後原因，即各個科技領域的廠商持續擴大產能。
  • 對等連接詞 as well as 連接並列項目
    Fixed assets include machinery, equipment, buildings and construction projects, as well as furniture, fixtures and vehicles.
    "as well as" 意為「以及、不但……而且……」，在此用於連接複數名詞項目，用來補充說明固定資產所涵蓋的完整範圍。
  • 過去分詞片語作受詞補語／獨立分詞結構（帶有附帶狀況）
    For the second half of the year, driven by continued global AI infrastructure development and high capital expenditures by major cloud service providers, domestic semiconductor... companies... should continue to expand...
    句首的 "driven by..." 是由過去分詞 "driven" 引導的片語，省略了主詞與be動詞（原句結構為 As it is driven by...），用來修飾後方的整個主句，表示「在……的驅動/帶動下」，是商業英文新聞中經常用來表達原因或背景的精鍊句型。
  • 被動語態表示預期或官方說法
    Furthermore, the continued net zero transition and energy infrastructure investment trends are also expected to sustain investment momentum in the manufacturing sector, it said.
    句尾使用被動語態 "are also expected to sustain..."（預期也能維持……），常應用於新聞報導中客觀引述官方說法或市場預期，避免過於主觀的斷言。
