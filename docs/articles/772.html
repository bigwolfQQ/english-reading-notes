金寶準備開始量產模組

Kinpo prepares to start up module volume production

單字解析

  • aim v. 旨在，目標是
    Kinpo Electronics Inc yesterday said it aims to start volume production of power modules for conventional servers...
    金寶電子昨天表示，其目標是在下個季度開始量產用於傳統伺服器的電源模組...
  • volume production n. phr. 量產
    ...it aims to start volume production of power modules for conventional servers...
    ...其目標是在下個季度開始量產用於傳統伺服器的電源模組...
  • enterprise-level adj. 企業級的
    ...power modules for conventional servers, enterprise-level power supplies and communication power modules...
    ...用於傳統伺服器、企業級電源供應器及通訊電源模組的電源模組...
  • expand v. 擴大，擴展
    ...as well as expand production of standalone and drawer-type power modules for artificial intelligence (AI) servers next quarter.
    ...並擴大生產用於人工智慧（AI）伺服器的獨立式與抽屜式電源模組。
  • mass production n. phr. 大規模生產，量產
    The company plans to enter mass production of second-generation charging pile products next quarter...
    該公司計畫在下個季度進入第二代充電樁產品的量產...
  • subsidiary n. 子公司
    ...which it codeveloped with subsidiary AcBel Polytech Inc (康舒科技), Kinpo chief technology officer Andy Lee (李盛宏) said at an earnings conference in Taipei.
    ...這是他們與子公司康舒科技共同開發的，金寶技術長李盛宏在台北舉行的法說會上表示。
  • earnings conference n. phr. 法人說明會，財報會議
    ...Kinpo chief technology officer Andy Lee (李盛宏) said at an earnings conference in Taipei.
    ...金寶技術長李盛宏在台北舉行的法說會上表示。
  • revenue n. 營收，收益
    ...to expand business scope and increase revenue, the company said.
    ...以擴大業務範圍並增加營收，該公司表示。
  • year-on-year adj. / adv. 與去年同期相比，年增率
    Kinpo’s revenue in the first half of this year fell 5.41 percent year-on-year to NT$75.06 billion...
    金寶今年上半年的營收較去年同期下降5.41%，至75.06億新台幣...
  • account for v. phr. 佔（比例）
    In the first half, consumer electronics accounted for 33.84 percent of the company’s revenue...
    上半年，消費性電子產品佔公司營收的33.84%...
  • net profit n. phr. 淨利
    Net profit was NT$1.05 billion, up 27.7 percent year-on-year...
    淨利為10.5億新台幣，較去年同期成長27.7%...
  • earnings per share n. phr. 每股盈餘（EPS）
    ...while earnings per share rose to NT$0.70 from NT$0.55...
    ...每股盈餘從0.55新台幣上升至0.70新台幣...
  • gross margin n. phr. 毛利率
    ...and gross margin advanced to 6.88 percent from 6.45 percent a year earlier.
    ...且毛利率從去年同期的6.45%提升至6.88%。
  • operate v. 營運，運作
    Outside Taiwan, the company operates 16 plants in Thailand...
    在台灣境外，該公司在泰國營運16座工廠...

文法重點

  • 關係代名詞非限定用法 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    The company plans to enter mass production of second-generation charging pile products next quarter, which it codeveloped with subsidiary AcBel Polytech Inc...
    句中的「which it codeveloped with...」前有逗號，屬於關係代名詞的非限定用法，用來補充說明前面的先行詞（second-generation charging pile products），在翻譯時順順地接在後面補充即可。
  • 分詞構句表附带結果 (Participle Phrase indicating Result)
    Kinpo’s revenue in the first half of this year fell 5.41 percent year-on-year to NT$75.06 billion (US$2.36 billion), weighed down by wearable electronics customers’ seasonal marketing strategies and softer end-market sales...
    句尾的「weighed down by...」是由過去分詞開頭的分詞構句，省略了主詞與be動詞（原意為 which was weighed down by...），用來修飾前面的子句，表示營收下跌的原因或受到的影響。
  • 介詞片語表讓步 (Prepositional Phrase of Concession)
    The increase in net profit and gross margin despite lower revenue reflected the company’s continued investment in higher-value-added businesses...
    片語「despite lower revenue」意為「儘管營收較低」，despite 是介詞，後面直接接名詞短語。這種結構在商業財經報導中非常常見，用來凸顯對比或轉折關係。
