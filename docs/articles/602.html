文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    ...as renewed fighting with the Yemeni government forces forced growing numbers of civilians to flee.
    句中的 "growing" 是現在分詞，置於名詞 "numbers" 前方作形容詞使用，用來修飾並強調數量「正在增加的」動態過程。
  • 關係代間詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    ...weapons depots and command and control centers that are managing the aggression against our nation and people...
    由關係代間詞 "that" 引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾先行詞 "weapons depots and command and control centers"，補充說明這些設施正在執行管理侵略行徑的功能。
  • 非限定性關係子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Clashes have increased last week between the Iran-allied Houthis and Yemeni government forces, which are backed by Riyadh.
    句尾的 ", which are backed by Riyadh" 是非限定性關係子句，前面帶有逗號，用來補充說明前面的先行詞 "Yemeni government forces"（葉門政府軍）背後受到利雅德的支持，刪除後不影響主要子句的完整性。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    ...but that the request was rejected.
    句型結構為 "be + 過去分詞"（was rejected），用來強調動作的承受者（the request 請求），而不需要特別指出拒絕請求的人是誰。
