單字解析

  • due to 片語介詞 由於、因為
    Noah Lyles, the reigning Olympic men’s 100-meter champion, said on Friday that he has ended this year’s season due to injury flare-ups only a week before the World Athletics Ultimate Championship.
    新科奧運男子100公尺金牌得主諾亞·萊爾斯於週五表示，由於傷勢復發，他在世界田徑終極錦標賽開賽前僅剩一週之際，已結束今年的賽季。
  • compete 動詞 競爭、參賽
    The 29-year-old American, a four-time world 200m champion who also won the 100m world title in 2023 at Budapest, will not compete at the major meet on Sept. 11 to 13 in Budapest.
    這位29歲的美國選手曾四度奪得世界200公尺冠軍，並於2023年在布達佩斯拿下100公尺世界冠軍，將不會參加9月11日至13日在布達佩斯舉行的這場重大賽事。
  • unfortunately 副詞 不幸地、遺憾地
    “Unfortunately, I come with some not great news.
    「不幸的是，我帶來了一些不太好的消息。」
  • establish 動詞 建立、設立
    Cycling’s governing body plans to establish rules for real-time tracking of riders and vehicles at professional road races following the death of British rider Finlay Tarling, the International Cycling Union (UCI) said on Friday.
    國際自由車總會（UCI）於週五表示，在英國車手芬利·塔林不幸身亡後，自行車運動的管理機構計劃針對職業公路賽事的車手及車輛即時追蹤建立新規則。
  • appeal 動詞 / 名詞 上訴、吸引力（此處為名詞/動詞上訴）
    Pierre Gasly lost the podium to Isack Hadjar for the second time after penalties from the Monaco Grand Prix were reinstated following a successful appeal by two Formula 1 teams.
    在兩個一級方程式車隊提出上訴並獲得勝訴後，摩納哥大獎賽的罰單恢復執行，皮埃爾·加斯利因此再次將頒獎台席位拱手讓給伊薩克·哈賈爾。
  • original 形容詞 原本的、最初的
    The decision to reinstate the original finish order was announced on Friday.
    恢復原始完賽順序的決定於週五宣布。
  • victory 名詞 勝利
    Hooker Mamoru Harada scored two tries as Japan warmed up for the start of the Pacific Nations Cup next week with a comprehensive 57-12 victory over Canada in Niigata yesterday.
    鉤球員原田衛達陣得分兩次，日本隊昨日在新潟以57比12的大比分痛宰加拿大隊，為下週即將開打的太平洋國家盃暖身。
  • formation 名詞 組成、成立、隊形
    Celebrating 100 years since the formation of the Japan Rugby Football Union in 1926, the home side scored nine tries in all as they dominated the forward battle and were able to find space for their electric backs.
    為了慶祝日本橄欖球協會於1926年成立100週年，主場作戰的日本隊全場共達陣9次，完全主宰了前鋒對抗，並為他們速度極快的後衛群找到進攻空間。
  • numerous 形容詞 許多的、為數眾多的
    It was by no means perfect from Japan, who had numerous handling errors and were at times put under pressure in the scrum.
    日本隊的表現絕非完美，他們出現了許多的傳接球失誤，在爭球時也一度承受壓力。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Celebrating 100 years since the formation of the Japan Rugby Football Union in 1926, the home side scored nine tries in all...
    句首的 "Celebrating..." 是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明主詞 "the home side"（主場球隊/日本隊）同時正在進行的動作，相當於副詞子句 "As they celebrated..." 的簡化。
  • 非限定關代子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Canada, who are also to take part in the Pacific Nations Cup, were outgunned up front...
    逗號後的 ", who are also to take part in..." 是一個非限定關係代名詞子句，對先行詞 "Canada" 做補充說明，提供額外的背景資訊，若拿掉此子句並不影響主要句意。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...penalties from the Monaco Grand Prix were reinstated following a successful appeal by two Formula 1 teams.
