文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    A New Taipei pharmacist accused of spiking his university student employee's water with a benzodiazepine-class drug was detained on suspicion of attempted sexual assault on Sunday.
    句中的「accused of...」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「A New Taipei pharmacist」，相當於形容詞子句「who was accused of...」，省略了關係代名詞與be動詞，使句子更為精簡緊湊，是多益閱讀測驗中常見的句構。
  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況 (Participial Phrase indicating Circumstance)
    Speaking with local media, Chen admitted to putting the drug in the employee's drink, but insisted it had been "a joke,"...
    句首的「Speaking with local media」是由現在分詞「Speaking」引導的分詞構句，用來補充說明主句主語 Chen 在坦承犯行時的背景狀況，還原成完整子句相當於「When he spoke with local media」。
  • 被動語態的運用 (Passive Voice)
    Authorities were alerted to the case by a recent social media post, in which the alleged victim's sibling accused Chen of drugging her.
    句中的「were alerted」使用了被動語態（be + 過去分詞），強調當局是「被」一則社群媒體貼文所驚動/引起注意。在新聞英語中，當動作的承受者比施事者更受關注時，經常會使用被動語態。
