賴清德於國慶演說中概述加重毒駕罰則的計畫

Lai outlines plans for tougher drug-driving penalties in National Day address

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • enforce v. 實施，執行（法律、規定等）
    The government will strictly enforce the law and show zero tolerance, Lai said in his address outside the Presidential Office.
    賴在總統府外發表演說時表示，政府將嚴格執法，零容忍。
  • amendment n. 修正案，修改
    Under proposed legal amendments, first-time offenders caught driving under the influence of drugs will face fines of up to NT$150,000...
    根據擬議中的法律修正案，首次被抓到吸毒後駕駛的違規者將面臨最高新台幣15萬元的罰款...
  • offender n. 罪犯，違規者
    Repeat offenders will face unlimited fines, license revocation and vehicle forfeiture.
    累犯將面臨無上限罰款、吊銷駕照以及沒收車輛。
  • revocation n. 撤銷，吊銷
    Repeat offenders will face unlimited fines, license revocation and vehicle forfeiture.
    累犯將面臨無上限罰款、吊銷駕照以及沒收車輛。
  • allowance n. 津貼，補助
    Turning to Taiwan's declining birthrate, Lai said the government plans to introduce a monthly allowance of NT$5,000 (US$156) starting next year for every child from birth until age 18.
    談到台灣出生率下降的問題，賴清德表示，政府計畫從明年開始，針對從出生到18歲的每個孩子，推出每月新台幣5,000元（約156美元）的津貼。
  • assistance n. 協助，援助
    The allowance will be accompanied by expanded childcare services, more family-friendly workplaces and housing assistance, he added.
    他補充說，這項津貼將伴隨擴大托育服務、更多對家庭友善的工作場所以及居住協助。
  • safeguard v. 維護，保護，捍衛
    To support a family is to safeguard Taiwan's future...
    支持家庭就是捍衛台灣的未來...
  • expand v. 擴大，擴展
    On housing, Lai said the government will continue its program supporting 1 million renter households by expanding supply and providing rental assistance.
    在居住方面，賴清德表示，政府將透過擴大供給和提供租金補貼，持續推動支援100萬戶租屋家庭的計畫。
  • pledge v. 保證，承諾
    Lai pledged to advance gender equality, noting that Taiwan was the first country in Asia to legalize same-sex marriage.
    賴清德承諾推動性別平等，並指出台灣是亞洲第一個同性婚姻合法的國家。
  • urge v. 敦促，力勸
    With local elections approaching, Lai urged voters to choose capable leaders and resist threats and inducements.
    隨著地方選舉逼近，賴清德敦促選民選擇有能力的領導人，並抵制威脅與利誘。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附帶狀況或結果狀語
    President Lai Ching-te outlined plans Saturday to toughen penalties for drug-impaired driving, as part of a National Day address setting out measures to strengthen public safety and support families.
    句尾的 "setting out measures..." 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 address，表示該演說「設定了/提出了」加強公共安全與支持家庭的措施，相當於形容詞子句 "which set out measures..." 的簡化。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    Under proposed legal amendments, first-time offenders caught driving under the influence of drugs will face fines of up to NT$150,000...
    片語 "caught driving under the influence of drugs" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "offenders"。完整結構為 "who are caught driving..."，省略了關係代名詞與be動詞，使句子更為精簡。
  • 獨立分詞構句（分詞帶有自己的主詞）
    With local elections approaching, Lai urged voters to choose capable leaders and resist threats and inducements.
    此句使用 "With + 名詞 + 現在分詞 (approaching)" 的結構，用來表達附帶狀況或時間背景，意思是「隨著地方選舉逼近」，在多益閱讀測驗與商業寫作中非常常 考。
