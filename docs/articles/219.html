數千人以生日快樂歌向法國機械大象遊行致敬

Thousands serenade French mechanical elephant parade with birthday song

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • spectator n. 觀眾、旁觀者（尤指觀看戶外活動或體育賽事）
    Tens of thousands of spectators gathered in Taipei on Sunday to serenade a street parade of mechanical elephants...
    數萬名觀眾周日在台北聚集，向一場機械大象街頭遊行獻唱生日快樂歌...
  • presentation n. 演出、呈現、發表
    ...marking the 100th show of the act.
    藉此慶祝該表演活動的第100場演出。
  • destination n. 目的地、終點
    A crowd from about 50,000 gathered at the destination of the "Trois éléphants passent ..." parade in the city's Shilin District...
    約有5萬名群眾聚集在「大象逛大街」遊行位於該市士林區的終點站...
  • feature v. 以……為特色、主演、由……組成
    The street theater presentation featured three mechanical elephants, each 4.5 meters tall and 8 meters long.
    這場街頭劇場表演以三隻機械大象為主體，每隻大象高4.5公尺、長8公尺。
  • combine v. 結合、聯合
    It also included cultural fusion elements that combined classical music, Breton folk tunes, and street percussion...
    表演中也融入了文化融合元素，結合古典音樂、布列塔尼民謠，以及街頭敲擊樂...
  • highlight v. 突顯、強調
    The 100th presentation of the show on Sunday highlighted all the locations the act had visited around the world...
    這場表演周日迎來第100場演出，並特別標示了該表演曾在全球造訪過的所有地點...
  • attend v. 出席、參加
    According to the TPAC, about 100,000 people attended the show on the streets of Shilin District on the opening night Saturday...
    根據北藝中心表示，周六開幕夜當天約有10萬人參與了士林區街頭的演出...
  • attract v. 吸引
    The two-day parade also streamed live, attracting 220,000 viewers and 6,000 comments...
    這為期兩天的遊行也進行了網路直播，吸引了22万名觀眾以及6,000則留言...
  • deploy v. 部署、調動（人員或裝備）
    The TPAC said some 800 police officers and volunteer traffic police were deployed for the show...
    北藝中心表示，為了這場演出，大約部署了800名警力與交通義警...
  • accommodate v. 容納、為……提供空間/住宿
    On the two nights of the show, the Taipei Metro system increased services on the Tamsui-Xinyi line to accommodate the increased number of passengers, the TPAC said.
    北藝中心表示，在這兩天的演出期間，台北捷運系統增加了淡水信義線的班次，以容納暴增的旅運乘客。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    Tens of thousands of spectators gathered in Taipei on Sunday to serenade a street parade of mechanical elephants, presented by a French performing arts group, marking the 100th show of the act.
    句中的 "presented by a French performing arts group" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的 "mechanical elephants"，相當於關代子句 "which was presented by..."。而句尾的 ", marking the 100th show of the act" 則是現在分詞片語作結果狀語，表示前面整件事情所帶來的結果（標誌著第100場演出）。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause with Preposition)
    The 100th presentation of the show on Sunday highlighted all the locations the act had visited around the world, including Taipei, some of which were featured on the exterior design of one of the giant mechanical elephants.
    句中的 "the act had visited" 是省略了關代 (that/which) 的限定子句，修飾 "locations"。此外，"some of which were featured..." 則是「代名詞 + 介系詞片語」引導的非限定關係子句，用來補充說明前面的 Taipei 與所有造訪過的地點，which 代替前面的 locations。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The TPAC said some 800 police officers and volunteer traffic police were deployed for the show...
    此句使用了被動語態 "were deployed"（被部署），因為警察和交通義警是「被」派駐到現場執行勤務的，而非主動去部署別人。主詞是承受動作的對象時，常使用「be + 過去分詞」結構。
