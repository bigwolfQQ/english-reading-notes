    Taiwanese wushu athlete Johnny Sun (孫家閎) won silver in the men's taijiquan-taijijian event at the 2026 Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games on Monday...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附加說明（補充結果）
    ...securing Taiwan's first wushu medal at this year's Games.
    句尾的「securing...」是由對等子句簡化而來的現在分詞構句，用來補充說明主句動作所產生的自然結果（在此指奪得銀牌「進而為台灣拿下第一面獎牌」）。
  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense)
    Sun said he had missed important moments in his son's childhood while preparing for the previous Asian Games...
    句中的「had missed」表示「過去的過去」。因為孫家閎「說」這句話是在過去，而「錯過」兒子成長的時間點發生在說話之前更早的亞運備戰期間，因此使用過去完成式。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾
    The two disciplines were combined into a single medal event at the 2022 Hangzhou Asian Games and the 2026 Aichi-Nagoya Games, after being contested separately at some previous international competitions.
    介系詞片語「after」後面接了動名詞的被動語態「being contested」，用來修飾並說明在過去某些國際賽事中「被分開比試」的情況。
