集邦科技表示：全球電網將面臨艱苦挑戰

Global power grid to struggle, TrendForce says

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • demand n. 需求
    Despite rapid growth in data center power demand, global grid capacity has failed to keep pace, market researcher TrendForce Corp said yesterday.
    市場研究機構集邦科技昨天表示，儘管資料中心的電力需求快速成長，但全球電網容量卻未能跟上腳步。
  • capacity n. 容量、產能、能力
    Despite rapid growth in data center power demand, global grid capacity has failed to keep pace, market researcher TrendForce Corp said yesterday.
    市場研究機構集邦科技昨天表示，儘管資料中心的電力需求快速成長，但全球電網容量卻未能跟上腳步。
  • forecast v. / n. 預測、預估
    Global data center power demand is forecast to grow 31 percent to 161 gigawatts (GW) this year from 122.9GW last year
    今年全球資料中心電力需求預估將從去年的 122.9 百萬瓩（GW）成長 31%，達到 161 GW
  • statement n. 聲明、陳述
    the Taipei-based researcher said in a statement.
    這家總部設於台北的研究機構在聲明中表示。
  • attribute to phr. v. 將……歸因於
    TrendForce attributed the growth to rising power demand from general servers, artificial intelligence servers, other information technology equipment
    集邦科技將這項成長歸因於一般伺服器、人工智慧伺服器、其他資訊科技設備不斷攀升的電力需求
  • sufficient adj. 足夠的、充分的
    The researcher said grid deliverability was sufficient to meet growing data center power demand through last year
    該研究機構表示，直到去年為止，電網的輸送能力還足以應付不斷成長的資料中心電力需求
  • markedly adv. 顯著地、明顯地
    with the gap expected to widen markedly after 2028, it said.
    並表示預期這個差距在 2028 年之後將顯著擴大。
  • total v. / n. 總計、總數
    while grid capacity available to data centers would total 222.6GW, it said.
    而可供資料中心使用的電網容量總計則為 222.6 GW。
  • facility n. 設施、設備
    operators’ self-built power supplies and existing facilities are expected to meet a significant share of demand through 2028
    業者自建的電源供應與現有設施預期能滿足截至 2028 年為止相當大比例的需求
  • delay n. / v. 延誤、延遲
    However, grid-delivery delays are expected to become more pronounced from 2028
    不過，預期電網輸送延誤的情況從 2028 年起將更加明顯

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    the Taipei-based researcher said in a statement.
    句中的「Taipei-based」是過去分詞形成的複合形容詞，放在名詞「researcher」前面作修飾，用來簡化形容詞子句（原意為 which is based in Taipei）。
  • 被動語態的應用 (Passive Voice)
    Global data center power demand is forecast to grow 31 percent...
    句型使用「is forecast to...」，為被動語態結構，常出現在新聞英語中客觀報導預測、預估的數據或未來趨勢。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Nominative Absolute Construction)
    Assuming all other electricity demand is fully met, the gap would still total 268GW, it added.
    句首的「Assuming」在此作為分詞引導的條件句（相當於 If we assume...），省略了主詞，用來表達假設的情況。
  • with + 受詞 + 形容詞/分詞 (With-construction)
    with the gap expected to widen markedly after 2028, it said.
    「with + 名詞 (the gap) + 過去分詞 (expected)」構成附帶狀況子句，用來補充說明前面子句發生時同時存在的狀態或結果。
