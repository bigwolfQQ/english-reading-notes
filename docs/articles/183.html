中國將在國是訪問期間帶領企業高管前往華盛頓

China to bring business execs to DC during state visit

單字解析

  • delegation n. 代表團
    Chinese President Xi Jinping is preparing a large business delegation to accompany him on his visit to Washington...
    中國國家主席習近平正準備籌組一個大型商業代表團陪同他訪問華盛頓...
  • accompany v. 陪同，伴隨
    ...is preparing a large business delegation to accompany him on his visit to Washington...
    ...正準備籌組一個大型商業代表團陪同他訪問華盛頓...
  • investment n. 投資
    ...an unusual move given US skepticism toward Chinese investment and Beijing’s strained ties with private enterprise.
    ...考慮到美國對中國投資的懷疑態度以及北京與私營企業緊張的關係，這是一個不尋常的舉動。
  • enterprise n. 企業，公司
    ...and Beijing’s strained ties with private enterprise.
    ...以及北京與私營企業緊張的關係。
  • corporate adj. 公司的，企業的
    Xi rarely travels abroad with corporate executives, many of whom fell from of favor after Beijing’s regulatory crackdowns...
    習近平很少帶著企業高管出國訪問，其中許多人在北京的監管打擊後失寵了...
  • executive n. 行政主管，高階主管
    Xi rarely travels abroad with corporate executives...
    習近平很少帶著企業高管出國訪問...
  • summit n. 高峰會，峰會
    The business delegation for the Sept. 24 summit has not been reported before.
    這個將於9月24日舉行的峰會商業代表團此前未被報導過。
  • agreement n. 協議，協定
    During that visit, China signed a US$38 billion agreement for 300 Boeing Co aircraft...
    在那次訪問期間，中國簽署了價值380億美元、購買300架波音公司飛機的協議...
  • access n. 進入權，通道，機會
    ...seeking greater access to Chinese markets.
    ...尋求更多進入中國市場的管道。
  • deliverables n. 具體成果，交付成果
    ...the US-China Board of Investment is unlikely to produce significant deliverables at the summit...
    ...美中投資委員會不太可能在峰會上產生重大具體成果...

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作補充說明
    The move is aimed in part at signaling China’s willingness to support investment and commercial ties with the US, while offering the White House some possible economic wins ahead of midterm elections...
    句尾的「while offering...」是由連接詞 while 引導的分詞構句，省略了主語與be動詞（while [it is] offering），用來表達伴隨發生的動作或情況，意指「同時提供...」。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    Three separate sources briefed on the matter said the US-China Board of Investment is unlikely to produce significant deliverables...
    片語「briefed on the matter」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「sources」（消息人士）。原意相當於形容詞子句「who were briefed on the matter」（被告知此事內情的），化繁為簡以精煉新聞語氣。
  • 形容詞子句（關係代名詞所有格 whose 的變化）
    Xi rarely travels abroad with corporate executives, many of whom fell from of favor after Beijing’s regulatory crackdowns...
    句型「many of whom」是多益常見的關係子句結構，用逗號隔開做非限定用法。whom 代替前面的 corporate executives，用來指「這些高管中的許多人」，補充說明他們失寵的背景。
