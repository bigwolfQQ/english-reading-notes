台泥通過收購烏克蘭水泥生產商及其他實體計畫

TCC approves plan to acquire Ukrainian cement producer, other entities

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • acquire v. 收購、取得
    Taipei, Sept. 23 (CNA) TCC Group Holdings' board of directors has approved a plan for the company's Dutch subsidiary to acquire a 100 percent stake in Ukrainian cement company Ivano-Frankivskcement (IFCEM) and three other companies for up to €750 million (US$857 million).
    台北，9月23日（中央社）——台泥企業團董事會已通過一項計畫，由該公司的荷蘭子公司以最高7.5億歐元（約8.57億美元）收購烏克蘭水泥公司Ivano-Frankivskcement（IFCEM）及其他三家公司的100%股權。
  • subsidiary n. 子公司

  • transaction n. 交易、業務
    The transaction also covers roofing materials company Ivano-Frankivsk-Dakh, gypsum products maker KRU Gips and dry-mix mortar producer KRU Mix, TCC Group Holdings Vice President Shelly Yeh (葉毓君) said at a press conference Wednesday.
    台泥企業團副總經理葉毓君在週三的記者會上表示，該交易還涵蓋屋頂材料公司Ivano-Frankivsk-Dakh、石膏製品製造商KRU Gips以及乾拌砂漿生產商KRU Mix。
  • finalize v. 定案、完成（協議或計畫）
    The final acquisition price will be adjusted when the deal is finalized based on factors such as net debt and net working capital, Yeh said.
    葉毓君指出，最終的收購價格將在交易完成時，根據淨負債和淨營運資金等因素進行調整。
  • regulatory adj. 監管的、管理規則的
    The plan, approved at a special board meeting Tuesday, is still subject to regulatory approvals in Taiwan, Ukraine and other relevant jurisdictions before the transaction can be completed, Yeh cautioned.
    葉毓君特別提醒，這項於週二臨時董事會上通過的計畫，在交易完成前仍須獲得台灣、烏克蘭及其他相關管轄機構的監管批准。
  • operation n. 營運、運作
    In pursuing the acquisition, the company is hoping to extend its European cement operations from Southern and Western Europe into Eastern Europe and position itself to participate in Ukraine's long-term reconstruction once the war ends.
    台泥在尋求這項收購時，希望將其歐洲水泥業務從南歐和西歐擴展至東歐，並為自己在戰爭結束後參與烏克蘭的長期重建做好準備。
  • market share n. 市場佔有率
    IFCEM represents a strong vehicle through which to expand its European presence, with a roughly 36 percent market share in Ukraine and a sales network that extends mainly to Poland and Romania but also to Moldova, Slovakia and Hungary, TCC said.
    台泥表示，IFCEM是擴大其歐洲版圖的強大載體，在烏克蘭擁有約36%的市佔率，其銷售網路主要延伸至波蘭和羅馬尼亞，亦涵蓋摩爾達瓦、斯洛伐克和匈牙利。
  • revenue n. 收益、營業收入
    According to IFCEM's 2025 annual financial report, revenue rose 20.3 percent year-on-year to UAH 16.25 billion (US$362.41 million), while gross profit climbed 40.3 percent to UAH 6.44 billion.
    根據IFCEM的2025年度財務報告，營收年增20.3%達到162.5億格里夫納（約3.6241億美元），而毛利則攀升40.3%至6.44億格里夫納。
  • financial adviser n. 財務顧問
    TCC said it has appointed Morgan Stanley as financial adviser for the transaction, while international legal, accounting, tax and environmental consultants have conducted due diligence covering sanctions, labor, operations and foreign exchange controls.
    台泥表示，已委任摩根士丹利（Morgan Stanley）擔任該交易的財務顧問，同時國際法律、會計、稅務和環境顧問已進行了涵蓋制裁、勞動、營運及外匯管制的盡職調查。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作為後置修飾語 (Present/Past Participle Phrases as Post-modifiers)
    The plan, approved at a special board meeting Tuesday, is still subject to regulatory approvals in Taiwan, Ukraine and other relevant jurisdictions before the transaction can be completed...
    過去分詞片語「approved at a special board meeting Tuesday」在此作為後置修飾語，用來修飾前面的主詞「The plan」。其完整結構原本是主動或被動形容詞子句（which was approved at...），經過簡化（省略關代與be動詞）後形成精簡的分詞片語，常出現在商業新聞和正式合約中。
  • 介系詞加關係代名詞的複合結構 (Preposition + Relative Pronoun)
    IFCEM represents a strong vehicle through which to expand its European presence...
    句中的「through which」引導一個形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的先行詞「vehicle」（此處指載體或工具）。介系詞「through」放在關係代名詞「which」之前，表示「透過這個載體來擴展……」，這是正式英文寫作中非常道地且多益閱讀常考的句構。
  • 對等連接詞連接的對比或並列子句 (Coordinating Conjunctions for Contrast/Parallelism)
    According to IFCEM's 2025 annual financial report, revenue rose 20.3 percent year-on-year to UAH 16.25 billion (US$362.41 million), while gross profit climbed 40.3 percent to UAH 6.44 billion.
    連接詞「while」在此處連接兩個對等的子句，用來表達「同時」或「然而（對比）」的概念。前半句描述營收成長，後半句則並列報導毛利的攀升，使財報數據的陳述更加流暢且具比較性。
