單字解析

  • sentenced v. 宣判、判刑
    A former Catholic bishop was yesterday sentenced to six years and six months in prison for sexually abusing two young indigenous men.
    一名天主教前主教昨天因性侵兩名年輕原住民男子，被判處六年六個月有期徒刑。
  • convicted v. 定罪、宣判有罪
    A District Court of Western Australia state jury convicted Christopher Saunders, 76, in August of 13 out of 19 charges of sexual abuse...
    西澳省地方法院陪審團於八月裁定，現年76歲的克里斯托弗·桑德斯在19起性虐待指控中，其中13起罪名成立...
  • eligible adj. 符合條件的、有資格的
    Saunders would become eligible for parole in February 2031.
    桑德斯將於2031年2月符合假釋資格。
  • acquitted v. 宣告無罪、無罪釋放
    Saunders was acquitted of six charges involving a third accuser.
    桑德斯另涉及第三名指控者的六項指控則獲判無罪。
  • postponed v. 延期、延後
    The government has indefinitely postponed elections due to be held later this year, officials said on Tuesday.
    官員於週二表示，政府已無限期延後原定於今年稍後舉行的選舉。
  • statement n. 聲明、陳述
    “The period originally scheduled for the electoral campaign has been postponed,” the Provisional Electoral Council said in a statement...
    「原定於競選活動的期間已被延後，」臨時選舉委員會在聲明中表示...
  • timetable n. 時間表、時刻表
    ...adding that the timetable “will be made public as soon as possible.”
    ...並補充說時間表「將盡快公佈」。
  • registration n. 登記、註冊
    However, it added that voter registration is continuing.
    不過，它補充表示，選民登記工作仍在持續進行中。
  • stabilizing v. 使穩定、穩定
    Holding elections has been seen as a crucial step to stabilizing the government and tackling rampant gang crime.
    舉行選舉被視為穩定政府以及打擊猖獗幫派犯罪的關鍵一步。
  • operating v. 營運、運作、經營
    Police in Ho Chi Minh City arrested 12 men suspected of operating a citywide ring that stole and resold about 2,700 cats for meat, state media said.
    國家媒體報導，胡志明市警方逮捕了12名男子，他們涉嫌營運一個全市性的犯罪集團，該集團盜竊並轉售約2,700隻貓來製成貓肉。
  • facility n. 設施、場所
    Investigators said Binh bought stolen cats, kept them at a facility in the city’s north and resold them for meat.
    調查人員表示，賓收購被竊的貓隻，將其飼養在該市北部的一處設施中，然後轉售做為肉品。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後置修飾（分詞構句/形容詞片語）
    Four charges involving a child younger than 13 were dropped on the first day of Saunders’ three-week trial.
    句中的「involving a child younger than 13」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「Four charges」（四項指控），相當於關代子句「which involved a child younger than 13」，在多益閱讀測驗中常用於簡化句型。
  • be due to + V（預定要……）
    The government has indefinitely postponed elections due to be held later this year, officials said on Tuesday.
    「due to」在此處接原形動詞「be」，形成「due to be held」（預定要舉行），用來修飾前面的名詞「elections」。在商業與多益考試中，「due to」除了常作「由於」解之外，也常作「預定、到期」解。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The first round of presidential and legislative elections was set for Dec. 13, and the second round for Feb. 21 next year.
    句型使用「was set for」（被安排在 / 定於），採用了主詞 + be動詞 + 過去分詞的被動語態結構，強調選舉日期是由官方安排決定的。這是多益考試中非常基礎且頻繁出現的語法結構。
  • 分詞作形容詞（過去分詞修飾名詞）
    Eva Marie Saint, the pale, willowy actor who won an Academy Award for her first movie, On the Waterfront, and starred opposite Cary Grant in the Alfred Hitchcock thriller North by Northwest, has died.
    句中結合了關係代間子句與形容詞，其中「starred opposite Cary Grant...」是過去分詞片語引導的修飾成分，在新聞英語中常省略「who starred」，直接用過去分詞或動詞片語來增加句子的緊湊感。
