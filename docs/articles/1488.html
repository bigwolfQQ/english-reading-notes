  • contributions n. 貢獻
    Lai expressed his gratitude to the personnel involved in the mission for their contributions to diplomatic ties between the two countries.
    賴總統向參與該任務的人員表達謝意，感謝他們對兩國外交關係的貢獻。

    同時，恩史瓦帝國王感謝台灣捐贈直升機並協助培訓史瓦帝尼的飛行員。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Visiting King Mswati III of Eswatini on Sunday thanked Taiwan for its previous donation of military helicopters to the African kingdom as he toured an aviation training center in Tainan accompanied by President Lai Ching-te (賴清德).
    句尾的「accompanied by President Lai Ching-te」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的主詞 King Mswati III，相當於副詞子句「as he was accompanied by President Lai Ching-te」，省略了主代名詞與be動詞，使語氣更為精煉。
  • 非限定關代子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    The helicopters, which were formally handed over to the Umbutfo eSwatini Defence Force (UEDF) Air Wing, have already played a crucial role...
    句中的「which were formally handed over to...」是由逗號隔開的非限定關係子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞 The helicopters。此類子句拿掉後，主句意思依然完整，主要提供額外的背景資訊。
  • 形容詞子句修飾先行詞 (Adjective Clause modifying a Preceding Noun)
    ...including transporting patients who are unable to reach medical facilities on their own or require urgent medical attention, the king said.
    「who are unable to reach...」是由關係代名詞 who 引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的先行詞 patients（病人），詳細說明這些病人的特徵。
