資料勾稽旨在揪出醫療上不適任的駕駛

Data checks aim to identify medically unfit drivers

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • identify v. 識別、辨識、確認
    Taiwan plans to allow road authorities to use health data to identify drivers who might no longer be medically fit to hold a license, the government said yesterday.
    政府昨天表示，台灣計畫允許公路主管機關利用健康資料，來識別可能不再適合持有駕照的駕駛。
  • proposal n. 提案、建議
    The proposal follows amendments to the Road Traffic Management and Penalty Act (道路交通管理處罰條例) promulgated in November last year.
    這項提案是繼去年 11 月公布《道路交通管理處罰條例》修正案之後所提出的。
  • periodically adv. 定期地、周期性地
    Under the plan, road authorities would periodically obtain data on the three conditions held by government agencies, including information collected from people applying for certain government subsidies or insurance programs.
    根據該計畫，公路主管機關將定期取得政府機關所掌握關於這三種狀況的資料，包括從申請特定政府補助或保險計畫之民眾所收集的資訊。
  • subsidy n. 補助金、津貼
    Under the plan, road authorities would periodically obtain data on the three conditions held by government agencies, including information collected from people applying for certain government subsidies or insurance programs.
    根據該計畫，公路主管機關將定期取得政府機關所掌握關於這三種狀況的資料，包括從申請特定政府補助或保險計畫之民眾所收集的資訊。
  • program n. 計畫、方案
    Under the plan, road authorities would periodically obtain data on the three conditions held by government agencies, including information collected from people applying for certain government subsidies or insurance programs.
    根據該計畫，公路主管機關將定期取得政府機關所掌握關於這三種狀況的資料，包括從申請特定政府補助或保險計畫之民眾所收集的資訊。
  • surrender v. 交出、放棄、投降
    Those who fail would have to surrender their licenses.
    未通過者將必須繳回駕照。
  • effect n. 效力、影響、效果（片語 take effect 意為「生效、實施」）
    About 47,000 drivers could be affected by the system, which is expected to take effect by the end of this year, the ministry said.
    交通部長指出，大約有 47,000 名駕駛可能會受到該系統影響，預計該系統將於今年底前上路。
  • renew v. 換新、更新、展延（如換發駕照、護照、合約等）
    Currently, such checks might not occur until drivers renew their licenses.
    目前，這類查核可能要等到駕駛換發駕照時才會進行。
  • amendment n. 修正案、修改
    The proposal follows amendments to the Road Traffic Management and Penalty Act (道路交通管理處罰條例) promulgated in November last year.
    這項提案是繼去年 11 月公布《道路交通管理處罰條例》修正案之後所提出的。
  • require v. 要求、規定
    Those amendments require drivers to undergo a new qualification review if changes in their physical or mental condition mean they no longer meet the standards required to drive.
    該等修正案要求，若駕駛的身心狀況變化意味著他們不再符合駕駛所需的標準，則必須接受新的資格審查。
  • regulation n. 規章、法規
    The proposed regulations set out what data government agencies would be permitted to provide and how authorities could collect, process and use them.
    擬議中的法規規定了政府機關獲准提供哪些資料，以及主管機關如何收集、處理和使用這些資料。
  • limit v. 限制、限定（常用被動語態 be limited to）
    The information collected would be limited to visual function, epilepsy and moderate or severe dementia, in accordance with the principle of data minimization, the ministry said.
    交通部表示，根據資料最小化原則，所收集的資訊將僅限於視力功能、癲癇以及中度或重度失智症。
  • certificate n. 證書、證明書
    Under current regulations, people with epilepsy can apply for a driver’s license if they provide a doctor’s certificate showing they have been free of seizures for at least two years.
    根據現行法規，癲癇患者若能提供醫師證明顯示其至少兩年未發作，即可申請駕照。

文法重點

  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    Taiwan plans to allow road authorities to use health data to identify drivers who might no longer be medically fit to hold a license, the government said yesterday.
    句中的「who might no longer be medically fit to hold a license」是由關係代名詞 who 引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的先行詞 drivers，補充說明這些駕駛的特徵。
  • 被動語態（Passive Voice）
    Drivers identified as potentially no longer fit to drive would be asked to undergo a new qualification review.
    此句使用了被動語態「would be asked」，強調動作的承受者（即被識別出的駕駛）而非動作的發動者，這在新聞英語與商業合約、公告中非常常見。
  • 非限定性關係子句（補充說明）
    About 47,000 drivers could be affected by the system, which is expected to take effect by the end of this year, the ministry said.
    句中的「which is expected to take effect by the end of this year」前方帶有逗號，屬於非限定性關係子句，用來補充說明前面整套系統的預計上路時程，不能省略。
  • 條件句與假設語氣應用
    Those amendments require drivers to undergo a new qualification review if changes in their physical or mental condition mean they no longer meet the standards required to drive.
    句中的「if changes in their physical or mental condition mean...」為條件副詞子句，用來表達在何種前提或狀況發生時，法規所規範的動作（require...）將會被觸發。
  • 分詞作後置修飾語
    The information collected would be limited to visual function, epilepsy and moderate or severe dementia...
    句中的「collected」是過去分詞，放在名詞「The information」後面作為後置修飾語，相當於「The information that is collected」的簡化寫法，省略了「that is」。
