官員表示：中國正破壞太平洋國家討論

China disrupting Pacific nations’ discussions: official

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • disrupt v. 擾亂，破壞，打斷
    China’s continued efforts to inject geopolitical rivalry into the region and disrupt Pacific nations’ discussions of regional issues warrant greater international attention, a foreign affairs official said yesterday.
    一名外交官員昨天表示，中國持續企圖將地緣政治競爭注入該地區，並破壞太平洋國家對區域議題的討論，這值得國際社會給予更多關注。
  • warrant v. 使有必要，正當，保證
    China’s continued efforts to inject geopolitical rivalry into the region and disrupt Pacific nations’ discussions of regional issues warrant greater international attention, a foreign affairs official said yesterday.
    一名外交官員昨天表示，中國持續企圖將地緣政治競爭注入該地區，並破壞太平洋國家對區域議題的討論，這值得國際社會給予更多關注。
  • delegation n. 代表團
    Minister of Foreign Affairs Lin Chia-lung (林佳龍) last week led a delegation to diplomatic ally Palau to attend events related to the 55th Pacific Islands Forum (PIF) summit.
    外交部長林佳龍上週率領代表團前往邦交國帛琉，出席與第55屆太平洋島國論壇（PIF）高峰會相關的活動。
  • absence n. 缺席，不在
    However, the absence of some member-state leaders has been interpreted by some people as a sign of fracturing in Pacific unity.
    然而，部分成員國領導人的缺席，被有些人解讀為太平洋團結出現裂痕的徵兆。
  • interpret v. 解釋，解讀，口譯
    However, the absence of some member-state leaders has been interpreted by some people as a sign of fracturing in Pacific unity.
    然而，部分成員國領導人的缺席，被有些人解讀為太平洋團結出現裂痕的徵兆。
  • representative n. 代表
    Thirteen PIF members were represented by their heads of state or government, while Fiji, New Caledonia, Vanuatu and Samoa sent ministerial-level representatives, a diplomatic official said on condition of anonymity.
    一名不具名的外交官員表示，有13個PIF成員國由其國家元首或政府首長代表出席，而斐濟、新喀里多尼亞、萬那杜和薩摩亞則派遣部長級代表。
  • substantive adj. 實質性的，重大的
    The nation has long respected the “Pacific Way,” which emphasizes mutual respect, consensus and solidarity, and has continued to make substantive contributions through regional mechanisms, including supporting the Pacific Resilience Facility and the 2050 Strategy for the Blue Pacific Continent, the official said.
    該官員表示，我國長期尊重強調相互尊重、共識與團結的「太平洋方式」，並透過區域機制持續做出實質貢獻，包括支持「太平洋韌性設施」及「2050年藍色太平洋大陸戰略」。
  • autonomy n. 自治，自主權
    By contrast, China’s actions over the past few years have increasingly raised concerns among Pacific nations over their security and political autonomy, they said.
    他們表示，中國過去幾年來的行動，日益引起太平洋國家對其自身安全與政治自主的擔憂。
  • transparency n. 透明度
    Palauan President Surangel Whipps Jr had called on countries conducting intercontinental ballistic missile tests, such as China in July, to “ensure transparency and provide at least 24 hours’ advance notice,” the official said.
    官員指出，帛琉總統惠恕仁曾呼籲進行洲際彈道飛彈試驗的國家（例如中國在7月份的試驗）應「確保透明度並至少提前24小時預先通知」。
  • oppose v. 反對，對抗
    However, Nauru opposed the call, they added.
    不過，他們補充說，諾魯反對這項呼籲。
  • attendance n. 出席，出席率，參加
    Pacific unity should not be judged solely by leaders’ attendance at summits, but by whether countries can continue to independently develop a collective voice on issues of common concern, they added.
    他們補充說，太平洋團結不應僅由領導人出席高峰會的情況來衡量，而應由各國是否能繼續獨立發展出對共同關注議題的集體發言權來判定。
  • contrast n. 對比，差異
    Meanwhile, China has continued to heighten regional security concerns through its military activities and use of political influence to hinder PIF members from reaching common positions, highlighting the stark contrast between the two sides’ roles and approaches in the Pacific, they said.
    同時，他們表示，中國透過其軍事活動與政治影響力的運用，持續升高區域安全疑慮，阻礙PIF成員達成共同立場，凸顯了雙方在太平洋地區的角色與作法形成強烈對比。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作後位修飾
    Palauan President Surangel Whipps Jr had called on countries conducting intercontinental ballistic missile tests, such as China in July, to “ensure transparency and provide at least 24 hours’ advance notice,” the official said.
    句中的 "conducting intercontinental ballistic missile tests" 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "countries"，相當於形容詞子句 "that/who conducted..."，在英文新聞中常用來簡潔地附加說明名詞的特徵或動作。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    However, the absence of some member-state leaders has been interpreted by some people as a sign of fracturing in Pacific unity.
    句中的 "has been interpreted" 使用了現在完成式的被動語態（have/has been + 過去分詞），強調「領導人的缺席」這個動作是被某些人所解讀，焦點在於事件本身以及其帶來的影響，而非解讀的人是誰。
  • 對等連接詞連接對等子句 (while)
    Thirteen PIF members were represented by their heads of state or government, while Fiji, New Caledonia, Vanuatu and Samoa sent ministerial-level representatives, a diplomatic official said on condition of anonymity.
    句中的 "while" 在此作對等連接詞使用，意為「然而、而」，用來連接兩個對等的子句，藉此對比說明不同國家參與該論壇的代表層級差異。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞 (Gerund Phrase as Subject)
    Pacific unity should not be judged solely by leaders’ attendance at summits, but by whether countries can continue to independently develop a collective voice on issues of common concern, they added.
    句尾的 "whether countries can continue to independently develop a collective voice..." 是一個引導名詞子句，作為介系詞 "by" 的受詞。這種結構在多益考試和商業英文中極常出現，用來具體說明某個標準或依據的內容。
