慶祝台灣文化的珍珠奶茶節震撼馬里蘭州

Festival celebrating Taiwanese culture with bubble tea rocks Maryland

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • annual adj. 年度的、每年的
    The annual Taiwan Bubble Tea Festival was held in Rockville, Maryland, on Saturday, celebrating Taiwanese culture through the island's iconic beverage.
    一年一度的台灣珍珠奶茶節於週六在馬里蘭州洛克ville舉行，透過該島的標誌性飲品來慶祝台灣文化。
  • organizer n. 組織者、主辦單位
    According to the organizers, Taiwan Sister Cities (TSC) and the Rockville Sister Cities Corporation, visitors were able to sample Taiwanese specialties and enjoy themed performances at the festival.
    據主辦單位台灣姊妹市協會（TSC）與洛克ville姊妹市公司表示，參觀民眾在節慶中能夠品嚐台灣特產並欣賞主題表演。
  • sample v. 品嚐、嘗試、抽樣
    According to the organizers, Taiwan Sister Cities (TSC) and the Rockville Sister Cities Corporation, visitors were able to sample Taiwanese specialties and enjoy themed performances at the festival.
    據主辦單位台灣姊妹市協會（TSC）與洛克ville姊妹市公司表示，參觀民眾在節慶中能夠品嚐台灣特產並欣賞主題表演。
  • feature v. 以...為特色、由...主演
    In addition to bubble tea, the eighth edition of the celebration featured minced pork rice and gua bao buns as well as Taiwanese songs and dance.
    除了珍珠奶茶之外，這場第八屆的慶祝活動還以滷肉飯和割包以及台灣歌曲和舞蹈為特色。
  • official n. 官員、高級職員
    The event was attended by officials from Taiwan and the United States, including Taiwan's representative to the U.S. Alexander Yui (俞大㵢), his deputy Yang Yi-shan (楊懿珊), American Institute in Taiwan (AIT) Washington Office Managing Director Ingrid Larson, and Rockville Mayor Monique Ashton.
    出席該活動的有來自台灣和美國的官員，包括台灣駐美代表俞大㵢、副代表楊懿珊、美國在台協會（AIT）華盛頓總部執行董事藍鶯以及洛克ville市長莫妮卡·艾希頓。
  • cooperation n. 合作、協力
    Speaking at the festival, Ashton said Taiwan and the U.S. are countries that share the values of democracy, inclusivity and cooperation.
    艾希頓在音樂節上發言表示，台灣和美國是共享民主、包容與合作價值觀的國家。
  • partnership n. 夥伴關係、合夥關係
    Taiwan's iconic beverage is now commonplace in her city, and if bubble tea can become so easily accepted, then so can love, acceptance and partnerships, Ashton said.
    艾希頓說，台灣的標誌性飲品現在在她所居住的城市已司空見慣，如果珍珠奶茶能如此輕易被接受，那麼愛、包容與夥伴關係也同樣可以。
  • inception n. 創辦、起初、成立
    Speaking with CNA, TSC Director Minze Chien (簡明子) said the weather Saturday was the best the festival had experienced since its inception, helping draw 18,000 visitors to its 43 booths that showcased not only Taiwan but also Hong Kong and Ukraine.
    TSC主任簡明子接受中央社採訪時表示，週六的天氣是該節慶自創辦以來遇過最好的一次，助長吸引了1萬8千名遊客造訪其43個攤位，這些攤位不僅展示了台灣，還展示了香港和烏克蘭。
  • contrast n. 對比、差異
    The good weather was in stark contrast to one year when there was heavy rain and only 3,000 people attended, Chien said.
    簡明子說，這好天氣與前一年下大雨且僅有3千人出席的情況形成了強烈對比。
  • dedicated adj. 專用的、奉獻的、專注的
    Of the stalls, Chien said 22 were dedicated to food and beverages, of which five, including Taiwan's popular bubble tea brand 50 Lan, sold bubble tea specifically.
    簡明子表示，在這些攤位中，有22個專門提供餐飲，其中5個（包含台灣知名珍珠奶茶品牌50嵐）專門販售珍珠奶茶。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    The annual Taiwan Bubble Tea Festival was held in Rockville, Maryland, on Saturday, celebrating Taiwanese culture through the island's iconic beverage.
    句尾的「celebrating Taiwanese culture...」是由現在分詞片語充當副詞氣氛或結果狀語，補充說明主句發生的動作及伴隨的狀況，常用於新聞英語中以精簡連接兩個相關動作。
  • In addition to 引導的名詞片語 (In addition to + Noun/Gerund)
    In addition to bubble tea, the eighth edition of the celebration featured minced pork rice and gua bao buns as well as Taiwanese songs and dance.
    「In addition to」為介系詞片語，意指「除了...之外（還包含）」，後面接名詞或動名詞，常出現在多益考試的閱讀測驗中，用來擴充資訊。
  • 條件句與倒裝句型 (Conditional Sentence with 'So can...')
    Taiwan's iconic beverage is now commonplace in her city, and if bubble tea can become so easily accepted, then so can love, acceptance and partnerships, Ashton said.
    此句包含一個第一類條件句（if 帶領），後半句使用「so + 助動詞 + 主語」的倒裝句構，用來表示前面所述的情況也適用於後面的主語（「...也是如此」），增添語氣的對稱與強調。
