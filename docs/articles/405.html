數據顯示：台灣人口連續32個月下降

Population declines for 32nd straight month, data show

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • consecutive adj. 連續的，不間斷的
    Taiwan’s population last month declined for the 32nd consecutive month, with births totaling 7,492, household registration data released yesterday by the Ministry of the Interior showed.
    內政部昨天發布的戶籍登記數據顯示，台灣上個月的人口連續第32個月下降，出生人數總計為7,492人。
  • total v. 總計，總數達
    Taiwan’s population last month declined for the 32nd consecutive month, with births totaling 7,492, household registration data released yesterday by the Ministry of the Interior showed.
    內政部昨天發布的戶籍登記數據顯示，台灣上個月的人口連續第32個月下降，出生人數總計為7,492人。
  • release v. 發布，發行，釋放
    Taiwan’s population last month declined for the 32nd consecutive month, with births totaling 7,492, household registration data released yesterday by the Ministry of the Interior showed.
    內政部昨天發布的戶籍登記數據顯示，台灣上個月的人口連續第32個月下降，出生人數總計為7,492人。
  • decline v. 下降，減少，衰退
    Taiwan’s population last month declined for the 32nd consecutive month, with births totaling 7,492, household registration data released yesterday by the Ministry of the Interior showed.
    內政部昨天發布的戶籍登記數據顯示，台灣上個月的人口連續第32個月下降，出生人數總計為7,492人。
  • stand at phr. 位於（某數量或水平），達
    Taiwan’s population stood at 23,224,721 at the end of last month, down 103,622 from a year earlier and 10,281 from the previous month, the data showed.
    數據顯示，上月底台灣人口為23,224,721人，比去年同期減少103,622人，比前一個月減少10,281人。
  • previous adj. 先前的，以前的
    Taiwan’s population stood at 23,224,721 at the end of last month, down 103,622 from a year earlier and 10,281 from the previous month, the data showed.
    數據顯示，上月底台灣人口為23,224,721人，比去年同期減少103,622人，比前一個月減少10,281人。
  • record v. 記錄，創下（紀錄）
    Lienchiang County (Matsu) recorded the highest rate at 6.05 per 1,000 people, followed by Taitung County at 6.01 and Hsinchu County at 4.98, the data showed.
    數據顯示，連江縣（馬祖）記錄了最高比率，為每千人6.05人，其次是台東縣的6.01人和新竹縣的4.98人。
  • mean v. 意味著，意指
    Meanwhile, 16,470 deaths were recorded in the month, 1,244 more than in the same month last year, but 649 fewer than in July, meaning Taiwan had a natural population decrease of 8,978, the ministry said.
    同時，部會表示，當月記錄了16,470人死亡，比去年同月多1,244人，但比7月少649人，這意味著台灣自然人口減少了8,978人。
  • account for phr. 佔（比例），說明
    Taiwan became a super-aged society last year, meaning that people aged 65 or older account for at least 20 percent of the population.
    台灣去年成為超高齡社會，這意味著65歲或以上的人口占總人口的至少20%。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾語 (Past Participle Phrase)
    household registration data released yesterday by the Ministry of the Interior showed.
    句中的「released yesterday by the Ministry of the Interior」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「household registration data」，相當於關代子語「which was released yesterday...」，省略了「which was」以簡化句子結構。
  • 分詞構句表結果 (Participial Phrase indicating result)
    meaning Taiwan had a natural population decrease of 8,978, the ministry said.
    句尾的「meaning Taiwan had...」是由對等子句簡化而來的分詞構句，在此處表自然而然的結果（前段數據所帶來的意義），主動使用現在分詞「meaning」。
  • 形容詞片語後置修飾 (Postpositive Adjective Phrase)
    people aged 65 or older
    「aged 65 or older」是形容詞片語，放在名詞「people」後面進行修飾，完整說法為「people who are aged 65 or older」，省略了關代與be動詞使表達更為簡潔。
