文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Present Participle）作後置定語
    Taiwan High Speed Rail Corp's (THSRC) new trains could pave the way for a pilot program involving family cars and expanded use of senior citizen cards...
    句中的 involving family cars 為現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 a pilot program（試辦計畫），相當於關代子句「which involves family cars」，在多益閱讀中常見於簡化形容詞子句以精簡句意。
  • 被動語態（Passive Voice）
    ...after some parents said they were asked by train staff to calm noisy children and later take them out of the carriage.
    句中的 were asked 採用了被動語態（be + 過去分詞），用來表達家長「被」列車人員要求做某事。在商業與新聞英語中，當動作的承受者是焦點時，經常使用被動語態。
  • 名詞子句作受詞（Noun Clause as Object）
    Chen said the Ministry of Transportation and Communications had discussed the proposal with THSRC, but noted that space on existing trains is limited.
    動詞 noted 後面接了一個由 that 引導的名詞子句（that space on existing trains is limited），用來當作 noted 的受詞，表達他所「指出」的內容。
  • 條件句與未來時間的表達（Conditional / Time Clause）
    Once the first of THSRC's new N700ST trains enter service, currently expected in July 2027, and capacity improves, the ministry will seek to launch a trial as soon as possible, he added.
    句首由 Once（一旦）引導時間副詞子句。在英文中，當用 Once、When 等連接詞表達未來發生的條件或時間時，子句中使用現在式（enter service, improves）來代替未來式，而主要子句則使用未來式（will seek）。
