文法重點

  • 非限定關係代名詞子句（使用 which）
    President Lai Ching-te has sent greetings from the Taiwan government and people to Pope Leo XIV on his 71st birthday, which was on Sept. 14, according to Taiwan's embassy in the Holy See.
    句型中的「which was on Sept. 14」前面有逗號，屬於非限定用法（Non-defining relative clause），用來補充說明前面的先行詞「his 71st birthday」，在句中扮演補充資訊的角色，不能省略，且不可以用 that 代替。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Formal bilateral diplomatic relations were established in 1942, when the Republic of China government was still based in mainland China.
    句中的「were established」使用了「be 動詞 + 過去分詞」的被動語態結構，強調「雙邊外交關係」是被建立的，焦點在於動作的承受者而非建立關係的人。
  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾（分詞構句變體）
    ...according to Taiwan's embassy in the Holy See.
    片語「based in mainland China」是用過去分詞開頭的形容詞片語，用來修飾前面的主詞補語或子句的主體（Republic of China government），相當於「which was based in mainland China」的簡化寫法，在新聞英語中非常常見。
