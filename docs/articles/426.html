日本體操選手在賽前壟罩在傷病陰影下

Japanese gymnasts under injury cloud before games

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • include v. 包含，包括
    Japan men’s gymnastics team for their home Asian Games this month includes multiple Olympic and world champions...
    日本男子體操隊將參加本月在主場舉行的亞洲運動會，陣中包含多位奧運與世界冠軍...
  • face v. 面臨，面對
    ...is facing a race to be fit after suffering a lower back injury last week.
    ...上週遭受下背部受傷後，目前正與時間賽跑以恢復健康。
  • competition n. 競賽，比賽
    ...with the men’s gymnastics competition getting under way two days later.
    男子體操賽事則於兩天後展開。
  • intend v. 打算，計畫
    ...but told reporters afterwards that he still intended to compete in Nagoya.
    ...但事后告訴記者，他仍然打算在名古屋出賽。
  • assess v. 評估，評價
    Japan team boss Kensuke Murata said he would assess his team’s fitness and decide on any changes before the Sept. 16 deadline.
    日本隊領隊村田憲亮表示，他將在 9 月 16 日截止日期前評估球隊的健康狀況並決定是否做出任何變動。
  • deadline n. 截止期限，最後期限
    Japan team boss Kensuke Murata said he would assess his team’s fitness and decide on any changes before the Sept. 16 deadline.
    日本隊領隊村田憲亮表示，他將在 9 月 16 日截止日期前評估球隊的健康狀況並決定是否做出任何變動。
  • perform v. 表現，表演，執行
    Murata said that Oka would perform less difficult routines if he does compete.
    村田表示，如果岡慎之助確實參賽，他將會執行難度較低的套路。
  • situation n. 情況，處境
    “I think the athletes themselves understand their situation better than anyone,” the 25-year-old said.
    「我想運動員本身比任何人都清楚他們的狀況，」這位 25 歲的選手說。

文法重點

  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    Shinnosuke Oka, who won gold in the all-around, team and horizontal bar events at the 2024 Paris Olympics, is facing a race to be fit...
    句中的「who won gold in the all-around...」是非限定關係代名詞子句，用逗號隔開，用來補充說明主詞 Shinnosuke Oka 的背景資訊。
  • 對等連接詞連接對稱子句
    Japan are the reigning Olympic team champions, but China won the Asian Games title on home soil in Hangzhou in 2023.
    使用對等連接詞「but」連接兩個完整的獨立子句（主詞＋動詞），形成轉折語氣，對比日本與中國在不同賽事的奪冠情況。
  • 條件句與強調助動詞
    Murata said that Oka would perform less difficult routines if he does compete.
    句中「if he does compete」使用了現在式條件句，其中的「does」是用來加強語氣（強調「真的」參賽），置於原形動詞 compete 之前。
  • 條件副詞子句表達時間與條件
    “As long as they’re thinking about what they can do and what they should do, I’m not worried...
    「As long as...」意指「只要……」，用來引導條件副詞子句，表達主句動作（不擔心）成立的前置條件。
