分析人士：中國國防動員法暗示為全面衝突做準備

China defense mobilization law signals preparations for full-scale conflict: Analysts

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • mobilize verb 動員、調動
    China's revised National Defense Mobilization Law, which will take effect on Oct. 1, indicates that the Chinese Communist Party (CCP) plans to mobilize the country's full resources...
    中國修訂後的《國防動員法》將於 10 月 1 日生效，這表明中國共產黨計畫動員國家全部資源……
  • take effect phrasal verb 生效、實施
    China's revised National Defense Mobilization Law, which will take effect on Oct. 1...
    中國修訂後的《國防動員法》將於 10 月 1 日生效……
  • transition noun 轉變、轉換
    ...ensure a 'rapid transition between peacetime and wartime' in response to threats...
    ……確保為應對威脅而進行「平時與戰時的迅速轉換」……
  • target verb 針對、把……作為目標
    ...said the revision was not intended solely for Taiwan, but also targeted other countries with which China has territorial disputes...
    ……表示該修訂案並非專門針對台灣，同時也針對與中國有領土爭議的其他國家……
  • invoke verb 援引、調用（法律等）
    ...it could invoke the law to seek legal justification for its actions in the international community...
    ……它可能會援引該法律，在國際社會中為其行動尋求法律正當性……
  • justification noun 正當理由、合理性
    ...to seek legal justification for its actions in the international community...
    ……在國際社會中為其行動尋求法律正當性……
  • interpret verb 解釋、詮釋
    You could interpret it as [Chinese President] Xi Jinping (習近平) arrogating more power...
    你可以將其解釋為[中國國家主席]習近平集權更多……
  • provision noun （法律的）條款、規定
    Another key difference in the revision is the addition of the term 'expropriation' to provisions on the requisitioning of 'civilian resources'...
    修訂案的另一個關鍵不同點在於，在關於徵用「民間資源」的條款中加入了「徵收」一詞……
  • comply verb 遵守、服從
    ...all state agencies, nongovernmental organizations, businesses and individuals must comply unconditionally with investigations...
    ……所有國家機關、非政府組織、企業和個人都必須無條件地配合調查……
  • scope noun 範圍、範疇
    Yang noted that the law does not clearly define the scope of requisition and expropriation.
    楊太源指出，該法並未明確定義徵用和徵收的範圍。
  • accommodate verb 順應、迎合、容納
    ...in order to accommodate systemic changes following China's sweeping military reforms in 2015.
    ……以適應 2015 年中國全面軍事改革後的體制變革。
  • consolidate verb 鞏固、加強（權力、地位等）
    By consolidating his control over the PLA, Xi is also tightening his grip on power...
    透過鞏固對解放軍的控制，習近平也正在收緊對權力的掌控……

文法重點

  • 非限定關係代名詞子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    China's revised National Defense Mobilization Law, which will take effect on Oct. 1, indicates that the Chinese Communist Party (CCP) plans to mobilize the country's full resources...
    句中的「which will take effect on Oct. 1」是用逗號隔開的非限定關係代名詞子句，用來補充說明前面的主詞「China's revised National Defense Mobilization Law」，提供額外的附加資訊。若拿掉這個子句，主要句意依然完整。
  • 分詞構句作附帶說明 (Participle Clause)
    By consolidating his control over the PLA, Xi is also tightening his grip on power as he stands on the brink of announcing his fourth five-year term...
    句首的「By consolidating his control over the PLA」是以介詞片語帶動動名詞的結構，表達「透過……的方式」，用來修飾後方主句的動作，展現行動與結果的因果或手段關係。
  • 被動語態的應用 (Passive Voice)
    ...the revision was drafted with the intention of wartime requisition and expropriation from all civilians and businesses in China.
    句型「was drafted」使用了被動語態（be + 過去分詞），強調「修訂案被擬定」這個動作本身以及其背後的意圖，而不需要特別強調是誰去草擬的，在新聞英文與正式商業書信中非常常見。
