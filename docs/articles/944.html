單字解析

  • assessment n. 評估、看法
    Dennis Wilder, who previously served as director for China at the U.S. National Security Council, echoed Glaser's assessment, saying Beijing would likely raise the issue...
    先前曾擔任美國國家安全委員會中國事務主任的韋德曼呼應了葛來儀的評估...
  • concession n. 讓步、妥協
    The Trump administration is "clearly trying to squeeze Iran" for concessions, she said.
    她說，川普政府「顯然正試圖對伊朗施壓」以換取妥協。
  • objective n. 目標、目的
    Glaser said she hoped the two leaders could agree on the issues and objectives of the planned AI dialogue...
    葛來儀表示，她希望兩位領導人能就規劃中的 AI 對話之議題與目標達成共識...
  • extend v. 延長、延展
    Glaser also said she would like Trump and Xi to extend the existing tariff agreement and ensure China follows through on its commitments...
    葛來儀亦表示，她希望川普和習近平能延長現有的關稅協議，並確保中國落實其承諾...
  • commitment n. 承諾、交辦事項
    Glaser also said she would like Trump and Xi to extend the existing tariff agreement and ensure China follows through on its commitments...
    葛來儀亦表示，她希望川普和習近平能延長現有的關稅協議，並確保中國落實其承諾...
  • bilateral adj. 雙邊的
    The two leaders are scheduled to hold bilateral talks Thursday, followed by a state banquet that evening.
    兩位領導人預定於週四舉行雙邊會談，當天傍晚並將出席國宴。
  • delivery n. 運送、交貨
    Both scholars hoped the summit will produce progress on AI and the delivery of rare earths...
    兩位學者皆期盼峰會能在 AI 以及稀土供應（運送）方面取得進展。
  • vulnerable adj. 易受傷的、脆弱的
    ...leaving other economies vulnerable to Beijing's threats to stop shipments of the critical minerals.
    ...這使得其他經濟體易受北京揚言停止出貨這些關鍵礦產的威脅所影響。
  • reciprocal adj. 互惠的、相對的、互相的
    If both leaders were to make those trips, Glaser said, it would be "unprecedented" for the U.S. and Chinese leaders to make two reciprocal visits in a single year.
    葛來儀表示，如果兩位領導人都成行，美中領導人在單一年度內進行兩次互訪將是「史無前例」的。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Under the framework being prepared by the two countries, up to US$30 billion in non-sensitive goods could be subject to tariff reductions.
    句中的 "being prepared by the two countries" 是現在分詞被動片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "framework"（架構），相當於形容詞子句 "which is being prepared by the two countries" 的簡化版，多益閱讀測驗常考這種分詞修飾的簡化結構。
  • 非限定關係代間詞子句帶介系詞 (Non-defining Relative Clause with Preposition)
    ...the production of which is dominated by China, leaving other economies vulnerable to Beijing's threats...
  • 假設語氣未來式 (Future Subjunctive / Unreal Conditional)
    If both leaders were to make those trips, Glaser said, it would be "unprecedented" for the U.S. and Chinese leaders to make two reciprocal visits in a single year.
    句型使用 "If + 主詞 + were to + V..., 主詞 + would + V..."，用來表達對未來極度不確定或可能性極低的假設情況（如果雙方真的都要成行...）。
  • 被動語態的應用 (Passive Voice)
    Xi is expected to arrive at Joint Base Andrews outside Washington, D.C., on Wednesday afternoon, where Trump is scheduled to attend a welcoming ceremony.
    句中運用多個被動語態 "is expected to"（被預期）與 "is scheduled to"（被排定、預定），這是新聞英文中報導未來官方行程時極為標準且高頻的句型。
