Are warnings of uncontrollable AI coming true?

單字解析

  • threshold noun 門檻、開端
    Trager, the director of the Oxford Martin AI Governance Initiative, was speaking in the week OpenAI claimed the technology had crossed the threshold known as AGI...
    牛津馬丁人工智慧治理倡議主任特拉格是在 OpenAI 聲稱該技術已跨越稱為 AGI 的門檻的這週發表上述言論的。
  • autonomy noun 自主、自治（文章中為形容詞 autonomous）
    The San Francisco company defines AGI as “autonomous systems that outperform humans at most economically valuable work”.
    這家總部設在舊金山的公司將 AGI 定義為「在多數具經濟價值的工作上表現超越人類的自主系統」。
  • outperform verb 表現勝過、超越
    The San Francisco company defines AGI as “autonomous systems that outperform humans at most economically valuable work”.
    這家總部設在舊金山的公司將 AGI 定義為「在多數具經濟價值的工作上表現超越人類的自主系統」。
  • automate verb 使自動化
    The tasks it claims Astra can automate include designing circuit boards, filling out tax returns, building video games, financial modeling, engineering design and helping assemble legal documents.
    它聲稱 Astra 能夠自動化的任務包括設計電路板、填寫報稅表、製作電玩遊戲、財務建模、工程設計以及協助彙編法律文件。
  • implicit adjective 含蓄的、不言而喻的
    The threat to some white-collar jobs is implicit.
    對某些白領工作的威脅是不言而喻的。
  • infrastructure noun 基礎設施
    Concerns currently focus on AIs mounting cyber-attacks that could cripple real-world social and economic infrastructure, but future concerns include their ability to create biohazards and control military hardware.
    目前的擔憂集中在 AI 發動網路攻擊，這可能會癱瘓現實世界的社會和經濟基礎設施，但未來的擔憂包括它們製造生物危害和控制軍事硬體的的能力。
  • prohibit verb 禁止
    And next week a bill will be proposed by the Labor MP Alex Sobel to prohibit superintelligent AI development in the UK.
    下週，工黨下議員亞歷克斯・索貝爾將提出一項法案，禁止在英國發展超級智慧 AI。
  • listing noun （公司股票）上市
    OpenAI’s rival Anthropic, which is targeting a US$2 trillion stock exchange listing, this week admitted its own AIs were “not perfectly aligned” with human values...
    OpenAI 的競爭對手 Anthropic 正力爭在證券交易所上市並籌集 2 兆美元，該公司本週承認其自身的 AI 與人類價值觀「並非完全一致」...
  • alignment noun 對齊、一致（文章中為動詞/形容詞 aligned）
    OpenAI’s rival Anthropic... admitted its own AIs were “not perfectly aligned” with human values...
    OpenAI 的競爭對手 Anthropic... 承認其自身的 AI 與人類價值觀「並非完全一致」...
  • draft verb 起草、草擬（亦可當名詞：草稿、徵召）
    ...a law firm executive being helped to draft some contracts.
    ...一名律師事務所主管獲得協助來起草一些合約。
  • monitor verb 監控、監測（文章中為名詞 monitorability）
    The ability to monitor what AI models are “thinking” as they advance is another growing source of safety fears.
    隨著 AI models 不斷進步，監控它們在「想些什麼」的能力是引發安全恐懼的另一大來源。
  • efficient adjective 有效率的
    This week it was reported that OpenAI had been training Astra to reason not only in natural language, but in a more opaque manner that is faster and more efficient...
    本週有報導稱，OpenAI 一直在訓練 Astra 不僅能用自然語言進行推理，還能以一種更不透明、更快、更有效率的方式進行推理...
  • conflicted adjective 內心矛盾的、意見分歧的
    OpenAI’s chief executive, Sam Altman, said this week he felt conflicted about the advances of AI models.
    OpenAI 執行長山姆・阿特曼本週表示，他對 AI 模型的進展感到內心矛盾。
  • urgent adjective 緊急的
    On Tuesday he addressed the G20 ministerial summit in North Carolina in the US and told politicians that “some things are going to go very wrong with cybersecurity unless people act quite urgently”.
    週二他在美國北卡羅來納州舉行的 G20 部長級峰會上發言，告訴政客們「除非人們採取相當緊急的行動，否則在網路安全方面有些事情將會出大錯」。
