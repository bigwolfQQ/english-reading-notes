AI 熱潮促使亞洲開發銀行將台灣成長預測上調至 11%

AI boom prompts ADB to lift Taiwan growth outlook to 11%

單字解析

  • forecast n. / v. 預測，預報
    The Asian Development Bank (ADB) on Wednesday raised its forecast for Taiwan's gross domestic product (GDP) growth in 2026 to 11 percent...
    亞洲開發銀行（ADB）於週三將台灣 2026 年國內生產毛額（GDP）成長預測上調至 11%...
  • projection n. 預估，投影
    In its Asian Development Outlook September 2026 report, the ADB raised its projection for Taiwan... by 1.5 percentage points from its July projection...
    在其《2026年9月亞洲發展展望》報告中，亞銀將對台灣的預測較 7 月份的預測上調了 1.5 個百分點...
  • benefit v. / n. 受益，獲益；利益
    ...saying the economy had benefited from the AI boom in the first half of 2026 despite global shocks.
    ...並表示儘管面臨全球衝擊，台灣經濟在 2026 年上半年仍受惠於 AI 熱潮。
  • data n. 數據，資料
    Customs data showed technology exports rose 65 percent in U.S. dollar terms in the first half of 2026...
    海關數據表明，2026 年上半年科技出口以美元計算成長了 65%...
  • consumption n. 消費，消耗量
    Private consumption grew nearly 6 percent in the first half of the year...
    私人消費成長了近 6%...
  • stable adj. 穩定的
    ...stable property prices and wage growth spilling over from the technology sector to other industries...
    ...穩定的房價以及科技產業外溢至其他產業的薪資成長...
  • optimistic adj. 樂觀的
    Businesses also remained optimistic, increasing fixed investment by nearly 10 percent...
    企業也保持樂觀，將固定投資增加了近 10%...
  • driver n. 驅動力，駕駛員
    The ADB said technology exports are expected to remain the main driver of Taiwan's economic growth...
    亞銀表示，預期科技出口仍將是台灣經濟成長的主要驅動力...
  • moderate v. / adj. 趨緩，使溫和；適度的
    Growth is expected to moderate in 2027 as investment growth slows...
    隨著投資成長放緩，預計 2027 年的成長將趨於溫和...
  • maintain v. 維持，保持
    The bank maintained its 2026 inflation forecast at 2 percent...
    該銀行維持 2026 年通膨預測在 2% 不對...
  • impact n. / v. 衝擊，影響
    ...efforts by state-owned energy companies to restrain domestic oil prices should limit the impact on overall inflation, the ADB said.
    ...國營能源企業抑制國內油價的努力應能限制對整體通膨的影響，亞銀表示。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附帶結果或原因狀語（Citing...）
    ...raising its forecast for Taiwan's gross domestic product (GDP) growth in 2026 to 11 percent, citing a surge in artificial intelligence (AI)-related exports.
    句尾的「citing...」是由現在分詞帶出的分詞構句，用來補充說明前面主句動作（調升預測）的原因或依據，意即「援引／指出……」。
  • 被動語態與過去分詞作形容詞用（designated as...）
    ...its projection for Taiwan, designated as "Taipei,China" in the report, by 1.5 percentage points...
    句中的「designated as 'Taipei,China'」是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的專有名詞 Taiwan，省略了「which is」，用來被動說明台灣在該報告中被指定的名稱。
  • 獨立分詞構句（with + 名詞 + 分詞/形容詞）
    ...with the economy expanding 12.9 percent in the second quarter, the report said.
    「with + 名詞 (the economy) + 現在分詞 (expanding)」構成獨立分詞構句，用來補充說明主句發生時的伴隨狀況，表示「隨著經濟在第二季擴張12.9%」。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞（...driving up energy and food prices）
    ...could weaken global demand by driving up energy and food prices, the report said...
    介系詞 by 後方接動名詞短語「driving up energy and food prices」作為受詞，用以表達達成某個結果的手段或方式（透過推高能源與食品價格）。
