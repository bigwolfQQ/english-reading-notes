建立水門規範刻不容緩

Building floodgates rules needed

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • estimate v. 估計，估算
    Total losses remain difficult to estimate, while residents blame building management committees and property managers for failing to close floodgates in time.
    總損失目前仍難以估算，居民則將矛頭指向大樓管理委員會和物業經理，責怪他們未能及時關閉水門。
  • management committee n. 管理委員會（管委會）
    Apartment complexes generally cannot make decisions as quickly as buildings under single ownership can.
    公寓大廈通常無法像單一所有權的大樓那樣迅速做出決定。
  • property manager n. 物業經理，物業管理人
    Building bylaws also often fail to fully authorize management committees or property managers to close floodgates when disasters strike, leaving them in an impossible position when floodwaters approach.
    大樓規約也經常未能充分授權管理委員會或物業經理在災難來臨時關閉水門，這使得他們在洪水逼近時陷入動輒得咎的困境。
  • operational adj. 營運的，操作的
    In addition to operational and training issues, the decisionmaking structure and emergency response capacity of apartment buildings must also be taken into account to effectively address holes in the system.
    除了操作和培訓問題外，公寓大樓的決策結構和應變能力也必須納入考量，才能有效彌補制度上的漏洞。
  • authority n. 權力，權威，官方
    Decisionmaking authority is shared equally among owners and management committees are typically only empowered to handle routine matters of maintenance and management.
    決策權由所有權人平均分享，而管理委員會通常僅被授權處理日常的維護與管理事務。
  • routine adj. 例行的，日常的
    Decisionmaking authority is shared equally among owners and management committees are typically only empowered to handle routine matters of maintenance and management.
    決策權由所有權人平均分享，而管理委員會通常僅被授權處理日常的維護與管理事務。
  • liable adj. 負有法律責任的
    Conversely, someone returning home to closed gates might threaten to hold the property manager liable for any damage resulting from having to leave their vehicle outside.
    相反地，回到家卻發現水門深鎖的人，可能會威脅要讓物業管理員為被迫將車停在外面所導致的任何損壞負起賠償責任。
  • compensate v. 賠償，補償
    A local government leader who makes the wrong call in declaring a typhoon day off might face some public criticism, but apartment management committees can come under enormous pressure to directly compensate residents for their losses.
    地方政府首長在宣佈放颱風假時如果做出錯誤決定，可能會面臨一些公眾批評，但公寓管理委員會卻可能承受巨大壓力，必須直接賠償居民的損失。
  • assess v. 評估，評價
    Expecting residential communities, which lack the capacity to forecast weather or assess urban hydrology, to decide when to close floodgates is a recipe for disaster.
    要求缺乏預測天氣或評估都市水文能力的住宅社區來決定何時關閉水門，簡直是自取滅亡。
  • stipulate v. 規定，約定
    Residential communities then need only stipulate in their bylaws or committee meeting resolutions that floodgates are to be closed according to government warnings.
    如此一來，住宅社區只需要在規約或管委會會議決議中規定，水門必須按照政府的警告來關閉。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附加說明（補充結果）
    Total losses remain difficult to estimate, while residents blame building management committees and property managers for failing to close floodgates in time.
    句中的 while 連接詞引導對比子句，而後方加上動詞的ing型態或形容詞片語，用來描述主句發生的同時所伴隨的情況或結果。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞 (Gerund Phrase as Subject)
    Expecting residential communities, which lack the capacity to forecast weather or assess urban hydrology, to decide when to close floodgates is a recipe for disaster.
    句首的「Expecting... floodgates」是一個大型的動名詞片語，作為整個句子的主詞。在多益閱讀測驗中，常考認出動名詞當主詞時，後方搭配的動詞須使用單數形（在此為 is）。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    A local government leader who makes the wrong call in declaring a typhoon day off might face some public criticism...
    句中的 who 引導一個形容詞子句（關係代名詞子句），用來修飾先行詞 A local government leader，補充說明這位領導者的特徵。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Decisionmaking authority is shared equally among owners and management committees are typically only empowered to handle routine matters...
    句中使用了「be + 過去分詞」（如 is shared、are empowered），強調動作的承受者（決策權、管委會），這是商業及新聞英語中非常常見的句型結構。
