文法重點

  • 現在進行式的被動語態 (Present Continuous Passive)
    Under the agreement, a “Literature from Taiwan” track is being added to the center’s Emerging Translator Mentorships...
    結構為「主詞 + is/are/am being + 過去分詞 (P.P.)」。用來表達一個動作在目前說話的當下正在被進行或處理中。
  • 非限定關係代名詞子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Minister of Culture Li Yuan (李遠), who on Tuesday attended the signing in Norwich, England, expressed hope that...
    句中的逗號後面接由 who 引導的子句，對先行詞 Minister of Culture Li Yuan 做補充說明。如果去掉這個子句，主要子句的意思依然完整明確。
  • 分詞片語作為後置修飾語 (Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    ...expressed hope that the partnership would help build a long-term pipeline of translators capable of bringing more Taiwanese literature to British readers.
    形容詞片語「capable of bringing...」用來修飾前方名詞 translators，原句省略了「who are」，讓句子更簡潔緊湊，是多益閱讀測驗常考的簡化句型。
  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense)
    National Centre for Writing chair Alan Waters said the center had begun promoting Taiwanese literature even before author Yang Shuang-zi’s... Taiwan Travelogue gained international recognition...
    結構為「主詞 + had + 過去分詞 (P.P.)」。用來表達在過去某個時間點（gain 獲得認可）之前就已經發生的動作（begin 開始推廣），藉此釐清兩個過去動作的先後順序。
