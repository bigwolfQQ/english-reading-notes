  • dominant adj. 主導的，佔優勢的（文章中使用其動詞 dominate）

    運動會提供所有奧運常規項目，但它們與不受中國和日本等強權國家主導的區域性項目共享舞台。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞當作後置定語（修飾名詞）
    There are many regional sports being played — sepak takraw or wushu or kurash, for instance — but the curiosity champion is probably kabaddi.
    句中的「being played」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「regional sports」，相當於「that are being played」的簡化形式，用精簡的語氣表達「正在被進行的區域性運動」。
  • 關係代名詞子句（限定修飾）
    The Games offer all the Olympic regulars, but they share the stage with the regional offerings that are not dominated by powerhouse countries such as China and Japan.
    句中的「that are not dominated by...」是一個由關係代名詞「that」引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾先行詞「the regional offerings」，補充說明這些地區性項目的特徵。
  • 被動語態
    It has also been proposed as an Olympic sport, but has never been voted in.
  • 過去分詞片語當作形容詞修飾名詞
    Competition in taolu involves performing sets and choreographed routines that show off principles of attack and defense.
    句中的「choreographed」是由動詞 choreograph 變來的過去分詞，在此作為形容詞放在名詞 routines 前面，用來修飾「經過編排的」套路動作。
