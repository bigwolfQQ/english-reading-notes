文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    ...the World Alliance for Disaster Management (WADM), an initiative launched by Taiwan, of which Eswatini is a founding member.
    句中的 "launched by Taiwan" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "an initiative"（一項倡議）。這相當於形容詞子句 "which was launched by Taiwan" 的簡化版，省略了關係代名詞與be動詞，使句子更為精煉。
  • 「介系詞 + 關係代名詞」引導的形容詞子句
    ...an initiative launched by Taiwan, of which Eswatini is a founding member.
    句尾的 "of which Eswatini is a founding member" 是一個由「介系詞 (of) + 關係代名詞 (which)」所引導的非限定性關係子句。其中的 "which" 代替前面的 "WADM"，而 "of" 則是跟後方 "founding member" 的搭配（Eswatini is a founding member of WADM）。
  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況
    "Deputy Prime Minister Dladla personally accompanied our delegation, traveling tens of thousands of kilometers with us to ensure that our journey there and back proceeded smoothly," Lai said.
    句中的 ", traveling tens of thousands of kilometers with us..." 是現在分詞構句，放在主句後面用來補充說明主句發生的同時所伴隨的動作，主詞 Dladla 與 traveling 之間為主動關係。
  • 連接詞 during which 引導的關係子句
    Dladla accompanied Lai's delegation on the May 2-5 trip, during which it traveled about 25,000 kilometers aboard King Mswati III's aircraft...
    句中的 "during which" 用來連接兩個句子。「during」指在該行程期間，「which」則代指前面的 "the May 2-5 trip"，用來補充說明在這段期間內代表團所進行的活動。
