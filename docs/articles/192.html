亞運代表團陣容包含奧運獎牌得主

Olympic medalists among delegation to Asian Games

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • delegation n. 代表團
    Taiwan is to send 482 athletes, including several Olympic medalists, to compete in 34 sports at the Asian Games in Japan’s Aichi Prefecture later this month, the Ministry of Sports said.
    體育署表示，台灣將派出 482 名運動員，其中包括數名奧運獎牌得主，將於本月稍晚前往日本愛知縣參加亞運會的 34 項運動競賽。
  • compete v. 競爭，參賽
    Taiwan is to send 482 athletes, including several Olympic medalists, to compete in 34 sports at the Asian Games in Japan’s Aichi Prefecture later this month, the Ministry of Sports said.
    體育署表示，台灣將派出 482 名運動員，其中包括數名奧運獎牌得主，將於本月稍晚前往日本愛知縣參加亞運會的 34 項運動競賽。
  • include v. 包含，包括
    Some of the biggest names in the delegation include Taiwan’s first Olympic boxing gold medalist Lin Yu-ting...
    代表團中最大咖的選手包括台灣首位奧運拳擊金牌得主林郁婷……
  • member n. 成員
    At 45, sport shooter Lin Yi-chun is the oldest member of the Taiwanese delegation.
    現年 45 歲的射擊選手林怡君是台灣代表團中最年長的成員。
  • represent v. 代表
    She would be representing Taiwan, competing under the name Chinese Taipei, at the Asian Games for the eighth time.
    她將代表台灣、以中華台北的名義第八次參加亞運會。
  • session n. 期間，會議，場次
    Most of the athletes competing at the Asian Games began training as early as August 2024, with intensive training sessions beginning in July.
    大多數參加亞運的運動員早在 2024 年 8 月就開始訓練，密集訓練課程則於 7 月開始。
  • provide v. 提供
    ...to provide better care for the athletes.
    ……以為運動員提供更好的照顧。
  • hold v. 舉辦，舉行（常用被動態 be held）
    The Asian Games, also known as Aichi-Nagoya 2026, are to be held from Sept. 19 to Oct. 4 at venues across Aichi Prefecture in Japan.
    亞運會又稱為 2026 年愛知-名古屋亞運，將於 9 月 19 日至 10 月 4 日在日本愛知縣各地的場館舉行。
  • record n. 紀錄
    Taiwan’s delegation won a record 19 gold medals at a single Asian Games on two occasions...
    台灣代表團曾在兩屆單一亞運會上創下奪得 19 面金牌的紀錄……
  • overall adj. 總體的，全面的
    Taiwan finished sixth in the overall medal standings at both editions.
    台灣在兩屆賽事的總獎牌榜上均名列第六。

文法重點

  • be to do 句型（表預定、安排）
    Taiwan is to send 482 athletes, including several Olympic medalists, to compete in 34 sports at the Asian Games...
    「be + to V」可用於新聞英語中，用來表達官方計畫、預定或即將發生的正式安排，此處「is to send」意指「預計將派出」。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾
    Some of the biggest names in the delegation include Taiwan’s first Olympic boxing gold medalist Lin Yu-ting, Olympic gold medalist weightlifter Kuo Hsing-chun, Olympic silver medalist judoka Yang Yung-wei and gymnast Lee Chih-kai, the first Taiwanese athlete to win an Olympic medal in gymnastics.
    句尾的「to win an Olympic medal in gymnastics」是不定式片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「athlete」，說明他是第一位「在體操項目獲得奧運獎牌」的運動員。
  • with + 名詞 + 分詞（附帶狀況）
    Gymnastics is the largest sport in the delegation, with 37 gymnasts competing, followed by e-sports, with 33 athletes, the ministry said.
    「with + O. + 分詞」結構在英文中常表示伴隨的情況或狀態。此處「with 37 gymnasts competing」表示「有 37 名體操選手參賽（的情況下）」，「competing」為主動分詞，修飾 gymnasts。
  • also known as（同位語/被動分詞簡化）
    The Asian Games, also known as Aichi-Nagoya 2026, are to be held from Sept. 19 to Oct. 4...
    「also known as」是由「which are also known as」簡化而來的過去分詞片語，夾在主詞與動詞之間，用來補充說明亞運會的另一個名稱。
