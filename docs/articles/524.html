文化部長表示：台灣的存在是揭露中國的「鏡子」

Culture minister says Taiwan's existence is a 'mirror' that reveals China

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • exist v. 存在
    Taiwan's existence and culture were painful for China, describing Taiwan as "a mirror that reveals the true nature of things."
    台灣的存在與文化對中國而言是件痛苦的事，他形容台灣是「一面照出事物本質的鏡子」。
  • achieve v. 達成、實現
    Speaking to Radio France Internationale (RFI) on Friday, Li said China had been unable to achieve what Taiwan has demonstrated and therefore strongly wished Taiwan did not exist.
    李遠週五接受法國國際廣播電台（RFI）專訪時表示，中國一直無法達成台灣所展現的成就，因此強烈希望台灣不存在。
  • demonstrate v. 展現、證明
    Speaking to Radio France Internationale (RFI) on Friday, Li said China had been unable to achieve what Taiwan has demonstrated and therefore strongly wished Taiwan did not exist.
    李遠週五接受法國國際廣播電台（RFI）專訪時表示，中國一直無法達成台灣所展現的成就，因此強烈希望台灣不存在。
  • emphasize v. 強調
    Responding to China's frequent emphasis on the grandeur and long history of Chinese culture...
    針對中國頻繁強調中華文化的宏大與悠久歷史...
  • frequent adj. 頻繁的
    Responding to China's frequent emphasis on the grandeur and long history of Chinese culture...
    針對中國頻繁強調中華文化的宏大與悠久歷史...
  • promote v. 推廣、促進、晉升
    ...arguing that genuine culture is reflected in people's everyday lives and does not need to be deliberately defined or promoted.
    ...並主張真正的文化體現在人們的日常生活中，不需要被刻意定義或推廣。
  • perceive v. 看待、感知、察覺
    "A truly strong culture does not need to boast about itself, nor does it need to fear how others perceive or treat it," Li said in Chinese.
    李遠以中文表示：「一個真正強大的文化不需要自吹自擂，也不需要畏懼別人如何看待或對待它。」
  • partnership n. 夥伴關係、合夥
    ...where he visited cultural institutions and witnessed the forging of a partnership between Taiwan's National Museum of Taiwan Literature and the United Kingdom's National Centre for Writing.
    ...參訪了當地文化機構，並見證了台灣國立台灣文學館與英國國家寫作中心之間夥伴關係的締結。
  • schedule v. 預定、安排
    He is scheduled to return to Taiwan next Wednesday.
    他預計於下週三返回台灣。
  • cooperation n. 合作
    During the European visit, Li also met with cultural and literary figures and discussed further cooperation in areas including literature, comics and digital arts.
    在歐洲訪問期間，李遠也與文化及文學界人士會面，並討論在文學、漫畫和數位藝術等領域的進一步合作。
  • potential adj. 潛在的
    In France, he visited a planned cultural facility in Saint-Denis and discussed potential cooperation with Taiwan in digital art...
    在法國，他參觀了聖德尼一座規劃中的文化設施，並討論了台灣與之在數位藝術方面的潛在合作...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞當後位修飾語 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    Speaking to Radio France Internationale (RFI) on Friday, Li said...
    句首的「Speaking...」是由連接詞引導的副詞子句簡化而來（原句約為 As he spoke to...），現在分詞短語用來修飾主詞 Li，表示「當他正在對...發言時」，是多益閱讀測驗中常見的句構。
  • 過去分詞當後位修飾語 (Past Participle as Post-modifier)
    ...where he attended events related to the Taiwan-France Cultural Award...
    「related to the Taiwan-France Cultural Award」是一個過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞 events。這裡省略了關代與be動詞（which are related to...），在多益商業英文中經常用來修飾文件、活動或計畫。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    He is scheduled to return to Taiwan next Wednesday.
    句型「be scheduled to」採用了被動語態的概念，用來表達「預定於何時做某事」。在多益職場情境中，常出現在描述行程、會議或航班安排的考題裡。
