社群媒體數據顯示民進黨面臨逆風

Social media data show DPP faces headwinds

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • headwinds n. 逆風、阻力、困難
    Social media analytics show that Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) candidates in next month’s local elections, including Legislator Puma Shen (沈柏文), continue to face headwinds, despite strong online engagement, the party said yesterday.
    民進黨昨天表示，社群媒體分析顯示，下個月地方選舉的民進黨候選人（包括立法委員沈柏文）儘管擁有強烈的網路參與度，但仍持續面臨逆風。
  • engagement n. 互動、參與度
    Social media analytics show that Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) candidates in next month’s local elections, including Legislator Puma Shen (沈柏文), continue to face headwinds, despite strong online engagement, the party said yesterday.
    民進黨昨天表示，社群媒體分析顯示，下個月地方選舉的民進黨候選人（包括立法委員沈柏文）儘管擁有強烈的網路參與度，但仍持續面臨逆風。
  • consultancy n. 顧問公司、諮詢公司
    DPP New Media Center director Juan Chun-ta (阮俊達) made the comments citing a study conducted by analytics consultancy QSearch from Monday last week to Sunday.
    民進黨新媒體中心主任阮俊達引述分析顧問公司QSearch從上週一到週日進行的一項研究，作出了上述評論。
  • opponent n. 對手、敵手
    His main opponent, Taipei Mayor Chiang Wan-an (蔣萬安) of the Chinese Nationalist Party (KMT), featured in 1,643,538 social media posts, with 55.8 percent negative and 11.7 percent positive, or 44 percent net negativity, he said.
    他說，其主要對手、中國國民黨（國民黨）籍台北市長蔣萬安出現在1,643,538篇社群媒體貼文中，其中55.8%為負面、11.7%為正面，淨負面比率達44%。
  • campaign n. 競選活動、競選陣營
    The Chiang campaign’s attacks targeting Shen’s stance on drug policy and the death penalty, as well as personal attacks using a childhood parent-teacher notebook of the DPP candidate, have increased Shen’s negative coverage by 10 points in the past two weeks, Juan said.
    阮俊達表示，蔣營針對沈柏文在毒品政策與死刑立場的攻擊，以及使用該民進黨候選人童年聯絡簿進行的人身攻擊，在過去兩週內使沈柏文的負面報導增加了10個百分點。
  • underdog n. 居於劣勢者、不被看好者
    Shen remains the underdog in the race across all polls, he said.
    他說，在所有民調中，沈柏文在選戰中仍然居於劣勢。
  • morale n. 士氣、民心
    KMT supporters and the Taiwan People’s Party (TPP) base have been silent on social media due to low morale, but that does not mean they have lost the motivation to vote, he said.
    他說，國民黨支持者與台灣民眾黨（民眾黨）基本盤因士氣低落而在社群媒體上保持沈默，但這並不意味著他們失去了投票的動力。
  • motivation n. 動機、積極性
    KMT supporters and the Taiwan People’s Party (TPP) base have been silent on social media due to low morale, but that does not mean they have lost the motivation to vote, he said.
    他說，國民黨支持者與台灣民眾黨（民眾黨）基本盤因士氣低落而在社群媒體上保持沈默，但這並不意味著他們失去了投票的動力。
  • initiate v. 發起、創始、開始
    Although no foreign power has initiated a large-scale saturation cognitive warfare attack yet, bots have become increasingly sophisticated in manipulating public opinion and researchers have found an increasing number of bot campaigns, he said.
    他說，儘管尚未有外國勢力發動大規模的飽和式認知戰攻擊，但網路機器人（bots）在操縱輿論方面變得日益精進，研究人員也發現越來越多的機器人網軍行動。
  • manipulate v. 操縱、巧妙處理
    Although no foreign power has initiated a large-scale saturation cognitive warfare attack yet, bots have become increasingly sophisticated in manipulating public opinion and researchers have found an increasing number of bot campaigns, he said.
    他說，儘管尚未有外國勢力發動大規模的飽和式認知戰攻擊，但網路機器人（bots）在操縱輿論方面變得日益精進，研究人員也發現越來越多的機器人網軍行動。
  • vigilant adj. 警戒的、警惕的
    The public should remain vigilant about foreign-directed election meddling, he said.
    他說，大眾應對外國主導的選舉干預保持高度警覺。
  • perception n. 感知、看法、洞察力
    A voter’s perception of progress or lack of progress in a campaign could be affected by a social media “echo chamber” that does not correspond to reality, Juan said.
    阮俊達表示，選民對選情進展或缺乏進展的看法，可能會受到不符合現實的社群媒體「同溫層」所影響。
  • endorse v. 公開支持、背書
    DPP supporters should openly endorse the party’s candidates to help boost real support, he added.
    他補充說，民進黨支持者應該公開表態支持黨的候選人，以助推真實的支持度。

文法重點

  • Despite 引導的讓步片語
    Social media analytics show that Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) candidates in next month’s local elections, including Legislator Puma Shen (沈柏文), continue to face headwinds, despite strong online engagement, the party said yesterday.
    「despite」是介系詞，後面接名詞或名詞片語（在此為 strong online engagement），用來表達「儘管……卻……」的讓步語氣，等同於 「in spite of」。
  • 分詞構句作為後置修飾（過去分詞）
    Shen, the party’s candidate for Taipei mayor — featured in 2,324,543 social media posts, with 42.8 percent of them negative and 28.2 percent positive, a “net negativity” rate of 14.6 percent, Juan said.
    句中的「featured in...」是省略了「who was」的過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「Shen」，說明他被收錄或提及於貼文中，使句子更為精簡。
  • 被動語態的運用
    A voter’s perception of progress or lack of progress in a campaign could be affected by a social media “echo chamber” that does not correspond to reality, Juan said.
    句型「could be affected by」使用了被動語態（助動詞 + be + 過去分詞），強調選民的看法是「受到」社群媒體同溫層所影響，突顯受事者的角色。
  • 關係代名詞子句（that 子句）
    A voter’s perception of progress or lack of progress in a campaign could be affected by a social media “echo chamber” that does not correspond to reality, Juan said.
    「that does not correspond to reality」是一個由關代「that」引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾先行詞「a social media 'echo chamber'」，補充說明這個同溫層的特徵（不符合現實）。
