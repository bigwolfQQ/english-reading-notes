Agency unveils southern Taiwan tourism campaign

單字解析

  • campaign n. 活動，競選運動
    The Tourism Administration yesterday unveiled a new marketing campaign — which includes airline partnerships, travel platforms, tourism products and international content creators — to attract international visitors to southern Taiwan.
    觀光署昨天推出一項全新的行銷活動——其中包括與航空公司合作、旅遊平台、旅遊產品以及國際內容創作者——旨在吸引國際旅客前往南臺灣。
  • partnership n. 合作關係，合夥
    The campaign offers more than NT$10 million (US$315,298) in travel incentives through partnerships with EVA Air, ezTravel and Klook, officials said.
    官方表示，該活動透過與長榮航空、易遊網和客路合作，提供超過新台幣 1,000 萬元（合 315,298 美元）的旅遊獎勵。
  • incentive n. 激勵，獎勵，優惠

    The campaign goes beyond destination exposure by connecting marketing with bookable travel products, aiming to turn international interest into actual arrivals, they said.
    他們表示，該活動超越了單純的目的地曝光，將行銷與可預訂的旅遊產品結合，旨在將國際旅客的興趣轉化為實際造訪人數。

文法重點

  • 非限定關係代名詞子句（補充說明）
    The Tourism Administration yesterday unveiled a new marketing campaign — which includes airline partnerships, travel platforms, tourism products and international content creators — to attract international visitors to southern Taiwan.
    句中的「— which includes... —」使用了破折號夾入一個非限定關係子句，對前面的「a new marketing campaign」進行補充說明。這種結構在商業英文與新聞寫法中很常見，用來在不打斷主要句意的情況下塞入細節。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾（簡化形容詞子句）
    The Check in and Discover Southern Taiwan campaign, organized by the Siraya National Scenic Area Administration, brings together four national scenic areas...
    句中的「organized by the Siraya National Scenic Area Administration」是由過去分詞開頭的片語，用來修飾前面的主詞「The Check in and Discover Southern Taiwan campaign」。它的完整原貌是「which is organized by...」，省略了「which is」之後讓句子更簡潔流暢。
  • 現在分詞表結果（分詞構句）
    The campaign goes beyond destination exposure by connecting marketing with bookable travel products, aiming to turn international interest into actual arrivals, they said.
    句尾的「aiming to turn...」是現在分詞片語作結果或目的的補充說明，主詞「The campaign」本身就是執行「aim（旨在）」這個動作的主體。這種用法能有效串連動作，避免使用過多的連接詞。
  • 對等連接詞連接多個受詞或名詞片語
    EzTravel has launched a dedicated Discover Southern Taiwan Web page featuring 32 travel products, with NT$500 discounts available on up to 20,000 bookings, for a total value of NT$10 million.
    句中的「feature」使用了現在分詞「featuring」來修飾前面的「Web page」，後面接著列舉細節與附帶狀況（with NT$500 discounts...），展現了多益英文中常見的長句結構，透過分詞與獨立分詞結構（with + 名詞 + 形容詞/分詞）來堆疊商業資訊。
