農業部將放寬種子、種苗販售規定

MOA to relax seed, seedling sale rules

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • operator n. 經營者、業者
    Seed and seedling business operators without a fixed place of business could apply for a plant seed enterprise registration certificate at the end of this month at the earliest, the Ministry of Agriculture (MOA) said.
    農業部表示，沒有固定營業場所的種子與種苗業者，最快這個月底即可申請植物種苗業登記證。
  • apply for v. phr. 申請
    Seed and seedling business operators without a fixed place of business could apply for a plant seed enterprise registration certificate at the end of this month at the earliest, the Ministry of Agriculture (MOA) said.
    農業部表示，沒有固定營業場所的種子與種苗業者，最快這個月底即可申請植物種苗業登記證。
  • stipulate v. 規定、約定
    The Plant Variety and Plant Seed Act (植物品種及種苗法) stipulates that seed and seedling businesses cannot operate without such a certificate, and those who want to apply for the certificate need to own a fixed place of business.
    《植物品種及種苗法》規定，種苗業者未持有此類證照不得營業，而想要申請證照的人必須擁有固定的營業場所。
  • fine v. 處以罰鍰
    Those who sell seeds or seedlings without a certificate could be fined by local authorities, the act states.
    該法指出，未持有證照而販售種子或種苗者，可由地方主管機關處以罰鍰。
  • authority n. 當局、官方機構
    Those who sell seeds or seedlings without a certificate could be fined by local authorities, the act states.
    該法指出，未持有證照而販售種子或種苗者，可由地方主管機關處以罰鍰。
  • relax v. 放寬（規定、限制）
    Given the booming online demand for seeds and seedlings, requirements for the certificate would be relaxed, it said.
    該部指出，鑑於對種子和種苗蓬勃發展的線上需求，將放寬對證照的要求。
  • issue v. 發布、發行
    In June, it issued an administrative interpretation to local governments, saying that registration certificates are not required for the sale of seeds or seedlings that are edible, or for ornamental or educational purposes.
    該部於 6 月向地方政府發布了一項行政解釋，表示販售食用、觀賞或教育用途的種子或種苗不需要登記證。
  • revise v. 修改、修訂（法規、文件等）
    To ensure sellers’ rights, the ministry’s Agriculture and Food Agency announced it would revise the Requirements and Facility Standards for the Qualification of Plant Seed Enterprises (種苗業者應具備條件及設備標準) to align with the trend of e-commerce...
    為保障賣家權益，該部農糧署宣布將修正《種苗業者應具備條件及設備標準》，以順應電子商務的趨勢...
  • aim v. 旨在、目的是
    Vegetable and Seedling Industry Division section chief Fu Li-chung (傅立忠) said the regulatory change aims to make local administrative work more flexible.
    種苗組科長傅立忠表示，此次法規修改旨在使地方行政工作更有彈性。
  • implement v. 實施、執行
    If no disagreement is put forward during the period, the revised act and requirements would be implemented by the end of this month at the earliest, it added.
    該部並補充說，若在此期間沒有提出不同意見，修正後的法規與要求最快將於本月底實施。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Draft revisions regarding the relaxation of restrictions for the certificate application have a 14-day notice period, the ministry said.
    句中的「regarding the relaxation of restrictions for the certificate application」是由分詞片語組成，用來修飾前面的名詞 revisions（修訂草案），說明這些草案的內容是關於「放寬證照申請的限制」。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Those who sell seeds or seedlings without a certificate could be fined by local authorities, the act states.
    句中的「could be fined」使用了被動語態（助動詞 + be + 過去分詞），強調未持有證照的賣家「會被」地方主管機關處以罰鍰，焦點在於受罰者身上。
  • 條件句與假設用法 (Conditional Clause with 'If')
    If no disagreement is put forward during the period, the revised act and requirements would be implemented by the end of this month at the earliest, it added.
    此句使用「If...」引導條件子句，表達「如果在此期間沒有提出不同意見，修訂後的法規就會實施」。主句使用助動詞 would 搭配原形動詞，展現對未來可能情況的推論。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    The situation continued until a horticultural operator allegedly filed multiple reports to several local governments regarding online seed and seedling sellers that were operating without a certificate.
    句尾的「that were operating without a certificate」是限制性關係子句，以關代 that 代替前面的「online seed and seedling sellers」（網路種苗賣家），並補充說明這些賣家當時的違規營業狀態。
