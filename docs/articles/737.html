台灣計畫進行資料查核以辨識不適宜駕駛的駕駛人

Taiwan plans data checks to identify medically unfit drivers

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • identify v. 辨識，識別
    Taiwan plans to allow road authorities to use health data to identify drivers who may no longer be medically fit to hold a license, the government said Tuesday.
    政府週二表示，台灣計畫允許公路主管機關利用健康資料，來辨識可能不再符合醫療適格條件而持有駕照的駕駛人。
  • authority n. 官方，當局，主管機關
    The Ministry of Transportation and Communications (MOTC) said that the proposed system would cover drivers with visual impairments, epilepsy and moderate or severe dementia.
    交通部（MOTC）表示，該擬議中的系統將涵蓋患有視力障礙、癲癇以及中度或重度失智症的駕駛人。
  • periodically adv. 定期地，週期性地
    Under the plan, road authorities would periodically obtain data on the three conditions held by government agencies, including information collected from people applying for certain government subsidies or insurance programs.
    根據該計畫，公路主管機關將定期取得政府機關所掌握關於這三種狀況的資料，包括從申請特定政府補助或保險計畫的人員所收集的資訊。
  • subsidy n. 補助金，津貼
    Under the plan, road authorities would periodically obtain data on the three conditions held by government agencies, including information collected from people applying for certain government subsidies or insurance programs.
    根據該計畫，公路主管機關將定期取得政府機關所掌握關於這三種狀況的資料，包括從申請特定政府補助或保險計畫的人員所收集的資訊。
  • undergo v. 經歷，接受（審查、檢驗等）
    Drivers identified as potentially no longer fit to drive would be asked to undergo a new qualification review.
    被識別為可能不再適宜駕駛的駕駛人將被要求接受新的資格審查。
  • surrender v. 交出，放棄，繳回
    Those who fail would have to surrender their licenses.
    未通過者將必須繳回駕照。
  • take effect phr. 生效，實施
    About 47,000 drivers could be affected by the system, which is expected to take effect by the end of 2026, the MOTC said.
    交通部表示，約有 47,000 名駕駛人可能會受到該系統影響，該系統預計於 2026 年底前生效實施。
  • renew v. 換新，延期（如駕照、護照、合約）
    Currently, such checks may not take place until drivers renew their licenses.
    目前，這類查核可能要等到駕駛人換發駕照時才會進行。
  • address v. 處理，解決（問題、狀況）
    The MOTC said the proposed system is intended to address cases in which drivers develop medical conditions or experience age-related changes after receiving their licenses that could affect their ability to drive safely.
    交通部長表示，該擬議系統旨在處理駕駛人在領取駕照後，罹患醫療狀況或經歷與年齡相關的變化，而可能影響其安全駕駛能力的情況。
  • amendment n. 修正案，修改
    The proposal follows amendments to the Road Traffic Management and Penalty Act promulgated last November.
    該提案是在去年 11 月公布修正《道路交通管理處罰條例》之後提出的。
  • promulgate v. 頒布，公佈（法律等）
    The proposal follows amendments to the Road Traffic Management and Penalty Act promulgated last November.
    該提案是在去年 11 月公布修正《道路交通管理處罰條例》之後提出的。
  • regulation n. 規章，法規
    The proposed regulations set out what data government agencies would be permitted to provide and how authorities could collect, process and use it.
    擬議中的法規規定了政府機關獲准提供哪些資料，以及主管機關如何收集、處理和使用這些資料。
  • in accordance with phr. 依照，與……一致
    The ministry said the information collected would be limited to visual function, epilepsy and moderate or severe dementia, in accordance with the principle of data minimization.
    該部表示，根據資料最小化原則，所收集的資訊將僅限於視力功能、癲癇以及中度或重度失智症。

文法重點

  • 關係代間子句（修飾主詞）
    Taiwan plans to allow road authorities to use health data to identify drivers who may no longer be medically fit to hold a license, the government said Tuesday.
    句中的「who may no longer be medically fit to hold a license」是關係代間子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「drivers（駕駛人）」，限定並描述這些駕駛人的特徵。
  • 分詞片語作後置修飾（過去分詞）
    Drivers identified as potentially no longer fit to drive would be asked to undergo a new qualification review.
    句首的「Drivers identified as...」中，過去分詞片語「identified as...」修飾前面的名詞「Drivers」，相當於「Drivers who are identified as...」，省略了主格關代與be動詞，使句子更為精簡。
  • 被動語態
    About 47,000 drivers could be affected by the system, which is expected to take effect by the end of 2026, the MOTC said.
    句中的「could be affected」與「is expected」皆使用了被動語態（助動詞 + be + 過去分詞），強調動作的承受者（47,000名駕駛人與系統本身），在多益商業新聞中非常常見。
  • 條件句片語 (Under...) 與名詞子句
    Under current regulations, people with epilepsy, for example, may apply for a driver's license if they provide a doctor's certificate showing they have been free of seizures for at least two years.
    句首使用「Under current regulations」作為介系詞片語表達條件或依據；後方的「if they provide...」引導條件副詞子句；而「showing they have been...」中的「showing」後方則省略了連接詞 that，接了一個名詞子句作為受詞，結構層次豐富。
