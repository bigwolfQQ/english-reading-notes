Taipei Times · 2026-10-14

波灣地區已進入不穩定新時代

The Gulf has entered a new era of instability

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • fatality n. 死亡人數，災禍
    In Saudi Arabia, a country that is unaccustomed to such fatalities, the death toll is bewilderingly high and likely to prompt a bloody response.
    在沙烏地阿拉伯這個不習慣此類傷亡的國家，死亡人數高得令人困惑，並可能引發血腥的回應。
  • temporary adj. 暫時的，臨時的
    Over the past year, the Gulf has entered a new era of instability that is not temporary and cannot be reversed.
    在過去的一年裡，波灣地區進入了一個既非暫時也無法逆轉的不穩定新時代。
  • retaliation n. 報復
    In retaliation for what Iran perceives to be political, military and logistical support for the offensive against it, Tehran struck Saudi Arabia, Qatar, the UAE, Bahrain, Kuwait and Oman.
    為了報復伊朗認為針對其的進攻所提供的政治、軍事和後勤支援，德黑蘭打擊了沙烏地阿拉伯、卡達、阿聯、巴林、科威特和阿曼。
  • logistical adj. 後勤的，物流的
    In retaliation for what Iran perceives to be political, military and logistical support for the offensive against it, Tehran struck Saudi Arabia, Qatar, the UAE, Bahrain, Kuwait and Oman.
    為了報復伊朗認為針對其的進攻所提供的政治、軍事和後勤支援，德黑蘭打擊了沙烏地阿拉伯、卡達、阿聯、巴林、科威特和阿曼。
  • infrastructure n. 基礎設施
    Iran targeted military sites, energy facilities, desalination plants — an entire infrastructure that underpins the economies of these countries.
    伊朗的目標是軍事基地、能源設施、海水淡化廠——支撐這些國家經濟的整個基礎設施。
  • underpin v. 支撐，作為...的基礎
    Iran targeted military sites, energy facilities, desalination plants — an entire infrastructure that underpins the economies of these countries.
    伊朗的目標是軍事基地、能源設施、海水淡化廠——支撐這些國家經濟的整個基礎設施。
  • suspension n. 暫停，中止，停職
    Much damage was inflicted by the suspension of normal life, the shuttering of schools for months, the closure of airports and airspace.
    正常生活的暫停、學校關閉數月、機場和領空的關閉，造成了巨大的破壞。
  • disrupt v. 干擾，打斷，使中斷
    The most disastrous consequence of all, from the perspective of Gulf rulers, has been the way the attacks have disrupted the integration of these countries into the cosmopolitan international community as places for tourism, entrepreneurs, sports and entertainment.
    從波灣統治者的角度來看，所有後果中最災難性的，是這些攻擊打擾了這些國家融入將其視為觀光、企業家、體育和娛樂場所的國際大都會社群之進程。
  • franchise n. 經銷權，特許經營權
    They spent billions in securing Formula One, golf and boxing franchises and tournaments, only for a flurry of cheap drones to put it all at risk.
    他們斥巨資爭取一級方程式賽車、高爾夫球和拳擊的特許經營權與錦標賽，結果卻被一陣廉價的無人機將一切置於風險之中。
  • commercial adj. 商業的
    The closure of the Strait of Hormuz, for example, shifted more commercial traffic along Yemen’s coastline, prompting the Houthis to make advances and seize territory to gain control of the now crucial Bab al-Mandab Strait in the Red Sea.
    例如，荷莫茲海峽的關閉將更多的商業交通轉移到葉門海岸線，促使胡塞武裝推進並奪取領土，以控制紅海中現已至關重要的曼德海峽。
  • assistance n. 協助，援助
    Last month, the Trump administration refused to extend military assistance to Saudi Arabia to fight the Houthis, after a meeting of US officials with the Houthis, who assured them that they would not attack US ships in the Red Sea, only Saudi vessels.
    上個月，在美國官員與胡塞武裝會面後，川普政府拒絕提供軍事援助給沙烏地阿拉伯以打擊胡塞武裝，而胡塞武裝向美方保證他們不會攻擊紅海上的美國船隻，只會攻擊沙烏地船隻。
  • capital n. 資本，資金，首都
    The Houthi resurgence represents a new reality in a region that is bewildered and losing lives and capital, stuck between old alliances that no longer work and new hostilities that are too calamitous to confront.
    胡塞武裝的死灰復燃代表了一個地區的新現實，這個地區感到困惑並正在失去生命與資本，卡在不再管用的舊同盟與過於災難而無法面對的新敵意之間。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    At the time of writing, the missile strike and another one on Sunday on King Khalid International Airport have killed at least 14 people and left 300 injured, marking a significant turn in a regional conflagration that is only getting more intense.
  • 過去分詞作後位修飾 (Past Participle as Postmodifier)
    The first audacious and unthinkable thing happened just more than a year ago, when Iran fired missiles at an airbase in Qatar that hosts US troops, in retaliation for US strikes on Iranian nuclear facilities.
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    Over the past year, the Gulf has entered a new era of instability that is not temporary and cannot be reversed.
  • 假設語氣/條件句應用 (Conditional Structure)
    If the 2015 campaign is anything to go by, it would claim thousands of lives with only a remote possibility of a decisive outcome.
