Most expect housing prices to stabilize in Q4: Survey

  • respondent n. 受訪者

  • stabilize v. 使穩定，平穩

    該調查還發現，50.2%的受訪者表示未來兩年不是買房的好時機，與6月份進行的類似民調相比，下降了近10個百分點。

  • property market n. 房地產市場
    In a statement, Chuang Ssu-min, deputy head of Chinatrust's research department, said that with inflationary pressure on the rise, more potential homebuyers were inclined to enter the property market to hedge against inflation.
    中信房屋研究部副理莊思敏在聲明中表示，隨著通膨壓力上升，更多潛在購屋者傾向進入房地產市場以對抗通膨。

    莊思敏指出，尋求自住購屋的受訪者數量也有所增加，顯示當地房市信心正在復甦。
  • mortgage n. 房貸，抵押貸款
    In March, the central bank relaxed its selective credit controls on second-home mortgages for individual buyers following a seventh round of restrictions imposed in June 2024.
    今年3月，繼2024年6月實施第七波限制措施後，央行放寬了對個人買方第二戶房貸的選擇性信用管制。
  • lending n. 借貸，放款
    Chuang said there is room for the central bank to further ease its selective credit controls on the housing market, as the ratio of home mortgages to banks' total lending has shown signs of falling.
    莊思敏表示，由於銀行總放款中房貸的比例已出現下降跡象，央行進一步放寬房市選擇性信用管制仍有空間。

    She said further easing could encourage more potential owner-occupier homebuyers to enter the market and boost market momentum.
    她表示，進一步放寬可以鼓勵更多潛在的自住購屋者進入市場，並提振市場動能。

    Chuang said the poll showed that more than 50 percent of respondents supported either keeping rates unchanged or lowering them, as they hoped to avoid an increase in their financial burden by paying higher interest when buying a home.
    莊思敏表示，民調顯示超過50%的受訪者支持維持利率不變或調降利率，因為他們希望避免在買房時因支付較高利息而增加財務負擔。

文法重點

  • with + 名詞 + 分詞（複合結構/附帶狀況）
    In a statement, Chuang Ssu-min (莊思敏), deputy head of Chinatrust's research department, said that with inflationary pressure on the rise, more potential homebuyers were inclined to enter the property market to hedge against inflation.
    「with + 名詞 + 形容詞/分詞」結構用來表示附帶狀況，意思是「隨著……（情況發生）」。在此句中，「with inflationary pressure on the rise」意指「隨著通膨壓力不斷上升」，用來補充說明潛在購屋者傾向進入市場的原因。
  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾
    The number of respondents looking to buy a home for their own use also increased, indicating a recovery in confidence in the local housing market, Chuang said.
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    In March, the central bank relaxed its selective credit controls on second-home mortgages for individual buyers following a seventh round of restrictions imposed in June 2024.
