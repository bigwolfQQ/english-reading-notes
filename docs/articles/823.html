文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 / 表附帶狀況
    Moolenaar released an official statement after meeting with a delegation of Chinese Nationalist Party (KMT) lawmakers on Capitol Hill in Washington.
    句中的 "after meeting with..." 是由介系詞後接動名詞短語構成，用來表示時間或動作發生的先後順序（在與……會面之後）。
  • 完成式分詞構句
    Moolenaar said that, having met with members of the leading parties in Taiwan, they “all share the belief that there is an urgent need to strengthen Taiwan’s defense”...
    句中的 "having met with..." 是現在分詞的完成式 (Having + p.p.)，用來表示該動作（會面）發生在主句謂語動詞（said）之前。
  • 形容詞片語作後位修飾
    The party fully supports the key defense spending necessary to maintain Taiwan’s self-defense capabilities...
    形容詞短語 "necessary to maintain Taiwan’s self-defense capabilities" 放於名詞 "spending" 後方，用來補充修飾該國防預算。
  • 非限定關代子句（帶有插入語性質）
    The party referenced its proposal to hold a referendum on restarting nuclear power, which could be used to provide a stable supply of electricity during a blockade or crisis.
    句尾的 ", which could be used to..." 是非限定關代子句，逗號前的主句內容作為先行詞，用來補充說明該公投提案的功能。
