社論：蔣萬安的名聲正在侵蝕

EDITORIAL: Chiang’s reputation is eroding

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • controversy n. 爭議、爭論
    Taipei Mayor Chiang Wan-an (蔣萬安) was under constant fire last month over the city government’s questionable public works projects and child protection issues, and last week he sparked another controversy involving his son.
    台北市長蔣萬安上個月因市府有爭議的公共工程和兒童保護問題而飽受批評，上週他又挑起了一場涉及他兒子的爭議。
  • participate v. 參與、參加
    Chiang on Monday last week confirmed that his eldest son, who was recently admitted to Taipei Municipal Chien Kuo Senior High School, would take a leave of absence to participate in a year-long exchange program in the US.
    蔣萬安上週一證實，他最近考上台北市立建國高級中學的大兒子，將請假參與在美國為期一年的交換計畫。
  • eligible adj. 符合條件的、有資格的
    The exchange program Chiang’s son was selected for is funded by the Taipei City Government, and is limited to 25 spots for eligible junior-high and high-school students.
    蔣萬安的兒子被選中的交換計畫是由台北市政府資助，且名額只限25名符合資格的國中與高中生。
  • advantage n. 優勢、有利條件
    Chiang was accused of giving his son an unfair advantage in the selection process due to his position, fueling public criticism that the mayor, who should act in the public interest, is instead competing for limited public resources.
    蔣萬安被指控利用職權在選拔過程中賦予兒子不公平的優勢，助長了公眾的批評，即本應以公眾利益為優先的市長，卻在與有限的公共資源競爭。
  • conflict of interest n. phr. 利益衝突
    While city councilors and residents questioned why Chiang did not recuse himself to avoid a conflict of interest, they also questioned why Chiang’s son needed to apply for the program...
    當市議員和居民質疑蔣萬安為何不主動迴避以避免利益衝突時，他們也質疑蔣萬安的兒子為何需要申請該計畫...
  • subsidy n. 補助金、津貼
    Chiang on Wednesday said that his family decided to turn down the subsidy without withdrawing from the exchange program and insisted that his son was selected without preferential treatment.
    蔣萬安週三表示，他的家庭決定放棄補助但不會退出交換計畫，並堅稱他的兒子是在沒有受到特權待遇的情況下被選中的。
  • violate v. 違反、違背
    Although this does not violate any law, it has a considerable impact on his political reputation, as it differs from an unwritten tradition.
    儘管這並未違反任何法律，但這對他的政治聲譽產生了相當大的影響，因為這與不成文的傳統不同。
  • impact n. 影響、衝擊
    Although this does not violate any law, it has a considerable impact on his political reputation, as it differs from an unwritten tradition.
    儘管這並未違反任何法律，但這對他的政治聲譽產生了相當大的影響，因為這與不成文的傳統不同。
  • donate v. 捐贈、捐獻
    Former mayors across party lines, including Chen Shui-bian (陳水扁), Ma Ying-jeou (馬英九), Hau Lung-bin (郝龍斌) and Ko Wen-je (柯文哲) have all donated the subsidy to public welfare foundations and charitable groups.
    跨黨派的前市長，包括陳水扁、馬英九、郝龍斌和柯文哲，都將補助捐給了社會福利基金會和慈善團體。
  • resolve v. 解決、下決心
    However, Chiang continues to dodge accountability and fails to take any action in resolving the issues.
    然而，蔣萬安繼續逃避問責，並未採取任何行動來解決這些問題。
  • intervene v. 介入、干涉
    Even if Chiang did not intervene directly during the exchange program review, many believe education department officials would naturally feel pressured to approve Chiang’s son’s application...
    即使蔣萬安在交換計畫審查期間沒有直接介入，許多人仍然認為教育局官員自然會感到壓力而批准蔣萬安兒子的申請...
  • trust n. 信任、信賴
    These doubts are eroding public trust in his ability to serve Taipei’s residents, let alone all Taiwanese nationals, as an opinion poll had shown that he is considered the Chinese Nationalist Party’s (KMT) best presidential candidate for 2028.
    這些疑慮正在侵蝕大眾對他服務台北市居民能力的信任，更不用說所有台灣國民了，正如一項民意調查顯示，他被認為是中國國民黨（KMT）2028年最佳的總統候選人。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作為後置修飾語 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The exchange program Chiang’s son was selected for is funded by the Taipei City Government, and is limited to 25 spots for eligible junior-high and high-school students.
    句中的 "funded by the Taipei City Government" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的主詞 "The exchange program"，相當於關代子句 "which is funded by..." 的簡化，能讓句子更精簡流暢。
  • 讓步子句 (Concessive Clause with Although)
    Although this does not violate any law, it has a considerable impact on his political reputation, as it differs from an unwritten tradition.
    使用 "Although" 引導副詞子句來表達「儘管……但是……」的讓步語氣，突顯雖然合法但仍會造成負面影響的對比。
  • 現在分詞片語表達附帶結果 (Present Participial Phrase indicating Result)
    Chiang was accused of giving his son an unfair advantage in the selection process due to his position, fueling public criticism that the mayor... is instead competing for limited public resources.
    句尾的 ", fueling public criticism..." 是現在分詞片語作結果狀語，表示前面整件事情所引發的自然結果（助長了公眾批評），在新聞英文中非常常見。
  • 動名詞片語作受詞 (Gerund Phrase as Object)
    However, Chiang continues to dodge accountability and fails to take any action in resolving the issues.
    介系詞 "in" 後方接動名詞片語 "resolving the issues" 作為其受詞，用以說明在「解決問題」這件事情上採取（或未採取）行動。
