孟加拉麻疹兒童死亡人數逼近 1,000 人

Bangladesh measles child toll nears 1,000

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • outbreak n. （疾病、暴動等的）爆發，發作
    Bangladesh’s deadliest measles outbreak in decades is nearing the grim milestone of 1,000 child deaths...
    孟加拉數十年來最致命的麻疹疫情正逼近兒童死亡人數 1,000 人的慘痛里程碑……
  • surges v. 急遽上升，激增
    ...while suspected and confirmed cases have surged past 180,000, Bangladeshi Ministry of Health and Family Welfare figures showed.
    ……而孟加拉衛生與家庭福利部數據顯示，疑似與確診病例已激增超過 18 萬例。
  • contagious adj. 具傳染性的，會傳染的
    Measles is highly contagious, spreading through coughs and sneezes, and has no specific treatment once caught.
    麻疹具有高度傳染性，透過咳嗽和打噴嚏傳播，一旦感染並沒有特效藥。
  • vulnerable adj. 易受傷的，脆弱的
    Although anyone can catch the disease, young children are particularly vulnerable.
    雖然任何人都有可能感染此疾病，但幼童特別脆弱。
  • admitted v. 准許進入，（尤指住院）收治
    He was first admitted to hospital with pneumonia in July, then contracted measles before finally securing a bed at the capital’s specialized children’s hospital last month.
    他先是在 7 月因肺炎被送進醫院，接著又感染了麻疹，最後才在上個月於首都的專科兒童醫院取得一張病床。
  • malnutrition n. 營養不良
    Doctors said many of the children arriving at the hospital are already critically ill, with malnutrition further weakening their ability to fight the virus.
    醫生表示，抵達醫院的許多孩子病情已經危急，營養不良進一步削弱了他們對抗病毒的能力。
  • delayed v. 延誤，使延期
    ...but a measles drive planned for 2024 was delayed by the uprising that toppled former Bangladeshi prime minister Sheikh Hasina.
    ……但原定於 2024 年推動的麻疹接種行動因推翻前孟加拉總理謝赫·哈西娜的起義而延誤。
  • campaign n. （政治、社會或商業）活動，運動
    Workers’ strikes and political instability delayed a 2025 campaign.
    工人的罷工和政治動盪延誤了 2025 年的宣導活動。
  • combat v. 對抗，打擊
    The country has since rolled out a mass vaccination drive to combat the outbreak, but the number of cases continues to climb.
    此後，該國已經展開大規模疫苗接種行動以對抗疫情，但病例數持續攀升。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附加說明（分詞構句）
    Bangladesh’s deadliest measles outbreak in decades is nearing the grim milestone of 1,000 child deaths, as a devastating epidemic sweeps through the country and crowded hospital wards fill with struggling babies.
    句尾的「as a devastating epidemic sweeps through the country...」是以連接詞引導的原因/同時發生子句；而文章中也有許多如「..., spreading through coughs and sneezes」或「..., cradling her six-month-old son Ayan」等現在分詞片語（短暫省略主詞與be動詞），用來補充說明主句動作發生時伴隨的狀況或結果。
  • 關係代名詞子句（限定與非限定用法）
    Cries of sick children and the hiss of oxygen machines fill the air at Dhaka’s Shishu Hospital, where Rima Akter keeps a vigil beside her nine-month-old son.
    句中的「where Rima Akter keeps a vigil beside her nine-month-old son」是非限定用法的地方關係子句（等同於 at which），前面有逗號，用來補充說明前面先行詞「Dhaka’s Shishu Hospital」發生的地點狀況。
  • 被動語態的使用
    More than 1,000 suspected cases are reported daily.
    新聞報導經常使用被動語態來強調「動作的承受者」或當動作執行者不明/不重要時的情形。此句中「are reported」即為現在式被動語態，強調每天有超過 1,000 起疑似病例被通報出來。
