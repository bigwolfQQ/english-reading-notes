文法重點

  • 非限定性關係子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    The annual training course, which has been held for decades, is intended to provide professional training for leaders in the region...
    句中的「which has been held for decades」前有逗號，用來補充說明前面的主詞「The annual training course（年度培訓課程）」。非限定性關係子句用來提供額外資訊，若拿掉該子句，主要子句的意思依然完整。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    A MOFA press statement released on Sept. 16 said Foreign Minister Lin Chia-lung had met with Cálix a day earlier.
    句中的「released on Sept. 16」是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞「A MOFA press statement（外交部新聞稿）」。完整概念是「A MOFA press statement which was released on Sept. 16」，省略了「which was」使句子更簡鍊。
  • 動名詞片語作受詞 (Gerund Phrase as Object)
    Han would not comment, however, on whether Cálix's visit was related to the possibility of re-establishing ties...
    介系詞「of」後面接動名詞「re-establishing（重新建立）」，形成動名詞片語「re-establishing ties」作為複合名詞片語「the possibility of...」中介系詞的受詞。英文中介系詞後面若要接動詞，一律必須使用動名詞（V-ing）型態。
