社論：付出的代價

EDITORIAL: Price to pay

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • campaign n. 競選活動
    Kevin Tsai (蔡康永), former host of the variety show Here Comes Kangxi (康熙來了), on Sunday last week attended a campaign event of Legislator Puma Shen (沈伯洋)...
    前綜藝節目《康熙來了》主持人蔡康永，上週日出席了立法委員沈伯洋的競選活動...
  • substantial adj. 大量的、重大的、龐大的
    ...like some other Taiwanese entertainers, has a substantial following in China.
    ...和某些其他台灣藝人一樣，在中國擁有龐大的粉絲群。
  • background n. 背景
    Given his background, people on the other side of the Taiwan Strait were under the impression that he was pan-blue...
    鑑於他的背景，海峽對岸的人們一直印象中他是泛藍的...
  • profit v. 獲利、營利
    Without naming him, they warned that entertainers who profit from the Chinese market while “flirting with Taiwan independence” would “pay a price too heavy to bear.”
    在未點名他的情況下，他們警告那些一邊從中國市場獲利、一邊「與台獨眉來眼去」的藝人將付出「承受不起的代價」。
  • commercial adj. 商業的
    She asked whether he is ready to give up his “massive commercial interests” in China.
    她詢問他是否準備好放棄在中國的「龐大商業利益」。
  • endorse v. 背書、公開支持
    Chiang said he respects Tsai’s decision to endorse whoever he feels like and invited Tsai to attend one of his own campaign events.
    蔣萬安表示，他尊重蔡康永支持他所心儀對象的決定，並邀請蔡康永出席他自己的其中一場競選活動。
  • interfere v. 干涉、介入
    He said that everyone is free to make their own choices and their freedom should not be interfered with in any form.
    他表示，每個人都有自由做出自己的選擇，且他們的自由不應以任何形式受到干涉。
  • associating v. 交往、往來 (associate 的動名詞)
    By associating with Shen, Tsai runs a great risk of being boycotted, she said.
    她表示，透過與沈伯洋往來，蔡康永冒著被抵制的巨大風險。
  • risk n. 風險
    By associating with Shen, Tsai runs a great risk of being boycotted, she said.
    她表示，透過與沈伯洋往來，蔡康永冒著被抵制的巨大風險。
  • tradable adj. 可交易的
    ...Cheng, as leader of the KMT, seems to believe that political ideals are tradable items and that only a fool would give up their own interests.
    ...身為國民黨領導人的鄭麗文似乎認為政治理想是可交易的商品，只有傻瓜才會放棄自己的利益。
  • interests n. 利益
    ...and that only a fool would give up their own interests.
    ...且只有傻瓜才會放棄自己的利益。

文法重點

  • 非限定關代子句 (Non-defining relative clause)
    Tsai, who was born in Taipei and is a second-generation waishengren (Mainlander) whose parents were from Shanghai, like some other Taiwanese entertainers, has a substantial following in China.
    使用 「who...」引導的非限定關代子句來補充說明主詞 Tsai 的身家背景，逗號前後可視為補充資訊，拿掉不影響主要句意（主句為 Tsai has a substantial following in China）。
  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況或原因 (Participial phrase)
    While Tsai said he wanted to hear all sides and that he did not speak, his attendance alone was interpreted by Chinese critics as supporting independence.
    句中的「using a Chinese idiom」是現在分詞片語當作形容詞修飾主詞，用來補充說明梁文傑發言時的動作（一邊使用成語，一邊說...），是多益閱讀與進階寫作常見的精簡表達方式。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Several Chinese businesses have distanced themselves from Tsai, while the Chinese propaganda machine pressed on with the attacks, with a social media commentary platform affiliated with the People’s Daily overseas edition naming Tsai, and saying he had failed to admit wrongdoing or distance himself from Taiwanese independence advocates.
    新聞報導經常使用被動語態來強調動作的承受者，或在不需強調施事者時保持客觀語氣。例如「was interpreted by...」（被...解讀為），將焦點放在行為本身而非批評者身上。
  • 虛詞 it 作為形式主詞 (Dummy subject 'it')
    The shamelessness of the KMT is plain to see.
    雖然此處列出的是名詞片語當主詞，但英文新聞中常使用「It is clear that...」或「It is important to...」來作為形式主詞，將較長的名詞子句或不定詞往後移，維持句子的平衡與流暢度。
