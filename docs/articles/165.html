文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Taiwanese artists and curators on Saturday welcomed the opening of the Gwangju Biennale’s Pavilion Project’s “Taiwan Pavilion,” following a naming controversy that led to China’s withdrawal from the event.
    句中的「following a naming controversy...」是由現在分詞 following 引導的片語，用來補充說明前面主句動作發生之後的情況或隨之而來的事件。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Exhibiting artist Chen Ting-ting (鄭亭亭) said she believed the foundation’s final decision was driven largely by an outpouring of support for Taiwan...
    句中的「driven largely by...」是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的名詞決議（decision），表達該決定「被（大批支持）」所驅動，隱含被動語態的意味。
  • 假設語氣（過去式表達對現在或未來假設） (Subjunctive Mood)
    “If our Pavilion’s name is not given back, we would rather not participate.”
    此句表達條件與結果。雖然使用現在式表達真實條件（If our Pavilion's name is not given back...），但主句使用了「would rather」來表達主觀意願與決心（寧願不參加），是多益閱讀與商務溝通中表達立場的常見句型。
