兄弟隊將在系列賽中對決龍隊

Brothers to take on Dragons in series

單字解析

  • claim v. 取得、奪得、聲稱
    The CPBL Taiwan Series is to begin at Taipei Dome on Saturday after the CTBC Brothers beat the Uni-Lions 8-2 on Monday to claim the last spot in the championship.
    中華職棒台灣大賽將於週六在台北大巨蛋開打，此前中信兄弟於週一以 8 比 2 擊敗統一獅，取得晉級總冠軍賽的最後一個名額。
  • advantage n. 優勢
    CTBC reached their third consecutive Taiwan Series by winning the playoff series 3-2, a tally that includes the one-game advantage it received as the second-half regular-season champion.
    中信以 3 比 2 贏得季後挑戰賽，連續第三年打進台灣大賽，此戰績包含了他們作為下半季冠軍所獲得的一場保送勝優勢。
  • opener n. 首場比賽、開幕戰
    The Brothers won Friday’s opener to move within one win of advancing, but the Uni-Lions took the next two games to force a deciding fourth game on Monday at Taichung Intercontinental Baseball Stadium.
    兄弟隊贏得了週五的首戰，距離晉級僅差一勝，但統一獅隨後拿下接下來的兩場比賽，逼得雙方於週一在台中洲際棒球場進行決勝的第四戰。
  • opponent n. 對手、敵手
    Cheng credited his teammates and recalled his coaches’ pregame message that to win, the team had to want it more than their opponents.
    鄭浩均將功勞歸於隊友，並回憶起教練們在賽前的叮囑：要想贏球，全隊的求勝企圖心必須比對手更強烈。
  • seasoned adj. 經驗豐富的、老練的
    The game was the end of a long career for seasoned Uni-Lions player Chen Yung-chi.
    這場比賽也是統一獅老將陳鏞基漫長職業生涯的尾聲。
  • retirement n. 退休
    The 43-year-old, who had announced his retirement this season, entered at first base in the sixth inning and singled to right field in his final plate appearance in the ninth.
    現年 43 歲的陳鏞基已宣布本季為其退休年，他在第六局以一壘手身分上場，並在第九局的生涯最後一次打席中敲出右外野一壘安打。
  • appearance n. 出賽、露面、登場
    The 43-year-old, who had announced his retirement this season, entered at first base in the sixth inning and singled to right field in his final plate appearance in the ninth.
    現年 43 歲的陳鏞基已宣布本季為其退休年，他在第六局以一壘手身分上場，並在第九局的生涯最後一次打席中敲出右外野一壘安打。
  • acknowledge v. 向…致意、承認、感謝
    Chen removed his cap to acknowledge the crowd before being replaced by a pinch runner and embracing Uni-Lions manager Lin Yueh-ping.
    陳鏞基脫下球帽向全場觀眾致意，隨後被代跑換下，並與統一獅總教練林岳平擁抱。

文法重點

  • be to do（預定、即將）
    The CPBL Taiwan Series is to begin at Taipei Dome on Saturday after the CTBC Brothers beat the Uni-Lions 8-2 on Monday to claim the last spot in the championship.
    「be動詞 + to + 原形動詞」可以用來表示按計畫、安排即將發生或預定要執行的動作，此處用 is to begin 表格台灣大賽「預定」在週六開始。
  • 分詞構句作補充說明（V-ing）
    Chen removed his cap to acknowledge the crowd before being replaced by a pinch runner and embracing Uni-Lions manager Lin Yueh-ping.
    句尾的「before being replaced... and embracing...」為分詞構句，省略了主詞並將動詞改為 V-ing 形式，用來接續補充說明陳鏞基在脫帽致意之後隨即發生的動作。
  • 非限定關代子句（who 引導）
    The 43-year-old, who had announced his retirement this season, entered at first base in the sixth inning...
    句中「who had announced his retirement this season」是以逗號隔開的非限定關係代名詞子句，對前面的主詞（The 43-year-old / 陳鏞基）進行補充說明，且其發生的動作早于主要子句的動詞，故使用過去完成式 (had announced)。
