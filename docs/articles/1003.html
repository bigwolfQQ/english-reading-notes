習近平預料將在對美訪問中就對台軍售施壓美國

Xi expected to press US on arms sales

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • press v. 敦促、施壓
    Chinese President Xi Jinping (習近平) is expected to press US President Donald Trump to halt Taiwan arms sales under a 1982 joint statement during a visit to Washington this week
    中國國家主席習近平本週訪問華府期間，預計將依據 1982 年的聯合公報，敦促美國總統川普停止對台軍售
  • halt v. 停止、暫停
    Chinese President Xi Jinping (習近平) is expected to press US President Donald Trump to halt Taiwan arms sales under a 1982 joint statement
    中國國家主席習近平預計將依據 1982 年的聯合公報，敦促美國總統川普停止對台軍售
  • bound adj. 受約束的、有義務的
    the US is bound by law to provide Taiwan with the means to defend itself
    美國受法律約束，須提供台灣自我防衛的手段
  • interpretation n. 解釋、詮釋
    Beijing is likely to bring up its interpretation of the third of the Three Joint Communiques between the two countries
    北京很可能提出其對兩國簽署的《中美三個聯合公報》中第三個公報的解讀
  • gradually adv. 逐漸地
    the US said that it intends to gradually reduce its arms sales to Taiwan
    美國表示意圖逐步減少對台灣的軍售
  • commitment n. 承諾、投入
    conditioned absolutely upon the continued commitment of China to the peaceful solution of differences with Taiwan
    絕對取決於中國持續承諾和平解決與台灣的分歧
  • precondition n. 先決條件
    abiding by the Three Joint Communiques and previous US promises was a “necessary precondition” for the stable, healthy development of ties
    恪守《中美三個聯合公報》和先前美國的承諾，是雙邊關係穩定、健康發展的「必要前提」
  • determination n. 決定、決心
    Trump has said he would make a determination on the new arms sales package in a fairly short time
    川普曾表示他將在相當短的時間內對新的軍售方案做出決定
  • deliveries n. 交付、遞送
    China has also asked for the US to stop deliveries of arms already ordered by Taiwan
    中國還要求美國停止交付台灣已經訂購的武器
  • status quo n. 現狀
    and that Taiwan is the one changing the status quo
    且台灣是改變現狀的一方
  • sovereignty n. 主權
    the US officially takes no position on Taiwan’s sovereignty
    美國官方對台灣的主權不採取立場
  • endorse v. 背書、贊同
    and acknowledges, but does not endorse, Beijing’s position that Taiwan is part of China
    並承認但不背書北京關於台灣是中國一部分的立場
  • on hold phr. 暫緩、擱置
    However, a second US$14 billion package has been on hold for months
    然而，第二筆 14 億美元的方案已經擱置數月
  • negotiating chip n. 談判籌碼
    described it as a “very good negotiating chip.”
    並將其形容為「非常好的談判籌碼。」

文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Chinese President Xi Jinping (習近平) is expected to press US President Donald Trump to halt Taiwan arms sales...
    「is expected to...」是被動語態的常見結構，用來表達「預期……、被期望……」。此處主詞是 Xi Jinping，由外界或大眾對他抱持這種預期，因此使用被動語態。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Three sources familiar with discussions between the US and China ahead of Xi’s trip... said that...
    片語「familiar with discussions...」是過去分詞片語，放在名詞 sources 後面用來修飾這些「熟悉討論內容的消息人士」，省略了「who are」，能讓句子更為精煉。
  • 條件句與過去分詞構句 (Conditional meaning with past participle)
    Under the communiques, the US said that it intends to gradually reduce its arms sales to Taiwan and does not seek long-term weapons sales to Taipei.
    句首使用介係詞片語「Under the communiques（根據這些公報）」作為狀語，引導出美國在公報中的立場與意圖（intends to..., does not seek...），為多益商業閱讀常見的法律與條約用語結構。
  • 名詞子句當受詞 (Noun Clause as Object)
    A White House official said that Trump has said he would make a determination on the new arms sales package in a fairly short time.
    「that Trump has said...」引導一個名詞子句，作為動詞 said 的受詞；而在該子句內部，「he would make a determination...」又是省略了 that 的另一個名詞子句，作為內層 said 的受詞，展示了多益閱讀中常見的巢狀子句結構。
