單字解析

  • emerge from phr. v. 從……中擺脫出來、浮現
    Taiwan’s housing market showed little sign of emerging from a prolonged downturn last month
  • downturn n. （經濟、市場的）低迷、衰退
    Taiwan’s housing market showed little sign of emerging from a prolonged downturn last month
  • hold back phr. v. 扣留、保留、阻礙、暫緩
    as developers held back new projects and buyers remained on the sidelines amid high prices, tight credit and a seasonal slowdown.
  • on the sidelines prep. phr. 作壁上觀、保持觀望
    as developers held back new projects and buyers remained on the sidelines amid high prices, tight credit and a seasonal slowdown.
  • amid prep. 在……之中
    as developers held back new projects and buyers remained on the sidelines amid high prices, tight credit and a seasonal slowdown.
  • weigh on phr. v. 給……帶來負擔、壓抑
    Ghost Month, heavy rains and summer travel all weighed on buyer activity
  • deteriorate v. 惡化、退化
    Two of the indicator’s six components — presale launches and completed-home units — deteriorated
  • command v. （價格等）應得、享有、博得
    Completed new homes generally command higher prices than other properties
  • overhang n. 過剩存量、懸置量
    highlighting the scale of the inventory overhang.
  • stockpile n. 存貨、儲藏
    Taipei and Taoyuan could face difficulty clearing inventory because of high prices and large stockpiles
  • catalyst n. 催化劑、引發變化的因素
    The earliest potential catalyst might instead come from the central bank’s December meeting

文法重點

  • as 引導的時間/背景副詞子句
    Taiwan’s housing market showed little sign of emerging from a prolonged downturn last month, as developers held back new projects and buyers remained on the sidelines amid high prices, tight credit and a seasonal slowdown.
    句中的 as 用來連接兩個同時發生或具因果關係的子句，意指「隨著」或「在……同時」，常被用來描述背景狀況。
  • 分詞構句（附加說明伴隨狀況）
    The value of presale projects launched last month fell by nearly NT$9 billion (US$284.54 million) from the previous month, with only a few projects carrying sales values of more than NT$2 billion, My Housing Monthly said.
    「with + 名詞 + 現在分詞 (carrying)」構成的獨立分詞構句，用來補充說明主句發生時伴隨的具體情況，意為「僅有少數建案帶有……的銷售價值」。
  • 過去分詞作後置定語
    The value of presale projects launched last month fell by nearly NT$9 billion...
    句中的 launched last month 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞短語 presale projects，相當於形容詞子句 which were launched last month 的簡化。
