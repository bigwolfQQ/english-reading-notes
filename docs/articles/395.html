陸軍將再進行天馬操演

Army to conduct another Tianma exercise

單字解析

  • conduct v. 執行、實施、舉辦
    The 6th Army Command is to conduct live-fire anti-armor drills in Hsinchu County from Wednesday to Friday next week...
    第六軍團指揮部將於下週三至週五在新竹縣執行實彈反裝甲演習...
  • procure v. 採購、取得
    Taiwan has received the full package it procured under its High-Performance Anti-Armor Missile Program...
    台灣已收到在「高效能反裝甲飛彈採購計畫」下採購的全套裝備...
  • program n. 計畫、方案、程式
    The NT$11.815 billion (US$374.51 million) program, launched in 2018, aimed to bolster Taiwan’s asymmetric warfare capabilities.
    這項於 2018 年啟動、總額達新台幣 118.15 億元的計畫，旨在強化台灣的不對稱作戰能力。
  • aim v. 旨在、目的於、瞄準
    ...aimed to bolster Taiwan’s asymmetric warfare capabilities.
    ...旨在強化台灣的不對稱作戰能力。
  • distribute v. 分發、分配、分銷
    The equipment has been distributed to military units, which have begun training and familiarizing themselves with the new systems...
    相關裝備已分發至各軍事單位，部隊已開始進行訓練並熟悉新系統...
  • contribute to phr. v. 促成、貢獻、導致
    ...contributing to a higher tempo of live-fire exercises in recent months.
    ...促成了近幾個月來實彈演習節奏的加快。
  • feature v. / n. 以...為特色、特徵、特色
    The Tianma exercise is the military’s major anti-armor live-fire exercise, and typically features TOW and Javelin missiles.
    天馬操演是軍方主要的實彈反裝甲演習，通常以拖式和標槍飛彈為特色。
  • restriction n. 限制、約束
    ...due to range and safety restrictions.
    由於射程和安全限制。
  • position n. 位置、職位
    ...the exercise this year was conducted at tactical positions within each combatant command’s area of responsibility...
    ...今年的演習是在各作戰區責任地帶內的戰術位置進行...
  • employ v. 採用、使用、雇用
    ...rather than the wire guidance employed by the older TOW 2A...
    ...而非舊型 TOW 2A 所使用的有線導引...
  • capability n. 能力、才能
    Its key feature is its top-attack capability: The missile flies above the target’s line of sight...
    其核心特色在於頂攻能力：飛彈飛越目標視線上方...
  • intend v. 打算、旨在、意欲
    The command’s intensive top-attack drills at Fongkeng Beach are intended to bolster northern Taiwan’s coastal defenses...
    軍團在鳳坑海灘進行的高強度頂攻演練，旨在強化台灣北部的海岸防禦...

文法重點

  • be to do 句型（表達預定、計畫或即將發生的官方行程）
    The 6th Army Command is to conduct live-fire anti-armor drills in Hsinchu County from Wednesday to Friday next week...
    「be + to V」常用於新聞英語中，表示官方預定、即將發生的計畫或安排，語氣比單純用未來式更具權威感與確定性。
  • 過去分詞片語當後位修飾語（分詞後置修飾名詞）
    Taiwan has received the full package it procured under its High-Performance Anti-Armor Missile Program...
    句中的「it procured」為省略關代（which/that）的形容詞子句，修飾前面的「the full package」。而在後面提到「...guidance employed by the older TOW 2A」時，則是直接使用過去分詞片語「employed by...」來後位修飾「wire guidance」，省去了關代與be動詞，使句子更精鍊。
  • 非限定性關係子句（用逗點連接的 which 子句）
    The equipment has been distributed to military units, which have begun training and familiarizing themselves with the new systems...
    「which have begun...」前方帶有逗點，屬於非限定性關係子句，用來對前面的先行詞（military units）做補充說明，不能改用 that。
  • 分詞構句表結果（動詞ing作結果狀語）
    ...familiarizing themselves with the new systems, contributing to a higher tempo of live-fire exercises in recent months.
    句尾的「contributing to...」為現在分詞構句，表示前段動作自然而然產生的結果（相當於 and contributed to...），在英文新聞與商業寫作中非常常見。
