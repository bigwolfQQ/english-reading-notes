美國審判對頭號恐怖嫌疑人而言依然遙遙無期

US trial remains elusive for top terror suspect

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • authorities n. 官方，當局
    Two decades after US authorities locked Khalid Sheikh Mohammed away...
    在美國當局將哈立德·謝赫·穆罕默德關押......二十年後
  • trial n. 審判，審理
    ...barely any closer to being put on trial than the day he arrived.
    ......距離受審似乎一點也沒有比他剛抵達的那天更近。
  • tribunal n. 法庭，審判庭
    A judge recently set a 2028 date for Mohammed to finally be tried before a military tribunal...
    一位法官最近為穆罕默德設定了 2028 年的日期，以便最終在軍事法庭上接受審判......
  • plea agreement n. phr. 認罪協商，認罪協議
    A plea agreement that would have kept Mohammed imprisoned for life... began to collapse...
    一項原本會讓穆罕默德終身監禁的認罪協議......開始瓦解......
  • attorney n. 律師
    “It’s all contaminated by politics and the lack of accountability on torture,” said Joshua Dratel, an attorney...
    「這一切都被政治和缺乏對酷刑追究責任所汙染，」律師約書亞·德拉特爾說道......
  • prosecutors n. 檢察官
    ...ruled that prosecutors could not use an alleged confession he made to the FBI in 2007.
    ......裁定檢察官不得使用他在 2007 年向聯邦調查局做出的疑似自白。
  • appeal v. 上訴
    While the ruling was a blow to the prosecution, the US government decided not to appeal...
    雖然這項裁決對檢方是一次打擊，但美國政府決定不上訴......
  • indefinitely adv. 無限期地
    ...she would rather have Mohammed rot at Guantanamo Bay indefinitely than get behind a resolution...
    ......她寧願讓穆罕默德在關塔那摩灣無限期腐爛，也不願支持一項決議......
  • proceedings n. 訴訟程序，法律程序
    Some of the delays in his legal proceedings have stemmed from his treatment after he fell into US control.
    他法律訴訟程序的一些延誤，源於他落入美國控制後的待遇。
  • efficiently adv. 有效率地
    The military tribunal system has proven itself to be a worse solution, unable to function efficiently...
    軍事法庭系統已經證明自己是一個更糟糕的解決方案，無法有效運作......

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表附帶狀況或結果）
    A judge recently set a 2028 date for Mohammed to finally be tried before a military tribunal, but some lawyers predict it will never happen — not that year or any other year.
    句尾使用破折號連接「not that year or any other year」作為補充說明，強調「無論是那一年還是其他任何一年都不會發生」，加強語氣。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Those statements were deemed tainted because Mohammed was subjected to “enhanced interrogation techniques” overseas...
  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    Terry Strada, whose husband, Tom, died at the World Trade Center in the 2001 attacks, said that she and other people who lost family members still hope a trial will someday happen.
  • 假設語氣（過去結果）
    A plea agreement that would have kept Mohammed imprisoned for life, but spared him from a possible execution, began to collapse...
