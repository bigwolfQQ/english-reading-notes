歐洲議會副議長讚譽蕭美琴為女性「典範」

European Parliament vice president praises Hsiao as 'role model' for women

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • praise v. 稱讚，讚揚
    European Parliament Vice President Pina Picierno on Saturday praised Taiwanese Vice President Hsiao Bi-khim (蕭美琴) as a role model for freedom-loving women around the world...
    歐洲議會副議長皮耶奇諾於週六讚揚台灣副總統蕭美琴是全球熱愛自由女性的典範……
  • invitation n. 邀請
    ...and told CNA that she would soon begin planning a trip to Taipei after accepting Hsiao's invitation to visit.
    ……並告訴中央社，在接受蕭美琴的訪問邀請後，她將很快開始規劃前往台北的行程。
  • attend v. 出席，參加
    Hsiao made an unannounced visit to Italy to attend the Second Ventotene European Conference for Freedom and Democracy...
    蕭美琴……前往義大利出席第二屆文托滕特歐洲自由與民主會議……
  • organizer n. 主辦者，組織者
    ...at the invitation of the event organizers, despite strong protests from Beijing.
    ……應活動主辦單位邀請，無視北京的強烈抗爭。
  • interview n. / v. 採訪，面試
    In an interview with CNA on the sidelines of the event on Saturday, Picierno...
    皮耶奇諾於週六在活動場邊接受中央社採訪時……
  • threaten v. 威脅
    As authoritarian regimes threaten our democracies, we have an even greater need for courageous women like Vice President Hsiao Bi-khim...
    隨著威脅我們民主的威權政體出現，我們比以往更需要像副總統蕭美琴這樣勇敢的女性……
  • recognize v. 承認，認可，體認到
    We must recognize this and send a clear message: women who cherish freedom and democracy are determined to stand united...
    我們必須體認到這一點，並傳遞一個明確的訊息：珍視自由與民主的女性決心團結一致……
  • official adj. 官方的，正式的
    It is rare for Taiwan's senior leaders, including the president or vice president, to visit countries with which Taipei does not have official diplomatic ties.
    台灣的高階領導人，包括總統或副總統，極少造訪台北與其沒有官方外交關係的國家。
  • address v. 發表演說，演講
    Her first visit was in November 2025 to Brussels to address a meeting of the Inter-Parliamentary Alliance on China.
    她的首次訪問是在2025年11月前往布魯塞爾，向「對中政策跨國議會聯盟」的會議發表演說。
  • concern adj. / v. 擔心的，關心的
    When asked if she was concerned about her personal safety from China...
    當被問及是否擔心來自中國的人身安全時……
  • compromise n. / v. 妥協，妥協方案
    Picierno said she believes there is absolutely 'no room for compromise with authoritarian regimes that violate human rights.'
    皮耶奇諾表示，她認為對於「違反人權的威權政體，絕對沒有妥協的空間。」
  • collaboration n. 合作
    Our collaboration -- between myself and the vice president of Taiwan -- is just beginning...
    我們之間的合作——我和台灣副總統之間的合作——才剛開始……

文法重點

  • 分詞作為形容詞（Present/Past Participle as Adjectives）
    European Parliament Vice President Pina Picierno on Saturday praised Taiwanese Vice President Hsiao Bi-khim (蕭美琴) as a role model for freedom-loving women around the world...
    句中的「freedom-loving」是由名詞結合現在分詞（-ing）構成的複合形容詞，用來修飾前面的「women」，表示「熱愛自由的（女性）」。在多益閱讀測驗中，常考分詞轉化為形容詞的用法。
  • 表目的的不定詞片語（Infinitive Phrase of Purpose）
    Hsiao made an unannounced visit to Italy to attend the Second Ventotene European Conference for Freedom and Democracy...
    句尾的「to attend...」是不定詞片語作副詞用，用來修飾前面的動詞「made a visit」，表示蕭美琴前往義大利的「目的」（為了出席……會議）。這是多益文法題中常見的動詞與不定詞搭配結構。
  • 虛主詞 It 的句構（Cleft/Introductory 'It' Structure）
    It is rare for Taiwan's senior leaders, including the president or vice president, to visit countries with which Taipei does not have official diplomatic ties.
    句首的「It」為虛主詞（無實際意義），真正的的主持是後方的「to visit...」不定詞片語。「for Taiwan's senior leaders」則是不定詞的語意上的主詞。這種「It is + 形容詞 + for + 某人 + to do」是多益中極高頻出現的句型。
  • 被動語態（Passive Voice）
    The trip was Hsiao's second visit to a European country that recognizes Beijing instead of Taipei since she took office in May 2024.
    雖然此句本身是主動，但文章第10段「...prompting Beijing to lodge protests...」以及第13段「...was made possible by...」展示了被動語態的運用（was made possible）。在多益商業閱讀與聽力中，被動語態（be + p.p.）經常用於強調動作的承受者或客觀事實。
  • 關係代名詞子句（Relative Clause with Preposition）
    It is rare for Taiwan's senior leaders, including the president or vice president, to visit countries with which Taipei does not have official diplomatic ties.
    句中的「with which Taipei does not have official diplomatic ties」是一個由「介系詞 + 關係代名詞 (which)」引導的形容詞子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「countries（國家）」。此結構在多益測驗的文法選填題中常考介系詞的選擇。
