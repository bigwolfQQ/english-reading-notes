Okinawa governor demands discipline for US military

單字解析

  • demand v. 要求，請求
    The governor of the southern Japanese island of Okinawa yesterday demanded that the central government do more to ensure effective discipline...
    日本南部沖繩縣知事昨天要求中央政府採取更多措施，確保美軍官兵遵守有效紀律...
  • ensure v. 確保，保證
    ...demanded that the central government do more to ensure effective discipline among US service members...
    ...要求中央政府採取更多措施，確保美軍官兵遵守有效紀律...
  • accusation n. 指控，控告
    ...following the arrest of a US Marine on accusations that he robbed and murdered a local woman.
    ...此前一名美國陸戰隊員因涉嫌搶劫並殺害一名當地女子而遭到逮捕。
  • express v. 表達，表示
    ...met with Japanese Minister of Defense Shinjiro Koizumi to express outrage over the killing and ask for further efforts...
    ...會見了日本防衛大臣小泉進次郎，對這起殺戮事件表達憤怒，並要求進一步努力...
  • burden n. 負擔，重擔
    ...ask for further efforts to reduce the burden of hosting US military bases on the island.
    ...並要求進一步努力減輕島上承擔美軍基地的負擔。
  • oppose v. 反對，對抗
    People in Okinawa have long opposed the heavy US military presence on the island...
    沖繩民眾長期以來一直反對島上沉重的美軍駐軍...
  • suspect n. 嫌疑犯
    Lance Corporal Jacob Ballard, a 20-year-old Ohio native belonging to the Marine Corps Air Station Futenma, was arrested over the weekend on suspicion of strangling...
    隸屬於普天間空軍基地的20歲俄亥俄州籍一等兵雅各·巴拉德，週末因涉嫌勒死...

    Koizumi said that he was aware of Okinawa’s concerns and that his government would cooperate with the US to discuss effective preventive measures.
    小泉表示，他了解沖繩的擔憂，他的政府將與美國合作，討論有效的預防措施。
  • announce v. 宣布，宣告

文法重點

  • Demand 後方名詞子句使用原形動詞（虛擬語氣 / Subjunctive）
    The governor of the southern Japanese island of Okinawa yesterday demanded that the central government do more to ensure effective discipline...
    當句子的動詞表示要求、建議、命令（如 demand, suggest, insist 等）時，其後的 that 子句中的動詞需使用原形動詞（或省略 should），在此例中為 "demand that the central government do more"，動詞 do 使用原形，不受主語 central government（第三人稱單數）影響。
  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾（Participial Phrase as Postmodifier）
    Okinawa Governor Genta Koja, in Tokyo for the first time since taking office last month, met with Japanese Minister of Defense Shinjiro Koizumi to express outrage...
    句中的 "in Tokyo for the first time since taking office last month" 為形容詞性質的片語，用來補充說明主詞 Okinawa Governor Genta Koja 的狀態，其中 taking 為介系詞 since 後方的動名詞。
  • 被動語態（Passive Voice）
    Lance Corporal Jacob Ballard, a 20-year-old Ohio native belonging to the Marine Corps Air Station Futenma, was arrested over the weekend on suspicion of strangling...
    本句使用了被動語態 "was arrested"（被逮捕），強調動作的承受者（雅各·巴拉德），而發動逮捕的執行者在此處未被特別提及或不重要，是新聞報導中常用的句型結構。
  • 現在分詞短語表附帶狀況或原因（Present Participle Phrase）
    ...Koja told Koizumi as they met in the minister’s office packed with reporters and cameras clicking.
    句尾的 "and cameras clicking" 形成了「with/and + 名詞 + 現在分詞」的獨立結構，用來形容當時現場相機快門不斷作響的背景狀況與畫面感。
