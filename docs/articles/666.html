乖乖員工抗議土地出售，要求賠償

Kuai Kuai workers protest land sale, demand compensation

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • compensation n. 補償、賠償金
    Workers at Taiwanese snack maker Kuai Kuai Co. protested outside the Presidential Office on Monday, demanding compensation after the company sold the site of its factory in Taoyuan's Zhongli District
    台灣零食製造商乖乖股份有限公司的員工周一在總統府外抗議，在公司出售位於桃園中壢區的工廠土地後要求賠償。
  • relocate v. 遷移、搬遷（公司或產線等）
    announced plans to relocate operations to Hsinchu.
    並宣布計畫將營運遷移至新竹。
  • adequately adv. 適當地、充分地
    accused San-Ti Group, which controls the snack maker, of failing to adequately safeguard the interests of long-serving workers.
    並指控掌控該零食製造商的三地集團未能充分保障資深員工的權益。
  • safeguard v. 維護、保護
    failing to adequately safeguard the interests of long-serving workers.
    未能充分保障資深員工的權益。
  • substantial adj. 重大的、大量的、可觀的
    Now that the group has made substantial profits from selling the land
    既然集團從出售土地中獲取了巨大利潤
  • priority n. 優先權、首要之務
    it should give priority to compensating long-serving, low-paid workers
    就應該優先賠償長期服務、低薪的員工
  • formally adv. 正式地
    and were not formally informed until Sept. 1 of plans to relocate the factory to Hsinchu
    直到9月1日才被正式告知將工廠遷往新竹的計畫
  • voluntarily adv. 自願地
    and accused the company of hoping employees would leave voluntarily so it could avoid paying severance
    並指控公司希望員工自願離職以便能避免支付資遣費
  • severance n. 資遣（費）
    so it could avoid paying severance
    以便能避免支付資遣費
  • acquire v. 收購、取得
    when it acquired the company than in 'operating the food manufacturer and looking after its workers' over the long term.
    在收購這家公司時，可能對乖乖土地的價值比長期『經營食品製造商並照顧員工』更感興趣。
  • proactive adj. 積極主動的
    the MOL said it had asked the company to engage in 'proactive and transparent' dialogue with workers
    勞動部表示已要求公司與員工進行『積極且透明』的對話
  • transparent adj. 透明的、公開的
    engage in 'proactive and transparent' dialogue with workers
    進行『積極且透明』的對話
  • comply v. 遵守、依從
    The MOL noted that any factory relocation or reassignment of workers must comply with the Labor Standards Act and other labor laws.
    勞動部指出，任何工廠搬遷或員工調職都必須遵守《勞動基準法》及其他勞動法規。
  • consult v. 諮詢、請教、商議
    urged the company to consult workers on issues including longer or more difficult commutes
    敦促公司就通勤時間更長或更困難等問題諮詢員工

文法重點

  • 現在分詞當形容詞（後置修飾）
    Workers at Taiwanese snack maker Kuai Kuai Co. protested outside the Presidential Office on Monday, demanding compensation after the company sold the site of its factory...
    句尾的「demanding compensation...」是由現在分詞片語引導的補充說明，用來表達主句動作發生的同時伴隨的狀況（主動語態，表「要求賠償」）。
  • 現在完成式的被動語態
    CNA has reached out to Kuai Kuai for comment but had received no response as of press time.
    句中運用了現在完成式（has reached out / had received），強調過去發生的動作對現在造成的影響（已聯繫但截至截稿前尚未收到回應）。
  • 關係代名詞子句（非限定用法）
    The Kuai Kuai Zhongli factory union said the company sold the land for NT$5.672 billion in late July and accused San-Ti Group, which controls the snack maker, of failing to adequately safeguard the interests of long-serving workers.
    「which controls the snack maker」是由關係代名詞 which 引導的非限定用法關係子句，用逗號隔開，對前面的先行詞 San-Ti Group 做額外的補充說明。
  • 否定對等連接詞 nor 倒裝句
    The rights of grassroots workers who have worked for the company for many years cannot be ignored, nor can workers' rights be allowed to suffer because of adjustments to business operations...
    當前面是否定句、後面要接「亦不……」時，可使用「nor + 助動詞 + 主詞 + 動詞」的倒裝句型，加強語氣並連接兩個否定概念。
