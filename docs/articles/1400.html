工會需要更好的規則：倡議人士

Unions need better rules: advocate

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • reform n. 改革
    Labor advocates yesterday called for reforms to make unions easier to organize in Taiwan
    勞工倡議人士昨天呼籲進行改革，讓台灣的工會更容易組織起來
  • collective bargaining n. phr. 團體協商、集體談判
    saying that stronger collective bargaining is needed to tackle persistent low pay among young workers beyond annual minimum wage increases.
    並表示需要更強而有力的團體協商，來解決年輕勞工長期低薪的問題，而不僅僅是依靠每年的基本工資調高。
  • threshold n. 門檻、界線
    citing structural barriers, including the 30-person threshold, for forming a union.
    他並指出結構性障礙，包含籌組工會的三十人門檻。
  • recruit v. 招募、吸納（會員或員工）
    Yang Shu-wei (楊書瑋) said that one change would be to allow outside industrial and professional unions to enter workplaces to recruit members
    台灣勞工陣線祕書長楊書瑋表示，其中一項改變是允許外部產業工會和職業工會進入工作場所招募會員
  • mechanism n. 機制、體制
    Companies need a mechanism for systematic wage increases.
    企業需要有系統性加薪的機制。
  • transparency n. 透明度
    adding that greater wage transparency was one way to support regular pay raises.
    他補充說，提高薪資透明度是支持定期調薪的方法之一。
  • median adj. 中位數的、中間的
    while the median monthly salary among all first-time employees was NT$36,000.
    而全體首度就業者的每月薪資中位數則為新台幣 36,000 元。
  • negotiate v. 談判、協商
    Yang said unions can help workers negotiate pay raises
    楊書瑋表示工會可以幫助勞工協商加薪
  • reluctant adj. 不情願的、勉強的
    where unions existed but younger workers might be reluctant to join them.
    在那些國家裡工會存在，但年輕勞工可能不願意加入。
  • employer n. 雇主、老闆
    urged the labor ministry to raise the NT$40,000 monthly salary threshold below which employers are required to show the salary range in job postings
    敦促勞動部調高每月新台幣 40,000 元的薪資門檻；在此門檻以下的雇主必須在職缺刊登中揭露薪資範圍
  • amendment n. 修正案、修改
    the ministry said it is studying amendments to raise the NT$40,000 salary threshold
    勞動部表示正在研議修正案，以調高新台幣 40,000 元的薪資門檻

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作後位修飾 / 形容詞用法 (Present Participle as Post-modifier)
    Labor advocates yesterday called for reforms to make unions easier to organize in Taiwan, saying that stronger collective bargaining is needed...
    句中的 "saying..." 是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前面主句發生的動作或情況，相當於 "and they said..." 的簡化結構，在新聞英語中非常常見，用於連接主動作與隨之發表的言論。
  • 關係副詞 where 引導的形容詞子句 (Adjective Clause with Relative Adverb 'where')
    ...added that Taiwan faced a more fundamental problem than some countries, where unions existed but younger workers might be reluctant to join them.
    在此句中，"where" 作為關係副詞，用來修飾先行詞 "some countries"（某些國家），並在子句中作為地方副詞（in which），補充說明這些國家內部的勞工狀況。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The ministry generally defines young workers as those aged 15 to 29 in its labor statistics.
    雖然本句是主動語態，但新聞中常大量使用被動語態（例如第 12 段的 "are required to show" 或第 14 段的 "is permitted"），用以強調承受動作的對象或客觀事實，弱化動作的執行者。
