文法重點

  • 非限定關係代名詞子句 (Non-defining relative clause)
    Fourteen Taiwanese tech companies and the National Development Council (NDC) on Saturday held a talent recruitment campaign at the annual Taiwan Tech Summit in Silicon Valley, which offered more than 1,000 job openings in Taiwan.
    句尾的「which offered...」前面帶有逗號，屬於非限定用法，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「Silicon Valley」或整個事件（在此指 Tech Summit），提供額外的資訊，翻譯時可順著語意接下去譯為「並提供了……」。
  • 分詞構句作修飾語 (Participle phrase as modifier)
    Launched by the NDC in 2018, the Employment Gold Card program aims to attract skilled professionals in 12 fields...
    句首的「Launched by the NDC in 2018」是由過去分詞片語引導的分詞構句，省略了主詞與be動詞（原句概念相當於：Which was launched by...），用來修飾後方的主詞「the Employment Gold Card program」，表示被動與完成的概念。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞補語 (Gerund phrase as complement)
    NDC Minister Yeh Chun-hsien (葉俊顯) said that locating the overseas talent recruitment platform in Silicon Valley was critical...
    名詞子句中，動名詞片語「locating the overseas talent recruitment platform in Silicon Valley」作為子句的主詞，動名詞當主詞時視為單數，因此搭配單數動詞「was」。
  • 介詞片語置於句首的倒裝/地方副詞片語提前 (Prepositional phrase in front position)
    Among the Taiwanese companies participating in Taiwan Career Day were Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co..., Delta Electronics Inc..., the NDC said.
    本句為了強調與平衡句構，將表處所的介詞片語「Among the Taiwanese companies...」置於句首，帶動主詞與動詞的倒裝（或是動詞提前），真正的動詞是「were」，主詞則是後面長串的企業名稱列表。
