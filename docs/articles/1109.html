管理台灣的極端高溫

Managing Taiwan’s extreme heat

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • approach n. 方法、途徑
    Taiwan’s approach to managing extreme heat is evolving.
    台灣應對極端高溫的方法正在演變。
  • implement v. 實施、執行
    ...a clear, tiered response system that the public can understand and that government agencies know how to implement.
    ...一個大眾能夠理解、政府機關知道如何執行的清晰、分級的應變系統。
  • adjust v. 調整
    Should outdoor activities be adjusted in schools?
    學校的戶外活動是否應該調整？
  • allocate v. 分配（資源、經費等）
    ...could all become factors in how local governments allocate resources.
    ...都可以成為地方政府分配資源的因素。
  • vulnerability n. 脆弱性、易受傷害性
    Taiwan should establish responses based on factors such as temperature, humidity, duration, overnight temperature lows and population vulnerability in different areas.
    台灣應該根據溫度、濕度、持續時間、夜間最低溫以及不同地區的人口脆弱度等因素來建立應變措施。
  • coordinate v. 協調、配合
    At extreme levels, the central emergency response system could take over with coordination between agencies.
    在極端級別下，中央緊急應變系統可以接手，並在各機構之間進行協調。
  • responsibility n. 責任、職責
    Ensuring that everyone knows their role and responsibilities before danger strikes is the most important part of disaster governance.
    確保每個人在危險來臨前都知道自己的角色與職責，是災害治理最重要的部分。
  • accumulate v. 累積
    Trees do not fall, roads do not flood and buildings do not collapse, yet harm still accumulates.
    樹木不會倒塌、道路不會淹水、建築物不會倒塌，然而傷害卻不斷累積。
  • conduct v. 舉辦、執行、導電
    Taiwan’s decision to conduct its first full-scale extreme heat drill is a good first step...
    台灣決定舉辦首次全面性極端高溫演習是個好的第一步...
  • concrete adj. 具體的
    The next step is ensuring that every heat warning is backed by concrete actions.
    下一步是確保每一個高溫警報背後都有具體的行動支持。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Present Participle）作後位修飾
    ...an elderly person living in a rooftop addition without air-conditioning...
    句中的 "living in a rooftop addition..." 是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前方的名詞 "an elderly person"（一個住在...的老人），相當於關代子句 "who lives in a rooftop addition..."。
  • 假設語氣（Subjunctive Mood / Conditional）
    If typhoons, torrential rain and earthquakes each have their own disaster-response systems, Taiwan should seriously consider similar measures for extreme heat.
  • 間接問句 / 名詞子句作受詞
    ...the question is whether it can lead to lasting institutional changes.
  • 關係代名詞子句（Relative Clause）
    ...a clear, tiered response system that the public can understand and that government agencies know how to implement.
