疾管署呼籲嚴防腸胃炎

CDC urges precautions against gastroenteritis

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • precautions n. 預防措施，防範
    The Centers for Disease Control (CDC) on Tuesday urged people to take precautions against gastroenteritis during the Mid-Autumn Festival...
    疾病管制署（CDC）於週二敦促民眾在中秋節期間防範腸胃炎...
  • ingredients n. 食材，原料
    People planning to barbecue during the holiday should use fresh ingredients, keep raw and cooked food separate...
    計畫在假期烤肉的民眾應使用新鮮食材、生熟食分開...
  • outpatient adj. 門診的
    Kuo said that 126,419 people sought outpatient or emergency treatment for acute diarrhea nationwide last week...
    郭宏偉指出，上週全國有126,419人因急性腹瀉尋求門診或急診治療...
  • concentrated adj. 集中的，聚集的
    The number of diarrhea cases was relatively high, with 70 outbreaks reported nationwide over the past four weeks, concentrated in the food and hospitality industries, he said.
    他表示，腹瀉病例數相對偏高，過去四周全國共通報了70起群聚感染，主要集中在餐飲及旅宿業。
  • contaminated adj. 受汙染的
    Norovirus is highly contagious and can be transmitted by consuming contaminated food or water, or through contact with infected people or contaminated surfaces, Lin said.
    林明誠表示，諾羅病毒具有高度傳染性，可以透過食用受汙染的食物或水，或透過與受感染者或受汙染表面的接觸而傳播。
  • substitute n. 替代品
    ...adding that alcohol-based hand sanitizers should not be considered a substitute for handwashing.
    ...並補充說，不應將酒精類乾洗手視為洗手的替代品。
  • symptoms n. 症狀
    People with symptoms of gastroenteritis, including nausea, vomiting, diarrhea or abdominal pain, should avoid preparing food for others...
    出現噁心、嘔吐、腹瀉或腹痛等腸胃炎症狀的民眾，應避免為他人準備食物...
  • replenish v. 補充，重新裝滿
    They should also drink plenty of fluids, and replenish electrolytes and nutrients to prevent dehydration.
    他們也應該多喝流質飲品，並補充電解質和營養素以預防脫水。

文法重點

  • 非限定關代子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    ...during the Mid-Autumn Festival, when barbecues, family gatherings and outings are popular.
    句中的「when」引導非限定關代子句，前面帶有逗號，用來補充說明前面的時間先行詞「the Mid-Autumn Festival」，翻譯時順譯即可。
  • 分詞作形容詞 (Participles as Adjectives)
    ...not served partially raw or undercooked, he said.
    句中的「raw」與「undercooked（未煮熟的）」皆為過去分詞或形容詞，用來修飾前面的食物狀態，省略了主詞與be動詞（如：which are undercooked）。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞 (Gerund Phrase as Subject)
    For example, using the same tongs or plates that have come into contact with raw meat to handle cooked food can increase the risk of cross-contamination...
    句首的「using the same tongs... to handle cooked food」是動名詞片語，作為整個句子的主詞，動詞則使用單數動詞「can increase」。
  • 對等連接詞連接分詞結構 (Coordinate Participles)
    They should also drink plenty of fluids, and replenish electrolytes and nutrients to prevent dehydration.
    句中的「drink」與「replenish」為兩個並列的原形動詞，皆受前面的助動詞「should」所支配，用來表達同時建議患者應進行的多項動作。
