中央氣象署預測週二全台大多為多雲到晴的天氣

Mostly cloudy to sunny skies forecast across Taiwan Tuesday

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • expect v. 預期，預料
    Most of Taiwan can expect cloudy to sunny skies on Tuesday...
    台灣大部分地區預期在週二將是多雲到晴的天氣……
  • strengthen v. 增強，加強
    ...but strengthening seasonal winds will likely bring rainfall to northern and eastern Taiwan on Wednesday and Thursday...
    ……但增強的季節性風勢可能會在週三和週四為台灣北部和東部帶來降雨……
  • forecast v. / n. 預報，預測
    ...the Central Weather Administration (CWA) has forecast.
    ……中央氣象署（CWA）已預報。
  • predict v. 預測
    Despite the generally clear weather expected Tuesday, the CWA did predict scattered showers...
    儘管預期週二天氣大致晴朗，氣象署確實預測有零星陣雨……
  • fall v. 下降，跌落
    ...before falling to 21-23 degrees in northern Taiwan and 24-25 degrees in the south at night.
    ……隨後在夜間下降至台灣北部的21至23度以及南部的24至25度。
  • increase v. / n. 增加，提高
    The humidity is expected to increase on Friday...
    預計週五濕度將會增加……
  • issue v. 發布，發行
    Also on Tuesday, the Ministry of Environment issued an "orange" air quality alert...
    同樣在週二，環境部發布了「橘色」空氣品質警報……
  • indicate v. 顯示，指出
    ...indicating "unhealthy levels of pollution for sensitive groups,"...
    ……顯示「對敏感族群不健康的汙染程度」……

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作修飾語（分詞形容詞）
    ...but strengthening seasonal winds will likely bring rainfall...
    句中的 "strengthening" 是現在分詞轉化的形容詞，用來修飾名詞 "seasonal winds"（季節風），表示「正在增強的」風。
  • 對等連接詞 while 引導對比子句
    ...while other parts of Taiwan will remain mostly cloudy to sunny, with brief afternoon showers possible in southern Taiwan, the CWA said.
    連接詞 "while" 在此用來連接兩個對比的情況，表達「然而／而」，對比不同地區在同一時間的天氣狀況。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞
    Air quality was "normal" in northern Taiwan...
    雖然此句並非動名詞作主詞，但在新聞英文中常使用簡單的過去被動語態與直接敘述來報導官方說法（例如 "Air quality was 'normal'... according to the ministry"），此句型在商業與新聞英文中非常常見，用以客觀引述資訊。
