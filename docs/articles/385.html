男子涉嫌開車撞死護理師後肇事逃逸遭逮捕

Man arrested for alleged hit-and-run killing of nurse

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • arrest v. / n. 逮捕
    A man was arrested in Taichung early Tuesday after allegedly hitting a nurse with his car...
    週二清晨，一名男子在台中涉嫌開車撞上一名護理師後遭到逮捕...
  • alleged adj. 涉嫌的、傳說中的
    Man arrested for alleged hit-and-run killing of nurse
    男子因涉嫌肇事逃逸撞死護理師遭逮捕
  • flee v. 逃跑、逃走
    ...before fleeing the scene, local authorities said.
    ...隨後逃離現場，當地官方表示。
  • refer v. 提交、轉交、參考
    ...police, who said they would refer the case to prosecutors on charges of homicide and hit-and-run...
    ...警方表示，他們將以謀殺和肇事逃逸罪嫌將此案移送檢察官...
  • charge n. / v. 指控、控告、費用
    ...refer the case to prosecutors on charges of homicide and hit-and-run...
    ...以謀殺和肇事逃逸的罪名將案件移送檢察官...
  • recommend v. 建議、推薦
    ...and recommend that he be detained.
    ...並建議將他予以羈押。
  • investigation n. 調查
    Police said their investigation showed that Lin struck Chen as she was crossing the street at a crosswalk.
    警方表示，他們的調查顯示，林男在陳女於斑馬線過馬路時撞擊了她。
  • autopsy n. 驗屍、解剖
    Meanwhile, Chen's body has been sent for an autopsy.
    同時，陳女的遺體已被送去進行驗屍。

文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    A man was arrested in Taichung early Tuesday after allegedly hitting a nurse...
    句型為「be + 過去分詞 (was arrested)」，強調動作的承受者（男子），而非動作的執行者，在新聞英語中經常用於報導犯罪或意外事件。
  • 現在分詞作時間或條件附帶狀況 (Present Participle)
    ...after allegedly hitting a nurse with his car, making a U-turn, and striking her again, before fleeing the scene...
    句中的 hitting、making、striking 與 fleeing 均為現在分詞片語，用來補充說明主詞在連續發生事件中的動作細節，使語氣緊湊流暢。
  • 表建議的子句中使用原形動詞（Subjunctive / 假設語氣應用）
    ...and recommend that he be detained.
  • 關係代間詞子句補充說明 (Relative Clause)
    The man, surnamed Lin (林), was tracked down and arrested by police, who said they would refer the case to prosecutors...
    句中使用逗號隔開的「who 關係子句」進行非限定修飾，對前面的先行詞 police（警方）補充說明其後續的行動計畫。
