BOOKMAN BILINGUAL 雙語書林： Chinglish or Natural English? 中式英語或道地英語？

單字解析

  • recall v. 回想起、想起
    He recalled that there would be a lecture in the evening.
    他想起晚上有演講。
  • remind v. 提醒、使想起
    I reminded him that there would be a lecture in the evening.
    我提醒他晚上有演講。
  • appointment n. 約會、約定（通常指商業或公務會面）
    He reminded me that I had an appointment with my client in the afternoon.
    他提醒我下午跟客戶有約。
  • client n. 客戶、委託人
    He reminded me that I had an appointment with my client in the afternoon.
    他提醒我下午跟客戶有約。
  • technician n. 技師、技術人員
    The technician taught them how to operate the machine.
    這位技師教他們怎樣操作機器。
  • operate v. 操作、運轉（機器等）
    The technician taught them how to operate the machine.
    這位技師教他們怎樣操作機器。
  • master v. 精通、掌握（知識或技能）
    The technician taught them how to master the machine.
    這位技師教他們怎樣操作機器。
  • researcher n. 研究員
    He has invented numerous tools that help researchers better study anatomy.
    他發明了許多有助於研究人員更好地研究解剖學的工具。
  • numerous adj. 許多的、大量的
    He has invented numerous tools that help researchers better study anatomy.
    他發明了許多有助於研究人員更好地研究解剖學的工具。
  • absent adj. 缺席的
    The pupil invented an excuse for his being absent.
    那名學生為他的缺席編造了一個藉口。

文法重點

  • 名詞子句 (Noun Clause as Object)
    He recalled that there would be a lecture in the evening.
    在此句中，that 引導一個名詞子句 (that there would be a lecture in the evening)，作為動詞 recalled 的受詞，用來補充說明「想起」的內容。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    This style was created by the Modern Language Association of America in 1985.
    句型採用「be 動詞 (was) + 過去分詞 (created)」，強調動作的承受者 (This style) 而非動作的執行者，或是當執行者為介系詞片語 (by the Modern Language Association of America) 帶出時使用。
  • 關係代間詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    He has invented numerous tools that help researchers better study anatomy.
    句中的 that 引導形容詞子句（關係代間詞子句），用來修飾先行詞 tools，說明這些工具具備什麼功能。
  • 動名詞作介系詞的受詞 (Gerund as Object of Preposition)
    The pupil invented an excuse for his being absent.
    介系詞 for 後方接動名詞 being，而所有格代間詞 his 則置於動名詞前方，用來標示該動作（缺席）的邏輯主詞。
