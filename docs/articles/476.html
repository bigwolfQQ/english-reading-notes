受缺水與颱風影響，文旦產量減少

Pomelo harvest affected by water shortages, typhoons

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • production n. 生產，產量
    Pomelo production in eastern Taiwan is not expected to be much more than 14,000 tonnes this year.
    今年台灣東部的文旦產量預計不會超過 1.4 萬公噸。
  • average n. / adj. 平均，平均數
    20 percent less than the average of the previous years
    比往年平均少了 20%
  • region n. 地區，區域
    Eastern Taiwan is a key pomelo production region with 1,066 hectares of planting area
    台灣東部是文旦的重要產區，種植面積達 1,066 公頃
  • primary adj. 主要的，首要的
    Yilan County’s Dongshan Township, Hualien County’s Rueisuei and Yuli townships, and Taitung County’s Donghe Township are the region’s primary pomelo producing areas
    宜蘭縣冬山鄉、花蓮縣瑞穗鄉與玉里鎮，以及台東縣東河鄉是該地區的主要文旦產區
  • projected adj. 預計的，預估的
    The region’s pomelo production is projected to be 14,334 tonnes this year
    今年該地區的文旦產量預計為 1 萬 4,334 公噸
  • affected adj. 受影響的
    as production was affected by water shortages in March and typhoons last year
    因為產量受到 3 月份缺水和去年颱風的影響
  • demand n. 需求
    to meet surging demand ahead of the Mid-Autumn Festival
    為了因應中秋節前激增的需求
  • export v. 出口
    adding that the association would export 8 tonnes of pomeloes to the country.
    並補充說該農會將向該國出口 8 公噸的文旦。
  • regular adj. 平常的，例行規律的
    most people in Singapore regard it as a regular fruit and purchase it more frequently
    新加坡多數人將其視為一般水果，並且購買頻率更高
  • frequently adv. 頻繁地
    purchase it more frequently
    購買頻率更高
  • store v. 儲存，保存
    Consumers should buy more pomeloes and store them to enhance the richness of their flavor
    消費者應該多買一些文旦並將其存放，以提升風味的濃郁度
  • enhance v. 提升，增加
    to enhance the richness of their flavor
    以提升風味的濃郁度

文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Pomelo production in eastern Taiwan is not expected to be much more than 14,000 tonnes this year...
    句型使用「be +過去分詞」(is not expected to be)，用來強調動作的承受者（文旦產量），而不需要特別指出是誰預估的，是新聞英語中非常常見的客觀表達方式。
  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況或結果 (Participial Phrase)
    ...adding that Taitung pomeloes are soft and naturally refreshing.
    句尾的「adding that...」是由對等子句簡化而來的分詞構句（原句形狀大約為 and he added that...），現在分詞 adding 用來主動補充說明前面陳述的內容。
  • 對比連接詞 while 的用法
    While Taiwanese pomelo consumption is highly concentrated around the Mid-Autumn Festival, most people in Singapore regard it as a regular fruit...
    在此句中，「While」作連接詞使用，意指「然而、儘管」，用來連接兩個對比的情況（台灣人集中在中秋節吃文旦 vs 新加坡人視為一般日常水果頻繁購買）。
