能源安全是時間問題

Energy security a question of time

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • import v. 進口
    For Taiwan, which imports almost all of its fuel, it raises a more basic question...
    對幾乎所有燃料都依賴進口的台灣來說，這引發了一個更根本的問題...
  • supplier n. 供應商
    Qatar, Taiwan’s second-largest gas supplier, stopped production.
    台灣第二大天然氣供應商卡達停止生產。
  • spot market n. phr. 現貨市場
    To keep the power on, Taiwan bought gas on the spot market, where single cargoes are sold to the highest bidder.
    為了維持供電，台灣在現貨市場購買天然氣，單批貨物會出售給出價最高者。
  • contract n. 合約、契約
    ...most of the long-term contracts that supply gas to Asia... do not fix a price in advance.
    ...向亞洲供應天然氣的大多數長期合約...並未提前固定價格。
  • shipment n. 一批運送的貨物、裝運
    Instead, the price of each gas shipment is set by a formula tied to the price of crude oil...
    相反地，每一批天然氣貨物的價格都是透過與原油價格掛鉤的公式來設定的...
  • benchmark n. 基準、參考標準
    ...using Brent crude or the average price of crude imported into Japan as the benchmark.
    ...以布蘭特原油或進口至日本的原油平均價格作為基準。
  • demand n. 需求
    Aramco estimates that refilling the world’s oil stocks will add about 2 million barrels a day to demand for at least 18 months...
    沙烏地阿拉伯國家石油公司估計，補充全球石油庫存將在至少 18 個月內使需求每天增加約 200 萬桶...
  • capacity n. 產能、容量
    ...its main export plant at Ras Laffan, damaged in March, is still running at reduced capacity.
    ...其在 3 月受損位於拉斯拉凡的主要出口廠目前仍以減產狀態運作。
  • shortage n. 短缺、不足
    ...so a shortage would quickly become a power problem for industry...
    ...因此短缺將迅速成為產業界的電力問題...
  • target n. 目標
    It set a target of 14 days of gas reserves in 2018...
    它在 2018 年設定了 14 天天然氣儲備的目標...
  • schedule n. 時程、進度
    The fourth faces land contamination problems and the fifth is behind schedule.
    第四接收站面臨土地汙染問題，第五接收站則進度落後。
  • asset n. 資產
    It expects its seventh straight annual loss this year, with debts above 90 percent of its assets.
    它預計今年將面臨連續第七年年度虧損，債務超過其資產的 90%。

文法重點

  • 非限定性關係代名詞子句 (Non-restrictive Relative Clause)
    For Taiwan, which imports almost all of its fuel, it raises a more basic question...
    句中的「which imports almost all of its fuel」前方帶有逗號，用來補充說明前面的專有名詞 Taiwan（台灣），提供額外的背景資訊，若省略該子句並不影響主要句子的完整性。
  • 分詞構句作後置修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...its main export plant at Ras Laffan, damaged in March, is still running at reduced capacity.
    句中的「damaged in March」是由過去分詞片語組成的後置修飾語，修飾前面的主詞 plant（工廠）。其原意為「which was damaged in March」，省略了關代與be動詞，使語氣更為精煉。
  • 條件句中的假設語氣 (Subjunctive Mood in Conditional Context)
    How long can it keep its power on if supplies are cut?
    此句使用連接詞 if 引導條件子句「if supplies are cut」（如果供應被切斷），搭配現在式被動語態來假設未來可能發生的狀況，主句則使用助動詞 can 來詢問在此假設情境下的能力。
