文法重點

  • 過去完成被動語態 (Past Perfect Passive)
    The vessel’s last position had been detected in the Java Sea, about 150km from its destination.
    結構為「had been + 過去分詞 (V-pp)」。用來強調在過去某個時間點之前，某個動作已經被完成。句中指在船隻失聯或被發現的過去時間點之前，其最後位置「已經被偵測到」了。
  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase)
    The Virgo Transport 8, carrying 243 passengers and crew, had lost contact with its shipping company early yesterday...
    句中的「carrying 243 passengers and crew」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前方的主詞「The Virgo Transport 8」，相當於形容詞子句「which carried...」的簡化形式，能使句子更為精煉。
  • 非限定關係代間詞子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    One of the passengers had died, he told reporters in the port city of Banjarmasin, where the vessel was headed.
    句尾的「where the vessel was headed」是非限定關係子句（前面有逗號），用來補充說明先行詞「Banjarmasin（馬辰）」這個地方，關係副詞 where 在子句中代替「in which」作地方副詞。
