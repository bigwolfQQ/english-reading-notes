文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Foreign exchange reserves are overseas assets held by a central bank that can be readily deployed, including foreign currency deposits and government securities.
    句中的 "held by a central bank" 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "overseas assets"。其完整結構為關代與be動詞省略（即 which are held by...），在多益閱讀測驗中是常見的簡化子句考點。
  • 現在分詞片語作結果狀語 (Present Participle Phrase indicating a result)
    To help maintain the local foreign exchange market stable, the central bank intervened by buying US dollars and selling NT dollars to moderate the greenback’s losses, he said.
    句中的 "by buying US dollars and selling NT dollars" 利用介系詞 by 加上動名詞片語，用來表達「藉由……方式」達成某個動作或手段。此外，句首的 "To help..." 則是表目的的不定詞片語。
  • 間接問句 (Indirect Question)
    Tsai said it remained unclear whether the yen’s appreciation was driven by official intervention or short-covering.
    此句包含一個由 "whether" 引導的名詞子句（"whether the yen’s appreciation was driven by..."），在句中作形容詞 unclear 的補語。間接問句的語序為「疑問詞/連接詞 + 主詞 + 動詞」，在多益文法題中經常考驗是否維持正常語序而非疑問句語序。
