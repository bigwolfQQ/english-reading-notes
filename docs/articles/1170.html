文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表示附帶狀況或結果）
    The death toll in a mass shooting in a South African tavern has risen to 18 after one person died in the hospital, police said yesterday, announcing at least two of the dead were Lesotho nationals.
    句尾的「announcing at least two of the dead were Lesotho nationals」是由現在分詞 announcing 引導的分詞構句，用來補充說明前面主句發生時的同時動作或附加結果，翻譯時可譯為「並宣佈……」。
  • 過去分詞片語作後置修飾（形容詞功能）
    The shooting west of Johannesburg on Saturday was part of a bloody weekend in the crime-weary country, with 10 people killed in another mass shooting in Cape Town on Sunday.
    句中的「armed with assault rifles」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「Eight gunmen」，相當於形容詞子句「who were armed with assault rifles」，在多益閱讀測驗中是非常常見的簡化子句考點。
  • 現在分詞片語作後置修飾
    The motive for the attack was not clear, but the area is reportedly gripped by rivalry between criminal organizations involved in illegal gold mining.
    句尾的「involved in illegal gold mining」是由過去分詞 involved 開頭的片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「criminal organizations」，省略了「which are」，是多益商業英文中常用來精簡句型的結構。
  • With 複合結構（with + 名詞 + 過去分詞）
    ...with 10 people killed in another mass shooting in Cape Town on Sunday.
    此處使用「with + 受詞 (10 people) + 過去分詞 (killed)」的複合結構，用來表達附帶狀況，意思是「在……的情況下」或「有……（人事物被……）」。這在多益測驗中常考其文法結構與介系詞搭配。
