不與中國達成人工智慧安全協議的藝術

The art of not making an AI safety deal with China

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • prevailing adj. 盛行的、現存的、普遍的
    Yet the prevailing assumption is that cooperation with Beijing on frontier AI risks is impossible.
    然而，普遍的假設是，就前沿人工智慧風險與北京合作是不可能的。
  • engagement n. 參與、互動、交流、約會
    After a two-year halt in official AI safety engagement, the US and China said they would resume talks.
    在官方人工智慧安全交流中斷兩年之後，美國和中國表示將恢復會談。
  • resume v. 恢復、重新開始
    After a two-year halt in official AI safety engagement, the US and China said they would resume talks.
    在官方人工智慧安全交流中斷兩年之後，美國和中國表示將恢復會談。
  • visibility n. 能見度、透明度、清晰度
    Each side needs enough visibility into what the other fears about AI’s capabilities...
    雙方需要對彼此對人工智慧能力的擔憂有足夠的了解...
  • insulate v. 使隔離、使免受影響
    Just as crucial, the talks must be insulated from the rest of the US-China economic contest.
    同樣至關重要的是，這些談判必須與美中經濟競爭的其他部分隔離開來。
  • justify v. 證明…正當、為…辯護
    Having seen Washington invoke “national security” to justify blacklists and export controls...
    看到華盛頓援引「國家安全」來為黑名單和出口管制辯護...
  • explicit adj. 明確的、清楚的
    Xi made this tension explicit in July...
    習近平在7月明確表達了這種緊張關係...
  • substantially adv. 相當大地、顯著地
    The number of papers published in the country on frontier AI safety has grown substantially...
    該國發表的前沿人工智慧安全論文數量大幅增長...
  • disclose v. 透露、揭露
    ...what would have happened if they had not disclosed the OpenAI agent-related cyberattack.
    ...如果當初他們沒有披露與OpenAI代理相關的網路攻擊，會發生什麼事。
  • enforce v. 執行、強制實施
    ...while acknowledging that any pause would be extraordinarily difficult to enforce.
    ...同時承認任何暫停都將極其難以執行。
  • sustain v. 維持、保持、支撐
    Avoiding catastrophe is essential to sustain confidence in the technology...
    避免災難對於維持對技術的信心至關重要；

文法重點

  • 現在分詞當作形容詞修飾名詞
    Safety calculations will be driven by “self-interest,” he said.
    句中的 "self-interest" 雖然由名詞組成，但在這裡作為概念。另一個常見例子如文章中的 "surging demand" 或 "developing nations"，此處展示了名詞前置修飾或特定形容詞的運用。
  • 假設語氣過去式（與過去事實相反）
    Hugging Face chief executive officer Clement Delangue wrote on X that he sometimes wonders what would have happened if they had not disclosed the OpenAI agent-related cyberattack.
    句型中包含 "if + 過去完成式" (if they had not disclosed...)，主句則使用 "would have + 過去分詞" (what would have happened)，用來假設在過去如果沒有發生某事，會導致什麼結果。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾
    Yuyuan Tantian (玉淵譚天), a social media account affiliated with state broadcaster China Central Television, has warned that safety talks could become another attempt to contain China’s technological rise.
    句中 "affiliated with..." 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞短語 "a social media account"，相當於關代子句 "which is affiliated with..." 的簡化版，能讓句子更為緊湊流暢。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞
    Avoiding catastrophe is essential to sustain confidence in the technology...
    動名詞片語 "Avoiding catastrophe" 作為主詞，其後的動詞使用單數形 "is"。這種結構在英文商業與新聞寫作中非常常見，用來表達某個行為或動作的重要性。
