賴清德敦促尊重二戰歷史與台灣「生存權」

Lai urges respect for WWII history, Taiwan's 'right to exist'

單字解析

  • reduce v. 減少，降低
    Taiwan welcomes efforts by the United States and China to reduce the risk of war through dialogue...
    台灣歡迎美國和中國透過對話降低戰爭風險的努力...
  • ability n. 能力
    ...worked to strengthen society's overall ability to defend itself by combining government and civilian capabilities...
    ...並透過結合政府與民間能力，致力於強化社會整體的防衛韌性...
  • ensure v. 確保，保證
    Such efforts are not meant as a provocation, but to ensure dialogue can take place on the basis of 'dignity and parity,' he said.
    他說，這些努力並非意在挑釁，而是為了確保對話能夠在「尊嚴與對等」的基礎上進行。
  • basis n. 基礎，根據
    ...to ensure dialogue can take place on the basis of 'dignity and parity,' he said.
    ...而是為了確保對話能夠在「尊嚴與對等」的基礎上進行。
  • maintain v. 維持，保持
    "But we must also point out that maintaining regional security, peace and stability requires a correct understanding of how history has unfolded."
    「但我們也必須指出，維護區域安全、和平與穩定，需要對歷史的發展有正確的理解。」
  • tolerance n. 容忍，寬容
    "There must be no tolerance for distorting the history of World War II."
    「絕不能容忍扭曲第二次世界大戰的歷史。」
  • relocate v. 搬遷，遷移（企業、機關等）
    The ROC government relocated to Taiwan from mainland China in 1949 after its defeat in the Chinese Civil War...
    在國共內戰中被擊敗後，中華民國政府於1949年從中國大陸遷台...
  • ally n. 盟友，同盟國
    ...visit to Eswatini, Taiwan's sole African ally, which took place amid Chinese attempts to block the trip...
    ...訪問台灣唯一的非洲友邦史瓦帝尼，此行是在中國企圖阻撓該行程的情況下進行的...
  • meet v. 滿足（需求、條件等）
    Taiwan has worked with its international partners on health care, agriculture, energy, technology, education and sustainable development to meet their needs...
    台灣一直在醫療衛生、農業、能源、科技、教育和永續發展方面與國際夥伴合作以滿足其需求...
  • commitment n. 承諾，奉獻
    "It is a commitment -- our willingness to share our capabilities and shoulder responsibility,"
    「這是一個承諾——代表我們願意分享能力並肩負責任，」
  • recognize v. 承認，認可（名詞形式為 recognition）
    Taiwan seeks international recognition despite sustained efforts by Beijing to isolate it.
    儘管北京持續企圖孤立台灣，台灣仍尋求國際承認。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Faced with...）作分詞構句
    Faced with sustained "gray zone" pressure from China, Taiwan has increased defense spending...
    句首的「Faced with...」是由被動語態的分詞構句簡化而來，原句形貌應為「(Being) Faced with...」。用來補充說明主句主詞 Taiwan 所處的背景狀況（面對來自中國持續的壓力）。
  • not... but... 對等連接詞片語
    Such efforts are not meant as a provocation, but to ensure dialogue can take place on the basis of "dignity and parity," he said.
    「not A but B」意為「不是 A，而是 B」。用來強烈對比並澄清台灣防衛努力的真正目的（不是挑釁，而是確保尊嚴對等的對話）。
  • 間接問句（名詞子句作受詞）
    ...requires a correct understanding of how history has unfolded.
    介系詞 of 後面接了一個由「疑問詞 how + 主詞 history + 動詞 has unfolded」構成的名詞子句，在句中作為 of 的受詞，表示「歷史是如何展開的」。
  • 形容詞子句（which引導的非限定性子句）
    ...in his National Day address, which marked the 115th anniversary of the founding of the ROC.
    逗號後面的「which marked...」是非限定性關係代間子句，先行詞為前面的整段談話（his National Day address），用來補充說明該場演說所代表的歷史意義。
