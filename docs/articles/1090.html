文法重點

  • be expected to 原形動詞（被動語態的預期）
    US President Donald Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping (習近平) are expected to discuss trade, artificial intelligence (AI) and Taiwan at a summit...
    「be expected to + V」是新聞英文中極為常見的句構，用來表達「預期會……」、「預料將……」，常用於報導尚未發生但各界普遍推測的行程或事件。
  • 現在分詞片語作附帶狀況（with + 名詞 + 現在分詞）
    ...at a summit beginning in Washington today, with academics saying the talks would also aim to manage competition between the two countries.
    此處使用「with + 受詞 (academics) + 分詞 (saying)」的結構，用來表達伴隨發生的狀況或背景原因，中文常譯為「隨著……」或「在……的情況下」。
  • 關係子句（形容詞子句）修飾先行詞
    The summit, the leaders’ second face-to-face meeting this year following talks in Beijing in May, is expected to build on their agreement to establish a “constructive relationship of strategic stability,”...
    句中的「to establish a...」是不定詞片語，作為同位語或後置修飾語，用來補充說明前面 agreement（協議）的具體內容是什麼。
  • 現在分詞短語作後置修飾語
    ...at a summit beginning in Washington today, with academics saying...
    句中的「beginning in Washington today」是現在分詞短語，用來修飾前面的名詞 a summit，相當於關代子句「which begins in Washington today」，使句子更為精簡。
