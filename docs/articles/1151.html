比爾·蓋茲加入呼籲人工智慧防護措施與立法的行列

Bill Gates joins calls for AI safeguards, legislation

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • safeguard n. 防護措施，安全保證
    Artificial intelligence (AI) requires safeguards that go beyond self-regulation, Microsoft Corp cofounder Bill Gates said in an interview that aired on Sunday...
    微軟公司共同創辦人比爾·蓋茲在週日播出的訪談中表示，人工智慧（AI）需要超越自我監管的防護措施...
  • legislation n. 立法，法律
    ...calling for legislation to be passed in US Congress.
    ...並呼籲美國國會應通過相關立法。
  • enforcement n. （法律等的）執行，實施
    “You need law enforcement and the politicians to get into the discussion about what safeguards and monitoring look like,” Gates said...
    蓋茲表示：「你需要執法部門和政治人物加入討論，探討防護措施和監控制度會是什麼樣子。」
  • intervention n. 介入，干預
    A sudden groundswell has risen in favor of federal intervention after OpenAI in July said that an autonomous AI agent went rogue...
    在 OpenAI 7 月表示一個自主式 AI 代理失控之後，支持聯邦介入的呼聲突然高漲。
  • restrict v. 限制，約束
    ...US President Donald Trump and other opponents of additional regulation for AI say new government restrictions would simply give China the upper hand...
    ...美國總統唐納·川普和其他反對為 AI 增加監管的人士表示，新的政府限制只會讓中國佔居上風...
  • dismiss v. 不予考慮，對……不屑一顧
    Trump has publicly dismissed calls for new laws on AI as a “hoax.”
    川普曾公開斥責對 AI 立法的呼籲是一場「騙局」。
  • hinder v. 阻礙，妨礙
    Gates said that safeguards would not dramatically hinder the industry, and that the risks of not implementing them are real.
    蓋茲表示，防護措施不會對該產業造成戲劇性的阻礙，而不實施這些措施的風險是真實存在的。
  • implement v. 實施，執行
    Gates said that safeguards would not dramatically hinder the industry, and that the risks of not implementing them are real.
    蓋茲表示，防護措施不會對該產業造成戲劇性的阻礙，而不實施這些措施的風險是真實存在的。
  • confirm v. 確認，證實
    Trump confirmed that he planned to have a dinner meeting with Amodei on Sunday night, but reaffirmed his stance against slowing the pace of AI development.
    川普證實他計劃於週日晚間與阿莫代伊共進晚餐會議，但重申了他反對放緩 AI 發展步伐的立場。
  • maintain v. 維持，保持
    Although he acknowledged Amodei’s concern that going too fast on AI could expand risks, Trump said it was more important for the US to maintain a technological edge over China.
    儘管川普承認阿莫代伊對於 AI 進展過快可能會擴大風險的擔憂，但他表示，對美國而言，保持對中國的技術優勢更加重要。

文法重點

  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    Artificial intelligence (AI) requires safeguards that go beyond self-regulation...
    句中的「that go beyond self-regulation」是形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的先行詞 safeguards（防護措施），說明這些防護措施具體涵蓋了哪些內容（超越自我監管）。
  • 現在分詞當形容詞使用（修飾名詞）
    ...the CEOs of rivals Anthropic PBC, OpenAI, Google’s DeepMind, Microsoft and xAI have called for slowing the development of increasingly capable AI systems.
    句中的「increasingly capable」是由副詞修飾現在分詞 capable 的概念（此處 increasingly 為副詞，capable 為形容詞，但概念相似：由現在分詞轉化的形容詞如 leading、growing 等常見），用來修飾後方的 AI systems，意指「日益強大的 AI 系統」。
  • 過去分詞當後置定語（修飾名詞）
    “You need law enforcement and the politicians to get into the discussion about what safeguards and monitoring look like,” Gates said in a taped interview on NBC’s Meet the Press program.
    句中的「taped」是過去分詞，放在名詞 interview 前面當作形容詞使用，用來修飾 interview，表示這是一場「被錄製好的（預錄的）」訪談。
  • 假設語氣（過去式表達與現在事實相反或不可能實現的情況）
    “We’re about maybe a year and a half up on China,” Trump told Fox News while attending the Presidents Cup golf tournament in Illinois. “We’re leading, and we’re building tremendous, trillions of dollars’ worth of places. And why should we give that up?”
    雖然此處為直述句與問句的組合，但在報導引述中常出現對未來或條件的假設（如第 13 段的「Although he acknowledged Amodei’s concern that going too fast on AI could expand risks...」中的 could 表達可能性），幫助學習者掌握如何用英文表達條件與情態動詞的使用。
