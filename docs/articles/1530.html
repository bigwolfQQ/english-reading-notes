文法重點

  • Despite 引導的讓步副詞片語
    Techman Robot Inc (達明機器人) remains positive about humanoid robots over the long term, despite near-term hurdles that lie in technological maturity and real-world application capability...
    「despite」為介系詞，後面接名詞或名詞片語（在此為 near-term hurdles），用來表達「儘管有...」的讓步語氣，等同於 in spite of 或雖然子句 (Although/Though)。
  • 關係代名詞子句修飾先行詞
    ...despite near-term hurdles that lie in technological maturity and real-world application capability...
    句中的「that lie in...」是由關係代名詞 that 引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的先行詞 hurdles（障礙），說明這些障礙所處的領域。
  • 被動語態的運用
    “I believe the long-term prospects for humanoid robots are very good, but there are still many challenges that need to be overcome at this stage,” Wang said...
    「that need to be overcome」使用了被動語態的不定詞（to be overcome），強調挑戰是「被」克服的對象。在商業與科技新聞中，當動作的承受者是主體時，常使用被動語態。
  • 現在分詞作後位修飾
    With continued collaborative robot demand from the semiconductor, electronics manufacturing, automotive and warehousing sectors, Techman expects revenue this year to exceed last year’s...
    句首的「With + 名詞 + 現在分詞/過去分詞/形容詞」結構（With continued collaborative robot demand...）常用於表達附帶狀況或原因，相當於中文的「隨著……」。
