單字解析

  • resolve v. 解決（問題、爭端等）
    Fundamental differences between the United States and China make major disputes difficult to resolve...
    美國與中國之間的基本分歧使得重大爭端難以解決...
  • ties n. 關係、聯繫
    ...creating an opportunity for Taiwan to deepen economic and security ties with Washington...

  • policy n. 政策

  • administration n. （政府的）行政當局、管理
    ...and a former China policy adviser in the first Trump administration...

    ...make major disputes, including over tariffs and regional security, difficult to resolve.
    ...使得包括關稅和區域安全在內的重大爭端難以解決。
  • strategic adj. 戰略性的
    For now, the two sides are seeking to maintain strategic stability...
    目前雙方正尋求維持戰略穩定...
  • integration n. 整合、結合
    Further integration between Taiwan and the U.S. in trade and economic affairs is important...
    台灣與美國在貿易與經濟事務上進一步整合很重要...

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present/Past Participle as Post-modifier)
    Yu, director of the China Center at the Washington-based think tank Hudson Institute... made the remarks...
    句中的「Washington-based」是由過去分詞構成的複合形容詞，放在名詞前面用來修飾「think tank」（智庫），意思是「以華盛頓為基地的／總部位於華盛頓的」。這在新聞英語中非常常見，能有效精簡句子結構。
  • 包含包括所有格與動名詞的受詞補語結構 (Object Complement with Gerund)
    ...differences in the two countries' political systems and how their governments operate make major disputes... difficult to resolve.
    此句使用了「make + 受詞 + 形容詞」的句型（make major disputes difficult to resolve）。其中受詞是很長的「major disputes, including over tariffs and regional security」，而受詞補語是形容詞「difficult」，後面再接不定詞片語「to resolve」補充說明在哪方面困難。
  • 被動語態與完成式 (Present Perfect Passive)
    ...the truce, originally set to expire on Nov. 10, had been extended for two months to Jan. 10, 2027.
    句中的「had been extended」使用了「過去完成式的被動語態」（had been + 過去分詞）。用來強調在過去某個時間點（峰會前夕）之前，這個休兵期「已經被延長了」，凸顯動作在過去之先已經完成的狀態。
