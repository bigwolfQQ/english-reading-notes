證券交易稅收創歷年九月新高

Securities tax revenue sets September record

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • revenue n. 收入、收益、稅收
    Tax revenue from securities transactions rose 71.2 percent year-on-year to NT$53.4 billion...
    上個月證券交易稅收較去年同期激增 71.2%，達到新台幣 534 億元...
  • decline n. / v. 下降、衰退、減少
    ...even as it marked the third consecutive monthly decline as market turnover continued to fall.
    ...儘管這已是市場成交量持續下滑所導致的連續第三個月衰退。
  • consecutive adj. 連續的
    ...and securities transaction tax revenue last month still posted positive year-on-year growth for the 14th consecutive month...
    ...且上個月的證券交易稅收仍創下連續 14 個月維持正成長的紀錄...
  • volume n. 量、總額、容量（此處指交易量）
    This year’s overall trading volume remains higher than last year...
    今年的整體交易量仍高於去年...
  • post v. 公布、發布、取得（佳績等）
    ...and securities transaction tax revenue last month still posted positive year-on-year growth...
    ...且上個月的證券交易稅收仍創下...正成長的紀錄（公布/交出...成績）
  • average adj. 平均的
    The average daily turnover of the local stock market was NT$1.032 trillion...
    本地股市平均每日成交量為新台幣 1.032 兆元...
  • turnover n. 成交量、營業額、人員流動率
    The average daily turnover of the local stock market was NT$1.032 trillion...
    本地股市平均每日成交量為新台幣 1.032 兆元...
  • cumulative adj. 累積的
    In the first nine months of this year, cumulative tax revenue from securities transactions totaled NT$506 billion...
    今年前九個月，累計證券交易稅收總計達新台幣 5,060 億元...
  • total v. 總計達
    ...cumulative tax revenue from securities transactions totaled NT$506 billion...
    ...累計證券交易稅收總計達新台幣 5,060 億元...
  • surge v. 急升、激增
    ...adding that average daily stock turnover surged 160 percent year-on-year to NT$1.146 trillion over the period.
    ...並補充指出，這段期間平均每日股市成交量飆升 160%，達到新台幣 1.146 兆元。
  • collect v. 徵收、收集
    Overall, the nation last month collected NT$496.5 billion in tax revenue...
    整體而言，該國上個月稅收總計 4,965 億元（徵收了...稅收）...
  • target n. 目標
    ...and was 78.8 percent of its full-year target, it said.
    ...並達到全年目標值的 78.8%。
  • consider v. 考量、考慮
    ...as it must consider factors such as the stock trading volume in the coming months...
    ...因為必須將未來幾個月的股票交易量等因素納入考量...

文法重點

  • even as 引導的讓步子句
    Tax revenue from securities transactions rose 71.2 percent year-on-year to NT$53.4 billion (US$1.66 billion) last month, a new September high, even as it marked the third consecutive monthly decline as market turnover continued to fall.
    「even as」在此處用作連接詞，意指「正當……的時候；儘管」，用來連接兩個同時發生但帶有對比或讓步意味的子句，強調雖然市場成交量持續下跌導致連續第三個月衰退，但上個月的證券交易稅收依然大幅上升。
  • 現在分詞短語作附加補充說明
    In the first nine months of this year, cumulative tax revenue from securities transactions totaled NT$506 billion, rising 1.6-fold year-on-year and marking the highest level for the same period, the ministry said, adding that average daily stock turnover surged 160 percent year-on-year to NT$1.146 trillion over the period.
    句中的「rising 1.6-fold...」、「marking the highest level...」以及結尾的「adding that...」均為現在分詞（V-ing）片語，用來接在主句之後，補充說明主句動作所產生的結果或同時發生的狀態，省去了連接詞與主詞，使新聞語氣更為緊湊流暢。
  • It remains to be seen 句型
    It remains to be seen whether the tax revenue collected this year could exceed the government’s budgeted figure...
    「It remains to be seen + whether/WH 子句」是英文新聞與商業報導中非常實用的固定句型，意為「……仍有待觀察」、「尚未分曉」。句首的 It 為虛主詞，真正的受詞是後方由 whether 引導的名詞子句。
