季節性東北季風為台灣北部、東部帶來降雨：氣象署

Seasonal winds bring rain to northern, eastern Taiwan: CWA

單字解析

  • range v. （在一定範圍內）變動、範圍介於
    Temperatures over the two days will range from 21 to 30 degrees Celsius in northern Taiwan, 22 to 32 degrees in central Taiwan and 22 to 28 degrees in the south, the CWA forecast.
    氣象署預報指出，這兩天的氣溫，北部地區約攝氏 21 至 30 度，中部地區約 22 至 32 度，南部地區則約 22 至 28 度。
  • forecast n. / v. 預報、預測
    Temperatures over the two days will range from 21 to 30 degrees Celsius in northern Taiwan, 22 to 32 degrees in central Taiwan and 22 to 28 degrees in the south, the CWA forecast.
    氣象署預報指出，這兩天的氣溫，北部地區約攝氏 21 至 30 度，中部地區約 22 至 32 度，南部地區則約 22 至 28 度。
  • record v. 記錄、記載
    The lowest temperature recorded in a low-lying area on Wednesday morning around Taiwan was 18.5 degrees Celsius in Guanxi Township, Hsinchu County...
    根據氣象署資料，週三早晨全台平地測得的最低溫為新竹縣關西鎮的攝氏 18.5 度...
  • issue v. 發布、發行（正式文件或警告）
    The CWA issued strong wind advisories for coastal areas from Miaoli County to Yunlin County...
    氣象署針對苗栗縣至雲林縣的沿海地區...發布強風特報
  • exceed v. 超過、超出
    It warned that winds could exceed 40 kilometers per hour (kph), with gusts exceeding 63 kph.
    氣象署警告，風速可能超過每小時 40 公里，陣風更可能超過每小時 63 公里。
  • expect v. 預期、期待
    On Friday, heavier rainfall is expected in Yilan and Hualien counties and along Taiwan's north coast...
    週五時，宜蘭縣、花蓮縣及台灣北海岸預計有較大雨勢...
  • remain v. 保持、維持
    Other parts of Taiwan will remain mostly cloudy to sunny, with afternoon showers possible, the CWA said.
    氣象署表示，台灣其他地區將維持大多多雲到晴的天氣，午後有局部陣雨的機率。
  • weaken v. 減弱、變弱
    Over the weekend, the seasonal northeasterly winds will weaken, with localized showers possible in the Greater Taipei area and eastern Taiwan.
    週末期間季節性東北季風將減弱，大台北地區與台灣東部可能出現局部陣雨。
  • uncertain adj. 不確定的、未定的
    The CWA cautioned that the depression's projected path remains uncertain, and it asked the public to pay attention to the latest forecasts.
    氣象署警告，該熱帶性低氣壓的預測路徑仍具不確定性，並呼籲民眾隨時關注最新預報。
  • pay attention to phr. 注意、留心
    ...and it asked the public to pay attention to the latest forecasts.
    ...並呼籲民眾隨時關注最新預報。

文法重點

  • 分詞作後置修飾 (Participle as Postmodifier)
    The lowest temperature recorded in a low-lying area on Wednesday morning around Taiwan was 18.5 degrees Celsius...
    過去分詞片語「recorded in a low-lying area on Wednesday morning around Taiwan」用來修飾前面的名詞「The lowest temperature」，相當於形容詞子句「that was recorded...」，省略了「which was」，使句子更為精簡。
  • 獨立分詞構句 / 附帶狀況 (Absolute Phrase / With-structure)
    It warned that winds could exceed 40 kilometers per hour (kph), with gusts exceeding 63 kph.
    「with + 名詞 + 現在分詞」結構用來表示附帶狀況或伴隨發生的情形，在此處用以補充說明風速超過 40 公里的同時，陣風也伴隨著超過 63 公里。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    On Friday, heavier rainfall is expected in Yilan and Hualien counties and along Taiwan's north coast...
    句型使用「is expected」的被動語態，強調降雨被預期會發生（發布預報的主體通常是氣象署，但此處著重於降雨這項客觀預測結果，故使用被動語態）。
