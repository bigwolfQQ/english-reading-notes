中央氣象署：科羅旺外圍環流預計為台灣北部帶來降雨：氣象署

Krovanh's outer bands expected to bring rain to northern Taiwan: CWA

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • linger v. 逗留，徘徊，持續存留
    The outer bands of Tropical Storm Krovanh, which is currently lingering near Okinawa, are expected to bring rain to northern Taiwan this weekend, the Central Weather Administration (CWA) said Saturday.
    中央氣象署於週六表示，目前正滯留在沖繩附近輕度颱風科羅旺的外圍環流，預計將在週末為台灣北部帶來降雨。
  • expect v. 預期，預計

  • forecast n. / v. 預報，預測
    Precipitation is forecast to intensify in the Greater Taipei area and along Keelung's north coast from Saturday night through Sunday, while Krovanh is unlikely to have a direct impact on Taiwan, the CWA said.
    氣象署表示，從週六夜間到週日，大台北地區及基隆北海岸的降雨預計將增強，不過科羅旺不太可能對台灣造成直接影響。
  • intensify v. 加強，增強

  • direct impact n. phr. 直接影響

  • affect v. 影響
    Echoing the CWA's forecast, meteorologist Wu Der-rong (吳德榮) said Taiwan's weather could be affected by strong convection on Saturday and Sunday afternoons, with heavier rainfall possible in mountainous areas in the north.
    氣象專家吳德榮呼應氣象署的預報表示，週六和週日下午台灣的天氣可能會受到強烈對流影響，北部山區可能出現較大雨勢。

    根據氣象署指出，週六中南部及東半部的白天高溫預計可達攝氏30至32度，而北部氣溫則預計介於28至30度之間。
  • remain v. 保持，維持（某種狀態）
    The CWA also forecast high waves along the northern, northeastern and eastern coasts, urging people engaging in water activities to remain alert.

文法重點

  • 非限定關係代名詞子句（帶有逗號的 which 子句）
    The outer bands of Tropical Storm Krovanh, which is currently lingering near Okinawa, are expected to bring rain to northern Taiwan this weekend...
    句中的「which is currently lingering near Okinawa」是一個非限定關係子句，前面帶有逗號，用來補充說明先行詞「Tropical Storm Krovanh」的額外資訊（目前滯留在沖繩附近）。刪除這個子句後，主要子句的句意依然完整。
  • 被動語態（be + 過去分詞）
    ...Taiwan's weather could be affected by strong convection on Saturday and Sunday afternoons...
    此處使用情態動詞與被動語態的結合（could be + 過去分詞 affected），強調台灣的天氣是「被」強烈對流所影響，突顯受事者為主體的表達方式。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾
    The CWA also forecast high waves along the northern, northeastern and eastern coasts, urging people engaging in water activities to remain alert.
    句尾的「urging people...」是由現在分詞（urging）開頭的分詞片語，用來補充說明前面主句所發生的動作所帶來的結果或伴隨的動作，相當於副詞子句的簡化。
