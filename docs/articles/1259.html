菲律賓神父為慶祝聖方濟各瞻禮為寵物祈福

Filipino priests bless pets for Saint Francis feast

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • annual adj. 一年一度的，年度的
    It was a day for wagging tails, happy faces and a special annual blessing at a church in the Philippine capital.
    在菲律賓首都的一座教堂裡，這是一個充滿搖尾巴、快樂面孔以及舉行一年一度特殊降福儀式的日子。
  • choose v. 選擇
    Many parishes choose the first Sunday of October to hold community animal blessings or Saint Francis service events.
    許多堂區選擇在10月的第一個星期日舉辦社區動物祈福或聖方濟各禮拜活動。
  • relate v. 與……有關，涉及
    We are blessing the animals and plants and this is so much in relation to the feast of Saint Francis of Assisi ... so the Malate parish has been advocating this one
    我們正在為動物和植物祈福，這與亞西西的聖方濟各的瞻禮息息相關……因此馬拉特堂區一直都在提倡這項活動。
  • advocate v. 提倡，主張，擁護
    so the Malate parish has been advocating this one
    因此馬拉特堂區一直都在提倡這項活動
  • patiently adv. 耐心地
    Parishioners formed a line as they walked up the aisle with their pets, waiting patiently for a priest to sprinkle their companions with holy water.
    教友們帶著寵物排成一列走過走道，耐心地等待神父用聖水灑向他們的動物伴侶。
  • expression n. 表達，表示
    For pet owners, it was a simple expression of gratitude for the unconditional love, joy and companionship these animal friends bring into their lives every day
    對寵物主人來說，這是一種簡單的感恩表現，感謝這些動物朋友每天為他們的生活帶來無條件的愛、快樂和陪伴
  • presence n. 出席，存在
    I want them to feel, in some way, that we are grateful for their presence in our lives; that’s why we have them blessed.
    我希望讓牠們在某种程度上感受到，我們很感激牠們的存在；這就是為什麼我們帶牠們來接受降福。
  • organize v. 組織，籌辦
    The church also organized free pet vaccinations for dogs and cats in the church parking lot.
    教堂還在教堂停車場為狗和貓組織了免費的寵物疫苗接種。

文法重點

  • 現在完成進行式 (Present Perfect Continuous Tense)
    the Malate parish has been advocating this one
    結構為「has/have + been + Ving」，用來表示一個動作從過去某個時間點開始，持續進行到現在，且可能還會持續下去。此處強調馬拉特堂區一段時間以來持續在推動與提倡這項活動。
  • 分詞構句作補充說明 (Participial Phrase)
    Parishioners formed a line as they walked up the aisle with their pets, waiting patiently for a priest to sprinkle their companions with holy water.
    句尾的「waiting patiently...」是由分詞構句簡化而來（原句約等於 and waited patiently 或 while they waited patiently），用來補充說明主詞 parishioners 在排隊走過走道時同時伴隨的動作與狀態。
  • 關係代間詞子句 (Relative Clause)
    ...said 32-year-old nurse Gaudine Supnet, who owns four Shih Tzu dogs.
    句中的「who owns four Shih Tzu dogs」是非限定性關係代間詞子句（前面有逗號），用來補充修飾先行詞「Gaudine Supnet」，提供關於她的額外資訊（她養了四隻西施犬）。
