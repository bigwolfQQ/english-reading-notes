文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...four days after Chang, indicted on corruption charges, cut off his electronic monitoring wristband and jumped bail.
    句中的「indicted on corruption charges」是由過去分詞開頭的片語，用來修飾前面的專有名詞 Chang，相當於形容詞子句「who was indicted on corruption charges」，用精簡的方式補充說明張嫌被起訴的背景。
  • 現在分詞片語作附帶狀況/結果 (Present Participle Phrase indicating Result/Circumstance)
    The High Court approved prosecutors’ application to detain Lin and Chen with communications restrictions to prevent collusion, evidence tampering and flight, it said.
    句尾的「to prevent collusion, evidence tampering and flight」是不定詞片語作副詞用，用來表達目的（為了防止串證、滅證及逃亡），在多益閱讀測驗中常考用來表達目的或結果的動詞不定詞用法。
