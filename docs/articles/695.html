社論：高齡化人口政策被忽視

EDITORIAL: Aging population policy overlooked

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • consecutive adj. 連續的，不間斷的
    Taiwan had a total population of 23,224,721, declining for the 32nd consecutive month...
    台灣總人口為 23,224,721 人，連續第 32 個月下降……
  • record adj. 創紀錄的
    ...that number was almost halved to a record-low 105,676 last year...
    ...該數字在去年幾乎減半，創下 105,676 人的歷史新低...
  • forecast v. 預測，預報
    The National Development Council last month forecast that the number of births could fall below 100,000...
    國家發展委員會上個月預測，出生人數可能會跌破 100,000 人...
  • strategy n. 策略，戰略
    In May, President William Lai (賴清德) announced an 18-point population strategy to counter the nation’s low birthrate...
    5 月份，總統賴清德公佈了一項 18 項人口戰略，以應對國家的低出生率...
  • funding n. 資金，經費
    The policy, which is expected to require NT$380 billion (US$11.99 billion) in funding...
    這項預計需要 3,800 億新台幣（119.9 億美元）資金的政策...
  • priority n. 優先事項，優先權
    ...addresses five priority areas: a secure start for families, strengthened childcare...
    ...涵蓋五大優先領域：家庭安心起步、強化托育...
  • incentivize v. 給予獎勵，激勵
    However, it remains focused on incentivizing births.
    然而，它仍然側重於提供生育誘因（激勵生育）。
  • demographic adj. 人口統計的，人口學的
    ...the demographic shift and changes in family structure necessitate new policies...
    ...人口結構的轉變和家庭結構的變化需要新的政策...
  • implement v. 實施，執行
    ...programs encouraging younger people to live with older people... have been implemented over the past few years.
    ...鼓勵年輕人與老人同住的計畫……在過去幾年中已經實施。
  • allocate v. 分配，分派（資金、資源等）
    ...it would be more viable for the government to allocate more resources to projects...
    ...政府將更多資源分配給專案會更具可行性...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞當形容詞（修飾名詞）
    As of the end of last month, Taiwan had a total population of 23,224,721, declining for the 32nd consecutive month...
    句中的「declining for the 32nd consecutive month」為現在分詞片語，置於逗號後做補充說明，用來修飾前面的人口總數，表示該人口數字「正在持續下降」的狀態。
  • 關係代名詞子句（非限定用法）
    The policy, which is expected to require NT$380 billion (US$11.99 billion) in funding, addresses five priority areas...
    句中的「which is expected to require...」是一個非限定用法的關係子句（前面有逗號隔開），用來補充說明前面的先行詞「The policy」，提供關於該政策所需經費的額外資訊。
  • 虛主詞 It 與表語形容詞 (it is [adj.] that... / to...)
    With a rapidly aging population, it is inevitable that the number of households in which elderly people live alone... would continue to increase.
    句型使用「it is + 形容詞 (inevitable) + that 子句」，其中「it」為形式主詞，真正的受詞/主詞是後面引導出的完整子句，用來表達某事是不可避免的趨勢。
