民進黨40週年：創辦人反思風險、崛起與考驗

DPP at 40: Founders reflect on risk, rise, reckoning

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • proposal n. 提案，建議
    During the meeting, a proposal was made from the floor to discuss forming a new political party...
    會議期間，會場中有人提出討論籌組一個新政黨的提案……
  • reluctant adj. 勉強的，不情願的
    When those present were asked to sign up as founding members, "some were reluctant, while others signed and left immediately," he said.
    他說，當在場的人被要求登記為創黨黨員時，「有些人猶豫不決，而另一些人則簽名後立刻離開。」
  • task v. 指派，交辦任務
    Hsieh, who was tasked by the "secret 10-member party-forming group" with drafting the party constitution, said he had prepared a letter of last words in case he was arrested.
    被「祕密十人組」指派草擬黨綱的謝長廷表示，他已經準備了一封遺書以防被逮捕。
  • significant adj. 重大的，顯著的
    Wang Chia-hua (王家驊), then head of Chiang's Office of Confidential Affairs, said in an oral-history interview for the book "Breaking the Party Ban: The Crucial 10 Days of the DPP's Founding in 1986" that intelligence officials did not consider the gathering significant enough to report to Chiang because "tangwai" figures frequently held meetings.
    時任蔣經國總統辦公室機要室主任的王家驊在為《打破黨禁：1986年民進黨創黨關鍵十天》一書所作的口述歷史訪談中表示，情治官員認為該聚會不重要，不足以向蔣經國報告，因為「黨外」人士經常舉行會議。
  • crackdown n. 鎮壓，取締
    According to Wang's account, Chiang held a meeting at the Presidential Office on the afternoon of Sept. 29, with some officials urging a crackdown and others advising against confrontation.
    根據王的說法，蔣經國於9月29日下午在總統府召開會議，會中部分官員力主鎮壓，另有人則建議避免對抗。
  • breakthrough n. 突破
    A breakthrough came in the 1997 local elections, when the party won 12 of the then 23 county and city leadership posts, paving the way for its historic presidential victory in 2000 and Taiwan's first transfer of power between political parties.
    一項突破出現在1997年的地方選舉中，當時該黨贏得了當時23個縣市首長席次中的12席，為其在2000年歷史性的總統勝選以及台灣首次政黨輪替鋪平了道路。
  • advantage n. 優勢
    He told CNA in a recent interview that the advantage stems from the DPP's role in Taiwan's democratization and its long-standing support among voters who strongly identify with Taiwan rather than China.
    他最近接受中央社採訪時表示，這項優勢源於民進黨在台灣民主化過程中所扮演的角色，以及長期強烈認同台灣而非中國的選民對其的支持。
  • strategy n. 策略、戰略（文章延伸自進階字彙，此處涵蓋行政推進、議程等概念）
    He said the administration must win the support of opposition lawmakers if it wants to advance its agenda.
    他表示，行政部門若要推進其議程，必須贏得在野黨議員的支持。
  • demonstrate v. 證明，展示
    The DPP can no longer rely primarily on that legacy or on ethnic issues to mobilize support, he said, and must instead demonstrate that it can meet Taiwan's current political challenges.
    他說，民進黨不能再主要依靠該遺產或族群議題來動員支持，而是必須證明它能夠應對台灣當前的政治挑戰。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句 (Participle Phrase)
    Building on decades of activism, long-running efforts to form a political party gained momentum in the 1980s after the 1979 Kaohsiung Incident...
    句首的 "Building on decades of activism" 是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明主句主語 "long-running efforts to form a political party" 的背景原因（基於數十年的社會運動累積），相當於副詞子句 "Because they built on decades of activism..."。
  • 非限定關代子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    ...after the 1979 Kaohsiung Incident, in which many 'tangwai' figures, including Lin I-hsiung (林義雄), were arrested.
    "in which many 'tangwai' figures... were arrested" 是非限定關係代間子句，用介系詞加關係代名詞 "in which"（代替前面的 the 1979 Kaohsiung Incident）來補充說明該事件發生的狀況，逗號後方不可改用 "that"。
  • 假設語氣（與過去事實相反） (Subjunctive Mood)
    You, a member of the '18-member party-forming working group' established during the early stages of the party's formation, said there would have been no need for secrecy or fear of arrest if that were true.
    句尾的 "if that were true"（條件句用過去式 were）搭配主句的 "there would have been no need..."（would have + 過去分詞），構成與過去事實相反的假設語氣，表達「如果當時那是真的（事實上不是），當初就不需要保密或害怕被逮捕了」。
  • 授受動詞被動語態 (Passive Voice of Task)
    Hsieh, who was tasked by the 'secret 10-member party-forming group' with drafting the party constitution, said he had prepared a letter of last words in case he was arrested.
    "who was tasked by... with..." 是被動語態的結構，原本主動用法為 "task someone with something"（指派某人某任務），改成被動後表示謝長廷「被指派」草擬黨綱的任務。
