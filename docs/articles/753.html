China using extremists to sow division, official says

單字解析

  • seek v. 尋求、試圖
    China is seeking to weaken Taiwan by influencing extremists to enter its democratic system...
    中國正尋求透過影響極端分子進入其民主體制來削弱台灣……
  • exploit v. 利用、剝削
    ...influencing extremists to enter its democratic system and exploit political divisions...
    ……影響極端分子進入其民主體制並利用政治分歧……
  • consensus n. 共識
    ...making it harder for society to reach consensus.
    ……使得社會更難達成共識。
  • exclusive adj. 獨家的、專有的
    ...Huang said in an exclusive interview with the Chinese-language Liberty Times...
    ……黃重諺在接受中文《自由時報》的獨家專訪時表示……
  • interference n. 干涉、干預
    Chinese election interference overseas is nothing new...
    中國在海外干預選舉並非新鮮事……
  • requirement n. 要求、必要條件
    ...people should be allowed to run as long as they meet legal requirements.
    ……只要人們符合法定要求，就應該被允許參選。
  • authority n. 官方、當局、權威
    ...adding that the Central Election Commission and other authorities conduct rigorous checks for Chinese citizenship.
    ……並補充說中央選舉委員會和其他主管機關會對中國公民身分進行嚴格審查。
  • rigorous adj. 嚴格的、嚴密的
    ...conduct rigorous checks for Chinese citizenship.
    ……進行嚴格的中華民國公民身分審查。
  • undermine v. 逐漸削弱、破壞
    ...adding that Beijing was trying to undermine democracy through its processes.
    ……並補充說北京正試圖透過民主過程來破壞民主。
  • obstruct v. 阻礙、阻塞
    Once elected, these people could become a source of risk by obstructing important legislations...
    這些人一旦當選，可能會透過阻撓重要立法而成為風險來源……
  • legislation n. 立法、法律
    ...while other organizations are being used to prevent legislation from advancing, he said.
    他說，而其他組織則被用來阻止立法推進。
  • obtain v. 獲得、取得
    Political infighting could prevent the government from obtaining essential uncrewed aerial vehicles...
    政治內鬨可能會阻止政府獲得必要的無人機……
  • sensitive adj. 敏感的
    ...and even lead to the leaking of sensitive information to undermine opponents, he said.
    他說，甚至導致敏感資訊外洩以破壞對手。
  • verify v. 核實、查證
    ...using content that is difficult to verify to confuse the public, Huang said.
    黃重諺表示，（行動）使用難以查證的內容來混淆大眾。
  • bolster v. 支持、強化、鞏固
    The government is exchanging information with international partners to bolster Taiwan’s information and democratic resilience...
    政府正與國際夥伴交換資訊以強化台灣的資訊與民主韌性……

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作附帶狀況/結果的補語 (V-ing)
    China is seeking to weaken Taiwan by influencing extremists to enter its democratic system and exploit political divisions, National Security Council (NSC) adviser Alex Huang (黃重諺) said yesterday, warning that the election of just one or two such politicians could make it harder for society to reach consensus.
    句尾的 "warning that..." 是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前面主句所發生的動作或帶來的結果，中文常譯為「同時警告……」或「並警告……」。
  • 使役動詞 make 的用法 (make + 受詞 + 形容詞)
    ...making it harder for society to reach consensus.
    此處使用 "make + O. + adj." 的結構，用來表達「使……變得……」。句中的 "it" 是虛受詞，真正的受詞是後面的不定詞片語 "for society to reach consensus"。
  • 對等連接詞連接的多個動詞片語 (Parallel Structure)
    Once elected, these people could become a source of risk by obstructing important legislations, Huang said.
    句中的 "Once elected" 是被動的分詞構句（省略了 they are），主詞 these people 身兼後面 could become 的發動者，表達「一旦被選上，這些人就會……」。
  • 現在分詞當形容詞修飾名詞 (Participial Adjective)
    These include generating tens of thousands of “live accounts” capable of real-time interaction as well as AI-generated videos targeting President William Lai...
    句中的 "targeting President William Lai..." 是現在分詞片語，放在被修飾的名詞 "AI-generated videos" 後面，做後置定語來修飾這些影片，相當於關代子句 "which target President William Lai..."。
