單字解析

  • inclusion n. 納入，包含
    As for any sport, the inclusion in the continental games is critical
    對於任何運動而言，被納入洲際運動會是至關重要的
  • governance n. 治理，管理結構
    Obviously the inclusion means that you already have a good governance structure
    顯然，被納入意味著你們已經具備良好的治理結構
  • obstacle n. 障礙，阻礙
    Getting everyone to agree on the rules of the competition, including what moves are allowed, is a major obstacle to MMA being at the Olympics.
    讓所有人就比賽規則（包括允許哪些動作）達成一致，是 MMA 進入奧運會的一大障礙。
  • pathway n. 途徑，路徑
    Because now there’s a clear pathway, right?
    因為現在有了清晰的途徑，對吧？
  • stakeholder n. 利益相關者
    We have been in talks with the IOC and other relevant stakeholders that have influence or decision on the recognition
    我們一直在與國際奧會（IOC）以及其他對認可具有影響力或決策權相關的利益相關者進行會談
  • recognition n. 認可，承認
    Singapore’s Teo Hui Hoon said that Asian Games and Olympic recognition would encourage a generation to take up the sport.
    新加坡的刁慧芬表示，亞運會和奧運會的認可將鼓勵一代人投身這項運動。
  • appeal n. 吸引力
    [It is] a sport which has global appeal and a sport that is ready and capable of fitting into a major Games schedule
    [這是一項]具有全球吸引力且準備好、有能力融入大型運動會賽程的運動
  • schedule n. 賽程，時間表
    [It is] a sport which has global appeal and a sport that is ready and capable of fitting into a major Games schedule
    [這是一項]具有全球吸引力且準備好、有能力融入大型運動會賽程的運動

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（作表語或修飾用）
    Mixed Martial Arts, known as MMA, is making its Asian Games debut this week in Japan...
    過去分詞片語「known as MMA」在此省略了「which is」，用來修飾前面的主詞「Mixed Martial Arts」，意為「被稱為……的綜合格鬥」，是新聞英文中非常常見且簡潔的修飾手法。
  • 條件句（假設語氣的應用）
    Plenty of hard work and political lobbying lies ahead if MMA is to make it.
    句尾的「if MMA is to make it」使用了「be + to V」的結構，用來表達未來預定發生的計畫或實現某個目標所需的條件（意即「如果 MMA 要成功入選的話」）。
  • 關係代名詞子句
    In UFC, which in June held an event on the South Lawn of the White House, the rough and tumble takes place inside a caged octagon.
    「which in June held an event...」為非限定用法的關係子句，在逗號後方補充說明先行詞「UFC」的相關資訊，幫助讀者在句子中獲得額外的背景細節。
  • 虛主詞 It 的用法
    Getting everyone to agree on the rules of the competition, including what moves are allowed, is a major obstacle to MMA being at the Olympics.
    句首動名詞片語「Getting everyone to agree...」作為真正的主詞太長，但在口語和寫作中常直接置於句首（亦可視為主詞片語）。此處示範了將長主詞放在句首作為句型重心的用法。
