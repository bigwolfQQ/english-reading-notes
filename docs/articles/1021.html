外銷訂單突破一千億美元

Export orders cross US$100bn

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • surpass v. 超越、勝過
    Export orders surpassed US$100 billion for the first time last month...
    外銷訂單上個月首次突破1000億美元...
  • demand n. 需求
    ...driven by strong demand for artificial intelligence (AI)...
    ...受惠於人工智慧（AI）的強勁需求...
  • performance n. 表現、績效
    The strong order performance reflects better-than-expected demand for custom chips...
    強勁的訂單表現反映了對客製化晶片的需求優於預期...
  • forecast n. / v. 預測、預報
    ...surpassing the ministry’s forecast of 61.4 to 64.8 percent growth.
    ...超越了經濟部預估的61.4%至64.8%的成長率。
  • cumulative adj. 累計的、累積的
    Cumulative orders in the first eight months of the year grew 55 percent to US$704.99 billion...
    今年前八個月的累計訂單成長55%，達到7049.9億美元...
  • component n. 零件、組件
    ...indicate product and component shipments to overseas markets one to three months before customs clearance.
    ...預示著在海關清關前一到三個月對海外市場的產品與零組件出貨情況。
  • boost v. 促進、提高、推動
    ...as makers of information and communications technology (ICT) products boosted production abroad, the ministry said.
    ...經濟部表示，主因是資訊通信科技（ICT）產品製造商提高了海外產能。
  • expansion n. 擴張、擴大
    In traditional industries, semiconductor firms’ continued capacity expansion and rising copper demand...
    在傳統產業方面，半導體企業持續擴充產能，加上銅需求上升...
  • destination n. 目的地、對象地區
    By destination, orders last month from the US registered the largest annual increase of 88.9 percent...
    按出口地區觀察，上個月來自美國的訂單登記了最大年增率88.9%...
  • expenditure n. 支出、開支
    ...and increased capital expenditure by cloud service providers...
    ...以及雲端服務供應商資本支出的增加...
  • outlook n. 前景、展望
    ...suggesting that firms remain confident in their outlook.
    ...顯示企業對前景維持信心。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...demand for custom chips — known as application-specific integrated circuits (ASICs) — from major cloud service providers...
    句中的「known as application-specific integrated circuits (ASICs)」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 custom chips（客製化晶片），相當於關代子句「which are known as...」，省略了「which are」使句子更簡鍊。
  • 分詞構句表附帶結果或原因 (Participial Phrase indicating Concomitant Result/Cause)
    ...driven by strong demand for artificial intelligence (AI), high-performance computing and cloud service applications, as well as rising international raw material prices, the Ministry of Economic Affairs said yesterday.
    句首的過去分詞片語「driven by...」是由被動語態的分詞構句簡化而來，用來解釋主句動作（經濟部說）發生的原因，即外銷訂單的成長是由這些強勁需求與物價上漲所「驅動」的。
  • 獨立分詞構句表同時發生的狀況 (Nominative Absolute Construction)
    ...up 2.3 percentage points from a year earlier, as makers of information and communications technology (ICT) products boosted production abroad, the ministry said.
    句中結合了「數字 + percentage points」來表示幅度上升，並透過連接詞 as 引導原因子句，說明海外生產比例增加的背景原因（即ICT業者提高海外產能）。
  • 長主詞與多重修飾語的句構 (Long Subject with Multiple Modifiers)
    The ministry-compiled diffusion index of export orders — a gauge of manufacturers’ order expectations for the following month — climbed above the neutral level to 52.1...
    主詞「The ministry-compiled diffusion index of export orders」帶有複合形容詞（ministry-compiled）與破折號夾註的名詞同位語（a gauge of...），用以精確補充說明該指數的定義與來源，隨後才接上真正的動詞 climbed。
