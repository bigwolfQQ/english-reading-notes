交通部長陳世凱：交通部達成假期疏運目標

MOTC meets holiday traffic goal: Chen

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • minister n. 部長、大臣
    Minister of Transportation and Communications Chen Shih-kai (陳世凱) yesterday said that the ministry met its goal...
    交通部長陳世凱昨天表示，交通部達成了……的目標
  • ministry n. (政府的)部
    ...said that the ministry met its goal of preventing travelers from being stranded...
    ……表示交通部達成了防止旅客滯留的目標……
  • destination n. 目的地
    ...ensuring they reached their destination during the holiday...
    確保他們在假期中抵達目的地
  • execution n. 實行、執行
    ...when he was asked about the execution of the transportation plan for the four-day Mid-Autumn Festival...
    當他被問及針對四天中秋節假期交通運輸計畫的執行情況時
  • capacity n. 容量、產能、能力
    ...adding that the high-speed rail operator reached the limit of the service capacity that day...
    並補充說高鐵營運商當天已達到服務產能的極限
  • operator n. 營運商、經營者
    I have also asked bus operators to increase Yilan-bound bus services to bring travelers home...
    我也要求客運業者增加宜蘭線的客運班次，以送旅客回家……
  • average n. / adj. 平均、平均的
    More than 846,000 passengers took TRC trains on the same day, about 1.3 times more than its daily average...
    當天有超過 846,000 名旅客搭乘台鐵列車，約為其日均運量的 1.3 倍……
  • peak n. / adj. 尖峰、高峰
    ...to prepare for the post-holiday travel peak.
    以因應收假旅遊尖峰。
  • measure n. 措施、方法
    The measure allowed the company to operate at least one extra northbound train per hour during the peak period...
    這項措施讓該公司在尖峰時段每小時至少能營運一列額外的北上列車……
  • congestion n. 擁塞、堵塞
    ...reducing waiting time for unreserved-seat riders at stations in Chiayi, Yunlin and Changhua, helping ease congestion.
    減少了嘉義、雲林和彰化車站自由座旅客的等候時間，有助於紓解擁擠情況。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞作附加補充說明 (Present Participle as Circumstantial Clause)
    ...adding that his trip to Germany did not interfere with his handling of holiday traffic.
    句型中的 "adding that..." 是分詞片語作後續補充說明，主詞陳部長一邊說了前面的話，同時也「加上了」後面的補充說法，省去了連接詞 and 讓語氣更緊湊。
  • 過去分詞作後置修飾 (Past Participle as Post-modifier)
    ...preventing travelers from being stranded and ensuring they reached their destination during the holiday...
    句中的 "stranded" 是過去分詞，用來修飾前面的受詞 travelers（旅客），表達旅客「被滯留」的被動狀態。
  • 使役動詞用法 (Causative Verb)
    A large number of passengers of non-reserved seat cabins reportedly overflowed to reserved seat cabins, causing many with reserved seats unable to get on their train on time.
    句型使用 "cause + O. + 補語（形容詞或形容詞片語）"，用來表達某事（大量自由座旅客湧入）導致了某個結果（許多對號座旅客無法準時上車）。
  • 條件與讓步副詞子句 (Adverbial Clause of Condition)
    The goal of the holiday transportation plan is not to leave any traveler behind at major transport hubs, and we achieved that goal on the first two days of the long weekend, whether they were at the airport, train station or bust station...
    "whether they were at the airport..." 是一個引導讓步意義的副詞子句，意思是「不論他們是在機場、火車站還是公車站」，用來強調在各種地點都達成了目標。
