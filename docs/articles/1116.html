台灣贏得雙不定向飛靶、武術與體操銅牌

Taiwan win skeet, wushu and gymnastics bronzes

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • event n. （比賽）項目、活動
    Taiwanese sharpshooter Lee Meng-yuan yesterday won bronze in the men’s skeet event at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games...
    台灣射擊選手李孟遠昨天在愛知-名古屋亞運男子不定向飛靶項目中奪得銅牌...
  • clinched v. （動詞 clinch 的過去式）取得、贏得（勝利、獎牌等）
    The 32-year-old clinched the bronze with 29 hits...
    現年32歲的他在擊中29個目標後拿下銅牌...
  • division n. （比賽的）組別、部門
    Zhang Huan-yi secured a bronze medal in the men’s 75kg wushu division after he was disqualified...
    張煥宜在男子75公斤級散打項目中...最終獲得銅牌。
  • secured v. （動詞 secure 的過去式）獲得、確保
    Zhang Huan-yi secured a bronze medal in the men’s 75kg wushu division...
    張煥宜在男子75公斤級散打項目中獲得銅牌...
  • disqualified adj. 被取消資格的
    ...after he was disqualified from his semi-final against Kazakhstan’s Abdurashid Abdullayev due to injury.
    ...因在對陣哈薩克選手阿卜杜拉希德·阿卜杜拉耶夫的準決賽中因傷失利而被取消資格...
  • team n. 團隊、隊伍
    ...gymnasts Tang Chia-hung, Hung Yuan-hsi, Chuang Chia-lung, Lee Chih-kai and Shiao Yu-jan won bronze in the men’s artistic gymnastics team event...
    ...體操選手唐嘉鴻、洪源禧、莊佳龍、李智凱與蕭佑然在男子競技體操團體項目中奪得銅牌...
  • respectively adv. 分別地
    ...finishing behind China and Japan, who took gold and silver respectively.
    ...名列分別奪得金牌與銀牌的中國與日本之後。
  • dedicate v. 奉獻、把……獻給
    He told reporters that he wanted to dedicate his hard-earned medal to his late father...
    他告訴記者，他想將這面得來不易的獎牌獻給已故的父親...
  • consecutive adj. 連續的
    Taiwan won bronze in the men’s artistic gymnastics team event for the second consecutive Asian Games.
    台灣在男子競技體操團體項目中連續第二屆亞運贏得銅牌。
  • including prep. 包含、包括
    Taiwan had won 13 medals at the Asian Games as of last night, including one gold medal in soft tennis mixed doubles, three silvers and eight bronzes.
    截至昨晚，台灣在亞運已贏得13面獎牌，其中包括軟式網球混合雙打的一面金牌、三面銀牌和八面銅牌。

文法重點

  • 非限定性關係代名詞子句（補充說明）
    The 32-year-old clinched the bronze with 29 hits, finishing behind Qatar’s Rashid Saleh al-Athba, who won gold with an Asian record of 34 hits...
    句中的「who won gold...」前面有逗號，屬於非限定性關係代名詞子句，用來補充說明前面「Rashid Saleh al-Athba」這名選手獲得金牌的細節，不可省略。
  • 分詞構句作結果狀語
    ...finishing behind China and Japan, who took gold and silver respectively.
    句尾的「finishing behind...」是由現在分詞 `-ing` 引導的分詞構句，用來表達前面選手們完賽後的名次結果，主詞與主要子句的主詞一致。
  • 被動語態
    ...after he was disqualified from his semi-final against Kazakhstan’s Abdurashid Abdullayev due to injury.
    句中的「was disqualified」使用了「be + 過去分詞」的被動語態結構，強調張煥宜「被」取消資格的這個動作，動作的承受者為主詞 he。
  • 名詞子句作受詞
    He told reporters that he wanted to dedicate his hard-earned medal to his late father...
    連接詞「that」引導的名詞子句「that he wanted to...」作為動詞「told」的直接受詞，用來表達他向記者「所說的內容」。
