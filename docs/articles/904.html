台灣作家吳明益《複眼人》立陶宛文版發行

Lithuanian edition of Wu Ming-yi’s ‘The Man with the Compound Eyes’ released

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • release v. / n. 發行、出版、釋放
    The Lithuanian translation of Taiwanese writer Wu Ming-yi’s (吳明益) The Man with the Compound Eyes (複眼人) has been released in Lithuania.
    台灣作家吳明益的小說《複眼人》立陶宛文譯本已在立陶宛發行。
  • challenge n. / v. 挑戰
    In a recent interview with the Central News Agency, he said the biggest challenge was not understanding the Chinese text, but conveying its nuances and style in his native language.
    他在最近接受中央通訊社採訪時表示，最大的挑戰不是理解中文文本，而是用他的母語傳達其語意與風格。
  • convey v. 傳達、表達
    Bacianskas said the biggest challenge in translating his first full-length Chinese novel was conveying the subtleties and style of the original work.
    巴奇安斯卡斯表示，翻譯他的第一部中文長篇小說最大的挑戰是傳達原著的微妙之處與風格。
  • achieve v. 實現、達成
    He said he spent considerable time adjusting the tone and wording to achieve natural fluency in Lithuanian while preserving the literary texture of the Chinese original.
    他表示自己花費了相當多的時間調整語調和措詞，以便在立陶宛語中實現自然的流暢度，同時保留中文原著的文學質地。
  • considerable adj. 相當大的、相當多的
    He said he spent considerable time adjusting the tone and wording to achieve natural fluency in Lithuanian while preserving the literary texture of the Chinese original.
    他表示自己花費了相當多的時間調整語調和措詞，以便在立陶宛語中實現自然的流暢度，同時保留中文原著的文學質地。
  • intentionally adv. 刻意地、故意地
    He therefore intentionally rendered some chapters in the present tense in hopes of conveying a sense that “the scene was happening in front of their eyes.”
    因此，他刻意將某些章節譯成現在式，希望能傳達出一種「場景正在他們眼前發生」的感覺。
  • invitation n. 邀請
    Bacianskas first became acquainted with Wu and his work in 2023, when he served as an interpreter for the author during a visit to Vilnius, the capital of Lithuania, at the invitation of the World Federation of Taiwanese Associations.
    巴奇安斯卡斯在2023年首次結識吳明益及其作品，當時他應世界台灣同鄉會聯合會之邀，在該作者訪問立陶宛首都維爾紐斯期間擔任口譯。
  • publisher n. 出版社、發行人
    The publisher knew of his connection with Wu and invited him to translate the novel, he said.
    他說，該出版社知道他與吳明益的淵源，便邀請他翻譯這部小說。
  • explicitly adv. 明確地
    However, he later realized that a book does not have to explicitly introduce Taiwan to achieve that goal, as literature can also convey the country through imagination and the issues explored through its characters.
    然而，他後來意識到，一本書不一定要明確地介紹台灣才能達到這個目標，因為文學也可以透過想像力及其角色探討的議題來傳達這個國家。
  • portray v. 描繪、描寫
    To more accurately portray a scene involving the cracking and eating of sunflower seeds, he bought a bag of sunflower seeds himself so he could experience it firsthand.
    為了更精確地描繪一個涉及嗑瓜子和吃瓜子的場景，他自己買了一袋瓜子，以便能親身體驗。
  • diversity n. 多樣性、多元性
    For Bacianskas, Taiwan’s diversity within a relatively small geographic area was particularly appealing.
    對巴奇安斯卡斯來說，台灣在相對較小的地理區域內呈現的多樣性特別吸引人。
  • appealing adj. 有吸引力的、令人著迷的
    For Bacianskas, Taiwan’s diversity within a relatively small geographic area was particularly appealing.
    對巴奇安斯卡斯來說，台灣在相對較小的地理區域內呈現的多樣性特別吸引人。

文法重點

  • 非限定關係代間子句（Whose 用法）
    The novel follows two very different people — an outcast from a mythical island and a woman on the verge of suicide — whose lives become unexpectedly intertwined when a tsunami sends a massive island of garbage crashing into Taiwan’s coast...
    句中的「whose lives」為所有格關係代名詞，用來引導非限定子句，修飾前方的人（two very different people），表示「這兩個人的生活」，在句中作所有格並連接兩個子句。
  • 分詞構句作後置修飾（V-ing）
    Bacianskas said he was impressed by the immersiveness of Chinese, contrasting it with European languages whose grammatical tenses can constantly remind readers that events took place in the past.
    句尾的「contrasting it with...」為現在分詞（V-ing）引導的分詞構句，在此表達伴隨的動作或結果，主詞是他（Bacianskas）主動進行對比的動作。
  • 現在分詞當形容詞使用
    To more accurately portray a scene involving the cracking and eating of sunflower seeds, he bought a bag of sunflower seeds himself...
    句中的「involving」是現在分詞當作形容詞，用來修飾前方的名詞「a scene」，相當於關代子句「which involved...」，表示「包含……的場景」。
