舉證責任在蔣萬安身上

The burden of proof is on Chiang

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • criticize v. 批評，指責
    Taipei City Mayor Chiang Wan-an (蔣萬安) of the Chinese Nationalist Party (KMT) wrote on Facebook a post criticizing Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) Legislator and Taipei mayoral candidate Puma Shen (沈伯洋) for a 2014 op-ed on drug policy.
    中國國民黨籍台北市長蔣萬安在臉書發文，批評民主進步黨立法委員兼台北市長候選人沈伯洋在2014年一篇關於毒品政策的投書。
  • orchestrate v. 策劃，精心安排
    Chiang said the flood of activity was orchestrated by the ruling party.
    蔣萬安表示，這波留言浪潮是由執政黨所策劃的。
  • evidence n. 證據，證物
    If he wishes to make such claims, Chiang cannot simply point to the volume of comments as evidence; he must look a little closer.
    如果他希望做出這樣的指控，蔣萬安不能只是指出留言的數量作為證據；他必須更仔細地檢視。
  • proportion n. 比例，部分
    At most, this proves that people could have used bots to leave comments on the post, but there is no indication of what proportion of comments they might account for, how much traffic was due to coordinated behavior, or who implemented any of it.
    最多這只能證明有人可能曾使用機器人程式在該貼文中留下留言，但完全沒有跡象表明這些留言佔了多少比例、有多少流量是由有組織的行為所造成的，或是由誰執行了这一切。
  • implement v. 實施，執行
    At most, this proves that people could have used bots to leave comments on the post, but there is no indication of what proportion of comments they might account for, how much traffic was due to coordinated behavior, or who implemented any of it.
    最多這只能證明有人可能曾使用機器人程式在該貼文中留下留言，但完全沒有跡象表明這些留言佔了多少比例、有多少流量是由有組織的行為所造成的，或是由誰執行了这一切。
  • disproportionate adj. 不成比例的
    Chiang claimed that Threads had 90 million registered users in Taiwan, disproportionate to its population size of 23 million.
    蔣萬安聲稱 Threads 在台灣擁有 9000 萬名註冊用戶，這與其 2300 萬的人口規模不成比例。
  • statistic n. 統計數據
    The Taiwan FackCheck Center has said that the 90 million figure is not from Meta’s official statistics.
    台灣事實查核中心表示，9000萬這個數字並非來自 Meta 的官方統計數據。
  • acknowledge v. 承認
    Resourcera has since acknowledged the error and removed the statistic from its Web site.
    Resourcera 隨後已承認這個錯誤，並將該統計數據從其網站上移除。
  • accusation n. 指控，控告
    In sum, Chiang’s first accusation offered no definitive evidence, while his second simply repeated debunked information.
    總而言之，蔣萬安的第一項指控沒有提供確鑿的證據，而他的第二項指控則只是重申已被推翻的錯誤資訊。
  • grounds n. 理由，根據
    However, we cannot be certain that someone posting about building a comment bot is grounds to dismiss the entire outcry as being the result of green-camp online armies and ruling party power privileges.
    然而，我們不能確定有人發文討論製作留言機器人，就能成為將整起抗議聲浪歸咎於綠營網軍及執政黨權力特權而予以駁回的理由。
  • serious adj. 嚴肅的，認真的
    As the election heats up, Chiang must accept the burden of proof for his accusations seriously and point to verifiable accounts, coordinated behaviors or official platform data that support his case.
    隨著選情升溫，蔣萬安必須嚴肅看待其指控的舉證責任，並支持其論點的可查證帳號、有組織的行為或官方平台數據。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作後置修飾（現在分詞片語）
    On Saturday last week, Taipei City Mayor Chiang Wan-an (蔣萬安) of the Chinese Nationalist Party (KMT) wrote on Facebook a post criticizing Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) Legislator and Taipei mayoral candidate Puma Shen (沈伯洋) for a 2014 op-ed on drug policy.
    句中的 "criticizing..." 為現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 "a post"（一則貼文），相當於關代子句 "which criticized..." 的簡化版，省略了主格關代與be動詞，直接用分詞來表達主動的修飾關係。
  • 條件句與助動詞倒裝結構
    If he wishes to make such claims, Chiang cannot simply point to the volume of comments as evidence; he must look a little closer.
    此句使用了 "If + 現在式動詞"（If he wishes）來表達真實條件句，主句則使用情態助動詞 "cannot" 與 "must" 表達必然性或建議，是多益商業英文中常用來規範條件與義務的句型。
  • 被動語態的應用
    Chiang said the flood of activity was orchestrated by the ruling party.
    子句中的 "was orchestrated" 採用了被動語態（be + 過去分詞），強調動作的承受者（the flood of activity）而非動作的執行者，在新聞英語中常用於客觀描述事件或歸咎責任。
  • 過去分詞作形容詞修飾
    In sum, Chiang’s first accusation offered no definitive evidence, while his second simply repeated debunked information.
    句中的 "debunked" 是過去分詞，在此作為形容詞放在名詞 "information" 前面，用來修飾「已被揭穿／被推翻的」資訊，這類過去分詞形容詞在多益測驗中經常出現（例如：updated report, approved budget）。
