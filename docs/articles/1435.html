書評：試圖阻止Doge的無名英雄們

Book review: The quiet heroes who tried to stop Doge

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • systemic adj. 系統性的、體制的
    US President Donald Trump’s catastrophic errors get so much attention that his systemic ones can easily be missed.
    美國總統川普災難性的錯誤受到了高度關注，以至於他系統性的錯誤很容易被忽視。
  • disintegrate v. 瓦解、碎裂、崩潰
    While the world looks on in dismay at his farcical war in Iran, the thuggery of Ice agents or clownish attacks on erstwhile allies like Canada and Denmark, the core functions of the American government are slowly disintegrating.
    正當全世界驚恐地看著他在伊朗荒謬的戰爭、移民及海關執法局暴徒般的行徑，或是對加拿大和丹麥等昔日盟友的滑稽攻擊時，美國政府的核心職能正在緩慢瓦解。
  • lay-off n. 解雇、裁員
    Outbreaks of disease — such as the recent wave of explosive diarrhea across the US — might have happened anyway, but were probably made worse by lay-offs at the Centers for Disease Control.
    疾病的爆發——例如最近美國各地爆發的嚴重腹瀉浪潮——可能本來就會發生，但很可能是因疾病管制與預防中心的裁員而惡化。
  • revenue n. （政府）稅收、收益、營業收入
    Cuts to tax collection also seem to have reduced revenue by multiple billions, though we cannot be sure of the causal relationship.
    對稅收徵管的刪減似乎也使稅收減少了數十億美元，儘管我們無法確定其中的因果關係。
  • bureaucracy n. 官僚體系、官僚主義
    The other problem is that bureaucracies are hard to make interesting.
    另一個問題是，官僚體系很難讓人感興趣。
  • transition n. 過渡、轉變
    Blockers is a loose sequel to the The Fifth Risk, in which Lewis focused on the transition from the Obama to the Trump administration and the disruption caused by the new team’s almost complete lack of interest in their jobs.
    《阻擋者》是《第五大風險》的續作，路易斯在前作中聚焦於從歐巴馬政府到川普政府的政權交接，以及新團隊對工作幾乎完全缺乏興趣所造成的混亂。
  • indifference n. 漠不關心、冷漠
    This time around the problem is not indifference but malicious intent to destroy.
    這回的問題不再是冷漠，而是具備破壞性的惡意。
  • resign v. 辭職
    In truth, they don’t do much blocking — since they all either end up resigning when they’re asked to do something illegal, or are fired.
    事實上，他們並沒有做太多的阻擋——因為他們最終要嘛在被要求做違法事情時辭職，要嘛就是被開除。
  • untenable adj. （立場等）站不住腳的、難以為繼的
    After Trump’s re-election he was effectively forced out, saying that his position had become “untenable.”
    川普連任後，他實際上被逼離開，並稱自己的職位已變得「難以為繼」。
  • enhance v. 提高、增加、增強
    Through Lavingia, Lewis gives us a taste of what serious work to enhance the state might have looked like.
    透過拉溫吉亞，路易斯讓我們見識到了強化國家的嚴肅工作原本可能是什麼樣子。
  • dysfunctional adj. 功能失常的、不正常的
    There are real problems with the way the government operates, both in the UK and the US, caused by poor resourcing, dysfunctional systems or low pay for technical staff compared with the private sector.
    無論是在英國還是美國，政府的運作方式都存在真正的問題，這些問題是由資源不足、體系失調，或是技術人員的薪酬遠低於私營部門所引起的。
  • enact v. 制定（法律）、實施、頒布
    One of the most irritating aspects of Doge’s brief existence was the stream of op-eds from people who really should have known better celebrating that someone was finally trying to enact reform...
    Doge 短暫存在期間最令人惱火的面向之一，是來自那些本該更懂事的評論文章串流，他們慶幸終於有人試圖推行改革...

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作後置修飾語
    The “blockers” are the dedicated officials who try to stop the wanton and often illegal destruction of their life’s work by Elon Musk’s Doge or other agents of chaos.
    句中的 "working on preventing wildfires" 或 "trying to block Musk’s goons..."（在此段前文出現的例子）等現在分詞片語，可用來修飾前面的名詞，相當於省略了「關係代代名詞 + be 動詞」（例如：who are trying to...），使句子更為精簡。
  • 表結果的分詞構句
    Musk causes havoc, but quickly gets bored of Doge and heads back to Silicon Valley.
    雖然本句是用對等連接詞 "but" 連接兩個動詞片語，但在文章其他地方（如："...saying that his position had become 'untenable.'"）常使用 "V-ing" 分詞構句來表達伴隨發生的狀況或自然而然的結果，多益閱讀測驗常考這種分詞構句的空格填空。
  • 過去分詞片語作後置定語與被動語態
    Much of the book is taken up with descriptions of the valuable work they did before being turfed out.
    句中的 "taken up with" 是被動語態的結構，用來表達「被……佔去／充斥著」；而後方的 "before being turfed out" 則是介系詞後接動名詞的被動式（before + being + p.p.），表示「在被趕出去之前」，這在多益的商業合規與職場文章中是非常道地的表達法。
  • 虛主詞 It 與名詞子句的搭配
    It feels as if there is more to say about the personal journeys that lead people to behave this way.
    句首的 "It" 是虛主詞，用來代替後方由 "as if" 引導的表語子句。這種 "It feels as if..."（感覺好像……）的句型在多益聽力測驗與日常職場對話中常用於表達個人主觀的主張或感受。
