文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The products, to be launched this month in Tokyo and neighboring regions, were created using AI around the theme...
    句中的 "were created" 採用了被動語態（be + 過去分詞），用來強調動作的承受者（產品 products），而不特別強調是誰去創造它們。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    The products, to be launched this month in Tokyo and neighboring regions, were created using AI...
    句中的 "to be launched this month in Tokyo and neighboring regions" 是不定詞片語作後位修飾，用來補充說明主詞 The products 即將被推出的時間與地點。
  • 過去分詞片語作修飾語 (Past Participle Phrase)
    “To generate ideas unconstrained by existing ways of thinking or market trends, we used AI...”
    句中的 "unconstrained by existing ways of thinking..." 是過去分詞片語，修飾前面的代名詞 ideas，相當於形容詞子句 "which were unconstrained by..." 的簡化用法，使語氣更精鍊。
  • 對等連接詞表達對比 (While for Contrast)
    Like Lawson’s products, AI was used primarily for brainstorming, while human specialists were still involved in product development, the company said.
    在此句中，"while" 作為對等連接詞使用，表示「而、然而」，用來對比說明 AI 的用途與人類專家的參與這兩者之間的差異。
