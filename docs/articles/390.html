Philippines races to find survivors of deadly ferry fire

單字解析

  • deploy v. 部署、調動
    The Philippines yesterday deployed coast guard, military and civilian vessels in a race to find survivors...
    菲律賓昨日部署了海岸防衛隊、軍方和民用船隻，全力搜救生還者...
  • survivor n. 生還者、幸存者
    ...in a race to find survivors on a smoldering passenger-and-cargo ferry...
    ...在仍在悶燒的客貨兩用渡輪上全力搜救生還者...
  • capacity n. 容量、載客量、能力
    According to the vessel’s manifest, the ferry was carrying 117 passengers and 17 crew members and had a maximum capacity of 330 people.
    根據船隻的乘客名單，該渡輪載有117名乘客和17名船員，其最大載客量為330人。
  • cooperate v. 合作、配合
    The vessel’s owner, Atienza Inter-Island Ferries, said it was fully cooperating with government authorities investigating the incident.
    該渡輪的擁有者「阿蒂恩薩島際渡輪公司」表示，正全面配合政府當局對事件的調查。
  • resources n. 資源
    “We are working around the clock and have mobilized all available resources to manage this crisis,” the company said...
    「我們正日夜趕工，並已動員所有可用資源來應對這場危機，」該公司表示...
  • investigation n. 調查
    ...she said it was too early to draw conclusions and that the investigation was ongoing.
    ...她表示現在下結論還言之過早，調查仍在進行中。
  • manifest n. 載貨單、旅客名單
    According to the vessel’s manifest, the ferry was carrying 117 passengers and 17 crew members...
    根據船隻的乘客名單，該渡輪載有117名乘客和17名船員...
  • inspection n. 檢查、檢驗
    The vessel had passed safety inspections, Cayabyab said.
    卡亞比亞布表示，該船已通過安全檢查。
  • statement n. 聲明、陳述
    “We are working around the clock and have mobilized all available resources to manage this crisis,” the company said in a statement...
    「我們正日夜趕工，並已動員所有可用資源來應對這場危機，」該公司在聲明中表示...
  • transport v. / n. 運輸、運送（註：文章衍生字彙應用）
    It was also carrying five motorcycles, an electric vehicle, a forklift, a pickup truck and mostly cargo goods, she said.
    她說，船上還載有五輛摩托車、一電動車、一輛堆高機、一輛小貨卡以及大部分的貨物。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（片語）作後置定語
    Drone footage released by the Philippine Coast Guard showed the blackened MV June Aster surrounded by clear blue-green waters, with the vessel still generating smoke hours after the fire broke out on Wednesday night.
    句中的 "surrounded by..." 是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的受詞 "the blackened MV June Aster"（被包圍在...）；而 "still generating smoke" 則是現在分詞片語，用來補充說明當下的狀態，分詞片語能讓句子在不使用完整子句的情況下，更精鍊地表達附加動作或狀態。
  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    So far 43 people, including two Chilean nationals, have been rescued from the ferry, which was on a voyage from Baseco in Manila to Coron, a popular tourist destination in Palawan known for its diving sites and island-hopping attractions.
    句尾的 ", which was on a voyage..." 是一個非限定性關係子句，用逗號隔開，對前面的先行詞 "the ferry" 補充說明其航行資訊。非限定性子句在多益閱讀測驗中常考，用來測試代名詞的指代與句意連貫。
  • 獨立分詞構句（With + 名詞 + 分詞）
    ...with the vessel still generating smoke hours after the fire broke out on Wednesday night.
    此處使用了 "with + 名詞 + 現在分詞 (generating)" 的結構，用來表達伴隨狀況（在...的同時/伴隨著...）。這在英文新聞寫作與多益高級商業閱讀中非常常見，能有效整合兩個相關的同時發生動作。
