單字解析

  • tarnish v. 玷污、敗壞（名譽等）
    an Asian Games dogged by organizational woes has tarnished that image and raised questions over future bids, experts said.
    專家表示，飽受組織問題困擾的亞運會玷污了這一形象，並引發了對未來申辦的質疑。
  • bid n. 投標、爭取、申辦
    If Japan were to bid for the Olympics again, trust in Japan may have fallen, and it might be harder to win a bid
    如果日本再次申辦奧運會，日本的信譽可能會下降，贏得申辦可能會更困難
  • grapple with v. phr. 努力應對、搏鬥
    Many in Japan have questioned the value of hosting major sporting events as they grapple with the fallout from the graft-stained and COVID-19 pandemic-delayed Tokyo Olympics.
    日本許多人在努力應對受醜聞玷污且因新冠肺炎疫情延期的東京奧運會帶來的後果時，質疑舉辦大型體育賽事的價值。
  • fallout n. 不良後果、放射性落塵
    Many in Japan have questioned the value of hosting major sporting events as they grapple with the fallout from the graft-stained and COVID-19 pandemic-delayed Tokyo Olympics.
    日本許多人在努力應對受醜聞玷污且因新冠肺炎疫情延期的東京奧運會帶來的後果時，質疑舉辦大型體育賽事的價值。
  • cost overrun n. phr. 成本超支
    Cost overruns, corruption and COVID-19 all tainted the public’s memory of the Olympic Games
    成本超支、腐敗和新冠肺炎都玷污了公眾對奧運會的記憶
  • corruption n. 腐敗、貪污
    Cost overruns, corruption and COVID-19 all tainted the public’s memory of the Olympic Games
    成本超支、腐敗和新冠肺炎都玷污了公眾對奧運會的記憶
  • subsequently adv. 隨後、後來
    The country subsequently dropped the idea of hosting the Winter Olympics in the northern city of Sapporo because of a lack of public support.
    由於缺乏公眾支持，該國隨後放棄了在北部城市札幌舉辦冬季奧運會的想法。
  • reaffirm v. 重申、再次確認

  • analyze v. 分析
    This is all part of the Games, but we need to analyze those issues so we can tackle them for the next future opportunity.
    這是賽事的一部分，但我們需要分析這些問題，以便為下一次的機會加以解決。
  • tackle v. 處理、應對、解決
    This is all part of the Games, but we need to analyze those issues so we can tackle them for the next future opportunity.
    這是賽事的一部分，但我們需要分析這些問題，以便為下一次的機會加以解決。
  • balloon v. 暴漲、迅速增加
    Asian Games organizers were presented with a headache when the number of athletes and officials that was initially expected ballooned by about 2,000.
    當最初預期的運動員和官員人數激增約 2,000 人時，亞運會主辦單位面臨了一個頭痛的問題。

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Japan is traditionally seen as a reliable host for major sporting events, but an Asian Games dogged by organizational woes has tarnished that image...
    句中的「dogged by organizational woes」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的主詞「an Asian Games」，相當於形容詞子句「which was dogged by organizational woes」，意指「飽受組織問題困擾的（亞運會）」。在多益閱讀測驗中，常考分詞作後位修飾的簡化與辨析。
  • 假設語氣第二態 (Second Conditional)
    “If I were to give them a grade, it would be an F.”
    此句表示對現在或未來假設情況的看法。結構為「If + 主詞 + were to V, 主詞 + would + V原形」。用來假設一個不太可能發生或純屬假設的情境（如果我要給他們打分數的話），是多益商業談判或情境題中常出現的句構。
  • 現在分詞片語作附帶狀況/結果 (Present Participle Phrase of Result/Circumstance)
    Many in Japan have questioned the value of hosting major sporting events as they grapple with the fallout from the graft-stained and COVID-19 pandemic-delayed Tokyo Olympics.
    句尾的「as they grapple with...」是由連接詞 as 引導的時間/原因副詞子句，用來補充說明主句動作發生時的背景狀況（正當他們在努力應對……後果時）。多益聽力與閱讀常利用這種結構表達「同時發生」或「因果關係」的商務情境。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Japan was praised by some for getting the Games on in unprecedented circumstances.
    此句使用了被動語態「was praised by...」（被……稱讚），焦點放在接受動作的主體（日本）身上，而非指出是誰稱讚。新聞英文中為了客觀報導或強調受事者，常大量使用被動語態，這也是多益文法題（尤其是動詞語態選擇）的核心考點。
