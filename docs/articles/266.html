電影產業在性別平權上走回頭路：導演如是說

Movie industry backsliding on gender: director

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • competition n. 競賽、比賽
    When Danish director May el-Toukhy was selected for the top competition at the Venice Film Festival in July...
    當丹麥導演梅·埃爾-圖基於 7 月入選威尼斯影展的最高主競賽單元時...
  • progress n. 進展、進步
    ...while pointing out that progress on gender bias made in the wake of the 2017 #MeToo movement appeared to be reversing.
    ...同時指出 2017 年 #MeToo 運動後在消除性別偏見方面所取得的進展似乎正在倒退。
  • imbalance n. 不平衡、失衡
    “After #MeToo, I think all of us were really aware of this gender imbalance,” she told reporters.
    「在 #MeToo 之後，我認為我們所有人都真正意識到這種性別失衡，」她告訴記者。
  • protagonist n. 主角、主演
    The protagonist of Woman Unknown is a bride-to-be struggling to hide her wartime secret...
    《Woman Unknown》的主角是一位即將結婚的新娘，她掙扎著隱瞞自己的戰時祕密...
  • process n. 過程、程序
    The dearth of females in the top Venice competition has sparked nagging questions about the selection process and female representation.
    威尼斯最高競賽單元中女性的匱乏，引發了對評選過程和女性代表性的質疑。
  • submissions n. 提交（的作品/文件）
    ...when he said the initial 20-1 male domination of the top competition was because the submissions from women “were not enough to put them into the competition.”
    ...當時他表示最高競賽單元最初 20 比 1 的男性主導局面，是因為女性投稿的「數量不足以讓她們進入競賽」。
  • premiere v. 首映、初演
    ...the documentary NAZA about the Israeli killing of civilians in Gaza, which is to premiere on Thursday.
    ...紀錄片《NAZA》內容關於以色列在加薩殺害平民，該片將於週四首映。
  • career n. 職業生涯、事業
    El-Toukhy, a Danish-Egyptian director who has worked on Netflix’s The Queen, said she had encountered gender bias throughout her career and even with her latest film.
    埃爾-圖基是一位曾參與 Netflix 影集《王冠》製作的丹麥埃及裔導演，她表示自己整個職業生涯中都遭遇過性別偏見，甚至在她的最新電影中也是如此。
  • pitch v. 推銷、兜售（提案）
    “When I pitched the film to people, I could see that they were glazing over...”
    「當我向人們推銷這部電影時，我可以看出他們眼神呆滯...」
  • financing n. 財務資助、資金
    After film school, she said she struggled to find financing for her first film.
    她表示，電影學院畢業後，她很難為自己的第一部電影找到資金。
  • peers n. 同儕、同輩
    “I think it took me six or seven years, whereas my male peers, they were much faster than me,” she said.
    「我想這花了我六、七年的時間，而我的男性同儕比我快得多，」她說。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present/Past Participle Phrases)
    When Danish director May el-Toukhy was selected for the top competition at the Venice Film Festival in July, she was the only female filmmaker in a 20-strong field — with a movie “about the invisibility of women.”
    句中的「selected for the top competition...」為過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的主詞片語（Danish director May el-Toukhy）；而內文中的「co-directed by a woman」則是過去分詞片語修飾前面的 documentary NAZA。這種結構能有效濃縮句子，省去關代與be動詞。
  • 現在分詞表附帶狀況或結果 (Present Participle as Adverbial)
    ...while pointing out that progress on gender bias made in the wake of the 2017 #MeToo movement appeared to be reversing.
    「while pointing out...」中使用了現在分詞（pointing out），前面省略了主詞與be動詞（while she was pointing out）。這常用於連接詞後方，表示主句動作同時發生的附帶動作，是新聞英文中非常道地且精鍊的寫法。
  • 關代子句修飾先行詞 (Relative Clause)
    The powerful film, which drew enthusiastic applause after a press screening on Sunday, is “about being objectified on one hand and on the other hand being unseen and voiceless,” el-Toukhy said.
    「which drew enthusiastic applause after a press screening on Sunday」是一個非限定性關代子句，補充說明前面的主詞「The powerful film」。非限定性子句前後皆需使用逗號隔開，能為句子添加額外細節。
  • 虛主詞 It 的句型 (Introductory 'It' Structure)
    “I do think it’s much harder for women to get their films financed,” Gyllenhaal said in the first week of the festival.
    句中的 「it's much harder for women to get their films financed」 使用了虛主詞 "it"，真正的受詞/主詞是後方的不定詞片語「to get their films financed」。同時配合「for + 恆量對象（women）」來指明動作的執行者，是英文表達意見時極常見的句構。
