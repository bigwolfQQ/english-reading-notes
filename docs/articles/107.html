  • revive v. 使復甦，重振

  • urge v. 敦促，力勸
    Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskiy on Friday said Ukraine would halt airstrikes during the envoys’ visit and urged Moscow to do the same.

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表附帶狀況或結果）
    Dmitriev shared photos of him greeting the US delegation as they disembarked, alongside the caption: “Welcome to Moscow Peacemakers.”
    句中的「greeting the US delegation」是由現在分詞 greeting 引導的分詞片語，用來修飾前面代名詞 him，表達附帶的動作，相當於形容詞子句「who greeted the US delegation」的簡化用法。
  • 被動語態（Present Perfect Passive）
    Washington’s push to end the fighting, now in its fifth year, has lost momentum as US attention has been focused for the past six months on its war with Iran.
    句中的「has been focused」使用了現在完成式的被動語態（have/has been + 過去分詞），用來強調美國的注意力「在過去六個月中一直被集中於」與伊朗的戰爭這項持續狀態。
  • 現在分詞當形容詞使用
    ...Russia had intensified its attacks on Kyiv and other cities with relentless bombardments recently stretching into daylight hours...
    句中的「stretching into daylight hours」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的「relentless bombardments（無情轟炸）」，表示這些轟炸持續延伸到白天的狀態。
  • 分詞構句（表目的）
    The long-range strikes by Ukraine have hit Russian military facilities, oil refineries and e-commerce hubs, seeking to undermine the Kremlin’s war effort and economy...
    句尾的「seeking to undermine...」是現在分詞片語作分詞構句，用來補充說明前面的長程打擊行動其「目的」為何（尋求破壞克里姆林宮的戰事與經濟），主詞與主要子句的主詞一致。
