文法重點

  • 受詞補語（分詞作受詞補語）
    A technology worker developed a bald patch after months of overtime and late nights, but showed improvement after four months of treatment and lifestyle adjustments...
    句中的 developed (developed a bald patch) 是主動動詞。此處句構展現了標準的主動語態與對等連接詞 (but) 連接兩個動詞片語（developed... but showed...），描述主詞在經歷一段時間後產生的狀態轉變。
  • 「介系詞 + 關係代名詞」的形容詞子句
    The condition is an autoimmune disease in which the immune system mistakenly attacks healthy hair follicles, disrupting normal growth cycles, she said.
    “in which” 引導一個關係子句，用來修飾前面的先行詞 “an autoimmune disease”。介系詞 in 提到前面是因為 immune system 在疾病「之中」運作，相當於 “...disease where the immune system...”，這在多益閱讀測驗的文法題中非常常考。
  • 現在分詞短語作後位修飾
    He was diagnosed with alopecia areata, a prevalent non-scarring form of hair loss, which typically presents as clearly defined round or oval smooth bald patches...
    句中的 “a prevalent non-scarring form of hair loss” 是同位語，用來補充說明前面的專有名詞 “alopecia areata”。隨後的 “which typically presents as...” 則是關係代名詞子句，用來修飾前面的圓形禿症狀。
  • 現在分詞引導附帶狀況（分詞構句）
    句中的 “when looking in the mirror, styling their hair...” 省略了主語與be動詞（原貌約等於 when they are looking...），簡化為「連接詞 + 現在分詞」的形式，用來表達「當……的時候」發生的動作，是多益測驗中常出現簡化子句的寫法。
