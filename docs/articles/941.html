勞動部長表示中國 APEC 會議未遭「降格」

Labor minister says no 'downgrading' at China APEC meeting

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • participate v. 參加，參與
    Labor Minister Hung Sun-han said Tuesday that Taiwan was not subjected to any "downgrading" during his participation in an Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) ministerial meeting...
    勞動部長洪申翰週二表示，台灣在參與亞太經濟合作會議（APEC）部長級會議期間，並未遭受任何「降格」...
  • ministerial adj. 部長的，內閣的
    ...during his participation in an Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) ministerial meeting in China...
    ...在他參與於中國舉行的亞太經濟合作會議（APEC）部長級會議期間...
  • grant v. 授予，給予
    ...and was granted the same rights as other members.
    ...並且被授予與其他成員相同的權利。
  • encounter v. 遭遇，遇到
    "During our participation in the meeting, we did not encounter any so-called downgrading," Hung told reporters...
    洪申翰告訴記者：「在我們參與會議期間，我們沒有遇到任何所謂的降格。」
  • administration n. 政府（執政團隊），管理
    ...becoming the first ministry head in President Lai Ching-te's (賴清德) administration to travel to China.
    ...成為賴清德總統政府中第一位赴中國訪問的部會首長。
  • assistance n. 協助，援助
    ...through our continued insistence and with the assistance of the APEC Secretariat...
    ...透過我們持續的堅持以及 APEC 秘書處的協助...
  • equal adj. 相等的，平等的
    ...the Chinese side granted us rights equal to those of other members in accordance with APEC rules.
    ...中方根據 APEC 規則，賦予我們與其他成員平等的權利。
  • in accordance with phr. 依照，與...一致
    ...the Chinese side granted us rights equal to those of other members in accordance with APEC rules.
    ...中方根據 APEC 規則，賦予我們與其他成員平等的權利。
  • consistent adj. 一致的
    That was consistent with Taiwan's insistence on "reciprocal dignity," Hung said...
    洪申翰表示，這與台灣堅持的「對等尊嚴」一致...
  • reciprocal adj. 相互的，對等的
    That was consistent with Taiwan's insistence on "reciprocal dignity," Hung said, referring to a principle he had laid out before departing for China...
    洪申翰表示，這與台灣堅持的「對等尊嚴」一致，他指的是他出發前往中國之前所闡明的原則...
  • depart v. 出發，離開
    ...a principle he had laid out before departing for China for APEC...
    ...他出發前往中國參加 APEC 之前所闡明的原則...
  • confirm v. 確認
    The minister also confirmed that he neither applied for nor used a "Taiwan compatriot permit"...
    部長也證實，他在這次行程中既未申請也未使用「台灣居民來往大陸通行證」...
  • apply for phr. 申請
    The minister also confirmed that he neither applied for nor used a "Taiwan compatriot permit"...
    部長也證實，他在這次行程中既未申請也未使用「台灣居民來往大陸通行證」...
  • reiterate v. 重申
    Asked whether the nameplate reading "Chinese Taipei" in front of him at the meeting constituted downgrading, Hung reiterated that Chinese Taipei is Taiwan's formal membership name in APEC.
    當被問及面前寫著「中華台北」的名牌是否構成降格時，洪申翰重申，中華台北是台灣在 APEC 的正式會籍名稱。
  • ensure v. 確保，保證
    ...and calling on China, as this year's host, to ensure equal participation for all members.
    ...並呼籲中國身為主辦方，應確保所有 members（成員）的平等參與。

文法重點

  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense)
    That was consistent with Taiwan's insistence on "reciprocal dignity," Hung said, referring to a principle he had laid out before departing for China for APEC...
    句中的「had laid out」使用了過去完成式（had + 過去分詞），用來表達在過去某個時間點（洪申翰說話/出發）之前就已經完成的動作（先前闡明原則）。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Asked whether the nameplate reading "Chinese Taipei" in front of him at the meeting constituted downgrading, Hung reiterated that...
    句首的「Asked whether...」是由過去分詞片語引導的結構，省略了主詞與be動詞（原句形如 When he was asked...），用來修飾主句的主詞 Hung，表被動與時間/條件關係。而「reading...」則是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 nameplate，表主動（名牌上面寫著...）。
  • 間接問句 / 名詞子句 (Indirect Question / Noun Clause)
    Asked whether the nameplate reading "Chinese Taipei" in front of him at the meeting constituted downgrading, Hung reiterated that...
    「whether the nameplate... constituted downgrading」是由連接詞 whether 引導的名詞子句，在句中作為過去分詞 Asked 的受詞，採用「主詞 + 動詞」的直述句語序，表示「是否...」。
  • 對等連接詞片語 neither... nor...
    The minister also confirmed that he neither applied for nor used a "Taiwan compatriot permit"...
    「neither... nor...」意為「既不...也不...」，是用來連接兩個對等動詞（applied for 與 used）的對等連接詞片語，強調兩者皆無。
