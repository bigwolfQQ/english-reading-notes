文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    ...local media reported that the Philippine law enforcement vessel, "BLINGNO.1," was tracked briefly leaving Kaohsiung Port before returning and docking on Wednesday.
    句中的「leaving Kaohsiung Port」為現在分詞片語，用來修飾前方被追蹤的船隻狀態，省略了關代與be動詞（which was leaving...），精簡了句子結構。
  • 關係子句補充說明 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    ...referring to three countries that, unlike the Philippines, maintain formal diplomatic relations with Taiwan.
    句中的「that... maintain...」為限定關係代間子句，用來修飾先行詞「three countries」，並在其中夾入插入語「unlike the Philippines」來對比菲律賓的情況。
  • 虛主詞 It 的用法 (Dummy Subject 'It')
    If the mission proceeds as planned, it will mark the first such visit by the CGA to the Philippines...
    句首的「It」為虛主詞，用來代替如果任務如期進行這整件事（conditional clause 所引導的狀況），作為主句的主語。
