文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Lightmatter Inc, a US-based optical interconnect solution start-up, yesterday said it plans to ship its first near-packaged optics (NPO) engines and high-density laser network-interface cards (NICs) next year, adding that this would pave the way for full-scale copackaged optics (CPO) engine production in 2028 and 2029.
    句尾的「adding that...」是由連接詞結合現在分詞構成的分詞構句，用來補充說明前面主句所發生的動作（said）所帶來的結果或進一步的資訊，相當於 and added that...
  • 非限定關係代間子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    The company’s photonic interposer technology, which is considered the most advanced and ultimate stage of CPO, will roll out on a very similar timeline, he said.
    句中的「which is considered...」是一個非限定關係子句，用逗號隔開，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「The company’s photonic interposer technology」。這種子句移開後不影響主要句意。
  • 形容詞子句省略 (Omitted Relative Clause)
    One of the CPO engines with detachable fiber array units is assembled in Taiwan by ASE Technology Holding Co (日月光投控), Lightmatter chief executive officer Nick Harris told a virtual media conference yesterday.
    雖然句中沒有直接使用關係代名詞，但像「assembled in Taiwan by ASE...」是過去分詞片語作後位修飾，省略了「which is / that is」，用來修飾前面的主詞核心「One of the CPO engines...」，讓句型更簡潔。
  • 虛主詞 It 句構 (Dummy Subject 'It' Structure)
    ...but it remains highly unlikely that a standalone 896Gbps SerDes technology would ever materialize, Harris said.
    句中的「it」為形式主詞（虛主詞），真正的受詞或主詞是後面由 that 引導的名詞子句「that a standalone 896Gbps SerDes technology would ever materialize」。這種結構常用於表達「某事極有可能／極不可能」等評價。
