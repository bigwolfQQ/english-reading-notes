發電廠發生事故後，仍有 11 名工人住院治療

11 workers remain hospitalized after power plant accident

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • contractor n. 承攬商，承包商
    A worker with a Taiwan Power Co. (Taipower) contractor has regained consciousness after falling into a hydrochloric acid tank...
    台電公司一名承攬商的工人掉入鹽酸槽中，在恢復意識後...
  • utility n. 公用事業，公用事業公司
    The state-run utility said the accident occurred at around 9:30 a.m. on Tuesday morning...
    這家國營公用事業表示，這起事故發生在週二上午 9 時 30 分左右...
  • maintenance n. 維護，保養
    ...when workers with a contractor were doing maintenance on a hydrochloric acid tank used in the plant's seawater electrolysis process.
    ...當時承攬商的工人正在該廠海水電解流程中所使用的鹽酸槽進行維修。
  • transfer v. 轉移，調動，轉帳
    One of the workers was reportedly transferring hydrochloric acid from the tank...
    據報導其中一名工人在移轉鹽酸時...
  • employ v. 雇用
    ...while five Taipower employees and five workers employed by the contractor who helped with rescue operations reportedly felt unwell...
    ...另外有 5 名台電員工以及 5 名協助救援的承攬商雇用工人，據報感到身體不適...
  • authorities n. 官方，當局
    Fire authorities were soon notified, and the 11 individuals were all rushed to area hospitals for treatment.
    消防單位隨即接獲通報，這 11 人皆被緊急送往鄰近醫院接受治療。
  • investigation n. 調查
    Taipower said it had initiated an investigation to determine the exact cause of the accident...
    台電表示已啟動調查以釐清事故確切原因...
  • operate v. 運作，營運
    ...and all of its generating units were operating normally.
    ...所有發電機組皆正常運作。
  • violate v. 違反，違背
    ...the Kaohsiung City Labor Affairs Bureau determined that the contractor had violated the Occupational Safety and Health Act...
    ...高雄市政府勞工局認定該承攬商違反了《職業安全衛生法》...
  • in accordance with phr. 依據，按照
    ...and would be fined up to NT$3 million (US$90,000) in accordance with the law.
    ...將依法處以最高新台幣 300 萬元（約 9 萬美元）的罰款。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾
    A worker with a Taiwan Power Co. (Taipower) contractor has regained consciousness after falling into a hydrochloric acid tank in the Nanpu Power Plant in Kaohsiung while 10 others remain hospitalized, the company said.
    句中的「falling into a hydrochloric acid tank...」是由現在分詞開頭的片語，用來補充說明工人在哪裡發生意外。介系詞後接動名詞 (after falling) 是多益常見的文法考點。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    ...while five Taipower employees and five workers employed by the contractor who helped with rescue operations reportedly felt unwell...
    句中的「employed by the contractor」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 workers。因為工人是「被」承攬商所雇用，所以這裡使用過去分詞 (employed) 表被動關係。
  • 受詞補語（使役動詞用法）
    Fire authorities were soon notified, and the 11 individuals were all rushed to area hospitals for treatment.
    句中使用被動語態「were all rushed」，be 動詞後接過去分詞 rushed，用來強調這 11 個人被「緊急送往」醫院的動作，在多益新聞英文中常用来描述救援或緊急事件。
  • 連接詞 while 引導的對比或同時發生的子句
    The worker who suffered burns has regained consciousness and his vital signs were stable as of 5 p.m. Tuesday, while the other 10 suspected of inhaling hydrochloric acid fumes were still hospitalized after being treated.
    在此句中，「while」用來連接兩個對比的情況（一個工人已清醒且生命徵象穩定，而另外 10 人仍住院），翻譯為「而」或「然而」，常出現在商業或新聞報導中進行前後狀況的對照。
