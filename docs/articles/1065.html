文法重點

  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice) 的廣泛運用
    U.S. President Donald Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping (習近平) are expected to discuss trade, artificial intelligence (AI) and Taiwan at a summit...
    新聞英語中常使用「be + 過去分詞」(如 are expected to) 來表達預期、報導或官方說法，能避免直接指稱特定消息來源，使語氣更客觀、正式。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Nominative Absolute Construction)
    ...with scholars saying the talks will also aim to manage competition between the two countries.
    「with + 名詞/代名詞 + 分詞 (V-ing/V-ed)」結構在英文新聞中極為常見，用來補充說明背景狀況、附帶結果或伴隨動作，譯為「隨著……」或「在……的情況下」。
  • 現在分詞 (Present Participle) 作後置修飾與補充
    Negotiators are also working on a reciprocal framework to reduce tariffs on about US$30 billion worth of non-sensitive goods from each country, with an announcement on the framework expected after the summit, he said.
    句中的 "to reduce..." 作不定詞片語用以修飾前面的名詞 framework，表達該框架的目的；而結尾的 "expected after the summit" 則是省略了 which is 的被動分詞片語，用來補充說明 announcement 的狀態。
