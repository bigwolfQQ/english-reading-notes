Taipei Times · 2026-10-12

法國學生覺得遭到拋棄

France’s students feel disowned

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • protest v. / n. 抗議，抗議活動
    When adolescents protest that the government is failing to uphold their right to an education and the government’s response is to send in riot police, the government has lost the plot.
    當 adolescents 抗議政府未能維護他們受教育的權利，而政府的反應卻是派遣鎮暴警察時，這個政府已經失去理智了。
  • statistics n. 統計數據
    In place of its lost plot, the French government has a collection of anecdotes and statistics that it should be ashamed of...
    為了掩飾其荒腔走板，法國政府有一堆令人羞恥的軼事與統計數據...
  • shortage n. 短缺，不足
    Julie, a 34-year-old teacher who joined the demonstrations, said that staff shortages were the fault of “low salaries and poor treatment.”
    34 歲參與示威的教師茱莉（Julie）表示，師資短缺是「低薪和待遇不佳」造成的。
  • substitute n. / v. 替代人選，代課老師 / 代替
    ...and half of primary and high schools missing teachers at the start of the school year — many of them with no substitutes or replacements.
    ...且在學年開始時，有一半的中小學缺乏教師——其中許多人沒有代課老師或替代人選。
  • commute v. / n. 通勤
    She has to commute for 1 hour and 45 minutes each way between Paris and the school on the city’s outskirts...
    她必須每天單趟通勤 1 小時 45 分鐘在巴黎與該市郊區的學校之間...
  • subsidy n. 補助金，津貼
    Teachers’ salaries in France are only 56 percent of what they are in Germany — even as more than 200 billion euros in public subsidies finds its way annually to businesses.
    法國教師的薪水僅為德國同行的 56%——即便每年有超過 2,000 億歐元的公共補貼流入企業手中。
  • arrest v. / n. 逮捕
    In total, 4,400 people, the vast majority minors, have been arrested in a little more than a week...
    總計在短短一個多星期內，已有 4,400 人被捕，絕大多數是未成年人...
  • facility n. 設施，設備
    It might be incoherent that protesting students shoot fireworks at high-school buildings, while at the same time denouncing the poor facilities they have to study in.
    抗議學生向高中建築發射煙火，同時又譴責他們必須在其中學習的簡陋設施，這或許有些矛盾。
  • pessimistic adj. 悲觀的
    In France and beyond, students and young workers actively dislike artificial intelligence and feel pessimistic about the future it portends.
    在法國及其他地區，學生和年輕勞工積極反感人工智慧，並對它所預示的未來感到悲觀。
  • priority n. 優先權，優先事項
    Centralized policymaking has also produced a national education system that often sends its least experienced teachers to these same areas, while granting more senior teachers priority in requesting transfers to other, less difficult, schools.
    中央集權的政策制定也產生了一個國民教育體系，經常將經驗最少的新進教師派往這些相同的地區，同時卻賦予資深教師優先申請轉調至其他較不困難學校的權利。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Julie, a 34-year-old teacher who joined the demonstrations, said that staff shortages were the fault of “low salaries and poor treatment.”
    句中的「who joined the demonstrations」是形容詞子句，用來修飾前面的名詞 teacher。在英文中，也可以簡化為分詞片語來修飾前面的名詞，讓語氣更為緊湊。
  • 現在完成式被動語態 (Present Perfect Passive)
    In total, 4,400 people, the vast majority minors, have been arrested in a little more than a week...
    「have been arrested」是由「助動詞 have + been + 過去分詞 (arrested)」構成的現在完成式被動語態。用來強調「過去發生的動作對現在造成的影響」（已經被逮捕了這件事），且主語 4,400 people 是動作的承受者。
  • 使役動詞用法 (Causative Verb)
    “Honestly, I don’t think the government cares, they just want to shut us up with false promises.”
    「want to shut us up」中使用了使役概念的片語動詞「shut (someone) up」（讓某人閉嘴、使安靜）。在此結構中，代名詞受詞 us 夾在動詞 shut 和副詞 up 之間。
  • 虛主詞 It 的用法 (Introductory 'It')
    I cannot imagine how hard it must be to be a teenager in general today.
    句首的「It」是虛主詞（形式主詞），用來代替真正的主詞——不定詞片語「to be a teenager in general today」。這樣做可以避免頭重腳輕（主詞過長），使句子結構更平衡。
