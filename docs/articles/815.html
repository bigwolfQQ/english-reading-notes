台灣數位部長訪美期間討論人工智慧與網路安全

Taiwan digital minister discusses AI, cybersecurity during U.S. visit

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • cooperation n. 合作
    Taiwan Digital Minister Lin Yi-jing (林宜敬) recently visited Washington, D.C., and met with United States officials, think tanks and major technology companies to discuss cooperation on artificial intelligence (AI) and cybersecurity, his ministry said Wednesday.
    數發部週三表示，台灣數位部長林宜敬近日訪問華盛頓特區，會見美國官員、智庫及主要科技公司，就人工智慧（AI）與網路安全合作進行討論。
  • delegation n. 代表團
    During the trip, Lin and his delegation held talks with U.S. government officials, whom the Ministry of Digital Affairs (MODA) did not identify, and several Washington-based think tanks, including the Hudson Institute and the Center for Strategic and International Studies.
    在行程期間，林宜敬及其代表團與美國政府官員（數位發展部未透露具體身分）以及多個華府智庫舉行會談，其中包括哈德遜研究所與戰略暨國際研究中心。
  • ensure v. 確保、保證
    One of the main issues discussed was "how AI models understand Taiwan," as the government seeks to ensure its language, history and culture are accurately represented by increasingly widely used AI systems.
    討論的主要議題之一是「AI模型如何理解台灣」，因為政府力求確保其語言、歷史和文化能夠被日益廣泛使用的AI系統準確呈現。
  • infrastructure n. 基礎設施
    Sovereign AI broadly refers to efforts by countries to gain greater control over the infrastructure, data and expertise underpinning their AI systems.
    主權AI廣泛指的是各國力求對其AI系統背後的基礎設施、數據與專業技術獲得更大控制權的努力。
  • evaluate v. 評估
    The government is also evaluating how international AI models handle Taiwan-related issues and sharing the results with developers to help improve their models, the ministry said.
    該部指出，政府同時也在評估國際AI模型如何處理台灣相關議題，並將結果與開發商分享，以協助改善其模型。
  • preserve v. 保存、維護
    He said this made it important for Taiwan to preserve its own data, language and perspectives and ensure the perspectives of democratic countries such as Taiwan and the U.S. were "properly understood in the AI era."
    他表示，這使得台灣保存自身的數據、語言與觀點變得至關重要，並確保像台灣和美國等民主國家的觀點在「AI時代能被適當理解」。
  • efficient adj. 有效率的
    MODA said Taiwan faced around 2.6 million cyberattacks per day and had recently observed hackers using AI to make attacks more efficient.
    數發部表示，台灣每天面臨約260萬次網路攻擊，且最近觀察到駭客利用AI來提高攻擊效率。
  • counterpart n. 相應的人或職務、對口
    The Taiwanese delegation discussed cyber defense, threat intelligence sharing, scenario exercises, post-quantum cryptography and frontier AI with its U.S. counterparts.
    台灣代表團與美國對口官員就網路防禦、威脅情資分享、情境演練、後量子密碼學以及前沿AI進行了討論。
  • respond v. 回應、反應
    The delegation also shared Taiwan's experience in responding to cyber threats, while MODA said Taiwan hopes to deepen exchanges with the U.S. on advanced AI models and technologies and explore their use in strengthening cybersecurity at government agencies and critical infrastructure.
    代表團同時分享了台灣因應網路威脅的經驗，數發部並表示，台灣希望深化與美國在先進AI模型與技術上的交流，並探討其在強化政府機關與關鍵基礎設施網路安全上的應用。
  • detection n. 偵測、發覺
    During talks with OpenAI, the company behind ChatGPT, the delegation discussed using advanced AI for threat analysis, vulnerability detection and incident response.
    在與開發ChatGPT的公司OpenAI進行會談時，代表團討論了利用先進AI進行威脅分析、漏洞偵測與事故應變。

文法重點

  • 關係代名詞所有格/受格子句 (whom引導)
    During the trip, Lin and his delegation held talks with U.S. government officials, whom the Ministry of Digital Affairs (MODA) did not identify, and several Washington-based think tanks...
    句中的 "whom the Ministry of Digital Affairs (MODA) did not identify" 是一個限定修飾前先行詞 "U.S. government officials" 的關係子句。因為在子句中作 identify 的受詞，故使用受格關係代名詞 whom。
  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾
    ...as the government seeks to ensure its language, history and culture are accurately represented by increasingly widely used AI systems.
    句中的 "increasingly widely used" 是由副詞與分詞組成的形容詞結構，用來修飾前方的名詞 "AI systems"，展現了分詞在商業與科技英文中簡潔且精準的修飾功能。
  • 虛受詞 it 的用法
    He said this made it important for Taiwan to preserve its own data, language and perspectives...
    句型為 make + 受詞 (it) + 受詞補語 (important) + 真正的受詞 (for Taiwan to preserve...)。當不定詞片語作真正的受詞且長度較長時，通常會移至句尾，並在受詞位置補上虛受詞 it，使句子結構更平衡。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    One of the main issues discussed was "how AI models understand Taiwan,"...
    句中的 "discussed" 是過去分詞，修飾前面的 "main issues"（即省略了 which were discussed 的被動語態結構），強調議題「被討論」的客觀事實，常見於新聞報導中以維持客觀語氣。
