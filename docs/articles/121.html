文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾
    People pose for a photograph with halal-certified peanut ice cream rolls in front of the Taiwan pavilion at the Malaysia International Travel Mart fair that opened in Kuala Lumpur yesterday.
    句中的「halal-certified」是由過去分詞（certified）構成的複合形容詞，用來修飾前面的名詞 peanut ice cream rolls，表「獲得清真認證的」；而結尾的「that opened in Kuala Lumpur yesterday」則是關代子句，用來修飾前面的 fair。
