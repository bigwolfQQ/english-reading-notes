中台灣札記：台灣的都市更新需要以人為本

Notes from Central Taiwan: Taiwan’s urban renewal needs a human focus

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • urban renewal n.片語 都市更新
    As skyscrapers claw at the sky across the nation’s major cities, urban renewal efforts continue.
    隨著全國各大城市的摩天大樓直插雲霄，都市更新工作仍在持續進行。
  • housing stock n.片語 房屋存量、住房總數
    Nearly 60 percent of the housing stock is over 30 years of age...
    近60%的房屋存量屋齡超過30年……
  • developer n. 開發商
    Initially the developer thought the project would go for a good price, but as construction costs rose, the developer abandoned the project...
    最初開發商認為該項目能賣出好價格，但隨著營建成本上升，開發商放棄了該項目……
  • auction n. 拍賣
    ...the developer abandoned the project and the site was put up for auction.
    ……開發商放棄了該項目，該基地隨之遭到法拍。
  • construction cost n.片語 營建成本、建築成本
    The result is that many projects are being abandoned.
    其結果是許多項目遭到放棄。
  • labor shortage n.片語 勞動力短缺
    Demography is having its day: construction industry labor shortages, domestic construction investment and factory construction demand, are pushing up demand for labor and in turn, salaries.
    人口統計正在發揮作用：營造業勞動力短缺、國內營建投資以及工廠建設需求，正在推動勞動力需求的增加，進而推動薪資上漲。
  • benchmark v. / n. 基準、作為水準點
    One problem identified in the UDN series on rising construction costs in Taipei is that the city benchmarks construction costs.
    聯合報系列報導中指出的關於台北營建成本上升的一個問題是，市政府對營建成本訂定基準。
  • financing n. 融資、財務安排
    Moreover, financing rates have risen.
    此外，融資利率已經上升。
  • non-performing loan n.片語 不良貸款、呆帳
    Banks face higher risk of non-performing loans in real estate and their provisions for loan non-performance are higher.
    銀行在房地產上面臨更高的不良貸款風險，其對貸款違約的備抵提列也更高。
  • provision n. （會計上的）提列、準備金；規定
    Banks face higher risk of non-performing loans in real estate and their provisions for loan non-performance are higher.
    銀行在房地產上面臨更高的不良貸款風險，其對貸款違約的備抵提列也更高。
  • subsidy n. 補助金、津貼
    The government has established subsidy programs to bring older buildings up to modern earthquake regulations and extend their lives.
    政府已經建立了補助計劃，以使較舊的建築達到現代地震法規的要求並延長其壽命。
  • allocate v. 分配、分派
    According to the Liberty Times, city government officials announced that the program around the R17 World Games MRT station allocates to the city government a 17-story mixed-use building...
    據《自由時報》報導，市政府官員宣布，R17世運捷運站周邊的計畫將分配給市政府一棟17層的綜合用途大樓……
  • slump v. / n. 暴跌、急速下降
    But an irony of urban renewal is that demand for housing will almost certainly slump within a decade or two.
    但都市更新的一大諷刺是，住宅需求幾乎確定會在十數年內滑落。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Present Participle）作後位修飾與附帶狀況
    As skyscrapers claw at the sky across the nation’s major cities, urban renewal efforts continue.
    句首的「As」引導時間副詞子句；而在後文中常見如「with Penghu... exhibiting the highest average building ages」或「with residents receiving two-thirds...」，則是使用「with + 名詞 + 現在分詞/過去分詞」的複合結構，用來表達伴隨發生的狀態，在多益閱讀測驗中非常常考。
  • 被動語態（Passive Voice）
    The result is that many projects are being abandoned.
    此句使用了現在進行式的被動語態（are being + 過去分詞 abandoned），用來強調許多都市更新項目「正在被」放棄的動作與動態過程，有別於單純的狀態描述。
  • 關係代間接句 / 形容詞子句（Relative Clause）
    One problem identified in the UDN series on rising construction costs in Taipei is that the city benchmarks construction costs.
    句中的「identified in the UDN series on rising construction costs in Taipei」是過去分詞片語作後位修飾，修飾前面的名詞「One problem」（省略了which was），這在商業英文與新聞英語中是極常見的濃縮句型，能使句子更為緊湊。
  • 假設語氣（Subjunctive Mood / Conditional）
    If planners view urban renewal as something that markets should determine, then they will get the spaces that markets build...
    此句使用第一類條件句（If + 現在式, ... will + 原形動詞），用來表達真實的假設狀況與必然會產生的結果，經常用於政策評論、商業邏輯推演與預測中。
