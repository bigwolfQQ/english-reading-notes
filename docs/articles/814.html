資產中心推動計畫提升台北全球金融地位：證交所

Asset hub push lifts Taipei's global financial standing: Stock exchange

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • ranking n. 排名
    Taipei has climbed to 42nd place in a global ranking of financial centers released Wednesday
    台北在週三發布的一項全球金融中心排名中爬升至第42名
  • attribute to phr. v. 把……歸因於
    a rise the Taiwan Stock Exchange Corporation (TWSE) attributed to the government's push to develop Taiwan into an Asian asset management hub.
    台灣證券交易所（TWSE）將此升幅歸因於政府推動將台灣發展成為亞洲資產管理中心的努力。
  • assess v. 評估、評價
    The index assessed financial centers on key policy priorities for the next five years.
    該指數評估了金融中心未來五年的關鍵政策優先事項。
  • transformation n. 轉型、轉變
    the latest ranking reflected the rapid transformation of Taiwan's financial market
    最新的排名反映出自從兩年前推出亞洲資產管理中心（AAMC）倡議以來，台灣金融市場的快速轉型
  • recognition n. 認可、承認、讚賞
    The improvement reflects growing international recognition of Taiwan's efforts to open its financial market
    這項改善反映出國際社會對台灣開放金融市場、推動法規創新以及加強全球互聯性的努力日益認可
  • deregulation n. 放寬管制、解除管制
    the Financial Supervisory Commission has sought to expand wealth management through deregulation
    金融監督管理委員會一直尋求透過鬆綁法規、新業務範疇、試辦計畫以及跨境合作來擴大財富管理
  • cooperation n. 合作、協力
    through deregulation, new business lines, pilot programs and cross-border cooperation
    透過鬆綁法規、新業務範疇、試辦計畫以及跨境合作
  • institution n. 機構、金融機構
    Financial institutions have also increased their investment in products, services and talent
    金融機構也增加了對產品、服務和人才的投資
  • investment n. 投資
    Financial institutions have also increased their investment in products, services and talent
    金融機構也增加了對產品、服務和人才的投資

文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    Taipei has climbed to 42nd place in a global ranking of financial centers released Wednesday...
    句中的「released Wednesday」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「global ranking of financial centers」，相當於形容詞子句「which was released Wednesday」，省略了關代與be動詞，使語氣更精鍊。
  • 省略關代之受格子句 (Reduced Relative Clause / Object Relative Clause)
    ...a rise the Taiwan Stock Exchange Corporation (TWSE) attributed to the government's push...
    「a rise」後面接了「the Taiwan Stock Exchange Corporation (TWSE) attributed to...」，中間省略了受格關代（如 that 或 which）。「the Taiwan Stock Exchange Corporation (TWSE) attributed to...」是用來形容前面的「a rise」的子句。
  • 現在分詞表達附帶結果 (Present Participle Expressing Result)
    Financial institutions have also increased their investment in products, services and talent, helping to strengthen Taiwan's asset management ecosystem...
    句尾的「helping to strengthen...」是現在分詞片語，在此處作分詞構句，表示前句動作所自然產生的結果（也就是金融機構增加投資，『因而有助於』強化台灣的資產管理生態系統）。
