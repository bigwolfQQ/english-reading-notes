文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附加說明（補充結果）
    Minister of Foreign Affairs Lin Chia-lung (林佳龍) made the comments shortly after the US Department of State on Tuesday told a virtual news conference that the US is opposed to China’s use of diplomatic and military pressure to isolate Taiwan.
    句中的「to isolate Taiwan」是不定詞片語作副詞用，用來修飾前面的「use of diplomatic and military pressure」，表達中國使用外交與軍事壓力的「目的」是為了孤立台灣。這是多益閱讀中常見的表目的用法。
  • be committed to + V-ing（致力於做……）
    Taipei is committed to showing its resolve in assisting its friends in the region to bolster their resilience, he said.
    「be committed to」意為「致力於、奉獻於」，其中的 to 是介系詞，因此後面接動詞時必須使用動名詞（V-ing），即「showing」。這個片語在多益商業書信與職場情境中非常高頻。
  • 現在分詞短語作附帶狀況（伴隨情況）
    Writing in a news release, Lin said Taiwan looks forward to enhancing its cooperation with the US and other partners in Palau as part of a greater drive to help the development of the Pacific region.
    句首的「Writing in a news release」是由分詞構句簡化而來（原句約為 As he wrote in a news release），用來表達林部長「在寫新聞稿的同時/透過寫新聞稿」做出此發言，是新聞英文中非常道地的寫法。
  • 名詞子句作受詞
    Asked about Washington’s views on China’s role in the region, Murray said that “the US certainly shares concerns with our Pacific Island partners related to China’s malign activities in the Pacific.”
    動詞「said」後面接了一個由「that」引導的名詞子句（that “the US certainly...）作為其受詞，用以完整表達墨菲所說的內容。多益文法題常考 say, tell, believe 等動詞後方接 that 子句的結構。
