央行面臨升息壓力

Central bank pressured to raise rates

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • consecutive adj. 連續的
    With domestic inflation topping the 2 percent alert level for a fourth consecutive month last month...
    隨著國內通膨率上個月連續第四個月突破百分之二的警戒線...
  • policymaking adj. 決策的
    ...Taiwan’s central bank is facing growing pressure to raise rates at its quarterly policymaking meeting on Thursday, economists said.
    經濟學家表示，台灣央行在周四的季度決策會議上面臨越來越大的升息壓力。
  • maintain v. 維持、保持
    Central Bank Governor Yang Chin-long (楊金龍) had said that the bank maintained a somewhat hawkish stance...
    中央銀行總裁楊金龍曾表示，該行維持稍微偏向鷹派的立場...
  • forecast v. / n. 預測、預報
    ...Cathay United Bank Co (國泰世華銀行) chief economist Lin Chi-chao (林啟超) last week said that the central bank had forecast in June that the consumer price index (CPI) would rise by less than 2 percent this year.
    ...國泰世華銀行首席經濟學家林啟超上周表示，央行在六月份曾預測今年消費者物價指數（CPI）將成長不到百分之二。
  • take into account phr. 將……納入考量、顧及
    The central bank must also take into account the monetary policy moves of other major central banks when deciding whether to adjust interest rates, he said.
    他說，央行在決定是否調整利率時，也必須將其他主要央行的貨幣政策動向納入考量。
  • benchmark n. 基準
    The Bank of Korea hiked its benchmark interest rates for a second consecutive time last month to 3 percent, Lin said.
    林啟超指出，韓國銀行上個月連續第二次調高基準利率至百分之三。
  • basis point n. 基點（金融用語，等於百分之一的一百分之一或 0.01%）
    The European Central Bank raised its key interest rates by 25 basis points last week...
    歐洲央行上週將主要利率調高了25個基點...
  • adjust v. 調整
    The central bank must also take into account the monetary policy moves of other major central banks when deciding whether to adjust interest rates, he said.
    他說，央行在決定是否調整利率時，也必須將其他主要央行的貨幣政策動向納入考量。
  • absorb v. 承受、吸收（衝擊或成本）
    With GDP growth forecast to top 11 percent this year, the economy is expected to be able to absorb the impact of a rate hike, Lin said.
    林啟超表示，隨著預測今年GDP成長率將突破百分之十一，預期經濟能夠承受升息帶來的衝擊。
  • aim at phr. 旨在、以……為目標
    A central bank rate hike would not primarily be aimed at curbing inflation, but at addressing tightening liquidity in the local market, Sun added.
    孫明德補充說，央行升息的主要目的將不是為了抑制通膨，而是為了解決本地市場資金流動性緊縮的問題。
  • respond v. 回應、做出反應
    Tight liquidity has pushed up market interest rates, so the central bank needs to respond by raising its key interest rates, he added.
    他並補充說，流動性緊縮推高了市場利率，因此央行需要透過調高其主要利率來做出回應。
  • burden n. 負擔
    ...so higher interest rates could place a heavier financial burden on traditional industries.
    ...因此更高的利率可能會對傳統產業帶來更沉重的財務負擔。

文法重點

  • With + 名詞 + 分詞（獨立分詞結構）
    With domestic inflation topping the 2 percent alert level for a fourth consecutive month last month and several major central banks around the world raising their key interest rates, Taiwan’s central bank is facing growing pressure...
    「With + 受詞 + 現在分詞 (V-ing)」構成附屬子句，用來表示伴隨的情況、原因或背景。在此句中，說明「隨著國內通膨率突破警戒線，且全球主要央行升息」這兩個背景情況，導致了台灣央行面臨壓力。
  • 現在分詞（V-ing）作補充說明（結果或動作）
    The European Central Bank raised its key interest rates by 25 basis points last week, marking its second rate hike this year, he said...
    句尾的「marking its second rate hike this year」是現在分詞片語作分詞構句，用來補充說明前面「歐洲央行升息 25 個基點」這一整件事所產生的結果或帶有的意義（即：這標誌著今年的第二次升息）。
  • Whether 引導的間接問句 / 譲步條件子句
    The central bank must also take into account the monetary policy moves of other major central banks when deciding whether to adjust interest rates, he said.
    「whether to adjust interest rates」是由「whether (是否)」加上不定詞片語 (to V) 所構成的名詞片語，作為介系詞 deciding 的受詞，簡化自「deciding whether it should adjust interest rates」。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    However, Sun also raised concerns about a rate hike, saying Taiwan’s strong economic growth is largely driven by AI development...
    句中的「is largely driven by AI development」使用了「be + 過去分詞 (driven)」的被動語態，強調台灣強勁的經濟成長是被「AI 發展」這股力量所推動的，受詞成為主詞的位置。
