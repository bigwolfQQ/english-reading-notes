文法重點

  • 分詞構句 (Present Participle as Adverbial Clause)
    National Tsing Hua University (NTHU) president John Kao (高為元) yesterday apologized for applying to become chancellor of a US university just three days after being confirmed for a second term at the Taiwanese institution in May, vowing to donate his monthly salary...
    句尾的「vowing to...」是由連接詞子句簡化而來的分詞構句，表示伴隨的情況或結果，主詞皆為 John Kao，意指他在道歉的同時，也發誓要捐出月薪。
  • 過去分詞作後位修飾 (Past Participle modifying a noun)
    Kao was not offered the position at the University of Hawaii at Manoa, despite being shortlisted with two other candidates.
    片語「shortlisted with two other candidates」是分詞片語，用來修飾主詞 Kao，說明他在被放入短名單（決選名單）的情況下，仍未獲得該職位。
  • 介系詞加動名詞 (Preposition + Gerund)
    Kao’s application for the position in the US sparked criticism following a report by the Liberty Times... in July.
    介系詞 following 在此當作連接詞或介系詞使用，後面接名詞片語 a report，用來表達「在……之後」的時間順序，是多益閱讀測驗常見的高頻用法。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause with Preposition)
    Prior to yesterday’s session, Kao issued a letter last month, in which he said that he was deeply sorry for the inconvenience and unease caused by the news of his job hunting...
    句中的「in which」引導一個非限定性關係子句，用來補充說明前面的先行詞 a letter（信件中），in 相當於 in the letter。
