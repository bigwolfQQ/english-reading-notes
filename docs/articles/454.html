中華航空將於 9 月 22 日調高國際航班訂位服務費

China Airlines to raise international flight booking fees Sept. 22

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾
    Starlux will raise its booking fee from US$30 to US$33 starting Oct. 7, while EVA Air and its wholly owned subsidiary UNI Air raised their booking fees from US$28 to US$33 for all flights except those departing from the Philippines and Hong Kong, effective Aug. 1.
    句中的「departing from the Philippines and Hong Kong」為現在分詞片語，用來修飾前方的代名詞 those（指代 flights），相當於形容詞子句「that/which depart from...」。
  • 連接詞 while 引導對比子句
    Starlux will raise its booking fee from US$30 to US$33 starting Oct. 7, while EVA Air and its wholly owned subsidiary UNI Air raised their booking fees from US$28 to US$33 for all flights except those departing from the Philippines and Hong Kong, effective Aug. 1.
    在此句中，while 作為對比連接詞，用來連接並對比兩家不同航空公司（星宇航空與長榮航空）在調整訂位費時的具體作法與時間點。
  • 動詞不定詞片語作目的副詞用
    Taiwan's major airlines, including CAL, EVA Air, Starlux Airlines and Tigerair Taiwan, charge booking service fees on a per-ticket basis to cover the cost of using global distribution systems (GDS) for reservations.
    句尾的「to cover the cost...」為不定詞片語，用來修飾前面的動詞片語「charge...」，表示收取費用的「目的」是為了支應成本。
