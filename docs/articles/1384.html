  • delegation n. 代表團
    A United States congressional delegation is in Taiwan for a four-day visit, during which it will meet with President Lai Ching-te...
    一個美國國會議員訪問團目前正在台灣進行為期四天的訪問，其間將會晤總統賴清德……
  • official n. 官員
    ...during which it will meet with President Lai Ching-te and other senior officials, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) said Tuesday.
    ……其間將會晤總統賴清德及其他高級官員，外交部於週二表示。
  • press release n. 新聞稿
    ...MOFA said in a press release.
    外交部透過新聞稿表示。
  • stay n. 停留、停留期間
    During their stay from Tuesday to Friday, the three lawmakers will meet with Lai...
    在週二至週五的停留期間，這三位議員將會晤賴總統……
  • economic adj. 經濟的
    ...to discuss U.S.-Taiwan relations, economic and trade issues, exchanges on technology and regional security...

文法重點

  • 介係詞加關係代名詞（during which）引導的形容詞子句
    A United States congressional delegation is in Taiwan for a four-day visit, during which it will meet with President Lai Ching-te...
    句中的「during which」用來修飾前面的「a four-day visit」。which 代替 visit，前面加上介係詞 during 表示「在……期間」，用以補充說明訪問期間所進行的活動。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾
    The all-Democratic delegation is led by U.S. Representative Brendan Boyle, the ranking member of the House Budget Committee, who is joined by Representatives Andrea Salinas and Tim Kennedy...
    句中的「led by...」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的主詞「The all-Democratic delegation」，相當於形容詞子句「which is led by...」的簡化形式。
  • 現在分詞表附帶結果或時間（ahead of）
    The delegation's visit to Taiwan ahead of the Republic of China (Taiwan) National Day on Oct. 10 shows its strong support for Taiwan...
    片語「ahead of」意為「在……之前」，用來表達時間上的先後順序，常用於商業及新聞英語中描述事件發生的時間點。
