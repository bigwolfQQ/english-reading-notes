單字解析

  • assessment n. 評量、評估
    Taiwanese students have achieved impressive results in four major international assessments in recent years.
    台灣學子近年在四大國際評比中取得令人印象深刻的成果。
  • participate v. 參與、參加
    The Trends in International Mathematics and Science Study (TIMSS) assesses fourth- and eighth-grade students in mathematics and science, with 70 countries and education systems participating.
    「國際數學與科學教育成就趨勢調查」（TIMSS）評估四、八年級學生在數學與科學的表現，有70個國家與教育體系參與。
  • investment n. 投資
    In addition to the government’s forward-looking education policies and investment in education, he expressed his gratitude to teachers...
    除了政府的前瞻教育政策與教育投資外，他也向教師表達感謝……
  • expression n. 表達、表情（此處衍生動詞 express 的應用）
    ...he expressed his gratitude to teachers for upholding their professional commitment and devoting themselves to educating students...
    ……他表達了對教師堅守專業承諾並致力於教育學生的感謝……
  • commitment n. 承諾、奉獻
    ...he expressed his gratitude to teachers for upholding their professional commitment and devoting themselves to educating students...
    ……他表達了對教師堅守專業承諾並致力於教育學生的感謝……
  • devote v. 奉獻、投入
    ...he expressed his gratitude to teachers for upholding their professional commitment and devoting themselves to educating students, as well as to parents for their support.
    ……他表達了對教師堅守專業承諾並致力於教育學生的感謝，同時也感謝家長的支援。
  • limit v. 限制
    Taiwan’s strength is no longer limited to outstanding performance in a single subject, she said.
    她表示，台灣的優勢不再侷限於單一學科的傑出表現。
  • comprehensive adj. 全面的、綜合的
    Instead, it has developed a comprehensive advantage in talent development.
    相反地，它已在人才發展方面建立起全面的優勢。
  • stable adj. 穩定的
    The results stem from stable basic education, a strong tradition in mathematics and science education...
    這些成果源自於穩固的基礎教育、在數理教育的深厚傳統……
  • application n. 應用、申請
    International assessments increasingly emphasize the application of knowledge to real-world situations, civic judgment and digital problem-solving.
    國際評比日益強調知識在真實世界情境中的應用、公民判斷力以及數位問題解決能力。
  • indicate v. 指出、顯示
    Taiwan’s students consistently rank near the top in these areas, indicating that the competency-based curriculum reforms are increasingly reflected in student performance.
    台灣學生在這些領域持續名列前茅，顯示出素養導向的課綱改革正日益反映在學生的表現上。
  • achieve v. 取得、實現

    教育部政務次長張廖萬堅表示，亮眼的成果反映了十二年國教課綱在……上的成就。
  • identify v. 辨別、識別、確定
    International assessments can also help identify students’ learning outcomes and areas that need improvement...
    國際評比亦有助於了解（辨識）學生的學習成果與需要改進之處……
  • improvement n. 改善、改進
    International assessments can also help identify students’ learning outcomes and areas that need improvement...

文法重點

  • 現在完成式 (Present Perfect Tense)
    Taiwanese students have achieved impressive results in four major international assessments in recent years.
    表示過去某一時間點開始發生的動作（或過去的經驗），一直持續到現在，或對現在造成影響。「in recent years（近年來）」是搭配現在完成式常見的時間副詞軍火庫之一。
  • 分詞構句作附帶狀況/結果 (Participial Phrase)
    ...with 70 countries and education systems participating.
    此處使用 「with + 名詞 + 現在分詞(participating)」的獨立分詞構句結構，用來補充說明前面的子句發生的附帶狀況，意指「在有 70 個國家與教育體系參與的情況下」。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞或受詞 (Gerund Phrase as Object)
    The 2019 curriculum guidelines, commonly known as the 108 Curriculum, further strengthened inquiry-based learning, hands-on exploration, interdisciplinary learning and problem-solving.
    句中的 strengthen 後方接了多個並列的動名詞片語（inquiry-based learning, hands-on exploration 等）作為其受詞，用來表達具體的學習方法。
  • 現在分詞作形容詞用 (Present Participle as Adjective)
    Education Minister Cheng Ying-yao said the results Taiwanese students have achieved in the international assessments are a source of pride.
    句中省去關代（which/that）的形動子句「Taiwanese students have achieved」修飾前面的先行詞 the results；同時 pride 前方搭配 a source of（……的來源）是多益常見的實用名詞片語。
