民調顯示立法院不滿意度高

Disapproval of legislature high: survey

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • dissatisfaction n. 不滿，不滿意
    Strong public dissatisfaction with the opposition-controlled Legislative Yuan is a stinging rebuke...
    公眾對在野黨掌控的立法院強烈不滿，是對……的一記沉重譴責
  • respondent n. 受訪者
    ...showed that 63.4 percent of respondents disapproved of the legislature...
    ……顯示63.4%的受訪者不滿意立法院……
  • performance n. 表現，績效
    Asked to rate the legislature’s performance, 5.9 percent of respondents said they strongly approved of it...
    當被要求評估立法院的表現時，5.9%的受訪者表示非常支持……
  • obstruct v. 妨礙，阻礙
    ...41.9 percent of those who disapproved of the legislative branch were dissatisfied with partisanship obstructing the government’s business...
    ……41.9%對黨派之爭妨礙政府運作感到不滿……
  • efficient adj. 有效率的
    ...36.1 percent said the budget review process had been slow and inefficient...
    ……36.1%表示預算審查過程緩慢且缺乏效率……
  • livelihood n. 生計，謀生
    ...25.3 percent said legislators did not solve problems affecting the livelihood of ordinary Taiwanese...
    ……25.3%表示立法委員未解決影響一般台灣人生計的問題……
  • proposal n. 提案，建議
    ...with 68 percent backing the proposal, the Taiwan Inspiration Association said...
    台灣勵進會指出，有68%的人支持該提案……
  • spending n. 開支，花費
    It said that 30.4 percent disapproved of boosting defense spending...
    該會表示，30.4%的人不贊成增加國防支出……
  • affiliation n. 隸屬關係，（政黨等）成員身分
    In a breakdown by political affiliation, 94 percent of Democratic Progressive Party supporters...
    按政治傾向細分，94%的民主進步黨支持者……
  • conduct v. 實施，執行（調查等）
    The poll, which was conducted by InvosData, garnered 1,200 valid samples...
    這項由InvosData進行的民調，獲得了1,200個有效樣本……

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後置修飾語（分詞構句/形容詞片語）
    Asked to rate the legislature’s performance, 5.9 percent of respondents said...
    句首的「Asked to rate the legislature’s performance」是由過去分詞引導的修飾語，原句意指「When they were asked to rate...（當他們被要求評估...時）」，省略了主代名詞與be動詞，直接以過去分詞表達被動概念。
  • 關係代名詞子句（限定與非限定用法）
    The poll, which was conducted by InvosData, garnered 1,200 valid samples...
    句中的「which was conducted by InvosData」是非限定關係代名詞子句，前面加有逗號，用來補充說明前面的先行詞「The poll」，提供額外資訊而不影響主句的核心意思。
  • 獨立分詞構句/附帶狀況
    ...with 68 percent backing the proposal, the Taiwan Inspiration Association said...
    此處使用「with + 名詞(68 percent) + 現在分詞(backing)」的結構，用來表達附帶發生的狀況或伴隨的背景資訊，意指「在有68%的人支持該提案的情況下」。
