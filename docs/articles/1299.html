文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作附加說明 (Present Participle Phrase)
    Taipei, Oct. 4 (CNA) Foreign Minister Lin Chia-lung (林佳龍) arrived in Phoenix, Arizona, late Saturday (local time) to begin a three-day visit, including the opening of Taiwan's new representative office in the city, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) announced Sunday.
    句尾的「including the opening of...」是由現在分詞 including 引導的分詞片語，用來補充說明前面「three-day visit（三天訪問）」所包含的具體內容，讓句子結構精簡而不需使用冗長的關代子句。
  • 過去完成式 (Past Perfect Tense, had + p.p.)
    Lin's visit to Arizona came after he had just wrapped up a week-long visit to Taiwan's two diplomatic allies in Central America, Belize and Guatemala.
    句型用來表達在過去某個動作（Lin's visit came）之前已經完成的動作（had just wrapped up）。「had wrapped up」突顯了林佳龍是「先」結束中美洲訪問，隨後「才」前往亞利桑那州，藉此釐清兩個過去動作的先後順序。
  • 獨立分詞構句與附帶狀況 (Absolute Phrase / Participle Phrase with 'with')
    TSMC's first fab in Arizona began mass production in the fourth quarter of 2024, while construction of a second fab has been completed, with production expected to begin in the second half of 2027.
    句尾的「with production expected to begin...」是「with + 名詞 + 分詞/形容詞」的複合結構（常譯為「隨著……」），用來補充說明第二座晶圓廠完工後的狀況，表示生產預期將在 2027 下半年展開，是多益閱讀與商業寫作中常見的高級句型。
  • 分詞構句作後置修飾 (Participle as Post-modifier)
    A third fab is currently under construction.
    雖然此句較短，但文章中經常利用過去分詞片語（如：officially named..., once established...）來修飾前面的名詞或子句，省去了代名詞與be動詞（如 which is established），使商業新聞報導的語氣更加緊湊、俐落。
