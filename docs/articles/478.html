文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾
    The Taipei Department of Education yesterday said it is terminating contracts with a school lunch caterer after dozens of students at two separate schools reported suspected food poisoning symptoms after eating meals provided by the vendor this week.
    句中的「provided by the vendor」為過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「meals」，省略了「which were」，用以精簡語句並說明餐點是由該供應商所提供的。
  • 現在進行式表達近期計畫或持續發生的動作
    The Taipei Department of Education yesterday said it is terminating contracts with a school lunch caterer...
    句子使用「is terminating」（現在進行式），在英文新聞報導中，除了表示當下正在進行的動作外，也常被用來表達已經確定、即將在近期執行的官方決策或計畫。
  • 連接詞 whether引導的名詞子句（作受詞）
    Taipei Department of Education Director-General Tang Chih-min (湯志民) said they had received reports of more than 10 students from Da Li feeling unwell this week and are investigating whether their symptoms were linked to the lunches.
    句中的「whether their symptoms were linked to the lunches」是一個由 whether 引導的名詞子句，在句中擔任動詞 investigating 的受詞，意為「是否...」，表達不確定的情況正在被調查中。
