自由就是知道國家支持著你

Freedom is knowing the country is behind you

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • agent n. 代理人，特務
    If a foreign government uses agents to secretly carry out operations and collect information on people in the US, its law enforcement agencies should uphold the rule of law and protect national security.
    如果外國政府利用代理人在美國境內秘密執行行動並蒐集人員資訊，其執法機構應當維護法治並保護國家安全。
  • allege v. 指控，宣稱
    The FBI has arrested Wanying “Heather” Zhang, a California real-estate agent who US authorities allege acted at the direction of the Chinese government to surveil and photograph President William Lai’s (賴清德) eldest son and his family.
    美國聯邦調查局（FBI）已逮捕加州房地產經紀人張萬英，美國當局指控她聽從中國政府的指示，對總統賴清德的長子及其家人進行監視與拍照。
  • investigation n. 調查
    The US investigation is being carried out under US law; it is not simply acting to protect the Taiwanese government.
    美國的調查是在美國法律下進行的；它不單單是為了保護台灣政府而採取行動。
  • enforce v. 執行（法律）
    If a foreign government uses agents to secretly carry out operations and collect information on people in the US, its law enforcement agencies should uphold the rule of law and protect national security.
    如果外國政府利用代理人在美國境內秘密執行行動並蒐集人員資訊，其執法機構應當維護法治並保護國家安全。
  • adequate adj. 足夠的，適當的
    Taiwan should establish reporting mechanisms for citizens abroad who are targeted by surveillance or threats, bolster the exchange of security information with democratic partners, and review whether laws are adequate to effectively prevent and hold foreign actors accountable for illegal surveillance, harassment and intimidation.
    台灣應為遭受監視或威脅的海外公民建立通報機制、加強與民主夥伴的安全資訊交流，並檢視相關法律是否足以有效防止外國勢力進行非法監視、騷擾與恐嚇，並追究其責任。
  • accountable adj. 負有責任的
    Taiwan should establish reporting mechanisms for citizens abroad who are targeted by surveillance or threats, bolster the exchange of security information with democratic partners, and review whether laws are adequate to effectively prevent and hold foreign actors accountable for illegal surveillance, harassment and intimidation.
    台灣應為遭受監視或威脅的海外公民建立通報機制、加強與民主夥伴的安全資訊交流，並檢視相關法律是否足以有效防止外國勢力進行非法監視、騷擾與恐嚇，並追究其責任。
  • mechanism n. 機制，構造
    Taiwan should establish reporting mechanisms for citizens abroad who are targeted by surveillance or threats...
    台灣應為遭受監視或威脅的海外公民建立通報機制……
  • bolster v. 加強，支撐
    ...bolster the exchange of security information with democratic partners, and review whether laws are adequate to effectively prevent...
    ……加強與民主夥伴的安全資訊交流，並檢視相關法律是否足以有效防止……
  • capacity n. 能力，容量
    What Taiwan needs is not louder condemnation, but for every citizen to know that, whether at home or abroad, whenever their freedom is unlawfully threatened, the nation has the institutions, channels and capacity to stand behind them.
    台灣需要的不是更大聲的譴責，而是讓每位公民都知道，無論在國內或海外，每當他們的自由遭到非法威脅時，國家都有體制、管道與能力作為他們的後盾。
  • involve v. 涉及，包含
    The incident has drawn much attention, because it involves Lai’s family, but what is concerning is not simply who was being followed.
    這起事件引發了高度關注，因為它牽涉到賴總統的家人，但令人擔憂的不僅僅是誰遭到跟蹤。

文法重點

  • 名詞子句作主詞（What-clause as subject）
    What Taiwan needs is not louder condemnation, but for every citizen to know that...
    句首的「What Taiwan needs」是所謂的「wh-名詞子句」，在句中擔任整個句子的主詞，意為「台灣所需要的東西」。這種結構常是用來強調或帶出重點的實用句型。
  • 假設語氣（Subjunctive / Conditional）
    If the allegations are proven true, it would be transnational repression, something that democratic societies must confront.
    第一句使用了「If + 現在式, it would + 原形動詞」的假設語氣結構，用來表達對未來或假設情況的推測；第二句則使用「If + 現在式, 助動詞 (could) + 原形動詞」表達條件成立時可能產生的結果。
  • 關係代名詞子句（Relative Clause）
    The FBI has arrested Wanying “Heather” Zhang, a California real-estate agent who US authorities allege acted at the direction of the Chinese government...
    句中的「who US authorities allege acted...」是一個由關係代名詞 who 引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾先行詞「a California real-estate agent」，其中還夾帶了插入語「US authorities allege」（美國當局指控）。
  • 現在分詞作後位修飾（Present Participle as Postmodifier）
    The issue is whether Taiwanese abroad can be free from surveillance and threats with political motives.
    句中的「surveillance and threats with political motives」雖然是用介系詞片語，但在文章其他地方如「a person watching someone’s home」中，使用現在分詞片語（watching...）來後位修飾名詞（a person），是英文中非常常見且高效的簡化形容詞子句用法。
