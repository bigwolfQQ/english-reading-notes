台股受美股科技股漲勢與貿易談判激勵而走高

Taiwanese shares buoyed by US tech rally and trade talks

單字解析

  • demand n. 需求
    continued global demand for artificial intelligence (AI) technology and positive signals from trade talks over the weekend between US and Chinese officials.
    全球對人工智慧（AI）技術持續的需求，以及美國與中國官員週末貿易談判的正面信號。
  • turnover n. 營業額，成交量
    Turnover on the local main board was NT$820.22 billion (US$25.83 billion), Taiwan Stock Exchange data showed.
    台灣證券交易所的數據顯示，本地主板市場的成交額為 8,202.2 億元新台幣（約合 258.3 億美元）。
  • rate hike n. 升息，利率調升
    Although the US Federal Reserve hinted at another rate hike by the end of this year after last week’s 25 basis point increase, major cloud service providers continued to pump funds into AI infrastructure...
    儘管美國聯準會暗示繼上週升息 25 個基點後，今年底前可能再度升息，但主要雲端服務供應商持續將資金注入 AI 基礎設施...
  • investor n. 投資人
    ...prompting investors to pick up tech stocks at home and abroad,” Concord Securities Co (康和證券) analyst Kerry Huang (黃志祺) said.
    ......促使國內外投資人進場承接科技股，康和證券分析師黃志祺表示。
  • sector n. 部門，行業，類股
    The financial sector continued an uptrend on hopes that the Fed’s rate hike would boost interest income...
    金融類股延續上升趨勢，主因市場期望聯準會升息將能提升利息收入...
  • boost v. 提高，促進，增長
    The financial sector continued an uptrend on hopes that the Fed’s rate hike would boost interest income...
    金融類股延續上升趨勢，主因市場期望聯準會升息將能提升利息收入...
  • uncertainty n. 不確定性
    ...although the Strait has remained largely closed and uncertainty persisted over US-Iran tensions.
    ......儘管該海峽大致仍處於封鎖狀態，且美伊緊張局勢的不確定性依然存在。
  • sentiment n. 市場情緒，氛圍
    “A fall in crude oil prices also improved market sentiment today, so investors should keep a close eye on how military conflicts in the Middle East will evolve,” Huang said.
    黃志祺表示：「原油價格下跌也改善了今天的大盤氣氛，因此投資人應密切關注中東軍事衝突將如何演變。」

文法重點

  • 分詞構句（表附帶狀況或結果）
    Shares in Taiwan closed sharply higher yesterday, with buying sparked by a US tech rally at the end of last week...
    「with + 名詞 + 過去分詞」是用來表示附帶狀況的常見句構。在此句中，with 引導名詞 buying（買脈/買盤）及其被動修飾語 sparked by（被……所激發），用來補充說明台股大漲時伴隨發生的市場狀況。
  • 關係代名詞子句（修飾先行詞）
    ...ahead of the expected meeting between US President Donald Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping (習近平) later this week in Washington which could cover trade, AI and geopolitics.
    句尾的「which could cover...」是一個非限定用法（或修飾 meeting 的限定用法）的關係代名詞子句，用來補充說明美中元首預期會面可能涵蓋的議題範圍。
  • 被動語態
    ...with buying sparked by a US tech rally at the end of last week...
    過去分詞片語「sparked by...」隱含被動意味（由……所激發），在新聞英文中常省略主動詞與關代（原句形如 which was sparked by），用來精簡語句並強調動作的承受者。
