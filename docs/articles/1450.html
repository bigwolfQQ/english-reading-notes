馬尼拉在台北開設全台最大海外菲律賓移工服務中心

Manila opens largest OFW service center in Taipei

單字解析

  • establish v. 建立、設立
    The Philippines yesterday opened its largest overseas Filipino worker (OFW) service center in Taipei, with hopes of establishing two more in central and southern Taiwan over the next two years...
    菲律賓昨天在台北開設了規模最大的海外菲律賓移工（OFW）服務中心，並期望在未來兩年內於台灣中部和南部再設立兩座...
  • facility n. 設施、場所
    The OFW Global Center-Taipei is the third such facility worldwide, following centers in Hong Kong and Kuala Lumpur, Administrator Patricia Yvonne Caunan said.
    署長 Patricia Yvonne Caunan 表示，「台北 OFW 全球中心」是繼香港和吉隆坡之後，全球第三個此類設施。
  • access v. / n. 使用、接近；通道、取得的權利
    It is designed as a one-stop hub where Filipino migrant workers in Taiwan can access government services, welfare assistance and training without returning to the Philippines.
    它被設計成一個一站式樞紐，在台灣的菲律賓移工無需返回菲律賓，即可在此獲得政府服務、福利援助和培訓。
  • entrepreneurship n. 創業精神、企業家身分
    Planned activities include Mandarin classes, financial literacy programs, computer and artificial intelligence training, and courses on content creation and entrepreneurship, Caunan said.
    Caunan 表示，計劃中的活動包括中文課程、金融素養課程、電腦與人工智慧培訓，以及內容創作和創業課程。
  • pursue v. 追求、尋求
    ...adding that the training can help workers pursue new career opportunities or start businesses after returning to the Philippines.
    ...並補充說，這些培訓可以幫助工人們在返回菲律賓後尋求新的職涯機會或創業。
  • highlight v. 強調、突顯
    She also highlighted the emotional challenges faced by migrant workers living away from their families...
    她還強調了生活在異鄉、遠離家人的移工們所面臨的情感挑戰...
  • expand v. 擴展、擴大
    Regarding plans to expand the service network in Taiwan, Caunan said the administration hopes to establish similar centers in Taichung and Kaohsiung over the next two years...
    關於擴大在台服務網路的計畫，Caunan 表示，該署希望在未來兩年內在台中和高雄設立類似的中心...
  • commitment n. 承諾、投入
    The new center in Taipei reflected the Philippine government’s commitment to supporting its citizens overseas, MECO Chairperson Corazon Avecilla-Padiernos said.
    MECO 主席 Corazon Avecilla-Padiernos 表示，台北的新中心體現了菲律賓政府支持海外公民的承諾。
  • convenient adj. 方便的、便利的
    “This is very convenient now because everything is here already, what a Filipino overseas worker needs,” Domingo said.
    Domingo 說：「現在非常方便，因為海外菲律賓移工需要的東西這裡全都應有盡有。」
  • administrative adj. 行政的
    Migrant workers often have to take time away from their jobs to handle administrative procedures and hoped the center would offer faster document processing and additional services, she said.
    她表示，移工們經常不得不請假來處理行政程序，並希望該中心能提供更快的文件處理和額外的服務。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞（Phrase as Modifier / 分詞片語）作後位修飾
    The Philippines yesterday opened its largest overseas Filipino worker (OFW) service center in Taipei, with hopes of establishing two more in central and southern Taiwan over the next two years, the country’s Overseas Workers Welfare Administration said.
    句中的 "with hopes of establishing..." 為介系詞片語，用來補充說明主句動作伴隨的情況或意圖。此外，文章中常使用 "...saying the center would provide..."（現在分詞片語當作補充說明，省略代名詞與連接詞）來引述說話者的進一步言論，是新聞英文中非常常見的結構。
  • 關係副詞 where 引導的形容詞子句
    It is designed as a one-stop hub where Filipino migrant workers in Taiwan can access government services, welfare assistance and training without returning to the Philippines.
    在此句中，"where" 作為關係副詞，用來引導一個形容詞子句，修飾先行詞 "a one-stop hub"（一個一站式樞紐），說明這個地點「在哪裡」可以進行後續的動作（存取政府服務與培訓）。
  • 過去分詞（Past Participle）作後位修飾
    Among those welcoming the center was Geraldine G. Domingo, 54, a Taipei-based caregiver who has worked in Taiwan for 12 years.
    句中的 "Taipei-based" 是由名詞結合過去分詞構成的複合形容詞，用來修飾後方的名詞 "caregiver"（看護），意思是「以台北為據點的／駐台北的」。這種結構在商業及新聞英文中常用來簡潔地表達某人或某機構的所在地點。
