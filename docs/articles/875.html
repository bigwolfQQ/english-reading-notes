文法重點

  • 包含分詞片語作為後置修飾語（Present/Past Participle Phrases as Post-modifiers）
    According to prosecutors, the banquet, where more than 10 tables of residents from Xihu Township were entertained, served as a covert campaign event to support Hsieh's aunt, KMT Xihu Township mayoral candidate Chen Chen-fei.
    句中的「to support Hsieh's aunt...」是不定詞片語，用來當作副詞用法的修飾語，說明「campaign event」的目的（為了支持謝的姑姑）。多益閱讀測驗常考不定詞片語在句首或句尾表示目的的用法。
  • 現在分詞短語表附帶結果或狀態（Present Participle Phrase indicating results）
    In a social media post, Wu... questioned whether the banquet was paid for privately or with county council resources, suggesting it could have violated the Public Officials Election and Recall Act.
    逗號之後的「suggesting it could have violated...」是由現在分詞「suggesting」引導的分詞構句，用來補充說明前面發文質疑的動作所產生的意涵或內容，相當於「and suggested that...」。
  • 間接問句與名詞子句（Indirect Questions / Noun Clauses）
    Wu... questioned whether the banquet was paid for privately or with county council resources...
    動詞 questioned 後方接了由「whether...」引導的名詞子句作為其受詞，用來表達「質疑（某事）是否...」。多益考試中常考動詞與名詞子句連接詞（如 whether, if, that）的搭配。
