電視主持人蔡康永因出席沈伯洋活動面臨反彈

TV host Kevin Tsai faces backlash over Shen event

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • backlash n. 強烈反彈、強烈反應
    Taiwanese television host Kevin Tsai (蔡康永) has faced a backlash in China after he on Sunday attended the opening of Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) Taipei mayoral candidate Puma Shen’s (沈伯洋) campaign headquarters.
    台灣電視主持人蔡康永於週日出席民主進步黨台北市長候選人沈伯洋的競選總部成立大會後，在中國大陸面臨了強烈反彈。
  • accountable adj. 應負責任的
    Shen has been called a “staunch Taiwanese independence separatist” by the Chinese Communist Party, which has threatened to hold him accountable under its anti-secessionist laws.
    沈伯洋被中國共產黨稱為「堅台獨分裂分子」，中共並威脅要依其反分裂國家法追究他的責任。
  • preliminary adj. 初步的、預備的
    His manager said Tsai’s attendance was preliminary research for possible interviews with both candidates.
    他的經紀人表示，蔡康永的出席是為了可能採訪兩位候選人而進行的初步研究。
  • reaffirm v. 重申、再次確證
    Chinese electric vehicle maker Leapmotor, which last month announced Tsai as a brand “partner,” removed promotional content related to the entertainer by Sunday evening and issued a statement reaffirming its commitment to Beijing’s “one China” principle...
    中國電動車製造商極氪（Leapmotor），已於週日晚間刪除了與該藝人相關的宣傳內容，並發表聲明重申其對北京「一個中國」原則的承諾...
  • discriminate v. 歧視
    Taipei Mayor Chiang Wan-an (蔣萬安) of the Chinese Nationalist Party (KMT), who is seeking re-election, urged China to respect Taiwan’s free and democratic way of life and not discriminate against people because of their political views.
    爭取連任的中國國民黨籍台北市長蔣萬安敦促中國尊重台灣自由民主的生活方式，不要因為政治立場而歧視民眾。
  • collaborate v. 合作（註：雖然文章未直接出現，但常與 partner/association 相關，改選文章內實用多益字）
    KMT caucus secretary-general Hsu Yu-chen (許宇甄) said the caucus opposed efforts by Chinese Internet users and businesses to blacklist Tsai...
    國民黨團書記長許宇甄表示，黨團反對中國網民和企業封殺蔡康永的企圖...
  • pluralistic adj. 多元的
    Taiwan is a democratic and pluralistic society in which everyone has the right to learn about candidates’ policies and ideas, Hsu said.
    許宇甄表示，台灣是一個民主多元的社會，每個人都有權利了解候選人的政策與理念。
  • illustrate v. 說明、闡明、圖解
    Hung Pu-chao (洪浦釗), deputy executive director of Tunghai University’s Center for Mainland China and Regional Development Research, said the incident illustrated how Beijing uses its market power to influence the politics of Taiwanese entertainers.
    東海大學中國大陸暨區域發展研究中心副執行長洪浦釗表示，這起事件說明了北京如何利用其市場力量來影響台灣藝人的政治。
  • consequence n. 後果、影響
    As China has already branded Shen a “die-hard Taiwanese independence separatist,” publicly attending his campaign event could carry commercial consequences for entertainers in the Chinese market, Hung said.
    洪浦釗指出，由於中國已經將沈伯洋打上「死硬台獨分裂分子」的烙印，公開出席他的競選活動可能會為中國市場的藝人帶來商業後果。
  • intervention n. 干預、介入
    Businesses know where the political lines are and take steps to protect their interests without direct government intervention, he added.
    他補充說，企業知道政治底線在哪裡，並在沒有直接政府干預的情況下採取步驟來保護自身的利益。
  • autonomy n. 自治、自主權
    Once Chinese market pressure spills into Taiwan’s elections, it threatens the autonomy of the nation’s democracy, Hung said.
    洪浦釗表示，一旦中國市場壓力蔓延到台灣的選舉中，它將威脅到國家民主的自主性。
  • impose v. 強制實行、強加
    China can draw red lines in its own market, but it has no right to impose them on Taiwanese, Hung said.
    洪浦釗表示，中國可以在自己的市場劃定紅線，但它沒有權利將其強加給台灣人。

文法重點

  • 關係代名詞非限定用法 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    A livestream showed Tsai, who previously hosted the popular variety show Kangsi Coming (康熙來了), holding a campaign balloon bearing the slogan “Let Taipei Get Back on Track” and waving a cheering stick alongside supporters...
    句中的「who previously hosted the popular variety show Kangsi Coming」是用逗號隔開的非限定關係子句，用來補充說明先行詞 Tsai 的額外背景資訊。如果拿掉這個子句，主要句子的意思依然完整。
  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participial Phrase as Postmodifier)
    A livestream showed Tsai, who previously hosted the popular variety show Kangsi Coming (康熙來了), holding a campaign balloon bearing the slogan “Let Taipei Get Back on Track” and waving a cheering stick alongside supporters...
    句中的「bearing the slogan...」與「waving a cheering stick...」是由現在分詞 (present participle) 引導的分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 balloon 與 Tsai，表達主動且同時發生的動作，常出現在多益閱讀的分詞考題中。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Political labeling, online pressure and corporate action are used to enforce Beijing’s political red lines, he said.
    此句使用被動語態「are used to...」，強調施受的主體是行為結果而不是發動者。在多益聽力與閱讀中，商業報導常使用被動語態來客觀描述政策、規定或商業行為的執行方式。
  • 連接詞 once 引導條件副詞子句 (Conjunction 'Once' introducing a conditional clause)
    Once Chinese market pressure spills into Taiwan’s elections, it threatens the autonomy of the nation’s democracy, Hung said.
    「Once」在此處作連接詞用，意為「一旦……」，引導一個條件副詞子句（Once Chinese market pressure spills into Taiwan’s elections），用來表達「只要這個情況發生，就會導致隨後的結果」。多益測驗中常考類似的連接詞用法（如 once, as soon as, provided that）。
