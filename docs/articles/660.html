教練：台灣放眼亞運英雄聯盟至少奪銀

Taiwan eyes at least silver in Asian Games League of Legends: Coach

單字解析

  • target v. 以……為目標，瞄準
    Taiwan's League of Legends team is targeting at least a silver medal at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games with gold as the ultimate goal
    台灣的英雄聯盟代表隊將以愛知-名古屋亞運至少奪得銀牌為目標，並將金牌視為最終目標
  • ultimate adj. 最終的，極致的
    Taiwan's League of Legends team is targeting at least a silver medal at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games with gold as the ultimate goal
    台灣的英雄聯盟代表隊將以愛知-名古屋亞運至少奪得銀牌為目標，並將金牌視為最終目標
  • compete v. 競爭，參賽
    "I hope we can at least win silver and then find a way to compete for gold,"
    「我希望我們至少能贏得銀牌，然後想辦法爭取金牌。」
  • immediate adj. 緊迫的，目前的，直接的
    South Korea remains the team Taiwan ultimately wants to beat, but Chen said Vietnam presents the more immediate hurdle.
    南韓依然是台灣最終想要擊敗的隊伍，但陳如治表示，越南則是眼前更迫切的障礙。
  • hurdle n. 障礙，難題
    South Korea remains the team Taiwan ultimately wants to beat, but Chen said Vietnam presents the more immediate hurdle.
    南韓依然是台灣最終想要擊敗的隊伍，但陳如治表示，越南則是眼前更迫切的障礙。
  • dominate v. 主宰，稱霸，在……中佔優勢
    Four players on Vietnam's roster play under Chen at Team Secret Whales (TSW), which has dominated the League of Legends Championship Pacific (LCP) throughout 2026.
    越南名單中有四名選手在陳如治麾下的 Secret Whales 戰隊（TSW）效力，該戰隊在整個 2026 年稱霸亞太英雄聯盟職業聯賽（LCP）。
  • familiarity n. 熟悉，精通
    said Chen, who believes his familiarity with their playing style and strategy will benefit Taiwan.
    陳如治表示，他相信自己對他們打法和策略的熟悉度將對台灣有利。
  • benefit v. 對……有益，受益
    said Chen, who believes his familiarity with their playing style and strategy will benefit Taiwan.
    陳如治表示，他相信自己對他們打法和策略的熟悉度將對台灣有利。
  • complicate v. 使複雜化

    台灣的備戰工作因打野選手余峻嘉和中路選手蔡明宏的俱樂部合約而變得複雜
  • commitment n. 承諾，投入，（此處指合約或工作義務）
    Taiwan's preparations are complicated by the club commitments of jungler Yu "JunJia" Jun-jia and mid laner Tsai "HongQ" Ming-hung
    台灣的備戰工作因打野選手余峻嘉和中路選手蔡明宏的俱樂部合約而變得複雜

    The full squad may have less than a week to practice together before departing Sept. 27

  • advance v. 晉級，前進
    Eight teams will play best-of-one round-robin matches in two groups Sept. 29-30, with the top two in each group advancing.
    8 支隊伍將於 9 月 29 日至 30 日在兩個組別中進行單循環賽事，每個組別的前兩名將晉級。

文法重點

  • 分詞構句作補充說明
    Eight teams will play best-of-one round-robin matches in two groups Sept. 29-30, with the top two in each group advancing.
    句尾使用了「with + 名詞 + 現在分詞（advancing）」的獨立分詞構句結構，用來補充說明比賽晉級的狀況，表示「隨著每個組別前兩名晉級」的附帶情況。
  • 非限定關代子句（補充說明主詞）
    said Chen, who believes his familiarity with their playing style and strategy will benefit Taiwan.
    逗號後面的「who believes...」是非限定關代子句，用來補充說明先行詞 Chen（陳如治）的看法，且在子句內又包含了一個省略連接詞 that 的名詞子句（believes [that] his familiarity...）。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Taiwan's preparations are complicated by the club commitments of jungler Yu...
    句型使用「be + 過去分詞（are complicated）」構成被動語態，強調備戰工作「受到」俱樂部合約事務的影響與干擾，動作的承受者是 Taiwan's preparations。
