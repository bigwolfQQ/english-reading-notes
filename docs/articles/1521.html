教育部擴增幼兒園

Ministry expanding preschools

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • allocate v. 分配、撥出（預算、資源等）
    The Ministry of Education is allocating more than NT$500 million (US$15.58 million) this year to expand public preschool capacity...
    教育部今年將撥款超過 5 億新台幣（合 1,558 萬美元），以擴大公立幼兒園的收托量能...
  • capacity n. 容量、收托量、生產力
    ...to expand public preschool capacity amid growing demand for affordable childcare among dual-income families.
    ...以擴大公立幼兒園的收托量能，因應雙薪家庭對平價托育日益增加的需求。
  • demand n. 需求、要求
    ...amid growing demand for affordable childcare among dual-income families.
    ...因應雙薪家庭對平價托育日益增加的需求。
  • alleviate v. 減輕、緩和（痛苦、負擔等）
    The measure is part of government efforts to alleviate the burden of parents with young children and boost the nation’s birthrate.
    此舉是政府減輕家長育兒負擔、提升國家生育率的政策環節之一。
  • subsidy n. 補助金、津貼
    ...while the ministry offers subsidies to fund the increases.
    ...教育部則提供補助經費來支應這些增長。
  • stipulate v. 規定、約定
    The Early Childhood Education and Care Act (幼兒教育及照顧法) stipulates that each preschool class for children aged two to under three may enroll no more than 16 children.
    《幼兒教育及照顧法》規定，招收二至未滿三歲幼兒的每個幼兒園班級，招收人數不得超過 16 人。
  • enroll v. 註冊、登記、招收、入學
    ...each preschool class for children aged two to under three may enroll no more than 16 children.
    ...招收二至未滿三歲幼兒的每個幼兒園班級，招收人數不得超過 16 人。
  • assessment n. 評估、評價（動詞為 assess）
    ...said that local governments are in charge of assessing and planning how to increase the number of preschools...
    ...表示各地方政府負責根據各區的兒童與教師人數以及實際招生情況，評估並規劃如何增加幼兒園...
  • in charge of phr. 負責、掌管
    ...said that local governments are in charge of assessing and planning how to increase the number of preschools...
    ...表示各地方政府負責根據各區的兒童與教師人數以及實際招生情況，評估並規劃如何增加幼兒園...
  • exempt adj. 被免除的、豁免的
    ...while children from low- and lower-middle-income families are exempt from fees.
    ...而低收入戶及中低收入戶家庭的兒童則免繳費用。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後位修飾 (Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Eligible families whose children do not attend public or quasi-public preschools can instead receive monthly childcare subsidies of NT$5,000 for the first child, NT$6,000 for the second and NT$7,000 for the subsequent children.
    句中的 subsequent children 前省略了形容詞或用代名詞概念，此處主要展示關係代名詞子句「whose children do not attend...」用來修飾前面的先行詞 Eligible families，在多益閱讀中常考關代與主被動分詞的修飾用法。
  • 現在分詞表達附帶狀況或同時進行的動作 (Present Participle indicating simultaneous action)
    ...the ministry offers subsidies to fund the increases, while the ministry offers... (參照原句: ...while the ministry offers subsidies to fund the increases.)
    文章中常使用連接詞引導分詞或子句來補充說明背景或同時發生的狀態。例如第 12 段「...while the number of preschool teachers has reached 270,000...」，用 while 引導子句來呈現「同時、然而」的對比或並存情況。
  • 被動語態的應用 (Passive Voice)
    Monthly fees at public preschools are capped at NT$1,000 for the first child...
    「are capped at」是被動語態，用來表達費用「被限制在」某個上限。在多益商業閱讀與合約、價格公告中，被動語態非常常見，用以客觀陳述規定或條款。
