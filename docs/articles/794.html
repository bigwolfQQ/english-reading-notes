《寡婦灣》以 14 項大獎打破艾美獎紀錄

‘Widow’s Bay’ breaks Emmys record with 14 wins

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • underperform v. 表現不如預期
    the HBO Max emergency room drama won no other Emmys and underperformed its expectations.
    但這部 HBO Max 的急診室題材戲劇未贏得其他任何艾美獎，表現低於預期。
  • unequivocally 毫無疑問地，明確地
    Every other comedy award went to Widow’s Bay, which unequivocally owned the night and broke the comedy series record set last year by The Studio when it won 13.
    其餘所有的喜劇獎項皆由《寡婦灣》包辦，該劇毫無疑問地稱霸了當晚，並打破了去年由《工作室》創下的 13 項喜劇影集得獎紀錄。
  • executive producer n. phr. 執行製作人
    and he gets one of the best comedy series trophies as an executive producer for Widow’s Bay.
    同時身為《寡婦灣》的執行製作人，他也共享了一座最佳喜劇影集獎盃。
  • eligible 具備參賽資格的
    The latter two shows were between seasons and not eligible this year.
    後兩部影集因正值季與季之間的空檔，今年不具備參賽資格。
  • nominee n. 被提名者
    The Pitt was the top drama nominee with 25 and Hacks was the top comedy nominee with 24
    《急診室》以 25 項提名成為獲得最多提名的戲劇，而《絕黛嬌醫》則以 24 項提名成為獲得最多提名的喜劇
  • telecast n. 電視播送，電視轉播
    as the Television Academy moved several categories off the main telecast to shorten the show.
    當時電視藝術與科學學院將多個類別移出主直播以縮短節目時間。
  • unknown n. 默默無名的人，新人
    Widow’s Bay brought a combination of younger, well-respected unknowns and widely known and beloved veterans to work on the show.
    《寡婦灣》結合了年輕、備受尊崇的新面孔，以及廣為人知且深受喜愛的資深演員來參與演出。
  • outcast n. 被逐出者，社會邊緣人
    plays Patricia Moyer, a city worker and social outcast dealing with a demonic darkness on Widow’s Bay.
    在劇中飾演派翠西亞·莫耶（Patricia Moyer），一名市府員工兼社會邊緣人，在《寡婦灣》中處理著一股惡魔般的黑暗力量。
  • cancellation n. 取消，停播
    COLBERT WINS AFTER CANCELLATION
    柯貝爾在遭到取消後獲獎

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作形容詞修飾（後置修飾）
    The Late Show with Stephen Colbert, taken off the air by CBS, won best variety series.
    句中的「taken off the air by CBS」是過去分詞片語，用來補充說明主詞「The Late Show with Stephen Colbert」，相當於一個被動語態的形容詞子句（which was taken off the air by CBS），使句子更為精簡。
  • 獨立分詞構句
    ‘THE PITT’ AND ‘HACKS’ FAIL TO RUN UP NUMBERS
    句中的「when it came time to claim trophies」是一個時間副詞子句，其中的「when it came time」常搭配不定詞片語（to claim trophies）使用，用來表達「當……的時機到來時」。
  • 關係代名詞引導的形容詞子句
    The 75-year-old has won eight Emmys overall in a career that was already more than respectable when she landed her defining role...
    句中的「that was already more than respectable」是由關係代名詞 that 引導的形容詞子句，用來修飾先行詞「a career」，限定並形容她的演藝生涯。
