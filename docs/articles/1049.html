ENGLISH DIGEST 實用美語(空中美語)

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • indispensable adj. 不可或缺的
    Hidden inside them are “rare earth elements” (REEs), indispensable to modern life yet central to global controversy.
    隱藏在它們之中的正是「稀土元素」（REEs），它們對現代生活不可或缺，卻也是全球爭議的核心。
  • controversy n. 爭議
    Hidden inside them are “rare earth elements” (REEs), indispensable to modern life yet central to global controversy.
    隱藏在它們之中的正是「稀土元素」（REEs），它們對現代生活不可或缺，卻也是全球爭議的核心。
  • abundant adj. 豐富的；大量的
    In fact, despite the name, many REEs are about as abundant in Earth’s crust as copper or lead.
    事實上，儘管名稱如此，許多稀土元素在地殼中的含量與銅或鉛差不多。
  • obtain v. 獲得；取得
    What makes them difficult to obtain is that they are rarely found in high concentrations...
    讓它們難以取得的原因在於它們很少以高濃度形式被發現……
  • exceptional adj. 傑出的； excepcional 在此指非凡的、卓越的
    They possess exceptional electrical, magnetic and other physical properties that ordinary metals cannot match.
    它們擁有普通金屬無法比擬的卓越導電性、磁性和其他物理特性。
  • contribute v. 貢獻；促成
    ...while others contribute to the bright, vivid colors seen on digital screens.
    ……而有些則讓數位螢幕呈現鮮明亮麗的色彩。
  • crucial adj. 至關重要的；關鍵的
    REEs also play a crucial role in advanced technologies, from clean energy to national defense.
    稀土元素在從乾淨能源到國防等先進技術中也扮演著關鍵角色。
  • shift v. 轉移；轉變
    As environmental regulations have tightened in the West, the industry has gradually shifted toward countries more willing and able to absorb these burdens.
    隨著西方國家的環保法規日益嚴格，該產業已逐漸轉向更願意且能夠吸收這些負擔的國家。
  • control v. 控制；掌控
    ...and today it controls the lion’s share of both mining and processing.
    ……如今已控制了開採和加工的絕大部分份額。
  • depend v. 依賴；視……而定
    If our technological infrastructure and thus our political security depend on these materials, we can no longer afford to treat our devices as though their convenience carried no environmental or political price.
    如果我們的科技基礎建設乃至政治安全都依賴於這些材料，我們便不能再將手中科技產品的便利性視為彷彿毫無環境與政治代價的存在。

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作後置修飾 (Participle phrase as post-modifier)
    Hidden inside them are “rare earth elements” (REEs), indispensable to modern life yet central to global controversy.
    此句為地方副詞片語提前的倒裝句型（原句主詞為 “rare earth elements”）。過去分詞片語「Hidden inside them」置於句首修飾主詞，用來加強語氣並描繪稀土元素藏匿的位置。
  • 現在分詞短語作結果狀語 (Present participle indicating result)
    ...as the process generates toxic acids, radioactive wastewater and chemical waste capable of contaminating soil and groundwater.
    句尾的「capable of contaminating...」是形容詞片語省略了關代與be動詞（which are capable of...），用來修飾前面的 chemical waste，補充說明其具有污染土壤與地下水的能力。
  • 假設語氣（與現在事實相反）(Subjunctive mood for present counterfactual)
    ...we can no longer afford to treat our devices as though their convenience carried no environmental or political price.
    「as though」（彷彿、好像）後方接子句時，若要表達與現在事實相反的假設，動詞需使用過去式（在此使用 carried）。這表示事實上這些設備的便利性背後確實帶有環境與政治代價。
