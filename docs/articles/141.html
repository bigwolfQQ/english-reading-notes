文法重點

  • 分詞構句表附帶狀況 (With + Noun + V-ing / V-ed)
    His body was returned to Serbia on a government plane on Thursday, with his casket draped in flags and welcomed with military honors.
    此處使用「with + 受詞 (his casket) + 過去分詞 (draped / welcomed)」的結構，用來補充說明遺體被運回時的伴隨狀況（棺木覆蓋著國旗並受到軍禮歡迎），在新聞英語中非常常見。
  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Past Participle as Post-modifier)
    Mladic died last week while serving a life sentence in The Hague for war crimes committed during the 1990s war in Bosnia.
    句中的「committed during the 1990s war in Bosnia」是過去分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「war crimes」，相當於形容詞子句「which were committed during...」，省略了關代與be動詞，使語氣更為緊湊。
  • 過去分詞作主詞補語 / 被動語態結構
    Despite this, he is still lauded by some Serbs as a hero, particularly in Bosnia’s Serb entity Republika Srpska — where his death was marked with vigils and gatherings.
    句型「be + 過去分詞 (lauded / marked)」構成被動語態，強調動作的承受者（他被讚譽為英雄、他的死被紀念）。
  • 現在分詞短語作表附帶狀況的補充說明
    As his coffin was driven from the airport toward the capital, groups of mostly young men gathered on motorway overpasses, displaying banners honoring Mladic and lighting flares.
    句尾的「displaying banners... and lighting flares」為現在分詞短語，表示主句動作發生時同時進行的動作（一邊展示橫幅、一邊點燃信號彈）。
