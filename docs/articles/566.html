台灣需要一個經濟防護罩

Taiwan needs an economic shield

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • deter v. 嚇阻，阻止
    Taiwan and its partners have devoted enormous attention to deterring a Chinese military attack.
    台灣與其夥伴們投入了巨大的注意力來嚇阻中國的軍事攻擊。
  • acquisition n. 獲得，取得
    Taiwan is increasing defense spending, acquiring asymmetric capabilities, expanding civil defense and preparing critical infrastructure for a potential blockade or invasion.
    台灣正在增加國防支出、獲得不對稱作戰能力、擴大民防，並為潛在的封鎖或入侵準備關鍵基礎設施。
  • objective n. 目標，目的
    These measures are necessary, but China does not rely exclusively on military power to pursue its political objectives...
    這些措施是必要的，但中國並非完全依賴軍事力量來追求其政治目標……
  • preferential adj. 優惠的，優先的
    Taiwan has experienced restrictions on agricultural products and the suspension of preferential tariffs.
    台灣曾經歷過農產品限制以及優惠關稅的暫停。
  • deteriorate v. 惡化，變壞
    Australia faced restrictions affecting products including barley, wine and coal after relations with Beijing deteriorated.
    澳洲在與北京關係惡化後，面臨了影響大麥、維基、煤炭等產品的限制。
  • coercion n. 脅迫，強迫
    These cases demonstrate a fundamental advantage China possesses when exercising economic coercion...
    這些案例證明了中國在施行經濟脅迫時擁有的一項根本優勢……
  • facilitate v. 促進，使容易
    When Beijing restricts imports in response to a political disagreement, participants could increase purchases of affected products, accelerate investment, provide financing, facilitate access to alternative markets or temporarily reduce trade barriers.
    當北京為了回應政治分歧而限制進口時，參與國可以增加購買受影響的產品、加速投資、提供融資、促進替代市場的准入，或是暫時降低貿易壁壘。
  • indispensable adj. 不可或缺的
    China remains one of the world’s largest economies and an indispensable trading partner for many countries.
    中國依然是世界最大經濟體之一，也是許多國家不可或缺的貿易夥伴。
  • capitulate v. 投降，屈服
    Australia did not capitulate politically, and many restrictions were eventually removed as bilateral relations improved.
    澳洲並未在政治上屈服，隨著雙邊關係改善，許多限制最終也被取消了。
  • subsidiary adj. 替代的，備用的（文章中用 alternative）
    Economic coercion becomes less effective when its target has alternatives and external support.
    當目標國擁有替代方案與外部支援時，經濟脅迫的效用就會降低。
  • consequence n. 後果，影響
    A government might eventually conclude that the economic benefits expected from relations with China have not materialized, that dependence on China has become excessive, or simply that restoring relations with Taiwan better serves its interests. Yet changing course could expose it to Chinese economic retaliation.
    一個政府最終可能會得出結論：從與中國的關係中預期獲得的經濟利益並未實現，對中國的依賴已經過度，或者單純是恢復與台灣的關係更能符合其利益。然而，改變方針可能會使其暴露於中國的經濟報復之下。
  • vulnerability n. 脆弱性，弱點
    It is also a vulnerability.
    這同時也是一項脆弱性。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Postmodifier)
    Chinese leaders contemplating force against Taiwan must assess not only whether...
    句中的「contemplating force against Taiwan」是現在分詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞「Chinese leaders」，相當於「who contemplate force against Taiwan」的簡化形式，用以使商業與新聞英語更為簡潔。
  • 假設語氣第二類 (Second Conditional)
    If a former Taiwanese diplomatic partner independently decided to restore relations with Taipei, it would know that it would not necessarily confront the consequences alone.
    此句用來假設目前不大可能發生或純屬假設的情境。條件句（If 子句）使用過去式動詞「decided」，而主要子句則使用「would + 原形動詞（would know / would not necessarily confront）」，表達對未來假設性結果的推論。
  • not only... but also... 結構與對稱原則
    Chinese leaders contemplating force against Taiwan must assess not only whether the People’s Liberation Army could achieve its military objectives, but also the economic and political consequences that would follow.
    這個對等連接詞用來連接兩個並列的名詞子句或受詞。要注意「not only」後面接的是子句（whether...），「but also」後面接的也是對等的名詞片語（the economic and political consequences...），保持文法結構的平衡與對稱。
  • 間接問句 (Indirect Questions)
    Taiwan’s partners have spent years asking how many countries might help defend it if China attacks. They should also ask how many countries could help ensure Beijing never believes an attack is worth the price.
    句中的「how many countries might help defend it...」與「how many countries could help ensure...」作為動詞「asking」與「ask」的受詞，採用「疑問詞 + 主詞 + 動詞」的間接問句語序，而非直述句的倒裝語序。
