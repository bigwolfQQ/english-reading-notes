沈伯洋舉辦首場競選車隊掃街；蔣萬安敦促協調辯論事宜

Shen holds first campaign motorcade; Chiang urges debate coordination

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • candidate n. 候選人
    Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) Taipei mayoral candidate Puma Shen (沈伯洋) held his first campaign motorcade in the capital yesterday...
    民主進步黨（民進黨）台北市長候選人沈伯洋昨天在首都舉辦了他的首場競選車隊掃街活動...
  • incumbent adj. 現任的
    ...while incumbent Chinese Nationalist Party (KMT) Mayor Chiang Wan-an (蔣萬安) urged Shen’s camp to discuss arrangements for a televised election debate or debates.
    ...而現任中國國民黨（國民黨）市長蔣萬安則敦促沈伯洋陣營討論電視選舉辯論的安排事宜。
  • urge v. 敦促、力勸
    Chiang said his campaign team would attend and urged Shen’s representatives to do the same.
    蔣萬安表示他的競選團隊將會出席，並敦促沈伯洋的代表也這麼做。
  • proposal n. 提議、建議
    The DPP candidate had made the proposal to Chiang in person when the two met at a temple event on Saturday evening.
    民進黨候選人是在週六傍晚於一場廟宇活動中兩人相遇時，當面向蔣萬安提出這項提議的。
  • focus n. 焦點、重心
    Chiang said the focus should be on actually holding debates, rather than turning them into a campaign talking point...
    蔣萬安表示，重點應該放在 actually 實際舉辦辯論，而不是將其變成競選話題...
  • representatives n. 代表
    ...with the broadcaster scheduled to meet representatives of both sides today to discuss the details.
    ...該電視台預定於今天會見雙方代表以討論細節。
  • vying v. (current participle as adj.) 角逐、競爭
    ...because voters in Taiwan’s 22 cities and counties will cast ballots for candidates vying for nine different local government posts...
    ...因為台灣22個縣市的選民將投下選票，選出角逐九種不同地方政府職位的候選人...
  • consent n. 同意、授權
    ...and signed consent forms, with the broadcaster scheduled to meet representatives of both sides today to discuss the details.
    ...並簽署了同意書，該電視台預定於今天會見雙方代表以討論細節。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    ...saying a single event would not allow enough time for detailed discussions of individual policy issues.
    句中的「saying...」是由連接詞省略與主詞一致後所形成的現在分詞片語，用來補充說明沈伯洋前面所說的話或當時的情況，等同於副詞子句「while he said...」的簡化用法。
  • 獨立分詞構句 (Absolute Nominative Construction)
    ...with the broadcaster scheduled to meet representatives of both sides today to discuss the details.
    「with + 名詞 + 分詞」結構在多益測驗中非常常見，用來表示附帶狀況。在此句中「the broadcaster」是邏輯主詞，「scheduled」是過去分詞表被動，指電視台預定於今天開會。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    In total, elections will be held for 11,051 officials at all levels of local government.
    句型使用未來式的被動語態「will be held」，強調選舉是被舉辦的，焦點放在選舉這個動作的承受者（elections）上，而非舉辦的主體。
