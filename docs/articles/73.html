美國國防企業增加在台採購自主系統零組件

U.S. defense firms increase Taiwan sourcing for autonomous systems

➜ 到Focus Taiwan (CNA)閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • supply chain n. 供應鏈
    Taiwan is playing a growing role in the supply chains of U.S. defense technology companies developing drones and autonomous systems, industry representatives said Thursday.
    業界代表週四表示，隨著美國開發無人機與自主系統的國防科技公司持續發展，台灣在這些公司的供應鏈中扮演著日益重要的角色。
  • component n. 零組件、成分
    Taiwan's share of the materials and components used in Shield AI aircraft has risen from less than 1 percent in 2024 to 16 percent...
    「盾牌人工智慧」飛行器所使用的材料與零組件中，台灣的佔比已從 2024 年的不到 1% 上升至 16%...
  • partner n. 夥伴、合夥人
    "If you want to be the best partner, you have to be close to your partner," Chen said...
    陳...表示：「如果你想成為最佳夥伴，你就必須貼近你的夥伴。」
  • integrate v. 整合、結合
    The company has also integrated its Hivemind autonomous piloting software with Taiwanese platforms...
    該公司也已將其 Hivemind 自主飛行軟體與台灣平台進行整合...
  • source v. 採購、向外採購（料件等）
    Meanwhile, AeroVironment has sourced more than US$20 million worth of panels, sensors, batteries and other components from Taiwan over the past three years...
    同時，AeroVironment 在過去三年中，已向台灣採購了價值超過 2,000 萬美元的面板、感測器、電池和其他零組件...
  • contractor n. 承包商
    The U.S. defense contractor, which is working with Taiwanese partners on its Switchblade 300 loitering munition, hopes to develop and manufacture additional systems in Taiwan...
    這家正與台灣夥伴合作開發「彈簧刀 300」巡飛彈藥的美國國防承包商，希望能在台灣開發並製造更多系統...
  • cooperation n. 合作
    The growing cooperation indicates that Taiwan is moving beyond supplying individual components toward greater involvement in the development and integration of autonomous systems...
    日益緊密的合作顯示，台灣正從供應單一零組件，轉向更深度參與自主系統的開發與整合...
  • standardize v. 使標準化
    Expanding that role, however, will require Taiwanese companies to standardize components and improve system interoperability...
    不過，要擴大這項角色將需要台灣企業將零組件標準化，並提升系統互通性...
  • challenge n. 挑戰
    Another challenge is establishing supply chains free of Chinese inputs, said Trent W. Emeneker...
    Trent W. Emeneker 表示，另一個挑戰是建立不含中國投入要素的供應鏈。
  • scale n. 規模
    U.S.-Taiwan cooperation also remains short of the production scale and operational readiness needed to deploy uncrewed systems widely, he cautioned.
    他警告說，美台合作仍然達不到大規模部署無人系統所需的生產規模與作戰備妥度。
  • invest v. 投資
    "A feasible business model is very important so that people are willing to invest," Sheng said.
    沈表示：「一個可行的商業模式非常重要，這樣大家才願意投資。」

文法重點

  • 分詞片語作為後置定用語（Present Participle as Postmodifier）
    Taiwan is playing a growing role in the supply chains of U.S. defense technology companies developing drones and autonomous systems...
    句中的「developing drones and autonomous systems」是由現在分詞（develop）帶受詞形成的形容詞片語，用來修飾前面的名詞 companies。這種結構可以精簡原本的關係代名詞子句（即 companies that are developing...），在新聞英文中非常常見。
  • 現在完成進行式（Present Perfect Continuous / Has been + V-ing）
    The U.S. defense contractor, which is working with Taiwanese partners on its Switchblade 300 loitering munition, hopes to develop and manufacture additional systems in Taiwan...
    雖然此處嚴格來說是「which is working」（現在進行式），但用來表達持續進行中的狀態。而在報導企業動態或長期合作時，常會使用現在完成式或進行式來強調「從過去持續到現在」的商業行為（例如文章前面出現的 has risen、has established 等）。
  • 動名詞片語作主詞與受詞（Gerund Phrases as Subject and Object）
    Expanding that role, however, will require Taiwanese companies to standardize components and improve system interoperability...
    句首的「Expanding that role」是動名詞片語，作為整句的主詞，中文譯為「擴大這項角色」。在多益閱讀與商業英文中，常利用動名詞片語來表達一項行動或策略作為主詞或受詞。
  • 條件句（Conditional Sentence with Imperative）
    "If you want to be the best partner, you have to be close to your partner," Chen said...
    此句為第一類條件句（First Conditional）的變形，用「If + 現在式動詞」表達真實的假設條件，而主要子句則使用助動詞 have to 加上原形動詞來表示強烈的建議或必然性，常用於引述發言與商業談判情境。
