文法重點

  • 過去分詞片語作後位修飾 (Appositive / Past Participle Phrase)
    Organized by the Ministry of Transportation and Communications’ Railway Bureau, the Taiwan Pavilion at InnoTrans has 14 partners from academia, research institutes and the private sector...
    句首的「Organized by...」是過去分詞片語，省略了主詞與be動詞（原句形如：Which is organized by...），用來修飾後方的名詞主詞「the Taiwan Pavilion」。這種結構在多益閱讀測驗的文法題和長篇閱讀中非常常見，能簡練地表達被動關係。
  • 現在分詞表附帶結果或說明 (Present Participle as Result/Modifier)
    ...aimed at demonstrating resilient railway solutions and attracting international partners, officials said at the event.
    句中的「aimed at... and attracting...」使用了分詞結構，補充說明前面國家館設立的目的。「attracting」與前面的「demonstrating」並列，皆由 aimed 帶出，用來修飾說明前面子句的動作意圖。
  • 關係代名詞子句 (Relative Clause with 'which')
    The ministry said that 26 Taiwanese firms are hosting exhibits at the event, which runs through tomorrow.
    句尾的「which runs through tomorrow」是非限定性關係子句（用逗號隔開），其中的關係代名詞 「which」代替前面的整起事件（the event），並補充說明該活動將持續到明天。多益測驗常考非限定性子句中不可用 that 代替 which 的規則。
  • 對等連接詞連接複雜子句與現在分詞作結果狀語 (Present Participle for Result)
    Taiwan would apply its expertise in AI, virtual simulations and information technology to bolster its capabilities in train design..., improving its capability to facilitate the growth of the rail industry in Taiwan, Chen said.
    句尾的「improving its capability...」是現在分詞作結果狀語，表示前面「應用專業知識以強化能力」的這個動作，進一步自然而然地帶出了「提升促進產業成長能力」的結果，這也是商業英文新聞報導中常見的句構方式。
