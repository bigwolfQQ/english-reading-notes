Taiwanese researcher Wong receives U.S. national chemistry award

單字解析

  • recognition n. 讚賞、表彰、認可
    The institute said the award represents another major international recognition of Wong's longstanding contributions to chemistry and biomedical science.
    該研究所表示，此獎項代表了國際間對翁啟惠長期以來對化學與生醫科學貢獻的又一項重大肯定。
  • contribution n. 貢獻
    Wong, who is also a professor at Scripps Research in California and a distinguished research fellow at Academia Sinica's Genomics Research Center, was recognized for his "outstanding contributions to carbohydrate chemistry"...
    翁啟惠同時也是加州斯克里普斯研究所教授及中央研究院基因體研究中心特聘研究員，他因「對碳水化合物化學的傑出貢獻」……而獲得表彰。
  • research n. / v. 研究
    Wong's research team at Scripps Research uses bioorganic and synthetic chemistry to investigate the diverse biological functions of carbohydrates, including glycans, the IBMI said.
    生策會表示，斯克里普斯研究所的翁啟惠研究團隊利用生物有機化學與合成化學，來探討碳水化合物（包括聚糖）的多樣化生物功能。
  • focus on phr. v. 專注於、聚焦於
    The team focuses on understanding how glycosylation affects protein structure and function and how these mechanisms influence the human body...
    該團隊專注於理解醣基化如何影響蛋白質的結構與功能，以及這些機制如何影響人體……
  • affect v. 影響
    The team focuses on understanding how glycosylation affects protein structure and function and how these mechanisms influence the human body...
    該團隊專注於理解醣基化如何影響蛋白質的結構與功能，以及這些機制如何影響人體……
  • development n. 發展、開發
    The team's research has contributed to the development of next-generation approaches with broad implications for biomedical research and drug discovery worldwide, according to the IBMI.
    根據生策會表示，該團隊的研究有助於開發下一代的方法，對全球的生醫研究與藥物開發具有廣泛的影響。
  • discovery n. 發現、發現
    The team's research has contributed to the development of next-generation approaches with broad implications for biomedical research and drug discovery worldwide, according to the IBMI.
    根據生策會表示，該團隊的研究有助於開發下一代的方法，對全球的生醫研究與藥物開發具有廣泛的影響。
  • establish v. 建立、設立
    The Alfred Bader Award was established in 1986 and is one of the ACS National Awards.
    阿爾弗雷德·巴德獎設立於1986年，是美國化學學會國家獎項之一。
  • achievement n. 成就
    It recognizes exceptional achievements in research and supports continued advancement in the field, the statement said.
    聲明指出，該獎項旨在表彰研究上的卓越成就，並支持該領域的持續進展。
  • ceremony n. 典禮、儀式
    Wong has been invited to the award ceremony and banquet, scheduled for March 2027 in New Orleans, according to the statement.
    聲明指出，翁啟惠已受邀出席預定於2027年3月在紐奧良舉行的頒獎典禮與宴會。

文法重點

  • 非限定性關係子句 (Non-defining Relative Clause)
    Wong, who is also a professor at Scripps Research in California and a distinguished research fellow at Academia Sinica's Genomics Research Center, was recognized for his "outstanding contributions..."
    句中的 "who is also a professor..." 前後有逗號隔開，用來補充說明主詞 Wong 的身分，這類子句即使拿掉也不影響主要句子的完整意思。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    The Alfred Bader Award was established in 1986 and is one of the ACS National Awards.
    句中的 "was established" 採用了被動語態（be + 過去分詞），強調獎項是由他人或機構在 1986 年所創立的，焦點在於獎項本身而非創立者。
  • 分詞作為形容詞 (Participle as Adjective)
    Wong has been invited to the award ceremony and banquet, scheduled for March 2027 in New Orleans, according to the statement.
    句中的 "scheduled for March 2027" 是過去分詞片語作為後置修飾語，用來修飾前面的 ceremony and banquet，相當於縮寫自形容詞子句 "which is scheduled for..."。
