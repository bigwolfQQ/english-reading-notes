文法重點

  • with + 名詞 + 分詞（複合結構 / 附帶狀況）
    Authorities fined drone operators a total of NT$16 million (US$505,849) in 72 cases last year and the first half of this year, with more than 80 percent of the contraventions occurring at airports, reservoirs and science parks...
    「with + 受詞 + 分詞」用來表達附帶狀況或背景情況。在此句中，with 引導補充說明 80% 以上違規發生的地點（occurring 為現在分詞，表示主動與進行的狀態），翻譯為「其中超過 80% 的違規發生在...」。
  • 被動語態 (Passive Voice)
    Drones are prohibited in reservoirs and science parks, and flying them without approval can result in fines of NT$30,000 to NT$1.5 million under the Civil Aviation Act...
    被動語態的結構為「be 動詞 + 過去分詞 (V-pp)」，用來強調動作的承受者或當動作執行者不重要時使用。句型「Drones are prohibited...」強調無人機是被禁止（由法律或當局規定）的。
  • 現在分詞子句作後位修飾（形容詞功能）
    Notifications from government apps about restricted airspace might not be enough for beginners, who might accidently contravene regulations, drone operator Wu Ming-yang (吳明陽) said.
    在此句中，形容詞子句「who might accidently contravene regulations」用來補充說明前面的先行詞 beginners（新手）。逗號後使用關係代名詞 who 引導非限定子句，提供額外的資訊。
  • 動名詞片語當主詞
    Wu called for the legalization of drone flights during the 30 minutes before sunrise and after sunset so photographers can capture better footage.
