聖嬰現象引發的乾旱重創斯里蘭卡農場

El Nino-driven drought slams Sri Lankan farms

➜ 到Taipei Times閱讀完整原文

單字解析

  • severe adj. 嚴重的、劇烈的
    He has fallen victim to one of the country’s worst El Nino-driven droughts.
    他成了該國最嚴重的聖嬰現象乾旱的受害者之一。
  • average n. / adj. 平均、平均值
    Sri Lanka is among the South Asian countries hardest hit by weather disruption this year, with some key farming districts recording rainfall deficits of 85 percent to 100 percent below long-term averages.
    斯里蘭卡是今年受天候異常打擊最嚴重的南亞國家之一，部分關鍵農業區記錄到雨量虧損比長期平均值低 85% 至 100%。
  • impact n. / v. 影響、衝擊
    The Sri Lankan government has said the impact this year could be the worst in decades and that water levels at hundreds of such tanks in the country have dropped to about 10 percent.
    斯里蘭卡政府表示，今年的影響可能是幾十年來最嚴重的一次，該國數百個此類水庫的水位已經下降到約 10%。
  • accessible adj. 可接近的、易到達的、可取得的
    Many houses on the main roads and those that are easily accessible get drinking water trucked in every three to five days...
    主幹道上的許多房屋以及交通便利的房屋每三到五天就會有卡車運送飲用水...
  • income n. 收入、收益
    Farmers’ incomes, as expected, are taking a big hit, making it difficult to repay loans and even manage proper meals daily.
    不出所料，農民的收入大受打擊，這使得償還貸款甚至每天吃上一頓像樣的飯菜都變得困難。
  • loan n. 貸款、借款
    Farmers’ incomes, as expected, are taking a big hit, making it difficult to repay loans and even manage proper meals daily.
    不出所料，農民的收入大受打擊，這使得償還貸款甚至每天吃上一頓像樣的飯菜都變得困難。
  • crisis n. 危機
    Before the drought, Pathmasena said he earned about 20,000 Sri Lanka rupees (US$60) a month growing cowpeas, but the crisis means he has lost it all...
    Pathmasena 表示，乾旱之前，他種植豇豆每個月大約能賺 20,000 斯里蘭卡盧比（60 美元），但這場危機意味著他失去了所有收入...
  • survive v. 存活、倖存
    ...forcing him to become a daily-wage laborer to survive.
    ...迫使他成為日薪勞工以求生存。

文法重點

  • 現在分詞片語作後位修飾 (Present Participle Phrase as Post-modifier)
    El Nino is a weather pattern characterized by unusually warm sea-surface temperatures in the central and eastern equatorial Pacific Ocean, often bringing drier conditions to parts of Asia and severe storms elsewhere.
    句中的 "often bringing..." 為現在分詞片語，用來補充說明前面的氣候模式所帶來的結果或伴隨的狀況，等同於副詞子句 "and it often brings..."。
  • 虛受詞 it 的用法 (Exploratory / Dummy Object 'it')
    Farmers’ incomes, as expected, are taking a big hit, making it difficult to repay loans and even manage proper meals daily.
    句型為 "make + it + 形容詞 + 名詞片語/不定詞"。因為真正的受詞（不定詞片語 "to repay loans and even manage proper meals daily"）太長，所以用虛受詞 "it" 代替，置於形容詞 "difficult" 之後，真正的受詞則移至句尾。
  • 分詞構句表條件或情況 (Participle Clause of Condition/Time)
    On some days, when we have nothing, we skip meals...
    此處雖然使用完整的副詞子句 "when we have nothing"，但在新聞英語中常透過分詞或簡化結構來表達條件或時間。文章多處運用分詞來精簡句子結構，使行文更緊湊流暢。
